# 5-HT6 Antagonist Negative-Class Map - 2026 Scan

This branch now reads like a mechanistically plausible failure class. The preclinical story stayed attractive because 5-HT6 blockade can disinhibit glutamate, acetylcholine, dopamine, and noradrenaline. The 2018 review already framed the class as promising while warning that later phase III [[idalopirdine]] and [[intepirdine]] programs had failed, and the 2025 review still treats the receptor as a plausible target for cognition-related effects in AD and schizophrenia. But the clinical record is mostly one phase 2 hint after another followed by larger null trials: [[sb-742457]] had an early global-impression signal but not a stable ADAS-Cog win; [[idalopirdine]] looked interesting in phase 2, then failed in three phase 3 trials; [[masupirdine]] was negative on primary cognition and only generated post hoc behavioral hypotheses; [[intepirdine]] failed in dementia with Lewy bodies. Standalone entity pages now exist for all four compounds, and they should be used as negative comparators for future serotonergic cognition claims rather than treated as a live enhancer lane.

For comparator framing, see [[pharmacology-comparator-map]] and [[negative-evidence-watchlist-for-cognition-interventions]]. For the broader serotonergic mechanism context, see [[medicine-cognition-summary]] and [[neurocognitive-interventions-under-study]].

## What Changed Since 2024

- The class now has enough late-stage human data to treat it as a negative comparator: idalopirdine's phase 3 program failed, masupirdine's phase 2 cognition trial was negative, intepirdine failed in DLB, and SB-742457 never produced a durable cognition win.
- The 2018 update already contained the key warning: the rationale stayed attractive, but the late-stage idalopirdine and intepirdine story had not converted into durable benefit.
- The 2025 review keeps the biological rationale alive, which is exactly why this branch is worth retaining as a failure case instead of deleting it.
- The masupirdine post hoc paper and the early SB-742457 study show how subgroup or global-impression hints can survive even when the primary cognition story collapses.

## Evidence Tiering

- Mechanistic / preclinical rationale: still attractive, especially for neurotransmitter disinhibition and network plasticity.
- Phase 2 mixed: SB-742457 and idalopirdine showed early hints, but not enough to stabilize a class claim.
- Phase 3 negative: idalopirdine's large AD program did not beat placebo.
- Follow-on negative: intepirdine in DLB also failed on the primary endpoint and did not rescue the class.
- Post hoc only: masupirdine's behavioral-symptom analysis is hypothesis-generating, not a cognition win.

## Safety / Adverse Events

- The class is not mainly a tolerability failure. Most papers describe acceptable safety or tolerability.
- Idalopirdine had more adverse events than placebo in meta-analysis, including GI symptoms and liver-enzyme elevations.
- Intepirdine had slightly more gastrointestinal adverse events than placebo, but no major new safety signal.
- Masupirdine and SB-742457 were generally framed as tolerable, which makes the repeated efficacy miss more important than the adverse-event profile.

## Responder Phenotypes

- No reproducible responder phenotype has survived across the class.
- Idalopirdine meta-analytic subgroup analyses hinted at a possible moderate-AD / higher-dose / male-skewed effect, but the overall program still failed.
- Masupirdine's post hoc symptom signal came from agitation/psychosis-enriched subgroups, not from a validated cognition-enriched population.
- SB-742457 and intepirdine did not establish a stable enrichment rule that would justify a healthy-adult or general AD enhancement claim.

## Protocol Parameters

- Masupirdine: 50 mg/day or 100 mg/day for 26 weeks in moderate AD, added to donepezil and memantine.
- Idalopirdine LADDER: 90 mg/day, or 30 mg three times daily, for 24 weeks in moderate AD on donepezil.
- Idalopirdine phase 3: 10 mg/day, 30 mg/day, or 60 mg/day for 24 weeks in mild-to-moderate AD added to cholinesterase inhibitors.
- SB-742457 phase 2: 5 mg, 15 mg, or 35 mg once daily for 24 weeks.
- SB-742457 later trials: 15 mg or 35 mg daily in mild-to-moderate AD, including an adjunct-to-donepezil design.
- Intepirdine HEADWAY-DLB: 35 mg/day or 70 mg/day for 24 weeks.

## Open Contradictions

- The main contradiction is the early phase 2 optimism versus later phase 3 failure.
- The class still has a clean mechanistic story, which is why repeated clinical misses matter more here than in a clearly implausible branch.
- Masupirdine's post hoc behavioral signal can be useful for hypothesis generation, but it should not be allowed to overwrite the negative primary cognition result.
- The branch is a reminder that "serotonergic" is not itself a cognition mechanism; endpoint and phenotype matter more than receptor label.
