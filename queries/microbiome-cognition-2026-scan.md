# Microbiome-Cognition Interventions - 2026 Scan

## Summary

The microbiome branch has more human RCTs than it used to, but the effect pattern is still inconsistent and highly phenotype-dependent. The clearest positive signals show up in older adults with MCI or declining memory, not in healthy adults. In healthy older adults, most studies are null or only weakly positive on narrow task measures such as executive-function reaction time, with the newer 2'-FL result looking responder-gated rather than class-defining.

What changed since 2024:

- A 2025 meta-analysis pooled 34 RCTs and found small improvements on MMSE, MoCA, and categorical fluency, but certainty was low to very low and adverse events were not reduced.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41291638-probiotic-supplements-cognitive-function-meta-analysis.md]
- A second 2025 meta-analysis in older adults pooled 20 RCTs and found only a non-significant positive trend on cognition, with a similarly non-significant inflammatory trend, which is a useful reality check against over-reading the earlier pooled result.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40483001-probiotics-cognitive-performance-older-adults-meta-analysis.md]
- A 2024 MCI/AD-specific meta-analysis stayed null across all reported cognitive domains, so the branch still depends on phenotype and endpoint choice rather than a class-wide effect.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40034358-probiotics-mci-ad-meta-analysis.md]
- A 2025 crossover trial in healthy older adults found an acute executive-function reaction-time benefit of about 65 ms, but chronic dosing did not improve memory and only nudged mood-reactivity measures.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40222448-multistrain-probiotic-healthy-older-adults-cognition.md]
- A 2025 elderly trial comparing micro-encapsulated with non-encapsulated L. rhamnosus HN001 found processing-speed and connectivity effects, but memory, sleep, depression, and perceived stress were mostly unchanged.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40976401-microencapsulation-probiotics-brain-structure-function.md]
- A 2025 CBT-LR5 pilot in suspected MCI reported improvements in MOCA-K naming and delayed recall in the low-dose arm, but the sample was only 20 participants and the dose-response pattern was awkward.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40005019-cbt-lr5-mci-cognition.md]
- A 2025 GOS trial in healthy young females moved Bifidobacterium and some neurochemical measures, but the cognition signal stayed narrow: faster reaction times appeared mainly in the high-anxious subgroup, not as a broad group-level effect.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40532960-galacto-oligosaccharides-gut-brain-axis-young-females.md]
- A 2025/2026 obesity-stratified sub-analysis of a 10-week diet intervention found that in obese older adults, fat loss correlated with episodic memory and cognitive flexibility gains, while microbiome and metabolite shifts tracked those changes; the non-obese pattern was weaker and sometimes paradoxical.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41907517-obesity-associated-gut-microbiome-influences-diet-induced-metabolic-and-cognitive-outcomes-in-older-adults.md]
- A 2025 2'-fucosyllactose RCT in healthy older adults missed its primary immune endpoint, but Bifidobacterium responders showed extra metabolic changes and better visual-memory performance, which keeps the signal in a narrow responder bucket rather than a broad enhancement bucket.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40738103-2-fucosyllactose-older-adults-cognition.md]
- A 2025 FMT review of clinical trials found 14 cognition-relevant human studies, only three randomized/blinded trials among them, and no trials in cognitively healthy people, which keeps FMT in the disease-state comparator lane rather than the healthy-enhancement lane.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40686934-faecal-microbiota-transplantation-cognitive-function-review.md]
- The Army acute-stress probiotic/prebiotic protocol is now marked completed with no results posted, so it should be treated as a freshness watch item rather than evidence of efficacy.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct05392556-prebiotic-probiotic-acute-stress-completed.md]
- A 2023 CleverAge biota crossover trial found no meaningful cognitive, affective, or physical benefit from human probiotics/prebiotics in community-dwelling older adults with normal or mildly impaired cognition.^[raw/articles/pubmed-37502424-cleverage-biota-probiotics-memory-psychological-physical.md]
- The 2022 LGG trial in middle-aged and older adults suggests microbiome state and cognitive status may interact, but the signal was strongest in the MCI subgroup and correlation-driven rather than a simple universal improvement.^[raw/articles/pubmed-36228569-lgg-mci-gut-microbiome-probiotics.md]
- GV-971 / sodium oligomannate is now tracked separately from probiotic/prebiotic products because it is a drug-like, geography-limited Alzheimer's treatment with a China phase 3 signal, a mixed phase 2 precursor, a gut-brain mechanism paper, and unresolved global replication. See [[sodium-oligomannate-gv971-2026-scan]].^[raw/articles/pubmed-33731209-sodium-oligomannate-phase3-china.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-32928279-sodium-oligomannate-phase2.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct04520412-gv971-green-memory-global.md]^[raw/articles/nature-2019-gv971-gut-microbiota-neuroinflammation.md]

## Evidence Tiering

- Meta-analysis: one recent pooled RCT synthesis suggests small average benefit, but certainty is low and heterogeneity is substantial.
- Meta-analysis: the newer broader older-adult synthesis is only directionally positive, while the MCI/AD-specific synthesis is null across domains.
- Positive RCTs: strongest in MCI or suspected MCI, especially memory-oriented endpoints.
- Null RCTs: healthy older adults and community-dwelling older adults often show no cognitive gain.
- Phenotype-gated signals now include young females with high anxiety, obese older adults whose body-composition shifts track cognitive change, and Bifidobacterium responders in the 2'-FL trial.
- Mechanistic / proxy RCTs: some studies move microbiota composition, serotonin, connectivity, or inflammation without robust cognitive change.
- FMT is not a healthy-adult enhancer story: the current human evidence sits in neurological disease cohorts and still lacks a healthy-volunteer trial.

## Effect Size Snapshot

- Meta-analysis: MMSE MD 4.23 at 12 weeks; MoCA MD 1.21; CFT MD 3.94, all with low or very low certainty.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41291638-probiotic-supplements-cognitive-function-meta-analysis.md]
- Meta-analysis: older-adult pooled cognition effect SMD 0.19 (95% CI -0.13 to 0.52) and inflammation SMD -0.44 (95% CI -0.94 to 0.06), both non-significant.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40483001-probiotics-cognitive-performance-older-adults-meta-analysis.md]
- MCI/AD pooled domains: MMSE 0.28, MoCA 0.51, language -0.12, naming 0.02, visuospatial 0.38, memory 0.20, attention -0.07; all CIs crossed zero.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40034358-probiotics-mci-ad-meta-analysis.md]
- B. breve A1: RBANS total score +11.3 points versus placebo over 16 weeks, with memory subdomains improving.^[raw/articles/pubmed-32623402-bifidobacterium-breve-mci-cognition.md]
- CBT-LR5: MOCA-K naming and delayed recall improved only in the low-dose arm; sample size was 20.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40005019-cbt-lr5-mci-cognition.md]
- Healthy older adults multistrain trial: acute executive-task RT improved by 64.91 ms; chronic memory outcomes did not.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40222448-multistrain-probiotic-healthy-older-adults-cognition.md]
- Micro-encapsulation trial: processing-speed/time-by-group effect was present, but several other domains were null.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40976401-microencapsulation-probiotics-brain-structure-function.md]
- 2'-FL: no primary cytokine win, but Bifidobacterium responders showed better visual-memory performance; the abstract does not provide a robust class-level effect size, which is itself part of the realism check.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40738103-2-fucosyllactose-older-adults-cognition.md]
- CleverAge biota: no meaningful cognitive improvement overall.^[raw/articles/pubmed-37502424-cleverage-biota-probiotics-memory-psychological-physical.md]

## Safety / Adverse Events

- Across the recent meta-analysis, probiotics did not clearly lower overall adverse events, but the class did not show a major safety signal either.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41291638-probiotic-supplements-cognitive-function-meta-analysis.md]
- Individual trials mostly report tolerability, with occasional gastrointestinal discomfort and occasional dropouts from GI effects or protocol burden.
- Product quality, strain identity, and encapsulation matter enough that results should not be generalized across all probiotic products.
- FMT belongs in a clinic-only safety frame because donor screening, infectious risk, and route dependence matter more than consumer-probiotic style extrapolation.

## Responder Phenotypes

- Most plausible responders: older adults with MCI, subjective memory decline, or baseline inflammatory/metabolic burden.
- Weak responders or nonresponders: healthy older adults already near ceiling on screening tests, physically active adults, and community samples without clear impairment.
- The current evidence also hints that baseline microbiome state may matter, but no validated responder atlas exists yet.
- Baseline Bifidobacterium presence looks relevant in the 2'-FL trial, but this is still only a responder hint, not a validated selection rule.

## Protocol Parameters

- Typical durations: 8 to 16 weeks, with one meta-analysis suggesting the signal is clearest at 12 weeks.
- Common dosing patterns: daily capsule/sachet dosing, often around 10^9 to 10^10 CFU/day, with strain-specific products rather than a class effect.
- Endpoint sensitivity matters: memory batteries and MoCA/RBANS seem more informative than coarse global screening alone.
- 2'-FL was given for 6 weeks; the abstract suggests the endpoint story is more about biomarker response and responder enrichment than about a simple dose-response on cognition.

## Open Contradictions

- The 2025 meta-analysis is more favorable than several clean null RCTs in healthy older adults; the gap is likely explained by baseline impairment, endpoint choice, and product heterogeneity rather than a single true contradiction.
- The 2025 older-adult meta-analysis is directionally more favorable than the MCI/AD-specific meta-analysis, but neither is significant, so the apparent contradiction is probably endpoint and cohort selection rather than a true class effect.
- The new GOS and obesity-stratified diet data reinforce that baseline anxiety, obesity, and body-composition change can gate whether any signal is visible.
- Some trials move biomarkers or connectivity without moving cognition, which argues against treating microbiome shifts as sufficient proof of cognitive benefit.
- Correlation between microbiome change and cognitive improvement is not the same as demonstrating mediation.
- The FMT review is internally mixed: its highlights soften the route effect on cognition, while the abstract says rectal administration looked better than oral for microbiome and cognitive improvements.
- 2'-FL keeps the same pattern seen elsewhere in the branch: a primary biomarker miss does not rule out a narrow responder-only cognitive signal, but it does cap how loudly the class can be marketed.
