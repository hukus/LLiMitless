# NMDA Co-Agonist / GlyT1 / DAAO Cognition - 2026 Scan

This branch now reads like a phenotype-sensitive glutamatergic state-restoration lane, not a general-purpose nootropic class. [[iclepertin|Iclepertin]] is effectively a double-null here: the adjunctive computerized cognitive training phase 2 paper was null on its efficacy endpoint, and CONNEX phase 3 was also negative. The broader DAOI pool still looks positive because [[sodium-benzoate]] keeps showing up in early AD and MCI, especially in women. [[luvadaxistat|Luvadaxistat]] is more of a target-engagement / secondary-endpoint story than a clean efficacy win. [[sarcosine|Sarcosine]] and [[d-serine]] are weaker still: useful as mechanistic comparators and combination clues, but not as broad enhancer evidence.

For comparison framing, see [[pharmacology-comparator-map]] and [[ampakines-and-glutamatergic-plasticity-2026-scan]]. For stack safety and attribution, see [[safety-interaction-matrix-for-multi-modal-stacks]]. For the failure ledger, see [[negative-evidence-watchlist-for-cognition-interventions]].

## What Changed Since 2024

- [[iclepertin]] did not generate a stable cognition signal in either the adjunctive computerized cognitive training phase 2 study or the CONNEX phase 3 program, which should cap any broad GlyT1 optimism.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39759424-iclepertin-cct-schizophrenia-phase2.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41233083-iclepertin-connex-phase3-schizophrenia.md]
- The class-level DAAO meta-analysis stayed positive overall, but it pooled a small number of heterogeneous trials and probably overweights sodium benzoate rather than proving a stable class effect.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40328785-daao-inhibitors-schizophrenia-meta-analysis.md]
- Sodium benzoate now has its strongest human signal in early AD / aMCI and in women, which makes it look phenotype-bound rather than universally pro-cognitive.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39315325-sodium-benzoate-neuropsychiatric-meta-analysis.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41858175-sodium-benzoate-mci-pooled-trials.md]
- [[luvadaxistat]] remains negative on its primary schizophrenia symptom endpoint, but its 50 mg arm still produced nominal cognitive and function signals, so it sits in the secondary-only / target-engagement lane.^[raw/articles/pubmed-38943928-luvadaxistat-interact-schizophrenia.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-36928351-luvadaxistat-mmn-biomarker-schizophrenia.md]
- [[sarcosine]] is not a robust cognition enhancer on its own; the better signal appears in combination with benzoate or in narrower chronic / non-refractory subgroups.^[raw/articles/pubmed-32122256-sarcosine-schizophrenia-meta-analysis.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-26691576-sarcosine-plus-benzoate-schizophrenia-rct.md]
- [[d-serine]] still looks more like a mechanistic probe than a broad cognition drug: the classic add-on RCT was null, while newer work is strongest on learning/plasticity biomarkers rather than everyday cognition.^[raw/articles/pubmed-22795211-d-serine-schizophrenia-rct.md]

## Evidence Tiering

- Phase 3 negative, class-defining: iclepertin CONNEX.
- Phase 2 adjunctive CCT null, and phase 3 also negative: iclepertin.
- Meta-analysis positive but heterogeneous: DAO inhibitors overall.
- RCT / pooled RCT positive in early AD and aMCI: sodium benzoate.
- Primary-endpoint negative with secondary cognitive/function signals: luvadaxistat INTERACT.
- Meta-analysis weak or null for cognition: sarcosine.
- Combination-specific signal: sarcosine plus benzoate.
- Broad add-on RCT null: d-serine.

## Safety / Adverse Events

- `iclepertin` was well tolerated in both phase 2 and phase 3, with adverse events similar to placebo and no new safety signal.
- `luvadaxistat` was also well tolerated, which keeps it alive as a mechanistic candidate even after the primary endpoint miss.
- Sodium benzoate looked tolerable in the pooled aMCI analysis and in older AD work; the main practical issues are dose, duration, and whether the tested product is the same product the user can actually source.
- Sarcosine and d-serine are generally described as tolerable in the schizophrenia literature, but the evidence base is small and the supplement / compound source quality problem is real.
- Any self-experiment report that combines [[sarcosine]], [[d-serine]], [[sodium-benzoate]], [[glycine]], methyl donors, stimulants, antidepressants, or sleep interventions should be routed through [[safety-interaction-matrix-for-multi-modal-stacks]] before being counted as molecule-specific cognition evidence.

## Responder Phenotypes

- Women showed the clearest sodium benzoate advantage in the 2024 meta-analysis and the 2026 aMCI pooled analysis.
- Early AD and aMCI are the most credible positive phenotype for sodium benzoate.
- Chronic, non-refractory schizophrenia looks more favorable for sarcosine than clozapine-refractory disease.
- The `luvadaxistat` biomarker signal suggests target engagement may occur without a primary clinical win, which makes responder selection more likely to depend on downstream circuitry than on DAAO occupancy alone.
- D-serine is most plausible in biomarker-enriched learning / remediation protocols, not as a stand-alone general enhancer.

## Protocol Parameters

- `iclepertin`: 10 mg once daily for 12 weeks in phase 2 with CCT; 10 mg once daily for 26 weeks in CONNEX phase 3.
- `luvadaxistat`: 50 mg, 125 mg, or 500 mg daily for 12 weeks in INTERACT; 50 mg for 8 days in the biomarker crossover study.
- Sodium benzoate: 250-1500 mg/day for 24 weeks in aMCI pooled trials; 500 mg twice daily for 12 weeks in early psychosis; 1 g/day in the sarcosine combination RCT.
- Sarcosine: 2 g/day in the main schizophrenia meta-analysis pool; 2 g/day plus benzoate 1 g/day in the combination trial.
- d-serine: 2 g/day in the classic multicenter schizophrenia RCT; 80-120 mg/kg weekly in the AudRem / biomarker work.

## Open Contradictions

- The clearest contradiction is the early reading of `iclepertin`: once the phase 2 adjunctive CCT abstract is read carefully, the branch is not a phase-2-positive / phase-3-negative story but a double-null story with a small tolerated drug and no efficacy win.
- The DAOI meta-analysis says the class is positive, but the best-known GlyT1 agent in the class failed in phase 3, so the pooled class claim is fragile.
- Sodium benzoate looks best in early AD / aMCI and women, but not every dementia-spectrum or psychosis trial replicates that signal.
- Sarcosine and d-serine keep generating mechanistic optimism, but the broad cognition endpoints remain weak.
- Biomarker and secondary-endpoint wins are common in this branch; they should not be collapsed into a generic enhancer claim.
