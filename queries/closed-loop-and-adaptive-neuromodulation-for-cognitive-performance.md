# Closed-Loop and Adaptive Neuromodulation for Cognitive Performance

This scan separates closed-loop/adaptive systems from fixed-parameter stimulation. The current pattern is sharper mechanistic specificity with mixed behavioral payoff: phase-locked or state-triggered stimulation can move rhythms, connectivity, or memory-related physiology, but the cognitive effect often depends on the phenotype, the learner, or the exact phase window.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40307237-closed-loop-theta-hippocampal-connectivity.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40306616-phase-dependent-closed-loop-deep-brain-stimulation-fornix-theta.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41962614-successful-closed-loop-neurofeedback-alpha-attention.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40928755-lateralised-modulation-posterior-alpha-closed-loop-auditory-stimulation.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41110657-auditory-stimulation-sleep-varies-thalamocortical-spindle-phase.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41229102-closed-loop-auditory-stimulation-ecological-memory-null.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40691439-personalized-targeted-memory-reactivation.md]

The strongest direct cognitive-performance signal in this slice is the 2026 alpha-frequency neurofeedback paper in healthy adults, but the benefit is conditional on successful learning. The strongest memory-related signal is still in sleep-state stimulation and hippocampal phase-locking, yet the ecological CLAS study shows that physiology can improve without a behavioral win, and the newer spindle-phase and spindle-targeting papers make clear that even within closed-loop sleep stimulation the timing window is not interchangeable. The 2025 fornix DBS paper adds a second mechanistic proof that phase can flip the direction of theta modulation instead of simply boosting it.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41962614-successful-closed-loop-neurofeedback-alpha-attention.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40928755-lateralised-modulation-posterior-alpha-closed-loop-auditory-stimulation.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41497605-closed-loop-acoustic-stimulation-cathodal-tdcs-retention.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41229102-closed-loop-auditory-stimulation-ecological-memory-null.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40626105-targeting-sleep-spindles-closed-loop-auditory-stimulation.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41110657-auditory-stimulation-sleep-varies-thalamocortical-spindle-phase.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40306616-phase-dependent-closed-loop-deep-brain-stimulation-fornix-theta.md]

For the sleep-specific acoustic-stimulation/TMR branch, see [[closed-loop-acoustic-stimulation-and-targeted-memory-reactivation]].

**What changed since 2024**

- Phase-locked stimulation moved from a conceptual promise to a human hippocampal-network result: theta-synchronized stimulation increased network connectivity in neurosurgical patients.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40307237-closed-loop-theta-hippocampal-connectivity.md]
- Phase-dependent fornix DBS now shows the same principle in an animal model: the target phase can either amplify or suppress hippocampal theta, and replayed open-loop stimulation is weaker.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40306616-phase-dependent-closed-loop-deep-brain-stimulation-fornix-theta.md]
- Healthy-adult adaptive neurofeedback now has a direct attention endpoint, not just a physiology endpoint: individual alpha frequency training improved attention in learners.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41962614-successful-closed-loop-neurofeedback-alpha-attention.md]
- Closed-loop auditory stimulation now has a working-memory lateralization result too: targeting opposite alpha phases over opposite cortices induced asymmetric alpha modulation that tracked behavioral asymmetry.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40928755-lateralised-modulation-posterior-alpha-closed-loop-auditory-stimulation.md]
- Sleep-state closed-loop work now has two timing refinements, not one: spindle-targeting validation showed the loop can hit the intended event without disturbing sleep, and spindle-phase analysis showed peak/rising phases are the windows that actually support spindle enhancement. Personalized TMR adds the separate clue that cue policy can be adapted to memory difficulty rather than held fixed.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40626105-targeting-sleep-spindles-closed-loop-auditory-stimulation.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41110657-auditory-stimulation-sleep-varies-thalamocortical-spindle-phase.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40691439-personalized-targeted-memory-reactivation.md]
- Sleep-state closed-loop work also split into a positive lab-style retention result and a null home/ecological replication, which is the most important contradiction in the branch.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41497605-closed-loop-acoustic-stimulation-cathodal-tdcs-retention.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41229102-closed-loop-auditory-stimulation-ecological-memory-null.md]
- The epilepsy/RNS literature now explicitly tracks cognition and neurobehavioral outcomes in a deployed closed-loop system, but that evidence remains disease-context, heterogeneous, and not a healthy-enhancement proof.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40528736-responsive-neurostimulation-cognition-meta-analysis.md]

## Evidence Tiering

| Tier | What it shows | What it does not show |
|---|---|---|
| Phase-locked animal control | Fornix stimulation in rodents can amplify or suppress hippocampal theta depending on phase; replayed trains are weaker than closed-loop delivery | Still not a human performance result |
| Mechanistic human physiology | Theta-synchronized stimulation increased hippocampal network connectivity; TOPOSO could target local slow oscillations; CLAS can enhance slow-oscillation amplitude; phase-sensitive sleep delivery can refine spindle timing | None of these alone prove robust cognitive enhancement |
| Healthy-adult behavioral signal | Alpha-frequency neurofeedback improved attention in learners; alpha-phase targeting linked lateralized modulation to behavior | The effect is not universal across all participants |
| Memory consolidation signal | CLAS plus cathodal tDCS improved figural paired-associate retention in a small preprint | The same CLAS family failed in a home/ecological setting |
| Phase-window physiology | Spindle-targeting and spindle-phase papers show the control loop only matters if the phase window is right | A "closed-loop" label alone does not guarantee the intended neural event will be enhanced |
| Clinical closed-loop outcomes | RNS/meta-analysis treats memory, attention, and executive outcomes as measurable downstream endpoints | Disease-context data do not generalize cleanly to healthy enhancement |

## Effect Size Snapshot

| Study | Direction | Practical read |
|---|---|---|
| Theta-synchronized DES | Positive physiology; late SEP amplitude increased vs phase-blind control, with persistent connectivity changes | Best read as a mechanism paper, not an enhancement trial |
| Phase-dependent fornix DBS | Trough/ascending phases amplified theta while peak/descending phases suppressed it | Strongest proof in this slice that phase is bidirectional dose control |
| Alpha-frequency neurofeedback | Positive attention signal in learners | The learner/non-learner split is the real story |
| Alpha-phase retention targeting | Phase-asymmetric alpha modulation tracked behavioral asymmetry | Stronger proof of controllable timing than broad transfer |
| Spindle-phase closed-loop stimulation | Peak/rising phases supported spindle enhancement; trough/falling phases did not | Phase window is part of the dose, not just a detail |
| Spindle-targeting validation | Stimulation hit the intended spindle before offset without disturbing sleep, but early delivery could terminate spindles | Useful for methods, but also a warning about overdriving the target |
| CLAS + cathodal tDCS | Positive overnight retention on a figural paired-associate task | Promising but small, and still preprint-level |
| Ecological CLAS | Slow-oscillation amplitude up, memory/vigilance flat | Real-world delivery can erase the behavioral signal |
| RNS meta-analysis | Cognition tracked across domains, but no simple universal enhancer claim emerges | Closed-loop systems can be measured with formal neuropsychology, yet heterogeneity stays high |

## Safety / Adverse Events

- The abstracts in this slice do not reveal a major new safety signal, but the practical risk profile differs sharply by platform.
- Sleep-stimulation studies can alter arousal, sleep architecture, or next-day alertness even when they do not improve memory.
- Spindle-targeting studies can also terminate the spindle if the cue lands too early, so closed-loop control is not automatically benign.
- Neurofeedback can fail at the individual level, which means time, fatigue, and frustration are part of the cost.
- Intracranial or implantable systems are not comparable to consumer enhancement tools and should not be treated as such.
- The bigger interpretive risk is false generalization: a physiology win, a learner-only win, or a disease-context win can look more universal than it is.

## Responder Phenotypes

- `Learners` in alpha-frequency neurofeedback are the clearest responder subgroup in the current set.
- `Phase-window` responders are now visible in sleep-state and alpha-phase tasks: the same intervention can help, do nothing, or disrupt the target depending on timing.
- `Sleep-state/phase-locked` responders appear to depend on the ability to hit the right oscillatory state at the right time.
- `Hippocampal-network` or `memory-consolidation` tasks are more likely than broad executive batteries to show a signal.
- `Disease-context` patients with implanted systems are not interchangeable with healthy volunteers.
- `Baseline oscillatory phenotype` probably matters, but it is still better implied than proven in this scan.

## Protocol Parameters

- Theta-locked DES in the 2025 Nature Communications study used direct electrical stimulation of lateral temporal cortex timed to ongoing hippocampal theta in seven neurosurgical patients.
- The 2025 fornix DBS paper used five male Lister Hooded rats, four theta phases, 15-minute stimulation trains, and biphasic 50-100 µA / 200 µs pulses to show bidirectional theta control.
- The alpha-frequency neurofeedback study used five EEG sessions in 108 healthy adults, with an active placebo control and the Attention Network Test after each session.
- The alpha-phase retention study targeted opposite alpha phases over opposite cortices during working-memory retention in healthy adults.
- The spindle-phase paper analysed 16 healthy adults across multiple nights, with stimulation at slow-oscillation up-states versus sham.
- The spindle-targeting validation paper studied 10 healthy adults over six nights each and reported successful pre-offset stimulation in 97.6% of detections.
- The CLAS plus cathodal tDCS preprint compared CLAS alone versus CLAS modified by cathodal tDCS during slow-wave sleep in 23 young adults.
- The ecological CLAS study delivered one night of closed-loop auditory stimulation at home in 34 participants.
- The TOPOSO sleep study used topographic EEG targeting of local UP- and DOWN-states in napping participants.
- The RNS meta-analysis required at least 12 months of postoperative follow-up and aggregated memory, language, executive-function, visuospatial, attention/processing-speed, mood, and quality-of-life outcomes.

## Open Contradictions

- Physiology can improve without behavior: theta connectivity and slow-oscillation amplitude both moved, but home-based CLAS stayed behaviorally null.
- Laboratory delivery can outperform ecological delivery, which means closed-loop effects may be more fragile than fixed-parameter stimulation effects.
- Closed-loop phase control can flip directionality instead of simply enhancing: in the fornix rat study, some phases amplified theta and others suppressed it.
- The most positive healthy-adult result is learner-dependent, so "closed-loop works" is too broad unless the learner phenotype is specified.
- Even inside the sleep branch, phase is a hidden moderator: spindle enhancement is timing-window dependent, and too-early stimulation can terminate the spindle instead of supporting it.
- Invasive epilepsy-device outcomes are useful for mechanism and endpoint design, but they should not be mistaken for healthy cognitive enhancement evidence.
