# Cerebrolysin 2026 Scan

## Summary

Cerebrolysin is the peptide branch with the best formal human cognition packet, but it is still a medical/recovery molecule rather than a casual nootropic. The cleanest cognition signals remain older blinded AD trials, a very-low-certainty vascular-dementia Cochrane signal, and a weak old healthy-elderly oral study. The newest stroke signal is MoCA-linked but observational, and it must be read beside the 2023 Cochrane acute-stroke caution on hard outcomes and non-fatal serious adverse events.^[raw/articles/pubmed-11552768-cerebrolysin-alz-28-week-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-20500802-cerebrolysin-dose-finding-ad-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pmc-6844361-cerebrolysin-vascular-dementia-cochrane-2019.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-10961443-oral-cerebrolysin-healthy-elderly-alpha-memory.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40851188-cerebrolysin-c-regs2-a-moderate-acute-ischemic-stroke.md]^[raw/articles/cochrane-2023-cerebrolysin-acute-ischaemic-stroke.md]

## What Changed Since 2024

- A live 2026 audit found two missing human-cognition anchors: the 2019 Cochrane vascular-dementia update, which reports a small positive cognition/global-function signal but rates the evidence very low quality, and an old healthy-elderly oral-dose study with EEG alpha and memory-item improvements that is direct but too weak/old to change the overall route.^[raw/articles/pmc-6844361-cerebrolysin-vascular-dementia-cochrane-2019.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-10961443-oral-cerebrolysin-healthy-elderly-alpha-memory.md]
- The same audit added a 2023 Cochrane acute-ischemic-stroke update as the main stroke counterweight: mortality benefit was not shown, several patient-centered endpoints were not adequately reported, and non-fatal serious adverse events probably increased in the Cerebrolysin subgroup.^[raw/articles/cochrane-2023-cerebrolysin-acute-ischaemic-stroke.md]
- Two newer 2025 animal cognition/mechanism studies strengthen the vascular-risk and psychiatric-state plausibility lanes, but they are preclinical only: hypertensive female rats improved in novel object recognition with spine/arborization changes, and ketamine-treated mice improved in recognition/anxiety behavior with hippocampal ATP/ROS and CREB/PGC-1alpha changes.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39826813-cerebrolysin-hypertensive-rat-memory-spines.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41204270-cerebrolysin-ketamine-cognitive-impairment-creb-pgc1a.md]
- A 2025 psychiatry scoping review found the psychiatric literature sparse and framed depression/autism/neuroleptic-adverse-effect use as limited-support territory, not direct human cognition proof.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40722733-cerebrolysin-psychiatry-scoping-review.md]
- The newest human cognition-linked publication is the 2025 C-REGS2-A comparative-effectiveness stroke study: 1,769 moderate ischemic stroke patients, standardized with a target-trial-emulation style design, showed superior 90-day mRS, NIHSS, and ordinal MoCA outcomes with Cerebrolysin, with a stronger MoCA signal in participants who already had cognitive impairment at baseline.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40851188-cerebrolysin-c-regs2-a-moderate-acute-ischemic-stroke.md]
- A second 2025 follow-up study in thrombectomy patients also favored Cerebrolysin on functional recovery, with better 90-day independence, lower symptomatic ICH, and better NIHSS/Barthel outcomes, but it was open-label and used historical controls.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40325343-cerebrolysin-post-thrombectomy-followup.md]
- A 2025 prospective observational AIS study again favored adjunctive Cerebrolysin on day-14 NIHSS and Barthel outcomes in 143 patients, but the design was non-randomized and the follow-up was short.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41010922-cerebrolysin-acute-ischemic-stroke-observational.md]
- The older AD RCTs still matter because they remain the cleanest blinded cognition signals in the branch. A 28-week placebo-controlled study reported a 3.2-point ADAS-cog advantage and sustained benefit after withdrawal, while the later dose-finding trial reported improved global function and cognition at 24 weeks.^[raw/articles/pubmed-11552768-cerebrolysin-alz-28-week-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-20500802-cerebrolysin-dose-finding-ad-rct.md]
- The 2024 aMCI comparative study still matters as the only longer-horizon cognition-prevention signal in the branch, but it remains open-label/comparative rather than blinded.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39435777-cerebrolysin-mci-dementia-prevention.md]
- Safety is not trivial: a 2024 case report described life-threatening anaphylaxis after IV Cerebrolysin, and the newer comparative-effectiveness stroke paper did not see a safety difference even though that does not erase rare hypersensitivity risk.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39055722-cerebrolysin-anaphylaxis-case-report.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40851188-cerebrolysin-c-regs2-a-moderate-acute-ischemic-stroke.md]
- The 2025-2026 trial landscape is still active in stroke recovery, aphasia, and delirium, with MoCA or related cognitive outcomes appearing in protocols, but none of those registry records have posted results yet.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct07043686-cerebrolysin-early-stroke-rehab-tertiary.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct06897176-cerebrolysin-post-stroke-aphasia.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct06677502-cerebrolysin-delirium.md]

## Effect Size Snapshot

- 2019 vascular-dementia Cochrane: six RCTs with 597 participants; cognitive outcomes from three studies with 420 people favored Cerebrolysin with SMD 0.36, but the evidence was very low quality and the authors emphasized heterogeneity/high risk of bias.^[raw/articles/pmc-6844361-cerebrolysin-vascular-dementia-cochrane-2019.md]
- Old healthy-elderly oral study: single 30 mL oral dose increased EEG alpha and reduced ADAS-cog memory-item errors from 6.9 to 4.9 in the abstracted result; this is direct healthy-human signal, but not a modern replicated trial.^[raw/articles/pubmed-10961443-oral-cerebrolysin-healthy-elderly-alpha-memory.md]
- 2023 acute-stroke Cochrane: seven RCTs with 1,773 participants; probably no all-cause mortality benefit and probable increase in non-fatal serious adverse events in the Cerebrolysin subgroup.^[raw/articles/cochrane-2023-cerebrolysin-acute-ischaemic-stroke.md]
- C-REGS2-A: 1,769 moderate AIS patients, median dose 30 mL and median duration 10 days, with superiority on mRS (MW 0.6157), NIHSS at day 21 and 90, and ordinal MoCA at 90 days (MW 0.5530), strongest in the baseline cognitive-impairment subgroup.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40851188-cerebrolysin-c-regs2-a-moderate-acute-ischemic-stroke.md]
- Post-thrombectomy follow-up: 50 treated vs 50 historical controls, with 68% vs 44% mRS 0-2 at 90 days (OR 2.7, NNT 4.2), lower symptomatic ICH (14% vs 40%), and better NIHSS/Barthel scores.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40325343-cerebrolysin-post-thrombectomy-followup.md]
- Prospective observational AIS study: 143 patients, NIHSS fell from 9.90 ± 2.90 to 3.40 ± 1.40 with Cerebrolysin vs 10.10 ± 2.80 to 4.80 ± 1.30 with standard therapy; Barthel gains were larger in the adjuvant group.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41010922-cerebrolysin-acute-ischemic-stroke-observational.md]
- AD 28-week trial: 149 patients, 3.2-point ADAS-cog advantage at week 16, sustained benefit at week 28, and higher CGI responder rate in the active arm.^[raw/articles/pubmed-11552768-cerebrolysin-alz-28-week-rct.md]
- AD dose-finding trial: 133-patient efficacy sample, with significant global-function and cognition improvements at 24 weeks and dose-specific secondary effects.^[raw/articles/pubmed-20500802-cerebrolysin-dose-finding-ad-rct.md]
- aMCI comparative study: 100 patients over 3 years, with less cognitive progression and lower conversion to dementia in the treated group, but no blinding.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39435777-cerebrolysin-mci-dementia-prevention.md]
- SAH pilot: 50 patients, 30 mL/day for 14 days, and no significant difference on 6-month GOSE, mRS, or MoCA.^[raw/articles/pubmed-33143640-cerebrolysin-sah-pilot.md]
- Acute stroke combination trial: no day-90 mRS win, but better early NIHSS responder rates during the treatment window.^[raw/articles/pubmed-23009193-cerebrolysin-alteplase-stroke-rct.md]
- Early neurorehabilitation trial: improved NIHSS, mRS, and Barthel outcomes over the first month after stroke.^[raw/articles/pubmed-29362596-cerebrolysin-early-neurorehabilitation-stroke-rct.md]
- 2025 preclinical cognition models: hypertensive female rats improved in novel object recognition with dendritic/spine changes, and ketamine-treated mice improved in novel object recognition/anxiety behavior with hippocampal ATP/ROS and CREB/PGC-1alpha changes.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39826813-cerebrolysin-hypertensive-rat-memory-spines.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41204270-cerebrolysin-ketamine-cognitive-impairment-creb-pgc1a.md]

## Safety / Adverse Events

- The main new safety counterexample is the 2024 anaphylaxis case report after IV Cerebrolysin.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39055722-cerebrolysin-anaphylaxis-case-report.md]
- The 2023 acute-stroke Cochrane review adds a broader safety counterweight by finding a probable increase in non-fatal serious adverse events in the Cerebrolysin subgroup, even though total serious adverse-event counts were not clearly different.^[raw/articles/cochrane-2023-cerebrolysin-acute-ischaemic-stroke.md]
- The 2025 comparative-effectiveness stroke study reported no safety differences, but that does not rule out rare hypersensitivity or product-dependent risk.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40851188-cerebrolysin-c-regs2-a-moderate-acute-ischemic-stroke.md]
- Older AD RCTs reported tolerability that was broadly comparable with placebo, but those older trials should not erase the possibility of rare hypersensitivity events.^[raw/articles/pubmed-11552768-cerebrolysin-alz-28-week-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-20500802-cerebrolysin-dose-finding-ad-rct.md]
- For acute stroke, the 2013 alteplase combination trial reported no treatment-group difference in adverse events.^[raw/articles/pubmed-23009193-cerebrolysin-alteplase-stroke-rct.md]
- The branch should keep Cerebrolysin in the "requires medical supervision" bucket, not the casual supplement bucket.

## Responder Phenotypes

- Best-supported cognition phenotype: mild to moderate AD in older blinded trials.
- Secondary human cognition phenotype: vascular dementia, but only with very-low-quality Cochrane evidence.
- Possible preventive phenotype: amnestic MCI with high progression risk, but the 2024 study is not definitive.
- Best post-2024 recovery/cognition phenotype: moderate ischemic stroke, especially cohorts with good collaterals, effective recanalization, and baseline cognitive impairment.
- Weak direct healthy-human phenotype: healthy elderly people in one old oral-dose EEG/memory study; not enough for broad enhancement ranking.
- Preclinical hypothesis phenotypes: vascular-risk memory impairment and ketamine/NMDA-hypofunction psychiatric-state cognition.
- Better stroke-recovery fit: moderate to severe acute ischemic stroke and early rehabilitation settings.
- Likely poor fit for casual self-experimentation: people with prior hypersensitivity to injected biologics or unclear cause for a change in neurologic status.

## Protocol Parameters

- C-REGS2-A: median 30 mL IV for a median 10 days in moderate AIS, with 90-day mRS/NIHSS/MoCA follow-up.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40851188-cerebrolysin-c-regs2-a-moderate-acute-ischemic-stroke.md]
- Post-thrombectomy follow-up: 30 mL IV within 8 h of stroke onset and continued to day 21 in the first cycle, then a second cycle during the 69-90 day recovery window.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40325343-cerebrolysin-post-thrombectomy-followup.md]
- Observational AIS study: 30 mL IV daily for 14 days alongside standard therapy.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41010922-cerebrolysin-acute-ischemic-stroke-observational.md]
- AD 28-week RCT: 30 mL IV 5 days per week for 4 weeks, repeated after a 2-month treatment-free interval.^[raw/articles/pubmed-11552768-cerebrolysin-alz-28-week-rct.md]
- AD dose-finding RCT: 10, 30, or 60 mL IV in 100 mL saline across a 12-week schedule, with 24-week outcomes.^[raw/articles/pubmed-20500802-cerebrolysin-dose-finding-ad-rct.md]
- aMCI comparative study: annual courses of 20 IV infusions of 20 mL over 4 weeks, repeated for 3 years.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39435777-cerebrolysin-mci-dementia-prevention.md]
- Acute stroke combination RCT: 30 mL daily IV for 10 days after thrombolysis.^[raw/articles/pubmed-23009193-cerebrolysin-alteplase-stroke-rct.md]
- Early rehabilitation RCT: 30 mL daily IV for 10 days starting 24-48 hours after stroke onset.^[raw/articles/pubmed-29362596-cerebrolysin-early-neurorehabilitation-stroke-rct.md]
- Active stroke rehab registry: MoCA at 90 days is explicitly in the endpoint set, which makes cognition a real secondary target rather than a loose narrative claim.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct07043686-cerebrolysin-early-stroke-rehab-tertiary.md]

## Open Contradictions

- The branch has real blinded AD efficacy signals, but the more recent MCI and stroke evidence is open-label/comparative and therefore weaker.
- The 2025 stroke MoCA/recovery signals conflict in tone with the 2023 acute-stroke Cochrane update, which is more cautious on hard outcomes and non-fatal serious adverse events.
- The 2025 MoCA signal is on a secondary endpoint in an observational stroke cohort, so it should not be overread as proof of a broadly reproducible cognition effect.
- Stroke studies often show early neurologic recovery without a clean day-90 disability win, so functional improvement should not be overread as durable cognitive enhancement.
- A meaningful safety signal exists in the form of anaphylaxis, which means the apparently good tolerability in older trials is not the whole story.
- Active protocols are more rehabilitation-focused than healthy-enhancement-focused, which limits how far the branch can be generalized.
