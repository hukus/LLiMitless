# Washout Durability for Cognition Interventions

This page tracks the narrow question that keeps getting blurred in cognition-enhancement research: which interventions still look positive after a real delay, and which only look good in the immediate post-treatment window. The current answer is no longer just "tPBM is the clearest durable-ish device." The strongest long-horizon retention signals now come from structured cognitive training in MCI with a 5-year follow-up and Tai Chi in T2D-MCI with a 2-year follow-up. LF-rTMS in chronic insomnia adds a 3-month cognition/glymphatic signal, tPBM still has one of the best device-adjacent follow-ups at 150 days, and tDCS remains weak at 1 month.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39246830-five-year-effects-cognitive-training-mci.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41196453-tai-chi-two-year-follow-up-t2d-mci.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40121525-lf-rtms-glymphatic-cognition.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39423445-transcranial-photobiomodulation-cognition-bdnf.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-38452749-tdcs-older-adults-mci-2024.md]

## Durability Ladder

| Follow-up window | Branch | Read |
|---|---|---|
| 5 years | Cognitive training in MCI | Durable positive signal on delayed memory and MoCA preservation. |
| 2 years | Tai Chi in T2D-MCI | Durable positive signal on MoCA, executive function, and visuospatial ability. |
| 3 months | LF-rTMS in chronic insomnia | Phenotype-specific positive signal tied to glymphatic improvement. |
| 150 days | tPBM in MCI | Best current device-adjacent delayed follow-up, but still small. |
| 3 months post-washout | PENSA / EGCG | Exploratory signal only; primary endpoint remained null. |
| 1 month | tDCS in older adults / MCI | Small immediate effects do not reliably survive. |
| Acute / short-term | CBT-I, sleep banking, sleep extension | Valuable for rescue or preservation, not yet durable enhancement. |

The most useful way to read the evidence is by separating four cases:

1. True delayed retention, where a benefit is still visible after a substantial gap.
2. Post-washout improvement, where the effect emerges after treatment ends but may still reflect delayed adaptation.
3. Acute rescue, where the intervention helps in the short term but retention is unproven.
4. Biomarker-only or phenotype-only movement, where the physiology changes but cognition does not clearly hold.

## What Changed Since 2024

- The wiki now has two genuine long-horizon retention anchors: a 5-year MCI cognitive-training follow-up and a 2-year Tai Chi follow-up in T2D-MCI. That shifts the branch from "device durability" to "phenotype plus training structure" as well.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39246830-five-year-effects-cognitive-training-mci.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41196453-tai-chi-two-year-follow-up-t2d-mci.md]
- The tPBM branch still matters because it has one of the few cognition papers with follow-up beyond the acute window, but it is no longer the only durable-ish signal worth citing.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39423445-transcranial-photobiomodulation-cognition-bdnf.md]
- LF-rTMS in chronic insomnia now adds a 3-month cognition/glymphatic readout, which makes it a better durability example than the older generic "stimulation works or does not" framing.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40121525-lf-rtms-glymphatic-cognition.md]
- The tDCS branch remains the clearest short-horizon counterexample: small immediate effects remain plausible, but the 1-month signal is not robust in older adults/MCI.^[raw/articles/pubmed-38452749-tdcs-older-adults-mci-2024.md]
- PENSA remains a useful cautionary example because washout can expose an exploratory subgroup signal even when the primary endpoint is null, which is not the same thing as a durable efficacy claim.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40664536-pensa-apoe-e4-scd-egcg-multimodal-rct.md]
- CBT-I still belongs in the discussion because cognition is now measured directly, but the wiki still does not support a clean durable-enhancer claim for insomnia therapy alone.^[raw/articles/pubmed-32128593-dcbt-i-cognitive-function-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-35691208-cbt-i-objective-sleep-cognition.md]
- Sleep banking and sleep extension remain acute countermeasures, not retained cognitive enhancers.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41892386-sleep-banking-cognitive-motor-impairments-sleep-restriction.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40984126-sleep-extension-cognitive-performance-military-aged-adults.md]

## Evidence Tiering

| Tier | What it contributes to durability |
|---|---|
| Long-horizon follow-up RCTs | Best source for actual retention claims, especially when follow-up reaches years rather than weeks. |
| Randomized follow-up trials | Best source for actual retention claims, especially when follow-up exceeds 1 month. |
| Meta-analysis | Good for spotting branches that fail to hold a 1-month signal even if they look positive immediately. |
| Narrative review | Useful for framing sleep banking and sleep extension as performance preservation, but not enough for durable cognition claims. |
| Biomarker-enriched trial | Helpful only if the post-washout effect is interpreted cautiously and anchored to the primary endpoint. |

## Effect Size Snapshot

- Cognitive training in MCI: the 5-year follow-up reports smaller delayed-memory decline and maintained MoCA versus active control and no-contact, which is the cleanest current long-horizon retention signal in the page.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39246830-five-year-effects-cognitive-training-mci.md]
- Tai Chi in T2D-MCI: 24 weeks of intervention with 2-year follow-up produced a mean MoCA advantage over health education and a clinically meaningful improvement rate near 50%.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41196453-tai-chi-two-year-follow-up-t2d-mci.md]
- LF-rTMS in chronic insomnia: by month 3, MoCA, DSST, DST, and CTT-B improved and DTI-ALPS rose, with the imaging change tracking MoCA and DST-backward. That is a real delayed signal, but still phenotype-specific.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40121525-lf-rtms-glymphatic-cognition.md]
- tPBM in MCI: the published note reports greater MoCA improvement with persistence to follow-up, with follow-up extending to 150 days.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39423445-transcranial-photobiomodulation-cognition-bdnf.md]
- tDCS in older adults/MCI: small immediate effects exist in the literature, but the 1-month durability read is weak to null.^[raw/articles/pubmed-38452749-tdcs-older-adults-mci-2024.md]
- PENSA: the primary endpoint was not met at 12 months, but exploratory analyses after 3-month washout favored EGCG.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40664536-pensa-apoe-e4-scd-egcg-multimodal-rct.md]
- CBT-I: cognition is now an explicit endpoint in the insomnia literature, but the current wiki does not yet support a clean durable-enhancer estimate.^[raw/articles/pubmed-32128593-dcbt-i-cognitive-function-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-35691208-cbt-i-objective-sleep-cognition.md]
- Sleep banking / sleep extension: the evidence is framed around later sleep-loss protection, not long-term retention of a learned cognitive gain.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41892386-sleep-banking-cognitive-motor-impairments-sleep-restriction.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40984126-sleep-extension-cognitive-performance-military-aged-adults.md]

## Safety / Adverse Events

No new durability-specific toxicity signal appears in these sources. The main risks are interpretive:

- Long follow-up can select for adherent responders and make a weak signal look more stable than it is.
- Washout can reveal delayed adaptation, placebo decay, or regression to the mean rather than true retention.
- Short crossover washouts are not enough to support durable efficacy claims.

## Responder Phenotypes

- Older adults with MCI are the clearest current long-horizon phenotype for structured cognitive training.
- T2D-MCI looks unusually favorable for Tai Chi durability, at least relative to fitness walking and health education.
- Older adults with MCI or early AD are the clearest current delayed-follow-up phenotype for tPBM.
- Chronic insomnia is a reasonable phenotype for LF-rTMS durability, but the evidence is still a single study and not a general enhancer claim.
- APOE ε4 / SCD cohorts remain interesting for post-washout effects, but only as an enrichment signal, not a proof of durable benefit.
- Chronic insomnia and sleep-loss phenotypes are good for acute countermeasure research, but not yet for retention claims.
- Sleep-deprived high-demand participants are the wrong phenotype to use if the question is long-term retention rather than immediate rescue.

## Protocol Parameters

- Cognitive training in MCI: follow-up at 5 years after the original randomized intervention.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39246830-five-year-effects-cognitive-training-mci.md]
- Tai Chi: 24-week intervention with cognitive follow-up at 36 weeks and 2 years in T2D-MCI.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41196453-tai-chi-two-year-follow-up-t2d-mci.md]
- LF-rTMS in chronic insomnia: 10 sessions over 2 weeks with cognitive and glymphatic readouts by month 3.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40121525-lf-rtms-glymphatic-cognition.md]
- tPBM MCI: 60 days of treatment with follow-up reported out to 150 days.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39423445-transcranial-photobiomodulation-cognition-bdnf.md]
- PENSA: 12-month multimodal intervention plus EGCG 5-6 mg/kg or placebo, followed by a 3-month washout.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40664536-pensa-apoe-e4-scd-egcg-multimodal-rct.md]
- tDCS dosage meta-analysis: current density <= 0.06 mA/cm2, duration > 20 minutes, and up to 15 sessions were the main subgroup signals, but the durability read still weakens by 1 month.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39905569-tdcs-dosage-parameters.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-38452749-tdcs-older-adults-mci-2024.md]
- CBT-I: 4-8 weekly sessions are typical in the current cognition-facing studies, with follow-up windows reported out to 24 weeks in the dCBT-I study.^[raw/articles/pubmed-32128593-dcbt-i-cognitive-function-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-35691208-cbt-i-objective-sleep-cognition.md]
- Sleep banking / extension: the intervention window is pre-load or short-term extension before anticipated sleep restriction, not a months-long retention protocol.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41892386-sleep-banking-cognitive-motor-impairments-sleep-restriction.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40984126-sleep-extension-cognitive-performance-military-aged-adults.md]

## Open Contradictions

- A post-washout signal is not the same thing as a durable effect; it can reflect delayed onset, delayed adherence, or removal of confounding stressors.
- The field now contains true long-horizon positives, but they cluster in MCI and T2D-MCI phenotypes rather than across healthy adults.
- The tPBM MCI signal is promising, but the field still lacks a second independent >90-day device trial to confirm it.
- The tDCS literature still contains small immediate gains, so the real contradiction is not "works vs fails" but "short-horizon vs retained benefit."
- Sleep interventions can improve cognition-related outcomes without proving that a specific cognitive gain is retained.
- Biomarker movement is not a substitute for retained cognitive performance.
