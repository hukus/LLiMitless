# Fampridine Working Memory Genome-Guided Repurposing - 2026 Scan

## Summary

Fampridine is the first potassium-channel blocker in the wiki with a modern healthy-volunteer working-memory RCT plus an explicit genome-guided repurposing pipeline behind it. The current human result is mixed rather than transformative: repeated 10 mg twice-daily fampridine did not improve the primary 3-back outcome across the full healthy sample, but lower baseline performers showed a better fampridine-versus-placebo result. The newer MS-context papers sharpen the same pattern rather than reversing it: a 2025 upper-extremity RCT was null, and a 2026 fatigue review found randomized evidence to be inconsistent even though observational reports were mostly positive. A 2026 preclinical TBI paper adds an axon-rescue rationale, but it also shows a higher-dose seizure signal and does not change the healthy-adult cognition reading. That keeps the branch interesting as a responder-enrichment lead, not as a general healthy-adult enhancer.

The companion entity page is [[fampridine]].

What changed since 2024:

- A 2025 genome-guided RCT in 43 healthy young adults established the first direct fampridine working-memory test in the modern literature and found no average benefit on the primary endpoint.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39516710-fampridine-working-memory-genome-guided-repurposing.md]
- The same paper reported a low-baseline responder pattern and a lowered resting motor threshold, which keeps the mechanism interesting even though the group-level cognition result was null.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39516710-fampridine-working-memory-genome-guided-repurposing.md]
- The Basel trial registry makes the protocol transparent enough to reuse: 10 mg SR twice daily, 3.5 days per condition, crossover design, and a cognitive battery that includes 3-back, 0-back, SDMT, digit span, and matrix reasoning.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct04652557-fampridine-working-memory.md]
- Older MS work still matters because it suggests a disease-context restoration signal on attention and executive tasks, but that evidence is small and preliminary.^[raw/articles/pubmed-30593980-4-aminopyridine-multiple-sclerosis-cognition.md]
- A 2025 MS upper-extremity RCT found no benefit on 9-Hole Peg, Box and Block, grip force, or self-reported arm/hand function, which argues against broad motor-domain transfer.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39990866-dalfampridine-upper-extremity-ms-null-rct.md]
- A 2026 fatigue review found that most observational studies were positive but only 3 of 13 randomized placebo-controlled studies showed benefit, which fits a placebo-prone symptom bucket rather than a robust universal effect.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41635596-fampridine-ms-fatigue-systematic-review.md]
- A 2026 preclinical TBI study strengthened the axon-conduction / white-matter rescue story, but the same paper showed seizure behavior at the higher dose and remained preclinical rather than cognition-specific.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41508250-4-aminopyridine-tbi-spatial-transcriptomics.md]
- Safety is not optional here: seizure history, renal impairment, and OCT2 interactions are the main gating issues on the label and in the protocol.^[raw/articles/accessdata-fda-ampyra-dalfampridine-label-2021.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct04652557-fampridine-working-memory.md]

## Evidence Tiering

- Human RCT, healthy adults: one modern crossover trial with a null mean effect and a responder pattern.
- Human RCT, RRMS: one small preliminary positive trial on attention/executive measures.
- Human RCT, MS upper extremity: one 2025 dalfampridine trial with no measurable benefit on arm/hand function.
- Human systematic review, MS fatigue: randomized evidence remains inconsistent, despite positive observational reports.
- Preclinical TBI: acute 4-AP reduced axonal injury and secondary molecular pathology at a clinically reasonable dose, but higher dose induced seizure behavior.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41508250-4-aminopyridine-tbi-spatial-transcriptomics.md]
- Registry evidence: strong for protocol and safety constraints, but no posted registry results.
- Regulatory evidence: the approved MS label is decisive for dose and risk handling.
- MS symptom evidence is domain-specific: walking remains the approved lane, while upper extremity and fatigue results are much less consistent.

## Effect Size Snapshot

- Healthy adults: no significant main effect on 3-back d-prime, Wilcoxon P = 0.87, r = 0.026.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39516710-fampridine-working-memory-genome-guided-repurposing.md]
- Moderator signal: lower baseline performance correlated with greater fampridine-versus-placebo benefit, r_s = -0.37, P = 0.014.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39516710-fampridine-working-memory-genome-guided-repurposing.md]
- Mechanistic signal: resting motor threshold decreased after repeated fampridine, F(1,37) = 5.31, P = 0.027.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39516710-fampridine-working-memory-genome-guided-repurposing.md]
- RRMS preliminary report: improved attention span, verbal fluency, planning, and graphomotor/constructive tasks versus placebo.^[raw/articles/pubmed-30593980-4-aminopyridine-multiple-sclerosis-cognition.md]
- MS upper extremity RCT: no significant group-by-time interaction across dexterity, strength, or self-reported arm/hand measures.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39990866-dalfampridine-upper-extremity-ms-null-rct.md]
- MS fatigue review: 19 of 20 observational/non-randomized reports were positive, but only 3 of 13 randomized placebo-controlled studies beat placebo.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41635596-fampridine-ms-fatigue-systematic-review.md]
- Preclinical TBI: 0.5 mg/kg reduced nodal Nav1.6 loss, Caspr heminode formation, and beta-amyloid precursor protein-labeled axon damage; 5 mg/kg induced seizure behavior.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41508250-4-aminopyridine-tbi-spatial-transcriptomics.md]

## Safety / Adverse Events

- The core risk is seizures, especially at higher exposure or in people with seizure history or reduced renal clearance.^[raw/articles/accessdata-fda-ampyra-dalfampridine-label-2021.md]
- The label contraindicates CrCl <= 50 mL/min and warns that mild renal impairment can elevate exposure toward a higher-risk range.^[raw/articles/accessdata-fda-ampyra-dalfampridine-label-2021.md]
- The Basel protocol also excluded OCT2 inhibitors/substrates and low seizure-threshold states such as sleep deprivation and alcohol withdrawal.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct04652557-fampridine-working-memory.md]
- In the RRMS trial, adverse events were mild to moderate and did not merit discontinuation, but that does not eliminate the broader label risk.^[raw/articles/pubmed-30593980-4-aminopyridine-multiple-sclerosis-cognition.md]
- The 2026 TBI mouse paper adds a dose reminder: a clinically reasonable dose looked repair-favorable, but 5 mg/kg induced seizure behavior in sham and TBI groups.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41508250-4-aminopyridine-tbi-spatial-transcriptomics.md]

## Responder Phenotypes

- Best-supported responder phenotype so far: lower baseline cognitive performance.
- Possible clinical phenotype: people with disease-related conduction deficits or MS-related cognitive slowing.
- Unproven but plausible hypothesis: a genotype- or phenotype-enriched subgroup could show a better WM signal than the average healthy adult.
- Preclinical phenotype: acute axonal injury / white-matter disruption may be a better fit than intact healthy cognition.

## Protocol Parameters

- Healthy-volunteer RCT: fampridine SR 10 mg twice daily for 3.5 days per period, crossover, with primary testing 4 hours after the last dose of each treatment period.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct04652557-fampridine-working-memory.md]
- RRMS preliminary report: 4-aminopyridine 10 mg three times daily for 20 weeks.^[raw/articles/pubmed-30593980-4-aminopyridine-multiple-sclerosis-cognition.md]
- FDA label: AMPYRA 10 mg twice daily, approximately 12 hours apart.^[raw/articles/accessdata-fda-ampyra-dalfampridine-label-2021.md]
- Preclinical TBI: 4-AP i.p. b.i.d. on days 1-7 post-injury; the paper reports benefit at 0.5 mg/kg and seizure behavior at 5 mg/kg.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41508250-4-aminopyridine-tbi-spatial-transcriptomics.md]

## Open Contradictions

- Healthy-adult data are null on the primary endpoint, while responder and mechanistic signals remain positive.
- The RRMS cognition signal may reflect disease-context restoration rather than a general enhancement effect.
- The study is called genome-guided repurposing, but the published abstract emphasizes a baseline-performance moderator more than a validated clinical genotype rule.
- The 2026 TBI study is supportive of axon rescue, but it is preclinical and does not justify a healthy-adult enhancement claim.
- The 2025 upper-extremity null and 2026 fatigue review both point to domain specificity inside MS rather than broad transferable enhancement.
- Fampridine is interesting enough to track, but the safety barrier is much higher than for caffeine or most benign nonprescription interventions.
