# Registry Result Status Watchlist

This page tracks the registry/result-status layer behind the wiki's highest-leverage freshness items. It is narrower than [[online-evidence-freshness-dashboard]] and is meant to answer one question quickly: is the branch still protocol-only, has it posted registry results, or is the journal layer lagging behind the registry?

The key split from this sweep is between active no-results records, completed no-results records, and posted-results records that still need journal-layer follow-up. That distinction matters because registry status can move well before a branch changes confidence.

## What Changed Since 2024

- LIGHT-COG mazdutide remains a recruiting cognition-primary incretin trial with no posted results and a 2026-03-05 registry update.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct07083154-light-cog-mazdutide-early-dementia.md]
- Klotho + follistatin gene therapy is a recruiting early-phase healthy-volunteer study with no posted results and a 2026-01-08 update.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct07285629-klotho-follistatin-gene-therapy.md]
- Temporal-interference has crossed into active human validation, but the cognition-facing studies are still recruiting and result-free.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct06607432-temporal-interference-social-cognition.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct07215299-temporal-interference-methods-study-1-1.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct07339072-temporal-interference-methods-study-1-2.md]
- STAK-Sleep is still recruiting with estimated 2026-08 completion, kCBF is still unknown status with no posted results and estimated 2024 completion dates already past, and MEKC is completed but still no-results-posted.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct05519644-stak-sleep-ketone-ester-record-2026-05-04.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct06032156-kcbf-ketone-dose-record-2026-05-04.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct06217159-mechanistic-effect-of-ketones-on-cerebral-blood-flow.md]
- BPN14770's adolescent fragile-X study is active, not recruiting, and has crossed its estimated completion dates without posting results, so it now belongs in the registry watchlist even though the adult fragile-X result is already posted elsewhere in the wiki.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct05163808-bpn14770-adolescent-watch.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct03569631-bpn14770-fragile-x-results.md]
- MB2 now has posted registry results, but the public study snapshot in this sweep did not expose numeric tables and I did not locate a peer-reviewed cognition paper.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct02380573-methylene-blue-results-posted.md]
- Samelisant is now a posted-results narcolepsy comparator, not a protocol-only program.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39504585-samelisant-results-narcolepsy.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct04072380-samelisant-narcolepsy.md]
- I did not locate a direct samelisant OSA-cognition registry in this sweep, so that watch item should be treated as an evidence-gap inference rather than a settled registry record.

## Branch Notes

- For the ketone branch, keep separating sleep restriction from acute CBF and hypoxia, because registry status and result status are not aligned with the mechanistic story.
- For tTIS, treat active registry status as validation-first until a direct cognition result posts.
- For BPN14770, keep the adolescent fragile-X study separate from the already-posted adult fragile-X signal so the branch does not inherit more confidence than the registry supports.
- For Klotho and mazdutide, the 2026 updates are protocol-stage signals only; they should not move efficacy confidence until results post.
- For samelisant, the open question is whether the wakefulness comparator ever enters an OSA-cognition protocol; this sweep did not find one.

## Open Contradictions

- Registry activity can move ahead of journal publication by years, so posted-results tracking and paper tracking need to stay separate.
- A completed trial with posted registry results is not the same thing as a peer-reviewed efficacy paper.
- The absence of a direct OSA-cognition samelisant registry is an inference from this sweep, not proof that no such trial exists anywhere.
