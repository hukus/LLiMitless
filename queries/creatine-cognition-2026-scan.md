# Creatine Cognition 2026 Scan

## Summary

Creatine is no longer just a sports-supplement side note in the cognition wiki. The 2024-2026 evidence stack now splits into two different stories: a modest but real healthy-adult fatigue-resilience signal under high demand, and a more phenotype-sensitive older-adult/clinical story where aging, female hormonal transition, MCI, and exercise synergy may matter more than creatine alone. The newest wrinkle is that formulation seems to matter too: the 2026 CONCRET-MENOPA trial found a medium-dose creatine hydrochloride arm outperforming placebo on reaction time and frontal brain creatine in perimenopausal/menopausal women, which is a narrow positive signal rather than a universal enhancer result.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39070254-creatine-cognition-adults-meta-analysis.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-38418482-creatine-sleep-deprivation-cognition.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40854087-creatine-menopause-cognition-rct.md]

What changed since 2024:

- The 2024 adult meta-analysis sharpened the signal to memory, attention time, and processing-speed time, while leaving global cognition and executive-function non-significant.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39070254-creatine-cognition-adults-meta-analysis.md]
- A 2024 sleep-deprivation RCT showed an acute rescue effect on cognitive performance and processing speed with measurable changes in brain high-energy phosphates, which is the cleanest healthy-adult high-demand signal in the current set.^[raw/articles/pubmed-38418482-creatine-sleep-deprivation-cognition.md]
- A 2025 re-analysis of the same high-demand sleep-deprivation cohort found right-hemisphere ATP fell more than left-hemisphere ATP and that 0.35 g/kg creatine partially balanced the asymmetry, giving the mechanism story a stronger state-dependent frame.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40520504-creatine-sleep-deprivation-hemispheric-asymmetry.md]
- A 2025 pilot biomarker study found serum creatine rose after 24 h sleep deprivation, suggesting creatine metabolism itself shifts under strain rather than remaining a static baseline trait.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40988912-sleep-deprivation-circulating-creatine-pilot.md]
- A 2025 older-adult systematic review found 5 of 6 studies positive, mostly in memory and attention, but the evidence was small, mixed-quality, and partly observational rather than definitive intervention proof.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40971619-creatine-cognition-older-adults-systematic-review.md]
- A 2025 safety review spanning 685 clinical trials and adverse-event databases found no overall excess side effects versus placebo, which helps keep the branch out of the "hidden toxicity" bucket.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40198156-creatine-safety-side-effects-adverse-event-reports.md]
- The 2026 CONCRET-MENOPA trial moved the field into phenotype-specific older female cohorts and reported a medium-dose creatine hydrochloride advantage on reaction time and frontal brain creatine, but it is still a narrow phenotype/formulation signal rather than a general healthy-adult win.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40854087-creatine-menopause-cognition-rct.md]
- Two new older-adult trials now test creatine in combination with resistance or multicomponent training, which should help separate standalone creatine effects from exercise synergy.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct06948149-creatine-resistance-training-mci.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct06677359-creatine-multicomponent-older-adults.md]
- A heat-stress crossover trial now tests whether creatine protects cognition under exertional heat, which is relevant to high-load real-world use rather than quiet-room testing.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct07472426-creatine-heat-exercise-cognition.md]
- The best current reading is that creatine is a context amplifier, not a universal nootropic: stronger when energy supply is strained, sleep is reduced, or the cohort is older or metabolically vulnerable.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41601887-creatine-supplementation-aging-brain-muscle-axis.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40673730-creatine-monohydrate-older-adults-clinical-populations.md]

## Evidence Tiering

- Meta-analysis: adult cognition meta-analysis and healthy-memory meta-analysis.
- Randomized trial: sleep-deprivation rescue RCT; CONCRET-MENOPA.
- Mechanistic human addenda: sleep-deprivation hemispheric asymmetry re-analysis and circulating creatine pilot.
- Older-adult review addendum: six-study synthesis with five positive studies, mostly memory and attention, but low causal certainty because most of the evidence is not intervention-grade.
- Safety addendum: 685-trial adverse-event review did not find an overall placebo-separated safety signal.
- Ongoing trials: MCI + resistance training; older-adult multicomponent training; heat-stress crossover.
- Mechanistic context: muscle-brain axis and exercise-linked neurogenesis reviews.

## Effect Size Snapshot

- Adult meta-analysis: memory SMD 0.31, attention-time SMD -0.31, processing-speed-time SMD -0.51; no significant overall cognition or executive-function effect.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39070254-creatine-cognition-adults-meta-analysis.md]
- Healthy-memory meta-analysis: overall memory SMD 0.29, with the clearest signal in older adults 66-76 years and little signal in younger adults.^[raw/articles/pubmed-35984306-creatine-memory-healthy-individuals.md]
- Sleep-deprivation RCT: acute 0.35 g/kg creatine partially reversed fatigue-related cognitive decline and altered cerebral phosphocreatine/ATP measures.^[raw/articles/pubmed-38418482-creatine-sleep-deprivation-cognition.md]
- Sleep-deprivation re-analysis: the same 0.35 g/kg dose balanced right-left ATP asymmetry and shifted PCr/Pi/ATP in a direction consistent with the rescue effect.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40520504-creatine-sleep-deprivation-hemispheric-asymmetry.md]
- Biomarker pilot: serum creatine increased after 24 h sleep deprivation in healthy adults, but this was not a cognition trial.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40988912-sleep-deprivation-circulating-creatine-pilot.md]
- CONCRET-MENOPA: the medium-dose creatine hydrochloride arm outperformed placebo on reaction time and frontal brain creatine, with a possible mood-swing benefit trend, but this was a small phenotype-specific trial rather than a broad healthy-adult enhancement result.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40854087-creatine-menopause-cognition-rct.md]
- Older-adult systematic review: 5/6 studies positive, mostly memory/attention, but with only two double-blind intervention studies and four cross-sectional dietary-intake studies.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40971619-creatine-cognition-older-adults-systematic-review.md]
- Safety review: no overall excess side effects versus placebo at the study or participant level, though GI complaints and cramping remain the usual practical tolerability discussion.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40198156-creatine-safety-side-effects-adverse-event-reports.md]

## Safety/Adverse Events

No new cognition-specific safety signal stands out in the sources captured here. The main practical adverse-event considerations remain standard creatine issues: gastrointestinal tolerance, hydration context, and the need to separate supplementation effects from heat, sleep loss, and exercise load. The 2025 safety review is the strongest aggregate reassurance so far: no overall excess side effects versus placebo and no meaningful renal-function signal in the compiled trial set.^[raw/articles/pubmed-38418482-creatine-sleep-deprivation-cognition.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40520504-creatine-sleep-deprivation-hemispheric-asymmetry.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40198156-creatine-safety-side-effects-adverse-event-reports.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct07472426-creatine-heat-exercise-cognition.md]

## Responder Phenotypes

- Older adults appear more likely than young healthy adults to show a memory signal.
- Females and disease/clinical cohorts may respond better than young healthy cohorts in the 2024 meta-analysis.
- Sleep-loss, heat-stress, and other high-demand states are the clearest use cases for acute benefit.
- MCI and older-adult exercise combinations are the main phenotype questions still worth testing.

## Protocol Parameters

- Meta-analysis included doses from roughly 2.2 to 20 g/day across 5 days to 24 weeks.
- Sleep-deprivation RCT used a single 0.35 g/kg oral dose during 21 h total sleep deprivation.
- CONCRET-MENOPA used 750 mg/day and 1,500 mg/day creatine hydrochloride, plus an 800 mg/day creatine hydrochloride + ethyl ester arm, for 8 weeks.
- NCT06948149 is a 26-week 2x2 factorial creatine + resistance-training trial.
- NCT07472426 uses 20 g/day for 7 days, followed by 5 g before exertional heat testing and cognitive testing.

## Open Contradictions

- Healthy-young-adult enhancement remains weak or absent, even though high-demand rescue signals exist.
- The menopause RCT suggests a formulation-sensitive positive signal in a female phenotype, but it is not strong enough to erase the broader healthy-young-adult null-ish picture.
- Older-adult and clinical-population effects are promising but not yet cleanly separated from exercise, diet, and baseline deficiency.
- Different creatine forms and doses are being used, so "creatine" may not be a single exposure in practice.
- Global cognition is often null even when memory or processing speed improves.
- Older-adult evidence still leans partly observational, so the age-specific signal is not yet a clean causal answer.
- The newer sleep-deprivation papers make the most defensible claim even narrower: creatine seems most credible as a strain-state buffer, not a broad baseline enhancer.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40520504-creatine-sleep-deprivation-hemispheric-asymmetry.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40988912-sleep-deprivation-circulating-creatine-pilot.md]
