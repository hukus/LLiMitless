# Self-Experimentation Stop Rules

## Summary

This page defines conservative stop rules for the wiki's self-study and n-of-1 work.

The goal is not to list every possible adverse event. The goal is to separate:

1. expected transient nuisance effects,
2. early warning signs that the stack is too activating, and
3. symptoms that should end the current block immediately.

The current evidence base makes one thing clear: the main failure mode for cognitive self-experimentation is usually not catastrophic toxicity. It is additive sleep disruption, arousal spillover, mood activation, headache burden, and attribution error when several small interventions are started together.^[raw/articles/pubmed-30214966-tdcs-tacs-adverse-events-review.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40984126-sleep-extension-cognitive-performance-military-aged-adults.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41892386-sleep-banking-cognitive-motor-impairments-sleep-restriction.md]

Use [[practical-cognitive-measurement-stack]] for the daily metrics and [[safety-interaction-matrix-for-multi-modal-stacks]] for combination-risk context. For neuropeptide/social-memory claims, [[oxytocin-vasopressin-desmopressin-social-memory-2026-scan]] is the routing page; desmopressin belongs here as a hard stop-rule example, not as a casual memory variable.

June 2026 peptide/nootropic update: stop rules are now also a provenance gate. For [[semax]], [[selank]], [[cerebrolysin]], recovery-bound [[bpc-157]], [[bromantane]], and de-ranked, metabolism-first, or state-bound nodes such as [[dihexa]], [[pe-22-28]], [[adamax]], [[epithalon]], [[pinealon]], [[mots-c]], and TB-500-style labels, the block should pause when source/COA, route/formulation, fixed-blend status, dose timing, stack order, washout, recovery or sleep state, adverse-event history, regulatory/sports status, or objective endpoint fields are missing and any adverse symptom appears. A vivid benefit report with an unverified source, new route, injection/nasal irritation, allergic symptom, tachycardia, BP rise, insomnia, mood activation, exercise-load change, or pain-relief/training-continuity mediator should be treated as a safety/attribution event before it is treated as efficacy evidence.^[raw/articles/reddit-2026-04-29-semax-selank-cerebrolysin-advice-thread.md]^[raw/articles/reddit-2026-bromantane-thread-trail.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40131143-bpc157-human-safety-pilot.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct07437547-bpc157-hamstring-repair-record-2026-05-06.md]^[raw/articles/reddit-2025-03-06-pinealon-tbi-recovery-sleep-cognition.md]^[raw/articles/reddit-2026-04-29-dihexa-thread.md]^[raw/articles/fda-bulk-drug-substances-peptide-risk-2026.md]

## What Changed Since 2024

- tDCS/tACS safety reviews now make the usual pattern explicit: most adverse events are mild and transient, but rare mood activation and skin injury still matter for self-experimentation.^[raw/articles/pubmed-30214966-tdcs-tacs-adverse-events-review.md]
- Nicotine replacement labels now clearly warn about heart-rate and blood-pressure effects, and the patch label also calls out vivid dreams and sleep disturbance.^[raw/articles/dailymed-nicotine-gum-label-2026.md]^[raw/articles/dailymed-nicotine-transdermal-system-patch.md]^[raw/articles/fda-want-to-quit-smoking-cessation-products.md]
- Sleep banking and sleep extension work now support a practical rule: if an intervention worsens sleep, it can wipe out the downstream cognition benefit even when the acute session looks promising.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41892386-sleep-banking-cognitive-motor-impairments-sleep-restriction.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40984126-sleep-extension-cognitive-performance-military-aged-adults.md]
- Pitolisant now has both a label-level caution about insomnia/anxiety and a 2026 mania case report, which makes mood activation a real stop condition rather than a theoretical one.^[raw/articles/dailymed-wakix-pitolisant-tablet-label.md]^[raw/articles/medscape-pitolisant-mania-case-report.md]
- Creatine safety data are reassuring enough that it should not be the default explanation for most adverse symptoms, which helps when separating signal from background noise.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40198156-creatine-safety-side-effects-adverse-event-reports.md]
- Desmopressin nasal spray has moved from "interesting memory rumor" into a hard stop-rule example: the label warns about hyponatremia, water intoxication, fluid restriction, and unreliable absorption with nasal-mucosa abnormalities, so any self-study that touches water balance needs a physician-level safety frame.^[raw/articles/dailymed-desmopressin-acetate-spray-label.md]^[raw/articles/reddit-2026-04-29-desmopressin-memory-enhancement-thread.md]
- Peptide/nootropic self-study has a separate stop-rule problem: adverse events are often impossible to assign when molecule identity, lot, route, blend, dose timing, washout, stimulant co-use, sports/regulatory context, recovery state, and endpoint choice change together. That makes missing provenance itself a pause condition once symptoms appear.^[raw/articles/reddit-2026-04-29-semax-selank-cerebrolysin-advice-thread.md]^[raw/articles/fda-bulk-drug-substances-peptide-risk-2026.md]

## Stop-Rule Tiers

| Tier | What it means | Action |
|---|---|---|
| `Monitor` | Mild, expected, short-lived nuisance effect that does not change sleep, mood, or adherence | Log it and continue cautiously |
| `Pause` | Reproducible symptom that changes sleep, mood, blood pressure, or daytime function | Stop the next dose and reassess after a washout |
| `Stop now` | Severe, escalating, or safety-relevant symptom | End the block immediately and seek medical review if needed |

## High-Priority Stop Flags

| Flag | Why it matters | Practical rule |
|---|---|---|
| Sleep disruption | Sleep loss is one of the easiest ways to erase a small cognitive benefit and one of the hardest things to notice accurately in the moment.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41892386-sleep-banking-cognitive-motor-impairments-sleep-restriction.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40984126-sleep-extension-cognitive-performance-military-aged-adults.md] | Pause if sleep latency, awakenings, or next-day sleepiness worsen for more than a transient day |
| Anxiety or irritability | Activating interventions can look like "productivity" while actually degrading judgment and adherence.^[raw/articles/dailymed-wakix-pitolisant-tablet-label.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-30214966-tdcs-tacs-adverse-events-review.md] | Pause if you feel keyed up, more reactive, or less able to tolerate normal friction |
| Impulsivity | Reduced inhibition can make the self-study itself unreliable, especially if it changes dosing discipline or risk-taking.^[raw/articles/pubmed-23072834-varenicline-healthy-non-smoking-adults.md] | Pause if you notice more impulsive spending, dosing, driving, messaging, or task switching without purpose |
| Blood-pressure rise | Nicotine products explicitly warn about heart rate and blood pressure, and similar activation can stack with stimulatory stacks.^[raw/articles/dailymed-nicotine-gum-label-2026.md]^[raw/articles/dailymed-nicotine-transdermal-system-patch.md]^[raw/articles/fda-want-to-quit-smoking-cessation-products.md] | Pause any variable that appears to raise BP relative to your baseline, especially if it recurs |
| Fluid/electrolyte shift | Desmopressin can trigger hyponatremia/water intoxication and has erratic nasal absorption when mucosa is abnormal.^[raw/articles/dailymed-desmopressin-acetate-spray-label.md] | Stop immediately for confusion, headache with nausea, swelling, or rapid weight gain; do not self-experiment without medical oversight |
| Headache | Headache is often mild in device and pharmacology studies, but a new or escalating headache is still a useful early warning signal.^[raw/articles/pubmed-30214966-tdcs-tacs-adverse-events-review.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-27612937-caffeine-effects-cognitive-physical-occupational-performance.md] | Pause if the headache is new, increasing, or paired with sleep loss, nausea, or BP rise |
| Visual symptoms | Visual symptoms are a conservative self-study red flag because they can reflect overstimulation, migraine-like intolerance, or a device/timing mismatch. This is an inference from the safety literature, not a common trial endpoint. | Pause if you get blur, flashes, photophobia, or any persistent visual change |
| Mood destabilization | Rare mania/hypomania has been reported with stimulation and activating wakefulness drugs, so mood elevation should not be treated as a success metric.^[raw/articles/pubmed-30214966-tdcs-tacs-adverse-events-review.md]^[raw/articles/medscape-pitolisant-mania-case-report.md] | Stop immediately if you feel unusually euphoric, agitated, sped up, less need for sleep, or behaviorally disinhibited |
| Allergic or systemic reaction | Rare hypersensitivity can be serious even for agents that are usually well tolerated.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39055722-cerebrolysin-anaphylaxis-case-report.md] | Stop immediately for rash, swelling, wheeze, throat tightness, syncope, or any anaphylaxis pattern |
| Peptide source / route uncertainty plus symptoms | Gray-market peptide and nootropic reports can combine identity, route, sterile-technique, solvent, blend, and stack uncertainty with strong expectancy effects.^[raw/articles/fda-bulk-drug-substances-peptide-risk-2026.md]^[raw/articles/reddit-2026-04-29-dihexa-thread.md] | Pause for any new symptom if source/COA, lot, route/formulation, fixed-blend status, or stack order is missing; stop now for injection-site infection signs, systemic allergic symptoms, chest/throat tightness, syncope, or escalating neurologic symptoms |
| Recovery or performance mediator drift | BPC-157/TB-500-style, GH-axis, Bromantane, and MOTS-c-style reports can improve perceived cognition indirectly through pain, training continuity, sleep, exercise load, body composition, or metabolic state. | Pause if the only "cognitive" gain arrives with changed injury pain, rehab volume, sleep, training load, weight/appetite, or stimulant timing; do not resume ranking interpretation until mediator and objective endpoint fields are filled |
| Sports/regulatory status unchecked | Some activation, recovery, GH-axis, and stimulant-adjacent reports carry WADA or regulatory context that changes the risk frame even if the subjective effect is positive. | Pause interpretation when WADA/FDA/prescription/research-chemical status is unchecked; use [[regulatory-and-sports-visibility-map]] and [[peptide-regulatory-live-watch-board]] before treating a report as routine self-study evidence |

## Common Nuisance Effects

These are not automatic stop conditions if they are brief, mild, and non-progressive:

- mild stimulation-site tingling or discomfort,
- brief adjustment insomnia on the first day of a wake-promoting intervention,
- short-lived GI upset from a supplement that resolves quickly,
- transient alertness without any mood or sleep penalty.

The key distinction is trend. If a nuisance effect becomes repetitive, longer-lasting, or clearly linked to worse sleep or worse function, it stops being a nuisance and becomes a pause signal.

## Operational Rules

1. Do not introduce more than one new activating variable at a time.
2. If sleep worsens first, assume the stack is too activating until proven otherwise.
3. If mood, irritability, or impulsivity changes, stop the current block before you try to "push through."
4. If blood pressure or headache worsens together, treat that as a stack interaction, not an isolated inconvenience.
5. Resume only after the symptom returns to baseline and the causal candidate is clearer.
6. If you cannot tell which variable caused the problem, roll back to the last stable configuration.
7. For peptide or gray-market nootropic runs, do not resume until [[stack-attribution-diary-for-self-study-and-n-of-1-protocols]] has exact identity, source/COA, route/formulation, lot, dose timing, stack order, washout, recovery/sleep state, adverse events, regulatory/sports status, objective endpoint, and dechallenge fields filled.
8. Keep [[bromantane]] plus [[semax]] / [[selank]], peptide stacks plus caffeine/nicotine/wake-promoters, and fixed blends in the pause-first category unless the protocol proves temporal separation.
9. Treat recovery-bound [[bpc-157]], TB-500-style stacks, [[mots-c]], and GH-axis peptides as mediator-first until pain, training, sleep, metabolic, and body-composition changes are separated from direct cognition.

## Open Contradictions

- Some interventions intentionally raise alertness, so a short-term feeling of activation can be either the intended effect or the first sign of overreach.
- Headache and sleep disruption may be acceptable in a one-off lab context but are poor tradeoffs in a multi-week self-study.
- A benign-looking symptom can still be a real stop condition if it destabilizes the measurement stack.
