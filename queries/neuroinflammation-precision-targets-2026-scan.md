# Neuroinflammation Precision Targets 2026 Scan

## Summary

The precision-target branch now looks much more specific than the older "anti-inflammatory nootropic" framing. NLRP3, TREM2, and C1q/complement all show plausible roles in AD pathology, but the strongest recent evidence is still about target biology, biomarker staging, synapse loss, and target failure in humans rather than human cognition improvement.

NLRP3 now has a sharper mechanism story. The 2025 review frames it as a microglial inflammasome linked to Aβ, tau, ROS, pyroptosis, autophagy, gut microbiota, and candidate therapies. The 2026 semaglutide paper then pushed the target-convergence story further: in APP/PS1 mice and LPS + ATP-stimulated BV2 microglia, semaglutide reduced cognitive impairment, Aβ aggregation, tau hyperphosphorylation, NLRP3 activation, microglial inflammatory polarization, and ferroptosis signaling. That makes NLRP3 a real target-validation lane, but still not a human cognition proof lane.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40260242-nlrp3-inflammasome-ad-review.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41173224-semaglutide-nlrp3-ad-model-progression.md]

TREM2 is now better supported as a longitudinal biomarker axis than as a treatment target. Human data tie peripheral TREM2 mRNA and CSF sTREM2 to worse cognition, hippocampal atrophy, amyloid, and tau burden in AD-spectrum cohorts, while the 2025 review frames TREM2 as a microglial regulator of inflammation resolution and pathological protein clearance. The 2026 TREM2-T96K paper adds a more cautionary layer: a variant described as gain-of-function still increased AD risk and impaired microglial Aβ42 uptake. The biological story is coherent; the interventional story is now actively challenged by the terminated AL002 phase 2 record.^[raw/articles/pubmed-28453482-peripheral-trem2-mrna-cognitive-deficits-hippocampal-atrophy.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-35305339-soluble-trem2-csf-longitudinal-autosomal-dominant-ad.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41280332-microglial-trem2-cognitive-impairment-review.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41109213-trem2-t96k-ad-risk-microglial-function.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct04592874-al002-phase2-results.md]

Complement moved from general pruning language to a more explicit human mediation story. The 2025/2026 complement cascade review keeps synaptic pruning central, the 2025 C1q biomarker paper adds a human two-cohort analysis with 217 ADNI participants and 535 CABLE participants, and the 2025 preprint now adds a mouse causal result: deleting C1q from young adult microglia reduced synapse engulfment and prevented cognitive impairment in an aggressive AD model. CSF C1q associated with sTREM2, GFAP, Aβ42, p-tau, and t-tau at baseline; longitudinally it tracked T-tau and ADAS-Cog 13; and mediation analysis suggested that Aβ partly mediates the C1q -> sTREM2 -> tau pathway. That is the strongest current argument that C1q sits upstream of a clinically relevant AD neuroinflammation pathway, but the timing of intervention still matters.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41398960-complement-cascade-ad-review.md]^[raw/articles/pmc-12271313-c1q-associated-neuroinflammation-s-trem2-ad-biomarkers.md]^[raw/articles/pmc-12458416-c1q-microglial-deletion-synapse-cognitive-impairment.md]

The wiki should treat this branch as disease biology first and enhancement second. These targets are relevant to AD progression, but none of the current sources justify ranking them as healthy-adult cognition enhancers.

## What Changed Since 2024

- NLRP3 moved from a broad inflammasome nameplate to a more specific microglial mechanism, with review-level target mapping in 2025 and semaglutide-linked target convergence in 2026.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40260242-nlrp3-inflammasome-ad-review.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41173224-semaglutide-nlrp3-ad-model-progression.md]
- Semaglutide added a 2026 preclinical bridge between incretin biology and the NLRP3/ferroptosis axis in AD, which strengthens target convergence but not human efficacy.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41173224-semaglutide-nlrp3-ad-model-progression.md]
- TREM2 moved from a generic microglial biomarker to a more clearly staged human association with cognitive decline and AD pathology, especially through DIAN and other biomarker cohorts, but the AL002 phase 2 result now shows that agonism can fail clinically even when the target is biologically plausible.^[raw/articles/pubmed-35305339-soluble-trem2-csf-longitudinal-autosomal-dominant-ad.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-28453482-peripheral-trem2-mrna-cognitive-deficits-hippocampal-atrophy.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct04592874-al002-phase2-results.md]
- C1q/complement moved from a synaptic-pruning concept to a biomarker-linked pathway with CSF mediation evidence involving sTREM2, tau, and ADAS-Cog 13, now backed by the 217-person ADNI cohort and the 535-person CABLE validation cohort plus a mouse deletion study showing that timing can change the outcome.^[raw/articles/pmc-12271313-c1q-associated-neuroinflammation-s-trem2-ad-biomarkers.md]^[raw/articles/pmc-12458416-c1q-microglial-deletion-synapse-cognitive-impairment.md]
- The overall branch is more precise than it was in 2024, but it still lacks a human cognition intervention that demonstrates target engagement translating into better memory or executive function. TREM2 has now accumulated a negative human agonist result, so it should be treated as a cautious target rather than an upgradeable nootropic lane.

## Evidence Tiering

- Review: NLRP3 review, TREM2 review, complement cascade review, complement-mediated synapse-loss review.
- Human observational / mediation: peripheral TREM2 mRNA / cognition, CSF sTREM2 longitudinal AD, CSF C1q mediation study, TREM2-T96K human genetics and microglial functional data.
- Preclinical: semaglutide AD-model paper, NLRP3 phagocytosis mechanism, and the C1q synapse-engulfment deletion study.
- Direct human intervention: AL002 phase 2 result is negative; no positive human cognition effect size surfaced for the target set.

## Effect Size Snapshot

- NLRP3: no human cognition effect size surfaced; the signal is preclinical target support only.
- TREM2: no positive target-modifying cognition effect size surfaced; the human biomarker and genetics signals point to a calibrated, stage-sensitive immune function rather than simple activation.
- C1q/complement: no complement-targeted cognition trial effect size surfaced; the human signal is associative and mediation-based, not interventional.

## Safety / Adverse Events

- Complement and NLRP3 are innate-immune targets, so over-inhibition could plausibly increase infection risk or blunt protective immune functions. That is an inference from class biology rather than a specific cognition trial result.
- TREM2 agonism has now shown clinical futility in AL002 phase 2, so the main risk is not just safety but false-positive target optimism.
- Semaglutide itself has a known systemic safety profile from the incretin literature, but this scan only uses the AD-model paper as a mechanistic bridge, not as a safety source.
- Because the branch is biomarker-heavy and intervention-light, the main wiki safety concern is overgeneralizing disease biology into consumer nootropic use.

## Responder Phenotypes

- Most plausible phenotypes are AD-spectrum and preclinical AD, especially high-amyloid, high-tau, or microglia-activated states.
- TREM2 looks most relevant when microglial dysfunction or AD-risk genetics are prominent, but the AL002 and T96K findings imply that genotype and disease stage matter.
- Complement/C1q seems most relevant when synapse loss, tau progression, and CSF inflammatory biomarkers are already active, with timing likely to matter more than broad blockade.
- There is no evidence here for a healthy-adult enhancement phenotype.

## Protocol Parameters

- No validated human cognition protocol surfaced for NLRP3, TREM2, or C1q/complement.
- The semaglutide paper used APP/PS1 mice and BV2 microglia, so its dosing is not directly portable to humans.
- Human cohort papers used CSF sampling and cognitive follow-up, not target-modifying treatment protocols.
- AL002 used intravenous dosing in early AD, but the completed phase 2 record does not support efficacy.

## Open Contradictions

- Biomarker elevation may mean compensatory activation rather than harmful overactivity, especially for TREM2.
- Complement signaling is required for normal developmental pruning, so the disease question is about reactivation and excess, not simple presence versus absence.
- NLRP3 may have different implications under acute versus chronic inhibition; the 2025 review and 2026 semaglutide paper both still sit at the target-validation stage rather than intervention proof.
- TREM2 is now explicitly contradictory: one human intervention failed, and one human mutation study suggests that apparently enhanced signaling can still worsen disease risk.
- The branch is promising mechanistically, but the clinical translation gap remains wide.
