## Signal Discipline

The current graph now separates:

- Medicinal signal: controlled human, clinical, regulatory, or preclinical evidence.
- Anecdote signal: community surveys, forum reports, or market salience.
- Route: benchmark comparator, state-restoration, impairment/recovery, nutraceutical proxy, preclinical hypothesis, or risk-first.
- Sourcing / regulatory burden: prescription status, WADA visibility, supplement quality, product identity, adulteration, and FDA warnings.

## Current Verdict
