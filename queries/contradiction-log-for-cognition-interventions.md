# Contradiction Log for Cognition Interventions

This page is the cross-cutting landing spot for branch-level contradictions that matter to the wiki. Some are true pooled-evidence disagreements; others are "positive review vs negative RCT" splits or "subdomain gain vs global null" splits. The point is to keep the graph from flattening a mixed field into a single yes/no answer.

The working inference is that most of these contradictions come from phenotype mismatch, endpoint mismatch, duration mismatch, and device or parameter mismatch rather than from a single class-wide failure. That is especially true when the same branch looks different in healthy adults, older adults, MCI/AD, or sleep-loss / recovery states.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40633904-tdcs-variability-age-2025.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40703476-intranasal-insulin-pet-aging-mci.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-38109202-tpbm-concussion-null-rct.md]

The senolytic branch now belongs in the same mixed-evidence bucket: D+Q has human feasibility and biomarker signals, but the only direct cognition-positive result is still preclinical, while fisetin's live human work is still aimed at frailty and inflammation rather than memory. See [[senolytic-cognition-2026-scan]] for the current branch-level readout.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40010154-senolytics-cognition-mobility-at-risk.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40274471-dasatinib-quercetin-phase1-mild-alzheimer-biomarkers.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct03675724-fisetin-frailty-inflammation-older-adults.md]

## What Changed Since 2024

- tPBM moved from a mostly exploratory signal into a small but more coherent older-adult / MCI / mTBI cluster, yet persistent-concussion stayed null, so the recovery phenotype still does not generalize cleanly.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40822571-tpbm-systematic-review-sleep-wakefulness-cognition.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40482941-tpbm-healthy-older-adults-working-memory-fnirs.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-38109202-tpbm-concussion-null-rct.md]
- Intranasal insulin now has a null chronic MCI/AD meta-analysis, a narrower AD-only meta that still favors 20 IU, a first-in-human PET validation layer, a new MS cognition null, and a stronger perioperative protection branch, which makes delivery and responder state part of the evidence story rather than a footnote.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41436338-intranasal-insulin-mci-ad-systematic-review-meta-analysis.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40195962-intranasal-insulin-ad-systematic-review-meta-analysis.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40703476-intranasal-insulin-pet-aging-mci.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40253245-intranasal-insulin-ms-null.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40359848-intranasal-insulin-postop-sleep-cardiac-surgery.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41146035-intranasal-insulin-hip-fracture-delirium.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41720686-intranasal-insulin-perioperative-delirium-meta.md]
- tDCS now has both dose-parameter and age-variability syntheses, which helps explain why average effects keep wobbling across older-adult and impairment cohorts.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39905569-tdcs-dosage-parameters.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40633904-tdcs-variability-age-2025.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-38452749-tdcs-older-adults-mci-2024.md]
- Creatine meta-analysis clarified that memory, attention, and processing speed can improve even when overall cognition and executive function do not.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39070254-creatine-cognition-adults-meta-analysis.md]
- taVNS now has a positive healthy-adult meta-analysis, a disease-focused systematic review, and an acute-stress null, which makes it a useful state-regulation example rather than a settled cognitive enhancer.^[raw/articles/pubmed-34043386-taVNS-healthy-cognition-meta-analysis.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41717223-taVNS-cognitive-deficits-systematic-review.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40908377-taVNS-mental-stressor.md]

## Contradiction Map

| Branch | Higher-level signal | Counterweight | Likely reason the branch splits |
|---|---|---|---|
| Intranasal insulin | PET validation confirms that insulin can reach multiple brain regions in humans, and perioperative RCTs/meta-analysis suggest a real protection branch.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40703476-intranasal-insulin-pet-aging-mci.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40359848-intranasal-insulin-postop-sleep-cardiac-surgery.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41146035-intranasal-insulin-hip-fracture-delirium.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41720686-intranasal-insulin-perioperative-delirium-meta.md] | Chronic MCI/AD meta-analysis is null, but the narrower AD-only review still favors 20 IU, and the MS cognition trial was safe but null on SDMT and secondary outcomes.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41436338-intranasal-insulin-mci-ad-systematic-review-meta-analysis.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40195962-intranasal-insulin-ad-systematic-review-meta-analysis.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40253245-intranasal-insulin-ms-null.md] | Inference: device, vascular-metabolic state, dose, and cognitive phenotype matter enough that uptake alone does not guarantee a behavioral win, and the branch is state-specific rather than disease-agnostic. |
| tDCS | Older-adult and impairment meta-analyses still show small immediate signals, and dose-parameter work keeps the branch alive.^[raw/articles/pubmed-38452749-tdcs-older-adults-mci-2024.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39905569-tdcs-dosage-parameters.md] | Response variability is larger in healthy older adults than in young adults, and adding tDCS to cognitive stimulation in aMCI did not beat sham.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40633904-tdcs-variability-age-2025.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40004217-tdcs-cognitive-stimulation-amci-null.md] | Inference: montage, current density, duration, and baseline vulnerability are doing more work than the class label suggests. |
| tACS | AD memory benefit survives in the older-adult meta-analysis, but healthy older adults do not separate from sham.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41034500-tacs-older-adults-meta-2025.md] | Healthy-adult generalization remains weak, so the branch is disease-state sensitive rather than broadly enhancer-like.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41034500-tacs-older-adults-meta-2025.md] | Inference: the target domain is probably state-bound, not modality-wide. |
| tPBM | The 2025 review plus newer older-adult and MCI/mTBI trials keep the branch credible.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40822571-tpbm-systematic-review-sleep-wakefulness-cognition.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40482941-tpbm-healthy-older-adults-working-memory-fnirs.md] | Persistent postconcussion symptoms stayed null after adjustment.^[raw/articles/pubmed-38109202-tpbm-concussion-null-rct.md] | Inference: recovery phenotype, target selection, and follow-up window matter more than "light on the head" as a generic intervention. |
| Creatine | Memory, attention, and processing speed improve in the pooled meta-analysis.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39070254-creatine-cognition-adults-meta-analysis.md] | Overall cognition and executive function do not improve, so the class is not a global enhancer.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39070254-creatine-cognition-adults-meta-analysis.md] | Inference: creatine is a domain-specific energy-buffering adjunct, not a universal cognitive booster. |
| taVNS | Healthy-adult meta-analysis and a disease-focused systematic review both keep interest alive.^[raw/articles/pubmed-34043386-taVNS-healthy-cognition-meta-analysis.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41717223-taVNS-cognitive-deficits-systematic-review.md] | Acute stress RCT was null on PASAT and self-report despite autonomic modulation.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40908377-taVNS-mental-stressor.md] | Inference: state regulation may be real while direct task-performance gains remain limited or context-specific. |
| Incretins / semaglutide | Secondary or biomarker enthusiasm can coexist with a plausible brain-metabolism story.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41865758-semaglutide-evoke-evokeplus-ad-phase3.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41218611-semaglutide-mdd-cognitive-dysfunction-rct.md] | Semaglutide was negative on the primary AD progression endpoint, exenatide in MCI was negative, and the MDD trial missed its primary executive-function endpoint.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41865758-semaglutide-evoke-evokeplus-ad-phase3.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-38565814-exenatide-mci-proof-of-concept-negative.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41218611-semaglutide-mdd-cognitive-dysfunction-rct.md] | Inference: biomarker movement and secondary composites are not enough to justify a broad cognition claim. |

## Why The Disagreements Keep Showing Up

- Phenotype mismatch: healthy adults, older adults, MCI/AD, MS, and recovery cohorts do not behave the same way.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40633904-tdcs-variability-age-2025.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40253245-intranasal-insulin-ms-null.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-38109202-tpbm-concussion-null-rct.md]
- Endpoint mismatch: some studies capture memory or processing speed, while others ask for global cognition, executive function, or delayed durability.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39070254-creatine-cognition-adults-meta-analysis.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41218611-semaglutide-mdd-cognitive-dysfunction-rct.md]
- Device or parameter mismatch: tDCS and intranasal insulin are especially sensitive to delivery details.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39905569-tdcs-dosage-parameters.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40703476-intranasal-insulin-pet-aging-mci.md]
- Duration mismatch: small immediate gains often fail to survive real follow-up.^[raw/articles/pubmed-38452749-tdcs-older-adults-mci-2024.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-38109202-tpbm-concussion-null-rct.md]
- Surrogate mismatch: biomarker movement, brain uptake, or autonomic change can be real without a corresponding cognition win.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40703476-intranasal-insulin-pet-aging-mci.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40908377-taVNS-mental-stressor.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41865758-semaglutide-evoke-evokeplus-ad-phase3.md]
