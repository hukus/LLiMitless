# Transcranial Photobiomodulation 2025-2026 Scan

## Summary

tPBM moved from a mostly exploratory healthy-adult signal into a small but more coherent 2024-2025 evidence cluster. The main pattern is not a large universal cognitive booster, but a repeatable signal in older or cognitively vulnerable groups plus physiologic target engagement in healthy adults. The 2025 systematic review frames the literature around sleep, wakefulness, glymphatic effects, and cognition, while the 2024-2025 trials add MoCA/working-memory signals, an mTBI rehabilitation signal, and biomarker or network changes. The new 2025 home-based MCI pilot adds the strongest metabolic readout yet, including lactate/pyruvate and IL-6 changes alongside memory and connectivity effects.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40822571-tpbm-systematic-review-sleep-wakefulness-cognition.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39423445-transcranial-photobiomodulation-cognition-bdnf.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40482941-tpbm-healthy-older-adults-working-memory-fnirs.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40485299-tpbm-mtbi-cognitive-postconcussion-ptsd.md]^[raw/articles/medrxiv-25333989-tpbm-mci-cognitive-metabolic-neuroimaging-pilot.md]

What changed since 2024 is that the evidence is less about a single positive pilot and more about convergence across domains:

- Older-adult / MCI trials now show cognition changes alongside BDNF or network findings, not just subjective impressions.
- Healthy-adult work now includes task performance and connectivity changes, not only physiology.
- A 2025 mTBI crossover RCT added a newer direct cognition-positive signal, but in a rehabilitation phenotype rather than healthy enhancement.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40485299-tpbm-mtbi-cognitive-postconcussion-ptsd.md]
- A 2025 home-based MCI pilot added a multimodal signal with cognition, metabolism, and DMN findings, strengthening the "mitochondria-first" interpretation even though the sample was tiny.^[raw/articles/medrxiv-25333989-tpbm-mci-cognitive-metabolic-neuroimaging-pilot.md]
- A 2024 persistent-postconcussion RCT was null after adjustment, which keeps the rehabilitation phenotype from becoming a blanket success story.^[raw/articles/pubmed-38109202-tpbm-concussion-null-rct.md]
- A 2025 dual-neuromodulation MCI RCT suggests tPBM may be more useful as an adjunct to another stimulation modality than as monotherapy in some settings.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40254536-combined-tms-tpbm-mci-working-memory.md]
- Active trials in AD and older adults suggest the field is still in a scale-up phase rather than a settled one.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct07224607-tpbm-alzheimers-study.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct04784416-trap-ad.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct07209683-near-infrared-light-young-older-adults.md]

The counterweight is still important: sample sizes remain small, sham design varies, and dose/target selection is not settled. The 2025 systematic review is helpful, but it is still descriptive rather than pooled, and its most useful guidance is methodological: study objective sleep measures, time-of-day effects, and consistent parameter reporting. So the best current summary is still a small-RCT plus systematic-review picture rather than a decisive meta-analytic one. This is still a protocol-sensitive modality, not a general-purpose cognitive enhancer.

## Evidence Tiering

- Meta/systematic review: 2025 review of sleep, wakefulness, and cognition; 17 eligible human studies spanning TBI, cognitive decline, and other populations; 2023 human systematic review as baseline.
- Randomized trials: 2024 MCI trial; 2025 healthy-older-adult crossover study; 2025 mTBI crossover trial; 2025 dual-neuromodulation MCI trial; 2025 sham-controlled AD/MCI study.
- Observational/biomarker: not the dominant evidence layer here; the strongest signals are interventional and physiologic.
- Preclinical: not central to this scan, though the mechanistic story still leans on mitochondrial and blood-flow hypotheses.

## Effect Size Snapshot

- 2024 MCI trial: MoCA improved more with tPBM than placebo, with a reported between-group advantage and persistence to follow-up; BDNF rose in the active arm.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39423445-transcranial-photobiomodulation-cognition-bdnf.md]
- 2025 healthy-older-adult crossover: active tPBM improved 3-back accuracy and reaction time, with concurrent increases in global/local efficiency and functional connectivity.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40482941-tpbm-healthy-older-adults-working-memory-fnirs.md]
- 2025 mTBI crossover: real tPBM improved visual working memory, verbal learning, sleep quality, post-concussion symptoms, pain, and PTSD symptoms versus sham.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40485299-tpbm-mtbi-cognitive-postconcussion-ptsd.md]
- 2024 persistent-concussion RCT: no significant difference between tPBM and placebo after adjustment, despite the rehabilitation rationale.^[raw/articles/pubmed-38109202-tpbm-concussion-null-rct.md]
- 2025 dual-neuromodulation MCI RCT: combined TMS + tPBM outperformed monostimulation and sham on a complex WM task, with supportive fNIRS connectivity changes.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40254536-combined-tms-tpbm-mci-working-memory.md]
- 2025 AD/MCI sham-controlled trial: K-MoCA improved at week 13; K-MMSE2 did not separate clearly; no serious adverse events were reported.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40939031-tpbm-mci-ad-sham-controlled.md]
- 2025 systematic review: the field supports potential improvements in sleep, wakefulness, and cognition, but the review is still descriptive rather than a pooled decisive meta-analysis; it is most useful as a reminder to measure objective sleep, control time of day, and report parameters consistently.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40822571-tpbm-systematic-review-sleep-wakefulness-cognition.md]

## Safety/Adverse Events

The current short-term safety picture is favorable in the published clinical trials captured here. The 2025 sham-controlled MCI/AD study reported no serious adverse events.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40939031-tpbm-mci-ad-sham-controlled.md] The main safety gap is not an obvious acute toxicity signal; it is the usual device-therapy problem of underpowered long-term safety surveillance, inconsistent sham blinding, and uncertain real-world misuse patterns.

## Responder Phenotypes

- Older adults with MCI or early AD look more promising than fully healthy young adults for measurable change.
- Healthy older adults still show task and network effects, which suggests a biologic response can exist before disease is present.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40482941-tpbm-healthy-older-adults-working-memory-fnirs.md]
- The literature still needs proper enrichment by baseline sleep quality, metabolic status, and cognitive reserve.

## Protocol Parameters

- 2024 MCI trial: near-infrared scalp tPBM over 60 days with follow-up at 150 days; 93 randomized, 76 completed.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39423445-transcranial-photobiomodulation-cognition-bdnf.md]
- 2025 healthy-older-adult study: 1064-nm laser to the left forehead in a randomized crossover design with pre/post fNIRS and n-back testing.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40482941-tpbm-healthy-older-adults-working-memory-fnirs.md]
- 2025 mTBI trial: randomized placebo-controlled crossover with 17 participants, 1-week washout, baseline vs real vs sham assessments, and visual working-memory / verbal-learning / symptom measures.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40485299-tpbm-mtbi-cognitive-postconcussion-ptsd.md]
- 2025 dual-neuromodulation MCI trial: 88 participants, combined TMS + tPBM, complex WM task, and fNIRS connectivity readouts.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40254536-combined-tms-tpbm-mci-working-memory.md]
- 2025 AD/MCI home-use study: self-administered tPBM six times per week for 12 weeks.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40939031-tpbm-mci-ad-sham-controlled.md]
- 2025 home-based MCI pilot: 810-nm pulsed tPBM for 20 minutes per session, 6 days per week for 6 weeks, with cognitive gains plus lactate/pyruvate, IL-6, and DMN connectivity changes.^[raw/articles/medrxiv-25333989-tpbm-mci-cognitive-metabolic-neuroimaging-pilot.md]
- TRAP-AD: 808-nm forehead delivery, 24 sessions over 8 weeks, ~11 minutes per session, 3 days per week, with imaging and target-engagement assessments.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct04784416-trap-ad.md]

## Open Contradictions

- Benefit appears stronger in older or impaired cohorts than in healthy adults, but the healthy-adult crossover study still shows objective signal.
- The mTBI result expands the phenotype space, but it is still a tiny crossover study and may be more about recovery than generic enhancement.
- The dual-neuromodulation result is encouraging but does not isolate the standalone effect of tPBM.
- The new home-based MCI pilot strengthens the biologic plausibility of tPBM, but it is still a 20-person study and may overstate durability or generalizability.
- Cognitive gains are reported, but the exact dose-response curve and ideal montage remain unsettled.
- Some studies emphasize biomarker or network change more than durable functional improvement.
- The review literature is still too small to settle whether tPBM is mainly a wakefulness tool, a memory tool, a rehabilitation adjunct, or a broad neuroenergetic intervention.
- The 2025 review makes the clearest case that sleep phenotyping and timing discipline are now core design variables, not optional details.
- The rehabilitation phenotype still includes nulls, so response is likely target- and population-sensitive rather than universal.
