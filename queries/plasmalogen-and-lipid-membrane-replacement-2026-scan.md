# Plasmalogen and Lipid-Membrane Replacement 2026 Scan

Related pages: [[plasmalogens]], [[supplement-identity-evidence-map]], [[cognition-intervention-master-table]], [[negative-evidence-watchlist-for-cognition-interventions]], [[molecule-intake-triage-table]], [[adjunctive-interventions-for-cognition]], and [[translational-gap-table]].

## Summary

- The branch is biologically plausible but human efficacy remains weak.
- The only clear human trial is the 2017 multicenter scallop-derived plasmalogen study in mild AD/MCI, and its primary MMSE-J endpoint was null in the intention-to-treat analysis. ^[raw/articles/pmc-5360580-plasmalogen-mci-mild-ad-rct.md]
- A subgroup signal remains in the mild-AD subgroup, especially in women and in participants age 77 or younger, so the branch is not a pure null, but the signal is not strong enough to call it a general enhancer. ^[raw/articles/pmc-5360580-plasmalogen-mci-mild-ad-rct.md]
- The 2022 college-athlete RCT adds a small secondary-endpoint mental-concentration and mood/fatigue signal at 2 mg/day purified scallop plasmalogen, but the primary mood endpoint was only marginal and the sample was small. ^[raw/articles/frontiers-2022-plasmalogen-athletes-mood-concentration-rct.md]
- The 2022 DHA-AAG precursor study should be kept as exposure/biomarker evidence rather than efficacy evidence, especially because Frontiers issued a 2026 Expression of Concern about compliance with local legislation. ^[raw/articles/frontiers-2022-dha-aag-plasmalogen-cognition-mobility-trial.md]^[raw/articles/frontiers-2026-expression-concern-targeted-plasmalogen.md]
- Newer lipidomics and APOE4 papers strengthen the deficiency hypothesis: AD-associated lipid remodeling is real, and APOE4 shows lower ethanolamine plasmalogen association in cognitively intact people. ^[raw/articles/pubmed-38667317-plasma-ev-lipidomics-ad.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41768790-plasmalogen-deficiency-apoe4.md]
- The key translation problem is product identity. The human trial used a scallop-derived purified plasmalogen jelly-like product, while the registry example describes an algae-derived omega-3 oil plasmalogen precursor. ^[raw/articles/pmc-5360580-plasmalogen-mci-mild-ad-rct.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct04484454-prodromeneuro-plasmalogen-precursor-cognitive-decline.md]
- Preclinical work is directionally supportive, but that does not repair the human endpoint mismatch. ^[raw/articles/pubmed-35253748-scallop-derived-plasmalogen-ad-mouse-hypoperfusion.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-33991494-scallop-derived-plasmalogen-ischemic-stroke-mouse.md]
- Anecdote signal is weak and product-specific: one ME/CFS forum report describes less brain fog on ProdromeGlia with product-switch reversal, while a Reddit thread mostly shows study-sharing interest rather than personal replication. ^[raw/articles/phoenixrising-2023-plasmalogen-experience-thread.md]^[raw/articles/reddit-2022-plasmalogen-study-sharing-thread.md]
- Treat this as a biomarker-backed, product-specific, recovery-oriented branch, not a broad nootropic lane.

## Evidence Tiering

- Meta / review: the 2019 and 2022 reviews support a plausible deficiency-and-replacement story, but they remain mechanistic and early.
- Human RCT: the 2017 multicenter scallop-derived plasmalogen trial is the branch anchor and is negative on the primary endpoint; the 2022 athlete trial is secondary-endpoint positive but small and short.
- Human observational / biomarker: the 2024 EV lipidomics study and the 2026 APOE4 paper support AD-related lipid remodeling and a possible causal deficit.
- Clinical registry / precursor: the 2020-2021 open-label ProdromeNeuro study shows commercial interest but no posted results; the 2022 DHA-AAG precursor paper is open-label and now expression-of-concern constrained.
- Preclinical: mouse studies in AD-like hypoperfusion and ischemic stroke models are directionally positive.

## Effect Size Snapshot

- Human cognition: no convincing between-group MMSE-J effect in the main RCT.
- Subgroup signal: memory-related improvement in mild AD, especially in women and younger participants, but this is not robust enough to generalize.
- Athlete concentration signal: Uchida-Kraepelin performance improved more with plasmalogen than placebo, but this was a small short trial and not a broad cognition battery.
- Biomarker signal: blood and CSF lipidomics support the idea that ethanolamine plasmalogen deficiency is real in AD-related states.

## Safety / Practical Notes

- Shellfish source matters for allergy risk and consumer confusion.
- The tested human product is not interchangeable with generic supplement labels that say "plasmalogen" or "plasmalogen precursor."
- The branch is low on classic toxicity concerns, but high on identity and reproducibility risk.
- Commercial products should not be treated as the same exposure without lot-level chemistry and source verification.

## Product Identity

- The human RCT used scallop-derived purified plasmalogen.
- The clinicaltrials registry example describes an algae-derived omega-3 oil with naturally occurring alkylglycerol and fatty acids that function as a plasmalogen precursor.
- Those are related ideas, not the same intervention.
- The wiki should treat commercial plasmalogen claims as product-specific until the active unit, source, and QA are anchored.

## Open Contradictions

- Deficiency plausibility is stronger than efficacy plausibility.
- Biomarker movement and mechanistic logic do not recover the null primary endpoint in the human RCT.
- The APOE4 paper makes causality more plausible, but it does not prove that replacing the lipid improves cognition in humans.
- Preclinical benefit does not bridge the gap to healthy-adult enhancement.

## What Changed Since 2024

- The 2026 APOE4 CSF lipidomics paper sharpened the causal argument by showing lower ethanolamine plasmalogen association in cognitively intact E4 carriers.
- The 2024 plasma EV lipidomics study reinforced that AD is associated with broad circulating lipid changes, not just a single marker.
- The registry record for ProdromeNeuro shows continued commercial interest, but it also makes the identity gap more obvious because the exposure is framed as a plasmalogen precursor oil rather than purified scallop plasmalogen.
- The 2026 Expression of Concern materially weakens the DHA-AAG precursor clinical-response story until the investigation is resolved.
- The human efficacy position has not improved materially: the main RCT is still the same negative primary-endpoint result with subgroup-only optimism.
