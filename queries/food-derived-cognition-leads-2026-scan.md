# Food-Derived Cognition Leads 2026 Scan

## Summary

This branch is not a single mechanism story. It is a product-identity story.

- Hericium erinaceus now has a mixed human record: a positive 2009 MCI trial, a positive 2020 erinacine-A mycelia AD pilot, a null 2022 healthy-adult exercise/fatigue trial, a 2024 healthy-young-adult pilot with only an acute Stroop signal, and a 2026 preprint with a stronger but sponsor-sensitive positive result. That is enough to keep the species in the wiki, but not enough to treat every lion's mane product as interchangeable.^[raw/articles/pubmed-18844328-hericium-erinaceus-mci-rct.md]^[raw/articles/frontiers-2020-hericium-erinacine-a-early-ad.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-36582308-hericium-erinaceus-null-cognition-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-38004235-hericium-erinaceus-young-adults-pilot.md]^[raw/articles/medrxiv-26350781-hericium-erinaceus-lions-mane-cognition-pilot.md]
- [[ergothioneine]] is promising but still immature: the 2024 MCI pilot is encouraging, the healthy-adult registry completed with `n=4` and no posted results, and the 2025 review reads more optimistic than the public intervention record justifies.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39544014-ergothioneine-mci-pilot.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct04556032-ergothioneine-cognition-mood-sleep.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40968729-ergothioneine-cognitive-health-review.md]
- [[dietary-nucleotides]] are the cleanest surprise positive in the scan, but the result is tightly tied to older adults, named RiboDiet / RiboCARE Neuro formulations, and continued spontaneous physical activity.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40362739-dietary-nucleotides-older-adults-rct.md]^[raw/articles/mdpi-2025-dietary-nucleotides-older-adults-rct-fulltext.md]^[raw/articles/prosol-2026-ribodiet-ribocare-product-identity.md]
- [[mfgm-phospholipids]] did not move the primary cognition endpoints in the largest older-adult trial, so the branch currently belongs on the alertness/biomarker side of the ledger. The smaller female episodic-memory and post-stress reaction-time hints are useful for phenotype tracking, but not enough to override the null primary readout.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40939691-mfgm-phospholipid-cognition-rct.md]^[raw/articles/sciencedirect-2025-mfgm-phospholipid-cognition-rct.md]^[raw/articles/sciencedirect-2023-mfgm-enriched-milk-episodic-memory-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-30172995-milk-phospholipids-stress-cognition-rct.md]
- [[5-htp-tryptophan|5-HTP]] produced a small MoCA and mood signal in older adults, but the single-blind design, sleep/mood mediation, and serotonergic mechanism make it better treated as a preliminary comparator than a general food-derived nootropic.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40944161-5htp-cognition-mood-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-16023217-5htp-serotonin-precursor-safety-review.md]
- [[soy-isoflavones]] now have a 2026 meta-analysis plus a 2025 fermented-soy RCT. The signal is modest and memory-leaning, with WISH global-cognition null and exposure-analysis counterweights, so it belongs in a separate food-derived / nutraceutical-proxy lane rather than in the herbal-extract ranking.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41483829-isoflavone-cognition-meta-analysis.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41010462-fermented-soy-cognition-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pmc-12472887-fermented-soy-cognition-rct-fulltext.md]^[raw/articles/pmc-3369523-wish-soy-isoflavones-cognition-rct-fulltext.md]^[raw/articles/pmc-4226524-wish-isoflavonoid-exposure-cognition-posthoc.md]
- The right wiki move is to keep these leads separated until product identity, phenotype, and endpoint durability are cleaner. This is exactly the kind of branch where the botanical-standardization gate prevents overpromotion.^[raw/articles/pubmed-36582308-hericium-erinaceus-null-cognition-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-38004235-hericium-erinaceus-young-adults-pilot.md]^[raw/articles/medrxiv-26350781-hericium-erinaceus-lions-mane-cognition-pilot.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39544014-ergothioneine-mci-pilot.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40362739-dietary-nucleotides-older-adults-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40939691-mfgm-phospholipid-cognition-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40944161-5htp-cognition-mood-rct.md]

## What Changed Since 2024

- Hericium gained both a null healthy-adult study and a newer positive preprint, which makes product identity more important than species-level enthusiasm.^[raw/articles/pubmed-36582308-hericium-erinaceus-null-cognition-rct.md]^[raw/articles/medrxiv-26350781-hericium-erinaceus-lions-mane-cognition-pilot.md]
- [[ergothioneine]] moved from mostly observational/biologic plausibility into at least one completed MCI pilot with learning and neurofilament findings, plus a tiny healthy-adult registry with no public efficacy result.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39544014-ergothioneine-mci-pilot.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct04556032-ergothioneine-cognition-mood-sleep.md]
- [[dietary-nucleotides]] and [[5-htp-tryptophan|5-HTP]] now have direct older-adult cognition trials, which means they can be compared on endpoint quality instead of only mechanism.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40362739-dietary-nucleotides-older-adults-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40944161-5htp-cognition-mood-rct.md]
- [[mfgm-phospholipids]] now have a large null cognition trial with a smaller alertness signal, plus older small-study hints that remain subgroup- and stress-context bound.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40939691-mfgm-phospholipid-cognition-rct.md]^[raw/articles/sciencedirect-2023-mfgm-enriched-milk-episodic-memory-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-30172995-milk-phospholipids-stress-cognition-rct.md]
- [[soy-isoflavones]] and fermented soy now add a food-derived nutraceutical lane with a modest memory signal, a clearer long-duration global-null counterweight, and weak menopause-confounded community signal, which makes product identity and phenotype the deciding factors.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41483829-isoflavone-cognition-meta-analysis.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41010462-fermented-soy-cognition-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-22665144-soy-isoflavones-cognition-rct.md]^[raw/articles/reddit-2024-soy-perimenopause-brain-fog-thread.md]
- The 2025-2026 evidence set reinforces that "food-derived" is not a sufficient category for ranking. Formulation, dose, phenotype, and endpoint type decide whether a branch is actionable.

## Evidence Tiering

- Meta/systematic review: Hericium narrative review; ergothioneine cognition review.
- Randomized clinical trials: 2009 Hericium MCI, 2020 erinacine A-enriched mycelia early-AD pilot, 2022 Hericium null healthy-adult study, 2024 Hericium pilot in young adults, 2026 Hericium preprint, 2024 ergothioneine MCI pilot, 2025 dietary nucleotide RCT, 2025 MFGM older-adult RCT, 2023 MFGM-enriched milk pilot, 2019 milk-phospholipid stress trial, 2025 5-HTP RCT.
- Registry / protocol: ergothioneine trial NCT04556032, a tiny completed healthy-adult registry with no posted results.
- Preclinical: lion's mane has a much larger rodent/mechanistic literature, but that layer should not be treated as human cognition proof.

## Safety / Adverse Events

- Hericium looked tolerable in the small published human trials, with mild gastrointestinal symptoms in the older MCI study and no adverse events in the 2026 preprint.
- [[ergothioneine]] was well tolerated in the MCI pilot, with no apparent liver or kidney toxicity in the abstract.
- [[dietary-nucleotides]], [[mfgm-phospholipids]], and 5-HTP did not surface major acute safety signals in the abstracts, but their sample sizes are too small to move them into a high-confidence safety tier. MFGM additionally carries dairy/lactose/allergen and mild GI tolerability caveats, and the stress trial's blood-pressure augmentation keeps it out of a clean safety-positive bucket. The adjacent TALENTs nucleotide aging RCT also looked tolerable, while its transient uric-acid finding keeps purine / gout context on the monitoring list.^[raw/articles/pmc-12412603-talents-nucleotides-aging-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-30172995-milk-phospholipids-stress-cognition-rct.md]
- [[5-htp-tryptophan|5-HTP]] remains the most interaction-sensitive branch because serotonin-raising supplements become more complicated when antidepressants or other serotonergic agents are in play; the trial does not settle that risk.
- [[soy-isoflavones]] carry food/supplement form caveats, soy allergy, GI effects, endocrine-context uncertainty, and tyramine-rich soy / MAOI interaction flags; those are product and phenotype gates, not proof of cognitive harm.^[raw/articles/nccih-2025-soy-usefulness-safety.md]

## Responder Phenotypes

- Hericium looks more convincing in older or cognitively compromised cohorts than in healthy young adults, but that is still an inference from a mixed sample.
- [[ergothioneine]] may be more plausible when baseline status is low, but the public trial record is not yet large enough to claim a validated responder phenotype.
- [[dietary-nucleotides]] were tested in physically independent older adults who kept spontaneous physical activity in place.
- [[mfgm-phospholipids]] were tested mainly in older adults with subjective memory complaints, with smaller female and high-perfectionist stress-task signals that should stay hypothesis-level.
- [[5-htp-tryptophan|5-HTP]]'s responder story is not established; the current signal is too small for anything stronger than a cautious "mood-linked older-adult" hypothesis.
- [[soy-isoflavones]] look most plausible as a memory-leaning older-adult or postmenopausal branch, but age, sex, product form, baseline soy intake, and equol-producer status remain unresolved.

## Protocol Parameters

- Hericium 2009 MCI: 3 g/day powdered Yamabushitake for 16 weeks.
- Hericium 2020 early AD pilot: three 350 mg erinacine A-enriched mycelia capsules per day for 49 weeks.
- Hericium 2022 null trial: 10 g/day for 4 weeks around a fatiguing exercise test.
- Hericium 2024 young-adult pilot: 1.8 g acute dosing plus a 28-day chronic phase.
- Hericium 2026 preprint: 2 g/day for 8 weeks.
- [[ergothioneine]] 2024 MCI pilot: 25 mg capsules, three times per week, for 1 year.
- [[dietary-nucleotides]]: 10-week intervention with 250 mg/day RiboDiet yeast-nucleotide formulation or 150 mg/day RiboCARE Neuro monophosphate formulation.
- [[mfgm-phospholipids]]: 1.7 g/day or 4.0 g/day phospholipid dosing for 16 weeks in the largest older-adult trial; smaller MFGM-enriched milk and milk-phospholipid stress studies should not be treated as interchangeable products.
- [[5-htp-tryptophan|5-HTP]]: 100 mg/day for 12 weeks.
- [[soy-isoflavones]]: 91 mg/day aglycone-weight isoflavones for 2.5 years in WISH; 12 weeks non-probiotic fermented soy powder in the newer older-adult RCT; these are not interchangeable products.

## Open Contradictions

- Whole-mushroom, standardized-extract, and erinacine-enriched mycelial Hericium products are not interchangeable.
- The 2025 lion's mane review reads more optimistic than the mixed trial record justifies.
- [[ergothioneine]] is easier to sell as a longevity nutrient than to prove as a cognition intervention.
- [[dietary-nucleotides]] may be real, but the benefit is tied to a specific formulation plus background activity.
- [[mfgm-phospholipids]] may be useful for alertness, inflammation, or stress-task resilience without clearing the bar for broad cognition efficacy.
- [[5-htp-tryptophan|5-HTP]] could be an arousal/mood signal masquerading as a cognition signal.
- [[soy-isoflavones]] show a memory-leaning meta-analytic signal, but the longest high-adherence postmenopausal trial stayed null on global cognition and the newer fermented-soy signal is small and subgroup-sensitive.
