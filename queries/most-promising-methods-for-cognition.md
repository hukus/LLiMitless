# Most Promising Methods for Improving Cognition

This page ranks the wiki's current intervention set in two different ways:

1. Scientific efficacy for cognition — human evidence first, with direct cognitive outcomes weighted above mechanistic plausibility.
2. Anecdotal strength — how often the intervention shows up as a persistent, enthusiastic, or self-experimenter-friendly signal in the wiki.

Important: anecdotal strength is not evidence of efficacy. It only tells us which interventions generate the loudest or most durable reports, and therefore deserve to be tracked rather than dismissed. The June 2026 nootropic pass makes that split sharper: [[gray-zone-nootropic-community-signal-ledger]] and [[nootropic-peptide-anecdote-frontier-2026-scan]] now treat [[bromantane]], [[semax]], [[selank]], [[cerebrolysin]], and recovery-bound [[bpc-157]] as the strongest current nootropic/peptide anecdote-priority nodes, while [[dihexa]], [[pe-22-28]], [[adamax]], [[epithalon]], and [[pinealon]] stay tracked but de-ranked as frontier or state-bound hypotheses.

## Tight top-5 shortlist

| Rank | Method | Confidence | Best for | Why it belongs here |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [[cbt-i-and-cognition]] | High | People whose cognition is being dragged down by insomnia, fragmented sleep, or hyperarousal | Best-supported route to better cognition is still fixing sleep, and CBT-I is the strongest sleep intervention in the wiki |
| 2 | [[bright-light-therapy-and-cognition]] | Moderate | Morning grogginess, circadian misalignment, seasonal worsening, shift-work patterns | Strong practical option when the real problem is timing and alertness rather than a primary memory disorder |
| 3 | [[sleep-banking-and-sleep-extension]] | Moderate for short-term performance, low for durable cognition | Anyone facing predictable sleep loss, heavy training, travel, or exam-like load | Best as a performance buffer; the cognitive benefit is real but mostly short-horizon |
| 4 | [[repetitive-transcranial-magnetic-stimulation-and-cognition]] | Moderate | Selected clinical populations and protocolized neuromodulation settings | Most mature brain-stimulation option here, but results depend heavily on protocol and patient phenotype |
| 5 | [[creatine-cognitive-support]] | Moderate | People with low baseline creatine intake, high energy demand, sleep loss, or aging-related cognitive vulnerability | Low-cost adjunct with plausible upside and decent tolerability |

A useful reading rule: the evidence ranking above is not the same as the anecdote ranking below. The most scientifically defensible options are usually sleep/circadian interventions, while the loudest stories often cluster around peptide, stimulant-like, recovery, and device-based interventions.

## Tight top-5 anecdotal shortlist

| Rank | Method | Confidence in anecdotal signal | Why it shows up |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [[cerebrolysin]] / [[peptide-neuroprotection]] | Very high, polarized | Strong recovery stories, recurring cognition/neurorecovery reports, and serious adverse-event counterreports |
| 2 | [[semax]] / [[selank]] | High | Strong survey-plus-forum salience, with Semax leaning focus/strain-state and Selank leaning calm/stress-state |
| 3 | [[bromantane]] | Moderate-strong | One of the cleaner state-bound activation anecdotes: drive, fatigue resistance, and work output, but formal evidence is stronger for asthenia than healthy cognition |
| 4 | [[bpc-157]] | Moderate-strong | Recovery-oriented peptide with injury, pain, and training-continuity stories; direct cognition remains weak and recovery-bound |
| 5 | [[transcranial-photobiomodulation]] | High | Popular in self-experimentation and biohacking circles; easy to frame as a cognitive enhancer |

A good rule here is that anecdotal strength is often highest where the intervention feels tangible, novel, activating, calming, or recovery-oriented. That makes peptides, stimulant-like nootropics, light-based devices, oxygen therapy, and device-based neuromodulation especially visible, even when the evidence is still immature. The current strongest anecdote-monitoring cluster breaks down like this: [[semax]] is a focus and strain-state peptide signal, [[selank]] is a calm/stress-state signal with indirect cognition relevance, [[cerebrolysin]] is the strongest formal peptide node but remains medical/recovery-bound, [[bpc-157]] belongs here as an injury, pain, sleep, rehab, and training-continuity mediator rather than as a direct cognition candidate, and [[bromantane]] is a non-peptide activation comparator whose signal is fatigue-rescue/work-output rather than broad healthy-adult cognition proof. [[dihexa]], [[pe-22-28]], and [[adamax]] remain important frontier nodes, but the June 2026 peptide scan de-ranks them below the Semax/Selank/Cerebrolysin/BPC-157/Bromantane cluster because their human cognition evidence, source identity, and route/formulation evidence are weaker. Older peptide bioregulators such as [[cortexin]], [[kortagen]], [[epithalon]], and [[pinealon]] remain searchable routing nodes, but they should not inherit the stronger cluster's anecdote priority.

## Practical synthesis

If the goal is the most defensible overall cognition improvement, the current shortlist is:

- [[cbt-i-and-cognition]] for sleep-driven cognitive drag
- [[bright-light-therapy-and-cognition]] for circadian misalignment and daytime alertness
- [[sleep-banking-and-sleep-extension]] when the near-term problem is sleep loss
- [[repetitive-transcranial-magnetic-stimulation-and-cognition]] when a protocolized neuromodulation approach is desired
- [[creatine-cognitive-support]] as a low-cost adjunct with a plausible upside

If the goal is to capture the strongest anecdotal signal, the current shortlist shifts toward:

- [[cerebrolysin]] and [[peptide-neuroprotection]]
- [[semax]] and [[selank]]
- [[bromantane]]
- [[bpc-157]]
- [[transcranial-photobiomodulation]]

That split is the main lesson so far: the interventions with the most convincing stories are not always the ones with the best proof, and the best-proven interventions are often the least glamorous.
