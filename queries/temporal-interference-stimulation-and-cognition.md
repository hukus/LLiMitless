# Temporal Interference Stimulation and Cognition

Temporal interference stimulation (TI or tTIS/tIS) has moved from a mostly theoretical deep-target concept to a real human research program. The newest direct cognition papers now add a 2025 verbal working-memory EEG signal and a 2026 cognitive-control paper on top of the 2025 memory-encoding preprint, so the branch is no longer just target-validation. The pattern is still strongly parameter- and phenotype-dependent: target, load, lateralization, and working-memory capacity all change the sign or size of the effect. Human tTIS is becoming mechanistically credible and validation-rich, but broad durable cognitive efficacy is still not established.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41167554-human-applications-temporal-interference-stimulation.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40520674-advances-in-the-application-of-temporal-interference-stimulation-scoping-review.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40925398-temporal-interference-experimental-studies-human-review.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40354819-differential-modulation-sensorimotor-network-ti-vs-hdtdcs.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41040248-temporal-interference-memory-encoding.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41808199-temporal-interference-cognitive-control.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40751659-temporal-interference-verbal-working-memory.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41079480-personalized-temporal-interference-striatum-sensorimotor-network.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41485554-ti-toolbox-open-source-software-temporal-interference-research.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41654211-temporal-interference-prolonged-disorders-consciousness-pilot.md]

**What changed since 2024**

- Human TI is no longer just a proof-of-principle animal story. The 2025 systematic review counted 48 records with 20 published studies and 28 ongoing trials, and it found mostly mild adverse events plus small acute symptom improvements in Parkinson's disease and essential tremor within 60 minutes of a single session.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41167554-human-applications-temporal-interference-stimulation.md]
- The 2025 scoping review is broader rather than more positive: it screened 708 records and included 33 studies, reinforcing that the field has crossed into deep-target applications while still lacking outcome-definitive cognition data.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40520674-advances-in-the-application-of-temporal-interference-stimulation-scoping-review.md]
- The direct cognition layer has now thickened: a 2025 verbal-working-memory EEG paper found a high-load, right-lateralized effect with low-load nulls, and a 2026 cognitive-control paper found dACC benefit regardless of working-memory capacity while dlPFC benefit was limited to low-WMC participants.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40751659-temporal-interference-verbal-working-memory.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41808199-temporal-interference-cognitive-control.md]
- The companion experimental review makes the parameter lesson sharper: motor-cortex connectivity gains were similar to tDCS, beta-band envelopes (20 Hz) were linked to learning-related plasticity, gamma-band envelopes (70 Hz) to immediate performance changes, and deep-target findings included episodic recall, motor learning, and spatial-navigation signals.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40925398-temporal-interference-experimental-studies-human-review.md]
- The first modern cognition-facing human evidence remains bidirectional: TI can improve memory at one frequency/target combination and impair it at another, so the same tool can help or hurt depending on the envelope and location.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41040248-temporal-interference-memory-encoding.md]
- A 2025 randomized crossover study against HD-tDCS showed TI can move network metrics too: 20 Hz / 2 mA stimulation of left M1 produced stronger sensorimotor-network connectivity and graph-theory efficiency changes than the comparator, which is target-validation evidence rather than cognition proof.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40354819-differential-modulation-sensorimotor-network-ti-vs-hdtdcs.md]
- A 2025 personalized striatal study suggests individual anatomy and target modeling matter: personalized TI at 20 Hz / 10 mA reduced SMA functional stability and striatal dynamic-connectivity variability without reported adverse cognitive effects.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41079480-personalized-temporal-interference-striatum-sensorimotor-network.md]
- Human target-engagement studies now show orbitofrontal, parahippocampal, hippocampal, basal-ganglia, and motor-cortex effects, but several are explicitly mechanistic rather than efficacy trials.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39059712-temporal-interference-orbitofrontal-bold.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41167554-human-applications-temporal-interference-stimulation.md]
- The 2026 TI-Toolbox paper adds an infrastructure layer: open-source MRI preprocessing, field simulation, montage optimization, and ROI analysis should make future TI work more reproducible and easier to personalize.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41485554-ti-toolbox-open-source-software-temporal-interference-research.md]
- A 2025 preclinical mouse study sharpened the target-precision story by showing superior-colliculus engagement and eye-movement modulation, but that remains mechanistic support only and does not change the human cognition-efficacy position.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40214945-temporal-interference-eye-movements.md]
- A 2026 pilot study moved TI into prolonged disorders of consciousness, which is clinically meaningful but still not a healthy-adult cognition result because the public PubMed entry does not expose an abstract or results summary yet.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41654211-temporal-interference-prolonged-disorders-consciousness-pilot.md]
- Registry activity has shifted toward cognition-adjacent and psychiatric targets, especially social cognition, cognitive control, decision-making, addiction, depression, and other deep-brain dysfunction programs.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct06607432-temporal-interference-social-cognition.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct07215299-temporal-interference-methods-study-1-1.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct07339072-temporal-interference-methods-study-1-2.md]
- As of the current scrape, those registry studies still show no posted results, so the branch remains validation-first rather than efficacy-proven.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct06607432-temporal-interference-social-cognition.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct07215299-temporal-interference-methods-study-1-1.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct07339072-temporal-interference-methods-study-1-2.md]

## Evidence Tiering

| Tier | What it shows | What it does not show |
|---|---|---|
| Systematic review | Human TI is feasible, often well tolerated, and now has a non-trivial trial pipeline | It still does not establish broad cognitive enhancement |
| Scoping review | The literature has expanded enough to show deep-target applications across multiple domains | It still does not establish outcome-definitive cognition benefit |
| Direct cognition paper | dACC stimulation improved cognitive control regardless of working-memory capacity; dlPFC only helped low-WMC individuals | It is task-specific, acute, and not a general enhancement claim |
| Direct cognition paper | Right-hemisphere TI under high verbal-load conditions shifted EEG markers of verbal working memory | Low-load conditions were null |
| Target engagement | fMRI and related measures show hippocampal, OFC, basal-ganglia, and motor-cortex effects | Target engagement is not the same thing as durable performance gain |
| Experimental review | Human studies can shift motor connectivity, learning-related plasticity, episodic recall, and spatial navigation | The evidence base is still small and protocol-fragmented |
| Head-to-head RCT | TI can change sensorimotor-network connectivity and graph-theory efficiency more than HD-tDCS in one healthy-volunteer protocol | Network metrics are not cognition endpoints |
| Personalized network study | Individualized striatal TI changes SMA functional stability and dFC variability | Still mechanistic and motor-network centered |
| Cognition preprint | Memory encoding can move up or down depending on envelope frequency and target | The signal is still preprint-level and task-specific |
| Tooling / reproducibility | MRI preprocessing, montage optimization, and field simulation are now being standardized in open-source software | Better tooling does not itself prove efficacy |
| Clinical pilot | Prolonged disorders of consciousness pilot study appears in 2026 literature | The public record does not yet expose abstract-level results |
| Registry pipeline | Trials are moving toward cognition-adjacent and psychiatric applications | Most are early-stage, small, and not yet outcome-definitive |

## Safety / Adverse Events

- Across the human review literature, adverse events are generally mild, with tingling and itching the usual report.
- The newer direct cognition papers did not surface a new serious safety signal in their abstracts.
- The main safety story today is still tolerability plus MRI/fMRI compatibility and device-specific constraints, not a new serious signal.
- Several registry studies are explicitly using healthy-volunteer safety and feasibility designs before any disease claims.
- The bigger risk is interpretive: the field can easily overread focal activation as functional improvement.

## Responder Phenotypes

- `Target-specific` responders are the clearest pattern so far: the memory study suggests the same intervention can flip sign depending on where and how it is delivered.
- `Frequency-specific` responders appear plausible, especially in the hippocampal memory branch.
- `Load-specific` responders are now visible in verbal working memory and Stroop control.
- `Working-memory-capacity` appears to moderate dlPFC response in the 2026 cognitive-control paper.
- `Lateralization` matters: the verbal working-memory study found a right-hemisphere effect under high load.
- `Task-coupled` effects matter: the human review notes stronger engagement when stimulation is paired with behavioral tasks.
- `Cognition-adjacent clinical` phenotypes may matter more than healthy enhancement, with current registry work leaning toward social cognition, addiction, and psychiatric disorders.

## Protocol Parameters

- The 2025 systematic review reports that published human studies most often use beta or gamma envelope frequencies, with deep-target engagement in motor cortex, basal ganglia, and hippocampus.
- The companion experimental review found 20 Hz beta envelopes linked to learning-related plasticity, 70 Hz gamma envelopes linked to immediate performance improvements, and several deep-target protocols spanning striatum, hippocampus, globus pallidus, and caudate.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40925398-temporal-interference-experimental-studies-human-review.md]
- The 2025 memory-encoding preprint used 130 Hz offset and 5 Hz offset conditions over bilateral hippocampi and temporal cortex, plus paired fMRI and recall testing.
- The 2025 verbal-working-memory EEG paper paired left/right-hemisphere TIS with a delayed recognition task and found high-load right-parietal EEG shifts; the PubMed abstract does not expose the full beat-frequency details.
- The 2026 cognitive-control paper compared TI over dlPFC, dACC, and sham while stratifying by working-memory capacity; the abstract emphasizes the target-by-WMC interaction more than the field settings.
- The 2024 orbitofrontal BOLD paper used 20 Hz beat frequency settings aimed at the left caudate and saw increased OFC and parahippocampal activation.
- The Indiana University study 1.2 uses 5 Hz and 10 Hz TI settings with 2 mA per electrode pair, 2-minute on / 2-minute off cycles, and 30-second ramp periods.
- The Columbia social-cognition study escalates dose in healthy volunteers and pairs TI with fMRI, MRS, EEG, and face-emotion recognition.

## Open Contradictions

- TI is now good enough to change human brain signals, but that does not mean it is good enough to improve cognition.
- The same deep-target logic can produce enhancement, nulls, or disruption depending on frequency, location, and task.
- Some current studies are about cognition directly, while others are only about feasibility or target validation.
- The 2025 head-to-head and personalization papers strengthen the target-validation story, but they are still network-metric studies rather than cognition trials.
- The 2025 verbal-working-memory and 2026 cognitive-control papers are real cognition papers, but they are acute and task-specific rather than broad enhancement proof.
- The 2026 TI-Toolbox makes modeling and reproducibility better, but it does not close the efficacy gap by itself.
- The 2026 DoC pilot shows the branch reaching consciousness recovery, but the public record is still too sparse to call it an efficacy win.
- The narrative review language is still more optimistic than the outcome data justify.
- The 2025 superior-colliculus paper strengthens the target-engagement story, but only in mice; human registry results are still the missing layer.
