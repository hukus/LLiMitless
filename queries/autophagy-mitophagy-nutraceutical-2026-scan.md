# Autophagy / Mitophagy Nutraceutical 2026 Scan

## Summary

This branch is not a single positive story. It splits into five different evidence shapes:

- [[spermidine]] has the clearest direct human cognition signal in this family, but the 12-month SmartAge phase IIb RCT is primary-endpoint null, so the branch remains low-confidence and product/dose-sensitive.
- [[nicotinamide-riboside]] is the strongest biomarker-positive branch, but two completed cognition trials did not show a clear cognitive gain.
- [[nicotinamide-mononucleotide]] / NMN looks more like a NAD+ / sleep / walking proxy branch than a cognition branch, and consumer product identity is a major transportability gate.
- [[ergothioneine]] now has a positive 2025 review and a 19-person MCI pilot, but the public intervention record is still tiny: the only completed healthy-adult registry enrolled 4 people and posted no results.
- [[urolithin-a]] is still preclinical/pending for cognition, though the human side now combines a 650-person active-not-recruiting cognition registry with muscle/mitochondrial proxy trials, GRAS/product-identity anchors, and mixed community reports.

The practical take-away is that this family should stay out of broad "most promising cognition methods" rankings until the direct human signals are larger, better standardized, and reproducible across product identity and phenotype.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct07060898-urolithin-a-clarity-cognition.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-30388439-spermidine-memory-performance-scd.md]^[raw/articles/pmc-9136623-smartage-spermidine-scd-rct-null.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-33211152-spermidine-dementia-first-results.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-37994989-nr-mci-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39817194-nr-scd-mci-biomarker-effects.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-38789831-nmn-older-adults-walking-sleep.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-36139790-ergothioneine-cognitive-functional-decline.md]

## Branch Map

| Compound | Best human readout | Bottom line | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| [[spermidine]] | Small phase IIa SCD memory pilot and dementia preliminary signal, counterweighted by SmartAge phase IIb null | Narrow memory-watch signal, but product identity, dose, and primary-endpoint replication limit confidence | Standalone entity exists; keep low-confidence until a larger product-normalized trial replicates benefit |
| [[nicotinamide-riboside]] | NAD+ and pTau217 move, cognition stays flat | Biomarker-positive, cognition-null so far | Entity exists for low-confidence tracking; one completed MCI pilot plus one completed SCD/MCI trial; one recruiting cognition/sleep trial remains and still uses sleep quality as the primary endpoint |
| [[nicotinamide-mononucleotide]] / NMN | Blood NAD+, sleep/drowsiness, walking, and lower-limb-function proxies | Proxy-positive, direct cognition not established | Standalone entity exists for proxy, FDA-status, product-QA, and anecdote tracking; no cognition-specific active registry found in this sweep |
| [[ergothioneine]] | 19-person MCI pilot, observational decline prediction, tiny registry lead | Low-confidence MCI/biomarker support; healthy-adult proof still missing | Standalone entity exists; finished healthy-adult registry had `n=4` and no posted results |
| [[urolithin-a]] | Active cognition registry; human proxy data in muscle, inflammation, and mitochondrial markers | Cognition is still pending; biology is plausible | Standalone entity exists; active-not-recruiting direct cognition registry, 650 enrolled, plus human proxy studies and product-identity gate |

## Evidence Tiering

- `Direct human cognition`
  - [[spermidine]] is the only branch here with repeated cognition-relevant human sources, but SmartAge is a larger null counterweight. Treat the branch as low-confidence memory-watch evidence rather than a demonstrated enhancer.^[raw/articles/pubmed-30388439-spermidine-memory-performance-scd.md]^[raw/articles/pmc-9136623-smartage-spermidine-scd-rct-null.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-33211152-spermidine-dementia-first-results.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40180050-dietary-spermidine-nhanes-cognition.md]
  - [[nicotinamide-riboside]] has direct cognition trials, but the measured cognitive outcomes stayed flat while biomarkers moved.^[raw/articles/pubmed-37994989-nr-mci-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39817194-nr-scd-mci-biomarker-effects.md]
  - [[ergothioneine]] has a small MCI pilot plus a registry lead, but the healthy-adult registry still has no informative posted efficacy result.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39544014-ergothioneine-mci-pilot.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct04556032-ergothioneine-cognition-mood-sleep.md]
  - [[urolithin-a]] has an active cognition registry, not a posted cognition result.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct07060898-urolithin-a-clarity-cognition.md]
- `Biomarker-only / mechanistic`
  - [[nicotinamide-riboside]] is the cleanest example: NAD+ rises, pTau217 falls, cognition does not clearly change.^[raw/articles/pubmed-37994989-nr-mci-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39817194-nr-scd-mci-biomarker-effects.md]
  - [[ergothioneine]] observational work links low levels to decline and suggests a vascular-brain mechanism, but causality is unproven.^[raw/articles/pubmed-36139790-ergothioneine-cognitive-functional-decline.md]
- `Muscle / metabolic proxy`
  - [[nicotinamide-mononucleotide]] and [[urolithin-a]] both have stronger proxy data than cognition data in humans right now.^[raw/articles/pubmed-38789831-nmn-older-adults-walking-sleep.md]^[raw/articles/pmc-8877443-nmn-sleep-fatigue-older-adults-rct.md]^[raw/articles/springer-2022-nmn-dose-dependent-healthy-adults-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39487653-urolithin-a-athletes-rct.md]^[raw/articles/jama-2022-urolithin-a-older-adults-muscle-endurance.md]^[raw/articles/sciencedirect-2022-urolithin-a-middle-aged-muscle-biomarkers-rct.md]
- `Animal-only cognition`
  - [[urolithin-a]] has a 2025 mouse cognition rescue paper and should still be treated as preclinical for cognition until the human registry reads out.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40818743-urolithin-a-domoic-acid-mice.md]

## What Changed Since 2024

- [[spermidine]] added two useful layers: a 2024 high-purity safety RCT and a 2025 NHANES association that both support plausibility without settling causality. The entity page now also captures the SmartAge null and current weak community signal.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39405978-spermidine-safety-hpspd.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40180050-dietary-spermidine-nhanes-cognition.md]^[raw/articles/pmc-9136623-smartage-spermidine-scd-rct-null.md]^[raw/articles/reddit-2022-spermidine-biohackers-experience-thread.md]
- [[nicotinamide-riboside]] moved from a single negative pilot into a two-trial pattern: cognition remains flat, but NAD+/pTau217/CBF signals moved enough to keep the branch alive as a biomarker story rather than a nootropic winner.^[raw/articles/pubmed-37994989-nr-mci-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39817194-nr-scd-mci-biomarker-effects.md]
- [[nicotinamide-mononucleotide]] now has enough source density for a standalone low-confidence entity: human NAD+ and physical/sleep proxy studies, a null metabolic meta-analysis, short-term high-dose tolerability data, an FDA 2025 supplement-status reversal, label-claim failure evidence, and bidirectional community reports. None of that is direct cognition proof.^[raw/articles/pubmed-38789831-nmn-older-adults-walking-sleep.md]^[raw/articles/springer-2024-nmn-glucose-lipid-metabolism-meta.md]^[raw/articles/jstage-2025-nmn-overdose-safety-rct.md]^[raw/articles/fda-2025-nmn-dietary-supplement-petition-response.md]^[raw/articles/springer-2024-nmn-urolithin-label-claim-testing.md]^[raw/articles/reddit-2021-nmn-day-50-experience-thread.md]
- [[urolithin-a]] crossed from purely preclinical cognition talk into an actual direct cognition registry, and now has a standalone page because the branch also has human proxy trials, product-identity sources, and direct community reports. The result is still pending and the page is not a recommendation upgrade.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct07060898-urolithin-a-clarity-cognition.md]^[raw/articles/jama-2022-urolithin-a-older-adults-muscle-endurance.md]^[raw/articles/sciencedirect-2024-urolithin-a-human-systematic-review.md]^[raw/articles/reddit-2023-urolithin-a-nootropics-fatigue-thread.md]
- [[ergothioneine]] now has a stronger longevity/cognition framing in the literature plus a small MCI pilot, but the public healthy-adult intervention data remain thin and the completed registry is not informative; the 2025 review is more optimistic than the tiny `n=4` registry can support.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39544014-ergothioneine-mci-pilot.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-36139790-ergothioneine-cognitive-functional-decline.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct04556032-ergothioneine-cognition-mood-sleep.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40968729-ergothioneine-cognitive-health-review.md]
- [[nicotinamide-riboside]] picked up a recruiting cognition/sleep registry, now estimated to complete in 2027, but the direct cognition record still reads as biomarker-positive and cognition-null, not as a demonstrated enhancer.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct05500170-nr-cognition-sleep.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-37994989-nr-mci-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39817194-nr-scd-mci-biomarker-effects.md]
- [[urolithin-a]] now has a clearly live 650-participant DTC cognition registry, which is enough to keep the branch tracked as a standalone low-confidence entity but not enough to upgrade the evidence tier before results post.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct07060898-urolithin-a-clarity-cognition.md]

## Safety And Product Identity

The safety picture is generally better than for peptide or stimulant branches, but the interpretive risk is not trivial. In this family, product identity matters as much as molecule identity:

- [[spermidine]] benefits seem tied to specific plant/wheat-germ extracts in the older trials, while the pure high-purity product trial mainly established tolerability. Commercial extract mass and active spermidine mass should not be confused.^[raw/articles/pubmed-30388439-spermidine-memory-performance-scd.md]^[raw/articles/pmc-9136623-smartage-spermidine-scd-rct-null.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39405978-spermidine-safety-hpspd.md]^[raw/articles/spermidinelife-2026-wheat-germ-product-identity.md]
- [[nicotinamide-riboside]] is well tolerated in the cognition trials and has cleaner Niagen/NR chloride identity than generic NAD+ booster labels, but it is still a biomarker-first branch.^[raw/articles/pubmed-37994989-nr-mci-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39817194-nr-scd-mci-biomarker-effects.md]^[raw/articles/fda-gras-2026-nicotinamide-riboside-chloride.md]^[raw/articles/truniagen-2026-niagen-nr-product-identity.md]
- [[nicotinamide-mononucleotide]] improved sleep-quality, drowsiness, walking, or lower-limb-function proxies in small studies without showing a direct cognition readout, and the 2024 label-claim study makes consumer product identity a live interpretation risk.^[raw/articles/pubmed-38789831-nmn-older-adults-walking-sleep.md]^[raw/articles/pmc-8877443-nmn-sleep-fatigue-older-adults-rct.md]^[raw/articles/springer-2024-nmn-urolithin-label-claim-testing.md]
- [[urolithin-a]] and [[ergothioneine]] are still mostly in the hypothesis-building phase for broad cognition, despite promising platform logic and better-than-average supplement acceptability. Urolithin A has a clearer branded-product and GRAS layer than many supplements, but label testing and marketplace reports still make generic products a transportability problem.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39487653-urolithin-a-athletes-rct.md]^[raw/articles/fda-gras-2026-urolithin-a-grn-791.md]^[raw/articles/mitopure-2026-urolithin-a-product-identity.md]^[raw/articles/reddit-2024-urolithin-a-amazon-product-quality-thread.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-36139790-ergothioneine-cognitive-functional-decline.md]^[raw/articles/fda-gras-2026-ergothioneine-notices.md]

## Open Contradictions

- [[spermidine]] looks positive in small trials, but the larger SmartAge wheat-germ extract trial was primary-endpoint null and the pure-molecule safety trial did not test cognition.
- [[nicotinamide-riboside]] repeatedly moves biomarkers without moving cognition, which is exactly the kind of biomarker-cognition mismatch the wiki should keep visible.
- [[nicotinamide-mononucleotide]] has NAD+ and physical/sleep proxy movement plus strong longevity-market visibility, but no direct cognition endpoint; the consumer-product layer is less reliable than the clinical-molecule layer.
- [[ergothioneine]] is supported by observational decline data, a positive-leaning review, and a small MCI pilot, but the healthy-adult supplementation evidence is still too small to decide whether it truly changes cognition in non-impaired users.
- [[urolithin-a]] has strong mechanistic appeal, a direct cognition registry, and visible endurance/energy anecdotes, but human cognition proof is still ahead of the biology story.
