# PDE, cAMP, and CREB Cognition Scan - 2026

## Summary

The PDE/cAMP/CREB branch has real human cognition data, and the 2026 ROSTMEMA post-stroke trial pushes roflumilast far enough to deserve its own entity page. PDE4 is the only part of the pathway with reproducible positive human findings: roflumilast improved verbal memory in healthy young adults, healthy older adults, schizophrenia experimental-medicine studies, and post-stroke cognitive impairment, while BPN14770, a PDE4D allosteric inhibitor, improved language-related cognition and caregiver-rated daily functioning in adult males with fragile X syndrome. The ClinicalTrials.gov results table for BPN14770 shows a small crystallized-cognition advantage (NIH-TCB CCC change 0.1 vs -0.9), plus VAS daily functioning 9.4 vs 6.8 and VAS language 18.2 vs 15.4, while the 2024 biomarker follow-up tied drug exposure to auditory N1 changes, which strengthens target-engagement credibility. The newest roflumilast result matters less because it breaks the branch open to broad enhancement and more because it reinforces a recovery-phenotype pattern: modest, dose-sensitive gains in memory-impaired or post-injury states, with tolerability still constraining the class.^[raw/articles/pubmed-29241652-roflumilast-healthy-young-adults.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-30776650-roflumilast-healthy-old-individuals.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-30536081-roflumilast-schizophrenia-memory.md]^[raw/articles/sagepub-02698811261436581-roflumilast-post-stroke-cognitive-performance.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct03569631-bpn14770-fragile-x-results.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39488698-bpn14770-fragile-x-biomarker-followup.md]

PDE5 looks much weaker for broad cognition. Sildenafil was null in schizophrenia, [[vardenafil]] was null in healthy adults with a worse 20 mg reaction-time signal, and the newest [[tadalafil]] work in cerebral small vessel disease did not improve cognition overall. The 2025 ETLAS-2 clinical trial showed poor adherence and more adverse events on tadalafil, while the cognitive sub-study did not produce a broad cognitive benefit. PDE5 label-level nitrate / guanylate-cyclase-stimulator / hypotension gates also matter because any marginal vascular benefit has to survive a real tolerability test.^[raw/articles/pubmed-23427190-vardenafil-healthy-adults.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40718899-tadalafil-csVD-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41332889-tadalafil-csVD-cognition-substudy.md]^[raw/articles/dailymed-2026-tadalafil-label.md]^[raw/articles/dailymed-2026-vardenafil-label.md]

The cAMP/CREB framing remains useful mechanistically, but in the human literature it is mostly an explanatory bridge rather than a proven endpoint. The cleanest current reading is that PDE4 inhibition is the credible translational branch, PDE5 is mostly a negative or phenotype-specific branch, and CREB is still a pathway story rather than a stand-alone clinical proof. For the roflumilast-specific node, see [[roflumilast]].

A fresh 2026 FAERS pharmacovigilance study adds a safety-only reminder for the PDE4 branch: roflumilast generated 2,142 primary-suspect reports in the analysis window, with male-skewed signals and a median time-to-onset of 42 days. That does not change the efficacy read, but it does reinforce that the branch is not safety-neutral and should be monitored by sex and exposure duration.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41988533-pde4-faers-sex-specific-ae.md]

For the PDE9/PDE10/cGMP negative-translator branch, see [[pde9-pde10-cgmp-cognition-2026-scan]].

A 2024 translational review from the Maastricht group pushes the same low-dose PDE4 argument and explicitly notes the ongoing stroke, MCI, AD, and Fragile X programs, but it also carries a disclosed proprietary interest in roflumilast for cognitive impairment. That makes it useful as a map of the branch, not as independent confirmation.

Pentoxifylline is the cautionary PDE-adjacent outlier. The adjacent negative-symptom trial was later retracted, and the registry record for NCT04094207 still shows a completed, no-results study with PANSS total as the prespecified primary endpoint, so the remaining positive paper should stay in the low-confidence lane.^[raw/articles/pubmed-37466276-pentoxifylline-schizophrenia-cognition.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-38465461-pentoxifylline-negative-symptoms-retraction.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct04094207-pentoxifylline-negative-symptoms-schizophrenia.md]

The Fragile X challenge record broadens the active human pipeline beyond the older healthy-adult and dementia phenotypes, but it is still a short-term, mechanism-oriented study. The main current Fragile X human positive is the separate BPN14770 trial, not the single-dose roflumilast challenge, so the branch should keep those lines distinct. The adolescent BPN14770 registry remains active/not recruiting and no-results-posted, so the branch still needs to keep the continuation trial separate from the adult signal.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct05163808-bpn14770-adolescent-watch.md]

What changed since 2024:

- The tadalafil ETLAS-2 results landed and weakened the idea that PDE5 inhibition will reliably help cognition in vascular-cognitive disease.
- The ETLAS-2 cognitive sub-study added a direct cognition readout and still did not produce a broad enhancement signal.
- [[tadalafil]] now has a standalone page so low-dose Cialis / vascular nootropic anecdotes are visible without being allowed to outweigh the CSVD cognition-null record or prescription vasodilator safety gates.
- [[vardenafil]] now has a standalone page so the direct healthy-adult PDE5 cognition-null study is visible beside [[sildenafil]] and [[tadalafil]] rather than buried as a class footnote.
- The 2026 ROSTMEMA post-stroke trial added a newer roflumilast-positive phenotype, with modest primary-phase effects, larger open-label-extension gains, and similar adverse events.
- The BPN14770 fragile X trial posted a real positive language-and-function signal, and the 2024 biomarker follow-up strengthened the PDE4D case with a PK-linked N1 readout.
- The adolescent BPN14770 registry (NCT05163808) is still active/not recruiting, past estimated completion, and no-results-posted, so the adult fragile-X result should not be generalized to the continuation trial.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct05163808-bpn14770-adolescent-watch.md]
- The 2025 adolescent BPN14770 registry update widened enrollment to ages 9 to <18 and set the NIH Toolbox Cognitive Battery crystallized composite score as the primary endpoint, which makes the continuation study more directly cognition-facing than the earlier adult proof-of-concept but still unpublished.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct05163808-bpn14770-adolescent-watch.md]
- The roflumilast MCI registry and Fragile X challenge keep the PDE4 branch active beyond the now-published post-stroke ROSTMEMA result.
- The 2026 FAERS PDE4 pharmacovigilance paper adds a sex-stratified safety layer for roflumilast, with male-skewed signals and delayed onset rather than any new cognition efficacy result.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41988533-pde4-faers-sex-specific-ae.md]
- The pentoxifylline schizophrenia mini-literature picked up a retraction notice, and the registry record remains completed with no posted results and a PANSS-total primary endpoint.
- The Fragile X challenge trial shows the roflumilast branch is still expanding into mechanistically rich neurodevelopmental phenotypes, but it remains a challenge study rather than proof of durable cognitive gain.
- The human evidence base now spans Maastricht roflumilast work and the Tetra/Rush fragile-X program, which is a sign of an active niche but also a warning about narrow replication.

## Evidence Tiering

- Human RCT / experimental medicine positive: roflumilast in healthy young adults, healthy older adults, schizophrenia, and post-stroke cognitive impairment; BPN14770 in fragile X syndrome.
- Human RCT / experimental medicine biomarker-positive: roflumilast EEG markers in schizophrenia; BPN14770 auditory N1 / PK follow-up in fragile X syndrome.
- Human registry / pending: roflumilast in MCI/mild dementia; roflumilast in the Fragile X challenge study; BPN14770 in adolescent fragile X syndrome.
- Human RCT negative: sildenafil in schizophrenia, [[vardenafil]] in healthy adults, tadalafil in CSVD overall.
- Human RCT mixed or weak: tadalafil sub-study showed one slower reaction-time outcome, not a broad cognitive benefit.
- Anecdote signal: tadalafil has weak-moderate but bidirectional Cialis/nootropic community salience, mostly confounded by gym, libido, blood-flow, and vasodilator-stack contexts.
- Mechanistic support: PDE4-cAMP-CREB is a plausible memory pathway, but the human evidence still hangs on a few small studies.

## Effect Size Snapshot

- Roflumilast in post-stroke cognitive impairment: the 2026 ROSTMEMA trial reported larger endpoint responses on VLT and RBMT stories, with non-significant moderate effects in the primary ANCOVA and significant effects after covariate adjustment; the open-label extension improved memory scores with medium-to-large effect sizes.
- Roflumilast in healthy older adults: delayed recall improved at 100 micrograms, with Cohen's d about 0.69.
- Roflumilast in schizophrenia: verbal memory improved at 250 micrograms, with effect size about 0.77.
- Roflumilast EEG study in schizophrenia: mismatch negativity and theta oscillations improved at 250 micrograms, but P300 did not.
- BPN14770 in fragile X: oral reading recognition +2.81, picture vocabulary +5.81, crystallized cognition +5.31, caregiver-rated language +14.04, and caregiver-rated daily functioning +14.53.
- BPN14770 ClinicalTrials.gov results table: NIH-TCB CCC change 0.1 vs -0.9; VAS daily functioning 9.4 vs 6.8; VAS language 18.2 vs 15.4.
- BPN14770 biomarker follow-up: auditory N1 amplitude tracked with serum drug concentration in the active-treatment period.
- Tadalafil ETLAS-2: no cognition difference overall.
- Sildenafil: no significant cognitive benefit.
- Vardenafil: no prominent cognition benefit.
- Zembrin showed small positive set-flexibility and executive-function effects in healthy adults.
- Pentoxifylline showed partial improvement in schizophrenia cognition and symptoms, but the nearby literature is cautionary.

## Safety / Adverse Events

- Roflumilast has a tolerability ceiling; higher acute doses brought headache, dizziness, insomnia, and diarrhea.
- The 2026 PSCI RCT reported similar adverse-event rates in the roflumilast and placebo groups.
- BPN14770 met the primary tolerability objective in the fragile X crossover and did not show a meaningful active-versus-placebo safety gap.
- BPN14770 ClinicalTrials.gov results table showed 16.7% overall AEs on active drug versus 20.0% on placebo, with one SAE on BPN14770 and none on placebo.
- Sildenafil produced a serious psychiatric adverse event in one schizophrenia participant after the 100 mg dose.
- Vardenafil caused more headache and weakness than placebo and worsened one reaction-time task.
- Tadalafil in CSVD had substantially more adverse events than placebo and worse adherence.
- Tadalafil's label-level hard gates include nitrates, soluble guanylate-cyclase stimulators, additive hypotension with alpha-blockers / antihypertensives / alcohol, CYP3A4 exposure issues, priapism, and vision/hearing warnings.
- The new 2026 FAERS PDE4 analysis suggests roflumilast adverse-event reporting is sex-skewed and not flat across organ systems, so future low-dose cognition work should keep sex and monitoring window in the safety frame.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41988533-pde4-faers-sex-specific-ae.md]
- The pathway is not safety-neutral, so any cognition claim has to be judged against the adverse-event burden, not only the cognitive delta.
- Pentoxifylline is a useful pathway-adjacent lead, but the literature should be read carefully because the adjacent schizophrenia negative-symptom paper later carried a retraction notice.

## Responder Phenotypes

- Best-supported roflumilast responders so far: healthy older adults with normal baseline verbal memory, and schizophrenia patients in experimental-medicine settings.
- Best-supported roflumilast responders so far now include a post-stroke cognitive-impairment phenotype with subjective memory complaints and below-normative delayed recall.
- Best-supported BPN14770 responders so far: adult males with fragile X syndrome and language / crystallized-cognition impairment.
- Possible vascular phenotype: stroke or MCI patients with episodic-memory complaints, but that remains unproven until the registry trials report results.
- PDE5 responders have not emerged cleanly; the current data look more like phenotype-specific nulls than a general enhancement class.
- The strongest `cAMP/CREB` logic still points to memory-consolidation biology, not to broad executive-function enhancement in healthy adults.
- Zembrin is the cleanest small human bridge from PDE4 into CREB-language, but it is still a tiny proof-of-concept dataset.

## Protocol Parameters

- Roflumilast healthy young adults: acute oral doses in a randomized crossover design, including 100, 250, and 1000 micrograms.
- Roflumilast healthy older adults: acute 100, 250, and 1000 micrograms in a 4-way crossover.
- Roflumilast schizophrenia experimental medicine: 100 and 250 micrograms daily for 8 days per period.
- Roflumilast PSCI RCT: 100 micrograms once daily for 24 weeks in 100 post-stroke adults, followed by a 3-month open-label extension in 42 placebo participants.
- Roflumilast EEG schizophrenia: 100 and 250 micrograms in a randomized crossover.
- BPN14770 fragile X: 25 mg twice daily for 24 weeks in a randomized, placebo-controlled two-period crossover.
- Tadalafil ETLAS-2: 20 mg daily for 3 months in CSVD.
- Sildenafil schizophrenia: 50 mg and 100 mg single-dose exposure.
- Vardenafil healthy adults: 10 mg and 20 mg single-dose exposure.

## Open Contradictions

- PDE4 looks promising on episodic memory, but the effect is narrow enough that it may not survive replication outside the same research groups.
- PDE4D-selective BPN14770 and low-dose roflumilast both look positive, but the branch still needs independent replication outside the Maastricht/Tetra ecosystem.
- PDE5 has plausible vascular/cGMP logic, yet the human cognition data are mostly null or mixed.
- The tadalafil CSVD program is a good example of a mechanistic idea that did not cleanly become a cognitive win.
- cAMP and CREB are central to the biology, but no human trial here proves that pushing those markers reliably changes everyday cognition.
- The pentoxifylline mini-literature exists, but it is hard to lean on because the closest related schizophrenia paper was later retracted and the registry record remains completed with no results posted.
- The 2024 roflumilast translational review is directionally consistent with the positive low-dose human studies, but the disclosed proprietary interest means it should be treated as a branch-position paper rather than neutral replication.
- The 2026 ROSTMEMA trial is directionally positive, but the primary analysis is modest enough that the effect should still be read as phenotype-bound rather than class-defining; the open-label extension is supportive, not definitive.
- Zembrin and pentoxifylline both add signal, but neither upgrades the branch to a general-purpose enhancer.
