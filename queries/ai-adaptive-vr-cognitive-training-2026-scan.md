# AI-Adaptive and VR Cognitive Training 2026 Scan

This branch is mostly a delivery and task-ecology story, not a broad enhancement story. The strongest current signal comes from rehab or impairment cohorts where personalization, ecological tasks, or virtual environments can be tied to specific functional goals. Direct AI guidance looks useful for access, adherence, and noninferiority to therapist-supervised delivery; VR looks most credible when the task set is concrete enough to show near transfer or ADL relevance rather than generic "brain training." The 2026 VRCT syntheses strengthen the MCI/rehab side of the branch: one review compares VRCT against conventional cognitive training across seven RCTs, and a newer meta-analysis pools 19 RCTs with signals on MoCA, TMT-B, IADL, and depressive symptoms. A 2026 MCI protocol now adds a longer-horizon AI-supported cognitive rehabilitation arm with daily AI-guided conversational sessions, but the record stays AI-branded rather than explicitly LLM-branded.^[raw/articles/frontiers-2026-vrct-mci-systematic-review.md]^[raw/articles/frontiers-2026-vrct-mci-meta-analysis.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct07474038-ai-supported-cognitive-rehabilitation-mci.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40895105-ai-driven-cognitive-telerehabilitation-for-stroke-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41492723-ai-guided-mobile-telerehabilitation-cognitive-impairment-feasibility.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41070261-adaptive-tablet-based-cognitive-training-stroke-survivors.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40682194-virtual-reality-cognitive-rehabilitation-stroke-patients.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39793970-virtual-reality-interventions-older-adults-mci-meta-analysis.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41701233-i-virtual-reality-telemedicine-pd-mci-prospective-memory-rct.md]

## What Changed Since 2024

- AI-guided cognitive telerehabilitation crossed from idea to a non-inferiority RCT in stroke, plus a smaller feasibility study in mixed cognitive impairment; a 2026 Kaiser Permanente protocol now adds daily AI-guided conversational rehabilitation for older adults with MCI.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40895105-ai-driven-cognitive-telerehabilitation-for-stroke-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41492723-ai-guided-mobile-telerehabilitation-cognitive-impairment-feasibility.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct07474038-ai-supported-cognitive-rehabilitation-mci.md]
- VR cognitive remediation moved from generic "immersive training" language toward explicit ecological task designs, including ADL-linked tablet training, prospective-memory scenarios, stroke rehabilitation protocols, and a 2025 MCI pilot with hippocampal connectivity changes.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41070261-adaptive-tablet-based-cognitive-training-stroke-survivors.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40682194-virtual-reality-cognitive-rehabilitation-stroke-patients.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41701233-i-virtual-reality-telemedicine-pd-mci-prospective-memory-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40746338-vr-cognitive-training-mci-pilot.md]
- VR now has a dense 2025-2026 MCI synthesis layer. The 2025 sources point to benefit plus immersion-sensitive heterogeneity, while the 2026 sources add conventional-training comparators and a larger 19-RCT meta-analysis with cognition, IADL, and depression signals.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40994868-vr-scd-mci-systematic-review.md]^[raw/articles/pmc-12634598-vr-cognitive-training-games-mci-meta-analysis.md]^[raw/articles/frontiers-2026-vrct-mci-systematic-review.md]^[raw/articles/frontiers-2026-vrct-mci-meta-analysis.md]
- Personalization now appears in the outcome model itself: baseline performance and age modulate training benefit, which is a cleaner signal than one-size-fits-all brain-game claims.^[raw/articles/pubmed-38043011-personalized-cognitive-training-older-adults.md]
- The VR evidence base in MCI now has a formal synthesis, but the effect looks moderate rather than transformative and still depends on immersion level and delivery details.^[raw/articles/pmc-12634598-vr-cognitive-training-games-mci-meta-analysis.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39793970-virtual-reality-interventions-older-adults-mci-meta-analysis.md]
- The active registry pipeline is now large enough to matter: HF+MCI, MCI feasibility, elderly MCI, and acute post-stroke VR programs are all in flight.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct05601817-virtual-reality-computerized-cognitive-intervention-heart-failure-mci.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct06270966-cognitive-remediation-vr-tool-mci-feasibility.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct06838494-immersive-vr-cognitive-therapy-elderly-mci.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct07381452-virtual-reality-enhanced-rehabilitation-acute-post-stroke-cognitive-recovery.md]
- Direct LLM-branded cognition-improvement trials still did not surface in this pass. The closest current LLM records are more about monitoring or therapy support than cognition enhancement, so they should stay separate unless a cognition endpoint appears.

## Summary

- The AI-specific signal is best read as a delivery layer: it can support cognitive telerehabilitation, but the field has not yet shown that AI itself is the causal ingredient that drives cognition gains. The current MCI protocol strengthens the delivery signal, not the efficacy signal.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct07474038-ai-supported-cognitive-rehabilitation-mci.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40895105-ai-driven-cognitive-telerehabilitation-for-stroke-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41492723-ai-guided-mobile-telerehabilitation-cognitive-impairment-feasibility.md]
- The VR-specific signal is best read as task ecology: the better papers make the task concrete, link it to ADLs or prospective memory, and compare against conventional rehab or active placebo rather than waitlist only. The 2025 MCI pilot and the 2025-2026 meta-analyses say the same thing at different resolution levels: benefit is plausible, but protocol details, immersion, bias, and follow-up matter.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41070261-adaptive-tablet-based-cognitive-training-stroke-survivors.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40682194-virtual-reality-cognitive-rehabilitation-stroke-patients.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41701233-i-virtual-reality-telemedicine-pd-mci-prospective-memory-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40746338-vr-cognitive-training-mci-pilot.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40994868-vr-scd-mci-systematic-review.md]^[raw/articles/pmc-12634598-vr-cognitive-training-games-mci-meta-analysis.md]^[raw/articles/frontiers-2026-vrct-mci-systematic-review.md]^[raw/articles/frontiers-2026-vrct-mci-meta-analysis.md]
- Personalization is real but narrow: baseline performance and age affect response, which argues for responder-aware trial design before anyone treats digital training as a universal enhancer.^[raw/articles/pubmed-38043011-personalized-cognitive-training-older-adults.md]
- The highest-value near-term use case is not healthy enhancement; it is phenotype-bound rehab, access expansion, and delayed-transfer testing under explicit ecological tasks.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39774282-home-based-computerized-adaptive-cognitive-training-post-stroke-cognitive-impairment.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct05601817-virtual-reality-computerized-cognitive-intervention-heart-failure-mci.md]

## Safety / Adverse Events

- The key practical risk is not dramatic toxicity; it is implementation burden, fatigue, and dropout when interfaces are awkward or sessions are too long.
- Headset tolerability and error tolerance matter because an immersive system can fail on usability long before any cognitive mechanism is tested.
- Rehabilitation cohorts can hide confounding from therapist contact, attention, and recovery trajectory.
- A trial can be safe and still be uninformative if the control condition is too weak or the task is too artificial.

## Responder Phenotypes

- `Lower baseline performers` may benefit more from adaptive difficulty than ceiling performers.
- `Older age` appears to change the size or direction of training response in the personalized training pilot.
- `Rehab-bound phenotypes` such as stroke, MCI, PD-MCI, and heart failure with cognitive impairment are the clearest current candidates.
- `Ecology-sensitive tasks` such as prospective memory, meal preparation, and ADL-linked exercises are more likely to show transfer than generic task drills.
- `Engaged users` are the practical responder class for any AI or VR delivery system; if people do not stay on task, the intervention collapses.

## Protocol Parameters

- AI-driven stroke telerehab: 24 sessions over 6 weeks on the same mobile platform, non-inferiority design.
- AI-guided mobile telerehab: 24 sessions within 6 weeks, feasibility-first, cognitive impairment sample.
- AI-supported MCI protocol: 12 months total, clinician telehealth every 2 weeks for 6 months then monthly, plus daily 10-30 minute AI-guided conversational exercises; primary MoCA-BLIND at 6 months.
- Adaptive tablet training in stroke survivors: 12 bi-weekly 30-minute sessions.
- Home-based adaptive post-stroke training: 4-week remotely delivered multidomain adaptive program.
- Personalized older-adult training: six-month BrainHQ regimen with baseline performance and age moderation analysis.
- VR MCI pilot: 12 weeks, twice-weekly 50-minute sessions, no control group.
- VR MCI synthesis: 19-study review spans 2019-2025; 11-RCT meta-analysis pooled Hedges's g 0.6 overall, with games g 0.68 and training g 0.52.
- VR stroke rehab: 132-patient randomized trial using egocentric versus allocentric perspective manipulation.
- PD-MCI i-VR trial: VR plus telemedicine, real-life scenarios, planning/shifting/updating, prospective memory endpoint.
- HF-MCI registry: 2x2 factorial design combining virtual reality cognitive restoration with computerized training.

## Open Contradictions

- AI may improve access and adherence without being the mechanism that improves cognition.
- VR may improve ecological validity while still failing to produce broad transfer.
- Higher immersion may improve effect sizes while also increasing cybersickness or tolerance issues.
- Stronger gains on trained or near-transfer tasks do not automatically imply durable functional improvement.
- Active controls matter: paper-and-pencil, therapist supervision, and TV-interface comparators can each move the apparent effect.
- The literature still conflates generic digital training, VR, and personalized adaptation too often; the branch should keep them separate.
- LLM-branded cognition training still appears to be ahead of the evidence; current LLM use is more visible in monitoring and therapy-support workflows than in direct cognitive enhancement trials.
