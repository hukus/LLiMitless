# Low-Dose Lithium and Lithium Orotate 2026 Scan

## Summary

The branch is now split into formal and anecdotal layers that should stay separate. First, the 2025 Nature paper argues that endogenous lithium deficiency may matter in MCI/AD and that low-dose lithium orotate rescued pathology and memory in mice.^[raw/articles/nature-2025-lithium-deficiency-onset-alzheimers-disease.md] Second, the 2025 mechanistic review on subtherapeutic lithium concentrations adds preclinical target-level plausibility without changing the human-efficacy picture.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40348943-lithium-neuroprotection-subtherapeutic-concentrations-preclinical-ad.md] Third, the 2026 MCI pilot RCT of low-dose lithium carbonate enrolled 80 treated participants, missed all six coprimary efficacy thresholds, but still generated a favorable verbal-memory slope estimate and an amyloid-positive exploratory signal that are hypothesis-generating rather than confirmatory.^[raw/articles/jama-2026-low-dose-lithium-mci-rct.md] Fourth, the 2026 AD dementia meta-analysis still does not support consistent cognitive or functional benefit across six RCTs, despite no clear serious-adverse-event increase.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41177743-lithium-ad-meta-analysis.md]

The supplement layer is not the same as the prescription layer. A 2025 opportunistic survey of 211 OTC lithium supplement users found perceived benefits in cognition, mood, and anxiety, but it also found side effects and withdrawal phenomena more often than expected.^[raw/articles/canadian-jpsychiatry-2025-lithium-supplement-survey.md] Two 2025 Reddit threads add visible product-demand and self-experimentation salience, including calm/focus positives, no-effect reports, brain fog, fatigue, headache, emotional flatness, dose confusion, and vendor-trust confounding.^[raw/articles/reddit-2025-07-13-lithium-orotate-supplements-thread.md]^[raw/articles/reddit-2025-12-13-lithium-orotate-nootropicsdepot-thread.md] That is useful as a community-signal map, not as efficacy proof. The live lithium-orotate registry is still not yet recruiting as of the 2026-05-04 check; it is a 40-participant phase 1/2 early-AD feasibility trial with CSF lithium as the central target-engagement readout, so it is relevant for mechanism and safety but not yet for efficacy claims.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct07459959-lio-ad-record-2026-05-04.md] The 2025 NIH summary is not new data, but it is useful translation context because it repeats the tissue/mouse signal and still says the safe human dose range is unknown.^[raw/articles/nih-2025-lithium-levels-tied-alzheimers-disease-and-dementia.md] A 2026 Neurotherapeutics paper adds a mechanism-only note: lithium chloride reduced ferroptosis-related injury in ApoE4/E4 human iPSCs from a sporadic AD patient, which strengthens plausibility but does not move the human cognition claim.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41760425-lithium-chloride-ferroptosis-ad-ipsc.md]

## What Changed Since 2024

- The branch moved from a mostly historical low-dose-lithium discussion to a current, publication-backed AD/MCI topic after the 2025 Nature tissue/mouse paper and the 2026 MCI pilot RCT.^[raw/articles/nature-2025-lithium-deficiency-onset-alzheimers-disease.md]^[raw/articles/jama-2026-low-dose-lithium-mci-rct.md]
- The 2025 preclinical review on subtherapeutic lithium concentrations tightened the mechanism story, but it still sits entirely below the human-efficacy line.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40348943-lithium-neuroprotection-subtherapeutic-concentrations-preclinical-ad.md]
- The 2026 lithium chloride iPSC paper adds a mechanistic ApoE4/ferroptosis bridge, but it remains preclinical and does not change the human efficacy verdict.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41760425-lithium-chloride-ferroptosis-ad-ipsc.md]
- The human trial picture is now less speculative: there is one recent MCI pilot RCT, a 2026 AD dementia meta-analysis, and an active lithium-orotate feasibility trial in biomarker-confirmed early AD.^[raw/articles/jama-2026-low-dose-lithium-mci-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41177743-lithium-ad-meta-analysis.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct07459959-lio-ad-lithium-orotate-feasibility.md]
- Consumer lithium supplementation has now been surveyed directly in humans, and the post-Nature product-demand layer is visible in forum threads, which lets the wiki separate anecdotal use from controlled efficacy claims instead of treating them as the same signal.^[raw/articles/canadian-jpsychiatry-2025-lithium-supplement-survey.md]^[raw/articles/reddit-2025-12-13-lithium-orotate-nootropicsdepot-thread.md]
- The older 2020 Springer commentary on the 2019 MCI trial remains useful as a historical optimism marker, but it is still commentary on a small study with unresolved blinding and endpoint caveats.^[raw/articles/pubmed-32743689-low-dose-lithium-against-dementia.md]

## Evidence Tiering

- `Human-tissue / preclinical bridge`: the Nature paper links lithium depletion, plaque sequestration, and mouse rescue to a mechanistic AD hypothesis, but the human evidence is tissue-level rather than therapeutic.^[raw/articles/nature-2025-lithium-deficiency-onset-alzheimers-disease.md]
- `Official summary`: the NIH Research Matters article repeats the same mechanistic message but adds the explicit translation caveat that the safe human dose range is not known yet.^[raw/articles/nih-2025-lithium-levels-tied-alzheimers-disease-and-dementia.md]
- `Human RCT`: the 2026 MCI pilot trial is the clearest direct human test of low-dose lithium carbonate; 83 were randomized and 80 started treatment, none of the six coprimary outcomes met the prespecified threshold, and the verbal-memory slope favored lithium by 0.69 points per year with exploratory enrichment in amyloid-positive participants.^[raw/articles/jama-2026-low-dose-lithium-mci-rct.md]
- `Human meta-analysis`: the 2026 AD dementia meta-analysis is the most important branch-level counterweight, because it compresses six RCTs / 394 participants and still does not produce a stable cognition or function signal.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41177743-lithium-ad-meta-analysis.md]
- `Human review`: the 2025 cognition review shows the broader cognitive literature is mixed across diagnoses and baseline affective state.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41104528-lithium-cognition-humans-review.md]
- `Preclinical review`: the 2025 review on subtherapeutic lithium concentrations strengthens biological plausibility in AD models, but it does not change the branch's human-efficacy status.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40348943-lithium-neuroprotection-subtherapeutic-concentrations-preclinical-ad.md]
- `Commentary / letter`: the 2020 Springer commentary is useful for historical context around the earlier MCI signal, but it is commentary on a small study with unresolved statistical caveats rather than a standalone efficacy trial.^[raw/articles/pubmed-32743689-low-dose-lithium-against-dementia.md]
- `Community survey`: the 2025 supplement survey captures real-world OTC use and perceived effects, but it is self-selected and cannot establish benefit.^[raw/articles/canadian-jpsychiatry-2025-lithium-supplement-survey.md]
- `Forum anecdote / product demand`: 2025 Reddit threads show moderate consumer salience after the Nature paper and mixed self-reports around calm, focus, mood, sleep, brain fog, headache, fatigue, and dose confusion.^[raw/articles/reddit-2025-07-13-lithium-orotate-supplements-thread.md]^[raw/articles/reddit-2025-12-13-lithium-orotate-nootropicsdepot-thread.md]
- `Registry`: the lithium-orotate AD feasibility study is now a live branch signal, but it has no results yet.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct07459959-lio-ad-record-2026-05-04.md]
- `Preclinical mechanistic update`: the 2026 iPSC ferroptosis paper supports a lithium/ApoE4/oxidative-stress mechanism, but it remains cell-based evidence and not a cognition result.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41760425-lithium-chloride-ferroptosis-ad-ipsc.md]

## Effect Size Snapshot

- 2026 MCI pilot RCT: six coprimary outcomes missed the prespecified significance threshold; verbal memory declined 0.69 points/year less in lithium than placebo (95% CI 0.01-1.37; P = .05), with exploratory benefit signals in amyloid-positive participants.^[raw/articles/jama-2026-low-dose-lithium-mci-rct.md]
- 2026 AD dementia meta-analysis: six RCTs / 394 participants, no consistent global cognition or functional benefit; pooled estimates reported MMSE MD -1.61 and ADAS-Cog MD -1.82, with high heterogeneity and mixed memory results.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41177743-lithium-ad-meta-analysis.md]
- 2019 long-term MCI trial: older RCTs had a more favorable interpretation, but the newer pooled view and the 2026 pilot trial keep the branch from being treated as settled.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41177743-lithium-ad-meta-analysis.md]^[raw/articles/jama-2026-low-dose-lithium-mci-rct.md]

## Safety / Adverse Events

- Lithium has a narrow therapeutic range and toxicity can occur close to therapeutic concentrations, so any real-world use needs monitoring rather than casual supplement logic.^[raw/articles/dailymed-lithium-oral-solution-medication-guide.md]
- Kidney, thyroid, sodium-balance, serotonergic, and antipsychotic interaction risks matter even when the intended dose is low or the product is sold as a supplement.^[raw/articles/dailymed-lithium-oral-solution-medication-guide.md]
- The 2026 MCI pilot trial reported common adverse events including creatinine increase, diarrhea, tiredness, and tremor, with no definitely treatment-related serious adverse event signal.^[raw/articles/jama-2026-low-dose-lithium-mci-rct.md]
- The lithium-orotate toxicology literature is reassuring in short-term animal work, but that is not the same thing as validated long-term human safety or a guarantee of benign self-dosing.^[raw/articles/sciencedirect-2021-lithium-orotate-toxicology.md]
- The LiO-AD registry is still not yet recruiting as of the 2026-05-04 check, with a planned 2026-06-01 study start and no posted results, so it remains a target-engagement study rather than evidence of benefit.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct07459959-lio-ad-record-2026-05-04.md]
- The live lithium-orotate feasibility registry excludes clinically significant kidney/thyroid problems, thiazides, and chronic daily NSAID use, which is a useful reminder that the supplement branch still inherits prescription-style interaction screening.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct07459959-lio-ad-record-2026-05-04.md]

## Responder Phenotypes

- Most plausible formal-evidence phenotype: older adults with MCI, especially amyloid-positive or other biomarker-enriched subgroups.^[raw/articles/jama-2026-low-dose-lithium-mci-rct.md]
- Possible disease-modification phenotype: early AD with lithium deficiency / plaque sequestration biology, but this is still a mechanistic hypothesis rather than a proven treatment effect.^[raw/articles/nature-2025-lithium-deficiency-onset-alzheimers-disease.md]
- Possible supplement-user phenotype: people self-reporting mood, anxiety, sleep, calm, or cognitive benefit from OTC lithium salts, but that signal currently lives at the survey/forum level only.^[raw/articles/canadian-jpsychiatry-2025-lithium-supplement-survey.md]^[raw/articles/reddit-2025-07-13-lithium-orotate-supplements-thread.md]

## Protocol Parameters

- JAMA MCI pilot: low-dose lithium carbonate versus placebo for 2 years, with over-encapsulated 150 mg or 300 mg capsules and weekly titration to maximum tolerated dose; 83 randomized and 80 treated.^[raw/articles/jama-2026-low-dose-lithium-mci-rct.md]
- LiO-AD registry: not yet recruiting as of 2026-05-04; lithium orotate 240 mg/day in week 1, 480 mg/day in week 2, 720 mg/day in week 3 and thereafter if tolerated, for 9 weeks total, with CSF lithium as the central target-engagement readout.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct07459959-lio-ad-record-2026-05-04.md]
- Supplement survey: most common OTC dose was 10 mg daily of lithium aspartate; lithium orotate and ionic lithium also appeared in the sample.^[raw/articles/canadian-jpsychiatry-2025-lithium-supplement-survey.md]

## Open Contradictions

- The 2025 Nature paper and the older orotate folklore imply a mechanistic reason to prefer lithium orotate, but the older rat PK study did not show a clear kinetic advantage and the human superiority claim is still unproven.^[raw/articles/pubmed-1260219-lithium-orotate-rat-pharmacokinetics.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-34196467-lithium-orotate-superior-option-review.md]
- The new MCI pilot RCT is directionally suggestive while the 2026 AD dementia meta-analysis is still negative at the branch level; this looks more like a phenotype- or endpoint-sensitive signal than a general enhancer.^[raw/articles/jama-2026-low-dose-lithium-mci-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41177743-lithium-ad-meta-analysis.md]
- The NIH summary and the 2020 commentary both keep the low-dose-lithium idea visible, but neither changes the branch-level conclusion that controlled evidence is still not strong enough for a general cognition claim.^[raw/articles/nih-2025-lithium-levels-tied-alzheimers-disease-and-dementia.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-32743689-low-dose-lithium-against-dementia.md]^[raw/articles/jama-2026-low-dose-lithium-mci-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41177743-lithium-ad-meta-analysis.md]
- The mechanistic review and Nature biology paper strengthen plausibility, but they still leave a translation gap between preclinical target engagement and human cognitive effect.^[raw/articles/nature-2025-lithium-deficiency-onset-alzheimers-disease.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40348943-lithium-neuroprotection-subtherapeutic-concentrations-preclinical-ad.md]
- Community survey and forum respondents report cognition, mood, sleep, anxiety, and calm benefits, but that signal is not strong enough to override controlled-data caution and is counterweighted by brain fog, fatigue, headache, emotional flatness, and dose-conversion confusion.^[raw/articles/canadian-jpsychiatry-2025-lithium-supplement-survey.md]^[raw/articles/reddit-2025-07-13-lithium-orotate-supplements-thread.md]^[raw/articles/reddit-2025-12-13-lithium-orotate-nootropicsdepot-thread.md]
