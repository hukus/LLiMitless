# Pinealon 2026 Scan

## Summary

Pinealon is the tripeptide Glu-Asp-Arg, usually written as `EDR`. The 2026 sweep found a small legacy literature dominated by anti-oxidant and anti-hypoxic cell work, rat learning and hypoxia models, one mixed human geroprotective study, and one 2024 in vitro human-neuron paper. The older mechanistic layer now also includes serotonin-expression work in aging cortex cultures and a broader bioregulatory tripeptide skin-culture paper that names T-33 as Glu-Asp-Arg. I did not find a Pinealon-only cognition RCT or a registry signal in this sweep.^[raw/articles/pubchem-10273502-pinealon-identity.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-21978084-pinealon-cell-viability.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-22567179-pinealon-hyperhomocysteinemia-rat-offspring.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-18546825-investigation-antihypoxic-properties-short-peptides.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-28539017-comparative-analysis-geroprotective-methods-pinealon-vezugen.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-28976148-pinealon-cortexin-rat-learning-caspase-3.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-25051764-cortexin-pinealon-old-rats-hypoxic-hypoxia-cytokines-caspase3.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-33396470-edr-peptide-mechanism-alzheimers-disease.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39518916-short-peptides-induced-neurons-age-related-changes.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-24909721-pinealon-serotonin-expression-brain-cortex.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-22803085-bioregulatory-tripeptides-skin-cells.md]

## Identity

PubChem normalizes Pinealon to Glu-Asp-Arg with sequence `EDR`, which matters because the literature often switches between `Pinealon`, `EDR peptide`, and `Glu-Asp-Arg` when discussing the same tripeptide.^[raw/articles/pubchem-10273502-pinealon-identity.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-33396470-edr-peptide-mechanism-alzheimers-disease.md]

## Evidence Tiering

- `Preclinical`: the strongest evidence is in stressed cells and rodents, where Pinealon lowered ROS, reduced necrotic death, improved learning or hypoxia tolerance in several models, and showed short-peptide signaling in cortex and skin cell cultures.^[raw/articles/pubmed-21978084-pinealon-cell-viability.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-22567179-pinealon-hyperhomocysteinemia-rat-offspring.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-28976148-pinealon-cortexin-rat-learning-caspase-3.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-25051764-cortexin-pinealon-old-rats-hypoxic-hypoxia-cytokines-caspase3.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-18546825-investigation-antihypoxic-properties-short-peptides.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-24909721-pinealon-serotonin-expression-brain-cortex.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-22803085-bioregulatory-tripeptides-skin-cells.md]
- `Human-adjacent`: the 2024 induced-neuron paper moves the branch into a human-cell aging model, but it is still not a clinical cognition outcome.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39518916-short-peptides-induced-neurons-age-related-changes.md]
- `Human`: the only human exposure I found was a comparative geroprotective study that bundled Pinealon with Vezugen and measured biological-age indicators rather than cognition directly.^[raw/articles/pubmed-28539017-comparative-analysis-geroprotective-methods-pinealon-vezugen.md]
- `Review-level`: a 2020 review argues for MAPK/ERK, apoptosis, antioxidant, serotonin, and calmodulin mechanisms and says the peptide improves memory issues in elderly patients, but that claim is not the same thing as a verified Pinealon-specific RCT.^[raw/articles/pubmed-33396470-edr-peptide-mechanism-alzheimers-disease.md]

## What Changed Since 2024

The main new item is the 2024 induced-neuron study. It strengthens the idea that EDR can affect aging-related dendritic structure and oxidative DNA damage in human neuronal cells derived from elderly donors, while the older serotonin-expression and skin-cell papers make the short-peptide mechanism story broader but still preclinical. I did not find a 2025-2026 Pinealon trial or registry update in this sweep.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39518916-short-peptides-induced-neurons-age-related-changes.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-24909721-pinealon-serotonin-expression-brain-cortex.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-22803085-bioregulatory-tripeptides-skin-cells.md]

## Safety And Regulatory Read

I did not find a Pinealon-specific FDA, EMA, ClinicalTrials.gov, or WADA entry in this sweep. That is a search result, not a proof of absence. On the present evidence, Pinealon belongs in the legacy investigational peptide bucket with unresolved sourcing/quality questions and no modern clinical safety dossier.^[raw/articles/pubchem-10273502-pinealon-identity.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-28539017-comparative-analysis-geroprotective-methods-pinealon-vezugen.md]

## Open Contradictions

- The 2020 review uses stronger human language than the primary evidence I could verify.
- The human comparative study is peptide-combination data, not a Pinealon-only trial.
- The branch has repeated cognition-adjacent animal results, but the human evidence remains thin and indirect.

## Practical Verdict

Pinealon deserves a separate wiki node because it has a distinct identity, a coherent legacy literature, and enough mechanistic and animal evidence to be searchable on its own. It should not be promoted into a cognition-shortlist page yet. The right label today is `low-confidence legacy neuroprotective peptide`, with the human section explicitly marked as indirect.^[raw/articles/pubchem-10273502-pinealon-identity.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-21978084-pinealon-cell-viability.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-28539017-comparative-analysis-geroprotective-methods-pinealon-vezugen.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39518916-short-peptides-induced-neurons-age-related-changes.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-24909721-pinealon-serotonin-expression-brain-cortex.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-22803085-bioregulatory-tripeptides-skin-cells.md]
