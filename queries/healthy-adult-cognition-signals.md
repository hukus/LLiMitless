# Healthy Adult Cognition Signals

This page is narrower than the main cognition-ranking pages: it focuses on healthy, well-slept adults who do not have an obvious sleep deficit, neurodegenerative syndrome, or other correction target. In that setting, the honest answer is that the evidence for durable cognitive enhancement is thin. The strongest signals are usually small, task-specific, or indirect.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41584102-transcranial-1064nm-laser-photobiomodulation.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40482941-tpbm-healthy-older-adults-working-memory-fnirs.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40718569-vagus-nerve-performance-optimization.md]

## Top 5 signals for healthy, well-slept adults

| Rank | Signal | Confidence | Best fit | Why it is here |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [[creatine]] / [[creatine-cognitive-support]] | Moderate | Healthy adults with low dietary creatine intake, high exercise load, heat stress, heavy mental workload, subtle sleep debt, or older adults with lower reserve | Best practical signal with the cleanest "healthy adult" upside and low cost; strongest when the brain is under load or reserve is lower, and now reinforced by a small menopause-phenotype/formulation signal |
| 2 | [[transcranial-photobiomodulation]] | Low-moderate | Self-experimenters and people interested in non-drug, acute performance tweaks | One of the few tracked interventions with a direct healthy-adult physiological signal and a plausible cognition link; the 2025 healthy-older-adult crossover study strengthened the working-memory signal |
| 3 | [[transcranial-direct-current-stimulation-and-cognition]] / [[transcranial-alternating-current-stimulation-and-cognition]] | Low | People willing to accept protocol sensitivity and modest effect sizes | Healthy-adult enhancement signal exists, but it is highly task- and setup-dependent |
| 4 | [[transcutaneous-auricular-vagus-nerve-stimulation]] | Low | Healthy adults interested in stress resilience or autonomic tuning | Healthy-adult evidence is protocol-sensitive: two small memory studies are positive, one acute-stress RCT is null for behavior, and autonomic shifts are more reproducible than cognition |
| 5 | [[bright-light-therapy-and-cognition]] | Low-moderate | Healthy adults with suboptimal morning alertness or circadian drift despite “good sleep” | Mostly an alertness and timing tool; the cognition signal is indirect but useful |

## Why these five, and not the more disease-focused pages

The wiki's other high-interest pages — [[incretin-therapies-brain-health]], [[hyperbaric-oxygen-neurocognitive-recovery]], [[cerebrolysin]], [[peptide-neuroprotection]], [[neurocognitive-interventions-under-study]], [[semax]], [[selank]], [[dihexa]], [[bpc-157]], [[thymosin-beta-4]], and [[mots-c]] — matter a lot for recovery, neuroprotection, or speculative enhancement, but they are not the cleanest signals for a healthy adult who already sleeps well. In that setting, the most realistic gains are usually incremental: better alertness, slightly better working memory, or more stable mental throughput rather than dramatic IQ-like changes.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41601887-creatine-supplementation-aging-brain-muscle-axis.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41584102-transcranial-1064nm-laser-photobiomodulation.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40718569-vagus-nerve-performance-optimization.md]

## Source notes

- Creatine: adult cognition/recovery signal with practical upside, especially when baseline intake, sleep loss, heat stress, or training load is relevant; the newer meta-analysis narrows the broad claim to memory, attention time, and processing-speed time, the 2025 older-adult review suggests the age-specific branch is worth watching even if the evidence is still thin, and the 2026 menopause RCT adds a small formulation-specific positive note without turning the class into a universal enhancer.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39070254-creatine-cognition-adults-meta-analysis.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-38418482-creatine-sleep-deprivation-cognition.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40971619-creatine-cognition-older-adults-systematic-review.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40854087-creatine-menopause-cognition-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41601887-creatine-supplementation-aging-brain-muscle-axis.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41514140-creatine-arginine-exercise-neurogenesis.md]
- Transcranial photobiomodulation: healthy-adult physiology signal is directly documented, and the 2025 crossover study adds objective working-memory and connectivity effects in healthy older adults.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41584102-transcranial-1064nm-laser-photobiomodulation.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40482941-tpbm-healthy-older-adults-working-memory-fnirs.md]
- Methylene blue: low-dose oral MB has a small healthy-adult fMRI/memory signal, but its safety exclusions and state-dependent effect keep it below the top tier.^[raw/articles/pubmed-27351678-methylene-blue-human-brain-fmri.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-26961091-methylene-blue-functional-connectivity-human-brain.md]^[raw/articles/dailymed-methylene-blue-injection-label-2026.md]
- tDCS/tACS: the signal is protocol-sensitive; useful to track, but not yet a broadly reliable enhancer. For the parameter-level split, see [[tdcs-tacs-parameter-response-2025-2026-scan]].^[raw/articles/pubmed-38060075-tdcs-cognitive-functions-review.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39905569-tdcs-cognitive-impairments-dosage-parameters.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40933163-theta-tacs-working-memory-healthy-adults.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41034500-tacs-cognitive-function-older-adults.md]
- taVNS: the newer healthy-adult literature is mixed. The signal now includes a corrected 2-week RAVLT trial and a small associative-memory pilot, but the PASAT stress RCT was behaviorally null and the main reproducible outcome is still autonomic modulation rather than broad cognition. A 2025 stress-regulation EEG study and a 2025 cortisol study strengthen the state-regulation story more than the enhancement story.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39039354-taVNS-memory-nonclinical-adults.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41046500-taVNS-associative-memory-healthy-adults.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40908377-taVNS-mental-stressor.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40958978-taVNS-stress-regulation-eeg-questionnaire.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39936474-taVNS-cortisol-stress-inflammation.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39124651-taVNS-autonomic-function.md]
- Bright light therapy: most useful when sleep is already okay but timing/alertness still lag, so the effect is indirect rather than a classic nootropic effect.^[raw/articles/pubmed-42015088-bright-light-therapy-cognition-sleep-stress.md]

## Bottom line

If you want the current wiki's best guess for healthy, well-slept adults, start with [[creatine]] / [[creatine-cognitive-support]] and then look at the device-based options ([[transcranial-photobiomodulation]], [[transcranial-direct-current-stimulation-and-cognition]], [[transcranial-alternating-current-stimulation-and-cognition]], and [[transcutaneous-auricular-vagus-nerve-stimulation]]) as experimental performance nudges rather than proven cognitive boosters. For the latest creatine evidence layer, see [[creatine-cognition-2026-scan]]. For the latest tPBM evidence layer, see [[transcranial-photobiomodulation-2025-2026-scan]]. For the latest taVNS evidence layer, see [[transcutaneous-auricular-vagus-nerve-stimulation-2025-2026-scan]].

For a deeper exploratory branch that is still mostly target-validation rather than enhancement, see [[temporal-interference-stimulation-and-cognition]].

For the narrower one-standard-deviation IQ target, see [[one-standard-deviation-iq-proposal]], which separates measured IQ-score acquisition from unsupported claims about one-SD latent `g` improvement.
