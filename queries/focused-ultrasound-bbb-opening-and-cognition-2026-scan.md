# Focused Ultrasound BBB Opening and Cognition 2026 Scan

Focused ultrasound blood-brain barrier opening (FUS-BBBO) is the more translationally grounded focused-ultrasound branch than pure neuromodulation. A 2026 umbrella review now makes the broader field structure explicit by separating lesioning, BBB opening, and low-intensity circuit stimulation into distinct human applications, which is useful because the evidence and risk profiles differ across those branches.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41610842-transcranial-focused-ultrasound-in-the-human-brain.md] In Alzheimer disease, the human BBBO literature now supports repeated, supervised BBB opening with acceptable short-term safety and no clear cognitive worsening, but not a controlled cognition benefit. The 2023 long-term follow-up in 10 mild AD participants across 30 treatments found immediate opening, closure within 24-48 hours, no serious procedure-related adverse events, and cognitive change comparable to ADNI controls.^[raw/articles/pubmed-36334289-focused-ultrasound-bbbo-ad-long-term-safety-imaging-cognitive-outcomes.md] The 2023 Brain open-label study in 9 AD participants added a direct DMN-targeted readout with no grade 3+ adverse events, no worsening on ADAS-cog11 or MMSE, and only modest regional PET changes.^[raw/articles/pubmed-36694943-bbbo-default-mode-network-ad.md] The 2025 J Neurosurg bilateral-frontal trial in 6 AD participants increased procedure extent, but still did not move MMSE; it did report amyloid reduction in 4 of 6 and caregiver NPI improvement in 5 of 6.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39793012-repetitive-bilateral-frontal-bbbo-ad.md] The 2025 meta-analysis pooled 8 clinical studies / 57 AD participants and reported no cognitive deterioration; its significant ORs are best read as procedure-level safety/BBBO metrics rather than evidence of cognitive benefit.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40406679-focused-ultrasound-bbbo-ad-meta-analysis.md] The stronger argument for the platform is still drug delivery and monitoring biology: a 2023 mixed mouse-plus-one-patient study tied mouse memory gains to opening volume and reported a single human amyloid-PET reduction in the treated hemisphere, while a 2025 EV paper showed that BBBO changes serum extracellular vesicles and amplifies biomarker detection.^[raw/articles/pubmed-37554284-focused-ultrasound-mitigates-pathology-improves-spatial-memory-ad.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40450507-serum-isolated-extracellular-vesicles-response-fus-bbbo.md] A 2025 in vivo study showed that FUS-BBBO can markedly increase aducanumab brain exposure without obvious tissue damage.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40404732-aducanumab-fus-bbbo-mouse.md] A second 2025 mouse study added mechanistic plausibility by showing that BBBO can shift aged wild-type and AD hippocampal astrocytes toward a glioprotective repair-associated program over days 2-7 after exposure.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40948563-focused-ultrasound-bbbo-astrocytes.md] A 2026 mechanism paper then separated safe low-pressure BBB opening from high-pressure injury by showing transient tight-junction reorganization at 450 kPa and persistent junction loss with microglial activation at 750 kPa.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41723237-safe-focused-ultrasound-bbb-opening-tight-junctions.md]

## What Changed Since 2024

- A 2023 long-term follow-up added the missing durability check: 10 mild AD participants, 30 BBB-opening treatments, 6-12 months follow-up, no serious procedure-related adverse events, and no apparent cognitive worsening relative to ADNI controls.^[raw/articles/pubmed-36334289-focused-ultrasound-bbbo-ad-long-term-safety-imaging-cognitive-outcomes.md]
- A 2023 Brain open-label study and a 2025 bilateral-frontal J Neurosurg trial added the most informative human nuance so far: BBB permeability and amyloid-related signals can move, and caregiver neuropsychiatric scores can improve, but standard cognition measures still do not show a clean efficacy win.^[raw/articles/pubmed-36694943-bbbo-default-mode-network-ad.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39793012-repetitive-bilateral-frontal-bbbo-ad.md]
- A 2024 systematic review and a 2025 meta-analysis both consolidated the AD BBB-opening literature and kept the safety signal broadly favorable; the newer synthesis pooled 8 studies / 57 participants, reported no post-procedure cognitive decline, and framed the branch as targeted-delivery support rather than a proven cognition treatment.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39044508-focused-ultrasound-bbbo-ad-systematic-review.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40406679-focused-ultrasound-bbbo-ad-meta-analysis.md]
- The 2025 meta-analysis is useful mainly as a clarification point: it strengthens the safety/feasibility case, but its significant pooled ORs should not be paraphrased as a cognition effect.
- The 2023 mouse-plus-one-patient paper and the 2025 EV paper shift the story toward opening-linked downstream biology, but the human cognition outcome remains indirect.^[raw/articles/pubmed-37554284-focused-ultrasound-mitigates-pathology-improves-spatial-memory-ad.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40450507-serum-isolated-extracellular-vesicles-response-fus-bbbo.md]
- The 2025 astrocyte transcriptomics paper adds a plausible glial-response layer, but it remains preclinical and does not change the human cognition verdict.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40948563-focused-ultrasound-bbbo-astrocytes.md]
- The 2026 sweep did not surface a new human cognition-positive BBBO paper beyond the long-term safety follow-up and the meta-analysis; the freshest addition is the mechanistic pressure-window paper.
- The Columbia registry posted results for NCT04118764 in 2024, which makes the clinical feasibility branch easier to interpret as a completed human study rather than an open-ended protocol.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct04118764-focused-ultrasound-bbbo-alz.md]
- Newer human reports added better operational control: portable-system cavitation mapping and repetitive bilateral frontal BBB opening both push the platform toward repeatable delivery workflows.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39113808-portable-focused-ultrasound-bbbo-ad.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39793012-repetitive-bilateral-frontal-bbbo-ad.md]
- A 2026 brain-tumor BBBO workflow paper further tightened the implementation story by reporting an optimized protocol, a 64-spot treatment grid, same-session MRI acquisition, and no clinical complications, which reinforces that clinical BBBO remains a specialist-monitored procedure rather than a consumer protocol.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41821761-focused-ultrasound-bbbo-brain-tumor-technical-nuances.md]
- The 2025 mouse aducanumab study sharpened the platform question: BBBO may matter more as a delivery enabler than as a stand-alone cognitive intervention.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40404732-aducanumab-fus-bbbo-mouse.md]
- The 2026 mechanism paper sharpened the safety window: low-pressure sonication produced transient tight-junction reorganization, while higher pressure produced persistent junction loss and microglial activation.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41723237-safe-focused-ultrasound-bbb-opening-tight-junctions.md]
- The 2026 umbrella review made the family taxonomy cleaner by splitting focused ultrasound into lesioning, BBB opening, and low-intensity circuit stimulation, which reduces the risk of mixing delivery, stimulation, and ablation evidence.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41610842-transcranial-focused-ultrasound-in-the-human-brain.md]

## Evidence Tiering

| Tier | What it shows | Practical read |
|---|---|---|
| Human long-term follow-up | Repeated BBB opening over months is feasible, with no serious procedure-related adverse events and no apparent cognitive worsening in the small cohort | Strongest durability readout so far, but still uncontrolled |
| Human feasibility/safety | Reversible BBB opening is achievable in small AD cohorts, with no major acute safety signal | This is the strongest clinical layer so far |
| Human biomarker/imaging follow-up | Perivenous and meningeal permeability patterns appear after sonication; amyloid signals may shift slightly in some studies | Interesting biology, not yet a cognition proof |
| Registry-level cognition endpoints | MMSE, ADAS-cog11, and related cognitive checks were built into trial design | The design acknowledges cognition, but published effects remain weak and often domain-specific |
| Preclinical drug-delivery proof | FUS-BBBO increases aducanumab brain exposure without obvious tissue damage | Supports the platform, not human clinical efficacy |
| Preclinical glial-response evidence | BBBO shifts aged mouse hippocampal astrocytes toward a glioprotective transcriptional state | Mechanistic plausibility only; not human outcome evidence |
| Mechanistic preclinical safety | Low-pressure opening uses transient tight-junction reorganization; high pressure causes persistent junction loss and microglial activation | The safety envelope is pressure-dependent, not binary |

## Effect Size Snapshot

- 2023 long-term follow-up: 10 mild AD participants, 30 treatments, 6-12 months follow-up, immediate opening and 24-48 hour closure, no serious procedure-related adverse events, and cognitive change comparable to ADNI controls.
- 2023 DMN-targeted Brain study: 9 participants, transient BBB permeability in bilateral hippocampi / anterior cingulate / precuneus, no grade 3+ AEs, no worsening on ADAS-cog11 or MMSE, and modest regional PET reduction.
- 2018 phase I AD work: 5 participants, repeated opening, no clinically significant cognitive worsening at 3 months, no serious adverse events.
- 2021 hippocampal/entorhinal work: 3 participants, repeated opening, no hemorrhage or other adverse effect, with a new perivenous enhancement pattern.
- 2021 frontal proof-of-concept: 5 of 6 participants completed, BBB opening reached about 95.7% of targeted volume; caregiver neuropsychiatric scores improved transiently but returned by 3 months.
- 2025 bilateral-frontal J Neurosurg trial: 6 participants, three bilateral frontal sessions, mean 43.1 cm3 opening, 14.9-centiloid average decrease in 4 of 6, no MMSE change, and caregiver NPI improvement in 5 of 6.
- 2025 meta-analysis: 57 participants across 8 studies, safety OR 0.88 (95% CI 0.75-0.98) and efficacy OR 0.91 (95% CI 0.61-1.00), with no post-procedure cognitive deterioration, minor temporary adverse events, and procedure-level pooled ORs that favor continued feasibility work rather than a claim of cognitive enhancement.
- 2023 mouse-plus-one-patient paper: spatial memory improved in mice, improvement tracked BBB-opening volume, and the single human case showed amyloid reduction in the treated hemisphere.
- 2025 mouse aducanumab delivery: roughly 7-fold and 60-fold delivery boosts at the two tested pressure conditions, which is the clearest platform-level dose-response signal in this set.
- 2025 serum EV paper: BBB opening increased serum EV abundance in mice and patients, with biomarker amplification that may help monitor exposure, but it did not measure cognition.
- 2025 astrocyte transcriptomics: hippocampal astrocytes in aged wild-type and AD mice shifted toward a repair-associated transcriptional program over days 2-7 after BBBO, which is biologically interesting but still not a cognition endpoint.
- 2026 mechanism paper: 450 kPa produced transient BBB opening via tight-junction reorganization, while 750 kPa produced persistent junction obliteration, fibrinogen passage, and microglial activation.

## Safety / Adverse Events

- Across the AD feasibility studies, the dominant message is no major hemorrhage or durable radiographic harm in the small samples reported so far.
- The longer 2023 follow-up adds repeated-treatment reassurance: immediate opening, reopening/closure dynamics within 24-48 hours, and no serious procedure-related adverse events.
- The 2025 meta-analysis characterized observed adverse events as minor and temporary.
- The main safety burden is operational, not just biological: contrast agent use, MRI guidance, screening for contraindications, acoustic monitoring, and repeat targeting all matter.
- The 2024 portable-system paper suggests that cavitation mapping can improve control, but it does not remove the need for specialist oversight.
- The 2026 mechanism paper adds a sharper warning: high-pressure settings can convert BBB opening from transient and reversible to persistent injury with microglial activation.

## Responder Phenotypes

- No validated human responder phenotype exists yet.
- The trial designs preferentially enroll biomarker-positive AD with demonstrable amyloid pathology, so the current branch is best read as phenotype-enriched by diagnosis rather than by a proven predictive biomarker.
- Target choice matters: hippocampus, entorhinal cortex, frontal lobe, and broader bilateral frontal fields are not interchangeable.

## Protocol Parameters

- Common human setup: MRI-guided focused ultrasound with intravenous microbubbles, usually Definity in the registry-level Columbia study.
- NCT02986932 used a 220-kHz system and staged escalation from a small frontal target to a larger 2.5-3.0 cm region.
- The 2018 and 2021 human studies used repeated sessions separated by weeks or months, not one-off exposures.
- The 2024 portable-system paper adds real-time 2-D cavitation mapping, which is a meaningful step toward better delivery control.
- The 2025 preclinical aducanumab paper tested 0.25 MPa and 0.42 MPa conditions and found higher delivery with higher pressure.
- The 2026 mechanism paper used 450 kPa as the lower, transient-opening condition and 750 kPa as the higher, injury-associated condition in mice.

## Open Contradictions

- The platform looks safe and targetable in small studies, but human cognition benefit is still not clearly demonstrated.
- The long-term follow-up compares favorably with ADNI controls, but that is not the same as a randomized cognitive benefit signal.
- The meta-analysis' reported "efficacy" signal refers to BBBO/procedural performance, not cognition.
- Some studies report amyloid-related or neuropsychiatric changes, while others report only safety and biomarker opening.
- A high-intensity AD trial in 2024 uses BBB-disruption language and should not be conflated with the lower-intensity BBBO feasibility literature.
- Delivery success does not automatically imply disease modification; that remains the central unresolved question, and the pooled 2025 meta-analysis does not resolve that gap.
- The 2023 mouse-plus-one-patient paper is easy to overread because the cognition gain is in mice and the human readout is just one treated patient with amyloid reduction.
- The 2025 J Neurosurg trial improved caregiver NPI more clearly than MMSE, which suggests symptom relief may move before standard cognition measures.
- Mechanistic glial-response papers improve plausibility, but they do not bridge the human outcome gap by themselves.
- Preclinical safety papers can be overread as blanket reassurance; the relevant variable is acoustic pressure window plus monitoring, not BBB opening as a generic label.
