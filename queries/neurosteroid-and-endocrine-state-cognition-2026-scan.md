# Neurosteroid and Endocrine-State Cognition 2026 Scan

## Summary

This branch looks much more like state restoration than universal enhancement.

[[allopregnanolone]] is the clearest active AD lead: the 2020 phase 1b/2a study established safety and tolerability, the 2022 imaging follow-up reported hippocampal and connectivity signals, and the 2026 REGEN-BRAIN phase 2 registry is still recruiting with a 2026-11-18 primary completion date.

[[testosterone-trt]] is mixed. TEAAM was negative across major cognitive domains, but the LITROS secondary analysis suggests a phenotype-sensitive benefit when testosterone is layered onto intensive lifestyle intervention in older obese hypogonadal men. A 2025 healthy-men study adds a narrower signal: a single testosterone dose shifted harm-avoidance learning and model-based learning rates.

[[dhea]] is negative in healthy older adults. [[pregnenolone]] has some symptom and functional-capacity signals in schizophrenia, but the controlled cognition readouts stayed neutral or inconsistent.

[[gnrh]] is the most interesting axis result right now: the 2022 Science paper and the ongoing Pulse-UP registry in Down syndrome both point to a disease-specific cognition signal that should not be generalized to enhancement claims. A 2025 MRI follow-up also reported MoCA improvement after 6 months of pulsatile GnRH in a tiny Down syndrome cohort.

[[kisspeptin]] remains upstream and mostly reproductive so far. The human work now has one biologically active crossover RCT with no anxiety, cortisol, blood pressure, or heart-rate effect, plus an anatomy paper pointing to sensorial-processing and behavioral-state-control regions, but still no direct cognition endpoint.

Related pages: [[growth-hormone-secretagogue-cognition-2026-scan]], [[incretin-therapies-brain-health]], and [[cholinergic-state-restoration-and-cognition]].

## What Changed Since 2024

- [[allopregnanolone]] moved from exploratory AD safety work into an active phase 2 efficacy trial with cognitive and hippocampal endpoints.
- [[testosterone-trt]] evidence became easier to separate into negative average TRT data versus a smaller phenotype-sensitive positive signal in obese hypogonadal older men.
- [[gnrh]] gained a published human cognition signal in Down syndrome plus an ongoing multicenter registry.
- [[kisspeptin]] human studies expanded, but still around reproductive endocrinology, null anxiety findings, and brain-state circuitry rather than cognition.
- The branch now has a cleaner way to keep mood, libido, motivation, and hormone-replacement effects separate from actual cognitive outcomes.

## Evidence Tiering

### [[allopregnanolone]]

- Human RCT safety: positive for safety/tolerability, not efficacy.
- Human imaging follow-up: exploratory positive structural/connectivity signals.
- Human efficacy: pending in REGEN-BRAIN.
- Mood/depression: current review literature treats it as a mood and neurosteroid candidate, but that is not the same as cognition proof.

### [[testosterone-trt]]

- Healthy or mixed older men: mostly neutral in controlled cognition studies.
- Low-T plus lifestyle + frailty/obesity phenotype: one positive secondary analysis.
- Observational association: low bioavailable testosterone tracks worse cognition in older men, but that does not establish treatment benefit.

### [[dhea]]

- Healthy older adults: negative RCT.
- Review literature: still mostly associative or mechanistic.

### [[pregnenolone]]

- Schizophrenia: may help some functional/symptom endpoints, but the main cognition composite stayed neutral.
- Healthy enhancement: no direct support surfaced in this pass.

### [[gnrh]] / [[kisspeptin]]

- Down syndrome: small human cognition signal with a current confirmatory registry.
- [[kisspeptin]]: human studies are currently reproductive-hormone, anxiety-null, or behavioral-state studies, not cognition efficacy studies.

## Branch Notes

### [[allopregnanolone]]

The branch is best read as regenerative neurosteroid development in early AD, not as a general nootropic. The 2020 phase 1b/2a study used weekly IV dosing in early AD and found no adverse cognitive or MRI outcomes, while the 2022 imaging paper reported slowed hippocampal atrophy and stronger white-matter and functional-connectivity measures in exploratory analyses. REGEN-BRAIN is still recruiting as of 2026-03-13 and now has a 2026-11-18 primary completion estimate. ^[raw/articles/pubmed-33344752-allopregnanolone-phase1b2a-ad.md] ^[raw/articles/pubmed-35310526-allopregnanolone-imaging-follow-up.md] ^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct04838301-allopregnanolone-regen-brain.md] ^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct04838301-allopregnanolone-regen-brain-status.md]

The 2025 review on AD-related depression is useful mainly as a reminder that allopregnanolone also lives in a mood/sleep/neurosteroid frame. That is a separate claim from cognitive enhancement and should stay in a lower-confidence lane unless a trial shows direct cognitive benefit. ^[raw/articles/pubmed-40854505-allopregnanolone-ad-depression-review.md]

### [[testosterone-trt]]

The long-duration TEAAM trial is the cleanest counterweight to the broad "testosterone sharpens the mind" claim: three years of replacement in older men with low or low-normal testosterone did not improve visuospatial ability, verbal fluency, verbal memory, dexterity, or executive function. The older literature is not perfectly flat, though. The LITROS analysis in obese hypogonadal men suggests testosterone may add cognitive benefit when it is paired with intensive lifestyle intervention and a frailty/metabolic phenotype, and that effect tracked gains in fitness and strength as well as hormone changes. A newer 2025 study in healthy men adds a more specific signal: testosterone shifted harm-avoidance learning and computational learning rates after a single dose, which is a decision-learning effect rather than broad cognition enhancement. ^[raw/articles/pubmed-27377542-testosterone-teaam-cognition.md] ^[raw/articles/pubmed-34375393-testosterone-lifestyle-hypogonadism-cognition.md] ^[raw/articles/pubmed-40818499-testosterone-harm-avoidance-learning.md]

Interpretation: TRT looks more like correction of a low-reserve state in a selected phenotype than a universal enhancer.

### [[dhea]] and [[pregnenolone]]

[[dhea]] is a good example of why community hormone claims need a hard cognition filter. The DAWN trial in healthy older adults found no cognitive benefit and no durable wellbeing benefit, despite the obvious temptation to treat DHEA as an anti-aging or vitality supplement. [[pregnenolone]] is similar: in schizophrenia it may move function or symptoms, but the main cognition composite stayed neutral in the better-controlled work surfaced here. ^[raw/articles/pubmed-18482290-dhea-dawn-trial.md] ^[raw/articles/pubmed-24496044-pregnenolone-schizophrenia-cognition.md]

These branches should not be upgraded above `confidence: low` for cognition in healthy adults.

### [[gnrh]] and [[kisspeptin]]

[[gnrh]] currently has the most striking axis-level human signal because the 2022 Science paper reported that pulsatile GnRH improved cognition and brain connectivity in a small Down syndrome sample, the ongoing Pulse-UP trial is now recruiting with a larger estimated enrollment through 2028-12, and a 2025 follow-up paper reported MoCA improvement after 6 months plus persistent MRI pattern differences. That is interesting, but it is still disease-specific and not evidence for a general enhancement protocol. ^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct04390646-gnrh-cognition-down-syndrome.md] ^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct04390646-gnrh-cognition-down-syndrome-status.md] ^[raw/articles/pubmed-36048943-gnrh-replacement-rescues-cognition-down-syndrome.md] ^[raw/articles/pubmed-40190351-gnrh-down-syndrome-mri-followup.md]

[[kisspeptin]] itself is still upstream of the cognition story. The human literature is now slightly more informative: one crossover RCT confirmed biological activity without changing anxiety or autonomic readouts, and the 2025 anatomy paper points to sensorial and behavioral-state circuitry. Neither paper gives a direct cognition efficacy signal. The practical wiki implication is to keep kisspeptin in the endocrine-routing layer unless a direct cognition trial appears. ^[raw/articles/pubmed-40215751-intranasal-kisspeptin-hormone-release.md] ^[raw/articles/pubmed-28112678-kisspeptin-sexual-emotional-brain-processing.md] ^[raw/articles/pubmed-40036336-kisspeptin-anxiety-rct.md] ^[raw/articles/pubmed-40065551-kisspeptin-state-control-anatomy.md]

## Safety and Adverse Events

- [[allopregnanolone]]: sedation emerges at higher doses; the phase 1b/2a work used that to set the MTD.
- [[testosterone-trt]]: keep hematocrit, prostate, cardiovascular, fertility, and sleep-apnea issues in view when interpreting TRT claims; acute learning effects do not remove those routine TRT risks.
- [[dhea]] and [[pregnenolone]]: endocrine side effects and product-identity problems matter more than any current cognition signal.
- [[gnrh]]: peptide-delivery burden and disease-specific selection are major practical constraints.
- [[kisspeptin]]: current human work is not a cognition protocol, so do not translate reproductive-hormone dosing into cognitive enhancement assumptions. The human safety-style RCT is reassuring, but it does not create an enhancement claim.

## Open Contradictions

- [[testosterone-trt]] is not simply positive or negative; the phenotype and co-intervention seem to determine whether any cognition signal appears.
- [[testosterone-trt]] now has three layers that should stay separate: null long-duration TRT, phenotype-sensitive add-on benefit with lifestyle intervention, and acute decision-learning shifts in healthy men.
- [[allopregnanolone]] has encouraging structural/imaging data but still lacks a posted efficacy result.
- [[gnrh]] is promising in Down syndrome, but it is unclear whether the effect generalizes to other populations or ages.
- [[pregnenolone]] and [[dhea]] are often discussed as "brain boosters," yet the better human cognition evidence is either null or indirect.
- [[kisspeptin]] is a good example of an endocrine axis that can look neurologically interesting without yet having a direct cognition endpoint.
