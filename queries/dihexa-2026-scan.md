# Dihexa 2026 Scan

Dihexa remains best described as a preclinical AngIV-analog / HGF-cMet candidate with rodent procognitive and recovery-adjacent signals. In the searches I ran, I found no published human cognition trial and no registered human Dihexa program, so the translational status is still preclinical-only as of 2026-04-27.

## What Changed Since 2024

The main update is not a new human dataset but a clearer framing of the evidence ceiling. The foundational 2012 AngIV-analog paper established oral, BBB-permeant procognitive activity in rodent models, the 2021 APP/PS1 mouse paper added a more explicit AD-like memory rescue and PI3K/AKT readout, and the 2021 sciatic-nerve paper extended Dihexa into limb-repair biology. None of those papers crosses into human cognition evidence.^[raw/articles/pubmed-23055539-dihexa-procognitive-antidementia.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-34827486-dihexa-app-ps1-memory.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-34703584-dihexa-sciatic-nerve-repair.md]

## Effect Size Snapshot

There is no human effect size to report. The rodent literature is directionally positive, but the practical meaning is still limited by the small number of studies, the concentration of work in a narrow research lineage, and the fact that the outcomes are animal behavioral proxies rather than clinical cognition endpoints.^[raw/articles/pubmed-23055539-dihexa-procognitive-antidementia.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-34827486-dihexa-app-ps1-memory.md]

## Safety / Adverse Events

No human safety dataset was found. That is the key safety issue: Dihexa is still a research compound with no published human PK, tolerability, or adverse-event series in the cognition space. The animal literature uses nontrivial mg/kg dosing and experimental formulations, including oral dosing in mice and hydrogel / intraperitoneal / muscle delivery in rats.^[raw/articles/pubmed-34827486-dihexa-app-ps1-memory.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-34703584-dihexa-sciatic-nerve-repair.md]

## Responder Phenotypes

- APP/PS1-like Alzheimer mouse models.
- Aged rat and scopolamine-impaired rodent models.
- Peripheral nerve injury / repair models.

No human responder phenotype is established.

## Protocol Parameters

- Foundational procognitive work: metabolically stabilized AngIV analogs with oral BBB-permeant behavior in rodent models.^[raw/articles/pubmed-23055539-dihexa-procognitive-antidementia.md]
- APP/PS1 mouse study: 1.44 mg/kg and 2.88 mg/kg Dihexa were tested in the mouse protocol.^[raw/articles/pubmed-34827486-dihexa-app-ps1-memory.md]
- Rat sciatic-nerve repair study: 2-4 mg/kg dosing was used across local, systemic, and muscle-delivery arms.^[raw/articles/pubmed-34703584-dihexa-sciatic-nerve-repair.md]

## Open Contradictions

- The loudest public claims about Dihexa are potency and nootropic strength, but the actual evidence base is still narrow and preclinical.
- Mechanism framing is not settled. Older AngIV/IRAP language and newer HGF/c-Met framing both circulate, but neither has human cognition validation.
- The recovery-oriented animal studies do not yet justify healthy-adult enhancement claims.
