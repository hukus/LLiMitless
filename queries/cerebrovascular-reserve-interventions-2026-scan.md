# Cerebrovascular Reserve Interventions - 2026 Scan

## Summary

The reserve branch is real, but it is not one intervention class. Acute exercise remains the strongest and most generalizable human cognition signal; beetroot/nitrate now has a 2025 AD vascular-responsiveness RCT showing reserve biomarkers can move without restoring chronically abnormal vascular properties; and breath protocols are still more convincing as physiology or biomarker interventions than as healthy-adult cognitive enhancers.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39883421-acute-exercise-meta-review.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-34018848-cardiorespiratory-fitness-exercise-training-cerebrovascular.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-37875637-beetroot-chewable-cognitive-performance.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-36839248-cr-beetroot-vascular-cognitive-functions.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40089052-nitrate-beetroot-ad-vascular-responsiveness.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41358607-mindfulness-slow-breathing-plasma-amyloid-beta.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41079240-anulom-vilom-pranayama-parkinson-cognition.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-32344403-unilateral-left-nostril-breathing-cognitive-function.md]

What changed since 2024:

- Acute exercise moved from "probably helpful" to a meta-reviewed effect with 383 unique studies and a small-to-medium overall cognition benefit.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39883421-acute-exercise-meta-review.md]
- Beetroot/nitrate gained a direct acute cognition RCT in healthy adults and a follow-on review, but the broader review still calls cognition evidence limited and inconsistent.^[raw/articles/pubmed-37875637-beetroot-chewable-cognitive-performance.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41470898-beetroot-juice-healthy-aging-systematic-review.md]
- Beetroot/nitrate also picked up a 2025 randomized trial in Alzheimer's disease showing acute nitrate increased plasma nitrate/nitrite and vascular responsiveness but did not fully restore the AD vascular phenotype, so the reserve signal is now cleaner than the cognition signal.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40089052-nitrate-beetroot-ad-vascular-responsiveness.md]
- Breath protocols got one clearer disease-context cognition RCT in Parkinson's disease, a 2025 slow-breathing/mindfulness trial on plasma amyloid beta, and a healthy-volunteer pilot that still read as a null versus breath awareness.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41079240-anulom-vilom-pranayama-parkinson-cognition.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41358607-mindfulness-slow-breathing-plasma-amyloid-beta.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-32344403-unilateral-left-nostril-breathing-cognitive-function.md]
- The mechanism layer tightened: higher cardiorespiratory fitness tracks with better cerebrovascular reactivity, but exercise training does not consistently increase global cerebral blood flow or MCA velocity.^[raw/articles/pubmed-34018848-cardiorespiratory-fitness-exercise-training-cerebrovascular.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-37003561-acute-exercise-cbf-systematic-review.md]

## Evidence Tiering

- Meta/review tier: acute exercise cognition, CRF/exercise training cerebrovascular reserve, beetroot healthy-aging review, acute exercise CBF review, and breath-hold CVR review.
- Human RCT tier: acute beetroot chewable, CR plus beetroot pilot, aquatic/land treadmill older-adult trial, PD alternate-nostril-breathing RCT, and healthy-volunteer ULNB pilot.
- Mechanistic tier: the exercise/CVR literature supports reserve biology, but the direct cognition claim remains more robust than the CBF claim for many protocols.
- Negative or weak tier: healthy-volunteer breath-awareness comparison did not separate out a unique unilateral-breathing cognitive effect.

## Effect Size Snapshot

- Acute exercise meta-review: overall cognition M SMD 0.33; attention 0.37; executive function 0.36; memory 0.23; information processing 0.20.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39883421-acute-exercise-meta-review.md]
- CRF/exercise training meta-analysis: higher CRF vs lower CRF effect on cerebrovascular resistance -0.54 and cerebrovascular reactivity 0.98; exercise training effect on MCA velocity 0.05 and CVR 0.60 (p = 0.09).^[raw/articles/pubmed-34018848-cardiorespiratory-fitness-exercise-training-cerebrovascular.md]
- Beetroot chewable RCT: immediate recall +20.69%, delayed recall +12.34%, frontal-lobe function +2.57%, cognitive flexibility +11.16%; no change in working memory or processing speed.^[raw/articles/pubmed-37875637-beetroot-chewable-cognitive-performance.md]
- CR plus beetroot pilot: microvascular flux p = 0.03, NO-dependent endothelial activity p = 0.02, and TMT-B p = 0.012 versus CR alone.^[raw/articles/pubmed-36839248-cr-beetroot-vascular-cognitive-functions.md]
- AD beetroot RCT: acute nitrate increased plasma nitrate/nitrite and vascular responsiveness over 4 hours (p < 0.001), but the AD group remained below healthy controls and no cognition endpoint was reported.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40089052-nitrate-beetroot-ad-vascular-responsiveness.md]
- Aquatic treadmill RCT: DSST improved within both arms; the aquatic arm showed the larger CBF rise, but the between-group cognition advantage was not decisive.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39395580-aquatic-treadmill-cognitive-function-cbf.md]
- PD alternate-nostril breathing: abstract reports significant improvements in cognition and HRV, but no standardized effect size was provided in the PubMed abstract.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41079240-anulom-vilom-pranayama-parkinson-cognition.md]
- Healthy ULNB pilot: no between-group cognitive effect versus breath awareness.^[raw/articles/pubmed-32344403-unilateral-left-nostril-breathing-cognitive-function.md]

## Safety / Adverse Events

- No major adverse-event signal surfaced in the abstracts reviewed here; the branch is generally lower risk than stimulant or device branches.
- Exercise still requires ordinary cardiovascular and musculoskeletal screening, especially if the goal is acute load rather than gentle activity.
- Beetroot/nitrate is not toxicity-free: practical concerns are GI tolerance, blood-pressure lowering, and medication interactions, especially in hypotension-prone users.
- Breath protocols are low-tech but not risk-free in practice; overbreathing, long breath-holds, or aggressive pacing can cause lightheadedness, presyncope, or panic-like symptoms, so the intervention should stay conservative.

## Responder Phenotypes

- Best-supported exercise responders are sedentary, older, or otherwise low-fitness people, where reserve gains are easiest to move.
- Beetroot/nitrate looks more plausible in metabolically stressed or less-trained phenotypes than in already optimized athletes.
- AD and older-adult vascular dysfunction may still show acute nitrate responsiveness, but that does not imply cognitive restoration.
- The PD breathing trial suggests neurologic disease contexts with autonomic imbalance may be more responsive than healthy-volunteer convenience samples.
- Healthy, yoga-naive adults did not show a clear incremental advantage for unilateral nostril breathing over breath awareness.

## Protocol Parameters

- Acute exercise meta-review: single bouts, post-exercise assessment, and heterogeneous intensity/duration across source reviews.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39883421-acute-exercise-meta-review.md]
- CR plus beetroot pilot: 14 days of caloric restriction with high-nitrate beetroot juice in overweight adults.^[raw/articles/pubmed-36839248-cr-beetroot-vascular-cognitive-functions.md]
- Beetroot chewable RCT: four chewable tablets containing 3 g Beta vulgaris extract, 90 min before testing, with a 4-day washout.^[raw/articles/pubmed-37875637-beetroot-chewable-cognitive-performance.md]
- Aquatic/land treadmill RCT: one moderate-intensity session in healthy older adults.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39395580-aquatic-treadmill-cognitive-function-cbf.md]
- CRF/exercise training meta-analysis: the training lane is more about longitudinal reserve adaptation than one-off acute dosing.^[raw/articles/pubmed-34018848-cardiorespiratory-fitness-exercise-training-cerebrovascular.md]
- PD alternate-nostril breathing: 12 weeks as adjunctive therapy to conventional treatment.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41079240-anulom-vilom-pranayama-parkinson-cognition.md]
- Healthy ULNB pilot: 15 minutes of unilateral left nostril breathing versus breath awareness.^[raw/articles/pubmed-32344403-unilateral-left-nostril-breathing-cognitive-function.md]

## Open Contradictions

- Exercise improves cognition more consistently than it increases global cerebral blood flow, so reserve biology and behavior do not map 1:1.
- Beetroot/nitrate has positive acute findings but a 13-week overweight/obese older-adult pilot that failed to move cognition or CBF, so the branch is phenotype-sensitive and not settled.
- Acute nitrate can move vascular biomarkers in AD without restoring the full vascular phenotype or demonstrating cognition, so reserve biology is still not the same thing as performance benefit.
- Breath-hold CVR is a measurement method, not a therapeutic intervention; it is easy to overread physiology papers as if they were treatment trials.
- Breath protocols split between disease-context benefit and healthy-volunteer nulls, so the branch should not be promoted as a general enhancer yet.
- The main unresolved question is whether any apparent cognition gains are driven by reserve, arousal, expectancy, or simple task practice.
