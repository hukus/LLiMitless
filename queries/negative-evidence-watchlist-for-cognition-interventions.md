# Negative Evidence Watchlist for Cognition Interventions

This page tracks the current null, failed, and biomarker-only branches so the wiki does not overread signal-heavy abstracts. The main pattern is not "everything fails"; it is "benefit often collapses when you ask for the harder endpoint, the longer follow-up, or the healthier phenotype." The strongest current negatives are exenatide in MCI, semaglutide in early symptomatic AD, AL002/TREM2 agonism in early AD despite target engagement, the [[iclepertin]] GlyT1 program's phase 2 and phase 3 misses, taVNS under acute mental stress, no durable 1-month tDCS effect in older adults, no added tDCS benefit over cognitive stimulation in aMCI, no healthy-older-adult tACS effect, tPBM's null persistent-concussion RCT, the intranasal-insulin MS cognition null, rapamycin's flat human cognition readouts despite biomarker movement, and creatine's lack of a broad overall cognition or executive-function win.^[raw/articles/pubmed-38565814-exenatide-mci-proof-of-concept-negative.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41865758-semaglutide-evoke-evokeplus-ad-phase3.md]^[raw/articles/nature-2026-al002-trem2-agonistic-antibody-early-ad-phase2.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39759424-iclepertin-cct-schizophrenia-phase2.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41233083-iclepertin-connex-phase3-schizophrenia.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40908377-taVNS-mental-stressor.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-38452749-tdcs-older-adults-mci-2024.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40004217-tdcs-cognitive-stimulation-amci-null.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41034500-tacs-older-adults-meta-2025.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-38109202-tpbm-concussion-null-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40253245-intranasal-insulin-ms-null.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-29408453-rapamycin-older-human-cohort-cognitive-effects.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40394335-rapamycin-adrd-phase1-open-label.md]^[raw/articles/medrxiv-2025-12-08-25340853-rapamycin-six-month-ad-pilot.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39070254-creatine-cognition-adults-meta-analysis.md]

The more specific failure mode this page watches for is the biomarker split: a branch can move physiology, imaging, or inflammatory markers without clearing the actual cognition endpoint. Semaglutide in AD is a clean example because the phase 3 program was negative on the primary progression endpoint even while the abstract frames AD-biomarker movement; AL002/TREM2 adds a precision-neuroimmune example because CSF target-engagement and pharmacodynamic markers moved while CDR-SB did not separate; iclepertin shows a different but related failure mode, where a plausible glutamatergic strategy stayed null in both a phase 2 adjunctive training study and the phase 3 CONNEX program; taVNS under acute mental stress moved autonomic measures without changing PASAT performance; intranasal insulin moved regional CBF and phenotype-linked physiology without establishing a causal cognition link; and sham-controlled HBOT in TBI stayed null overall even before the retracted severe-TBI trial is excluded.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41865758-semaglutide-evoke-evokeplus-ad-phase3.md]^[raw/articles/nature-2026-al002-trem2-agonistic-antibody-early-ad-phase2.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39759424-iclepertin-cct-schizophrenia-phase2.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41233083-iclepertin-connex-phase3-schizophrenia.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40908377-taVNS-mental-stressor.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-36219990-intranasal-insulin-cbf-cognition-adults-systematic-review.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-26403017-hbot-tbi-sham-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-38217421-hbot-severe-tbi-retraction-notice.md]

What changed since 2024:

- The incretin branch now has direct human cognition data, but the cleanest disease-stage readouts are negative at the primary endpoint level: exenatide in MCI and semaglutide in early symptomatic AD.^[raw/articles/pubmed-38565814-exenatide-mci-proof-of-concept-negative.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41865758-semaglutide-evoke-evokeplus-ad-phase3.md]
- The precision neuroinflammation branch now has a paper-level TREM2 failure: AL002 engaged the target and moved CSF pharmacodynamic markers, but CDR-SB did not improve and ARIA-like MRI changes emerged as the main safety signal.^[raw/articles/nature-2026-al002-trem2-agonistic-antibody-early-ad-phase2.md]
- Semaglutide in MDD also shows the branch-level pattern: a negative primary executive-function endpoint with a better secondary global-cognition composite, which is exactly the kind of split that can mislead if the wiki only reads headlines.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41218611-semaglutide-mdd-cognitive-dysfunction-rct.md]
- taVNS can move autonomic readouts without moving PASAT performance under acute stress, which makes it a useful example of biomarker without behavior.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40908377-taVNS-mental-stressor.md]
- The tDCS branch now has a newer aMCI combination-trial null: cognitive stimulation helped, but active tDCS did not add cognitive or biomarker benefit beyond sham.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40004217-tdcs-cognitive-stimulation-amci-null.md]
- The NMDA/GlyT1 lane now belongs on the watchlist too: iclepertin was null in the adjunctive computerized cognitive training phase 2 study and negative again in CONNEX phase 3, so the branch should be treated as a double-null failure rather than a positive class signal.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39759424-iclepertin-cct-schizophrenia-phase2.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41233083-iclepertin-connex-phase3-schizophrenia.md]
- AMPAkines belong on the watchlist for the same structural reason: the old primate and sleep-loss promise is real, but the human cognition studies stayed mixed-to-negative and the modern low-impact work is still PD/safety only.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40650226-ampa-kainate-modulators-cns-review.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-36153330-tak653-tms-cns-effects.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39302241-cx1739-safety-pharmacokinetics.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39892449-cx717-safety-pharmacokinetics.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-21940760-cx717-sleep-deprivation-human.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-17955941-cx717-night-shift-work.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-17487227-cx516-schizophrenia-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-17069542-cx516-fragile-x-controlled-trial.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-16104830-cx717-nonhuman-primate-sleep-deprivation.md]
- Rapamycin now belongs on the same watchlist: the 2018 healthy-older-adult feasibility RCT was cognitively flat, the 2025 MCI/early-dementia phase 1 trial found no significant cognitive change and no detectable CSF rapamycin, the 2025/2026 PK companion only clarified exposure and monitoring, the 2026 immune-aging study moved p21 but not cognition, and the 2025 early-AD pilot stayed null on its prespecified FDG-PET regions.^[raw/articles/pubmed-29408453-rapamycin-older-human-cohort-cognitive-effects.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40394335-rapamycin-adrd-phase1-open-label.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41046300-rapamycin-intermittent-pk-early-ad.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41524558-rapamycin-aging-cell-dna-damage-immune-resilience.md]^[raw/articles/medrxiv-2025-12-08-25340853-rapamycin-six-month-ad-pilot.md]
- The 5-HT6 antagonist lane now has a dedicated failure pattern too: SB-742457, idalopirdine, masupirdine, and intepirdine all showed how a plausible serotonergic cognition mechanism can still miss primary endpoints across AD and DLB.^[raw/articles/pubmed-20043816-sb742457-phase2-ad.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-29854923-sb742457-two-rcts-ad.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-25297016-idalopirdine-ladder-moderate-ad.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-29318278-idalopirdine-three-rcts-ad.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-35662833-masupirdine-suvn-502-moderate-ad-cognition.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-34189249-intepirdine-headway-dlb.md]
- AMPAkines now have a clearer split than they did in 2024: the 2024 toxicity-mechanism paper explains why high-impact compounds were hard to develop, and the 2026 juvenile ischemia rescue paper keeps only a narrow recovery-state niche alive.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39304968-high-impact-ampakines-toxic-calcium-release.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41612671-type-ii-ampakines-juvenile-global-cerebral-ischemia.md]
- GV-971 is not a simple negative, but it belongs on this watchlist as a contested/geography-limited branch: phase 2 missed the primary ADAS-Cog12 endpoint, China phase 3 was positive, and the planned global phase 3 confirmation remains suspended/terminated without posted efficacy results.^[raw/articles/pubmed-32928279-sodium-oligomannate-phase2.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct04520412-gv971-green-memory-global.md]^[raw/articles/greenvalley-2022-gv971-global-phase3-termination.md]
- TrkB / BDNF mimetics are not a failed human class yet; they are a missing-translation class. [[7-8-dihydroxyflavone|7,8-DHF]], [[lm22a-4]], [[deoxygedunin]], [[r13]], and [[zeb85]] have animal, prodrug, or antibody-specificity signals, but targeted ClinicalTrials.gov searches returned no named human cognition program, and the specificity literature keeps target-engagement questions open. Use [[target-specificity-gate-for-plasticity-molecules]] when deciding whether a future TrkB lead should stay in the comparator layer.^[raw/articles/pubmed-37488840-trkb-agonist-caution-review.md]^[raw/articles/pmc-8398766-small-molecule-trkb-agonist-limitations.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-trkb-bdnf-mimetic-registry-search-2026.md]
- Intranasal insulin remains phenotype-sensitive rather than broadly positive: the regional CBF review suggests physiology shifts without proving a direct cognition mechanism, and the newer delirium RCT missed its primary duration endpoint overall.^[raw/articles/pubmed-36219990-intranasal-insulin-cbf-cognition-adults-systematic-review.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41091693-long-acting-intranasal-insulin-delirium-rct.md]
- The newest MS trial adds a sharper null: intranasal insulin was safe and tolerable, but SDMT and secondary endpoints stayed flat versus placebo.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40253245-intranasal-insulin-multiple-sclerosis-cognition.md]
- The newest MCI/AD meta-analysis resets the chronic branch: five RCTs and 540 participants showed no meaningful cognitive, functional, or CSF biomarker benefit, despite acceptable overall tolerability.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41436338-intranasal-insulin-mci-ad-systematic-review-meta-analysis.md]
- The perioperative branch is the exception: intranasal insulin now has repeated RCT and meta-analytic support for reducing postoperative delirium, so the negative watchlist has to separate chronic disease use from surgical protection.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40359848-intranasal-insulin-postop-sleep-cardiac-surgery.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41146035-intranasal-insulin-hip-fracture-delirium.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41720686-intranasal-insulin-perioperative-delirium-meta.md]
- Rapamycin/rapalogs move a lot of biomarkers and remain popular in longevity circles, but the current human cognition branch is still flat enough to keep the compound here rather than in any positive ranker.^[raw/articles/pubmed-29408453-rapamycin-older-human-cohort-cognitive-effects.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40394335-rapamycin-adrd-phase1-open-label.md]^[raw/articles/medrxiv-2025-12-08-25340853-rapamycin-six-month-ad-pilot.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41046300-rapamycin-intermittent-pk-early-ad.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41524558-rapamycin-aging-cell-dna-damage-immune-resilience.md]
- AMPAkines now belong here too: the class keeps its primate-state-rescue credibility, but the human cognition bridge stayed weak, and the modern low-impact path is still PD/safety rather than efficacy.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40650226-ampa-kainate-modulators-cns-review.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-36153330-tak653-tms-cns-effects.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39302241-cx1739-safety-pharmacokinetics.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39892449-cx717-safety-pharmacokinetics.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-21940760-cx717-sleep-deprivation-human.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-17955941-cx717-night-shift-work.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-17487227-cx516-schizophrenia-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-17069542-cx516-fragile-x-controlled-trial.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-16104830-cx717-nonhuman-primate-sleep-deprivation.md]
- HBOT got worse, not better, as the evidence base matured: the sham-controlled TBI trial was null, the severe-TBI efficacy paper was later retracted, and adverse events are common enough to matter for any practical protocol.^[raw/articles/pubmed-26403017-hbot-tbi-sham-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-38217421-hbot-severe-tbi-retraction-notice.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-37275378-hbot-adverse-effects-meta.md]
- tPBM shows the same pattern in a recovery phenotype: the persistent-concussion RCT did not separate from placebo after adjustment, even though older-adult and MCI studies remain more encouraging.^[raw/articles/pubmed-38109202-tpbm-concussion-null-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40482941-tpbm-healthy-older-adults-working-memory-fnirs.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40939031-tpbm-mci-ad-sham-controlled.md]
- tDCS and tACS both look more phenotype- and parameter-sensitive than their average effect sizes imply; the older-adult tDCS meta-analysis loses its 1-month signal, and tACS does not separate from sham in healthy older adults.^[raw/articles/pubmed-38452749-tdcs-older-adults-mci-2024.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41034500-tacs-older-adults-meta-2025.md]
- tDCS also fails to add much when layered on top of cognitive stimulation in aMCI, which makes the combination story weaker than the standalone stimulation pitch.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40004217-tdcs-cognitive-stimulation-amci-null.md]
- Creatine is a reminder that domain wins do not equal global enhancement: memory and speed can improve while overall cognition and executive function remain non-significant.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39070254-creatine-cognition-adults-meta-analysis.md]
- The small-molecule failure library now exists as a companion pattern page, which keeps biomarker-positive / cognition-null examples separate from the branch-level null list.^[raw/articles/pubmed-31264943-donepezil-fragile-x-biomarker-null.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-20043816-sb742457-phase2-ad.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-33731209-sodium-oligomannate-phase3-china.md]

## Responder Hints

- `Insulin resistance` and metabolic dysfunction look like real enrichment candidates for intranasal insulin, because the T2DM and perfusion/connectivity studies consistently point in that direction.^[raw/articles/pubmed-35482079-memaid-intranasal-insulin-type2-diabetes-control-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40392946-intranasal-insulin-t2d-rsfc.md]
- `Sex` and `APOE` remain meaningful for intranasal insulin, especially in older MCI/AD cohorts, where the same nominal dose can behave differently across subgroups.^[raw/articles/pubmed-23507773-intranasal-insulin-sex-apoe-differences.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-25374101-intranasal-insulin-detemir-mci-ad.md]
- `Age` and vulnerability state matter for perioperative intranasal insulin, which looks more like neurocognitive protection than a generic nootropic.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39216470-intranasal-insulin-perioperative-cognitive-function-older-adults.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41091693-long-acting-intranasal-insulin-delirium-rct.md]
- `Disease state` is the main separator for tACS and tPBM: older adults with AD can differ from healthy older adults, and concussion recovery can differ from MCI.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41034500-tacs-older-adults-meta-2025.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-38109202-tpbm-concussion-null-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40939031-tpbm-mci-ad-sham-controlled.md]
- `Autonomic/stress load` may be the right enrichment axis for taVNS, but the current human stress RCT still shows a biomarker-behavior split rather than a clean cognitive win.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40908377-taVNS-mental-stressor.md]

## Evidence Tiering

- Phase 3 negative readout: semaglutide in early symptomatic AD and iclepertin in schizophrenia.
- Phase 2 adjunctive training null plus phase 3 negative readout: iclepertin.
- Proof-of-concept negative RCT: exenatide in MCI.
- Randomized healthy-volunteer null: taVNS under acute mental stress.
- Biomarker-positive but endpoint-negative: semaglutide AD, intranasal insulin CBF review, and HBOT TBI are the clearest examples.
- Recovery-phenotype null: tPBM in persistent postconcussion symptoms.
- Meta-analysis with durability null: tDCS in older adults/MCI.
- Meta-analysis with phenotype null: tACS in healthy older adults.
- Meta-analysis with domain-level null: creatine on overall cognition and executive function.
- Mixed disease-state evidence: intranasal insulin remains phenotype-sensitive rather than uniformly negative.

## Open Contradictions

- Biomarkers often move before cognition does, and sometimes cognition never follows.
- A negative primary endpoint can coexist with a positive secondary endpoint, so the page needs endpoint-level labeling rather than blanket "works" or "fails" language.
- Healthier cohorts often show weaker signals than disease or high-demand states, which argues for phenotype-specific rather than universal enhancer claims.
- The same branch can contain both a useful subdomain effect and a broad null, so the wiki should preserve both facts instead of flattening them.
- HBOT is handled separately in [[hbot-evidence-quality-review]] because comparator quality and retraction issues make it a different kind of interpretive problem.
