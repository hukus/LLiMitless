# Blood-Borne Rejuvenation and Plasma-Factor Cognition 2026 Scan

Related pages: [[exerkine-myokine-cognition-2026-scan]], [[msc-exosome-secretome-cognition-2026-scan]], [[klotho]], [[plasmalogen-and-lipid-membrane-replacement-2026-scan]], [[negative-evidence-watchlist-for-cognition-interventions]], and [[clinical-recovery-vs-healthy-enhancement-translation-map]].

## Summary

This branch is a recovery and disease-modification lane, not a healthy-adult nootropic lane. The current human evidence splits into two very different stories: a small young-plasma AD safety pilot that was feasible but did not show exploratory cognitive benefit, and the larger AMBAR plasma-exchange program that did show slower functional decline and a trend on cognition, with the strongest signal in moderate AD.^[raw/articles/pubmed-30383097-young-plasma-infusion-alzheimers-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-32715623-ambar-primary-results.md]

The biomarker follow-up strengthens the mechanism story without fixing the endpoint ambiguity: albumin-replacement plasma exchange shifts peripheral and CSF inflammatory mediators, and some mediator changes track with clinical trajectories.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39476248-ambar-inflammatory-mediator-levels.md]

[[pf4]], [[clusterin]], and [[gpld1]] remain the cleanest exercise-to-brain transfer factors in the mouse literature, but they are still preclinical transfer stories rather than validated human dosing strategies. [[gpld1]] now has a 2026 TNAP / BBB mechanism that makes the liver-to-brain pathway more concrete without converting it into human efficacy evidence.^[raw/articles/nature-platelet-derived-exerkine-cxcl4-platelet-factor-4-rejuvenates-hippocampal-neurogenesis-and-restores-cognitive-function-in-aged-mice.md]^[raw/articles/nature-exercise-plasma-boosts-memory-and-dampens-brain-inflammation-via-clusterin.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-32646997-gpld1-exercise-neurogenesis-cognition.md]^[raw/articles/sciencedirect-2026-gpld1-tnap-memory-loss-vasculature.md]

[[gdf11]] sits in the older young-blood / rejuvenation narrative rather than the exercise-transfer sub-branch. It has multiple mouse cognition, vascular, mood-memory, and neuronal-senescence signals, but the translation is especially controversy-sensitive because GDF11/GDF8 biology, assay specificity, recombinant-protein quality, dose, and high-dose cachexia risk all affect interpretation.^[raw/articles/pubmed-30470794-gdf11-hippocampus-aged-mice.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-29480172-gdf11-ad-cerebrovascular-cognition.md]^[raw/articles/nature-aging-2023-systemic-gdf11-memory-mood-autophagy.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-37978295-gdf11-neuronal-senescence-brain-aging-p21.md]^[raw/articles/pmc-10793994-gdf11-aging-biology-controversies.md]

The 2025 EV aging review is a useful cautionary overlay: EV cargo looks biologically meaningful, but context dependence, method heterogeneity, and reproducibility problems remain major blockers to transportable claims.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40202045-extracellular-vesicles-aging-age-related-diseases-review.md]

The right wiki interpretation is that circulating-factor biology is real, plasma exchange in AD is not fantasy, and the branch still falls short of a clean, reproducible enhancer claim. ^[raw/articles/pubmed-32715623-ambar-primary-results.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39476248-ambar-inflammatory-mediator-levels.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40202045-extracellular-vesicles-aging-age-related-diseases-review.md]

## What Changed Since 2024

- The AMBAR line now has a clearer split between clinical signal and mechanism: the primary trial suggests slower decline, while the 2024 biomarker follow-up shows actual inflammatory mediator shifts in serum and CSF.^[raw/articles/pubmed-32715623-ambar-primary-results.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39476248-ambar-inflammatory-mediator-levels.md]
- The young-plasma safety pilot remains only a feasibility signal, not efficacy proof, and it still does not justify consumer-style "young blood" claims.^[raw/articles/pubmed-30383097-young-plasma-infusion-alzheimers-rct.md]
- EV/plasma-factor reviews in 2025 emphasize the same limitation from a different angle: biomarker plausibility is outpacing reproducible intervention evidence.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40202045-extracellular-vesicles-aging-age-related-diseases-review.md]
- The existing exercise-factor literature still belongs on the neighboring [[exerkine-myokine-cognition-2026-scan]] page, not inside this branch, because [[pf4]], [[clusterin]], and [[gpld1]] are transfer-factor biology rather than direct plasma-therapy evidence.^[raw/articles/nature-platelet-derived-exerkine-cxcl4-platelet-factor-4-rejuvenates-hippocampal-neurogenesis-and-restores-cognitive-function-in-aged-mice.md]^[raw/articles/nature-exercise-plasma-boosts-memory-and-dampens-brain-inflammation-via-clusterin.md]^[raw/articles/sciencedirect-2026-gpld1-tnap-memory-loss-vasculature.md]
- [[gdf11]] now has a standalone caution page because it is both one of the most cognition-relevant plasma-factor candidates in mice and one of the easiest to overstate from rejuvenation framing.^[raw/articles/pmc-10793994-gdf11-aging-biology-controversies.md]

## Evidence Tiering

| Tier | Best-supported claim | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| Human safety pilot | Young plasma in mild to moderate AD was feasible and had no related serious adverse events | Safety/feasibility only; no efficacy win |
| Human randomized trial | AMBAR slowed ADCS-ADL decline and showed a cognition trend, with stronger signal in moderate AD | Real but phenotype-sensitive and still not a general enhancer |
| Human biomarker follow-up | Plasma exchange changed serum and CSF inflammatory mediators | Mechanism support, not a new treatment result |
| Human review / umbrella | EVs in aging are important but heterogeneous and hard to standardize | Useful caution, not treatment proof |
| Animal transfer / rejuvenation biology | [[pf4]], [[clusterin]], [[gpld1]], and [[gdf11]] can transfer, mimic, or reproduce parts of young-blood/exercise-like brain effects in mice | Hypothesis generation only |

## Effect Size Snapshot

- Young plasma: no statistically significant change in global cognition, mood, or default-mode connectivity; caregiver-reported functional ability looked better, but the study was underpowered.^[raw/articles/pubmed-30383097-young-plasma-infusion-alzheimers-rct.md]
- AMBAR: ADCS-ADL improved relative to placebo by 3.5 points at month 14, with a trend on ADAS-Cog of about 2.1 points and stronger moderate-AD subgroup effects.^[raw/articles/pubmed-32715623-ambar-primary-results.md]
- Global outcomes: CDR-SB and ADCS-CGIC also favored treatment in AMBAR.^[raw/articles/pubmed-32715623-ambar-primary-results.md]
- Biomarker follow-up: PE-Alb changed a cluster of inflammatory mediators, but that does not convert the branch into a biomarker-first success story by itself.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39476248-ambar-inflammatory-mediator-levels.md]
- No human effect size exists yet for direct [[pf4]], [[clusterin]], [[gpld1]], or EV administration as cognition interventions.^[raw/articles/nature-platelet-derived-exerkine-cxcl4-platelet-factor-4-rejuvenates-hippocampal-neurogenesis-and-restores-cognitive-function-in-aged-mice.md]^[raw/articles/nature-exercise-plasma-boosts-memory-and-dampens-brain-inflammation-via-clusterin.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-32646997-gpld1-exercise-neurogenesis-cognition.md]^[raw/articles/sciencedirect-2026-gpld1-tnap-memory-loss-vasculature.md]
- [[gdf11]] has no human cognition dosing effect size; its strongest direct cognition effect sizes are mouse memory-task changes and AD-model rescue.^[raw/articles/pubmed-29480172-gdf11-ad-cerebrovascular-cognition.md]^[raw/articles/nature-aging-2023-systemic-gdf11-memory-mood-autophagy.md]

## Safety / Adverse Events

- Young plasma had no related serious adverse events, but common infusion-related AEs included hypertension, dizziness, sinus bradycardia, headache, and sinus tachycardia.^[raw/articles/pubmed-30383097-young-plasma-infusion-alzheimers-rct.md]
- AMBAR had a meaningful procedural burden, with more adverse events in central-access procedures than peripheral access and a nontrivial dropout rate in active arms.^[raw/articles/pubmed-32715623-ambar-primary-results.md]
- The biomarker follow-up reinforces that the safety story is inseparable from apheresis logistics and invasive monitoring.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39476248-ambar-inflammatory-mediator-levels.md]
- EV and exosome commercialization still needs the separate regulatory and sourcing checks already used in the regenerative-medicine branch. [[msc-exosome-secretome-cognition-2026-scan]]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40202045-extracellular-vesicles-aging-age-related-diseases-review.md]
- [[gdf11]] high-dose and protein-quality issues mean it should be safety-first, not "replacement therapy" by default.^[raw/articles/pmc-10793994-gdf11-aging-biology-controversies.md]

## Responder Phenotypes

- Moderate AD looks more promising than mild AD for AMBAR-style plasma exchange.^[raw/articles/pubmed-32715623-ambar-primary-results.md]
- Mild-to-moderate AD is the only human young-plasma population tested so far, and it did not produce a clear efficacy signal.^[raw/articles/pubmed-30383097-young-plasma-infusion-alzheimers-rct.md]
- The branch may be more useful in recovery or inflammatory-stress states than in healthy enhancement, but that is still an inference from the current evidence rather than a proven effect modifier.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39476248-ambar-inflammatory-mediator-levels.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40202045-extracellular-vesicles-aging-age-related-diseases-review.md]

## Protocol Parameters

- Young plasma trial: 250 mL yFFP from donors age 18 to 30, infused once weekly for 4 weeks.^[raw/articles/pubmed-30383097-young-plasma-infusion-alzheimers-rct.md]
- AMBAR primary trial: 6 weeks of weekly conventional plasma exchange, followed by 12 months of monthly low-volume plasma exchange with albumin and/or IVIG replacement.^[raw/articles/pubmed-32715623-ambar-primary-results.md]
- AMBAR biomarker follow-up: serum and CSF sampling across the 14-month schedule, with 19 inflammatory mediators tracked.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39476248-ambar-inflammatory-mediator-levels.md]
- No standardized human protocol exists for direct [[pf4]], [[clusterin]], [[gpld1]], or EV administration as a cognition intervention.^[raw/articles/nature-platelet-derived-exerkine-cxcl4-platelet-factor-4-rejuvenates-hippocampal-neurogenesis-and-restores-cognitive-function-in-aged-mice.md]^[raw/articles/nature-exercise-plasma-boosts-memory-and-dampens-brain-inflammation-via-clusterin.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-32646997-gpld1-exercise-neurogenesis-cognition.md]^[raw/articles/sciencedirect-2026-gpld1-tnap-memory-loss-vasculature.md]
- [[gdf11]] mouse protocols include systemic delivery in aged mice and daily intravenous dosing in AD-model mice, but those are not human protocols.^[raw/articles/pubmed-30470794-gdf11-hippocampus-aged-mice.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-29480172-gdf11-ad-cerebrovascular-cognition.md]

## Open Contradictions

- The young-plasma pilot is safe and feasible, but it does not show a clear cognition win.^[raw/articles/pubmed-30383097-young-plasma-infusion-alzheimers-rct.md]
- AMBAR is more promising, but the signal is phenotype-sensitive, cognitively mixed, and procedurally invasive.^[raw/articles/pubmed-32715623-ambar-primary-results.md]
- Mechanistic biomarker movement does not settle whether the active ingredient is a removed toxic factor, a replenished beneficial factor, or the exchange procedure itself.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39476248-ambar-inflammatory-mediator-levels.md]
- The TODO entry cites PMID 31117671 for the plasma-exchange item, but the actual AD plasma-exchange primary results paper is PMID 32715623; PMID 31117671 is not the relevant branch anchor.^[raw/articles/pubmed-32715623-ambar-primary-results.md]
- The [[pf4]] and [[clusterin]] branch anchors should be normalized to PMID 37587147 and PMID 34880498, respectively; the TODO placeholders 37029840 and 33536270 point elsewhere.^[raw/articles/nature-platelet-derived-exerkine-cxcl4-platelet-factor-4-rejuvenates-hippocampal-neurogenesis-and-restores-cognitive-function-in-aged-mice.md]^[raw/articles/nature-exercise-plasma-boosts-memory-and-dampens-brain-inflammation-via-clusterin.md]
- [[pf4]], [[clusterin]], and [[gpld1]] are compelling in mice, but that does not validate a human injection, expression, TNAP-inhibition, or transfusion strategy.^[raw/articles/nature-platelet-derived-exerkine-cxcl4-platelet-factor-4-rejuvenates-hippocampal-neurogenesis-and-restores-cognitive-function-in-aged-mice.md]^[raw/articles/nature-exercise-plasma-boosts-memory-and-dampens-brain-inflammation-via-clusterin.md]^[raw/articles/sciencedirect-2026-gpld1-tnap-memory-loss-vasculature.md]
- [[gdf11]] is more contested than [[pf4]] or [[clusterin]] because broader aging-biology claims depend on assay specificity, GDF8 separation, recombinant-protein quality, and dose.^[raw/articles/pmc-10793994-gdf11-aging-biology-controversies.md]
