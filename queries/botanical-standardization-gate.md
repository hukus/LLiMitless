# Botanical Standardization Gate for Cognition

This page asks a narrow question: which traditional-medicine candidates are specific and reproducible enough to deserve a standalone cognition page instead of staying trapped in a broad "herbal nootropic" bucket.

## Gate Criteria

- Named product or extract, not a generic herb label.
- Fixed dose and duration.
- Some marker or chemistry control that makes the product transportable.
- A human cognition endpoint, not just a biomarker or a subjective wellness claim.
- At least some replication or a clearly durable product-specific signal.

## Verdict

The wiki already has justified standalone pages for `ginkgo`, `ginseng`, and `curcumin`. The cleanest next split candidate is `[[aronia-melanocarpa-extract]]`: it now has two product-specific cognition RCTs, both standardized, both small, and both pointing to the same narrow psychomotor-speed / reaction-time lane. `grape extract` and standardized `ashwagandha` remain close behind, but both are still thin on independent replication. `pomegranate` now has both a juice-memory trial and a newer extract trial, which makes it interesting, but the product identity is still split enough that it belongs in the comparison layer for now. `phlai-essential-oil-blend` is a near-pass because it now has two small human trials plus GC-MS characterization, but it remains route-specific and acute. `bacopa` and `saffron` are real cognition branches, yet they still read better as comparison-page entries than as separate pages. `Acacia + Scutellaria` is a useful weak/null comparator, and formula-level Chinese herbal medicine should stay separate from the single-extract gate because the transportable unit is the formula, not the herb class.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39335927-grape-extract-cognition-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40099725-ashwagandha-mci-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-32824483-aronia-melanocarpa-extract-cognition-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-38656355-aronia-melanocarpa-extract-cognition-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-31711104-pomegranate-juice-memory-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39997528-pomegranate-extract-cognition-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-20703343-bacopa-aami-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-29185780-saffron-cabg-cognition-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39701593-acacia-scutellaria-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-38425647-chinese-herbal-medicine-mci-meta-analysis.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39134455-chinese-herbal-medicine-donepezil-mci-meta-analysis.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-31618177-phlai-mood-stress-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41614069-phlai-cognition-rct.md]

## Candidate Triage

- `[[aronia-melanocarpa-extract]]`: near-pass to pass. Two small cognition RCTs now exist, with the same extract family and a repeat psychomotor-speed signal, but the endpoint scope is still narrow.
- `[[phlai-essential-oil-blend]]`: near-pass. Two human trials now exist, the exposure is chemically characterized, and the effect is not just folklore, but the route is inhaled and the cognition signal is still acute.
- `Ginkgo EGb 761`: pass. It has a standardized extract, dose clarity, multiple human studies, and safety visibility; it already deserves its own page.^[raw/articles/pubmed-27147264-ginkgo-egb761-smi-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39895346-ginkgo-egb761-mild-dementia-meta-analysis.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40121884-ginkgo-egb761-overview-systematic-reviews.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39476880-ginkgo-faers-safety.md]
- `Ginseng`: pass. The sprout-extract RCT plus the newer review package make this a product-specific branch rather than folklore.^[raw/articles/pubmed-38931306-panax-ginseng-sprout-extract-smi-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41863264-ginseng-cognitive-function-meta-analysis.md]^[raw/articles/springer-2026-panax-ginseng-cognitive-function-systematic-review.md]
- `Curcumin`: pass, but only as a formulation-sensitive branch. The page belongs to the standardized product or formulation family, not to the turmeric label in general.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40308636-curcumin-cognitive-function-meta-analysis.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-23107780-curcumin-ad-rct.md]
- `Grape extract`: near-pass. The named product and dose are clean, but the human cognition base is still basically one strong RCT.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39335927-grape-extract-cognition-rct.md]
- `Ashwagandha`: near-pass. The MCI pilot is promising and standardized, but replication is still thin and the registry branch remains pending.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40099725-ashwagandha-mci-rct.md]^[raw/articles/ctgov-nct04092647-ashwagandha-cognitive-dysfunction.md]
- `Pomegranate`: hold for now. The juice and extract studies are both interesting, but they point to a product family rather than one transportable exposure.
- `Bacopa standardized extract`: hold. The standardized extract is real, but the modern replication base is sparse and much of the interpretation still hinges on older work and bioavailability.^[raw/articles/pubmed-20703343-bacopa-aami-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40507208-bacopa-monnieri-preclinical-clinical-bioavailability.md]
- `Saffron`: hold. The signal is phenotype-specific and recovery-oriented, which makes it useful but not yet broad enough to split out as a general cognition page.^[raw/articles/pubmed-29185780-saffron-cabg-cognition-rct.md]
- `Acacia + Scutellaria`: do not split. It is standardized, but the placebo-separated cognition signal is weak enough that it works better as a null comparator than as a standalone branch.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39701593-acacia-scutellaria-rct.md]
- `Chinese herbal medicine`: keep separate from single-extract pages. The meta-analytic signal is interesting, but formula heterogeneity means this is a different unit of analysis.^[raw/articles/pubmed-38425647-chinese-herbal-medicine-mci-meta-analysis.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39134455-chinese-herbal-medicine-donepezil-mci-meta-analysis.md]

## What This Means

- For transportable knowledge, product identity matters more than the traditional label.
- For future page splits, `Aronia melanocarpa extract` is now the clearest candidate because two small RCTs in different age bands converged on speed / reaction-time endpoints. `Grape extract` looks stronger on effect size but still has only one clean recent RCT, while standardized `ashwagandha` is the next closest candidate if more independent replication arrives.
- For navigation, `pomegranate`, `bacopa`, `saffron`, and `Acacia + Scutellaria` belong in the comparison layer for now.
