# Sodium Oligomannate / GV-971 2026 Scan

## Summary

GV-971 is a marine-derived sodium oligomannate positioned as an Alzheimer's disease gut-brain-axis drug, not a healthy-adult cognitive enhancer. The best positive result is a China phase 3 trial in mild-to-moderate Alzheimer's dementia that reported a week-36 ADAS-Cog12 drug-placebo difference of -2.15 points and comparable adverse-event incidence. That signal is clinically relevant if it replicates, but it remains geography-bound and sponsor-linked. ^[raw/articles/pubmed-33731209-sodium-oligomannate-phase3-china.md]

The earlier phase 2 trial was mixed: the 900 mg/day arm did not significantly beat placebo on the primary ADAS-Cog12 endpoint at 24 weeks, though a CIBIC-Plus responder comparison and uncorrected FDG-PET subgroup signals favored the higher dose. That makes the later phase 3 result more interesting, but also more contested rather than cleanly cumulative. ^[raw/articles/pubmed-32928279-sodium-oligomannate-phase2.md]

The global replication layer is unresolved. GREEN MEMORY was designed as a 2046-participant global phase 3 trial, but the ClinicalTrials.gov record lists the study as suspended with no posted results, and a 2022 sponsor announcement says the global program was terminated early after 439 randomizations because of COVID-19, financing, dropout, and trial-quality concerns. The same announcement reports no DSMB safety concern at the 10% enrollment milestone, but that is not an efficacy result. ^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct04520412-gv971-green-memory-global.md]^[raw/articles/greenvalley-2022-gv971-global-phase3-termination.md]

The current wiki read: GV-971 belongs in a `geography-limited, contested gut-brain drug` lane. It is more serious than a generic microbiome supplement, but it should not upgrade the broader [[gut-microbiome-and-cognition]] branch until an independent or global efficacy result appears.

## What Changed Since 2024

- The live-status issue is now the main question, not the original China phase 3 headline: GREEN MEMORY remains the key global replication record, but it has no posted efficacy results in the registry source checked here. ^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct04520412-gv971-green-memory-global.md]
- The China postmarketing safety registry now has actual enrollment of 2500 and remains active-not-recruiting in the registry record, but public results were not posted in the record I checked. ^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct05058040-gv971-postmarketing-safety.md]
- The wiki now needs a geography/status field for drug-like gut-brain candidates: `approved in China`, `investigational or unavailable elsewhere`, `global confirmatory data missing`, and `postmarketing safety pending`.

## Evidence Tiering

- `Human RCT positive`: China phase 3, 36 weeks, ADAS-Cog12 positive.
- `Human RCT mixed`: China phase 2, primary ADAS-Cog12 not significant but secondary/global and FDG-PET subgroup signals favored 900 mg/day.
- `Human safety/registry`: China phase 4 long-term safety registry, no public results posted in this sweep.
- `Human mechanism`: small biomarker comparisons inside the mechanism paper.
- `Preclinical mechanism`: gut microbiota remodeling, phenylalanine/isoleucine, Th1 immune activation, and neuroinflammation model.
- `Unresolved replication`: global phase 3 suspended/no posted results; sponsor says early termination.

## Effect Snapshot

- Phase 3 ADAS-Cog12: -2.15 drug-placebo difference at week 36, 95% CI -3.07 to -1.23, p < 0.0001, effect size 0.531. ^[raw/articles/pubmed-33731209-sodium-oligomannate-phase3-china.md]
- Phase 2 ADAS-Cog12: placebo -1.45, GV-971 600 mg -1.39 (p = 0.89), GV-971 900 mg -2.58 (p = 0.30); no significant primary endpoint win. ^[raw/articles/pubmed-32928279-sodium-oligomannate-phase2.md]
- Phase 2 CIBIC-Plus responder signal: 900 mg 92.77% vs placebo 79.52%, p < 0.05. ^[raw/articles/pubmed-32928279-sodium-oligomannate-phase2.md]

## Safety And Status

- The phase 3 trial reported comparable treatment-emergent adverse-event incidence between GV-971 and placebo. ^[raw/articles/pubmed-33731209-sodium-oligomannate-phase3-china.md]
- The postmarketing registry is designed specifically because completed clinical studies had limited numbers, limited observation periods, and strict inclusion/exclusion criteria. ^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct05058040-gv971-postmarketing-safety.md]
- The sponsor termination note says the DSMB did not identify safety risks at the 10% enrollment milestone of the global phase 3 trial, but this is a sponsor announcement and should not be treated as a peer-reviewed safety result. ^[raw/articles/greenvalley-2022-gv971-global-phase3-termination.md]

## Open Contradictions

- The phase 2 primary cognitive endpoint was not significant, while the phase 3 primary cognitive endpoint was strongly positive.
- The proposed gut-brain mechanism is coherent and drug-specific, but most of the mechanistic weight is preclinical or small biomarker work.
- GV-971 is approved and marketed in China, while global replication remains incomplete or unavailable.
- The global trial record says suspended, while the sponsor announcement says terminated early; either way, the current public record does not provide a completed global efficacy readout.
