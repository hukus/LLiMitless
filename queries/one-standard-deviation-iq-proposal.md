# One Standard Deviation IQ Improvement Proposal

## Verdict

The most plausible way to improve healthy adults' IQ by one standard deviation is not a nootropic, peptide, or device stack. It is a psychometric skill-acquisition protocol that targets measured IQ-test performance while keeping the stronger claim, improvement in latent general intelligence, explicitly unproven.

If the target is a professionally defensible claim that latent `g` rose by 15 IQ points, the wiki does not currently support any method. Existing healthy-adult nootropic and stimulation effects are too small, too state-bound, or too task-specific. If the target is a 15-point increase on a measured IQ score, the best route is a deliberately trained, parallel-form, assessment-aware program: repeated exposure, explicit matrix and verbal-reasoning strategy instruction, adaptive item generation, speed-accuracy calibration, education-like knowledge acquisition, and strict sleep/state control.^[raw/articles/sciencedirect-2018-retest-effects-cognitive-ability-tests-meta-analysis.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-25395695-fluid-intelligence-score-gains-strategy.md]^[raw/articles/pmc-6088505-education-improves-intelligence-meta-analysis.md]

## Proposed Method: Parallel-Form IQ Skill Acquisition Protocol

Target population: healthy adults with stable sleep, no acute psychiatric or neurologic disorder, and no current sleep-debt or stimulant-withdrawal confound.

Target claim: raise measured IQ score by at least one standard deviation on a preselected, parallel-form cognitive-ability battery over 6 to 12 months. The claim must be reported as `measured IQ-score improvement` unless it survives the transfer tests below.

### Core Intervention

| Layer | Protocol | Why it is included |
|---|---|---|
| Baseline stabilization | Two weeks of fixed sleep/wake timing, fixed caffeine timing, and familiarization with the measurement battery without scoring the run-in | Practice effects are large enough to distort short-block cognition studies, and sleep/caffeine state can swamp small intervention signals.^[raw/articles/pubmed-20846444-practice-effects-frequent-repetitive-cognitive-testing.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-16840238-practice-effects-cogstate-battery-test-retest-intervals.md] |
| IQ-test strategy instruction | Teach rule taxonomies for matrices, analogies, number/letter series, mental rotation, symbol search, working-memory chunking, and test-time triage | Explicit strategy training can raise fluid-intelligence-style matrix scores without proving a general intelligence gain.^[raw/articles/pubmed-25395695-fluid-intelligence-score-gains-strategy.md] |
| Adaptive parallel-form practice | Generate or select novel items that share deep rules but not surface content with the final battery; interleave domains and difficulty | Retest and task-familiarity gains can approach a large fraction of one SD, but exact-item memorization would invalidate the claim.^[raw/articles/sciencedirect-2018-retest-effects-cognitive-ability-tests-meta-analysis.md] |
| Education-like cognitive load | Daily quantitative reasoning, statistics, logic, vocabulary, reading-comprehension, and writing practice, 5 to 8 h/week minimum; stronger version is a formal degree or equivalent curriculum | Education is the strongest durable human lever for intelligence-like scores, but its effect is measured in years, not days.^[raw/articles/pmc-6088505-education-improves-intelligence-meta-analysis.md] |
| Transfer forcing | Weekly untrained tasks from a different item family: novel matrices, spatial reasoning, verbal analogies, timed symbol coding, and delayed recall | Working-memory and inhibition training often transfer near the trained task but not broadly, so transfer must be engineered and measured rather than assumed.^[raw/articles/pubmed-27474138-working-memory-training-far-transfer-meta-analysis.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-37948381-near-transfer-inhibition-training-rct.md] |
| State support | Exercise, sleep regularization, and optionally creatine monohydrate when diet/load makes it plausible; avoid starting experimental drugs or peptides | Healthy-adult chemical/device effects look incremental and state-bound; use them as noise reduction, not as the main IQ lever.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39070254-creatine-cognition-adults-meta-analysis.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-38418482-creatine-sleep-deprivation-cognition.md] |

## Expected Effect Budget

| Component | Plausible contribution to measured IQ score | Confidence | Caveat |
|---|---|---|---|
| Retest / test-familiarity effect | Up to about 0.5 SD by later administrations in some cognitive-ability settings | Medium | This is score inflation unless it transfers to held-out forms and domains.^[raw/articles/sciencedirect-2018-retest-effects-cognitive-ability-tests-meta-analysis.md] |
| Explicit strategy training | Around 0.5 SD on a matrix-like trained outcome in one strategy-training study | Low-medium | Strong for the task family, weak as evidence of `g` improvement.^[raw/articles/pubmed-25395695-fluid-intelligence-score-gains-strategy.md] |
| Education-like curriculum | Roughly 1-5 IQ points per additional education year in quasi-experimental meta-analysis | Medium for years-long exposure, low for short bootcamp | Slowest but most credible broad lever.^[raw/articles/pmc-6088505-education-improves-intelligence-meta-analysis.md] |
| Working-memory / brain-game training | Large trained-task gains, moderate near transfer, small far transfer in newer meta-analysis | Medium | Not a standalone route to one SD broad IQ improvement.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40581145-cogmed-cognitive-training-meta-analysis.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-27474138-working-memory-training-far-transfer-meta-analysis.md] |
| Sleep, exercise, creatine, caffeine control | Small-to-moderate state correction where a bottleneck exists | Medium | Helps prevent underperformance more than it creates a new 15-point ability gain. |

The most honest forecast is therefore split: one SD on a selected measured IQ score is plausible if the protocol is allowed to train the score ecology; one SD on latent general intelligence is not currently credible.

## Decentralized Evidence Layer

Anecdotal evidence changes the frontier search strategy, but not the claim labels. The nootropic and biohacker community is unusually good at surfacing noticeable state changes, tolerance problems, route/dose sensitivity, and candidates that formal trials have not yet prioritized. It is much weaker at separating attention, mood, withdrawal relief, practice effects, and true intelligence gains. The practical rule for this proposal is therefore: use decentralized evidence to choose support layers and failure modes, but require held-out transfer before calling any result an IQ improvement.^[raw/articles/troof-nootropic-ratings-2022.md]^[raw/articles/troof-nootropics-ratings-analysis.md]^[raw/articles/astralcodexten-2020-nootropics-survey-results.md]

## How Anecdote Changes The Proposal

The active protocol should keep the psychometric training core, but add an anecdote-informed frontier module:

1. Track community-salient candidates as `state aids`, not as IQ mechanisms.
2. Allow only one support variable per block, with dechallenge/rechallenge and stop rules.
3. Prioritize low-friction support with plausible safety: sleep stabilization, light timing, exercise, and creatine.
4. Treat high-salience chemicals and peptides as research leads for separate trials, not as part of the first-pass one-SD method.
5. Include subjective "felt smarter" and productivity ratings, but do not let them count toward IQ success unless held-out far-transfer endpoints move.
6. Add tolerance, rebound, insomnia, anxiety, BP/HR, and next-day sleep to the daily log, because community evidence says these are where many apparent gains fail.

The decentralized movement's main contribution is not proof that a molecule raises IQ. It is a better search prior: people notice acute state shifts, tolerance, stack interactions, and subgroups before slow formal research does. The protocol should respect that by measuring those signals, not by pretending they are already validated intelligence gains.

## Measurement Rules

1. Use three score layers: `trained-task score`, `near-transfer IQ-style score`, and `far-transfer broad battery`.
2. Do not use exact final-test items in training.
3. Use parallel forms and hold out the final form until post-test.
4. Score baseline only after at least one familiarization run, because first-to-second administration gains can be large.
5. Report raw gain and practice-adjusted gain separately.
6. Require 3-month and 12-month follow-up before calling the gain durable.
7. If only the trained or near-transfer battery moves, label the result `IQ-score acquisition`, not `general intelligence enhancement`.

## Research Trial Design

| Arm | Description | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Active protocol | Full parallel-form IQ skill acquisition plus education-like curriculum and state stabilization | Test the proposed method |
| Active education control | Same time-on-task with non-IQ academic learning, reading, and problem solving | Separates education/time effects from IQ-specific strategy practice |
| Active brain-game control | Adaptive games matched for engagement and reward but not aligned to IQ subtest rules | Separates motivation/engagement from psychometric skill acquisition |
| Waitlist or minimal-contact control | Retest-only with measurement run-in | Estimates ordinary retest effects |

Primary endpoint: latent factor change on a held-out, broad cognitive-ability battery at 6 months.

Secondary endpoints: chosen IQ battery score, matrix reasoning, vocabulary, working memory, processing speed, delayed recall, task switching, PVT, sleep, caffeine adherence, and 3- and 12-month durability.

Success definition:

- `Measured-IQ success`: at least 15 IQ points on the chosen held-out IQ-style battery.
- `Broad-transfer success`: at least 0.5 SD on an untrained latent cognitive factor.
- `True one-SD success`: at least 1.0 SD on an untrained latent factor, sustained at follow-up. This is the hardest target and should be treated as unlikely until shown.

## Practical Proposal

If the goal is to build the highest-probability method now, implement the active protocol as a 6-month curriculum:

1. Weeks 1-2: stabilize sleep/caffeine and complete non-scored familiarization.
2. Weeks 3-10: intensive strategy instruction across matrices, analogies, series, spatial rotation, symbol coding, and working-memory chunking.
3. Weeks 11-20: adaptive parallel-form practice with interleaving, error classification, and speed-accuracy calibration.
4. Weeks 21-24: taper direct IQ-style practice, increase far-transfer academic problem solving, and run held-out transfer probes.
5. Week 24: final held-out IQ-style and broad-transfer assessment.
6. Months 3 and 12 after completion: durability testing.

Use sleep/circadian repair, exercise, and creatine only as support layers. Do not make peptides, research chemicals, tFUS, rTMS, tDCS/tACS, taVNS, or tPBM the core method unless the target changes from healthy-adult IQ to a narrower state-rescue or clinical-recovery endpoint.

## Bottom Line

The proposal is a test-score skill acquisition method, not a biological IQ enhancer. It is the only route in the current wiki that has a plausible path to a one-standard-deviation measured IQ gain in healthy adults. The stronger claim, one SD improvement in general intelligence, remains unsupported and should be treated as the trial's stretch endpoint rather than the expected result.
