# Target-Specificity Gate for Plasticity Molecules

## Summary

Yes, the wiki needs a reusable target-specificity gate before any plasticity molecule is promoted. The TrkB branch already shows why: one paper supports direct [[7-8-dihydroxyflavone|7,8-DHF]] binding to the TrkB extracellular domain with prolonged phosphorylation, while later caution and assay-limitations literature says side-by-side comparisons did not preserve specificity cleanly and recommends better candidates and validation. [[zeb85]] then becomes the useful counterexample because the antibody lane is explicitly framed as a more specific TrkB strategy than the older small-molecule claims.^[raw/articles/pubmed-25143381-7-8-dihydroxyflavone-binding-activation-trkb-receptor.md]^[raw/articles/pmc-8398766-small-molecule-trkb-agonist-limitations.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-37488840-trkb-agonist-caution-review.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-35845610-zeb85-trkb-agonist-antibody.md]

The practical conclusion is not that TrkB is dead. It is that the wiki should not treat "plasticity signal" as enough. Direct receptor engagement, orthogonal assay support, and specificity against plausible off-target explanations need to be visible before a branch moves from mechanistic comparator into promotion territory.^[raw/articles/pubmed-25143381-7-8-dihydroxyflavone-binding-activation-trkb-receptor.md]^[raw/articles/pmc-8398766-small-molecule-trkb-agonist-limitations.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-37488840-trkb-agonist-caution-review.md]

## Gate Criteria

| Criterion | Minimum bar | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Direct engagement | Binding, receptor activation, or a biologically credible surrogate in at least one orthogonal assay | Prevents downstream-only claims from being overread |
| Specificity | Evidence that the effect is not just generic neuroprotection, antioxidant action, or assay artifact | The TrkB literature already has specificity disputes |
| Replication style | Independent or side-by-side confirmation, not a single favorable assay | The caution review specifically flags comparative failures |
| Translation layer | Human exposure, registry activity, or at least a clear reason the molecule cannot yet be promoted | Keeps preclinical tools from being mislabeled as enhancers |
| Branch separation | Small molecule, prodrug, and antibody lanes stay distinct | [[r13]] and [[zeb85]] are related, but not interchangeable |
| Structural target and route | For ECM/PNN tools, show region, route, enzyme/antibody chemistry, and whether the target stabilizes mature circuits | [[chondroitinase-abc]] and PNN manipulation are local, preclinical, and safety-gated rather than systemic nootropics |

## How This Applies

- [[7-8-dihydroxyflavone|7,8-DHF]] can stay in the branch as the mechanistic anchor, but the binding paper and the caution review should be read together rather than as one-sided validation.
- [[r13]] belongs in a prodrug / druggability lane, not as proof that the parent small molecule solved specificity.
- [[zeb85]] is the best currently named TrkB-specific comparator in this branch because it is biologic rather than small-molecule and is presented as the answer to the specificity problem.
- [[lm22a-4]] and [[deoxygedunin]] remain comparator chemistry until they clear the same gate.
- [[chondroitinase-abc]] belongs in a separate [[perineuronal-net-extracellular-matrix-plasticity-gate|PNN/ECM gate]] because target specificity means brain region, extracellular-matrix chemistry, route, and mature-circuit stability, not just downstream plasticity.
