# Biomarker-Enriched Enrollment and Cognition Effect Sizes

## Summary

The current evidence says biomarker-enriched enrollment can sharpen a cognition signal, but it does not yet prove a universal effect-size multiplier. The best recent examples are the 2025 SUPERBRAIN-MEET reanalysis, where APOE ε4 and polygenic risk modified response to a multidomain intervention in MCI, and the galantamine responder papers, where basal forebrain volume and acute cholinergic challenge predicted better cognitive response in small samples. That sits alongside a 2025 APOE ε4-carrier trial in subjective cognitive decline, where the prespecified primary endpoint was still null, a 2025 exercise meta-analysis showing mixed APOE4 interaction results with very low to moderate certainty, and a 2026 foundation-model biomarker paper warning that a high-performing model can still produce unstable or biologically unfaithful markers. Digital phenotype and wearable physiology are now useful for state enrichment and near-term symptom prediction, but they still do not constitute a cognition-specific effect-size multiplier on their own. Morphology-based tDCS modeling and test-anxiety phenotype effects strengthen the personalization argument, but they still read as selection or timing tools rather than general efficacy multipliers.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41388319-polygenicity-apoe-e4-shape-response-intervention-mci.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40664536-pensa-apoe-e4-scd-egcg-multimodal-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39828710-apoe4-exercise-systematic-review-meta-analysis.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-33842683-galantamine-basal-forebrain-predictor.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-34964149-galantamine-cholinergic-challenge-response-predictor.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41876851-digital-physiological-biomarkers-predict-within-person-symptom-changes-complex-chronic-illness.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39706190-digital-phenotyping-wearables-ai-psychiatric-disorders-genetic-associations.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41909060-terminological-clarity-digital-biomarker-research.md]^[raw/papers/arxiv-2604.22018v1-foundation-models-for-discovering-robust-biomarkers-of-neuro.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40564472-anatomical-characteristics-predict-response-tdcs-computational-pipeline.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41633263-test-anxiety-tdcs-erp.md]

What changed since 2024:

- The SUPERBRAIN-MEET reanalysis added a direct example of genotype-sensitive intervention response in MCI, which is the clearest recent support for enrichment logic.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41388319-polygenicity-apoe-e4-shape-response-intervention-mci.md]
- PENSA showed that even an APOE ε4-selected cohort can miss its primary endpoint, so enrichment is not a substitute for a strong intervention.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40664536-pensa-apoe-e4-scd-egcg-multimodal-rct.md]
- The APOE4 exercise systematic review found directionally mixed results and very limited meta-analytic power, which argues against a blanket "APOE4-positive cohorts do better" rule.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39828710-apoe4-exercise-systematic-review-meta-analysis.md]
- Prospective enrichment is moving into protocol stage in exercise prevention, but the field still has few completed cognition trials built around biomarker selection rather than post hoc subgrouping.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40496973-exercise-apoe4-carriers-protocol.md]
- Association studies from the MIND trial and cognitive-reserve literature reinforce that biomarker state can modify lifestyle-cognition relationships, but they are not the same as prospective treatment enrichment.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40876538-mind-apoe4-carotenoids-cognitive-decline.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39392181-apoe-e4-lifestyle-cognitive-reserve.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40332937-apoe4-blood-neurodegenerative-biomarkers-cognitive-decline.md]
- Biomarker-enriched surrogate endpoints are already more efficient: the p-tau217 endpoint paper found higher effect sizes after Aβ enrichment and the smallest sample sizes when Aβ was in the intermediate Centiloid range, but that is still a biomarker endpoint paper rather than a cognition endpoint paper.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41364889-plasma-ptau217-endpoint-ad-clinical-trials.md]
- Digital phenotype is improving as a low-burden enrichment screen for state and baseline function, especially in older adults, MS, and AD-adjacent cohorts, but the evidence still looks like screening and monitoring rather than a universal effect-size multiplier. The 2026 wearable-physiology paper and the allostatic-load paper strengthen the state-signal interpretation; the home-based tDCS preprint and the emotional-disorder ML meta-analysis show that response prediction is feasible in psychiatric contexts, but not yet validated as a cognition-specific enrichment rule. The new wearable/HOMA-IR paper and conversational multimodal platform extend the state layer, not the responder layer.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39833903-digital-biomarkers-cognitive-assessment-older-adults.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40459924-digital-phenotyping-ms-severity.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41357423-passive-sensors-digital-markers-alzheimers.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41322085-game-based-cognitive-aging-assessment.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41876851-digital-physiological-biomarkers-predict-within-person-symptom-changes-complex-chronic-illness.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41223069-digital-phenotype-allostatic-load-military-training.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41646388-home-based-tdcs-eeg-biomarkers-response.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40493989-ml-response-emotional-disorders-meta-analysis.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39706190-digital-phenotyping-wearables-ai-psychiatric-disorders-genetic-associations.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41840032-insulin-resistance-prediction-wearables-routine-blood-biomarkers.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41921090-multimodal-digital-biomarkers-fatigue-cognition-mental-health-feasibility.md]
- Proxy biology from tPBM and intranasal insulin continues to support phenotype sensitivity, especially where biomarkers or physiology are mechanistically close to the intervention.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39423445-transcranial-photobiomodulation-cognition-bdnf.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40392946-intranasal-insulin-t2d-rsfc.md]
- The null MS intranasal-insulin trial is a useful reminder that selection markers do not guarantee response if the disease context and delivery system are wrong.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40253245-intranasal-insulin-multiple-sclerosis-cognition.md]
- Foundation-model biomarker papers are still a cautionary tale: better prediction is not the same thing as robust, neurobiologically faithful enrichment.^[raw/papers/arxiv-2604.22018v1-foundation-models-for-discovering-robust-biomarkers-of-neuro.md]

## Evidence Tiering

- Direct randomized evidence: SUPERBRAIN-MEET reanalysis, PENSA, and the exercise literature summarized in the APOE4 meta-analysis.
- Prospective design evidence: CYCLE-AD protocol.
- Biomarker-predictor evidence: basal forebrain MRI and acute cholinergic challenge for galantamine response.
- Moderator/association evidence: APOE ε4 lifestyle reserve paper, the MIND carotenoid analysis, and the APOE4 blood biomarker cohort.
- Endpoint-efficiency evidence: the p-tau217 paper, which is about biomarker trial design rather than cognition.
- Proxy mechanistic evidence: tPBM BDNF and intranasal-insulin responder-state studies.

## Effect Size Snapshot

- SUPERBRAIN-MEET: the main point is effect heterogeneity by APOE ε4 / polygenic risk, not a single universal effect size; the largest reported relative intervention efficacy was in APOE ε4 carriers with high PRS (β = 7.54, SE 2.59, p = 0.005).
- PENSA: the primary PACC-exe comparison was not statistically significant at 12 months; exploratory analyses after washout favored EGCG.
- APOE4 exercise meta-analysis: the review found some outcomes favoring carriers and some favoring non-carriers, with very low to moderate certainty and too little data for a strong pooled rule.
- CYCLE-AD: no effect size yet because it is still a protocol.
- Galantamine predictor studies: high basal forebrain volume and acute cholinergic challenge separated likely responders from non-responders, but the effect is still small-sample and not yet translated into a prospective enrichment trial.
- APOE4 blood biomarker cohort: t-tau and GFAP accelerated decline by about β = -0.03 and -0.07 per 1-log10 unit higher level in carriers vs noncarriers; useful for risk enrichment, not treatment efficacy.
- p-tau217 endpoint paper: effect size improved from 0.64/0.62 in all Aβ-positive groups to 0.85/0.72 in intermediate-Aβ groups, with estimated per-arm sample size falling from 610/664 to 342/492. That is a surrogate-endpoint gain, not a cognition gain.
- tPBM and intranasal insulin show that biomarker-linked physiology can move, but those are not clean examples of enrollment enrichment.

## Safety/Adverse Events

No new enrichment-specific safety signal appears in these sources. The practical issue is more epistemic than toxicologic: biomarker selection can reduce heterogeneity, but it can also hide nulls, inflate optimism, or make a small subgroup look more actionable than it really is.

## Responder Phenotypes

- APOE ε4 carriers are the clearest current enrichment candidate in cognition-prevention work.
- Higher polygenic-risk strata may be more likely to show intervention response in MCI.
- High basal forebrain cholinergic volume and acute galantamine challenge response look like the best mechanistic enrichment leads for the cholinergic branch.
- Intermediate-Aβ status looks useful for biomarker endpoint efficiency in AD trial design, but that is not yet a cognition-responder rule.
- Insulin-resistance phenotypes remain a strong candidate for metabolic-brain interventions, but that is still better supported for physiology and connectivity than for universal cognitive gain.
- Older adults with lower reserve are the most plausible beneficiaries of enrichment, but the evidence is still intervention-specific.
- Digital phenotype is most useful as a low-burden state-enrichment layer unless it is tied to a biologically validated biomarker or a prospective response model.

## Protocol Parameters

- SUPERBRAIN-MEET reanalyzed a multidomain MCI intervention using RBANS total index as the primary outcome.
- PENSA used a 12-month multimodal lifestyle intervention plus EGCG 5-6 mg/kg or placebo, followed by a 3-month washout, in APOE ε4 carriers with subjective cognitive decline.
- CYCLE-AD is an 18-month, home-based, high-intensity exercise trial in older APOE ε4 carriers.

## Open Contradictions

- Biomarker enrichment often looks promising in secondary analyses, but the best prospective or randomized evidence still includes null primary endpoints.
- APOE4 is not a universal "better responder" marker; the exercise literature is mixed.
- Some biomarker effects are likely intervention-specific, not platform-general.
- Association studies can motivate enrichment, but they do not prove enrichment improves effect sizes.
- Surrogate-endpoint efficiency is easier to improve than cognition effect sizes.
- Model-derived biomarkers need explicit robustness checks before they are used to justify enrichment.
- Anatomy-based personalization can improve target exposure without proving a stronger clinical effect.
