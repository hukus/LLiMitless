# Online Evidence Freshness Dashboard

This page is the wiki's signal-monitoring layer. It does not rank interventions; it tracks what changed recently enough to justify a wiki update: new RCTs, new reviews or meta-analyses, new safety guidance, failed trials, and active registry entries that are about to become real evidence.

The point is to keep the branch pages honest about freshness. A positive signal that is three years old should not outrank a fresh null or a new phase-3 failure.

## What Changed Since 2024

- tPBM now has both a cognition-positive randomized trial in mild TBI and an active AD/MCI pipeline, so the branch is moving from exploratory promise toward verification rather than stand-alone enthusiasm.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40485299-tpbm-mtbi-cognitive-postconcussion-ptsd.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct07224607-tpbm-alzheimers-study.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct04784416-trap-ad.md]
- Solriamfetol added a disorder-context cognition benchmark in OSA with excessive daytime sleepiness, which is useful as a comparator whenever an arousal intervention claims cognitive benefit; the objective endpoint moved by a modest but real amount.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39528111-solriamfetol-sharp-osa-cognition.md]
- Semaglutide's EVOKE/EVOKE+ phase 3 readout was negative on the primary AD endpoint, which is a true branch-level failure even if biomarker language remains attractive.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41865758-semaglutide-evoke-evokeplus-ad-phase3.md]
- Human tTIS now has a systematic review tallying 48 records and 28 ongoing trials, so the branch has crossed from novelty into a live research program.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41167554-human-applications-temporal-interference-stimulation.md]
- The current batch of protocol leads now splits into three buckets: active no-results registries such as LIGHT-COG mazdutide, Klotho + follistatin gene therapy, the named temporal-interference studies, and STAK-Sleep; unknown-status or completed-but-results-free CBF registries such as kCBF and MEKC; and comparator studies with posted results that still need interpretation, such as MB2 and samelisant's narcolepsy paper.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct07083154-light-cog-mazdutide-early-dementia.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct07285629-klotho-follistatin-gene-therapy.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct06607432-temporal-interference-social-cognition.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct07215299-temporal-interference-methods-study-1-1.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct07339072-temporal-interference-methods-study-1-2.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct05519644-stak-sleep-ketone-ester-record-2026-05-04.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct06032156-kcbf-ketone-dose-record-2026-05-04.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct06217159-mechanistic-effect-of-ketones-on-cerebral-blood-flow.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct02380573-methylene-blue-results-posted.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39504585-samelisant-results-narcolepsy.md]
- The ketone evidence family had a version upgrade rather than a direction flip: the 2025 medRxiv/PubMed preprint reported 29 studies and SMD 0.26, while the 2026 Frontiers article expanded the evidence set to 38 studies and 41 protocols with SMD 0.29. Registry status still needs to be tracked separately for STAK-Sleep sleep restriction, kCBF, and MEKC because none currently posts efficacy outcomes.^[raw/articles/medrxiv-2025-09-17-25335999-exogenous-ketone-bodies-cognition-systematic-review-meta-analysis.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41001501-exogenous-ketone-bodies-cognition-systematic-review-meta-analysis.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct05519644-stak-sleep-ketone-ester-record-2026-05-04.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct06032156-kcbf-ketone-dose-record-2026-05-04.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct06217159-mechanistic-effect-of-ketones-on-cerebral-blood-flow.md]
- KarXT now has a live Alzheimer's agitation registry even though the approved U.S. product is still schizophrenia-label Cobenfy, which is exactly why approval geography and registry status need separate fields.^[raw/articles/fda-cobenfy-approval-2024.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct07011745-karxt-adagio2-alzheimers-agitation.md]
- tUS safety is now parameterized by ITRUSST guidance rather than vague caution; the paper gives a concrete reporting frame, but it is not a consumer-use safety endorsement.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41072763-itrusst-biophysical-safety-transcranial-ultrasound-stimulation.md]
- taVNS, tDCS, and tACS now need endpoint-level separation. taVNS is stronger on autonomic and stress-state regulation than on broad cognition, while tDCS/tACS are mostly parameter maps rather than generic modality wins.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41717223-taVNS-cognitive-deficits-systematic-review.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40908377-taVNS-mental-stressor.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39936474-taVNS-cortisol-stress-inflammation.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39124651-taVNS-autonomic-function.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39905569-tdcs-cognitive-impairments-dosage-parameters.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41034500-tacs-older-adults-meta-2025.md]
- Intranasal insulin remains phenotype-sensitive rather than broadly positive, and the newer MS null plus delirium RCT keep the branch mixed despite active physiology and perfusion work.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40253245-intranasal-insulin-ms-null.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41091693-long-acting-intranasal-insulin-delirium-rct.md]
- Focused-ultrasound BBB opening is still better read as a delivery platform and safety/futility question than as a cognition-enhancement signal.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40406679-focused-ultrasound-bbbo-ad-meta-analysis.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41072763-itrusst-biophysical-safety-transcranial-ultrasound-stimulation.md]
- The registry layer itself is moving: tPBM, tTIS, near-infrared light, and focused-ultrasound-related studies are recruiting or scaling, so the wiki needs a freshness layer in addition to the modality pages.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct07209683-near-infrared-light-young-older-adults.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct06607432-temporal-interference-social-cognition.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct07215299-temporal-interference-methods-study-1-1.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct07339072-temporal-interference-methods-study-1-2.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct04118764-focused-ultrasound-bbbo-alz.md]
- BPN14770's adolescent fragile-X registry is also a freshness item now: it is active, not recruiting, has no results posted, and its estimated completion dates are already past, so it belongs on the switchboard rather than the ordinary comparator page.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct05163808-bpn14770-adolescent-watch.md]
- Samelisant is no longer protocol-only: the phase-2 narcolepsy paper is published, which moves H3-class tracking into the posted-results comparator layer even though the branch still lacks an OSA cognition trial.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39504585-samelisant-results-narcolepsy.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct04072380-samelisant-narcolepsy.md]

## Branch Notes

- Solriamfetol SHARP is a good ceiling-setter for wakefulness-linked cognition because the objective effect is modest, time-locked, and tied to a disorder-context population rather than healthy enhancement.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39528111-solriamfetol-sharp-osa-cognition.md]
- tPBM is now the most coherent "small but real" device cluster in the wiki, but it still needs a larger pooled estimate before the branch moves from promising to settled.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40822571-tpbm-systematic-review-sleep-wakefulness-cognition.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40482941-tpbm-healthy-older-adults-working-memory-fnirs.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40485299-tpbm-mtbi-cognitive-postconcussion-ptsd.md]
- taVNS should be read as a state-regulation intervention with a narrow cognition tail, not as a general enhancer.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41717223-taVNS-cognitive-deficits-systematic-review.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40908377-taVNS-mental-stressor.md]
- tDCS and tACS are sensitivity problems: target, dose, and disease state decide whether anything appears.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39905569-tdcs-cognitive-impairments-dosage-parameters.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41034500-tacs-older-adults-meta-2025.md]
- Intranasal insulin now has enough mixed evidence that phenotype filtering should be explicit in the scan pages and the master table.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40253245-intranasal-insulin-ms-null.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41091693-long-acting-intranasal-insulin-delirium-rct.md]
- FUS and tTIS are still more about target engagement and safety framing than about a stable cognition win.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40406679-focused-ultrasound-bbbo-ad-meta-analysis.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41072763-itrusst-biophysical-safety-transcranial-ultrasound-stimulation.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41167554-human-applications-temporal-interference-stimulation.md]
- The newest protocol-level leads are already good test cases for the "no results yet" bucket: mazdutide's LIGHT-COG trial, the Klotho/follistatin gene-therapy study, and the tTIS cognition-facing registries all have enough design detail to track, but not enough public outcome data to change branch confidence.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct07083154-light-cog-mazdutide-early-dementia.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct07285629-klotho-follistatin-gene-therapy.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct06607432-temporal-interference-social-cognition.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct07215299-temporal-interference-methods-study-1-1.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct07339072-temporal-interference-methods-study-1-2.md]

## Watch Rules

1. Treat registry status changes as evidence updates, not just admin noise.
2. Prefer direct endpoint readouts over biomarker-only press releases when the branch matters for cognition.
3. Record the date when a study changes status, publishes, or gets a phase readout.
4. Move a modality from "active" to "mature" only after it has at least one direct human outcome readout beyond feasibility.
5. Use the dashboard to decide whether a scan page needs a same-day refresh or can wait for the next batch.

## Open Contradictions

- Biomarkers can move while cognition does not, so freshness has to track endpoint class as well as publication date.
- Registry activity can outpace efficacy data by years, which makes active-trial tracking useful but not proof of benefit.
- Safety guidance can become more precise without making a home protocol valid.
- Lab signals can disappear in ecological delivery, so "fresh" is not the same as "generalizable."
