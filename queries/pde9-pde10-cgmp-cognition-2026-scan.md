# PDE9/PDE10 and cGMP Cognition Scan - 2026

## Summary

This branch is best read as a negative/translator lane rather than a positive enhancer lane. BI 409306 is the best-characterized PDE9A probe: healthy-male studies showed rapid CSF entry and dose-dependent CSF cGMP target engagement, but the later schizophrenia and prodromal/mild Alzheimer disease efficacy trials were null on cognition. PF-04447943 was also negative in mild-to-moderate AD. Taken together, the human PDE9 record supports mechanism plausibility but not a cognition win.^[raw/articles/pubmed-27378314-bi409306-first-in-human-healthy-males.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-28120486-bi409306-proof-of-mechanism-healthy-males.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-29718385-bi409306-schizophrenia-cognition.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-30755255-bi409306-prodromal-mild-ad.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-24801218-pf04447943-ad.md]

PDE10 is closer to striatal disease biology than to a validated cognition enhancer. [[tak-063]], a PDE10A inhibitor, failed its acute-schizophrenia primary PANSS endpoint, and the 2023 PDE10 PET/MRS study found altered striatal PDE10A availability plus a cognition correlation in controls, which is useful biomarker biology but not treatment proof.^[raw/articles/pubmed-30190165-tak063-acute-schizophrenia.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-36458958-pde10-schizophrenia-pet-cognition.md]

A 2020 systematic review already framed [[pf-04447943]] and [[bi-409306]] as ineffective in AD, and the 2025 PDE5 review only broadens the NO/cGMP rationale without reversing the trial record. [[sildenafil]] anchors the PDE5 drug-level caution because a placebo-controlled schizophrenia study found no cognitive benefit at 50 or 100 mg; [[tadalafil]] adds a longer-acting PDE5 comparator with ETLAS-2 CSVD cognition-null / mixed results; and [[vardenafil]] adds the direct healthy-adult cognition-null counterexample. The cleanest current read is that PDE9/PDE10/PDE5 remain mechanistically interesting, but the human cognition evidence is mostly null, mixed, or biomarker-only.^[raw/articles/pmc-7369141-phosphodiesterase-inhibitors-ad-systematic-review.md]^[raw/articles/mdpi-cells-14191505-pde5-cognitive-decline-review.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-18716761-sildenafil-schizophrenia.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-23427190-vardenafil-healthy-adults.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40718899-tadalafil-csVD-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41332889-tadalafil-csVD-cognition-substudy.md]

## What Changed Since 2024

- The PDE9 branch now has enough human data to separate target engagement from efficacy: BI 409306 can move CSF cGMP, but it still did not improve cognition in schizophrenia or prodromal/mild AD.
- PF-04447943 remains a clean negative in AD, so the PDE9 story is no longer just one failed compound.
- PDE10 development is still alive in schizophrenia-related programs, but the human record continues to look antipsychotic/target-engagement first and cognition second.
- The 2025 PDE5 review refreshed the cGMP rationale, and the current registry state still shows mevidalen AD as active-not-recruiting and ulotaront phase 3 as recruiting, but neither branch added a positive human cognition signal.
- Standalone [[sildenafil]], [[tadalafil]], [[vardenafil]], and [[tak-063]] entities now complement the existing [[bi-409306]] and [[pf-04447943]] PDE9 anchors.
- The branch now deserves its own page because it is a distinct translator/negative class, not a continuation of the PDE4-positive roflumilast story.

## Evidence Tiering

- Human phase I target engagement positive: BI 409306 reached CSF and increased CSF cGMP in healthy males.
- Human RCT negative: BI 409306 in schizophrenia; BI 409306 in prodromal/mild AD; PF-04447943 in mild-to-moderate AD.
- Human RCT negative / mixed PDE5: [[sildenafil]] was cognition-null in schizophrenia; [[vardenafil]] did not improve healthy-adult cognition and worsened one reaction-time task at 20 mg; [[tadalafil]] was cognition-null overall in CSVD, with a slower simple reaction-time signal in the cognitive sub-study.
- Human pipeline / disease-development negative or mixed: TAK-063 missed its primary schizophrenia efficacy endpoint.
- Human biomarker bridge: PDE10 PET/MRS in schizophrenia showed altered PDE10A availability and a control-group cognition correlation.
- Active registry checks: mevidalen AD is active, not recruiting; ulotaront phase 3 is recruiting.
- Review-level synthesis: the 2020 AD systematic review and the 2025 PDE5 review are map documents, not efficacy proof.

## Effect Size Snapshot

- BI 409306 schizophrenia: no significant MCCB or SCoRS difference; stage-1 CANTAB did not justify a positive confirmatory endpoint.
- BI 409306 prodromal/mild AD: no significant NTB total z-score, CDR-SB, ADAS-Cog11, or ADCS-ADL benefit.
- PF-04447943 AD: ADAS-cog treatment difference -0.31, 90 percent CI -1.52 to 0.90; NPI difference -0.16, 90 percent CI -1.78 to 1.48.
- TAK-063 acute schizophrenia: PANSS total least-squares mean difference -5.46, SE 3.44, p = 0.115; not a primary win.
- PDE10 PET study: control sensorimotor-striatal PDE10A correlated with Tower of London scores, r = -0.58.

## Safety / Adverse Events

- BI 409306 healthy-male studies: mostly mild-to-moderate AEs and no serious adverse events.
- BI 409306 schizophrenia phase II: AEs increased with dose, from 33.3 percent at 10 mg to 53.5 percent at 100 mg, vs 36.4 percent placebo.
- BI 409306 prodromal/mild AD: generally well tolerated.
- PF-04447943 AD: more diarrhea, nausea, and AE-related discontinuation than placebo.
- TAK-063: mostly mild or moderate AEs, but akathisia and dystonia were more frequent than placebo.
- Tadalafil: ETLAS-2 showed worse adherence and more adverse events than placebo, and label-level nitrate / riociguat / hypotension gates make vasodilator stacks safety-relevant.

## Responder Phenotypes

- No established responder phenotype has emerged for PDE9 cognition.
- The most defensible phenotype claim is biomarker-level: PDE10A availability tracks striatal function, and in controls it correlated with Tower of London performance.
- The AD and schizophrenia efficacy trials do not currently support a clinically useful responder subgroup.

## Protocol Parameters

- BI 409306 first-in-human: single rising doses from 0.5 mg to 500 mg.
- BI 409306 proof-of-mechanism: 25, 50, 100, and 200 mg single doses.
- BI 409306 schizophrenia: 10, 25, 50, or 100 mg once daily for 12 weeks.
- BI 409306 prodromal/mild AD: 10 to 50 mg daily or 25 mg twice daily for 12 weeks.
- PF-04447943 AD: 25 mg every 12 hours for 12 weeks.
- TAK-063 acute schizophrenia: 20 mg daily for 6 weeks, with blinded de-escalation to 10 mg for intolerance.
- PDE10 PET/MRS: no drug intervention; imaging bridge only.

## Open Contradictions

- The cGMP/CREB mechanism is plausible and target engagement is measurable, but the human cognition endpoints stay negative.
- PDE10 biology appears related to striatal function and cognitive symptoms in schizophrenia, yet PDE10 inhibitor development has not translated into a cognition rescue signal.
- The 2025 PDE5 review suggests ongoing translational interest, but it is a rationale paper and cannot outweigh the repeated nulls in the PDE9 branch.
- This branch sits awkwardly between disease biology and enhancement: mechanistic value is real, but clinical-value claims remain unproven.
