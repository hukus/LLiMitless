# Integrated-Stress-Response Cognition 2026 Scan

## Summary

The integrated-stress-response branch is now a real cognition frontier, but not a validated enhancement class. The most cognition-specific new anchor is [[dp71l]]: a 2026 Science paper reports that this viral PPP1R15B ortholog acted as a pan-ISR inhibitor and reversed cognitive and synaptic deficits in mouse models of Down syndrome, Alzheimer disease, and aging, while also enhancing synaptic plasticity and memory in healthy mice.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41926581-dp71l-integrated-stress-response-cognition.md]

[[dnl343]] gives the branch a human translational anchor, but not a cognition-efficacy anchor. It is a CNS-penetrant small-molecule eIF2B activator with phase 1 healthy-participant and phase 1b ALS safety / pharmacodynamic data, CSF distribution, and ISR biomarker reduction in ALS participants.^[raw/articles/nature-communications-2025-dnl343-eif2b-als.md] The newer HEALEY ALS Regimen G readout then missed ALSFRS-R / survival and key muscle / respiratory secondary endpoints, so DNL343 is now target-engagement-positive but clinical-function-negative in ALS so far.^[raw/articles/massgeneral-2025-dnl343-healey-als-topline.md]

[[fosigotifator-abbv-cls-7262]] adds a second clinical eIF2B-activation comparator. Its HEALEY Regimen F primary-dose analysis missed ALSFRS-R / survival and key secondary endpoints, though an exploratory high-dose arm showed nominal muscle-strength signals. This is still not cognition evidence.^[raw/articles/als-eu-2025-fosigotifator-healey-als-topline.md]

The mechanism layer is also more complicated than the hype version. A 2025 Nature paper describes a split ISR caused by low eIF2B activity without canonical eIF2alpha phosphorylation, and a 2025 neurodegeneration review emphasizes that acute ISR can be protective while prolonged ISR can become harmful. That means "inhibit ISR" is not a simple pro-cognition rule.^[raw/articles/nature-2025-plasticity-mammalian-integrated-stress-response.md]^[raw/articles/molecular-neurodegeneration-2025-integrated-stress-response-neurodegenerative-diseases.md]

## What Changed Since 2024

- [[dp71l]] created the clearest direct cognition signal in the ISR branch, but only in animal models and viral-tool delivery.
- [[dnl343]] moved eIF2B activation into human target-engagement territory, with healthy-participant and ALS clinical safety data, then failed the 2025 HEALEY ALS functional endpoint readout.
- [[fosigotifator-abbv-cls-7262]] makes the eIF2B activator clinical lane broader than DNL343, but its primary ALS endpoint also missed.
- ISR biology became less collapsible into one pathway because the split-ISR paper separates low-eIF2B biology from canonical eIF2alpha-phosphorylation biology.
- [[isrib]] remains relevant as a preclinical small-molecule reference, including a new vascular-cognitive-impairment rat paper, but it is still not a human cognition drug.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41380823-isrib-vascular-cognitive-impairment-rat.md]

## Safety And Translation Gates

- [[dp71l]] is a viral ortholog / tool strategy; it needs delivery, immunogenicity, durability, dose-control, and reversibility gates before it can be interpreted as a therapy.
- [[dnl343]] has human safety and pharmacodynamic data, but in ALS and healthy-participant pharmacology contexts, not in cognition trials; the HEALEY ALS functional readout was negative at 24 weeks.
- [[fosigotifator-abbv-cls-7262]] adds another eIF2B clinical comparator, but its primary-dose HEALEY ALS endpoint was also negative.
- [[isrib]] community use has high route and sourcing uncertainty, including analog confusion and reports of sleep and mood activation.
- ISR inhibition is not uniformly good. The review layer stresses an acute protective versus prolonged harmful split, so timing and disease state likely determine direction.
- PERK, PKR, GCN2, HRI, eIF2B, PPP1R15B, GADD34, and ATF4 sit close to core stress-adaptation and proteostasis biology. That raises the safety bar higher than for ordinary symptomatic nootropics.

## Current Verdict

This is a high-priority frontier branch for disease-rescue and cognitive-aging research, but a low-confidence branch for practical cognitive enhancement. The wiki should rank it as `preclinical-only` for direct cognition until a human cognition endpoint is positive. [[dnl343]] can be treated as a human target-engagement anchor, but not as cognition-positive, and its ALS functional miss lowers confidence in easy biomarker-to-benefit translation. [[isrib]] has meaningful anecdotal salience, but those reports are safety- and sourcing-heavy and do not upgrade medicinal confidence.

## Open Questions

- Can a small-molecule eIF2B activator reproduce [[dp71l]]-like cognitive effects without viral delivery, especially after [[dnl343]] and [[fosigotifator-abbv-cls-7262]] missed ALS functional endpoints?
- Which diseases have maladaptive chronic ISR rather than protective acute ISR?
- Are memory gains durable after washout, and does suppressing ISR harm stress resilience?
- Can ISR biomarkers predict responders before cognitive endpoints are tested?
- Will human trials measure cognition directly, or only motor, biomarker, or global disease endpoints?
