# Top Buyable Cognition Chemicals

Assumption: "buyable" means non-prescription, consumer-available oral chemicals or molecule-like products. This excludes prescription drugs, injected peptides, research chemicals, gray-market analogs, and device/procedure interventions even when their efficacy signal is stronger in the broader wiki. It also excludes stronger-but-riskier candidates such as nicotine and methylene blue from the top five because dependency, interaction, and safety friction dominate practical use.

## Peptide / Gray-Market Boundary

June 2026 peptide/nootropic frontier nodes stay outside this query even when gray-market vendors make them easy to buy. [[semax]], [[selank]], [[cerebrolysin]], recovery-bound [[bpc-157]], and [[bromantane]] can be high-priority monitoring nodes in [[nootropic-peptide-anecdote-frontier-2026-scan]], [[anecdote-led-chemical-heat-map]], and [[anecdote-to-trial-mismatch-bucketed-map]], but source/COA, route/formulation, legal/sports status, adverse-event capture, recovery-state confounding, and stack attribution make them different from ordinary buyable chemicals such as [[creatine]], [[caffeine]], [[ketone-ester]], [[citicoline]], and [[l-theanine]].

Keep [[dihexa]], [[pe-22-28]], [[adamax]], [[epithalon]], [[pinealon]], [[mots-c]], and TB-500-style labels de-ranked, metabolism-first, recovery-bound, or state-bound until source/COA, exact route, formulation, washout, adverse events, and objective endpoints improve. Any future promotion has to pass through [[molecule-triage-worksheet-for-online-scans]], [[delivery-validation-checklist-for-cognition-interventions]], [[peptide-stack-topology-ledger]], [[peptide-regulatory-live-watch-board]], [[stack-attribution-diary-for-self-study-and-n-of-1-protocols]], and [[self-experimentation-stop-rules]] before changing this practical shortlist.

| 1 | [[creatine]] | Sleep loss, high workload, heat/exercise stress, low dietary creatine intake, aging/lower reserve | Strongest low-friction molecule in the wiki: moderate strain-state signal, small domain-specific memory/attention/processing-speed effects, low cost, favorable aggregate safety | Not a universal enhancer; weakest in young, rested, already-replete adults |

| 4 | [[citicoline]] | Older-adult memory complaints, age-associated memory impairment, vascular/MCI-adjacent support | Cleanest widely available choline-donor candidate with a 12-week older-adult memory RCT and review-level MCI support | Not proven for young healthy adults; do not generalize to generic choline or Alpha-GPC |
| 5 | [[l-theanine]] | Calm attention, caffeine smoothing, anxiety-linked focus problems | Low-moderate healthy-adult signal for selected attention/visual-processing endpoints plus practical caffeine-combination logic | Not a memory or IQ enhancer; subjective calm can be mistaken for objective cognition |

## Near Misses

- [[dha-epa]]: more widely available than ketone esters and plausible for older-adult memory/executive support, but the wiki marks the signal as contested and status/product-form dependent.
- [[magnesium-l-threonate]]: product-specific Magtein trial is interesting, but the evidence is one sleep-dissatisfied-adult lane rather than broad cognition.
- [[dietary-nitrate-beetroot]]: attractive low-cost reserve-state option, but direct cognition evidence is still narrow.
- Standardized botanicals such as [[ginkgo]], [[ginseng]], [[grape-extract]], and [[bacopa]]: viable if extract mixtures count as "chemicals," but product identity is the evidence unit and the signal is usually older-adult, MCI, or product-specific rather than broadly general.
- [[methylene-blue]]: real narrow consolidation/redox signal, but serotonergic interaction, G6PD, dosing, and product-quality issues keep it out of a practical top-five buy list.
- [[tudca-ursodiol]] / TUDCA: buyable as a supplement and relevant to gut-liver-brain / bile-acid hypotheses, but healthy-adult cognition evidence is absent and ALS phase-3 guardrails are negative. Treat current brain-fog anecdotes as phenotype leads, not a top-buyable recommendation.
- [[nadh]]: buyable and relevant for ME/CFS-like cognitive fatigue and mitochondrial/redox hypotheses, but evidence is illness-state and combination-heavy, older dementia signals are mixed/null, and healthy-adult cognition endpoints are absent. Keep below cleaner buyable candidates.
- [[nad-plus-products]]: consumer-visible through patches, lozenges, clinics, and injections, but the route/product/safety burden is much higher than the healthy-adult cognition evidence. Exclude direct NAD+ products from the oral buyable top five; the patch/IV clinic branch belongs in [[redox-mitochondrial-cofactor-cognition-watchlist]] and [[post-viral-brain-fog-chemical-watchlist]].
- [[d-ribose]]: buyable and relevant for ME/CFS-like energy-envelope and mental-clarity hypotheses, but evidence is open-label and illness-state-bound with no objective healthy-adult cognition endpoint. Keep it below [[creatine]] and [[ketone-ester]] in energy-substrate ranking.
- [[berberine-dihydroberberine]]: buyable and highly visible for glucose/lipid/weight claims, with plausible postprandial brain-fog anecdotes, but direct cognition evidence is indirect and disease-state. Medication, glucose, pregnancy/lactation, GI, and form-identity gates keep it as a metabolic-state near miss rather than a top cognition chemical.
- [[s-adenosyl-l-methionine]] / SAMe and [[betaine-tmg]] / TMG: buyable and central to methyl-donor brain-fog lore, but SAMe's best signal is mood-state/depression-bound and TMG's best signal is homocysteine lowering without cognition endpoints. Bipolar/mania, serotonergic co-use, levodopa, GI/sleep/mood, homocysteine/methionine, and lab-status gates keep both out of a conservative buyable cognition shortlist.
- [[dimethylglycine-dmg]]: buyable and chemically adjacent to TMG/SAMe methylation stacks, but the strongest captured clinical read is negative or non-significant, including progressive-MS fatigue/cognition and autism/mitochondrial counterweights. Keep it as an attribution-control molecule, not a shortlist candidate.
- [[sarcosine]] / N-methylglycine: buyable and chemically adjacent to glycine/DMG/TMG while also sitting in the NMDA/GlyT1 lane, but sarcosine monotherapy cognition evidence is weak and the best formal clue is sarcosine plus [[sodium-benzoate]] in schizophrenia. Keep it as a phenotype/combination-gated comparator rather than a healthy-adult buyable pick.
- [[glycine]]: buyable and relevant for sleep-mediated next-day state, magnesium-glycinate/GlyNAC attribution, and a small sleep-restriction PVT clue, but acute high-dose healthy-control cognition was null and anecdotes are bidirectional. Keep it as a sleep-state near miss, not a top cognition chemical.
- [[myo-inositol]]: cheap and buyable, with small ASI/Nitrosigine healthy-adult cognition signals in the broader inositol lane, but plain myo-inositol evidence is sleep/anxiety/PCOS-state and bidirectional anecdote rather than direct enhancement. Keep ASI product claims separate from ordinary inositol powder.
- [[glutathione-products]]: buyable in reduced, liposomal, micellar, S-acetyl-style, and clinic-route forms, but direct product evidence is mostly peripheral GSH body-store or PK movement without cognitive endpoints. Keep below NAC/GlyNAC and treat illness-state brain-fog anecdotes as route/product hypotheses.
- [[methylsulfonylmethane-msm]]: cheap and buyable, with enough sulfur/methylation/detox brain-fog anecdotes and brain-exposure evidence to track, but no objective cognition endpoint. Keep MSM below NAC/GlyNAC/direct glutathione and separate from SAMe, methionine, DMSO, taurine, and joint-health claims.
- [[phenethylamine]] / beta-PEA: buyable enough to appear in supplement/nootropic contexts and has weak-moderate focus anecdotes, but the formal evidence is trace-amine / MAO-B mechanism rather than cognition efficacy. MAOI/stimulant-stack risk, BP/HR/anxiety gates, and BMPEA/N,N-DMPEA identity boundaries keep it out of the conservative buyable shortlist.
- [[nn-dimethylphenethylamine-eria-jarensis]] / Eria: preworkout-visible and anecdote-positive enough to track, but excluded because there is no cognition evidence and the product-identity, blend, hidden-stimulant, BP/HR/anxiety, and MAO-B/stimulant-stack burdens are higher than the signal.

## Bottom Line

For a conservative buyable-chemical shortlist, start with [[creatine]] and [[caffeine]]. Treat [[ketone-ester]], [[citicoline]], and [[l-theanine]] as context-specific second-tier candidates rather than general enhancers. If "widely available" is interpreted as ordinary retail access and low cost, swap [[ketone-ester]] for [[dha-epa]] or a phenotype-matched standardized botanical.
