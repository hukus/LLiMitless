# Top Buyable Cognition Chemicals

Assumption: "buyable" means non-prescription, consumer-available oral chemicals or molecule-like products. This excludes prescription drugs, injected peptides, research chemicals, and device/procedure interventions even when their efficacy signal is stronger in the broader wiki. It also excludes stronger-but-riskier candidates such as nicotine and methylene blue from the top five because dependency, interaction, and safety friction dominate practical use.

| Rank | Candidate | Best-fit use case | Why it makes the top five | Main caveat |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [[creatine]] | Sleep loss, high workload, heat/exercise stress, low dietary creatine intake, aging/lower reserve | Strongest low-friction molecule in the wiki: moderate strain-state signal, small domain-specific memory/attention/processing-speed effects, low cost, favorable aggregate safety | Not a universal enhancer; weakest in young, rested, already-replete adults |
| 2 | [[caffeine]] | Acute vigilance, reaction time, sleep-loss rescue, low-alertness mornings | Highest-confidence acute consumer chemical and the benchmark comparator for state support | Tolerance, withdrawal, anxiety, BP/HR sensitivity, and sleep disruption can erase benefits |
| 3 | [[ketone-ester]] / exogenous ketones | Metabolic vulnerability, mental fatigue, high-load exercise, selected energy-substrate experiments | Small pooled human cognition signal and a plausible state-support branch stronger than most supplement lore | Product identity, GI burden, high cost, and phenotype dependence; less ordinary-retail than creatine/caffeine |
| 4 | [[citicoline]] | Older-adult memory complaints, age-associated memory impairment, vascular/MCI-adjacent support | Cleanest widely available choline-donor candidate with a 12-week older-adult memory RCT and review-level MCI support | Not proven for young healthy adults; do not generalize to generic choline or Alpha-GPC |
| 5 | [[l-theanine]] | Calm attention, caffeine smoothing, anxiety-linked focus problems | Low-moderate healthy-adult signal for selected attention/visual-processing endpoints plus practical caffeine-combination logic | Not a memory or IQ enhancer; subjective calm can be mistaken for objective cognition |

## Near Misses

- [[dha-epa]]: more widely available than ketone esters and plausible for older-adult memory/executive support, but the wiki marks the signal as contested and status/product-form dependent.
- [[magnesium-l-threonate]]: product-specific Magtein trial is interesting, but the evidence is one sleep-dissatisfied-adult lane rather than broad cognition.
- [[dietary-nitrate-beetroot]]: attractive low-cost reserve-state option, but direct cognition evidence is still narrow.
- Standardized botanicals such as [[ginkgo]], [[ginseng]], [[grape-extract]], and [[bacopa]]: viable if extract mixtures count as "chemicals," but product identity is the evidence unit and the signal is usually older-adult, MCI, or product-specific rather than broadly general.
- [[methylene-blue]]: real narrow consolidation/redox signal, but serotonergic interaction, G6PD, dosing, and product-quality issues keep it out of a practical top-five buy list.

## Bottom Line

For a conservative buyable-chemical shortlist, start with [[creatine]] and [[caffeine]]. Treat [[ketone-ester]], [[citicoline]], and [[l-theanine]] as context-specific second-tier candidates rather than general enhancers. If "widely available" is interpreted as ordinary retail access and low cost, swap [[ketone-ester]] for [[dha-epa]] or a phenotype-matched standardized botanical.
