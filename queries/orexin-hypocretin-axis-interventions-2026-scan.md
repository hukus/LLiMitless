# Orexin/Hypocretin Axis Interventions - 2026 Scan

## Summary

The orexin axis is now a real cognition branch, but it is still mostly a state-restoration story rather than a general healthy-adult nootropic story. The strongest direct human signal is still in narcolepsy type 1, where the OX2R agonist [[oveporexton]] improved wakefulness, cataplexy, and later cognition measures over 8 weeks. On the antagonist side, [[daridorexant]] now has healthy-volunteer safety, early delirium, and pending cognition-registry anchors, while the broader DORA class has stronger insomnia evidence and a 2026 pairwise meta-analysis across 77 trials, but that still does not translate into healthy-adult enhancement.

What changed since 2024:

- Oveporexton now has a parent phase 2 NT1 efficacy trial plus a cognition-specific randomized-trial secondary analysis, which makes the orexin-agonist signal easier to interpret as a real disease-context mechanism rather than a post hoc cognitive fluke.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40367374-oveporexton-narcolepsy-type-1.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41359331-orexin-agonist-cognition-narcolepsy-secondary-analysis.md]
- A 2025 healthy-men study showed danavorexton could reduce opioid-induced sedation and respiratory depression without affecting analgesia, which is a strong state-restoration signal but not a direct enhancement trial.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39804333-danavorexton-opioid-induced-respiratory-depression-sedation-healthy-men.md]
- A 2025 healthy-volunteer suvorexant pilot did not improve fear-extinction recall even though REM percentage tracked with recall across the sample, which is a clean null for direct healthy cognition enhancement.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40087982-suvorexant-fear-extinction-recall-healthy-individuals.md]
- A 2025 daridorexant nighttime-safety study found minimal overnight cognitive and balance effects in healthy adults, supporting the "bedtime sleep aid without obvious crash" interpretation rather than a daytime performance boost.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39641404-daridorexant-nighttime-safety-cognitive-function.md]
- A 2025-2026 DORA cluster expanded from insomnia efficacy into delirium-prevention evidence, including a daridorexant feasibility trial after heart surgery and a 2026 meta-analysis that found lower incident delirium but very low certainty.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40374039-daridorexant-delirium-feasibility-trial.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41401488-dora-delirium-meta-analysis.md]
- A 2026 pairwise meta-analysis across 77 trials and 16,416 participants showed DORAs improve subjective total sleep time by about 19 minutes, but they also raise somnolence risk and are still not a healthy-adult cognition enhancer.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41610582-dora-sleep-pairwise-meta-analysis.md]
- A 2025 driving-simulator trial found no clinically meaningful next-morning impairment from vornorexant in healthy Japanese participants, supporting the idea that some DORAs can preserve next-day function when dosed at night.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40847765-vornorexant-driving-performance-simulator.md]
- A 2026 human PK/RO model estimated that vornorexant produces strong OX1 and OX2 receptor occupancy in healthy phase I subjects, with OX2 occupancy above 70% at 30 minutes after 5 mg and 10 mg doses and still around 50% to 60% at 6 hours; that is a target-engagement anchor for the DORA branch, but it still does not imply enhancement.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41622624-vornorexant-orexin-receptor-occupancy-human-pk.md]
- A 2025 Cell mechanistic paper tightened the sleep-pressure story by showing that NREM norepinephrine oscillations and vasomotion drive glymphatic clearance, while zolpidem suppresses both; that makes bedtime sleep drugs more relevant to clearance biology than a generic "sedation" label suggests.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39788123-norepinephrine-mediated-slow-vasomotion-glymphatic-clearance-during-sleep.md]
- A live Oxford healthy-volunteer study is now testing single-dose daridorexant on aversive learning, affective processing, and executive function in 18-30-year-old healthy volunteers, with variance-minimized randomization balancing chronotype and gender; it is the cleanest registered non-insomnia cognition readout for the DORA branch, but no results are posted yet.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct07267559-dorea-daridorexant-executive-function.md]
- The Oxford DOREA study is still recruiting and currently lists estimated primary completion for 2026-09-01 and final study completion for 2026-10-01, so the live healthy-volunteer DORA readout is still pending.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct07267559-dorea-daridorexant-executive-function.md]
- A 2026 chemistry disclosure added new cyclopentapyrrole orexin agonists for narcolepsy and hypersomnia, which keeps the agonist pipeline active but does not add a human cognition signal.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41704392-cyclopentapyrrole-orexin-agonists.md]
- The field still lacks a clean healthy sleep-deprivation cognition trial showing that an orexin agonist beats standard wake-promoting comparators on PVT or transfer tasks.

## Evidence Tiering

- Human RCT cognition: one meaningful positive signal in NT1 via oveporexton, now anchored by the parent wakefulness/cataplexy trial.
- Human wakefulness / state-restoration: positive signals for danavorexton and TAK-994 in NT1 or sedation models.
- Human sleep/restoration: DORAs have a broad insomnia evidence base, with meta-analytic sleep gains and predictable somnolence risk, but healthy-volunteer nighttime safety data do not show an enhancement effect.
- Human delirium prevention: early DORA data suggest a possible benefit, but certainty is low and the effect is still diagnosis-specific.
- Human healthy-volunteer cognition: suvorexant did not improve extinction recall in a pilot study, which pushes the class away from generic enhancement claims.
- Human target engagement: vornorexant now has a healthy-phase-I occupancy model that shows real OX1/OX2 receptor engagement at 5-10 mg, but the readout is still pharmacology, not cognition.
- Clinical trial registry evidence: healthy-volunteer DORA cognition work exists, but not yet as a published enhancement result.
- Preclinical: still important for mechanism, but no longer the whole story.

## Effect Size Snapshot

- Oveporexton in NT1: the parent phase 2 trial improved wakefulness and cataplexy, and the cognition secondary analysis then showed placebo-adjusted PVT lapses improving by about 8.7 to 10.8 lapses across dose groups; CPAL errors improved by about 15.5 to 22.5 errors; executive-function measures also improved.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40367374-oveporexton-narcolepsy-type-1.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41359331-orexin-agonist-cognition-narcolepsy-secondary-analysis.md]
- TAK-994 in NT1: large wakefulness and cataplexy effects, but the trial was cut short by hepatotoxicity concerns.^[raw/articles/pubmed-37494485-oral-orexin-receptor-2-agonist-narcolepsy-type-1.md]
- Danavorexton in healthy men: improved respiration and reduced sedation in an opioid challenge model, which supports arousal rescue but not direct cognition gain.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39804333-danavorexton-opioid-induced-respiratory-depression-sedation-healthy-men.md]
- Suvorexant in healthy adults: no meaningful fear-extinction recall advantage over placebo despite the REM logic, so it stays a mechanistic null rather than a cognition win.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40087982-suvorexant-fear-extinction-recall-healthy-individuals.md]
- Daridorexant in healthy adults: next-night cognition stayed close to placebo in the nighttime-safety study, which is better framed as non-impairment than improvement.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39641404-daridorexant-nighttime-safety-cognitive-function.md]
- Daridorexant delirium feasibility: 50 mg nightly for the first three nights after heart surgery, with only a numeric reduction in delirium burden because the pilot was tiny.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40374039-daridorexant-delirium-feasibility-trial.md]
- DORA delirium meta-analysis: pooled OR 0.23 for incident delirium, but very low certainty and no clear secondary-outcome win.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41401488-dora-delirium-meta-analysis.md]
- DORA sleep pairwise meta-analysis: subjective total sleep time improved by 18.91 minutes, sleep quality did not clearly improve, and somnolence risk increased (RR 2.91).^[raw/articles/pubmed-41610582-dora-sleep-pairwise-meta-analysis.md]
- Vornorexant PK/RO: human phase I modeling estimated OX2 occupancy above 70% at 30 minutes after 5 mg and 10 mg, with roughly 50% to 60% occupancy still present at 6 hours; OX1 occupancy was higher than OX2 occupancy.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41622624-vornorexant-orexin-receptor-occupancy-human-pk.md]
- Vornorexant and lemborexant: next-morning driving performance stayed within the predefined non-impairment margins in the published healthy-volunteer studies.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40847765-vornorexant-driving-performance-simulator.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-30597112-lemborexant-driving-healthy-adult-elderly-volunteers.md]
- Fazamorexant: 2-80 mg single-dose and 10-60 mg multiple-dose exposure produced dose-dependent sleepiness, not cognition enhancement.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40552089-fazamorexant-first-in-human-study.md]

## Safety / Adverse Events

- OX2R agonists are not automatically "clean stimulants"; TAK-994 showed clinically important liver-enzyme elevations and Hy's-law cases in NT1.^[raw/articles/pubmed-37494485-oral-orexin-receptor-2-agonist-narcolepsy-type-1.md]
- DORAs are not cognitively neutral in all contexts; somnolence is common enough that insomnia benefit has to be weighed against next-day sedation risk, especially outside the narrow bedtime use case.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41610582-dora-sleep-pairwise-meta-analysis.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40552089-fazamorexant-first-in-human-study.md]
- Danavorexton and related agents still need broader safety work before any healthy-performance claim can be generalized beyond controlled models.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39804333-danavorexton-opioid-induced-respiratory-depression-sedation-healthy-men.md]
- DORAs are primarily sleep drugs; their main practical risk for performance users is next-day somnolence or driving risk if dose, timing, or individual sensitivity are off.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40847765-vornorexant-driving-performance-simulator.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-30597112-lemborexant-driving-healthy-adult-elderly-volunteers.md]
- Vornorexant's human receptor-occupancy model shows that the class can hit strong target engagement in healthy phase I subjects; that helps explain sleep efficacy, but it does not convert the class into a daytime enhancer.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41622624-vornorexant-orexin-receptor-occupancy-human-pk.md]
- Sleep-architecture effects may matter beyond symptom control: the norepinephrine/glymphatic paper suggests that drugs that blunt NREM oscillations can change clearance physiology, so "good sleep latency" is not the same as "restorative sleep architecture."^[raw/articles/pubmed-39788123-norepinephrine-mediated-slow-vasomotion-glymphatic-clearance-during-sleep.md]
- The old assumption that all orexin antagonists must disrupt cognition is too broad; the actual data are more nuanced and dose/timing dependent.^[raw/articles/pubmed-23552372-orexin-receptor-antagonists-cognition-margin.md]

## Responder Phenotypes

- Best-supported responder phenotype: orexin-deficient narcolepsy type 1.
- Possible second-order phenotype: people whose impairment is driven by acute sedation, opioid exposure, or pronounced sleepiness rather than by a primary learning deficit.
- For DORAs, the target phenotype is insomnia with downstream daytime dysfunction, not enhancement in already alert adults.
- A third phenotype is emerging for DORAs: perioperative or delirium-prone patients where sleep-wake stabilization may reduce confusion burden, but this remains early and not settled.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40374039-daridorexant-delirium-feasibility-trial.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41401488-dora-delirium-meta-analysis.md]

## Protocol Parameters

- Oveporexton (TAK-861): 0.5/0.5 mg, 2/2 mg, 2/5 mg, or 7 mg/placebo, twice daily, three hours apart, for 8 weeks in NT1.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41359331-orexin-agonist-cognition-narcolepsy-secondary-analysis.md]
- TAK-994: 30 mg, 90 mg, or 180 mg twice daily in NT1 before the hepatotoxicity signal emerged.^[raw/articles/pubmed-37494485-oral-orexin-receptor-2-agonist-narcolepsy-type-1.md]
- Danavorexton: 11 mg and 19 mg IV in a remifentanil sedation model in healthy men.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39804333-danavorexton-opioid-induced-respiratory-depression-sedation-healthy-men.md]
- Vornorexant: 10 mg and 20 mg nightly for 8 nights in healthy Japanese participants with next-morning driving assessment.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40847765-vornorexant-driving-performance-simulator.md]
- Vornorexant PK/RO model: human phase I plasma data from 67 healthy subjects, using fasting 10 mg data in the model, estimated strong OX1/OX2 occupancy after oral 5 mg and 10 mg doses.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41622624-vornorexant-orexin-receptor-occupancy-human-pk.md]
- Lemborexant: 2.5 mg, 5 mg, or 10 mg nightly in healthy adult and elderly volunteers with next-morning highway driving testing.^[raw/articles/pubmed-30597112-lemborexant-driving-healthy-adult-elderly-volunteers.md]
- Daridorexant: 50 mg nightly for 3 nights after cardiac surgery in the feasibility delirium trial; separate healthy-volunteer work is single-dose and task-based.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40374039-daridorexant-delirium-feasibility-trial.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct07267559-dorea-daridorexant-executive-function.md]
- Healthy-volunteer daridorexant study (Oxford): single-dose 50 mg vs placebo, ~1-hour post-dose cognitive/emotional tasks, 18-30-year-old healthy volunteers, and variance-minimized randomization balancing chronotype and gender; the protocol includes aversive learning, affective go/no-go, pupilometry, salivary alpha amylase, PILT, color-change detection, facial emotion recognition, and executive-function tasks. The registry currently lists estimated primary completion on 2026-09-01 and study completion on 2026-10-01.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct07267559-dorea-daridorexant-executive-function.md]
- Fazamorexant: single ascending doses 2-80 mg and multiple ascending doses 10-60 mg in healthy adults.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40552089-fazamorexant-first-in-human-study.md]

## Open Contradictions

- The branch splits into two different use cases: orexin agonism as wake/cognition rescue versus orexin antagonism as sleep enhancement, delirium prevention, or low residual impairment. Those are not interchangeable.
- The strongest cognition result is disease-context evidence in NT1, so it is still an inference to generalize that result to healthy sleep-pressure enhancement.
- DORA studies often measure next-day non-impairment, not better cognition, so "no harm" should not be mistaken for "performance boost"; the healthy-volunteer studies reinforce that distinction.
- The delirium literature is promising but low-certainty and diagnosis-specific, so it should not be collapsed into general nootropic evidence.
- The chemistry pipeline is still active, but the newest 2026 cyclopentapyrrole disclosure is still a medicinal-chemistry lead, not evidence that healthy-adult cognition can be enhanced by orexin agonism.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41704392-cyclopentapyrrole-orexin-agonists.md]
- Better REM or easier awakening did not translate into better extinction recall in the healthy-volunteer suvorexant pilot, so sleep-architecture changes alone are not enough to claim memory enhancement.
