# MOTS-c 2026 Scan

## Summary

MOTS-c still looks like a metabolism-first mitochondrial peptide rather than a direct cognition intervention. The formal evidence base is now best read as three layers: preclinical metabolic/senescence biology, human endogenous-biomarker associations, and one recruiting Phase 2a metabolic trial with no cognition endpoint. The anecdotal layer is visible but not cognition-specific: community reports cluster around energy, workout capacity, weight-loss support, non-response, injection reactions, and at least one high-salience anaphylaxis report.^[raw/articles/pubmed-25738459-mots-c-metabolic-homeostasis.md]^[raw/articles/pmc-12411631-mots-c-pancreatic-islet-senescence-diabetes.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-34351816-mdp-exercise-humans.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-32052315-mots-c-beta-amyloid-cad.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct07505745-mots-c-prediabetes-record-2026-04-01.md]^[raw/articles/reddit-2025-12-13-have-anyone-tried-mots-c-thread.md]^[raw/articles/reddit-2026-04-13-no-more-mots-c-anaphylaxis-thread.md]

## What Changed Since 2024

- A 2025 diabetes/senescence paper added a stronger metabolic translational bridge: circulating MOTS-c was lower in people with type 2 diabetes than controls, while MOTS-c treatment reduced pancreatic islet senescence and improved glucose intolerance in mouse models. This is biomarker plus preclinical treatment support, not a human treatment result.^[raw/articles/pmc-12411631-mots-c-pancreatic-islet-senescence-diabetes.md]
- A 2024 mechanistic paper strengthened the CK2/metabolic-muscle model in mice, and a 2025 injury-repair paper extended the tissue-resilience story into lung ischemia-reperfusion, but neither adds human cognition evidence.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39559755-mots-c-ck2-muscle.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39827923-mots-c-lung-ischemia-reperfusion.md]
- A 2026 mechanistic paper went one step further by showing MOTS-c can bind CK2 and improve intrinsic muscle mitochondrial bioenergetics in a PGC-1alpha/AMPK-linked manner, which makes the exercise-metabolism bridge more concrete without changing the cognition story.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41520850-mots-c-muscle-bioenergetics.md]
- The main human interventional change is the 2026 Phase 2a MOTS-MET registry: 120 adults with prediabetes and overweight/obesity, subcutaneous MOTS-c or placebo once daily for 12 weeks, primary endpoints of Matsuda Index insulin sensitivity and treatment-emergent adverse events, and no cognition endpoint.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct07505745-mots-c-prediabetes-record-2026-04-01.md]
- A 2023 review and a 2024/2025 biomarker meta-analysis keep the field biologically active, but they still frame MOTS-c as a metabolic or aging-related candidate, not a validated cognitive enhancer.^[raw/articles/pubmed-36761202-mots-c-therapeutic-exploitation.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39160573-mdp-metabolic-states-meta-analysis.md]
- A 2026 sports-medicine peptide review strengthens the safety interpretation for gray-market MOTS-c: unapproved peptide demand is rising, animal outcomes often exceed human data, rigorous safety data are scarce, and social media can amplify placebo-driven efficacy perception.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41966639-peptide-therapies-sports-medicine-review.md]
- The community layer is now explicit: one recent /r/Biohackers experience thread has mixed energy/fat-loss/non-response reports, while a separate high-score adverse-event thread reports apparent first-dose anaphylaxis after MOTS-c. Both are low-confidence anecdotes, but the adverse-event thread is high enough salience to keep safety/risk routing visible.^[raw/articles/reddit-2025-12-13-have-anyone-tried-mots-c-thread.md]^[raw/articles/reddit-2026-04-13-no-more-mots-c-anaphylaxis-thread.md]

## Evidence Tiering

- Human intervention: recruiting Phase 2a prediabetes/overweight-obesity trial; metabolic and safety endpoints only.
- Human observational/biomarker: exercise-induced MDP changes, diabetic/CAD prognostic cohort, type 2 diabetes lower-circulating-MOTS-c signal, and MDP meta-analysis.
- Preclinical/metabolic: foundational obesity/insulin-resistance model, CK2/muscle bioenergetics, pancreatic islet senescence, lung ischemia-reperfusion, and tissue-resilience models.
- Review/regulatory: therapeutic reviews plus 2026 sports-medicine peptide safety review, FDA bulk-risk page, and WADA metabolic-modulator listing.
- Anecdote: metabolic/performance self-reports, non-response, and safety-negative MOTS-c threads.

## Effect Size Snapshot

- No cognition effect size.
- Human evidence is biomarker/prognostic only so far, with the type 2 diabetes/senescence paper adding lower circulating MOTS-c in T2D versus controls as another metabolic-state signal.^[raw/articles/pmc-12411631-mots-c-pancreatic-islet-senescence-diabetes.md]
- The active trial may eventually provide a metabolic effect size, but not a cognitive one unless the protocol changes.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct07505745-mots-c-prediabetes-record-2026-04-01.md]
- The 2026 mechanistic paper provides stronger pathway evidence, but still no cognitive effect size.

## Safety / Adverse Events

- No completed published human adverse-event series for administered MOTS-c surfaced in this sweep.
- The active registry includes TEAE, vital signs, ECG, laboratory monitoring, and immunogenicity, which is appropriate because MOTS-c is still investigational and subcutaneous.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct07505745-mots-c-prediabetes-record-2026-04-01.md]
- FDA's bulk-risk page and the 2026 sports-medicine peptide review both keep MOTS-c in a safety-first gray-market category rather than a validated clinical peptide category.^[raw/articles/fda-bulk-drug-substances-peptide-risk-2026.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41966639-peptide-therapies-sports-medicine-review.md]
- WADA's 2026 prohibited list names MOTS-c as a metabolic-modulator example, so athlete use has sports-regulatory risk separate from medical evidence.^[raw/articles/wada-2026-prohibited-list-peptide-gh-axis-expanded.md]
- The April 2026 Reddit anaphylaxis thread is not incidence evidence, but it is enough to keep allergy/immunogenicity, excipient, endotoxin, and source-testing concerns in the MOTS-c safety ledger.^[raw/articles/reddit-2026-04-13-no-more-mots-c-anaphylaxis-thread.md]
- No validated consumer-use protocol surfaced.

## Responders

- Best-supported responder phenotype is early metabolic dysfunction, not healthy enhancement.
- The 2026 trial explicitly enrolls adults with prediabetes and overweight/obesity.
- The human biomarker signal suggests MOTS-c may track cardiometabolic risk rather than cognition.
- The 2025 diabetes/senescence study adds pancreatic beta-cell senescence and type 2 diabetes as a metabolic-biology responder hypothesis.
- The 2026 CK2 paper strengthens the idea that muscle energetics is the primary translational lane.

## Protocol Parameters

- Human registry: subcutaneous MOTS-c, fixed dose once daily for 12 weeks, with 4-week safety follow-up; the dose amount is not captured in the raw note and no results are posted.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct07505745-mots-c-prediabetes-record-2026-04-01.md]
- No cognition protocol identified.

## Open Contradictions

- Biologically interesting mitochondrial signaling exists, but the wiki still has no direct human cognition trial or durable enhancement signal.
- The strongest human signal is prognostic/metabolic, which could be a false friend for cognition if the brain relevance does not generalize.
- The newer mechanism paper makes the peptide more legible, not more proven for cognition.
- Community energy and fat-loss enthusiasm conflicts with non-response and adverse-event reports; all three can be true in a noisy gray-market peptide environment.
- "Endogenous MOTS-c tracks metabolic state" and "exogenous MOTS-c is beneficial and safe" are separate claims.
