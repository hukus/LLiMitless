# Cholinergic and Nicotinic Agents 2026 Scan

This branch is best read as a state-restoration and responder-enrichment lane, not a broad healthy-adult nootropic class. Nicotine still has the clearest acute attention signal when the study context separates direct effect from withdrawal relief, [[varenicline]] has a small direct healthy-nonsmoker memory signal, [[tropisetron]] adds a schizophrenia P50 / sustained-attention alpha7-setron lane, [[galantamine]] is responder-gated, [[donepezil]] has narrow trained-skill / sleep-loss / executive-task signals with important null and adverse counterweights, and [[vu0467319-vu319]] adds a receptor-selective healthy-volunteer target-engagement anchor rather than an efficacy win. For the concept layer, see [[cholinergic-state-restoration-and-cognition]]. ^[raw/articles/pubmed-38582919-nicotine-cognitive-function-smokers-abstinence.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-23072834-varenicline-healthy-non-smoking-adults.md]^[raw/articles/springer-2010-tropisetron-schizophrenia-cognition-p50-rct.md]^[raw/articles/nature-2020-one-day-tropisetron-schizophrenia-rbans-p50.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-33842683-galantamine-basal-forebrain-predictor.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-12105320-donepezil-flight-simulator.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-19725251-donepezil-sleep-deprivation-vulnerable.md]^[raw/articles/pmc-3169575-donepezil-healthy-older-memory-impairment.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40598606-vu0467319-m1-pam-healthy-participants.md]

The newer human data sharpen the boundary lines rather than broaden the class: galantamine appears to need a cholinergic-reserve phenotype to show benefit, the SAH pilot is a tolerability and safety readout rather than a cognition win, the 12-week HIV trial was null for cognition and moved inflammatory markers in the wrong direction, and choline donors now split into recovery/impairment signals, product-matrix hypotheses, narrow visuomotor precision, and memory-null cautionary results rather than one generic "choline" effect. ^[raw/articles/pubmed-33842683-galantamine-basal-forebrain-predictor.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40877496-galantamine-sah-safety-trial.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41427614-galantamine-hiv-no-cognition.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39300341-choline-alphoscerate-amnestic-mci.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40353278-choline-alfoscerate-mtbi-recovery.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39896163-lysolecithin-cognition-choline.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-9377589-phosphatidylcholine-explicit-memory.md]^[raw/articles/plos-2016-choline-bitartrate-memory-null.md]

The 2025 systematic review of human cholinergic pharmacology pulls the mechanism back into focus: the human literature points to sustained attention, perceptual detection, cue-response updating, and information-processing speed, with muscarinic and nicotinic modulators doing different things. ^[raw/articles/pubmed-41076097-cholinergic-signaling-cognition-human-pharmacological-studies.md]

The muscarinic sub-branch is now clinically real but still not a generic cognition story. [[xanomeline-trospium]] / Cobenfy / KarXT was FDA-approved for adult schizophrenia in 2024, the pivotal EMERGENT papers are symptom-first rather than cognition-first, [[emraclidine]] has M4 schizophrenia-development data plus posted phase-2 registry results, [[vu0467319-vu319]] is the healthy-volunteer M1 target-engagement anchor, and older [[xanomeline]] AD trials remain the clearest direct cognition signal in the branch. The 2026 review searched 24 studies through 2025-05-01 and reports verbal-learning, delayed-memory, and reaction-time improvements, but its conflict profile means that claim should stay in the hypothesis-strengthening bucket until a modern cognition-first trial exists. ^[raw/articles/fda-cobenfy-approval-2024.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-38691387-xanomeline-trospium-schizophrenia-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39488504-xanomeline-trospium-pooled-emergent.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41418563-xanomeline-trospium-cognitive-impairment-review.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-36528376-emraclidine-m4-phase1b.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct05227690-emraclidine-phase2-results.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-9109749-xanomeline-alzheimer-cognitive-behavior.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-9876958-xanomeline-ad-cntb.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40598606-vu0467319-m1-pam-healthy-participants.md]

For the benchmark frame, see [[pharmacology-comparator-map]], [[healthy-adult-cognition-signals]], and [[negative-evidence-watchlist-for-cognition-interventions]]. The practical question here is not "does acetylcholine matter?" but "which phenotype, which task, and which safety ceiling?".

## Summary

- Nicotine remains the strongest acute attention signal in the branch, but the cleanest evidence is in abstinence or withdrawal-adjacent contexts rather than in well-rested healthy adults. In the 2024 crossover study, sustained attention improved with nicotine, with effect sizes in the medium-to-large range. ^[raw/articles/pubmed-38582919-nicotine-cognitive-function-smokers-abstinence.md]
- [[varenicline]] has a direct healthy-nonsmoker cognition signal, but it is small and old: a 38-person randomized study found better working memory and declarative memory, with no change in mood or impulsivity. ^[raw/articles/pubmed-23072834-varenicline-healthy-non-smoking-adults.md]
- [[tropisetron]] has two small schizophrenia cognition/P50 anchors and a 5-HT3 / alpha7 nicotinic rationale, but no captured healthy-adult cognition trial. The strongest formal read is disease-state sensory gating plus sustained attention, not broad nootropic efficacy. ^[raw/articles/springer-2010-tropisetron-schizophrenia-cognition-p50-rct.md]^[raw/articles/nature-2020-one-day-tropisetron-schizophrenia-rbans-p50.md]^[raw/articles/nature-2022-5ht3-antagonists-sensory-processing-review.md]
- Muscarinic-selective modulation is now human-real rather than hypothetical: [[vu0467319-vu319]] showed healthy-volunteer target engagement and no dose-limiting safety signal, but it remains an exploratory FIH platform rather than a cognition-proven drug. ^[raw/articles/pubmed-40598606-vu0467319-m1-pam-healthy-participants.md]
- [[xanomeline-trospium]] / Cobenfy / KarXT made the muscarinic branch clinically real by reaching FDA approval for adult schizophrenia, but that approval is symptom-first and does not resolve the cognition question. ^[raw/articles/fda-cobenfy-approval-2024.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-38691387-xanomeline-trospium-schizophrenia-rct.md]
- [[emraclidine]] advanced the M4 side of the branch with a positive phase 1b signal and completed phase 2 registry reporting, but the posted results remain PANSS-first and have not produced a cognition-specific readout. ^[raw/articles/pubmed-36528376-emraclidine-m4-phase1b.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct05227690-emraclidine-phase2-results.md]
- The older [[xanomeline]] AD papers still matter because they are the only clear direct cognition signal here: ADAS-cog, CNTB, reaction time, and delayed verbal recall improved in a trial that was badly constrained by cholinergic toxicity. ^[raw/articles/pubmed-9109749-xanomeline-alzheimer-cognitive-behavior.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-9876958-xanomeline-ad-cntb.md]
- The 2026 systematic review pushes the branch toward a direct pro-cognitive interpretation, but because it is a synthesis with industry-adjacent conflicts, it should be treated as a lead rather than a settled verdict. ^[raw/articles/pubmed-41418563-xanomeline-trospium-cognitive-impairment-review.md]
- [[galantamine]] no longer looks like a uniform enhancer. Healthy older adults with larger basal forebrain cholinergic volume showed the best response, which argues for responder enrichment rather than average-case benefit. ^[raw/articles/pubmed-33842683-galantamine-basal-forebrain-predictor.md]
- [[donepezil]] now has its own split route: one pilot trained-skill-retention signal, one sleep-loss-vulnerability signal, and one acute executive-task signal, counterweighted by a sleep-loss null, memory impairment in healthy older adults, chronic cognitive-training null, MCI primary-endpoint misses, and label-level safety burden. ^[raw/articles/pubmed-12105320-donepezil-flight-simulator.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-19725251-donepezil-sleep-deprivation-vulnerable.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-22161694-donepezil-sleep-deprivation-null.md]^[raw/articles/pmc-3169575-donepezil-healthy-older-memory-impairment.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-18818443-donepezil-cognitive-training-older-adults-null.md]
- [[rivastigmine]] fills the other prescription AChEI comparator slot: dementia-context evidence exists, but the HIV-associated cognitive-impairment pilot did not show significant global cognition benefit and no healthy-adult enhancement signal is captured. ^[raw/articles/ncbi-bookshelf-2020-dementia-drugs-donepezil-rivastigmine.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-28854283-rivastigmine-hiv-cognitive-impairment-pilot.md]
- The 2025 SAH galantamine pilot did not establish cognition benefit, but it did show tolerability comparable to placebo under acute brain-injury conditions. ^[raw/articles/pubmed-40877496-galantamine-sah-safety-trial.md]
- The 2026 galantamine HIV RCT was null for cognition and increased several inflammatory markers, which is a warning against assuming cholinergic boosting will track cognition in disease contexts. ^[raw/articles/pubmed-41427614-galantamine-hiv-no-cognition.md]
- Choline alphoscerate posted positive amnestic-MCI and mTBI recovery signals, but those should be read as disease-relevant recovery findings, not as evidence for healthy-adult enhancement. A 2025 healthy-adult [[lysolecithin]] trial was null for memory even though it nudged plasma choline and LPC upward; [[phosphatidylcholine]] / lecithin is old and mixed-to-negative except for a narrow high-dose serial-learning signal and an egg-yolk product-matrix trial; [[choline-bitartrate]] is visuomotor-positive but memory-null and TMAO-gated. ^[raw/articles/pubmed-39300341-choline-alphoscerate-amnestic-mci.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40353278-choline-alfoscerate-mtbi-recovery.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39896163-lysolecithin-cognition-choline.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-6346908-lecithin-memory-normal-adults.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-9377589-phosphatidylcholine-explicit-memory.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-12917896-lecithin-dementia-cognitive-impairment-cochrane.md]^[raw/articles/bmc-2023-egg-yolk-choline-cognitive-functions.md]^[raw/articles/nature-2015-choline-bitartrate-visuomotor.md]^[raw/articles/plos-2016-choline-bitartrate-memory-null.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-12502178-choline-ingestion-performance-null.md]^[raw/articles/pmc-8410632-choline-forms-tmao-response.md]
- A new nicotine-tablet dose-response trial in occasional nicotine-pouch users shows the branch is still active clinically, but the study has now passed its estimated completion date without posted results. ^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct07408180-nicotine-tablet-attention-2026-04-30.md]
- Safety is the hard ceiling: nicotine is addictive, can raise heart rate and blood pressure, and can worsen sleep; varenicline and the broader cholinergic class add nausea, vivid dreams, bradycardia, and other tolerability issues. ^[raw/articles/dailymed-nicotine-gum-label-2026.md]^[raw/articles/fda-want-to-quit-smoking-cessation-products.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40877496-galantamine-sah-safety-trial.md]

## What Changed Since 2024

- The strongest new direct cognition delta is still the 2024 nicotine abstinence crossover result, which supports state restoration more than clean enhancement. ^[raw/articles/pubmed-38582919-nicotine-cognitive-function-smokers-abstinence.md]
- The 2025 systematic review makes the domain split explicit: acetylcholine seems most relevant to sustained attention, perceptual detection, cue-response updating, and processing speed, not just memory. ^[raw/articles/pubmed-41076097-cholinergic-signaling-cognition-human-pharmacological-studies.md]
- Tropisetron is now normalized as a named alpha7 / 5-HT3 comparator. The formal data are schizophrenia-specific and the Reddit signal is mostly mechanism-post enthusiasm, so it should not be treated as a healthy-adult nicotinic nootropic. ^[raw/articles/nature-2022-5ht3-antagonists-sensory-processing-review.md]^[raw/articles/reddit-2022-2026-tropisetron-alpha7-nootropic-threads.md]
- The 2026 nicotine tablet trial is an important translational signal because it tests attention and dose-response directly in humans, but it remains unpublished and overdue. ^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct07408180-nicotine-tablet-attention-2026-04-30.md]
- The muscarinic branch re-entered the clinic with [[xanomeline-trospium]] approval and [[emraclidine]] development, but the direct cognition claim still rests on older [[xanomeline]] AD trials and a cautious 2026 review, not on a modern cognition-first study. ^[raw/articles/fda-cobenfy-approval-2024.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-38691387-xanomeline-trospium-schizophrenia-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-36528376-emraclidine-m4-phase1b.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41418563-xanomeline-trospium-cognitive-impairment-review.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-9109749-xanomeline-alzheimer-cognitive-behavior.md]
- Galantamine now has a sharper responder-enrichment story: healthy older adults with stronger basal forebrain cholinergic reserve may benefit, SAH safety looks acceptable, but a 12-week HIV trial was cognitively null. ^[raw/articles/pubmed-33842683-galantamine-basal-forebrain-predictor.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40877496-galantamine-sah-safety-trial.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41427614-galantamine-hiv-no-cognition.md]
- Donepezil and rivastigmine are now explicit prescription-AChEI comparator pages, which prevents their dementia-label evidence, healthy-adjacent pilots, and Reddit nootropic lore from being mixed into generic choline-donor claims.
- Choline alphoscerate added positive MCI and mTBI recovery signals, which keeps the class alive for recovery or impairment settings even though broad enhancement remains unsupported. ^[raw/articles/pubmed-39300341-choline-alphoscerate-amnestic-mci.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40353278-choline-alfoscerate-mtbi-recovery.md]
- [[lysolecithin]] added a newer healthy-adult choline-source datapoint, but the null between-group memory result means the class still does not have a convincing broad enhancement signal. ^[raw/articles/pubmed-39896163-lysolecithin-cognition-choline.md]
- Muscarinic-selective PAMs now have a healthy-volunteer target-engagement anchor, but they still need actual efficacy studies before the wiki can treat them as a cognition class. ^[raw/articles/pubmed-40598606-vu0467319-m1-pam-healthy-participants.md]
- Net effect: the branch moved toward phenotype gating, withdrawal rescue, and recovery rather than any generic cholinergic nootropic claim.

## Evidence Tiering

- RCT / crossover: nicotine abstinence study, varenicline healthy-nonsmoker study, tropisetron schizophrenia cognition/P50 studies, galantamine basal-forebrain predictor study, galantamine post-stroke proof-of-concept, SAH galantamine pilot, galantamine HIV RCT, choline alphoscerate amnestic-MCI RCT, mTBI choline alfoscerate case-control study, phosphatidylcholine/lecithin healthy-adult studies, egg-yolk phospholipid-choline older-adult RCT, choline-bitartrate visuomotor study, choline-bitartrate memory-null experiments, and choline-ingestion load-carriage null.
- Prescription AChEI comparator evidence: donepezil trained-skill and sleep-loss-vulnerability positives, donepezil sleep-loss and chronic-training nulls, donepezil healthy-older impairment, MCI primary-null counterweights, rivastigmine dementia-context evidence, rivastigmine HIV pilot null, and official donepezil / rivastigmine labels.
- Phase 1 / pharmacodynamic: muscarinic M1 PAM healthy-volunteer study and emraclidine phase 1b.
- Active registry: nicotine tablet dose-response study and emraclidine phase 2 posted results.
- Regulatory / safety: nicotine gum label and FDA cessation guidance.
- Mechanistic enrichment: basal forebrain MRI predictor for galantamine response, the 2025 human cholinergic-system systematic review, and the 2026 xanomeline-trospium cognition review.
- Historical cognition evidence: older xanomeline AD trials with direct ADAS-cog/CNTB signals and major cholinergic toxicity.

## Effect Size Snapshot

- Nicotine: sustained attention improved in abstinent smokers, with medium-to-large effect sizes in the 2024 crossover study. ^[raw/articles/pubmed-38582919-nicotine-cognitive-function-smokers-abstinence.md]
- Varenicline: improved working memory and declarative memory in a 38-person healthy-nonsmoker study, but the sample is too small to treat as stable enhancement proof. ^[raw/articles/pubmed-23072834-varenicline-healthy-non-smoking-adults.md]
- Tropisetron: improved schizophrenia P50/sensory-gating and sustained-attention measures in small trials; one-day RBANS/P50 effects also exist, but both are disease-state and risperidone-adjunctive rather than healthy-adult enhancement. ^[raw/articles/springer-2010-tropisetron-schizophrenia-cognition-p50-rct.md]^[raw/articles/nature-2020-one-day-tropisetron-schizophrenia-rbans-p50.md]
- Muscarinic M1 PAM: exploratory CNS engagement in healthy volunteers with no dose-limiting safety signal, but no real efficacy readout yet. ^[raw/articles/pubmed-40598606-vu0467319-m1-pam-healthy-participants.md]
- Xanomeline-trospium / KarXT: PANSS improved meaningfully in acute schizophrenia, with the phase 3 EMERGENT-3 paper reporting an 8.4-point advantage over placebo and the pooled EMERGENT paper reporting a 9.9-point advantage with Cohen d 0.65. ^[raw/articles/pubmed-38691387-xanomeline-trospium-schizophrenia-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39488504-xanomeline-trospium-pooled-emergent.md]
- Xanomeline in older AD: direct cognition signals were present, including ADAS-cog, CNTB, reaction time, and delayed verbal recall, but tolerability was poor enough that the branch never became a practical enhancement story. ^[raw/articles/pubmed-9109749-xanomeline-alzheimer-cognitive-behavior.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-9876958-xanomeline-ad-cntb.md]
- Galantamine in SAH: tolerability and mortality were similar to placebo, but the study was designed for safety rather than cognitive efficacy. ^[raw/articles/pubmed-40877496-galantamine-sah-safety-trial.md]
- Galantamine in HIV: essentially null for cognition, with an adjusted standardized difference near zero and no reliable benefit signal. ^[raw/articles/pubmed-41427614-galantamine-hiv-no-cognition.md]
- Choline alphoscerate: improved ADAS-cog versus placebo in amnestic MCI; the signal is phenotype-bound and should not be generalized to healthy adults. ^[raw/articles/pubmed-39300341-choline-alphoscerate-amnestic-mci.md]
- Choline alfoscerate in mTBI: small case-control recovery signal, but no healthy-adult enhancement inference should be drawn from it. ^[raw/articles/pubmed-40353278-choline-alfoscerate-mtbi-recovery.md]
- [[phosphatidylcholine]] / lecithin: old direct evidence is mixed, with one acute 20 g lecithin memory-null study, one high-dose 25 g PC serial-learning signal, and a Cochrane dementia review that does not support lecithin as treatment. ^[raw/articles/pubmed-6346908-lecithin-memory-normal-adults.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-9377589-phosphatidylcholine-explicit-memory.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-12917896-lecithin-dementia-cognitive-impairment-cochrane.md]
- [[choline-bitartrate]]: small acute visuomotor-precision signal, counterweighted by acute memory-null experiments and a load-carriage cognitive null despite plasma choline increase. ^[raw/articles/nature-2015-choline-bitartrate-visuomotor.md]^[raw/articles/plos-2016-choline-bitartrate-memory-null.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-12502178-choline-ingestion-performance-null.md]
- Galantamine in healthy older adults: benefit concentrated in the larger basal-forebrain subgroup rather than the full sample. ^[raw/articles/pubmed-33842683-galantamine-basal-forebrain-predictor.md]
- Donepezil in nondemented adults: tiny pilot positives exist, but the healthy-older memory-impairment and chronic-training-null studies make the net signal contested rather than recommendation-grade. ^[raw/articles/pubmed-12105320-donepezil-flight-simulator.md]^[raw/articles/pmc-3169575-donepezil-healthy-older-memory-impairment.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-18818443-donepezil-cognitive-training-older-adults-null.md]

## Safety / Adverse Events

- Nicotine is the clearest safety problem in the branch: addiction, withdrawal cycling, higher heart rate, higher blood pressure, and sleep disruption are the main practical limits. ^[raw/articles/dailymed-nicotine-gum-label-2026.md]^[raw/articles/fda-want-to-quit-smoking-cessation-products.md]
- Varenicline is not nicotine, but it still carries a tolerability burden, especially nausea, vivid dreams, sleep disruption, and alcohol-tolerance changes. ^[raw/articles/fda-want-to-quit-smoking-cessation-products.md]
- Cholinesterase-boosting strategies keep the usual cholinergic GI and bradycardic liabilities, even when cognition improves; the SAH galantamine pilot makes that risk concrete rather than theoretical. ^[raw/articles/pubmed-40877496-galantamine-sah-safety-trial.md]
- Donepezil and rivastigmine add prescription-label risks: bradycardia / heart block, syncope, GI bleeding monitoring, seizures, pulmonary caution, insomnia, multiple-patch rivastigmine overdose errors, dehydration from vomiting / diarrhea, weight loss, and application-site reactions. ^[raw/articles/dailymed-2026-donepezil-label.md]^[raw/articles/dailymed-2025-rivastigmine-patch-label.md]
- Muscarinic PAMs may eventually reduce the classic GI burden, but the healthy-volunteer data are too early to claim a safety advantage yet. ^[raw/articles/pubmed-40598606-vu0467319-m1-pam-healthy-participants.md]
- Cobenfy/KarXT still carries the expected cholinergic burden: nausea, dyspepsia, vomiting, constipation, hypertension, tachycardia, urinary retention, and hepatic or renal restrictions are not trivial tradeoffs even after trospium is added. ^[raw/articles/fda-cobenfy-approval-2024.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-38691387-xanomeline-trospium-schizophrenia-rct.md]
- The older xanomeline AD program shows why pure muscarinic agonism never became a simple enhancement strategy: the cognitive signal came with frequent discontinuation and syncope at the top dose. ^[raw/articles/pubmed-9109749-xanomeline-alzheimer-cognitive-behavior.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-9876958-xanomeline-ad-cntb.md]
- The active nicotine-tablet study should be treated with sponsor-bias caution until outcomes are public, and the overdue status makes a future positive especially important to replicate independently. ^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct07408180-nicotine-tablet-attention-2026-04-30.md]

## Responder Phenotypes

- Nicotine-deprived smokers or nicotine-pouch users in withdrawal-adjacent settings.
- Healthy non-smokers under acute cognitive load, where varenicline has shown a small memory signal.
- Healthy volunteers in early muscarinic-PAM proof-of-target-engagement work.
- Adults with acute schizophrenia, where KarXT/Cobenfy is now a real symptom-treatment option.
- Mild-to-moderate AD, where xanomeline showed a cognition signal but not a viable tolerability profile.
- Schizophrenia patients in the emraclidine pipeline remain symptom-first rather than cognition-enriched.
- Healthy older adults with greater basal forebrain cholinergic reserve.
- Nondemented pilots, young adults under acute executive-task testing, and sleep-deprivation-vulnerable responders are the narrowest [[donepezil]]-positive groups; healthy older adults and chronic cognitive-training cohorts are counterexamples.
- SAH recovery phenotypes, where galantamine is currently a tolerability/safety question rather than a cognition win.
- Amnestic MCI, where choline alphoscerate showed a positive signal.
- Mild TBI recovery phenotypes, where choline alfoscerate showed a small recovery signal.
- Healthy adults using choline-source supplements are not a clearly responder-enriched group yet; [[lysolecithin]] was null in the 2025 trial.
- Post-stroke cognitive-rehabilitation phenotypes, where cholinergic augmentation is still exploratory.
- ART-suppressed HIV patients did not show a cognition response to galantamine in the 2026 trial.

## Protocol Parameters

- Nicotine abstinence study: 2024 randomized crossover design; nicotine vs placebo in adult smokers after abstinence, with sustained attention as the key outcome. ^[raw/articles/pubmed-38582919-nicotine-cognitive-function-smokers-abstinence.md]
- Varenicline healthy-nonsmoker study: short-term randomized double-blind dosing in 38 adults; working memory and declarative memory were the cognition endpoints. ^[raw/articles/pubmed-23072834-varenicline-healthy-non-smoking-adults.md]
- Tropisetron schizophrenia studies: 10 mg/day for 8 weeks as risperidone adjunct in the 2010 study, and 5, 10, or 20 mg/day in a one-day non-smoking-schizophrenia experiment using RBANS and P50 endpoints. ^[raw/articles/springer-2010-tropisetron-schizophrenia-cognition-p50-rct.md]^[raw/articles/nature-2020-one-day-tropisetron-schizophrenia-rbans-p50.md]
- Galantamine basal-forebrain study: single-dose crossover in healthy older adults with MRI enrichment. ^[raw/articles/pubmed-33842683-galantamine-basal-forebrain-predictor.md]
- Donepezil positive anchors: 5 mg/day for 30 days in 18 licensed pilots; 5 mg/day for about 17 days in sleep-deprivation-vulnerable young adults; and 5-7.5 mg acute dosing in 42 young healthy men. ^[raw/articles/pubmed-12105320-donepezil-flight-simulator.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-19725251-donepezil-sleep-deprivation-vulnerable.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-21262858-donepezil-healthy-young-executive.md]
- Donepezil counterweights: 5 mg/day for 14-17 days in a sleep-deprivation null study, 5 mg/day for 4 weeks in healthy older adults with memory impairment, and 5 then 10 mg/day with cognitive training over a year in older adults with memory complaints. ^[raw/articles/pubmed-22161694-donepezil-sleep-deprivation-null.md]^[raw/articles/pmc-3169575-donepezil-healthy-older-memory-impairment.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-18818443-donepezil-cognitive-training-older-adults-null.md]
- Rivastigmine HIV pilot: transdermal 9.5 mg daily over 48 weeks, nonsignificant global-cognition result with domain trends only. ^[raw/articles/pubmed-28854283-rivastigmine-hiv-cognitive-impairment-pilot.md]
- Galantamine HIV trial: 12 weeks of treatment in ART-suppressed people with HIV. ^[raw/articles/pubmed-41427614-galantamine-hiv-no-cognition.md]
- Choline alphoscerate MCI trial: 12 weeks at 600 mg in amnestic MCI. ^[raw/articles/pubmed-39300341-choline-alphoscerate-amnestic-mci.md]
- [[phosphatidylcholine]] / lecithin: 20 g acute lecithin was null in a tiny healthy-adult crossover; 25 g phosphatidylcholine produced a narrow serial-learning signal in students; 300 mg/day egg-yolk phospholipid choline for 12 weeks produced selected verbal-memory signals in older adults. ^[raw/articles/pubmed-6346908-lecithin-memory-normal-adults.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-9377589-phosphatidylcholine-explicit-memory.md]^[raw/articles/bmc-2023-egg-yolk-choline-cognitive-functions.md]
- [[choline-bitartrate]]: 2 g acute choline bitartrate improved target precision in the visuomotor study, 2.0-2.5 g was null across acute memory tasks, and 50 mg/kg was null after load carriage. ^[raw/articles/nature-2015-choline-bitartrate-visuomotor.md]^[raw/articles/plos-2016-choline-bitartrate-memory-null.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-12502178-choline-ingestion-performance-null.md]
- Nicotine tablet trial: five tablet variants, attention endpoint, dose-response design, occasional nicotine-pouch users, and now an overdue no-results-posted status. ^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct07408180-nicotine-tablet-attention-2026-04-30.md]
- SAH galantamine pilot: 60 aneurysmal SAH patients, 8 mg or 12 mg every 12 h, started within 36 h after aneurysm securing and continued for 90 days. ^[raw/articles/pubmed-40877496-galantamine-sah-safety-trial.md]
- mTBI choline alfoscerate study: 30 adults, 400 mg twice daily for 8 weeks, K-MMSE reassessed at 8 weeks. ^[raw/articles/pubmed-40353278-choline-alfoscerate-mtbi-recovery.md]
- [[lysolecithin]] trial: 23 healthy adults aged 40-74, 480 mg LPC for 8 weeks, memory primary endpoint, no between-group benefit. ^[raw/articles/pubmed-39896163-lysolecithin-cognition-choline.md]
- VU0467319 phase 1: 52 healthy volunteers, 60-600 mg single-ascending-dose cohorts plus a 120 mg food-effect study, with exploratory cognitive and ERP readouts 5 h post-dose. ^[raw/articles/pubmed-40598606-vu0467319-m1-pam-healthy-participants.md]
- Cobenfy / KarXT pivotal trials: 5-week inpatient schizophrenia studies with starting 50/20 mg BID, titration to 100/20 mg BID, and optional 125/30 mg BID. ^[raw/articles/fda-cobenfy-approval-2024.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-38691387-xanomeline-trospium-schizophrenia-rct.md]
- Emraclidine phase 1b: 5-40 mg ascending doses in stable schizophrenia, then 30 mg QD or 20 mg BID in the acute-schizophrenia part of the study. ^[raw/articles/pubmed-36528376-emraclidine-m4-phase1b.md]
- Emraclidine phase 2 registry: 10 mg QD and 30 mg QD in acute schizophrenia, with posted PANSS results but no cognition endpoint. ^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct05227690-emraclidine-phase2-results.md]
- Xanomeline AD trial: 25, 50, or 75 mg TID for 24 weeks, with a 4-week placebo washout, and a companion CNTB analysis that tracked reaction time and delayed verbal recall. ^[raw/articles/pubmed-9109749-xanomeline-alzheimer-cognitive-behavior.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-9876958-xanomeline-ad-cntb.md]

## Open Contradictions

- Nicotine clearly helps attention in abstinence contexts, but that may be withdrawal reversal rather than a direct enhancement effect.
- Varenicline has a small positive healthy-nonsmoker signal, yet the class has not reproduced that into a broad modern enhancer story.
- Galantamine looks promising in a responder-enriched subgroup, but the SAH and HIV trials keep it in the recovery/safety lane rather than a generic enhancement lane.
- Donepezil has some of the most interesting healthy-adjacent cholinesterase data, but the same packet includes direct healthy-older impairment and chronic-use nulls.
- Rivastigmine has clinical dementia evidence and one neuroimmune-context pilot, but no direct healthy-adult bridge.
- Choline alphoscerate is positive in amnestic MCI and mTBI, but that does not tell us whether healthy adults gain anything.
- [[phosphatidylcholine]] has a high-dose acute serial-learning signal and a recent egg-yolk choline product-matrix signal, but old lecithin healthy-adult and dementia sources stop it from becoming a general memory-enhancement claim.
- [[choline-bitartrate]] can move visuomotor precision while still failing memory and load-carriage cognition tasks; its free-choline TMAO signal makes product form a safety variable.
- [[lysolecithin]] shows that a newer choline-source formulation can move biomarkers without moving memory versus placebo in healthy adults.
- Muscarinic M1 PAMs are promising as a mechanistic class, but the current evidence is target engagement rather than efficacy.
- Cobenfy/KarXT is now a real antipsychotic, but its approval does not prove a cognition effect.
- The 2026 xanomeline-trospium review argues for pro-cognitive benefit across 24 studies, yet that claim is still downstream of symptom-first trials and older AD data.
- Emraclidine has posted phase 2 results, but the public registry view does not yet give a cognition-specific readout or a clear healthy-adult enhancement story.
- The newest active nicotine trial could sharpen the branch, but the sponsor profile means a future positive will need careful independent replication.
