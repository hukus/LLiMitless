# Non-Hallucinogenic Psychoplastogens - 2026 Scan

## Summary

The class is promising as a mechanistic idea, but the current cognition story is still almost entirely preclinical. I did not find a registered human cognition trial for tabernanthalog, 2-Br-LSD, or a comparable non-hallucinogenic psychoplastogen in the open sources I searched.

What changed since 2024:

- The evidence base now includes a 2025 review that found only five animal studies and one case report for non-hallucinogenic psychedelics in mood/anxiety, which reinforces how little human data exists.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40354769-non-hallucinogenic-psychedelics-mood-anxiety-systematic-review.md]
- A 2025 TBG paper clarified that cortical plasticity can occur without proximate glutamate burst or immediate early gene activation, keeping the class mechanistically interesting.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40760185-tabernanthalog-neuroplasticity-no-iea.md]
- A 2025 2-Br-LSD paper strengthened the case that a non-hallucinogenic LSD-like scaffold can show plasticity and stress-related behavioral signals in animals.^[raw/articles/pubmed-36884348-2-br-lsd-nonhallucinogenic-lsd-analog.md]
- A 2025 5-HT2A pharmacology paper cautioned that signaling efficacy, not a simple biased-agonism story, may separate psychedelic from non-psychedelic agonists.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40545270-5ht2a-efficacy-nonpsychedelic-drugs.md]

Classic psychedelic microdosing is a nearby but separate lane, and the newest pooled evidence does not turn it into a broad enhancer story. See [[classic-psychedelic-microdosing-vs-psychoplastogens]] for the controlled-evidence comparison.

## Evidence Tiering

- Meta-analysis: one human BDNF meta-analysis, but not specific to non-hallucinogenic compounds.
- Review: one 2025 systematic review of non-hallucinogenic psychedelics plus a 2025 mechanistic review of 5-HT2A agonists.
- Preclinical: TBG and 2-Br-LSD dominate the direct class evidence.
- Human cognition trials: none surfaced.

## Effect Size Snapshot

No human cognition effect size is available for this class.

The best available behavioral signal is rodent-only and state-dependent: TBG rescued stress-induced cognitive flexibility deficits after unpredictable mild stress.

## Safety / Adverse Events

- Human safety package: not established for cognition use.
- 2-Br-LSD preclinical profile suggests a possible reduction in hallucinatory burden and a lack of 5-HT2B agonism in vitro, but this is not the same as a completed human safety dossier.
- TBG safety in humans remains unproven in the open literature I reviewed.
- The class should be treated as research-stage, not consumer-ready.

## Responder Phenotypes

Unknown in humans.

The only plausible responder hints right now are preclinical and phenotype-like: stress load, cognitive flexibility impairment, and chronic stress exposure.

## Protocol Parameters

No validated human protocol.

Preclinical work uses rodent dosing paradigms and stress challenge models, which should not be translated directly into self-use or consumer protocol design.

## Open Contradictions

- "Non-hallucinogenic" is assay- and dose-dependent, so the label can fail at other concentrations or in humans.
- The field still has to prove that plasticity biomarkers translate to meaningful cognition gains.
- A lack of hallucinatory effects does not automatically imply lower risk or better efficacy.
