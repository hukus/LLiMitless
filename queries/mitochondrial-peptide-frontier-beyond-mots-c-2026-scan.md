# Mitochondrial Peptide Frontier Beyond MOTS-c 2026 Scan

## Summary

Humanin is the most cognition-relevant mitochondrial-derived peptide in the current wiki set, but the human evidence is still mostly genetic association and biomarker-linked resilience rather than a cognition intervention trial. The strongest brain-facing signals are humanin P3S in APOE4 carriers and humanin-associated cognitive aging, plus preclinical protection in age-related decline and TBI models.^[raw/articles/pubmed-30242290-humanin-age-related-cognitive-decline.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-38520065-humanin-p3s-apoe4-brain-pathology.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-37926362-humanin-tbi-related-cognitive-impairment.md]

SHLP2 is the family member that moved furthest beyond review-only status. The 2023 energy-homeostasis paper tied SHLP2 to hypothalamic neurons and metabolic control, and the 2024 Parkinson's paper linked a SHLP2 variant to reduced PD risk. SHLP6 then added a new preclinical, cognition-adjacent zebrafish toxicity paper, which makes the SHLP family feel more real biologically without changing the human-evidence gap. Elamipretide/SS-31 is now a real approved mitochondrial drug under the Forzinity name for Barth syndrome, but that approval is disease-specific and muscle-strength-focused; its cognition relevance is still preclinical, with a mouse LPS-memory paper and mitochondrial mechanism reviews, not human cognitive endpoints.^[raw/articles/pubmed-37468558-shlp2-energy-homeostasis.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-38167865-shlp2-parkinson-protective-factor.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40292418-shlp6-cu-induced-oxidative-stress-neurodegeneration.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-35499074-mdp-aging-healthspan-review.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40294492-elamipretide-mitochondrial-mechanism-review.md]^[raw/articles/pmc-6865061-elamipretide-ss31-lps-memory-neuroinflammation.md]^[raw/articles/fda-forzinity-elamipretide-approval.md]

The anecdote layer is also now explicit: SS-31 has one cautionary Reddit self-report, and humanin has one vendor-style guide with promotional framing. Both belong in the low-signal lane rather than the evidence lane.^[raw/articles/reddit-2026-04-30-ss31-undesirable-effects-thread.md]^[raw/articles/reddit-2026-04-30-humanin-longevity-signal-guide.md]

The right wiki interpretation is therefore a split branch: endogenous MDP biology on one side, approved/elamipretide disease use on another, MOTS-c metabolism on a third, and cognition claims kept separate until human intervention data appear. This is one of the more serious peptide frontiers in the wiki because the family has real biology, one approved drug, one healthy-aging pilot with cognitive secondary endpoints, and a stronger Humanin brain-resilience story than most peptide classes. It is still not part of the strongest nootropic anecdote-priority cluster: Humanin, SHLP2, SHLP6, and elamipretide/SS-31 should be tracked as biology/translation nodes, while [[mots-c]] remains metabolism-first and safety-gated.

## New Since Last Pass

- Humanin moved from a generic aging signal into a sharper APOE4-resilience story when the P3S variant paper linked the peptide to longevity in APOE4 carriers and reduced amyloid-beta accumulation in an APOE4 mouse model.^[raw/articles/pubmed-38520065-humanin-p3s-apoe4-brain-pathology.md]
- Humanin also gained another cognition-positive preclinical anchor in a TBI model, which strengthens the "brain protection" side of the family without changing the lack of human intervention proof.^[raw/articles/pubmed-37926362-humanin-tbi-related-cognitive-impairment.md]
- SHLP2 stopped being a pure review footnote once the energy-homeostasis and Parkinson's papers landed. It now has a real translational phenotype map, but the evidence is still metabolic and neurodegeneration-adjacent rather than cognitive.^[raw/articles/pubmed-37468558-shlp2-energy-homeostasis.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-38167865-shlp2-parkinson-protective-factor.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-30310092-shlp2-macular-degeneration.md]
- Elamipretide changed regulatory status in 2025: FDA granted accelerated approval for Barth syndrome, so the compound is no longer just an investigational mitochondria-targeting peptide. That approval does not extend to cognition.^[raw/articles/fda-forzinity-elamipretide-approval.md]
- The SHAPE healthy-aging trial started in late 2025 and is the first human elamipretide study in this branch with cognitive function as a secondary endpoint. It is open-label and single-arm, so it will be useful for safety and signal hunting, not definitive efficacy.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct07275424-elamipretide-shape.md]
- The 2025 neurodegeneration review is still mainly a roadmap paper: it expands the MDP neuroprotection narrative, but it does not add clinical cognition data.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40715951-mdp-neurodegeneration-review.md]
- No visible Humanin or SHLP2 intervention program surfaced in the current pass, which keeps the family split clear: Humanin is biologically stronger for cognition, while elamipretide is stronger for formal clinical translation.

## What Changed Since 2024

- SHLP6 added a fresh preclinical signal in zebrafish, which is enough to keep SHLPs in the map but not enough to promote the family into a cognition branch.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40292418-shlp6-cu-induced-oxidative-stress-neurodegeneration.md]
- A 2026 approval review adds a small but useful contradiction check: the randomized crossover program did not significantly improve 6-minute walk or fatigue, which keeps the approved-drug branch disease-specific rather than enhancement-forward.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41260682-elamipretide-first-approval-review.md]
- The anecdote layer is now explicit too: SS-31 has one cautionary Reddit self-report, and humanin has one vendor-style guide with promotional framing. Both belong in the low-signal lane.^[raw/articles/reddit-2026-04-30-ss31-undesirable-effects-thread.md]^[raw/articles/reddit-2026-04-30-humanin-longevity-signal-guide.md]

## Evidence Tiering

- Review/meta: the 2022 family review and the 2025 neurodegeneration review.
- Human observational/genetic: humanin rs2854128 and cognitive aging; APOE4 carrier enrichment for humanin P3S.
- Human regulatory/clinical: elamipretide is FDA-approved for Barth syndrome, with approved non-cognition use.
- Preclinical: humanin lifespan/healthspan work, humanin TBI model, SHLP6 zebrafish toxicity model, SS-31 LPS memory model.
- Anecdote/vendor: one SS-31 adverse-experience Reddit thread and one humanin vendor-style guide, both low-signal and not outcome evidence.

## Effect Size Snapshot

- Humanin: no intervention effect size in humans. The best human signal is association with cognitive age and genotype-linked resilience.
- SHLP2: no cognition effect size in humans. The best human signals are metabolic and PD-adjacent, not memory or executive function.
- SHLP6: no cognition effect size in humans. The only signal is zebrafish locomotion, AChE, and stress-response biology.
- Elamipretide: no cognition effect size in humans yet. The approved clinical effect is on Barth syndrome muscle strength, while SHAPE is still pending.
- SHLPs: no usable cognition effect size surfaced.

## Safety / Adverse Events

- Elamipretide now has the clearest safety anchor in the branch: once-daily subcutaneous use, mostly injection-site reactions, with serious reactions also reported in FDA material.^[raw/articles/fda-forzinity-elamipretide-approval.md]
- Humanin and SHLP2 have no validated consumer or cognition protocol, so safety remains largely preclinical or absent from the human literature reviewed here.
- The SS-31 anecdote thread adds a cautionary self-report of lethargy, heart-rate increase, and electrolyte abnormalities, but it is confounded and cannot be generalized.^[raw/articles/reddit-2026-04-30-ss31-undesirable-effects-thread.md]
- Because elamipretide is now an approved drug for a rare mitochondrial disease, it should not be treated as a research peptide in the same bucket as unregulated nootropic claims.
- Route MDP and SS-31 reports through [[safety-interaction-matrix-for-multi-modal-stacks]] before treating fatigue, exercise tolerance, anti-aging clarity, or secondary cognitive outcomes as direct cognition. The key split is endogenous biomarker/genotype biology versus administered peptide exposure, approved drug versus gray-market source, open-label aging pilot versus placebo-controlled cognition evidence, and metabolic or muscle-state mediation.

## Responder Phenotypes

- Humanin appears most interesting in aging, APOE4-linked resilience, and cognition-vulnerability phenotypes.
- The P3S variant may matter most in APOE4 carriers, especially in longevity-enriched cohorts.
- SHLP2 appears most interesting in metabolic and PD-risk phenotypes rather than cognition-first cohorts.
- SHLP6 currently looks like a family-advance marker only, with no responder phenotype beyond copper-toxicity biology in zebrafish.
- Elamipretide responders are currently Barth syndrome and primary mitochondrial/myopathic phenotypes, not healthy adults.
- SHAPE is currently enriching for healthy older adults with lower aerobic capacity / lower mitochondrial function, so it is a phenotype-first safety and signal study rather than a broad enhancement trial.

## Protocol Parameters

- Elamipretide / Forzinity: 40 mg subcutaneous once daily in the approved Barth syndrome program and in the supporting trial ecosystem.^[raw/articles/fda-forzinity-elamipretide-approval.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40294492-elamipretide-mitochondrial-mechanism-review.md]
- SHAPE / elamipretide healthy-aging pilot: daily subcutaneous dosing for 4 weeks in adults ages 65-80, with cognitive function and biomarkers as secondary endpoints; the exact dose amount was not visible in the ClinicalTrials.gov snippet I reviewed.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct07275424-elamipretide-shape.md]
- No humanin or SHLP2 cognition protocol surfaced.
- No SHLP6 human protocol surfaced.
- No validated home protocol surfaced for SS-31 cognition use.

## Open Contradictions

- The family has strong mechanistic appeal and a real human regulatory foothold through elamipretide, but the cognition signal is still much weaker than the biochemistry signal.
- Humanin has human cognitive associations, but not a treatment trial.
- SHLP6 is still one preclinical paper, so it should not be promoted above family-level curiosity.
- Elamipretide is approved for a mitochondrial disease, but approval should not be generalized to cognition or healthy enhancement.
