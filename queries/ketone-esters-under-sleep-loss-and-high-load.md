# Ketone Esters Under Sleep Loss and High Load

This page tracks whether exogenous ketones, especially ketone monoesters and ketone diesters, do anything meaningful for attention, memory, and fatigue resilience when cognition is stressed by sleep loss, hypoxia, or repeated high-load work. The newest peer-reviewed Frontiers meta-analysis now supersedes the earlier 2025 medRxiv/PubMed preprint: the pooled direction stayed positive, but the final article expanded the evidence set to 38 studies and 41 protocols and lifted the pooled effect to SMD 0.29 from the preprint's SMD 0.26. The practical branch is still dose-sensitive, formulation-sensitive, and heterogeneous enough that a broad enhancer claim would still be too strong. A 2024 postexercise trial in trained females adds another selective high-load positive signal: KME + carbohydrate improved postexercise vigilance and flanker measures, but not cycling performance or the full battery. The newest hypoxia papers also make the state split explicit: ketones can help under resting acute hypoxia, but the same branch goes null when hypoxia is paired with load-bearing exercise.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41001501-exogenous-ketone-bodies-cognition-systematic-review-meta-analysis.md]^[raw/articles/medrxiv-2025-09-17-25335999-exogenous-ketone-bodies-cognition-systematic-review-meta-analysis.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-34605026-ketone-monoester-obesity-cognition-cbf.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-38051034-ketone-monoester-carbohydrate-postexercise-trained-females.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-36297060-ketone-ester-simulated-soccer-mental-fatigue.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-29451991-ketone-salts-high-intensity-exercise-null.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39190580-ketone-monoester-hypoxic-exposure-resting.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40315254-ketone-ester-hypoxia-null.md]

What changed since 2024:

- The direct sleep-loss question is now on a live registry: `NCT05519644` explicitly tests whether twice-daily ketone diester improves cognitive, marksmanship, reaction-time, physical, mood, glucose/ketone, and blood-marker outcomes during 4 nights of 50% sleep restriction. It remains recruiting, with estimated completion in 2026-08 and no posted outcomes.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct05519644-ketone-conferred-resiliency-against-sleep-restriction.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct05519644-stak-sleep-ketone-ester-record-2026-05-04.md]
- The 2025 dose-response CBF trial changed the mechanistic story: in healthy young adults, low- and high-dose ketone monoester lowered resting CBF in a dose-dependent way and also lowered end-tidal CO2.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40349325-acute-ketone-monoester-ingestion-lowers-resting-cerebral-blood-flow.md]
- The 2024 trained-female exercise trial adds a smaller but useful high-load positive: KME + carbohydrate improved postexercise vigilance and executive-control metrics, but did not improve cycling performance or the full cognitive battery.^[raw/articles/pubmed-38051034-ketone-monoester-carbohydrate-postexercise-trained-females.md]
- The resting acute-hypoxia crossover adds the clearest challenge-state rescue signal in the branch: ketone monoester attenuated code-substitution decline and oxygen-saturation loss during severe hypoxia at rest.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39190580-ketone-monoester-hypoxic-exposure-resting.md]
- The ketone meta-analysis moved from a 2025 medRxiv/PubMed preprint to a 2026 Frontiers article without reversing direction: the preprint reported 29 studies, 18 meta-analysis protocols, 1,347 participants, and SMD 0.26, while the final paper expanded to 38 studies, 41 protocols, 1,602 participants, and SMD 0.29.^[raw/articles/medrxiv-2025-09-17-25335999-exogenous-ketone-bodies-cognition-systematic-review-meta-analysis.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41001501-exogenous-ketone-bodies-cognition-systematic-review-meta-analysis.md]
- The strongest published positive signal still comes from adults with obesity, where 14 days of ketone monoester improved cerebral blood flow and digit-symbol substitution performance.^[raw/articles/pubmed-34605026-ketone-monoester-obesity-cognition-cbf.md]
- The high-load literature remains mixed: a simulated soccer + mental-fatigue trial showed a small attenuation of reaction-time decrement, while repeated-Wingate and acute-hypoxia studies were null for cognition.^[raw/articles/pubmed-36297060-ketone-ester-simulated-soccer-mental-fatigue.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-29451991-ketone-salts-high-intensity-exercise-null.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40315254-ketone-ester-hypoxia-null.md]
- Safety evidence is materially better than it was a few years ago: short-term sustained ketosis looked tolerable in healthy adults, and a 12-week older-adult pilot extended the safety window.^[raw/articles/pubmed-31655093-ketone-monoester-28d-safety.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39137624-ketone-ester-older-adults-safety.md]
- The `NCT06032156` kCBF registry record is still an unknown-status / no-results watch item with estimated 2024 completion dates, so use the published acute CBF paper for interpretation and keep the registry in surveillance rather than treating it as a separate posted result.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct06032156-ketone-dose-cerebral-blood-flow-cognition.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct06032156-kcbf-ketone-dose-record-2026-05-04.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40349325-acute-ketone-monoester-ingestion-lowers-resting-cerebral-blood-flow.md]
- The community/product layer is visible but mixed: a Ketone-IQ ultramarathon thread includes sustained-energy/focus reports, no-effect reports, taste/cost complaints, and explicit reminders that ketone diol product claims should not be treated as ketone-ester trial evidence.^[raw/articles/reddit-2023-11-02-ketone-iq-ultramarathon-thread.md]

## Evidence Tiering

- Registry-level direct sleep-loss evidence: `NCT05519644`, still recruiting/no-results as of 2026-05-04.
- Published acute CBF dose-response evidence: resting CBF falls after ketone monoester in healthy young adults.
- Registry-level dose-response evidence: `NCT06032156`, unknown-status/no-results, now best treated as a registry companion to the published CBF trial rather than an efficacy source.
- Published pooled human evidence now includes both a preprint and a peer-reviewed meta-analysis; the final paper is modestly stronger, but the branch still needs direct sleep-loss results before the practical claim is upgraded further.
- Positive human cognition evidence in a metabolic phenotype: obesity crossover trial.
- Weak positive human evidence under induced mental fatigue and intermittent exercise: simulated soccer study.
- Negative physiologic evidence in healthy young adults: acute ketone monoester lowered resting CBF rather than raising it.
- Null human evidence under repeated high-intensity exercise and acute hypoxia.
- Safety/tolerability evidence in healthy adults and healthy older adults.
- Anecdotal / market evidence: mixed endurance and focus salience, but mostly product-specific and formulation-confounded.

## Effect Size Snapshot

- Obesity crossover trial: DSST improved by `+2.7 correct responses`; extracranial flow metrics also improved.^[raw/articles/pubmed-34605026-ketone-monoester-obesity-cognition-cbf.md]
- Trained-female postexercise trial: PVT speed improved by about `4%`, and flanker reaction time, speed, and correct responses improved by about `14%`, `15%`, and `13%` respectively, but the 10-km time trial did not improve.^[raw/articles/pubmed-38051034-ketone-monoester-carbohydrate-postexercise-trained-females.md]
- Acute CBF trial: low-dose ketone lowered CBF at 45 min; high-dose lowered CBF at 45 and 120 min, with dose-dependent end-tidal CO2 reductions.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40349325-acute-ketone-monoester-ingestion-lowers-resting-cerebral-blood-flow.md]
- Resting acute hypoxia trial: 6.8 more correct responses per minute on DANA code substitution, plus attenuation of oxygen-saturation decline during hypoxia.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39190580-ketone-monoester-hypoxic-exposure-resting.md]
- Meta-analysis: pooled cognitive benefit SMD `0.29` in the 2026 Frontiers article, versus SMD `0.26` in the 2025 preprint; the final paper used 29 protocols in meta-analysis and showed a positive daily-dose association.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41001501-exogenous-ketone-bodies-cognition-systematic-review-meta-analysis.md]^[raw/articles/medrxiv-2025-09-17-25335999-exogenous-ketone-bodies-cognition-systematic-review-meta-analysis.md]
- Simulated soccer + mental fatigue: ketone ester reduced the decrement in choice-reaction performance (`1.3%` vs `3.4%` reduction in correct answers).^[raw/articles/pubmed-36297060-ketone-ester-simulated-soccer-mental-fatigue.md]
- Repeated Wingates: no cognitive improvement.^[raw/articles/pubmed-29451991-ketone-salts-high-intensity-exercise-null.md]
- Hypoxia plus exercise: oxygen desaturation improved, but cognition did not.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40315254-ketone-ester-hypoxia-null.md]
- Acute hypoxia: no cognitive improvement.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40315254-ketone-ester-hypoxia-null.md]
- Direct sleep-loss effect size: not yet available in published or posted results.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct05519644-stak-sleep-ketone-ester-record-2026-05-04.md]

## Safety/Adverse Events

- 28-day ketone monoester study: mild nausea was reported, but weight, glucose, lipids, electrolytes, gases, and kidney function stayed normal.^[raw/articles/pubmed-31655093-ketone-monoester-28d-safety.md]
- 12-week older-adult pilot: supports tolerability, but cognition endpoints were not the main objective.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39137624-ketone-ester-older-adults-safety.md]
- Hypoxia study: no cognition benefit, and the exercise-performance tradeoff looked unfavorable.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40315254-ketone-ester-hypoxia-null.md]
- Practical implication: ketone esters appear tolerable enough for controlled trials, but the GI burden, cost, taste, product identity, and protocol burden are real.^[raw/articles/reddit-2023-11-02-ketone-iq-ultramarathon-thread.md]

## Responder Phenotypes

- Best-supported responder phenotype so far: adults with obesity / metabolic risk.^[raw/articles/pubmed-34605026-ketone-monoester-obesity-cognition-cbf.md]
- Possible responder state: repeated intermittent exercise after mental fatigue.^[raw/articles/pubmed-36297060-ketone-ester-simulated-soccer-mental-fatigue.md]
- Unresolved phenotype: healthy adults under sleep restriction.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct05519644-stak-sleep-ketone-ester-record-2026-05-04.md]
- Weak or negative contexts: repeated Wingates, acute severe hypoxia, and unselected healthy young men with ketone salts.^[raw/articles/pubmed-29451991-ketone-salts-high-intensity-exercise-null.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40315254-ketone-ester-hypoxia-null.md]

## Protocol Parameters

- 25 g ketone monoester, three times daily for 14 days, in the obesity crossover trial.
- 375 mg/kg ketone monoester with 6% carbohydrate in three boluses during the trained-female exercise trial.
- 25 g ketone monoester after mental-fatigue induction, before a 45-minute simulated soccer match.
- 11.38 g ketone salt before repeated Wingates in the null exercise trial.
- 25 mL ketone monoester, three times daily for 28 days, in the 2019 safety study.
- 25 g bis-octanoyl (R)-1,3-butanediol daily for 12 weeks in the older-adult pilot.
- 0.4 g/kg ketone monoester in the acute hypoxia trial.
- Twice-daily C6 ketone diester during four nights of 50% sleep restriction in the STAK-Sleep registry.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct05519644-stak-sleep-ketone-ester-record-2026-05-04.md]

## Open Contradictions

- The obesity trial is positive, but the exercise and hypoxia trials are null.
- The trained-female exercise trial is positive on select postexercise cognitive measures, but it does not generalize to endurance performance.
- The acute healthy-young-adult CBF trial went in the opposite physiologic direction from the obesity trial, so CBF is not a simple blanket mechanism for the branch.
- Resting acute hypoxia and hypoxia plus exercise now split in opposite directions for cognition, which strongly argues against a blanket hypoxia claim.
- Ketone monoester, ketone diester, ketone diol products, ketone salts, and ketogenic diets are not interchangeable, so formulation and product identity may be driving part of the disagreement.
- The direct sleep-loss trial is still recruiting/no-results, so the headline question remains unresolved.
- The pooled review did not find clean subgroup separation by health status or acute stressor, even though the overall effect was positive.
- The signal may be more about modest, dose-dependent state support than true broad cognitive enhancement.
