# Dopamine-State Precision Pharmacology 2026 Scan

## Summary

D1 positive allosteric modulators and TAAR1 agonists sit closer to salience / wakefulness / psychiatric-symptom modulation than to broad healthy-adult cognition enhancement. [[mevidalen]] is the cleanest D1-PAM example: in Lewy body dementia it missed the primary and secondary cognition endpoints, but improved motor symptoms and clinical global impression, with more blood-pressure and cardiovascular burden at the highest dose.^[raw/articles/pubmed-34859493-mevidalen-lbd-phase-2.md]^[raw/articles/pubchem-mevidalen.md]

[[ulotaront]] was initially framed as a non-D2 TAAR1 agent with an encouraging phase 2 schizophrenia signal, but the later DIAMOND 1 and DIAMOND 2 phase 3 studies failed their primary endpoints.^[raw/articles/pmc-10465394-ulotaront-taar1-review.md]^[raw/articles/otsuka-us-diamond-ulotaront-phase3-failure.md] A 2025 dose-response meta-analysis then found only a small symptom signal, centered around 100 mg, with no clear global-severity dose-response and more anxiety-related adverse events at lower tested doses.^[raw/articles/pmc-12421877-ulotaront-dose-response-meta-analysis.md] The mechanism remains interesting for dopamine/serotonin/glutamate modulation, but the human record still points more strongly to disease-context symptom effects than to durable cognition enhancement.

What changed since 2024:

- Ulotaront moved from "promising TAAR1 schizophrenia candidate" to a phase 3 failure story, even though sponsor development has continued into a new 2026 phase 3 trial.
- Mevidalen stayed alive after the Lewy body dementia cognition miss and is now being tested again in Alzheimer's disease.
- Standalone [[mevidalen]] and [[ulotaront]] entities now make the molecule-level failure modes explicit.
- No healthy-adult cognition benchmark emerged for either class, so neither should be promoted out of the comparator lane.

## Effect Size Snapshot

- Mevidalen PRESENCE: cognition endpoints were negative; motor symptoms improved at 10, 30, and 75 mg, and clinical global impression improved at 30 mg and 75 mg.^[raw/articles/pubmed-34859493-mevidalen-lbd-phase-2.md]
- Ulotaront phase 2: PANSS total improvement versus placebo was reported with effect size 0.45 in the sponsor-authored review.^[raw/articles/pmc-10465394-ulotaront-taar1-review.md]
- Ulotaront dose-response meta-analysis: the 100 mg dose showed the greatest PANSS total improvement, with a small effect size (SMD -0.23), PANSS negative symptoms also favored higher doses, and CGI-S did not show a clear dose-response.^[raw/articles/pmc-12421877-ulotaront-dose-response-meta-analysis.md]
- DIAMOND 1: placebo-adjusted week 6 PANSS total means were -16.9 and -19.6 for 50 and 75 mg versus -19.3 for placebo, so the trial did not separate on the primary endpoint.^[raw/articles/otsuka-us-diamond-ulotaront-phase3-failure.md]
- DIAMOND 2: placebo-adjusted week 6 PANSS total means were -16.4 and -18.1 for 75 and 100 mg versus -14.3 for placebo, again without a statistically significant primary-endpoint win.^[raw/articles/otsuka-us-diamond-ulotaront-phase3-failure.md]

## Safety/Adverse Events

- Mevidalen showed dose-related blood-pressure increases and more cardiovascular serious adverse events at 75 mg.^[raw/articles/pubmed-34859493-mevidalen-lbd-phase-2.md]
- The ulotaront phase 2 review reported a numerically lower adverse-event rate than placebo and good tolerability, but that positive safety framing did not translate into phase 3 efficacy success.^[raw/articles/pmc-10465394-ulotaront-taar1-review.md]^[raw/articles/otsuka-us-diamond-ulotaront-phase3-failure.md]
- For both classes, activation, insomnia, and psychiatric-state shifts remain plausible practical concerns even when the direct trial adverse-event signal is not dramatic.

## Responder Phenotypes

- Mevidalen looks phenotype-bound to Lewy body dementia / Parkinson disease dementia / dementia with Lewy bodies and possibly broader AD-dementia symptom management, not to healthy enhancement.
- Ulotaront's best signal sits in acutely psychotic schizophrenia rather than in a cognition-only phenotype.
- Neither class has a validated healthy-adult responder atlas yet.

## Protocol Parameters

- Mevidalen PRESENCE: 10 mg, 30 mg, or 75 mg once daily for 12 weeks in LBD.^[raw/articles/pubmed-34859493-mevidalen-lbd-phase-2.md]
- Mevidalen AD registry: 26-week phase 2, endpoints spanning cognition, daily activity, sleep, irritability, and anxiety.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct06538116-mevidalen-ad.md]
- Ulotaront phase 2 / review framing: 50 to 75 mg once daily in acute schizophrenia, with an open-label extension; DIAMOND 2 moved to 75 mg and 100 mg once daily.^[raw/articles/pmc-10465394-ulotaront-taar1-review.md]^[raw/articles/otsuka-us-diamond-ulotaront-phase3-failure.md]
- Ulotaront phase 3 registry: acutely psychotic schizophrenia study remains recruiting in 2026.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct06894212-ulotaront-phase3-schizophrenia.md]

## Open Contradictions

- Sponsor narrative: both programs still have mechanistic appeal around dopamine-state modulation and symptom relief.
- Human outcome record: mevidalen missed cognition in LBD, and ulotaront missed phase 3 schizophrenia primaries.
- The 2025 ulotaront dose-response meta-analysis complicates the flat-null story slightly, but only by showing a small symptom signal, not a cognition signal.
- Pipeline reality: both classes remain under active study, so negative results did not end development.
- Interpretation risk: improvements in motor function, global impression, or PANSS should not be relabeled as broad cognition enhancement without a separate cognitive readout.
