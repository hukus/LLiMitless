# Transcranial Random Noise Stimulation 2026 Scan

Transcranial random noise stimulation (tRNS) now has enough human literature to stand as its own branch rather than being buried inside a generic "other stimulation" bucket. The current picture is mixed but no longer narrow: a 2024 healthy-adult working-memory RCT found an online DLPFC benefit, a 2025 attention-bias study found a left-DLPFC effect in anxious healthy men, a 2026 schizophrenia crossover found executive and affective gains, and a 2026 psychiatric systematic review still points to phenotype-specific rather than modality-wide benefit. The branch also has clean boundary cases, including a 2025 numerical-cognition null and a preregistered motion-processing null, so tRNS should read as task- and phenotype-sensitive stochastic resonance rather than broad enhancement.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39402554-trns-working-memory-healthy-individuals.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41387754-trns-attention-bias-negative-emotional-stimuli.md]^[raw/articles/pmc-12949120-trns-schizophrenia-bilateral-prefrontal.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41582468-trns-psychiatric-systematic-review.md]^[raw/articles/nature-s41598-025-21890-x-trns-numerical-acuity.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40669128-trns-motion-processing-null-registered-report.md]

## What Changed Since 2024

- tRNS moved from a niche experimental idea to a small but explicit human evidence cluster with one healthy working-memory positive study, one emotion-attention positive study, one clinical schizophrenia positive study, and two null boundary cases.
- The best healthy-adult signal is online and task-specific, not a broad after-the-fact enhancement effect.
- The 2026 schizophrenia study makes bilateral DLPFC tRNS look more plausible as an adjunctive clinical tool than as a general healthy-adult nootropic.
- The 2025 attention-bias study adds a phenotype clue: anxious or threat-biased participants may be more likely to show a measurable effect.
- The 2025 numerical-cognition null and the preregistered motion null prevent the branch from being overgeneralized into "tRNS helps cognition everywhere."
- The 2025 perceptual-motor paper adds a performance-adjacent positive signal, but it still belongs below the executive-function evidence.

## Top Sources

1. [PubMed 41582468](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41582468/) - 2026 systematic review of tRNS in psychiatric disorders, 22 studies / 642 participants, PROSPERO CRD420251040192.
2. [PubMed 39402554](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39402554/) - 2024 randomized controlled trial in 117 healthy adults showing online DLPFC working-memory benefit.
3. [PubMed 41387754](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41387754/) - 2025 crossover study in anxious healthy men showing reduced negative-emotion attention bias.
4. [PMC 12949120](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12949120/) - 2026 schizophrenia crossover showing planning, working-memory, and affective gains.
5. [Nature Scientific Reports](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-025-21890-x) - 2025 numerical-acuity RCT with a null primary result.
6. [PubMed 40487366](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40487366/) - 2025 DLPFC tRNS study linking divergent thinking to alpha coherence.
7. [PubMed 40669128](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40669128/) - 2025 preregistered motion-processing null.
8. [PubMed 39901001](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39901001/) - 2025 perceptual-motor training RCT with performance-adjacent positives.

## Evidence Tiering

| Tier | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Systematic review | Mixed psychiatric-disorder signal, with ADHD and dyslexia clearest, schizophrenia somewhat positive, depression mixed |
| Healthy-adult RCT | Online DLPFC working-memory benefit during stimulation, but not a broad post-stimulation enhancer |
| Emotion-attention crossover | Left DLPFC tRNS reduced negative-emotion attention bias in anxious healthy men |
| Clinical crossover | Bilateral DLPFC tRNS improved planning and working memory in schizophrenia, with affective benefit |
| Creativity / network study | Divergent-thinking gains tracked with bilateral DLPFC alpha coherence |
| Numerical-cognition RCT | No significant accuracy or reaction-time effect on multidirectional number-line estimation |
| Registered report | No facilitatory effect on motion processing over hMT+ |
| Perceptual-motor RCT | Training-plus-tRNS improved movement-time components, but this sits adjacent to cognition rather than inside it |

## Effect Size Snapshot

- Healthy working memory: tRNS improved d-prime during stimulation versus sham in the 117-participant healthy-adult RCT.
- Attention bias: left DLPFC tRNS produced a corrected p = 0.047 effect with Cohen's d = 0.72 versus sham in anxious healthy men.
- Schizophrenia: tRNS improved planning and also improved working-memory accuracy/strategy score versus sham.
- Numerical acuity: no significant accuracy or reaction-time effect after covariate adjustment.
- Motion processing: preregistered null, so no recovery of a general perceptual-enhancement effect.
- Net read: the largest individual healthy-adult effect in the current branch is the attention-bias result, but the broader healthy-adult WM result is still online and task-specific rather than durable and domain-general.

## Safety / Adverse Events

- The 2026 review reported tRNS as generally well-tolerated with mild, transient adverse effects.
- The 2026 schizophrenia crossover reported minimal side effects and effective sham blinding.
- The newer positive studies did not surface a serious safety signal, but they also do not justify casual home-use framing.
- The practical safety issue is interpretive overreach: the branch has multiple narrow positives, but the nulls show that target and task matter.

## Responder Phenotypes

- Healthy adults doing high-demand online working-memory tasks.
- Anxiety-linked or negative-bias phenotypes for left-DLPFC emotion-attention work.
- Schizophrenia patients with executive-function deficits.
- Possibly ADHD and dyslexia, per the 2026 review.
- Not obviously a fit for general numerical acuity or motion-processing enhancement in healthy adults.

## Protocol Parameters

- Most studies still cluster around 20-minute sessions.
- Healthy working memory used left DLPFC stimulation at 2 mA during dual n-back.
- Emotion-attention used left DLPFC tRNS with a 7-day washout in a crossover design.
- Schizophrenia used bilateral DLPFC, 2 mA, 100-640 Hz, 20 min, with extracephalic returns and 1-week intervals.
- The numerical-cognition RCT used single-session tRNS over IPS or DLPFC with a 2x2x2 crossover design.
- The branch still needs cleaner reporting on spectrum, target, control class, and whether the task is online or offline.

## Open Contradictions

- Healthy working-memory positive versus numerical-cognition null.
- Emotion-attention positive versus no significant advantage over tDCS in that same study.
- Schizophrenia positive versus broad healthy-adult enhancement still unproven.
- Creativity and attention signals versus preregistered motion null.
- Performance-adjacent motor-learning gains versus the need for proof that they transfer to core cognition.
