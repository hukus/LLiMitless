# GHRH / Growth-Hormone Secretagogue Cognition 2026 Scan

## Summary

This branch is best read as a state-restoration and aging-axis story, not as a clean nootropic story. The strongest formal cognition signal is the 2006 [[ghrh]] trial in healthy older adults, which reported better executive-function and composite cognition outcomes after 6 months. A later 2012 controlled [[tesamorelin]] trial extended that signal into adults with MCI and healthy older adults, again with favorable cognition and substantial IGF-1 target engagement. The older [[ghrh]] sleep-disturbance registry helps explain why the class was originally framed as a restorative hormone signal rather than a pure enhancer. The physiology side is now better anchored too: a 1996 [[ibutamoren]] / MK-677 trial showed robust GH/IGF-1 target engagement, and a 2008 healthy-older-adult MK-677 trial increased fat-free mass without improving strength or function. The newer 2025 HIV + abdominal-obesity [[tesamorelin]] study is much less supportive: it showed waist reduction and IGF-1 rise, but the between-group cognitive difference was not significant, so the branch now looks phenotype-bound and underpowered rather than broadly positive.

[[ibutamoren]] / MK-677, [[cjc-1295]], [[hexarelin]], and [[ipamorelin]] mostly add target-engagement, sleep, or physiology context. MK-677 improved sleep architecture in healthy young and older adults, increased GH/IGF-1 in healthy elderly, and increased fat-free mass in healthy older adults, but a much larger AD trial found no clinical cognitive benefit and the body-composition trial did not improve strength or function despite target engagement. [[cjc-1295]] and [[hexarelin]] show GH/IGF-1 release in humans, and [[ipamorelin]] is still mostly pharmacology/preclinical scaffolding. None of those agents has a modern human cognition package that justifies broad nootropic language.

Safety is not trivial. Tesamorelin's current label includes elevated IGF-1, fluid retention, glucose intolerance/diabetes, hypersensitivity, injection-site reactions, and increased neoplasm risk warnings. That makes the branch more suitable for medically supervised metabolic or rescue contexts than for casual self-experimentation. The peptide-forum signal is strong enough to merit an anecdote ledger, but it clusters around sleep, recovery, physique, and subjective energy rather than objective cognition.

## Evidence Tiering

- `Human RCT, cognition-positive`: the 2006 [[ghrh]] healthy-older-adults trial and the 2012 controlled trial both reported favorable cognition effects, especially executive function, with concurrent IGF-1 increases.
- `Human RCT, cognition-mixed`: the 2025 HIV + abdominal-obesity tesamorelin trial showed a within-arm trend but no significant between-group cognitive benefit.
- `Human RCT, cognition-negative`: the 2008 MK-677 AD trial increased IGF-1 but did not improve clinical or cognitive outcomes.
- `Human RCT, physiology-positive but function-null`: the 1996 and 2008 MK-677 healthy-older-adult trials improved GH/IGF-1 and fat-free mass but did not improve strength or function.
- `Human physiology / target engagement`: [[cjc-1295]], [[hexarelin]], and [[ipamorelin]] mostly stay in GH/IGF-1 release or receptor-selectivity territory.
- `Sleep / state-restoration`: MK-677 improved sleep architecture in healthy young and older adults, which may explain some subjective forum enthusiasm without proving cognition enhancement.
- `Sleep / state-restoration`: the older [[ghrh]] sleep-disturbance registry and MK-677 sleep study both point to a restoration phenotype, which helps explain why the branch should not be read as pure enhancement.
- `Anecdote / community signal`: the BodyHackGuide and PeptideGuide threads cluster around sleep, recovery, and body composition, with a separate safety outlier for the blend thread.
- `Registry`: NCT02572323 is the freshness anchor for the tesamorelin cognition branch.
- `Regulatory`: the current EGRIFTA label is a central safety source and should override casual supplement framing.

## Effect Size Snapshot

- 2006 [[ghrh]] trial: better performance IQ and multiple executive-function measures after 6 months, with IGF-1 rise and body-fat reduction.
- 2012 controlled trial: favorable cognition at 20 weeks, executive-function benefit, verbal-memory trend, and 117% IGF-1 increase.
- 2025 tesamorelin HIV trial: cognition trend within tesamorelin arm but no significant between-group difference; waist circumference improved by a median 2.7 cm versus standard of care.
- 1996 MK-677 trial: 97% rise in mean 24-hour GH at 25 mg/day, plus IGF-I into the young-adult range.
- 2008 MK-677 body-composition trial: fat-free mass increased, but strength and function did not.
- 2008 MK-677 AD trial: strong IGF-1 increase but no cognitive or functional benefit over 12 months.

## Safety / Adverse Events

- Tesamorelin label warnings are the core safety anchor: neoplasms, elevated IGF-1, fluid retention, glucose intolerance/diabetes, hypersensitivity, and injection-site reactions.
- The 2012 controlled trial reported frequent but mostly mild adverse events, including local skin reactions, arthralgias, gastrointestinal upset, paresthesias, weight gain, and fluid retention.
- The 1996 and 2008 MK-677 trials add glucose and insulin-sensitivity concerns, plus edema and appetite increases.
- The Reddit blend thread adds a sourcing/safety outlier with a severe flushing/tachycardia/chest-tightness event.
- MK-677 sleep and AD studies point to a class that meaningfully shifts somatotropic physiology, so glucose and edema monitoring matter even when the intended goal is cognition.
- For self-experimentation, this is not a low-risk, over-the-counter class.

## Responder Phenotypes

- Healthy older adults with age-related somatotropic decline were the original positive phenotype.
- Adults with MCI may still be a plausible enriched phenotype, but the newer evidence suggests effect size is fragile and sensitive to endpoint selection.
- HIV + abdominal obesity is the clearest modern metabolic rescue phenotype, but the cognitive readout is not yet strong enough to call that an enhancement signal.
- Sleep-fragility or sleep-disruption phenotypes may explain the strongest subjective peptide-forum enthusiasm, especially for MK-677-like compounds.

## Protocol Parameters

- GHRH / tesamorelin 2006 trial: daily subcutaneous dosing for 6 months in healthy older adults.
- GHRH sleep-disturbance registry: older-adult GHRH study focused on sleep outcomes, with cognition later emerging as a linked publication rather than the original primary aim.
- 2012 controlled trial: tesamorelin 1 mg/day by bedtime subcutaneous injection for 20 weeks, then 10-week washout.
- 2025 HIV trial: tesamorelin 2 mg/day subcutaneously for 6 months in an open-label randomized comparison.
- 1996 MK-677 target-engagement trial: 2, 10, or 25 mg orally once daily for two 14- to 28-day periods in healthy elderly adults.
- 2008 MK-677 body-composition trial: 25 mg orally once daily for up to 2 years in healthy older adults.
- MK-677 sleep study: bedtime oral dosing, 5 or 25 mg in young adults and 2 or 25 mg in older adults, with 7- to 14-day periods.
- MK-677 AD trial: 25 mg daily for 12 months.
- [[cjc-1295]] trial: single-dose and repeated-dose ascending studies with subcutaneous administration in healthy adults.
- [[hexarelin]] dose-response: single intravenous boluses in healthy male volunteers.

## Open Contradictions

- The 2006 and 2012 cognition-positive [[ghrh]] / [[tesamorelin]] papers conflict with the 2008 MK-677 AD null and the 2025 [[tesamorelin]] HIV null/trend, so the class is not uniformly cognitive.
- The 1996 and 2008 MK-677 trials reinforce the split between target engagement, body composition, and function, so lean-mass gain should not be treated as performance gain by default.
- Forum narratives treat GH secretagogues as sleep/recovery/body-comp agents, but the formal cognition evidence is much narrower.
- [[tesamorelin]] is a regulated HIV drug with a clear label; [[cjc-1295]], [[ipamorelin]], and many forum stack materials are not in the same evidence or quality class.
- There is still no modern human cognition trial for [[ipamorelin]] itself, and [[hexarelin]]/[[cjc-1295]] are mainly target-engagement sources.
