# Safety Interaction Matrix for Multi-Modal Stacks

This page is the safety layer for combination research. The main failure mode is still not catastrophic toxicity; it is additive arousal, sleep fragmentation, mood destabilization, and attribution error when several small-effect interventions are started at once. The newer wake-promoter and comparator sources sharpen that point: solriamfetol, pitolisant, nicotine, caffeine, and orexin agonists can all improve state-specific wakefulness or cognition, but that does not make them clean healthy-adult enhancers or safe to stack casually.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40822571-tpbm-systematic-review-sleep-wakefulness-cognition.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39528111-solriamfetol-sharp-osa-cognition.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41359331-orexin-agonist-cognition-narcolepsy-secondary-analysis.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-37494485-oral-orexin-receptor-2-agonist-narcolepsy-type-1.md]^[raw/articles/dailymed-wakix-pitolisant-tablet-label.md]^[raw/articles/dailymed-sunosi-solriamfetol-tablet-label.md]^[raw/articles/dailymed-nicotine-gum-label-2026.md]

The newest source layer since 2024 strengthens three points: tPBM and bright light remain plausibly useful but still protocol-sensitive; taVNS and creatine look generally well tolerated; and the peptide bucket still has the weakest human safety base, with only thin pilot data for BPC-157, a real hypersensitivity counterexample for cerebrolysin, explicit FDA compounding-risk overlays for multiple research peptides, and stack-confounded forum reports that often mix sleep, recovery, arousal, and sourcing variables.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40822571-tpbm-systematic-review-sleep-wakefulness-cognition.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-42015088-bright-light-therapy-cognition-sleep-stress.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41717223-taVNS-cognitive-deficits-systematic-review.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40198156-creatine-safety-side-effects-adverse-event-reports.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40131143-bpc157-human-safety-pilot.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct07437547-bpc157-hamstring-repair-record-2026-05-06.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39055722-cerebrolysin-anaphylaxis-case-report.md]^[raw/articles/fda-bulk-drug-substances-peptide-risk-2026.md]

June 2026 peptide/nootropic stack rule: strong anecdote is not a safety clearance. [[semax]], [[selank]], [[cerebrolysin]], recovery-bound [[bpc-157]], and [[bromantane]] stay visible as the strongest monitoring cluster, but combination reports must still capture source/COA, route/formulation, dose timing, stack order, washout or stable background, sleep/recovery phenotype, adverse events, regulatory/sports status, stop rules, and objective endpoints through [[molecule-triage-worksheet-for-online-scans]], [[delivery-validation-checklist-for-cognition-interventions]], [[peptide-safety-and-sourcing-risk-map]], [[peptide-regulatory-live-watch-board]], and [[self-experimentation-stop-rules]]. [[dihexa]], [[pe-22-28]], [[adamax]], [[epithalon]], [[pinealon]], [[mots-c]], GH-axis peptides, and TB-500-style labels should default to high-uncertainty or state-bound stack risk unless those fields are complete.^[raw/articles/reddit-2026-bromantane-thread-trail.md]^[raw/articles/fda-bulk-drug-substances-peptide-risk-2026.md]

Methylene blue now needs its own combination gate when paired with red/NIR light or tPBM. The current source packet supports mitochondrial plausibility and weak community experimentation, but the clinical safety anchors remain serotonergic-drug/opioid avoidance, G6PD hemolysis risk, dose-dependent toxicity, cardiovascular/neurologic symptoms, and photosensitizer parameter discipline.^[raw/articles/ncbi-bookshelf-2026-methylene-blue-statpearls.md]^[raw/articles/dailymed-methylene-blue-injection-label-2026.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-33549685-methylene-blue-photobiomodulation-hepatic-encephalopathy.md]^[raw/articles/reddit-2025-methylene-blue-red-light-gym-thread.md]

## What Changed Since 2024

- tPBM now has a stronger sleep/wakefulness framing and a small sham-controlled MCI study, which keeps it in the state-optimization bucket rather than a pure nootropic bucket.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40822571-tpbm-systematic-review-sleep-wakefulness-cognition.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40939031-tpbm-mci-ad-sham-controlled.md]
- Bright-light therapy still matters mostly as a timing-sensitive circadian lever, so it should be treated as a stack component with phase effects, not a generic alertness booster.^[raw/articles/pubmed-42015088-bright-light-therapy-cognition-sleep-stress.md]
- taVNS now has both a 2026 systematic review and a small healthy-adult associative-memory pilot, which supports "possible phenotype-specific signal" rather than "proven enhancement."^[raw/articles/pubmed-41717223-taVNS-cognitive-deficits-systematic-review.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41046500-taVNS-associative-memory-healthy-adults.md]
- Creatine’s safety discussion got a 2025 adverse-event review, which supports keeping it in the low-risk orthogonal bucket rather than treating anecdotal complaints as a major signal.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40198156-creatine-safety-side-effects-adverse-event-reports.md]
- Caffeine and nicotine now read more clearly as state-restoration comparators: they can rescue sleep-loss or abstinence states, but they also change the measured state and can create withdrawal, insomnia, or tolerance confounds if the comparator schedule is sloppy.^[raw/articles/pubmed-35791877-repeated-low-dose-caffeine-sleep-deprivation-cognition.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-38830861-repeated-caffeine-intake-sleep-restriction-gm.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-38582919-nicotine-cognitive-function-smokers-abstinence.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-31686175-nicotine-galantamine-healthy-never-smokers-cognition.md]
- Solriamfetol, pitolisant, and orexin agonists now have cleaner disorder-context cognition data, but the same wake-drive makes them poor candidates for casual stacking with other arousal levers.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39528111-solriamfetol-sharp-osa-cognition.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-31917607-pitolisant-osa-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41359331-orexin-agonist-cognition-narcolepsy-secondary-analysis.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-37494485-oral-orexin-receptor-2-agonist-narcolepsy-type-1.md]
- Peptide safety remains uneven and should now be split rather than treated as one bucket: cerebrolysin has a known anaphylaxis case report; BPC-157 human safety is still a tiny pilot-literature island; Semax/Selank lean on older/regional and mechanistic support; Epithalon/Pinealon are state-bound sleep/recovery hypotheses with product-identity uncertainty; Dihexa/PE-22-28/Adamax are de-ranked for human cognition and safety use; and GH-axis peptide stacks create sleep/recovery and fixed-blend attribution problems.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39055722-cerebrolysin-anaphylaxis-case-report.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40131143-bpc157-human-safety-pilot.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-16634437-semax-selank-oligopeptides.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-30383932-semax-transthyretin-neuroprotection.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-32342318-selank-semax-functional-connectomics.md]^[raw/articles/fda-bulk-drug-substances-peptide-risk-2026.md] See [[peptide-stack-topology-ledger]] before assigning any multi-peptide report to one molecule.

|---|---|---|

## Practical Rules

1. Build stacks in layers: sleep/circadian first, then one metabolic lever, then one stimulation lever, then anything experimental.
2. Do not add more than one arousal-raising intervention at once if the goal is clean cognition data.
3. Hold caffeine, nicotine, and wake-promoter withdrawal state constant when the comparison is supposed to be "active vs placebo."
4. If sleep worsens, assume the stack is failing until proven otherwise.
5. Treat mood activation, palpitations, blood-pressure rise, headaches, visual symptoms, liver warnings, QT concerns, or allergic symptoms as stop conditions.
6. Avoid mixing research-chemical peptides with active optimization experiments unless the safety base is much stronger than it is now.
7. For peptides and adjacent gray-market nootropics, do not infer molecule-specific safety or benefit from a stack report unless exact identity, route, dose timing, source/COA, washout, dechallenge, adverse events, and objective endpoints are captured.
8. Treat sports/regulatory status as a safety-context field, not a side note, for [[bromantane]], GH-axis peptides, BPC-157/TB-500-style recovery stacks, and stimulant-adjacent blends.

## Open Contradictions

- Some interventions look "safe" in isolation but become hard to interpret when layered together.
- Caffeine and nicotine can look like enhancement in the right state, but a large part of the signal may be withdrawal reversal or sleep-loss rescue rather than baseline boosting.^[raw/articles/pubmed-35791877-repeated-low-dose-caffeine-sleep-deprivation-cognition.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-38582919-nicotine-cognitive-function-smokers-abstinence.md]
- Wake-promoting drugs improve narcolepsy or OSA outcomes, but that does not establish safety or usefulness as a multi-modal stack component in healthy adults.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39528111-solriamfetol-sharp-osa-cognition.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41359331-orexin-agonist-cognition-narcolepsy-secondary-analysis.md]
- A sleep-optimizing stack can improve performance by itself, which makes any additional enhancer look better than it is.
- Peptide claims are often the most enthusiastic and the least standardized, so the safety matrix should stay conservative until more human data arrive.
