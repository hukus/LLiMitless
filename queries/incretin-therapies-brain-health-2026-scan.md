# Incretin Therapies Brain Health 2026 Scan

Entity coverage note: the main molecule-level branches now have standalone pages for [[semaglutide]], [[exenatide]], and [[mazdutide]]. These pages preserve the branch split: semaglutide is mixed and endpoint-contested, exenatide is the MCI negative anchor, and mazdutide is preclinical-plus-protocol pending LIGHT-COG results.

## Summary

The incretin branch changed materially since 2024. It now has direct human cognition data rather than only mechanistic speculation, but the result is mixed rather than broadly positive. A 2026 meta-analysis in T2DM reported modest MMSE and MoCA gains with GLP-1 receptor agonists, a 2026 randomized clinical trial in MDD found semaglutide negative on its primary executive-function endpoint but positive on a secondary global-cognition composite, and a 2024 exenatide MCI proof-of-concept trial was negative. The large EVOKE/EVOKE+ semaglutide phase 3 program in early AD also missed its primary progression endpoint despite biomarker changes.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41104525-glp1-ra-cognitive-function-t2dm-meta-analysis.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41218611-semaglutide-mdd-cognitive-dysfunction-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-38565814-exenatide-mci-proof-of-concept-negative.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41865758-semaglutide-evoke-evokeplus-ad-phase3.md]

What changed since 2024:

- Human cognition evidence moved from sparse and indirect to mixed but real: T2DM meta-analysis positive, MDD RCT mixed, MCI exenatide negative, AD phase 3 negative.
- The strongest signal is still phenotype-sensitive, especially in T2DM and longer-duration exposure, not a universal enhancer effect.
- I did not find a human cognition-outcome trial for a GIP-based agent in the primary-source set for this pass; the only cognition-primary registry lead I found was mazdutide, which is GLP-1/glucagon rather than GIP.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct07083154-light-cog-mazdutide-early-dementia.md]
- Mazdutide now has a 2025 preclinical cognition paper in db/db mice, so the branch is no longer just a registry lead; it has a mechanistic animal layer that points in the positive direction, while the LIGHT-COG human study still has no posted results and remains the real readout gate.^[raw/articles/pmc-12205698-mazdutide-diabetes-associated-cognitive-dysfunction.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct07083154-light-cog-mazdutide-early-dementia.md]
- The main interpretive risk is still confounding by glycemic improvement, weight loss, sleep, and mood, which can mimic a direct neurocognitive effect.

## Evidence Tiering

- Meta-analysis: 2026 T2DM cognition meta-analysis.
- Randomized clinical trial: 2026 oral semaglutide in MDD.
- Phase 3 disease-modification trial: EVOKE and EVOKE+ in early symptomatic AD.
- Proof-of-concept RCT: long-acting exenatide in MCI.
- Active trial registry: mazdutide in T2D with early dementia.
- Review/mechanism: GLP-1RA neuropsychiatric/autophagy and GIP reviews, plus CNS-penetrance review.
- Inference: no direct human cognition-outcome trial for a GIP-based agent surfaced in this pass.

## Effect Size Snapshot

- T2DM meta-analysis: MMSE WMD 1.33 and MoCA WMD 1.70, with larger MMSE effects when treatment lasted at least 24 weeks.
- MDD semaglutide RCT: primary executive-function composite negative, adjusted Z difference 0.32; secondary global cognition positive at 2.39.
- Exenatide MCI trial: ADAS-Cog11 did not improve.
- EVOKE/EVOKE+: no meaningful slowing of AD progression on CDR-SB despite biomarker improvement.
- GIP/dual-agonist human cognition: no pooled effect yet because the human cognition evidence base is still essentially GLP-1RA-driven.

## Safety / Adverse Events

The cognition trials do not reveal a new neurologic safety signal. The recurring adverse-event pattern is the class-expected one: gastrointestinal effects, appetite suppression, and weight loss. In the semaglutide MDD trial, GI side effects were common but there were no serious adverse events. The main safety issue for cognition users is interpretability: improved glucose control, weight loss, sleep, or mood may create a false impression of a direct nootropic effect.

## Responder Phenotypes

- Best-supported phenotype so far is T2DM, especially with longer exposure.
- Phenotype-enrichment by insulin resistance, metabolic dysfunction, and possibly earlier disease stage is plausible but not yet proven.
- The exenatide MCI trial suggests that early cognitive impairment alone is not enough to guarantee benefit.
- The semaglutide MDD trial suggests the branch may have domain-specific effects rather than a uniform executive-function effect.
- Mazdutide and other newer dual agonists should be watched for phenotype-stratified readouts, not just aggregate averages.
- No validated GIP responder phenotype emerged in the primary human cognition set for this pass.

## Protocol Parameters

- Semaglutide MDD trial: oral semaglutide 14 mg, 16 weeks, placebo-controlled, primary executive-function composite.
- EVOKE / EVOKE+: oral semaglutide up to 14 mg once daily for up to 156 weeks in early symptomatic AD.
- Exenatide MCI trial: slow-release exenatide 2 mg subcutaneously once weekly for 32 weeks.
- LIGHT-COG: weekly mazdutide with stepwise escalation to 4 mg maintenance and optional 6 mg increase over 76 weeks; primary endpoint is iADRS change at Weeks 28, 52, and 76, with no posted results yet.
- Mazdutide preclinical study: db/db mice received escalating doses of mazdutide 50, 100, or 200 μg/kg every 3 days versus dulaglutide 200 μg/kg, with behavioral and multi-omics readouts.
- The human signal is currently strongest in longer-duration protocols, not short acute dosing paradigms.
- I did not find a direct human cognition protocol for a GIP-based agent in this pass.

## Open Contradictions

- The T2DM meta-analysis suggests benefit, but the MCI and AD trials do not confirm a broad class effect.
- Biomarker improvement without clinical benefit is now a real issue in the semaglutide AD program.
- Direct cognition benefit may depend on disease state, duration, or metabolic phenotype rather than the incretin label alone.
- GIP biology is attractive mechanistically, but the direct human cognition proof is still missing.
- A positive secondary cognition outcome in MDD should not be overread as a generalized nootropic effect.
