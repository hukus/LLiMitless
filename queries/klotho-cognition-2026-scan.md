# Klotho cognition 2026 scan

## Summary

Klotho is a plausible cognitive-resilience biomarker, but not a settled intervention target. The human literature is mostly observational and mixed in direction, with a stronger signal for better performance in some older-adult cohorts and a weaker or null causal signal in Mendelian randomization. A 2025 AD/aMCI paper added a more specific genotype-stage signal, but circulating α-Klotho still did not behave like a robust standalone biomarker. Preclinical work supports dose-sensitive cognitive effects, and a 2026 gene-therapy trial has made Klotho an active intervention lead, but not yet a human efficacy story.

Current run note: the 2026-05-04 scan did not surface a newer primary human Klotho cognition intervention result beyond the existing 2025 meta-analysis, 2025 AD/aMCI genotype-stage paper, 2026 review, and active Klotho plus follistatin gene-therapy registry. The current update adds registry-design detail, sponsor/waitlist status, and a weak community-interest thread; none upgrades efficacy confidence.

## What Changed Since 2024

- The 2025 meta-analysis is the biggest update: it pooled 8 studies and 6645 subjects and found an overall positive association between Klotho and cognitive outcomes, while also flagging heterogeneity and limited RCT evidence.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40048920-klotho-cognition-meta-analysis.md]
- The 2025 Alzheimer-disease cohort adds a more granular split: KL-VS heterozygosity tracked with better memory in aMCI due to AD, but soluble α-Klotho itself did not separate diagnostic groups or predict memory on its own.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41163083-klotho-vs-cognitive-performance-ad.md]
- The 2023 Translational Psychiatry paper tightened the split between association and causality by pairing a positive NHANES association with a null Mendelian randomization result for dementia.^[raw/articles/nature-s41398-023-02632-x-klotho-cognition-dementia-mr.md]
- The 2026 review broadened Klotho's clinical framing to Alzheimer's disease, Parkinson's disease, multiple sclerosis, and stroke recovery, but that is still synthesis-level evidence rather than a new human efficacy result.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41983942-klotho-neuroprotective-roles-review.md]
- The active Klotho + follistatin gene-therapy study means Klotho is no longer just a biomarker hypothesis; it is now being explored as an intervention target, albeit in an open-label, single-group, 30-person, non-results-bearing trial record.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct07285629-klotho-follistatin-gene-therapy.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct07285629-klotho-follistatin-gene-therapy-record-2026-05-04.md]
- The gene-therapy registry is still open-label and no-results as of the current scrape, so Klotho remains a protocol-level lead. Its primary readouts are serum alpha-Klotho, serum follistatin, and adverse-event monitoring; cognition is secondary. The registry also states that treatment occurs outside the United States at a site not under FDA jurisdiction while U.S. assessments are non-treatment visits.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct07285629-klotho-follistatin-gene-therapy-record-2026-05-04.md]
- Sponsor/waitlist pages now market Klotho gene therapy as "coming 2026" with cognition and longevity claims, but also describe the therapy as investigational, not FDA-reviewed, and administered through independent international partner clinics. This is a market/safety signal, not clinical efficacy evidence.^[raw/articles/minicircle-2026-klotho-gene-therapy-marketing-page.md]
- A representative Reddit biohacking thread shows weak prospective interest in Klotho/follistatin gene therapy but no direct Klotho self-experiment outcome. The anecdote layer is therefore absent-to-weak for cognition.^[raw/articles/reddit-2025-03-07-klotho-follistatin-gene-therapy-interest-thread.md]
- The current scan did not identify any newer post-2024 human efficacy paper that would move Klotho out of the biomarker/resilience lane.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40048920-klotho-cognition-meta-analysis.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct07285629-klotho-follistatin-gene-therapy.md]

## Biomarker Read

The practical read is that Klotho may behave like a resilience marker for older adults, especially in processing-speed style measures and possibly in vascular-heavy phenotypes, but the protein readout is still noisy and stage-dependent. It is not yet validated as a decision-grade biomarker for enrichment or response prediction.^[raw/articles/pmc-9932504-klotho-cognitive-performance-us-adults.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-29768278-klotho-vascular-dementia-memory-clinic.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41163083-klotho-vs-cognitive-performance-ad.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40048920-klotho-cognition-meta-analysis.md]

## Intervention Read

Klotho itself is best treated as a preclinical target. The primate result is encouraging but fragile because it appears dose-sensitive, species-limited, and not yet translated into human cognition outcomes. The active 2026 gene-therapy study makes the target actionable, but the field still lacks a published human efficacy signal.^[raw/articles/pubmed-37400721-klotho-nonhuman-primates-cognition.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct07285629-klotho-follistatin-gene-therapy.md]
The 2025 AD/aMCI study strengthens the idea that any future Klotho intervention may need to track genotype or disease stage rather than just total circulating protein.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41163083-klotho-vs-cognitive-performance-ad.md]

The registry does at least define measurable endpoints for a future signal: pattern comparison processing speed, picture sequence memory, flanker inhibitory control and attention, dimensional change card sort, picture vocabulary, and quality-of-life measures. That makes it easier to judge whether a later result reflects cognition specifically or a broader wellness effect.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct07285629-klotho-follistatin-gene-therapy-record-2026-05-04.md]
The cognitive readouts are secondary and sit alongside serum Klotho/follistatin, adverse events, epigenetic age, inflammatory markers, renal/mineral markers, PF4/coagulation markers, metabolic markers, physical performance, and safety labs; if results post, those will tell us whether the intervention moved cognition specifically or only changed general health markers.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct07285629-klotho-follistatin-gene-therapy-record-2026-05-04.md]
The biggest attribution problem is that the intervention combines Klotho and follistatin. Any future improvement in strength, body composition, mood, energy, or cognition should be treated as a combination-gene-therapy result unless a Klotho-only comparator exists.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct07285629-klotho-follistatin-gene-therapy-record-2026-05-04.md]

## Open Contradictions

- 2014 human genetic data suggest benefit, but 2017 older-men data suggest increased dementia incidence for KL-VS carriers.^[raw/articles/pmc-4176932-klotho-cognition-enhancement.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-28539162-klotho-dementia-older-men.md]
- Higher circulating Klotho correlates with better cognition in NHANES, but MR does not support a causal dementia effect.^[raw/articles/pmc-9932504-klotho-cognitive-performance-us-adults.md]^[raw/articles/nature-s41398-023-02632-x-klotho-cognition-dementia-mr.md]
- KL-VS heterozygosity may matter more than soluble α-Klotho in AD/aMCI, but that signal is stage-specific and not yet generalizable.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41163083-klotho-vs-cognitive-performance-ad.md]
- The biomarker signal is clearer than the intervention signal, and vascular phenotypes may be driving part of the association.^[raw/articles/pubmed-29768278-klotho-vascular-dementia-memory-clinic.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40048920-klotho-cognition-meta-analysis.md]
- Review-level enthusiasm now stretches across AD, PD, MS, and stroke, but the primary human data still do not support a causal enhancement claim.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41983942-klotho-neuroprotective-roles-review.md]^[raw/articles/nature-s41398-023-02632-x-klotho-cognition-dementia-mr.md]
- The gene-therapy branch combines Klotho with follistatin, uses an open-label design, and includes many health/aging endpoints, so future positive changes could be non-cognitive, nonspecific, or combination-driven.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct07285629-klotho-follistatin-gene-therapy-record-2026-05-04.md]
- Sponsor/waitlist claims and community curiosity are visible before peer-reviewed human cognition efficacy, creating a safety and expectation-management problem.^[raw/articles/minicircle-2026-klotho-gene-therapy-marketing-page.md]^[raw/articles/reddit-2025-03-07-klotho-follistatin-gene-therapy-interest-thread.md]

## Actionable Wiki Updates

- Keep Klotho in the resilience/biomarker cluster rather than the proven enhancer cluster.
- Treat KL-VS genotype as a more informative hypothesis handle than circulating α-Klotho when stage-specific cognition is the endpoint.
- Use Klotho as a candidate moderator when building responder atlases or biomarker-enrichment hypotheses.
- Route any future intervention claims through the new raw captures and the 2026 trial record before upgrading confidence.
- Keep the gene-therapy trial in the watchlist until a posted human readout exists; do not treat the open-label registry, sponsor waitlist, or Reddit interest as an efficacy signal.
