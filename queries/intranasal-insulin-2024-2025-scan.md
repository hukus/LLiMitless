# Intranasal Insulin 2024-2026 Scan

## Summary

Intranasal insulin no longer reads as a broad chronic enhancer story. The latest 2025 MCI/AD meta-analysis found no meaningful cognitive, functional, or CSF biomarker benefit, so the chronic dementia branch is now mostly a null or at best highly subgroup-sensitive signal.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41436338-intranasal-insulin-mci-ad-systematic-review-meta-analysis.md] A separate 2025 AD-only meta-analysis still favored 20 IU on ADAS-cog and reported an APOE4-negative verbal-memory signal, which is why the older AD-only optimism survives only as a narrow dose/phenotype claim rather than a general branch-level effect.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40195962-intranasal-insulin-ad-systematic-review-meta-analysis.md]

The more interesting modern readout is perioperative neurocognitive protection: a 2025 cardiac-surgery RCT, a 2025 hip-fracture RCT, and two 2026 meta-analyses now point to lower postoperative delirium, better short-term cognition or POCD prevention, and lower inflammatory markers in surgical patients.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40359848-intranasal-insulin-postop-sleep-cardiac-surgery.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41146035-intranasal-insulin-hip-fracture-delirium.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41720686-intranasal-insulin-perioperative-delirium-meta.md]^[raw/articles/sage-2026-intranasal-insulin-postoperative-delirium-meta.md]

Metabolic and phenotype-sensitive mechanisms remain plausible, especially in T2DM/insulin resistance and in older or perioperative states, but the branch should now be framed as state-specific rather than disease-agnostic.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40392946-intranasal-insulin-t2d-rsfc.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40703476-intranasal-insulin-pet-aging-mci.md]

What changed since 2024:

- The latest 2025 MCI/AD meta-analysis replaced the older optimism with a null: 5 RCTs and 540 participants showed no significant benefit on cognition, function, or CSF biomarkers, with GI events slightly higher and cardiovascular events lower.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41436338-intranasal-insulin-mci-ad-systematic-review-meta-analysis.md]
- The same year, a narrower AD-only meta-analysis still favored 20 IU on ADAS-cog and found APOE4-negative verbal-memory benefit, which keeps a small-dose responder hypothesis alive even though the broader pooled MCI/AD result is null.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40195962-intranasal-insulin-ad-systematic-review-meta-analysis.md]
- The PubMed-indexed 2026 perioperative meta-analysis added the strongest modern positive signal at that point: 7 RCTs, 732 postoperative patients, lower POD and POCD, lower IL-6/TNF-alpha, and no hypoglycemic reactions or serious adverse events.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41720686-intranasal-insulin-perioperative-delirium-meta.md]
- A later SAGE 2026 perioperative meta-analysis extended the signal to 9 randomized trials and 1039 surgical patients, with postoperative-delirium risk ratio 0.36 and subgroup reductions after orthopedic, gastrointestinal, and cardiac surgeries.^[raw/articles/sage-2026-intranasal-insulin-postoperative-delirium-meta.md]
- The 2025 cardiac-surgery RCT connected the perioperative benefit to sleep architecture: 20 IU pre-op and on postoperative days 1-2 reduced POD and improved postoperative sleep efficiency, total sleep time, and MMSE recovery.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40359848-intranasal-insulin-postop-sleep-cardiac-surgery.md]
- The 2025 hip-fracture RCT extended the signal to another surgical phenotype, with lower POD and inflammatory markers after 20 units twice daily around surgery.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41146035-intranasal-insulin-hip-fracture-delirium.md]
- The 2025 MS cognition trial stayed negative on SDMT and secondary outcomes despite acceptable tolerability, which is a useful reminder that another neurologic phenotype still does not automatically reproduce the AD/MCI signal.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40253245-intranasal-insulin-multiple-sclerosis-cognition.md]
- The PET validation study showed measurable brain uptake with a specialized Aptar device and subgroup variation by cognitive status, sex, and vascular/metabolic markers, which keeps the delivery-system story central to any future protocol.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40703476-intranasal-insulin-pet-aging-mci.md]
- The 2025 phase 2A/B metabolic-modulator trial added a new positive, short-duration signal in MCI/early AD: INI 40 IU QID and empagliflozin 10 mg daily were both safe, with INI improving mPACC5 and several fluid/imaging markers and empagliflozin shifting CSF tau and vascular markers.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41057918-intranasal-insulin-empagliflozin-mci-early-ad.md]
- The 2024 high-dose healthy-volunteer escalation study added a safety ceiling: most doses were tolerated, but 600 U produced episodic severe and mild hypoglycemia, so very high intranasal doses are not metabolically inert.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39558506-high-dose-intranasal-insulin-healthy-human-participants.md]
- The older T2DM and responder studies still matter because they explain why the effect may be enriched by insulin resistance, sex, APOE, and disease state rather than by diagnosis alone.^[raw/articles/pubmed-35482079-memaid-intranasal-insulin-type2-diabetes-control-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40392946-intranasal-insulin-t2d-rsfc.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-23507773-intranasal-insulin-sex-apoe-differences.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-25374101-intranasal-insulin-detemir-mci-ad.md]

## Evidence Tiering

- Chronic MCI/AD: the latest synthesis is null enough that routine use is not supported.
- Short-duration enriched MCI/early AD trials now look more promising than the pooled MCI/AD average, but they are still not durability proof.
- Perioperative neurocognitive protection: now the clearest positive human branch, with both RCT and meta-analytic support.
- Metabolic-brain / T2DM: mixed but still mechanistically plausible, especially in insulin-resistance phenotypes.
- Imaging/physiology sub-studies: crucial for mechanism and responder stratification, especially because delivery device and vascular/metabolic state matter.
- Healthy adults: still mostly mechanistic or indirect; no recent study moved the field toward a generic enhancement claim.

## Effect Size Snapshot

- 2025 MCI/AD meta-analysis: 5 RCTs, 540 participants, ADAS-Cog13 MD -1.09 (95 percent CI -4.89 to 2.71), no significant functional or CSF biomarker benefit, GI AEs RR 1.57, cardiovascular events RR 0.30.
- 2025 AD-only meta-analysis: 12 studies favored 20 IU on ADAS-cog (MD -0.13, 95 percent CI -0.22 to -0.05), while 40 IU and memory-composite effects were not clearly significant overall; APOE4-negative verbal memory looked better in that narrower review.
- 2026 perioperative meta-analyses: the PubMed-indexed analysis pooled 7 RCTs and 732 postoperative patients, with POD RR 0.39, POCD RR 0.51, lower IL-6/TNF-alpha, and no hypoglycemic reactions or serious adverse events; the later SAGE analysis pooled 9 RCTs and 1039 patients, with delirium RR 0.36 and consistent orthopedic, gastrointestinal, and cardiac surgery subgroup reductions.
- 2025 cardiac-surgery RCT: 76 middle-aged CPB patients, POD 17.1 percent vs 38.9 percent, MMSE difference +1.71, sleep efficiency +21.3 percent, TST +27.1 percent.
- 2025 hip-fracture RCT: 92 completers, POD 10.9 percent vs 30.4 percent, day-3 POD 2.2 percent vs 19.6 percent, IL-6 and CRP lower.
- 2025 MS RCT: 105 participants, 10 IU BID or 20 IU BID for 24 weeks via ViaNase, no SDMT or secondary outcome benefit versus placebo.
- 2025 PET validation: 16 participants, measurable uptake in olfactory cortex, hippocampus, parahippocampus, amygdala, temporal pole, insula, caudate, putamen, thalamus, and anterior cingulum.
- 2022 MemAID RCT: 24-week 40 IU Novolin R once daily; T2DM participants walked faster and showed lower HOMA-IR, while controls improved executive function and verbal memory.
- 2014 acute T2D trial: a single 40 IU dose improved visuospatial memory and increased insular perfusion in diabetics.

## Safety/Adverse Events

The safety profile remains fairly benign in the published trials captured here. Major studies report no serious adverse events, no clinically important hypoglycemia in the perioperative branch, and little to no weight signal; the usual nuisance effects are nasal irritation or congestion.^[raw/articles/pubmed-35482079-memaid-intranasal-insulin-type2-diabetes-control-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-24101698-intranasal-insulin-type2-diabetes-vasoreactivity-cognition.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39216470-intranasal-insulin-perioperative-cognitive-function-older-adults.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41720686-intranasal-insulin-perioperative-delirium-meta.md]

The latest MCI/AD meta-analysis adds a small safety wrinkle: gastrointestinal adverse events were more frequent with insulin, while cardiovascular events were less frequent, and overall serious adverse-event rates were not meaningfully worse.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41436338-intranasal-insulin-mci-ad-systematic-review-meta-analysis.md] The 2025 MS trial adds headache, rhinorrhea, and dizziness as the common side effects, but no drug-related death or major safety signal.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40253245-intranasal-insulin-multiple-sclerosis-cognition.md]

## Responder Phenotypes

- Insulin resistance and T2DM remain the clearest metabolic enrichment hypothesis.
- APOE and sex matter in older MCI/AD trials: 20 IU benefited both sexes on delayed story memory, while 40 IU favored men more than women; APOE-ε4-negative men looked especially responsive in the responder analysis.
- APOE-ε4 carriers may respond better to 40 IU detemir than non-carriers, which is a recurring but not universal pattern.
- Perioperative and delirium studies suggest age, surgical stress, and vulnerability state may matter at least as much as diagnosis label.
- Delivery system is now part of the responder story: the PET study suggests that measurable uptake depends on the inhalation device and baseline physiology.

## Protocol Parameters

- Common doses in cognition studies: 20 IU, 40 IU, and occasional long-acting analogs such as detemir.
- Device heterogeneity is a real issue: older MCI/AD trials used nasal delivery devices, newer perioperative studies used a 400 μL intranasal administration format, the 2025 MS trial used ViaNase, and the PET validation study used an Aptar Cartridge Pump System.
- MemAID: 40 IU Novolin R once daily for 24 weeks, then 24 weeks follow-up.
- 2025 cardiac-surgery trial: 20 IU pre-op and on postoperative days 1-2.
- 2025 hip-fracture trial: 20 units twice daily starting two days preoperatively and continuing for five days.
- 2026 perioperative meta-analyses: the PubMed-indexed analysis pooled 20 IU to 40 IU regimens, while the later SAGE analysis broadened the perioperative evidence set to 9 randomized trials but still concluded that protocol standardization is missing.
- 2025 MS trial: 10 IU BID or 20 IU BID for 24 weeks via ViaNase.
- 2024 perioperative trial: 40 IU/400 μL once daily starting 5 minutes before anesthesia induction through postoperative day 3.
- 2025 delirium treatment trial: 20 IU long-acting insulin twice daily until delirium resolution, discharge, or futility.

## Open Contradictions

- Older pooled reviews were more optimistic, but the latest 2025 MCI/AD meta-analysis is null, so the chronic-disease branch has moved from “promising but heterogeneous” to “routine use not supported.”
- The narrower 2025 AD-only meta-analysis still favors 20 IU and APOE4-negative verbal memory, so dose and disease mix can reverse the apparent direction of effect.
- The perioperative branch is the opposite: repeated RCTs and two 2026 meta-analyses now suggest a genuine protective effect, but external validity, standardization, and adoption into treatment protocols are still open.
- The best dose is not settled; 20 IU and 40 IU appear to behave differently across sex, APOE, perioperative stress, and insulin-resistance strata.
- Very high doses are not automatically benign; the healthy-volunteer escalation study showed episodic hypoglycemia at 600 U even though mean glucose stayed stable.
- Device choice is no longer a footnote: the PET validation study suggests that brain exposure depends on the delivery system and responder physiology, so older trial heterogeneity may partly reflect pharmacokinetic differences rather than pure pharmacology.
- Healthy-adult and younger-adult cognitive claims remain weak compared with the perioperative or metabolically vulnerable cohorts.
- De-rank consumer nootropic or self-study claims by default when they do not identify the delivery device, meal/glucose state, dose timing, and objective endpoint; `intranasal` alone is not enough exposure detail.
