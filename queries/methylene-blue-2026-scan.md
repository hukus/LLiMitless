# Methylene Blue 2026 Scan

## Summary

Methylene blue has a real but narrow human cognition signal. The best-supported healthy-adult findings are from a single-dose 280 mg oral imaging trial showing higher task-evoked fMRI activity during sustained attention and short-term memory, plus a 7% retrieval gain, and a companion healthy-adult connectivity study showing stronger resting-state coupling in perception-memory networks.^[raw/articles/pubmed-27351678-methylene-blue-human-brain-fmri.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-26961091-methylene-blue-functional-connectivity-human-brain.md]

The AD disease-modifying branch is mixed rather than cleanly positive: an exploratory 321-person phase 2 methylthioninium trial found a signal at 138 mg/day on ADAS-cog and regional cerebral blood flow, but the later large phase 3 leuco-methylthioninium trial was negative on its coprimary clinical outcomes.^[raw/articles/pubmed-25550228-methylthioninium-phase2-ad.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-27863809-lmtm-phase3-ad-negative.md]

What changed since 2024:

- The safety story is now clearer than the wellness hype: the current U.S. label still centers methylene blue on methemoglobinemia and carries a boxed warning for serotonin syndrome with serotonergic drugs and opioids, plus a G6PD contraindication.^[raw/articles/dailymed-methylene-blue-injection-label-2026.md]
- The 2026 StatPearls update adds a fuller safety frame: off-label medical use is separate from nootropic use, toxicity is dose-dependent, and hemolysis, anaphylaxis, cardiovascular effects, neurologic symptoms, diagnostic-monitoring interference, serotonergic drugs/opioids, and G6PD deficiency all remain material constraints.^[raw/articles/ncbi-bookshelf-2026-methylene-blue-statpearls.md]
- The best human cognition evidence still comes from small Texas-area studies using low-dose oral USP methylene blue around 260-282 mg, with strong MRI readouts but limited sample size and limited replication.^[raw/articles/pubmed-27351678-methylene-blue-human-brain-fmri.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-26961091-methylene-blue-functional-connectivity-human-brain.md]
- The translational branch is strongest when MB is treated as a memory-consolidation adjunct after a successful learning or exposure session, not as a universal baseline nootropic.^[raw/articles/pubmed-25018057-methylene-blue-claustrophobia-contextual-memory.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-28686823-methylene-blue-ptsd-exposure-rct.md]
- The older AD methylthioninium program is not dead-positive: the phase 2 signal was followed by a negative phase 3, which makes formulation, exposure, and patient-state dependence central to interpretation.^[raw/articles/pubmed-25550228-methylthioninium-phase2-ad.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-27863809-lmtm-phase3-ad-negative.md]
- The phase 2 MB2 registry now has ClinicalTrials.gov API-accessible result tables: 117 actual enrollment, results first posted on 2024-09-19, descriptive fMRI / memory / FNAME / psychomotor-vigilance / MMSE / CLOX / cerebral-blood-flow / connectivity outcomes, small imaging denominators, near-empty mild-AD cells, no deaths or serious adverse events, and common urinary/GI dye effects. This is no longer protocol-only, but it is still not a peer-reviewed positive cognition trial.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct02380573-methylene-blue-cognition-fmri.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct02380573-methylene-blue-results-posted.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct02380573-mb2-results-api-2026-05-04.md]
- Two 2025 animal papers add recovery-state preclinical support: mouse TBI spatial-working-memory protection with mitochondrial-quality-control / inflammatory-marker movement, and a mouse postoperative-delirium model with neuroinflammation and blood-brain-barrier repair. These do not upgrade healthy-human enhancement confidence.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40879922-methylene-blue-tbi-mice-spatial-working-memory.md]^[raw/articles/nature-2025-methylene-blue-postoperative-delirium-mice.md]
- A recent monotherapy Reddit report and the broader Troof nootropics ratings corpus support that MB remains salient for subjective focus, energy, and memory claims, but the anecdotal layer is dose-, product-, medication-, and screening-confounded.^[raw/articles/reddit-2026-01-23-methylene-blue-capsules-focus-thread.md]^[raw/articles/troof-nootropics-ratings-analysis.md]
- The MB + red/NIR light idea now has its own safety page, [[methylene-blue-plus-light-redox-interaction-scan]]. The current evidence is mechanism/preclinical plus weak anecdote; it should not upgrade the MB medicinal signal.^[raw/articles/pubmed-33549685-methylene-blue-photobiomodulation-hepatic-encephalopathy.md]^[raw/articles/bmc-2020-methylene-blue-photobiomodulation-neuroprotection-review.md]^[raw/articles/reddit-2025-methylene-blue-red-light-gym-thread.md]
- For label, prescription-status, and sports/regulatory comparison across adjacent compounds, route MB through [[regulatory-and-sports-visibility-map]] before treating it as a wellness nootropic.

## Evidence Tiering

- Human RCT / imaging: the strongest signal comes from the 2016 26-person healthy-adult oral-dose trial with task fMRI and memory retrieval.
- Human RCT / connectivity: the 2017 study adds resting-state and task-network changes but remains small.
- Human AD RCT / dose finding: the 321-person phase 2 methylthioninium study found a signal at 138 mg/day on cognition and regional cerebral blood flow, but not across all doses.
- Human AD RCT / confirmatory: the later 891-person phase 3 leuco-methylthioninium trial was negative on the prespecified coprimary outcomes.
- Human clinical translation: claustrophobia and PTSD exposure-therapy studies suggest a state-dependent memory-consolidation effect.
- Registry-level evidence: MB2 now has posted descriptive ClinicalTrials.gov result tables, but the public readout is denominator-thin and lacks paper-level statistical interpretation.
- Preclinical recovery-state evidence: 2025 mouse TBI and postoperative-delirium papers support neuroinflammation / mitochondrial / BBB hypotheses but do not bridge to healthy-human enhancement.
- Anecdotal monotherapy evidence: current Reddit and survey signals make MB worth tracking, but the layer is subjective and heavily product/dose/safety-screening confounded.
- Safety: the prescribing label is the anchor, not the supplement market.

## Effect Size Snapshot

- Healthy-adult fMRI trial: 7% increase in correct memory retrieval, plus task-related activation increases in bilateral insula and prefrontal/parietal/occipital regions.^[raw/articles/pubmed-27351678-methylene-blue-human-brain-fmri.md]
- Connectivity trial: stronger resting-state coupling and task-network deactivation changes, but no large clinical endpoint.
- Claustrophobia trial: benefit depended on a successful extinction session; a weak session could flip the direction of effect.^[raw/articles/pubmed-25018057-methylene-blue-claustrophobia-contextual-memory.md]
- PTSD RCT: MB-augmented exposure improved evaluator-rated response with NNT 7.5 and d = 0.58 versus placebo augmentation.^[raw/articles/pubmed-28686823-methylene-blue-ptsd-exposure-rct.md]
- Phase 2 AD dose-finding: the 138 mg/day arm produced the clearest signal, including a roughly 5.4-point ADAS-cog advantage in moderately affected subjects and a small regional cerebral blood flow benefit in mildly affected subjects.^[raw/articles/pubmed-25550228-methylthioninium-phase2-ad.md]
- Phase 3 AD confirmatory: 75 mg BID and 125 mg BID LMTM did not beat control on ADAS-Cog or ADCS-ADL at 15 months.^[raw/articles/pubmed-27863809-lmtm-phase3-ad-negative.md]
- MB2 registry: 282 mg/day oral USP methylene blue produced posted descriptive tables across imaging and behavioral endpoints, but the public registry snapshot does not establish a clean positive result; several endpoint cells are small, especially in mild AD.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct02380573-mb2-results-api-2026-05-04.md]
- 2025 recovery-state animal work: mouse TBI and postoperative-delirium models showed spatial-working-memory or delirium-like-behavior protection with inflammatory, mitochondrial, and BBB markers; keep this below the human-efficacy layer.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40879922-methylene-blue-tbi-mice-spatial-working-memory.md]^[raw/articles/nature-2025-methylene-blue-postoperative-delirium-mice.md]

## Safety / Adverse Events

- Avoid methylene blue with SSRIs, SNRIs, MAOIs, and opioids because of serotonin syndrome risk.^[raw/articles/dailymed-methylene-blue-injection-label-2026.md]
- Do not treat G6PD deficiency as a minor caution; it is a real hemolysis contraindication.^[raw/articles/dailymed-methylene-blue-injection-label-2026.md]
- Treat dose errors as a safety issue, not just an efficacy issue: StatPearls describes clinically important dose-dependent toxicity and cardiovascular/neurologic adverse effects.^[raw/articles/ncbi-bookshelf-2026-methylene-blue-statpearls.md]
- In the phase 3 AD trial, gastrointestinal and urinary effects were the most common adverse events, and small dose-dependent hemoglobin reductions were the main lab abnormality.^[raw/articles/pubmed-27863809-lmtm-phase3-ad-negative.md]
- MB2 reported no deaths or serious adverse events in the posted registry snapshot, but common non-serious events included blue/orange urine, green stool, urinary frequency/urgency, nausea/vomiting, diarrhea/loose stool, bladder irritation, and leg cramps.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct02380573-mb2-results-api-2026-05-04.md]
- Trial-level exclusions also repeatedly screen out pregnancy, breastfeeding, MRI contraindications, severe renal/hepatic disease, psychosis/panic disorder history, colorblindness, and current serotonergic medication use.^[raw/articles/pubmed-27351678-methylene-blue-human-brain-fmri.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct02380573-methylene-blue-cognition-fmri.md]
- The current label is for IV rescue therapy, so oral nootropic use sits outside the approved indication and safety context.^[raw/articles/dailymed-methylene-blue-injection-label-2026.md]

## Responder Phenotypes

- Best signal: healthy adults doing short-term memory and vigilance tasks under imaging.
- Better translational fit: people whose benefit depends on a successful learning or extinction session, especially exposure therapy.
- Possible future enrichment: healthy aging, MCI, and early AD, but the MB2 registry still needs a paper-level readout before confidence changes.
- Poor fit: serotonergic polypharmacy, G6PD deficiency, significant renal/hepatic disease, pregnancy, or MRI-disqualified participants.

## Protocol Parameters

- Healthy-adult imaging trial: single oral 280 mg USP methylene blue, scan repeated about 60 minutes later.^[raw/articles/pubmed-27351678-methylene-blue-human-brain-fmri.md]
- Connectivity trial: single oral 280 mg USP methylene blue, same 60-minute absorption window.^[raw/articles/pubmed-26961091-methylene-blue-functional-connectivity-human-brain.md]
- Phase 2 AD dose-finding trial: oral methylthioninium 69, 138, or 228 mg/day in 321 mild/moderate AD subjects over 24 weeks, with optional blinded extensions and an MRI substudy.^[raw/articles/pubmed-25550228-methylthioninium-phase2-ad.md]
- Phase 3 AD trial: oral LMTM 75 mg BID or 125 mg BID, with a 4 mg BID control used to preserve masking over 15 months in 891 participants.^[raw/articles/pubmed-27863809-lmtm-phase3-ad-negative.md]
- Claustrophobia trial: 260 mg oral immediately after six 5-minute extinction trials.^[raw/articles/pubmed-25018057-methylene-blue-claustrophobia-contextual-memory.md]
- PTSD trial: 260 mg oral after each of five daily 50-minute imaginal exposures.^[raw/articles/pubmed-28686823-methylene-blue-ptsd-exposure-rct.md]
- MB2 registry: 282 mg oral daily across 2-week and 12-week windows, with fMRI, Wechsler Memory Scale, FNAME, psychomotor vigilance, Logical Memory, MMSE, CLOX, cerebral blood flow, functional connectivity, and CO2-challenge endpoints. The trial is completed, enrolled 117, posted results on 2024-09-19, and still lacks a peer-reviewed full-results paper in this capture.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct02380573-methylene-blue-cognition-fmri.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct02380573-mb2-results-api-2026-05-04.md]
- Label dose anchor: 1 mg/kg IV over 5-30 minutes for acquired methemoglobinemia, with one repeat dose permitted if needed.^[raw/articles/dailymed-methylene-blue-injection-label-2026.md]

## Open Contradictions

- The human signal is real but small, and it is not yet clear whether the task-network and memory-retrieval effects generalize beyond the original study group.
- MB looks like a consolidation adjunct rather than a universal enhancer, which means session quality may determine whether it helps or harms.
- The methylthioninium / LMTM development path shows a phase 2 to phase 3 divergence, so dose, formulation, and target engagement appear to matter more than the molecule name alone.
- MB2 has moved past protocol-only status on ClinicalTrials.gov, but the posted descriptive tables are not enough to upgrade the branch without paper-level statistics or an independent replication.
- The wellness/nootropic narrative often ignores the label-level serotonergic risk and G6PD issue.
- Oral supplement use and IV-label use are being conflated online even though the regulatory and dosing contexts are different.
- Red/NIR light and tPBM can be discussed beside MB mechanistically, but no human MB + light cognition synergy trial is captured; keep the combination in [[methylene-blue-plus-light-redox-interaction-scan]].
