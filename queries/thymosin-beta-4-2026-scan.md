# Thymosin Beta 4 2026 Scan

## Summary

Thymosin beta-4 still looks like a recovery / tissue-repair peptide with a plausible CNS-adjacent neuroprotective story, not a validated cognition enhancer. The key discipline is identity: full-length thymosin beta-4 has human ophthalmic, healthy-volunteer, and STEMI exposure, while TB-500/LKKTETQ is a short fragment with much thinner human evidence and a separate FDA/WADA status layer.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct04555850-thymosin-beta4-healthy-volunteers.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41229390-thymosin-beta4-acute-mi-reperfusion.md]^[raw/articles/pmc-4445951-thymosin-beta4-dry-eye-phase2.md]^[raw/articles/pmc-9820614-rgn259-neurotrophic-keratopathy-phase3.md]^[raw/articles/fda-bulk-drug-substances-peptide-risk-2026.md]^[raw/articles/wada-2026-prohibited-list-peptide-gh-axis-expanded.md]

For cognition, the strongest formal signal is still the 2025 AD organoid / 5xFAD paper and the older APP/PS1 and TBI animal work. Those are CNS-relevant, but they do not cross the human-cognition threshold.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40816274-thymosin-beta4-ad-candidate.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-34183019-thymosin-beta4-glial-polarization-cognitive-impairment.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-22324420-thymosin-beta4-tbi-rats.md]

## What Changed Since 2024

- The biggest change is the 2025 organoid paper, which moves thymosin beta-4 from generic wound-repair biology into a more specific AD/neurogenesis hypothesis.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40816274-thymosin-beta4-ad-candidate.md]
- Two older mouse papers now matter for the cognition-adjacent branch: a 2021 APP/PS1 study reported reduced amyloid burden, glial reprogramming, and better learning / memory with Tβ4, and a 2023 APP/PS1 + LPS study reported lower plaque load plus preserved food burrowing and sickness behavior.^[raw/articles/pubmed-34183019-thymosin-beta4-glial-polarization-cognitive-impairment.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-36878045-thymosin-beta4-plaque-load-app-ps1-mice.md]
- A 2023 hippocampal neuronal-cell study added a mechanistic neurotrophic-factor signal, but it is still cell-model only.^[raw/articles/pubmed-37175330-thymosin-beta4-hippocampal-neuronal-cells.md]
- The older TBI rat literature still matters because it links delayed dosing to better spatial learning and recovery outcomes, but nothing in the human literature yet matches that signal.^[raw/articles/pubmed-22324420-thymosin-beta4-tbi-rats.md]
- The human record is broader than cognition but still not cognition: phase 1b intravenous healthy-volunteer safety/PK, topical ophthalmic dry-eye and neurotrophic-keratopathy trials, and the 2025 acute-STEMI reperfusion paper all support full-length thymosin beta-4 as a legitimate drug-development object without proving neurocognitive benefit.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct04555850-thymosin-beta4-healthy-volunteers.md]^[raw/articles/pmc-4445951-thymosin-beta4-dry-eye-phase2.md]^[raw/articles/pmc-9820614-rgn259-neurotrophic-keratopathy-phase3.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41229390-thymosin-beta4-acute-mi-reperfusion.md]
- FDA's July 23, 2026 PCAC agenda now lists TB-500 free base/acetate for wound-healing review on the 503A bulk-substance track; this is a regulatory-compounding signal, not approval and not a cognition indication.^[raw/articles/fda-2026-pcac-tb500-bpc157-kpv-motsc-bulk-substances.md]
- Recent Reddit threads keep TB-500/BPC-157 highly salient as a recovery stack, with both dramatic injury-recovery reports and explicit non-response/placebo/source-identity cautions. That is useful for safety and trial design, not for medicinal cognition confidence.^[raw/articles/reddit-2026-04-25-bpc157-tb500-meniscus-mri-thread.md]^[raw/articles/reddit-2026-01-12-bpc157-tb500-three-months-mixed-thread.md]

## Evidence Tiering

- Human interventional, non-cognition: phase 1b healthy-volunteer safety/PK study, topical ophthalmic trials in dry eye and neurotrophic keratopathy, and a 2025 acute-STEMI reperfusion paper; none has a cognitive endpoint.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct04555850-thymosin-beta4-healthy-volunteers.md]^[raw/articles/pmc-4445951-thymosin-beta4-dry-eye-phase2.md]^[raw/articles/pmc-9820614-rgn259-neurotrophic-keratopathy-phase3.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41229390-thymosin-beta4-acute-mi-reperfusion.md]
- Human disease-model / translational: 2025 AD brain-organoid rescue study with a 5xFAD mouse bridge.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40816274-thymosin-beta4-ad-candidate.md]
- Preclinical cognition-adjacent: 2021 APP/PS1 cognitive-impairment study, 2023 APP/PS1 + LPS plaque-load study, 2023 hippocampal neuronal-cell protection, 2012 TBI rat recovery study.
- Review / context: broader CNS and tissue-repair literature is suggestive but not decisive.

## Effect Size Snapshot

- No human cognition effect size.
- Preclinical direction is positive for cell survival, neurogenesis, glial state shifts, plaque-load reduction, and learning in injury models, but the translation gap is still large.
- Human ophthalmic and cardiac papers should be treated as tissue-repair/disease-context signals, not cognition effect-size estimates.
- The organoid paper is the best current bridge toward a neurodegeneration rationale, not a performance claim.

## Safety / Adverse Events

- The healthy-volunteer trial explicitly monitored safety, tolerability, PK, and immune response; the registry record does not surface cognition outcomes, and the 2025 acute-STEMI paper still speaks to disease recovery rather than enhancement.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct04555850-thymosin-beta4-healthy-volunteers.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41229390-thymosin-beta4-acute-mi-reperfusion.md]
- Topical ophthalmic thymosin beta-4 trials provide route-specific human tolerability context, not injected-fragment safety.^[raw/articles/pmc-4445951-thymosin-beta4-dry-eye-phase2.md]^[raw/articles/pmc-9820614-rgn259-neurotrophic-keratopathy-phase3.md]
- FDA's current risk page flags LKKTETQ/TB-500 for immunogenicity/aggregation/peptide-impurity uncertainty and no identified human exposure data for drug products; the July 2026 PCAC agenda is a watch point, not clearance.^[raw/articles/fda-bulk-drug-substances-peptide-risk-2026.md]^[raw/articles/fda-2026-pcac-tb500-bpc157-kpv-motsc-bulk-substances.md]
- WADA lists thymosin-beta-4 derivatives, including TB-500, under growth factors / growth-factor modulators, so sports status is independent of cognition evidence.^[raw/articles/wada-2026-prohibited-list-peptide-gh-axis-expanded.md]
- A 2026 sports-medicine review of approved and unapproved peptide therapies includes thymosin beta-4 and TB-500 among gray-market performance/recovery peptides where rigorous human safety evidence is scarce and social-media amplification can distort perceived efficacy.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41966639-peptide-therapies-sports-medicine-review.md]
- No cognition-specific adverse-event profile surfaced in this sweep.
- Because the human work is still investigational and identity-confounded in the public market, this should stay in the research-only bucket for cognition.

## Responder Phenotypes

- Most plausible responder phenotype is neurodegeneration or injury-recovery biology, not healthy enhancement.
- The AD organoid paper points toward APP/fAD-like biology and possibly a neurogenesis deficit phenotype.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40816274-thymosin-beta4-ad-candidate.md]
- The APP/PS1 mouse papers point toward inflammation-linked and plaque-load-sensitive phenotypes rather than a healthy enhancement use case.^[raw/articles/pubmed-34183019-thymosin-beta4-glial-polarization-cognitive-impairment.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-36878045-thymosin-beta4-plaque-load-app-ps1-mice.md]
- The TBI work suggests post-injury recovery windows may be where any signal would be easiest to detect.^[raw/articles/pubmed-22324420-thymosin-beta4-tbi-rats.md]

## Protocol Parameters

- Human registry: recombinant human thymosin beta-4, intravenous, 0.5 / 2.0 / 5.0 μg/kg for 10 consecutive days in healthy volunteers.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct04555850-thymosin-beta4-healthy-volunteers.md]
- Human topical ophthalmic trials: 0.1% thymosin beta-4 / RGN-259 in dry-eye and neurotrophic-keratopathy settings.^[raw/articles/pmc-4445951-thymosin-beta4-dry-eye-phase2.md]^[raw/articles/pmc-9820614-rgn259-neurotrophic-keratopathy-phase3.md]
- Preclinical TBI rat study: 6 mg/kg intraperitoneally starting 6 hours after injury, then every 3 days for 4 more doses.^[raw/articles/pubmed-22324420-thymosin-beta4-tbi-rats.md]
- APP/PS1 mouse cognitive-impairment study used lentiviral overexpression in brain regions and assessed learning / memory plus glial phenotypes.^[raw/articles/pubmed-34183019-thymosin-beta4-glial-polarization-cognitive-impairment.md]
- APP/PS1 + LPS plaque-load study used 5 mg/kg intravenous Tβ4 immediately after challenge and repeated doses over 6 days.^[raw/articles/pubmed-36878045-thymosin-beta4-plaque-load-app-ps1-mice.md]
- No cognition protocol identified in humans.

## Open Contradictions

- The peptide is biologically active in CNS-relevant models, but the current human dataset is still too thin to say it improves cognition.
- The strongest human signal is safety/PK, while the strongest CNS signal is organoid/animal rescue, so the evidence layers are not aligned yet.
- Full-length thymosin beta-4 human exposure cannot be transferred automatically to the short TB-500/LKKTETQ fragment sold online.
- Strong recovery-stack anecdotes can be real-world signal for injury communities while still being weak evidence for cognition and weak evidence for TB-500 alone.
