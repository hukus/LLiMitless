# TrkB / BDNF-Mimetic Cognition - 2026 Scan

Companion first-pass scan: [[trkb-bdnf-mimetic-2026-scan]]. This page is the expanded target-specificity and registry-status version.

## Summary

TrkB / BDNF-mimetic compounds are now a distinct plasticity branch in the wiki. The named small-molecule leads are [[7-8-dihydroxyflavone|7,8-DHF]], [[lm22a-4]], and [[deoxygedunin]]; the next-generation comparators are 7,8-DHF prodrugs such as [[r13]] and TrkB agonist antibodies such as [[zeb85]].

The evidence is still overwhelmingly preclinical. 7,8-DHF has rodent memory-consolidation, TBI-recovery, aging/stress, AD-model, and NHE6/SLC9A6 plasticity signals, but no registered human cognition trial surfaced in targeted ClinicalTrials.gov API searches on 2026-04-28. LM22A-4 now has a clearer Rett mouse object-location-memory / LTP rescue anchor, while deoxygedunin remains older discovery-stage natural-product chemistry. These are useful TrkB-mimetic and animal-model comparators, not clinical cognition leads.^[raw/articles/pubmed-24070857-78-dhf-memory-consolidation-rodents.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-25661191-78-dhf-tbi-trkb.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39341363-78-dhf-nhe6-plasticity.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-20407211-lm22a4-bdnf-mimetics-trkb.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-28679669-lm22a4-rett-object-location-memory.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-38785269-lm22a4-rett-spine-behavior.md]^[raw/articles/plos-2010-deoxygedunin-natural-product-trkb.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-trkb-bdnf-mimetic-registry-search-2026.md]

The branch should be linked to [[non-hallucinogenic-psychoplastogens]] only as a plasticity-mechanism comparator. It is not a non-hallucinogenic psychedelic lane. It belongs more directly with [[translational-gap-table]], [[state-challenge-matrix-for-cognition-interventions]], [[clinical-recovery-vs-healthy-enhancement-translation-map]], and [[negative-evidence-watchlist-for-cognition-interventions]].

## What Changed Since 2024

- A 2025 issue paper made the branch more interesting by connecting 7,8-DHF to NHE6/SLC9A6-linked hippocampal spine density, AMPAR transmission, and LTP rescue. That points toward a possible endosomal-trafficking / synaptic-plasticity responder axis rather than generic enhancement.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39341363-78-dhf-nhe6-plasticity.md]
- The 2024 caution review and 2021 assay-limitations paper keep confidence low: several small-molecule TrkB agonist claims have assay-specificity concerns, and some benefits may reflect broader neuroprotection, antioxidant effects, or non-TrkB mechanisms rather than clean TrkB agonism.^[raw/articles/pubmed-37488840-trkb-agonist-caution-review.md]^[raw/articles/pmc-8398766-small-molecule-trkb-agonist-limitations.md]
- The new follow-up lanes sharpen the branch split: [[r13]] is a prodrug / pharmacokinetics page for 7,8-DHF, while [[zeb85]] is the biologic comparator that the caution review itself points toward when it argues for better specificity.^[raw/articles/pmc-5777001-r13-7-8-dhf-prodrug-ad.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-35845610-zeb85-trkb-agonist-antibody.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-37488840-trkb-agonist-caution-review.md]
- ClinicalTrials.gov still does not show a named human cognition program for 7,8-DHF, LM22A-4, deoxygedunin, or the broad `TrkB agonist cognition` query as of 2026-04-28.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-trkb-bdnf-mimetic-registry-search-2026.md]

## Evidence Tiering

- `Human cognition trials`: none found in the targeted ClinicalTrials.gov search snapshot.
- `Human safety`: no named clinical safety package found for the core cognition candidates in this pass.
- `Preclinical cognition`: strongest for 7,8-DHF in object recognition / object-location memory, TBI recovery, and synaptic plasticity rescue.
- `Preclinical chemistry / target engagement`: 7,8-DHF, LM22A-4, deoxygedunin, R13, and ZEB85 are credible enough to track as separate entities, but not strong enough to rank as human interventions.
- `Anecdote`: direct-use anecdotes are captured only for [[7-8-dihydroxyflavone]] in this branch; [[lm22a-4]], [[deoxygedunin]], [[r13]], and [[zeb85]] have `absent` anecdote ledgers in the current corpus.
- `Review / caution`: the branch is mechanistically plausible but contested because TrkB specificity and disease-reversal claims are not settled.

## Target-Specificity Gate

This branch now justifies a reusable target-specificity gate rather than a one-off note. The reason is simple: the same literature contains a direct-binding positive for 7,8-DHF, a prodrug optimization paper that mostly improves exposure, a biologic antibody lane that is being positioned as more specific, and a direct assay-limitations paper saying the small-molecule activation story is not settled. That combination is exactly when the wiki should stop promoting a molecule on the strength of a single favorable assay.^[raw/articles/pubmed-25143381-7-8-dihydroxyflavone-binding-activation-trkb-receptor.md]^[raw/articles/pmc-5777001-r13-7-8-dhf-prodrug-ad.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-35845610-zeb85-trkb-agonist-antibody.md]^[raw/articles/pmc-8398766-small-molecule-trkb-agonist-limitations.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-37488840-trkb-agonist-caution-review.md]

## Effect Size Snapshot

No human effect size is available.

The animal readouts should be tagged by state:

- `Consolidation`: 7,8-DHF improved object memory after post-learning dosing in rats and object-location memory in APPswe/PS1dE9 mice and wild-type littermates.^[raw/articles/pubmed-24070857-78-dhf-memory-consolidation-rodents.md]
- `Injury recovery`: 7,8-DHF improved Barnes maze memory after rat TBI and normalized TrkB/plasticity/metabolic markers.^[raw/articles/pubmed-25661191-78-dhf-tbi-trkb.md]
- `Synaptic defect rescue`: 7,8-DHF restored spine density and functional/structural LTP in Nhe6 knockout neurons.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39341363-78-dhf-nhe6-plasticity.md]

## Safety And Translation

The safety burden is mostly unknown because the open evidence here does not include a named human cognition trial or a practical clinical dosing package. The main risks are interpretive rather than only toxicologic:

- BDNF/TrkB is pleiotropic, so indiscriminate activation is not automatically safe or cognition-selective.
- Some small-molecule benefits may be TrkB-independent, so target engagement needs specific assays rather than broad kinase inhibition or downstream markers alone.^[raw/articles/pubmed-37488840-trkb-agonist-caution-review.md]^[raw/articles/pmc-8398766-small-molecule-trkb-agonist-limitations.md]
- Rodent dosing, route, timing, and disease state should not be converted into self-experimentation protocols.

## Responder Phenotypes

The branch is most plausible in `plasticity-deficit` or `recovery` contexts:

- TBI or injury recovery, where TrkB signaling and energy/plasticity markers are disrupted.
- Neurodevelopmental or neurodegenerative models with synaptic-structure or endosomal-trafficking deficits, especially NHE6/SLC9A6-like biology.
- Stress or aging models where BDNF/TrkB signaling is reduced or maladaptive.

The branch is least supported for rested healthy-adult acute enhancement.

## Open Contradictions

- The mechanism is attractive, but the human translation layer is missing.
- 7,8-DHF is often described as a selective TrkB agonist, while later reviews warn that specificity is not always rigorously demonstrated.
- The same molecule can look like a memory-consolidation probe, neuroprotective agent, antioxidant, anti-inflammatory molecule, or druggability scaffold depending on assay context.
- A plasticity-opening compound could plausibly improve learning when paired with training, but the wiki currently has no human timing/training evidence for this class.
