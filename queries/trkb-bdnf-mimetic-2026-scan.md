# TrkB / BDNF-Mimetic Cognition Scan - 2026

Companion / expanded page: [[trkb-bdnf-mimetic-cognition-2026-scan]] adds the target-specificity caution review, the explicit ClinicalTrials.gov negative-search snapshot, and the standalone [[r13]] / [[zeb85]] comparator pages.

## Summary

The TrkB / BDNF-mimetic branch is still preclinical. [[7-8-dihydroxyflavone]] has the cleanest mechanistic trail, with direct TrkB binding and activation, plus multiple animal rescue papers in stress, TBI, glaucoma, MS, and NHE6-loss plasticity models.^[raw/articles/pubmed-25143381-7-8-dihydroxyflavone-binding-activation-trkb-receptor.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-25661191-flavonoid-derivative-7-8-dhf-tbi-pathology-trkb-activation.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39341363-slc9a6-nhe6-hippocampal-plasticity-trkb-agonist-7-8-dhf.md]

[[deoxygedunin]] is the older natural-product comparator: orally available in mice, TrkB-active, and learning-enhancing in animal systems, but still without a human cognition program in the captured registry snapshot.^[raw/articles/plosone-2010-deoxygedunin-natural-product-potent-neurotrophic-activity-mice.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-trkb-bdnf-mimetic-registry-search-2026.md]

[[lm22a-4]] stays in the tool-compound / preclinical lane despite a clearer Rett mouse object-location-memory and LTP rescue anchor. [[r13]] and [[zeb85]] now have separate prodrug and antibody-comparator pages, but none of these changes the human-evidence ceiling. The branch therefore belongs beside [[non-hallucinogenic-psychoplastogens]] as a mechanistic comparator, not as a recommended enhancer class.^[raw/articles/pubmed-28679669-lm22a4-rett-object-location-memory.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-38785269-lm22a4-rett-spine-behavior.md]^[raw/articles/pmc-5777001-r13-7-8-dhf-prodrug-ad.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-35845610-zeb85-trkb-agonist-antibody.md]

## What Changed Since 2024

The most relevant new item is the 2025 SLC9A6/NHE6 paper, which extends 7,8-DHF into a more specific synaptic-plasticity rescue phenotype rather than just a generic neuroprotection story.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39341363-slc9a6-nhe6-hippocampal-plasticity-trkb-agonist-7-8-dhf.md] That matters because it points to a sharper responder phenotype: impaired TrkB-linked plasticity, not healthy baseline enhancement.

What did not change is the human evidence ceiling. The captured registry snapshot does not show a named human cognition trial for the named compounds, and the visible literature remains dominated by animal models, cell systems, and review-level extrapolation.

## Effect Size Snapshot

There is no human effect size to report. In the animal literature, the signal is directionally positive but model-specific: memory rescue after stress or TBI, plasticity rescue in NHE6 loss, and broader neuroprotective claims in other tissue models. Those are hypothesis-generating rather than transportable effect sizes.

## Safety / Adverse Events

No human tolerability series surfaced in this pass. That is the main safety gap. In the preclinical literature, the compounds are used systemically and sometimes at pharmacologic doses that are not obviously translatable to casual self-experimentation.

## Responder Phenotypes

- Stress-exposed rodents with hippocampal memory impairment.
- Rodent TBI models with plasticity and energy-homeostasis disruption.
- Nhe6 / SLC9A6 loss-of-function plasticity phenotypes.
- Other injury / disease models where TrkB signaling is plausibly impaired.

## Protocol Parameters

- 7,8-DHF TBI study: 5 mg/kg intraperitoneal daily for 7 days.
- 7,8-DHF binding / activation work: cell and rodent-brain assays, with prolonged TrkB phosphorylation in the biochemical study.
- LM22A-4 preclinical studies: systemic dosing in mouse / rodent injury models, often in the 10-15 mg/kg range in the spinal-cord paper.
- Deoxygedunin discovery paper: orally available in mice, with learning-enhancement claims in animal assays.

## Open Contradictions

- The 2016 review language is more optimistic than the current human evidence justifies.
- 7,8-DHF is often treated as a general BDNF substitute, but the mechanistic papers show dependence on neuronal maturation and receptor context.
- TrkB activation may be beneficial in rescue models, but it is not automatically a universal enhancer signal.
- Deoxygedunin, LM22A-4, and 7,8-DHF are often lumped together as if they were one branch, even though their chemistry and evidence depth differ.
