# Exerkine and Myokine Cognition Scan

## Summary

This branch is a mediator map, not a therapeutic recommendation. The best-supported claims are that exercise changes circulating factors, some of those factors track with brain health, and a subset can transfer partial benefits in mice. The strongest current human evidence is still mostly biomarker or association data, not direct supplementation trials.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40560607-correlation-between-irisin-and-cognitive-functions-in-alzheimer-dementia.md]^[raw/articles/pmc-12284321-association-between-irisin-level-and-cognitive-function-systematic-review-meta-analysis.md]^[raw/articles/pmc-12315660-irisin-emerging-therapeutic-targets-for-cognitive-impairment-related-diseases.md]^[raw/articles/pmc-7261628-cathepsin-b-in-neurodegeneration-of-alzheimers-disease-traumatic-brain-injury-and-related-brain-disorders.md]^[raw/articles/nature-platelet-derived-exerkine-cxcl4-platelet-factor-4-rejuvenates-hippocampal-neurogenesis-and-restores-cognitive-function-in-aged-mice.md]^[raw/articles/nature-exercise-plasma-boosts-memory-and-dampens-brain-inflammation-via-clusterin.md]

[[irisin]] is the most active 2025 sub-branch: a biomarker-defined AD cohort found lower CSF and serum irisin in AD and MCI, with correlations to global cognition and several domains, while the 2025 meta-analysis still reads as association-positive overall but subdomain-null and the review frames the therapeutic literature as exploratory.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40560607-correlation-between-irisin-and-cognitive-functions-in-alzheimer-dementia.md]^[raw/articles/pmc-12284321-association-between-irisin-level-and-cognitive-function-systematic-review-meta-analysis.md]^[raw/articles/pmc-12315660-irisin-emerging-therapeutic-targets-for-cognitive-impairment-related-diseases.md]

[[cathepsin-b]] remains plausible but mixed. The review literature still treats it as a candidate exercise mediator, but the human mediation story remains heterogeneous rather than settled.^[raw/articles/pmc-7261628-cathepsin-b-in-neurodegeneration-of-alzheimers-disease-traumatic-brain-injury-and-related-brain-disorders.md]

[[pf4]] and [[clusterin]] are the cleanest exercise-to-brain transfer factors in the mouse literature. [[pf4]] now also has a 2025 review that frames the translational story as promising but still mouse-first, while [[clusterin]] continues to have the best human exercise-plasma biomarker support and direct inflammatory readouts.^[raw/articles/nature-platelet-derived-exerkine-cxcl4-platelet-factor-4-rejuvenates-hippocampal-neurogenesis-and-restores-cognitive-function-in-aged-mice.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40749766-pf4-cognition-review.md]^[raw/articles/nature-exercise-plasma-boosts-memory-and-dampens-brain-inflammation-via-clusterin.md]

[[gpld1]] is now the strongest liver-to-brain exercise-factor mechanism in this scan. The 2020 paper established the exercise plasma / aged-mouse cognition transfer story and active-older-human correlation, while the 2026 Cell follow-up adds a TNAP / BBB mechanism in aging and Alzheimer's disease mouse models.^[raw/articles/pubmed-32646997-gpld1-exercise-neurogenesis-cognition.md]^[raw/articles/sciencedirect-2026-gpld1-tnap-memory-loss-vasculature.md]

[[osteocalcin]] is a useful comparator, but it is closer to a bone-brain endocrine axis than a canonical exerkine. It has mixed human cognition associations and strong mechanistic GPR158 literature, yet the exercise link is indirect and the human translational story is still not a validated enhancer class.^[raw/articles/pubmed-36094482-undercarboxylated-osteocalcin-cognition-women-hiv.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-28851741-gpr158-osteocalcin-cognition.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40337551-osteocalcin-gpr158-bone-brain-review.md]

[[apelin]] is the weakest branch for cognition specifically. Current literature supports neuroprotective, affective-behavior, and metabolic-state biology, but it does not yet supply a convincing human cognition endpoint story.^[raw/articles/pubmed-35305406-apelin-emotional-behavior-age-metabolic-state.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39681256-apelin-cns-diseases-review.md]

What changed since 2024:

- [[irisin]] moved from a mostly exercise-biology candidate to a biomarker-defined human AD/MCI correlation paper plus several new mechanistic mouse papers.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40560607-correlation-between-irisin-and-cognitive-functions-in-alzheimer-dementia.md]
- [[cathepsin-b]] is now explicitly framed in the review literature as mixed in humans rather than a settled mediator.^[raw/articles/pmc-7261628-cathepsin-b-in-neurodegeneration-of-alzheimers-disease-traumatic-brain-injury-and-related-brain-disorders.md]
- [[pf4]] and [[clusterin]] remain the best-supported exercise-transfer factors, but their support is still mostly preclinical for cognition and human biomarker-level for exercise response.^[raw/articles/nature-platelet-derived-exerkine-cxcl4-platelet-factor-4-rejuvenates-hippocampal-neurogenesis-and-restores-cognitive-function-in-aged-mice.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40749766-pf4-cognition-review.md]^[raw/articles/nature-exercise-plasma-boosts-memory-and-dampens-brain-inflammation-via-clusterin.md]
- [[gpld1]] remains important as the liver-to-brain bridge. The 2026 TNAP / BBB paper strengthens the mechanism, but the intervention evidence is still mouse-first with human activity correlation rather than human GPLD1 dosing.^[raw/articles/pubmed-32646997-gpld1-exercise-neurogenesis-cognition.md]^[raw/articles/sciencedirect-2026-gpld1-tnap-memory-loss-vasculature.md]
- [[osteocalcin]] is still mechanistically rich, but the human cognitive signal is mixed association rather than practical nootropic target evidence.^[raw/articles/pubmed-36094482-undercarboxylated-osteocalcin-cognition-women-hiv.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40337551-osteocalcin-gpr158-bone-brain-review.md]
- [[apelin]] is still better treated as a CNS biology lead than a cognition lead.^[raw/articles/pubmed-35305406-apelin-emotional-behavior-age-metabolic-state.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39681256-apelin-cns-diseases-review.md]

## Evidence Tiering

| Layer | Best-supported branch claims | Current interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| Human cognition association | [[irisin]] in AD/MCI/SMC, [[osteocalcin]] in a women-with/without-HIV cohort | Correlation, not causation; useful for biomarker enrichment only. |
| Human exercise biomarker | [[clusterin]] after structured exercise; active older humans with higher [[gpld1]]; activity-linked irisin and osteocalcin signals | Supports exercise-response biology, not a direct supplement or injection recommendation. |
| Human RCT / intervention | No direct human exerkine or myokine cognition supplementation trial with a clear, reproducible efficacy win | The therapeutic class is not validated. |
| Animal transfer / mechanistic | [[pf4]], [[clusterin]], [[gpld1]], [[osteocalcin]], and [[irisin]] all have transfer, rescue, or receptor-mechanism evidence in mice | Strong mechanistic hypothesis generation, weak human translation. |
| Review / synthesis | [[cathepsin-b]], [[osteocalcin]], and [[apelin]] reviews are hypothesis-shaping but not decisive | Keep them in the comparator lane until better human endpoints appear. |

## Effect Size Snapshot

- No direct, reproducible human supplementation effect size exists yet for [[pf4]], [[clusterin]], [[gpld1]], [[irisin]], [[osteocalcin]], or [[apelin]] as cognition interventions.
- [[pf4]] and [[gpld1]] are currently mouse-transfer stories with no human dosing effect size to quote.
- [[clusterin]] is the strongest human exercise-linked biomarker signal in this set, but the cited 2021 study is still a biomarker-plus-transfer paper rather than a direct cognition trial.
- The [[irisin]] AD cohort gives correlation strength, not treatment effect size.

## Safety/Adverse Events

- None of these factors is a validated self-administered cognition therapy.
- Exogenous [[pf4]], [[clusterin]], [[gpld1]], [[irisin]], [[osteocalcin]], or [[apelin]] should be treated as research biology, not consumer nootropics.
- Plasma/exosome commercialization needs the separate regulatory and sourcing checks in the blood-factor branch.
- [[cathepsin-b]] and [[pf4]] also sit close to inflammation/coagulation biology, so any future translational work needs explicit safety monitoring rather than enthusiasm-driven escalation.

## Responder Phenotypes

- Older adults with lower reserve are the clearest exercise-transfer phenotype across [[pf4]], [[clusterin]], and [[gpld1]].
- AD/MCI and biomarker-defined neurodegenerative cohorts are where [[irisin]], [[gpld1]], and [[osteocalcin]] are most likely to keep appearing.
- Metabolic or inflammatory stress states may be where [[apelin]] and [[cathepsin-b]] effects are easiest to detect, but that is still an inference from the current literature.
- Healthy-enhancement use cases remain the weakest translation target.

## Protocol Parameters

- [[pf4]] mouse transfer: 500 ng intravenous PF4 every second day for 1 week in young adult mice, and every third day for 24 days in aged mice.
- [[pf4]] review: no human dosing protocol exists yet, so the review is still translational rather than interventional.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40749766-pf4-cognition-review.md]
- [[clusterin]] exercise study: 6 months of structured exercise in patients with cognitive impairment.
- [[gpld1]] 2020 study: older humans were stratified by activity level using a step-count threshold; the mouse rescue arm used liver expression of Gpld1.
- [[gpld1]] 2026 mechanism study: mouse work tied GPLD1 to TNAP cleavage on brain vasculature, BBB function, and cognition in aging and Alzheimer's disease models.
- [[irisin]] mouse studies: exogenous irisin or exercise-induced irisin in chronic cerebral hypoperfusion, LPS neuroinflammation, and gut-brain inflammation models.
- [[cathepsin-b]] review: exercise protocols were heterogeneous across the 8 included studies, which is part of why the human mediator story stays mixed.
- No standardized human protocol exists for direct factor administration in this branch.

## Open Contradictions

- Mediator versus biomarker is unresolved for [[irisin]], [[osteocalcin]], [[clusterin]], and [[gpld1]].
- [[cathepsin-b]] looks promising in some exercise contexts and inconsistent in others.
- [[pf4]] and [[clusterin]] are convincing in mice, but mouse transfer does not equal a validated human injection strategy, and PF4's 2025 review still reads as a preclinical-to-translational bridge rather than a human trial result.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40749766-pf4-cognition-review.md]
- [[apelin]] has CNS biology but not enough cognition-specific human evidence to justify recommendation or confidence promotion.
- Exercise is the proven intervention; these factors are the hypothesis layer.
