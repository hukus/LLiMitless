# Transcranial Photobiomodulation

Transcranial photobiomodulation (tPBM) uses red or near-infrared light delivered through the scalp to modulate brain function. The current literature now supports a slightly stronger claim than it did in 2024: tPBM is still not a settled cognitive enhancer, but it has repeated signals in older adults, MCI, and a small mTBI rehabilitation cohort, plus physiologic target engagement in healthy older adults. The 2025 systematic review also broadened the frame to sleep, wakefulness, and glymphatic mechanisms, and it explicitly argued for objective sleep measures, time-of-day control, and tighter parameter reporting in future work.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40822571-tpbm-systematic-review-sleep-wakefulness-cognition.md] The new home-based MCI pilot adds a multimodal readout with cognition, metabolism, and DMN connectivity, which makes the mechanistic story more coherent even though the sample is tiny.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39423445-transcranial-photobiomodulation-cognition-bdnf.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40482941-tpbm-healthy-older-adults-working-memory-fnirs.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40485299-tpbm-mtbi-cognitive-postconcussion-ptsd.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40939031-tpbm-mci-ad-sham-controlled.md]^[raw/articles/medrxiv-25333989-tpbm-mci-cognitive-metabolic-neuroimaging-pilot.md]

The branch is not uniformly positive. A 2024 randomized trial in persistent postconcussion symptoms did not show benefit over placebo after adjustment for prior concussions and multiple comparisons, which is the main counterweight to reading tPBM as a general recovery enhancer.^[raw/articles/pubmed-38109202-tpbm-concussion-null-rct.md]

A 2025 insomnia RCT adds a state-sensitive anchor: three frontal 810 nm sessions improved PSQI, ESS, and frontal delta power versus sham, with no serious adverse events. That supports tPBM as a sleep/wakefulness candidate, but it still does not establish chronotype-stratified cognition benefit.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41125953-tpbm-insomnia-rct.md]

When tPBM is combined with [[methylene-blue]], use [[methylene-blue-plus-light-redox-interaction-scan]] rather than importing the MB signal into this concept. The combination evidence is currently mechanism/preclinical plus weak anecdote: a hepatic-encephalopathy rat study tested separate MB and PBM arms, while a PBM mechanism review cautions that the cytochrome-c-oxidase story is not settled enough to infer synergy from mechanism language alone.^[raw/articles/pubmed-33549685-methylene-blue-photobiomodulation-hepatic-encephalopathy.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-32716711-photobiomodulation-cco-no-evidence-review.md]

The raw notes that anchor the claim are `raw/articles/pubmed-41584102-transcranial-1064nm-laser-photobiomodulation.md`, `raw/articles/pubmed-39423445-transcranial-photobiomodulation-cognition-bdnf.md`, `raw/articles/pubmed-40482941-tpbm-healthy-older-adults-working-memory-fnirs.md`, and `raw/articles/pubmed-40939031-tpbm-mci-ad-sham-controlled.md`.

For the timing-specific scan, see [[chronotype-specific-response-across-sleep-stimulation-interventions]].

The strongest practical takeaway is not that tPBM is established, but that it is biologically plausible and now producing measurable changes in cognition, cortical dynamics, and functional connectivity in multiple small studies. The newest direct cognition-positive RCT is the mTBI crossover study, which looks more like a rehabilitation signal than a broad enhancement signal. That makes tPBM a candidate for careful comparison against [[focused-ultrasound-neuromodulation]] and [[peptide-neuroprotection]] rather than a finished solution. It also belongs in the same light-based neighborhood as [[bright-light-therapy-and-cognition]] and [[hyperbaric-oxygen-neurocognitive-recovery]], though the latter is an oxygen-delivery intervention rather than a direct brain-stimulation analogue. For the creatine combination hypothesis, see [[tpbm-creatine-combination-logic]]. For side-by-side framing with broader adjunctive options and the early-stage neuromodulation cluster, see [[adjunctive-interventions-for-cognition]] and [[neurocognitive-interventions-under-study]].^[raw/articles/pubmed-40485299-tpbm-mtbi-cognitive-postconcussion-ptsd.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40482941-tpbm-healthy-older-adults-working-memory-fnirs.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40822571-tpbm-systematic-review-sleep-wakefulness-cognition.md]

## What Changed Since 2024

- The 2024 MCI placebo-controlled trial reported better MoCA change and a BDNF increase with active tPBM, which strengthened the biomarker story.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39423445-transcranial-photobiomodulation-cognition-bdnf.md]
- The 2025 healthy-older-adult crossover study added objective working-memory and network-level effects in a non-demented cohort.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40482941-tpbm-healthy-older-adults-working-memory-fnirs.md]
- The 2025 mTBI randomized crossover trial added the newest direct cognition-positive signal, with gains in visual working memory, verbal learning, sleep quality, post-concussion symptoms, pain, and PTSD symptoms.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40485299-tpbm-mtbi-cognitive-postconcussion-ptsd.md]
- The 2025 home-based MCI pilot added a metabolic and neuroimaging layer, with improved global cognition and episodic memory plus higher pyruvate/lactate, lower lactate-to-pyruvate ratio, increased DMN connectivity, and lower IL-6.^[raw/articles/medrxiv-25333989-tpbm-mci-cognitive-metabolic-neuroimaging-pilot.md]
- The 2024 persistent-concussion RCT was null after adjustment, so the recovery phenotype is not automatically stronger than the healthy-older-adult or MCI signals.^[raw/articles/pubmed-38109202-tpbm-concussion-null-rct.md]
- The 2025 dual-neuromodulation MCI trial suggests tPBM may work better as an adjunct to another stimulation modality than as monotherapy in some settings.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40254536-combined-tms-tpbm-mci-working-memory.md]
- The 2025 AD/MCI sham-controlled trial added a small home-use safety and feasibility signal.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40939031-tpbm-mci-ad-sham-controlled.md]
- The 2025 systematic review reframed tPBM as a sleep/wakefulness and glymphatic question as much as a cognition question, and it called out objective sleep metrics and timing discipline as future design requirements.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40822571-tpbm-systematic-review-sleep-wakefulness-cognition.md]
- Multiple active trials are now registered, so the field is moving toward confirmation rather than speculation.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct07224607-tpbm-alzheimers-study.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct04784416-trap-ad.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct07209683-near-infrared-light-young-older-adults.md]

Anecdotal reports should be indexed here only as signal, not proof, and ideally linked back to a formal study or case report in [[anecdotal-evidence-in-cognitive-intervention-research]].
