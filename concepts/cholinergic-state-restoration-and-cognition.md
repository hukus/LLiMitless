# Cholinergic State-Restoration and Cognition

This concept page separates two different claims that often get collapsed together:

1. Cholinergic interventions can restore performance when the baseline state is low, disrupted, or recovering.
2. Cholinergic interventions can produce broad healthy-adult enhancement.

The wiki currently has support for the first claim and only narrow, phenotype-bound support for the second. The evidence cluster points most strongly to sustained attention, perceptual detection, cue-response updating, and information-processing speed, with memory effects usually appearing in specific subgroups or recovery settings rather than as a stable class-wide gain.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41076097-cholinergic-signaling-cognition-human-pharmacological-studies.md]

## What Counts As Support

| Intervention | Best-supported context | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| Nicotine | Smoking abstinence or other withdrawal-adjacent states | The cleanest acute attention signal in the branch, but much of the apparent benefit can be read as state restoration rather than direct enhancement.^[raw/articles/pubmed-38582919-nicotine-cognitive-function-smokers-abstinence.md] |
| Nicotine tablet | Occasional nicotine-pouch users in a live dose-response registry study | The newest registry readout is still unpublished even though the estimated completion date has passed, so it is useful as a current watch item but not as a new efficacy win yet.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct07408180-nicotine-tablet-attention-2026-04-30.md] |
| [[varenicline]] | Healthy non-smokers in a small older study | A small direct memory signal exists, but it is old, tiny, and not yet reproduced in newer healthy-adult work.^[raw/articles/pubmed-23072834-varenicline-healthy-non-smoking-adults.md] |
| Nicotine + [[galantamine]] | Never-smokers on attention tasks | One small study suggests the combination can shift reaction time on a task-specific basis, which supports mechanism interaction but not a general nootropic claim.^[raw/articles/pubmed-31686175-nicotine-galantamine-healthy-never-smokers-cognition.md] |
| [[galantamine]] | People with more cholinergic reserve or recovery phenotypes | Basal-forebrain enrichment mattered in healthy older adults, while SAH safety looked acceptable and the HIV trial was cognitively null.^[raw/articles/pubmed-33842683-galantamine-basal-forebrain-predictor.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40877496-galantamine-sah-safety-trial.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41427614-galantamine-hiv-no-cognition.md] |
| Choline alphoscerate / alfoscerate | Amnestic MCI and mild TBI recovery | Positive signals here belong in impairment or recovery buckets, not in healthy-adult enhancement buckets.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39300341-choline-alphoscerate-amnestic-mci.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40353278-choline-alfoscerate-mtbi-recovery.md] |
| Choline-source supplements like [[lysolecithin]], [[phosphatidylcholine]], and [[choline-bitartrate]] | Healthy adults, older adults with forgetfulness concerns, and narrow task probes | Biomarker movement and form differences matter, but the cognitive read is fragmented: [[lysolecithin]] was memory-null, lecithin/PC is old mixed-to-negative except a high-dose serial-learning signal and an egg-yolk product-matrix signal, and [[choline-bitartrate]] is visuomotor-positive but memory-null and TMAO-gated.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39896163-lysolecithin-cognition-choline.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-6346908-lecithin-memory-normal-adults.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-9377589-phosphatidylcholine-explicit-memory.md]^[raw/articles/bmc-2023-egg-yolk-choline-cognitive-functions.md]^[raw/articles/nature-2015-choline-bitartrate-visuomotor.md]^[raw/articles/plos-2016-choline-bitartrate-memory-null.md]^[raw/articles/pmc-8410632-choline-forms-tmao-response.md] |
| Muscarinic M1 PAMs | Healthy-volunteer target engagement | VU0467319 gives a human mechanism anchor: 52 healthy volunteers, 60-600 mg single-ascending-dose cohorts, food-effect testing, and exploratory cognitive / ERP readouts, but still no efficacy proof.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40598606-vu0467319-m1-pam-healthy-participants.md] |
| Xanomeline-trospium / Cobenfy | Acute schizophrenia symptom control | FDA approval and the EMERGENT trials make this a real clinical branch, but the pivotal evidence is symptom-first rather than cognition-first. The 2026 review strengthens the pro-cognitive hypothesis, yet it is still downstream of mixed preclinical and clinical evidence rather than a modern cognition-first trial.^[raw/articles/fda-cobenfy-approval-2024.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-38691387-xanomeline-trospium-schizophrenia-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39488504-xanomeline-trospium-pooled-emergent.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41418563-xanomeline-trospium-cognitive-impairment-review.md] |
| Emraclidine | Schizophrenia symptom development | A selective M4 PAM with a positive phase-1b signal and posted phase-2 registry results, but the public results remain PANSS-first and do not yet supply a dedicated cognition win.^[raw/articles/pubmed-36528376-emraclidine-m4-phase1b.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct05227690-emraclidine-phase2-results.md] |
| Xanomeline in AD | Direct cognition signal with poor tolerability | The older AD trial program is the clearest direct cognition evidence in the muscarinic branch, with ADAS-Cog and CNTB improvements plus reaction-time and delayed-recall signals, but high-dose cholinergic toxicity keeps it from being a practical enhancer story.^[raw/articles/pubmed-9109749-xanomeline-alzheimer-cognitive-behavior.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-9876958-xanomeline-ad-cntb.md] |

## Current State

The best current model is a responder-enrichment framework:

- Withdrawal-adjacent or low-arousal states are where nicotine looks strongest.
- Structural or biological reserve may determine whether galantamine helps.
- Impairment, recovery, food-matrix, or narrow visuomotor contexts are where choline donors still have a plausible role.
- Muscarinic selectivity is now clinically real in schizophrenia, but the modern human evidence is symptom-first rather than cognition-first.
- The 2026 xanomeline-trospium review is useful as a hypothesis generator because it aggregates 24 studies and reports verbal-learning, delayed-memory, and reaction-time improvements, but the conflict profile means it should not be treated as settled proof.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41418563-xanomeline-trospium-cognitive-impairment-review.md]
- The only clear direct cognition signal in the muscarinic branch still comes from older xanomeline AD trials, which were limited by cholinergic toxicity.
- Choline-source supplements can improve biomarkers without producing a clear placebo-separated memory gain in healthy adults.

That makes this branch different from caffeine or modafinil. Those are cleaner comparator baselines in [[pharmacology-comparator-map]], while cholinergic interventions are better understood as state-dependent tools that should be benchmarked against [[healthy-adult-cognition-signals]] and then interpreted through [[digital-phenotype-and-biomarker-responder-atlas]].

## Why It Is Not A Generic Enhancer

- The strongest signals are phenotype-bound, not universal.
- Effects are often task-specific, especially for attention and updating.
- The safety ceiling is real: dependence, blood-pressure and heart-rate effects, sleep disruption, nausea, vivid dreams, and bradycardia all matter.
- Small studies and older positive signals are easy to overread when the branch includes both restoration and recovery settings.

The practical implication is that a cholinergic result should not be called a "nootropic" unless it beats the comparator stack in [[pharmacology-comparator-map]] without simply correcting a low baseline or a withdrawal state.

## Open Questions

- Which baseline marker best predicts response: basal-forebrain structure, smoking status, inflammatory state, sleep state, or something else?
- Is the overdue nicotine-tablet registry study going to read out as a meaningful attention effect, or just another state-restoration result?
- Can a modern muscarinic PAM show a real cognition effect after target engagement is established?
- Can Cobenfy or emraclidine separate symptom control from actual cognition improvement in a modern cognition-first trial?
- Do the apparent benefits of nicotine and galantamine reflect direct enhancement, or mostly state restoration and task-specific rescue?
- Can any choline-donor result be reproduced in cleaner, healthier cohorts without collapsing [[alpha-gpc]], [[citicoline]], [[lysolecithin]], [[phosphatidylcholine]], and [[choline-bitartrate]]?

For practical use, keep this branch linked to [[cholinergic-and-nicotinic-agents-2026-scan]] and [[self-experimentation-stop-rules]], because the same interventions that can sharpen attention can also create tolerance, sleep loss, or cardiovascular noise.
