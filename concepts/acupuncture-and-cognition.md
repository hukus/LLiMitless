# Acupuncture And Cognition

Acupuncture is a traditional medicine treatment rather than a molecule. It still matters for the wiki because it appears in MCI, vascular cognitive impairment, post-stroke rehabilitation, pain/sleep, and brain-fog anecdote contexts.

## Bottom Line

Medicinal cognition signal: `low and impairment-context`. Meta-analyses in MCI and vascular MCI lean positive on global screening scores such as MoCA and MMSE, but the higher-quality sham-controlled 2025 pilot did not separate on the ADAS-cog primary endpoint.^[raw/articles/frontiers-2023-acupuncture-mci-meta-analysis.md]^[raw/articles/bmc-2025-acupuncture-mci-sham-pilot.md]^[raw/articles/nature-2024-electroacupuncture-vamci-meta-analysis.md]

Healthy-adult enhancement signal: `absent-to-very-low`. Brain-fog anecdotes exist, but they are mixed, practitioner-dependent, and confounded by pain, sleep, fatigue, stress, and lifestyle changes.^[raw/articles/reddit-2022-2023-acupuncture-brain-fog-threads.md]

## Evidence Layers

The 2023 MCI meta-analysis included 11 RCTs with 602 patients and reported low-quality evidence for better overall cognition on MMSE and MoCA. The same review did not find clear memory-function or ADL advantages versus conventional medicine, and the authors called for higher-quality studies.^[raw/articles/frontiers-2023-acupuncture-mci-meta-analysis.md]

The 2024 electroacupuncture vascular-MCI meta-analysis included 15 RCTs with 1,033 participants and favored adjunctive electroacupuncture for MoCA, MMSE, and ADL. Its own limitations are the practical ones: bias concerns, limited randomization/allocation detail, short follow-up, and heterogeneous point/stimulation protocols.^[raw/articles/nature-2024-electroacupuncture-vamci-meta-analysis.md]

The 2025 sham-controlled pilot is the most useful reality check. Thirty participants were randomized, 28 completed 24 sessions over 12 weeks, both acupuncture and sham groups improved, and the between-group ADAS-cog difference was not significant. A memory-domain secondary outcome had a moderate non-significant effect size favoring acupuncture.^[raw/articles/bmc-2025-acupuncture-mci-sham-pilot.md]

## Safety Gate

NCCIH's safety summary says complications are relatively few when acupuncture is delivered properly, but improper delivery can cause infections, punctured organs, or central nervous system injury. FDA-regulated acupuncture needles should be sterile and labeled for single use.^[raw/articles/nccih-2026-acupuncture-effectiveness-safety.md]

For cognition/brain-fog use, the minimum safety gate is a qualified practitioner, sterile single-use needles, no improvisation with implanted or electrical devices, and symptom stop-rules for dizziness, worsening neurologic symptoms, infection signs, unusual pain, syncope, or new weakness/numbness.

## Signal Judgment

Acupuncture is worth tracking as a treatment for cognitive impairment and possibly brain-fog phenotypes where pain, sleep, autonomic state, fatigue, or vascular injury are part of the case. It is not a proven healthy-adult nootropic. The most defensible research question is whether a standardized protocol can beat credible sham in a defined MCI or post-injury brain-fog phenotype with delayed follow-up.
