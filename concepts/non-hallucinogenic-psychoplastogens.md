# Non-Hallucinogenic Psychoplastogens

Non-hallucinogenic psychoplastogens are compounds designed to preserve the plasticity-promoting biology associated with psychedelics while minimizing or removing the subjective hallucinatory state.

## Current State

The class is still mostly a preclinical and mechanistic literature. The strongest current examples in the wiki’s source set are tabernanthalog (TBG) and 2-bromo-LSD (2-Br-LSD). Both show rodent plasticity and behavioral signals, but neither has a human cognition trial in the open evidence I found.

The 2025 review of non-hallucinogenic psychedelics for mood and anxiety found only five animal studies and one case report, which is a strong signal that the human evidence base is still thin.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40354769-non-hallucinogenic-psychedelics-mood-anxiety-systematic-review.md]

## Why It Matters For Cognition

The main cognition-relevant hypothesis is not "acute IQ boost." It is whether a non-hallucinogenic 5-HT2A-linked plasticity signal can support learning, recovery, or flexibility without the monitoring burden and subjective disruption of a classic psychedelic.

Classic psychedelic microdosing is adjacent but not interchangeable. The 2026 microdosing meta-analysis found a cognitive-control decrement rather than a broad enhancement signal, the two 2026 placebo-controlled psilocybin microdosing trials did not produce a general cognitive or subjective benefit, the 2025 low-dose LSD ADHD RCT was placebo-equivalent, and the 2026 creativity mega-analysis found only a narrow originality/fluency signal rather than a broad creativity upgrade.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41314362-effects-of-psychedelic-microdosing-on-cognitive-functions.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41110634-psilocybin-microdosing-cognitive-subjective-effects.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41187880-psilocybin-microdosing-creativity-trials.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40105807-repeated-low-dose-lsd-adhd-adults.md]

That hypothesis is still indirect. The best rodent cognition-adjacent signal in this source set is TBG rescuing stress-induced cognitive flexibility deficits after unpredictable mild stress.^[raw/articles/pubmed-34035476-tabernanthalog-stress-cognitive-flexibility.md]

## Leading Exemplars

- `tabernanthalog` (TBG): non-hallucinogenic psychoplastogen scaffold with rodent neuroplasticity and stress-rescue data.
- `2-bromo-LSD` (2-Br-LSD): non-hallucinogenic LSD analog with preclinical plasticity and stress-related behavior data.

## Human Evidence

No human cognition trial or registered human psychoplastogen cognition study surfaced in the current search.

Human biomarker data on the broader psychoplastogen class are mixed. The BDNF meta-analysis did not resolve a clean peripheral biomarker story, which weakens any simple "plasticity biomarker equals cognitive benefit" inference.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39613915-psychoplastogens-bdnf-meta-analysis.md]

## Mechanism Notes

Recent mechanistic work suggests the field should be cautious about assuming "non-hallucinogenic" is just a biased-agonism problem. A 2025 study argued that 5-HT2A signaling efficacy, not biased agonism alone, may separate psychedelic from non-psychedelic drugs.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40545270-5ht2a-efficacy-nonpsychedelic-drugs.md]

The TBG paper also suggests that immediate glutamate burst or immediate early gene activation may not be required for plasticity, which is relevant because some earlier psychedelic models treated those as necessary steps.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40760185-tabernanthalog-neuroplasticity-no-iea.md]

TrkB / BDNF-mimetic compounds are now tracked separately in [[trkb-bdnf-mimetic-cognition-2026-scan]]. They are useful plasticity-mechanism comparators because 7,8-DHF, LM22A-4, deoxygedunin, R13, and ZEB85-style leads target BDNF/TrkB rather than 5-HT2A psychoplastogen signaling. The translation status is similarly early, but the failure mode is different: TrkB specificity and human registry absence, not hallucinogenicity removal.^[raw/articles/pubmed-26740873-78-dhf-trkb-review.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-37488840-trkb-agonist-caution-review.md]
