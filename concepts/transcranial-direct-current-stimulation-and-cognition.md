# Transcranial Direct Current Stimulation and Cognition

Transcranial direct current stimulation (tDCS) is attractive because it is cheap, portable, and easy to combine with cognitive training. The trade-off is that its cognitive effects are usually smaller and more protocol-sensitive than rTMS, so dose parameters and stimulation target matter a lot. The newest broad meta-analysis keeps the branch alive at the umbrella level: across 69 studies and 5,545 participants, tDCS showed significant cognitive effects, but those effects were moderated by timing, population, duration, intensity, stimulation site, and age rather than by a one-size-fits-all montage rule.^[raw/articles/pubmed-38060075-tdcs-cognitive-functions-review.md]

The newest dose-parameter meta-analysis pushes this from a vague warning to a specific map: current density at or below 0.06 mA/cm2, stimulation durations above 20 minutes, up to 15 sessions, and temporal targets were the clearest subgroup signals, while montage category itself was not the main discriminator.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39905569-tdcs-dosage-parameters.md]

The most relevant raw notes for that framing are `raw/articles/pubmed-39905569-tdcs-cognitive-impairments-dosage-parameters.md` and `raw/articles/pubmed-41549257-noninvasive-neuromodulation-cognitive-function-adl.md`.

Timing is now part of the tDCS dose, not just a nuisance variable. In a 2021 chronotype study, motor learning and cognitive performance were better at circadian-preferred times, and the authors observed stimulation-by-chronotype-by-daytime interactions for cortical excitability and after-effects. A 2018 episodic-memory paper then found that prefrontal anodal tDCS was easier to detect in morning sessions. The practical read is that session timing belongs in the protocol if you want to interpret a null or a positive tDCS result cleanly.^[raw/articles/pubmed-34344864-chronotype-and-tdcs-timing-cognition.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-30035659-tdcs-time-of-day-episodic-memory-retrieval.md]

For the timing-specific branch, see [[chronotype-specific-response-across-sleep-stimulation-interventions]].

The most reproducible framing is that tDCS may help a subset of older adults or patients with cognitive impairment, especially when anodal prefrontal stimulation is paired with structured training. That said, the newer evidence is more nuanced: older-impaired meta-analytic data favored tDCS alone over tDCS plus training for global cognition, while post-stroke rehabilitation meta-analysis favored combined tDCS plus cognitive rehab. The likely answer is that context, phenotype, and target matter more than a blanket "pair with training" rule.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39905569-tdcs-cognitive-impairments-dosage-parameters.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40567421-tdcs-combined-cognitive-rehabilitation-post-stroke.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41549257-noninvasive-neuromodulation-cognitive-function-adl.md]

A 2025 HD-tDCS randomized trial sharpens the target lesson further: M1 and DLPFC stimulation improved cognitive flexibility and processing speed, while cerebellar stimulation selectively improved working memory. That makes the cerebellum a real target class rather than a footnote, and it weakens any assumption that frontal stimulation is the only meaningful tDCS setup.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40174444-hdtdcs-region-specific-cognition-older-adults.md]

Two 2025-2026 focal-language studies push the responder story beyond age alone. In a preregistered picture-naming study, older adults benefited from left IFG stimulation only when baseline naming was poor. In a separate verbal-fluency study, left IFG and left TPJ were not interchangeable, and the fluency effect varied over time rather than staying uniformly positive. The practical reading is that baseline cognition and site are part of the dose, not just after-the-fact subgroup labels.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40347355-focal-tdcs-picture-naming-baseline-performance.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41870727-tdcs-verbal-fluency-baseline-cognition-site.md]

Durability is the weak spot. The 2024 older-adult/MCI meta-analysis did not show a durable 1-month signal, so the branch still reads as a short-horizon or parameter-sensitive tool rather than a proven retained enhancer. For the retention-focused comparison, see [[washout-durability-for-cognition-interventions]].^[raw/articles/pubmed-38452749-tdcs-older-adults-mci-2024.md]

A newer amnestic-MCI trial adds a specific null to the combination story: cognitive stimulation improved cognition and biomarkers, but active tDCS did not enhance either the cognitive or the modulatory effects beyond sham. That is a useful reminder that the stimulation component can be the weak link even when the overall program looks biologically active.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40004217-tdcs-cognitive-stimulation-amci-null.md]

Two additional parameter-response signals matter. First, age seems to increase response variability, which is a direct argument for personalization rather than standard dosing. Second, specific targets can matter in challenge states: a vmPFC target outperformed dlPFC for stress-induced working-memory deficits, a broad-frontal montage beat the other tested montages for 2-back reaction time in healthy adults, and focal language-network stimulation appears to depend on starting performance as much as on the site itself. A separate healthy-participant null also shows that a montage shaped around a hallucination model does not automatically produce the expected false-perception effect.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40633904-tdcs-variability-age-2025.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40292419-tdcs-stress-working-memory-2025.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-36563998-tdcs-montage-attention-working-memory.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40347355-focal-tdcs-picture-naming-baseline-performance.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41870727-tdcs-verbal-fluency-baseline-cognition-site.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41449818-tdcs-auditory-false-perceptions-null.md]

For the sleep-loss and sequencing lens, see [[cbt-i-and-neuromodulation-sequence-effects-on-cognition]]. The key point is that tDCS during sleep deprivation is not the same question as tDCS after insomnia treatment.

The wiki should treat tDCS as a plausible, low-risk neuromodulation candidate with uncertain effect sizes and strong parameter dependence. For navigation, it also belongs with [[healthy-adult-cognition-signals]], [[medicine-cognition-summary]], [[most-promising-methods-for-cognition]], and [[tdcs-tacs-parameter-response-2025-2026-scan]].

The newest parameter lesson is that target may outrank montage in some settings: the same current family can land on different cognitive domains depending on whether the lead target is M1, DLPFC, or cerebellum, and in focal language studies the baseline performer can matter as much as the site. The newest contradiction layer is that a model-shaped montage can still be null on a healthy false-perception task, so "plausible target" is not the same thing as "behaves as predicted."^[raw/articles/pubmed-40174444-hdtdcs-region-specific-cognition-older-adults.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40347355-focal-tdcs-picture-naming-baseline-performance.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41870727-tdcs-verbal-fluency-baseline-cognition-site.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41449818-tdcs-auditory-false-perceptions-null.md]
