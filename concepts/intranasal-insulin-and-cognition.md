# Intranasal Insulin and Cognition

Intranasal insulin is one of the clearest examples of a metabolic-brain intervention: it is designed to boost central insulin signaling without the peripheral hypoglycemia risk that would make systemic insulin impractical for cognition. That makes it conceptually adjacent to [[incretin-therapies-brain-health]] and more broadly to [[peptide-neuroprotection]], because all three track the idea that brain function can be nudged by metabolic signaling.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41436338-intranasal-insulin-mci-ad-systematic-review-meta-analysis.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40195962-intranasal-insulin-ad-systematic-review-meta-analysis.md]

June 2026 attribution gate: intranasal insulin should be treated as a peptide-hormone delivery intervention, not a general nootropic. Future reports need insulin type/analog, delivery device, dose per nostril and total dose, timing relative to meals/surgery/stimulation/cognitive testing, metabolic phenotype, glucose or hypoglycemia monitoring, nasal status, APOE/sex/cognitive-status context where relevant, co-interventions, adverse events, and objective cognition or delirium endpoints before ranking changes.

The main raw evidence anchors now include the 2025 AD/MCI meta-analysis, the 2023 human cognition meta-analysis, and the 2023 CBF/cognition systematic review, plus the older stratifier and phenotype-trial papers that explain why the signal is not uniform.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40195962-intranasal-insulin-ad-systematic-review-meta-analysis.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-37379265-intranasal-insulin-cognition-humans.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-36219990-intranasal-insulin-cbf-cognition-adults-systematic-review.md]

Relevant raw PubMed notes: `raw/articles/pubmed-40195962-intranasal-insulin-ad-systematic-review-meta-analysis.md` and `raw/articles/pubmed-36530613-hypoglycemic-drugs-ad-mci-network-meta-analysis.md`.

The human literature is mixed. Small trials and some reviews suggest short-term improvements in memory or attention in at least some participants, but the signal is heterogeneous and sensitive to baseline status, genotype, sex, device, and dose. Safety is generally acceptable, with nasal irritation and congestion more common than serious systemic adverse events.^[raw/articles/pubmed-37564252-safety-efficacy-intranasal-insulin-ad.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-37379265-intranasal-insulin-cognition-humans.md]

The latest layer changes the overall interpretation rather than just sharpening the responder map. The 2025 MCI/AD meta-analysis found no meaningful cognitive, functional, or CSF biomarker benefit, so the older chronic-disease optimism is no longer a good default reading.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41436338-intranasal-insulin-mci-ad-systematic-review-meta-analysis.md] The strongest modern human signal has shifted toward perioperative neurocognitive protection: a cardiac-surgery RCT, a hip-fracture RCT, and two 2026 meta-analyses now point to lower postoperative delirium and better short-term recovery or POCD prevention in surgical patients.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40359848-intranasal-insulin-postop-sleep-cardiac-surgery.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41146035-intranasal-insulin-hip-fracture-delirium.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41720686-intranasal-insulin-perioperative-delirium-meta.md]^[raw/articles/sage-2026-intranasal-insulin-postoperative-delirium-meta.md]

A narrower 2025 AD-only meta-analysis still favored 20 IU on ADAS-cog and suggested an APOE4-negative verbal-memory signal, so the most defensible reading is not “INI never works” but “the signal collapses once you broaden disease state, dose, or endpoint too far.”^[raw/articles/pubmed-40195962-intranasal-insulin-ad-systematic-review-meta-analysis.md] The responder story therefore remains part of the concept, but it is now a dose- and phenotype-specific hypothesis rather than a general enhancer claim.

A new 2025 phase 2A/B factorial trial complicates the broad-null reading without overturning it: in MCI, early AD, or amyloid-positive participants, intranasal insulin 40 IU QID and empagliflozin 10 mg daily were both well tolerated, with INI improving mPACC5 and some fluid/imaging biomarkers while empagliflozin shifted CSF tau and vascular/neurodegenerative markers.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41057918-intranasal-insulin-empagliflozin-mci-early-ad.md] That is encouraging, but it is still short-duration, enriched, and not a durability test.

In people with insulin resistance or diabetes, the signal still looks more plausible than in healthy baseline enhancement: acute T2D studies improved visuospatial memory and perfusion, MemAID separated benefits by diabetes status, and the 2025 T2D imaging sub-study tied better connectivity to lower HOMA-IR.^[raw/articles/pubmed-24101698-intranasal-insulin-type2-diabetes-vasoreactivity-cognition.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-35482079-memaid-intranasal-insulin-type2-diabetes-control-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40392946-intranasal-insulin-t2d-rsfc.md]

Two newer 2025 sources make the delivery problem harder to ignore. A phase Ib/II MS cognition trial using ViaNase delivery and 10 IU BID versus 20 IU BID found no superiority over placebo on SDMT or secondary endpoints, even though safety was acceptable.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40253245-intranasal-insulin-ms-null.md] A first-in-human PET study using an Aptar Cartridge Pump System confirmed measurable brain uptake in humans and showed that uptake varied by cognitive status, sex, and vascular/metabolic markers, which argues that delivery device and physiologic state are part of the active ingredient, not just implementation details.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40703476-intranasal-insulin-pet-aging-mci.md]

The safety ceiling is also becoming clearer. A 2024 healthy-volunteer dose-escalation study found that doses up to 1000 U were mostly tolerated, but 600 U produced episodic severe and mild hypoglycemia in individual participants, so very high intranasal doses are not metabolically inert even when mean glucose barely moves.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39558506-high-dose-intranasal-insulin-healthy-human-participants.md]

The MS null matters because it broadens the list of phenotypes that do not obviously reproduce the AD/MCI signal. Intranasal insulin remains interesting, but it is not a generic neurologic enhancer: the same drug that can show subgroup-dependent benefit in metabolic or perioperative settings can still fail outright in a different disease state.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40253245-intranasal-insulin-ms-null.md]

The perioperative branch now looks like the clearest human use case. A 2024 RCT in older surgical patients reported better short-term MMSE/MoCA-B and less postoperative delirium with 40 IU/400 uL dosing, a 2025 delirium-treatment RCT found a shorter length of stay and an age-sensitive response even though the primary delirium-duration endpoint was not significant overall, the PubMed-indexed 2026 meta-analysis found lower POD and POCD across 7 RCTs and 732 postoperative patients, and the later SAGE 2026 meta-analysis extended the delirium-prevention signal to 9 randomized trials and 1039 patients.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39216470-intranasal-insulin-perioperative-cognitive-function-older-adults.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41091693-long-acting-intranasal-insulin-delirium-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41720686-intranasal-insulin-perioperative-delirium-meta.md]^[raw/articles/sage-2026-intranasal-insulin-postoperative-delirium-meta.md]

I did not find a direct human intranasal-insulin plus brain-stimulation cognition trial in this sweep. The best proxy evidence is that intranasal insulin changes connectivity in a way that depends on peripheral insulin sensitivity, while tDCS has its own systemic glucose effect, so the interaction looks metabolically coupled rather than orthogonal; see [[intranasal-insulin-brain-stimulation-interaction]] for the dedicated interaction scan.^[raw/articles/pubmed-28487570-intranasal-insulin-functional-connectivity-hunger.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40392946-intranasal-insulin-t2d-rsfc.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-28392373-tdcs-glucose-reduction.md]

For the wiki, the right framing is “biologically plausible, clinically interesting, not yet settled.” It belongs in [[adjunctive-interventions-for-cognition]] because it may help a subset of patients, but it is not established as a disease-modifying treatment for Alzheimer's disease or mild cognitive impairment.^[raw/articles/pubmed-36530613-hypoglycemic-drugs-ad-mci-network-meta-analysis.md]

Anecdotal reports of improved clarity on insulin-related therapies should be captured through [[anecdotal-evidence-in-cognitive-intervention-research]] rather than treated as proof of efficacy.
