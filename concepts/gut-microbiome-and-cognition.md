# Gut Microbiome and Cognition

Gut-microbiome intervention research is best treated as a mixed, phenotype-sensitive branch rather than a broad nootropic class. The strongest human signals so far cluster in older adults with mild cognitive impairment or declining memory, while healthy older-adult studies are often null or show only small task-specific changes such as faster executive-function reaction time.^[raw/articles/pubmed-32623402-bifidobacterium-breve-mci-cognition.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40222448-multistrain-probiotic-healthy-older-adults-cognition.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-37502424-cleverage-biota-probiotics-memory-psychological-physical.md]

The current working model is that probiotics and related interventions may help a subset of older adults by shifting inflammation, microbial taxa, or metabolite signaling, but the human evidence is too heterogeneous to treat the class as a reliable enhancer in healthy people.^[raw/articles/pubmed-36228569-lgg-mci-gut-microbiome-probiotics.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40976401-microencapsulation-probiotics-brain-structure-function.md]

The 2025-2026 pooled evidence reinforces that ceiling: one broad older-adult meta-analysis found only a non-significant positive trend on cognition (SMD 0.19), while a separate MCI/AD-specific meta-analysis stayed null across all reported domains. That means the branch is still driven more by cohort selection, endpoint sensitivity, and product identity than by a generic probiotic class effect.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40483001-probiotics-cognitive-performance-older-adults-meta-analysis.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40034358-probiotics-mci-ad-meta-analysis.md]

The 2025-2026 additions sharpen the phenotype story rather than overturn it: GOS in healthy young females mainly moved Bifidobacterium, brain chemistry, and a narrow reaction-time endpoint in a high-anxious subgroup, while an obesity-stratified diet sub-analysis in older adults linked cognitive change to fat loss and metabolite shifts only within the obese group.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40532960-galacto-oligosaccharides-gut-brain-axis-young-females.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41907517-obesity-associated-gut-microbiome-influences-diet-induced-metabolic-and-cognitive-outcomes-in-older-adults.md]

The newest 2025 additions also reinforce the responder-gating pattern: 2'-FL in healthy older adults missed its primary immune endpoint but still produced a visual-memory signal in Bifidobacterium responders, while the FMT clinical-trial review found no healthy-adult cognition trials at all and only a tiny, disease-state clinical evidence base.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40738103-2-fucosyllactose-older-adults-cognition.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40686934-faecal-microbiota-transplantation-cognitive-function-review.md]

The product-level layer now lives in [[psychobiotic-cognition-product-watchlist]]. Use it before comparing [[bifidobacterium-breve-a1]], [[lacticaseibacillus-rhamnosus-cbt-lr5]], [[lacticaseibacillus-rhamnosus-gg]], [[lacticaseibacillus-rhamnosus-hn001]], [[galacto-oligosaccharides]], and [[2-fucosyllactose]], because each page has a different phenotype, endpoint, and product-identity gate.

The metabolite-output layer now lives in [[scfa-postbiotic-brain-fog-cognition-watchlist]]. [[butyrate-tributyrin]] and colon-delivered SCFA mixtures have a healthier mechanistic bridge to stress-axis physiology than to direct cognition: one healthy-men trial attenuated cortisol response after colon-delivered SCFAs, while a wheat-bran trial raised serum acetate/butyrate without changing measured psychobiological outcomes. Modern tributyrin and DS-01 synbiotic trials are biomarker/product-identity anchors, not cognition wins.^[raw/articles/nature-2020-colon-delivered-scfa-stress-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-35694161-extruded-wheat-bran-scfa-null-rct.md]^[raw/articles/sage-2024-butyragen-tributyrin-healthy-adults-pilot.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40944126-ds01-synbiotic-butyrate-urolithin-rct.md]

The Army acute-stress probiotic/prebiotic protocol is now completed with no results posted, which keeps it in the freshness-watch lane rather than the efficacy lane until the registry changes.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct05392556-prebiotic-probiotic-acute-stress-completed.md]

The best way to read the branch is to separate three questions:

- Does the intervention move a cognitive endpoint?
- Does the effect depend on baseline impairment, age, or stress load?
- Are the gains large enough to matter compared with placebo, diet, and test-retest noise?

At present, the answer to the first question is sometimes yes in MCI, the second appears likely, and the third is still unresolved for most products.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41291638-probiotic-supplements-cognitive-function-meta-analysis.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40005019-cbt-lr5-mci-cognition.md]

GV-971 / sodium oligomannate is the main drug-like exception to the supplement/probiotic pattern. It has a positive China phase 3 ADAS-Cog12 result in mild-to-moderate AD, but the global confirmatory layer remains unresolved, so it should be read through [[sodium-oligomannate]] and [[sodium-oligomannate-gv971-2026-scan]] rather than as evidence that generic microbiome manipulation improves cognition.^[raw/articles/pubmed-33731209-sodium-oligomannate-phase3-china.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct04520412-gv971-green-memory-global.md]

FMT is best treated as a medical microbiome-modulation comparator, not a consumer enhancer. The current review-level evidence says it has been studied mainly in hepatic encephalopathy, Parkinson's disease, dementia, and MCI cohorts, with no cognition trials in healthy adults yet.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40686934-faecal-microbiota-transplantation-cognitive-function-review.md]
