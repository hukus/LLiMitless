# Creatine Cognitive Support

For the molecule-level profile and anecdote ledger, see [[creatine]]. This page remains the deeper synthesis for responder state, mechanism, and adjacent combination logic.

Creatine is increasingly discussed as more than a sports supplement: recent reviews frame it as part of a muscle-brain axis, and mechanistic work links creatine plus exercise to hippocampal neurogenesis, spatial learning, and cognitive flexibility in aging and neurodegenerative disease.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41601887-creatine-supplementation-aging-brain-muscle-axis.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41514140-creatine-arginine-exercise-neurogenesis.md]

The newer human evidence makes the story more specific. A 2024 adult meta-analysis found modest gains in memory, attention time, and processing-speed time, but no clear overall cognition or executive-function effect. A 2024 sleep-deprivation RCT found an acute rescue effect on cognitive performance and processing speed with measurable changes in brain high-energy phosphates. A 2025 re-analysis of the same sleep-deprivation cohort found right-hemisphere ATP declined more than left-hemisphere ATP and that high-dose creatine partially balanced the asymmetry, while a 2025 biomarker pilot found serum creatine rose after 24 h sleep deprivation. A 2025 older-adult systematic review found five of six studies positive, mostly for memory and attention, but the evidence base was still small, mixed-quality, and partly observational. A 2025 safety review across 685 clinical trials and adverse-event databases found no overall excess side effects versus placebo. The 2026 CONCRET-MENOPA RCT adds a small phenotype/formulation-specific signal: medium-dose creatine hydrochloride outperformed placebo on reaction time and frontal brain creatine in perimenopausal and menopausal women, with a possible mood-swing benefit trend, while multiple ongoing older-adult trials keep the longer-horizon question centered on phenotype and context rather than universal enhancement.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39070254-creatine-cognition-adults-meta-analysis.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-38418482-creatine-sleep-deprivation-cognition.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40520504-creatine-sleep-deprivation-hemispheric-asymmetry.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40988912-sleep-deprivation-circulating-creatine-pilot.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40971619-creatine-cognition-older-adults-systematic-review.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40198156-creatine-safety-side-effects-adverse-event-reports.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40854087-creatine-menopause-cognition-rct.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct06948149-creatine-resistance-training-mci.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct07472426-creatine-heat-exercise-cognition.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct06677359-creatine-multicomponent-older-adults.md]

The current research story is best summarized as "plausible, context-dependent, and still being sorted out." That makes sense for older adults and clinical populations where energy buffering, training status, nutrition, and baseline deficiency may all modulate effect size. The cleanest current claim is not broad nootropic superiority but fatigue-resilience under strain, with the newer sleep-deprivation biomarker papers suggesting the strain-state itself may alter creatine handling. Older-adult and MCI trials now test whether that advantage becomes more durable in phenotypes with lower reserve, and the 2025 older-adult review suggests the observational signal is real enough to keep watching even before better intervention trials land. The menopause RCT also warns against treating all creatine forms as interchangeable: a small low-dose-solubility formulation study produced a reaction-time and brain-creatine signal in one female phenotype, but that still does not collapse into a universal healthy-adult enhancer claim.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40673730-creatine-monohydrate-older-adults-clinical-populations.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40971619-creatine-cognition-older-adults-systematic-review.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-38418482-creatine-sleep-deprivation-cognition.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40520504-creatine-sleep-deprivation-hemispheric-asymmetry.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40988912-sleep-deprivation-circulating-creatine-pilot.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct06948149-creatine-resistance-training-mci.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40854087-creatine-menopause-cognition-rct.md]

The closest exercise-combination trial in this cluster was a 24-week older-women study: creatine and strength training improved strength and mood, but not cognition, which keeps the exercise partner in the "context amplifier" category rather than a proven synergy partner.^[raw/articles/pubmed-24098469-creatine-strength-training-older-women.md] For the broader exercise-plus-metabolic-peptide hypothesis, see [[exercise-creatine-metabolic-peptide-triad]].
