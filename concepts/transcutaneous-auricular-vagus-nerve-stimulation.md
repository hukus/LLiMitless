# Transcutaneous Auricular Vagus Nerve Stimulation

Transcutaneous auricular vagus nerve stimulation (taVNS) is a non-invasive neuromodulation method that has moved from physiology into cognition-adjacent psychiatry, sleep, stress, and memory research. The healthy-adult literature still looks protocol-sensitive, but there are now two direct memory signals to track: a corrected 2-week RAVLT trial with improved immediate recall and short-term memory, and a small associative-memory pilot with a timepoint interaction but no ERP change.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39039354-taVNS-memory-nonclinical-adults.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41046500-taVNS-associative-memory-healthy-adults.md]

The 2025-2026 human scan is still mixed rather than broadly positive. A short-burst emotional-memory study found a selective effect on negative-event memory, but sham stimulation and real stimulation both moved pupil dilation and delayed recognition, so the sensory component is hard to separate from any vagal mechanism. A separate healthy-volunteer stress RCT found autonomic changes without PASAT improvement, a stress-regulation EEG study suggested blunting of perceived stress and frontal theta/alpha changes without a direct cognition win, and a continuous-stimulation study increased self-reported arousal plus long-latency sensory processing but left other common LC/NA proxies unchanged.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39815765-taVNS-memory-pupil-dilation.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40908377-taVNS-mental-stressor.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40958978-taVNS-stress-regulation-eeg-questionnaire.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40130364-taVNS-long-latency-sensory-processing.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39124651-taVNS-autonomic-function.md]

A 2026 protocol for the first quantitative taVNS cognition meta-analysis is also live, which is a useful signal that the field still has not settled its pooled estimate. In disease-context work, taVNS now has a primary-insomnia RCT with symptom and connectivity improvements and a published large-vessel-occlusion stroke RCT focused on inflammatory biomarkers, which strengthens the state-regulation and anti-inflammatory branches more than the healthy-enhancement claim.^[raw/articles/pubmed-42030358-taVNS-cognitive-functions-protocol.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-34043386-taVNS-healthy-cognition-meta-analysis.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40629377-taVNS-primary-insomnia-bf-fc.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41428140-taVNS-stroke-inflammatory-biomarkers.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39936474-taVNS-cortisol-stress-inflammation.md]

The current best landing page for the updated evidence picture is [[transcutaneous-auricular-vagus-nerve-stimulation-2025-2026-scan]].

The timing story is now important enough to track explicitly. A circadian-stage HRV study found that morning taVNS produced larger vagal/autonomic shifts than evening taVNS, and lower baseline vagal tone predicted a bigger response. That does not prove a cognition benefit, but it does support a state-gated use case and makes morning pairing with light exposure a plausible hypothesis worth testing rather than assuming broad, time-agnostic efficacy.^[raw/articles/pubmed-36327249-taVNS-circadian-hrv.md]

A useful way to think about taVNS is as a low-friction autonomic and neuromodulatory intervention: the same physiological channel implicated in stress resilience, insomnia response, and inflammation may also matter for cognition and performance under load. That makes it a close cousin of [[focused-ultrasound-neuromodulation]] in the graph, while the comparison page [[adjunctive-interventions-for-cognition]] captures its place among broader adjunctive options and [[neuroinflammation-and-cognition]] captures its immune link.
