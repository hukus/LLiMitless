# Bright Light Therapy and Cognition

Bright light therapy is a circadian intervention rather than a transcranial neuromodulation tool, but it is directly relevant to cognitive improvement because it can shift sleep timing, stress physiology, and daytime alertness. A recent pilot study in long-term care facilities found the intervention interesting enough to measure cognition, sleep, and psychological stress together in older adults with mild-to-moderate cognitive impairment.^[raw/articles/pubmed-42015088-bright-light-therapy-cognition-sleep-stress.md]

Relevant raw PubMed notes: `raw/articles/pubmed-42015088-bright-light-therapy-cognition-sleep-stress.md|pilot study` and `raw/articles/pubmed-37002704-light-therapy-insomnia-disorder.md|insomnia review`.

Bright light also has a completed CPAP-resistant OSA crossover with results posted: the registry-linked paper reported improved wake-after-sleep-onset, rest-activity amplitude, and early-stage sleepiness/depression versus sham, which makes the intervention relevant to residual daytime sleepiness and not just to chronotype or lab-state shifting.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct04299009-bright-light-osa-rds.md]

Bright light also has a state-setting autonomic effect in healthy adults: a 2022 HRV study found that 5 hours of afternoon blue-enriched light altered time-domain HRV versus dimmer light, which makes the intervention relevant to timing-sensitive stacks rather than just sleep phase shifting.^[raw/articles/pubmed-35209793-bright-light-hrv.md]

That same timing logic is why bright light is now linked to [[bright-light-tavns-circadian-autonomic-scan]]: taVNS also appears circadian-stage dependent, so the most plausible stack hypothesis is a morning-aligned circadian and autonomic reset rather than a generic "more stimulation is better" model.^[raw/articles/pubmed-36327249-taVNS-circadian-hrv.md]

A 2025 QEEG paper on colored light adds a narrower chronotype signal: the wavelength response itself appeared to differ by morning, intermediate, and evening chronotype. That is useful as a physiology/timing anchor, but it still does not show direct cognition benefit.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40709491-colored-light-chronotype-qeeg.md]

Chronotype is now a real moderator worth tracking, but the direction is not one-size-fits-all. In evening-chronotype unipolar depression, chronotype-tailored bright light with gradual advance shortened time to remission, while a fixed 8 AM light schedule in SAD showed no chronotype-response relationship. A 2026 pooled analysis of five morning-light trials then softened the response claim further: chronotype and photoperiod predicted baseline depression severity, but they did not robustly predict symptom improvement, and adherence was not a clear driver in that sample.^[raw/articles/pubmed-32924897-bright-light-evening-chronotype-mdd.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-27259079-chronotype-light-therapy-fixed-time-sad.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-37975923-chronotype-tailored-bright-light-breast-cancer.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40939989-morning-light-chronotype-adherence-prediction.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-31530241-nb-uvb-chronotype-mood.md]
