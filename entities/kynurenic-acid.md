# Kynurenic Acid

Kynurenic acid, usually abbreviated KYNA, is the cognition-relevant kynurenine metabolite with the clearest schizophrenia target-validity story. It is tracked here as an endogenous metabolite and biomarker/target node, not as a direct-use nootropic.

Medicinal signal: `low / schizophrenia-biased biomarker and mechanism`. The 2023 schizophrenia review concluded that kynurenine-pathway activation affects cognition, but the evidence remains limited. The 2023 tryptophan challenge raised KYNA strongly and shifted CBF patterns, while cognition stayed mostly unchanged. A 2025 patient-derived model strengthened the schizophrenia mechanism by showing that KYNA promoted microglia-mediated synapse internalization and linking KYNA-producing KATs to synaptic and schizophrenia-risk signals.^[raw/articles/springer-2023-kynurenine-pathway-schizophrenia-cognition-review.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-37118058-tryptophan-challenge-schizophrenia-cognition-cbf.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40165559-kynurenic-acid-synapse-elimination-schizophrenia.md]

Dementia and stroke evidence are less clean. The 2025 dementia meta-analysis found higher CSF KYNA in AD dementia but lower blood KYNA, while the 2023 AD meta-analysis found central/peripheral divergence. In a post-stroke cohort, the KA/QA ratio and KA itself tracked selected cognitive domains, but plasma kynurenines did not predict global post-stroke cognitive impairment odds.^[raw/articles/springer-2025-kynurenine-pathway-dysregulation-cognitive-impairment-dementia.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-36754068-kynurenine-pathway-alzheimers-meta-analysis.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-37852105-kynurenine-pathway-cognitive-function-stroke.md]

## Signal Judgment

KYNA is important because elevated KYNA is a plausible cognitive-liability signal in schizophrenia and a target for [[kat-ii-inhibitors]]. That does not mean "lower KYNA everywhere" is safe or useful. Compartment, diagnosis, inflammatory state, age, and downstream glutamate/NMDA tone all decide whether the interpretation is protective, harmful, or uninterpretable.
