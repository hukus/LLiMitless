# ISRIB

ISRIB is the landmark small-molecule integrated-stress-response inhibitor / eIF2B modulator. It is relevant because it anchors the ISR cognition story in animal work and biohacker lore, but it is not a validated human cognitive enhancer.

Medicinal cognition signal: `preclinical-only`. The current raw-backed cognition anchor is a 2026 rat vascular-cognitive-impairment study reporting CREB/BDNF signaling and attenuation of cognitive deficits with ISRIB.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41380823-isrib-vascular-cognitive-impairment-rat.md] Broader ISRIB literature makes the molecule an important reference tool, but the wiki has no human cognition trial for ISRIB itself.

Mechanism signal: `plausible but safety-sensitive`. ISRIB-like compounds are relevant because memory formation depends on protein synthesis and chronic ISR activation can impair plasticity. The mechanism is not a simple on/off nootropic switch: split-ISR biology and neurodegeneration reviews stress that ISR timing, stress intensity, disease state, and pathway node matter.^[raw/articles/nature-2025-plasticity-mammalian-integrated-stress-response.md]^[raw/articles/molecular-neurodegeneration-2025-integrated-stress-response-neurodegenerative-diseases.md]

Safety and sourcing signal: `high uncertainty`. Captured self-experiment reports include oral, DMSO, injection-style, and analog use; they also include disturbed sleep, vivid dreams, hypomania-like mood, route pain, forehead pressure, hand twitching, food / grogginess effects, and heavy stack confounding.^[raw/articles/reddit-2020-isrib-memory-enhancer-thread.md] ISRIB-A15 discussion should be treated as analog-confounded rather than direct ISRIB evidence.^[raw/articles/reddit-2025-isrib-a15-limitless-thread.md]

Route ISRIB anecdotes through [[safety-interaction-matrix-for-multi-modal-stacks]] before treating them as cognition evidence. The main confounds are route/solvent exposure, analog naming, source identity, sleep disruption, mood activation, polypharmacy, prior drug-use or brain-fog recovery state, and the possibility that stress-response suppression trades short-term plasticity for worse resilience under load.
