# Naftidrofuryl

Naftidrofuryl, also called nafronyl and commonly used as naftidrofuryl oxalate, is a legacy vasoactive drug with 5-HT2 antagonist and ischemic-metabolism framing. It should be kept separate from [[nicergoline]], [[hydergine-ergoloid-mesylates]], [[propentofylline]], and generic "blood-flow nootropic" claims.^[raw/articles/pubchem-2026-naftidrofuryl-identity.md]

## Bottom Line

Medicinal signal: `low/legacy dementia and PAD-state, not healthy enhancement`. The cognition-relevant evidence is old dementia-state evidence: a 2011 Cochrane review found low-quality signals on performance/behavior, mood, and cognition across nine RCTs, while clinical global benefit was inconsistent and unconvincing. A small 1-year vascular/mixed dementia RCT also reported cognitive/global improvements. The stronger current evidence and guidance is for intermittent claudication walking distance, not cognition.^[raw/articles/pubmed-22161372-naftidrofuryl-dementia-cochrane-expanded.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-10945510-naftidrofuryl-vascular-mixed-dementia-rct.md]^[raw/articles/cochrane-2022-naftidrofuryl-intermittent-claudication.md]^[raw/articles/nice-2011-ta223-naftidrofuryl-pad-recommendation.md]

Anecdotal signal: `absent-to-weak`. The captured Reddit corpus contains a single old-nootropics list thread asking whether anyone has experience with naftidrofuryl, but no direct naftidrofuryl use report or cognitive outcome was captured.^[raw/articles/reddit-2023-naftidrofuryl-old-nootropics-mention.md]

Practical route: track [[naftidrofuryl]] as a legacy vascular/dementia comparator and PAD prescription-drug safety case, not as a modern consumer cognitive enhancer. It belongs beside [[legacy-vasoactive-racetam-nootropic-watchlist]], [[negative-evidence-watchlist-for-cognition-interventions]], and [[molecule-intake-triage-table]].

## Human Cognition Evidence

The dementia Cochrane review identified nine randomized placebo-controlled trials with 847 patients with Alzheimer's disease, vascular dementia, mixed dementia, senile dementia, or unspecified dementia. Standardized signals favored naftidrofuryl for functional performance/behavior, mood, and cognitive function, but the cognitive effect was small, heterogeneity was present, evidence quality was low, and clinical global measures did not convincingly confirm the benefit.^[raw/articles/pubmed-22161372-naftidrofuryl-dementia-cochrane-expanded.md]

The 2000 multicenter double-blind trial tested 600 mg/day for 1 year in vascular or mixed dementia. It had 84 intent-to-treat patients after a 2-month washout/compliance-selection phase and reported statistically significant cognitive and global improvements without clinically significant laboratory abnormalities. The study supports a legacy clinical-state signal but is too small, old, and phenotype-bound to support healthy-adult enhancement.^[raw/articles/pubmed-10945510-naftidrofuryl-vascular-mixed-dementia-rct.md]

## PAD And Regulatory Context

Naftidrofuryl has a clearer non-cognition clinical lane. Cochrane's intermittent-claudication review included seven studies and 1266 patients; pain-free walking distance improved more with naftidrofuryl than placebo, with a relative-improvement ratio of 1.37 and an NNT around 4.5 for a 50% walking-distance response over six months.^[raw/articles/cochrane-2022-naftidrofuryl-intermittent-claudication.md]

NICE TA223 recommends naftidrofuryl oxalate as an option for intermittent claudication in peripheral arterial disease when vasodilator therapy is appropriate. The French ANSM product record similarly frames NAFTILUX as symptomatic treatment for stage-2 lower-limb arterial disease, with only moderate walking-distance improvement and only as an adjunct to cardiovascular-risk management, exercise, and smoking cessation.^[raw/articles/nice-2011-ta223-naftidrofuryl-pad-recommendation.md]^[raw/articles/ansm-2025-naftilux-naftidrofuryl-smpc.md]

This PAD evidence is important for pharmacological plausibility and real-world status, but it should not be imported into the cognition ranking as a nootropic effect.

## Mechanism And Dosing Context

PubChem and ANSM converge on a vascular/ischemic mechanism frame: naftidrofuryl blocks 5-HT2 effects linked to vasoconstriction and platelet aggregation, and it is described as improving aerobic glucose metabolism in ischemic conditions. The current capsule dose in the ANSM record is 200 mg three times daily, taken with meals and swallowed with a large glass of water.^[raw/articles/pubchem-2026-naftidrofuryl-identity.md]^[raw/articles/ansm-2025-naftilux-naftidrofuryl-smpc.md]

## Safety And Status

Safety is not supplement-like. ANSM lists contraindications for known hyperoxaluria and recurrent calcium renal stones, warns of calcium oxalate renal-stone risk from the oxalate load, and recommends sufficient hydration. It also flags severe acute cytolytic liver injury, esophagitis risk if taken without water at bedtime, digestive adverse effects, rash, oral mucosal ulceration, and confusion/convulsions in massive overdose.^[raw/articles/ansm-2025-naftilux-naftidrofuryl-smpc.md]

PubChem's toxicology summary is consistent with a risk-first posture: overdose may involve conduction disturbance, ventricular arrhythmia, convulsions, acute renal failure, or acute hepatic necrosis, and listed signs include hepatitis/hepatic failure and calcium oxalate stones.^[raw/articles/pubchem-2026-naftidrofuryl-identity.md]

## Signal Judgment

Naftidrofuryl deserves standalone coverage because it is the remaining legacy vasoactive comparator with formal dementia and PAD evidence plus current prescription-drug safety constraints. It remains low-confidence for cognitive improvement: the cognition evidence is old and dementia-bound, the stronger current evidence is a leg-walking PAD endpoint, and the anecdotal layer does not currently add a usable signal.

Use [[safety-interaction-matrix-for-multi-modal-stacks]] before interpreting naftidrofuryl as a nootropic, especially when PAD walking-distance benefit, hydration, oxalate stone risk, hepatic symptoms, bedtime/esophagitis risk, or old nootropic-list mentions are the main signal.
