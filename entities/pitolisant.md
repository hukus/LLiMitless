# Pitolisant

Pitolisant is a histamine H3 receptor antagonist / inverse agonist and approved wakefulness drug. In the cognition wiki it belongs as a lower-abuse wakefulness comparator, not as a proven direct cognitive enhancer.

## Medicinal Signal

- Formal signal: `moderate` for narcolepsy / OSA sleepiness; `low` for direct cognition.
- The captured DailyMed label describes pitolisant as indicated for excessive daytime sleepiness or cataplexy in narcolepsy and highlights insomnia, nausea, anxiety, and QT-prolongation cautions.^[raw/articles/dailymed-wakix-pitolisant-tablet-label.md]
- In a 2020 randomized trial of 268 patients with moderate-to-severe OSA who refused CPAP, pitolisant improved Epworth Sleepiness Scale scores, fatigue, and patient-reported outcomes, but did not improve wake-maintenance testing.^[raw/articles/pubmed-31917607-pitolisant-osa-rct.md]
- The H3 class scan keeps pitolisant in the state-restoration lane because adult ADHD and schizophrenia H3 studies did not establish a broad cognition benefit.^[raw/articles/pubmed-33667687-pitolisant-meta-analysis-sleepiness.md]

## Safety And Interpretation

- Main burden: insomnia, nausea, anxiety, QT prolongation, hepatic and drug-interaction constraints, and possible activation / mood effects.
- Pitolisant is useful when comparing lower-abuse wakefulness strategies, but it is not the strongest direct cognition comparator.

## Current Read

Pitolisant is a real clinical wake-promoter with indirect cognition relevance through sleepiness reduction. The direct cognition signal remains thin, so the correct route is state restoration rather than broad enhancement.
