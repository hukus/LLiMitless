# Agmatine

## Bottom Line

Agmatine is a neuromodulator/tolerance/pain-mood gray-zone molecule with `preclinical-only` cognition evidence in the current corpus. Anecdotal signal is `moderate`, but it mostly concerns anxiety, pain, stimulant/opioid tolerance, and body-state changes rather than objective cognition.

## What It Is

Agmatine is a decarboxylated arginine metabolite discussed as a neuromodulator with interactions across nitric oxide, imidazoline, NMDA, monoamine, and stress-response biology. In the wiki it belongs with [[risk-first-legacy-gray-zone-nootropic-watchlist]], not with controlled human cognition leads.

## Medicinal Research Signal

The formal cognition packet is animal/mechanistic. One captured study reports cognition rescue in an STZ Alzheimer rat model, and an older review frames agmatine as a possible neurotransmitter or neuromodulator. That is not enough to claim human cognitive benefit.^[raw/articles/pmc-3990080-agmatine-stz-alzheimer-rat-model.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-9327985-agmatine-novel-neurotransmitter-review.md]

## Anecdote Signal

The captured Reddit thread gives a `moderate` anecdotal signal, but the reports focus on anxiety, pain, pre-workout response, tolerance-like effects, and sedation/fog counterexamples. This is a state-modulation hypothesis, not evidence of improved learning or memory.^[raw/articles/reddit-2023-agmatine-experience-thread.md]

## Safety And Status

The practical risk is not label-level prescription toxicity; it is unsupervised stack use with cardiovascular, sedation, glucose-like, and interaction complaints in anecdotes. Keep dose and co-use context visible.

## Signal Judgment

Medicinal signal: `preclinical-only`.

Anecdotal signal: `moderate`, but mostly mood/pain/tolerance-state.

Practical route: mechanism and tolerance-lore watch item.
