# Cromolyn Sodium

Cromolyn sodium is a mast-cell stabilizer used in oral, inhaled, nasal, and ophthalmic contexts. In this wiki it is a mast-cell / histamine-phenotype brain-fog watch item, not a proven cognitive enhancer. It should not be collapsed with [[h1-h2-antihistamines]], [[ketotifen]], [[dao-enzyme]], [[quercetin]], [[palmitoylethanolamide-luteolin]], or low-histamine diet claims.

Formal signal: `low for brain fog, established only as mast-cell treatment context`. DailyMed identifies cromolyn sodium oral solution concentrate as a prescription oral product with 100 mg cromolyn sodium per 5 mL ampule. StatPearls describes cromolyn as preventing mast-cell mediator release and notes poor oral absorption plus delayed onset of 2 to 6 weeks. The captured MCAS systematic-review context is small and bias-prone; it does not prove cognition benefit.^[raw/articles/dailymed-2025-cromolyn-sodium-oral-solution-label.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-32491405-cromolyn-sodium-statpearls.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-26095756-h1-antihistamines-primary-mcas-systematic-review.md]

Anecdote signal: `moderate but mixed`. MCAS threads contain repeated reports that cromolyn helped brain fog, dissociation, GI symptoms, pain, hives, or activity threshold, but also reports of no direct benefit or worsened brain fog/fatigue. Interpretation is heavily confounded by MCAS diagnosis, POTS/IST, low-histamine diet, H1/H2 use, dose titration, GI improvement, and flare regression.^[raw/articles/reddit-2024-2026-cromolyn-ketotifen-mcas-brainfog-threads.md]

Safety signal: `moderate medical-gate`. The oral label reports headache and diarrhea as the most frequent adverse events in mastocytosis clinical studies, with pruritus, nausea, myalgia, abdominal pain, rash, irritability, malaise, and postmarketing GI effects also visible. It is a prescription medication, not a casual supplement.^[raw/articles/dailymed-2025-cromolyn-sodium-oral-solution-label.md]

## Practical Interpretation

Cromolyn belongs in [[mast-cell-histamine-brain-fog-chemical-watchlist]] as a medical, phenotype-gated treatment candidate. A meaningful record needs route, dose, titration speed, timing relative to meals, GI symptoms, POTS/dysautonomia status, H1/H2 use, diet triggers, sleep, and adverse effects. It should not be ranked on [[healthy-adult-cognition-signals]] without a defined mast-cell phenotype.
