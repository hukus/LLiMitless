# Nicotinamide Mononucleotide

Nicotinamide mononucleotide (NMN; often beta-NMN) is a nucleotide NAD+ precursor sold as a longevity / NAD booster supplement and also investigated in pharmaceutical form as MIB-626. This page covers oral NMN cognition and cognition-adjacent claims, not [[nicotinamide-riboside]], niacin, nicotinamide, NADH, IV NAD+, direct NAD+ products, resveratrol stacks, or generic "NAD booster" blends.

Medicinal cognition signal: `low/proxy-only`. The best cognition-adjacent human evidence is not a cognition trial. A 60-person older-adult RCT using 250 mg/day NMN for 12 weeks increased blood NAD+ metabolites, improved 4-meter walking time, and improved PSQI daytime-dysfunction/global sleep-quality scores, but the primary stepping test was not significantly different and no memory, attention, processing-speed, or executive-function endpoint was tested.^[raw/articles/pubmed-38789831-nmn-older-adults-walking-sleep.md]

The broader human trial record supports target engagement and physical/sleep proxies more than cognition. A 108-person older-adult timing trial found the strongest signals for lower-limb function and drowsiness with afternoon NMN. An older-men RCT found blood NAD+ increases plus nominal gait/grip hints. A dose-ranging healthy-adult trial found dose-related NAD+ increases, short-term tolerability up to 900 mg/day, and 6-minute-walk/SF-36 proxy movement. None of these studies establishes direct cognitive improvement.^[raw/articles/pmc-8877443-nmn-sleep-fatigue-older-adults-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pmc-9158788-nmn-older-men-muscle-nad-rct.md]^[raw/articles/springer-2022-nmn-dose-dependent-healthy-adults-rct.md]

Metabolic claims remain limited. A 2024 systematic review/meta-analysis of 8 small human RCTs found that NMN often raised blood NAD+, but did not significantly improve fasting glucose, fasting insulin, HbA1c, HOMA-IR, or lipid profile in mainly relatively healthy adults. The review also highlighted uncertain tissue-level NAD effects, oral conversion to nicotinamide, dose/duration gaps versus animal studies, and uncertain long-term high-dose safety.^[raw/articles/springer-2024-nmn-glucose-lipid-metabolism-meta.md]

Safety and regulatory signal: `low-moderate, short-term only and product-gated`. A 2025 short-term "overdose" RCT reported no problematic findings at 750 or 1500 mg/day for 4 weeks and increased blood NAD+, but it had only 30 healthy volunteers and no cognition endpoint. FDA's 2025 citizen-petition response now says NMN is not excluded from the dietary-supplement definition because it was marketed in the United States before drug authorization, reversing the earlier drug-preclusion cloud. That is a supplement-status point, not approval for safety or efficacy.^[raw/articles/jstage-2025-nmn-overdose-safety-rct.md]^[raw/articles/fda-2025-nmn-dietary-supplement-petition-response.md]

Product identity is the practical bottleneck. A 2024 quality-control study found large deviations between label claims and measured active ingredient in NMN/urolithin products, with three NMN products containing non-detectable NMN and NMN label-claim deviation reaching -100%. Any consumer interpretation should require beta-NMN identity, dose, lot-level COA, storage conditions, and independent assay rather than a label claim alone.^[raw/articles/springer-2024-nmn-urolithin-label-claim-testing.md]

Anecdote signal: `moderate for energy/focus/sleep salience, weak for objective cognition, bidirectional for tolerability`. Community reports include intense energy, sharper recall, easier conversation/work/study drive, sleep disruption, irritability, crash, dose reduction, no-effect/product-quality uncertainty, fatigue, and rash. Anecdote makes NMN worth tracking but cannot upgrade the medicinal signal beyond proxy-only.^[raw/articles/reddit-2019-nmn-three-month-experience-thread.md]^[raw/articles/reddit-2021-nmn-day-50-experience-thread.md]^[raw/articles/reddit-2023-nmn-fatigue-rash-thread.md]

## Identity Gates

- Separate beta-NMN from [[nicotinamide-riboside]], niacin, nicotinamide, NADH, IV NAD+, direct NAD+, and multi-precursor "NAD complete" blends.
- Track dose, route, timing, and product format: 250 mg/day, 300-900 mg/day, 750-1500 mg/day, liposomal powder, sublingual powder, capsules, and combined NMN-resveratrol products are not interchangeable.
- Require lot-level COA or independent assay because label-claim failures are common enough to change interpretation.
- Do not classify sleep quality, drowsiness, walking speed, SF-36, biological-age calculators, or blood NAD+ as direct cognition.
- Watch sleep disruption, irritability/activation, rash, fatigue, cancer-treatment context, pregnancy/breastfeeding, liver/kidney disease, high-dose nicotinamide exposure, stimulant/caffeine timing, and long-term high-dose use.

## Practical Ranking

NMN deserves standalone tracking because it is popular, regulatory status has changed, product quality is a real transportability problem, and human studies show NAD+ movement plus physical/sleep proxy signals. It does not deserve a cognition ranking upgrade. The current route is `NAD+ / sleep-fatigue / physical-function proxy watch`, with medicinal confidence held at low until a blinded trial measures and improves a pre-registered cognitive endpoint.
