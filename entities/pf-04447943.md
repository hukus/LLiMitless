# PF-04447943

PF-04447943 is a Pfizer phosphodiesterase 9A inhibitor studied in Alzheimer disease. It reached the phase II stage in mild-to-moderate AD, but the trial did not improve cognition, behavior, or global change. It is therefore a negative anchor for the PDE9 branch rather than a live cognition lead. See [[pde9-pde10-cgmp-cognition-2026-scan]] and [[pde-camp-creb-cognition-2026-scan]].

## Overview

- Drug class: selective PDE9A inhibitor.
- Main rationale: increase brain cGMP and support synaptic plasticity.
- Human status: one phase II AD trial, negative.
- Best interpretation: historical translator compound with some target rationale, but no efficacy win.

## Key facts

- Trial size: 191 randomized participants with mild-to-moderate probable AD.
- Dose: 25 mg twice daily for 12 weeks.
- Primary endpoint: ADAS-cog.
- Result: no significant improvement on ADAS-cog, NPI, or CGI-I.
- Safety: generally tolerable, but GI adverse events and discontinuation were somewhat higher than placebo.
