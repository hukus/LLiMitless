# Semaglutide

## Bottom Line

Semaglutide is the most important single-molecule incretin cognition comparator because it now has both positive-adjacent and negative clinical cognition data. Medicinal signal is `low-moderate` for phenotype-sensitive clinical brain-health relevance, but `low` for broad cognitive enhancement: a T2DM GLP-1RA meta-analysis is positive, an MDD cognition RCT missed its primary executive-function endpoint but favored semaglutide on a secondary global-cognition composite, and EVOKE/EVOKE+ in early symptomatic AD was negative on the primary progression endpoint despite biomarker movement. Anecdotal cognition signal is `weak` in the current wiki corpus even though weight-loss and longevity discourse around the molecule is large.

## What It Is

Semaglutide is a GLP-1 receptor agonist used in metabolic disease and weight-management contexts. In this wiki it belongs with [[incretin-therapies-brain-health]], [[incretin-therapies-brain-health-2026-scan]], [[negative-evidence-watchlist-for-cognition-interventions]], and [[neuroinflammation-and-cognition]] as a metabolism-brain and biomarker-behavior-split comparator, not as a general nootropic.

## Medicinal Research Signal

The broad class-level positive anchor is a 2026 meta-analysis of 18 randomized trials in T2DM, which reported small statistically significant improvements in MMSE and MoCA with GLP-1 receptor agonists and stronger MMSE effects when treatment lasted at least 24 weeks. This supports phenotype-sensitive clinical relevance, but it is not semaglutide-specific healthy-adult evidence.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41104525-glp1-ra-cognitive-function-t2dm-meta-analysis.md]

The molecule-specific psychiatric anchor is the oral semaglutide MDD cognition trial. It was the first randomized clinical trial of oral semaglutide for cognitive dysfunction in MDD. The primary executive-function outcome was negative, while a prespecified secondary global-cognition composite favored semaglutide. That is enough to keep semaglutide in the brain-health graph, but not enough to upgrade it into a settled cognitive enhancer.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41218611-semaglutide-mdd-cognitive-dysfunction-rct.md]

The strongest negative anchor is the EVOKE/EVOKE+ phase 3 program in early symptomatic AD. The trials were negative on the primary progression endpoint; biomarker improvement did not translate into delayed clinical decline. This is the key reason semaglutide should be scored as contested rather than promising-by-default.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41865758-semaglutide-evoke-evokeplus-ad-phase3.md]

The preclinical NLRP3 paper gives a plausible neuroinflammation bridge in APP/PS1 mice and BV2 microglia, including cognitive, amyloid, tau, inflammasome, microglial-polarization, and ferroptosis-related signals. That supports mechanism mapping, not human enhancement confidence.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41173224-semaglutide-nlrp3-ad-model-progression.md]

## Safety And Status

For cognition users, the main practical safety issue is not a new neurological signal in the captured cognition trials; it is interpretability plus prescription/metabolic-drug risk. GI effects, appetite suppression, weight loss, glycemic change, and mood or sleep shifts can change perceived cognition without proving a direct nootropic effect. Route semaglutide brain-fog or clarity anecdotes through [[safety-interaction-matrix-for-multi-modal-stacks]] before interpreting them as direct cognition evidence.

June 2026 metabolic-peptide attribution gate: semaglutide reports should not update cognition rankings unless they capture exact product and prescription status, oral versus injectable formulation, dose and titration stage, indication, baseline diabetes/obesity/insulin-resistance phenotype, weight-loss trajectory, appetite and calorie/carbohydrate intake, hydration/electrolytes, sleep apnea or sleep changes, mood/depression state, GI adverse events, hypoglycemia risk from co-medications, co-interventions such as diet, exercise, metformin, SGLT2 inhibitors, stimulants, or sleep drugs, washout or stable-dose period, and an objective cognition endpoint. Route informal reports through [[molecule-triage-worksheet-for-online-scans]], [[delivery-validation-checklist-for-cognition-interventions]], [[stack-attribution-diary-for-self-study-and-n-of-1-protocols]], [[30-day-self-study-template-for-nonprescription-interventions]], [[30-day-self-study-workbook-schema]], and [[self-experimentation-stop-rules]] before comparing semaglutide with stronger anecdotal nootropic nodes such as [[semax]], [[selank]], [[cerebrolysin]], recovery-bound [[bpc-157]], or [[bromantane]].

## Signal Judgment

Medicinal signal: `low-moderate` for phenotype-sensitive clinical brain-health relevance; `low` for broad cognitive enhancement.

Anecdotal signal: `weak` for cognition-specific claims in the current corpus.

Practical route: clinical metabolic-brain comparator and negative-evidence anchor. Any upgrade requires cognition-primary benefit that survives endpoint, disease-stage, and metabolic-confound checks.
