# Cordyceps / Cordycepin

Cordyceps is a traditional medicinal fungus branch, while cordycepin is a nucleoside constituent often used in mechanistic or purified-compound discussion. The wiki keeps Cordyceps mushroom products, standardized Cordyceps militaris extracts, fermented beverages, and purified cordycepin separate.

## Bottom Line

Medicinal cognition signal: `low/contested`. A 2025 indexed record reports a 12-week Cordyceps militaris extract trial in subjective cognitive decline with MoCA, delayed recall, sleep, BDNF, and hs-CRP improvements, but it needs publication-quality, registry, and replication audit before confidence rises. A 2024 Scientific Reports healthy-adult trial supports Cordyceps militaris immune-modulation exposure, not cognition efficacy.^[raw/articles/garuda-2025-cordyceps-militaris-scd-cognition-sleep-rct.md]^[raw/articles/nature-2024-cordyceps-militaris-immune-healthy-adults-rct.md]

Anecdote signal: `moderate but product-split`. The strongest captured nootropic report is about purified cordycepin, not ordinary Cordyceps supplements. A second thread reports acute clarity/endurance but possible later fatigue, irritability, and sleep-quality concerns with a mushroom extract.^[raw/articles/reddit-2022-cordycepin-cognition-brainfog-report.md]^[raw/articles/reddit-2021-cordyceps-fatigue-sleep-thread.md]

## Evidence Layers

The 2024 fermented Cordyceps militaris beverage trial used a product containing 2.85 mg cordycepin per 75 mL serving for 60 days in healthy adults. NK-cell and selected cytokine changes support immune plausibility, while measured safety labs were reassuring. Because no cognition endpoint was tested, this source belongs in the mechanism and tolerability layer only.^[raw/articles/nature-2024-cordyceps-militaris-immune-healthy-adults-rct.md]

The 2025 subjective-cognitive-decline abstract reports Cordyceps militaris mycelial extract 300 mg/day standardized to 3% cordycepin. The claimed effect size is large enough to watch closely, but the branch should stay low-confidence until the full paper, trial registration, independent replication, and endpoint handling are checked.^[raw/articles/garuda-2025-cordyceps-militaris-scd-cognition-sleep-rct.md]

## Safety And Status

MSKCC warns that Cordyceps may lower blood sugar and may increase bleeding risk with blood thinners such as warfarin. Product quality also matters: Cordyceps sinensis, Ophiocordyceps sinensis, Cordyceps militaris fruiting body, mycelium, fermented beverage, blends, and purified cordycepin are not interchangeable.^[raw/articles/mskcc-2026-cordyceps-safety.md]

## Signal Judgment

Cordyceps now deserves standalone coverage because it has a direct cognition-sleep watch item, a formal immune-exposure trial, and clear brain-fog anecdotes. It does not yet deserve a general cognition ranking. The next useful work is verification: trial registration, journal quality, exact extract COA, cordycepin dose, sleep mediation, and adverse fatigue/sleep-stop rules.
