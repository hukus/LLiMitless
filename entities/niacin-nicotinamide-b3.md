# Niacin / Nicotinamide / Vitamin B3

Niacin is vitamin B3. The practical forms for this wiki are nicotinic acid / niacin and nicotinamide / niacinamide. This page is the bridge between the [[s-adenosyl-l-methionine]] / one-carbon branch and the NAD branch: [[nicotinamide-riboside]], [[nicotinamide-mononucleotide]], and [[nadh]]. It should not borrow the evidence of any of those pages.

Current cognition read: `low and status-gated`. Severe B3 deficiency can cause pellagra with dementia-like symptoms, so correction of real deficiency is medically important. Outside deficiency, the human cognition signal is mostly observational diet association plus a high-dose early-Alzheimer nicotinamide trial that failed its tau-phosphorylation target and did not establish cognitive efficacy. Anecdotal B3 / niacinamide brain-fog and methylation reports are visible but bidirectional and weak.

## Evidence Snapshot

| Layer | Current read | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| Deficiency/status | `real medical route` | Pellagra can include dementia; this is deficiency treatment, not enhancement |
| Dietary epidemiology | `low/status clue` | NHANES analyses link higher dietary niacin or B-vitamin intake with lower odds of low cognitive performance, but diet quality and protein/nutrient confounding are unresolved |
| High-dose nicotinamide in early AD | `negative/unclear` | 3 g/day for 48 weeks did not reduce the prespecified p-tau231 endpoint or establish cognitive benefit |
| PK/PD | `exposure without translation` | Nicotinamide metabolites can move while CSF exposure and cognition translation stay weak |
| SAMe/one-carbon bridge | `mechanistic only` | NNMT uses SAM to methylate nicotinamide, producing SAH and N1-methylnicotinamide; this does not prove methyl-drain brain fog |
| Anecdote | `weak-moderate and bidirectional` | Calm/clarity/spatial-awareness reports coexist with fatigue, illness, brain fog, and liver-dose concern |
| Safety | `dose/form gated` | Nicotinic acid flushing, high-dose liver and metabolic concerns, GI effects, fatigue/sedation, and form confusion matter |

## Formal Evidence

ODS frames niacin as vitamin B3 and a precursor for NAD and NADP, with roles in energy metabolism and redox biology. It also keeps the deficiency boundary explicit: pellagra can include dementia, but adult supplement/fortified-food upper-intake limits and high-dose adverse effects prevent casual escalation from status correction to nootropic use.^[raw/articles/ods-2026-niacin-health-professional-fact-sheet.md]

The two NHANES cognition papers are useful but non-causal. One analysis of 2,523 older adults found higher dietary niacin intake associated with lower odds of cognitive impairment and described an L-shaped association. A later 2,716-person B-vitamin mixture analysis again found niacin prominent among B-vitamin intake associations. These are dietary-pattern signals, not niacin-pill trials.^[raw/articles/pubmed-37576033-dietary-niacin-cognition-nhanes.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40056822-b-vitamin-intakes-cognition-nhanes.md]

The best high-dose nicotinamide intervention counterweight is the NEAT early-Alzheimer program. In the phase 2A trial, 3 g/day nicotinamide for 48 weeks did not reduce the prespecified CSF p-tau231 endpoint and did not establish cognitive efficacy. The companion PK/PD assessment supports biological exposure but also highlights the translation problem: nicotinamide metabolism can move without a clear brain/cognition win.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39671543-nicotinamide-early-ad-phase2a.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40069789-nicotinamide-early-ad-phase2a.md]

The one-carbon connection is real but easy to overread. NNMT methylates nicotinamide using SAM and generates SAH plus N1-methylnicotinamide. That justifies routing high-dose nicotinamide, NR/NMN stack, and TMG/SAMe anecdotes through [[one-carbon-methyl-donor-cognition-watchlist]], but it is not proof that niacinamide causes cognitive methyl depletion or that adding methyl donors improves cognition.^[raw/articles/pmc-8372200-nnmt-nicotinamide-sam-methylation-review.md]

## Safety And Use Gates

- Do not collapse forms: nicotinic acid / niacin, nicotinamide / niacinamide, [[nicotinamide-riboside]], [[nicotinamide-mononucleotide]], NAD+, and [[nadh]] have different exposure and evidence packets.
- Do not collapse goals: deficiency correction, lipid-dose niacin, dermatology/oncology-style nicotinamide, AD high-dose nicotinamide, and nootropic self-experimentation are different contexts.
- Track dose and liver context. The adverse-effect review keeps flushing, rash, GI symptoms, liver dysfunction, fatigue, sedation, and energy abnormalities visible.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40999226-niacin-nicotinamide-adverse-effects-review.md]
- Track one-carbon context when doses are high or when NR/NMN/SAMe/TMG stacks are involved: homocysteine, B12/MMA, folate, B6/P5P, riboflavin/B2, choline, creatine, diet, kidney/liver status, and medication context.
- Treat positive B3 anecdotes as hypothesis generation only. The current evidence does not put niacin or niacinamide near [[creatine]], [[caffeine]], [[modafinil]], or [[methylphenidate]] for practical cognition ranking.

## Current Route

Niacin / nicotinamide belongs in the wiki because it closes a real attribution gap between B vitamins, NAD boosters, and methyl-donor lore. The best current role is `status and mechanism triage`: look for deficiency, dietary-pattern context, high-dose safety, and whether a report is really about NAD precursors, SAMe/TMG methylation, or ordinary B3 form/dose. It is not a broad healthy-adult cognition enhancer in the current corpus.
