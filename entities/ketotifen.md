# Ketotifen

Ketotifen is an H1-antihistamine and mast-cell-stabilizing antiallergic drug. In this wiki it is a mast-cell / MCAS brain-fog watch item, not a general cognition enhancer. It should not be collapsed with topical OTC eye drops, [[h1-h2-antihistamines]], [[cromolyn-sodium]], [[dao-enzyme]], [[quercetin]], or prescription stimulant/wakefulness branches.

Formal signal: `low for brain fog`. The captured formal anchor is a chronic urticaria review and older study summary, not a cognition trial. Ketotifen has plausible mast-cell and H1 mechanisms, and the review reports symptom improvement in chronic urticaria contexts, but it also notes slow onset for prophylactic benefit in some conditions. No captured source proves brain-fog benefit or healthy-adult enhancement.^[raw/articles/pmc-4309375-ketotifen-chronic-urticaria-review.md]

Anecdote signal: `weak-moderate`. MCAS threads include ketotifen as a high-salience stabilizer in brain-fog discussions, often compared with cromolyn or used alongside H1/H2 agents, quercetin, luteolin, diet, PEA-LUT, magnesium L-threonate, or POTS treatments. Some reports describe cognition improvement; others emphasize weight/appetite problems, drowsiness, reactions, or incomplete benefit.^[raw/articles/reddit-2024-2026-cromolyn-ketotifen-mcas-brainfog-threads.md]

Safety signal: `moderate-high`. The oral ketotifen safety page lists weight gain, drowsiness, dry mouth, increased appetite, irritability, nervousness, trouble sleeping, unusual tiredness or weakness, dizziness, headache, GI symptoms, and severe/overdose symptoms such as confusion, convulsions, severe drowsiness, fast or irregular heartbeat, palpitations, and loss of consciousness. These effects can directly confound brain-fog interpretation.^[raw/articles/mayo-2026-ketotifen-oral-route-safety.md]

## Practical Interpretation

Ketotifen belongs in [[mast-cell-histamine-brain-fog-chemical-watchlist]] as a medical off-label/region-dependent mast-cell candidate. It should be interpreted through sedation, appetite/weight, sleep, autonomic status, MCAS diagnostic rigor, H1/H2 background therapy, and compounding/filler exposure before any cognition read.
