# Nomifensine

Nomifensine is a historical norepinephrine-dopamine reuptake inhibitor and former antidepressant. In the wiki it is a safety-first historical comparator for dopamine-reuptake / motivation / nootropic lore, especially beside [[sibutramine]], [[tesofensine]], and [[brasofensine]].

## Current Read

Medicinal cognition signal: `deprioritize / risk-first`. The relevant captured source is withdrawal status, not cognitive efficacy. PubChem describes nomifensine as formerly marketed and associated with increased hemolytic anemia, and states that it was withdrawn worldwide in 1986 because of acute hemolytic anemia with intravascular hemolysis.^[raw/articles/pubchem-2026-nomifensine-withdrawn-hemolytic-anemia.md]

The nootropic-adjacent temptation is obvious: a norepinephrine-dopamine reuptake inhibitor can sound like a clean motivation or energy drug. The class lesson is the opposite: mechanism appeal can be dominated by rare but severe safety liabilities.
