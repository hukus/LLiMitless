# Selank

Selank is the calmer, anxiolytic-side companion to [[semax]], and the current wiki now has a better reason to keep it prominent. Its cognitive relevance is still mostly indirect, but the phenotype signal is increasingly coherent across literature and anecdotes: lower stress reactivity, less overthinking, and better emotional steadiness are the outcomes people keep describing. That does not prove direct cognition enhancement. It does make Selank one of the more serious state-restoration peptides in the frontier set.

## Evidence Split

- Human anchor: the older randomized study in generalized anxiety disorder and neurasthenia is still the main reason Selank remains more than a pure forum compound.^[raw/articles/pubmed-18454096-selank-gad-neurasthenia.md]
- Mechanism and preclinical layer: ethanol-memory rescue, connectomics work, and the broader Selank molecular review keep the branch tied to BDNF, GABA-related, and network-modulation explanations without creating a modern human cognition result.^[raw/articles/pubmed-31625062-selank-ethanol-memory-impairment-rats.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-32342318-selank-semax-functional-connectomics.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-30255741-peptide-based-anxiolytics-heptapeptide-selank-biological-activity.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-16634437-semax-selank-oligopeptides.md]
- Safety and sourcing layer: FDA still treats Selank acetate as a low-data bulk peptide with meaningful impurity / limited-safety caveats.^[raw/articles/fda-bulk-drug-substances-peptide-risk-2026.md]

## Frontier Read

Selank is more convincing as a `phenotype-bound calming peptide` than as a general nootropic. That is still a meaningful upgrade in frontier seriousness. A peptide that repeatedly shows up in structured surveys, older human anxiety data, and present-day community use for stress-heavy performance situations deserves a more serious read than a one-thread novelty compound. The caution is that this mostly upgrades triage priority, not direct cognition confidence.^[raw/articles/pubmed-18454096-selank-gad-neurasthenia.md]^[raw/articles/troof-nootropics-ratings-analysis.md]^[raw/articles/darktka-2017-nootropics-survey-on-r-nootropics.md]
