# Selank

Selank is a synthetic regulatory peptide usually positioned as an anxiolytic and stress-modulating companion to [[semax]]. Its cognitive relevance is mostly indirect: if it helps reduce stress reactivity or sharpen network-level efficiency, then attention and working-memory outcomes may improve as a downstream effect.

Relevant raw PubMed notes: `raw/articles/pubmed-18454096-selank-gad-neurasthenia.md`, `raw/articles/pubmed-31625062-selank-ethanol-memory-impairment-rats.md`, `raw/articles/pubmed-16634437-semax-selank-oligopeptides.md`, `raw/articles/pubmed-32342318-selank-semax-functional-connectomics.md`, and `raw/articles/pubmed-24738258-neuroprotective-peptides-age.md`.
The current literature is much thinner than for Semax, and the signal is more connectomic or regional than decisive. The one older human randomized study is encouraging for anxiety/asthenia, but that still falls short of proving Selank as a generalized cognitive enhancer. The quality-scored split now lives in [[semax-selank-2026-scan]]: Selank scores `low-moderate` for anxiety/asthenia and `low` for direct cognition.^[raw/articles/pubmed-18454096-selank-gad-neurasthenia.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-16634437-semax-selank-oligopeptides.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-32342318-selank-semax-functional-connectomics.md]
FDA's current bulk-compounding page also treats Selank acetate as a low-data bulk peptide with aggregation, impurity, and limited safety-information concerns, so the safety/risk read is still much weaker than the public nootropic marketing suggests.^[raw/articles/fda-bulk-drug-substances-peptide-risk-2026.md]
The community layer keeps Selank visible for a different reason: the 2017 /r/Nootropics survey and the newer Troof / SSC / ACX rating datasets repeatedly place it in the same peptide cluster as Semax, even though the reported effects skew calmer and more anxiolytic than activating.^[raw/articles/troof-nootropic-ratings-2022.md]^[raw/articles/astralcodexten-2020-nootropics-survey-results.md]^[raw/articles/slatestarcodex-2016-nootropics-survey-results.md]^[raw/articles/darktka-2017-nootropics-survey-on-r-nootropics.md]
