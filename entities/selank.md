# Selank

Selank is the calmer, anxiolytic-side companion to [[semax]], and the current wiki now has a better reason to keep it prominent. Its cognitive relevance is still mostly indirect, but the phenotype signal is increasingly coherent across literature and anecdotes: lower stress reactivity, less overthinking, and better emotional steadiness are the outcomes people keep describing. The June 2026 read is `strong salience, low confidence`: Selank is one of the more serious state-restoration peptides in the frontier set, not a proven direct cognitive enhancer. It belongs in the strongest current anecdote-priority cluster only as a watchlist signal that still needs source/COA, route/formulation, stack order, washout, recovery state, adverse-event, and objective-endpoint logging before any ranking upgrade.^[raw/articles/troof-nootropics-ratings-analysis.md]^[raw/articles/darktka-2017-nootropics-survey-on-r-nootropics.md]^[raw/articles/reddit-2026-04-29-semax-selank-cerebrolysin-advice-thread.md]

## Evidence Split

- Human anchor: the older randomized study in generalized anxiety disorder and neurasthenia is still the main reason Selank remains more than a pure forum compound.^[raw/articles/pubmed-18454096-selank-gad-neurasthenia.md]
- Mechanism and preclinical layer: ethanol-memory rescue, connectomics work, and the broader Selank molecular review keep the branch tied to BDNF, GABA-related, and network-modulation explanations without creating a modern human cognition result.^[raw/articles/pubmed-31625062-selank-ethanol-memory-impairment-rats.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-32342318-selank-semax-functional-connectomics.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-30255741-peptide-based-anxiolytics-heptapeptide-selank-biological-activity.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-16634437-semax-selank-oligopeptides.md]
- Safety and sourcing layer: FDA still treats Selank acetate as a low-data bulk peptide with meaningful impurity / limited-safety caveats.^[raw/articles/fda-bulk-drug-substances-peptide-risk-2026.md]
- Attribution layer: Selank reports are incomplete unless they separate intranasal/subcutaneous/oral route, formulation/salt, source/COA, dose timing, stack order, washout, sleep state, stimulant or anxiolytic co-use, anxiety/recovery phenotype, adverse events, and objective endpoints. ## Frontier Read

Selank is more convincing as a `phenotype-bound calming peptide` than as a general nootropic. That is still a meaningful upgrade in frontier seriousness. A peptide that repeatedly shows up in structured surveys, older human anxiety data, and present-day community use for stress-heavy performance situations deserves a more serious read than a one-thread novelty compound. The caution is that this mostly upgrades triage priority, not direct cognition confidence; keep the formal/anecdote split in [[semax-selank-2026-scan]] visible before interpreting reports as molecule-specific efficacy.^[raw/articles/pubmed-18454096-selank-gad-neurasthenia.md]^[raw/articles/troof-nootropics-ratings-analysis.md]^[raw/articles/darktka-2017-nootropics-survey-on-r-nootropics.md]

For stack safety, route Selank through [[safety-interaction-matrix-for-multi-modal-stacks]] as a stress-state peptide rather than an arousal enhancer. New Selank exposure should not be combined with stimulants, wake-promoters, [[bromantane]], bright-light timing shifts, anxiolytics, or other peptides if the goal is clean cognition attribution.
