# Apelin

Apelin is an endogenous peptide ligand for APJ / APLNR and an adipokine-like CNS biology lead. In the cognition wiki it belongs as a weak exerkine-adjacent comparator, not as a direct enhancer candidate.

Medicinal cognition signal: `preclinical / indirect`. The most cognition-adjacent captured behavioral source is not a cognition trial: higher plasma apelin was associated with depressive symptoms in older adults, and apelin knockout produced age- and metabolic-state-dependent affective and monoaminergic effects in mice.^[raw/articles/pubmed-35305406-apelin-emotional-behavior-age-metabolic-state.md]

CNS mechanism signal: `review-level`. The 2025 CNS review describes apelin/APJ signaling across apoptosis, autophagy, oxidative stress, angiogenesis, inflammation, neurotrauma, stroke, spinal cord injury, neurodegenerative disease, psychiatric disease, epilepsy, and pain. That makes apelin relevant to neuroprotection and metabolic-brain state, but not yet to human cognitive enhancement.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39681256-apelin-cns-diseases-review.md]

## Safety And Translation Caveats

- Apelin biology is cardiovascular, metabolic, angiogenic, and CNS-active; any future intervention should be safety-first and phenotype-specific.
- The current evidence is mood/metabolic/preclinical CNS biology, not memory or executive-function efficacy.
- Do not infer that an exercise- or adipokine-linked pathway is equivalent to a consumer nootropic route.
