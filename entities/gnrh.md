# GnRH

## Bottom Line

GnRH is the strongest endocrine-axis cognition signal in this slice, but it is disease-specific and delivery-intensive. Medicinal signal is `low-moderate` for Down syndrome cognition research because a small human study and a tiny MRI/cognition follow-up reported improvement with pulsatile GnRH, and a larger Pulse-UP trial is recruiting. Medicinal signal is `low` for general enhancement. Anecdotal cognition signal is `absent-to-weak` in the current corpus.

## What It Is

Gonadotropin-releasing hormone is a hypothalamic peptide hormone normally associated with reproductive endocrine signaling. In this wiki it belongs with [[neurosteroid-and-endocrine-state-cognition-2026-scan]], [[kisspeptin]], [[peptide-safety-and-sourcing-risk-map]], and [[cognitive-fidelity-split]] as a disease-specific axis-restoration lead, not a nootropic peptide.

## Medicinal Research Signal

The 2022 Science anchor combined Ts65Dn mouse work with a small adult Down syndrome human study. Pulsatile GnRH reportedly improved cognition and brain connectivity in adult Down syndrome patients, while the mouse work included rescue of olfaction and novel-object recognition. This is the strongest direct cognition signal on the GnRH axis, but its disease model and patient population are central to the interpretation.^[raw/articles/pubmed-36048943-gnrh-replacement-rescues-cognition-down-syndrome.md]

The 2025 longitudinal MRI follow-up is supportive but very small. In adults with Down syndrome, MoCA rose from 11.4 +/- 5.5 to 15.1 +/- 5.6 after 6 months of pulsatile GnRH, with persistent MRI pattern differences; the longitudinal Down syndrome sample was only eight participants, with seven in the white-matter analysis. This strengthens the disease-specific signal but does not settle generalizability.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40190351-gnrh-down-syndrome-mri-followup.md]

The Pulse-UP registry is the key ongoing test. The 2026 captured status lists a recruiting randomized, double-blind pulsatile GnRH pump versus saline trial in people with Down syndrome, with estimated enrollment of 56 and primary completion in 2028-12. Cognition, olfaction, MRI, and biomarker endpoints over 24 weeks are the relevant readouts.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct04390646-gnrh-cognition-down-syndrome-status.md]

## Safety And Status

The practical gate is clinical delivery and endocrine supervision. The captured human cognition work uses pulsatile administration and Down syndrome selection, so route, monitoring, fertility-axis effects, and population specificity are not optional details.

## Signal Judgment

Medicinal signal: `low-moderate` for Down syndrome clinical research; `low` for broad cognitive enhancement.

Anecdotal signal: `absent-to-weak`.

Practical route: disease-specific axis-restoration watchlist; wait for Pulse-UP before extrapolating.
