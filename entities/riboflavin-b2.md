# Riboflavin / Vitamin B2

Riboflavin, or vitamin B2, is the precursor to FMN and FAD. In this wiki it is the B2 cofactor branch for [[folate-b-vitamins]], [[vitamin-b6-p5p]], [[methylcobalamin-b12]], [[l-methylfolate-5-mthf]], [[folinic-acid-leucovorin]], [[s-adenosyl-l-methionine]], [[l-methionine]], and [[one-carbon-methyl-donor-cognition-watchlist]]. It should not inherit SAMe, methylfolate, or TMG evidence.

Mechanistic/status signal: `real but indirect`. The NCBI Bookshelf chapter frames MTHFR as a flavin-dependent enzyme that produces 5-methyltetrahydrofolate for homocysteine-to-methionine remethylation. It also notes that the common MTHFR 677C>T variant reduces enzyme activity and lowers affinity for the flavin cofactor, making riboflavin status relevant to homocysteine in T-allele carriers. This is one-carbon plausibility, not cognitive efficacy.^[raw/articles/ncbi-bookshelf-2013-riboflavin-mthfr.md]

Human formal signal: `biomarker/genotype, not cognition`. A targeted RCT in treated hypertensive adults with the MTHFR 677TT genotype randomized 91 participants to placebo or riboflavin 1.6 mg/day for 16 weeks. Riboflavin improved riboflavin biomarker status and lowered systolic blood pressure relative to placebo, but the study did not test cognition. A 771-adult genotype/status study found that the MTHFR 677T-homocysteine relationship depended on riboflavin status and that MTRR effects interacted with cobalamin and riboflavin context. These are useful lab/status anchors, not nootropic evidence.^[raw/articles/pubmed-23608654-riboflavin-mthfr-677tt-bp-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-25322900-riboflavin-status-mthfr-homocysteine.md]

Anecdote signal: `weak-moderate and stack-confounded`. MTHFR-community threads repeatedly frame B2 as a missed cofactor for brain fog, fatigue, B12 sensitivity, folinic/methylfolate tolerance, choline reactions, or C677T protocols. The reports are plausible enough for triage but rarely isolate riboflavin from B12, folate, choline, histamine, iron, iodine, sleep, electrolytes, or autonomic illness.^[raw/articles/reddit-2023-2026-p5p-riboflavin-mthfr-brainfog-threads.md]

## Practical Interpretation

Riboflavin/B2 deserves standalone coverage because it explains a real cofactor gap in SAMe/methylfolate/TMG discussions. It should be read as `status/genotype and homocysteine-context support`, not as a general cognitive enhancer. Any subjective brain-fog response should be interpreted through riboflavin status, MTHFR/MTRR genotype only when clinically useful, folate, B12, B6/PLP, MMA, homocysteine, kidney function, diet, alcohol, thyroid/iodine/iron state, sleep, and co-supplement changes.

Any confidence upgrade needs riboflavin-alone cognition endpoints, baseline riboflavin and one-carbon markers, genotype/status stratification, and explicit separation from folate, B12, P5P/B6, SAMe, TMG, choline, and creatine.
