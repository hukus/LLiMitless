# Deoxygedunin

Deoxygedunin is a gedunin-family natural-product TrkB agonist claim from neem-related chemistry. It belongs in the wiki as a preclinical TrkB / BDNF-mimetic comparator, not as a human cognition intervention.

Medicinal cognition signal: `preclinical-only`. The discovery paper reports oral activity in mice, TrkB and downstream signaling activation, neuroprotection, antidepressant-like effects, and learning-enhancement effects in animal systems. That makes deoxygedunin worth tracking as a named molecule, but the current captured corpus has no human cognition trial.^[raw/articles/plos-2010-deoxygedunin-natural-product-trkb.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-trkb-bdnf-mimetic-registry-search-2026.md]

Mechanism signal: `plausible but contested`. Later TrkB caution and assay-limitations sources keep all small-molecule TrkB agonist claims under the target-specificity gate. Deoxygedunin should not borrow [[7-8-dihydroxyflavone]]'s larger animal literature, [[lm22a-4]]'s Rett object-memory signal, [[r13]]'s prodrug/pharmacokinetic logic, or [[zeb85]]'s antibody-specificity argument.^[raw/articles/pmc-8398766-small-molecule-trkb-agonist-limitations.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-37488840-trkb-agonist-caution-review.md]
