# PF4 / Platelet Factor 4

## Bottom Line

PF4 / CXCL4 is a strong preclinical exercise-transfer factor, not a validated human cognition therapy. Medicinal signal is `preclinical-only` for cognition: a Nature Communications mouse study showed platelet-derived PF4 can rejuvenate hippocampal neurogenesis and cognition in aged mice, and a 2025 review frames PF4 as a promising cognition-relevant exerkine, but no human PF4 dosing trial is indexed. Anecdotal signal is `absent`.

## What It Is

Platelet factor 4, also called CXCL4, is a platelet-derived chemokine and exercise-linked circulating factor. In this wiki it belongs with [[exerkine-myokine-cognition-2026-scan]], [[blood-borne-rejuvenation-and-plasma-factor-cognition-2026-scan]], [[clusterin]], [[irisin]], and [[cathepsin-b]] as a preclinical transfer-factor node.

## Medicinal Research Signal

The key source is the 2023 Nature Communications mouse study. Exercise activated platelets, PF4 acted as a platelet-derived exerkine, and systemic PF4 mimicked exercise-linked rejuvenation of hippocampal neurogenesis and cognition in aged mice. The study positions PF4 as sufficient and necessary in that mouse exercise-response pathway, but human efficacy, dosing, and safety remain unvalidated.^[raw/articles/nature-platelet-derived-exerkine-cxcl4-platelet-factor-4-rejuvenates-hippocampal-neurogenesis-and-restores-cognitive-function-in-aged-mice.md]

The 2025 PF4 cognition review supports the translational hypothesis but does not change the evidence layer. It frames PF4 as a coagulation-linked chemokine with mouse cognitive benefits and mechanistic plausibility, not as a human intervention.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40749766-pf4-cognition-review.md]

## Safety And Status

PF4 sits near platelet, chemokine, coagulation, and inflammatory biology. That makes direct administration an explicitly research-only idea. It should not be collapsed into general exercise benefits or plasma-factor marketing.

Route PF4 or CXCL4 extrapolations through [[safety-interaction-matrix-for-multi-modal-stacks]] before interpreting mouse exercise-transfer effects as human cognition claims. Platelet activation, coagulation context, inflammatory signaling, dose/route, species translation, and source identity are core gates.

## Signal Judgment

Anecdotal signal: `absent`.

Practical route: mouse exercise-transfer benchmark and translational caution node.
