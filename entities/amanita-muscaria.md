# Amanita Muscaria

Amanita muscaria, muscimol, ibotenic acid, and muscarine belong in the wiki as a risk-first "legal mushroom nootropic" branch, not as a cognition candidate. The formal cognition signal is `negative/risk-first`: there is no controlled cognitive-enhancement evidence in the current corpus, and the strongest current public-health sources are FDA and CDC safety warnings.^[raw/articles/fda-2024-amanita-muscaria-food-constituent-update.md]^[raw/articles/cdc-mmwr-2026-mushroom-chocolate-severe-illness.md]

FDA says A. muscaria, its extracts, muscimol, ibotenic acid, and muscarine are not authorized for use as ingredients in conventional food and do not meet the GRAS standard. The FDA industry letter points to gastrointestinal symptoms, delirium, sleepiness, alternating drowsiness/excitement, convulsions, coma, CNS depression, seizures, and hospitalizations.^[raw/articles/fda-2024-amanita-muscaria-industry-letter-not-gras.md]

The 2026 CDC MMWR outbreak report makes the sourcing problem concrete: 180 severe illness cases were associated with Diamond Shruumz or other mushroom-containing chocolates; 73 were hospitalized, 38 required ICU admission, 29 required intubation, and two deaths were reported. Testing found variable mixtures including muscimol, psilocin, kavalactones, acetylpsilocin, pregabalin, and ibotenic acid in a raw ingredient, so product anecdotes cannot be treated as clean muscimol evidence.^[raw/articles/cdc-mmwr-2026-mushroom-chocolate-severe-illness.md]
