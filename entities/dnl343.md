# DNL343

DNL343 is a CNS-penetrant small-molecule eIF2B activator and integrated-stress-response modulator. It is relevant to cognition because ISR biology is tied to protein synthesis, synaptic plasticity, and neurodegeneration, but DNL343 itself is not a cognition-positive molecule.

Medicinal cognition signal: `low / target-engagement only`. A 2025 Nature Communications paper reported DNL343 phase 1 healthy-participant and phase 1b ALS safety / pharmacodynamic data, including once-daily-compatible PK, extensive CSF distribution, general tolerability, and reduced ISR biomarkers in peripheral blood mononuclear cells and CSF of ALS participants.^[raw/articles/nature-communications-2025-dnl343-eif2b-als.md] Those trials were safety and target-engagement studies, not cognitive-performance trials.

Clinical translation signal: `negative for ALS function so far`. HEALEY ALS Regimen G topline results reported that DNL343 did not meet the primary endpoint of ALSFRS-R / survival through 24 weeks and did not separate on key secondary endpoints of muscle strength or respiratory function. It was still reported safe and well tolerated.^[raw/articles/massgeneral-2025-dnl343-healey-als-topline.md] This failure does not directly answer cognition, but it lowers broad confidence that eIF2B activation will translate easily from biomarkers to clinical benefit.

Mechanism caveat: `direction and timing matter`. The split-ISR and neurodegeneration-review sources make it unsafe to compress the pathway into "ISR inhibition is good." Acute ISR can be protective, chronic ISR can be maladaptive, and eIF2B-dependent ISR biology can diverge from canonical eIF2alpha-phosphorylation models.^[raw/articles/nature-2025-plasticity-mammalian-integrated-stress-response.md]^[raw/articles/molecular-neurodegeneration-2025-integrated-stress-response-neurodegenerative-diseases.md]
