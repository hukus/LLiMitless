# TAK-063

TAK-063 is a PDE10A inhibitor and the main drug-candidate anchor for the PDE10/cGMP cognition branch. It is a schizophrenia disease-development comparator, not a validated cognitive enhancer.

Medicinal cognition signal: `low / not cognition-positive`. In a 6-week phase 2 acute-schizophrenia trial, TAK-063 20 mg daily with blinded de-escalation to 10 mg for intolerance did not meet the primary PANSS total endpoint. The trial is relevant to PDE10A translation, but it was not a cognition efficacy win.^[raw/articles/pubmed-30190165-tak063-acute-schizophrenia.md]

Target biology: `biomarker-only`. A 2023 PET/MRS study found altered striatal PDE10A availability in schizophrenia and a control-group correlation between sensorimotor-striatal PDE10A and Tower of London performance. That supports PDE10A as a striatal biology marker, not as proof that PDE10A inhibition improves cognition.^[raw/articles/pubmed-36458958-pde10-schizophrenia-pet-cognition.md]

## Safety And Caveats

- In the TAK-063 schizophrenia trial, adverse events were mostly mild or moderate, but akathisia and dystonia were more frequent than placebo.
- PANSS or antipsychotic-activity signals should not be relabeled as cognitive enhancement without a separate cognitive endpoint.
- PDE10A imaging correlations may identify disease biology without implying that inhibition is directionally helpful.
