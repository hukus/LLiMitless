# ACD856

ACD856, also written ACD-856, is a small-molecule pan-Trk positive allosteric modulator from AlzeCure's NeuroRestore program. It belongs in the wiki because it is one of the few Trk/neurotrophin plasticity chemicals with human healthy-volunteer safety, PK, CSF exposure, and qEEG data, and because it has become visible in gray-market nootropic stack discussion.

Medicinal cognition signal: `human exposure / target-engagement only`. A phase 0 microdose study and an oral single-ascending-dose study tested ACD856 in healthy subjects, with oral doses up to 150 mg. The paper reports tolerability, rapid absorption, dose-proportional exposure, approximately 20-hour half-life, and no dose-related adverse-event trend, but no cognition-efficacy endpoint.^[raw/articles/springer-2024-acd856-single-ascending-dose-healthy-subjects.md] A multiple-ascending-dose study tested 10-90 mg once daily for 7 days in 24 healthy subjects, found CSF exposure and dose-dependent qEEG changes, and again stays in the pharmacodynamic rather than behavioral-efficacy tier.^[raw/articles/springer-2023-acd856-multiple-dose-qeeg-healthy-subjects.md]

Preclinical signal: `plausible but sponsor-linked`. The main preclinical ACD856 paper reports Trk-pathway, BDNF, neurite, SNAP25, neuroprotection, and animal-model signals, but all authors were employed by AlzeCure and the work should be read under the [[target-specificity-gate-for-plasticity-molecules]].^[raw/articles/mdpi-2023-acd856-trk-pam-neuroprotective.md] A 2026 AlzeCure press release says a Phase Ib higher-repeat-dose study reached last-patient-last-visit and that Phase II preparation is underway, but it does not provide efficacy results.^[raw/articles/alzecure-2026-acd856-phase-ib-lplv.md]

Mechanism signal: `Trk-PAM, not generic BDNF boosting`. ACD856 should not be collapsed with [[7-8-dihydroxyflavone]], [[lm22a-4]], [[deoxygedunin]], or [[r13]]. Those are older TrkB-mimetic / agonist or prodrug lanes; ACD856 is better treated as the newer human-exposed Trk-PAM comparator in [[trkb-bdnf-mimetic-cognition-2026-scan]].

## Anecdote Signal

Community signal is `weak-moderate` for nootropic salience and `weak` for direct cognition. Visible NooTopics-style reports describe subtle mood regulation, lower anxiety or irritability, sociability, sleep changes, behavioral shifts after 1-2 weeks, and occasional clarity, word-recall, memory, or cognition claims. Negative and cautionary reports include drowsiness, fatigue, sleepiness, possible blood-pressure increase, weak effect, and heavy stack attribution with [[tak-653]], [[nsi-189]], usmarapride, bromantane, and other chemicals.^[raw/articles/reddit-2025-2026-acd856-nootropic-experience-threads.md]

## Current Read

ACD856 is worth tracking because it has human CNS exposure data and current nootropic-market salience. It is not yet a validated healthy-adult cognitive enhancer.
