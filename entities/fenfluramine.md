# Fenfluramine

Fenfluramine is a serotonergic drug with old anorectic history and current FINTEPLA seizure-indication labeling. In this wiki it is a safety precedent for [[phentermine]] / fen-phen history and serotonergic anorectic risk, not a cognitive-enhancement candidate.

## Current Read

Medicinal cognition signal: `risk-first / absent`. The strongest captured sources are safety and status anchors. The current FINTEPLA label is for seizure indications, not cognition or nootropic use, and it carries valvular-heart-disease / pulmonary-arterial-hypertension monitoring requirements.^[raw/articles/dailymed-2026-fenfluramine-fintepla-rems.md]

Old stimulant/anorectic comparison signal: `not cognition-positive for fenfluramine`. A 1984 acute healthy-volunteer comparison included fenfluramine alongside ephedrine, phentermine, and prolintane, but the captured takeaway identified phentermine and prolintane as most active on some mental tasks. That leaves fenfluramine as a comparator in the old study, not the cognition-active signal.^[raw/articles/pubmed-6471970-phentermine-ephedrine-fenfluramine-prolintane-mental-effects.md]

## Safety And Status Layer

The FINTEPLA label warns that fenfluramine can cause valvular heart disease and pulmonary arterial hypertension, links the risk to serotonergic drugs with 5-HT2B receptor agonist activity, requires echocardiographic monitoring before, during, and after treatment, and restricts access through the FINTEPLA REMS program.^[raw/articles/dailymed-2026-fenfluramine-fintepla-rems.md]

The historical fenfluramine-phentermine case series is the key anorectic safety precedent: the PubMed abstract describes valvular heart disease associated with fenfluramine-phentermine therapy and says candidates should be informed about serious potential adverse effects including pulmonary hypertension and valvular heart disease.^[raw/articles/pubmed-9271479-fenfluramine-phentermine-valvular-disease.md]

## Interpretation Gates

- Use fenfluramine to separate serotonergic valvulopathy risk from generic stimulant focus claims.
- Do not use old fen-phen history as evidence that [[phentermine]] alone has fenfluramine's 5-HT2B-linked valve-risk mechanism, but do keep the combination history visible.
- Do not generalize FINTEPLA seizure-indication exposure into healthy-adult cognition, appetite, or brain-fog claims.
- Watch REMS/echo-monitoring status, VHD/PAH, serotonergic stacking, appetite/weight-loss nostalgia, historical-withdrawal context, and misleading "similar to phentermine" simplifications.
