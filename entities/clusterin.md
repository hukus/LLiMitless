# Clusterin

## Bottom Line

Clusterin is an exercise-plasma and neuroinflammation transfer-factor lead, not a validated human cognition treatment. Medicinal signal is `preclinical-plus-biomarker`: runner plasma and clusterin reduced inflammatory signals and improved memory-related outcomes in mouse models, while human exercise data show clusterin biomarker movement in cognitive-impairment patients after structured exercise. No direct human clusterin cognition intervention is indexed. Anecdotal signal is `absent`.

## What It Is

Clusterin is a circulating protein linked to complement regulation, inflammation, exercise-plasma biology, and neurodegeneration hypotheses. In this wiki it belongs with [[exerkine-myokine-cognition-2026-scan]], [[blood-borne-rejuvenation-and-plasma-factor-cognition-2026-scan]], [[pf4]], [[irisin]], and [[neuroinflammation-and-cognition]].

## Medicinal Research Signal

The key Nature source showed that runner plasma reduced neuroinflammatory gene expression in mice, proteomics identified complement cascade inhibitors including clusterin, and intravenous clusterin bound brain endothelial cells and reduced inflammatory gene expression in mouse inflammation and AD models. The same paper reported higher plasma clusterin in humans with cognitive impairment after six months of structured exercise. This is strong exercise-transfer biology plus human biomarker support, not direct human clusterin treatment proof.^[raw/articles/nature-exercise-plasma-boosts-memory-and-dampens-brain-inflammation-via-clusterin.md]

## Safety And Status

Clusterin should stay in the blood-factor mediator lane. Its relationship to complement, inflammation, and vascular/endothelial biology means direct administration claims require dedicated safety data before any practical interpretation.

Route clusterin claims through [[safety-interaction-matrix-for-multi-modal-stacks]] when runner-plasma, complement inhibition, endothelial binding, neuroinflammation, cognitive-impairment exercise response, or direct-protein delivery is being used as a cognition proxy.

## Signal Judgment

Anecdotal signal: `absent`.

Practical route: exercise-plasma mediator and inflammation comparator, not a standalone nootropic.
