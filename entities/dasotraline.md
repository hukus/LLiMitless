# Dasotraline

Dasotraline (SEP-225289) is a long-acting dopamine/norepinephrine reuptake inhibitor development compound previously studied for ADHD and binge eating disorder. It is useful here as a comparator for [[centanafadine]], [[tesofensine]], [[solriamfetol]], and classic stimulants because it shows how ADHD symptom scales, long half-life, and direct cognition endpoints can diverge.

## Current Read

Medicinal cognition signal: `low/discontinued`. In adults with ADHD, 8 mg/day improved ADHD symptom scores over 4 weeks, but a computerized cognitive battery showed no significant main effects on attention, working memory, or episodic memory.^[raw/articles/nature-2015-dasotraline-adult-adhd-proof-of-concept.md]

Regulatory / development status: negative for practical use. Sunovion withdrew the ADHD and binge eating disorder NDAs in 2020 and said further studies would be needed for approval; it also said it would not pursue further development at that time.^[raw/articles/businesswire-2020-dasotraline-discontinued.md]

Anecdote signal: effectively absent in current practical terms. Because dasotraline did not become a marketed medicine, forum value is mostly pipeline curiosity rather than direct nootropic self-report.

## Human Evidence

| Evidence layer | What it says | Wiki interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| Adult ADHD proof of concept | 341 adults randomized; 8 mg/day significantly improved ADHD RS-IV total score versus placebo, while 4 mg/day was only trend-level on the primary comparison.^[raw/articles/nature-2015-dasotraline-adult-adhd-proof-of-concept.md] | ADHD symptom signal, not healthy enhancement |
| Direct cognition battery | The trial measured attention, working memory, and episodic memory with a computerized battery and found no significant main effects.^[raw/articles/nature-2015-dasotraline-adult-adhd-proof-of-concept.md] | Key downgrade for nootropic claims |
| Discontinuation / regulatory trail | Sunovion withdrew NDAs and stopped further development in 2020.^[raw/articles/businesswire-2020-dasotraline-discontinued.md] | Historical comparator, not a current option |

## Safety And Interpretation Gates

- Long half-life and slow accumulation matter: the adult study describes stable exposure over 24 hours and steady-state timing around 2 weeks.^[raw/articles/nature-2015-dasotraline-adult-adhd-proof-of-concept.md]
- The adult proof-of-concept trial had frequent insomnia, decreased appetite, nausea, and dry mouth; adverse-event discontinuation was much higher at 8 mg/day than placebo.^[raw/articles/nature-2015-dasotraline-adult-adhd-proof-of-concept.md]
- HR/BP, insomnia, appetite/weight loss, anxiety/panic, mood activation, psychosis vulnerability, stimulant stacking, and washout effects are first-order confounds.
- The cautionary lesson is that clinical ADHD symptom improvement can coexist with a direct cognition-battery null.
