# BPC-157

BPC-157 is one of the few gray-market peptides that is now hard to dismiss as pure niche lore. The branch has unusually broad regenerative preclinical literature, very high recovery-oriented community salience, and as of 2026 one clearly registered randomized human trial in acute muscle injury. That still does not make it a validated cognitive enhancer. The direct cognition story remains weak; the serious signal is recovery, pain, tissue repair, and the possibility of indirect brain-performance gains when injury, inflammation, poor training continuity, or sleep disruption are the real bottleneck.^[raw/articles/pubmed-30915550-bpc157-soft-tissue-healing.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40789979-bpc157-musculoskeletal-healing-review.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40005999-bpc157-literature-patent-review.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct07437547-bpc157-hamstring-repair-record-2026-05-06.md]

The formal literature is better described as promising than mature. The older soft-tissue-healing review and the newer 2025 reviews all present a coherent repair story around angiogenesis, nitric-oxide signaling, fibroblast activity, endothelial repair, and poorly vascularized tissue healing. The 2025 musculoskeletal review also says the human literature still boils down to only three small pilot studies at the time of review: knee pain, interstitial cystitis, and intravenous safety/pharmacokinetics.^[raw/articles/pubmed-30915550-bpc157-soft-tissue-healing.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40789979-bpc157-musculoskeletal-healing-review.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40005999-bpc157-literature-patent-review.md]

The newest real upgrade is the registry layer. In the current wiki corpus, `NCT07437547` is the first clearly posted randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled BPC-157 trial with meaningful enrollment size: 120 participants with MRI-confirmed acute grade II hamstring strain, co-primary endpoints of return-to-sport time and MRI lesion-volume change, and standardized rehabilitation in both arms. That does not answer the efficacy question yet, but it materially improves the seriousness of the branch compared with a pilot-only human record.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct07437547-bpc157-hamstring-repair-record-2026-05-06.md]

The published human exposure base remains tiny. The wiki still only has a 16-patient retrospective knee-pain series plus a two-person IV safety pilot, which is enough to say there has been real administered-human exposure but nowhere near enough to claim broad safety or reliable efficacy.^[raw/articles/pubmed-34324435-bpc157-knee-pain-pilot.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40131143-bpc157-human-safety-pilot.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40789979-bpc157-musculoskeletal-healing-review.md]

## Ocular Health

The ocular lane is more real than I expected, but it is still preclinical only. In the current sweep I found two rat corneal-injury papers from the Zagreb group: one on epithelial defects and one on perforating corneal injury. Both report faster healing with topical BPC-157 eye drops, and the later perforation model also reports faster return of corneal transparency with less pathologic neovascularization. That is enough to say BPC-157 has a specific ophthalmic tissue-repair hypothesis rather than only a vague "heals everything" aura. It is not enough to claim human eye benefit, retinal protection, glaucoma utility, or visual-performance enhancement, because I did not find a human ocular BPC-157 trial or registry in this pass.^[raw/articles/pubmed-16117343-bpc157-corneal-epithelial-defects-rats.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-25912999-bpc157-perforating-corneal-injury-rat.md]

## Anecdote Ledger

The anecdote layer is now large enough to matter as a tracking signal. It is still mostly recovery-state rather than cognition-state evidence, and much of it is confounded by TB-500 stacks, rehab, training changes, or natural healing. But the signal is no longer trivial: BPC-157 keeps showing up in large community-rating datasets and in detailed injury-recovery threads with some attempt at self-tracking.^[raw/articles/troof-nootropics-ratings-analysis.md]^[raw/articles/reddit-2026-04-25-bpc157-tb500-meniscus-mri-thread.md]^[raw/articles/reddit-2026-01-12-bpc157-tb500-three-months-mixed-thread.md]^[raw/articles/reddit-2026-05-06-bpc157-tb500-shoulder-recovery-update-thread.md]

## Safety and Sourcing

The safety and sourcing picture is still the hard limiter. FDA's current bulk-substance page says BPC-157 has limited human safety information and raises immunogenicity plus peptide-impurity / API-characterization concerns. WADA's 2026 S0 guidance separately uses BPC-157 as an example of a non-approved substance. The market-quality evidence is mixed rather than cleanly one-sided. The strongest peer-reviewed counterweight in the current wiki is still the Belgian analytical study of falsified peptide drugs bought from suspected illegal internet pharmacies, which found major purity variation and toxic elemental contamination. But the newer public market-monitoring layer for BPC-157 is less bleak: Finnrick's live dashboard currently summarizes 528 BPC-157 samples from 75 vendors with 5th-95th percentile purity of 96.49%-99.95%, which suggests high-purity product is common in at least some active channels. The same dashboard also reports quantity divergence up to +/-71% at the 95th percentile and is not a peer-reviewed sterility or human-grade validation dataset. The right read is that some gray-market BPC-157 likely does test very pure, while identity, dose accuracy, contamination, and route-safety questions remain unresolved.^[raw/articles/fda-bulk-drug-substances-peptide-risk-2026.md]^[raw/articles/wada-2026-prohibited-list-peptide-gh-axis-expanded.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-30029448-falsified-polypeptide-drugs-belgian-market.md]^[raw/articles/finnrick-2026-bpc157-testing-dashboard.md]

Working read: BPC-157 now deserves serious tracking as a recovery-state peptide with unusually strong preclinical repair logic, a real ocular-tissue-repair preclinical lane, very high community heat, and one real move toward controlled human evidence. It should still be kept separate from direct cognition enhancers and from any blanket assumption that gray-market sourcing solves the validation problem.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40789979-bpc157-musculoskeletal-healing-review.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-25912999-bpc157-perforating-corneal-injury-rat.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct07437547-bpc157-hamstring-repair-record-2026-05-06.md]^[raw/articles/troof-nootropics-ratings-analysis.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-30029448-falsified-polypeptide-drugs-belgian-market.md]
