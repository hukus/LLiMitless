# BPC-157

BPC-157 is best known as a recovery / tissue-repair peptide and is much more established in sports-medicine and healing conversations than in cognition. Its cognitive relevance is therefore indirect: any effect on brain function would more likely come through recovery, inflammation, or injury pathways than through a direct nootropic mechanism.

Relevant raw PubMed notes: `raw/articles/pubmed-30915550-bpc157-soft-tissue-healing.md` and `raw/articles/pubmed-40756949-bpc157-orthopaedic-sports-medicine-review.md`.
That makes BPC-157 worth indexing, but the wiki should keep the confidence low because the evidence base is still dominated by healing and preclinical discussion.^[raw/articles/pubmed-30915550-bpc157-soft-tissue-healing.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40756949-bpc157-orthopaedic-sports-medicine-review.md]
The safety picture is still thin enough that the new [[peptide-safety-and-sourcing-risk-map]] treats BPC-157 as investigational: FDA flags limited human safety data plus immunogenicity and peptide-impurity/API-characterization concerns, while the current human record is still just a tiny retrospective knee-pain series and a two-person IV safety pilot.^[raw/articles/fda-bulk-drug-substances-peptide-risk-2026.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-34324435-bpc157-knee-pain-pilot.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40131143-bpc157-human-safety-pilot.md]
