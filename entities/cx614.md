# CX614

CX614 is an older high-impact AMPAkine / AMPA receptor positive modulator. It is important for the wiki because it is one of the clearest "plasticity biology is real, but safety and translation dominate" examples in the AMPAkine family.

Medicinal cognition signal: `mechanism-only / preclinical`. CX614 markedly and reversibly increased BDNF mRNA and protein in cultured rat entorhinal/hippocampal slices.^[raw/articles/pmc-6774091-cx614-neurotrophin-expression.md] It also rapidly stimulated BDNF release and dendritic mRNA translation in cultured neurons and hippocampal slices, linking AMPA receptor modulation to BDNF/TrkB and protein-synthesis pathways that plausibly support plasticity.^[raw/articles/pmc-2761758-cx614-bdnf-release-mrna-translation.md]

Compensatory signal: `not enhancement-only`. Prolonged CX614 exposure reduced AMPA receptor subunit GluR1-3 mRNA and protein in cultured hippocampal slices while also increasing BDNF content and TrkB activation.^[raw/articles/pmc-2746455-cx614-bdnf-signaling-ampa-downregulation.md] That makes CX614 a useful warning against upgrading "BDNF up" or "LTP support" into a nootropic claim without behavioral, dose, sleep, and chronic-safety data.

Safety signal: `high-impact AMPAkine ceiling`. A 2024 mechanistic paper used CX614 as part of the high-impact AMPAkine problem: CX614 and cyclothiazide triggered Gq/PLCbeta/InsP3-linked ER calcium release and neuronal hyperexcitability, while low-impact CX516, CX717, and CX1739 did not show the same pattern in the captured summary.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39304968-high-impact-ampakines-toxic-calcium-release.md] This is the main reason CX614 should stay in a safety-first router rather than a healthy-adult enhancement tier.

## Practical Interpretation

- CX614 is a mechanistic anchor, not a candidate to promote.
- It belongs beside [[idra-21]] as a high-impact / older AMPAkine warning branch, but with less direct nootropic anecdote than IDRA-21.
- It should be compared against low-impact [[cx717]], [[cx516]], [[cx1739]], [[cx691-farampator]], and [[tak-653]] rather than grouped into a generic "AMPA boost" bucket.
- The relevant wiki read is `preclinical BDNF/plasticity plus excitatory-ceiling caution`.
