# Iron

Iron is an essential mineral for oxygen transport, mitochondrial energy metabolism, and neurological function. In this wiki it is a brain-fog / cognition status-correction candidate, not a general nootropic. It should not be collapsed with multivitamins, heme-iron foods, ferritin-optimization protocols, [[folate-b-vitamins]], [[vitamin-d3]], thyroid interventions, or generic fatigue stacks.

Medicinal signal: `low-moderate when iron deficient, not established when replete`. The cleanest cognition anchor is a young-women supplementation trial where cognitive improvement tracked iron-status improvement, especially ferritin improvement. That makes iron important for menstruating adults, low-ferritin states, restrictive diets, blood loss, or deficiency symptoms, but the result is not evidence that extra iron improves cognition in iron-replete healthy adults.^[raw/articles/pubmed-17344500-iron-treatment-cognition-young-women.md]

Safety/status signal: `high gate burden`. ODS treats iron as essential but risk-sensitive. Adult needs vary by sex, menstruation, pregnancy, age, diet, and blood-loss history. The adult tolerable upper intake level is 45 mg/day unless higher-dose therapy is medically supervised, and excess iron can be dangerous. Ferritin can rise with inflammation, so iron interpretation needs hemoglobin, ferritin, transferrin saturation, inflammation context, medications, and symptoms together.^[raw/articles/ods-2025-iron-health-professional-fact-sheet.md]

## Signal Split

| Layer | Signal | Readout |
|---|---|---|
| Iron-deficient young women | `low-moderate/status-gated` | Supplementation-associated cognitive normalization tracked improved iron stores |
| Iron-replete adults | `not established` | No broad enhancer claim from the captured evidence |
| Brain-fog anecdotes | `moderate salience` | Low-ferritin threads often frame cognition as deficiency correction |
| Safety / monitoring | `high` | Excess iron, hemochromatosis, GI intolerance, drug interactions, and inflammation-confounded ferritin dominate |

## Practical Interpretation

Iron belongs near the top of [[mineral-status-brain-fog-cognition-watchlist]] for people with plausible deficiency or low ferritin, and near the bottom for replete adults looking for enhancement. The minimum useful record is ferritin, hemoglobin/CBC, transferrin saturation, inflammation context, menstruation or blood-loss history, diet, dose/form, GI tolerance, and follow-up labs.
