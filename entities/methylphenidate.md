# Methylphenidate

Methylphenidate is the prescription attention/control comparator that novel focus compounds need to be compared against. It has a clearer rested healthy-adult cognition signal than modafinil in the 2020 meta-analysis, but the effect is still small, task-limited, and safety-constrained rather than a broad durable enhancement.

## Medicinal Signal

- Formal signal: `moderate` for acute attention, recall, sustained attention, and inhibitory-control tasks; `low` for durable learning after washout.
- The 2020 healthy-adult meta-analysis found methylphenidate had a small positive effect, driven by recall, sustained attention, and inhibitory control. In the same dataset, modafinil had a smaller overall benefit and d-amphetamine had no overall effect.^[raw/articles/pubmed-32709551-pharmaceutical-cognitive-enhancement-healthy-adults.md]
- A 2025 multimodal imaging crossover study in 37 healthy adults found that 60 mg methylphenidate shifted frontoparietal-dominant brain states and improved response speed on a lower-demand visuospatial task, but not clearly on the harder version.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40101961-methylphenidate-frontoparietal-state-cognition.md]
- ADHD washout data argue against reading stimulant effects as durable cognitive residue: working-memory and response-speed gains during treatment did not persist after one week off medication.^[raw/articles/pubmed-33735707-methylphenidate-cognitive-performance-washout.md]
- Drug-holiday and N-of-1 discontinuation work support phenotype review and side-effect management, not a generic "cycle for bigger enhancement" claim.^[raw/articles/pubmed-15319017-weekend-holidays-methylphenidate-adhd.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39629771-methylphenidate-drug-holidays-adhd-emotion-regulation.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39601808-methylphenidate-discontinuation-n1-pilot.md]

## Safety And Interpretation

- Main burden: prescription status, abuse liability, insomnia, appetite suppression, anxiety/activation, blood-pressure and heart-rate concerns, and rebound / discontinuation effects.
- Methylphenidate should be the comparator for claims about attention and control, while [[modafinil]] should be the comparator for wakefulness and sleep-pressure claims.
- Do not generalize d-amphetamine from reputation alone. In the captured healthy-adult meta-analysis, d-amphetamine did not show an overall effect, which makes it a cautionary comparator rather than a class win.

## Current Read

Methylphenidate is strong enough to keep novelty claims honest and constrained enough that it should not be treated as a model for safe broad enhancement.
