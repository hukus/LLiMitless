# Amantadine

Amantadine is a prescription antiviral / antiparkinsonian drug with weak NMDA-antagonist and dopamine-system relevance. In this wiki it is a post-viral fatigue and arousal-state candidate, not a proven direct cognition enhancer.

## Bottom Line

Post-COVID signal: `low formal, fatigue-state`. A 2024 randomized open-label trial tested amantadine 100 mg twice daily for two weeks in post-COVID fatigue. Fatigue scores improved more than no-treatment control scores, but there was no placebo, no blinding, short follow-up, and no dedicated cognition endpoint.^[raw/articles/nature-2024-amantadine-post-covid-fatigue-open-label-rct.md]

Brain-fog relevance: indirect. If fatigue, sleepiness, or central arousal is driving cognitive failure, amantadine could matter as a fatigue-state drug. If the problem is pure memory/executive impairment without fatigue-state limitation, this source does not prove benefit.

Practical route: prescription and safety-gated. The label emphasizes renal accumulation, psychiatric effects, suicide-related warnings, hallucinations/confusion/agitation, seizure caution, blurred vision / impaired mental acuity, orthostatic and edema concerns, anticholinergic-like effects, corneal edema, and abrupt-discontinuation neuropsychiatric syndromes.^[raw/articles/dailymed-2025-amantadine-hydrochloride-label.md]

## Formal Evidence

The post-COVID fatigue trial enrolled 66 people and analyzed 62 completers. The intervention group had larger VAFS and FSS improvements than the no-treatment group after two weeks. One participant stopped for severe nausea and abdominal pain; other side effects were reported as transient or tolerable.^[raw/articles/nature-2024-amantadine-post-covid-fatigue-open-label-rct.md]

Because the design was open-label and no-placebo, the trial is a serious hypothesis generator but not enough to anchor a strong recommendation. It does, however, justify keeping amantadine visible beside [[methylphenidate]], [[modafinil]], and other state-support drugs when post-viral fatigue and brain fog are intertwined.
