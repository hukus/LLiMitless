# Centanafadine

Centanafadine (EB-1020 / CTN) is an investigational nonstimulant ADHD drug described as a norepinephrine, dopamine, and serotonin reuptake inhibitor. In this wiki it belongs beside [[bupropion]], [[atomoxetine]], [[viloxazine]], [[reboxetine]], [[dasotraline]], and [[tesofensine]], but the right framing is ADHD symptom treatment and attention-state pharmacology, not healthy-adult nootropic proof.

## Current Read

Medicinal cognition signal: `low/state-bound`. Two adult phase 3 trials reported statistically significant AISRS symptom improvement versus placebo at 200 mg/day and 400 mg/day, but the endpoint was adult ADHD symptoms, not healthy-adult cognition, learning transfer, or brain-fog remission.^[raw/articles/pmc-9426730-centanafadine-adult-adhd-phase3.md]

Regulatory status: live but not yet approved as of 2026-05-15. Otsuka announced FDA acceptance and priority review of the centanafadine NDA on 2026-01-27, with a PDUFA target action date of 2026-07-24.^[raw/articles/otsuka-2026-centanafadine-nda-priority-review.md]

Anecdote signal: `weak/speculative`. Current community discussion is mostly anticipation around a nonstimulant triple-reuptake ADHD option, Cognitive Disengagement Syndrome / brain-fog adjacency, possible anxiety/depression comorbidity relevance, and worries about price or SNRI-like tolerability. Direct first-person efficacy anecdotes are not expected yet because the drug is investigational.^[raw/articles/reddit-2023-2026-centanafadine-metadoxine-attention-threads.md]

## Safety And Interpretation Gates

- Do not equate NDSRI mechanism with clean cognition. Symptom improvement in ADHD does not establish benefit in healthy, well-slept adults.
- Appetite, headache, nausea, dry mouth, somnolence/fatigue, sleep effects, BP/HR, anxiety/activation, serotonergic stacking, and discontinuation-style worries are the practical watch points until label-level data exists.
- The adult phase 3 trials used a placebo run-in that excluded high placebo responders, so cross-drug effect-size comparisons against [[atomoxetine]], [[viloxazine]], [[methylphenidate]], or lisdexamfetamine need caution.
- Do not borrow [[tesofensine]] anecdotes. Both are monoamine-reuptake compounds, but centanafadine is an ADHD-development NDSRI, not an anorectic nootropic self-experiment branch.
