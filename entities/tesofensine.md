# Tesofensine

Tesofensine (NS 2330 / NS2330; often misspelled "tesonfensine") is a centrally acting monoamine reuptake inhibitor originally studied in Alzheimer's disease and Parkinson disease, then redirected toward obesity and rare hyperphagia / obesity disorders. It is not a peptide.

## Current Read

Medicinal cognition signal: `low/contested`. Tesofensine has formal human CNS exposure in Alzheimer's and Parkinson populations, but the captured public evidence does not establish healthy-adult cognitive enhancement. The old Alzheimer's program is best treated as a disease-context development history plus pharmacokinetic warning, not a modern cognition result.^[raw/articles/pmc-2000606-tesofensine-alzheimers-pk-phase2.md]

Weight/appetite signal: `moderate-to-strong but not cognition`. The cleanest human efficacy data are obesity and appetite studies: 203 adults with obesity lost 4.5%, 9.2%, or 10.6% on 0.25, 0.5, or 1.0 mg tesofensine plus diet versus 2.0% on placebo plus diet over 24 weeks; a separate 32-man trial found increased satiety/fullness and 1.8 kg more weight loss than placebo after 2 weeks.^[raw/articles/pubmed-18950853-tesofensine-obesity-phase2.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-20479765-tesofensine-energy-metabolism-appetite.md]

Mechanistic / target-engagement signal: `real but easy to overread`. A PET study found dose-dependent striatal dopamine-transporter occupancy after repeated 0.125-1 mg dosing, with mean occupancy ranging from 18% to 77% and roughly half-maximal occupancy around 0.25 mg / 4 ng/mL. That supports why some users describe stimulant-like focus or appetite effects, but it is not a cognition endpoint.^[raw/articles/pubmed-24239329-tesofensine-dat-occupancy-pet.md]

Regulatory / development read: tesofensine has not become a general cognition medicine. A 2024 sponsor press release said the Mexican obesity application had not yet been approved by Cofepris. Saniona's current 2026 pipeline page still frames tesofensine as an obesity program with a Mexican NDA / favorable technical-committee opinion, while Tesomet (tesofensine + metoprolol) is a paused investigational rare-obesity program due funding limitations, not safety/efficacy or cognition-positioning news.^[raw/articles/saniona-2024-mexico-tesofensine-not-approved.md]^[raw/articles/saniona-2026-tesofensine-pipeline-status.md]^[raw/articles/saniona-2026-tesomet-pipeline-status.md]

## Formal Human Evidence

- Parkinson disease: ADVANS tested 0.125-1 mg once daily for 14 weeks in advanced PD with levodopa-related motor fluctuations. The best findings were modest motor/off-time improvements at specific doses, no clear dose-response, and more GI / neuropsychiatric adverse events at higher doses.^[raw/articles/pubmed-18474731-tesofensine-advanced-parkinson-advans.md]
- Alzheimer's disease: a 14-week randomized proof-of-concept study exists in the background of the PK modeling paper, but the captured full article is mainly a pharmacokinetic model. It reports very long parent/metabolite half-lives and exposure differences by sex and creatinine clearance.^[raw/articles/pmc-2000606-tesofensine-alzheimers-pk-phase2.md]
- Obesity / appetite: phase 2 obesity and short metabolic-chamber work show appetite/weight effects; these are not direct cognition or brain-fog trials.^[raw/articles/pubmed-18950853-tesofensine-obesity-phase2.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-20479765-tesofensine-energy-metabolism-appetite.md]

## Safety And Interpretation Gates

- The long half-life is not trivia: the AD PK model estimated parent and metabolite half-lives around 234 h and 374 h, and the PD paper references an approximately 8-day human half-life. This makes accumulation and slow washout central to any self-experiment interpretation.^[raw/articles/pmc-2000606-tesofensine-alzheimers-pk-phase2.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-18474731-tesofensine-advanced-parkinson-advans.md]
- Watch insomnia, dry mouth, GI effects, appetite suppression, dehydration, HR/BP, anxiety, mood flattening/anhedonia, neuropsychiatric activation, stimulant/caffeine stacking, serotonergic or dopaminergic medications, cardiovascular history, renal function, and product identity.^[raw/articles/pubmed-18950853-tesofensine-obesity-phase2.md]^[raw/articles/endotext-2026-tesofensine-obesity-pharmacotherapy.md]
- Sports/status signal: the captured WADA 2026 stimulant-list note names tesofensine in S6.B specified stimulants, so athlete-facing discussions should treat it as sports-visible even before any nootropic interpretation.^[raw/articles/wada-2026-stimulant-list-afinils-pemoline-tesofensine.md]
- Do not infer cognitive benefit from weight loss, appetite suppression, or reduced food noise. Those may improve perceived energy in some users while worsening sleep, hydration, anxiety, or nutrient intake in others.
