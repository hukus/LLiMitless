# Vitamin B6 / P5P

Vitamin B6 covers pyridoxine, pyridoxal, pyridoxamine, and phosphorylated vitamers including pyridoxal-5'-phosphate (PLP/P5P). In this wiki it is the B6/P5P branch beside [[folate-b-vitamins]], [[riboflavin-b2]], [[methylcobalamin-b12]], [[l-methylfolate-5-mthf]], [[folinic-acid-leucovorin]], [[s-adenosyl-l-methionine]], [[l-methionine]], and [[betaine-tmg]]. It is not a methyl donor like SAMe or TMG; it is a cofactor/status variable.

Mechanistic/status signal: `real but indirect`. ODS describes B6 as active in more than 100 enzyme reactions, amino-acid metabolism, one-carbon metabolism, neurotransmitter biosynthesis, immune function, hemoglobin formation, and maintaining normal homocysteine. Plasma PLP is the common status marker. That makes B6 relevant to brain-fog triage, but not automatically cognition-positive.^[raw/articles/ods-2023-vitamin-b6-health-professional-fact-sheet.md]

Medicinal cognition signal: `low and mostly negative`. The 2003 Cochrane review found no vitamin-B6 trials in cognitive impairment or dementia and only two placebo-controlled healthy-older-adult trials. Neither the 75 mg/day older-women arm nor the 20 mg/day older-men trial found statistically significant mood or cognition benefits, even though biochemical B6 status improved in one trial. A 2002 trial in 211 healthy women found short-term folate, B12, or B6 supplementation slightly affected some memory measures but did not improve mood; it is a weak B-vitamin clue rather than clean P5P evidence.^[raw/articles/pubmed-14584010-vitamin-b6-cognition-cochrane.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-12042457-b-vitamins-memory-women-rct.md]

Anecdote signal: `weak-moderate and bidirectional`. MTHFR and supplement-community threads discuss P5P/B6 form choice for brain fog, fatigue, focus, and methylation protocols, but the same thread cluster includes insomnia, anxiety, wired state, and concern about high-dose B6 or B-complex use. The signal is useful for safety and responder hypotheses, not efficacy upgrading.^[raw/articles/reddit-2023-2026-p5p-riboflavin-mthfr-brainfog-threads.md]

Safety signal: `dose/form gated`. ODS notes the U.S. adult tolerable upper intake level of 100 mg/day and EFSA's lower 2023 adult upper limit of 12 mg/day. Chronic high-dose pyridoxine can cause severe sensory neuropathy with ataxia, and adverse effects can include dermatological lesions, photosensitivity, nausea, and heartburn. Do not treat P5P form as permission for open-ended high-dose use.^[raw/articles/ods-2023-vitamin-b6-health-professional-fact-sheet.md]

## Practical Interpretation

Vitamin B6/P5P is worth tracking because it is a real one-carbon/neurotransmitter cofactor and because P5P appears often in SAMe/MTHFR brain-fog stacks. It should not be ranked as a broad nootropic. The current route is `status and safety gate`: measure or infer B6 status, dose history, neuropathy symptoms, folate, B12, MMA, homocysteine, kidney function, medications, alcohol, sleep, and co-supplement use before interpreting subjective cognition changes.

Any confidence upgrade needs B6-form-separated trials with objective cognition endpoints, baseline PLP and homocysteine, explicit P5P versus pyridoxine dosing, and adverse-event tracking for neuropathy, insomnia, anxiety, and sensory symptoms.
