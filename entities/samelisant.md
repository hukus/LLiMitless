# Samelisant

## Bottom Line

Samelisant / SUVN-G3031 is the named H3 inverse-agonist pipeline lead with published phase 2 narcolepsy evidence. Medicinal signal is `low-moderate` for disorder-context wakefulness, not direct cognition: the trial improved Epworth Sleepiness Scale and supportive patient/clinician scales, but it was not a cognition trial. Anecdotal cognition signal is `absent-to-weak`.

## What It Is

Samelisant is an investigational histamine H3 receptor inverse agonist developed for hypersomnia disorders. In this wiki it belongs with [[histamine-h3-inverse-agonists-2026-scan]], [[pharmacology-comparator-map]], [[pitolisant]], and [[orexin-axis-molecule-watchlist]] as a state-restoration comparator.

## Medicinal Research Signal

The phase 2 randomized double-blind placebo-controlled narcolepsy study randomized 190 participants to 2 mg, 4 mg, or placebo once daily for 14 days. The paper reports a statistically significant improvement on the primary Epworth Sleepiness Scale endpoint and supportive patient/clinician perspective scales. Common adverse events included insomnia, abnormal dreams, nausea, and hot flush.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39504585-samelisant-results-narcolepsy.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct04072380-samelisant-narcolepsy.md]

The 2026 drug-evaluation review reinforces the wakefulness rationale and development context, including potential cardiovascular and drug-interaction advantages and possible pairing with orexin-based therapies. It does not add a direct cognition efficacy signal.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41725284-samelisant-phase2-review.md]

## Safety And Status

Samelisant should stay in the investigational wakefulness lane. Its adverse-event pattern is activation/sleep-adjacent rather than cognition-specific, and insomnia or abnormal dreams can themselves affect next-day performance.

## Signal Judgment

Medicinal signal: `low-moderate` for narcolepsy sleepiness; `low` for direct cognition.

Anecdotal signal: `absent-to-weak`.

Practical route: H3 state-restoration comparator, not a healthy-adult enhancer.
