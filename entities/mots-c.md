# MOTS-c

MOTS-c is a mitochondrial-derived peptide tied to metabolic homeostasis, exercise biology, and aging research. Its cognition relevance is still indirect, but it is now one of the clearer `upcoming` peptides in the broader category because a real Phase 2a human study is recruiting. The right read is metabolism-to-brain frontier with live translational momentum, not direct nootropic proof.

Relevant raw notes: raw/articles/pubmed-25738459-mots-c-metabolic-homeostasis.md, raw/articles/pubmed-34351816-mdp-exercise-humans.md, raw/articles/pubmed-32052315-mots-c-beta-amyloid-cad.md, raw/articles/pmc-12411631-mots-c-pancreatic-islet-senescence-diabetes.md, raw/articles/pubmed-41520850-mots-c-muscle-bioenergetics.md, raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct07505745-mots-c-prediabetes-record-2026-04-01.md, raw/articles/pubmed-41966639-peptide-therapies-sports-medicine-review.md, raw/articles/reddit-2025-12-13-have-anyone-tried-mots-c-thread.md, and raw/articles/reddit-2026-04-13-no-more-mots-c-anaphylaxis-thread.md.

Because the signal is mostly mechanistic and preclinical, MOTS-c belongs in the peptide map as a low-confidence but interesting bridge between metabolism and cognition. The formal human evidence is endogenous-biomarker and registry-stage: acute endurance exercise can move circulating mitochondrial-derived peptides, a diabetic/CAD cohort linked lower MOTS-c to worse outcomes, and a 2025 diabetes/senescence paper found lower circulating MOTS-c in type 2 diabetes while showing mouse pancreatic-islet treatment effects. None of these is a human cognition trial.^[raw/articles/pubmed-25738459-mots-c-metabolic-homeostasis.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-34351816-mdp-exercise-humans.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-32052315-mots-c-beta-amyloid-cad.md]^[raw/articles/pmc-12411631-mots-c-pancreatic-islet-senescence-diabetes.md]

The current interventional human signal is the MOTS-MET Phase 2a registry: 120 adults with prediabetes and overweight/obesity, subcutaneous MOTS-c or placebo once daily for 12 weeks, study start actual 2026-02-02, primary completion estimated 2027-02-14, and no cognition endpoint. That makes MOTS-c more formally "upcoming" than most gray-market peptides, but still outside the cognition-efficacy lane.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct07505745-mots-c-prediabetes-record-2026-04-01.md]

The 2026 MOTS-c muscle paper sharpened the mechanism side by showing direct CK2 binding and mitochondrial-bioenergetic effects in muscle, which strengthens the exercise-metabolism bridge but still does not produce a human cognition result.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41520850-mots-c-muscle-bioenergetics.md]

Safety and sourcing are central. FDA's current bulk-compounding page says compounded MOTS-c may present significant safety risk and that FDA has not identified human exposure data on drug products containing MOTS-c, WADA names MOTS-c as a metabolic-modulator concern, and a 2026 sports-medicine peptide review frames unapproved peptides as gray-market products with scarce rigorous human safety data and social-media amplification of perceived efficacy.^[raw/articles/fda-bulk-drug-substances-peptide-risk-2026.md]^[raw/articles/wada-2026-prohibited-list-peptide-gh-axis-expanded.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41966639-peptide-therapies-sports-medicine-review.md]

The anecdotal signal is metabolic/performance-heavy and polarized, not cognition-specific. A Reddit experience thread includes energy, workout, fat-loss-support, non-response, and product-quality themes, while a high-salience April 2026 thread reports an apparent first-dose anaphylaxis event. These route through the Anecdote Ledger in [[mots-c-2026-scan]] and the safety overlays, and they do not upgrade medicinal confidence.^[raw/articles/reddit-2025-12-13-have-anyone-tried-mots-c-thread.md]^[raw/articles/reddit-2026-04-13-no-more-mots-c-anaphylaxis-thread.md]

## Frontier Read

MOTS-c is one of the best examples of how the peptide category has become more serious without becoming more proven. It has real metabolic biology, a live human trial, sports visibility, and broad market salience. That is enough to keep it on the short list of upcoming peptides to monitor closely, even though the cognition branch is still only indirect.
