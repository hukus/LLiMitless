# Huperzine A

Huperzine A is an alkaloid cholinesterase-inhibitor candidate derived from Huperzia serrata and related traditional-medicine contexts. The wiki should treat it as a disease / impairment-context cholinergic molecule with meaningful bias and safety caveats, not as a casual daily nootropic.

Medicinal signal: `low-moderate`, contested. A 2019 MCI meta-analysis reported improved memory quotient and MMSE versus placebo across nine RCTs, but warned that original-study quality was low. A 2013 AD meta-analysis included 20 RCTs / 1,823 participants and found multiple cognitive and ADL signals, but most trials had high risk of bias. A 2012 Cochrane MCI review found no eligible placebo-controlled MCI trials under its criteria, which keeps the MCI branch explicitly contested.^[raw/articles/pubmed-30989926-huperzine-a-mci-meta-analysis.md]^[raw/articles/pmc-3781107-huperzine-a-alzheimers-meta-analysis.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-23235666-huperzine-a-mci-cochrane.md]

## Practical Interpretation

Route Huperzine A through [[cholinergic-state-restoration-and-cognition]] and [[cholinergic-and-nicotinic-agents-2026-scan]]. Do not combine it casually with other cholinergic drugs or high-cholinergic stacks in wiki recommendations without a safety-first note.
