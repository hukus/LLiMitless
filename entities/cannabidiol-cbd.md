# Cannabidiol / CBD

## Bottom Line

CBD is relevant to cognition mostly through anxiety, sleep, sedation, and safety, not through proven enhancement. Medicinal signal is `low`: the captured healthy-adult RCT and systematic review support cognitive-safety questions more than broad benefit. Anecdotal signal is `weak-moderate`, mainly evening downshift and sleep support.

## What It Is

Cannabidiol is a cannabinoid with pharmaceutical use as Epidiolex for seizure disorders and broad consumer use in oils, beverages, gummies, and sleep/anxiety products. The wiki keeps CBD separate from kratom, kava, THC, and generic "calm focus" claims.

## Medicinal Research Signal

The captured healthy-adult RCT directly measured cognition/psychomotor function after 300 mg CBD, while the systematic review found a heterogeneous animal and human literature. This packet does not establish CBD as a cognitive enhancer.^[raw/articles/pubmed-36625844-cbd-healthy-adults-cognition-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-37792394-cbd-cognition-systematic-review.md]

The Epidiolex prescribing information is the safety anchor: pharmacologic CBD can require liver-enzyme monitoring and carries somnolence/sedation and drug-interaction concerns. Consumer-dose products may not map cleanly onto pharmaceutical dosing or purity.^[raw/articles/epidiolex-2018-cannabidiol-prescribing-information.md]

## Anecdote Signal

The captured nootropic anecdote is a recent CBD-after-stack report with sleep/downshift framing and next-morning slowness. Signal is `weak-moderate` for subjective state support and `weak` for objective cognition.^[raw/articles/reddit-2026-cbd-nootropics-experience-thread.md]

## Safety And Status

CBD should be routed by dose, product identity, liver/interactions, and sedation. Anxiety or sleep benefits can improve perceived cognition, but that does not prove direct cognitive enhancement.

## Signal Judgment

Medicinal signal: `low`.

Anecdotal signal: `weak-moderate` for calm/sleep state support.

Practical route: calm/sleep/safety branch with interaction gates.
