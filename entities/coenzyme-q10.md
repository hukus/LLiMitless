# Coenzyme Q10

Coenzyme Q10 (CoQ10, ubiquinone, ubiquinol) is an endogenous mitochondrial electron-transport cofactor and antioxidant-like molecule sold as a supplement for energy, cardiovascular support, statin-associated symptoms, and aging. For cognitive improvement, it belongs in the mitochondrial cofactor lane of [[redox-mitochondrial-cofactor-cognition-watchlist]], with strong formulation and disease-state caveats.

Medicinal cognition signal: `low/mixed`. The 2025 CoQ10 cognition review found that eight human trials were available; four reported cognitive benefits and two reported increased cerebral blood flow, but trial populations, cognitive tests, formulation, dose, and bioavailability varied enough to keep confidence low. This is inclusion-grade as a mitochondrial cofactor, but not a clean healthy-adult nootropic signal.^[raw/articles/pmc-12430131-coq10-cognition-review.md]

Disease and mechanism context: `plausible but not portable`. CoQ10's strongest rationale comes from mitochondrial dysfunction, oxidative stress, cardiovascular/metabolic state, and neurodegenerative disease hypotheses. The wiki should not treat ubiquinone, ubiquinol, mitochondrial-targeted analogs, and combination products as interchangeable.^[raw/articles/pmc-12430131-coq10-cognition-review.md]

Long-COVID signal: `negative/weak`. A 119-completer randomized double-blind crossover trial in post-COVID condition used CoQ10 500 mg/day for six weeks and found no significant benefit versus placebo on EQ-5D health index or post-COVID symptom score. This is a direct counterweight to mitochondrial-fatigue marketing claims.^[raw/articles/sciencedirect-2023-coq10-post-covid-crossover-rct.md]

Safety signal: `moderate reassurance with interactions`. NCCIH reports no serious side effects, with mild insomnia or digestive upset possible, and flags interactions with warfarin, insulin, and some cancer treatments. That makes medication context more important than casual supplement status suggests.^[raw/articles/nccih-coq10-safety-and-interactions.md]

Community signal: `weak`. The captured Reddit thread is adverse rather than efficacy-positive: one user reported fatigue and brain fog after 100 mg CoQ10. This keeps responder variability visible but does not create a positive anecdote denominator.^[raw/articles/reddit-2025-coq10-brain-fog-adverse-thread.md]

## Practical Interpretation

CoQ10 should be routed through [[molecule-intake-triage-table]] as a `mitochondrial / vascular-state cofactor`, not a broad focus compound. The right question is whether a specific formulation helps a specific metabolic, vascular, neurodegenerative, statin, or fatigue phenotype. For post-COVID symptom treatment, the captured high-dose trial is negative; for healthy cognition ranking, it remains below cleaner state-support comparators such as [[creatine-cognitive-support]], [[ketone-ester]], and [[lactate]].
