# Vasopressin

## Bottom Line

Vasopressin is a social-memory, social-threat, and context-dependent peptide signal, not a general nootropic. Medicinal cognition signal is `low`: one healthy-men intranasal AVP study reported selective social-face memory encoding, and newer 2026 human challenge studies show social-comparison and looming-threat modulation, while broader social-cognition testing is null. Anecdotal cognition signal is `absent-to-weak` in the current captured corpus.

## What It Is

Arginine vasopressin is a neuropeptide and hormone involved in water balance, stress/arousal, and social behavior. In this wiki it belongs with [[oxytocin-vasopressin-desmopressin-social-memory-2026-scan]], [[oxytocin]], [[desmopressin]], and [[self-experimentation-stop-rules]] as a social-behavior comparator and receptor-cross-talk node.

## Medicinal Research Signal

The clearest social-memory source is a randomized placebo-controlled study in 48 healthy men using 20 IU intranasal arginine vasopressin. AVP increased remembering of happy and angry faces as known relative to neutral faces. This is a narrow social-memory encoding effect, not broad memory or executive enhancement.^[raw/articles/pubmed-20447617-arginine-vasopressin-social-memory-faces.md]

The broad counterweight is the randomized social-cognition battery trial that tested both oxytocin and vasopressin and found broadly null results across multiple paradigms. This keeps vasopressin below any general social-cognition enhancer threshold.^[raw/articles/pubmed-31128568-oxytocin-vasopressin-null-social-paradigms.md]

The newer 2026 human studies sharpen the phenotype rather than broadening it. Intranasal vasopressin intensified contrastive emotions during spontaneous social comparison in a 111-person neuroimaging study, linked to mPFC activity and connectivity. A separate randomized looming-threat study found that AVP increased time overestimation and changed pupil-response dynamics. These are self-image defense and threat-processing signals, not general cognition benefits.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41765123-vasopressin-social-comparison-2026.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41734211-vasopressin-fear-response-dynamics-2026.md]

The review and animal layers make interpretation more conditional. A 2026 review frames oxytocin/vasopressin crosstalk as sexually dimorphic and diagnosis-dependent, while a preclinical social-play paper points to sex-specific glutamate signaling in the lateral septum. That supports a moderator-heavy model rather than a universal enhancer model.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41862144-oxytocin-vasopressin-crosstalk-sexually-dimorphic-psychiatric-conditions.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39304743-vasopressin-social-play-sex-specific-glutamate-lateral-septum.md]

June 2026 attribution gate: vasopressin should stay a narrow social-memory / threat-processing peptide, not a broad memory or executive-function enhancer. Future reports need product identity, route/formulation and device, dose schedule, sex/context, threat or social-comparison state, hydration/BP context, receptor-cross-talk assumptions, social-memory task, adverse behavioral or cardiovascular effects, and objective transfer endpoints before any ranking comparison with [[oxytocin]], [[desmopressin]], [[semax]], [[selank]], [[cerebrolysin]], recovery-bound [[bpc-157]], or [[bromantane]].

## Safety And Status

The practical cognition risk is behavioral directionality. Vasopressin can plausibly sharpen threat, social comparison, or self-protective processing, which may not be desirable and may not improve performance. Hydration, blood pressure, sex/context dependence, and receptor cross-talk with oxytocin pathways matter for interpretation.

## Signal Judgment

Medicinal signal: `low` and social-domain-specific.

Anecdotal signal: `absent-to-weak`.

Practical route: social-memory and threat-processing comparator, not a broad enhancer.
