# DMAE / Centrophenoxine

## Bottom Line

DMAE and centrophenoxine / meclofenoxate are legacy cholinergic/lipofuscin nootropic candidates with weak and inconclusive formal cognition evidence. Anecdotal signal is `moderate`, but inconsistent and mood-cost-heavy.

## What It Is

DMAE is dimethylaminoethanol. Centrophenoxine, also called meclofenoxate, is a DMAE-containing legacy drug/nootropic. The wiki keeps this pair separate from [[alpha-gpc]], [[citicoline]], and [[huperzine-a]] because the evidence base, product identity, and safety caveats differ.

## Medicinal Research Signal

The captured ADDF Cognitive Vitality note summarizes centrophenoxine clinical evidence as weak and inconclusive, with mixed old trials and DMAE safety concerns. This is not comparable to modern controlled cognition branches.^[raw/articles/alzdiscovery-2017-centrophenoxine-cognitive-vitality.md]

## Anecdote Signal

The captured Reddit thread gives `moderate` anecdotal salience for clarity, memory, or choline-like effects, but also irritability, mood cost, and inconsistent response. This is hypothesis-generating only.^[raw/articles/reddit-2019-centrophenoxine-experience-thread.md]

## Safety And Status

Pregnancy safety concerns, cholinergic mood effects, and product availability/identity make DMAE/centrophenoxine a poor upgrade candidate without better human evidence.

## Signal Judgment

Medicinal signal: `low/inconclusive`.

Anecdotal signal: `moderate`, inconsistent.

Practical route: legacy cholinergic gray-zone page.
