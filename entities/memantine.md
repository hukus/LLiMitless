# Memantine

## Bottom Line

Memantine is a prescription NMDA-antagonist clinical-state comparator, not a broad nootropic. Medicinal signal is `low-moderate` for selected impairment contexts: an age-associated memory impairment trial suggested attention/processing-speed movement more than memory, while the label indication remains moderate-to-severe Alzheimer-type dementia. Anecdotal signal is `moderate` but split and safety-limited.

## What It Is

Memantine is an NMDA receptor antagonist used clinically in Alzheimer-type dementia. In this wiki it belongs with [[risk-first-legacy-gray-zone-nootropic-watchlist]], [[nmda-coagonist-glyt1-daao-cognition-2026-scan]], and [[pharmacology-comparator-map]] as a prescription clinical-state comparator.

## Medicinal Research Signal

The captured AAMI trial in older adults suggests possible attention and processing-speed effects rather than a clean memory-enhancement result. The official label anchors the practical boundary: indication is moderate-to-severe dementia of the Alzheimer type, with dizziness, headache, confusion, constipation, urine-pH elimination issues, and caution with other NMDA antagonists.^[raw/articles/pubmed-17117395-memantine-age-associated-memory-impairment.md]^[raw/articles/dailymed-2021-memantine-hydrochloride-label.md]

## Anecdote Signal

The captured Reddit thread gives memantine a `moderate` anecdotal salience signal for mood, anhedonia, focus, and tolerance-like states. The same anecdotal layer also contains fog, dissociation, migraine/headache, dose-escalation, and psychiatric-state confounding, so it should prioritize safety monitoring rather than upgrade efficacy.^[raw/articles/reddit-2020-memantine-one-month-thread.md]

## Safety And Status

Memantine should stay prescription-first. Nootropic use often underweights its long half-life, accumulation, confusion/dizziness risk, and interaction risk with other NMDA antagonists.

## Signal Judgment

Medicinal signal: `low-moderate` clinical-state cognition, not healthy enhancement.

Anecdotal signal: `moderate`, safety-limited and confounded.

Practical route: prescription comparator and risk-gated experimental/nootropic caution page.
