# CX516

CX516, also known historically as Ampalex, is the older low-impact AMPAkine failure comparator. It belongs in the wiki because it anchors the class's negative human cognition translation before newer CX717, CX1739, CX691, and TAK-653 programs.

Medicinal cognition signal: `negative / weak`. In stable schizophrenia, CX516 900 mg three times daily for 4 weeks did not improve a composite cognitive score or individual cognitive tests versus placebo and caused fatigue, insomnia, and epigastric discomfort.^[raw/articles/pubmed-17487227-cx516-schizophrenia-rct.md] In fragile X syndrome, a controlled trial found acceptable tolerability but no significant improvement in memory, language, attention/executive function, behavior, or overall functioning.^[raw/articles/pubmed-17069542-cx516-fragile-x-controlled-trial.md] The MCI program in the captured corpus is represented by a progress report rather than a final efficacy result.^[raw/articles/pubmed-12212780-cx516-mci-progress-report.md]

Mechanism and rescue signal: `class-context only`. CX516 remains useful as a low-impact AMPAkine reference and as a comparator in sleep-deprivation animal work, but the human clinical record does not support it as a cognitive enhancer.^[raw/articles/pubmed-38823543-caffeine-modafinil-avl3288-cx516-sleep-deprivation.md] A 2024 toxicity-mechanism paper uses CX516 as part of the low-impact branch that did not show the same Gq-linked ER-calcium-release pattern as high-impact compounds, but safer mechanism does not rescue efficacy.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39304968-high-impact-ampakines-toxic-calcium-release.md]
