# Urolithin A

Urolithin A is a gut-microbiome-derived ellagitannin metabolite and commercial postbiotic / mitophagy ingredient, commonly encountered as Mitopure. This page covers direct urolithin A cognition and cognition-adjacent claims, not pomegranate juice, pomegranate extract, ellagic acid, punicalagins, generic polyphenols, urolithin B, [[nicotinamide-mononucleotide]], [[nicotinamide-riboside]], [[resveratrol]], or generic mitochondrial / longevity stacks.

Medicinal cognition signal: `low/preclinical-pending`. The most important human cognition source is the CLARITY registry, a 650-person randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled direct-to-consumer study of a Mitopure-containing nutritional product. It uses PROMIS Cognitive Function 8A as the primary outcome and includes DANA Brain Vital app cognitive assessment, fatigue, sleep, and adverse-event endpoints. As of the 2026-03-06 registry update captured here, the study is active, not recruiting, with actual primary completion on 2025-11-20 but no posted results.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct07060898-urolithin-a-clarity-cognition.md]

The human evidence outside cognition is stronger but proxy-only. A first-in-human elderly study found safety and mitochondrial / cellular-health molecular signatures. A 66-person older-adult JAMA Network Open RCT found improved muscle endurance and mitochondrial/inflammatory biomarker movement, while the primary 6-minute-walk and maximal ATP-production endpoints did not significantly separate from placebo. A middle-aged adult Cell Reports Medicine RCT found muscle-strength, high-dose exercise-performance, and skeletal-muscle mitophagy-biomarker signals, but not the primary peak-power endpoint. A 2024 systematic review summarized five human studies and concluded that urolithin A may improve inflammation markers and muscle strength/endurance, but not broad anthropometric, cardiovascular, physical-function, or mitochondrial ATP-production endpoints.^[raw/articles/pubmed-32694802-urolithin-a-first-in-human-mitochondrial-health.md]^[raw/articles/jama-2022-urolithin-a-older-adults-muscle-endurance.md]^[raw/articles/sciencedirect-2022-urolithin-a-middle-aged-muscle-biomarkers-rct.md]^[raw/articles/sciencedirect-2024-urolithin-a-human-systematic-review.md]

The animal cognition layer is plausible but not transportable. A 2025 mouse paper reported protection against domoic-acid-induced cognitive deficits via an estrogen receptor-alpha / mitochondrial-biogenesis route. That keeps urolithin A relevant to the cognitive-aging graph, but it remains preclinical for cognition until the human CLARITY result or another direct cognitive endpoint reports.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40818743-urolithin-a-domoic-acid-mice.md]

Safety and identity signal: `low-moderate, product-specific`. FDA GRN 791 gives a food-ingredient GRAS status anchor for Amazentis urolithin A under specified food-use levels up to 500-1000 mg/serving, and Mitopure presents a branded high-purity, NSF Certified for Sport product identity. Those facts do not validate every generic urolithin A label. A 2024 NMN/urolithin A label-claim study found substantial deviations across tested products, and community product-quality reports describe suspicious Amazon products and possible adverse effects that resolved after discontinuation.^[raw/articles/fda-gras-2026-urolithin-a-grn-791.md]^[raw/articles/mitopure-2026-urolithin-a-product-identity.md]^[raw/articles/springer-2024-nmn-urolithin-label-claim-testing.md]^[raw/articles/reddit-2024-urolithin-a-amazon-product-quality-thread.md]

Anecdote signal: `weak-moderate for energy/endurance/muscle salience, weak for objective cognition, mixed for tolerability and value`. Community reports include mental-fatigue / concentration / motivation benefit, energy and workout improvements, muscle-tone reports, no-effect reports after one to 18 months, sleep disruption, next-day depletion, diarrhea around a questionable product, and persistent price skepticism. Anecdote makes urolithin A worth tracking but cannot move the medicinal signal above preclinical/pending for cognition.^[raw/articles/reddit-2023-urolithin-a-nootropics-fatigue-thread.md]^[raw/articles/reddit-2022-urolithin-a-biohackers-mitopure-thread.md]^[raw/articles/reddit-2026-urolithin-a-supplements-experience-thread.md]^[raw/articles/reddit-2024-urolithin-a-amazon-product-quality-thread.md]

## Identity Gates

- Separate direct urolithin A / Mitopure from pomegranate juice, pomegranate extract, ellagic acid, punicalagins, walnuts/berries, and the user's microbiome conversion capacity.
- Do not treat muscle endurance, VO2, 6-minute walk, skeletal-muscle mitophagy markers, CRP, acylcarnitines, ceramides, or GRAS status as cognitive efficacy.
- Require product identity: Mitopure or another independently assayed urolithin A product, dose, lot-level COA, storage, and label-claim verification.
- Keep synthetic urolithin A, food-derived precursor metabolism, and gut-microbiome conversion phenotypes separate.
- Watch for sleep disruption, GI intolerance, questionable marketplace products, cancer-treatment context, pregnancy/breastfeeding, liver/kidney disease, high-dose long-term use, and stacking with other longevity / mitochondrial agents.

## Practical Ranking

Urolithin A deserves standalone coverage because it has a live large cognition registry, repeated human mitochondrial/muscle proxy trials, a preclinical cognition-rescue signal, a branded product-identity layer, and visible community use. It does not deserve a cognition ranking upgrade yet. The current route is `preclinical/pending cognition + mitochondrial-muscle proxy watch`, with medicinal confidence held at low until a blinded human trial reports a placebo-separated cognitive endpoint.
