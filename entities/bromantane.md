# Bromantane

Bromantane is an adamantane-derived stimulant-like compound marketed in Russia as Ladasten. In the wiki it belongs on the "state-adjacent activation" side of the map: it has more evidence for fatigue / asthenia / work-capacity support than for broad healthy-adult cognition enhancement.

The June 2026 read is `moderate-strong anecdotal salience, low formal cognition confidence`. Bromantane keeps showing up as a drive / work-output / exercise-intensity compound, but the best formal human match is still asthenia or fatigue-rescue physiology rather than rested healthy-adult enhancement. It belongs near the strongest peptide/nootropic anecdote-priority cluster as a non-peptide activation comparator, but it still needs source/COA, route/formulation, dose timing, stack order, washout, sleep, stimulant history, exercise load, WADA/sports context, adverse-event, and objective-endpoint gates before any ranking upgrade.^[raw/articles/reddit-2026-bromantane-thread-trail.md]^[raw/articles/troof-nootropics-ratings-analysis.md]^[raw/articles/darktka-2017-nootropics-survey-on-r-nootropics.md]

## What It Is

- Russian-origin actoprotector / psychostimulant-adjacent compound.
- Often discussed as a mild stimulant with anxiolytic overtones rather than as a classic amphetamine-like driver.
- The practical nootropic conversation around it is usually about energy, motivation, flow, and fatigue resistance.

## Current Read

- Healthy-volunteer evidence is small and old, and the best paper mainly shows EEG and psychophysiology changes rather than a clear subjective or broad cognitive shift.^[raw/articles/pubmed-10998997-bromantane-human-volunteers.md]
- Russian clinical evidence is much stronger in asthenia / neurasthenia / psychoautonomic-syndrome contexts than in healthy enhancement contexts.^[raw/articles/pubmed-21322821-ladasten-asthenic-disorders-multicenter.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-22834121-ladasten-neurasthenia-alpha-rhythm.md]
- Community reports line up with that same split: users often describe motivation, energy, or task activation, while others report no effect, haze, or sleep disruption.^[raw/articles/reddit-2026-bromantane-thread-trail.md]
- Bromantane therefore fits the wiki as a `state-bound` lead, not a proven universal cognition enhancer.
- The top-level [[anecdote-signal-ledger]] now treats bromantane as a `moderate-strong` state-bound activation signal. This upgrades monitoring priority, not efficacy confidence.

## Evidence Layering

- Human healthy-volunteer study: 10 healthy men, single oral dose, EEG and psychophysiology changes, no dramatic subjective change in untired participants.^[raw/articles/pubmed-10998997-bromantane-human-volunteers.md]
- Human clinical rescue context: 728-patient Russian multicenter asthenia study with rapid symptom improvement and low reported serious harm.^[raw/articles/pubmed-21322821-ladasten-asthenic-disorders-multicenter.md]
- Human responder phenotype signal: neurasthenia patients with reduced EEG alpha activity and emotional lability looked more responsive.^[raw/articles/pubmed-22834121-ladasten-neurasthenia-alpha-rhythm.md]
- Mechanism claims: indirect dopaminergic / monoaminergic support is mostly supported by animal work, not direct human proof.^[raw/articles/pubmed-20369592-ladasten-dopamine-serotonin-rat-mechanism.md]
- Sports visibility: WADA explicitly names Bromantan as an in-competition stimulant.^[raw/articles/wada-2025-prohibited-list-bromantan.md]

## Community Signal

- Current Reddit thread trail suggests repeated first-person reports of better work output, exercise intensity, and a cleaner drive state, but also a real ceiling on enthusiasm and a meaningful sleep/tolerance complaint rate.^[raw/articles/reddit-2026-bromantane-thread-trail.md]
- The thread language often sounds like "better stamina" more than "better cognition."
- Route and formulation noise are unusually high because users discuss sublingual, intranasal, DMSO, PEG, and MCT preparations.

## Safety And Status

- The published multicenter asthenia study reported few adverse effects and no serious adverse effects, but that does not make bromantane a low-risk universal enhancer.^[raw/articles/pubmed-21322821-ladasten-asthenic-disorders-multicenter.md]
- Community reports highlight sleep disruption, flatness, and tolerance drift.
- WADA visibility means athletes should treat it as a prohibited in-competition stimulant, regardless of nootropic marketing.^[raw/articles/wada-2025-prohibited-list-bromantan.md]
- In the wiki, treat US sourcing as grey-market / vendor-mediated and therefore quality-sensitive; that status is an inference from current community sourcing patterns, not a formal market audit.^[raw/articles/reddit-2026-bromantane-thread-trail.md]
- Route Bromantane stack or productivity reports through [[safety-interaction-matrix-for-multi-modal-stacks]], [[stack-attribution-diary-for-self-study-and-n-of-1-protocols]], [[molecule-triage-worksheet-for-online-scans]], and [[regulatory-and-sports-visibility-map]] before treating them as cognition evidence. Sleep debt, stimulant history, route/formulation changes, exercise load, tolerance drift, WADA context, source/COA uncertainty, and caffeine/nicotine/wake-promoter co-use can all explain perceived focus or drive.
- Keep Bromantane separate from the [[semax]] / [[selank]] peptide cluster when interpreting "clean focus plus calm" stack reports. The combination can blend monoamine-like activation, stress-state relief, sleep timing, route/formulation changes, and expectancy into a single story that is impossible to assign to one molecule.

## Protocol Parameters

- Human healthy-volunteer paper: single oral administration, exact public abstract details limited.^[raw/articles/pubmed-10998997-bromantane-human-volunteers.md]
- Russian clinical use: 50-100 mg/day for 28 days in asthenia / psychoautonomic syndrome.^[raw/articles/pubmed-21322821-ladasten-asthenic-disorders-multicenter.md]
- Community anecdotes cluster around 25-250 mg, with route changes used to force onset or bioavailability; those numbers should not be treated as validated dose guidance.^[raw/articles/reddit-2026-bromantane-thread-trail.md]
- Any N-of-1 row should record source/COA, route/formulation, dose timing, washout days, sleep/caffeine/nicotine state, exercise load, WADA-relevant status, adverse events, and objective task endpoints using [[30-day-self-study-template-for-nonprescription-interventions]] and [[30-day-self-study-workbook-schema]].

## Open Contradictions

- The community often treats bromantane like a nootropic, but the strongest formal evidence is in rescue states and fatigue syndromes.
- The mechanism story emphasizes dopamine synthesis, yet the human evidence does not directly validate that mechanism as a cognition enhancer.
- Some users report clean drive and less irritability; others report flattening, haze, or no benefit.
