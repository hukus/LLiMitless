# CE-123

CE-123 is an experimental modafinil-family dopamine-transporter inhibitor that appears in the wiki as a preclinical executive-function and motivation lead, not as a validated human nootropic. It belongs beside [[modafinil]], [[adrafinil]], and [[unapproved-modafinil-analog-eugeroic-watchlist]], but it should not be dose- or status-equated with prescription wake-promoters.

Medicinal signal: `preclinical-only for cognition`. In healthy male rats, CE-123 improved attentional set-shifting / cognitive flexibility without producing impulsive responding in the captured assay. In experienced aged rats, S-CE-123 enhanced motivation and improved a new operant discrimination task plus a social-cognition cooperation assay, but did not improve attention, spatial learning, or motor learning. That is a meaningful animal cognition/motivation profile, but still no human cognition efficacy or safety packet.^[raw/articles/pmc-5711856-ce123-cognitive-flexibility-rats.md]^[raw/articles/pmc-8671572-sce123-aged-rats-cognitive-profiling.md]

PK/status signal: `identity-gated`. S-CE-123 and R-modafinil differ in CNS distribution and metabolism; the captured PK source reports much faster S-CE-123 hepatic metabolism than R-modafinil in the relevant comparison. This makes direct conversion from [[modafinil]], armodafinil, flmodafinil, or [[adrafinil]] dosing especially weak.^[raw/articles/pmc-10707468-ce123-modafinil-cns-distribution.md]

Anecdote signal: `weak-moderate/emerging`. Recent community threads discuss CE-123 as a modafinil analog with possible wakefulness, task-initiation, and cleaner dopamine-like effects, but they also emphasize sparse dosing information, product/source uncertainty, and comparisons against flmodafinil, hydrafinil, armodafinil, and prescription modafinil. This is enough to track salience, not enough to infer efficacy.^[raw/articles/reddit-2025-2026-modafinil-analog-experience-threads.md]

## Signal Split

| Layer | Signal | Readout |
|---|---|---|
| Rat cognitive flexibility | `preclinical-positive` | Improved attentional set-shifting in healthy male rats |
| Aged-rat motivation / learning | `preclinical-mixed-positive` | Improved motivation-linked operant and social-cognition tasks; no attention/spatial/motor-learning effect |
| Human cognition | `absent` | No controlled human cognition trial captured |
| PK / identity | `important` | S-CE-123 is not interchangeable with R-modafinil or other afinil analogs |
| Anecdote | `weak-moderate/emerging` | CE-123 threads exist, but dose, route, source, and comparator confounds dominate |

## Practical Interpretation

Use CE-123 as a preclinical comparator for dopamine-transporter selectivity and motivation-linked task performance. It should not enter healthy-adult cognition rankings until a human exposure and cognition packet exists. Any self-experiment or vendor claim should route through [[unapproved-modafinil-analog-eugeroic-watchlist]], [[regulatory-and-sports-visibility-map]], and [[molecule-intake-triage-table]] first.
