# P021 (P21)

P021, often shortened to `P21` and called `PB021` in the Phanes Biotech development program, is a synthetic CNTF-derived neurotrophic peptide mimetic. It has a coherent rodent cognition and Alzheimer-model program, but no verified human efficacy, human pharmacokinetic, human dosing, or human safety evidence was found in the July 2026 refresh. It belongs in the de-ranked preclinical frontier beside [[dihexa]], not in the human-exposed monitoring tier occupied by agents such as [[cerebrolysin]].^[raw/articles/addf-2025-p021-cognitive-vitality.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-p021-intervention-search-2026-07-21.md]

July 2026 read: `promising but concentrated rodent evidence; very low human confidence`. The strongest positive results come from long-duration mouse and rat studies involving aging, 3xTg-AD pathology, early-development intervention, neurogenesis, synaptic markers, and memory tasks. A 2024 CDKL5 study supplies a useful counter-signal: cell effects did not translate into BDNF or neuroanatomical rescue in mice. A 2026 diffusion-MRI study adds partial normalization of selected mouse brain microstructure measures, not clinical validation.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39592934-p021-cdkl5-model.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41740658-p021-diffusion-mri.md]

## Identity and naming

P021 is based on the active CNTF region around residues 148-151 (`DGGL`) and adds a C-terminal adamantylated glycine, with terminal modifications intended to improve stability and lipophilicity. Papers and reviews alternate between calling the construct a tetra- or pentapeptide and render the modified residue inconsistently as `Ac-DGGLᴬG-NH2` or plain-text `Ac-DGGLAG-NH2`. The safe identity statement is therefore the modified CNTF-derived P021 structure, not an ordinary unmodified six-residue `DGGLAG` peptide.^[raw/articles/pubmed-28655344-p021-synaptic-compensation.md]^[raw/articles/addf-2025-p021-cognitive-vitality.md]

`P21` is ambiguous. It can also mean the CDKN1A/p21 cell-cycle and senescence protein, which already appears elsewhere in this wiki. A vendor vial labeled only `P21` does not establish that it matches the P021/PB021 research compound.

## Evidence snapshot

| Evidence layer | Model and exposure | Main signal | Important boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cognitive aging | Old Fischer rats; chronic oral treatment | Better learning/memory, neurogenesis, BDNF, synaptic markers, and hippocampal myo-inositol | Animal aging model; no human exposure bridge |
| Established AD-like pathology | Female 3xTg-AD mice; treatment started at 9-10 months for up to 12 months | Cognitive rescue, lower tau hyperphosphorylation, lower soluble amyloid-beta, improved neurogenesis/plasticity | Limited plaque effect; very long treatment; one transgenic model |
| Early prevention | Female 3xTg-AD mice; treatment from 3 months for up to 18 months | Better water-maze/object-location performance and synaptic/neurogenesis measures | Began before overt pathology; some marker groups were small |
| Developmental prevention | Female 3xTg-AD mice; birth through postnatal day 120 | Novel-object and target-quadrant improvements plus BDNF/pCREB and synaptic changes | Escape latency and target crossings were not improved; human timing is remote |
| Model counter-signal | CDKL5-knockout cells and mice | Cell rescue, but no in-vivo BDNF or neuroanatomical rescue and only limited behavior benefit | Shows that the proposed mechanism is not disorder-general |
| Latest imaging extension | 8-month-old 3xTg-AD mice; 2026 diffusion MRI | Partial normalization of selected white- and gray-matter diffusion measures | Imaging biomarker only; the July corrigendum added funding, not a result correction |

Primary sources: aged-rat cognition, chronic 3xTg-AD treatment, synaptic-compensation prevention, early postnatal treatment, CDKL5 testing, and 2026 MRI.^[raw/articles/pubmed-24702821-p021-cognitive-aging-rats.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-25046994-p021-3xtg-ad-chronic-oral.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-28655344-p021-synaptic-compensation.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-34057082-p021-postnatal-3xtg-ad.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39592934-p021-cdkl5-model.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41740658-p021-diffusion-mri.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41945082-p021-mri-corrigendum.md]

## Mechanism bridge

The proposed chain is inhibition of leukemia inhibitory factor signaling, increased CREB/BDNF activity, TrkB-PI3K-Akt signaling, inhibitory phosphorylation of GSK3-beta, and reduced tau hyperphosphorylation, alongside increased neurogenesis and synaptic plasticity. The pieces recur across P021 rodent studies, but they remain a preclinical mechanism package rather than a demonstrated human target-engagement pathway.^[raw/articles/pubmed-25046994-p021-3xtg-ad-chronic-oral.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-28655344-p021-synaptic-compensation.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39592934-p021-cdkl5-model.md]

The molecule was designed for improved stability and brain penetration. One aged-rat paper calls it BBB-permeable, while the 2025 ADDF review says direct measurement of P021 reaching mouse brain was not documented. The defensible synthesis is `brain-penetration intended and indirectly supported, direct exposure characterization incomplete`.^[raw/articles/pubmed-26401692-p021-aged-rat-csf-tau.md]^[raw/articles/addf-2025-p021-cognitive-vitality.md]

## Translation and development status

The NIA-hosted Phanes Biotech profile projects Phase 1 trials in 2026 and Phase 2 in 2028. An exact ClinicalTrials.gov intervention query for `P021` returned zero records on 2026-07-21, as did exact checks for two structure/name variants. Treat the company timeline as a development plan, not proof that human dosing has begun.^[raw/articles/nia-2025-phanes-biotech-pb021.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-p021-intervention-search-2026-07-21.md]

The evidence base also has substantial developer overlap. Khalid Iqbal is an author across the core program, holds P021-related patents, and co-founded the licensee; the 2026 MRI paper additionally disclosed a proposed myelin-use patent involving several authors. This does not invalidate the animal findings, but it raises the value of independent replication and preregistered human work.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39592934-p021-cdkl5-model.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41740658-p021-diffusion-mri.md]

## Safety and sourcing

- Human safety, interactions, contraindications, dose, and route are unknown.
- Long rodent exposures did not reproduce several full-length CNTF toxicities in the captured studies, and one rat paper reported no detectable immune reaction. That is not a human safety dossier.^[raw/articles/pubmed-26401692-p021-aged-rat-csf-tau.md]^[raw/articles/addf-2025-p021-cognitive-vitality.md]
- Animal feed and gavage exposures do not validate gray-market subcutaneous, intranasal, or oral protocols.
- `P21` label ambiguity, modified-residue notation, purity, mass confirmation, and chain of custody are first-order risks. Route any future claim through [[delivery-validation-checklist-for-cognition-interventions]] and [[peptide-safety-and-sourcing-risk-map]].
- No structured direct-use anecdote corpus is captured in the wiki as of 2026-07-21. That means anecdotal signal is `unknown`, not that human self-use or harm is absent.

## Read

P021 is worth monitoring as a CNTF/BDNF neuroregeneration hypothesis with repeated animal cognition signals and a live development story. It should remain de-ranked until a public human protocol, verified exposure data, safety results, and independent replication appear. New evidence should route through [[peptide-regulatory-live-watch-board]], [[peptide-neuroprotection]], and [[self-experimentation-stop-rules]] before it changes any cognition ranking.
