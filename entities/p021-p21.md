# P021 (P21)

P021, often shortened to `P21` and called `PB021` in the Phanes Biotech development program, is a synthetic CNTF-derived neurotrophic peptide mimetic. It has a coherent rodent cognition and Alzheimer-model program, but no verified human efficacy, human pharmacokinetic, human dosing, or human safety evidence was found in the July 2026 refresh. Gray-market self-reports do show attempted human use of products labeled `P21`, but none chemically verifies exposure to the modified P021 research compound. It belongs in the de-ranked preclinical frontier beside [[dihexa]], not in the human-evidence tier occupied by agents such as [[cerebrolysin]].^[raw/articles/addf-2025-p021-cognitive-vitality.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-p021-intervention-search-2026-07-21.md]^[raw/articles/reddit-2023-p21-mixed-effects-sourcing.md]

July 2026 read: `promising but concentrated rodent evidence; mixed weak-to-moderate anecdotal salience; very low human confidence`. The strongest positive results come from long-duration mouse and rat studies involving aging, 3xTg-AD pathology, early-development intervention, neurogenesis, synaptic markers, and memory tasks. A 2024 CDKL5 study supplies a useful counter-signal: cell effects did not translate into BDNF or neuroanatomical rescue in mice. A 2026 diffusion-MRI study adds partial normalization of selected mouse brain microstructure measures, not clinical validation.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39592934-p021-cdkl5-model.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41740658-p021-diffusion-mri.md]

## Identity and naming

P021 is based on the active CNTF region around residues 148-151 (`DGGL`) and adds a C-terminal adamantylated glycine, with terminal modifications intended to improve stability and lipophilicity. Papers and reviews alternate between calling the construct a tetra- or pentapeptide and render the modified residue inconsistently as `Ac-DGGLᴬG-NH2` or plain-text `Ac-DGGLAG-NH2`. The safe identity statement is therefore the modified CNTF-derived P021 structure, not an ordinary unmodified six-residue `DGGLAG` peptide.^[raw/articles/pubmed-28655344-p021-synaptic-compensation.md]^[raw/articles/addf-2025-p021-cognitive-vitality.md]

`P21` is ambiguous. It can also mean the CDKN1A/p21 cell-cycle and senescence protein, which already appears elsewhere in this wiki. A vendor vial labeled only `P21` does not establish that it matches the P021/PB021 research compound.

|---|---|---|---|

## Mechanism bridge

The proposed chain is inhibition of leukemia inhibitory factor signaling, increased CREB/BDNF activity, TrkB-PI3K-Akt signaling, inhibitory phosphorylation of GSK3-beta, and reduced tau hyperphosphorylation, alongside increased neurogenesis and synaptic plasticity. The pieces recur across P021 rodent studies, but they remain a preclinical mechanism package rather than a demonstrated human target-engagement pathway.^[raw/articles/pubmed-25046994-p021-3xtg-ad-chronic-oral.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-28655344-p021-synaptic-compensation.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39592934-p021-cdkl5-model.md]

The molecule was designed for improved stability and brain penetration. One aged-rat paper calls it BBB-permeable, while the 2025 ADDF review says direct measurement of P021 reaching mouse brain was not documented. The defensible synthesis is `brain-penetration intended and indirectly supported, direct exposure characterization incomplete`.^[raw/articles/pubmed-26401692-p021-aged-rat-csf-tau.md]^[raw/articles/addf-2025-p021-cognitive-vitality.md]

## Translation and development status

The NIA-hosted Phanes Biotech profile projects Phase 1 trials in 2026 and Phase 2 in 2028. An exact ClinicalTrials.gov intervention query for `P021` returned zero records on 2026-07-21, as did exact checks for two structure/name variants. Treat the company timeline as a development plan, not proof that human dosing has begun.^[raw/articles/nia-2025-phanes-biotech-pb021.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-p021-intervention-search-2026-07-21.md]

The evidence base also has substantial developer overlap. Khalid Iqbal is an author across the core program, holds P021-related patents, and co-founded the licensee; the 2026 MRI paper additionally disclosed a proposed myelin-use patent involving several authors. This does not invalidate the animal findings, but it raises the value of independent replication and preregistered human work.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39592934-p021-cdkl5-model.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41740658-p021-diffusion-mri.md]

## Human-use anecdote synthesis

The captured community layer is directionally mixed. Desired-effect reports repeatedly mention clarity, calm, focus, reading or recall fluency, mood lift, sleep, and short-term learning or work output. The counter-cluster includes no effect, unclear long-term effect, waning perceived benefit, sleepiness or accumulated fatigue, whole-body tingling after intranasal use, an intensely burning injection, and a stacked report of altered cannabis response and worse next-day headache.^[raw/articles/reddit-2024-p21-nasal-clarity-recall-calm.md]^[raw/articles/reddit-2021-p21-semax-fatigue-cannabis-response.md]^[raw/articles/reddit-2023-p21-mixed-effects-sourcing.md]^[raw/articles/longecity-2013-2017-p21-human-use-thread.md]

This is enough to change the anecdote field from `unknown` to `mixed weak-to-moderate salience`, but not enough to infer efficacy, safety, dose, route, or incidence. Reports span different years, vendors, routes, amounts, health states, and stacks; no captured user supplied independent identity testing or objective cognitive data. A dramatic P21-plus-orexin-A thread was deliberately down-weighted because it did not isolate P21 and a forum moderator identified the posting pattern as promotional.^[raw/articles/longecity-2017-p21-combination-promotional-thread.md]

## Safety and sourcing

- Human safety, interactions, contraindications, dose, and route are unknown.
- Long rodent exposures did not reproduce several full-length CNTF toxicities in the captured studies, and one rat paper reported no detectable immune reaction. That is not a human safety dossier.^[raw/articles/pubmed-26401692-p021-aged-rat-csf-tau.md]^[raw/articles/addf-2025-p021-cognitive-vitality.md]
- Animal feed and gavage exposures do not validate gray-market subcutaneous, intranasal, or oral protocols.
- `P21` label ambiguity, modified-residue notation, purity, mass confirmation, and chain of custody are first-order risks. Route any future claim through [[delivery-validation-checklist-for-cognition-interventions]] and [[peptide-safety-and-sourcing-risk-map]].
- A structured direct-use corpus is now captured, but it contains unverified products rather than confirmed P021 exposures. Tingling, burning, fatigue, sleepiness, and interaction-like reports are stop-rule hypotheses, not incidence estimates or proven adverse drug reactions.

## Read

P021 is worth monitoring as a CNTF/BDNF neuroregeneration hypothesis with repeated animal cognition signals, mixed gray-market human-use salience, and a live development story. The anecdote layer justifies safety and identity monitoring but does not upgrade the evidence rank. P021 should remain de-ranked until a public human protocol, verified exposure data, safety results, and independent replication appear. New evidence should route through [[peptide-regulatory-live-watch-board]], [[peptide-neuroprotection]], and [[self-experimentation-stop-rules]] before it changes any cognition ranking.
