# BPN14770

BPN14770 is a PDE4D allosteric inhibitor that has a positive human cognition signal in adult males with fragile X syndrome. It matters because it shows the PDE4 branch is not just a roflumilast story; there is a separate, PDE4D-selective human result with language and daily-function benefits plus a biomarker follow-up.

## Key Facts

- Mechanism: PDE4D allosteric inhibition with the goal of increasing cAMP-linked plasticity signaling.
- Human study: phase 2 randomized crossover in 30 adult males with fragile X syndrome.
- Dose: 25 mg twice daily for 24 weeks.
- Positive domains: oral reading recognition, picture vocabulary, crystallized cognition, caregiver-rated language, caregiver-rated daily functioning.
- Registry outcome table: NIH-TCB crystallized cognition composite change was 0.1 on BPN14770 versus -0.9 on placebo; VAS daily functioning was 9.4 versus 6.8; VAS language was 18.2 versus 15.4.
- Safety: the trial met its primary tolerability goal without a meaningful active-versus-placebo safety gap; total adverse events were 16.7% on BPN14770 versus 20.0% on placebo, with one SAE on active drug and none on placebo.
- Biomarker follow-up: auditory N1 ERP amplitude tracked with serum BPN14770 concentration in the active period.
- Related registry: NCT05163808 in male adolescents with fragile X syndrome is active, not recruiting, had 163 enrolled, has no results posted, and had already passed its estimated completion dates as of 2026-04-28; the 2025 update lowered the age range to 9 to <18 years and set NIH Toolbox Cognitive Battery crystallized composite score at week 13 as the primary endpoint.

## What It Suggests

- PDE4D-selective inhibition may be more clinically informative than broad PDE4 or PDE5 framing.
- The signal is phenotype-bound, not a generic healthy-adult enhancer claim.
- The biomarker follow-up supports a mechanistic interpretation instead of a one-off behavioral readout.
- The result is still small-sample and sponsor-linked, so it needs replication before it can be treated as settled.

## Limits

- The evidence base is still small.
- The positive result is in fragile X syndrome, not healthy adults.
- It is unclear whether the effect depends on PDE4D selectivity, fragile-X biology, or both.
- There is not yet evidence that BPN14770 generalizes to broad cognitive enhancement outside that phenotype.
