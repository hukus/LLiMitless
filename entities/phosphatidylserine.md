# Phosphatidylserine

Phosphatidylserine is a phospholipid-class supplement candidate with older-adult and cognitive-decline salience. It belongs in the product-composition lane: different phosphatidylserine preparations are not automatically equivalent.

Medicinal signal: `low-moderate`. ADDF Cognitive Vitality summarizes the branch as having some modest clinical-trial cognitive-function signals but emphasizes that more rigorous trials are needed, chemical composition has varied widely across studies, and long-term data are lacking.^[raw/articles/alzdiscovery-2023-phosphatidylserine-cognitive-vitality.md]
