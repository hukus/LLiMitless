# Phosphatidylserine

Phosphatidylserine (PS) is a membrane phospholipid supplement candidate with older-adult memory, MCI, stress/cortisol, and ADHD-inattention lore. It belongs in the product-composition lane: bovine cortex PS, soy/sunflower PS, PS-DHA, PS+phosphatidic acid, and PS-containing multinutrient formulas are not interchangeable.

## Current Read

Medicinal cognition signal: `low-moderate but phenotype- and product-bound`. The strongest historical signal is in older or cognitively impaired adults, not healthy young adults. A large 1993 multicenter trial tested 300 mg/day bovine cortex-derived PS for 6 months in 494 elderly patients with moderate-to-severe cognitive decline and reported behavioral and cognitive improvements versus placebo; that is meaningful but poorly transportable to modern soy/sunflower products.^[raw/articles/pubmed-8323999-phosphatidylserine-cognitive-decline-elderly.md]

Modern supplement-relevant signal: `low and subgroup-sensitive`. A 2010 double-blind trial randomized 78 Japanese adults aged 50-69 with memory complaints to soy-derived PS 100 mg/day, soy-derived PS 300 mg/day, or placebo for 6 months. Overall test scores improved in all groups, but lower-baseline subjects showed delayed-verbal-recall improvement in PS arms while placebo remained unchanged.^[raw/articles/pmc-2966935-soy-phosphatidylserine-memory-elderly.md]

Combination-formula signal: `low-moderate but not PS-isolated`. PS-DHA and PS/ALA formulas add useful older-adult memory clues, but they are not isolated PS. The PS-DHA program randomized 157 nondemented elderly participants with memory complaints to 300 mg/day PS-DHA or placebo for 15 weeks, then followed an open-label extension at 100 mg/day; the extension reported sustained-attention and recognition-memory movement in previously naive participants. A 2025 MCI trial used a formula containing PS, ALA, small Ginkgo flavonoid exposure, B vitamins, and folate for 12 months and reported arithmetic, similarity, and short-term-memory improvements, with serum ALA mediating part of the short-term-memory signal.^[raw/articles/pmc-3136416-ps-dha-safety-memory-complaints.md]^[raw/articles/karger-2014-ps-dha-open-label-extension.md]^[raw/articles/sciencedirect-2025-phosphatidylserine-ala-mci-rct.md]

Stress / focus signal: `state-bound`. A soy PS+phosphatidic-acid complex study supports HPA-axis stress-reactivity relevance in chronically stressed men, while a pediatric ADHD meta-analysis found a small inattention effect at 200-300 mg/day but not statistically significant overall ADHD or hyperactivity/impulsivity effects. Neither source establishes healthy-adult cognition enhancement.^[raw/articles/pmc-4237891-phosphatidylserine-phosphatidic-acid-stress.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-33539192-phosphatidylserine-adhd-meta-analysis.md]

Regulatory read: FDA lists phosphatidylserine qualified-health-claim letters for dementia / cognitive dysfunction, but the qualified-claim framework is explicitly below the significant-scientific-agreement standard and should not be read as efficacy approval.^[raw/articles/fda-2004-phosphatidylserine-qualified-health-claim.md]

## Signal Split

| Layer | Signal | Readout |
|---|---|---|
| Older-adult / cognitive-decline evidence | `low-moderate` | Largest historical signal is BC-PS 300 mg/day in impaired elderly, but formulation is obsolete for most supplement use |
| Soy-derived PS | `low/subgroup-sensitive` | 100-300 mg/day for 6 months in older memory-complaint adults; stronger in lower-baseline subgroup |
| PS-DHA / PS-ALA formulas | `low-moderate but not isolated` | Memory/attention/MCI signals exist but formulas contain omega-3 lipids and sometimes additional nutrients |
| Healthy rested adults | `low/absent direct proof` | No clean healthy-adult cognition packet captured in this pass |
| Stress / HPA axis | `low/state-bound` | PS+PA complex may matter for chronically stressed users; not a memory endpoint |
| Pediatric ADHD inattention | `low/phenotype-bound` | Meta-analysis suggests small inattention effect, but quality is low and population is children with ADHD |
| Anecdote | `moderate` salience | Focus, stress, sleep, stimulant-smoothing, ADHD, and irritability reports are visible but confounded |

## Practical Interpretation

Route phosphatidylserine through [[common-nootropic-gray-zone-molecule-watchlist]], [[gray-zone-nootropic-community-signal-ledger]], [[supplement-identity-evidence-map]], [[nutrient-supplement-cognition-signal-map]], and [[cognitive-improvement-molecule-signal-atlas]].

Practical ranking: keep PS below [[citicoline]], [[alpha-gpc]], [[caffeine]], [[modafinil]], [[methylphenidate]], and [[creatine]] for healthy adults. It is more plausible as an older-adult memory-complaint / stress-state / inattention-phenotype candidate than as a universal enhancer. Any future upgrade needs an isolated modern soy/sunflower PS trial in healthy adults, or at minimum a product-matched older-adult replication with delayed-memory endpoints.

Route PS anecdotes through [[safety-interaction-matrix-for-multi-modal-stacks]] before interpreting stress control, stimulant smoothing, sleep, ADHD focus, or memory reports as direct cognition. The first gates are bovine versus soy/sunflower source, PS-DHA or PS+PA formulas, omega-3/B-vitamin/Ginkgo co-use, cortisol/stress state, stimulant history, sleep timing, mood irritability, and anticoagulant/bleeding context.

## Interpretation Gates

- Do not transport bovine cortex PS results directly to soy or sunflower PS.
- Do not treat PS-DHA, PS+PA, or PS+ALA/B-vitamin/Ginkgo formulas as isolated phosphatidylserine.
- Separate memory complaints, MCI, pediatric ADHD, chronic stress, and healthy rested adults.
- Track dose and duration: most cognition-oriented studies cluster around 100-300 mg/day and weeks-to-months exposure, while stress/exercise lore can cite different PAS-style products.
- Watch sleep timing, stimulant/caffeine co-use, cortisol-state assumptions, mood/irritability, GI tolerance, soy allergy/product source, anticoagulant/bleeding context if stacked with omega-3s, and product testing.
