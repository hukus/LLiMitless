# Humanin

Humanin is the best-supported cognition-relevant member of the mitochondrial-derived peptide family, but the current evidence still stops short of a human intervention trial. It sits between endogenous aging biology, APOE4-linked resilience, and preclinical neuroprotection rather than a validated enhancement drug. Among the mitochondrial peptides, it is the clearest `brain-first biological frontier`, even though it is not yet a formal clinical-development program for cognition.^[raw/articles/pmc-7343442-humanin-lifespan-healthspan.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-30242290-humanin-age-related-cognitive-decline.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-38520065-humanin-p3s-apoe4-brain-pathology.md]

Humanin was originally described as a mitochondrial-derived neuroprotective peptide and later expanded into a broader lifespan and healthspan signal. In the modern MDP framing, it is one of the family members most often linked to age, stress resistance, and cognition, with levels that generally decline with age and appear higher in longevity-enriched humans.^[raw/articles/pmc-7343442-humanin-lifespan-healthspan.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-35499074-mdp-aging-healthspan-review.md]

The strongest cognition-linked human source is the 2018 study showing that humanin improved cognition in aged mice and that a mitochondrial SNP in the humanin-coding region was associated with accelerated cognitive aging in a large older-adult cohort. The 2024 P3S paper sharpened the story further by connecting a humanin variant to APOE4 centenarians, amyloid-beta clearance, and reduced brain pathology in an APOE4 mouse model.^[raw/articles/pubmed-30242290-humanin-age-related-cognitive-decline.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-38520065-humanin-p3s-apoe4-brain-pathology.md]

The 2024 TBI paper adds another preclinical cognition-positive model: humanin ameliorated TBI-related cognitive impairment through mitochondrial and inflammatory mechanisms. That keeps the peptide in the recovery/neuroprotection lane rather than the healthy-adult enhancement lane.^[raw/articles/pubmed-37926362-humanin-tbi-related-cognitive-impairment.md]

June 2026 attribution gate: Humanin should stay an endogenous-resilience / preclinical-treatment hypothesis, not a protocol-ready nootropic. Future reports need source/COA, exact analog or variant identity, route/formulation, dose timing, stack order, washout, APOE4/genotype or injury/aging phenotype, biomarker-versus-administered exposure split, adverse events, and objective cognition endpoints before any ranking upgrade or comparison with [[semax]], [[selank]], [[cerebrolysin]], recovery-bound [[bpc-157]], [[bromantane]], or [[mots-c]].

## Frontier Read

Humanin now deserves to stay near the top of the peptide watchlist for one reason: the signal is repeated across multiple evidence families rather than resting on a single hype source. It has human cognitive-aging association data, a genotype/resilience story in APOE4-enriched settings, and preclinical rescue data in both aging and injury models. That does not establish a treatment effect. It does make Humanin one of the more defensible biological peptide frontiers in the wiki.

I did not surface a visible ClinicalTrials.gov Humanin intervention program in the current pass; that is a search result, not a proof of absence. For now, Humanin remains stronger as an endogenous resilience signal than as an upcoming therapeutic program.
