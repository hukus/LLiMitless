# Humanin

Humanin is the best-supported cognition-relevant member of the mitochondrial-derived peptide family, but the current evidence still stops short of a human intervention trial. It sits between endogenous aging biology, APOE4-linked resilience, and preclinical neuroprotection rather than a validated enhancement drug.^[raw/articles/pmc-7343442-humanin-lifespan-healthspan.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-30242290-humanin-age-related-cognitive-decline.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-38520065-humanin-p3s-apoe4-brain-pathology.md]

Humanin was originally described as a mitochondrial-derived neuroprotective peptide and later expanded into a broader lifespan and healthspan signal. In the modern MDP framing, it is one of the family members most often linked to age, stress resistance, and cognition, with levels that generally decline with age and appear higher in longevity-enriched humans.^[raw/articles/pmc-7343442-humanin-lifespan-healthspan.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-35499074-mdp-aging-healthspan-review.md]

The strongest cognition-linked human source is the 2018 study showing that humanin improved cognition in aged mice and that a mitochondrial SNP in the humanin-coding region was associated with accelerated cognitive aging in a large older-adult cohort. The 2024 P3S paper sharpened the story further by connecting a humanin variant to APOE4 centenarians, amyloid-beta clearance, and reduced brain pathology in an APOE4 mouse model.^[raw/articles/pubmed-30242290-humanin-age-related-cognitive-decline.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-38520065-humanin-p3s-apoe4-brain-pathology.md]

The 2024 TBI paper adds another preclinical cognition-positive model: humanin ameliorated TBI-related cognitive impairment through mitochondrial and inflammatory mechanisms. That keeps the peptide in the recovery/neuroprotection lane rather than the healthy-adult enhancement lane.^[raw/articles/pubmed-37926362-humanin-tbi-related-cognitive-impairment.md]
