# Vinpocetine

Vinpocetine is a synthetic compound marketed in some supplement contexts for memory, focus, energy, and brain function. In this wiki it belongs in the risk-first gray-zone lane, not as a positive nootropic.

Medicinal signal: `risk-first/low`. FDA states that it has not evaluated vinpocetine's efficacy for the marketed brain-function uses and warns pregnant women and women who could become pregnant not to take it because data reviewed by FDA associated vinpocetine with adverse reproductive effects. A PDE-inhibitor review mentions vinpocetine as a historical comparator but does not turn the PDE branch into a positive cognition class.^[raw/articles/fda-2019-vinpocetine-dietary-supplements.md]^[raw/articles/pmc-7369141-phosphodiesterase-inhibitors-ad-systematic-review.md]
