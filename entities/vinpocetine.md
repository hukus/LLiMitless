# Vinpocetine

Vinpocetine is a synthetic compound marketed in some supplement contexts for memory, focus, energy, and brain function. In this wiki it belongs in the risk-first gray-zone lane, not as a positive nootropic.

Medicinal signal: `risk-first/low`. FDA states that it has not evaluated vinpocetine's efficacy for the marketed brain-function uses and warns pregnant women and women who could become pregnant not to take it because data reviewed by FDA associated vinpocetine with adverse reproductive effects. A small 2021 randomized crossover study found no significant cognitive benefits in healthy volunteers or patients with focal epilepsy, while the older Cochrane dementia/cognitive-impairment trail is historical, heterogeneous, and disease-context only. A PDE-inhibitor review mentions vinpocetine as a historical comparator but does not turn the PDE branch into a positive cognition class.^[raw/articles/fda-2019-vinpocetine-dietary-supplements.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-33957389-vinpocetine-cognition-epilepsy-healthy-volunteers.md]^[raw/articles/cochrane-2026-vinpocetine-cognitive-impairment-dementia.md]^[raw/articles/pmc-7369141-phosphodiesterase-inhibitors-ad-systematic-review.md]

Product-quality signal: `strong caution`. The USDA/ARS supplement-label study found that vinpocetine-labeled products did not reliably contain the ingredient or match a predictable daily exposure: 6 of 23 sampled products had no detectable vinpocetine, while 17 contained 0.3-32 mg per recommended daily serving.^[raw/articles/ars-2015-vinpocetine-supplement-label-accuracy.md]
