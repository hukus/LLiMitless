# Resveratrol

Resveratrol is a stilbene polyphenol associated with grapes, berries, peanuts, red wine, SIRT1/AMPK signaling, vascular biology, metabolic aging, and longevity claims. In this wiki it is a supplement molecule, not a proxy for [[grape-food-products]], standardized [[grape-extract]], grape seed, pterostilbene, wine, or generic polyphenol intake.

Medicinal cognition signal: `low/contested and phenotype-bound`. The strongest positive human cognition anchor is RESHAW: postmenopausal women aged 45-85 took 75 mg trans-resveratrol twice daily, and the 12-month interim plus 24-month crossover analyses reported improved overall cognitive performance alongside cerebrovascular responsiveness and metabolic markers. This is plausibly a vascular/metabolic/postmenopausal signal, not proof of broad healthy-adult nootropic benefit.^[raw/articles/pmc-7146200-resveratrol-postmenopausal-women-12mo-cognition.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-32900519-resveratrol-postmenopausal-women-24mo-cognition.md]

The main positive counterweight outside RESHAW is narrow: a 90-day pilot in 32 sedentary overweight older adults found psychomotor-speed improvement at 1000 mg/day, but not broad improvement across verbal learning, working memory, flanker, task switching, or fluency outcomes. The 2023 older-adult systematic review/meta-analysis included six studies and found no significant pooled effects on delayed memory, immediate memory, working memory, or processing speed. This makes the current best read "interesting vascular/metabolic subgroup candidate" rather than "effective cognition enhancer."^[raw/articles/pmc-6065512-resveratrol-cognition-older-adults-pilot.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-37522434-resveratrol-cognition-meta-analysis.md]

[[pterostilbene]] now has its own low-confidence entity as a stilbene-adjacent hypothesis. It is a dimethylated resveratrol analogue with higher reported bioavailability and preclinical AD/cognition evidence, but the current capture still does not establish isolated human cognition efficacy. Do not treat pterostilbene, resveratrol, wine, grape foods, and grape extracts as interchangeable.^[raw/articles/pubmed-23808710-pterostilbene-biomedical-applications-review.md]

Anecdote signal: `strong longevity salience, weak cognition-specific evidence`. Resveratrol is widely discussed in longevity and biohacking spaces, and the broad Troof nootropics dataset supports community visibility. The direct Reddit capture found in this pass is study-sharing about RESHAW, with comments focusing on the 75 mg twice-daily dose, older-women phenotype, cost, and lifestyle substitutes. That is not a repeated firsthand cognition corpus, so it stays low-confidence anecdote.^[raw/articles/troof-nootropics-ratings-analysis.md]^[raw/articles/reddit-2023-resveratrol-24mo-nootropics-thread.md]

Safety signal: `low-moderate supplement caution`. LiverTox rates resveratrol as an unlikely cause of clinically apparent liver injury, but notes rare serum-enzyme elevations, especially at high doses, plus minor gastrointestinal upset, nausea, headache, fatigue, poor/variable oral bioavailability, undefined active commercial forms, and possible interactions with estrogens and anticoagulants. Product purity and dose form matter more than food-level red-grape exposure.^[raw/articles/ncbi-bookshelf-2024-resveratrol-livertox.md]

## Practical Interpretation

Resveratrol clears a low-confidence entity threshold because it has multiple human cognition trial anchors and a negative meta-analytic counterweight. Its most defensible use case is not general enhancement; it is a vascular/metabolic aging hypothesis, especially postmenopausal or older-adult phenotypes where cerebral perfusion and insulin sensitivity are relevant.

Do not merge resveratrol with [[grape-food-products]], [[grape-extract]], [[blueberry-anthocyanins]], wine, grape seed, or [[pterostilbene]]. A confidence upgrade would require a newer adequately powered trial with prespecified cognitive endpoints, clear trans-resveratrol dose/formulation, adherence and metabolite verification, vascular/metabolic moderators, and replication outside the RESHAW/postmenopausal lane.
