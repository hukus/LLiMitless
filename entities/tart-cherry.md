# Tart Cherry

Tart cherry is the wiki's product-specific branch for Prunus cerasus products, especially Montmorency tart-cherry juice and concentrate. It should not be merged with sweet cherry, generic cherry foods, anthocyanin capsules, grape/raisin products, sleep aids, or gout/recovery products. The active unit needs product form, cultivar, dose, polyphenol / anthocyanin markers, sugar load, timing, and endpoint domain.

Medicinal cognition signal: `low/product-specific and sleep-recovery-confounded`. The direct human cognition layer is stronger than a mere watchlist stub now: a small 12-week older-adult RCT found higher contentment-with-memory, faster movement time, fewer paired-associates-learning errors, within-group visual sustained-attention improvement, and a trend in spatial working memory after two cups/day Montmorency juice. A 3-month middle-aged-adult trial using 30 ml concentrate twice daily found improved digit-vigilance accuracy, fewer false alarms, higher alertness, and lower mental fatigue, but no cerebral-blood-flow, sleep, or health-measure signal. A 2026 plant-active-substance NMA ranks tart cherry highly for learning/memory and executive function, but that ranking is discovery evidence rather than proof of broad transportability.^[raw/articles/rsc-2019-montmorency-tart-cherry-cognition-rct.md]^[raw/articles/cambridge-2022-montmorency-tart-cherry-middle-aged-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pmc-12864429-plant-active-substances-healthy-older-adults-nma.md]

The sleep/recovery layer is relevant but should not be counted as direct cognition. A 2023 sleep systematic review found objective sleep-efficiency and total-sleep-time signals, while emphasizing small short-term studies, subjective/objective mismatch, and unclear mechanism. A 2024 crossover trial of 500 mg Montmorency tart-cherry capsules in adults with sleep problems and overweight/obesity found no significant sleep or inflammation effects. That product/formulation null is important: juice, concentrate, powder capsules, gummies, and whole fruit are not interchangeable.^[raw/articles/springer-2023-tart-cherry-sleep-systematic-review-meta-analysis.md]^[raw/articles/mdpi-2024-montmorency-tart-cherry-powder-sleep-inflammation-null-rct.md]

Anecdote signal: `weak-moderate sleep/recovery salience, weak cognition efficacy`. Reddit reports are mostly about sleep onset, sleep quality, less grogginess, muscle relaxation, or insomnia non-response. The mixed threads include positive reports, null reports, short-lived effects, sugar concerns, GERD/heartburn concerns, capsule non-response, and placebo/melatonin-responder speculation. There is no clean community cognition signal; the anecdote layer mainly explains why users may perceive next-day cognition through better or worse sleep.^[raw/articles/reddit-2022-tart-cherry-juice-natural-sleep-aid-thread.md]^[raw/articles/reddit-2023-tart-cherry-juice-sleep-thread.md]

Safety and product-quality signal: `low-moderate caution`. The older-adult cognition RCT reported no adverse events, but its 480 ml/day drink contained about 181 kcal and 43 g carbohydrate, so juice sugar load is a real product variable. Trial exclusions and selected samples limit generalization. Product-form, diabetes/metabolic context, GERD/tartness, evening sugar timing, sleep-medication co-use, gout/uric-acid use, and capsule standardization should be tracked before treating tart cherry as a cognitive tool.^[raw/articles/rsc-2019-montmorency-tart-cherry-cognition-rct.md]^[raw/articles/mdpi-2024-montmorency-tart-cherry-powder-sleep-inflammation-null-rct.md]^[raw/articles/reddit-2023-tart-cherry-juice-sleep-thread.md]

## Signal Split

| Layer | Signal | Readout |
|---|---|---|
| Older-adult Montmorency juice cognition RCT | `low/direct` | Two cups/day for 12 weeks improved selected subjective memory, movement time, paired-associates learning, and within-group sustained attention |
| Middle-aged Montmorency concentrate cognition RCT | `low/direct` | 30 ml twice daily for 3 months improved digit-vigilance accuracy, false alarms, alertness, and mental fatigue |
| Plant-active-substance NMA | `supportive/discovery` | Tart cherry ranked highly for learning/memory and executive function, but rankings are probabilistic and source-count sensitive |
| Sleep systematic review / meta-analysis | `supportive/indirect` | Objective sleep efficiency and total sleep time favored tart cherry; subjective effects and long-term response were less clear |
| 2024 powder capsule trial | `negative/product-form` | 500 mg capsules for 14 days did not improve sleep or inflammation in adults with sleep issues and overweight/obesity |
| Anecdote | `weak-moderate salience, weak efficacy` | Sleep and recovery reports are visible and mixed; direct cognitive enhancement reports are sparse |
| Safety / quality | `low-moderate caution` | Juice sugar load, tartness/GERD, metabolic context, dose timing, and product-form transportability are gating variables |

## Practical Interpretation

Tart cherry now clears a low-confidence standalone threshold because there are two direct Montmorency cognition RCTs plus network-meta discovery support. It still belongs below cleaner comparators in [[molecule-intake-triage-table]]. The defensible claim is not "cherry improves cognition" in general; it is that Montmorency juice or concentrate has a small product-specific signal for sustained attention, fatigue, and selected older-adult memory tasks, with sleep/recovery and sugar/timing as major confounds.

Future updates must keep these exposures separate: Montmorency juice, Montmorency concentrate, powder capsules, gummies, whole tart cherries, sweet cherries, anthocyanin extracts, and grape/raisin products. Any upgrade requires replicated product-normalized trials, clear polyphenol/anthocyanin marker reporting, sleep and glucose/timing controls, and objective cognition endpoints after washout.
