# Kratom / Mitragynine / 7-OH

## Bottom Line

Kratom, mitragynine, and concentrated 7-OH products are risk-first, not nootropic leads. Medicinal signal for cognition is `risk-first`: FDA safety/status warnings and animal cognitive-impairment/abuse-potential data dominate. Anecdotal signal is `moderate` for mood, pain, energy, focus, and withdrawal contexts, but those same contexts make cognition attribution weak.

## What It Is

Kratom is Mitragyna speciosa leaf material. Mitragynine is a major kratom alkaloid, while 7-hydroxymitragynine / 7-OH is a more potent opioid-like alkaloid that can appear at enhanced levels in modern products. The wiki keeps leaf kratom, mitragynine, and concentrated or added 7-OH product identities separate.

## Medicinal Research Signal

The FDA warning packet is the main current status anchor. The 2024 OPMS Black Liquid Kratom warning and the 2025 FDA warning letters around 7-OH products show why this branch cannot be evaluated like an ordinary supplement. The FDA described added or enhanced 7-OH products as unproven for safety or effectiveness, not lawful in dietary supplements, not lawful as food additives, and not approved drug ingredients.^[raw/articles/fda-2024-opms-black-liquid-kratom-warning.md]^[raw/articles/fda-2025-7oh-kratom-warning-letters.md]

The captured mitragynine animal paper reported abuse-potential and adverse learning/memory effects, which is the opposite direction from a cognition-enhancement claim.^[raw/articles/pubmed-25262913-mitragynine-cognitive-impairment-abuse-potential.md]

## Anecdote Signal

The captured kratom thread gives `moderate` salience for subjective memory, focus, creativity, mood, pain, and energy claims. This is not clean cognition evidence because opioid-like mood/analgesia, dependence, withdrawal management, and comedication confounding are central.^[raw/articles/reddit-2024-kratom-memory-focus-thread.md]

June 2026 nootropic routing: kratom / mitragynine / 7-OH reports should not be compared with high-priority nootropic or peptide monitoring nodes. Require exact product identity, leaf versus extract versus added/enhanced 7-OH status, dose, frequency, duration, dependence or withdrawal context, pain/mood baseline, opioid/sedative/stimulant/antidepressant co-use, liver or seizure history, adverse events, washout/dechallenge, and objective cognition endpoints before treating any focus or creativity claim as molecule-specific. Missing product or dependence-state fields keep the report in the `risk-first anecdote` bucket.
