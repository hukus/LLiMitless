# Testosterone / TRT

## Bottom Line

Testosterone and TRT are phenotype-sensitive endocrine-state comparators, not general cognitive enhancers. Medicinal signal is `low-moderate` only for selected low-testosterone / obese / frail / lifestyle-intervention contexts and `low` for broad cognition: a 3-year older-men TRT trial was cognition-null, a smaller lifestyle-plus-testosterone analysis was positive, and an acute healthy-men study shifted specific learning parameters rather than broad cognition. Anecdotal cognition signal is `weak` in the current captured corpus because hormone-state lore is not yet represented by stable cognition-specific raw notes.

## What It Is

Testosterone is an androgenic hormone; TRT is the replacement-treatment frame for clinically selected low-testosterone states. In this wiki it belongs with [[neurosteroid-and-endocrine-state-cognition-2026-scan]], [[cholinergic-state-restoration-and-cognition]], and [[cognitive-fidelity-split]] as a state and phenotype variable that can change motivation, libido, energy, sleep, mood, and learning style without proving a universal nootropic effect.

## Medicinal Research Signal

The cleanest negative anchor is TEAAM: 308 older men with low or low-normal testosterone were randomized to daily testosterone gel or placebo for 3 years. Across visuospatial ability, verbal fluency, verbal memory, manual dexterity, and attention/executive function, testosterone did not improve major cognitive domains, and cognitive change did not track testosterone or estradiol change.^[raw/articles/pubmed-27377542-testosterone-teaam-cognition.md]

The main positive human signal is narrower. In the LITROS secondary analysis, 83 older obese hypogonadal men with frailty received lifestyle intervention plus testosterone or placebo for 6 months. Global cognition improved more in the testosterone arm, with additional attention/information-processing and memory gains. This is useful but co-intervention-bound: fitness, strength, weight, and endocrine correction are entangled.^[raw/articles/pubmed-34375393-testosterone-lifestyle-hypogonadism-cognition.md]

The 2025 healthy-men study adds a different kind of signal. A single testosterone dose shifted harm-avoidance learning and computational learning rates about 3 hours after dosing, especially around negative-outcome learning in the self condition and prosocial learning from negative outcomes. That is a decision-learning and social-learning modulation signal, not evidence for better memory, attention, or executive performance overall.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40818499-testosterone-harm-avoidance-learning.md]

## Anecdote Signal

TRT and testosterone communities are high-salience for motivation, drive, libido, gym performance, mood, and "brain fog" claims, but the current wiki corpus does not have a captured, stable cognition-specific anecdote packet with dose, lab values, indication, denominator, and adverse counterreports. Anecdotal cognition signal is therefore `weak` and should not upgrade the medicinal ranking.

## Safety And Status

The practical gate is medical indication and monitoring. Hematocrit, prostate, cardiovascular, fertility, sleep-apnea, mood/irritability, and endocrine-axis suppression concerns matter when interpreting cognitive claims. Acute learning effects do not remove ordinary TRT risk management.

## Signal Judgment

Medicinal signal: `low-moderate` for selected low-testosterone / obese / frail clinical-state correction with lifestyle co-intervention; `low` for broad cognitive enhancement.

Anecdotal signal: `weak` in the current captured corpus.

Practical route: phenotype-gated endocrine-state comparator; require lab-defined indication, objective cognition endpoint, and separation from mood/libido/energy effects.
