# Brasofensine

Brasofensine / BMS-204756 is a dopamine-transporter-antagonist development compound that belongs in the historical comparator layer around [[tesofensine]], [[nomifensine]], [[mazindol]], and dopamine-reuptake nootropic claims.

## Current Read

Medicinal cognition signal: `absent / low`. The captured human anchor is a tiny Parkinson disease crossover pharmacology study, not a cognition-enhancement trial.

In eight men with moderate Parkinson disease receiving levodopa/carbidopa, single oral doses of 0.5, 1, 2, and 4 mg brasofensine increased exposure more than proportionally with dose but did not change UPDRS motor-subscale performance. The study reported tolerability up to 4 mg, with mild adverse events including headache, insomnia, phlebitis, dizziness, ecchymosis, and vomiting.^[raw/articles/pubmed-11847938-brasofensine-parkinsons-levodopa.md]
