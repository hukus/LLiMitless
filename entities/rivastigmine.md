# Rivastigmine

## Bottom Line

Rivastigmine is a prescription acetylcholinesterase-inhibitor comparator with disease-context evidence in Alzheimer's-type dementia and Parkinson's disease dementia, but no healthy-adult enhancement signal in the current wiki corpus. A small HIV-associated cognitive-impairment pilot showed nonsignificant trends, not a clear cognitive win. It belongs in the [[prescription-cholinesterase-inhibitor-cognition-watchlist]] as a clinical-state cholinergic drug, not a brain-fog nootropic.

## What It Is

Rivastigmine is available as oral and transdermal formulations and is labeled for dementia contexts. In this wiki it sits beside [[donepezil]], [[galantamine]], [[huperzine-a]], and [[cholinergic-state-restoration-and-cognition]].

## Medicinal Research Signal

The AHRQ / NCBI review found small cognition improvements for standard-dose rivastigmine in mild-to-moderate clinical Alzheimer's-type dementia, with insufficient attention evidence and increased withdrawals due to adverse events. Lower-dose rivastigmine showed no cognition difference. This is disease-treatment evidence, not healthy-adult enhancement.^[raw/articles/ncbi-bookshelf-2020-dementia-drugs-donepezil-rivastigmine.md]

The HIV-associated cognitive-impairment pilot tested transdermal rivastigmine 9.5 mg daily against lithium and no new medication. Global cognition improved in all groups with no significant between-arm difference. Information-processing-speed and executive-function trends favored rivastigmine, but the authors concluded that significant cognitive benefit was not shown.^[raw/articles/pubmed-28854283-rivastigmine-hiv-cognitive-impairment-pilot.md]

## Anecdote Signal

No direct rivastigmine cognition anecdote raw note is indexed. Treat anecdote as `absent-to-weak`; do not borrow donepezil, galantamine, huperzine, or generic cholinergic-stack anecdotes.

## Safety And Status

DailyMed identifies rivastigmine transdermal system as a prescription acetylcholinesterase inhibitor for Alzheimer's-type and Parkinson's disease dementia. The patch label is unusually practical for self-experimentation risk because multiple-patch medication errors have caused hospitalization and rare deaths. Other key gates include nausea, vomiting, diarrhea, anorexia / reduced appetite, weight loss, dehydration, allergic contact dermatitis / application-site reactions, and interactions with beta-blockers, metoclopramide, cholinomimetics, and anticholinergic drugs.^[raw/articles/dailymed-2025-rivastigmine-patch-label.md]

## Signal Judgment

Medicinal signal: `low-moderate` in labeled dementia contexts; `low` for HIV-associated cognitive impairment; `absent` for healthy-adult enhancement in the current corpus.

Anecdotal signal: `absent-to-weak`.

Practical route: disease-context prescription comparator with patch-specific safety gates.
