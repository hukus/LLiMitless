# TAK-653

TAK-653 is a modern AMPAR positive allosteric modulator / low-impact AMPAkine-adjacent drug candidate. It is relevant because it has a clean healthy-volunteer CNS pharmacodynamic study and unusually visible nootropic-community anecdotes, while still lacking a cognition-efficacy trial.

Medicinal cognition signal: `target-engagement only`. In a 24-participant randomized healthy-volunteer crossover study, TAK-653 0.5 mg and 6 mg moved TMS / NeuroCart measures and produced a psychostimulant-like pharmacodynamic profile, including some saccadic, adaptive-tracking, and Stroop-related effects. The study was designed around CNS pharmacodynamic activity, not a validated memory or cognitive-enhancement endpoint.^[raw/articles/pubmed-36153330-tak653-tms-cns-effects.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct03792672-tak653-cns-pd-healthy-volunteers.md]

Development-status signal: `uncertain`. A planned treatment-resistant-depression phase 2 registry record listed TAK-653 6 mg daily but enrolled 0 participants, so it does not add efficacy evidence.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct03312894-tak653-trd-zero-enrollment.md] In this wiki TAK-653 therefore stays below CX717 for disease-state evidence despite stronger gray-market discussion.

Safety and anecdote signal: `mixed and stack-sensitive`. The formal healthy-volunteer study did not show a major subjective-drug-effect burden, but the captured user corpus reports insomnia, anxiety/dread, head pressure, possible fogginess, dose sensitivity, and stimulant-stack concerns alongside focus, introspection, speech/articulation, creativity, mood, and memory claims.^[raw/articles/reddit-2025-tak653-experiences-mixed.md]^[raw/articles/reddit-2023-tak653-experience-introspection-memory.md]^[raw/articles/reddit-2026-tak653-best-nootropic-stack.md] Anecdotes are useful for hazard and phenotype hypotheses but do not upgrade the medicinal signal.

June 2026 AMPAR-PAM attribution gate: TAK-653 anecdotes should not update nootropic rankings unless they capture product identity/source, dose, timing, route, acute versus repeated use, baseline sleep and anxiety state, psychiatric or ADHD/depression phenotype, caffeine/nicotine/stimulant/wake-promoter/[[bromantane]]/[[semax]]/[[selank]] co-use, other plasticity-stack agents such as [[acd856]], [[tropisetron]], [[nsi-189]], racetams, or high-impact AMPAkines, washout and dechallenge/rechallenge, adverse events including insomnia, head pressure, anxiety, dysphoria, tinnitus, or fogginess, and objective task endpoints rather than only productivity lore.
