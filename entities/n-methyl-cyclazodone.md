# N-Methyl-Cyclazodone

N-methyl-cyclazodone (NMC; N-methylcyclazodone) is a gray-market pemoline-adjacent stimulant discussed as a longer or cleaner version of [[cyclazodone]]. The captured evidence makes it a risk-first entity: it has community focus claims, but the strongest formal anchor is a laboratory-confirmed toxicity case.

## Current Read

Medicinal cognition signal: `absent/risk-first`. PubChem provides an identity anchor, but no controlled human cognition trial was captured. Claims around focus, motivation, and ADHD-like benefit come from forum self-experimentation rather than clinical evidence.^[raw/articles/pubchem-2026-cyclazodone-nmethylcyclazodone-identity.md]^[raw/articles/reddit-2020-2026-cyclazodone-nmc-threads.md]

Clinical toxicity signal: `direct case-report anchor`. A 2022 Clinical Toxicology / CFSRE abstract reports a 38-year-old man who self-treated attention deficit disorder for five days with online-purchased N-methyl-cyclazodone, consumed about 5 g total, and presented with choreiform movements, autonomic activation, widened QRS, prolonged QTc, elevated CK, and mildly elevated ALT. LC-HRMS confirmed cyclazodone as an expected metabolite and did not identify pemoline or 4-methylaminorex.^[raw/articles/clinical-toxicology-2022-n-methyl-cyclazodone-toxicity.md]

Liver/DILI signal: `concerning but not isolated`. The ACG 2022 case included N-methylcyclazodone for ADHD in a multi-agent online-drug exposure with estradiol, cyproterone acetate, laxative/water intake, milk thistle, and delta-8 THC. The patient developed biopsy-consistent DILI and acute liver failure requiring transplantation. This is not clean NMC-only causality, but it is enough to keep liver-risk language prominent.^[raw/articles/acg-2022-n-methylcyclazodone-online-hormones-dili.md]

Anecdote signal: `moderate, polarized`. Forum users describe NMC as cleaner, longer-lasting, more functional, or stronger than cyclazodone, while others describe body-load, liver/kidney-area discomfort, customs/source anxiety, and disagreement over whether pemoline-like hepatotoxicity transfers to NMC.^[raw/articles/reddit-2020-2026-cyclazodone-nmc-threads.md]

## Interpretation Gates

- Treat NMC as a toxicity-tracked research chemical, not a supplement.
- Do not infer safety from normal self-reported liver labs in forum comments; pemoline's liver-risk history and NMC case reports make idiosyncratic and high-dose risk central.
- Watch ECG/QRS/QTc, autonomic activation, movement disorder, CK/rhabdomyolysis, liver markers, psychiatric medications, serotonergic agents, and product identity.
- For cognition claims, benchmark against [[modafinil]], [[methylphenidate]], [[solriamfetol]], and [[bupropion]] before calling NMC unusually useful.
