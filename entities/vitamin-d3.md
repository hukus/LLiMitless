# Vitamin D3

Vitamin D3 is cholecalciferol, a vitamin D form used in foods, supplements, and trials. In this wiki it is tracked as a nutrient-status and deficiency-correction molecule, not a general cognitive enhancer. It should not be collapsed with vitamin D2, calcifediol, calcitriol, UV/sunlight exposure, calcium co-supplementation, K2/magnesium stacks, multivitamins, or folic-acid/DHA nutrient combinations.

Medicinal cognition signal: `low/contested and status-gated`. The 2026 nutrient NMA reports a vitamin D3 visuospatial signal, and a 2024 RCT meta-analysis across 24 trials found a small global-cognition effect that was larger in vulnerable or vitamin-D-deficient populations. A 2020 MCI RCT using 800 IU/day for 12 months reported broad cognitive-test improvements and oxidative-stress/telomere biomarker changes. Those sources justify standalone coverage, but only as a status-sensitive nutrient branch.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41764841-nutritional-supplements-cognition-nma.md]^[raw/articles/springer-2024-vitamin-d-cognition-rct-meta.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-33164936-vitamin-d-mci-12mo-rct-positive.md]

The counterweights keep the signal low. VitaMIND, a 24-month 620-person RCT in adults with mild to moderate vitamin D deficiency and early cognitive impairment, found no significant benefit on executive function, other cognition, function, or well-being. VITAL's vitamin D3 ancillary cognition studies found no global cognitive-decline benefit from 2,000 IU/day in generally healthy older adults, and the SYNERGIC MCI trial found exercise/cognitive training effects but no added vitamin D effect. A healthy-adult RCT review is hypothesis-supportive, but it is not strong enough to override the larger nulls.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40480279-vitamind-deficiency-rct-cognition-null.md]^[raw/articles/pmc-8636504-vital-vitamin-d-cognitive-decline-null.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-36524160-vitamin-d-healthy-rct-review.md]

Anecdote signal: `weak-moderate and deficiency-skewed`. Community reports often describe brain-fog, mood, fatigue, or "feeling like myself again" improvement after vitamin D, usually with low sun exposure or bloodwork-confirmed deficiency. The adverse side is also visible: insomnia, early waking, palpitations, activation, fatigue, and electrolyte/cofactor speculation. Anecdote should be used to flag baseline 25(OH)D testing, dose/timing, calcium/magnesium/K2 context, and adverse monitoring, not to upgrade cognition efficacy.^[raw/articles/reddit-2024-vitamin-d-brain-fog-deficiency-thread.md]^[raw/articles/reddit-2026-vitamin-d-insomnia-adverse-thread.md]

Safety and status signal: `moderate lab-guided caution`. NIH ODS treats serum 25(OH)D as the main status marker, with levels below 30 nmol/L linked to deficiency and levels at or above 50 nmol/L adequate for most people, while levels above 125 nmol/L can be adverse. Adult tolerable upper intake is 4,000 IU/day. Excess supplementation can cause hypercalcemia, hypercalciuria, neuropsychiatric disturbances, kidney stones, renal failure, soft-tissue calcification, arrhythmias, and death. Medication interactions or status effects can involve orlistat, statins, steroids, thiazide diuretics, kidney disease, obesity, malabsorption, calcium intake, and supplement stacking.^[raw/articles/ods-2025-vitamin-d-health-professional-fact-sheet.md]

## Practical Interpretation

Vitamin D3 belongs in the nutrient lane because status correction is medically real and some cognition data are positive in vulnerable or deficient phenotypes. It should not be ranked as a broad nootropic. The defensible claim is "possible cognitive support when low vitamin D status is a limiting variable," with large null trials showing that supplementation does not reliably improve cognition in generally sufficient or mildly deficient older adults.

A confidence upgrade requires trials stratified by baseline 25(OH)D severity, achieved serum level, calcium/kidney context, race/latitude/season, cognition endpoint, and adherence. For self-experimentation notes, the minimum useful record is baseline 25(OH)D, dose, form, timing, calcium/magnesium/K2 co-use, sleep, and follow-up level.
