# Higenamine / Hordenine / Octopamine

Higenamine, hordenine, and octopamine are stimulant-adjacent amines found in some preworkout, fat-loss, and energy supplements. This grouped entity keeps the three identities visible without pretending that any has a mature standalone cognition dossier. It covers higenamine / norcoclaurine, hordenine / N,N-dimethyltyramine, and p-octopamine / norsynephrine as a risk-first preworkout-amine cluster, not [[p-synephrine]], [[yohimbine]], [[caffeine]], [[paraxanthine]], methylliberine, DMAA/DMHA-style stimulants, or generic bitter-orange extract.

Medicinal cognition signal: `risk-first/low, no direct cognition proof`. The formal human packet is not cognitive enhancement evidence. Higenamine has a small single-ingredient exercise/safety RCT in recreational female athletes that did not improve cardiopulmonary fitness or weight-loss outcomes, plus a confounded acute higenamine/caffeine/yohimbe supplement study in the surrounding literature. Hordenine has human beer-exposure pharmacokinetics showing low plasma levels that the authors judged too low for direct D2 food-reward interaction. Octopamine has review-level pharmacology but no captured human oral performance, weight-loss, or cognition study. This is a regulatory/safety and product-identity lane, not an enhancer lane.^[raw/articles/frontiers-2021-higenamine-exercise-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-31984737-hordenine-human-beer-biokinetics.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-24654910-octopamine-pharmacology-review.md]

Regulatory and sports signal: `high burden`. FDA's ingredient directory and 2022 warning-letter update keep higenamine, hordenine, and octopamine in an adulterated-supplement / new-dietary-ingredient lane rather than a clean supplement lane. The current WADA page names higenamine under S3 beta-2 agonists prohibited at all times and octopamine under S6.B specified stimulants prohibited in competition. Hordenine was not captured as an explicitly named WADA entry in this pass, but it still needs product-specific athlete checks because this market overlaps with prohibited stimulants, label inaccuracy, and high-stim blends.^[raw/articles/fda-2026-dietary-supplement-ingredient-directory-higenamine-hordenine-octopamine.md]^[raw/articles/fda-2022-higenamine-hordenine-octopamine-warning-letters.md]^[raw/articles/wada-2026-higenamine-octopamine-prohibited-list.md]

Product-quality signal: `high burden`. A Clinical Toxicology analysis found higenamine in U.S. weight-loss and sports/energy supplements at trace-to-high serving amounts, with possible daily exposures above a typical single serving and inaccurate amount labeling. A newer sports-supplement label-accuracy case series found octopamine products with both no-detect and labeled-dose-like results, reinforcing that label presence is not exposure certainty and label absence is not full safety certainty.^[raw/articles/pubmed-30188222-higenamine-supplement-quantity.md]^[raw/articles/jama-2023-botanical-sports-supplement-label-accuracy.md]

Anecdote signal: `weak-moderate subjective focus/euphoria, strong confounding and adverse counterweight`. Hordenine threads report focus, multitasking, energy, and euphoria, but also chest/heart pressure and no-effect reports. The high-stim preworkout thread includes tachycardia-like adverse reports and debate over higenamine versus yohimbine, DMHA, caffeine, and the full blend. The useful signal is not "this improves cognition"; it is "this cluster appears in arousal-heavy product contexts that can feel focus-positive or physically bad."^[raw/articles/reddit-2020-hordenine-focus-thread.md]^[raw/articles/reddit-2022-hordenine-leg-day-thread.md]^[raw/articles/reddit-2025-higenamine-preworkout-tachycardia-thread.md]

## Identity Gates

- Separate higenamine from hordenine, octopamine, [[p-synephrine]], [[yohimbine]], caffeine, methylliberine, DMAA/DMHA, eria jarensis / PEA-like ingredients, rauwolscine, and multi-stimulant proprietary blends.
- For higenamine, check WADA status before any athlete use because it is prohibited at all times in the captured 2026 list.
- For octopamine, check WADA in-competition status and bitter-orange contamination/product identity before treating it as an ordinary supplement ingredient.
- For hordenine, avoid assuming WADA named-list status from this pass; still treat it as high-risk in athlete-facing products because FDA warning status and stimulant product overlap are enough to trigger caution.
- Track assayed milligrams, salt form, botanical source, serving size, number of scoops/capsules, caffeine load, yohimbine/rauwolscine, DMHA/DMAA-like ingredients, stimulant medication, MAOI/antidepressant exposure, blood pressure, heart rhythm, anxiety/panic vulnerability, sleep timing, and product-lot identity.
- Route higenamine, hordenine, octopamine, and high-stim blend anecdotes through [[safety-interaction-matrix-for-multi-modal-stacks]] before interpreting focus, euphoria, workout intensity, or energy as cognition.

## Practical Ranking

This cluster deserves standalone coverage because it is common enough in preworkout and fat-loss contexts, and because the regulatory/sports consequences are stronger than the cognition evidence. It should not be ranked as a cognitive enhancer. The current route is `preworkout stimulant-amine risk comparator`, with anecdotal focus treated as arousal and product-stack salience until objective cognition data appears.
