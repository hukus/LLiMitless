# D-ribose

D-ribose is a pentose sugar and nucleotide-building-block chemical sold as a supplement for "ATP support," exercise recovery, fibromyalgia, chronic fatigue syndrome, and mitochondrial-energy stacks. In this wiki it belongs near [[energy-substrate-comparator-map]], [[nadh]], [[coenzyme-q10]], and [[post-viral-brain-fog-chemical-watchlist]], but it should not be collapsed with glucose, lactate, ketone esters, [[creatine]], ATP products, or generic mitochondrial blends.^[raw/articles/pubchem-2026-d-ribose-identity.md]

The cognition read is `low/illness-state and uncontrolled`. D-ribose has explicit "mental clarity" signals in CFS/fibromyalgia open-label studies and visible ME/CFS brain-fog anecdotes, but it lacks a placebo-controlled cognition trial in healthy adults or an objective neuropsychological endpoint. The best interpretation is a ME/CFS-like energy-envelope / symptom-relief hypothesis, not a broad cognitive enhancer.^[raw/articles/pubmed-17109576-d-ribose-cfs-fms-pilot.md]^[raw/articles/openpainjournal-2012-d-ribose-cfs-fms-open-label.md]^[raw/articles/reddit-2021-2026-d-ribose-cfs-brainfog-threads.md]

## Evidence Snapshot

| Layer | Signal | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| Identity | D-ribose / ribose pentose sugar | Separate from NADH, CoQ10, creatine, ketones, lactate, and ATP blends |
| 2006 CFS/FMS pilot | Open-label D-ribose improved energy, sleep, mental clarity, pain, and well-being, with about two-thirds reporting significant improvement | Direct mental-clarity symptom signal, but no placebo and no objective cognition |
| 2012 CFS/FMS open-label study | Larger uncontrolled multicenter study reported similar symptom-domain improvements | Reinforces illness-state salience, not causal efficacy |
| Healthy exercise counterweight | Healthy-subject exercise trial did not support a broad performance-enhancement assumption under tested conditions | Weakens generic ATP-restoration marketing |
| Safety review | EFSA reviewed D-ribose as a novel food and supported safety at proposed adult use levels | Safety packet exists, but GI/glycemic context and dose still matter |
| Anecdote | ME/CFS / long-COVID / fibromyalgia users report energy-envelope and brain-fog effects, but mixed with no-effect and adverse reports | Hypothesis-generation only |

## Practical Interpretation

- Best fit: ME/CFS, fibromyalgia, post-viral fatigue, or post-exertional-crash contexts where subjective energy and mental clarity track each other.
- Weak fit: young healthy adults seeking memory, executive-function, or sustained-attention enhancement.
- Main attribution problem: D-ribose is often stacked with [[nadh]], [[coenzyme-q10]], magnesium, carnitine, creatine, B vitamins, pacing, and diet changes.
- Ranking: lower than [[creatine]], [[ketone-ester]], lactate procedures, and caffeine in [[energy-substrate-comparator-map]] because the D-ribose cognition packet is uncontrolled and symptom-scale-based.

## Safety And Use Boundaries

- Treat D-ribose as a sugar-like metabolic exposure, not a neutral vitamin. GI upset, dose timing, meal context, diabetes/hypoglycemia risk, pregnancy/lactation exclusion from safety opinions, and product-dose accuracy matter.
- Do not use the CFS/FMS open-label studies as proof of healthy-adult cognition enhancement; they measured subjective symptom domains in symptomatic cohorts.
- If used in self-experimentation logic, D-ribose needs explicit fatigue, PEM, sleep, activity, glucose/meal, GI, and objective-task tracking to avoid confusing better energy with better cognition.
