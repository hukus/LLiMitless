# Pomegranate

Pomegranate (Punica granatum) is a polyphenol-rich fruit and supplement source used as juice, extract, or less commonly other plant-part preparations. For cognition, product identity is the key issue: juice, standardized extract, punicalagin content, ellagic acid content, sugar load, urolithin metabolism, and background diet are not interchangeable.

Medicinal cognition signal: `low/product-specific`. A small 4-week randomized placebo-controlled juice trial in older adults with mild memory complaints found verbal-memory improvement, antioxidant and urolithin biomarker movement, and increased task-related fMRI activity. A larger 12-month randomized double-blind juice trial in nondemented adults aged 50-75 found preservation of BVMT-R learning versus placebo decline, while other memory outcomes were not significantly different. A 12-week randomized double-blind extract trial in adults aged 55-70 found its clearest cognitive signal on WCST performance, with favorable trends on digit span, Tower of Hanoi, and Stroop. The 2025 correction clarifies that the extract capsule contained 36% punicalagins and 1.3% ellagic acid, so transportability should use the corrected product composition.^[raw/articles/pmc-3736548-pomegranate-juice-memory-fmri-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-31711104-pomegranate-juice-memory-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39997528-pomegranate-extract-cognition-rct.md]^[raw/articles/mdpi-2025-pomegranate-extract-cognition-rct-correction.md]

Anecdote signal: `weak/bidirectional`. The cleanest direct nootropic-community capture found so far is an adverse / paradoxical Reddit thread reporting marked cognitive worsening after pomegranate extract, with another similar report and speculative mechanism discussion. This is useful for safety and responder-phenotype monitoring, not for efficacy upgrading.^[raw/articles/reddit-2021-pomegranate-extract-cognitive-decline-thread.md]

Safety and product-form signal: `moderate caution`. NCCIH treats pomegranate juice as believed safe and extract as possibly safe, while warning that large amounts of root, stem, or peel may not be safe and that digestive symptoms and allergic reactions can occur. The practical wiki caution is to separate juice from supplement extracts, monitor medication context, and account for juice sugar load and product-marker uncertainty before treating pomegranate as a general cognitive enhancer.^[raw/articles/nccih-2025-pomegranate-safety-interactions.md]

## Signal Split

| Layer | Signal | Readout |
|---|---|---|
| Juice, short term | `low/direct` | Small 4-week RCT in mild memory complaints with verbal-memory, biomarker, and fMRI signals |
| Juice, long term | `low/stabilization` | 12-month RCT preserved BVMT-R learning versus placebo decline, but did not move all memory endpoints |
| Extract | `low/product-specific` | 740 mg/day extract RCT showed clearest WCST signal and trends on other tasks; correction sets punicalagins at 36% |
| Mechanism | `plausible but indirect` | Polyphenols, antioxidant capacity, urolithin metabolism, vascular/metabolic effects, and gut-microbiome conversion remain mediators, not proof |
| Anecdote | `weak/bidirectional` | Direct thread is adverse-heavy rather than pro-nootropic |
| Safety | `moderate caution` | Juice generally food-like; extracts and non-fruit plant parts need product-form, medication, allergy, GI, sugar, and sourcing checks |

## Practical Interpretation

Pomegranate now clears the wiki's standalone-entity threshold, but only as a low-confidence product-specific botanical. It belongs beside [[polyphenol-nrf2-cognition-watchlist]], [[standardized-herbal-extracts-for-cognition]], and [[plant-active-substance-product-watchlist]], not in the broad enhancer tier. A future upgrade would require replicated product-normalized trials, clear juice-versus-extract dosing, safety and interaction monitoring, and a stronger community signal that is not dominated by adverse or non-specific reports.
