# KAT II Inhibitors

Kynurenine aminotransferase II inhibition is the main target lane for reducing brain [[kynurenic-acid]] in schizophrenia-linked cognition hypotheses. This page covers KAT II inhibition as a target class, including BFF-816 preclinical logic and the TrypNAC-II human challenge registry, not generic NAC supplementation.

Medicinal signal: `low / preclinical-plus-registry`. The 2014 KAT II review frames elevated brain KYNA as a contributor to cognitive dysfunction in schizophrenia and reports that KAT II inhibition reduced KYNA while increasing glutamate, dopamine, and acetylcholine in rat brain regions. That is target-validity support, not human cognition efficacy.^[raw/articles/pubmed-24562494-targeting-kat-ii-psychiatric-diseases-review.md]

TrypNAC-II is the current human watch item. It is a completed double-blind crossover challenge study in schizophrenia-spectrum participants that used NAC 140 mg/kg up to 15 g or placebo before 6 g tryptophan, with serum kynurenine/KYNA, CBF, glutamate, glutathione, white-matter FA, and cognition as readouts. The registry has posted results, but until a full paper resolves the cognitive direction and responder logic, the wiki should keep the signal low and registry-level.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct04013555-kat-ii-schizophrenia-trypnac-ii.md]

## Signal Judgment

KAT II inhibition is most relevant for schizophrenia or KYNA-enriched states, not healthy-adult enhancement. The 2025 KYNA synapse-elimination paper strengthens the disease mechanism, but it still stops short of a tested cognitive treatment.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40165559-kynurenic-acid-synapse-elimination-schizophrenia.md]

## Safety And Protocol Caveats

- TrypNAC-II is a supervised challenge protocol, not a DIY NAC-plus-tryptophan recommendation.
- NAC anecdotes belong on [[n-acetylcysteine]] unless a report measures kynurenine, KYNA, or cognition under controlled conditions.
- Tryptophan challenge logic belongs partly on [[5-htp-tryptophan]], but serotonin-precursor and kynurenine-enzyme interpretations must stay separate.
