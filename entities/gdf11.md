# GDF11

GDF11, growth differentiation factor 11, is a TGF-beta superfamily protein in the blood-borne rejuvenation and brain-aging lane. It is one of the highest-salience plasma-factor molecules, but its cognition evidence remains preclinical and controversy-sensitive.

Medicinal cognition signal: `preclinical-only for intervention`. Systemic GDF11 improved hippocampal neurogenesis, vasculature, and neuronal activity / plasticity markers in aged mice, apparently through brain endothelial cells rather than direct BBB crossing.^[raw/articles/pubmed-30470794-gdf11-hippocampus-aged-mice.md]

Disease-model signal: `preclinical`. In A-betaPP/PS1 Alzheimer's model mice, daily intravenous GDF11 rescued Morris-water-maze performance and improved cerebrovascular and inflammatory markers.^[raw/articles/pubmed-29480172-gdf11-ad-cerebrovascular-cognition.md]

Mood-memory signal: `preclinical plus biomarker clue`. A 2023 Nature Aging study reported that systemic recombinant GDF11 improved memory measures and depression-like phenotypes in aged mice through hippocampal neuronal autophagy and reduced mTOR activity, with lower GDF11 in serum from young adults with major depressive disorder.^[raw/articles/nature-aging-2023-systemic-gdf11-memory-mood-autophagy.md]

Endogenous brain-aging signal: `mechanistic`. A 2023 Nature Communications study found GDF11 in excitatory neurons across mouse, marmoset, and human brain tissue; deleting GDF11 from mouse excitatory neurons induced senescence, hyperexcitability, object-recognition memory impairment, and shortened lifespan through a Smad2-p21 pathway.^[raw/articles/pubmed-37978295-gdf11-neuronal-senescence-brain-aging-p21.md]

## Safety And Translation Caveats

- There is no captured human GDF11 cognition dosing trial.
- GDF11 is closely related to GDF8 / myostatin, and the aging literature has been contested because of assay specificity, recombinant-protein quality, dose, and experimental-design differences.
- High-dose GDF11 has caused cachexia and premature death in mice in the broader aging-biology literature.
- The simple "GDF11 declines with aging and should be replaced" story is not supported by the controversy review.^[raw/articles/pmc-10793994-gdf11-aging-biology-controversies.md]
- Route GDF11 rejuvenation or cognition claims through [[safety-interaction-matrix-for-multi-modal-stacks]] before treating mouse memory, vascular, autophagy, or mood findings as human enhancement evidence. GDF8/myostatin separation, assay specificity, recombinant-protein quality, dose, cachexia risk, disease model, and delivery route are all first-order interpretation gates.
