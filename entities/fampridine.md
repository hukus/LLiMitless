# Fampridine

Fampridine, also called 4-aminopyridine or dalfampridine, is a voltage-gated potassium-channel blocker best known in the US as AMPYRA for walking impairment in multiple sclerosis. In the cognition wiki it belongs in the "promising but narrow" bucket: there is a real human working-memory signal in a genome-guided healthy-volunteer crossover study, but the effect is not universal and the drug has a nontrivial seizure-and-renal safety ceiling. The newer MS-context papers keep it narrow rather than broad: an upper-extremity RCT was null, and a 2026 fatigue review found randomized evidence to be inconsistent despite mostly positive observational reports.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39990866-dalfampridine-upper-extremity-ms-null-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41635596-fampridine-ms-fatigue-systematic-review.md] A 2026 preclinical TBI paper adds axon-rescue rationale, but not human cognition proof.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41508250-4-aminopyridine-tbi-spatial-transcriptomics.md]

The best current reading is that fampridine may help low-baseline or impaired performers more than already-high performers, which makes it a candidate for responder-enriched cognitive-deficit studies rather than a broad healthy-adult nootropic.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39516710-fampridine-working-memory-genome-guided-repurposing.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-30593980-4-aminopyridine-multiple-sclerosis-cognition.md]
The cleanest human protocol is the Basel crossover trial: 10 mg twice daily for 3.5 days, with 3-back d-prime as the primary outcome and resting motor threshold as a mechanistic secondary endpoint.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct04652557-fampridine-working-memory.md]
Safety is the limiting factor: the current FDA label contraindicates seizure history and CrCl <= 50 mL/min, and it warns that mild renal impairment can push plasma exposure toward a higher-risk range.^[raw/articles/accessdata-fda-ampyra-dalfampridine-label-2021.md]
The 2025 upper-extremity null and 2026 fatigue review both argue that symptom effects in MS are domain-specific, with walking still looking most plausible.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39990866-dalfampridine-upper-extremity-ms-null-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41635596-fampridine-ms-fatigue-systematic-review.md]
The preclinical TBI paper also matters because 0.5 mg/kg reduced axonal injury markers while 5 mg/kg induced seizure behavior, which reinforces the narrow therapeutic window.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41508250-4-aminopyridine-tbi-spatial-transcriptomics.md]
