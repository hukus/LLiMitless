# Grape Food Products

Grape food products are the wiki's food-matrix branch for raisins, Concord grape juice, whole grapes, and similar grape-derived foods. This page exists to keep them separate from [[grape-extract]], grape-seed extracts, mixed grape-blueberry products such as Memophenol, isolated resveratrol / pterostilbene, wine, and generic polyphenols. The active unit is not "grape" alone; it is product form, cultivar, dose, polyphenol profile, sugar/fluid load, meal timing, and cognitive endpoint.

Medicinal cognition signal: `low/product-specific and food-matrix-confounded`. The strongest raisin-specific result is a 6-month randomized trial in 80 healthy adults over 70 years old: 50 g/day Malaga muscatel raisins improved MoCA total score, visuospatial/executive capacity, language, orientation, immediate recall, delayed recall, quality of life, and instrumental activities versus a no-supplement control. The signal is useful but fragile because the comparator was not a matched placebo and the whole-diet / behavior change layer is hard to isolate. The 2026 plant-active-substance NMA ranks raisin highly for learning/memory and language, but that is a discovery ranking rather than product-transportability proof.^[raw/articles/pmc-10301980-raisin-cognition-older-adults-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pmc-12864429-plant-active-substances-healthy-older-adults-nma.md]

The Concord grape juice layer adds food-product plausibility without solving the raisin problem. A 12-person older-adult memory-decline trial found a verbal-learning signal after 12 weeks of Concord grape juice versus a matched placebo, with non-significant verbal/spatial recall trends and a fasting-insulin increase. A 25-person working-mother crossover trial using 12 oz/day Concord grape juice found immediate spatial-memory and driving-performance signals, but also possible carryover that complicates washout interpretation. A grape-intervention systematic review found eight RCTs and summarized chronic grape studies as positive in some domains, while acute memory effects were inconsistent.^[raw/articles/cambridge-2010-concord-grape-juice-mci-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-26864371-concord-grape-juice-mothers-cognition-driving-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pmc-8829676-grape-interventions-cognition-systematic-review.md]

Anecdote signal: `weak and mostly non-specific`. The broad nootropic-rating capture supports only weak community visibility for raisin/grape food products. Direct forum material found in this pass is adverse-direction rather than pro-cognitive: a brain-fog thread mentions raisins as a possible dried-fruit/sulfite trigger in sensitive users. This is a responder/safety hypothesis, not evidence that raisins generally cause or fix cognition problems.^[raw/articles/troof-nootropics-ratings-analysis.md]^[raw/articles/reddit-2021-sulfites-raisins-brain-fog-thread.md]

Safety and product-quality signal: `low-moderate food-dose caution`. Raisins and grape juice are ordinary foods, but cognition-trial doses are not frictionless. The raisin trial used 50 g/day, while NHS dried-fruit guidance frames about 30 g as a portion and cautions that dried fruit can be high in sugar and best eaten with meals for dental risk. The 2010 grape-juice trial reported good tolerability but a fasting-insulin increase in the juice arm. Product form, diabetes/metabolic context, dental risk, sulfite sensitivity, juice volume, evening sugar timing, and weight-change monitoring should gate interpretation.^[raw/articles/nhs-5-a-day-dried-fruit-raisins-sugar.md]^[raw/articles/cambridge-2010-concord-grape-juice-mci-rct.md]^[raw/articles/reddit-2021-sulfites-raisins-brain-fog-thread.md]

## Practical Interpretation

Grape food products now clear a low-confidence standalone threshold because raisins and Concord grape juice both have direct human cognition anchors, and the 2026 plant-active NMA keeps raisin visible. They still belong below [[grape-extract]] and below cleaner comparator molecules in [[molecule-intake-triage-table]]. The useful wiki claim is narrow: certain grape food matrices may support selected memory, language, attention, or everyday-task endpoints in older adults or demanding-life contexts, but the evidence cannot yet separate polyphenols from sugar, fluid, meal timing, gut effects, expectation, or broader diet replacement.

Future updates must keep these exposures separate: Malaga muscatel raisins, generic raisins, sultanas/currants, Concord grape juice, whole grapes, grape seed extract, [[grape-extract]], Memophenol-style grape-blueberry extract, resveratrol, pterostilbene, and wine. A confidence upgrade requires matched food-placebo designs, metabolic/dental monitoring, polyphenol marker reporting, objective cognitive endpoints, and replication outside sponsor- or product-specific settings.
