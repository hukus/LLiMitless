# Saffron

Saffron is a phenotype-specific standardized-botanical cognition candidate. Its current wiki signal is not healthy-adult enhancement; it is recovery or impairment context with mood and anxiety overlap.

## Medicinal Signal

- Formal signal: `low`.
- The main captured trial used saffron 15 mg twice daily for 12 weeks in CABG patients and assessed cognition, anxiety, and depression.^[raw/articles/pubmed-29185780-saffron-cabg-cognition-rct.md]
- The 2025 natural-extract NMA keeps saffron in the broader botanical comparison layer, but it is not the strongest healthy-adult extract candidate.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40213691-natural-extracts-healthy-adults-network-meta-analysis.md]

## Current Read

Saffron belongs in [[standardized-herbal-extracts-for-cognition]] as a recovery-context botanical. Its mood/anxiety reputation should not be reclassified as cognition evidence without objective endpoints.
