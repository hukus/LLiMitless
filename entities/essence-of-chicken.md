# Essence Of Chicken

Essence of chicken is a cooked, concentrated liquid extract of chicken used as a traditional remedy in parts of Asia for fatigue, recovery, stress, and cognitive support. In this wiki it is a product-level food extract, not pure [[anserine-carnosine]], not ordinary meat intake, not [[beta-alanine]], and not every chicken-peptide supplement.

Medicinal cognition signal: `low-moderate for stress-linked working memory, contested`. The cleanest larger healthy-adult RCT tested 14 days of essence of chicken in 235 per-protocol participants aged 18-45. Digit Span Backward improved more than placebo, and Digit Span Forward improved in moderate-stress participants with a near-signal in severe-stress participants. Selective and sustained attention did not show treatment effects. The practical read is therefore memory-task and stress-state specific.^[raw/articles/mdpi-2018-essence-of-chicken-healthy-adults-cognition-rct.md]

The meta-analytic layer is split. A 2016 systematic review found seven RCTs, mostly high risk of bias, and concluded that convincing evidence was lacking. A later 2019 meta-analysis included eight RCTs and 794 subjects and reported a favorable working-memory standardized mean difference. This justifies keeping the branch visible but not ranking it as settled.^[raw/articles/pmc-4728668-chicken-essence-cognition-systematic-review.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-31131735-essence-of-chicken-cognition-meta.md]

Hydrolyzed chicken extract strengthens the product-specific route. ProBeptigen, a hydrolyzed peptide product derived from essence of chicken, improved WMS Logical Memory and CANTAB Spatial Working Memory in 90 mildly stressed healthy middle-aged people over 8 weeks, while high-sensitivity CRP declined. This is relevant to the same family but should not be collapsed into bottled essence of chicken.^[raw/articles/pubmed-32397609-probeptigen-hydrolyzed-chicken-extract-cognition.md]

Anecdote signal: `weak direct nootropic self-report in the captured corpus`. The formal literature explicitly says essence of chicken is traditionally used for cognition, fatigue, recovery, and postpartum restoration, but this pass did not find a strong English-language nootropic self-experiment corpus. Treat consumer use as cultural/product salience unless direct self-report notes are captured later.

Safety and product signal: `low-moderate food-product caution`. Trials do not create a major acute safety signal, but product composition matters: bottled essence, placebo protein controls, hydrolyzed peptides, added caramel, combination products with ginseng/cordyceps/dong quai, sodium/protein load, chicken allergy, seafood/collagen placebo issues, and sponsor/manufacturer involvement all affect interpretation.

## Signal Split

| Layer | Signal | Readout |
|---|---|---|
| 2018 healthy-adult RCT | `low-moderate/stress-linked` | Working-memory benefit; short-term-memory signal in higher-stress subgroups; no attention benefit |
| 2016 systematic review | `quality warning` | Most trials high risk of bias; evidence judged unconvincing |
| 2019 meta-analysis | `favorable but quality-gated` | Working-memory SMD 0.31 in 8 RCTs / 794 subjects |
| ProBeptigen | `product-specific low-moderate` | Verbal memory, spatial working memory, and hs-CRP signal in mildly stressed middle-aged adults |
| Anecdote | `weak direct corpus` | Traditional and consumer salience visible; direct English nootropic reports not strong in this pass |

## Product And Protocol Gates

- Separate bottled essence of chicken, hydrolyzed chicken extract / ProBeptigen, chicken meat extract, pure [[anserine-carnosine]], and [[beta-alanine]].
- Record brand, serving size, protein/peptide content, anserine/carnosine content if assayed, caramel/additives, placebo comparator, sodium, and combination herbs.
- Stratify by stress, fatigue, age, baseline mood, memory versus attention endpoint, duration, and practice effects.
- Track chicken allergy, gout/purine context if relevant, kidney/liver labs in longer studies, and whether the study was manufacturer-funded.

## Practical Interpretation

[[essence-of-chicken]] belongs in the wiki because it has both traditional-medicine salience and multiple human cognition trials, including a larger healthy-adult RCT. Its best route is `stress-linked working-memory / fatigue-recovery food extract`, not broad rested-baseline enhancement. Product identity and sponsor risk are central, and the pure-dipeptide [[anserine-carnosine]] page should handle purified active-unit claims.
