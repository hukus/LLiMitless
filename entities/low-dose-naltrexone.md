# Low-Dose Naltrexone

Low-dose naltrexone (LDN) is off-label low-milligram use of the opioid antagonist naltrexone. It is discussed as a neuroimmune, glial, pain, fatigue, and post-viral recovery intervention, so the wiki routes it through [[neuroinflammation-and-cognition]], [[neuroimmune-brain-fog-chemical-guide]], and [[biohacker-anecdote-methodology]].

## Bottom Line

Brain-fog clearance signal: `low formal, moderate anecdotal`. Among the neuroimmune brain-fog chemicals in this pass, LDN has the strongest community brain-fog signal and the most relevant observational long-COVID symptom data. It still lacks a published positive randomized brain-fog outcome in the captured sources.^[raw/articles/sciencedirect-2024-ldn-post-covid-symptoms-cohort.md]^[raw/articles/pmc-9250701-ldn-longcovid-prepost.md]^[raw/articles/medrxiv-2025-ldn-longcovid-systematic-review.md]^[raw/articles/reddit-2024-ldn-longcovid-brainfog-thread.md]

Formal status: `pending controlled readout`. A 160-person double-blind post-COVID fatigue-syndrome RCT protocol titrates LDN from 1 mg to 4.5 mg and follows fatigue plus broader symptom outcomes. The associated ClinicalTrials record lists actual enrollment of 160 and completion dates in February 2026, but no outcome result is captured here.^[raw/articles/bmjopen-2024-ldn-post-covid-fatigue-protocol.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct05430152-ldn-post-covid-fatigue.md]

Practical route: LDN is not benign because it is still naltrexone. The major hard gate is opioid use or opioid dependence, followed by acute hepatitis/liver failure context, withdrawal risk, analgesia blockade, and common nausea, headache, dizziness, insomnia, fatigue, and anxiety-like effects.^[raw/articles/dailymed-2026-naltrexone-label.md]

## Formal Evidence

The 2024 Clinical Therapeutics post-COVID cohort included 108 consecutive patients and compared LDN, amitriptyline, duloxetine, and physical therapy. LDN had an adjusted relative hazard of improvement of 5.04 versus physical therapy alone, and fatigue and pain improved in LDN users. The abstract included brain fog in the symptom set but did not establish a controlled brain-fog-specific effect.^[raw/articles/sciencedirect-2024-ldn-post-covid-symptoms-cohort.md]

The 2022 long-COVID pre-post study and a 2024 LDN plus NAD+ pilot support feasibility and symptom-improvement hypotheses, but both are uncontrolled or combination-limited for cognition. The 2025 systematic-review preprint summarizes observational improvements in fatigue, brain fog, pain, sleep, and function, with no serious adverse events reported, but its own evidentiary layer remains low certainty and preprint-level.^[raw/articles/pmc-9250701-ldn-longcovid-prepost.md]^[raw/articles/sciencedirect-2024-ldn-nad-post-covid-fatigue.md]^[raw/articles/medrxiv-2025-ldn-longcovid-systematic-review.md]

## Pending Trial Watch

The BMJ Open protocol and ClinicalTrials record make LDN one of the cleaner watch-list candidates for post-COVID fatigue and cognitive symptom clusters. Until outcome data are posted or published, the wiki should treat it as observational/anecdotal plus RCT-pending, not as established brain-fog treatment.^[raw/articles/bmjopen-2024-ldn-post-covid-fatigue-protocol.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct05430152-ldn-post-covid-fatigue.md]

## Safety And Status

DailyMed's naltrexone label contraindicates use with opioid analgesics, current physiologic opioid dependence, acute opioid withdrawal, failed naloxone challenge or positive opioid screen, hypersensitivity, and acute hepatitis or liver failure. It also warns about precipitated withdrawal, loss of opioid analgesia, liver dysfunction, nausea, headache, dizziness, nervousness, fatigue, insomnia, vomiting, anxiety, and somnolence.^[raw/articles/dailymed-2026-naltrexone-label.md]

## Signal Judgment

LDN is the category's strongest anecdotal brain-fog candidate and a serious RCT-watch item, but current formal confidence remains low. It should be scored as a medically supervised off-label neuroimmune candidate with opioid/liver gates, not as a supplement-like clarity aid.
