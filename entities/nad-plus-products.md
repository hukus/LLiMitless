# NAD+ Products / IV NAD+ / NAD+ Patches

This page covers direct NAD+ products: IV NAD+, injectable or IM NAD+ marketing, NAD+ patches, NAD+ lozenges, oral NAD+ labels, and clinic "NAD drip" protocols. It does not cover [[nicotinamide-riboside]], [[nicotinamide-mononucleotide]], [[nadh]], [[niacin-nicotinamide-b3]], or vague "NAD booster" blends unless the source actually administers NAD+ itself.

Chemical identity is real but route-specific. ChEBI identifies NAD+ as the oxidised form of nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide, a coenzyme/cofactor involved in redox electron transfer. That chemical identity does not imply that every commercial route meaningfully raises brain NAD+ or improves cognition.^[raw/articles/chebi-2026-nad-plus-identity.md]

Medicinal cognition signal: `very low/direct-product and route-gated`. The most useful human IV NAD+ metabolome pilot showed that a 6-hour 3 micromol/min IV NAD+ infusion was rapidly cleared from plasma during at least the first 2 hours, with later urinary NAD+ and methyl-nicotinamide excretion. It was a fate/metabolism pilot, not a cognition or brain-fog trial.^[raw/articles/pubmed-31572171-iv-nad-metabolome.md]

The best current real-world IV product paper is a 2026 commercial-clinic retrospective pilot comparing four days of 500 mg IV NAD+ with 500 mg IV NR. It was small, uncontrolled, and focused on tolerability and biomarkers. All six NAD+ clients reported moderate to severe infusion symptoms, including GI symptoms, increased heart rate, and chest pressure; symptoms resolved when infusion stopped, and NAD+ infusions took far longer than NR infusions. No cognition-efficacy conclusion follows.^[raw/articles/pmc-12907335-iv-nad-vs-nr-tolerability.md]

Patch evidence is especially attribution-limited. A 2024 long-COVID fatigue pilot used low-dose naltrexone 4.5 mg/day plus NAD+ patches in 36 patients and reported improved fatigue and quality-of-life scores, but the design does not isolate NAD+, does not test healthy adults, and is not an objective cognition trial.^[raw/articles/sciencedirect-2024-ldn-nad-post-covid-fatigue.md]

Safety and sourcing signal: `moderate caution`. FDA has specifically warned compounders not to use food-grade NAD+ ingredients for sterile products without appropriate processing and reported adverse events after NAD+ injectable drugs, including severe chills, shaking, vomiting, and fatigue, consistent with endotoxin exposure. This makes sterile route and supplier quality central, not peripheral.^[raw/articles/fda-2026-nad-sterile-compounding-warning.md]

## Interpretation Gates

- Separate direct NAD+ from [[nadh]], [[nicotinamide-riboside]], [[nicotinamide-mononucleotide]], niacin, nicotinamide, tryptophan/kynurenine, and multi-ingredient NAD booster blends.
- State the route: IV, IM/injectable, patch, lozenge, oral, or topical. The evidence does not transport cleanly across routes.
- For IV or injectable use, require sterile-drug ingredient suitability, compounding source, batch testing, infusion protocol, dose, rate, and adverse-event monitoring.
- Treat blood NAD+, urinary metabolites, ALP, HbA1c, lipids, sleep, fatigue, and PROMIS survey measures as indirect until objective cognition separates from placebo.
- Track cointerventions: LDN, glutathione, B vitamins, TMG, CoQ10, D-ribose, NAC, ALCAR, stimulants, tVNS, pacing, and long-COVID recovery trajectory.
- Do not rank direct NAD+ products above NR/NMN/NADH/B3 just because the label says NAD+. The route-specific human cognition packet is thinner.

## Practical Ranking

Direct NAD+ products deserve a standalone page because clinic use, patch marketing, injection DIY, long-COVID fatigue interest, and FDA compounding warnings are all visible. The current cognition read stays `very low`: useful as a route/safety and attribution-control branch, not as a healthy-adult cognition lead. A confidence upgrade would require a blinded direct NAD+ trial with product identity, sterile route control, objective attention/executive-function outcomes, fatigue and sleep endpoints, biomarker transport, and enough follow-up to separate infusion novelty from durable brain-fog change.
