# PE-22-28

PE-22-28 is a shortened spadin-derived peptide sometimes called `mini-spadin`. It belongs in the wiki as a separate entity because the identity is stable enough to track, the preclinical story is coherent, and the forum/vendor layer now repeats the name often enough that it reads as an `emerging peptide branch`, not a passing mention.

The core biology is TREK-1 inhibition. The parent spadin paper framed sortilin-derived peptides as antidepressant-design leads, and the shortened-analog paper then produced PE-22-28 as a smaller derivative with stronger in vivo stability and rodent antidepressant-like effects. The review paper keeps the same target framing alive, but none of these sources add a human cognition trial or a human dosing dossier.^[raw/articles/pubmed-20405001-spadin-sortilin-derived-peptide-trek1-antidepressant-design.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-28955242-shortened-spadin-analogs-display-better-trek1-inhibition-vivo-stability-antidepressant-activity.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-30291907-fighting-against-depression-trek-1-blockers-spadin-review.md]

June 2026 read: PE-22-28 has `weak-to-moderate emerging curiosity, very low human cognition confidence`. A fresh public check on 2026-06-15 did not surface a human cognition trial or registered protocol; the visible layer is still reference-style chemistry pages, vendor availability, mechanism paraphrase, and a few mild mood/anhedonia reports rather than objective cognition data. Treat it as a TREK-1 / mini-spadin mood-anhedonia curiosity, not as a cognition lead.

## Evidence Snapshot

- Preclinical: spadin and PE-22-28 are tied to antidepressant-like behavior, TREK-1 inhibition, neurogenesis, CREB signaling, synaptogenesis, and related plasticity readouts in rodents.^[raw/articles/pubmed-20405001-spadin-sortilin-derived-peptide-trek1-antidepressant-design.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-28955242-shortened-spadin-analogs-display-better-trek1-inhibition-vivo-stability-antidepressant-activity.md]
- Human: I did not surface a human cognition trial or a credible human exposure dossier in this sweep, so the branch stays preclinical-first.
- Translational read: the peptide is interesting as a TREK-1 / antidepressant-design node, but not as a validated nootropic or recovery peptide.

## Frontier Read

PE-22-28 currently looks `upcoming by mechanism and community repetition`, not by formal development. That still matters. A real TREK-1 / synaptogenesis preclinical story plus repeated vendor/forum curiosity is enough to keep it on the peptide frontier map, but not enough to promote it above low confidence.

## Safety / Sourcing

- No human safety dossier surfaced in this pass.
- Vendor and forum discussion make PE-22-28 look more available than it is clinically validated.
- The main practical risk is identity and sourcing opacity, not a known human adverse-event profile.
- Dose claims should not be treated as protocol evidence until human data exist.

## Read

PE-22-28 should stay in the preclinical / curiosity bucket. It is useful as a routing node for the TREK-1 family and as a negative example of how a plausible mechanism can accumulate community chatter before any real human validation appears.
