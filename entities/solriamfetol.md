# Solriamfetol

Solriamfetol is a prescription wake-promoter best treated as a disorder-context state-restoration comparator. It has a stronger direct cognition anchor than most modern wakefulness drugs because SHARP measured objective cognition in OSA patients with excessive daytime sleepiness, but that still does not make it a healthy-adult nootropic.

## Medicinal Signal

- Formal signal: `moderate` for OSA / excessive-daytime-sleepiness cognition support; `low` for healthy-adult enhancement.
- DailyMed lists solriamfetol as indicated to improve wakefulness in adults with excessive daytime sleepiness associated with narcolepsy or obstructive sleep apnea; it is Schedule IV in the captured label note.^[raw/articles/dailymed-sunosi-solriamfetol-tablet-label.md]
- SHARP randomized 59 OSA participants with excessive daytime sleepiness and cognitive impairment. Solriamfetol improved the primary objective cognition endpoint versus placebo on the repeated RBANS coding / DSST-like measure, with Cohen's d = 0.37, plus subjective cognition and sleepiness improvements.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39528111-solriamfetol-sharp-osa-cognition.md]
- The effect was time-locked to post-dose windows, so the most conservative interpretation is arousal-state rescue, not durable learning or transfer.

## Safety And Interpretation

- Main burden: prescription status, controlled-substance visibility, blood-pressure / heart-rate monitoring, insomnia, anxiety, appetite effects, and overreading wakefulness as cognition.
- Best comparator pages: [[pharmacology-comparator-map]], [[histamine-h3-inverse-agonists-2026-scan]], [[sleep-and-circadian-interventions-for-cognition]], [[cognitive-fidelity-split]], and [[cognitive-improvement-molecule-signal-atlas]].
- Solriamfetol is more relevant when the target state is EDS, OSA, narcolepsy, or fatigue burden than when the target is a well-rested healthy adult baseline.

## Current Read

Solriamfetol is one of the better modern wake-promoter comparators because it has a direct OSA cognition trial. Its wiki role is to set a realistic bar for state-restoration claims, not to expand the broad enhancer category.
