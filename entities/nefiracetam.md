# Nefiracetam

Nefiracetam is a pyrrolidone / racetam-adjacent small molecule also known as DM-9384, Translon, and Motiva. It should be separated from [[pramiracetam]], piracetam, oxiracetam, [[aniracetam]], and [[noopept]] because its evidence shape is mostly cholinergic/GABAergic mechanism plus clinical-state apathy/dementia interest, not a modern healthy-adult cognition packet.^[raw/articles/pubchem-2026-nefiracetam-identity.md]

## Bottom Line

Medicinal signal: `low/clinical-state and mechanistic, not healthy enhancement`. Nefiracetam has formal clinical visibility through a completed phase 2 Alzheimer registry with no posted results and a 2016 post-stroke apathy RCT that randomized only 13 patients and did not show significant benefit over placebo. Its best supportive material is mechanistic: cholinergic/GABAergic transmission, calcium-channel effects, and alpha4beta2 nicotinic receptor potentiation in preclinical systems.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-2026-nct00001933-nefiracetam-ad-no-results.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-26915605-nefiracetam-poststroke-apathy-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-9062681-nefiracetam-cellular-mechanisms-expanded.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-11462769-nefiracetam-nachr-expanded.md]

Anecdotal signal: `weak-moderate to moderate`, mostly anti-apathy, anxiety, brain-fog, and cannabis-aftereffect claims from a small 2025 thread. It is source-, route-, and mood-state confounded and does not upgrade the medicinal rating.^[raw/articles/reddit-2025-nefiracetam-apathy-experience-thread.md]

Practical route: keep nefiracetam as a cautionary racetam-adjacent comparator near [[legacy-vasoactive-racetam-nootropic-watchlist]], [[cholinergic-state-restoration-and-cognition]], and [[racetam-long-tail-watchlist]], not as a top cognition-enhancement candidate.

## Human Evidence

The strongest direct clinical counterweight is the 2016 post-stroke apathy RCT. It screened 2514 recent-stroke patients, found a much smaller eligible/apathy subgroup, randomized only 13 patients, and tested 900 mg/day nefiracetam for 12 weeks against placebo. The Apathy Scale improved numerically but there was no significant between-group difference at week 12. This leaves the result negative/noninformative for apathy and not a cognition endpoint.^[raw/articles/pubmed-26915605-nefiracetam-poststroke-apathy-rct.md]

ClinicalTrials.gov lists NCT00001933 as a completed 50-participant phase 2 NIH/NINDS Alzheimer-type dementia study, started in 1999 and completed in 2002. It was designed to test memory, thinking, and activities of daily living over 20 weeks in mild-to-moderate Alzheimer disease, but no results are posted in the registry. That establishes formal development interest without usable efficacy evidence.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-2026-nct00001933-nefiracetam-ad-no-results.md]

## Mechanism And Preclinical Evidence

The 1997 mechanism paper reports rat basal-forebrain-lesion and scopolamine amnesia rescue plus cholinergic/GABAergic transmitter effects and calcium-channel effects. This supports a cholinergic/GABAergic state-restoration hypothesis, but it is still preclinical/translational.^[raw/articles/pubmed-9062681-nefiracetam-cellular-mechanisms-expanded.md]

The 2001 nicotinic receptor paper reports alpha4beta2 receptor potentiation in rat cortical neurons, with a bell-shaped dose-response pattern and Gs-protein mediation. This is mechanistically interesting for learning/memory hypotheses but not a substitute for objective human cognition outcomes.^[raw/articles/pubmed-11462769-nefiracetam-nachr-expanded.md]

## Safety And Status

PubChem identity data keep nefiracetam separate from other racetams and list chemical hazard classifications including harmful-if-swallowed and eye-irritation warnings. The Alzheimer registry's exclusions also flag renal/hepatic/cardiovascular/hematological screening, cholinergic contraindications, hypersensitivity, interacting centrally acting drugs, and male conception concerns tied to potential adverse effects on spermatogenesis. These are safety and development-context signals, not a clean consumer dosing guide.^[raw/articles/pubchem-2026-nefiracetam-identity.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-2026-nct00001933-nefiracetam-ad-no-results.md]

The practical consumer problem is evidence asymmetry: community interest is renewed, but current sourcing, powder identity, dose, solvent/route, and cessation mood effects remain anecdotal.

## Signal Judgment

Nefiracetam belongs in the wiki because it is a named racetam-adjacent molecule with formal trial and mechanism anchors plus renewed community interest. The signal should remain low: the modern human apathy trial did not show superiority to placebo, the Alzheimer phase 2 record has no posted results, and the positive story is mostly preclinical mechanism plus subjective apathy/brain-fog anecdotes.
