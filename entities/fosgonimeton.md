# Fosgonimeton

## Bottom Line

Fosgonimeton / ATH-1017 is the practical human check on the HGF/MET synaptogenic story that often gets invoked around [[dihexa]]. Its medicinal signal for cognition is currently `negative-to-low`: the large LIFT-AD phase 2/3 trial missed its primary Global Statistical Test and key ADAS-Cog11 and ADCS-ADL23 secondary endpoints. Anecdotal signal is absent in the current source corpus.

## What It Is

Fosgonimeton is a small-molecule positive modulator of HGF/MET signaling. It matters to the wiki because it is a clinically tested, humanized comparator for HGF/MET-linked synaptic-repair claims, whereas [[dihexa]] remains preclinical-only.

## Medicinal Research Signal

LIFT-AD was a randomized, placebo-controlled 26-week trial in mild-to-moderate AD. The primary analysis compared daily subcutaneous fosgonimeton 40 mg with placebo in participants not receiving concomitant acetylcholinesterase inhibitors. The trial missed the primary Global Statistical Test and the secondary ADAS-Cog11 and ADCS-ADL23 endpoints. The paper reported small, nonsignificant placebo-vs-treatment separation and treated severity/APOE4 subgroup patterns as exploratory.^[raw/articles/pmc-12701236-fosgonimeton-lift-ad.md]

The ClinicalTrials.gov record is important for status discipline: NCT04488419 enrolled 554 participants, completed in July 2024, and posted results in April 2025. That moves the branch out of the "pending exciting mechanism" bucket and into the completed negative-endpoint comparator bucket.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct04488419-fosgonimeton-lift-ad.md]

## Safety And Status

Fosgonimeton was generally well tolerated at 40 mg in the captured LIFT-AD note, but injection-site reactions increased treatment-emergent adverse events and 70 mg had lower tolerability. The main wiki concern is not an overwhelming safety signal; it is failure to clear efficacy endpoints.

## Signal Judgment

Medicinal signal: `negative-to-low`. The HGF/MET mechanism remains biologically interesting, but the best human comparator has not shown a convincing cognition or function win.

Anecdotal signal: `absent-to-weak`.

Practical route: negative human comparator for HGF/MET and synaptic-rescue extrapolations. It should lower, not raise, confidence in preclinical-only HGF/MET cognition claims.
