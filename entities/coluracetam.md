# Coluracetam

## Bottom Line

Coluracetam / MKC-231 / BCI-540 is a choline-uptake research-chemical branch with `preclinical-only` cognition support and human mood-trial exposure without posted cognition results. Anecdotal signal is `moderate` in nootropic lore but not formally captured as a molecule-specific ledger in the current corpus.

## What It Is

Coluracetam is usually discussed as a racetam-adjacent high-affinity choline-uptake enhancer. In this wiki it belongs with [[racetam-long-tail-watchlist]], [[choline-donor-cognition-signal-map]], and [[racetam-comparison-2026-scan]] as a cholinergic-rescue hypothesis, not a human-proven enhancer.

## Medicinal Research Signal

The cognition anchor is preclinical. MKC-231 improved water-maze learning impairment in AF64A-treated rats after repeated oral administration at 1 or 3 mg/kg for 8 days, with effects lasting about 24 hours after the last dose. The study also reported increased high-affinity choline uptake on a similar time course, supporting a transporter-regulation hypothesis.^[raw/articles/pubmed-18461272-coluracetam-mkc231-af64a-rats.md]

The human-exposure anchor is a completed phase 2 ClinicalTrials.gov record for BCI-540 in adults with major depressive disorder and concomitant anxiety. The randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial tested 80 mg once daily or 80 mg three times daily over six weeks in 115 participants, using depression and anxiety outcomes. The captured registry view has no posted results and no cognition endpoint, so it cannot upgrade cognition confidence.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct00621270-coluracetam-bci540-mdd-anxiety.md]

## Safety And Status

The practical safety problem is thin human evidence. A mood/anxiety trial registry proves human exposure was studied, but absent posted results and absent cognition outcomes leave the nootropic-use risk/benefit unresolved. Route coluracetam reports through [[safety-interaction-matrix-for-multi-modal-stacks]] before treating visual, mood, focus, or choline-stack claims as molecule-specific cognition evidence.
