# Ipamorelin

## Bottom Line

Ipamorelin is a GH-secretagogue peptide with `preclinical/mechanism-only` cognition evidence in the current corpus. It appears frequently in CJC-1295 peptide stacks, where anecdotal signal is `strong` for sleep, recovery, and body composition, but the cognition-specific anecdote signal is `weak` and no human cognition trial is indexed.

## What It Is

Ipamorelin is a selective growth-hormone secretagogue often discussed as a cleaner GHRP-like peptide. In this wiki it belongs with [[growth-hormone-secretagogue-cognition-2026-scan]], [[cjc-1295]], [[ghrh]], and [[peptide-safety-and-sourcing-risk-map]] as a stack-confounded recovery/sleep molecule rather than a demonstrated cognitive enhancer.

## Medicinal Research Signal

The captured formal source is a pharmacology and development paper centered on rat and pituitary data. Ipamorelin showed GH-releasing potency and receptor selectivity in preclinical systems. That supports mechanism and target logic, but it is not a human cognition result and not even a direct human efficacy result in the current corpus.^[raw/articles/pubmed-9849822-ipamorelin-selective-gh-secretagogue.md]

No modern human cognition trial for ipamorelin itself is currently indexed. Its relevance comes from how it is used and discussed in GH-axis stacks, not from direct cognitive evidence.

June 2026 attribution gate: ipamorelin stays a stack-confounded sleep/recovery/body-composition signal unless a report separates it from CJC-1295, tesamorelin, MK-677, training, caloric deficit, stimulant use, and sleep recovery. Future reports need source/COA and lot, exact product or blend identity, route/formulation, dose timing, stack order, washout, fasted/training phase, sleep data, glucose/edema/BP/HR and IGF-1 context where medically available, adverse events, and objective cognition endpoints before any ranking comparison with [[semax]], [[selank]], [[cerebrolysin]], recovery-bound [[bpc-157]], [[bromantane]], [[cjc-1295]], or [[tesamorelin]].

## Anecdote Signal

The main anecdote signal is stack-based. The large CJC-1295 "no DAC" plus ipamorelin guide had visible score +313 at capture and emphasized recovery, deeper sleep/REM, leaner body composition, and collagen/skin repair. The thread includes dose and cycling lore plus adverse reports such as flushing, itching, lethargy, and elevated heart rate.^[raw/articles/reddit-bodyhackguide-cjc1295-no-dac-ipamorelin-guide-2025.md]

A second thread repeats sleep, recovery, muscle-preservation, and dieting-support claims, while the blend thread contributes a safety outlier involving tesamorelin/CJC-1295/ipamorelin and severe acute symptoms. These sources make ipamorelin community-salient, but they do not create objective cognition evidence.^[raw/articles/reddit-bodyhackguide-cjc1295-ipamorelin-2025.md]^[raw/articles/reddit-peptideguide-tesamorelin-cjc1295-mod-grf-129-ipamorelin-blend-2025.md]

## Safety And Status

Ipamorelin is not a low-risk cognition supplement. Sourcing, sterile handling, injection reactions, palpitations, edema/fluid shifts, glucose/insulin physiology, appetite, sleep changes, and blend identity all affect interpretation. Any perceived focus benefit could be downstream of sleep or recovery rather than direct cognition.
