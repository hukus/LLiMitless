# Allopregnanolone

## Bottom Line

Allopregnanolone is the clearest neurosteroid development lead for cognition in the current wiki, but the medicinal signal is still `low` for cognition efficacy because the human evidence is safety, tolerability, imaging, and active-trial status rather than a posted cognitive win. It is relevant to early Alzheimer disease biology and [[neurosteroid-and-endocrine-state-cognition-2026-scan]], not to broad nootropic use. Anecdotal cognition signal is `absent-to-weak` in the current captured corpus.

## What It Is

Allopregnanolone is an endogenous neurosteroid and GABAergic modulator being tested as a regenerative / neurosteroid intervention in Alzheimer disease contexts. In this wiki it belongs with [[neurosteroid-and-endocrine-state-cognition-2026-scan]], [[negative-evidence-watchlist-for-cognition-interventions]], and [[neuroinflammation-and-cognition]] as an active clinical-state lead whose biomarker story still needs cognition translation.

## Medicinal Research Signal

The first human anchor is a 24-person phase 1b/2a early-AD study using weekly IV allopregnanolone or placebo for 12 weeks plus follow-up. The primary endpoint was safety and tolerability; mild sedation helped define the maximum tolerated dose, and no adverse cognitive or MRI outcomes were evident. That supports dosing feasibility, not efficacy.^[raw/articles/pubmed-33344752-allopregnanolone-phase1b2a-ad.md]

The follow-up imaging analysis reported favorable exploratory hippocampal, white-matter, and functional-connectivity signals in the same small cohort, with strongest hippocampal signals around 4 mg and in APOE epsilon4 carriers. This is the reason the molecule remains important, but the evidence is still exploratory and structural rather than a confirmed cognitive endpoint.^[raw/articles/pubmed-35310526-allopregnanolone-imaging-follow-up.md]

The live REGEN-BRAIN registry is now the key test. As captured in 2026, it is a recruiting phase 2 randomized placebo-controlled mild-AD trial with estimated enrollment of 100, plasma p-tau217 positivity, weekly 4 mg IV dosing for 6 months, and cognition plus imaging endpoints. Until results post, medicinal cognition signal stays low and protocol-watch rather than positive.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct04838301-allopregnanolone-regen-brain-status.md]

The 2025 AD-depression review keeps allopregnanolone visible in mood and neurosteroid discussions, but mood, sleep, and affective-state arguments should not be treated as cognition efficacy unless direct cognitive outcomes move in a controlled trial.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40854505-allopregnanolone-ad-depression-review.md]

## Safety And Status

The current cognition-relevant safety gate is clinical supervision and route burden. The captured early-AD study used IV weekly dosing and identified sedation at higher doses as a practical dose-limiting issue. Over-the-counter hormone or neurosteroid analog claims should not be equated with the tested protocol.

Route allopregnanolone claims through [[safety-interaction-matrix-for-multi-modal-stacks]] when sedation, mood/depression, sleep, AD stage, APOE status, IV dosing, neurosteroid analogs, or imaging-only endpoints are part of the interpretation.

## Signal Judgment

Medicinal signal: `low` for direct cognition efficacy; `low-moderate` as an active AD biomarker/protocol lead.

Anecdotal signal: `absent-to-weak`.

Practical route: watch the REGEN-BRAIN readout; do not upgrade on imaging or mood plausibility alone.
