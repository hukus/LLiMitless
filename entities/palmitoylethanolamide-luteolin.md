# Palmitoylethanolamide Luteolin

Palmitoylethanolamide plus luteolin, often written PEA-LUT or PEALUT, is a co-ultramicronized lipid-mediator / flavonoid combination. In this wiki it is a post-viral neuroimmune brain-fog candidate, not proof that generic [[palmitoylethanolamide]], generic luteolin, quercetin-like flavonoids, or mast-cell supplements improve cognition in healthy adults.

Clinical-recovery signal: `low`. A 2022 long-COVID clinical neurophysiology study used co-ultramicronized PEA plus luteolin, described as 700 mg + 70 mg twice daily for 8 weeks, in patients with fatigue and cognitive problems. The paper reported restoration of TMS-derived GABA(B)-ergic activity and cortical plasticity measures. This is mechanistically relevant to brain fog, but it is not a blinded healthy-adult cognition trial.^[raw/articles/sciencedirect-2022-pea-lut-long-covid-gabab-plasticity.md]

Symptom signal: `low / uncontrolled`. A 2023 real-world retrospective analysis reported broad long-COVID symptom improvement during PEALUT exposure, including brain fog falling from 63.8% at baseline to 17.4% at 60 days and 11.1% at 90 days. Difficulty multitasking, memory loss, word-finding difficulty, and fatigue also decreased, but the design was retrospective, uncontrolled, and symptom-report based.^[raw/articles/mdpi-2023-pealut-long-covid-brain-fog-retrospective.md]

Relationship to PEA: [[palmitoylethanolamide]] already has a separate small healthy-adult memory / BDNF signal using formulated PEA alone. PEA-LUT should not borrow that healthy-adult signal automatically, because adding luteolin, changing particle size, changing product, and switching to post-viral illness all alter the claim.^[raw/articles/mdpi-2024-palmitoylethanolamide-healthy-cognition-bdnf.md]^[raw/articles/frontiers-2022-palmitoylethanolamide-cognitive-decline-review.md]

## Practical Interpretation

PEA-LUT belongs in [[post-viral-brain-fog-chemical-watchlist]] and [[neuroimmune-brain-fog-chemical-guide]] as a lower-friction but still unproven illness-state candidate. It is relevant because it directly names brain fog and has a plausible neuroimmune / GABA(B) physiology bridge. It should stay out of healthy-adult nootropic rankings until objective cognition endpoints and controlled replication exist.
