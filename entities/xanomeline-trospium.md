# Xanomeline-Trospium

## Bottom Line

Xanomeline-trospium, marketed as Cobenfy and developed as KarXT, is a clinically important muscarinic M1/M4 program, but the current cognition signal is still `low`. FDA approval and EMERGENT trials establish adult-schizophrenia symptom efficacy; they do not by themselves prove direct cognitive enhancement. Anecdotal cognition signal is absent in the current wiki corpus.

## What It Is

The combination pairs centrally active [[xanomeline]] with trospium chloride, a peripherally acting muscarinic antagonist intended to reduce peripheral cholinergic adverse effects. It makes the muscarinic branch clinically real while keeping the cognition question separate from PANSS symptom improvement.

## Medicinal Research Signal

The FDA approved Cobenfy on 2024-09-26 for schizophrenia in adults. The approval was based on two 5-week randomized placebo-controlled trials and the FDA highlighted nausea, indigestion, constipation, vomiting, hypertension, abdominal pain, diarrhea, tachycardia, dizziness, reflux, urinary retention, elevated heart rate, decreased gastric motility, angioedema, and liver/kidney restrictions. That is a regulatory and safety anchor, not a cognition endpoint.^[raw/articles/fda-cobenfy-approval-2024.md]

EMERGENT-3 reported an 8.4-point greater reduction in PANSS total score than placebo at week 5, with Cohen d 0.60. The adverse-event profile remained mainly cholinergic and gastrointestinal, with nausea, dyspepsia, vomiting, constipation, hypertension, and transient blood-pressure/heart-rate increases more common than placebo.^[raw/articles/pubmed-38691387-xanomeline-trospium-schizophrenia-rct.md]

The pooled EMERGENT analysis reported a 9.9-point least-squares mean PANSS total-score difference at week 5 and Cohen d 0.65, with consistency across demographic subgroups. This is the compact modern efficacy summary for KarXT/Cobenfy, but it is still symptom-first rather than cognition-first.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39488504-xanomeline-trospium-pooled-emergent.md]

The 2026 cognition review argues for pro-cognitive effects across xanomeline and xanomeline-trospium studies, but that claim is downstream of mixed clinical populations and conflict-disclosed synthesis. The wiki should treat it as a reason to track cognition-first trials, not as an upgrade to a settled enhancer.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41418563-xanomeline-trospium-cognitive-impairment-review.md]

## Safety And Status

Cobenfy is an approved prescription drug for adult schizophrenia. The label-relevant safety gates are not trivial: cholinergic and anticholinergic effects, cardiovascular changes, urinary retention, motility issues, and hepatic/renal restrictions matter before any cognition-focused interpretation.

## Signal Judgment

Medicinal signal: `clinical for schizophrenia symptoms`, `low for direct cognition`.

Anecdotal signal: `absent-to-weak`.

Practical route: disease-first muscarinic comparator and regulatory anchor. Do not treat FDA approval as proof of cognitive enhancement.
