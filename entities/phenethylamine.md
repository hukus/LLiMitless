# Phenethylamine / beta-PEA

Phenethylamine is the trace amine also called 2-phenethylamine, 2-phenylethylamine, beta-phenethylamine, beta-PEA, or PEA HCl in supplement contexts. This page covers the simple trace amine, not [[palmitoylethanolamide]] / PEA, [[phenylalanine]], [[p-synephrine]], [[higenamine-hordenine-octopamine]], BMPEA, amphetamine, [[nn-dimethylphenethylamine-eria-jarensis|eria-jarensis / N,N-DMPEA product labels]], or the broad substituted-phenethylamine drug family.^[raw/articles/pubchem-2026-phenethylamine-identity.md]

Medicinal cognition signal: `very low/risk-first`. The captured formal sources do not show a controlled healthy-adult cognition benefit for oral phenethylamine. The mechanistic case is real but indirect: trace-amine and TAAR1 literature places beta-phenylethylamine near monoamine-transporter and dopamine-state modulation, while mouse metabolomics identifies phenethylamine as a MAO-B substrate in the paraventricular thalamic nucleus and notes that it crosses the blood-brain barrier. That makes PEA biologically plausible as a short-lived stimulant-state molecule, not a validated cognition enhancer.^[raw/articles/frontiers-2016-trace-amines-taar1-review.md]^[raw/articles/nature-2022-phenethylamine-maob-pvt.md]

The most important practical gate is metabolism and interaction risk. Because phenethylamine is a MAO-B substrate, forum logic that combines PEA with [[selegiline]], [[rasagiline]], broader MAOIs, or MAO-B "potentiation" should be treated as pharmacology, not ordinary supplementation. Any apparent effect can be arousal, mood, appetite, euphoria, anxiety, or cardiovascular state rather than improved working memory, learning, or executive control.

Supplement-identity signal: `high burden`. FDA's BMPEA page is not a PEA page, but it is directly relevant to the market boundary: substituted phenethylamine analogs can be presented in dietary-supplement contexts without the status of ordinary nutrients. A 2016 in-vitro proximal-tubule study also found that 2-phenethylamine alone was not strongly toxic in that model, but high-concentration combination with N,N-dimethyl-beta-phenethylamine produced evidence of potent cell injury. That is a stack-safety warning, not clinical incidence evidence.^[raw/articles/fda-2023-bmpea-dietary-supplements.md]^[raw/articles/sage-2016-phenethylamine-dimethyl-beta-pea-nephrotoxicity.md]

Anecdote signal: `weak-moderate, fast-effect and identity-confused`. Reddit reports include focus, attention, and "moved the needle" claims around 500 mg PEA HCl, but also flushing, strange body feelings, short trial windows, and naming confusion around beta-phenethylamine versus phenethylamine. The anecdote layer is strong enough to keep PEA in the chemical heat map, but not strong enough to rank it as a healthy-adult nootropic.^[raw/articles/reddit-2019-2026-phenethylamine-focus-threads.md]

## Identity Gates

- Write out phenethylamine when possible. `PEA` is ambiguous and already maps to [[palmitoylethanolamide]] in other cognition/brain-fog pages.
- Separate phenethylamine from [[phenylalanine]] / DLPA precursor logic, [[p-synephrine]], hordenine, octopamine, BMPEA, [[nn-dimethylphenethylamine-eria-jarensis|N,N-DMPEA / eria-jarensis products]], amphetamine, and multi-stimulant preworkouts.
- Track salt form, assayed milligrams, product COA, lot identity, caffeine/yohimbine/DMHA/DMAA co-use, appetite/calorie effects, sleep timing, BP/HR, anxiety/panic, bipolar/psychosis vulnerability, and cardiovascular history.
- Treat MAO-B inhibitors, nonselective MAOIs, serotonergic drugs, stimulants, and adrenergic stacks as major stop-or-medical-review gates.
- Do not count "felt stimulation" as improved cognition unless task accuracy, working memory, sustained attention, learning, or delayed retention is measured.

## Practical Ranking

Phenethylamine deserves coverage because it is chemically central to trace-amine lore, appears in nootropic anecdotes, and sits at the collision point of MAO-B, TAAR1, stimulant-product identity, and PEA abbreviation confusion. It should rank as `risk-first / anecdote-tracked`, below cleaner caffeine-class and prescription-comparator evidence. Its most useful wiki role is to prevent phenethylamine, [[phenylalanine]], [[palmitoylethanolamide]], BMPEA, [[nn-dimethylphenethylamine-eria-jarensis|eria-jarensis/N,N-DMPEA products]], and MAO-B potentiation anecdotes from being collapsed into one claim.
