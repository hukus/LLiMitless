# Bacopa

Bacopa monnieri is a standardized botanical cognition candidate with older controlled human memory evidence and newer standardization / bioavailability context. It belongs in the botanical branch, not the stimulant or acute-focus branch.

## Medicinal Signal

- Formal signal: `low-moderate`; stronger for memory / older or impaired phenotypes than for acute performance.
- The foundational standardized Bacopa monniera RCT in age-associated memory impairment used 125 mg twice daily for 12 weeks and improved mental control, logical memory, and paired-associate learning versus placebo.^[raw/articles/pubmed-20703343-bacopa-aami-rct.md]
- Newer review and registry material keeps the branch active, but it also emphasizes the need to lock extract identity, bacoside profile, dose, and duration before comparing products.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40507208-bacopa-monnieri-preclinical-clinical-bioavailability.md]^[raw/articles/ctgov-nct04927338-bacopa-gwi.md]
- The 2026 plant-active-substance NMA places a Bacopa compound among the better-ranked plant substances for executive and language domains, but the evidence remains probabilistic and product-dependent.^[raw/articles/pmc-12864429-plant-active-substances-healthy-older-adults-nma.md]

## Current Read

Bacopa is a memory-oriented standardized-extract candidate. It should be tracked beside [[grape-extract]], [[ginkgo]], [[ginseng]], [[ashwagandha]], and [[saffron]], with product identity treated as part of the evidence rather than a nuisance variable.
