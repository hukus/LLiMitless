# NSI-189

NSI-189 is an investigational neurogenic-antidepressant candidate that nootropic communities often discuss as a memory, mood, or emotionality compound. The wiki should keep it in a mood-first translational lane, not a validated cognition-enhancement lane.

## Medicinal Signal

- Formal signal: `low` for cognition; human evidence is depression-context and not cognition-specific.
- The phase 1b randomized placebo-controlled study is the main human anchor. It keeps NSI-189 out of the pure-mystery bucket, but it does not establish healthy-adult cognitive enhancement.^[raw/articles/pubmed-27528461-nsi-189-phase1b-depression.md]
- The cognitive material is secondary to the antidepressant development program.
