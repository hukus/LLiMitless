# Sceletium Tortuosum

Sceletium tortuosum, often sold as kanna or the standardized extract Zembrin, is a South African botanical with serotonin-linked and PDE4-related pharmacology. For the wiki, the important distinction is product identity: `Zembrin` is not the same evidence unit as generic kanna powder, tincture, vape, or mixed extract.

Medicinal signal: `low`. The best cognition evidence is small and product-specific. A 21-person healthy middle-aged crossover trial of 25 mg/day Zembrin for three weeks reported better cognitive flexibility and executive function, but no broad memory, processing-speed, psychomotor-speed, reaction, or attention win. A 60-person young-adult trial found better complex reactive performance and reactive agility after eight days, but null mood, alertness, energy, visual tracking, and simpler reaction outcomes.^[raw/articles/pubmed-25389443-zembrin-pde4-healthy-cognition.md]^[raw/articles/jscr-2020-sceletium-tortuosum-reactive-performance.md]

Safety signal: short-term Zembrin looks reasonably tolerable in small studies, including a three-month 8 mg/day and 25 mg/day safety trial, but long-term safety is not established and serotonin/PDE4 interaction logic matters.^[raw/articles/sage-2013-zembrin-safety-tolerability.md]^[raw/articles/alzdiscovery-2026-sceletium-tortuosum-cognitive-vitality.md]

## Signal Split

| Layer | Signal | Readout |
|---|---|---|
| Standardized Zembrin cognition trials | `low` medicinal signal | Small healthy-adult trials; possible complex-task benefit |
| Generic kanna products | `uncataloged` medicinal signal | Product identity too variable to infer from Zembrin |
| Community survey | `moderate-strong` anecdote signal | ACX 2020 found 37 kanna users, 20 using Zembrin, with Zembrin standing out in adjusted ratings |
| Safety | `moderate` caution | Short-term standardized-extract tolerability, but serotonergic/PDE4 interaction concerns and limited long-term data |

## Practical Interpretation

Sceletium belongs between [[standardized-herbal-extracts-for-cognition]] and [[pde-camp-creb-cognition-2026-scan]]. It has more human signal than many herbal curiosities, but it does not clear the bar for a broad cognitive enhancer. Treat it as a product-specific, complex-task candidate with serotonergic and PDE4 interaction gates.
