# Ashwagandha

Ashwagandha is a standardized botanical candidate whose cognition signal is currently more specific than the broad stress-adaptogen reputation. The best current cognition anchor is a small MCI trial using a defined extract with Sominone.

## Medicinal Signal

- Formal signal: `low`; human-positive but small and product-specific.
- A pilot RCT in adults with MCI used 250 mg/day standardized ashwagandha extract with Sominone for 60 days and reported improvements in immediate memory, general memory, working memory, and visuospatial processing.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40099725-ashwagandha-mci-rct.md]
- The result is cleaner than generic wellness claims, but still too small to support broad healthy-adult enhancement.
- Registry context keeps the branch active, but product identity and adverse-event monitoring remain central.^[raw/articles/ctgov-nct04092647-ashwagandha-cognitive-dysfunction.md]

## Current Read

Ashwagandha belongs in [[standardized-herbal-extracts-for-cognition]] as an MCI/product-specific candidate. It should not be merged with generic anxiety, sleep, testosterone, or stress anecdotes unless objective cognition endpoints and product identity are available.
