# Emoxypine / Mexidol

Emoxypine is 2-ethyl-6-methyl-3-hydroxypyridine; Mexidol is the emoxypine succinate regional-drug identity. PubChem and FDA GSRS separate the base molecule from the succinate salt, and GSRS explicitly does not imply regulatory review or approval. This page therefore treats `emoxypine`, `emoxypine succinate`, and `Mexidol` as a linked identity cluster, not as ordinary vitamin B6, generic antioxidants, or U.S.-approved products.^[raw/articles/pubchem-2026-emoxypine-mexidol-identity.md]^[raw/articles/fda-gsrs-2026-emoxypine-succinate-identity.md]

## Bottom Line

Medicinal signal: `low-moderate/clinical-state, region-specific`. The strongest formal cognition-relevant source is the MEMO phase 3 randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled chronic cerebral ischemia trial, where sequential Mexidol improved MoCA change more than placebo at day 75 (+4.22 vs +2.17) and also favored DSST, fatigue, anxiety, autonomic, and quality-of-life measures. That is meaningful for vascular/cognitive-impairment context, but it is not healthy-adult enhancement proof.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-2026-nct06834490-mexidol-memo-results.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-36412160-mexidol-memo-age-subanalysis.md]

Anecdotal signal: `strong salience / low cognition confidence`. Reddit reports cluster around anxiety, hangovers, alcohol recovery, relaxation, blood-flow/oxygenation feelings, and brain fog, with no-effect, mood-worsening, lethargy, diarrhea, and chronic-use concerns. That makes it a monitoring priority, not a recommendation upgrade.^[raw/articles/reddit-2023-emoxypine-thread.md]

Practical route: treat [[emoxypine-mexidol]] like [[picamilon]] and [[pyritinol]] in the regional-drug lane: potentially real clinical-state evidence, strong community salience, and enough regulatory/geography ambiguity that sourcing and indication matter before any ranking.

## Identity And Regulatory Guardrails

Identity matters. PubChem CID 114681 is emoxypine base (C8H11NO, MW 137.18); PubChem CID 122298 is Mexidol / emoxypine succinate (C12H17NO5, MW 255.27). FDA GSRS maps EMOXYPINE SUCCINATE to UNII 2R985002CT and EMOXYPINE base to UNII V247P5H4E1, while warning that UNII availability does not imply approval.^[raw/articles/pubchem-2026-emoxypine-mexidol-identity.md]^[raw/articles/fda-gsrs-2026-emoxypine-succinate-identity.md]

The U.S. regulatory check in this slice found identity records but no matching openFDA Drugs@FDA entries for `EMOXYPINE` or brand `MEXIDOL`. That is a negative database check rather than a universal legal conclusion, but it supports keeping U.S. product claims separate from Russian/regional prescription-drug evidence.^[raw/articles/openfda-2026-emoxypine-mexidol-drugsfda-no-match.md]

## Formal Evidence

The best formal cognition-relevant record is NCT06834490 / MEMO: 318 participants with chronic cerebral ischemia, randomized double-blind phase 3, 14 days IV Mexidol 500 mg daily followed by 60 days Mexidol FORTE 250 mg three times daily. ClinicalTrials.gov reports day-75 MoCA 25.72 vs 23.95 and baseline change +4.22 vs +2.17 for Mexidol versus placebo, with DSST change +9.84 vs +5.40 and no deaths or serious adverse events. This gives a real clinical-state signal, but it is sponsor-linked, regional, disease-context, and not a clean healthy-volunteer cognition study.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-2026-nct06834490-mexidol-memo-results.md]

The PubMed age subanalysis of MEMO reports stronger cognitive, emotional, motor, asthenia, anxiety, vegetative, and quality-of-life improvement with Mexidol than placebo in chronic brain ischemia with cognitive impairment. The 76-90 subgroup was only n=13, so the age-extension claim should stay cautious.^[raw/articles/pubmed-36412160-mexidol-memo-age-subanalysis.md]

Two recovery-state records broaden the medical context without proving nootropic use. NCT06221826 was a small open-label rehabilitation trial in acute cerebral failure: day-66 MoCA was 23.8 vs 22.9 for Mexidol+standard rehabilitation versus standard rehabilitation, but the study was small, open, and baseline-imbalanced on attentiveness/performance. NCT06437626 / MIR was a 304-person randomized double-blind ischemic-stroke phase 3 trial with a primary disability endpoint; its secondary MoCA change was +4 vs +3. These are vascular/recovery signals, not broad enhancement signals.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-2026-nct06221826-mexidol-acute-cerebral-failure-rehab-results.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-2026-nct06437626-mexidol-mir-stroke-results.md]

## Mechanism And Interaction Layer

The 2022 review frames emoxypine / Mexidol as antihypoxic, antioxidant, membrane-modulating, neuroprotective, and cardioprotective, but it is a broad plausibility review and calls for more regulatory-standard clinical work. It is useful for mechanism mapping, not for upgrading cognition confidence by itself.^[raw/articles/pmc-9389226-emoxypine-review.md]

The 2023 transporter study adds a stacking caution: EMHPS was not an ABCB1 or SLCO1B1 substrate in vitro, but it inhibited transporter activity. The authors judged systemic ABCB1/SLCO1B1 inhibition unlikely to be clinically significant while noting that gastrointestinal ABCB1 inhibition needs in vivo testing. That matters for users combining it with other drugs or gray-zone nootropics.^[raw/articles/pubmed-38004395-emhps-transporter-interaction.md]

## Safety And Sourcing

MEMO looks short-term reassuring in its registry table: no deaths, no serious adverse events, and fewer participants with other adverse events in the Mexidol arm than placebo. The event list still includes GI symptoms, dizziness, rash, dysgeusia, urinary findings, and respiratory/itching complaints. MIR, in a sicker stroke population, reports deaths and serious adverse events in both arms, with 2/152 deaths and 6/152 serious adverse-event participants on Mexidol versus 1/152 and 4/152 on placebo. Those events do not automatically imply causality, but they keep the clinical-state safety context distinct from casual supplement-like use.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-2026-nct06834490-mexidol-memo-results.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-2026-nct06437626-mexidol-mir-stroke-results.md]

Sourcing is not a footnote. The formal evidence is for named Mexidol regimens, including IV-to-oral sequential therapy. Internet emoxypine powder or capsule claims may not map to the tested product, salt form, dose, route, or medical supervision.

## Signal Judgment

For the molecule atlas, [[emoxypine-mexidol]] deserves standalone inclusion because the MEMO record is stronger than a generic watchlist mention. The signal should still be routed as `clinical-state/regional`, not `healthy-adult nootropic`: CCI and stroke-recovery data do not establish benefit in rested healthy adults, and Reddit salience mostly tracks anxiety, alcohol recovery, and subjective clarity.
