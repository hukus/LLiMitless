# BI 409306

BI 409306 is a selective phosphodiesterase 9A (PDE9A) inhibitor from Boehringer Ingelheim. It is the clearest human PDE9 probe in the wiki: early studies showed rapid CSF penetration and cGMP target engagement, but the later schizophrenia and prodromal/mild AD efficacy trials were both negative for cognition. That makes it a useful translator compound, not a validated enhancer. See [[pde9-pde10-cgmp-cognition-2026-scan]] and [[pharmacology-comparator-map]].

## Overview

- Drug class: oral PDE9A inhibitor.
- Main rationale: elevate cGMP and support cGMP-linked plasticity / CREB signaling.
- Human status: target engagement is real; cognition efficacy is not.
- Best interpretation: a negative anchor for PDE9 cognition claims.

## Key facts

- First-in-human work in healthy males showed rapid absorption, short half-life, and acceptable tolerability.
- Proof-of-mechanism work in healthy males showed dose-dependent CSF cGMP increases.
- The schizophrenia phase II learn-and-confirm trial did not improve MCCB or SCoRS scores.
- The prodromal/mild AD phase II studies did not improve NTB, CDR-SB, ADAS-Cog11, or ADCS-ADL outcomes.
