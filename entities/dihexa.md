# Dihexa

Dihexa is an AngIV-analog peptide that sits at the sharp speculative edge of the peptide-cognition map. It deserves continued tracking because the route-specific animal story is unusually explicit and the community keeps returning to it as a high-upside "rewiring" candidate. It should not sit in the strongest current anecdote-priority tier: as of 2026-05-24, I still did not surface a published human cognition trial or a visible ClinicalTrials.gov development program for Dihexa.

June 2026 read: Dihexa has `moderate high-upside rewiring salience, low-to-very-low human confidence`. It should be visible in nootropic-peptide routing because the lore is unusually persistent and mechanism-specific, but it should be de-ranked for practical cognition claims because human exposure is missing, route lore is unsettled, FDA flags no identified human-exposure data for Dihexa acetate, and the founding oral/HGF-c-Met story is now integrity-caveated.

Evidence-integrity update: the foundational 2013 JPET paper that anchors oral and intraperitoneal rodent procognitive claims now has a 2021 Notice of Concern, and the 2014 HGF/c-Met mechanistic paper has a 2025 retraction notice. This does not erase the independent 2021 APP/PS1 and sciatic-nerve animal papers, but it means Dihexa's mechanism and oral efficacy story should be read as provisional, not cleanly validated.^[raw/articles/pubmed-34551989-dihexa-2013-notice-of-concern.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40312093-dihexa-hgf-cmet-retraction.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-34827486-dihexa-app-ps1-memory.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-34703584-dihexa-sciatic-nerve-repair.md]

## Evidence Split

- Foundational formal signal: the original AngIV-analog paper describes Dihexa as an orally active, BBB-permeant procognitive / antidementia candidate in rodent models.^[raw/articles/pubmed-23055539-dihexa-procognitive-antidementia.md]
- AD-model extension: the 2021 APP/PS1 mouse paper reports better Morris water maze performance, synaptophysin support, neuron preservation, lower inflammatory signaling, and PI3K/AKT-pathway activation.^[raw/articles/pubmed-34827486-dihexa-app-ps1-memory.md]
- Recovery-adjacent extension: the 2021 sciatic-nerve paper keeps Dihexa inside a broader repair / regeneration story rather than a direct healthy-adult nootropic story.^[raw/articles/pubmed-34703584-dihexa-sciatic-nerve-repair.md]
- Mechanistic framing: the HGF/c-Met review still explains why Dihexa remains unusually interesting and unusually safety-uncertain, but the now-retracted 2014 causal HGF/c-Met paper should no longer be treated as a settled mechanism anchor.^[raw/articles/pubmed-25649658-hgf-cmet-dihexa-alzheimers.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40312093-dihexa-hgf-cmet-retraction.md]
- Human ceiling: no human cognition trial, human PK dossier, or credible clinical safety series surfaced in the current pass.

## Administration / Route Read

There is no clinically recommended human administration process for Dihexa. The cleanest answer to "how is it usually administered?" is split by evidence layer:

| Layer | Route read | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| Formal cognition animal work | Oral delivery is the main route with cognition-behavior precedent; the foundational rodent paper described Dihexa as orally active and BBB-permeant, and the ADDF report summarizes oral and i.p. routes as effective in scopolamine-impaired rats, with oral dosing also tested in aged rats. | Best formal route signal, but animal-only and now Notice-of-Concern-caveated. |
| Other animal work | Dihexa has also been used i.c.v., i.v., and i.p. in animal pharmacology, and in the sciatic-nerve repair paper it was administered locally via hydrogel, intraperitoneally, and into gastrocnemius muscle. | Useful for research-route context, not a cognition protocol and not a human process. |
| Current regulatory read | FDA says it has not identified human exposure data for drug products containing Dihexa acetate administered by any route. | Blocks any claim that a human route is validated. |
| Internet / community protocol pages | Online protocol pages conflict: one frames oral administration as the primary published animal-behavior route and says no standardized human protocol exists, while another labels subcutaneous use as the community "primary" route. | Treat as market/community lore, not medical recommendation. |
| Anecdote layer | Captured Reddit discussion centers on oral Dihexa feeling subtle or unclear, then pivots into nasal, DMSO/transdermal, sublingual, or enteric-route speculation. | Evidence of route uncertainty rather than route efficacy. |

Practical wiki wording: Dihexa is usually discussed as an orally active research compound because the cognition literature was designed around oral rodent activity. In human gray-market use, reported or promoted routes are mixed - oral, sublingual, subcutaneous, intranasal, and DMSO/transdermal - with no validated process, no human PK, and unresolved safety/sourcing risk.^[raw/articles/pubmed-23055539-dihexa-procognitive-antidementia.md]^[raw/articles/alzdiscovery-2021-dihexa-route-safety.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-34703584-dihexa-sciatic-nerve-repair.md]^[raw/articles/fda-bulk-drug-substances-peptide-risk-2026.md]^[raw/articles/peptideprotocolwiki-2026-dihexa-route-no-human-protocol.md]^[raw/articles/peptidewiki-2026-dihexa-subq-community-protocol-claim.md]^[raw/articles/reddit-2026-04-29-dihexa-thread.md]

## Frontier Read

Dihexa still merits a visible watch position, but now as a de-ranked frontier hypothesis rather than a front-rank nootropic lead. The reason to keep it visible is that a coherent synaptogenic mechanism, repeated rodent memory signals, and persistent community salience have all survived multiple years without disappearing. The reason to de-rank it is stronger: missing human exposure, route uncertainty, FDA no-human-exposure visibility, and integrity caveats on the founding oral/HGF-c-Met story.^[raw/articles/pubmed-23055539-dihexa-procognitive-antidementia.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-34827486-dihexa-app-ps1-memory.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-34551989-dihexa-2013-notice-of-concern.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40312093-dihexa-hgf-cmet-retraction.md]^[raw/articles/reddit-2026-04-29-dihexa-vs-semax-ultimate-peptide-nootropic-showdown.md]

The important distinction is `upcoming in community imagination` versus `upcoming in formal development`. Dihexa currently looks like the first and not the second. The current FDA bulk-compounding page reinforces that by saying Dihexa acetate has no identified human exposure data, which keeps the branch in a research-compound bucket rather than a clinically translated one.^[raw/articles/fda-bulk-drug-substances-peptide-risk-2026.md]
