# Dihexa

Dihexa is an AngIV-analog peptide that is often discussed as a synaptogenic / HGF-cMet-pathway candidate for memory and cognition. In the wiki it belongs on the speculative edge of the map: the preclinical signal is interesting, but human evidence is absent in the searches I ran.

Key facts:

- The foundational procognitive paper describes Dihexa as an orally active, BBB-permeant AngIV analog with antidementia activity in rodent models.^[raw/articles/pubmed-23055539-dihexa-procognitive-antidementia.md]
- The 2021 APP/PS1 mouse paper reports improved Morris water maze performance, synaptophysin and neuron-preservation effects, and PI3K/AKT activation.^[raw/articles/pubmed-34827486-dihexa-app-ps1-memory.md]
- The 2021 rat sciatic-nerve repair paper extends Dihexa into recovery biology rather than direct cognition.^[raw/articles/pubmed-34703584-dihexa-sciatic-nerve-repair.md]
- I found no published human cognition trial and no registry signal in the searches I ran, so the translational status is still preclinical-only.

Relevant raw PubMed notes: `raw/articles/pubmed-23055539-dihexa-procognitive-antidementia.md`, `raw/articles/pubmed-34827486-dihexa-app-ps1-memory.md`, `raw/articles/pubmed-34703584-dihexa-sciatic-nerve-repair.md`, and `raw/articles/pubmed-25649658-hgf-cmet-dihexa-alzheimers.md`.
The strongest data are still animal and mechanistic, especially around memory recovery, synaptogenesis, and PI3K/AKT signaling. That makes Dihexa useful as a high-interest exploratory node, not as established clinical guidance.^[raw/articles/pubmed-23055539-dihexa-procognitive-antidementia.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-34827486-dihexa-app-ps1-memory.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-34703584-dihexa-sciatic-nerve-repair.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-25649658-hgf-cmet-dihexa-alzheimers.md]
On the safety side, the current FDA bulk-compounding page says dihexa acetate has no identified human exposure data, which keeps the branch in a research-compound bucket rather than a human-use bucket.^[raw/articles/fda-bulk-drug-substances-peptide-risk-2026.md]
