# Reboxetine

Reboxetine is a selective norepinephrine reuptake inhibitor marketed as an antidepressant in some jurisdictions but not approved for marketing in the United States by FDA. In this wiki it belongs in [[nonstimulant-noradrenergic-attention-agent-watchlist]] as a mechanistic NRI comparator beside [[atomoxetine]], [[bupropion]], and [[viloxazine]].^[raw/articles/pubmed-30000825-reboxetine-review-status.md]

## Bottom Line

Cognition signal: `low but direct`. A small 2021 healthy-human crossover study reported that acute reboxetine improved motor sequence learning, Stroop attention/interference performance, and n-back working memory versus placebo. That makes reboxetine a real replication-worthy NRI probe, but not a mature nootropic evidence base.^[raw/articles/oxford-2021-reboxetine-motor-learning-attention-working-memory.md]

Social/affective signal: `probe only`. A tiny flatmate-pair study found a trend toward more assertive/command behavior after two weeks of reboxetine, which is useful for noradrenergic state interpretation but not an objective cognition endpoint.^[raw/articles/pubmed-16944408-reboxetine-flatmate-social-behavior.md]

Safety/status: status- and tolerability-gated. The UK SmPC and PubMed status note keep reboxetine outside the normal U.S. prescription comparator stack; practical concerns include heart-rate/BP context, insomnia, dry mouth, constipation, sweating, tachycardia, dizziness/vertigo, urinary difficulty or retention, and sexual adverse effects.^[raw/articles/emc-2025-edronax-reboxetine-smpc.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-30000825-reboxetine-review-status.md]

## Evidence Layers

| Layer | Current read | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| Healthy adults | Small crossover signal for motor learning, attention, and working memory | Interesting NRI probe; needs replication and sleep/arousal control |
| Social behavior | Tiny assertiveness/social-behavior probe | Not cognition proof |
| Anecdote | Direct nootropic-community packet not captured yet | Do not borrow bupropion or atomoxetine anecdotes |
| Safety/status | Non-U.S.-approved antidepressant status in this pass | Practical status burden is higher than atomoxetine/viloxazine U.S. labels |

## Current Read

Reboxetine is the most "mechanistic probe" member of this branch: it has a direct healthy-human cognition signal but weaker current community salience and a less favorable U.S. availability/status story. Keep it as a class comparator until larger healthy-adult replication or a clinically relevant responder phenotype appears.
