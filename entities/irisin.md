# Irisin

## Bottom Line

Irisin is the most human-visible exerkine cognition biomarker in the current corpus, but it is not a validated cognitive intervention. Medicinal signal is `biomarker/association-only`: 2025 human data link lower CSF/serum irisin to AD/MCI and global cognition, and a meta-analysis reports a modest global-cognition correlation, but no direct human irisin cognition treatment trial is indexed. Anecdotal signal is `absent` in the current corpus.

## What It Is

Irisin is a muscle-derived glycosylated polypeptide hormone often discussed as an exercise-linked muscle-brain mediator. In this wiki it belongs with [[exerkine-myokine-cognition-2026-scan]], [[clusterin]], [[pf4]], [[cathepsin-b]], and [[exercise-creatine-metabolic-peptide-triad]] as a biomarker and mediator hypothesis node.

## Medicinal Research Signal

The strongest human source is a 2025 observational cohort in biomarker-confirmed dementia-related symptomatology. CSF and serum irisin were lower in AD and MCI than subjective memory complaint controls, and both compartments correlated with global cognition plus memory, executive function, attention, visuospatial ability, and language. This supports irisin as a potential cognitive biomarker of AD progression, not a treatment.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40560607-correlation-between-irisin-and-cognitive-functions-in-alzheimer-dementia.md]

The 2025 systematic review and meta-analysis is positive but modest. Across human correlation studies, global cognition correlated with irisin at r = 0.26, but subdomain analyses were mostly null for immediate memory, short-term memory, executive function, attention, language, and visuospatial ability. That pattern is useful for biomarker mapping and weak for therapeutic ranking.^[raw/articles/pmc-12284321-association-between-irisin-level-and-cognitive-function-systematic-review-meta-analysis.md]

The 2025 therapeutic-target review frames irisin as relevant to learning, memory, hippocampal neuron growth, and muscle-brain signaling, but it is a hypothesis-shaping review rather than direct clinical efficacy evidence.^[raw/articles/pmc-12315660-irisin-emerging-therapeutic-targets-for-cognitive-impairment-related-diseases.md]

## Safety And Status

Direct irisin administration should be treated as research biology, not a nootropic protocol. The proven actionable layer is exercise and metabolic health, while irisin remains a candidate mediator and disease biomarker.

## Signal Judgment

Medicinal signal: `biomarker/association-only` for cognition.

Anecdotal signal: `absent`.

Practical route: biomarker and exercise-mediator hypothesis; no direct dosing recommendation.
