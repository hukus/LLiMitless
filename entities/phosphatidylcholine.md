# Phosphatidylcholine / Lecithin

Phosphatidylcholine is a membrane phospholipid and major lecithin constituent. It belongs in the wiki as a product-form-sensitive choline donor, not as interchangeable evidence for [[alpha-gpc]], [[citicoline]], [[lysolecithin]], or [[choline-bitartrate]].

Medicinal cognition signal: `low/contested`. The direct healthy-adult evidence is old and mixed: a 1983 20 g lecithin crossover study raised plasma choline but did not improve memory or psychomotor speed, while a 1993 80-student phosphatidylcholine study found a narrow serial-learning signal at a very high 25 g dose, apparently concentrated among slow learners. The dementia/cognitive-impairment Cochrane review did not support lecithin as a treatment despite one subjective-memory-problem outlier.^[raw/articles/pubmed-6346908-lecithin-memory-normal-adults.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-9377589-phosphatidylcholine-explicit-memory.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-12917896-lecithin-dementia-cognitive-impairment-cochrane.md]

Egg-yolk phospholipid choline is a separate product-matrix signal. A 2023 12-week older-adult Japanese trial found better verbal-memory change and delayed correct hits with 300 mg/day egg-yolk choline, but the formulation included a mixture of phosphatidylcholine, lysophosphatidylcholine, and Alpha-GPC forms, was sponsor-linked, and had mixed secondary outcomes. A 2019 absorption trial found higher plasma choline response from egg-yolk phospholipid choline than choline bitartrate, but that was a biomarker study rather than a cognition trial.^[raw/articles/bmc-2023-egg-yolk-choline-cognitive-functions.md]^[raw/articles/pmc-6893749-egg-yolk-phospholipid-choline-absorption.md]

Safety / product-form signal: `form-sensitive`. In a randomized trial comparing choline forms, phosphatidylcholine capsules and eggs did not significantly raise TMAO or platelet reactivity over 4 weeks, while [[choline-bitartrate]] tablets did. That supports separating free choline salts from phosphatidylcholine, not calling phosphatidylcholine risk-free.^[raw/articles/pmc-8410632-choline-forms-tmao-response.md]

## Signal Split

| Layer | Signal | Readout |
|---|---|---|
| Healthy adults, acute lecithin | `negative/low` medicinal | 20 g lecithin raised plasma choline but did not improve memory or psychomotor speed |
| Healthy adults, high-dose phosphatidylcholine | `low-positive/narrow` medicinal | 25 g phosphatidylcholine improved serial-learning explicit memory, likely slow-learner driven |
| Dementia / cognitive impairment | `negative/unsupported` medicinal | Cochrane review found no clear clinical benefit for dementia |
| Egg-yolk phospholipid choline | `low-positive/product-matrix` medicinal | 300 mg/day egg-yolk choline improved selected verbal-memory outcomes in older adults |
| Biomarker / absorption | `low-positive` | Egg-yolk phospholipid drink produced higher plasma choline response than choline bitartrate |
| Anecdote | `weak/mixed` | Community discussion mostly compares forms, racetam-headache support, TMAO concern, and mild/no-effect reports |

## Practical Interpretation

Route phosphatidylcholine through [[choline-donor-cognition-signal-map]] and [[cholinergic-state-restoration-and-cognition]]. It is not a broad healthy-adult enhancer. The best positive human read is narrow and dose-heavy, the dementia evidence is negative, and the cleaner recent signal belongs to egg-yolk phospholipid choline as a food-matrix formulation.
