# ZEB85

ZEB85 is a fully human TrkB agonist antibody / biologic comparator in the BDNF-mimetic branch. It is not a small-molecule nootropic and should not be collapsed with [[7-8-dihydroxyflavone]], [[r13]], [[lm22a-4]], or [[deoxygedunin]].

Medicinal cognition signal: `preclinical-only`. The captured ZEB85 paper reports that the antibody modulated hippocampal neuron architecture, activity, and synaptic plasticity in preclinical systems and prevented beta-amyloid toxicity in cultured hippocampal neurons in a manner comparable to BDNF. It does not provide a human cognition outcome.^[raw/articles/pubmed-35845610-zeb85-trkb-agonist-antibody.md]

Mechanism signal: `best specificity comparator, still translationally early`. ZEB85 matters because the same literature warns that several small-molecule TrkB agonist claims lack adequate specificity. The antibody lane is positioned as a more specific way to activate TrkB without p75NTR binding, but delivery, dosing, safety, and human CNS translation remain open.^[raw/articles/pubmed-37488840-trkb-agonist-caution-review.md]^[raw/articles/pmc-8398766-small-molecule-trkb-agonist-limitations.md]

Human translation signal: `absent in captured registry search`. No named human cognition program is captured for this branch, so ZEB85 remains a comparator for target-specificity logic rather than a ranked intervention.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-trkb-bdnf-mimetic-registry-search-2026.md]
