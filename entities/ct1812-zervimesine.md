# CT1812 / Zervimesine

## Bottom Line

CT1812, now named zervimesine, is a sigma-2 receptor modulator being developed for Alzheimer's disease and dementia with Lewy bodies. The medicinal signal is currently `low`: human data support target engagement, qEEG/CSF biomarker movement, and exploratory DLB outcomes, but the branch still lacks a clean long-duration cognitive or functional win. No molecule-specific anecdotal cognition signal is captured in the current wiki corpus.

## What It Is

CT1812 is an orally dosed synaptic-rescue candidate designed to prevent or displace amyloid-beta oligomer binding at synaptic receptor complexes. In the wiki it belongs with the [[synaptic-rescue-small-molecule-2026-scan]] rather than with general nootropic stimulants, because the clinical question is disease-modification or synaptic rescue in AD/DLB, not acute healthy-adult enhancement.

## Medicinal Research Signal

The AD biomarker anchor is SEQUEL, a completed 4-week randomized placebo-controlled crossover trial in mild-to-moderate AD using 300 mg CT1812 once daily. The follow-up paper reported improved qEEG markers of spontaneous brain activity and CSF proteomic changes consistent with pathway engagement, vesicle trafficking, synaptic biology, and lipoprotein biology. That is a target-engagement signal, not a direct cognition-efficacy result.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40547328-ct1812-sequel-biomarkers.md]

The DLB anchor is a 26-week phase 2a randomized study in probable DLB with 100 mg, 300 mg, and placebo arms. The abstract reports that zervimesine was safe and well tolerated and that exploratory outcomes favored treatment, but it does not provide enough endpoint detail to estimate a stable effect size from the captured note alone. Dose-related discontinuation matters: AE discontinuations were 4.5% at 100 mg, 16.3% at 300 mg, and 4.8% on placebo.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41416579-zervimesine-dlb.md]

The unresolved efficacy test is NCT05531656, an active, not-recruiting early-AD phase 2 trial with about 540 planned participants and an 18-month CDR-SB primary endpoint. Until that readout is available, CT1812 should be treated as mechanistically interesting but unproven for patient-relevant cognition or function.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct05531656-ct1812-early-ad.md]

## Safety And Status

The current safety read is tolerability-limited rather than alarm-positive. The DLB study reported higher AE discontinuation at 300 mg than at 100 mg or placebo, so future efficacy interpretation should preserve dose and dropout details. CT1812 remains investigational.

## Signal Judgment

Medicinal signal: `low`, leaning hypothesis-positive because target engagement and exploratory DLB outcomes exist but durable cognition/function is unresolved.

Anecdotal signal: `absent-to-weak`, with no source corpus that can be scored.

Practical route: disease-modifying / synaptic-rescue trial candidate. It should not be ranked as a healthy-adult cognitive enhancer.
