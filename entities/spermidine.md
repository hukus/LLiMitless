# Spermidine

Spermidine is a naturally occurring polyamine involved in cellular metabolism and autophagy. This entity covers spermidine cognition claims, not spermine, putrescine, semen-retention claims, generic fasting, wheat germ as a whole food, natto, mushroom/legume diets, or broader autophagy supplements such as NMN, nicotinamide riboside, [[ergothioneine]], and urolithin A.

Medicinal cognition signal: `low/contested, with small memory hints and a larger null counterweight`. The best positive human cognition signal is a 2018 phase IIa pilot in 30 cognitively intact older adults with subjective cognitive decline using a spermidine-rich plant extract for 3 months. Memory performance and mnemonic discrimination improved with medium effect-size estimates, but the sample was small and the product was an extract rather than pure spermidine.^[raw/articles/pubmed-30388439-spermidine-memory-performance-scd.md]

The governing counterweight is SmartAge. In a 100-person 12-month phase IIb RCT in older adults with subjective cognitive decline, wheat-germ-derived spermidine supplementation providing about 0.9 mg/day did not significantly improve mnemonic discrimination or the broader secondary outcome set versus placebo. Exploratory verbal-memory, inflammation, and subgroup hints remain hypothesis-generating only.^[raw/articles/pmc-9136623-smartage-spermidine-scd-rct-null.md]

The dementia and observational layers support plausibility, not confirmation. A preliminary 3-month nursing-home dementia study found better MMSE performance in the higher-intake group, but it remained small, extract/nourishment-specific, and disease-state oriented. A 2025 NHANES analysis associated higher dietary spermidine intake with better recall, fluency, digit-symbol substitution, and composite cognition in adults 60+, but that is observational diet-pattern evidence and may track healthier foods rather than spermidine itself.^[raw/articles/pubmed-33211152-spermidine-dementia-first-results.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40180050-dietary-spermidine-nhanes-cognition.md]

Safety/product signal: `low-moderate, identity-sensitive`. A 2024 double-blind safety RCT of high-purity spermidine trihydrochloride at 40 mg/day for up to 28 days in healthy older men found no product-related adverse events and no major clinical, lipid, chemistry, hematology, serum-polyamine, or urine-polyamine changes. That supports short-term pure-molecule tolerability, but it does not prove cognition efficacy and is not the same exposure as the wheat-germ extracts used in SmartAge-style trials or commercial products.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39405978-spermidine-safety-hpspd.md]^[raw/articles/spermidinelife-2026-wheat-germ-product-identity.md]

Anecdote signal: `weak-moderate longevity salience, weak direct cognition`. Community reports are mostly autophagy, hair/skin, energy, sleep, odor, and dosing-timing discussions. Direct cognition improvements are sparse; several users report no noticeable effect, while others report body heat, sleep disturbance, or nonspecific physical changes.^[raw/articles/reddit-2022-spermidine-biohackers-experience-thread.md]^[raw/articles/reddit-2025-spermidine-dosing-biohackers-thread.md]

## Identity Gates

- Separate wheat-germ extract mass from active spermidine mass. Commercial labels can show hundreds of milligrams of extract but 1-3 mg spermidine, while high-purity trials can use 40 mg pure spermidine trihydrochloride.
- Separate dietary spermidine from supplement causality. Beans, mushrooms, grains, natto, legumes, and wheat germ carry many other nutrients.
- Track wheat/gluten intolerance, histamine/natto context, pregnancy/breastfeeding warnings, malignancy exclusions in trials, dose, product assay, spermine/putrescine co-content, and whether the intervention is an extract, food, or pure molecule.
- Do not treat autophagy activation as proven cognitive benefit; SmartAge is the cautionary anchor.

## Practical Ranking

Spermidine deserves standalone tracking because it has repeated human cognition-relevant sources and strong longevity community visibility. Its current best route is `autophagy / polyamine memory-watch`, not a broad nootropic recommendation. Rank it below cleaner positive candidates until a larger trial uses a well-characterized dose, separates extract from active molecule, and replicates memory benefit against the SmartAge null.
