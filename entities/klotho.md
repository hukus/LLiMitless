# Klotho

Klotho is a longevity-associated protein and gene product best known for its role in aging biology, mineral metabolism, and FGF23 signaling. In the cognitive literature it is treated as a resilience candidate, not a settled intervention target.

## Why it matters

- Human observational studies repeatedly link higher circulating Klotho with better cognitive performance in older adults, especially on processing-speed style tasks such as DSST.^[raw/articles/pmc-9932504-klotho-cognitive-performance-us-adults.md]^[raw/articles/nature-s41398-023-02632-x-klotho-cognition-dementia-mr.md]
- A 2025 AD/aMCI cohort added nuance: KL-VS heterozygosity tracked with better memory in aMCI due to AD, while circulating soluble α-Klotho did not separate cases from controls or predict memory on its own.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41163083-klotho-vs-cognitive-performance-ad.md]
- The strongest human genetic signal is mixed: KL-VS heterozygosity was associated with better cognition in 2014, but a later cohort of older men found higher dementia incidence with KL-VS carrier status.^[raw/articles/pmc-4176932-klotho-cognition-enhancement.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-28539162-klotho-dementia-older-men.md]
- A 2025 meta-analysis found an overall positive association between Klotho and cognition/dementia outcomes, but most included studies were observational and heterogenous.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40048920-klotho-cognition-meta-analysis.md]
- A 2026 review broadened the clinical framing to Alzheimer's disease, Parkinson's disease, multiple sclerosis, and stroke recovery, but it remains synthesis-level evidence rather than new primary efficacy data.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41983942-klotho-neuroprotective-roles-review.md]
- A Mendelian randomization analysis did not support a causal dementia-protective effect, so the biomarker signal does not yet justify a therapeutic claim.^[raw/articles/nature-s41398-023-02632-x-klotho-cognition-dementia-mr.md]
- Intervention evidence remains preclinical: low-dose Klotho improved memory in aged nonhuman primates, while high-dose did not.^[raw/articles/pubmed-37400721-klotho-nonhuman-primates-cognition.md]

## Signal Strength Ledger

| Layer | Strength | Current read | Main caveat |
|---|---|---|---|
| Human soluble-Klotho biomarker | `low-moderate` | Older-adult observational studies and the 2025 meta-analysis support association with better cognition or dementia outcomes | Noncausal, assay/phenotype heterogeneity, renal/vascular/inflammatory confounding |
| KL-VS genotype | `low/contested` | 2025 aMCI-due-to-AD memory signal makes genotype-stage interaction plausible | 2014 benefit and 2017 adverse-direction dementia signals conflict |
| Human intervention | `protocol-only` | Klotho plus follistatin gene therapy is active in a 30-person early phase 1 open-label registry | No results, no placebo control, combination treatment, and outside-U.S. treatment administration |
| Preclinical intervention | `preclinical-only` | Aged nonhuman-primate low-dose Klotho memory signal is the best intervention-like anchor | Dose-sensitive and not yet human-translated |
| Anecdote / market | `weak prospective; absent direct-use cognition report` | Sponsor waitlist and Reddit interest exist | Marketing and curiosity do not establish outcome |
| Safety / regulatory | `high-friction` | Gene therapy, mineral/renal biology, Klotho plus follistatin attribution, and investigational access require safety-first routing | Long-term human safety and attribution remain unresolved |

## Relationship To Cognition

The current pattern looks more like a state or resilience marker than a standalone cognitive enhancer. In humans, Klotho is most consistently associated with better cognitive performance in older adults, but the causal and disease-specific picture remains unstable and may depend on vascular burden, age, genotype, diagnostic stage, and dose-response shape.^[raw/articles/pubmed-29768278-klotho-vascular-dementia-memory-clinic.md]^[raw/articles/pmc-9932504-klotho-cognitive-performance-us-adults.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41163083-klotho-vs-cognitive-performance-ad.md]^[raw/articles/nature-s41398-023-02632-x-klotho-cognition-dementia-mr.md]

## Intervention Status

There is no established human Klotho cognition trial with published outcomes. The 2026 Klotho + follistatin plasmid study is the clearest active intervention lead, but it is exploratory, open-label, small, non-placebo-controlled, and not evidence of efficacy.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct07285629-klotho-follistatin-gene-therapy.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct07285629-klotho-follistatin-gene-therapy-record-2026-05-04.md]

The registry's cognitive readouts are specific enough to matter later: pattern-comparison processing speed, picture sequence memory, flanker inhibitory control and attention, dimensional change card sort, picture vocabulary, and quality-of-life measures are listed, which makes this more than a vague wellness claim even though it is still uncontrolled.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct07285629-klotho-follistatin-gene-therapy-record-2026-05-04.md]
The registry's primary outcomes are serum alpha-Klotho, serum follistatin, and treatment-emergent adverse events. The intervention combines Klotho and follistatin and the registry states that treatment occurs outside the United States at a site not under FDA jurisdiction, so this branch needs a safety/regulatory frame before any efficacy interpretation.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct07285629-klotho-follistatin-gene-therapy-record-2026-05-04.md]
The newer AD/aMCI paper does not change that intervention status, but it does suggest that genotype may be a better hypothesis handle than circulating protein when Klotho is used as a cognitive-resilience marker.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41163083-klotho-vs-cognitive-performance-ad.md]

## Anecdote And Market Signal

The captured anecdotal layer is weak and mostly prospective. Minicircle's Klotho page is a consumer-facing waitlist and marketing signal for a 2026 investigational therapy, not an outcome report, and it says the therapy is not FDA-reviewed.^[raw/articles/minicircle-2026-klotho-gene-therapy-marketing-page.md] A representative Reddit thread shows curiosity about Klotho/follistatin gene therapy and some intent to try gene therapy later, but no direct Klotho treatment result or objective cognition claim was captured.^[raw/articles/reddit-2025-03-07-klotho-follistatin-gene-therapy-interest-thread.md]

For the atlas, score Klotho anecdotes as `weak prospective salience` and direct cognition anecdotes as `absent` until a user-outcome corpus or posted clinical result exists.
