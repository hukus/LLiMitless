# Neflamapimod

## Bottom Line

Neflamapimod / VX-745 is a p38alpha inhibitor with a phenotype-sensitive dementia-with-Lewy-bodies signal and a later phase 2b miss. Medicinal signal is `low`: early DLB data and biomarker/subgroup interpretations keep it worth tracking, but RewinD-LB missed its primary and key secondary endpoints. Anecdotal signal is absent in the current source corpus.

## What It Is

Neflamapimod is an oral selective p38alpha kinase inhibitor framed around neuroinflammation, basal-forebrain cholinergic degeneration, and synaptic dysfunction. It belongs in the [[synaptic-rescue-small-molecule-2026-scan]] because the main question is whether target engagement and phenotype enrichment can translate into cognition or function in DLB, not whether it works as a general nootropic.

## Medicinal Research Signal

AscenD-LB was a completed randomized phase 2 DLB study with posted results. It used 40 mg neflamapimod with BID or TID dosing depending on body weight, with a study-specific Cogstate neuropsychological battery as the primary endpoint and CDR-SB, MMSE, NPI-10, Timed Up and Go, and EEG as secondary or biomarker outcomes. This is the key proof-of-principle record for the branch, but it should be read alongside later enrichment and replication attempts rather than as broad efficacy proof.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct04001517-ascend-lb-neflamapimod.md]

RewinD-LB is the major counterweight. It was designed as a follow-on DLB phase 2b trial with early-stage DLB enrichment, plasma ptau181 exclusion for likely AD co-pathology, 40 mg TID dosing, and CDR-SB as the primary endpoint. Top-line reporting said the 159-participant double-blind phase did not meet statistical significance on the primary endpoint or key secondary endpoints; target plasma concentrations were not achieved, although safety/tolerability was described as favorable.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct05869669-rewind-lb-neflamapimod.md]

A 2026 systematic review found only two eligible clinical studies and could not run a meta-analysis. It described primary cognitive outcomes such as NTB and HVLT-R as not statistically different, while selected episodic memory, executive-function, attention, gait, motor, and biomarker measures were more favorable, especially in participants with elevated plasma ptau181. The review's own bottom line is uncertainty on cognition with continued interest in biomarkers.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41510854-neflamapimod-systematic-review.md]

## Safety And Status

The captured RewinD-LB note describes favorable safety/tolerability, but the central interpretive problem is exposure and efficacy rather than a decisive toxicity barrier. Until a replicated enriched-population trial moves cognition or function, the branch remains contested.

## Signal Judgment

Medicinal signal: `low`, with a phenotype-enrichment hypothesis but no replicated phase 2b cognition/function win.

Anecdotal signal: `absent-to-weak`.

Practical route: enrichment-sensitive DLB research candidate and cautionary example for subgroup-positive synaptic-rescue narratives.
