# Phenylalanine

Phenylalanine is an essential amino acid and tyrosine precursor. This page covers L-phenylalanine, D-phenylalanine, and DLPA as nootropic-community molecules, while keeping them separate from [[tyrosine]], [[mucuna-pruriens]] / L-DOPA, aspartame exposure, ordinary dietary protein, dopamine drugs, and generic catecholamine stacks.

Medicinal cognition signal: `low/metabolic-vulnerability, not healthy enhancement`. The strongest cognition-relevant human source is not a nootropic trial: it is a 2024 randomized crossover trial of high phenylalanine intake in adults with PKU. That source makes phenylalanine cognitively relevant in a vulnerable metabolic phenotype, but it does not show that supplementation improves cognition in healthy adults.^[raw/articles/pubmed-38569786-high-phenylalanine-pku-cognition-rct.md]

DLPA and D-phenylalanine evidence should not be imported into cognition ranking. A 1979 DLPA depression comparison against imipramine is old, mood-state, and methodologically cautious. A 1986 D-phenylalanine chronic-pain crossover trial was null against placebo. These sources keep DLPA/DPA lore visible, but they do not establish focus, memory, executive-control, or learning benefit.^[raw/articles/pubmed-387000-dlpa-imipramine-depression.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-3524509-d-phenylalanine-chronic-pain-null.md]

Safety and phenotype gate: `high for PKU, moderate for casual supplement interpretation`. MedlinePlus frames PKU as a lifelong phenylalanine-processing disorder where high phenylalanine can damage the brain and cause severe intellectual disability. For the wiki, that means phenylalanine supplements are phenotype-sensitive and should be routed through metabolic status, aspartame/food exposure, diet, psychiatric state, stimulant stacks, sleep, and dose before any "dopamine support" claim is considered.^[raw/articles/medlineplus-2025-phenylketonuria-pku.md]

Anecdote signal: `moderate focus/motivation salience, bidirectional, weak objective cognition`. The captured /r/Nootropics thread describes 500 mg phenylalanine as increasing motivation, mood, energy, and study focus, but also as producing too much focus, less daydreaming, less reflective thought, and discomfort. That is state-shift salience, not proof of better cognition.^[raw/articles/reddit-2020-phenylalanine-focus-thread.md]

## Identity Gates

- Separate L-phenylalanine, D-phenylalanine, DLPA, [[tyrosine]], L-DOPA / [[mucuna-pruriens]], aspartame, and ordinary dietary protein.
- Screen the claim through PKU/metabolic status first; do not treat phenylalanine as a harmless universal precursor.
- Track dose, timing, stimulant/caffeine co-use, sleep debt, anxiety/irritability, bipolar or psychosis vulnerability, migraine/headache tendency, pregnancy/breastfeeding, and use under age 18.
- Do not count "more focus" as improved cognition unless working memory, task accuracy, learning, or executive-control outcomes are measured.

## Practical Ranking

Phenylalanine deserves standalone tracking because it appears in nootropic focus lore and has real metabolic-cognition relevance, but it should stay below [[tyrosine]] and far below validated wakefulness/attention comparators for practical cognition ranking. The current route is `phenotype-sensitive catecholamine precursor / metabolic-risk branch`, not a general enhancer.
