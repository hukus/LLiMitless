# Choline Bitartrate / Free Choline Salts

Choline bitartrate is a free choline salt used as a cheap supplement choline source. This page also stages generic free choline-salt claims, but it should not be treated as interchangeable with [[alpha-gpc]], [[citicoline]], [[lysolecithin]], or [[phosphatidylcholine]].

Medicinal cognition signal: `low/narrow and mostly memory-null`. The best positive direct result is a 2015 placebo-controlled double-blind study where 2 g choline bitartrate improved target-hit precision and constricted pupil size about 70 minutes after ingestion, suggesting a shift toward action precision. That is not a broad memory or executive-function signal. A 2016 PLOS ONE paper ran three double-blind crossover experiments in healthy young adults and found no acute benefit for visuospatial working memory, declarative picture memory, or verbal working memory after 2.0-2.5 g choline bitartrate. A load-carriage crossover study found that 50 mg/kg choline raised plasma choline but did not improve reaction time, logical reasoning, vigilance, spatial memory, working memory, or physical performance.^[raw/articles/nature-2015-choline-bitartrate-visuomotor.md]^[raw/articles/plos-2016-choline-bitartrate-memory-null.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-12502178-choline-ingestion-performance-null.md]

Safety / biomarker signal: `form-sensitive caution`. A randomized trial comparing choline sources found that choline-bitartrate-containing arms raised fasting plasma TMAO and platelet reactivity over 4 weeks, while eggs and [[phosphatidylcholine]] capsules providing comparable total choline did not. This does not prove clinical cardiovascular harm in a nootropic user, but it is enough to keep free choline salts safety-gated.^[raw/articles/pmc-8410632-choline-forms-tmao-response.md]

| Layer | Signal | Readout |
|---|---|---|
| Visuomotor precision | `low-positive/narrow` medicinal | 2 g choline bitartrate improved target-hit precision in a small double-blind study |
| Healthy-adult memory | `negative` medicinal | Three acute crossover memory experiments were null |
| Load-carriage / depletion hypothesis | `negative` medicinal | Plasma choline rose, but reaction time, logical reasoning, vigilance, spatial memory, and working memory did not improve |
