# GlyNAC

GlyNAC is a combination protocol using [[glycine]] plus N-acetylcysteine as glutathione-precursor support. In this wiki it is a combination entity, not a synonym for [[n-acetylcysteine]], glycine alone, glutathione, cysteine, or generic antioxidant supplementation. The relevant exposure includes glycine dose, NAC dose/form, aging or oxidative-stress phenotype, duration, and withdrawal.

Medicinal cognition signal: `low/aging-phenotype and open-label`. The main direct human cognition anchor is a tiny 36-week open-label older-adult pilot: eight older adults improved on MoCA and Trails A after 24 weeks of GlyNAC, and some gains declined after 12 weeks off supplementation. A later 2024 aging-cognition pilot abstract reports cognition and mechanistic improvements with washout reversal, but still stays proof-of-concept. The aging-HIV open-label trial adds a premature-aging clinical-state signal, with improvements across MoCA, Trails A/B, DSST, and verbal fluency after 12 weeks. None of these are large blinded cognition-efficacy trials.^[raw/articles/pmc-8002905-glynac-older-adults-open-label-cognition.md]^[raw/articles/pmc-11689385-glynac-aging-cognition-pilot.md]^[raw/articles/pmc-7601820-glynac-aging-hiv-open-label-cognition.md]

Mechanistic and safety signal: `low-moderate plausibility, not cognition proof`. A double-blind placebo-controlled aging-hallmarks trial in 24 older adults found GlyNAC improved glutathione, oxidative-stress, mitochondrial, inflammatory, endothelial, insulin-resistance, physical-function, and aging-hallmark measures after 16 weeks and was safe/well tolerated, but it was not a cognition-efficacy trial. A separate 114-person healthy-aging dose RCT did not improve circulating glutathione versus placebo on the primary endpoint, suggesting responder selection by oxidative stress / low glutathione status matters. Old-mouse work strengthens brain-mechanism plausibility but remains preclinical.^[raw/articles/academic-oup-2022-glynac-older-adults-rct-aging-hallmarks.md]^[raw/articles/pmc-8968300-glynac-healthy-aging-glutathione-dose-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pmc-10215265-glynac-old-mice-brain-cognition.md]

Anecdote signal: `weak-moderate, mixed and safety-relevant`. Direct community evidence is not a clean cognition-upgrade corpus. One thread includes a long-term user with no attributable effect and another user with activation, easier waking, restlessness, and anhedonia. A second thread reports acute sharp energy/clear mind followed by poor sleep, headache, and next-day tiredness. A 2025 Biohackers thread centers on feeling useless, low motivation, lethargy, or anhedonia after GlyNAC or related glycine/NAC use. These anecdotes support monitoring and responder hypotheses, not efficacy ranking.^[raw/articles/reddit-2023-glynac-experiences-thread.md]^[raw/articles/reddit-2023-glynac-energy-hangover-thread.md]^[raw/articles/reddit-2025-glynac-anhedonia-thread.md]

Safety signal: `moderate supplement caution`. Human trials report short-term tolerability, but the studied protocols used high amino-acid mass, monitoring, exclusions, and product control. NAC also has a contested dietary-supplement/regulatory history in the United States. Practical interpretation should track GI tolerance, sleep disruption, headache, motivation/anhedonia, renal/hepatic context, acetaminophen/alcohol context, and medication/supplement stack interactions.^[raw/articles/academic-oup-2022-glynac-older-adults-rct-aging-hallmarks.md]^[raw/articles/fda-2022-nac-enforcement-discretion-guidance.md]^[raw/articles/reddit-2023-glynac-energy-hangover-thread.md]^[raw/articles/reddit-2025-glynac-anhedonia-thread.md]

## Signal Split

| Layer | Signal | Readout |
|---|---|---|
| Older-adult open-label cognition | `low/proof-of-concept` | MoCA and Trails A improved after 24 weeks, with partial decline after withdrawal |
| Aging-HIV open-label cognition | `low/clinical-state` | Multiple cognition measures improved after 12 weeks, but no randomized placebo arm |
| Blinded aging-hallmarks RCT | `mechanistic positive` | Controlled glutathione / mitochondrial / inflammation / physical-function support, not cognition efficacy |
| Healthy-aging dose RCT | `mixed/null primary biomarker` | Circulating glutathione primary endpoint null; possible oxidative-stress responder subgroup |
| Old-mouse brain cognition | `preclinical positive` | Aging-brain defects and cognition improved in mice |
| Anecdote | `weak-moderate mixed` | No effect, activation/clarity, restlessness, hangover, lethargy, and anhedonia reports |
| Safety / sourcing | `moderate caution` | High-dose combination, NAC status, monitoring/exclusions, sleep and motivation adverse signals |

## Practical Interpretation

GlyNAC deserves standalone tracking because it has direct human cognition pilot data plus controlled aging-mechanism support, but its confidence should stay low. The best current read is `older metabolic-redox responder hypothesis`, not broad nootropic enhancement. It ranks above NAC alone for mechanistic aging plausibility, but below cleaner controlled cognition candidates because the cognition evidence is still small, open-label, and phenotype-bound.

A confidence upgrade requires a randomized, blinded GlyNAC cognition trial with prespecified cognitive domains, objective adherence, dose/form reporting, baseline glutathione/oxidative-stress stratification, and active tracking of sleep, headache, GI effects, motivation, and anhedonia.
