# Phentermine

Phentermine is a prescription sympathomimetic amine anorectic used short-term for obesity management. In this wiki it is a stimulant/anorectic comparator beside [[tesofensine]], [[sibutramine]], [[mazindol]], [[solriamfetol]], [[methylphenidate]], and [[d-amphetamine]], not a validated nootropic.

## Current Read

Medicinal cognition signal: `low/state-bound`. Phentermine has an old acute healthy-volunteer mental-task signal and a sleep-deprivation performance-rescue signal, but those do not establish rested healthy-adult enhancement or chronic brain-fog treatment.^[raw/articles/pubmed-6471970-phentermine-ephedrine-fenfluramine-prolintane-mental-effects.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-12887140-phentermine-sleep-deprivation-performance.md]

Regulatory / clinical role: short-term obesity adjunct. The DailyMed label frames phentermine as a sympathomimetic amine anorectic for short-term weight reduction with diet, exercise, and behavioral modification, and states it is a Schedule IV controlled substance.^[raw/articles/dailymed-2024-phentermine-label.md]

Anecdote signal: `moderate but highly confounded`. Current Reddit reports include ADHD-like focus, motivation, task initiation, clear mind, and reduced brain fog, plus counterreports of exhaustion, loss of focus, brain fog, short energy windows, crashes, and medication-transition concerns.^[raw/articles/reddit-2021-2026-phentermine-focus-brainfog-threads.md]

## Human Evidence

| Evidence layer | What it says | Wiki interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| Sleep-deprivation performance trial | Healthy young men received phentermine 37.5 mg, caffeine, d-amphetamine, tyrosine, or placebo after sleep deprivation. The abstract reports that all tested drugs improved at least some cognitive/motor performance after sleep deprivation.^[raw/articles/pubmed-12887140-phentermine-sleep-deprivation-performance.md] | Fatigue-rescue signal; recovery-sleep cost matters |
| Acute healthy-volunteer task study | In medical students, phentermine and prolintane appeared most active on some mental tasks, while ephedrine had the strongest cardiovascular effects.^[raw/articles/pubmed-6471970-phentermine-ephedrine-fenfluramine-prolintane-mental-effects.md] | Old acute task hint, not broad proof |
| Prescription label | Short-term obesity indication, primary pulmonary hypertension / valvular heart disease warnings, hazardous-task warning, tolerance, abuse/dependence, MAOI and alcohol interactions, hypertension caution, and Schedule IV status.^[raw/articles/dailymed-2024-phentermine-label.md] | Safety and status dominate interpretation |
| Phentermine/topiramate label | Qsymia explicitly warns about cognitive impairment, including attention, memory, and speech/language problems; one-year adult trials reported cognitive-related adverse reactions above placebo.^[raw/articles/dailymed-2025-qsymia-cognitive-impairment-label.md] | Do not generalize phentermine-alone anecdotes to the topiramate combination |

## Safety And Interpretation Gates

- Separate phentermine alone from phentermine/topiramate. Topiramate adds direct cognitive adverse-event risk, word-finding problems, metabolic acidosis, kidney-stone, and abrupt-withdrawal seizure gates.^[raw/articles/dailymed-2025-qsymia-cognitive-impairment-label.md]
- Separate phentermine from old fen-phen. Fenfluramine/phentermine history is a safety precedent, but fenfluramine's 5-HT2B-linked valvulopathy mechanism should not be casually assigned to phentermine alone.^[raw/articles/pubmed-9271479-fenfluramine-phentermine-valvular-disease.md]
- Watch insomnia, HR/BP, palpitations, anxiety, irritability, appetite/nutrient reduction, dehydration, crash, hazardous-task impairment, tolerance, dependence, alcohol, MAOIs, serotonergic or stimulant stacking, cardiovascular history, pregnancy, and eating-disorder context.
- Do not treat "less food noise" or binge-eating control as direct cognition. It can feel cognitively liberating while being appetite, reward, mood, or caloric-state driven.
- Route phentermine anecdotes through [[safety-interaction-matrix-for-multi-modal-stacks]] so calorie deficit, sleep, caffeine, BP/HR, stimulant access, and phentermine/topiramate confusion stay separate from cognition claims.

## Current Route

Phentermine belongs in [[sympathomimetic-anorectic-stimulant-cognition-watchlist]] and [[pharmacology-comparator-map]] as the main modern prescription comparator in the classic anorectic-stimulant class. The evidence makes it relevant for sleep-loss rescue and ADHD-like anecdotal focus signals, but not enough to rank it as a healthy-adult cognition enhancer.
