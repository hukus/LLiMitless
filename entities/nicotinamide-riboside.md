# Nicotinamide Riboside

Nicotinamide riboside (NR) is a vitamin B3-derived NAD+ precursor, usually sold as nicotinamide riboside chloride in commercial supplement products such as Niagen / TruNiagen. This page covers oral NR cognition claims, not niacin, nicotinamide, NMN, NAD+ injections/IVs, NADH, tryptophan/kynurenine metabolism, B3-deficiency correction, or generic "NAD booster" stacks.

Medicinal cognition signal: `low/biomarker-positive but cognition-null so far`. The best completed human cognition trial in MCI randomized 20 older adults to NR or placebo for 10 weeks, escalating to 1 g/day. NR was tolerated and increased blood NAD+ about 2.6-fold, but MoCA and other neurocognitive/psychometric metrics stayed stable. Uncorrected cerebral-blood-flow and methylation signals make the study biologically interesting, not cognition-positive.^[raw/articles/pubmed-37994989-nr-mci-rct.md]

The second completed cognition trial makes the pattern harder to dismiss as one underpowered miss. In older adults with subjective cognitive decline and MCI, 8 weeks of 1 g/day oral NR lowered plasma pTau217 and was well tolerated, but RBANS, Lumosity gameplay, other plasma biomarkers, and step counts did not show significant between-group cognitive or functional separation. That is a clean biomarker-cognition mismatch: target-adjacent markers move, measured cognition does not.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39817194-nr-scd-mci-biomarker-effects.md]

The live registry signal is still pending. NCT05500170 is recruiting older adults with poor sleep quality for 500 mg NR twice daily versus placebo over 12 weeks. Sleep quality is the primary endpoint; VA-SLUMS cognition and serum BDNF, NfL, Aβ42/Aβ40, and p-tau are secondary/other outcomes. As of 2026-05-03, the registry has no posted results and an estimated completion date of 2027-08-30.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct05500170-nr-cognition-sleep.md]

Safety and product signal: `moderate identity clarity, low efficacy transport`. FDA's GRAS notice response for ChromaDex nicotinamide riboside chloride supports a clearer ingredient identity than vague NAD+ booster labels, with specifications around purity, residual solvents, microbial controls, and heavy metals. FDA also explicitly did not make its own GRAS determination and did not evaluate health-benefit labeling claims. Tru Niagen's current product page shows a 300 mg Niagen capsule and positions Niagen as a patented, safety-reviewed NR form used in many NR trials. That improves transportability of ingredient identity, but it does not upgrade cognition efficacy.^[raw/articles/fda-gras-2026-nicotinamide-riboside-chloride.md]^[raw/articles/truniagen-2026-niagen-nr-product-identity.md]

Anecdote signal: `weak-moderate for energy/longevity salience, weak and bidirectional for direct cognition`. Community reports include no subjective change after a year-long self-experiment, positive energy/sharpness/motivation reports in a stacked NR/polyphenol product context, poor-sleep/no-effect reports, and at least one fatigue/lethargy report after Tru Niagen. The strongest anecdotal theme is felt energy or longevity belief, not measured memory or executive function.^[raw/articles/reddit-2021-nr-one-year-self-experiment-thread.md]^[raw/articles/reddit-2025-nr-experience-energy-sleep-thread.md]^[raw/articles/reddit-2023-tru-niagen-fatigue-thread.md]

## Identity Gates

- Separate NR chloride / Niagen / TruNiagen from NMN, niacin, nicotinamide, NADH, IV NAD+, direct NAD+ lozenges, and multi-precursor NAD+ blends.
- Track dose and salt/form: 300 mg commercial capsules, 500 mg twice daily in the live sleep/cognition registry, and 1 g/day in the two completed cognition trials are not interchangeable exposure claims.
- Treat blood NAD+, plasma pTau217, methylation, cerebral blood flow, BDNF, NfL, Aβ42/Aβ40, and p-tau as biomarkers until a cognitive endpoint separates from placebo.
- Separate energy, mood, workout, sleep, and longevity anecdotes from measured memory, attention, processing speed, or executive-function outcomes.
- Watch methylation / one-carbon context, liver/renal history, cancer-treatment context, pregnancy/breastfeeding, stimulant/caffeine timing, sleep disruption, and high-dose long-term use as practical safety/context gates.

## Practical Ranking

NR deserves standalone tracking because it is widely used, product identity is clearer than many supplement branches, and completed human trials show real biomarker movement. It should not be ranked as a demonstrated cognitive enhancer. The current route is `NAD+ biomarker watch / sleep-cognition registry watch`, with medicinal confidence held at low until a larger trial moves a pre-registered cognitive endpoint.
