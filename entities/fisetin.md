# Fisetin

Fisetin is a dietary flavonol and senotherapeutic candidate found in foods such as strawberries, apples, persimmons, onions, and cucumbers. In this wiki it is a preclinical cognition / senolytic-longevity molecule, not an established human cognitive enhancer and not interchangeable with [[quercetin]], [[resveratrol]], D+Q, or generic flavonoid-rich foods.

Medicinal cognition signal: `preclinical-only/low for humans`. The ADDF Cognitive Vitality summary explicitly frames fisetin as preclinical for cognition / Alzheimer's disease with no human cognition research at the time of review. The main cognition-positive anchors are animal studies: an APP/PS1 Alzheimer's mouse study found prevention of learning/memory deficits with long-term fisetin exposure, and other preclinical models support neuroinflammation / synaptic-plasticity plausibility. This is not enough for a human cognition recommendation.^[raw/articles/alzdiscovery-2016-fisetin-cognitive-vitality.md]^[raw/articles/pmc-3954948-fisetin-ad-mouse-cognition.md]

The senolytic signal is stronger than the cognition signal. A 2018 preclinical study identified fisetin as a senotherapeutic candidate with healthspan/lifespan effects in mice. Human fisetin work in the captured pipeline is AFFIRM-LITE, a Mayo Clinic phase 2 frailty/inflammation trial using 20 mg/kg/day for two days, with inflammatory markers as the primary endpoint and no cognition-first outcome. Therefore fisetin belongs in [[senolytic-cognition-2026-scan]] as a support molecule, not a cognition-efficacy molecule.^[raw/articles/pubmed-30279143-fisetin-senotherapeutic-health-lifespan.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct03675724-fisetin-frailty-inflammation-older-adults.md]

Anecdote signal: `moderate longevity/senolytic salience, weak and adverse-skewed cognition evidence`. The direct fisetin thread captured here is adverse-direction: a user reports brain fog, poorer memory, low motivation, and knee pain after intermittent fisetin dosing. A second senolytic-stack thread reports feeling worse during high-dose fisetin-plus-quercetin cycles. These reports are weak and confounded, but they are important because they oppose simple "senolytic equals mental clarity" narratives.^[raw/articles/reddit-2023-fisetin-brain-fog-adverse-thread.md]^[raw/articles/reddit-2025-fisetin-quercetin-senolytics-adverse-thread.md]^[raw/articles/troof-nootropics-ratings-analysis.md]

Safety signal: `low-moderate uncertainty`. Dietary fisetin is food-level safe, but high-dose intermittent senolytic supplementation has limited human safety data. The AFFIRM-LITE registry exclusions are extensive: they screen for QTc, liver/bilirubin, infection, cancer, interacting CYP substrates/inhibitors/inducers, senolytic agents, chemotherapy-like senescence-inducing agents, selected antibiotics/antivirals/antifungals, PPIs/H2 antagonists, many cardiovascular/metabolic/psychiatric drugs, caffeine reduction, and vitamin D sufficiency. This is a major practical caution for self-experimentation.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct03675724-fisetin-frailty-inflammation-older-adults.md]

## Practical Interpretation

Fisetin clears a standalone page threshold because it is highly visible in senolytic/longevity cognition conversations and has multiple preclinical cognition and senescence anchors. It should still be ranked below molecules with direct human cognition evidence. The useful wiki claim is conservative: fisetin is a preclinical/senolytic hypothesis with no established human cognition benefit, and available anecdote is at least as useful for adverse monitoring as for efficacy.

A confidence upgrade requires completed human fisetin trials with cognition endpoints, not just inflammatory markers, plus product assay, dose-cycle safety, medication interaction handling, and objective cognitive measures.
