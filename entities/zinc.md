# Zinc

Zinc is an essential mineral involved in immune function, DNA/protein synthesis, wound healing, taste/smell, and many enzymatic processes. In this wiki it is a status-correction and copper-balance candidate for cognition/brain fog, not a broad enhancer. It should not be collapsed with cold lozenges, testosterone/libido stacks, multivitamins, [[iodine]], [[selenium]], or unmonitored high-dose protocols.

Medicinal signal: `low and status-sensitive`. The ZENITH older-adult trial gives zinc a human cognition anchor, but it is not enough to rank zinc as a general nootropic. Baseline status, age, geography, endpoint choice, dose, duration, and diet all matter. ODS also notes that zinc status assessment is difficult because serum zinc is context-sensitive.^[raw/articles/pubmed-17010236-zinc-zenith-cognition-rct.md]^[raw/articles/ods-2026-zinc-health-professional-fact-sheet.md]

Safety/status signal: `moderate-high`. The adult tolerable upper intake level is 40 mg/day. Chronic high-dose zinc can induce copper deficiency, anemia, neutropenia, immune dysfunction, and neurological problems. That copper gate is central: subjective improvement from zinc and later brain fog or neuropathy-like symptoms can both be plausible in the same community ecosystem.^[raw/articles/ods-2026-zinc-health-professional-fact-sheet.md]

## Signal Split

| Layer | Signal | Readout |
|---|---|---|
| Older-adult RCT | `low` | ZENITH gives a human cognition anchor but not a broad enhancer proof |
| Zinc-deficient or low-intake states | `plausible/status-gated` | Useful hypothesis when diet, immune state, smell/taste, or labs suggest deficiency |
| Replete healthy adults | `not established` | No direct broad enhancement signal |
| Anecdote | `weak-moderate bidirectional` | Brain-fog/anxiety improvement claims coexist with imbalance and adverse concerns |
| Safety | `moderate-high` | Copper depletion, GI effects, antibiotic interactions, and lozenge/high-dose confusion gate use |

## Practical Interpretation

Zinc belongs in [[mineral-status-brain-fog-cognition-watchlist]] as a low-confidence, safety-gated status candidate. The minimum useful record is diet, dose, form, duration, serum/plasma zinc if available, copper/ceruloplasmin if high-dose or chronic, CBC if symptomatic, immune context, and medication interactions.
