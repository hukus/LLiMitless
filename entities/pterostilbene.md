# Pterostilbene

Pterostilbene is a dimethylated stilbene polyphenol related to [[resveratrol]]. It is found in blueberries, grapes, and Pterocarpus marsupium, and it is more bioavailability-forward in the literature than resveratrol. In this wiki it is a preclinical Nrf2 / SIRT1 / anti-inflammatory AD hypothesis, not a proven human cognition enhancer.

Medicinal cognition signal: `preclinical-only`. The strongest direct cognition anchor is a beta-amyloid 1-42 mouse study where pterostilbene improved Y-maze, novel object recognition, Morris water maze, and passive avoidance performance while reducing oxidative-stress markers and activating Nrf2-linked antioxidant pathways.^[raw/articles/pubmed-33280250-pterostilbene-abeta-nrf2-mice.md] A separate SAMP8 aging / AD-model paper is useful because it argues that pterostilbene and resveratrol should not be treated as interchangeable, but it is still animal evidence.^[raw/articles/pubmed-21982274-pterostilbene-aging-ad-samp8.md]

Human cognition signal: `not established`. The 2025 AD review summarizes pterostilbene's anti-AD mechanisms across in vivo, in vitro, network-pharmacology, and docking evidence, but it does not create a human cognition-efficacy signal.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40291020-pterostilbene-ad-review.md] The earlier biomedical-applications review supports mechanism and bioavailability interest, not isolated clinical cognition efficacy.^[raw/articles/pubmed-23808710-pterostilbene-biomedical-applications-review.md]

Human safety / translational signal: `short-term safety and combination-program exposure`. A randomized human safety trial in 80 adults with hypercholesterolemia found no major biochemical or self-reported adverse-drug-reaction signal over 6-8 weeks at up to 250 mg/day, but it did not test cognition.^[raw/articles/pmc-3575612-pterostilbene-human-safety-trial.md] EH301, a nicotinamide riboside plus pterostilbene combination, is in an active-not-recruiting ALS registry as of the 2026-01-12 update, with no results posted in the captured view. That is a disease-modification combination program, not an isolated pterostilbene nootropic result.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct04562831-nr-pterostilbene-als.md]

## Practical Interpretation

Pterostilbene now deserves its own low-confidence page because it is repeatedly named in the resveratrol / blueberry / longevity polyphenol lane. The page should prevent evidence borrowing: pterostilbene cannot inherit [[resveratrol]]'s RESHAW human cognition signal, and resveratrol cannot inherit pterostilbene's animal AD-model signals.

Route pterostilbene through [[polyphenol-nrf2-cognition-watchlist]], [[resveratrol]], [[blueberry-anthocyanins]], and [[grape-food-products]]. A confidence upgrade would require an isolated human trial with prespecified cognitive endpoints, a defined trans-pterostilbene dose/formulation, biomarker confirmation, and responder phenotype reporting.
