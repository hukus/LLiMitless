# Caffeine

Caffeine is the baseline molecule for acute cognitive-state support. It should be treated as a comparator, not as a breakthrough nootropic: the human signal is strong for alertness, vigilance, attention, reaction time, and sleep-loss performance preservation, while memory and executive-function effects are less consistent and strongly constrained by dose, timing, tolerance, and withdrawal.

## Medicinal Signal

- Formal signal: `high` for acute vigilance and state support; `low` for durable general enhancement.
- Best context: sleep restriction, long duty cycles, acute fatigue, or low-alertness baseline.
- Main caveat: the same compound that improves performance can damage later sleep, which can erase the next-day benefit.
- The 2016 review anchors caffeine as a low-to-moderate-dose attention and vigilance aid, while repeated-dose sleep-loss work shows state rescue without proving durable cognitive gain.^[raw/articles/pubmed-27612937-caffeine-effects-cognitive-physical-occupational-performance.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-35791877-repeated-low-dose-caffeine-sleep-deprivation-cognition.md]
- Newer schedule work makes timing non-negotiable: 400 mg too close to bedtime worsens objective sleep, and the 2025 sleep meta-analysis found lower total sleep time, sleep efficiency, and slow-wave sleep across crossover trials.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39377163-caffeine-dose-timing-subsequent-sleep.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41124973-age-dose-specific-effects-caffeine-sleep-meta-analysis.md]
- Withdrawal is a major confound. Caffeine can look better when it is reversing withdrawal rather than enhancing above a stable baseline.^[raw/articles/pubmed-22992376-caffeine-attention-withdrawn-consumers.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-30861208-expectancy-caffeine-withdrawal-balanced-placebo-design.md]

## Safety And Use-Interpretation

- Main safety burden: insomnia, anxiety, blood-pressure / heart-rate effects in sensitive users, withdrawal, and next-day sleep debt.
- Any self-study or trial using caffeine needs a fixed timing rule, stable baseline intake, and washout accounting.
- Caffeine belongs in [[pharmacology-comparator-map]] and [[energy-substrate-comparator-map]] because it is the cheap baseline state-shifter that most novel interventions should beat or complement.

## Current Read

Caffeine is one of the strongest practical molecules in the wiki, but only because it is a reliable state tool. It is not evidence for a durable IQ increase, and it should not be used to validate a broad "cognitive enhancement" category without sleep, withdrawal, and timing controls.
