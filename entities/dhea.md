# DHEA

## Bottom Line

DHEA is a negative healthy-aging cognition comparator in this wiki. Medicinal signal is `low/negative` for cognitive enhancement because the DAWN trial found no cognitive benefit from 50 mg/day oral DHEA over 1 year in healthy older adults. Anecdotal cognition signal is `weak` in the current captured corpus and should be treated as hormone-state lore unless stable reports are captured.

## What It Is

DHEA is an adrenal steroid precursor often discussed in anti-aging and vitality contexts. In this wiki it belongs with [[neurosteroid-and-endocrine-state-cognition-2026-scan]], [[pregnenolone]], [[testosterone-trt]], and [[negative-evidence-watchlist-for-cognition-interventions]] as a cautionary example of a plausible endocrine molecule whose controlled cognition evidence does not support the popular "brain booster" frame.

## Medicinal Research Signal

The DAWN trial randomized 225 healthy older adults to oral DHEA 50 mg/day or placebo for 1 year. The study included six cognitive tests plus quality-of-life, mood, and sexual-function outcomes. Cognitive function did not improve over time versus placebo, and the trial also did not find durable wellbeing or sexual-function benefit. For cognition, this is a direct negative anchor in the population most often targeted by anti-aging claims.^[raw/articles/pubmed-18482290-dhea-dawn-trial.md]

## Anecdote Signal

No DHEA-specific cognition anecdote raw note is currently indexed. Public claims usually blend vitality, libido, mood, sleep, training, adrenal-state language, and anti-aging expectations. That makes them hypothesis-generating at most until captured with dose, age, sex, baseline hormone status, duration, cognitive domain, and adverse counterreports.

## Safety And Status

For cognition tracking, DHEA should be routed through endocrine and product-identity gates before any benefit claim is considered. Hormone-sensitive adverse effects, sex-specific responses, lab context, and product quality matter more than the current cognition signal.

## Signal Judgment

Medicinal signal: `low/negative` for healthy older-adult cognition.

Anecdotal signal: `weak` in the current captured corpus.

Practical route: negative endocrine comparator; do not upgrade anti-aging or vitality claims without objective cognition evidence.
