# Picamilon / Nicotinoyl-GABA

Picamilon is a synthetic nicotinoyl-GABA compound, often encountered as picamilon sodium / nicotinoyl-GABA sodium salt. The wiki treats it as a regional drug and U.S. regulatory gray-zone molecule, not as ordinary GABA, niacin, nicotinamide, or a lawful dietary-supplement ingredient.^[raw/articles/pubchem-2026-picamilon-sodium-identity.md]^[raw/articles/fda-picamilon-dietary-supplements.md]

## Medicinal Evidence

Picamilon's formal cognition evidence is not the same as a healthy-enhancement evidence base. The strongest current sources captured here are regional, clinical-state, and open-label or open comparative. In the 2024 chronic cerebral ischemia study, 44 patients were randomized to oral Picamilon 50 mg three times daily for 60 days, or 10 days of 100 mg intramuscular Picamilon followed by 50 mg oral three times daily. The abstract reports MoCA improvement over visits, sleep-quality improvement, neurological-scale improvement, 98% tolerability, and adverse events under 8.6%. This is relevant to vascular cognitive impairment, but it is small, open, and not placebo-controlled.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39113452-picamilon-chronic-cerebral-ischemia-open-rct.md]

The larger 2022 Picamilon-Ginkgo study is even more combination-bound. In 278 chronic cerebral ischemia patients with cognitive impairment, Picamilon-Ginkgo outperformed ginkgo monotherapy on MMSE, MoCA, and EQ-5D, with no significant Hamilton depression difference and no significant adverse events reported. Because the active arm combined picamilon with standardized ginkgo, it should not be used as isolated picamilon proof.^[raw/articles/pubmed-36168693-picamilon-ginkgo-chronic-cerebral-ischemia.md]

No captured source justifies routing picamilon as a broad healthy-adult nootropic. Its practical medicinal status is `low/region-specific`, with any possible cognitive effect currently tied to vascular clinical-state contexts rather than normal enhancement.

## Regulatory And Product Gates

FDA's position is the central routing fact for U.S.-market interpretation. FDA states that picamilon is a unique synthetic chemical entity made from niacin and GABA, crosses the blood-brain barrier, accumulates in the brain as a separate chemical entity, does not meet the statutory definition of a dietary ingredient, and is not approved as a drug in the United States. Products marketing it as a dietary ingredient are misbranded.^[raw/articles/fda-picamilon-dietary-supplements.md]

The label-accuracy problem is not theoretical. In the 2016 Drug Testing and Analysis study, 30 of 31 supplements labeled as picamilon contained picamilon, with daily labeled-serving exposure ranging from 2.7 mg to 721.5 mg. The authors concluded consumers could not get accurate information from labels about presence or quantity. This makes self-directed dosing and product comparisons unreliable.^[raw/articles/pubmed-26426301-vinpocetine-picamilon-supplement-labeling.md]

## Practical Interpretation

[[picamilon]] earns standalone coverage because it is a common nootropic-market name with formal regional clinical-state evidence and unusually clear U.S. regulatory/product-quality problems. It should be ranked risk-first: anecdotal calm-focus reports do not overcome unapproved-drug status, label inaccuracy, and the absence of clean healthy-adult cognition trials.
