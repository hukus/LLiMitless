# Hexarelin

## Bottom Line

Hexarelin is a human GH-release pharmacology comparator, not a cognition-proven nootropic. Medicinal cognition signal is `physiology-only`: a small double-blind placebo-controlled human dose-response study showed dose-dependent GH release after IV boluses, but no cognition endpoint. Anecdotal cognition signal is `absent-to-weak` in the current captured corpus.

## What It Is

Hexarelin is a synthetic growth-hormone secretagogue in the GHRP/GH-axis family. In this wiki it belongs with [[growth-hormone-secretagogue-cognition-2026-scan]], [[ghrh]], [[cjc-1295]], [[ipamorelin]], and [[peptide-safety-and-sourcing-risk-map]] as a physiology and safety comparator.

## Medicinal Research Signal

The captured formal anchor is a 1994 double-blind, placebo-controlled rising-dose study in 12 adult male volunteers. Hexarelin increased GH dose-dependently after single intravenous boluses. This confirms human GH-releasing activity, but it does not measure cognition, sleep architecture, function, or durable performance.^[raw/articles/pubmed-7957536-hexarelin-dose-response-humans.md]

Hexarelin appears in GH-secretagogue discussions because of pharmacology and stack logic, not because a cognition package exists. It should stay below [[ghrh]], [[tesamorelin]], and [[ibutamoren]] in any cognition ranking.
