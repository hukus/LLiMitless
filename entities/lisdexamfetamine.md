# Lisdexamfetamine

Lisdexamfetamine / Vyvanse is a prescription central-nervous-system stimulant and prodrug of [[d-amphetamine]]. It belongs in the wiki as a prodrug-amphetamine comparator beside [[methylphenidate]], [[modafinil]], [[phentermine]], and the ADHD-pipeline attention agents, not as a supplement or low-risk nootropic.

## Current Read

Medicinal cognition signal: `low/acute-stimulant`. A direct healthy-volunteer crossover study found acute task gains after a high lisdexamfetamine dose, but the evidence is single-dose, small, and safety-constrained rather than durable brain-fog or learning improvement proof.^[raw/articles/pubmed-29511807-lisdexamfetamine-damphetamine-healthy-cognition.md]

Clinical-state signal: `ADHD/BED prescription context`. Adult ADHD executive-function RCT evidence supports symptom / self-reported executive-function improvement in diagnosed ADHD with clinically significant executive-function deficits, and a 2024 crossover trial tested lisdexamfetamine for new-onset cognitive complaints after risk-reducing salpingo-oophorectomy. Those are clinical or phenotype-specific contexts, not general healthy-adult enhancement.^[raw/articles/pubmed-23945447-lisdexamfetamine-adult-adhd-executive-function.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39146756-lisdexamfetamine-rrso-cognitive-complaints.md]

Regulatory / safety signal: `Schedule II / risk-first`. The label identifies Vyvanse as a Schedule II stimulant indicated for ADHD and adult moderate-to-severe binge eating disorder, and explicitly says it is not indicated or recommended for weight loss.^[raw/articles/dailymed-2026-vyvanse-lisdexamfetamine-label.md]

## Human Evidence

| Evidence layer | What it says | Wiki interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| Healthy-volunteer crossover | Twenty-four healthy volunteers received placebo, d-amphetamine 40 mg, and lisdexamfetamine 100 mg. Both amphetamines improved go accuracy and vigilance; lisdexamfetamine improved DSST processing speed; neither affected Digit Span.^[raw/articles/pubmed-29511807-lisdexamfetamine-damphetamine-healthy-cognition.md] | Direct healthy-adult evidence exists, but it is acute, high-dose, and task-specific |
| Adult ADHD executive-function RCT | Adults with ADHD and executive-function deficits received optimized 30, 50, or 70 mg/day lisdexamfetamine or placebo for 10 weeks, with significant improvement on self-reported BRIEF-A executive-function ratings.^[raw/articles/pubmed-23945447-lisdexamfetamine-adult-adhd-executive-function.md] | Clinical ADHD function evidence; do not convert to healthy nootropic proof |
| Risk-reducing salpingo-oophorectomy cognitive-complaint trial | Sixty-nine participants with new-onset cognitive complaints after RRSO completed active and placebo crossover periods.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39146756-lisdexamfetamine-rrso-cognitive-complaints.md] | Rare non-ADHD cognitive-complaint context, still phenotype-specific |
| DailyMed label | CNS stimulant / amphetamine prodrug; ADHD and adult BED indications; boxed abuse, misuse, and addiction warning; Schedule II status; not for weight loss.^[raw/articles/dailymed-2026-vyvanse-lisdexamfetamine-label.md] | Safety, scheduling, and indication boundaries dominate any off-label cognition reading |

## Safety And Interpretation Gates

- Do not treat prodrug status as a safety waiver. The label still carries the stimulant boxed warning for abuse, misuse, addiction, overdose, and death.^[raw/articles/dailymed-2026-vyvanse-lisdexamfetamine-label.md]
- Main gates: Schedule II status, serious-cardiac-disease exclusions, blood pressure and heart-rate increases, psychiatric adverse reactions, Raynaud / peripheral vasculopathy, serotonin syndrome, tics / Tourette's, renal dose limits, insomnia, appetite and weight effects, dependence, tolerance, and overdose.
- The label's abuse-liability section is useful for interpretation: oral 100 mg lisdexamfetamine produced lower drug-liking than 40 mg immediate-release d-amphetamine, but 150 mg lisdexamfetamine produced similar drug-liking to 40 mg d-amphetamine and 200 mg diethylpropion in a drug-abuse-history study.^[raw/articles/dailymed-2026-vyvanse-lisdexamfetamine-label.md]
- Do not borrow weight-loss logic from [[phentermine]] or [[tesofensine]]. Vyvanse has a BED indication, but the label says it is not for weight loss.
- For stack interpretation, watch caffeine, tyrosine, phenylalanine, alpha-GPC, rhodiola, NAC, taurine, omega-3, protein timing, sleep, hydration, and antidepressant co-medication because these repeatedly appear in community explanations for benefit or brain fog.^[raw/articles/reddit-2020-2026-vyvanse-focus-brainfog-threads.md]

## Current Route

Lisdexamfetamine should be used as the prodrug-amphetamine comparator when a claim is about smooth onset, ADHD-state task initiation, long work blocks, or stimulant response with less immediate-release volatility. It sits below [[methylphenidate]] and [[modafinil]] as a healthy-adult cognition benchmark because its direct healthy evidence is acute and high-dose, while its practical signal is mostly clinical ADHD / BED use and confounded self-report.
