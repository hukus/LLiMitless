# Kynurenine

Kynurenine is the central tryptophan-catabolism branch-point metabolite for the wiki's immunometabolic cognition lane. It should not be collapsed with [[5-htp-tryptophan]], food protein, serotonin-precursor supplementation, or melatonin-stack claims.

Medicinal signal: `low / biomarker-and-challenge only`. The strongest human evidence says kynurenine-pathway biology changes with dementia, schizophrenia, stroke, and aging, but kynurenine itself is not a validated cognition intervention. The 2023 AD meta-analysis found a higher peripheral kynurenine-to-tryptophan ratio, while kynurenine itself and quinolinic acid were not consistently changed. The 2024 normal-aging review found age-related drift in kynurenine and the KYN/TRP ratio, which makes age correction mandatory before interpreting any single value.^[raw/articles/pubmed-36754068-kynurenine-pathway-alzheimers-meta-analysis.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-38056721-kynurenine-pathway-normal-age-review.md]

The best direct human perturbation source is the 2023 tryptophan-challenge study: 6 g oral tryptophan sharply raised kynurenine and [[kynurenic-acid]], but cognitive performance was mostly unchanged. That makes kynurenine useful as a pathway-stress readout, not a broad nootropic marker.^[raw/articles/pubmed-37118058-tryptophan-challenge-schizophrenia-cognition-cbf.md]

The gut-brain-axis layer makes this branch broader but not stronger as an enhancer. A 2026 review frames kynurenine, indole, and serotonin pathways as microbiome-host signaling routes and highlights IDO/TDO/KMO/KAT control points, while still treating therapeutic modulation as translational rather than cognition-proven.^[raw/articles/springer-2026-tryptophan-metabolism-gut-brain-signaling-review.md]

## Evidence Split

| Layer | Signal | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| AD / dementia meta-analysis | `low / altered pathway` | Lower blood tryptophan and higher KYN/TRP can appear in AD, but cognitive correlations are mostly inconclusive |
| Aging review | `biomarker context` | Blood tryptophan tends to fall and kynurenine/KYN-TRP tends to rise with age |
| Tryptophan challenge | `mechanistic probe` | KYN and KYNA can be raised acutely without a clear cognition win |
| Stroke cohort | `exploratory` | Baseline plasma kynurenines did not predict post-stroke cognitive impairment odds |

## Practical Interpretation

Use [[kynurenine]] as a routing node for inflammation-linked state deterioration, aging, and NMDA/glutamate comparator logic. It is not a supplement recommendation and should not be used to upgrade tryptophan or NAC anecdotes.
