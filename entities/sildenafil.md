# Sildenafil

Sildenafil is the PDE5 inhibitor anchor for the cGMP cognition hypothesis. It is mechanistically relevant through NO/cGMP signaling and neurovascular/plasticity arguments, but the current direct cognition evidence does not support using it as a nootropic.

Medicinal cognition signal: `low / direct negative`. A placebo-controlled schizophrenia study tested sildenafil 50 mg and 100 mg in 17 outpatients, with 15 completing all conditions. Neither dose improved cognition or symptoms versus placebo. One participant developed severe irritability requiring hospitalization after the 100 mg condition.^[raw/articles/pubmed-18716761-sildenafil-schizophrenia.md]

Class-level signal: `mechanistic but unproven`. A 2025 review argues that PDE5 inhibitors including sildenafil, tadalafil, and vardenafil remain plausible for cognitive decline through NO-cGMP signaling, neurovascular coupling, synaptic plasticity, and CREB, but it also states that human evidence is mixed and no PDE5 inhibitor is approved for cognition. [[tadalafil]] and [[vardenafil]] now have separate companion pages because the 2025 ETLAS-2 CSVD program and the healthy-adult vardenafil study add direct PDE5 cognition-null / mixed evidence that should not be hidden under sildenafil.^[raw/articles/mdpi-cells-14191505-pde5-cognitive-decline-review.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40718899-tadalafil-csVD-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41332889-tadalafil-csVD-cognition-substudy.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-23427190-vardenafil-healthy-adults.md]

## Signal Judgment

Sildenafil belongs beside [[bi-409306]], [[pf-04447943]], and [[tak-063]] as a cGMP translator comparator. It should not be ranked as a cognitive enhancer unless a controlled human cognition endpoint separates from placebo in an appropriate population.
