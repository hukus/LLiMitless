# Alpha-GPC

Alpha-GPC, usually called choline alphoscerate or choline alfoscerate in clinical sources, is a choline donor and cholinergic-support candidate. It belongs in the wiki as a phenotype-bound impairment / recovery molecule, not as a proven healthy-adult attention enhancer.

Medicinal signal: `low-moderate`. The strongest current captured source is a 100-person randomized double-blind placebo-controlled amnestic-MCI trial where 600 mg choline alphoscerate for 12 weeks improved ADAS-cog versus placebo without serious adverse events. A smaller prospective mild-TBI recovery study used 400 mg twice daily for 8 weeks and reported greater K-MMSE improvement than control. The negative choline-bioavailability comparator is the lysolecithin trial: higher plasma choline did not improve memory in healthy adults.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39300341-choline-alphoscerate-amnestic-mci.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40353278-choline-alfoscerate-mtbi-recovery.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39896163-lysolecithin-cognition-choline.md]

## Signal Split

| Layer | Signal | Readout |
|---|---|---|
| Amnestic MCI | `low-moderate` medicinal | Small placebo-controlled trial; ADAS-cog improvement |
| Mild TBI recovery | `low` medicinal | Prospective case-control recovery signal |
| Healthy adults | `negative/unclear` | Choline-bioavailability movement does not guarantee memory improvement |
| Anecdote | `weak` | Choline appears mostly as a stack cofactor, not as an isolated signal |
