# L-Methylfolate / 5-MTHF

L-methylfolate, 5-MTHF, levomefolate, or 5-methyltetrahydrofolate is a supplemental folate form that sits inside the one-carbon cycle. In this wiki it is the form-specific methylfolate route for users who discuss "methylation," MTHFR, methylated B vitamins, SAMe, and homocysteine. It belongs beside [[folate-b-vitamins]], [[folinic-acid-leucovorin]], [[methylcobalamin-b12]], [[s-adenosyl-l-methionine]], [[l-methionine]], [[betaine-tmg]], and [[choline-donor-cognition-signal-map]], but it should not inherit their evidence automatically.

Medicinal cognition signal: `low and indirect`. The strongest folate/B-vitamin cognition evidence remains status-gated and mostly MCI/high-homocysteine rather than healthy-adult enhancement. For L-methylfolate specifically, the formal human evidence captured here is depression-adjunct evidence, not objective cognition evidence. Mood improvement can plausibly reduce depression-linked brain fog, but that is not the same claim as direct cognitive enhancement.^[raw/articles/ods-2025-folate-health-professional-fact-sheet.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-23212058-lmethylfolate-ssri-resistant-depression-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-34450256-folate-adjunct-depression-meta.md]

Mood-state signal: `low-moderate, clinical-state bound`. A 2012 two-trial SSRI-resistant MDD study found the first L-methylfolate trial null, while the second, smaller trial found 15 mg/day adjunctive L-methylfolate improved response and depression-score outcomes with a response NNT of about 6. A 2021 meta-analysis of folate adjunct therapy found improved depression scores, response, and remission, but it pooled L-methylfolate and folic acid. This keeps methylfolate visible as a mood-state comparator to [[s-adenosyl-l-methionine]], not as a nootropic-grade molecule.^[raw/articles/pubmed-23212058-lmethylfolate-ssri-resistant-depression-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-34450256-folate-adjunct-depression-meta.md]

Status and safety signal: `lab-gated`. ODS treats folate as a one-carbon-transfer nutrient required for DNA/RNA synthesis, amino-acid metabolism, and homocysteine-to-methionine conversion. It also separates food folates, folic acid, and supplemental 5-MTHF / methylfolate. Interpretation needs folate, B12, methylmalonic acid, homocysteine, kidney function, medications, and dose/form context, because high folate exposure can complicate low-B12 interpretation and cognitive-risk discussions.^[raw/articles/ods-2025-folate-health-professional-fact-sheet.md]

Anecdote signal: `weak-moderate and adverse-prone`. Captured community reports include methylfolate or methylated B-complexes worsening anxiety, panic, rumination, depressed feelings, histamine-like reactions, body sensations, insomnia, and brain fog. Broader methyl-donor threads also include focus, mood, task-initiation, and brain-fog improvement claims, but methylfolate is often embedded in SAMe, TMG, B12, choline, histamine, COMT/MTHFR, medication, and long-COVID/MCAS narratives.^[raw/articles/reddit-2025-methylfolate-mental-side-effects-thread.md]^[raw/articles/reddit-2022-2026-same-tmg-methyl-donor-brainfog-threads.md]

## Practical Interpretation

L-methylfolate should be routed as a `status/mood-state methylated folate`, not as a general cognition enhancer. It is worth tracking because methylfolate is common in brain-fog self-experimentation and because formal depression-adjunct evidence exists, but the current cognition claim is indirect: deficiency or high-homocysteine correction belongs to [[folate-b-vitamins]], depression-linked fog belongs near [[s-adenosyl-l-methionine]], and adverse activation belongs in the anecdote/safety layer.

Any future confidence upgrade needs a trial that isolates methylfolate form and dose, measures objective cognition, stratifies folate/B12/MMA/homocysteine status, separates mood response from cognitive response, and records antidepressant, stimulant, and methyl-donor co-use.
