# p-Synephrine

p-Synephrine is a protoalkaloid associated with bitter orange / Citrus aurantium products. It appears in weight-loss, energy, sports-performance, and preworkout supplements, sometimes as "bitter orange" rather than a clearly assayed p-synephrine dose. This page covers p-synephrine and bitter-orange extract as stimulant-adjacent supplement ingredients, not phenylephrine/m-synephrine, ephedrine, octopamine, higenamine, hordenine, yohimbine, [[caffeine]], [[paraxanthine]], or multi-ingredient fat burners.

Medicinal cognition signal: `risk-first/low, no direct cognition evidence`. The formal sources are safety, weight-loss, performance, and regulatory-context sources rather than cognition trials. NCCIH says bitter orange has not been clearly shown helpful for any health-related purpose and notes inconsistent small human studies, combination-product confounds, possible cardiovascular concerns, label inaccuracies, and adulteration with synthetic amines. The 2022 meta-analysis found inconclusive benefits and blood-pressure/heart-rate safety concerns, not cognition efficacy.^[raw/articles/nccih-bitter-orange-synephrine-safety.md]^[raw/articles/pmc-9572433-bitter-orange-synephrine-meta-analysis.md]

Sports/regulatory signal: `visible but not a cognition qualifier`. NCCIH notes NCAA bans synephrine as a stimulant, while the WADA 2026 monitoring program lists synephrine for in-competition monitoring rather than prohibition. This makes it a sports-visibility and product-quality branch, not a cognitive enhancer.^[raw/articles/nccih-bitter-orange-synephrine-safety.md]^[raw/articles/wada-2026-monitoring-program.md]

Anecdote signal: `weak-moderate energy/well-being salience, weak cognition-specific, adverse counterweight`. The captured /r/Supplements energy thread includes subjective enjoyment and general well-being from synephrine; the adverse bitter-orange thread points to anxiety, irritability, and aggression in a multi-ingredient product. Both are low-confidence, product-contextual, and far weaker than controlled cognition evidence.^[raw/articles/reddit-2024-synephrine-supplements-energy-thread.md]^[raw/articles/reddit-2025-bitter-orange-adverse-reaction-thread.md]

## Identity Gates

- Separate p-synephrine from phenylephrine/m-synephrine, ephedrine, octopamine, higenamine, hordenine, yohimbine, and bitter-orange whole-product claims.
- Track actual assayed dose, not just "bitter orange extract" milligrams.
- Treat caffeine, yohimbine/rauwolscine, higenamine, hordenine, octopamine, DMAA-like adulterants, and multi-stimulant fat burners as major confounds.
- Watch blood pressure, heart rhythm, anxiety, irritability, panic vulnerability, stimulant medications, MAO inhibitors, cardiovascular disease, pregnancy/breastfeeding, age under 18, athlete testing, and product-label accuracy.
- Route p-synephrine and bitter-orange anecdotes through [[safety-interaction-matrix-for-multi-modal-stacks]] before interpreting energy, well-being, fat-loss, workout, or focus reports as cognition.

## Practical Ranking

p-Synephrine deserves standalone coverage because it is common in energy/preworkout/fat-loss stacks and has safety and sports-visibility consequences. It should not be ranked as a cognitive enhancer. The current route is `risk-first stimulant-adjacent / sports-visible supplement ingredient`.
