# Schisandra Chinensis

Schisandra chinensis, also called wu wei zi or five-flavor berry, is a traditional Chinese medicine fruit used in vitality, fatigue, sleep, and liver formulas. In this wiki it is a low-confidence adaptogen ingredient, not a proven standalone nootropic.

## Bottom Line

Medicinal cognition signal: `low/formula-limited`. The strongest captured cognition source is ADAPT-232, a fixed combination of [[rhodiola-rosea]], Schisandra, and [[eleutherococcus-senticosus]], which improved d2 attention/speed/accuracy after a single dose in stressed/tired healthy women. That cannot be attributed to Schisandra alone.^[raw/articles/sciencedirect-2010-adapt232-stressed-healthy-adults-cognition-rct.md]

Anecdote signal: `weak-moderate`. Captured Reddit sources show nootropic source-sharing and a symptom-state brain-fog/mood report in endometriosis, but neither provides a controlled denominator, product assay, or objective cognitive test.^[raw/articles/reddit-2021-schisandra-nootropics-thread.md]^[raw/articles/reddit-2024-schisandra-endo-brainfog-thread.md]

## Evidence Layers

The ADAPT-232 trial is best read as stress-state formula evidence. It fits the wiki's state-restoration branch because the participants were chronically stressed and the task design intentionally induced cognitive fatigue before testing. It does not show rested-baseline enhancement.^[raw/articles/sciencedirect-2010-adapt232-stressed-healthy-adults-cognition-rct.md]

MSKCC's Schisandra monograph says human studies are too limited to draw conclusions. It notes traditional use as a vitality tonic and in fatigue/sleep formulas, with lab and animal signals for antioxidant and nervous-system protection, but not enough human proof.^[raw/articles/mskcc-2026-schisandra-safety.md]

## Safety And Status

Schisandra is a drug-interaction watch item. MSKCC flags CYP1A2, CYP3A4, CYP3A5, and P-glycoprotein substrate concerns, tacrolimus interaction evidence, and possible liver-enzyme lab effects. These are stronger practical gates than the current cognition signal.^[raw/articles/mskcc-2026-schisandra-safety.md]

## Signal Judgment

Schisandra is page-worthy because it is common in adaptogen/nootropic discourse and appears in a cognition-positive formula. It should remain below [[rhodiola-rosea]], [[ginseng]], [[bacopa]], and [[cordyceps-cordycepin]] until Schisandra-alone products have controlled cognition data.
