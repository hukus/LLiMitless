# Magnesium

Magnesium is an essential mineral involved in energy metabolism, nerve and muscle function, blood pressure regulation, glucose control, and normal rhythm. This page covers generic magnesium status and common supplement forms. It should not be collapsed with [[magnesium-l-threonate]], where the product-specific Magtein cognition/sleep trial and direct anecdote thread are tracked separately.

Medicinal signal: `low for generic cognition, low-moderate only for product/status-specific branches`. A 2024 systematic review/meta-analysis supports an association between magnesium exposure and adult cognitive health, but randomized trial evidence is too limited to infer a reliable generic supplement effect. The stronger wiki signal belongs to [[magnesium-l-threonate]]: a 2026 Magtein RCT in sleep-dissatisfied adults found NIH Toolbox and reaction-time improvements while Raven's and several sleep outcomes were null. That result does not transfer automatically to glycinate, citrate, oxide, chloride, dietary magnesium, or mixed electrolyte stacks.^[raw/articles/pmc-11362647-magnesium-cognitive-health-meta.md]^[raw/articles/frontiers-2026-magnesium-l-threonate-cognition-sleep-rct.md]

Safety/status signal: `moderate`. ODS notes that magnesium status is hard to assess from serum alone. The adult tolerable upper intake level for supplemental magnesium is 350 mg/day, excluding food magnesium. High-dose supplemental magnesium can cause diarrhea, nausea, cramping, hypotension, lethargy, confusion, arrhythmia, and cardiac arrest in severe toxicity; kidney impairment increases risk.^[raw/articles/ods-2026-magnesium-health-professional-fact-sheet.md]

## Signal Split

| Layer | Signal | Readout |
|---|---|---|
| Dietary/status magnesium | `low/associational` | Observational support, weak RCT inference |
| [[magnesium-l-threonate]] / Magtein | `low-moderate/product-specific` | Sleep-dissatisfied-adult NIH cognition and reaction-time signal with Raven's null |
| Sleep/anxiety/migraine context | `plausible indirect` | Benefits may be sleep or symptom mediated rather than direct cognition |
| Anecdote | `moderate mixed salience` | Form-specific calm, focus, sleep, fogginess, and paradoxical effects |
| Safety | `moderate` | GI effects, kidney function, medication interactions, and dose/form matter |

## Practical Interpretation

Generic magnesium belongs in [[mineral-status-brain-fog-cognition-watchlist]] as a sleep/status/metabolic gate, while [[magnesium-l-threonate]] remains the product-specific cognition branch. The minimum useful record is form, elemental dose, timing, kidney function, bowel tolerance, sleep metrics, migraine/cramp/anxiety context, diet, and interacting medications.
