# Folate / B Vitamins

This page covers the cognition-relevant folate/B-vitamin lane: folate or folic acid (B9), [[folinic-acid-leucovorin]], vitamin B12 / cobalamins including [[methylcobalamin-b12]], [[vitamin-b6-p5p]], [[riboflavin-b2]], [[l-methylfolate-5-mthf]], and [[homocysteine]]-lowering combinations. It is tracked as a nutrient-status and one-carbon-metabolism branch, not as a generic energy or focus enhancer. It should not be collapsed with [[dha-epa]], [[vitamin-d3]], multivitamins, prenatal folate, methylation-genotype protocols, B12 injections for confirmed deficiency, or folic-acid+DHA stacks without attribution.

For adjacent SAMe, TMG, choline, creatine methyl-sparing, folinic-acid, P5P/B6, riboflavin/B2, and methylation-community claims, use [[one-carbon-methyl-donor-cognition-watchlist]], [[folinic-acid-leucovorin]], [[vitamin-b6-p5p]], [[riboflavin-b2]], [[s-adenosyl-l-methionine]], [[l-methylfolate-5-mthf]], and [[betaine-tmg]]. This page stays focused on folate/B12/B6/B2 status, homocysteine, and B-vitamin safety rather than treating all methyl donors as one intervention.

Medicinal cognition signal: `low/contested and homocysteine-status gated`. The strongest clean signal is VITACOG: folic acid 0.8 mg/day plus B12 0.5 mg/day plus B6 20 mg/day for 2 years slowed whole-brain atrophy in MCI, especially when baseline homocysteine was high, and the cognitive-outcome paper found benefit in elevated-homocysteine subgroups. A 2025 methyl-nutrients network meta-analysis also reports folate plus vitamin B12 as the best methyl-nutrient option for older adults with MCI, while a 2024 MCI meta-analysis reports positive vitamin effects with folic acid prominent and the 2026 nutrient NMA reports folic acid plus DHA memory support. This is enough for a standalone entity, but the route is `MCI / high homocysteine / deficiency or insufficiency`, not broad enhancement.^[raw/articles/pmc-2935890-vitacog-b-vitamins-mci-brain-atrophy.md]^[raw/articles/oxford-2012-vitacog-b-vitamins-mci-cognition.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40249981-methyl-nutrients-mci-nma.md]^[raw/articles/pmc-11139751-vitamins-pufas-mci-meta.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41764841-nutritional-supplements-cognition-nma.md]

The counterweights are equally important. A DARE-assessed 2012 meta-analysis concluded that B12, B6, or folic acid supplementation did not improve cognitive function in older adults with or without cognitive impairment. A 2021 B12/B-complex RCT meta-analysis found no evidence of benefit on any cognitive subdomain, depressive symptoms, or general fatigue in people without overt B12 deficiency or advanced neurological disorders. A 2020 trial of methylcobalamin 500 mcg plus folic acid 400 mcg in older MCI patients with elevated homocysteine lowered homocysteine but did not significantly reduce cognitive decline at 24 months. ODS's B12 fact sheet similarly emphasizes that RCTs generally do not show cognitive improvement from B12 alone or with folate/B6 when deficiency treatment is not the question.^[raw/articles/ncbi-bookshelf-2012-homocysteine-lowering-b-vitamins-cognition-meta.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-33809274-b12-cognition-fatigue-meta.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-31787369-methylcobalamin-folic-acid-mci-rct.md]^[raw/articles/ods-2025-vitamin-b12-health-professional-fact-sheet.md]

Combination attribution is fragile. A 2024 MCI trial reported cognitive and oxidative/mitochondrial biomarker benefits from folic acid, DHA, and their combination, with the combination outperforming either alone. However, an earlier Journal of Alzheimer's Disease folic-acid-plus-DHA paper from the same broad claim area was retracted for ethics approval and registry issues. The wiki should keep FA, DHA, FA+DHA, B12, B6, and homocysteine-lowering stacks separate until trial lineage, registry status, and independent replication are clear.^[raw/articles/karger-2024-folic-acid-dha-mci-cognition-rct.md]^[raw/articles/jad-2023-folic-acid-dha-retraction.md]

Folinic acid / leucovorin is now separated from generic folate and methylfolate. FDA's 2026 Wellcovorin expansion covers confirmed `FOLR1`-variant cerebral folate deficiency, a rare folate-transport disorder, not healthy-adult brain fog. Pediatric ASD/FRAA studies make the branch clinically visible, but one recent positive ASD RCT is now indexed as retracted, so leucovorin belongs in a source-integrity and phenotype-gated route rather than a broad folate win.^[raw/articles/fda-2026-wellcovorin-cfd-folr1-approval.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-27752075-folinic-acid-autism-language-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-34834493-leucovorin-cfd-autism-meta-review.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39243316-41606385-folinic-acid-autism-retraction.md]

B6/P5P and riboflavin/B2 now sit beside the folate and B12 form pages. [[vitamin-b6-p5p]] has real PLP cofactor relevance and safety importance, but the direct B6 cognition review is mostly null. [[riboflavin-b2]] has a cleaner MTHFR/FAD and homocysteine-status story, including an MTHFR 677TT blood-pressure RCT and a genotype/status homocysteine study, but no direct cognition endpoint. These pages close cofactor attribution gaps without upgrading B-vitamin stacks into broad nootropics.^[raw/articles/pubmed-14584010-vitamin-b6-cognition-cochrane.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-12042457-b-vitamins-memory-women-rct.md]^[raw/articles/ncbi-bookshelf-2013-riboflavin-mthfr.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-23608654-riboflavin-mthfr-677tt-bp-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-25322900-riboflavin-status-mthfr-homocysteine.md]

Anecdote signal: `weak-moderate, deficiency-skewed, and adverse-prone`. Community reports often describe B12, folate, folinic acid, P5P/B6, B2/riboflavin, or B-complexes improving brain fog, migraines, mood, or baseline energy when deficiency or cofactor mismatch is suspected. The same communities report demotivation, wired anxiety, panic, insomnia, rumination, irritability, histamine-like reactions, and "too methylated" experiences, especially with high-dose methylcobalamin, [[l-methylfolate-5-mthf]], [[folinic-acid-leucovorin]], P5P, or methylated B-complexes. Anecdote should prompt lab-status and dose/form tracking; it cannot upgrade efficacy.^[raw/articles/reddit-2025-b12-methylcobalamin-brain-fog-demotivation-thread.md]^[raw/articles/reddit-2025-methylfolate-mental-side-effects-thread.md]^[raw/articles/reddit-2024-2026-folinic-acid-methylfolate-brainfog-threads.md]^[raw/articles/reddit-2023-2026-p5p-riboflavin-mthfr-brainfog-threads.md]

Safety and status signal: `moderate lab-guided caution`. Folate status, B12 status, methylmalonic acid, homocysteine, kidney function, medication exposure, and form/dose all matter. High folic acid can mask B12-deficiency anemia while neurological injury progresses, and ODS notes concern around low B12 plus high folic acid in older adults. B12 has low toxicity and no established UL, but deficiency cause matters. B6 is not benign at high dose: chronic high-dose pyridoxine can cause sensory neuropathy, and EFSA's adult UL is much lower than the US UL. Confirmed deficiency treatment is medical care, not nootropic experimentation.^[raw/articles/ods-2025-folate-health-professional-fact-sheet.md]^[raw/articles/ods-2025-vitamin-b12-health-professional-fact-sheet.md]^[raw/articles/ods-2023-vitamin-b6-health-professional-fact-sheet.md]

## Practical Interpretation

Folate/B vitamins belong in the molecule atlas because the homocysteine/MCI branch has real RCT and biomarker support, because B12/folate/B6 deficiency can cause cognitive or neurological symptoms, because folinic acid now has a narrow FDA-approved folate-transport-defect route, and because B2/B6 cofactors clarify methylation-stack attribution. They should not be ranked as broad cognitive enhancers. The best read is `status correction, rare folate-transport treatment, elevated-homocysteine MCI support, and cofactor safety triage`, with broad B12/B-complex supplementation in non-deficient people looking null.

Any confidence upgrade requires trials that pre-register endpoints, stratify by folate, B12, MMA, homocysteine, kidney function, baseline cognition, B6/PLP status, riboflavin status, and DHA status, and clearly separate folic acid, [[folinic-acid-leucovorin]], 5-MTHF, B12 form, B6/P5P dose, riboflavin/B2, and DHA co-supplementation. Anecdote should be used for adverse-event and status-screening questions, not recommendation strength.
