# CSTI-500

CSTI-500 / BMS-866949 is a newer monoamine triple reuptake inhibitor with first-in-human healthy-volunteer safety, PK, and PET transporter-occupancy data. It belongs in [[triple-reuptake-inhibitor-cognition-watchlist]] and sits near [[tesofensine]] only at the broad SERT/NET/DAT target-engagement layer.

## Current Read

Medicinal cognition signal: `target-engagement only`. The captured human source is not a cognition trial. It is valuable because it measured tolerability, half-life, SERT occupancy, and DAT occupancy in healthy volunteers after single ascending doses.^[raw/articles/pmc-12979414-csti500-first-in-human-tri.md]

After a single 100 mg dose, the PET substudy estimated mean striatal SERT occupancy of 72% at 4-6 hours and 62% at 24 hours, plus mean striatal DAT occupancy of 36% at 4-9 hours and 31% at 24 hours. The estimated half-life was 44-50 hours.^[raw/articles/pmc-12979414-csti500-first-in-human-tri.md]

Safety gate: the maximum tolerable acute dose was 100 mg. At 150 mg, most active-treated subjects had acute moderate to severe nausea and dizziness, one had severe vomiting, and heart rate increased markedly.^[raw/articles/pmc-12979414-csti500-first-in-human-tri.md]

## Interpretation Gates

- Do not treat PET transporter occupancy as cognitive efficacy.
- Separate SERT/DAT occupancy from antidepressant, appetite, alertness, and cognition endpoints.
- Watch long-ish half-life, dose accumulation, nausea/dizziness/vomiting, HR effects, insomnia/activation, and monoamine drug stacking.
