# Selenium

Selenium is an essential trace element incorporated into selenoproteins involved in thyroid hormone metabolism, antioxidant defense, DNA synthesis, reproduction, and immune function. In this wiki it is a thyroid/redox/status candidate with a narrow safety margin, not a general nootropic. It should not be collapsed with Brazil nuts, [[iodine]], thyroid supplements, multivitamins, or antioxidant stacks.

Medicinal cognition signal: `low and clinical-state specific`. The captured human cognition anchor is a small Brazil-nut pilot in older adults with mild cognitive impairment, where selenium status movement was linked to selected cognitive measures. This supports selenium as a low-confidence watch item in low-status or MCI-like contexts, not as healthy-adult enhancement proof.^[raw/articles/pubmed-25567069-brazil-nut-selenium-mci-rct.md]

Safety/status signal: `high for dose discipline`. ODS lists an adult recommended intake of 55 mcg/day and an adult tolerable upper intake level of 400 mcg/day. Excess selenium can cause hair and nail changes, garlic breath odor, metallic taste, rash, nausea, diarrhea, fatigue, irritability, nervous-system abnormalities, and severe toxicity. Brazil nuts can contain highly variable selenium amounts.^[raw/articles/ods-2025-selenium-health-professional-fact-sheet.md]

| Layer | Signal | Readout |

| Brazil nut MCI pilot | `low/clinical-state` | Small older-adult MCI food-matrix signal, not healthy enhancement |

| Replete healthy adults | `not established` | No broad cognition-enhancement evidence |
| Anecdote | `weak-moderate thyroid-status salience` | Often bundled with iodine, kelp, Brazil nuts, and thyroid explanations |
| Safety | `high` | Narrow margin, variable Brazil-nut dose, and multivitamin stacking risk |

## Practical Interpretation

Selenium belongs in [[mineral-status-brain-fog-cognition-watchlist]] as a low-confidence, narrow-margin status candidate. The minimum useful record is dietary selenium sources, Brazil-nut count and origin if relevant, multivitamin use, standalone dose, thyroid labs, selenium status if measured, hair/nail/GI symptoms, and total daily exposure.
