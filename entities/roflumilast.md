# Roflumilast

Roflumilast is the canonical small-molecule PDE4 inhibitor in the cognition literature. It is approved for COPD at 500 micrograms daily, but the cognition work has consistently used much lower doses to stay inside a tolerability window and probe episodic memory, working memory, and task-specific plasticity.

## Key Facts

- Mechanism: selective PDE4 inhibition with downstream cAMP signaling effects.
- Healthy-adult studies: low-dose acute roflumilast improved verbal memory in young adults and delayed verbal recall in older adults.
- Schizophrenia studies: low-dose roflumilast improved verbal memory and working-memory-related brain activity.
- Post-stroke cognitive impairment: the 2026 ROSTMEMA trial added a newer phenotype-bound positive signal in delayed recall and story memory, with a non-significant primary ANCOVA and a significant post hoc covariate-adjusted analysis.
- The 2026 PSCI result is the clearest newer signal, but it remains modest and analysis-sensitive rather than class-defining; the open-label extension is supportive rather than independent replication.
- Safety surveillance: a 2026 FAERS study of 2,142 roflumilast reports found sex-specific heterogeneity, with male-skewed signals and median time-to-onset of 42 days, which is a monitoring reminder rather than an efficacy change.
- Ongoing or pending phenotypes: amnestic MCI / mild dementia and Fragile X challenge work have been active but have not changed the general interpretation yet.
- Safety: higher doses run into a tolerability ceiling, so the cognition literature stays in the low-dose range.

## What It Suggests

- PDE4 inhibition is a real human cognition signal, not just a preclinical story.
- The effect is phenotype-bound and dose-sensitive rather than a broad healthy-adult enhancer effect.
- The strongest roflumilast signal now lives in memory-impaired or recovery phenotypes, especially PSCI.
- The newest human result strengthens the "recovery phenotype" reading more than a general enhancement reading.
- The compound has enough human density to deserve its own page rather than remaining only a line item in the pathway scan.

## Limits

- The positive literature is still small and centered in one translational ecosystem.
- The better signals are modest and task-specific rather than domain-wide.
- Some analyses are sensitive to covariate handling, so effect size interpretation matters.
- Open-label extension data are informative but not equivalent to independent replication.
