# Pramiracetam

Pramiracetam is a racetam-class small molecule, historically associated with CI-879 / Pramistar-style products. It should not be collapsed into piracetam, oxiracetam, aniracetam, [[noopept]], or generic racetam-stack anecdotes.^[raw/articles/pubchem-2026-pramiracetam-identity.md]

## Bottom Line

Medicinal signal: `low-moderate/state-rescue, not broad enhancement`. The strongest human sources are not ordinary healthy-adult trials: pramiracetam partially reduced scopolamine-induced amnesia in healthy volunteers, and a small head-injury/anoxia study reported delayed-recall improvement. A cerebrovascular/stroke-consequence source adds clinical-state support. This keeps pramiracetam above many racetam long-tail names but below modern, replicated healthy-adult candidates.^[raw/articles/pubmed-15374306-pramiracetam-scopolamine-amnesia-expanded.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-1786500-pramiracetam-head-injury-anoxia-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-14965012-pramistar-cerebrovascular-memory.md]

Anecdotal signal: `moderate`, mostly racetam-community focus/memory salience with emotional blunting, irritability, and motivation-tone caveats. It does not upgrade the medicinal rating.^[raw/articles/reddit-2020-pramiracetam-review-thread.md]

Practical route: track [[pramiracetam]] as a recovery/challenge-state racetam comparator near [[hydergine-ergoloid-mesylates]], [[nicergoline]], [[citicoline]], and [[cholinergic-state-restoration-and-cognition]], while keeping it out of the top healthy-enhancement tier.

## Human Evidence

The cleanest healthy-volunteer signal is pharmacological challenge, not baseline enhancement. In the 1994 scopolamine study, two groups of 12 men received pramiracetam 600 mg twice daily or placebo for 10 days, then scopolamine. Pramiracetam partially reduced scopolamine-induced amnesic effects in young and older volunteers. This is relevant to cholinergic impairment and anticholinergic burden hypotheses, not proof that unimpaired users should expect a memory upgrade.^[raw/articles/pubmed-15374306-pramiracetam-scopolamine-amnesia-expanded.md]

The 1991 Brain Injury study tested pramiracetam sulfate 400 mg three times daily in young males with memory and cognitive problems after head injury and anoxia. The abstract reports clinically significant memory improvement, especially delayed recall, versus placebo, with maintenance during an open-label continuation and after discontinuation follow-up. This is meaningful recovery-state evidence but narrow and old.^[raw/articles/pubmed-1786500-pramiracetam-head-injury-anoxia-rct.md]

The Pramistar cerebrovascular source reports statistically significant heterogeneous effects on memory-related neuropsychological symptoms in chronic cerebral insufficiency and stroke consequences. It supports clinical-state tracking but is not a modern placebo-controlled healthy cognition trial.^[raw/articles/pubmed-14965012-pramistar-cerebrovascular-memory.md]

## Safety And Status

PubChem summarizes prior approval in some eastern European countries and historical U.S. orphan-drug designation, but this is not equivalent to a current widely approved nootropic indication. Chemical-hazard entries flag acute oral toxicity classification from ECHA notifications, which should be treated as a product-handling / sourcing warning rather than a dose-specific clinical risk estimate.^[raw/articles/pubchem-2026-pramiracetam-identity.md]

Community use often involves gray-market sourcing, powder/capsule identity uncertainty, choline-stack assumptions, and mood-tone changes. These are interpretation gates, not proof of efficacy.

## Signal Judgment

Pramiracetam deserves standalone coverage because it has a better human cognition bridge than many racetam names: an anticholinergic-challenge study and a recovery-state memory study. It still remains low-confidence for general nootropic use because the direct evidence is old, small, and state-bound.

Route pramiracetam reports through [[safety-interaction-matrix-for-multi-modal-stacks]] when choline donors, stimulant co-use, recovery from injury/anoxia, anticholinergic challenge logic, emotional blunting, irritability, or gray-market sourcing shape the perceived effect.
