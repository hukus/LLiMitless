# Pinealon

Pinealon is the tripeptide Glu-Asp-Arg, usually written as `EDR`, and it sits in the legacy peptide-bioregulator cluster alongside [[semax]], [[cortexin]], and [[kortagen]]. The branch now deserves a more serious read than a background bioregulator footnote because it has developed a repeated sleep / circadian / TBI-recovery anecdote lane on top of its narrow but coherent preclinical literature. That still does not create human cognition proof; it upgrades Pinealon into a real state-restoration frontier node.^[raw/articles/pubchem-10273502-pinealon-identity.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-21978084-pinealon-cell-viability.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-28539017-comparative-analysis-geroprotective-methods-pinealon-vezugen.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39518916-short-peptides-induced-neurons-age-related-changes.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-24909721-pinealon-serotonin-expression-brain-cortex.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-22803085-bioregulatory-tripeptides-skin-cells.md]

## Identity

PubChem normalizes Pinealon to Glu-Asp-Arg with sequence `EDR`, which matters because the literature often switches between `Pinealon`, `EDR peptide`, and `Glu-Asp-Arg` when discussing the same tripeptide.^[raw/articles/pubchem-10273502-pinealon-identity.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-33396470-edr-peptide-mechanism-alzheimers-disease.md]

## Evidence Snapshot

- In vitro: Pinealon reduced ROS accumulation, decreased necrotic cell death, and altered ERK1/2 / cell-cycle behavior in stressed cells.^[raw/articles/pubmed-21978084-pinealon-cell-viability.md]
- Rodents: Pinealon improved offspring learning in a prenatal hyperhomocysteinemia model, improved maze learning in young and old rats, and shifted hypoxic / inflammatory / caspase-3 readouts in older rats.^[raw/articles/pubmed-22567179-pinealon-hyperhomocysteinemia-rat-offspring.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-28976148-pinealon-cortexin-rat-learning-caspase-3.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-25051764-cortexin-pinealon-old-rats-hypoxic-hypoxia-cytokines-caspase3.md]
- Human-adjacent: the 2024 induced-neuron paper suggests EDR can protect aging human neurons from dendritic loss and oxidative DNA damage, but that is still not a clinical cognition endpoint.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39518916-short-peptides-induced-neurons-age-related-changes.md]
- Mechanistic layer expansion: the 2014 cortex-culture paper links Glu-Asp-Arg to serotonin-expression changes, and the broader tripeptide culture paper names T-33/Glu-Asp-Arg as a proliferation signal in young rat skin explants. These help normalize the short-peptide story, but they still do not establish cognition efficacy.^[raw/articles/pubmed-24909721-pinealon-serotonin-expression-brain-cortex.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-22803085-bioregulatory-tripeptides-skin-cells.md]
- Human: a 2016 comparative geroprotective study included oligopeptide preparations containing Pinealon and reported biological-age effects, but it combined Pinealon with another peptide complex and did not isolate a Pinealon-only cognition effect.^[raw/articles/pubmed-28539017-comparative-analysis-geroprotective-methods-pinealon-vezugen.md]

## Mechanism Bridge

- Preclinical cognition / recovery: the antihypoxic review, rodent learning papers, and hypoxia/inflammation/caspase-3 findings line up with a recovery-state story after injury or stress, not with a broad healthy-adult enhancer.^[raw/articles/pubmed-18546825-investigation-antihypoxic-properties-short-peptides.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-22567179-pinealon-hyperhomocysteinemia-rat-offspring.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-28976148-pinealon-cortexin-rat-learning-caspase-3.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-25051764-cortexin-pinealon-old-rats-hypoxic-hypoxia-cytokines-caspase3.md]
- Cell-only: the cortex serotonin-expression paper and the induced-neuron aging paper show a real neural-cell signal, but they stop well short of a human cognition endpoint.^[raw/articles/pubmed-24909721-pinealon-serotonin-expression-brain-cortex.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39518916-short-peptides-induced-neurons-age-related-changes.md]
- Human physiology: the 110-person geroprotective comparison suggests Pinealon can move aging-related markers in humans, which is the closest thing to a human bridge in the branch so far.^[raw/articles/pubmed-28539017-comparative-analysis-geroprotective-methods-pinealon-vezugen.md]
- Anecdote-only: forum reports cluster around sleep, TBI recovery, alertness, and no-effect counterreports, which keeps the branch in a recovery-state lane rather than a generic nootropic lane.^[raw/articles/reddit-2025-03-06-pinealon-tbi-recovery-sleep-cognition.md]^[raw/articles/reddit-2026-03-17-pinealon-sleep-quality.md]^[raw/articles/reddit-2026-04-12-pinealon-omg-brain-acuity-tbi-insomnia.md]^[raw/articles/reddit-2026-04-03-pinealon-not-feeling-any-effects.md]^[raw/articles/reddit-2025-05-25-pinealon-brain-fog-improvement.md]^[raw/articles/reddit-2025-09-30-pinealon-the-peptide-that-fixes-sleep-and-boosts-mood.md]^[raw/articles/reddit-2026-04-09-pinealon-for-sleep-my-short-experience-no-noticeable-effect.md]

Inference: Pinealon's best bridge is recovery-state support or sleep regulation via hypoxia / oxidative-stress resilience, not standalone healthy-adult cognition.

## Anecdotal Signal

Pinealon's forum layer is smaller than Epitalon's but still real. A TBI / hypoxia thread and a newer mild-TBI insomnia thread both report better sleep and next-day alertness or reduced brain fog, while other Reddit threads report no effects after 1 mg for 5 days, no consistent change at 1-3 mg, or only later side chatter about sleep timing. A short sleep-quality thread adds one more timing-positive reply, and a separate repeated-use Reddit post describes Pinealon as a favorite for sleep and mood. The no-effect and worse-HRV/worse-sleep reports matter because they make Pinealon look state-bound rather than enhancer-like.^[raw/articles/reddit-2025-03-06-pinealon-tbi-recovery-sleep-cognition.md]^[raw/articles/reddit-2026-04-12-pinealon-omg-brain-acuity-tbi-insomnia.md]^[raw/articles/reddit-2026-04-03-pinealon-not-feeling-any-effects.md]^[raw/articles/reddit-2026-04-09-pinealon-for-sleep-my-short-experience-no-noticeable-effect.md]^[raw/articles/reddit-2026-03-17-pinealon-sleep-quality.md]^[raw/articles/reddit-2025-05-25-pinealon-brain-fog-improvement.md]^[raw/articles/reddit-2025-09-30-pinealon-the-peptide-that-fixes-sleep-and-boosts-mood.md]

## Frontier Read

Pinealon now looks `upcoming in state-restoration interest`, not `upcoming in formal clinical development`. That is still enough to keep it on the active peptide map. Repeated sleep and recovery anecdotes give it more practical salience than many equally preclinical peptides, but the signal remains too phenotype-bound and route-loose to treat as a general cognition enhancer.

## Read

Pinealon belongs in the neuroprotective / geroprotective peptide bucket, not the cognition-shortlist bucket. The main open question is not whether there is any signal at all, but whether the signal is distinct enough from the broader EDR / short-peptide literature to justify stronger translational confidence. Right now the answer is no.^[raw/articles/pubmed-33396470-edr-peptide-mechanism-alzheimers-disease.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39518916-short-peptides-induced-neurons-age-related-changes.md]
