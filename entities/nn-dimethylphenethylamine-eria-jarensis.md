# N,N-Dimethylphenethylamine / Eria Jarensis

N,N-dimethylphenethylamine is a substituted phenethylamine also described as N,N-dimethylphenylethylamine, N,N-dimethyl-2-phenylethanamine, N,N-DMPEA, or N,N-dimethyl-beta-phenethylamine. In supplement marketing it can appear under "Eria jarensis" or PEA-like stimulant labels. This page covers the N,N-dimethyl compound and its product-label ecosystem, not [[phenethylamine]] / beta-PEA, BMPEA, [[phenylalanine]], DMAA/DMHA, [[p-synephrine]], [[higenamine-hordenine-octopamine]], or broad amphetamine-class claims.^[raw/articles/pubchem-2026-nn-dimethylphenethylamine-identity.md]

Medicinal cognition signal: `unsupported/risk-first`. The captured formal sources do not establish a healthy-adult cognition benefit. The main formal value is identity and risk context: PubChem identifies the compound and linked occurrence records, FDA's food-substance inventory has an N,N-dimethylphenethylamine entry that should not be read as stimulant-dose efficacy or safety, and the broader phenethylamine-analog supplement literature shows why phenethylamine-like ingredients need product-identity scrutiny.^[raw/articles/pubchem-2026-nn-dimethylphenethylamine-identity.md]^[raw/articles/fda-2026-eafus-nn-dimethylphenethylamine.md]^[raw/articles/pmc-2022-reinforcing-phenethylamine-analogs-supplements.md]

Safety and product-identity signal: `high burden`. The in-vitro proximal-tubule paper found little toxicity for 2-phenethylamine or N,N-dimethyl-beta-phenethylamine alone in that model, but high-concentration combination exposure produced evidence of potent cell injury. That does not quantify real-world kidney risk, but it is enough to treat multi-phenethylamine stimulant stacks as safety-relevant rather than just stronger focus products.^[raw/articles/sage-2016-phenethylamine-dimethyl-beta-pea-nephrotoxicity.md]

Anecdote signal: `weak-moderate and preworkout-confounded`. Reddit threads include motivation, focus, clean-energy, and "absurd focus" claims, but the same threads often ask about blood pressure, heart strain, neurotoxicity, selegiline/MAO-B combinations, dose uncertainty, and whether the active effect really came from caffeine, DMAA, DMHA, DMBA, or other stimulants in a blend. The current read is stimulant-product salience, not objective cognition.^[raw/articles/reddit-2020-2026-eria-jarensis-dmpea-threads.md]

## Identity Gates

- Separate N,N-DMPEA from [[phenethylamine]] / beta-PEA, BMPEA, N-methylphenethylamine, DMAA, DMHA, DMBA, hordenine, octopamine, [[p-synephrine]], rauwolscine/yohimbine, and generic "Eria jarensis extract."
- Treat "Eria jarensis" as a product label until the actual assayed ingredient and dose are known.
- Track caffeine, yohimbine/rauwolscine, DMAA/DMHA/DMBA, synephrine, hordenine, beta-PEA, MAO-B inhibitors, antidepressants, stimulant medication, BP/HR, anxiety/panic, sleep, and exercise/heat context.
- Do not transfer [[phenethylamine]]'s TAAR1/MAO-B mechanism, food-substance inventory status, or PEA anecdotes directly to N,N-DMPEA.
- Do not count preworkout intensity, euphoria, or appetite suppression as cognitive improvement unless objective task performance improves.

## Practical Ranking

N,N-DMPEA / Eria jarensis deserves standalone tracking because it is a named chemical/product-label gap inside the stimulant-adjacent supplement market. It should remain below [[phenethylamine]], [[paraxanthine]], [[caffeine]], and prescription attention comparators for evidence quality. The best current route is `risk-first product-identity and anecdote watch`, with no healthy-adult cognition upgrade.
