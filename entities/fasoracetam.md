# Fasoracetam

## Bottom Line

Fasoracetam / NFC-1 is a genotype-enriched ADHD / mGluR-network hypothesis, not a validated racetam enhancer. Medicinal signal is `low`: one adolescent ADHD study with disruptive metabotropic-glutamate-network CNVs reported improvement during an open-label active phase after a single-blind placebo week, but this is not a definitive randomized cognition trial and not healthy-adult evidence. Anecdotal signal is `weak-moderate` and research-chemical-confounded.

## What It Is

Fasoracetam is often grouped with racetams in nootropic markets, but its current formal signal is more specific: metabotropic glutamate receptor network variants in ADHD. It belongs with [[racetam-long-tail-watchlist]], [[nmda-coagonist-glyt1-daao-cognition-2026-scan]], and [[molecule-intake-triage-table]] as a genotype-enrichment / symptom-context comparator.

## Medicinal Research Signal

The captured clinical study tested fasoracetam in 30 adolescents aged 12-17 with ADHD and disruptive copy-number variants in metabotropic glutamate receptor network genes. The five-week design included an open-label active phase after a fixed single-blind placebo week, with pharmacokinetic profiling from 50-800 mg and dose escalation up to 400 mg twice daily. The study reported significant improvement on Clinical Global Impressions and parent Vanderbilt scores in subjects with Tier 1 mGluR variants; adverse events were generally mild and the authors reported no difference in adverse-event incidence between the placebo week and active-drug weeks.^[raw/articles/pmc-5770454-fasoracetam-adhd-mglur-trial.md]

This is a useful precision-psychiatry signal, but it is symptom-first, small, and not a broad cognition efficacy result.

Fasoracetam has research-chemical and ADHD-interest salience, but no dedicated current anecdote raw note is indexed. Anecdote signal is `weak-moderate` and does not upgrade the clinical interpretation.
