# Viloxazine

Viloxazine is a selective norepinephrine reuptake inhibitor marketed in the U.S. as Qelbree for ADHD. In this wiki it belongs in [[nonstimulant-noradrenergic-attention-agent-watchlist]] beside [[atomoxetine]], [[bupropion]], and [[reboxetine]], but its current cognition story is clinical ADHD symptom reduction rather than healthy-adult enhancement.

## Bottom Line

Cognition signal: `low/clinical-state`. The strongest captured formal source is a phase III adult ADHD trial of viloxazine ER 200-600 mg/day for 6 weeks. The trial showed greater ADHD symptom improvement than placebo and included executive-function related secondary measures, but it does not establish cognition benefit in already-healthy adults.^[raw/articles/pmc-9328182-viloxazine-er-adult-adhd-phase3.md]

Anecdote signal: `weak`. The captured Reddit sample contains some Qelbree/viloxazine cognition and switching discussion, but it is sparse and skewed toward tolerability, mood, and safety-warning concerns rather than consistent nootropic benefit.^[raw/articles/reddit-2023-2026-bupropion-atomoxetine-viloxazine-cognition-threads.md]

Safety/status: prescription-first. DailyMed identifies Qelbree as an ADHD medication and flags suicidal thoughts/behaviors, BP/HR increases, somnolence/fatigue, mania/hypomania activation, and clinically important CYP1A2 interactions.^[raw/articles/dailymed-2026-qelbree-viloxazine-label.md]

## Current Read

Viloxazine belongs on the map because it is a modern nonstimulant ADHD medication in the same attention-agent neighborhood as [[atomoxetine]]. It should not be upgraded into a nootropic branch unless healthy-adult cognition data, stronger direct anecdote, or a distinct responder phenotype appears.
