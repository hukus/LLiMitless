# Quercetin

Quercetin is a dietary flavonol found in onions, apples, berries, tea, and many plant extracts. In this wiki it has two separate identities: isolated quercetin / quercetin glycosides as a supplement or beverage ingredient, and quercetin as the non-oncology flavonoid partner in dasatinib-plus-quercetin senolytic protocols. These should not be merged with [[fisetin]], [[resveratrol]], [[sulforaphane]], generic flavonoids, whole foods, or senolytic stacks.

Medicinal cognition signal: `low/mixed and mostly weak`. The largest isolated-supplement cognition trial is negative: 941 community participants took 500 mg/day or 1000 mg/day quercetin or placebo for 12 weeks, with no significant improvement in memory, psychomotor speed, reaction time, attention, or cognitive flexibility. A later product-specific quercetin-glycoside beverage trial in 80 adults aged 60-75 found a reaction-time improvement after 40 weeks, but the CBF readout was suggestive rather than significant between groups. This combination supports only narrow reaction-time / vascular-cognition interest, not broad enhancement.^[raw/articles/pmc-3736941-quercetin-community-cognition-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-36524489-quercetin-glycoside-cognition-cbf-rct.md]

The senolytic lane is not isolated quercetin efficacy. D+Q has open-label cognition-adjacent human signals in older adults at AD risk and phase-1 mild AD biomarker work, plus rat cognition rescue, but the active unit is intermittent dasatinib plus quercetin with oncology-grade screening and safety burden. Quercetin should therefore be indexed as part of [[senolytic-cognition-2026-scan]] while keeping isolated supplement cognition evidence separate.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40010154-senolytics-cognition-mobility-at-risk.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40274471-dasatinib-quercetin-phase1-mild-alzheimer-biomarkers.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-35042834-dasatinib-quercetin-aged-rats-cognition.md]

Anecdote signal: `strong longevity/senolytic salience, weak cognition-specific evidence`. The direct brain-fog thread captured here mentions quercetin only as part of a histamine/immune stack with Allegra, DAO, vitamin C, peppermint, and probiotics. A separate senolytic-stack thread reports a temporary wellbeing trough while using fisetin plus quercetin. These are safety and responder-hypothesis signals, not isolated nootropic reports.^[raw/articles/troof-nootropics-ratings-analysis.md]^[raw/articles/reddit-2026-quercetin-brain-fog-histamine-thread.md]^[raw/articles/reddit-2025-fisetin-quercetin-senolytics-adverse-thread.md]

Safety signal: `low-moderate supplement caution`. LiverTox rates quercetin as unlikely to cause clinically apparent liver injury and describes supplements as generally well tolerated, with occasional abdominal discomfort, nausea, and headache. The practical caution is product quality, high-dose supplement use, medication interactions, and D+Q stack context rather than hepatotoxicity.^[raw/articles/ncbi-bookshelf-2020-quercetin-livertox.md]^[raw/articles/dailymed-dasatinib-tablet-label.md]

## Signal Split

| Layer | Signal | Readout |
|---|---|---|
| 12-week isolated quercetin RCT | `negative` | 500 or 1000 mg/day did not improve memory, speed, reaction time, attention, or flexibility in 941 completers |
| 40-week quercetin-glycoside beverage RCT | `low/product-specific` | 110 mg/day quercetin glycoside improved reaction time; CBF claims were suggestive |
| D+Q senolytic human branch | `low/combination-only` | Open-label cognition-adjacent and biomarker studies; active unit is dasatinib plus quercetin |
| D+Q preclinical branch | `preclinical rescue-positive` | Rat cognition rescue exists but does not isolate quercetin |
| Anecdote | `strong longevity, weak cognition` | Histamine/brain-fog stack mention plus senolytic-stack adverse thread |
| Safety / quality | `low-moderate caution` | Liver injury unlikely; supplement dose, interactions, and D+Q screening burden dominate |

## Practical Interpretation

Quercetin deserves a low-confidence standalone page because it has both a large negative isolated-supplement cognition trial and a narrower positive quercetin-glycoside reaction-time trial, plus a separate D+Q senolytic branch. Its practical signal is weaker than [[cocoa-flavanols]], [[blueberry-anthocyanins]], [[resveratrol]], or [[sulforaphane]] for direct cognition, and the most prominent current human senolytic use is not casual supplementation.

A confidence upgrade would require a replicated quercetin-glycoside cognition trial with prespecified domains, clear product chemistry, vascular/CBF markers, and separation from dasatinib, [[fisetin]], vitamin C, antihistamines, and whole-food flavonoid intake.
