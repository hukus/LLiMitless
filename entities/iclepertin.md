# Iclepertin

Iclepertin, also known as BI 425809, is a GlyT1 inhibitor developed for cognitive impairment associated with schizophrenia. It is the cleanest negative anchor in [[nmda-coagonist-glyt1-daao-cognition-2026-scan]] because it failed both an adjunctive computerized cognitive training phase 2 study and the larger CONNEX phase 3 program.

## Bottom Line

Medicinal signal: `negative / medium-confidence failure anchor`. The phase 2 CCT study did not show between-group efficacy differences after 12 weeks, and the phase 3 CONNEX program was negative on the MCCB primary endpoint after 26 weeks.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39759424-iclepertin-cct-schizophrenia-phase2.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41233083-iclepertin-connex-phase3-schizophrenia.md]

Anecdotal signal: `not captured`. The current wiki corpus has no structured self-experiment, patient-forum, or nootropic-community packet for iclepertin, and no anecdotal evidence should be imported from the broader GlyT1 or NMDA-coagonist class.

Practical route: keep iclepertin in [[negative-evidence-watchlist-for-cognition-interventions]] and [[failed-mechanism-atlas-for-cognition-interventions]] as a reminder that plausible glutamatergic target engagement does not guarantee a durable cognition win.

## Human Evidence

The phase 2 trial paired iclepertin 10 mg with at-home computerized cognitive training for 12 weeks in schizophrenia. Of 200 randomized patients, 154 completed the study, but no efficacy endpoint separation was observed. Safety was acceptable and no new risks were identified.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39759424-iclepertin-cct-schizophrenia-phase2.md]

The CONNEX phase 3 program tested iclepertin 10 mg once daily for 26 weeks in 1,835 treated adults with schizophrenia across three multinational randomized trials. The adjusted mean difference on the MCCB overall composite T-score was 0.127 (95% CI -0.396 to 0.650; p=0.63), with key secondary cognition/function measures also negative. Tolerability looked acceptable.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41233083-iclepertin-connex-phase3-schizophrenia.md]

## Signal Judgment

Iclepertin deserves standalone coverage because it defines the negative boundary of the GlyT1/NMDA-coagonist lane. Its failure should not erase [[sodium-benzoate]], [[luvadaxistat]], [[sarcosine]], or [[d-serine]], but it should cap any class-wide enhancer claim.
