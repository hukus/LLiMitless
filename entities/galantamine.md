# Galantamine

## Bottom Line

Galantamine is a cholinesterase-inhibitor / nicotinic-allosteric comparator with a `low-moderate` responder-gated medicinal cognition signal and a `low` broad-enhancement signal. The best current read is enrichment and state context: basal-forebrain volume predicted benefit in healthy older adults, an acute challenge response predicted longer-term AD response, nicotine plus galantamine produced a task-specific reaction-time synergy, but HIV and post-stroke/recovery trials do not support a uniform cognition effect. Anecdotal cognition signal is `absent-to-weak` in the current corpus.

## What It Is

Galantamine is a cholinergic drug used in cognitive-impairment clinical contexts and sometimes discussed in nootropic or lucid-dreaming communities. In this wiki it belongs with [[cholinergic-and-nicotinic-agents-2026-scan]], [[cholinergic-state-restoration-and-cognition]], [[nicotine]], and [[varenicline]] as a responder-enrichment and state-restoration comparator.

## Medicinal Research Signal

The healthy-older-adult enrichment anchor is a single-dose randomized, double-blind crossover study using structural MRI of the basal forebrain. Galantamine did not produce a uniform cognitive response; the positive signal was concentrated in participants with larger basal forebrain cholinergic volume, where attention-dependent filtering during working-memory encoding improved. This is a biomarker-enrichment lead, not a broad enhancement result.^[raw/articles/pubmed-33842683-galantamine-basal-forebrain-predictor.md]

The AD responder-biomarker anchor is a small acute galantamine-challenge study. Acute CNS and EEG changes after a single galantamine dose distinguished 6-month responders from non-responders, but the responder definition mixed MMSE, NPI, and DAD rather than a pure cognition endpoint.^[raw/articles/pubmed-34964149-galantamine-cholinergic-challenge-response-predictor.md]

The nicotine-interaction anchor is a 26-person randomized counterbalanced study in adult never-smokers. Nicotine improved reaction time and signal detection; galantamine 4 mg alone did not modulate those effects, but nicotine plus galantamine synergistically reduced reaction time on one change-detection task. That supports mechanism interaction, not a standalone galantamine enhancer claim.^[raw/articles/pubmed-31686175-nicotine-galantamine-healthy-never-smokers-cognition.md]

The recovery and disease-state counterweights are important. In post-stroke cognitive impairment, both groups improved with cognitive rehabilitation, but galantamine did not clearly separate from placebo on the primary conversion outcome. In ART-suppressed people with HIV, 12 weeks of galantamine did not improve composite neurocognition versus placebo and increased several pro-inflammatory cytokines. In treatment-seeking smokers before a quit attempt, galantamine ER reduced smoking choice and cotinine but had minimal cognitive-performance effect.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39359162-galantamine-post-stroke-cognitive-rehab.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41427614-galantamine-hiv-no-cognition.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-29926988-galantamine-smoking-cognitive-performance-prequit.md]

## Safety And Status

The captured SAH pilot makes the safety ceiling concrete: galantamine 8 mg or 12 mg every 12 hours for 90 days had discontinuation and mortality similar to placebo, but bradycardia was the most common adverse event. Cholinergic GI and cardiovascular tolerability remain practical gates before any cognition framing.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40877496-galantamine-sah-safety-trial.md]

## Signal Judgment

Medicinal signal: `low-moderate` in responder-enriched or clinical-state contexts; `low` for broad healthy-adult enhancement.

Anecdotal signal: `absent-to-weak`.

Practical route: enrichment-gated cholinergic comparator, not a general nootropic.
