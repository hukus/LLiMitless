# Vardenafil

Vardenafil is a PDE5 inhibitor used clinically for erectile dysfunction. It belongs in the cognition wiki because it has a direct healthy-adult PDE5 cognition study, not because that study was positive. PubChem establishes the small-molecule identity; DailyMed establishes prescription safety boundaries.^[raw/articles/pubchem-2026-vardenafil-identity.md]^[raw/articles/dailymed-2026-vardenafil-label.md]

Medicinal cognition signal: `low / direct negative`. A healthy-volunteer behavioral-EEG study tested oral vardenafil at 10 mg and 20 mg. It did not find a convincing cognition-enhancement effect; the clearest behavioral signal was worse reaction-time performance at 20 mg, with incidental ERP changes and more headache/weakness.^[raw/articles/pubmed-23427190-vardenafil-healthy-adults.md]

Mechanistic signal: `plausible but unproven`. Vardenafil shares the PDE5 / NO-cGMP rationale with [[sildenafil]] and [[tadalafil]], and the 2025 PDE5 cognitive-decline review keeps the class mechanistically interesting. That review-level rationale does not override vardenafil's healthy-adult null/negative cognition study.^[raw/articles/mdpi-cells-14191505-pde5-cognitive-decline-review.md]

## Signal Judgment

Vardenafil is the cleanest PDE5 healthy-adult counterexample in the current wiki: it directly tested a plausible vascular/cGMP intervention in healthy adults and did not produce broad cognitive improvement. Treat it as a negative-class comparator for PDE5 nootropic claims, especially when tadalafil or sildenafil anecdotes try to generalize from blood-flow or gym effects to cognition.

## Safety Gates

- Prescription PDE5 inhibitor; do not generalize supplement-style use.
- Avoid with organic nitrates and soluble guanylate-cyclase stimulators such as riociguat.^[raw/articles/dailymed-2026-vardenafil-label.md]
- Treat alpha-blockers, antihypertensives, alcohol, dehydration, heat exposure, and vasodilator stacks as hypotension/dizziness amplifiers.
- CYP3A4 inhibitors and hepatic/renal status can change exposure and risk.
- QT-prolongation cautions make vardenafil less interchangeable with tadalafil/sildenafil in people with congenital long-QT risk or relevant antiarrhythmic exposure.
- Watch for headache, flushing, rhinitis/nasal congestion, dyspepsia, dizziness, priapism, sudden vision loss, and sudden hearing loss.
