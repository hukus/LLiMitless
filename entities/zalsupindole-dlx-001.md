# Zalsupindole / DLX-001

Zalsupindole, also called DLX-001 and AAZ-A-154, is a clinical-stage non-hallucinogenic neuroplastogen / psychoplastogen candidate from the isotryptamine branch. It matters for this wiki because it is one of the first non-hallucinogenic neuroplastogens with human phase 1 and phase 1b exposure, but the current evidence is mood/biomarker development rather than cognition or brain-fog efficacy.

Medicinal cognition signal: `low / not yet tested directly`. PubChem anchors the AAZ-A-154 / zalsupindole identity, and the 2025 ACS paper supports a real preclinical neuroplasticity package. The human layer is more important than for [[tabernanthalog]] or [[2-bromo-lsd]]: a phase 1 report describes 106 healthy volunteers with safety, PK, and pharmacodynamic-marker assessment, and the company later reported phase 1b MDD biomarker and symptom data plus FDA clearance for a phase 2 design. None of these sources is a controlled cognition or healthy-adult enhancement result.^[raw/articles/pubchem-2026-zalsupindole-identity.md]^[raw/articles/acs-2025-zalsupindole-nonhallucinogenic-neuroplastogen.md]^[raw/articles/psychiatrictimes-2025-dlx001-phase1-healthy-volunteers.md]^[raw/articles/businesswire-2025-zalsupindole-phase1b-fda-phase2.md]

Human safety/status signal: `early clinical, sponsor-linked`. The phase 1 report says no psychotomimetic, hallucinatory, or dissociative effects were observed in 106 healthy volunteers. The sponsor phase 1b release in 18 adults with MDD reports no serious adverse events, mild resolved adverse events, no hallucinatory/dissociative/psychotomimetic effects, and a cleared phase 2 outpatient design. This is useful for translation status, but it is not equivalent to peer-reviewed phase 2 efficacy or cognition proof.^[raw/articles/psychiatrictimes-2025-dlx001-phase1-healthy-volunteers.md]^[raw/articles/businesswire-2025-zalsupindole-phase1b-fda-phase2.md]
