# Ibutamoren

Ibutamoren, usually discussed as MK-677, is the oral ghrelin receptor / growth-hormone secretagogue comparator in this branch. It has robust human GH/IGF-1 target engagement and clear sleep effects, but the cognition story is weak: older-adult physiology and body-composition trials were function-null for strength or performance, and the AD trial was negative on cognition and function. That makes ibutamoren better read as a sleep/state-restoration and somatotropic-physiology comparator than as a nootropic.^[raw/articles/pubmed-8954023-mk677-gh-igf1-axis-healthy-elderly.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-9349662-mk677-sleep-quality-man.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-18981485-mk677-body-composition-clinical-outcomes-healthy-older-adults.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-19015485-mk677-alzheimers-randomized-trial.md]

June 2026 GH-axis routing gate: MK-677 should not inherit priority from peptide-frontier nodes or from CJC-1295/ipamorelin recovery anecdotes. Future nootropic reports need exact product identity and source, dose/timing, duration, washout or stable-background period, sleep measurement, appetite and caloric intake, training phase, glucose/insulin or diabetes risk, edema/fluid weight, BP/HR, stimulant or peptide co-use, adverse events, and objective cognition or work-output endpoints before any ranking comparison with [[ghrh]], [[tesamorelin]], [[cjc-1295]], [[ipamorelin]], [[semax]], [[selank]], [[cerebrolysin]], recovery-bound [[bpc-157]], or [[bromantane]]. Missing glucose, edema, sleep, or co-use fields keep the report in `state-restoration anecdote`, not cognition evidence.

## Key Facts

- Oral MK-677 produces strong GH/IGF-1 release in humans.
- Bedtime dosing improved sleep architecture in young and older adults.
- Healthy older adults gained fat-free mass, but strength and function did not improve.
- The Alzheimer disease trial showed no cognitive or functional benefit despite target engagement.
- Main safety flags: appetite increase, edema, and glucose / insulin-sensitivity changes.

## What It Suggests

- Somatotropic target engagement is real, but it does not translate into a reliable cognition win.
- Subjective recovery or sleep benefits can plausibly drive community enthusiasm without proving enhancement.
- The compound is useful as a comparator when the wiki needs a strong human GH-axis signal that is still cognition-negative.

## Limits

- No modern positive cognition trial in healthy adults.
- The clearest human signals are physiology and sleep, not durable behavioral transfer.
- Lean-mass gain should not be treated as cognitive gain.
