# 5-HTP / Tryptophan

This page covers the serotonin-precursor lane relevant to cognition claims: 5-hydroxytryptophan (5-HTP, often sold as Griffonia simplicifolia seed extract) and L-tryptophan supplement/challenge exposures. It is a serotonergic state, sleep, mood, and product-quality branch. It should not be collapsed with [[melatonin]], SSRIs/SNRIs/MAOIs, St. John's wort, methylene blue, triptans, 5-HT6 antagonists, kynurenine enzyme modulators, protein-rich foods, or the DHA/EPA/vitamin E/tryptophan/melatonin nutrient stack.

Medicinal cognition signal: `low/preliminary and mood-sleep confounded`. The cleanest direct 5-HTP cognition source is a 2025 single-blind randomized trial in 30 Singapore older adults using 100 mg/day for 12 weeks. MoCA increased by about one point within the 5-HTP group, GDS improved transiently, and serum serotonin rose; anxiety and other biomarkers did not clearly move. That is enough for standalone tracking, but the design, sample size, no-supplement control, and mood readout make it a preliminary state signal rather than proof of general cognitive enhancement.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40944161-5htp-cognition-mood-rct.md]

Tryptophan evidence is even more attribution-sensitive. The 2026 nutrient NMA reports a global-cognition and memory signal for a DHA+EPA+vitamin E+tryptophan+melatonin stack, but the stack has too many active units to assign the effect to tryptophan. A 2023 tryptophan challenge study in schizophrenia and healthy controls showed that 6 g tryptophan can raise kynurenine and kynurenic acid, yet cognition was mostly unchanged. The wiki should use those sources to separate nutrient-stack and pathway-probe logic from 5-HTP supplementation.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41764841-nutritional-supplements-cognition-nma.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-37118058-tryptophan-challenge-schizophrenia-cognition-cbf.md]

Mechanistic plausibility is real but cuts both ways. 5-HTP bypasses tryptophan hydroxylase and sits directly upstream of serotonin, so it can plausibly change mood, sleep, appetite, and arousal. Those are legitimate cognition-adjacent states, but they also create false-positive interpretation risk: a calmer or better-slept user may feel sharper without a direct memory, executive-function, or processing-speed effect.^[raw/articles/pubmed-16023217-5htp-serotonin-precursor-safety-review.md]

Anecdote signal: `weak-moderate and bidirectional`. Reddit reports include sleep initiation, better mood, calmer anxiety, and next-day recovery narratives, but also insomnia, repeated awakenings, dullness, zombie-like flattening, depressed mood, agitation, and dose sensitivity. This is useful for adverse-event and responder-phenotype tracking, not for efficacy upgrading.^[raw/articles/reddit-2026-5htp-sleep-mood-thread.md]^[raw/articles/reddit-2024-5htp-kept-awake-adverse-thread.md]

Safety signal: `moderate serotonergic and product-quality caution`. MedlinePlus frames serotonin syndrome as potentially life-threatening and most common when serotonergic agents are combined or dose-changed. The 5-HTP review explicitly discusses serotonin syndrome and eosinophilia-myalgia syndrome. NCCIH and the historical L-tryptophan contaminant investigation make the sourcing story concrete: the 1989 EMS outbreak was tied to contaminated synthetic L-tryptophan from a manufacturer, and 5-HTP has been watched because of its biochemical relationship to L-tryptophan. Modern analyses are more reassuring than the outbreak history, but the wiki should still route 5-HTP through supplement QA, medication interactions, and dose/timing gates.^[raw/articles/medlineplus-2026-serotonin-syndrome.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-16023217-5htp-serotonin-precursor-safety-review.md]^[raw/articles/nccih-2026-sleep-disorders-tryptophan-5htp-safety.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-8346973-l-tryptophan-ems-contaminants.md]

## Signal Split

| Layer | Signal | Readout |
|---|---|---|
| 2025 older-adult 5-HTP RCT | `low/preliminary` | Small within-group MoCA increase, GDS improvement, and serotonin increase after 100 mg/day for 12 weeks |
| 2026 nutrient NMA tryptophan stack | `low/combination-only` | Multi-nutrient DHA/EPA/vitamin E/tryptophan/melatonin signal cannot isolate tryptophan |
| Tryptophan challenge / kynurenine pathway | `mechanistic comparator` | 6 g tryptophan raised kynurenine/KYNA but mostly did not improve cognition |
| Serotonin-precursor mechanism | `plausible but confounded` | Mood, sleep, appetite, and arousal changes can masquerade as cognitive improvement |
| Anecdote | `weak-moderate bidirectional` | Sleep/mood benefit reports coexist with insomnia, dullness, emotional flattening, and agitation reports |
| Safety / interactions | `moderate caution` | Serotonergic stacking, EMS history, supplement QA, dose, timing, and product identity dominate practical interpretation |

## Product And Protocol Gates

- Identify whether the exposure is 5-HTP, L-tryptophan, a Griffonia extract, a protein/food exposure, or a multi-nutrient stack.
- Record dose, timing, release form, duration, meal context, and sleep/circadian state.
- Exclude or separately flag serotonergic medications and agents: SSRIs/SNRIs, MAOIs, TCAs, triptans, dextromethorphan, linezolid, methylene blue, St. John's wort, MDMA, and other serotonin-active stacks.
- Require product identity and QA when using supplement anecdotes or commercial claims.
- Treat sleep and mood improvement as mediators until objective cognitive testing shows independent benefit.

## Practical Interpretation

5-HTP / tryptophan belongs in the molecule atlas because it has a direct small older-adult RCT and high practical safety relevance. It should rank below established wakefulness and state-support comparators because the current cognitive signal is small, preliminary, and entangled with mood and sleep. The best current route is `serotonergic state support / sleep-mood confound / interaction-sensitive supplement`, not broad nootropic enhancement.

Confidence would improve only with blinded placebo-controlled trials that stratify baseline sleep quality, depression/anxiety symptoms, serotonergic medications, product identity, dose, and objective cognition domains. Anecdote should keep adverse sleep, activation, and emotional-flattening reports visible.
