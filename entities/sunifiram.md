# Sunifiram

## Bottom Line

Sunifiram / DM235 is a potent preclinical nootropic-claim compound with no captured human efficacy or safety package. Medicinal signal is `preclinical-only`: animal and slice pharmacology suggest antiamnesic and AMPA-linked activity, but that is not human cognition evidence. Anecdotal signal is `moderate` for potency lore and `high-risk` for sourcing/safety uncertainty.

## What It Is

Sunifiram is usually discussed with unifiram / DM232 and racetam-adjacent AMPA-linked compounds, even though it is not a simple clinical racetam comparator. In this wiki it belongs with [[racetam-long-tail-watchlist]], [[ampakines-and-glutamatergic-plasticity-2026-scan]], and [[target-specificity-gate-for-plasticity-molecules]] as a preclinical potency / safety-uncertainty node.

## Medicinal Research Signal

The captured pharmacology paper characterizes DM232 / unifiram and DM235 / sunifiram as potent preclinical cognition-enhancing compounds. The abstract reports that they were far more potent than piracetam in tested preclinical models, did not show affinity for major central receptors or channels in binding studies, prevented amnesia induced by several neurotransmission perturbations, increased acetylcholine release from rat cerebral cortex, and likely involved AMPA-receptor-related mechanisms. This is mechanism and animal/slice evidence only.^[raw/articles/pmc-6741768-sunifiram-unifiram-pharmacological-characterization.md]
