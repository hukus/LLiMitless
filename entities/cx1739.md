# CX1739

CX1739 is a newer low-impact AMPAkine with human safety / pharmacokinetic exposure but no cognition-efficacy evidence. It belongs in the wiki as a development-stage comparator, not as an enhancer.

Medicinal cognition signal: `not established`. The main 2025 healthy-volunteer paper was a safety and PK study, not an efficacy trial. It reported single-dose escalation from 100 to 1200 mg and multiple-dose regimens of 300 to 600 mg BID for 7-10 days, with tolerability up to 900 mg once daily or 450 mg BID and headache/nausea as prominent adverse effects.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39302241-cx1739-safety-pharmacokinetics.md]

Development-status signal: `non-cognition human exposure`. A phase 2 registry record tested CX1739 300, 600, and 900 mg for antagonism of remifentanil-induced respiratory depression while preserving analgesia and sedation measures in healthy male volunteers.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct02735629-cx1739-oird.md] That supports respiratory-drive development logic and human exposure, not cognitive enhancement.

Mechanism signal: `low-impact safety rationale`. CX1739 is part of the safer low-impact AMPAkine lane and is used in the class toxicity-mechanism literature as a compound without the same high-impact hyperexcitability pattern.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39304968-high-impact-ampakines-toxic-calcium-release.md] It still has no human cognition endpoint.
