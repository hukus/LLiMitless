# Kai-Xin-San

Kai-Xin-San is a traditional Chinese medicine formula usually described as a combination of [[ginseng]], Poria, Polygala tenuifolia, and Acorus tatarinowii. It belongs in the fixed-formula traditional-medicine lane, not as generic evidence for any single constituent.

## Bottom Line

Medicinal cognition signal: `preclinical-to-low disease-state`, `very low for healthy adults`. The best current KXS-specific evidence is animal-model and network-pharmacology work in Alzheimer's disease models. The 2026 preclinical meta-analysis included 44 animal studies and reported improvements in behavioral cognition plus amyloid, tau, inflammatory, oxidative-stress, and cholinergic markers, but evidence certainty was low or very low and bias/heterogeneity were major caveats.^[raw/articles/frontiers-2026-kai-xin-san-ad-preclinical-meta.md]

Human evidence is weaker and less product-specific. A 2023 meta-analysis of formulas containing Polygala tenuifolia plus Acorus tatarinowii found adjunctive benefits in Alzheimer's disease outcomes when added to conventional treatment, but the unit was a formula family rather than exact KXS, and certainty was mostly low or very low.^[raw/articles/frontiers-2023-polygala-acorus-ad-meta-analysis.md]

## Formula Identity

KXS should be tracked as a formula exposure. Ratios vary across sources, and formula versions may differ in ginseng, Poria, Polygala, Acorus species, processing, extract ratio, and marker chemistry. That matters because [[polygala-bt11]], generic Polygala root, Acorus volatile oil, Panax ginseng products, and KXS decoctions are not interchangeable.

## Evidence Layers

The 2020 integrated meta-analysis/network-pharmacology paper found Morris water maze benefits in AD animal models and highlighted cholinergic, monoaminergic, adrenergic, calcium, and cGMP/PKG pathway hypotheses. This is useful mechanism scaffolding, but it does not establish human cognition efficacy.^[raw/articles/tandfonline-2020-kai-xin-san-ad-preclinical-meta-network.md]

The 2026 preclinical review is fresher and broader, but still animal-only. It supports keeping KXS in the research graph and fixed-formula watchlist, not ranking it beside standardized human-RCT botanicals such as [[sailuotong]], [[ginkgo]], [[ginseng]], [[spearmint-extract]], or [[grape-extract]].^[raw/articles/frontiers-2026-kai-xin-san-ad-preclinical-meta.md]

## Safety And Product Gates

Acorus tatarinowii adds a specific safety gate because alpha-asarone and beta-asarone exposure is a recognized concern. A review of Acorus tatarinowii notes hepatoma, mutagenic, genotoxic, teratogenic, vomiting, and beta-asarone limit concerns. Any KXS-style product should therefore document Acorus species, extraction method, beta-asarone content, and contaminant testing before efficacy claims are interpretable.^[raw/articles/pmc-10254448-acorus-tatarinowii-review-safety.md]

## Signal Judgment

KXS is worth tracking because it is a historically important memory formula and has a substantial preclinical AD packet. It is not yet a practical healthy-adult cognition lead. The next useful evidence would be a standardized KXS product, verified Acorus/asarone chemistry, placebo-controlled human cognition endpoints, and clear adverse-event reporting.
