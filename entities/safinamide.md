# Safinamide

Safinamide / XADAGO is a reversible MAO-B inhibitor approved as add-on therapy to levodopa/carbidopa for Parkinson disease "off" episodes. It is relevant to cognition because it sits near [[selegiline]] and [[rasagiline]] while adding glutamatergic modulation claims, but the formal signal remains PD-state-specific.

## Current Read

Medicinal cognition signal: `low/state-bound`. A 2026 network meta-analysis of MAO-B inhibitors in PD found safinamide did not significantly improve global cognition versus placebo and no MAO-B inhibitor showed significant domain-specific cognitive effects.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41524120-maob-inhibitors-pd-cognition-network-meta.md]

The positive-looking safinamide evidence is small and context-specific. A 32-person exploratory study in fluctuating PD found improved Frontal Assessment Battery and Stroop-related measures after 12 weeks of safinamide 100 mg/day at the end of levodopa dose, while a separate 20-person 6-month prospective study found improvements in interest, motivation, fatigue, apathy, urinary symptoms, and off time but no neuropsychological change versus baseline.^[raw/articles/pubmed-33068177-safinamide-executive-functions-pd.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-34031800-safinamide-nonmotor-cognition-pd.md]

Mechanism read: safinamide is often discussed as more than MAO-B because of non-dopaminergic/glutamatergic effects. That is a hypothesis for PD wearing-off / inhibitory-control states, not proof of healthy-adult cognition enhancement.^[raw/articles/pubmed-36065929-maob-cognitive-functions-pd-review.md]

## Safety Gates

DailyMed frames safinamide as an adjunct PD drug at 50 to 100 mg/day with levodopa/carbidopa. Contraindications include other MAOIs or linezolid, opioids, SNRIs, tricyclic/tetracyclic/triazolopyridine antidepressants, cyclobenzaprine, methylphenidate, amphetamine derivatives, St. John's wort, dextromethorphan, and severe hepatic impairment.^[raw/articles/dailymed-2025-xadago-safinamide-label.md]

Warnings include hypertension, serotonin syndrome, sleep attacks, dyskinesia, hallucinations/psychotic behavior, impulse-control behaviors, withdrawal-emergent hyperpyrexia/confusion, and retinal pathology. The label says MAO-B selectivity decreases above recommended doses and advises avoiding very high tyramine exposures above 150 mg.^[raw/articles/dailymed-2025-xadago-safinamide-label.md]

## Current Route

Safinamide belongs in [[mao-b-propargylamine-cognition-watchlist]] beside [[rasagiline]] and [[selegiline]]. Current verdict: interesting for fluctuating-PD executive/off-state hypotheses, but no healthy-adult cognition proof and a heavy interaction profile.
