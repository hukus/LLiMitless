# Cerebrolysin

Cerebrolysin is a peptide-based neurotrophic drug that sits closer to recovery medicine than to casual nootropic culture. The human evidence is stronger than most peptide pages: older blinded AD trials report cognitive/global benefit, a 2019 vascular-dementia Cochrane update reports a small positive but very-low-certainty cognition/global-function signal, a 2024 aMCI comparative study suggests slower conversion to dementia, and a 2025 stroke comparative-effectiveness study adds a MoCA-linked recovery signal. The direct healthy-human packet is much weaker: one old healthy-elderly oral-dose EEG/memory study, not a modern replicated enhancement trial. The latest mechanism additions broaden the recovery-state plausibility story in aged mice, hypertensive rats, ketamine-treated mice, and case-report contexts, but they do not close the healthy-enhancement gap.^[raw/articles/pubmed-11552768-cerebrolysin-alz-28-week-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-20500802-cerebrolysin-dose-finding-ad-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pmc-6844361-cerebrolysin-vascular-dementia-cochrane-2019.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39435777-cerebrolysin-mci-dementia-prevention.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40851188-cerebrolysin-c-regs2-a-moderate-acute-ischemic-stroke.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-10961443-oral-cerebrolysin-healthy-elderly-alpha-memory.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41460391-cerebrolysin-age-induced-memory-decline.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39826813-cerebrolysin-hypertensive-rat-memory-spines.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41204270-cerebrolysin-ketamine-cognitive-impairment-creb-pgc1a.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41178897-cerebrolysin-post-hsv-encephalitis.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41532044-cerebrolysin-glioma-case-report.md]

The AD RCTs are still the cleanest cognition signals: the 28-week placebo-controlled study reported an ADAS-cog advantage and sustained benefit after withdrawal, while the dose-finding study reported improved global clinical function and cognition at week 24. The vascular-dementia Cochrane signal is positive but downgraded to very-low certainty. The newer aMCI comparative study is directionally encouraging but open-label, the 2025 stroke comparative-effectiveness study adds a MoCA-linked signal but is still observational, and the aneurysmal subarachnoid hemorrhage pilot was blinded but neutral on MoCA, so all of that should be read as phenotype-specific evidence rather than a general nootropic claim.^[raw/articles/pubmed-11552768-cerebrolysin-alz-28-week-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-20500802-cerebrolysin-dose-finding-ad-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pmc-6844361-cerebrolysin-vascular-dementia-cochrane-2019.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39435777-cerebrolysin-mci-dementia-prevention.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40851188-cerebrolysin-c-regs2-a-moderate-acute-ischemic-stroke.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-33143640-cerebrolysin-sah-pilot.md]

Stroke evidence is more mixed. The 2025 comparative-effectiveness study and the 2025 thrombectomy follow-up both favored Cerebrolysin on functional recovery, and the comparative-effectiveness study also carried a MoCA signal; the 2025 observational AIS study was similarly positive over 14 days. That said, the 2023 Cochrane acute-stroke update found no hard mortality benefit and a probable increase in non-fatal serious adverse events, the older alteplase-combination RCT missed the day-90 disability endpoint, the 2017 early-rehabilitation study mainly improved short-term recovery, and the 2024 rapid HTA still reads as recovery support rather than durable cognition enhancement.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40851188-cerebrolysin-c-regs2-a-moderate-acute-ischemic-stroke.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40325343-cerebrolysin-post-thrombectomy-followup.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41010922-cerebrolysin-acute-ischemic-stroke-observational.md]^[raw/articles/cochrane-2023-cerebrolysin-acute-ischaemic-stroke.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-23009193-cerebrolysin-alteplase-stroke-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-29362596-cerebrolysin-early-neurorehabilitation-stroke-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-38552072-cerebrolysin-acute-ischemic-stroke-hta.md]

Newer preclinical work keeps the mechanism story alive without upgrading human confidence. A 2025 aged-mouse study reports short-term-memory and hippocampal-spine-density rescue in a region- and age-dependent pattern, a 2025 hypertensive-rat study links recognition-memory rescue to hippocampal/cortical arborization and spine changes, and a 2025 ketamine mouse study links recognition/anxiety rescue to mitochondrial and CREB/PGC-1alpha changes. Recent post-HSV-encephalitis and glioma case reports belong in the recovery-context lane, not the efficacy lane. A 2025 psychiatry scoping review keeps psychiatric use in a sparse, low-confidence support lane rather than a direct cognition lane.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41460391-cerebrolysin-age-induced-memory-decline.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39826813-cerebrolysin-hypertensive-rat-memory-spines.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41204270-cerebrolysin-ketamine-cognitive-impairment-creb-pgc1a.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41178897-cerebrolysin-post-hsv-encephalitis.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41532044-cerebrolysin-glioma-case-report.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40722733-cerebrolysin-psychiatry-scoping-review.md]

Safety needs to stay visible: the 2024 anaphylaxis case report is a concrete reminder that IV Cerebrolysin is not a benign supplement, and the 2023 Cochrane acute-stroke safety signal keeps non-fatal serious adverse events on the table even if older dementia trials were generally well tolerated. Community adverse-event anecdotes are tracked in [[nootropic-peptide-anecdote-frontier-2026-scan]], stack attribution is routed through [[peptide-stack-topology-ledger]], and injection/source/hypersensitivity/co-intervention variables should be read through [[safety-interaction-matrix-for-multi-modal-stacks]] rather than treated as efficacy evidence.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39055722-cerebrolysin-anaphylaxis-case-report.md]^[raw/articles/cochrane-2023-cerebrolysin-acute-ischaemic-stroke.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40851188-cerebrolysin-c-regs2-a-moderate-acute-ischemic-stroke.md]

Older peptide-review literature groups Cerebrolysin with other neuroprotective bioregulators such as [[semax]] and [[cortexin]], framing the class as a bridge between experimental neuroprotection and functional recovery. That framing still works, but the human evidence now makes Cerebrolysin one of the more clinically grounded names in the broader [[peptide-neuroprotection]] cluster, especially for stroke recovery and selected older cognitive-impairment phenotypes.^[raw/articles/pubmed-24738258-neuroprotective-peptides-age.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-30383932-semax-transthyretin-neuroprotection.md]
