# Kortagen

Kortagen is one of the lesser-known peptide bioregulators mentioned in the older neuroprotective-peptide review literature. PubMed often surfaces the same label as `cortagen`, so the identity layer is partly transliteration and partly legacy terminology rather than a clean modern drug-development program.^[raw/articles/pubmed-24738258-neuroprotective-peptides-age.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-11276314-cortagen-sciatic-nerve-regeneration.md]

The current evidence stays thin. The direct cortagen paper in PubMed is a rat sciatic-nerve regeneration study, and the brain-ischemia review keeps it in the same older bioregulator cluster as Cortexin and Pinealon. That is enough to preserve the search node, but not enough to justify a modern human cognition claim.^[raw/articles/pubmed-11276314-cortagen-sciatic-nerve-regeneration.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-21476278-cortexin-cortagen-chronic-brain-ischemia.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-24738258-neuroprotective-peptides-age.md]
