# Semax

Semax is a regulatory peptide derived from ACTH fragments and is usually discussed as a nootropic / neuroprotective peptide rather than a mainstream medication. In the wiki it belongs next to [[peptide-neuroprotection]], [[cerebrolysin]], [[semax-selank-2026-scan]], and [[neurocognitive-interventions-under-study]] because the literature repeatedly frames it around memory, stroke recovery, and resilience rather than around simple stimulation.

Relevant raw PubMed and article notes: `raw/articles/pubmed-10741256-semax-optic-nerve-disease.md`, `raw/articles/pubmed-15792140-semax-cerebrovascular-insufficiency.md`, `raw/articles/pubmed-10199046-semax-posthypoxic-encephalopathy.md`, `raw/articles/pubmed-39442746-semax-antistress-rats.md`, `raw/articles/pubmed-41171324-semax-intracellular-calcium-dynamics-rat-brain-neurons.md`, `raw/articles/actanaturae-2025-semax-derivative-app-ps1-alzheimers-model.md`, `raw/articles/pmc-12151629-semax-copper-ros-abeta-cell.md`, `raw/articles/pubmed-30383932-semax-transthyretin-neuroprotection.md`, `raw/articles/pubmed-16634437-semax-selank-oligopeptides.md`, and `raw/articles/pubmed-24738258-neuroprotective-peptides-age.md`.
The evidence profile is mixed: older review literature and regional clinical use keep the signal alive, but high-quality international evidence for healthy-cognition enhancement is limited. The current 2025 packet is preclinical/mechanistic rather than human-confirmatory: a rat slice study reports increased spontaneous intracellular Ca2+ fluctuation frequency in hippocampal CA1 pyramidal neurons, an APP/PS1 mouse study reports behavioral and amyloid-pathology improvements after intranasal Semax or derivative, and an Abeta-copper cell-model paper reports reduced ROS/cytotoxicity. Together these support a translational disease-model story, not a settled human cognitive enhancer.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41171324-semax-intracellular-calcium-dynamics-rat-brain-neurons.md]^[raw/articles/actanaturae-2025-semax-derivative-app-ps1-alzheimers-model.md]^[raw/articles/pmc-12151629-semax-copper-ros-abeta-cell.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-10741256-semax-optic-nerve-disease.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-15792140-semax-cerebrovascular-insufficiency.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-10199046-semax-posthypoxic-encephalopathy.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39442746-semax-antistress-rats.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-24738258-neuroprotective-peptides-age.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-30383932-semax-transthyretin-neuroprotection.md]
For safety and sourcing, FDA's 2026 PCAC agenda now lists Semax-related bulk substances for 503A bulks-list discussion, which is the current compounding/status signal to watch. That does not endorse efficacy, but it is the relevant regulatory marker for the branch.^[raw/articles/fda-2026-advisory-epitalon-semax.md]
Community datasets keep Semax in the visible peptide cluster: Troof, SSC, ACX, and the 2017 /r/Nootropics survey all surface it as a repeat community signal, with the 2017 survey putting the reported median dose at 0.6 mg. That is anecdote, not proof, but it helps explain why the branch stays hot enough to track.^[raw/articles/troof-nootropic-ratings-2022.md]^[raw/articles/astralcodexten-2020-nootropics-survey-results.md]^[raw/articles/slatestarcodex-2016-nootropics-survey-results.md]^[raw/articles/darktka-2017-nootropics-survey-on-r-nootropics.md]
