# Pyridostigmine

Pyridostigmine / Mestinon is a prescription acetylcholinesterase inhibitor used for myasthenia gravis. In this wiki it belongs in the autonomic / ME/CFS / post-viral brain-fog treatment lane, not in the ordinary memory-enhancer lane: the useful signal is peripheral neuromuscular and autonomic state support, especially when orthostatic intolerance, POTS-like physiology, exertional intolerance, or PEM shape the brain-fog phenotype.

## Current Read

Medicinal cognition signal: `low/state-bound`. The best captured formal signal is not a cognition battery. A 45-person randomized double-blind placebo-controlled ME/CFS iCPET study found that a single 60 mg pyridostigmine dose improved peak VO2, cardiac output, and right atrial pressure versus placebo during repeat invasive exercise testing. This supports a neurovascular / exertional-intolerance route, not a healthy-adult nootropic claim.^[raw/articles/chest-2022-pyridostigmine-mecfs-icpet-rct.md]

Autonomic / fatigue physiology signal: `low-moderate for phenotype, low for broad symptoms`. A 2025 within-subject post-infectious ME/CFS study found that 30 mg pyridostigmine improved repeated hand-grip strength in 16/20 patients and reduced orthostatic heart-rate increase in the subset that completed standing testing. The study was small and nonrandomized, but it adds an objective short-term physiology anchor.^[raw/articles/frontiers-2025-pyridostigmine-handgrip-mecfs.md]

POTS signal: `hemodynamic stronger than cognitive`. A 2025 POTS treatment review found one small crossover RCT and two non-RCTs for pyridostigmine. The RCT improved standing heart-rate measures after a single 30 mg dose, while symptom-burden movement was not significant in the review's summary. That makes pyridostigmine relevant to orthostatic brain fog, but only when autonomic symptoms are actually present.^[raw/articles/clinical-autonomic-research-2025-pots-treatment-review-pyridostigmine.md]

## Safety And Interpretation Gates

DailyMed labels pyridostigmine as a prescription cholinesterase inhibitor for myasthenia gravis. Key gates include mechanical intestinal or urinary obstruction, asthma caution, renal dosing, cholinergic crisis risk, GI cramping/diarrhea/nausea, salivation/bronchial secretions/sweating, miosis, muscle cramps, fasciculation, weakness, and medication-supervision requirements.^[raw/articles/dailymed-2026-pyridostigmine-bromide-label.md]

The central-cognition analogy is weak. Pyridostigmine is not [[donepezil]], [[rivastigmine]], [[galantamine]], or [[huperzine-a]] in practical nootropic routing. The captured evidence points to peripheral cholinergic/autonomic effects and exertional physiology; if cognition improves, the likely path is less orthostatic load, better exertional tolerance, or less PEM-driven cognitive shutdown.

## Current Route

Use pyridostigmine when the question is ME/CFS, long COVID, POTS, orthostatic intolerance, PEM, exertional intolerance, or autonomic brain fog. Route it through [[post-viral-brain-fog-chemical-watchlist]] and [[prescription-cholinesterase-inhibitor-cognition-watchlist]] as a peripheral/autonomic AChEI exception. Do not rank it on [[healthy-adult-cognition-signals]] unless direct healthy-adult cognition data appear.
