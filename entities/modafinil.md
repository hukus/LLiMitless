# Modafinil

Modafinil is the wiki's main prescription wake-promoter benchmark. Armodafinil belongs in the same comparator lane unless a page is specifically about enantiomer-specific PK or labeling. The human cognition signal is real but smaller and narrower than the cultural reputation: it is strongest as wakefulness and state restoration, and only modest as rested healthy-adult enhancement.

## Medicinal Signal

- Formal signal: `high` as a wakefulness comparator; `low-moderate` as a rested healthy-adult enhancer.
- The 2020 healthy-adult meta-analysis found a small overall modafinil benefit, with the clearest domain signal in memory updating. Methylphenidate was somewhat stronger in that dataset, and d-amphetamine had no overall effect.^[raw/articles/pubmed-32709551-pharmaceutical-cognitive-enhancement-healthy-adults.md]
- DailyMed labels place modafinil and armodafinil in adult narcolepsy, OSA, and shift-work-disorder wakefulness indications, not in cognitive enhancement indications. Both are Schedule IV controlled substances.^[raw/articles/dailymed-modafinil-tablet-label.md]^[raw/articles/dailymed-armodafinil-tablet-label.md]
- Long-term narcolepsy and aviation/sleep-loss data keep the branch relevant for performance preservation, but that does not prove durable learning or transfer.^[raw/articles/pubmed-10767651-modafinil-long-term-narcolepsy.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-38790126-modafinil-aviators-sleep.md]

## Safety And Sourcing

- Practical burden: prescription access, insomnia, anxiety / fast heart rate, headache, appetite suppression, psychiatric activation risk, and tolerance reports.
- The Gwern survey is useful because it captures tolerance and side-effect patterns, but it remains anecdotal.
- Modafinil is the comparator to beat for clean wakefulness claims; it is not a general proof that arousal equals cognitive improvement.

## Current Read

Modafinil belongs in [[pharmacology-comparator-map]] as the serious prescription benchmark. A novel compound claiming focus, wakefulness, or study performance should be interpreted against modafinil, caffeine, methylphenidate, and sleep-state controls before being called meaningfully better.
