# Methylene Blue

Methylene blue is the main small-molecule case where community nootropic heat overlaps with a narrow human memory and imaging signal. The right reading is not "general enhancer"; it is a safety-constrained redox / consolidation candidate whose benefit depends heavily on dose, formulation, learning context, exclusion criteria, and whether the outcome is healthy enhancement or recovery-state support.

June 2026 anecdote read: `strong redox/nootropic salience with narrow formal anchor`. Methylene blue should now sit beside the strongest anecdote-led nootropic signals because Troof-scale community heat, a current capsule-focus report, and red/NIR stack experimentation all point in the same direction of user interest. That still does not make it a broad enhancer: the formal human cognition layer remains small, task/context-dependent, and tightly safety-screened, while the strongest practical signal may be memory consolidation after a learning/exposure session rather than everyday stimulation.

## Medicinal Signal

- Formal signal: `low-moderate`; stronger than pure folklore, weaker than a mature comparator.
- Healthy-adult imaging work reported increased task-evoked fMRI activity during sustained attention and short-term memory, stronger resting-state coupling in perception-memory networks, and about a 7% retrieval gain in the key single-dose study.^[raw/articles/pubmed-27351678-methylene-blue-human-brain-fmri.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-26961091-methylene-blue-functional-connectivity-human-brain.md]
- Exposure-therapy work suggests the molecule may be better framed as a consolidation adjunct after a successful learning session than as a baseline stimulant.^[raw/articles/pubmed-25018057-methylene-blue-claustrophobia-contextual-memory.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-28686823-methylene-blue-ptsd-exposure-rct.md]
- The AD methylthioninium branch is mixed: a phase 2 signal was followed by a negative phase 3 LMTM result on coprimary outcomes.^[raw/articles/pubmed-25550228-methylthioninium-phase2-ad.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-27863809-lmtm-phase3-ad-negative.md]
- The MB2 healthy-aging / MCI / mild-AD registry is now results-posted rather than protocol-only. ClinicalTrials.gov exposes descriptive tables for 117 enrolled participants and many imaging/behavioral outcomes, but several endpoint denominators are small, mild-AD cells are near-empty, and no peer-reviewed statistical paper is captured here. This keeps the MB2 layer `low`, not confidence-upgrading.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct02380573-methylene-blue-results-posted.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct02380573-mb2-results-api-2026-05-04.md]
- 2025 mouse TBI and postoperative-delirium papers support a recovery-state preclinical branch through neuroinflammation, mitochondrial-quality-control, and BBB-repair hypotheses. They do not upgrade healthy-human enhancement confidence.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40879922-methylene-blue-tbi-mice-spatial-working-memory.md]^[raw/articles/nature-2025-methylene-blue-postoperative-delirium-mice.md]

## Safety And Exclusions

- The current DailyMed label is for IV methemoglobinemia treatment, not oral nootropic use.
- The label-level safety anchors are not optional: serotonin-syndrome risk with serotonergic drugs and opioids, and G6PD-related hemolysis risk.^[raw/articles/dailymed-methylene-blue-injection-label-2026.md]
- The 2026 StatPearls clinical reference adds the practical toxicity layer: dose-dependent adverse effects, cardiovascular and neurologic symptoms, anaphylaxis, pulse-oximetry interference, renal caution, and the same serotonergic/G6PD gates.^[raw/articles/ncbi-bookshelf-2026-methylene-blue-statpearls.md]
- MB2's posted registry snapshot reported no deaths or serious adverse events, but common non-serious dye/urinary/GI events included blue/orange urine, green stool, urinary frequency/urgency, nausea/vomiting, diarrhea/loose stool, bladder irritation, and leg cramps.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct02380573-mb2-results-api-2026-05-04.md]
- Color, formulation, USP/pharmaceutical identity, and oral-vs-IV context all matter.

## Light And Redox Interaction

The red/NIR-light stack belongs in [[methylene-blue-plus-light-redox-interaction-scan]], not as an upgrade to this entity. The strongest formal combination-adjacent evidence is preclinical or mechanistic: a hepatic-encephalopathy rat study found separate MB and PBM arms both improved a disease-model memory deficit, and a neuroprotection review argues MB and PBM share mitochondrial targets. No captured human trial tests combined oral MB plus tPBM or red/NIR light for cognition.^[raw/articles/pubmed-33549685-methylene-blue-photobiomodulation-hepatic-encephalopathy.md]^[raw/articles/bmc-2020-methylene-blue-photobiomodulation-neuroprotection-review.md]

The direct community signal is weak: one r/Biohackers thread reports low-mg MB plus red/NIR light before strength training, with exercise, citrulline, tadalafil, and broader supplement confounds. It is useful as a surveillance signal that people are trying the combination, not as efficacy evidence.^[raw/articles/reddit-2025-methylene-blue-red-light-gym-thread.md]

## Current Read

Methylene blue deserves entity-level tracking because it has both a formal human signal and a strong anecdotal signal. The current entity read is `low-moderate` medicinal for narrow human cognition/consolidation contexts, `low` medicinal for MB2 aging/MCI/AD registry results until paper-level analysis appears, `preclinical-only` for TBI/postoperative-delirium recovery-state extension, and `weak-to-strong` anecdotal depending on whether the claim is a specific monotherapy/combo report or broad nootropic-survey salience.
