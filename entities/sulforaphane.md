# Sulforaphane

Sulforaphane is an isothiocyanate generated from glucoraphanin in cruciferous vegetables, especially broccoli sprouts, when myrosinase activity converts the precursor. In this wiki it is an Nrf2 / oxidative-stress / inflammation molecule, not a generic broccoli-food claim and not interchangeable across broccoli sprouts, glucoraphanin-only powders, myrosinase-containing products, stabilized sulforaphane, Avmacol-style products, Broccomax/BrocElite-style supplements, or ordinary cruciferous-vegetable intake.

Medicinal cognition signal: `low/disease-context and mostly cognition-null`. The first- and early-episode schizophrenia trial is the main direct human cognition anchor: sulforaphane missed the primary MATRICS composite endpoint but showed secondary-domain signals in spatial working memory and verbal learning and was described as well tolerated. The 2025 schizophrenia RCT meta-analysis is the stronger current governor: four RCTs / 369 patients showed no pooled cognitive benefits on MCCB total, spatial working memory, verbal learning, or reasoning/problem-solving, despite modest non-cognitive symptom and metabolic signals.^[raw/articles/pmc-11205988-sulforaphane-schizophrenia-cognition-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pmc-12581406-sulforaphane-schizophrenia-rct-meta-analysis.md]

The ASD literature explains why sulforaphane has community salience, but it should not be converted into a general cognition claim. A 2025 ASD meta-analysis found Social Responsiveness Scale improvements at selected follow-up windows and no significant adverse-event increase, with proposed oxidative-stress, inflammation, autophagy, PI3K-Akt, MAPK, TNF, Th17, mTOR, and IL-17 links. These are social/behavioral and mechanistic signals, not objective nootropic memory or attention proof.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41275316-sulforaphane-autism-meta-analysis-safety.md]

Preclinical signal: `rescue-positive but not transportable`. In a traumatic-brain-injury rat model, early post-injury sulforaphane improved Morris water maze and working-memory outcomes, supporting Nrf2/TBI plausibility. It does not establish a supplement enhancement claim in healthy humans.^[raw/articles/pmc-2700200-sulforaphane-tbi-cognition-rats.md]

Anecdote signal: `weak-moderate ASD/social salience, weak cognition evidence`. The captured Reddit thread contains a firsthand autism-adjacent social/extraversion claim plus replies about ASD studies, energy, rumination, spacing out, acne, dose, and product identity. That is relevant for hypothesis generation and safety/product QA, but it is not repeated objective cognition testing.^[raw/articles/reddit-2025-sulforaphane-personal-experience-thread.md]^[raw/articles/troof-nootropics-ratings-analysis.md]

Safety and product-quality signal: `low-moderate product-form caution`. Clinical meta-analytic and ASD captures did not show a strong adverse-event signal, but product identity dominates interpretation. A broccoli sprout beverage trial directly addressed thyroid-function / thyroid-autoimmunity concerns over 12 weeks. Future reads should track thyroid context, gastrointestinal tolerability, pregnancy/lactation caution, concomitant psychiatric medication, whether the product contains glucoraphanin plus active myrosinase or stabilized sulforaphane, and whether claimed micromole dose is verified.^[raw/articles/pmc-12581406-sulforaphane-schizophrenia-rct-meta-analysis.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41275316-sulforaphane-autism-meta-analysis-safety.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-30735751-broccoli-sprout-beverage-thyroid-safety.md]

## Practical Interpretation

Sulforaphane now clears a low-confidence standalone threshold because it has direct human cognition endpoints in schizophrenia, a fresh meta-analytic cognitive null, ASD social-response evidence, preclinical TBI rescue, safety anchors, and active community salience. The entity should be used as a disease-context / Nrf2 test case, not as a healthy-adult cognition enhancer.

A confidence upgrade requires an adequately powered cognition-first trial with verified sulforaphane yield, clear glucoraphanin/myrosinase chemistry, objective cognitive endpoints, thyroid/metabolic/adverse-event monitoring, and phenotype stratification. Until then, sulforaphane sits below cleaner comparators in [[molecule-intake-triage-table]] and remains linked to [[polyphenol-nrf2-cognition-watchlist]].
