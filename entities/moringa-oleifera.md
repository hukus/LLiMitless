# Moringa Oleifera

Moringa oleifera is a food and traditional-medicine plant used as a nutritional, metabolic, anti-inflammatory, and immune-support supplement. Its cognition relevance is indirect.

## Bottom Line

Medicinal cognition signal: `very low / indirect`. A 2026 review of 22 clinical trials and nine case reports found human evidence centered on immune, glycemic, antioxidant, HIV/prediabetes/malnutrition, and maternal/infant nutrition contexts, not direct cognition outcomes.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41710586-moringa-human-studies-review.md]

Mechanism signal: `preclinical`. A neuroprotective review summarizes antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, anti-amyloid, and neuronal-survival pathways in cellular and animal models, but this does not establish human brain-fog or cognition benefit.^[raw/articles/frontiers-2023-moringa-neuroprotective-review.md]

## Safety And Product Gate

LiverTox describes moringa as generally well tolerated in small clinical trials but a possible rare cause of clinically apparent liver injury after a positive rechallenge case. That keeps liver symptoms, product identity, co-ingredients, dose, and duration visible even though the overall safety profile looks better than many risk-first botanicals.^[raw/articles/ncbi-bookshelf-2024-livertox-moringa.md]

## Signal Judgment

Moringa is a nutritional/metabolic adjunct candidate, not a cognition lead. It deserves coverage because it is common and biologically plausible, but any brain-fog benefit should be interpreted through nutrition, glucose, inflammation, anemia/malnutrition, or general health status until direct cognition trials exist.
