# Mazdutide

## Bottom Line

Mazdutide is a GLP-1/glucagon dual agonist and the main active protocol watch item in the incretin cognition branch. Medicinal signal is `preclinical-plus-protocol`: a 2025 db/db mouse study reports cognition and multi-omics neuroprotection signals, and LIGHT-COG is a recruiting 76-week randomized cognition-primary trial in T2D with early dementia. There are no posted human cognition results yet. Anecdotal cognition signal is `absent-to-weak` in the current corpus.

## What It Is

Mazdutide, also known as IBI362, is a dual GLP-1R/GCGR agonist. It is not a GIP agonist; this matters because the current human cognition evidence in [[incretin-therapies-brain-health-2026-scan]] remains GLP-1RA-heavy, while mazdutide is a GLP-1/glucagon protocol lead.

## Medicinal Research Signal

The preclinical anchor is a 2025 db/db mouse study. Mazdutide improved cognitive performance versus dulaglutide in a diabetes-associated cognitive dysfunction model and was paired with transcriptomic, proteomic, and metabolomic analyses suggesting neuronal-integrity, demyelination, neuroprotection, energy-metabolism, and synaptic-plasticity pathways. This strengthens plausibility, but it remains animal evidence.^[raw/articles/pmc-12205698-mazdutide-diabetes-associated-cognitive-dysfunction.md]

The human gate is LIGHT-COG, a recruiting 76-week randomized trial in T2D with early dementia. The protocol uses weekly mazdutide with escalation to 4 mg maintenance and optional 6 mg increase, with iADRS change at Weeks 28, 52, and 76 as the primary cognition/function endpoint. No results are posted in the captured registry note, so confidence must not be upgraded yet.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct07083154-light-cog-mazdutide-early-dementia.md]

## Safety And Status

Mazdutide should stay in the protocol-watch bucket. The key safety and interpretation gates are GLP-1/glucagon metabolic effects, weight and glucose changes, GI tolerability, trial dropout, and whether cognition changes exceed metabolic-state improvement.

## Signal Judgment

Medicinal signal: `preclinical-plus-protocol`, no human cognition efficacy yet.

Anecdotal signal: `absent-to-weak`.

Practical route: high-priority surveillance item. Upgrade only after LIGHT-COG or another human cognition-primary readout posts.
