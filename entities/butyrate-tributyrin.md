# Butyrate and Tributyrin

Butyrate is a short-chain fatty acid made by gut microbial fermentation of fiber and sold directly as sodium, calcium, magnesium, or tributyrin-style products. Tributyrin is a butyrate prodrug / triglyceride route. In this wiki, the branch is a gut-metabolite / postbiotic-output brain-fog watch item, not a proven cognitive enhancer. It should not be collapsed with generic fiber, [[psychobiotic-cognition-product-watchlist]], [[urolithin-a]], [[ketone-ester]], [[lactate]], or [[metabolic-microclot-long-covid-chemical-watchlist]].

Formal signal: `low, stress-axis and biomarker adjacent`. The cleanest healthy-human gut-brain signal is a colon-delivered SCFA-mixture trial in 66 healthy men: serum SCFAs rose and cortisol response to psychosocial stress was attenuated, but BDNF, cortisol awakening response, fear learning/extinction, and mood ratings were not improved. A separate extruded-wheat-bran trial in 69 healthy men increased serum acetate and butyrate but did not modulate psychobiological functions. The modern tributyrin pilot in healthy adults was tolerability/biomarker focused and did not test cognition.^[raw/articles/nature-2020-colon-delivered-scfa-stress-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-35694161-extruded-wheat-bran-scfa-null-rct.md]^[raw/articles/sage-2024-butyragen-tributyrin-healthy-adults-pilot.md]

Illness-state signal: `low but plausible for sleep/inflammation`. In active ulcerative colitis, sodium butyrate improved inflammatory markers, clock-gene expression, sleep quality, and IBD quality of life. That is relevant to brain fog only indirectly through sleep, inflammation, and gut symptoms. It is not healthy-adult cognition evidence.^[raw/articles/springer-2024-sodium-butyrate-ulcerative-colitis-sleep-rct.md]

PK/product signal: `important identity gate`. Older oral tributyrin pharmacology in advanced solid-tumor patients showed tolerability and measurable but variable butyrate exposure, but dosing was high and capsule burden limited escalation. Consumer sodium-butyrate capsules, tributyrin softgels, colon-delivered SCFA mixtures, resistant starch, inulin, cereal fiber, and synbiotic products are different interventions.^[raw/articles/pubmed-12736763-tributyrin-oral-prodrug-pk.md]

Anecdote signal: `moderate but bidirectional`. Reddit and older nootropic-forum reports include explicit brief clarity / reduced brain-fog reports, but also severe brain fog, dizziness, panic/anxiety, lethargy, no effect, and GI-context reactions. The most useful interpretation is a gut-state / dose / product-form responder hypothesis, not a broad nootropic claim.^[raw/articles/reddit-2024-2026-butyrate-tributyrin-brainfog-threads.md]^[raw/articles/longecity-2015-butyrate-nootropic-thread.md]

## Signal Split

| Layer | Signal | Readout |
|---|---|---|
| Colon-delivered SCFA mixture | `low-moderate stress-axis` | Cortisol response attenuated in healthy men, but mood/fear/BDNF endpoints null |
| Fiber-mediated SCFA increase | `null psychobiological` | Serum SCFAs increased without measured psychobiological benefit |
| Tributyrin supplement pilot | `biomarker/tolerability only` | Healthy-adult pilot did not test cognition or brain fog |
| Active UC sleep/inflammation | `low illness-state` | Sleep and inflammation improved in active UC, transportability limited |
| Brain-fog anecdotes | `moderate bidirectional` | Brief clarity reports coexist with adverse fog, anxiety, lethargy, and no effect |

## Practical Interpretation

Butyrate/tributyrin belongs in [[scfa-postbiotic-brain-fog-cognition-watchlist]] as a gut-metabolite branch. A useful record needs product form, dose, timing with meals, sodium load, baseline GI symptoms, stool pattern, resistant starch / inulin / fiber intake, fermented foods, histamine or sulfur reactions, sleep, anxiety, electrolytes, and dechallenge/rechallenge.

For healthy adults, the current strongest formal read is not "improves cognition." It is "colon-delivered SCFAs can move stress-axis physiology, while modest food-mediated SCFA increases may do nothing measurable for psychobiological outcomes." For brain fog, the anecdote layer is real enough to preserve, but too bidirectional and gut-confounded to rank as a general enhancer.
