# Alpha-Lipoic Acid

Alpha-lipoic acid (ALA, thioctic acid) is a mitochondrial cofactor and redox-active antioxidant supplement often discussed for diabetic neuropathy, metabolic stress, metal-chelation lore, and cognitive aging hypotheses. For this wiki it belongs in [[redox-mitochondrial-cofactor-cognition-watchlist]] as a disease-context / neuropathy-redox candidate, not a broad nootropic.

Medicinal cognition signal: `low/combination-limited`. The main clinical cognition anchor is a 2014 AD pilot RCT with 39 participants comparing placebo, omega-3, and omega-3 plus alpha-lipoic acid for 12 months. The primary oxidative-stress endpoint did not differ; ADAS-cog and ADL did not significantly differ from placebo; omega-3 plus ALA showed less decline on MMSE and IADL. This is interesting but small, AD-specific, and combination-confounded.^[raw/articles/pmc-3886557-omega3-alpha-lipoic-acid-ad-rct.md]

Mechanistic AD signal: `plausible but non-decisive`. A 2021 AD review frames ALA through antioxidant, mitochondrial, anti-inflammatory, and metal-chelation mechanisms. That supports why ALA belongs in the redox cofactor graph, but does not establish isolated cognitive efficacy in humans.^[raw/articles/pubmed-34450170-alpha-lipoic-acid-ad-review.md]

Safety and phenotype signal: `moderate caution`. StatPearls frames ALA mainly around diabetic neuropathy and metabolic oxidative stress, with mechanisms including ROS scavenging, antioxidant regeneration, glutathione synthesis, and inflammatory signaling modulation. Common adverse effects include headache, heartburn, nausea, and vomiting; cautions include diabetes / glucose lowering, liver disease, heavy alcohol use, thyroid disorder, thiamine deficiency, and severe overdose risks such as seizures and metabolic acidosis.^[raw/articles/ncbi-bookshelf-2024-alpha-lipoic-acid-statpearls.md]

Community signal: `moderate but narrow`. The captured Reddit report describes marked brain-fog and neuropathy-like symptom improvement at 1200 mg/day racemic ALA. That is useful for phenotype generation, not efficacy proof.^[raw/articles/reddit-2021-alpha-lipoic-acid-brain-fog-thread.md]

## Signal Split

| Layer | Signal | Readout |
|---|---|---|
| AD pilot cognition | `low/combination-limited` | Omega-3 plus ALA showed secondary MMSE/IADL signals, not ADAS-cog or primary biomarker success |
| Isolated healthy cognition | `unclear/unsupported` | No clean healthy-adult cognition trial packet captured |
| Mechanism | `plausible` | Mitochondrial, glutathione, inflammatory, antioxidant, and chelation hypotheses |
| Neuropathy/metabolic phenotype | `stronger adjacent context` | ALA is more established around diabetic neuropathy/metabolic oxidative stress than cognition |
| Anecdote | `moderate narrow` | Brain-fog/neuropathy-like report, high dose, single-user |
| Safety | `moderate caution` | Watch glucose lowering, thyroid/thiamine/alcohol context, GI/headache effects, and overdose risk |

## Practical Interpretation

Alpha-lipoic acid should be routed through [[molecule-intake-triage-table]] as a `redox / neuropathy / metabolic-state hypothesis`. It is inclusion-worthy because it has human AD pilot data and a coherent mechanism, but its best evidence is combination- and disease-context limited. ALA should not be ranked as a general focus compound without specifying form, dose, glucose status, thiamine/alcohol risk, neuropathy phenotype, and whether the target is symptom relief or objective cognition.
