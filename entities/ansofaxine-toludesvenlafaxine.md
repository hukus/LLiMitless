# Ansofaxine / Toludesvenlafaxine

Ansofaxine / toludesvenlafaxine / LY03005 / Ruoxinlin is an SNDRI antidepressant: a serotonin, norepinephrine, and dopamine reuptake inhibitor. In this wiki it belongs beside [[liafensine]], [[amitifadine]], [[gsk372475]], [[csti-500]], and [[triple-reuptake-inhibitor-cognition-watchlist]], not in the same practical bucket as supplement stimulants.

## Current Read

Medicinal cognition signal: `low / mood-state`. The strongest evidence is MDD symptom improvement, including depression-scale anhedonia and cognitive-impairment-factor signals. That is relevant to depression-linked brain fog, fatigue, concentration difficulty, and anhedonia, but it is not objective healthy-adult cognition enhancement.

The phase 3 China trial randomized 588 adults with MDD to ansofaxine 80 mg, ansofaxine 160 mg, or placebo for 8 weeks. Both active doses beat placebo on MADRS total-score change, and the paper reported superiority on anhedonia factor and Sheehan Disability Scale improvement.^[raw/articles/nature-2023-ansofaxine-phase3-mdd.md]

The sponsor endpoint announcement adds cognition-adjacent depression-scale detail: HAMD-17 cognitive-impairment factor, blocking factor, anxiety/somatization, MADRS anhedonia factor, and function scores improved versus placebo. This is useful, but it is not an independent cognitive battery.^[raw/articles/luye-2019-ly03005-phase3-endpoints.md]

Regulatory status: Luye states Ruoxinlin was approved in China in 2022 for major depressive disorder. That makes ansofaxine more clinically real than [[amitifadine]] or [[gsk372475]], while still not making it a nootropic.^[raw/articles/luye-2022-ruoxinlin-approval-china.md]

## Interpretation Gates

- Do not call HAMD cognitive-impairment-factor improvement a healthy cognition endpoint.
- Separate depression remission, anhedonia relief, fatigue relief, sexual-function claims, and objective cognition.
- Watch serotonergic/noradrenergic/dopaminergic stacking, BP/HR, insomnia, anxiety/activation, nausea, dry mouth, appetite/weight change, and medication-switching washout.
- Use [[triple-reuptake-inhibitor-cognition-watchlist]] and [[pharmacology-comparator-map]] before comparing ansofaxine with [[tesofensine]], [[bupropion]], [[centanafadine]], or wake-promoters.

Current verdict: clinically important SNDRI antidepressant; cognition relevance is depression-state and anhedonia-linked, not broad enhancement.
