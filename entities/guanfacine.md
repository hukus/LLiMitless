# Guanfacine

Guanfacine is a central alpha-2A adrenergic agonist used as an ADHD medication and discussed off-label for prefrontal-cortex dysfunction, traumatic brain injury, PTSD, and long-COVID brain fog. In this wiki it belongs in [[post-viral-brain-fog-chemical-watchlist]], near [[n-acetylcysteine]], [[low-dose-naltrexone]], [[low-dose-aripiprazole]], and [[neuroinflammation-and-cognition]].

## Bottom Line

Brain-fog signal: `low formal, moderate-strong anecdotal`. The strongest formal source is a 12-patient open-label guanfacine plus NAC case series in long-COVID brain fog: eight of 12 patients improved, especially in working memory, concentration, executive function, and work capacity, while two stopped for hypotension and/or dizziness and two were lost or discontinued for other reasons.^[raw/articles/sciencedirect-2023-guanfacine-nac-long-covid-brain-fog.md]

Practical route: medical-supervision-first. Guanfacine is not a casual stimulant or supplement; its key safety gates overlap with long-COVID dysautonomia: hypotension, bradycardia, syncope, sedation, somnolence, cardiac-conduction issues, rebound hypertension if stopped abruptly, high-fat-meal exposure effects, and CYP3A4 drug interactions.^[raw/articles/dailymed-2025-guanfacine-er-label.md]

Healthy-adult enhancement signal: `not established`. The current page is about post-viral / prefrontal executive dysfunction, not improving already-healthy cognition.

## Formal Evidence

The long-COVID report used guanfacine 1 mg at bedtime for one month, then 2 mg if tolerated, with 600 mg NAC daily. Reported gains centered on working memory, concentration, executive function, and resuming normal workloads. A dechallenge/rechallenge-like clue appeared in one patient whose deficits returned when guanfacine was stopped because of hypotension and improved after restarting.^[raw/articles/sciencedirect-2023-guanfacine-nac-long-covid-brain-fog.md]

The same design limits are major: no placebo, no randomization, no blinding, tiny sample, combination treatment with NAC, and strong selection effects. Treat this as a high-priority trial hypothesis rather than efficacy proof.

## Safety And Status

DailyMed identifies guanfacine ER as a prescription alpha-2A agonist for ADHD. Key cognition-relevant warnings are hypotension, bradycardia, syncope, sedation/somnolence, cardiac-conduction abnormalities, rebound hypertension after abrupt discontinuation, CYP3A4 interactions, and the instruction not to crush or chew extended-release tablets.^[raw/articles/dailymed-2025-guanfacine-er-label.md]

## Signal Judgment

Guanfacine is one of the more direct post-viral brain-fog chemical leads because it targets prefrontal executive dysfunction and has both a formal case series and repeated community reports. Confidence remains low because the formal evidence is uncontrolled and because autonomic/sedation risks are exactly the symptoms many long-COVID users are trying to escape.
