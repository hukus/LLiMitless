# Donepezil

## Bottom Line

Donepezil is a prescription acetylcholinesterase inhibitor with a real but narrow cognition-relevant signal in trained-skill retention, acute young-adult executive tasks, and sleep-deprivation-vulnerable responders. The same source packet has hard counterweights: a sleep-deprivation null, memory impairment in healthy older adults, a chronic cognitive-training null, MCI primary-endpoint misses, and label-level bradycardia / GI / insomnia / syncope risks. It belongs in the [[prescription-cholinesterase-inhibitor-cognition-watchlist]], not in a casual healthy-adult nootropic tier.

## What It Is

Donepezil is indicated for dementia of the Alzheimer's type. In this wiki it sits beside [[galantamine]], [[rivastigmine]], [[huperzine-a]], [[nicotine]], and [[varenicline]] inside the broader [[cholinergic-state-restoration-and-cognition]] branch.

## Medicinal Research Signal

The positive healthy-adjacent signals are small and specialized. A tiny pilot study in licensed pilots reported better 30-day retention of complex flight-simulator tasks after 5 mg/day donepezil. A young healthy male study found selective acute gains in sustained attention, reaction time, dual-task performance, and an executive digit-span component after 5-7.5 mg. A sleep-deprivation crossover found no rested benefit but improved delayed recognition in volunteers most vulnerable to sleep-loss decline.^[raw/articles/pubmed-12105320-donepezil-flight-simulator.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-21262858-donepezil-healthy-young-executive.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-19725251-donepezil-sleep-deprivation-vulnerable.md]

The counterweight is just as important. A separate sleep-deprivation study did not find significant cognitive rescue despite subjective-alertness movement. In healthy older adults, donepezil impaired paired-associates learning while placebo participants improved with practice, and several participants withdrew with severe GI symptoms. In older adults with memory complaints, adding donepezil to cognitive training failed to outperform placebo over a year. MCI trials missed primary endpoints despite some secondary signals, and fragile-X work moved fMRI activation without significant cognitive or behavioral benefit.^[raw/articles/pubmed-22161694-donepezil-sleep-deprivation-null.md]^[raw/articles/pmc-3169575-donepezil-healthy-older-memory-impairment.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-18818443-donepezil-cognitive-training-older-adults-null.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-15326237-donepezil-mci-24week-primary-null.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-19176895-donepezil-mci-48week-primary-null.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-31264943-donepezil-fragile-x-biomarker-null.md]

## Anecdote Signal

Community signal is `weak`. Reddit threads show occasional memory-recall enthusiasm and comparisons with huperzine A, but the reports are sparse, dose-variable, and mixed with insomnia / no-effect counterreports. They are useful for tracking prescription off-label interest and cholinergic adverse effects, not for upgrading confidence.^[raw/articles/reddit-2021-2026-donepezil-memory-nootropic-threads.md]

## Safety And Status

DailyMed frames donepezil as a prescription dementia drug. Key off-label gates include bradycardia / heart block, syncope, nausea, diarrhea, vomiting, GI bleeding / ulcer monitoring, weight loss at higher dose, bladder-outflow concerns, seizure potential, pulmonary caution, insomnia, fatigue, muscle cramps, anesthesia interaction, and conflicts with anticholinergic or other cholinergic drugs.^[raw/articles/dailymed-2026-donepezil-label.md]

## Signal Judgment

Medicinal signal: `low-moderate` for narrow trained-skill, executive-task, and sleep-loss-vulnerability contexts; `low` for broad healthy-adult enhancement.

Anecdotal signal: `weak`.

Practical route: prescription-only cholinergic comparator. Treat as state / phenotype research signal, not a general brain-fog supplement.
