# Pemoline

Pemoline is the historical prescription stimulant anchor for the [[pemoline-adjacent-gray-market-stimulant-watchlist]]. It matters to cognition research mainly because [[cyclazodone]] and [[n-methyl-cyclazodone]] are discussed as pemoline-adjacent functional stimulants, not because pemoline is a safe nootropic precedent.

## Current Read

Medicinal cognition signal: `risk-first/clinical-state`. FDA-approved CYLERT pemoline was an ADHD stimulant, but the captured label and withdrawal trail are dominated by liver-toxicity risk rather than healthy-adult cognitive enhancement.^[raw/articles/fda-2002-cylert-pemoline-label-liver-warning.md]^[raw/articles/federalregister-2023-cylert-pemoline-approval-withdrawal.md]

Safety/status signal: `high concern`. The FDA label warned that pemoline could produce liver abnormalities up to liver failure and death, said there was no way to predict who was likely to develop liver failure, recommended baseline and every-two-week ALT monitoring, and required written informed consent. The 2023 Federal Register approval-withdrawal notice states FDA's 2005 communication concluded the overall liver-toxicity risk of CYLERT and generic pemoline outweighed benefit.^[raw/articles/fda-2002-cylert-pemoline-label-liver-warning.md]^[raw/articles/federalregister-2023-cylert-pemoline-approval-withdrawal.md]

Sports/status signal: WADA's 2026 S6.B specified-stimulant list names pemoline. This keeps pemoline and close stimulant analog discussions out of any casual sports-performance lane.^[raw/articles/wada-2026-stimulant-list-afinils-pemoline-tesofensine.md]

## Interpretation Gates

- Do not use pemoline as proof that pemoline-adjacent research chemicals are safe; the regulatory history points the other way.
- Do not infer healthy-adult cognition benefit from ADHD stimulant history.
- Treat liver monitoring, idiosyncratic injury possibility, product identity, and sports status as first-order variables.
- Use [[methylphenidate]], [[modafinil]], and [[solriamfetol]] as mature comparator drugs before upgrading any pemoline-adjacent nootropic claim.

## Current Route

Pemoline belongs in [[pemoline-adjacent-gray-market-stimulant-watchlist]], [[pharmacology-comparator-map]], and [[regulatory-and-sports-visibility-map]] as a warning comparator. It is not a candidate to rank beside low-risk supplements or current prescription wake-promoter benchmarks.
