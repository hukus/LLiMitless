# Polygala / BT-11

Polygala tenuifolia / Polygalae Radix / Yuan Zhi is a traditional-medicine root used in cognitive, mood, and sleep lore. The wiki keeps it product-normalized: BT-11, PDC-1421, generic Polygala root powder, Polygalae Radix multi-herb use, and isolated constituents such as tenuifolin or tenuigenin should not be collapsed into one interchangeable intervention.

## Medicinal Evidence

The direct cognition anchor is BT-11, an extract from Polygala tenuifolia root. The small randomized double-blind placebo-controlled elderly-human study used CERAD and MMSE endpoints with 28 treated and 25 placebo participants. The abstract reports larger total CERAD improvement with BT-11, especially word-list recognition, constructional recall and praxis, and modified Boston naming. This is enough for standalone coverage, but not enough for a broad recommendation upgrade because it is small, elderly-biased, and product-specific.^[raw/articles/pubmed-19699261-polygala-bt11-elderly-cognition.md]

The 2025 healthy-adult natural-extract network meta-analysis adds a useful but still thin bridge. It included 27 randomized trials in 2,334 healthy adults and summarized extract-specific wins, including Polygala tenuifolia for overall cognition. The wiki treats this as supportive because the signal is extract-level and network-meta-analytic; it does not prove that every commercial Polygala product, route, dose, or constituent has the same effect.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40213691-natural-extracts-healthy-adults-network-meta-analysis.md]

PDC-1421 is a separate standardized Radix polygalae extract development program. NCI defines it as a botanical formulation containing dry-root Polygala tenuifolia extract and lists BLI-1005 / PDC-1421 aliases. The phase 1 healthy-volunteer record used single doses of 380-3,800 mg, reported no serious adverse events, and found mild non-serious events such as abdominal fullness, constipation, sleepiness, and oral ulcer. The phase 2 MDD record found a larger MADRS reduction in the high-dose PDC-1421 arm than placebo, with no deaths or serious adverse events posted. That matters for human exposure and mood-adjacent plausibility, but the endpoint is depression, not cognition.^[raw/articles/nci-2026-pdc1421-drug-dictionary.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-2026-nct01774045-pdc1421-phase1-safety.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-2026-nct02395978-pdc1421-mdd-results.md]

The adult-ADHD phase 2 trial is the current attention-relevant watch item. It completed in 2023 with 70 participants and primary ADHD-RS-IV response plus attention/sluggish-cognitive-tempo secondary endpoints, but ClinicalTrials.gov had no posted results in the 2026-05-03 check. Until results post, it is a watch signal only.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-2026-nct05202327-pdc1421-adhd-no-results.md]

## Safety And Product Gates

The safety read is not "unsafe," but it is not clean enough for casual nootropic ranking. PDC-1421 phase 1 and phase 2 records are reassuring about short exposures under trial screening, yet posted adverse-event tables still include GI symptoms, sleepiness, dry mouth, dizziness, liver-enzyme increases, potassium increase, anxiety, and palpitations-like entries across active and placebo groups.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-2026-nct01774045-pdc1421-phase1-safety.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-2026-nct02395978-pdc1421-mdd-results.md]

The broader Polygalae Radix review is the main product-chemistry caution. It reports more than 160 identified metabolites and notes that traditional practice recognizes throat irritation, while large or long-term use can stimulate the digestive tract, with saponins named as the main toxic metabolite class. That makes processing, extract standardization, route, dose, and duration part of the evidence, not details to ignore.^[raw/articles/pubmed-38873413-polygalae-radix-toxicology-review.md]

## Practical Interpretation

[[polygala-bt11]] is now a standalone low-confidence entity because it has more than anecdote: a small direct cognition RCT, a healthy-adult extract-level meta-analytic signal, official PDC-1421 trial exposure data, and a real community corpus. It still should not be ranked as a top-tier cognitive enhancer. The most defensible route is product-specific, state-dependent calm-focus or mood/attention restoration, with healthy-adult objective cognition still uncertain.
