# Cyclazodone

Cyclazodone is a pemoline-adjacent gray-market stimulant and research chemical. It is relevant to this wiki because users discuss it as a functional stimulant for focus, motivation, and task completion, but the formal packet captured here is identity, toxicology, regulatory, and anecdote-first rather than cognition-efficacy-first.

## Current Read

Medicinal cognition signal: `risk-first/no human cognition packet`. PubChem gives a chemical identity anchor, and a 2022 toxicology abstract describes cyclazodone as a centrally acting dopaminergic stimulant related to [[pemoline]] and aminorex, but no controlled healthy-adult cognition trial was captured.^[raw/articles/pubchem-2026-cyclazodone-nmethylcyclazodone-identity.md]^[raw/articles/clinical-toxicology-2022-n-methyl-cyclazodone-toxicity.md]

Anecdote signal: `moderate but low-confidence`. Reddit threads describe cyclazodone as a functional stimulant for focus, motivation, work completion, social energy, and a modafinil/amphetamine-like use case. The same threads include no-effect or body-load concerns, liver-marker debate, source scarcity, customs/status uncertainty, and comparisons against [[n-methyl-cyclazodone]].^[raw/articles/reddit-2020-2026-cyclazodone-nmc-threads.md]

Safety/status signal: `high uncertainty`. Cyclazodone is not the same as [[pemoline]], but the pemoline reference class has a serious liver-toxicity history. N-methyl-cyclazodone has a laboratory-confirmed toxicity case with autonomic, movement, ECG, CK, and ALT signals, and a separate multi-agent DILI case includes N-methylcyclazodone in an online-drug stack. These do not prove cyclazodone-only liver failure, but they make casual "clean stimulant" framing too weak for the wiki.^[raw/articles/fda-2002-cylert-pemoline-label-liver-warning.md]^[raw/articles/clinical-toxicology-2022-n-methyl-cyclazodone-toxicity.md]^[raw/articles/acg-2022-n-methylcyclazodone-online-hormones-dili.md]

Sports/status signal: WADA 2026 S0 and S6 logic matters. Cyclazodone was not the named line item captured in this pass, but non-approved pharmacological substances and unlisted stimulants create athlete-risk logic even before a molecule-specific federation check.^[raw/articles/wada-2026-stimulant-list-afinils-pemoline-tesofensine.md]

## Interpretation Gates

- Do not rank cyclazodone beside [[modafinil]], [[methylphenidate]], or [[solriamfetol]] without human cognition and safety data.
- Separate cyclazodone from [[n-methyl-cyclazodone]]; forum posts often compare them, but the direct toxicology case is NMC exposure with cyclazodone metabolite confirmation.
- Treat liver markers, ECG/autonomic effects, CK/rhabdomyolysis risk, sleep timing, stimulant stacking, serotonergic/psychiatric co-medications, and product identity as first-order gates.
