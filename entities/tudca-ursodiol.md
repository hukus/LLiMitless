# TUDCA / Ursodiol

TUDCA, tauroursodeoxycholic acid, taurursodiol, and related synonym labels sit in the hydrophilic bile-acid neuroprotection lane beside UDCA / ursodiol. This page covers TUDCA/TURSO, UDCA/ursodiol, and the AMX0035 sodium-phenylbutyrate plus taurursodiol comparator. It does not treat generic "liver detox," bear-bile lore, bile salts, ox bile, or ordinary digestive-aid blends as interchangeable cognition evidence.

Medicinal cognition signal: `low/clinical-state`. The strongest formal case for this branch is not a healthy-adult cognition trial. It is a disease-state and target-engagement packet: UDCA has Parkinson's disease mitochondrial target-engagement data, TUDCA has preclinical Alzheimer's and neuroprotection mechanisms, TUDCA supplementation is biologically active and apparently tolerable in a short progressive-MS trial, and ALS programs now provide major negative phase-3 guardrails.^[raw/articles/ucl-2023-udca-parkinsons-phase2.md]^[raw/articles/frontiers-2024-tudca-ad-review.md]^[raw/articles/pmc-2025-bile-acid-tudca-progressive-ms.md]^[raw/articles/mndassociation-2024-tudca-als-phase3-negative.md]^[raw/articles/amylyx-2024-amx0035-phoenix-withdrawal.md]

The identity boundary matters. PubChem separates TUDCA / taurursodiol from UDCA / ursodiol, and DailyMed's ursodiol label is a prescription liver/bile/gallstone anchor, not a nootropic label. AMX0035 adds another boundary: taurursodiol in a fixed combination with sodium phenylbutyrate was removed from the U.S. and Canadian ALS markets after PHOENIX failed confirmatory endpoints.^[raw/articles/pubchem-2026-tudca-udca-identity.md]^[raw/articles/dailymed-2023-ursodiol-label.md]^[raw/articles/amylyx-2024-amx0035-phoenix-withdrawal.md]

Anecdote signal: `moderate mixed` for liver/gut/mood/energy and `weak-moderate` for direct brain fog. Reddit reports include better mood, energy, exercise tolerance, constipation, anxiety, liver enzymes, digestive function, and one direct brain-fog clearance report at 500 mg twice daily for about 4 months. These are useful responder hypotheses, not efficacy evidence, because B12 deficiency, hormones, post-viral state, gallbladder/bile function, liver enzymes, alcohol, diet, and supplement stacks dominate attribution.^[raw/articles/reddit-2024-2026-tudca-brainfog-mood-threads.md]

## Signal Split

| Layer | Signal | Readout |
|---|---|---|
| Healthy adult cognition | `absent` | No clean healthy-adult cognitive endpoint packet captured |

## Practical Interpretation

TUDCA / UDCA belongs in [[bile-acid-neuroprotection-cognition-watchlist]] as a `low-confidence, phenotype-gated gut-liver-brain candidate`. It is relevant when brain fog plausibly tracks bile/gallbladder/liver dysfunction, gut dysbiosis, cholestatic disease context, post-viral digestive change, or neuroinflammatory disease-state hypotheses. It should not rank with [[creatine]], [[caffeine]], [[ketone-ester]], or [[citicoline]] for practical healthy-adult cognition.

Do not collapse TUDCA supplements, prescription [[tudca-ursodiol|ursodiol]], AMX0035, generic bile salts, ox-bile products, or bear-bile-derived claims. The formal evidence is mostly neuroprotection and target engagement, while the clearest human efficacy guardrails are negative ALS phase-3 outcomes. The safest interpretation is "watch closely, especially for responder phenotype," not "validated nootropic."
