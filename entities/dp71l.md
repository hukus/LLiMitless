# DP71L

DP71L is a viral PPP1R15B-like integrated-stress-response inhibition tool. It is the strongest direct cognition signal in the ISR branch, but only as preclinical delivery-sensitive biology.

Medicinal cognition signal: `preclinical-only, strong within animal models`. The 2026 Science raw note reports that DP71L acted as a potent pan-ISR inhibitor, reversed cognitive and synaptic deficits in mouse models of Down syndrome, Alzheimer disease, and aging, and enhanced synaptic plasticity and memory in healthy mice.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41926581-dp71l-integrated-stress-response-cognition.md]

Translation signal: `tool, not consumer molecule`. DP71L is not a practical nootropic candidate in the captured corpus. The unresolved gates are delivery, immunogenicity, dose control, durability, reversibility, and whether similar effects can be achieved by small-molecule eIF2B activators without suppressing useful stress responses.

Mechanism caveat: `broad ISR inhibition needs timing gates`. The split-ISR and neurodegeneration-review sources keep confidence low because ISR modulation can be protective or harmful depending on state and pathway node.^[raw/articles/nature-2025-plasticity-mammalian-integrated-stress-response.md]^[raw/articles/molecular-neurodegeneration-2025-integrated-stress-response-neurodegenerative-diseases.md]

Safety route: DP71L should route through [[safety-interaction-matrix-for-multi-modal-stacks]] as a delivery- and durability-gated preclinical tool. It should not be converted into a consumer peptide/gene-therapy idea without immune, dose-control, reversibility, disease-state, stress-resilience, and long-term plasticity gates.
