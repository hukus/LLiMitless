# Low-Dose Aripiprazole

Low-dose aripiprazole, often called low-dose Abilify or LDA in ME/CFS and long-COVID communities, is off-label sub-antipsychotic-range aripiprazole use for fatigue, post-exertional malaise, sensory sensitivity, and brain fog. It belongs in [[post-viral-brain-fog-chemical-watchlist]] and [[neuroinflammation-and-cognition]], not in casual nootropic stacks.

## Bottom Line

Brain-fog signal: `low formal, moderate-strong anecdotal, risk-heavy`. The main formal source is a 101-patient retrospective ME/CFS study from Stanford clinical practice. Doses ranged from 0.2 to 2.0 mg/day. Seventy-five of 101 patients improved in at least one symptom category, and responders with complete ratings had brain-fog scores fall from 4.39 to 2.06 on a 0-10 scale.^[raw/articles/springer-2021-low-dose-aripiprazole-mecfs.md]

Safety signal: `high practical burden`. Even at low doses, this is aripiprazole. Label-level gates include antipsychotic boxed-warning context, tardive dyskinesia, metabolic changes, impulse-control problems, orthostatic hypotension, falls, seizures, cognitive/motor impairment, temperature regulation, akathisia/agitation-like effects, and CYP metabolism issues.^[raw/articles/dailymed-2025-aripiprazole-label.md]

Healthy-adult enhancement signal: `absent`. The current signal is illness-state recovery, not healthy cognition improvement.

## Formal Evidence

The retrospective study included ME/CFS patients treated with low-dose aripiprazole for an average of 7.8 months. Responders improved in fatigue, brain fog, unrefreshing sleep, and PEM frequency. Twelve of 101 had no observable difference, and 14 worsened or stopped because of side effects. Reported adverse effects included headache, irritability/agitation, insomnia, extreme agitation, and variable weight changes.^[raw/articles/springer-2021-low-dose-aripiprazole-mecfs.md]

The design is not randomized or blinded, and symptom scores were clinical-practice ratings with missing/descriptive records. The signal is important enough to track but not enough to treat LDA as proven.

## Safety And Status

DailyMed frames aripiprazole as an atypical antipsychotic with major warnings and psychiatric indications. Low-dose ME/CFS or long-COVID use is off-label. The wiki should route any practical discussion through medical supervision, slow titration, metabolic/weight monitoring, akathisia/agitation screening, impulse-control screening, orthostatic and fall risk, drug interactions, and clear stopping rules.^[raw/articles/dailymed-2025-aripiprazole-label.md]

## Signal Judgment

Low-dose aripiprazole is now too important to leave out of a brain-fog graph. It may be one of the stronger post-viral / ME/CFS brain-fog signals, but its drug-class risks are much heavier than LDN, H1/H2 antihistamines, NAC, or PEA. Keep it low-confidence and risk-first until controlled evidence exists.
