# Nicotine

Nicotine is a high-salience cholinergic comparator with a real acute attention signal and a large addiction/safety burden. The wiki should treat it as a state-restoration and risk comparator, not as a clean nootropic. The hardest interpretive problem is separating direct cognition from withdrawal reversal, craving relief, arousal, and expectancy.

June 2026 anecdote read: `strong risk-first attention salience, not a clean nootropic`. Nicotine belongs in the strongest nootropic-anecdote routing because focus, motivation, and brain-fog reports are recurrent and because the formal branch has a real acute attention anchor. The safety interpretation runs the other way: dependence, tolerance, withdrawal cycling, sleep disruption, cardiovascular cautions, route differences, and post-viral overexertion risk should cap any positive ranking.

## Medicinal Signal

- Formal signal: `moderate` for acute attention and state restoration; `low` for broad enhancement.
- The cleanest current branch is an exploratory randomized crossover study in smokers after abstinence, which makes nicotine relevant to withdrawal and state restoration rather than rested healthy-adult enhancement.^[raw/articles/pubmed-38582919-nicotine-cognitive-function-smokers-abstinence.md]
- A nicotine + galantamine healthy-never-smoker study and varenicline literature keep the broader nicotinic/cholinergic branch alive, but they do not remove addiction and cardiovascular constraints.^[raw/articles/pubmed-31686175-nicotine-galantamine-healthy-never-smokers-cognition.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-21942262-varenicline-bupropion-nicotine-deprived-smokers-cognition.md]
- The 2026 nicotine-tablet attention study is directly relevant, but it remains a 40-participant sponsor-run active-not-recruiting record with no posted results in the captured update.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct07408180-nicotine-tablet-attention-2026-04-30.md]

## Post-Viral Patch Signal

Nicotine patches now have a distinct long-COVID anecdote / hypothesis branch. The formal source is only a four-person post-COVID case series plus cholinergic hypothesis paper using low-dose transdermal nicotine, usually 7.5 mg/24 h, with reported improvements in fatigue, concentration, retentiveness, sleep, dyspnea, smell/taste, and weakness. A 2026 long-COVID candidate-treatment review rated nicotine evidence as very low and unclear, with no robust efficacy trials reported.^[raw/articles/bioelectronicmedicine-2023-nicotine-post-covid-case-series.md]^[raw/articles/frontiers-2026-long-covid-candidate-treatments-review.md]

This branch should be read as experimental and risky in nicotine-naive users. Reddit reports are split: some users report brain-fog/fatigue/focus improvement, while others report no effect, worse brain fog, PEM-like sickness, nausea, and dependence without recovery.^[raw/articles/reddit-2024-nicotine-patches-longcovid-brainfog-thread.md]

## Safety And Label Layer

- OTC nicotine gum is a smoking-cessation aid, not a cognition product. The label warns that nicotine can increase heart rate and blood pressure and advises caution in cardiovascular disease, recent heart attack, irregular heartbeat, uncontrolled hypertension, stomach ulcer, diabetes, and seizure history.^[raw/articles/dailymed-nicotine-gum-label-2026.md]
- Transdermal and oral nicotine delivery are not equivalent for onset, exposure, misuse risk, or cognitive interpretation.
- The main practical risk is not just acute adverse effects; it is dependence, tolerance, withdrawal cycling, and sleep disruption.
- In post-viral self-experiment contexts, add PEM/overexertion risk: feeling temporarily more energetic can cause activity overshoot without true recovery.

## June 2026 Attribution Gate

Treat nicotine reports as high-salience but attribution-fragile. Before comparing a focus, motivation, or brain-fog report with stronger peptide/nootropic monitoring nodes such as [[bromantane]], [[semax]], [[selank]], or [[cerebrolysin]], capture route and product type (gum, patch, lozenge, pouch, tablet, cigarette, vape, or other), nicotine-naive/current/former status, abstinence or withdrawal interval, baseline dependence/tolerance, dose, timing, sleep/circadian state, meal state, heart-rate and blood-pressure context, anxiety state, cardiovascular or seizure history, and post-viral PEM/overexertion risk.

Stack context is mandatory: caffeine, prescription stimulants, wake-promoters, racetams, choline donors, [[galantamine]] or other AChE inhibitors, and peptide/nootropic stacks can all make nicotine look cleaner than it is. Route future reports through [[molecule-triage-worksheet-for-online-scans]], [[stack-attribution-diary-for-self-study-and-n-of-1-protocols]], [[30-day-self-study-template-for-nonprescription-interventions]], [[30-day-self-study-workbook-schema]], [[self-experimentation-stop-rules]], [[safety-interaction-matrix-for-multi-modal-stacks]], and [[active-comparator-and-caffeine-scheduling-confound-audit]]. Require washout, dechallenge/rechallenge where practical, adverse events, and objective attention or work-output endpoints before any ranking change.
