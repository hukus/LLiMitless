# Nicotine

Nicotine is a high-salience cholinergic comparator with a real acute attention signal and a large addiction/safety burden. The wiki should treat it as a state-restoration and risk comparator, not as a clean nootropic. The hardest interpretive problem is separating direct cognition from withdrawal reversal, craving relief, arousal, and expectancy.

## Medicinal Signal

- Formal signal: `moderate` for acute attention and state restoration; `low` for broad enhancement.
- The cleanest current branch is an exploratory randomized crossover study in smokers after abstinence, which makes nicotine relevant to withdrawal and state restoration rather than rested healthy-adult enhancement.^[raw/articles/pubmed-38582919-nicotine-cognitive-function-smokers-abstinence.md]
- A nicotine + galantamine healthy-never-smoker study and varenicline literature keep the broader nicotinic/cholinergic branch alive, but they do not remove addiction and cardiovascular constraints.^[raw/articles/pubmed-31686175-nicotine-galantamine-healthy-never-smokers-cognition.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-21942262-varenicline-bupropion-nicotine-deprived-smokers-cognition.md]
- The 2026 nicotine-tablet attention study is directly relevant, but it remains a 40-participant sponsor-run active-not-recruiting record with no posted results in the captured update.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct07408180-nicotine-tablet-attention-2026-04-30.md]

## Safety And Label Layer

- OTC nicotine gum is a smoking-cessation aid, not a cognition product. The label warns that nicotine can increase heart rate and blood pressure and advises caution in cardiovascular disease, recent heart attack, irregular heartbeat, uncontrolled hypertension, stomach ulcer, diabetes, and seizure history.^[raw/articles/dailymed-nicotine-gum-label-2026.md]
- Transdermal and oral nicotine delivery are not equivalent for onset, exposure, misuse risk, or cognitive interpretation.
- The main practical risk is not just acute adverse effects; it is dependence, tolerance, withdrawal cycling, and sleep disruption.
