# LM11A-31

## Bottom Line

LM11A-31 is a p75NTR modulator with a clean target-engagement story but no demonstrated cognitive benefit in the captured AD trial. Medicinal signal is `low`: biomarker and imaging domains moved in a phase 2a trial, while cognitive tests did not. Anecdotal signal is absent in the current source corpus.

## What It Is

LM11A-31 is an orally bioavailable small-molecule ligand for the p75 neurotrophin receptor. It is meant to downshift degenerative p75NTR signaling and support trophic/synaptic processes in neurodegenerative disease. It belongs in the [[synaptic-rescue-small-molecule-2026-scan]] as a biomarker-positive but cognition-negative translation example.

## Medicinal Research Signal

The main human anchor is a 26-week multicenter randomized, placebo-controlled phase 2a AD trial in 242 people with mild-to-moderate Alzheimer's disease. Participants received placebo, 200 mg LM11A-31, or 400 mg LM11A-31 twice daily. The trial met its primary safety/tolerability endpoint and showed significant drug-placebo differences in prespecified biomarker and imaging domains including structural MRI, FDG-PET, and CSF biomarkers. It did not show a significant cognitive benefit on active treatment.^[raw/articles/pubmed-38760589-lm11a31-phase-2a-ad.md]

That pattern makes LM11A-31 useful for the wiki's target-engagement discipline: a plausible synaptic-degeneration mechanism and biomarker movement cannot be treated as cognition efficacy unless behavior moves too.

## Safety And Status

The phase 2a trial met the safety/tolerability endpoint, but the 400 mg group had more GI-related adverse events and discontinuations. One gastrointestinal bleeding serious adverse event was considered possibly related; MRI did not raise a new safety concern in the captured note.^[raw/articles/pubmed-38760589-lm11a31-phase-2a-ad.md]

## Signal Judgment

Medicinal signal: `low`. LM11A-31 is a strong example of "biology moved, cognition did not."

Anecdotal signal: `absent-to-weak`.

Practical route: target-engagement reference and translational-gap exemplar, not a validated enhancer.
