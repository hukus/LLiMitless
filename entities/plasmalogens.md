# Plasmalogens

Plasmalogens are ether phospholipids enriched in neuronal membranes, usually discussed here as phosphatidylethanolamine plasmalogens, scallop-derived purified plasmalogen, or DHA-containing plasmalogen precursor oils. This page covers plasmalogens as a cognition-relevant lipid-membrane and biomarker-replacement branch, not ordinary [[dha-epa]] fish oil, krill oil, phosphatidylserine, phosphatidylcholine, lecithin, [[mfgm-phospholipids]], [[uridine]] stacks, or generic omega-3 supplements.

Medicinal cognition signal: `low/product-specific and phenotype-bound`. The main controlled clinical anchor remains the multicenter 24-week Japanese MCI/mild-AD trial of 1 mg/day scallop-derived purified plasmalogen: the primary MMSE-J endpoint did not clearly separate in the intention-to-treat analysis, while subgroup/secondary memory signals were more favorable in mild AD, women, and younger participants. A separate 40-person athlete RCT used 2 mg/day scallop-derived purified plasmalogen for four weeks and reported secondary mood, fatigue, sleep, and Uchida-Kraepelin concentration signals, but the primary mood endpoint was only marginal. These results justify tracking, not ranking as a broad enhancer.^[raw/articles/pmc-5360580-plasmalogen-mci-mild-ad-rct.md]^[raw/articles/frontiers-2022-plasmalogen-athletes-mood-concentration-rct.md]

Precursor and biomarker signal: `contested/low`. The 22-person DHA-AAG plasmalogen-precursor study reported biomarker movement and mixed cognition/mobility changes, but it was small, uncontrolled for clinical efficacy, industry-linked, and now carries a 2026 Expression of Concern about compliance with local legislation. Lipidomics and APOE4 work strengthen the plausibility that plasmalogen deficiency is part of AD-risk biology, but deficiency plausibility is not intervention proof.^[raw/articles/frontiers-2022-dha-aag-plasmalogen-cognition-mobility-trial.md]^[raw/articles/frontiers-2026-expression-concern-targeted-plasmalogen.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-38667317-plasma-ev-lipidomics-ad.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41768790-plasmalogen-deficiency-apoe4.md]

Product-identity signal: `high burden`. The human trial used purified scallop-derived plasmalogen, while the ProdromeNeuro registry describes algae-derived omega-3 oil with alkylglycerol/fatty-acid plasmalogen precursor framing. These are related lipid-replacement hypotheses, not the same exposure. Commercial labels need source species, active lipid class, precursor-vs-finished plasmalogen status, dose, shellfish/allergy handling, lot chemistry, and contamination/oxidation controls before trial results can be transported.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct04484454-prodromeneuro-plasmalogen-precursor-cognitive-decline.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-30992016-plasmalogen-ad-review.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-35970332-plasmalogen-brain-cognitive-functions-review.md]

Anecdote signal: `weak product-specific symptom salience`. The strongest direct anecdote found in this pass is an ME/CFS forum N=1 report claiming weaker brain fog and better mental acuity with ProdromeGlia, loss of benefit after switching products, and recurrence after switching back. A Reddit /r/Nootropics thread mainly shows study-sharing interest, not personal replication. Anecdote here should prioritize product-identity questions, not efficacy confidence.^[raw/articles/phoenixrising-2023-plasmalogen-experience-thread.md]^[raw/articles/reddit-2022-plasmalogen-study-sharing-thread.md]

## Identity Gates

- Separate purified scallop-derived plasmalogen from DHA-AAG precursor oils, alkylglycerol oils, algae omega-3 oils, krill/fish oil, phosphatidylserine, phosphatidylcholine, and ordinary lipid blends.
- Track plasmalogen class, source species, milligrams of active plasmalogen or precursor, fatty-acid composition, oxidation control, shellfish allergy risk, dose duration, baseline lipidomics, APOE4 status, MCI/AD phenotype, and whether outcomes are mood, sleep, concentration, memory, or disease progression.
- Treat cognitive-impairment, athlete psychobehavioral, and ME/CFS brain-fog contexts separately.
- Do not use the DHA-AAG study as a strong efficacy anchor while the 2026 Expression of Concern remains unresolved.

## Practical Ranking

Plasmalogens deserve standalone coverage because they combine a real lipid-biology hypothesis, at least one controlled human cognition-adjacent trial, commercial precursor products, and direct brain-fog anecdotes. The current route is `biomarker-backed lipid-membrane replacement / product-identity watch`, not a healthy-adult nootropic winner.
