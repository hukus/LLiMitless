# Lacticaseibacillus Rhamnosus HN001

Lacticaseibacillus rhamnosus HN001 is a probiotic strain with a 2025 cognition-adjacent older-adult trial where microencapsulation was part of the exposure question. This page keeps HN001 separate from [[lacticaseibacillus-rhamnosus-gg]], [[lacticaseibacillus-rhamnosus-cbt-lr5]], and generic probiotic products.

Medicinal signal: `low / processing-speed and connectivity only`. A 2025 randomized placebo-controlled trial in 87 older adults compared microencapsulated versus non-encapsulated L. rhamnosus HN001. The notable signal was processing speed and resting-state connectivity; memory, mood, sleep, depression, perceived stress, and most other cognitive outcomes were not clearly changed.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40976401-microencapsulation-probiotics-brain-structure-function.md]

Interpretation: this is a delivery-form and endpoint-sensitivity page as much as a strain page. If a trial changes connectivity or processing speed but not memory or sleep, it belongs in a narrow biomarker/throughput bucket. The broader older-adult probiotic evidence is still heterogeneous and low-certainty, so HN001 does not support a broad probiotic nootropic claim.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40483001-probiotics-cognitive-performance-older-adults-meta-analysis.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41291638-probiotic-supplements-cognitive-function-meta-analysis.md]

Product and protocol gates:

- Strain identity: HN001 must be explicit.
- Delivery identity: microencapsulated and non-encapsulated products are not interchangeable.
- Endpoint: processing speed and connectivity, not global cognition.
- Population: older adults; no healthy young adult or brain-fog recovery proof.
- Replication need: independent trial with prespecified cognition endpoints and clear encapsulation QA.
- Safety screen: same probiotic precautions as other live-strain products.

Anecdote layer: no HN001-specific cognition anecdote source is currently captured.
