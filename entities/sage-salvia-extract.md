# Sage / Salvia Extract

Sage / Salvia extract is the wiki's product-specific branch for cognition studies using common sage (`Salvia officinalis`), Spanish sage (`Salvia lavandulaefolia`), their standardized extracts, essential-oil / terpenoid fractions, and branded products such as Cognivia and Sibelius Sage. This page does not cover Salvia divinorum, and it does not treat culinary sage tea, dried leaf, essential oil, branded blends, and standardized polyphenol / terpenoid extracts as interchangeable exposures.

Medicinal cognition signal: `low-moderate/product-specific and cholinergic-state-sensitive`. Sage crosses the standalone threshold because there are multiple small placebo-controlled human studies, a product-specific acute/chronic Cognivia RCT, older-adult crossover data, stressor-battery data, a Spanish-sage essential-oil trial, a fatigue-state athlete trial, and a systematic review that keeps Salvia visible. The useful pattern is not a broad nootropic claim: signals cluster around secondary memory, attention accuracy, working memory, task performance under stress or fatigue, and selected acute endpoints. Product identity, dose response, small samples, sensory / reflux blinding leakage, sponsor involvement, and heterogeneous preparations prevent a higher confidence rating.^[raw/articles/pubmed-33466627-cognivia-sage-extract-acute-chronic-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-18350281-sage-memory-attention-older-volunteers-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-16205785-sage-stressor-battery-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-20937617-salvia-lavandulaefolia-monoterpenoid-cognition-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-34926550-salvia-athletes-fatigue-cognition-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pmc-6493168-salvia-cognition-systematic-review.md]

The mechanism story is plausibly cholinergic but still product-bound. The older Salvia officinalis and Salvia lavandulaefolia studies explicitly involved cholinesterase-inhibiting preparations, and Cognivia combines a Salvia officinalis polyphenol-rich extract with Salvia lavandulaefolia terpenoid essential oil. That makes this branch adjacent to [[huperzine-a]] and the broader [[cholinergic-and-nicotinic-agents-2026-scan]], but the clinical strength is far weaker than a prescription or impairment-context cholinergic comparator. Product chemistry is the transportable unit, not the herb name.^[raw/articles/pubmed-18350281-sage-memory-attention-older-volunteers-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-20937617-salvia-lavandulaefolia-monoterpenoid-cognition-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-33466627-cognivia-sage-extract-acute-chronic-rct.md]

Anecdote signal: `weak-moderate salience, weak efficacy`. The captured /r/Nootropics thread shows visible interest in Spanish sage cognition papers and repeated nomenclature correction, especially confusion with Salvia divinorum. It is more useful as a product-identity and expectation-risk signal than as evidence that users reliably experience cognitive benefit.^[raw/articles/reddit-2020-salvia-lavandulaefolia-memory-thread.md]

Safety and product-quality signal: `moderate caution`. NCCIH distinguishes common sage and Spanish sage from the broader Salvia genus, says the cognition evidence is small, and flags thujone toxicity risk for high doses or long-term use of some sage species. The Cognivia trial also reported more possibly related adverse events in the sage group, especially headache / migraine reports, with two severe headache reports despite resolution during the study. Practical use therefore needs species identity, extract type, dose, duration, thujone exposure, cholinergic sensitivity, headache liability, and medication-interaction context before ranking.^[raw/articles/nccih-2025-sage-usefulness-safety.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-33466627-cognivia-sage-extract-acute-chronic-rct.md]

## Signal Split

| Layer | Signal | Readout |
|---|---|---|
| Cognivia acute/chronic RCT | `low-moderate/product-specific` | 600 mg/day Salvia officinalis plus Salvia lavandulaefolia product improved selected working-memory and recall endpoints; blinding and AE caveats matter |
| Older-adult acute crossover | `low/product-specific` | 333 mg Salvia officinalis extract gave the clearest secondary-memory and attention-accuracy signal among tested doses |
| Healthy-adult stressor battery | `low/state-dependent` | 600 mg dried Salvia officinalis leaf improved alertness/calm/contentedness and task performance; 300 mg reduced anxiety but also reduced alertness during performance |
| Spanish-sage essential oil | `low/product-specific` | 50 microliters Salvia lavandulaefolia essential oil improved secondary memory / attention and shifted fatigue / alertness |
| Athlete fatigue-state trial | `low/state-bound` | Acute Cognivia reduced perceived exertion and improved simple reaction time / backward digit span in a small final sample |
| Systematic review context | `low/supportive` | Review found beneficial effects across heterogeneous Salvia studies but emphasized product-detail and trial-quality limitations |
| Preprint / sponsor-linked product | `supportive/low-weight` | Sibelius Sage preprint reported an acute young-adult word-recall signal; sponsor involvement and preprint status cap confidence |
| Anecdote | `weak-moderate salience, weak efficacy` | Community interest and Salvia-nomenclature confusion are visible; direct self-experiment benefit evidence is weak |
| Safety / quality | `moderate caution` | Thujone, species identity, extract chemistry, duration, headaches, sensory leakage, and cholinergic interaction risk are gating variables |

## Practical Interpretation

Sage / Salvia extract is entity-worthy but should stay below stronger benchmarks in [[molecule-intake-triage-table]]. The most defensible interpretation is a small, acute or short-duration, product-specific cholinergic botanical signal with possible use under stress, fatigue, older-adult memory challenge, or low-arousal task states. It is not a general healthy-adult enhancer until larger, independently replicated trials separate species, extract chemistry, thujone exposure, blinding integrity, and endpoint domains.

Future updates should keep at least five lanes separate: Salvia officinalis extract, Salvia lavandulaefolia essential oil, Cognivia, Sibelius Sage, and culinary sage / tea. Do not merge this page with [[bacopa]], [[ginkgo]], [[saffron]], [[cocoa-flavanols]], or [[blueberry-anthocyanins]] just because all are botanicals; the product chemistry and likely mechanism differ.
