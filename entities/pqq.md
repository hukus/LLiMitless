# Pyrroloquinoline Quinone (PQQ)

Pyrroloquinoline quinone (PQQ) is a redox-active quinone marketed as BioPQQ and other supplement forms for mitochondrial support, energy, and cognitive aging. For this wiki it belongs in the product-specific redox / mitochondrial-biogenesis lane of [[redox-mitochondrial-cofactor-cognition-watchlist]], not in the broad healthy nootropic tier.

Medicinal cognition signal: `low/product-specific`. The clearest direct cognition source is a 2016 randomized placebo-controlled double-blind BioPQQ study in 41 healthy older adults using 20 mg/day for 12 weeks. The abstract reports Stroop interference and lower-baseline visual-spatial signals, but the sample is small, the population is older, and one result is stratified by baseline performance.^[raw/articles/pubmed-26782228-pqq-cognition-rct.md]

MCI / combination-product signal: `low attribution-limited`. A 2024 double-blind RCT in 34 older adults with MCI tested a commercial dihydrogen-producing minerals plus PQQ mixture for 6 weeks, with BDNF as primary endpoint and cognition as secondary. This keeps PQQ visible in the MCI / mitochondrial-product lane, but it cannot isolate PQQ from the hydrogen/mineral product.^[raw/articles/pmc-12878601-dihydrogen-pqq-mci-rct.md]

Safety / product-identity signal: `moderate for BioPQQ conditions`. EFSA concluded BioPQQ was safe for healthy adults, excluding pregnant and lactating women, at 20 mg/day under intended use conditions. The same opinion noted limited human ADME data and that human studies were not comprehensive renal-function assessments, so safety transportability should stay product- and dose-specific.^[raw/articles/efsa-2017-pqq-novel-food-safety.md]

Community signal: `weak-moderate`. The captured Reddit thread is mainly research-sharing around the cognition trial, with some timing/insomnia and bioavailability discussion. It is visibility evidence, not a lived-experience efficacy corpus.^[raw/articles/reddit-2021-pqq-cognition-rct-thread.md]

## Practical Interpretation

PQQ should be routed through [[molecule-intake-triage-table]] as a `low-confidence product-specific mitochondrial cofactor`. It has a better direct cognition anchor than many supplement cofactors, but the evidence is still too small, product-tied, and endpoint-fragile for broad ranking. Keep BioPQQ, dihydrogen-PQQ mixtures, dose, age, baseline cognition, sleep timing, and formulation separate before scoring.
