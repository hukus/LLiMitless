# Quinolinic Acid

Quinolinic acid is the kynurenine-pathway metabolite most often invoked in neurotoxic / NMDA-overactivation narratives. The current wiki should treat it as a contested biomarker and mechanism node, not as a simple dementia severity gauge.

Medicinal signal: `low / contested biomarker`. The 2025 cognitive-impairment and dementia meta-analysis specifically weakened the simple "more quinolinic acid equals more dementia cognition loss" model: AD dementia pooled lower CSF quinolinic acid rather than a uniform increase, and cognitive correlations were mostly inconclusive. The 2023 AD meta-analysis also found no consistent quinolinic-acid change.^[raw/articles/springer-2025-kynurenine-pathway-dysregulation-cognitive-impairment-dementia.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-36754068-kynurenine-pathway-alzheimers-meta-analysis.md]

The post-stroke cohort keeps quinolinic acid relevant only as part of ratios and domain-specific associations. The kynurenic-acid / quinolinic-acid ratio tracked episodic memory, but plasma kynurenines did not predict global post-stroke cognitive impairment odds.^[raw/articles/pubmed-37852105-kynurenine-pathway-cognitive-function-stroke.md]

The schizophrenia biomarker story is still worth tracking. A 2021 Scientific Reports study found that plasma quinolinic acid correlated negatively with composite cognition in schizophrenia but not major depressive disorder, after covariate adjustment. That is human disease-biomarker support, not causal proof or an intervention signal.^[raw/articles/pmc-8113521-quinolinic-acid-cognitive-deficits-schizophrenia.md]

## Interpretation

Quinolinic acid is useful for falsifying oversimplified neuroinflammation stories. If a page claims that kynurenine-pathway activation always causes cognitive decline through high quinolinic acid, it should be routed back here and to [[kynurenine-tryptophan-immunometabolic-cognition-2026-scan]]. [[gsk3335065-kmo-inhibitor]] and other KMO programs may affect the upstream branch, but current human evidence is target-engagement and safety-first rather than cognition-positive.^[raw/articles/pubmed-26589832-kynurenine-3-monooxygenase-review-structure-mechanism-inhibitors.md]
