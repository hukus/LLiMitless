# Pregnenolone

## Bottom Line

Pregnenolone is a neurosteroid precursor with a weak clinical-state signal that does not currently translate into direct cognition efficacy. Medicinal signal is `low`: in a controlled recent-onset schizophrenia trial, pregnenolone did not significantly improve the main MCCB cognitive composite, though functional capacity improved. Anecdotal cognition signal is `weak` in the current captured corpus.

## What It Is

Pregnenolone is a steroid precursor and neurosteroid-adjacent supplement candidate. In this wiki it belongs with [[neurosteroid-and-endocrine-state-cognition-2026-scan]], [[dhea]], [[allopregnanolone]], and [[negative-evidence-watchlist-for-cognition-interventions]] as a functional/symptom-context lead rather than a validated enhancer.

## Medicinal Research Signal

The current captured human anchor randomized 60 people with recent-onset schizophrenia or schizoaffective disorder to pregnenolone 50 mg/day or placebo for 8 weeks, added to antipsychotics. The primary MCCB cognitive composite did not significantly improve, while UPSA-B functional capacity improved. That keeps pregnenolone visible for clinical-state and symptom/function hypotheses, but not as a direct cognition-positive molecule.^[raw/articles/pubmed-24496044-pregnenolone-schizophrenia-cognition.md]

## Anecdote Signal

No pregnenolone-specific cognition anecdote raw note is currently indexed. Supplement discourse often blends mood, stress, sleep, hormone balance, libido, and motivation claims, which are not interchangeable with objective cognitive improvement.

## Safety And Status

Pregnenolone should be interpreted through endocrine-state, psychiatric-state, and product-identity gates. Functional-capacity movement in schizophrenia does not justify generalized self-experiment cognition claims.

## Signal Judgment

Medicinal signal: `low` for direct cognition; symptom/function hypothesis only.

Anecdotal signal: `weak` in the current captured corpus.

Practical route: clinical-state research comparator; require direct cognition replication before ranking above low.
