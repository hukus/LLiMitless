# Spearmint Extract / Neumentix

Spearmint extract here means a proprietary high-polyphenol Mentha spicata extract, often surfaced as Neumentix, not spearmint tea, peppermint oil, or rosmarinic acid in isolation. The current evidence is product-specific and attention/working-memory leaning.

## Signal Snapshot

| Evidence layer | Direction | Signal strength | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Age-associated memory impairment | positive but product-specific | `low-moderate` | A 90-person RCT found 900 mg/day improved quality of working memory and spatial working-memory accuracy over 90 days.^[raw/articles/pubmed-29314866-spearmint-aami-working-memory-rct.md] |
| Healthy active adults | attention-positive | `low` | A 142-person healthy-adult trial reported sustained-attention advantages at days 30 and 90 and a complex-attention signal at day 7.^[raw/articles/pubmed-30802720-spearmint-healthy-adults-attention-rct.md] |
| Psychomotor attention | narrow positive | `low` | A separate 142-person reactive-agility trial found specific Makoto footplate hit/reaction-time advantages, but several tests were null.^[raw/articles/springer-2018-spearmint-reactive-agility-rct.md] |
| Anecdote | clearheaded/focus reports | `weak-moderate` | A small NootropicsDepot thread reports clearheadedness, focus, working memory, and social-anxiety-mediated cognition benefits.^[raw/articles/reddit-2022-neumentix-spearmint-cognition-thread.md] |
| Safety | favorable short-term trial exposure | `low-moderate caution` | Trial blood safety panels were reassuring, but product identity, long-term use, pregnancy, and supplement QA remain practical gates.^[raw/articles/springer-2018-spearmint-reactive-agility-rct.md] |

## Medicinal Evidence

The older-adult / memory-complaint anchor is a randomized placebo-controlled 90-day trial in age-associated memory impairment. The 900 mg/day dose improved quality of working memory by 15% and spatial working-memory accuracy by 9% versus placebo, while 900 mg/day also improved self-reported ability to fall asleep. This supports a real product-specific signal, but it does not prove that generic spearmint preparations improve cognition.^[raw/articles/pubmed-29314866-spearmint-aami-working-memory-rct.md]

The cleaner healthy-adult cognition bridge is the Nutrition Research trial in recreationally active adults aged 18-50. Chronic 900 mg/day supplementation improved sustained attention at day 30 and day 90, with a complex-attention advantage at day 7 and no clear mood, sleep, or quality-of-life effects. That makes the signal more attention-specific than globally nootropic.^[raw/articles/pubmed-30802720-spearmint-healthy-adults-attention-rct.md]

The reactive-agility trial is relevant because it tests cognition under active choice-response conditions. Spearmint improved selected Makoto Arena footplate hit and reaction-time outcomes, but not all tests. The best interpretation is psychomotor attention / response selection, not broad memory or intelligence improvement.^[raw/articles/springer-2018-spearmint-reactive-agility-rct.md]

## Product And Safety Gates

The product gate is strict: the captured trials used proprietary high-polyphenol spearmint extract around 900 mg/day. Do not transfer this to culinary mint, peppermint oil, lemon balm, sage, or generic rosmarinic acid just because they share some constituents.

The short-term safety read is favorable in the captured trials, but that is not the same as long-term general-use proof. For supplement triage, require product identity, contaminant testing, pregnancy/lactation caution, and separation from sedating or stimulant stack effects.

## Practical Interpretation

[[spearmint-extract]] is one of the cleaner missing Lamiaceae entries because it has both older-adult working-memory and healthy-adult attention data. It stays low-confidence because the effects are domain-specific, product-specific, and not yet independently broad.
