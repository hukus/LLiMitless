# Eleutherococcus Senticosus

Eleutherococcus senticosus, also called Eleuthero or Siberian ginseng, is not Panax ginseng. It enters the wiki through adaptogen formulas and one small healthy-adult Eleuthero-plus-Drynaria cognition trial, not through strong Eleuthero-alone enhancement evidence.

## Bottom Line

Medicinal cognition signal: `low/formula-limited`. A 2020 randomized double-blind study in 31 healthy adults found an RBANS figure-recall advantage after 12 weeks of Eleutherococcus senticosus leaf extract plus Drynaria fortunei rhizome extract. ADAPT-232 also contains Eleuthero and had an acute stressed-state cognition signal. Neither source proves Eleuthero alone improves cognition.^[raw/articles/mdpi-2020-eleuthero-drynaria-healthy-adults-cognition-rct.md]^[raw/articles/sciencedirect-2010-adapt232-stressed-healthy-adults-cognition-rct.md]

Human exposure signal: `weak`. An elderly quality-of-life trial reported a social-functioning benefit at 4 weeks that did not persist at 8 weeks, with no adverse events reported, but it was not a cognition trial.^[raw/articles/pubmed-15207399-eleuthero-elderly-qol-rct.md]

## Evidence Layers

The Eleuthero-plus-Drynaria trial used 203 mg/day Eleuthero leaf extract plus 20 mg/day Drynaria extract. The cognition result was narrow: figure recall reached between-group significance, while several other signals were within-group only. The right interpretation is product-specific, not generic Siberian-ginseng proof.^[raw/articles/mdpi-2020-eleuthero-drynaria-healthy-adults-cognition-rct.md]

The ADAPT-232 trial adds stress-state relevance, but the formula also includes [[rhodiola-rosea]] and [[schisandra-chinensis]]. It should be cited as an adaptogen formula result, not Eleuthero monotherapy.^[raw/articles/sciencedirect-2010-adapt232-stressed-healthy-adults-cognition-rct.md]

## Safety And Status

MSKCC warns not to confuse Siberian ginseng with Asian ginseng, American ginseng, or Panax notoginseng. It also flags digoxin interaction/assay concerns, possible CYP450 substrate issues, and insufficient breastfeeding safety data.^[raw/articles/mskcc-2026-siberian-ginseng-safety.md]

## Signal Judgment

Eleuthero is worth standalone coverage because it is a common adaptogen label and appears in multiple cognition-relevant formulas. The branch stays low-confidence until Eleuthero-alone products have controlled cognitive endpoints, reproducible extract chemistry, and better safety interaction data.
