# Ladostigil

Ladostigil is an investigational propargylamine-style neurodegeneration candidate combining MAO-B / cholinesterase-adjacent logic. In this wiki it is a negative-to-mixed mild-cognitive-impairment comparator for [[mao-b-propargylamine-cognition-watchlist]], not a healthy-adult nootropic lead.

## Current Read

Medicinal cognition signal: `low/negative with biomarker hint`. A 3-year randomized phase 2 trial tested ladostigil 10 mg/day in 210 MCI patients with medial temporal lobe atrophy. It did not significantly delay progression to Alzheimer's disease and did not significantly improve the Neuropsychological Test Battery composite, Disability Assessment for Dementia, or Geriatric Depression Scale.^[raw/articles/pubmed-31492718-ladostigil-mci-phase2.md]

The same trial reported less whole-brain and hippocampal volume loss in the ladostigil arm, with p = 0.025 and p = 0.043, and similar serious-adverse-event counts versus placebo. That keeps ladostigil as a translational clue, but not an efficacy win.^[raw/articles/pubmed-31492718-ladostigil-mci-phase2.md]

## Safety And Interpretation

The trial-level safety read was tolerable, but the core cognition result was not positive. For the wiki, this is exactly the kind of molecule that should prevent mechanism enthusiasm from outrunning endpoint reality.

## Current Route

Route ladostigil with [[sembragiline]] and [[kds2010-tisolagiline]] as a disease-program comparator inside [[mao-b-propargylamine-cognition-watchlist]]. It also belongs near [[negative-evidence-watchlist-for-cognition-interventions]] because structural or biomarker hints did not translate into a clear cognitive endpoint.
