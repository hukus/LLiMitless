# CJC-1295

## Bottom Line

CJC-1295 is a GH/IGF-1 target-engagement peptide, not a cognition-proven molecule. Medicinal signal for cognition is `physiology-only`: randomized healthy-adult studies show sustained GH and IGF-1 increases, but no cognition endpoint. Anecdotal signal is `strong` for stack-confounded sleep/recovery/body-composition claims with [[ipamorelin]], but `weak` for objective cognition.

## What It Is

CJC-1295 is a long-acting GHRH analog label in the GH-axis peptide ecosystem. Community sources often blur CJC-1295, "CJC-1295 no DAC," Mod GRF 1-29, [[ipamorelin]], and blended vials. In this wiki it belongs with [[growth-hormone-secretagogue-cognition-2026-scan]], [[ghrh]], [[tesamorelin]], and [[peptide-safety-and-sourcing-risk-map]] as a target-engagement and sourcing-risk node.

## Medicinal Research Signal

The captured formal source is a pair of randomized, placebo-controlled, double-blind ascending-dose studies in healthy adults. CJC-1295 produced sustained, dose-dependent GH and IGF-1 increases lasting days to weeks. That supports somatotropic target engagement and short-term safety/tolerability under study conditions, but it does not test cognition.^[raw/articles/pubmed-16352683-cjc1295-gh-igf1-healthy-adults.md]

No CJC-1295 human cognition trial is currently indexed. Its cognition relevance is therefore indirect: sleep, recovery, body composition, and endocrine state can change subjective performance, but the molecule has not shown objective cognitive benefit in the captured corpus.

## Anecdote Signal

The community signal is visible but mostly stack-confounded. A high-salience Reddit guide for CJC-1295 "no DAC" plus ipamorelin had visible score +313 at capture and centered on recovery, deeper sleep/REM, leaner body composition, and collagen/skin repair. Replies discussed dosing, cycling, fasted timing, separate vials, and adverse effects such as flushing, itching, lethargy, and elevated heart rate. This is `strong` anecdote for sleep/recovery/body-comp claims, not cognition.^[raw/articles/reddit-bodyhackguide-cjc1295-no-dac-ipamorelin-guide-2025.md]

A smaller Reddit thread repeated sleep, recovery, muscle-preservation, and dieting-support claims around CJC-1295 plus ipamorelin, again without objective cognition measures. A blend thread adds a safety outlier: flushing, tachycardia, nausea, chest/throat tightness, syncope, and ER/ambulance use after a tesamorelin/CJC-1295/ipamorelin blend from a supplier.^[raw/articles/reddit-bodyhackguide-cjc1295-ipamorelin-2025.md]^[raw/articles/reddit-peptideguide-tesamorelin-cjc1295-mod-grf-129-ipamorelin-blend-2025.md]

## Safety And Status

CJC-1295 should be routed safety-first in nootropic contexts. Product identity is a major issue, especially when forum posts use CJC-1295, no-DAC CJC, Mod GRF 1-29, or blends interchangeably. GH/IGF-1 manipulation, glucose, edema, injection reactions, palpitations, and sourcing/sterility risk all matter before any cognition claim.

## Signal Judgment

Medicinal signal: `physiology-only` for cognition; GH/IGF-1 target engagement is real but not a cognitive endpoint.

Anecdotal signal: `strong` for sleep/recovery/body-composition stack claims; `weak` for cognition.

Practical route: source-quality and target-engagement comparator, not an enhancement recommendation.
