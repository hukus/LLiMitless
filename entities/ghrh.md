# GHRH

## Bottom Line

GHRH is the historical GH-axis cognition-positive anchor, but the signal is old, older-adult-biased, and state-restoration framed. Medicinal signal is `low-moderate` for healthy older adults and MCI/aging-axis research, based on a 2006 older-adult GHRH trial and a 2012 GHRH-analog / tesamorelin controlled trial. It is `low` for broad enhancement. Anecdotal cognition signal is `absent-to-weak` in the current corpus.

## What It Is

Growth hormone-releasing hormone is an endogenous hypothalamic peptide that drives pituitary growth-hormone release and downstream IGF-1 physiology. In this wiki it belongs with [[growth-hormone-secretagogue-cognition-2026-scan]], [[tesamorelin]], [[ibutamoren]], and [[peptide-safety-and-sourcing-risk-map]] as a somatotropic-state comparator rather than a general nootropic.

## Medicinal Research Signal

The 2006 randomized placebo-controlled trial in 89 healthy older adults is the cleanest molecule-axis cognition-positive source. Six months of daily GHRH improved performance IQ and several executive-function-style measures, including picture arrangement, finding A's, verbal sets, and single-dual task. This remains notable, but the trial is old and modest in size.^[raw/articles/pubmed-16399214-ghrh-healthy-older-adults.md]

The ClinicalTrials.gov sleep-disturbance record explains the original state-restoration frame: the older-adult GHRH program was tied to sleep disturbance, and the registry itself did not post results. That matters because sleep restoration, anabolic state, and cognition can move together without proving a clean enhancer effect.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct00000380-ghrh-age-related-sleep-disturbances.md]

The 2012 controlled trial used tesamorelin, a GHRH analog, in 152 adults aged 55-87, including 66 with MCI. Cognition favored treatment, executive function improved, verbal memory trended favorable, IGF-1 rose strongly, and adverse events were common but generally mild. This supports the GH-axis cognition hypothesis, while also reinforcing that the practical intervention is a drug analog with metabolic and safety baggage.^[raw/articles/pubmed-22869065-ghrh-mci-healthy-older-adults-controlled-trial.md]

## Anecdote Signal

No GHRH-specific cognition anecdote raw note is currently indexed. Peptide-community discussions more often involve [[cjc-1295]], [[ipamorelin]], [[tesamorelin]], or blends, with claims about sleep, recovery, lean mass, and subjective energy rather than objective cognition. Anecdotal cognition signal is therefore `absent-to-weak`.

## Safety And Status

The practical safety gate is somatotropic-axis manipulation rather than the word "peptide." IGF-1 elevation, glucose/insulin effects, fluid retention, joint symptoms, injection reactions, neoplasm-risk screening, and product identity all affect how a cognition claim should be interpreted.

## Signal Judgment

Medicinal signal: `low-moderate` for older-adult / MCI state-restoration research; `low` for healthy enhancement.

Anecdotal signal: `absent-to-weak`.

Practical route: historical positive comparator and class anchor; use [[tesamorelin]] and [[ibutamoren]] as modern molecule-specific counterweights.
