# Gastrodia Elata

Gastrodia elata Blume, also called Gastrodiae Rhizoma or Tianma, is a traditional East Asian medicinal rhizome now relevant to the wiki because a modern controlled cognition trial has appeared. It belongs beside [[standardized-herbal-extracts-for-cognition]], [[traditional-medicine-cognition-2026-scan]], [[ginkgo]], [[ginseng]], [[bacopa]], and [[polygala-bt11]].

## Bottom Line

Medicinal cognition signal: `low but direct`. A 2025 randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial tested a Gastrodia elata extract tablet in 100 adults aged 40-65 with normal or impaired cognition. After 12 weeks, the extract improved ADAS-cog total score more than placebo and also improved K-MMSE, BDNF, and total antioxidant status, with no serious adverse events reported.^[raw/articles/sciencedirect-2025-gastrodia-elata-cognition-rct.md]

Healthy-adult relevance: `low-moderate hypothesis`. The participant pool included middle-aged adults with normal or mildly impaired cognition rather than a clean high-performing healthy-adult sample. That makes the signal closer to early decline prevention / subtle impairment than strong enhancement.

Product-identity gate: high. The trial used a specific 930 mg tablet and confirmed gastrodin by HPLC; generic Tianma powder, culinary products, mixed formulas, and isolated gastrodin should not be treated as equivalent.

## Formal Evidence

The primary endpoint was ADAS-cog. The treatment group improved by -2.15 +/- 2.22 versus -0.92 +/- 2.03 in placebo. Secondary movement in K-MMSE, BDNF, and total antioxidant status supports a cognition-plus-biomarker hypothesis, while the lack of amyloid beta change keeps the mechanism modest. The paper says the product became a Korean health functional food in 2024.^[raw/articles/sciencedirect-2025-gastrodia-elata-cognition-rct.md]

## Safety And Status

The captured RCT reported no serious adverse events, but this is not enough for a broad safety claim. Practical safety interpretation still needs product identity, dose, contaminants, interactions, sedation/dizziness monitoring, and herb-drug review when used in multi-herb TCM formulas.

## Signal Judgment

Gastrodia now clears the wiki's standalone threshold because it has a current controlled cognition RCT with product chemistry. It stays low-confidence because replication, durability, independent sponsor context, dose transportability, and healthy-adult transfer are still unresolved.
