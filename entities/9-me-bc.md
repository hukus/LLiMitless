# 9-Me-BC

9-Me-BC is a preclinical dopaminergic / neurotrophic hypothesis node with moderate nootropic-community curiosity. It has no convincing human cognition evidence in the current wiki, and the safety story is too uncertain for anything stronger than watchlist status.

## Medicinal Signal

- Formal signal: `preclinical-only` for cognition.
- The current PubMed anchor is a cell / astrocyte / dopaminergic mechanism paper reporting MAO inhibition and increased neurotrophic-factor expression.^[raw/articles/pubmed-32285253-9mebc-astrocytes.md]
- That mechanism is the bridge behind forum claims about dopamine reset or neuroprotection, but it is not a human cognition result.
