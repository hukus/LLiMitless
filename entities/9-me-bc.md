# 9-Me-BC

9-Me-BC is a preclinical dopaminergic / neurotrophic hypothesis node with moderate nootropic-community curiosity. It has no convincing human cognition evidence in the current wiki, and the safety story is too uncertain for anything stronger than watchlist status.

June 2026 anecdote read: `weak-moderate dopamine-recovery lore, red-flagged for human-evidence gap`. 9-Me-BC is useful to track because the captured thread is specific about motivation, focus, confidence, vivid dreams, and dopamine-reset / addiction-recovery framing, but it is not a strong nootropic signal. The formal layer is still cell/preclinical mechanism and the safety layer is unusually prominent because phototoxicity/light-sensitivity, sleep, sourcing, and long-tail cycle concerns are part of the same anecdote source.^[raw/articles/pubmed-32285253-9mebc-astrocytes.md]^[raw/articles/reddit-9-me-bc-questions-experiences-2022.md]

## Medicinal Signal

- Formal signal: `preclinical-only` for cognition.
- The current PubMed anchor is a cell / astrocyte / dopaminergic mechanism paper reporting MAO inhibition and increased neurotrophic-factor expression.^[raw/articles/pubmed-32285253-9mebc-astrocytes.md]
- That mechanism is the bridge behind forum claims about dopamine reset or neuroprotection, but it is not a human cognition result.
