# L-Methionine

L-methionine is an essential sulfur-containing amino acid and direct upstream precursor to [[s-adenosyl-l-methionine]] / SAMe. It belongs beside [[homocysteine]], [[betaine-tmg]], [[folate-b-vitamins]], [[glycine]], [[dimethylglycine-dmg]], [[choline-donor-cognition-signal-map]], and [[creatine]] in the [[one-carbon-methyl-donor-cognition-watchlist]], but it should not inherit their evidence.

Medicinal cognition signal: `very low / not established`. No direct healthy-adult cognition-enhancement packet is captured. The formal human signal is mostly metabolic: acute methionine loading can move SAMe, SAH, methionine, folate, and [[homocysteine]] markers; supplemented free L-methionine tolerance work uses homocysteine as the primary safety readout rather than cognition as an efficacy endpoint.^[raw/articles/pubmed-8774264-methionine-loading-same-homocysteine.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-28637772-methionine-tolerance-healthy-older-adults.md]

The best interpretation is `SAMe precursor with homocysteine/SAH risk gates`. A 2020 clinical-context review frames supplemented methionine as a sulfur-amino-acid intervention tied to protein synthesis, methylation, remethylation, transsulfuration, cysteine, glutathione, and cardiovascular-risk biology. The 2023 amino-acid upper-limit review keeps the same safety emphasis: methionine tolerability is mainly a homocysteine and dose-boundary problem, not a cognition-positive finding. The separate [[homocysteine]] page now carries the SAH and high-homocysteine cognition-attribution context.^[raw/articles/pubmed-33000166-supplemented-methionine-clinical-context.md]^[raw/articles/pmc-2023-amino-acid-ul-methionine-review.md]^[raw/articles/pubchem-2026-homocysteine-identity.md]^[raw/articles/pubchem-2026-s-adenosylhomocysteine-identity.md]

Anecdote signal: `weak and mostly class-adjacent`. Reddit searches find much more direct signal for SAMe, TMG, methylfolate/B12, glycine, and "methylation" stacks than for L-methionine alone. L-methionine appears mainly as a SAMe precursor or sulfur/methylation lever, with mixed curiosity around mood/energy beside anxiety, overstimulation, sulfur-intolerance, and homocysteine concerns.^[raw/articles/reddit-2022-2026-l-methionine-same-methylation-threads.md]

Safety signal: `lab-gated caution`. The healthy-older-adult tolerance study set a NOAEL of 46.3 mg/kg/day and a LOAEL of 91 mg/kg/day on the basis of plasma homocysteine, with no meaningful questionnaire or wellbeing change at the boundary in only 15 people. That is a safety/tolerance anchor, not a practical target dose. Interpretation should account for folate, B12, B6, methylmalonic acid, homocysteine, kidney function, diet/protein intake, alcohol, rare homocystinuria/CBS issues, psychiatric vulnerability, and methyl-donor co-supplementation.^[raw/articles/pubmed-28637772-methionine-tolerance-healthy-older-adults.md]

## Practical Interpretation

L-methionine is worth tracking because it closes the actual SAMe-precursor gap. It is not a healthy-adult nootropic lead. The current route is `do not supplement blindly for cognition`: if a subjective brain-fog response appears, the interpretation should be status/lab, mood-state, sleep, protein intake, and cofactor gated before it is counted as a cognitive effect.

Any confidence upgrade would require controlled cognition endpoints, baseline and achieved homocysteine/SAMe/SAH data, folate/B12/B6/MMA stratification, diet and protein normalization, and explicit separation from SAMe, TMG, methylfolate, B12, choline, creatine, and glycine-family co-use.
