# Nattokinase

Nattokinase is a fibrinolytic enzyme from natto, the traditional Japanese fermented soybean food. In this wiki it is an anecdote-heavy long-COVID / microclot-adjacent supplement lead with in-vitro spike-protein evidence and substantial bleeding/interactions caveats.

## Bottom Line

Long-COVID brain-fog signal: `anecdotal, not clinically proven`. Captured Reddit reports include users describing improved alertness, executive function, brain fog, head pressure, or "coming back online" after nattokinase or natto-serra/lumbrokinase/aspirin stacks. This is self-selected, uncontrolled, and stack-confounded.^[raw/articles/reddit-2023-nattokinase-longcovid-brainfog-thread.md]

Formal COVID mechanism signal: `in-vitro only`. A 2022 Molecules study reported nattokinase degradation of SARS-CoV-2 spike protein in cell lysate/cell-surface experiments. This does not prove oral nattokinase treats COVID, long COVID, microclots, or brain fog.^[raw/articles/molecules-2022-nattokinase-spike-protein-in-vitro.md]

Safety gate: high. MSKCC warns about bleeding risk with blood-thinning drugs, daily aspirin, anticoagulant/antiplatelet/fibrinolytic use, coagulation disorders, deep-vein-thrombosis history, and serious case reports including mechanical-valve thrombosis after replacing warfarin, fatal internal bleeding, allergic reactions, and harm from injecting an oral enzyme product.^[raw/articles/mskcc-2026-nattokinase-safety.md]

## Signal Judgment

Nattokinase belongs in [[metabolic-microclot-long-covid-chemical-watchlist]] because the anecdote signal is too visible to ignore, but it should be safety-first and low-confidence. The practical boundary is clear: do not upgrade in-vitro spike degradation or forum "clot remover" stories into clinical efficacy.
