# AXA1125

AXA1125 is an endogenous metabolic modulator composition made from five amino acids plus [[n-acetylcysteine]]. It is relevant to the wiki because it has a double-blind fatigue-predominant long-COVID trial with cognitive-fatigue framing, but it is not an objective cognition enhancer.

## Bottom Line

Post-viral signal: `low, fatigue-state`. In a 41-person phase 2a pilot trial, AXA1125 did not improve the primary mitochondrial phosphocreatine recovery endpoint or six-minute walk distance versus placebo, but it did improve Chalder Fatigue Questionnaire score after four weeks. The study framed symptoms as physical and mental/cognitive fatigue, not as objective cognitive test improvement.^[raw/articles/sciencedirect-2023-axa1125-long-covid-fatigue-rct.md]

Product identity: high. AXA1125 is not equivalent to generic BCAA, arginine, glutamine, or NAC supplementation. It used a large fixed composition, 33.9 g twice daily, in a sponsored clinical-trial context.

Healthy-adult cognition signal: `not established`. This is a long-COVID fatigue candidate.

## Formal Evidence

The trial randomized adults in the UK to AXA1125 or placebo for four weeks with two-week follow-up. The primary 31P-MRS muscle bioenergetics endpoint missed; fatigue scores improved. Treatment-emergent adverse events were more common with AXA1125 than placebo, but no serious adverse events or treatment discontinuations were reported.^[raw/articles/sciencedirect-2023-axa1125-long-covid-fatigue-rct.md]

## Signal Judgment

AXA1125 is worth tracking because it is one of the few metabolic long-COVID candidates tested in a blinded trial. The correct summary is "fatigue signal despite primary mechanistic miss," not "mitochondrial repair proven" and not "brain fog treatment proven."
