# Piracetam

## Bottom Line

Piracetam is the historical racetam class anchor, but its medicinal cognition signal is `low/negative` in the captured review-level evidence. The Cochrane dementia / cognitive-impairment review found a global-impression hint but no specific cognitive-measure support in the limited published data and concluded that the evidence did not support clinical use. Anecdotal signal is `moderate` for legacy nootropic salience, but the current corpus does not give piracetam a clean molecule-specific denominator.

## What It Is

Piracetam is the prototypical racetam and a frequent comparator for [[aniracetam]], [[noopept]], [[phenylpiracetam]], [[pramiracetam]], [[nefiracetam]], and [[oxiracetam]]. In this wiki it belongs with [[racetam-long-tail-watchlist]] and [[racetam-comparison-2026-scan]] as a historical / negative comparator rather than a validated healthy-adult enhancer.

## Medicinal Research Signal

The formal anchor is the Cochrane review of piracetam for dementia or cognitive impairment. It included 24 randomized double-blind studies and 11,959 participants, but many trials were old, crossover first-phase data were often unavailable, and unpublished or untraceable manufacturer data raised publication-bias concerns. The pooled global-impression-of-change signal favored piracetam, but specific cognitive outcomes did not show significant differences in the limited usable data. The review concluded that published evidence did not support piracetam use for dementia or cognitive impairment.^[raw/articles/pmc-12016011-piracetam-dementia-cognitive-impairment-cochrane.md]

## Anecdote Signal

Piracetam has strong historical nootropic visibility, but the captured structured community sources do not provide a clean current piracetam-specific effect-size ledger. The broader racetam lane has persistent community salience; in the captured Troof and /r/Nootropics survey notes, the cleaner signals are more visible for phenylpiracetam and noopept-style branches than for piracetam itself.^[raw/articles/troof-nootropic-ratings-2022.md]^[raw/articles/darktka-2017-nootropics-survey-on-r-nootropics.md]

## Safety And Status

The main risk in this wiki is interpretive rather than a single captured toxicity signal: piracetam's reputation can cause users to import class-level anecdotes into a negative / insufficient dementia evidence base. Product identity and jurisdictional status should be checked before any practical use discussion. Route piracetam stack reports through [[safety-interaction-matrix-for-multi-modal-stacks]] when choline, caffeine, stimulant history, sleep, or task-practice effects could explain the perceived result.

## Signal Judgment

Medicinal signal: `low/negative` for dementia / cognitive impairment; no healthy-adult proof in the captured corpus.

Anecdotal signal: `moderate` historical salience, but weak molecule-specific evidence.

Practical route: historical class anchor and negative comparator.
