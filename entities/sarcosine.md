# Sarcosine

Sarcosine, or N-methylglycine, is an NMDA-coagonist / glycine-transporter-adjacent comparator in the glutamatergic state-restoration lane. Its current cognition signal is weak as monotherapy and stronger only as a combination clue with [[sodium-benzoate]].

## Bottom Line

Medicinal signal: `low / weak monotherapy, combination-specific hint`. A 2020 schizophrenia meta-analysis found symptom benefit but not a robust cognition effect; the pooled cognition estimate was positive but statistically insignificant. A separate sarcosine-plus-benzoate trial favored the combination over sarcosine alone, which argues for a double-hit or phenotype-specific hypothesis rather than a sarcosine nootropic claim.^[raw/articles/pubmed-32122256-sarcosine-schizophrenia-meta-analysis.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-26691576-sarcosine-plus-benzoate-schizophrenia-rct.md]

Anecdotal signal: `not captured`. The current wiki corpus has no structured sarcosine nootropic or patient-experience packet suitable for signal scoring.

Practical route: keep sarcosine as a mechanistic comparator and combination clue inside [[nmda-coagonist-glyt1-daao-cognition-2026-scan]], not as a standalone broad enhancer.

## Human Evidence

The 2020 meta-analysis of double-blind randomized sarcosine trials in schizophrenia found overall symptom improvement, but cognition did not clear the bar. The pooled cognitive effect was SMD 0.27 with a 95% CI from -0.06 to 0.60 and p=0.10.^[raw/articles/pubmed-32122256-sarcosine-schizophrenia-meta-analysis.md]

The 12-week sarcosine-plus-benzoate trial compared sarcosine 2 g/day plus benzoate 1 g/day against sarcosine alone in chronic schizophrenia. The combination improved cognitive and global functioning, while sarcosine alone did not. This is important, but it points to a combination hypothesis rather than sarcosine monotherapy efficacy.^[raw/articles/pubmed-26691576-sarcosine-plus-benzoate-schizophrenia-rct.md]

## Safety And Sourcing Caveats

The captured sources describe sarcosine as generally tolerable in the schizophrenia literature, but the evidence base is small and consumer-source identity is not established by the clinical-trial corpus. Sarcosine should not borrow [[sodium-benzoate]]'s phenotype-sensitive aMCI signal.
