# Sarcosine

Sarcosine, or N-methylglycine, is a methylated-glycine molecule and an NMDA / glycine-transporter-adjacent comparator. It sits at a useful boundary: chemically it belongs near [[glycine]], [[dimethylglycine-dmg]], [[betaine-tmg]], and [[s-adenosyl-l-methionine]] in one-carbon/methylation discussions, while its clinical cognition literature belongs near [[sodium-benzoate]], [[d-serine]], [[luvadaxistat]], and GlyT1 / DAAO state-restoration hypotheses.^[raw/articles/pubchem-2026-sarcosine-identity.md]

## Bottom Line

Medicinal signal: `low / weak monotherapy, combination-specific hint`. A 2020 schizophrenia meta-analysis found symptom benefit but not a robust cognition effect; the pooled cognition estimate was positive but statistically insignificant. A separate sarcosine-plus-benzoate trial favored the combination over sarcosine alone, which argues for a double-hit or phenotype-specific hypothesis rather than a sarcosine nootropic claim.^[raw/articles/pubmed-32122256-sarcosine-schizophrenia-meta-analysis.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-26691576-sarcosine-plus-benzoate-schizophrenia-rct.md]

Anecdote signal: `weak-moderate salience, weak direct cognition`. Reddit threads and search trails show sarcosine is visible in nootropic communities, but the signal is mostly NMDA/glycine-site study-sharing, mood, sociability, verbal-flow, negative-symptom, or clarity reports. Direct healthy-adult focus or memory claims are sparse and mixed with no-effect, headache/uncomfortable stimulation, product-quality, and stack-confounding reports.^[raw/articles/reddit-2019-2026-sarcosine-nootropic-threads.md]

Practical route: keep sarcosine as an attribution-control molecule in two places. In [[nmda-coagonist-glyt1-daao-cognition-2026-scan]], it is a weak monotherapy comparator and a [[sodium-benzoate]] combination clue. In [[one-carbon-methyl-donor-cognition-watchlist]], it is the N-methylglycine sibling that prevents [[glycine]], [[dimethylglycine-dmg]], [[betaine-tmg]], and [[s-adenosyl-l-methionine]] stories from being collapsed.

## Human Evidence

The 2020 meta-analysis of double-blind randomized sarcosine trials in schizophrenia found overall symptom improvement, but cognition did not clear the bar. The pooled cognitive effect was SMD 0.27 with a 95% CI from -0.06 to 0.60 and p=0.10.^[raw/articles/pubmed-32122256-sarcosine-schizophrenia-meta-analysis.md]

The 12-week sarcosine-plus-benzoate trial compared sarcosine 2 g/day plus benzoate 1 g/day against sarcosine alone in chronic schizophrenia. The combination improved cognitive and global functioning, while sarcosine alone did not. This is important, but it points to a combination hypothesis rather than sarcosine monotherapy efficacy.^[raw/articles/pubmed-26691576-sarcosine-plus-benzoate-schizophrenia-rct.md]

## Safety And Sourcing Caveats

The captured sources describe sarcosine as generally tolerable in the schizophrenia literature, but the evidence base is small and consumer-source identity is not established by the clinical-trial corpus. Sarcosine should not borrow [[sodium-benzoate]]'s phenotype-sensitive aMCI signal or [[s-adenosyl-l-methionine]] / TMG methyl-donor anecdotes. For nootropic use, the main gates are psychiatric baseline, NMDA-coagonist stacking, sodium benzoate co-use, glycine/DMG/TMG naming confusion, product identity, sleep, stimulants, and dose.

## Practical Interpretation

Sarcosine deserves standalone coverage because it is one of the few consumer-buyable GlyT1/NMDA-adjacent chemicals with clinical cognition-adjacent literature and visible nootropic-community discussion. It should not be ranked as a broad healthy-adult cognition enhancer. The current read is `low-confidence, phenotype/combination-gated`: more relevant as a glutamatergic comparator and methylated-glycine boundary than as a top buyable nootropic.

Safety route: use [[safety-interaction-matrix-for-multi-modal-stacks]] when sarcosine anecdotes involve [[sodium-benzoate]], [[glycine]], [[d-serine]], methyl-donor stacks, stimulants, antidepressants, or psychosis-spectrum baselines. A positive clarity report can be NMDA-state, mood/social-anxiety, methylation, sleep, or combination-specific rather than sarcosine monotherapy.
