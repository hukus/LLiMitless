# Shankhpushpi

Shankhpushpi, also spelled Shankhapushpi, is an Ayurvedic memory and nerve-tonic label usually linked to Convolvulus pluricaulis but sometimes applied to different regional plant identities. The wiki treats it as a tradition-salient hypothesis node beside [[bacopa]], [[centella-asiatica]], [[polygala-bt11]], and [[shilajit]], not as a controlled nootropic.

## Bottom Line

Medicinal cognition signal: `low`. The captured human evidence is open-label or multi-herb and lacks well-controlled placebo-separated trials. One open-label study of 102 young healthy volunteers used Shankhpushpi tablets 500 mg twice daily for two months and compared the arm with yoga rather than placebo; another schoolchild study used a multi-herb formula, blocking attribution to Shankhpushpi alone.^[raw/articles/alzdiscovery-2020-shankhpushpi-cognitive-vitality.md]

Mechanistic signal: `preclinical-only`. A Convolvulus pluricaulis aqueous extract improved lifespan, locomotion, oxidative-stress / antioxidant measures, acetylcholinesterase-related measures, and tau readouts in a Drosophila tauopathy model. This is useful for mechanism tracking but does not establish human cognitive benefit.^[raw/articles/pubmed-2019-convolvulus-pluricaulis-tau-drosophila.md]

Product-identity gate: very high. The Shankhpushpi label can point to Convolvulus pluricaulis or other regional plant identities, and formula products may include multiple herbs. Any useful future evidence needs Latin binomial, plant part, extraction method, marker chemistry, dose, and contaminant testing.

## Evidence Layers

The ADDF Cognitive Vitality review is the current human-evidence anchor in this pass: it explicitly states that well-controlled human trials are lacking, while noting two open-label clinical studies and numerous preclinical studies. That makes Shankhpushpi page-worthy because of tradition salience, but not rank-worthy among standardized extracts with placebo-controlled cognition endpoints.^[raw/articles/alzdiscovery-2020-shankhpushpi-cognitive-vitality.md]

The tauopathy-model paper keeps Convolvulus pluricaulis in the mechanism map: the extract reduced tau and oxidative stress while improving model-organism survival and movement. The right interpretation is neuroprotection hypothesis generation, not proof that an over-the-counter Shankhpushpi product improves brain fog in healthy adults.^[raw/articles/pubmed-2019-convolvulus-pluricaulis-tau-drosophila.md]

## Safety And Status

The captured review flags possible blood-pressure lowering and a possible interaction with phenytoin. Practical gates include hypotension/lightheadedness monitoring, antiseizure-medication review, sedative stacking, pregnancy/lactation caution, plant-identity verification, and heavy-metal/adulterant testing common to some Ayurvedic-market products.^[raw/articles/alzdiscovery-2020-shankhpushpi-cognitive-vitality.md]

## Signal Judgment

Shankhpushpi deserves coverage because it is a high-salience traditional cognition label with some human exposure and plausible preclinical mechanisms. It should stay low-confidence until placebo-controlled, product-identified trials exist, and it should not be merged with [[bacopa]] or [[centella-asiatica]] even when regional names overlap.
