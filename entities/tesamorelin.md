# Tesamorelin

Tesamorelin is a synthetic growth hormone-releasing hormone analog and the regulated GH-axis peptide with the clearest cognition-specific human evidence in this branch. The direct tesamorelin story is mixed: an older controlled trial in healthy older adults and people with MCI looked favorable, while the newer 2025 HIV + abdominal-obesity study reduced waist circumference and increased IGF-1 but did not show a significant between-group cognitive benefit. That makes tesamorelin a phenotype-sensitive rescue candidate, not a broad nootropic.^[raw/articles/pubmed-22869065-ghrh-mci-healthy-older-adults-controlled-trial.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39813152-tesamorelin-hiv-abdominal-obesity-neurocognitive-impairment.md]

The ClinicalTrials.gov record for NCT02572323 posted results in 2026 after the study completed in 2023, which keeps the branch current even though the cognitive readout stayed cautious.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct02572323-tesamorelin-cognition-aging-hiv.md]

## Key Facts

- Approved use: reduction of excess abdominal fat in HIV-associated lipodystrophy.
- Cognition: the direct human signal is narrow and phenotype-bound.
- 2012 controlled study: favorable cognition in healthy older adults and MCI, with IGF-1 rising strongly.
- 2025 HIV + abdominal-obesity study: waist circumference improved, IGF-1 rose, but the between-group cognitive difference was not significant.
- Safety: elevated IGF-1, fluid retention, glucose intolerance/diabetes, hypersensitivity, injection-site reactions, and neoplasm warnings.
- Best read as a metabolically active rescue / aging-axis intervention rather than a general enhancer.

## What It Suggests

- If tesamorelin helps cognition, the effect is likely narrow and phenotype-dependent.
- Waist reduction and IGF-1 rise do not guarantee a cognition benefit.
- The label and trial pattern keep tesamorelin in medically supervised territory rather than casual self-experimentation.

## Limits

- There is no modern placebo-controlled replication of the cognition signal.
- The HIV/abdominal-obesity trial was open-label and underpowered for a definitive cognition claim.
- The older positive signal does not justify broad enhancement language outside enriched phenotypes.
