# R13 / 7,8-DHF Prodrug

R13 is a prodrug lead designed to improve the pharmacokinetics, oral exposure, and brain delivery of [[7-8-dihydroxyflavone]]. It belongs in the wiki as a prodrug / AD-model comparator, not as evidence that 7,8-DHF is clinically solved.

Medicinal cognition signal: `preclinical-only`. In the captured 5XFAD mouse AD-model source, R13 improved 7,8-DHF exposure, activated TrkB signaling, reduced Abeta pathology, preserved hippocampal synapses, and improved memory deficits in a dose-dependent manner. There is no captured human cognition efficacy or clinical dosing package.^[raw/articles/pmc-5777001-r13-7-8-dhf-prodrug-ad.md]

Mechanism signal: `prodrug/druggability support, not independent clinical proof`. R13 addresses one practical weakness of the parent molecule: poor oral bioavailability. It does not by itself resolve the target-specificity debate around small-molecule TrkB agonism, and it should not be merged with [[lm22a-4]], [[deoxygedunin]], or [[zeb85]].^[raw/articles/pmc-8398766-small-molecule-trkb-agonist-limitations.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-37488840-trkb-agonist-caution-review.md]

Human translation signal: `absent in captured registry search`. The TrkB-mimetic registry snapshot did not surface a human cognition program for the named branch. R13 remains preclinical until a human safety or cognition program is visible.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-trkb-bdnf-mimetic-registry-search-2026.md]

## Practical Interpretation

R13 is important because it separates parent-molecule enthusiasm from druggability. Its page should be used when a claim says "7,8-DHF works better as a prodrug"; it should not upgrade the human cognition signal without human data.
