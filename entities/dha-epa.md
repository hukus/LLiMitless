# DHA / EPA

DHA and EPA are long-chain omega-3 fatty acids used as nutrients, food components, and supplement ingredients. In this wiki they are tracked together because cognition trials and community reports usually involve DHA-rich fish oil, EPA/DHA fish oil, algae oil, or multi-nutrient combinations. They should not be collapsed into ALA, generic fish oil capsule mass, krill oil, whole fish intake, DHA-only infant-development claims, or DHA+EPA+vitamin E+tryptophan+melatonin stacks.

Medicinal cognition signal: `low-moderate but domain-specific and contested`. The 2026 nutrient network meta-analysis reports DHA benefits for memory and processing speed and a DHA/EPA-containing multi-nutrient combination for global cognition and memory, but it explicitly frames nutrient effects as nuanced and sensitive to baseline status, age, duration, and sample size. A 2024 dose-response meta-analysis of 24 RCTs and 9,660 participants found no overall global-cognition association, but did find an executive-function signal over the first 12 months, especially above 500 mg/day n-3 PUFA and up to about 420 mg/day EPA. The best single-ingredient positive RCT is MIDAS: 900 mg/day DHA for 24 weeks improved visuospatial learning/episodic-memory errors and verbal-recognition memory in older adults with age-related cognitive decline. Counterweights are substantial: a 2020 DHA meta-analysis found minimal or no pooled effects across memory, attention, working memory, and executive function, and CANN found no cognitive benefit from a DHA-rich fish oil plus cocoa-flavanol stack in older adults with memory complaints.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41764841-nutritional-supplements-cognition-nma.md]^[raw/articles/bmc-2024-omega3-cognition-dose-response-meta.md]^[raw/articles/sciencedirect-2010-dha-midas-arcd-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-32060571-dha-arcd-meta-analysis.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-37315924-cann-dha-cocoa-flavanols-cognition-negative.md]

Anecdote signal: `weak-moderate and bidirectional`. Community reports include users claiming better cognition/memory, TBI recovery support, or clearer brain state with high-concentration EPA/DHA or DHA-rich products. The same community layer includes adverse reports of brain fog, low mood, fatigue, head pressure, irritability, melancholy, headache, sexual dysfunction, or feeling off. Product identity dominates these reports: EPA:DHA ratio, dose of actual EPA/DHA rather than fish-oil mass, oxidation/rancidity, triglyceride/phospholipid/algae forms, and clinical-state context all change interpretation.^[raw/articles/reddit-2022-omega3-epa-dha-cognition-thread.md]^[raw/articles/reddit-2021-fish-oil-adverse-brain-fog-thread.md]^[raw/articles/troof-nootropics-ratings-analysis.md]

Safety signal: `moderate supplement caution`. NIH ODS notes that FDA considers supplements providing no more than 5 g/day EPA plus DHA safe when used as recommended, while common side effects include unpleasant taste, bad breath, heartburn, nausea, GI discomfort, diarrhea, headache, and odoriferous sweat. High doses can have antiplatelet effects and should be monitored with warfarin or similar anticoagulants. Large cardiovascular trials using 4 g/day for years found a small atrial-fibrillation increase in cardiovascular or high-risk populations.^[raw/articles/ods-2026-omega3-health-professional-fact-sheet.md]

## Signal Split

| Layer | Signal | Readout |
|---|---|---|
| 2026 nutrient NMA | `low-moderate/domain-specific` | DHA improved memory and processing speed; multi-nutrient DHA/EPA stack improved global cognition/memory |
| 2024 n-3 PUFA dose-response meta-analysis | `low/executive-function trend` | No global-cognition effect overall; executive-function signal depended on duration, dose, and EPA level |
| MIDAS DHA RCT | `positive but phenotype-bound` | 900 mg/day DHA improved PAL errors and verbal-recognition memory in older ARCD adults |
| 2020 DHA ARCD meta-analysis | `negative/null pooled` | No supportive pooled effects across memory, attention, working memory, or executive function |
| CANN DHA+cocoa-flavanol trial | `negative/stack counterweight` | DHA-rich fish oil plus cocoa flavanols did not improve primary or most secondary cognition outcomes |
| Anecdote | `weak-moderate bidirectional` | Positive memory/clarity reports and adverse brain-fog/mood/fatigue reports coexist |
| Safety / quality | `moderate caution` | EPA/DHA dose, anticoagulants, atrial fibrillation risk context, GI effects, oxidation, and product form matter |

## Practical Interpretation

DHA/EPA belongs in the molecule atlas because it has large exposure, plausible brain biology, a positive memory RCT, and current meta-analytic domain-specific support. It should not be ranked as a universal nootropic. The best read is `nutrient-status / older-adult / memory-executive-function support`, with baseline omega-3 status, EPA:DHA ratio, dose, duration, form, and product oxidation as gates.

A confidence upgrade requires trials that stratify by omega-3 index or baseline dietary status, separate DHA from EPA and multi-nutrient stacks, prespecify memory/executive-function endpoints, and report product form and oxidation quality. Anecdote should be used mainly for product-quality and responder-adverse monitoring.
