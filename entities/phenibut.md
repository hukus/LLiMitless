# Phenibut

Phenibut is a risk-first GABAergic anxiolytic/sedative-adjacent compound in the wiki. Its community signal is strong, but the formal literature and FDA trail are dominated by dependence, withdrawal, intoxication, impairment, and supplement misbranding rather than objective cognition.

## Medicinal Signal

- Formal signal: `risk-first`; no robust cognition-enhancement evidence in the current wiki.
- FDA says phenibut does not meet the statutory definition of a dietary ingredient, so dietary supplements listing it are misbranded.^[raw/articles/fda-phenibut-dietary-supplements.md]
- A 2026 FDA advisory for Addall products found phenibut plus undeclared stimulant ingredients and explicitly listed poor balance, fatigue, diminished or lost consciousness, addiction after only a few uses, and withdrawal when stopped.^[raw/articles/fda-phenibut-addall-2026-advisory.md]
- The clinical literature includes dependence, severe withdrawal, withdrawal delirium, forensic driving cases, and variable supplement quantities before and after FDA warnings.^[raw/articles/pubmed-23391959-phenibut-dependence.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-35442585-phenibut-chronic-use-severe-withdrawal-recovery.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-37930202-phenibut-intoxication-withdrawal-delirium.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-38146811-phenibut-driving-cases.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-34550038-phenibut-supplement-quantity-before-after-fda-warnings.md]

## Safety And Interpretation

- Main burden: dependence, withdrawal, rebound anxiety, sedation, impaired balance, diminished consciousness, driving/psychomotor risk, product misbranding, and dose variability in supplements.
- June 2026 nootropic-routing gate: phenibut reports belong in the dependence/withdrawal risk lane, not in the strong positive nootropic tier. Before interpreting calm focus, sociability, sleep, anxiety relief, or productivity claims, require exact product identity, dose, frequency, schedule, duration, co-use with alcohol/benzodiazepines/opioids/kratom/tianeptine/stimulants/cannabinoids, baseline anxiety or insomnia, tolerance/rebound/withdrawal context, psychomotor/driving impairment, adverse events, dechallenge/washout, and objective endpoints. Missing dose, dependence, or co-use fields keep the report as `risk-first anecdote`.

- Apparent benefit is often anxiolysis or withdrawal reversal rather than cognition. That makes phenibut a cautionary comparator, not a candidate enhancer.

## Current Read

Phenibut is included as a safety exemplar. The wiki should preserve the strong anecdote signal, but the signal should lower rather than raise recommendation priority because dependence and withdrawal dominate the interpretation.

Use the safety matrix before interpreting phenibut stack reports: calm focus, sociability, sleep, or anxiety relief can reflect sedation, rebound relief, withdrawal reversal, stimulant smoothing, alcohol/benzodiazepine overlap, or product-label uncertainty rather than cognition.
