# Oxiracetam

## Bottom Line

Oxiracetam is an old disease-context racetam with a `low` medicinal cognition signal. Two small 1992 dementia studies reported selected cognitive, quality-of-life, or daily-function improvements at 800 mg twice daily, but the evidence is old, small, and not a healthy-adult enhancement package. Anecdotal signal is `weak-moderate` and not source-normalized in the current corpus.

## What It Is

Oxiracetam is a racetam-family compound that should stay separate from [[piracetam]], [[aniracetam]], [[pramiracetam]], [[nefiracetam]], and [[noopept]]. In this wiki it belongs with [[racetam-long-tail-watchlist]] as an old dementia / multi-infarct-dementia branch.

## Medicinal Research Signal

One multicentre double-blind placebo-controlled trial tested oxiracetam 800 mg twice daily for 12 weeks in primary degenerative, multi-infarct, or mixed dementia. Sixty-five patients enrolled and 58 completed treatment. The abstract reports significant effects on quality of life and selected neuropsychological tests, including controlled associations and short-story measures, with adverse events reported by four oxiracetam patients and one placebo patient.^[raw/articles/pubmed-1414239-oxiracetam-dementia-placebo-study.md]

A second old study compared oxiracetam 800 mg twice daily with placebo over 90 days in 60 outpatients with mild-to-moderate Alzheimer-type or multi-infarct dementia, followed by longer follow-up. The abstract reports significant improvements favoring oxiracetam on MMSE, continuous-performance testing, Rey 15 Words, Block Tapping, Mattis Word Fluency, Luria Alternating Series, and instrumental activities of daily living.^[raw/articles/pubmed-1603291-oxiracetam-dementia-follow-up.md]

These signals keep oxiracetam visible, but their age, size, and disease context prevent a healthy-adult upgrade.

## Safety And Status

The captured human studies are too old and small to define a modern safety profile for self-directed enhancement. Treat the trial adverse-event imbalance and old study designs as caution flags, not dosing guidance. Route oxiracetam self-experiment reports through [[safety-interaction-matrix-for-multi-modal-stacks]] before treating them as molecule-specific cognition evidence, especially when choline co-use, stimulants, sleep loss, tolerance, or source identity are active.

## Signal Judgment

Medicinal signal: `low`, old disease-context only.

Anecdotal signal: `weak-moderate`, not source-normalized.

Practical route: old dementia-context racetam comparator.
