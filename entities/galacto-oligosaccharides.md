# Galacto-Oligosaccharides

Galacto-oligosaccharides, or GOS, are prebiotic carbohydrates used to shift gut microbiota rather than live probiotic strains. For cognition, GOS belongs in the prebiotic / gut-brain responder lane beside [[2-fucosyllactose]], not inside generic fiber, dairy, or probiotic claims.

Medicinal signal: `low / high-anxiety subgroup reaction-time signal`. A 2025 randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial in 83 healthy females aged 17-25 gave GOS or placebo for 28 days. GOS transiently increased Bifidobacterium and shifted brain-chemistry measures, but trait anxiety did not change at endline. The cognition signal was narrow: faster reaction times appeared mainly in the high-anxious subgroup.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40532960-galacto-oligosaccharides-gut-brain-axis-young-females.md]

Interpretation: this is one of the cleaner healthy-young-adult gut-brain signals in the wiki, but it is still phenotype-gated and endpoint-narrow. It should be read as "GOS may interact with anxiety state, microbiome response, and reaction-time tasks" rather than "prebiotics improve cognition." The broader probiotic/prebiotic cognition field remains heterogeneous, and even healthy-older-adult probiotic data usually show only narrow acute or chronic task effects.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41291638-probiotic-supplements-cognitive-function-meta-analysis.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40222448-multistrain-probiotic-healthy-older-adults-cognition.md]

Product and protocol gates:

- Active unit: GOS dose and purity should be stated, not just "prebiotic fiber."
- Population: young healthy females, with high-anxiety subgroup signal.
- Endpoint: reaction time, not broad memory or executive function.
- Responder gate: Bifidobacterium movement may be relevant but is not yet a validated selection rule.
- Tolerability: gas, bloating, diarrhea, FODMAP sensitivity, IBS status, and dose escalation matter.
- Translation risk: fermented dairy, inulin/FOS, resistant starch, and other prebiotics are separate hypotheses.

Anecdote layer: no GOS-specific cognition anecdote source is currently captured.
