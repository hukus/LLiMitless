# Emraclidine

## Bottom Line

Emraclidine is an M4 positive allosteric modulator with symptom-first schizophrenia data and no direct cognition readout in the current corpus. Medicinal signal for cognition is `low`; its importance is as a modern muscarinic disease-program comparator, not as an enhancer. Anecdotal signal is absent.

## What It Is

Emraclidine is a selective positive allosteric modulator of the M4 muscarinic acetylcholine receptor. It is relevant to cognition because M4 modulation sits in the same muscarinic psychiatry lane as [[xanomeline-trospium]], but the available outcomes are PANSS-first rather than cognitive-performance-first.

## Medicinal Research Signal

The phase 1b study enrolled stable and acutely ill schizophrenia participants, found acceptable tolerability, and reported a clinically meaningful PANSS signal in the acute-schizophrenia part of the study. This is a psychiatry efficacy anchor, but not a direct cognition trial.^[raw/articles/pubmed-36528376-emraclidine-m4-phase1b.md]

The phase 2 registry record is completed and has posted results. It shows placebo and both dose arms with week-6 PANSS reductions, but the public results page does not provide a clean cognition readout. For the wiki, that means emraclidine is useful for symptom-efficacy surveillance and muscarinic branch maturity, not for cognitive-enhancement ranking.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct05227690-emraclidine-phase2-results.md]

## Safety And Status

The phase 1b note describes acceptable tolerability; the phase 2 registry confirms the program moved into larger schizophrenia testing. The main interpretive limit remains endpoint choice rather than a current cognition-specific safety/benefit calculation.

## Signal Judgment

Medicinal signal: `low for cognition`, `clinical-development for schizophrenia symptoms`.

Anecdotal signal: `absent-to-weak`.

Practical route: M4 muscarinic comparator and negative/unknown cognition endpoint watch item.
