# Holy Basil / Tulsi

Holy basil, also called Tulsi, Ocimum tenuiflorum, or Ocimum sanctum, is an Ayurvedic adaptogen candidate with a small direct healthy-volunteer cognition study and a stronger stress/sleep-state trial. It should be interpreted as stress-state support, not as a proven broad enhancer.

## Signal Snapshot

| Evidence layer | Direction | Signal strength | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Healthy-volunteer cognition | positive but small | `low` | A 30-day 300 mg/day ethanolic leaf-extract study favored holy basil on Sternberg, Stroop, and P300 latency outcomes.^[raw/articles/pubmed-26571987-holy-basil-healthy-volunteers-cognition.md] |
| Stress and sleep | positive stress-state support | `low-moderate` | An 8-week Holixer trial improved perceived stress, insomnia scale scores, hair cortisol, and Maastricht Acute Stress Test responses.^[raw/articles/frontiers-2022-holy-basil-holixer-stress-sleep-rct.md] |
| Traditional relevance | strong | `context only` | The herb is central in Ayurvedic use, but tradition does not upgrade efficacy beyond the controlled evidence.^[raw/articles/merck-2025-holy-basil-safety.md] |
| Safety | generally short-term favorable, several cautions | `moderate caution` | Merck flags limited evidence quality, short-term safety only, nausea/diarrhea, pregnancy/fertility, thyroid, bleeding, sedative, and glucose-medication concerns.^[raw/articles/merck-2025-holy-basil-safety.md] |

## Medicinal Evidence

The direct cognition anchor tested 300 mg/day ethanolic leaf extract for 30 days in healthy male volunteers. Practice effects appeared in both groups, but intergroup comparisons favored holy basil on Sternberg reaction time and error rate, Stroop neutral reaction time, and Stroop interference reaction time and error rate. P300 latency improved between groups at day 30. This is enough for a page, but not enough for high confidence because the trial was small and short.^[raw/articles/pubmed-26571987-holy-basil-healthy-volunteers-cognition.md]

The Holixer trial is better read as stress-state evidence. Stressed adults took 125 mg twice daily for 8 weeks. Compared with placebo, the extract improved perceived stress and Athens Insomnia Scale scores, lowered hair cortisol, and buffered salivary cortisol, salivary amylase, blood pressure, and subjective stress during the Maastricht Acute Stress Test. That supports a plausible brain-fog route through hyperarousal, cortisol, and sleep quality rather than direct memory enhancement.^[raw/articles/frontiers-2022-holy-basil-holixer-stress-sleep-rct.md]

## Safety And Product Gates

Holy basil should not be merged across tea, essential oil, generic powder, ethanolic leaf extract, and Holixer. Merck describes the evidence as limited and small-study-heavy, says oral use appears safe for most people up to 8 weeks, and cautions about nausea/diarrhea, pregnancy or trying to conceive, breastfeeding uncertainty, hypothyroidism, bleeding/surgery, sedatives, anticoagulants/antiplatelets, and glucose-lowering medications.^[raw/articles/merck-2025-holy-basil-safety.md]

## Practical Interpretation

[[holy-basil]] earns a low-confidence traditional-medicine entity because it has both a direct cognitive-task study and a controlled stress/sleep trial. Its best hypothesis is stress-mediated cognitive rescue in healthy adults, not universal enhancement.
