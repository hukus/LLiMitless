# Ergothioneine

Ergothioneine is a sulfur-containing dietary amino-thione concentrated in some mushrooms and transported by OCTN1/SLC22A4. In this wiki it is tracked as a pure-molecule nutraceutical / food-derived aging candidate, not as generic mushroom intake. It should not be collapsed with [[hericium-erinaceus]], mushroom powder blends, spermidine, NR/NMN, urolithin A, [[resveratrol]], or antioxidant stacks.

Medicinal cognition signal: `low/preliminary and MCI-biased`. The strongest direct intervention source is a 19-person randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled MCI pilot: 25 mg ergothioneine three times weekly for one year improved learning performance and stabilized plasma neurofilament light chain versus placebo, with no blood-count, kidney, or liver toxicity signal in the captured note. That is encouraging, but it is still a tiny disease-risk / MCI study, not healthy-adult enhancement proof.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39544014-ergothioneine-mci-pilot.md]

The observational layer is stronger than the intervention layer. A 2022 memory-clinic cohort found that lower plasma ergothioneine predicted poorer baseline cognition and faster cognitive/functional decline over up to five years, with mediation largely explained by white-matter hyperintensities and brain atrophy. That supports biomarker relevance, but it does not prove that supplementation reverses decline. The 2025 review is directionally positive and argues that ergothioneine may support cognition, memory, sleep, and neurodegeneration biomarkers, but it also calls for larger, longer trials and has a material industry-affiliation caveat.^[raw/articles/pubmed-36139790-ergothioneine-cognitive-functional-decline.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40968729-ergothioneine-cognitive-health-review.md]

Healthy-adult evidence remains weak. The ClinicalTrials.gov cognition/mood/sleep registry planned 10 mg/day and 25 mg/day arms for 16 weeks, but public enrollment was only 4 and no efficacy result was posted. That registry prevents over-reading the MCI pilot as a healthy-user nootropic result.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct04556032-ergothioneine-cognition-mood-sleep.md]

Anecdote signal: `weak-moderate for longevity/energy/sleep salience, weak for cognition`. Community reports cluster around longevity, cleaner stimulant feel, energy, exercise, mood lift, sleep, and product purity. Direct memory, attention, or executive-function claims are sparse. Adverse signal is not dominant in the captured threads, but there are cost, dose, sourcing, purity, placebo, and unknown-long-term-use concerns, plus at least one possible chronic nasal-stuffiness report.^[raw/articles/reddit-2024-ergothioneine-longevity-mood-thread.md]^[raw/articles/reddit-2026-ergothioneine-evidence-stack-thread.md]

Safety and product signal: `low-moderate caution with favorable food-ingredient status`. FDA GRAS notices for ergothioneine and fermentation-produced ergothioneine ingredients support a better-than-average food/supplement acceptability profile, and the MCI pilot reported no obvious blood, kidney, or liver safety problems. The remaining safety burden is mostly product identity, purity, dose escalation beyond studied ranges, long-term use, and whether mushroom-food sources are being substituted for pure ergothioneine capsules.^[raw/articles/fda-gras-2026-ergothioneine-notices.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39544014-ergothioneine-mci-pilot.md]

## Product And Protocol Gates

- Separate pure L-ergothioneine capsules from mushroom powders, mushroom extracts, mixed mushroom products, and fortified foods.
- Track production route, purity, third-party testing, dose per capsule, serving count, and whether the product maps to a GRAS notice or clinical-study exposure.
- Separate food-use levels such as 5 mg/serving from clinical-study doses such as 25 mg three times weekly or 10-25 mg/day.
- Treat sleep, energy, mood, exercise, and longevity anecdotes as separate from cognition unless objective cognitive testing is present.
- Stratify future interpretation by baseline cognitive status, plasma ergothioneine level, age, vascular-brain burden, diet/mushroom intake, and neurodegeneration biomarkers.

## Practical Interpretation

Ergothioneine deserves standalone tracking because it has a clean molecule identity, a plausible transporter-backed mechanism, observational cognitive-decline data, a small MCI intervention signal, and better-than-average regulatory acceptability. It does not yet deserve a broad enhancer ranking. The current best route is `MCI / vascular-brain-risk / nutrient-status or biomarker-adjacent watch`, with healthy-adult claims held at low confidence.

Confidence would improve with larger blinded trials that enroll by baseline plasma ergothioneine or cognitive-risk phenotype, pre-register memory/executive endpoints, track NfL and imaging, and disclose product identity and COA. Anecdote should mainly inform product-quality, dose, sleep, and long-term-use questions.
