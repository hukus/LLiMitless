# Tianeptine

Tianeptine is a risk-first chemical in this wiki. It has strong subjective and community salience, but the formal source trail is dominated by misuse, dependence, withdrawal, poison-center exposure, and product adulteration rather than cognition enhancement.

## Medicinal Signal

- Formal signal: `risk-first`; no compelling cognition-specific enhancement bridge in the current wiki.
- FDA warns U.S. consumers not to purchase or use tianeptine products, says tianeptine is not approved in the U.S., and notes marketing as a nootropic, supplement, research chemical, or gas-station product despite serious risks.^[raw/articles/fda-tianeptine-2025-serious-risks.md]
- National Poison Data System data reported 892 single-substance tianeptine exposures from 2015 to 2023, a 1,400% increase, with substantial moderate/major effects, medical admissions, abuse, and withdrawal.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39724478-tianeptine-poison-centers-2015-2023.md]
- A 2025 New Jersey outbreak case series adds a product-identity problem: products sold as tianeptine were adulterated with synthetic cannabinoids.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40102319-tianeptine-new-jersey-adulteration-case-series.md]
- A 2026 FDA recall also found undeclared tianeptine in a supplement marketed for "brain function," which makes nootropic-market contamination part of the branch rather than a side issue.^[raw/articles/fda-2026-modern-warrior-ready-aniracetam-recall.md]
