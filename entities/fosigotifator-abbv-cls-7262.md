# Fosigotifator / ABBV-CLS-7262

Fosigotifator, also known as ABBV-CLS-7262, is an investigational eIF2B activator in the integrated-stress-response branch. It belongs in the wiki as a clinical comparator for eIF2B activation, not as a cognitive enhancer.

Medicinal cognition signal: `absent`. The captured clinical source is an ALS platform-trial readout, not a cognition trial. HEALEY ALS Regimen F did not meet its primary-dose primary endpoint of ALSFRS-R / survival through 24 weeks and did not meet key secondary endpoints of respiratory function and muscle strength.^[raw/articles/als-eu-2025-fosigotifator-healey-als-topline.md]

Clinical translation signal: `mixed but not cognition-positive`. The exploratory high-dose group showed nominal muscle-strength differences and a possible respiratory-function signal, while fosigotifator was reported safe and well tolerated. Those signals are ALS-motor / respiratory hypotheses and do not generalize to cognitive enhancement.

Mechanism caveat: `shared eIF2B/ISR uncertainty`. Like [[dnl343]] and [[isrib]], fosigotifator sits in a pathway where acute stress adaptation, chronic stress pathology, and eIF2B-specific translation-control biology can point in different directions.^[raw/articles/molecular-neurodegeneration-2025-integrated-stress-response-neurodegenerative-diseases.md]^[raw/articles/nature-2025-plasticity-mammalian-integrated-stress-response.md]
