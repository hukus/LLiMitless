# Dextromethorphan / Bupropion

Dextromethorphan / bupropion is the AUVELITY fixed-dose antidepressant combination. It belongs in [[glutamatergic-rapid-antidepressant-cognition-watchlist]] because dextromethorphan is framed around NMDA / sigma-1 pharmacology and bupropion raises dextromethorphan exposure, but it should not inherit [[ketamine-esketamine]] evidence or [[bupropion]] attention evidence as if the combination were a clean cognition drug.

## Current Read

Medicinal cognition signal: `low/indirect`. GEMINI supports antidepressant efficacy and rapid mood-score movement in MDD, but the captured endpoints were depression, remission/response, global impression, disability, symptom self-rating, and quality of life, not objective cognition.^[raw/articles/jcp-2022-axs05-gemini-phase3-mdd.md]

Brain-fog signal: `anecdote-only and bidirectional`. Some users report early mental clarity, energy, better memory, and rapid relief. Other threads are dominated by fog, inability to focus, dissociation, feeling drugged, sleepiness, racing heart, spotty memory, and worsening with closer or higher dosing.^[raw/articles/reddit-2024-2026-ketamine-auvelity-cognition-brainfog-threads.md]

Safety signal: `high-priority`. AUVELITY combines bupropion's seizure, BP, activation, and psychosis/mania gates with dextromethorphan's serotonergic and dissociative/intoxication-adjacent risks. The label's serotonin-syndrome and MAOI warnings make this especially relevant beside [[mao-b-propargylamine-cognition-watchlist]], [[methylene-blue]], and [[5-htp-tryptophan]].^[raw/articles/dailymed-2026-auvelity-dextromethorphan-bupropion-label.md]

## Formal Evidence

- GEMINI randomized 327 adults with MDD to dextromethorphan-bupropion or placebo for 6 weeks and found greater MADRS improvement, remission, and response with the combination.^[raw/articles/jcp-2022-axs05-gemini-phase3-mdd.md]
- GEMINI's source text frames dextromethorphan-bupropion as an oral NMDA receptor antagonist and sigma-1 receptor agonist approach, with bupropion included to increase dextromethorphan exposure.^[raw/articles/jcp-2022-axs05-gemini-phase3-mdd.md]
- The trial does not provide a dedicated cognition endpoint; mood, disability, and quality-of-life improvements should stay separate from cognitive-performance claims.^[raw/articles/jcp-2022-axs05-gemini-phase3-mdd.md]
- DailyMed anchors the current practical boundary: adult MDD indication, common dizziness/somnolence/headache/dry-mouth events, seizure contraindications, BP monitoring, serotonin syndrome, and interaction risk.^[raw/articles/dailymed-2026-auvelity-dextromethorphan-bupropion-label.md]

## Safety And Interpretation Gates

- Screen for seizure risk, eating-disorder history, abrupt sedative/alcohol withdrawal, other bupropion products, hypertension, bipolar/mania risk, psychosis/neuropsychiatric reactions, angle-closure glaucoma, and falls/machinery risk.^[raw/articles/dailymed-2026-auvelity-dextromethorphan-bupropion-label.md]
- Treat serotonergic stacks as a hard gate: SSRIs/SNRIs/TCAs, MAOIs, linezolid, IV [[methylene-blue]], serotonergic supplements, and cough-medicine duplication can change the risk picture.^[raw/articles/dailymed-2026-auvelity-dextromethorphan-bupropion-label.md]
- Do not treat "diet ketamine" or "oral ketamine substitute" marketing language as evidence. The combination has its own pharmacology and label constraints.
- Do not combine the [[bupropion]] entity's narrow attention-lapse signal with GEMINI's depression signal to manufacture a direct cognitive-efficacy claim for AUVELITY.
