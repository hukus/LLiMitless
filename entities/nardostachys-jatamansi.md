# Nardostachys Jatamansi

Nardostachys jatamansi, often called Jatamansi or spikenard, is a Himalayan Ayurvedic and ethnomedicine plant used in CNS, sleep, agitation, epilepsy-like, and tonic contexts. Its cognition evidence is currently preclinical.

## Bottom Line

Medicinal cognition signal: `preclinical-only`. The captured sources include a broad phytochemistry/pharmacology review, a 2025 AD-target computational profiling paper, and an animal stress-memory study. No controlled human cognition trial is captured.^[raw/articles/pubmed-38042505-nardostachys-jatamansi-review.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40027104-nardostachys-ad-computational.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-23225973-nardostachys-stress-memory-rat.md]

The best practical interpretation is sleep/calm/neuroprotection hypothesis, not healthy-adult enhancement. Conservation and species/product identity also matter because the plant is a Himalayan high-altitude resource and substitution/adulteration risk is plausible in commerce.

## Mechanism Layer

The 2024 review reports CNS, anticonvulsant, tranquilizing, neuroprotective, antiparkinson's, antioxidant, and other pharmacological activities. The 2025 computational paper adds AD-target plausibility but does not replace animal or human efficacy testing.^[raw/articles/pubmed-38042505-nardostachys-jatamansi-review.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40027104-nardostachys-ad-computational.md]

## Signal Judgment

Jatamansi stays in the long-tail watchlist until human data appear. It is worth tracking as a traditional CNS plant and possible sleep/stress-state modifier, but it should not be promoted above controlled human botanicals or low-risk basics.
