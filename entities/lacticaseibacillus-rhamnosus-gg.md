# Lacticaseibacillus Rhamnosus GG

Lacticaseibacillus rhamnosus GG, historically labeled Lactobacillus rhamnosus GG or LGG, is a probiotic strain with a cognition-adjacent human RCT in middle-aged and older adults. It should not be confused with [[lacticaseibacillus-rhamnosus-cbt-lr5]], [[lacticaseibacillus-rhamnosus-hn001]], or generic Lactobacillus products.

Medicinal signal: `low / microbiome-state interaction`. A 2022 randomized clinical trial in 169 middle-aged and older adults connected LGG supplementation, microbiome shifts, and cognitive status. The useful signal was not a broad cognition win: improved cognitive score correlated with decreases in Prevotella and Dehalobacterium in the MCI subgroup. This makes LGG a baseline-microbiome and impairment-phenotype lead, not a general enhancer.^[raw/articles/pubmed-36228569-lgg-mci-gut-microbiome-probiotics.md]

Class context stays cautious. A 2025 older-adult meta-analysis found a non-significant positive trend for cognition and inflammation, and a broader 2025 probiotic RCT synthesis judged certainty low to very low for several pooled signals. LGG should therefore be used as a clue about responder biology, not as evidence that any LGG product will clear brain fog or improve focus.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40483001-probiotics-cognitive-performance-older-adults-meta-analysis.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41291638-probiotic-supplements-cognitive-function-meta-analysis.md]

Product and protocol gates:

- Strain identity: LGG must be explicit; other L. rhamnosus strains are not interchangeable.
- Population: signal is strongest in MCI subgroup logic.
- Endpoint: cognitive-score correlation with microbiome shifts, not a clean primary cognitive endpoint.
- Responder hypothesis: baseline taxa and cognitive status may matter more than dose alone.
- Safety screen: immunocompromise, severe illness, central venous catheter, antibiotic timing, and GI symptoms.

Anecdote layer: no strain-specific LGG cognition anecdote source is currently captured. Generic probiotic brain-fog reports should be routed to a separate anecdote page before changing this confidence.
