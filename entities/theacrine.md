# Theacrine

Theacrine is a stimulant-adjacent purine alkaloid most relevant to the wiki as a caffeine-stack and fatigue-state candidate. The current captured evidence is not enough to treat theacrine alone as a proven nootropic.

Medicinal signal: `low-moderate` for combination / state challenge, `uncataloged` for theacrine alone. The 2025 tactical-personnel source tested caffeine plus theacrine under physically fatiguing conditions and reported cognitive-performance relevance in that state, but the attribution is a stack signal and caffeine remains the obvious comparator.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40693646-caffeine-theacrine-tactical-fatigue-cognition.md]
