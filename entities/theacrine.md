# Theacrine

Theacrine / TeaCrine is a stimulant-adjacent purine alkaloid most relevant to the wiki as a caffeine-stack, fatigue-state, and subjective-energy candidate. It has more human evidence than a pure forum molecule, but the direct cognition claim remains weak because the standalone evidence is pilot/subjective and the stronger performance signals are caffeine-containing stacks.

Medicinal signal: `low for standalone subjective cognition`, `low-moderate for caffeine-stack / fatigue-state performance`. The standalone TeaCrine pilot used 200 mg in 15 healthy participants and mainly supports subjective energy, concentration, and fatigue claims; the captured source itself treats objective cognitive-performance confirmation as future work.^[raw/articles/pmc-4271659-theacrine-pilot-subjective-cognition.md]

Stack evidence is more practical but attribution-confounded. A 50-person e-gamer crossover compared caffeine, caffeine + Dynamine + TeaCrine, and placebo, while tactical-personnel studies tested caffeine + methylliberine + theacrine or caffeine + theacrine under vigilance / marksmanship / fatigue conditions. These support visibility in stimulant stacks, not theacrine-alone cognition proof.^[raw/articles/pubmed-35103121-caffeine-theacrine-dynamine-egamers.md]^[raw/articles/tandfonline-2022-caffeine-methylliberine-theacrine-tactical-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40693646-caffeine-theacrine-tactical-fatigue-cognition.md]

Safety and PK read: short-term product-specific safety looks acceptable in healthy adults, but the evidence base is small and sponsor/product-linked. An 8-week TeaCrine study in 60 healthy adults tested 200 and 300 mg/day and reported no meaningful hemodynamic or clinical-safety blood-marker movement; a separate 8-person crossover found caffeine increased theacrine exposure while acute HR/BP did not differ at tested doses.^[raw/articles/pmc-4711067-teacrine-8-week-safety.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-28875060-theacrine-caffeine-pk-interaction.md]

## Practical Interpretation

Route theacrine through [[common-nootropic-gray-zone-molecule-watchlist]], [[gray-zone-nootropic-community-signal-ledger]], [[caffeine]], [[methylliberine]], and [[state-challenge-matrix-for-cognition-interventions]]. It is best scored as a subjective-energy / caffeine-stack modifier, not a standalone cognitive enhancer. Any self-study or trial interpretation should log caffeine dose, habitual caffeine state, sleep timing, BP/HR, palpitations, anxiety, and product identity.

Route theacrine / TeaCrine anecdotes through [[safety-interaction-matrix-for-multi-modal-stacks]] before treating clean energy, longer caffeine duration, fatigue-state vigilance, gaming, tactical, fasting, workout, or focus reports as direct cognition. The first gates are caffeine co-use, methylliberine co-use, caffeine increasing theacrine exposure, sleep timing, BP/HR, palpitations, anxiety, fatigue challenge, product identity, and whether any objective cognition endpoint survives the stimulant-stack context.
