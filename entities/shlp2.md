# SHLP2

SHLP2, or small humanin-like peptide 2, is an endogenous mitochondrial-derived peptide that has enough translational biology to deserve its own page, but not enough cognition evidence to be treated as a validated cognitive enhancer. The current signal is strongest in metabolism, retinal protection, and Parkinson's disease genetics/biology. In the mitochondrial peptide family, SHLP2 now looks like a real emerging sub-branch rather than a review-only side mention.^[raw/articles/pubmed-30310092-shlp2-macular-degeneration.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-37468558-shlp2-energy-homeostasis.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-38167865-shlp2-parkinson-protective-factor.md]

The 2023 energy-homeostasis paper moved SHLP2 out of a purely review-level bucket by showing circulating SHLP2 in humans, hypothalamic-neuron activation, and metabolic effects in high-fat-diet-fed male mice. That makes SHLP2 relevant to energy balance and body-state biology, but it still does not establish a cognition intervention.^[raw/articles/pubmed-37468558-shlp2-energy-homeostasis.md]

The 2024 Parkinson's disease paper added a brain-relevant genetic/translational hook: an mtSNP within the SHLP2 open reading frame produced the K4R variant, the variant was detected in neuronal cells, it bound mitochondrial complex 1, and it was associated with reduced PD risk.^[raw/articles/pubmed-38167865-shlp2-parkinson-protective-factor.md]

The older macular-degeneration paper is the third anchor in the current set. It supports SHLP2 as a tissue-protective mitochondrial peptide in retinal models, which strengthens the family-level translational story without creating a direct cognition claim.^[raw/articles/pubmed-30310092-shlp2-macular-degeneration.md]

## Frontier Read

SHLP2 is important less because it is a cognition peptide today and more because it makes the mitochondrial peptide family look structurally deeper. A peptide with circulating-human detection, hypothalamic/metabolic signaling, and a distinct PD-protective variant story is no longer just abstract family biology. It is an emerging translational node with plausible future CNS relevance.

I did not surface a visible SHLP2 intervention program in the current pass, so the branch still reads as biologically serious but clinically early.
