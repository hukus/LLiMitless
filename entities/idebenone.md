# Idebenone

## Bottom Line

Idebenone is a CoQ-like legacy mitochondrial candidate with contested dementia-era cognition evidence and weak healthy-adult support. Medicinal signal is `low/contested`: older AD trials include positives, but a later one-year AD trial failed to slow decline. Anecdotal signal is `weak-moderate`.

## What It Is

Idebenone is a synthetic benzoquinone / CoQ analog sometimes marketed or discussed as Noben or a mitochondrial nootropic. In the wiki it belongs beside [[coenzyme-q10]], [[pqq]], and [[redox-mitochondrial-cofactor-cognition-watchlist]], but with a stronger legacy-drug caveat.

## Medicinal Research Signal

The older Alzheimer-type dementia dose trial is a positive historical anchor, but the later one-year AD trial failed to slow cognitive decline. The correct read is contested legacy dementia evidence, not a broad mitochondrial cognition win.^[raw/articles/pubmed-9267856-idebenone-alzheimer-dose-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-14663031-idebenone-ad-fails-trial.md]

## Anecdote Signal

The captured Reddit thread gives `weak-moderate` salience for mitochondrial clarity or energy, with non-response and stack attribution uncertainty. It does not establish objective cognition benefit.^[raw/articles/reddit-2021-idebenone-experience-thread.md]

## Safety And Status

The main safety/interpretation gate is not a dramatic current warning; it is overreading old dementia data and extrapolating mitochondrial-energy lore to healthy cognition.

## Signal Judgment

Medicinal signal: `low/contested`.

Anecdotal signal: `weak-moderate`.

Practical route: legacy mitochondrial comparator, not a top candidate.
