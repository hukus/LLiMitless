# Nicergoline

Nicergoline is an ergot-derived vasoactive drug, historically marketed as Sermion in some countries and discussed in nootropic communities as a cerebral-blood-flow or postprandial-brain-fog candidate. It should not be collapsed into [[hydergine-ergoloid-mesylates]], ergotamine migraine products, or generic vasodilator supplements.^[raw/articles/pubchem-2026-nicergoline-identity.md]

## Bottom Line

Medicinal signal: `low-moderate/legacy clinical-state`. Nicergoline has a stronger old formal packet than many legacy nootropics: an 11-study Cochrane review, a 315-patient 1989 dementia RCT, a multi-infarct dementia trial, and a 12-month senile-dementia trial. The signal stays clinical-state and legacy because the evidence is old, phenotype-bound, and not healthy-adult enhancement.^[raw/articles/pmc-7025776-nicergoline-dementia-cochrane.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-2646350-nicergoline-dementia-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-8997547-nicergoline-multi-infarct-dementia-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-27519492-nicergoline-long-term-senile-dementia.md]

Anecdotal signal: `moderate but narrow`. The strongest captured anecdote is a single postprandial brain-fog report, which is useful for responder hypotheses but not for general cognition claims.^[raw/articles/reddit-2022-nicergoline-postprandial-brain-fog-thread.md]

Practical route: track [[nicergoline]] as a legacy vascular/clinical-state comparator, not as a general nootropic. It sits near [[hydergine-ergoloid-mesylates]], [[citicoline]], [[roflumilast]], and [[sodium-benzoate]], but safety/regulatory constraints are heavier than for many supplement-like entities.

## Formal Evidence

The 1989 multicenter placebo-controlled double-blind trial randomized 315 mild-to-moderate dementia patients to nicergoline 60 mg/day or placebo for up to 6 months after a 1-month placebo run-in. Both arms improved, but nicergoline improved more and more sustainably on SCAG, with mean total-score differences versus placebo of 5.5 at 3 months and 9.8 at 6 months. Safety judged by hemodynamic changes and drug-related adverse reactions was described as satisfactory in the abstract.^[raw/articles/pubmed-2646350-nicergoline-dementia-rct.md]

The Cochrane review found positive clinical global impression effects across six studies and 857 patients, with cognition/behavioral signals but also a modest adverse-effect increase. ADAS-Cog data in Alzheimer's disease tended to favor nicergoline but did not reach statistical significance. This supports low-moderate clinical-state tracking, not modern broad enhancement.^[raw/articles/pmc-7025776-nicergoline-dementia-cochrane.md]

Additional RCTs reinforce the same lane: a 6-month multi-infarct dementia trial used 30 mg twice daily in 136 intent-to-treat patients, and a 12-month mild-to-moderate senile dementia trial randomized 108 patients to 30 mg twice daily or placebo and found SCAG/global evaluation advantages with MMSE maintenance.^[raw/articles/pubmed-8997547-nicergoline-multi-infarct-dementia-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-27519492-nicergoline-long-term-senile-dementia.md]

## Safety And Regulatory Status

EMA's 2013 ergot-derivative referral is the main safety gate. The committee recommended restrictions for ergot-derivative medicines including nicergoline because risks of fibrosis and ergotism outweighed benefits for several blood-circulation, memory/sensation, and migraine-prevention indications. This does not invalidate every dementia-country authorization, but it blocks casual "blood-flow nootropic" framing.^[raw/articles/ema-2013-ergot-derivatives-restrictions.md]

Any current use claim needs country, product, prescription status, dose, blood pressure, vascular phenotype, and ergot-risk review. This is not a supplement-style low-friction candidate.

## Signal Judgment

Nicergoline deserves standalone coverage because the formal old evidence is deeper than the watchlist row implied and because EMA's ergot-derivative restriction makes safety context essential. It should not be upgraded into the top tier: the evidence is dementia/vascular-state rather than healthy enhancement, and modern anecdote is narrow.
