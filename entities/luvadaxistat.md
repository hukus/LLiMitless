# Luvadaxistat

Luvadaxistat, also known as TAK-831, is a D-amino acid oxidase inhibitor in the NMDA-coagonist comparator lane. It is more credible than a pure preclinical lead, but its current cognition status is secondary-only rather than primary-endpoint positive.

## Bottom Line

Medicinal signal: `low / secondary-only`. INTERACT was negative on its primary schizophrenia symptom endpoint, but the 50 mg arm produced nominal BACS and SCoRS signals. A short biomarker crossover study found mismatch-negativity movement without an eyeblink-conditioning effect, supporting target engagement rather than broad cognitive efficacy.^[raw/articles/pubmed-38943928-luvadaxistat-interact-schizophrenia.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-36928351-luvadaxistat-mmn-biomarker-schizophrenia.md]

Anecdotal signal: `not captured`. No structured consumer, patient, or nootropic-community luvadaxistat report is present in the current source corpus.

Practical route: treat [[luvadaxistat]] as a mechanistic / secondary-endpoint comparator beside [[sodium-benzoate]], not as a general enhancer or a clean schizophrenia-cognition win.

## Human Evidence

INTERACT randomized 256 adults with schizophrenia to luvadaxistat 50, 125, or 500 mg versus placebo for 12 weeks. The primary negative-symptom endpoint was negative. The 50 mg arm still generated nominal secondary signals on BACS and SCoRS, and tolerability was acceptable with no new safety signal.^[raw/articles/pubmed-38943928-luvadaxistat-interact-schizophrenia.md]

The 8-day biomarker crossover study in 31 schizophrenia patients found no effect on eyeblink conditioning. The 50 mg condition improved mismatch negativity nominally and trended on 40 Hz ASSR, which keeps the compound relevant for target-engagement or responder-selection questions.^[raw/articles/pubmed-36928351-luvadaxistat-mmn-biomarker-schizophrenia.md]

The 2025 DAO-inhibitor meta-analysis reported a positive pooled cognition signal, but that pool is heterogeneous and likely compound/phenotype dependent rather than a class-wide proof.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40328785-daao-inhibitors-schizophrenia-meta-analysis.md]

## Signal Judgment

Luvadaxistat should remain low-confidence and contested. It is a useful comparator for biomarker-positive / primary-endpoint-negative reasoning, but its current package does not justify healthy-adult cognitive-enhancement claims.
