# Lactate

Lactate is a brain fuel and signaling molecule, not a ready-made nootropic. The human physiology evidence is now strong enough for a molecule page, but the cognition-intervention evidence is still early and state-dependent.

## Medicinal Signal

- Formal signal: `low-moderate` for mechanistic human brain metabolism; `low` for cognition intervention.
- Human hyperlactatemia work shows that the brain preferentially oxidizes circulating lactate when available, with lactate accounting for roughly a quarter of cerebral oxidative metabolism at high arterial levels.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41037311-preferential-lactate-metabolism-human-brain-hyperlactataemia.md]
- Hyperpolarized 13C MRI shows visual task activation increases regional lactate signal in activated cortex, strengthening the task-metabolism bridge without proving behavioral benefit.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39862181-task-activation-regional-13c-lactate-signal-human-brain.md]
- The LEAN study moved the branch from protocol to early human readout: global cognition improved during infusion and AD fluid biomarkers fell acutely, but the study was single-arm and provisional.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39236780-lean-lactate-turnover-aging-alzheimers.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41376120-lean-lactate-infusion-cognition-biomarkers.md]

## Current Read

Lactate belongs beside [[ketone-ester]], [[creatine-cognitive-support]], and [[energy-substrate-comparator-map]] as a metabolic-state molecule. The strongest claim is "human brain substrate bridge"; the weak claim is "direct cognitive enhancer."
