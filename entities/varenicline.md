# Varenicline

## Bottom Line

Varenicline is a nicotinic partial agonist with a narrow `low` medicinal cognition signal: a 38-person randomized study in healthy non-smokers found working-memory and declarative-memory improvement, but the result is small, old, and not yet a replicated healthy-adult enhancer package. Anecdotal cognition signal is `absent-to-weak` in the current wiki corpus; smoking-cessation and tolerability signals dominate.

## What It Is

Varenicline is an FDA-approved smoking-cessation drug and a partial agonist at nicotinic acetylcholine receptors. In this wiki it belongs with [[cholinergic-and-nicotinic-agents-2026-scan]], [[cholinergic-state-restoration-and-cognition]], [[nicotine]], and [[pharmacology-comparator-map]] as a nicotinic cognition comparator.

## Medicinal Research Signal

The direct cognition anchor is a randomized, double-blind study of short-term varenicline in 38 healthy, non-smoking adults. The captured summary reports improved working memory and declarative memory versus placebo, with no change in mood, negative emotional bias, or impulsivity. This is the cleanest non-nicotine nicotinic healthy-adult cognition signal in the current branch, but the sample is too small to support a stable enhancement claim.^[raw/articles/pubmed-23072834-varenicline-healthy-non-smoking-adults.md]

## Safety And Status

Varenicline should stay prescription/smoking-cessation-first. The DailyMed label notes neuropsychiatric events, seizures, alcohol interaction, accidental injury, cardiovascular events in patients with underlying cardiovascular disease, somnambulism, and nausea. FDA consumer guidance also highlights nausea, constipation, gas, vomiting, trouble sleeping, vivid dreams, and alcohol-tolerance changes.^[raw/articles/dailymed-varenicline-tablets-label.md]^[raw/articles/fda-want-to-quit-smoking-cessation-products.md]
