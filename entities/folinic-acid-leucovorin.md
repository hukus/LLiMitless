# Folinic Acid / Leucovorin

Folinic acid, leucovorin, calcium folinate, or 5-formyl-tetrahydrofolate is a reduced folate form used clinically in oncology rescue and folate-disorder contexts and discussed in methylation forums as a non-methylfolate alternative. In this wiki it is the folinic-acid route beside [[folate-b-vitamins]], [[l-methylfolate-5-mthf]], [[methylcobalamin-b12]], and [[one-carbon-methyl-donor-cognition-watchlist]]. It should not inherit methylfolate, folic-acid, or B12 evidence automatically.

Medicinal cognition signal: `low for healthy adults; high relevance only for narrow medical status`. The strongest current reason to track leucovorin is FDA's 2026 approval of Wellcovorin for adult and pediatric patients with confirmed `FOLR1`-variant cerebral folate deficiency / cerebral folate transport deficiency. That is a rare, serious neurological condition and the approval was based on literature/case-report and mechanistic evidence, not a healthy-adult cognition trial.^[raw/articles/fda-2026-wellcovorin-cfd-folr1-approval.md]

Pediatric autism / folate-receptor-autoantibody signal: `low-moderate but phenotype-bound and contested`. A small randomized trial in children with ASD and language impairment found 12 weeks of high-dose folinic acid improved verbal communication, with larger response in folate-receptor-alpha-autoantibody-positive participants. A 2021 review/meta-analysis presents a broader supportive CFD/FRAA/leucovorin map, but it is author/conflict-aware and still pediatric/clinical-state evidence. A more recent 2024 positive European Journal of Pediatrics RCT is now indexed as retracted, with a 2026 retraction notice, so it should be treated only as a source-integrity warning.^[raw/articles/pubmed-27752075-folinic-acid-autism-language-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-34834493-leucovorin-cfd-autism-meta-review.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39243316-41606385-folinic-acid-autism-retraction.md]

Anecdote signal: `weak-moderate and bidirectional`. MTHFR / methylation communities often use folinic acid as a "gentler" alternative when methylfolate or methylated B-complexes are blamed for anxiety, insomnia, or overactivation. The captured thread cluster also includes folinic-acid brain fog, anxiety, insomnia, or crash-like reactions. This is useful for dose/form and adverse-event tracking, not efficacy upgrading.^[raw/articles/reddit-2024-2026-folinic-acid-methylfolate-brainfog-threads.md]

Safety and interpretation signal: `medical-context gated`. FDA lists possible leucovorin side effects including itching, rash, hives, dyspnea, rigors, impaired thermoregulation, and serious anaphylaxis. For cognition or brain-fog interpretation, folate, B12, methylmalonic acid, homocysteine, kidney function, seizure/antiepileptic context, oncology-drug context, `FOLR1`/FRAA status where clinically relevant, dose, route, and co-supplement forms matter.

## Practical Interpretation

Folinic acid deserves a standalone page because the 2026 FDA approval and autism/FRAA literature make it too specific to hide under generic folate. It is still not a healthy-adult cognition lead. The practical route is `transport/status phenotype first`: confirmed `FOLR1`-CFD, cerebral-folate-deficiency workup, FRAA/ASD clinical context, folate/B12 labs, and medication interactions should come before any subjective brain-fog interpretation.

Any confidence upgrade for healthy adults would require folinic-acid-alone trials with objective cognition endpoints, baseline folate/B12/MMA/homocysteine status, separation from methylfolate/folic acid/B12/choline/SAMe/TMG co-use, and adverse-event reporting for anxiety, insomnia, activation, and hypersensitivity.
