# Kava / Kavalactones

## Bottom Line

Kava is a sedative/anxiolytic botanical with risk-first cognition routing. Medicinal signal is `risk-first/low`: anxiety evidence does not cleanly translate to cognition, and the captured GAD RCT was null with poorer memory and more liver-function abnormalities. Anecdotal signal is `weak-moderate` for calm/social focus.

## What It Is

Kava comes from Piper methysticum and contains kavalactones. The wiki keeps kava separate from CBD, kratom, alcohol, benzodiazepines, and generic "calm focus" stacks because liver, sedative, cultivar, preparation, and product-quality issues materially change interpretation.

## Medicinal Research Signal

NCCIH states that kava has human anxiety research, little evidence for other conditions, and rare but sometimes severe liver-injury concerns. The captured 2020 16-week GAD RCT was not positive and reported poorer memory, tremor/shakiness, and more liver-function-test abnormalities in the kava group.^[raw/articles/nccih-2025-kava-usefulness-safety.md]^[raw/articles/sage-2020-kava-gad-rct.md]

## Anecdote Signal

The captured nootropic thread gives `weak-moderate` salience for calm, social ease, and focus-like effects. These are likely mood/anxiety-mediated and should not be interpreted as direct cognition improvement.^[raw/articles/reddit-2021-kava-natural-nootropics-thread.md]

## Safety And Status

Kava should be treated as liver- and sedative-interaction-sensitive. Product identity, cultivar, plant part, extraction method, co-use with alcohol/sedatives, and chronic high intake all matter.

## Signal Judgment

Medicinal signal: `risk-first/low`.

Anecdotal signal: `weak-moderate` for calm/social state.

Practical route: anxiolytic botanical with liver/product gates, not a cognition enhancer.
