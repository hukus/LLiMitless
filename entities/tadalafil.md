# Tadalafil

Tadalafil is a long-acting PDE5 inhibitor best known through Cialis and generic tadalafil labels. It belongs in the wiki as a PDE5 / cGMP / vascular-cognition comparator, not as a proven cognitive enhancer. PubChem establishes the small-molecule identity; DailyMed establishes prescription indication and safety boundaries, neither of which is cognition efficacy evidence.^[raw/articles/pubchem-2026-tadalafil-identity.md]^[raw/articles/dailymed-2026-tadalafil-label.md]

Medicinal cognition signal: `low / negative-to-indirect`. The strongest direct recent human evidence is ETLAS-2 in cerebral small vessel disease: tadalafil 20 mg daily for 3 months did not improve cognition overall, had worse adherence, and produced substantially more adverse events than placebo. The preregistered cognitive sub-study also failed to show broad cognitive benefit and reported slower simple reaction time in the tadalafil group at follow-up.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40718899-tadalafil-csVD-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41332889-tadalafil-csVD-cognition-substudy.md]

Mechanistic signal: `plausible but unproven`. PDE5 inhibition can be linked to nitric-oxide / cGMP signaling, vascular function, neurovascular coupling, synaptic plasticity, and CREB-style memory biology. A 2025 PDE5 cognitive-decline review keeps the class mechanistically interesting, but it does not override the tadalafil, [[sildenafil]], and [[vardenafil]] human cognition misses.^[raw/articles/mdpi-cells-14191505-pde5-cognitive-decline-review.md]

## Signal Judgment

Tadalafil should be treated as a vascular/cGMP hypothesis and anecdote-surveillance node. It is more relevant when a brain-fog story is explicitly vascular, endothelial, exercise, ED/libido, or hypotension/autonomic-context linked. It should not be promoted into the healthy-adult cognition tier without a controlled cognitive endpoint that separates from placebo and from blood-pressure, sleep, sex-function, and exercise confounds.

## Safety Gates

- Prescription PDE5 inhibitor; do not generalize supplement-style use.
- Avoid with organic nitrates; dangerous hypotension is the core hard gate.^[raw/articles/dailymed-2026-tadalafil-label.md]
- Avoid with soluble guanylate cyclase stimulators such as riociguat.
- Treat alpha-blockers, antihypertensives, alcohol, dehydration, heat exposure, and aggressive vasodilator stacks as hypotension/dizziness amplifiers.
- Be cautious around nitrate/beetroot, citrulline, arginine, nitrite, and other nitric-oxide stacks; this matters for [[dietary-nitrate-beetroot]] and gym-focused nootropic anecdotes.
- CYP3A4 inhibitors, renal impairment, hepatic impairment, and cardiovascular disease change exposure or risk.
- Watch for headache, flushing, dyspepsia, nasal congestion, back pain, myalgia, limb pain, dizziness, priapism, sudden vision loss, and sudden hearing loss.
