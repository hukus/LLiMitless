# KDS2010 / Tisolagiline

KDS2010 / tisolagiline is a reversible MAO-B inhibitor being developed for Alzheimer's disease. It is the live frontier item in the [[mao-b-propargylamine-cognition-watchlist]] branch.

## Current Read

Medicinal cognition signal: `preclinical-plus-protocol`. The strongest published cognition-adjacent signal is still mouse-model work: KDS2010 reduced astrocytic GABA and astrogliosis, enhanced synaptic transmission, and rescued learning/memory impairments in APP/PS1 mice. The same paper argues that prolonged [[selegiline]] failed in the model because compensatory D-amino acid oxidase limited astrocytic-GABA reduction.^[raw/articles/pubmed-30906861-kds2010-reversible-maob-ad-mice.md]

Human development signal: `active trial, no efficacy result yet`. ClinicalTrials.gov lists NCT07027072 as a recruiting randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled dose-finding phase 2a trial in MCI or mild dementia due to Alzheimer's disease, with 114 planned participants randomized to placebo, KDS2010 60 mg, or KDS2010 120 mg for 24 weeks.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-2026-kds2010-ad-phase2a.md]

Endpoints include CDR-SB, MMSE, ADAS-Cog13, A-IADL-Q-SV, and biomarkers including MAO-B activity, GFAP, p-tau181/217, A-beta 40/42, NfL, BDNF, IL-1beta, and TNF-alpha. Estimated primary completion is 2027-06-30 and estimated full completion is 2027-12-31.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-2026-kds2010-ad-phase2a.md]

## Safety And Trial Gates

The active AD trial excludes recent MAOIs/linezolid, several opioids, cyclobenzaprine, St. John's wort, serotonergic drugs, sympathomimetics, and dextromethorphan. That exclusion pattern is a practical reminder that reversible does not mean stack-friendly.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-2026-kds2010-ad-phase2a.md]

## Current Route

Track KDS2010 through [[mao-b-propargylamine-cognition-watchlist]] and compare it to [[sembragiline]] before upgrading any biomarker claim. Current verdict: promising enough to watch, but as of 2026-05-15 it is not a human cognition-positive molecule.
