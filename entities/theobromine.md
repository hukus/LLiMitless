# Theobromine

Theobromine is a cocoa-rich methylxanthine related to [[caffeine]], [[paraxanthine]], theophylline, [[theacrine]], and [[methylliberine]]. It belongs in the wiki as a caffeine-adjacent, cocoa-confounded stimulant/mood candidate, not as proof that chocolate or cacao is a clean nootropic.

## Current Read

Medicinal cognition signal: `low / not caffeine-equivalent`. The direct healthy-volunteer psychopharmacology study tested 250, 500, and 1000 mg theobromine against placebo and 200 mg caffeine. It is the key theobromine-alone human anchor, and its readout is dose-sensitive: limited positive subjective effects at 250 mg, with more negative mood effects at higher doses.^[raw/articles/pubmed-23420115-theobromine-healthy-volunteers-psychopharmacology.md]

Vascular / metabolic proxy signal: `weak and mostly not cognition-relevant`. A 4-week crossover trial of 500 mg/day theobromine in 44 overweight or obese adults with low HDL-C did not improve fasting or postprandial endothelial function; selected arterial-diameter / augmentation-index measures moved, but the authors concluded the findings did not suggest that theobromine alone explains cocoa cardioprotection. A 12-week metabolic-syndrome trial used 450 mg/day with a low-calorie diet and focused on metabolic indices rather than cognition.^[raw/articles/pmc-6499748-theobromine-vascular-function-null.md]^[raw/articles/rsc-2023-theobromine-metabolic-syndrome-rct.md]

Product-confounding read: cocoa cognition evidence should mostly stay on [[cocoa-flavanols]]. Cocoa products mix flavanols, epicatechin/procyanidins, caffeine, theobromine, fat/sugar matrix, alkalization, and heavy-metal/product-quality variables. The young-adult cocoa review is useful here because it makes cocoa cognition evidence visible while showing why matched caffeine/theobromine controls matter.^[raw/articles/mdpi-2020-cocoa-young-adults-cognition-review.md]

Anecdote signal: `weak-moderate / smoother stimulant`. 2021-2026 forum threads include focus, smoother energy than caffeine, workout mood, anxiolysis/calm, sleep-compatible stimulation, and stack-synergy claims with caffeine, [[theacrine]], or L-theanine. These reports are too confounded by cacao matrix, caffeine, flavanols, magnesium, exercise context, product quality, and expectancy to upgrade confidence.^[raw/articles/reddit-2021-2026-theobromine-cocoa-focus-threads.md]

## Safety And Interpretation Gates

- Separate isolated theobromine from cocoa powder, cacao paste, dark chocolate, cocoa extract, and high-flavanol supplements.
- Do not borrow [[caffeine]], [[paraxanthine]], [[theacrine]], or [[cocoa-flavanols]] effects by analogy.
- Track total methylxanthine load: theobromine plus caffeine, theacrine, methylliberine, guarana, preworkout stimulants, or prescription stimulants can change sleep, palpitations, BP/HR, anxiety, and tolerance.
- Watch GI effects, headache, sweating/tremor at high intake, sleep timing, palpitations, anxiety/panic sensitivity, cardiovascular disease, pregnancy/breastfeeding, and cocoa-product heavy metals.
- Route theobromine, cocoa/cacao, and methylxanthine-stack anecdotes through [[safety-interaction-matrix-for-multi-modal-stacks]] before treating calm stimulation, workout mood, or smoother focus as a molecule-specific cognition signal.

## Current Route

Theobromine belongs in [[common-nootropic-gray-zone-molecule-watchlist]] as a standalone low-confidence methylxanthine comparator. It is useful for interpreting cacao/cocoa focus claims and theacrine/caffeine stack reports, but current evidence keeps it below [[caffeine]] and below [[paraxanthine]] for direct cognition evidence.
