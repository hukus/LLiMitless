# Oveporexton

## Bottom Line

Oveporexton / TAK-861 is the strongest current orexin-agonist cognition molecule in the wiki, but the signal is still disease-context state restoration, not healthy enhancement. Medicinal signal is `moderate` for narcolepsy type 1 cognition/wakefulness: the phase 2 trial improved wakefulness and cataplexy, and a cognition secondary analysis reported attention, memory, and executive-function gains. Anecdotal cognition signal is `absent-to-weak` in the current corpus.

## What It Is

Oveporexton is an oral orexin receptor 2-selective agonist developed for narcolepsy type 1. In this wiki it belongs with [[orexin-axis-molecule-watchlist]], [[orexin-hypocretin-axis-interventions-2026-scan]], [[pharmacology-comparator-map]], and [[cognitive-fidelity-split]] as a frontier state-restoration comparator.

## Medicinal Research Signal

The parent phase 2 randomized placebo-controlled trial in narcolepsy type 1 showed large improvements in wakefulness and cataplexy over 8 weeks in 112 randomized participants. That is the cleanest human proof-of-mechanism for orexin agonism in the current corpus.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40367374-oveporexton-narcolepsy-type-1.md]

The cognition secondary analysis then made the molecule directly cognition-relevant. It reported improvements in attention, memory, and executive-function measures, with placebo-adjusted PVT lapse reductions of roughly 8.7 to 10.8 lapses and sizable CPAL improvements. This is the strongest direct orexin-axis cognition signal in the wiki, but it still sits inside orexin-deficient NT1 and should not be generalized to well-rested healthy adults.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41359331-orexin-agonist-cognition-narcolepsy-secondary-analysis.md]

## Safety And Status

Oveporexton should be interpreted as a clinical development molecule. The key safety comparison is not "orexin agonism is clean"; TAK-994 already showed the class can have serious liver-safety problems, so molecule-specific safety and replication matter before any broader performance framing. Route orexin-agonist cognition reports through [[safety-interaction-matrix-for-multi-modal-stacks]] so sleepiness-state restoration, sleep debt, liver-safety monitoring, and stimulant/wake-promoter co-use stay separate from healthy-enhancement claims.

June 2026 wakefulness-state attribution gate: oveporexton should not be promoted as a broad nootropic unless future reports capture narcolepsy type, orexin/hypocretin deficiency status where available, baseline sleepiness and cataplexy burden, prior night's sleep, sleep debt, circadian timing, dose and split-dose timing, trial versus non-trial product status, liver enzymes and adverse events, co-use of modafinil-class drugs, solriamfetol, pitolisant, stimulants, caffeine, nicotine, antidepressants, or sedatives, washout from other wake-promoters, and objective endpoints such as PVT lapses, memory tasks, executive-function tasks, MWT, ESS, or work-output measures. Route informal claims through [[molecule-triage-worksheet-for-online-scans]], [[stack-attribution-diary-for-self-study-and-n-of-1-protocols]], [[30-day-self-study-template-for-nonprescription-interventions]], [[30-day-self-study-workbook-schema]], and [[self-experimentation-stop-rules]] before comparing it with [[modafinil]], [[solriamfetol]], [[pitolisant]], [[bromantane]], [[semax]], or [[selank]].
