# Tropisetron

Tropisetron, also known as Navoban or ICS 205-930, is a 5-HT3 receptor antagonist antiemetic with alpha7 nicotinic partial-agonist relevance in the cognition literature. It belongs in the wiki because small schizophrenia trials and nootropic-community science posts treat it as an alpha7 / setron cognition candidate, while the healthy-adult cognition bridge remains missing.

Identity signal: `antiemetic first`. NCBI MeSH describes tropisetron as an indole derivative and 5-HT3 receptor antagonist used for preventing nausea and vomiting, with entry terms including Navoban, ICS 205-930, and tropisetron hydrochloride.^[raw/articles/ncbi-mesh-2026-tropisetron-identity.md]

Medicinal cognition signal: `low / disease-state`. In a 2010 randomized double-blind trial, 40 chronic schizophrenia patients on risperidone were assigned to tropisetron 10 mg/day or placebo for 8 weeks. Tropisetron improved P50 sensory-gating deficits and CANTAB rapid visual information processing in non-smoking patients, with 33 completers and no reported adverse side effects.^[raw/articles/springer-2010-tropisetron-schizophrenia-cognition-p50-rct.md] A 2020 one-day study in 40 non-smoking schizophrenia patients found RBANS total-score or immediate-memory improvements in some 5-20 mg tropisetron groups and P50-ratio improvement at 5 and 10 mg.^[raw/articles/nature-2020-one-day-tropisetron-schizophrenia-rbans-p50.md]

Class-level caution: `sensory-gating stronger than general cognition`. A 2022 systematic review found consistent evidence that ondansetron and tropisetron improved sensory gating in schizophrenia, while evidence for other sensory-processing outcomes was inconsistent and healthy-participant evidence did not establish a general enhancer effect.^[raw/articles/nature-2022-5ht3-antagonists-sensory-processing-review.md]

## Anecdote Signal

The visible threads mostly build a science-post argument from alpha7 nicotinic receptor pharmacology, schizophrenia trials, GTS-21 comparisons, setron tolerability, and nonhuman evidence rather than dense first-person healthy-adult testing.^[raw/articles/reddit-2022-2026-tropisetron-alpha7-nootropic-threads.md]
