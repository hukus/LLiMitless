# Bupropion

Bupropion is a prescription aminoketone antidepressant and smoking-cessation drug with norepinephrine/dopamine reuptake and nicotinic-antagonist relevance. In this wiki it belongs near [[atomoxetine]], [[nonstimulant-noradrenergic-attention-agent-watchlist]], [[nicotine]], [[methylphenidate]], and [[pharmacology-comparator-map]], but it should not be treated as a casual nootropic.

## Bottom Line

Brain-fog / cognition signal: `low formal, moderate mixed anecdotal`. The cleanest healthy-adult cognition study found that acute bupropion 150 mg reduced lapses on a simple reaction-time attention task, while stop-signal, delay/probability discounting, and risk-taking endpoints did not improve. The 300 mg condition increased subjective arousal, which is a warning that felt focus can be stimulation rather than broad cognition.^[raw/articles/pubmed-18489015-bupropion-attention-healthy-adults.md]

Clinical-state signal: `low-to-moderate for ADHD-like symptoms, not healthy enhancement`. Cochrane-level adult ADHD evidence suggests possible benefit versus placebo but remains limited, and the nicotine-deprivation literature is a withdrawal/state-restoration lane rather than proof of enhancement in already-rested healthy adults.^[raw/articles/cochrane-2017-bupropion-adult-adhd-review.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-21942262-varenicline-bupropion-nicotine-deprived-smokers-cognition.md]

Practical route: prescription/safety-first. DailyMed warning gates include dose-related seizure risk, hypertension, mania/hypomania activation, neuropsychiatric adverse events, suicidality language, CYP2B6 exposure modifiers, and CYP2D6 inhibition. Eating-disorder history, alcohol/sedative withdrawal, stimulant stacking, insomnia/anxiety, and BP/HR should dominate any self-experiment framing.^[raw/articles/dailymed-2026-wellbutrin-xl-label.md]

## Evidence Layers

| Layer | Current read | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| Healthy adults | Small acute attention signal only | A reaction-time lapse reduction is useful, but not enough to call bupropion a broad enhancer |
| Adult ADHD | Possible benefit versus placebo | Clinical-state/off-label attention evidence; do not export directly to healthy adults |
| Nicotine-deprived smokers | State-bound cognition effects appear in cessation literature | Withdrawal relief and smoking state confound any nootropic interpretation |
| Anecdote | Motivation/focus positives plus word-finding, memory, anxiety, and fog counterreports | Mixed enough to track, not clean enough to rank high |

## Current Read

Bupropion is worth keeping in the molecule atlas because it has one direct healthy-adult attention signal, a clinical ADHD/off-label evidence trail, and a visible community signal around motivation and focus. It should stay below [[modafinil]], [[methylphenidate]], and [[caffeine]] as a benchmark for healthy-adult cognition, and it should not inherit evidence from [[tesofensine]], [[nomifensine]], or other dopamine/norepinephrine reuptake compounds by analogy.
