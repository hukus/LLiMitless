# Lithium Orotate

Lithium orotate is an over-the-counter lithium salt and formulation-specific AD hypothesis, not a proven cognition enhancer. It should not be collapsed into prescription lithium carbonate/citrate, trace dietary lithium, or the broad lithium mood-stabilizer literature.

## Medicinal Signal

- Formal cognition signal: `protocol-only/contested` for humans.
- The 2025 Nature paper gives the strongest reason to track lithium orotate: low-dose lithium orotate outperformed lithium carbonate in the paper's mouse AD rescue work, while human tissue data showed lower endogenous lithium and plaque sequestration in MCI/AD.^[raw/articles/nature-2025-lithium-deficiency-onset-alzheimers-disease.md]
- The NIH translation summary explicitly keeps the dose/safety question open and says human testing is still needed before lithium orotate can be recommended.^[raw/articles/nih-2025-lithium-levels-tied-alzheimers-disease-and-dementia.md]
- The LiO-AD registry is the key human bridge: not yet recruiting/no-results as of 2026-05-04, 40 planned biomarker-confirmed early-AD participants, 9 weeks of lithium orotate versus placebo, primary feasibility/safety/tolerability outcomes, CSF lithium target engagement, NfL, NPI-Q, and delayed recall.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct07459959-lio-ad-record-2026-05-04.md]
- Older rat pharmacokinetic data did not show a clear uptake, distribution, or excretion advantage for lithium orotate over carbonate or chloride under the tested conditions, which counters simple "orotate is automatically superior" claims.^[raw/articles/pubmed-1260219-lithium-orotate-rat-pharmacokinetics.md]

## Anecdote And Market Signal

- Anecdote signal: `moderate` salience for calm/mood/anxiety/focus/sleep reports, `weak` for objective cognition.
- A 2025 survey of 211 OTC lithium supplement users found perceived cognition, mood, and anxiety benefits, but also side effects and withdrawal phenomena; the most common product was lithium aspartate, with lithium orotate and ionic lithium also common.^[raw/articles/canadian-jpsychiatry-2025-lithium-supplement-survey.md]
- A 2025 r/Supplements thread had mixed reports: strong calm/focus positives and long-term use claims alongside no-effect, fatigue/grogginess, headache, emotional flatness, crying, brand differences, and monitoring confusion.^[raw/articles/reddit-2025-07-13-lithium-orotate-supplements-thread.md]
- A 2025 r/NootropicsDepot product-demand thread shows the Nature paper translating into vendor interest and self-directed dosing discussion, including 1-5 mg dosing claims and a brain-fog counterexample.^[raw/articles/reddit-2025-12-13-lithium-orotate-nootropicsdepot-thread.md]

## Signal Strength Ledger

| Evidence layer | Signal strength | Read |
|---|---|---|
| Mouse/tissue AD mechanism | `preclinical/mechanistic` | High-salience hypothesis, not human efficacy |
| LiO-AD registry | `protocol-only` | Safety, feasibility, CSF target engagement, and delayed recall are pending |
| Supplement survey | `moderate` anecdote | Useful real-world signal but self-selected and salt-mixed |
| Forum / vendor-demand anecdotes | `moderate` salience, `weak` cognition evidence | Mixed calm/focus/sleep reports plus adverse/no-effect/dose-confusion counterweights |
| Safety / monitoring | `high-burden` | Prescription lithium risks and LiO-AD exclusions remain relevant even if dose is lower |

## Safety Read

The safety anchor is still the lithium prescription label and the LiO-AD exclusion logic, not supplement marketing. Lithium has narrow-therapeutic-index history, renal and thyroid concerns, sodium-balance and dehydration sensitivity, and clinically important drug-interaction issues.^[raw/articles/dailymed-lithium-oral-solution-medication-guide.md] Lithium-orotate animal toxicology and narrative reviews are not enough to validate chronic unsupervised cognitive use.^[raw/articles/pubmed-34196467-lithium-orotate-superior-option-review.md]^[raw/articles/sciencedirect-2021-lithium-orotate-toxicology.md]

## Current Read

Lithium orotate deserves standalone tracking because it is formulation-specific, high-salience after the 2025 Nature paper, and safety-sensitive. It remains a `protocol-only` human medicinal signal until LiO-AD or another controlled human trial posts target-engagement, cognition, renal/thyroid, and adverse-event results.
