# Xanomeline

## Bottom Line

Xanomeline is the historical direct-cognition anchor for the muscarinic branch, but its signal is capped by tolerability. Medicinal signal is `low-moderate in Alzheimer's disease, not healthy enhancement`: older AD trials reported ADAS-Cog, CIBIC+, CNTB, reaction-time, and delayed-recall benefits, but high-dose discontinuation and syncope make it a poor nootropic model. Anecdotal signal is absent in the current corpus.

## What It Is

Xanomeline is a centrally acting muscarinic receptor agonist with strongest relevance to M1/M4 cognition and psychosis hypotheses. It is the active central component later paired with trospium in [[xanomeline-trospium]] / Cobenfy / KarXT. In this wiki it belongs beside [[muscarinic-agent-cognition-watchlist]] and [[cholinergic-state-restoration-and-cognition]], not with low-risk supplements.

## Medicinal Research Signal

The key older AD trial randomized 343 people with mild-to-moderate AD. High-dose xanomeline improved ADAS-Cog and CIBIC+ versus placebo and reduced behavioral symptoms including delusions, hallucinations, agitation, and suspiciousness. The same note records the practical problem: GI adverse effects were dose-dependent, 52% of the high-dose group discontinued because of adverse events, and syncope occurred in the high-dose arm.^[raw/articles/pubmed-9109749-xanomeline-alzheimer-cognitive-behavior.md]

The companion CNTB analysis strengthened the cognition signal by reporting a dose-response trend and a 75 mg TID versus placebo signal for the CNTB summary score, with benefits on simple reaction time and delayed verbal recall. It also showed how fragile interpretation can be around washout, so the signal should be read as old disease-context pharmacology rather than durable enhancement proof.^[raw/articles/pubmed-9876958-xanomeline-ad-cntb.md]

A 2026 systematic review argues that xanomeline and xanomeline-trospium have cognitive relevance in schizophrenia, bipolar disorder, and possibly AD, with verbal-learning, delayed-memory, and reaction-time signals. Because the source is a synthesis with industry-adjacent conflict disclosures, it should strengthen the hypothesis rather than settle the efficacy claim.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41418563-xanomeline-trospium-cognitive-impairment-review.md]

## Safety And Status

Xanomeline's main lesson is that muscarinic cognition can be real and still impractical. The high-dose AD signal came with heavy cholinergic toxicity, including GI events, high discontinuation, and syncope. This is why the modern branch shifted toward [[xanomeline-trospium]] and receptor-selective PAMs such as [[vu0467319-vu319]] and [[emraclidine]].

## Signal Judgment

Medicinal signal: `low-moderate` for impaired AD cognition, but with high tolerability burden.

Anecdotal signal: `absent-to-weak`.

Practical route: historical clinical comparator and proof that muscarinic activation can move cognition, not a viable broad enhancer.
