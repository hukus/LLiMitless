# S-Adenosyl-L-Methionine / SAMe

S-adenosyl-L-methionine is an endogenous [[l-methionine]]-derived methyl donor, also called S-adenosylmethionine, SAMe, SAM-e, ademetionine, SAM, or AdoMet. This page is the wiki route for the user's "S-adenosyl-L-methionin / methionin" spelling variants. It belongs in the [[one-carbon-methyl-donor-cognition-watchlist]] beside [[homocysteine]], [[l-methionine]], [[l-methylfolate-5-mthf]], [[folinic-acid-leucovorin]], [[methylcobalamin-b12]], [[betaine-tmg]], [[dimethylglycine-dmg]], [[glycine]], [[folate-b-vitamins]], [[choline-donor-cognition-signal-map]], and [[creatine]]. PubChem returns multiple closely related zwitterionic or cationic SAMe records for the queried name, so the wiki treats chemical identity, salt/formulation identity, and clinical evidence as separate gates.^[raw/articles/pubchem-2026-s-adenosyl-l-methionine-identity.md]

Medicinal cognition signal: `low and mood-state bound`. The best formal SAMe evidence is for depressive symptoms, not healthy-adult cognition. A 2024 depression meta-analysis found SAMe monotherapy superior to placebo for depressive symptoms, while adjunctive SAMe plus antidepressants did not clearly beat placebo plus antidepressants and SAMe did not clearly differ from antidepressants alone. A broad 2024 CNS systematic review also found most positive SAMe studies were depressive-symptom studies, with mixed results and limited long-term data.^[raw/articles/pubmed-38423354-same-depression-meta-analysis.md]^[raw/articles/pmc-11435340-same-cns-systematic-review.md]

The closest direct cognitive-symptom signal is a small 2012 secondary analysis in 46 SRI-nonresponding MDD patients: adjunctive oral SAMe improved self-rated recall versus placebo, with only a trend for word-finding and no significant signal on the remaining questionnaire items. This is useful for depression-linked cognitive symptoms, but it is subjective, secondary, and not a healthy-adult endpoint.^[raw/articles/pubmed-21665441-same-cognitive-symptoms-mdd.md]

The strongest current disease-context counterweight is a 2026 phase 2 AD/MCI trial: 400 mg/day oral SAMe for 180 days did not significantly improve plasma p-tau217, safety, tolerability, or other secondary endpoints, including cognitive outcomes. A 2010 multinutrient formulation containing folic acid, B12, vitamin E, SAMe, NAC, and ALCAR improved memory and Trail-Making measures in adults without dementia, but attribution belongs to the formula, not SAMe alone.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41980907-same-ad-phase2.md]^[raw/articles/sciencedirect-2010-nutriceutical-formulation-same-cognition.md]

Anecdote signal: `weak-moderate, polarized, and medication-confounded`. Reddit reports include focus, mood, anhedonia, stimulant-break, and brain-fog improvement claims, but also depression, anxiety, insomnia, depersonalization-like feelings, memory problems, and "overmethylation" interpretations. The class-level anecdote is useful for safety and responder hypotheses, not for efficacy ranking.^[raw/articles/reddit-2022-2026-same-tmg-methyl-donor-brainfog-threads.md]

Safety signal: `moderate caution`. NCCIH flags limited long-term safety data, limited pregnancy safety data, bipolar/mania risk, possible levodopa interaction, possible interaction with serotonin-increasing drugs or supplements, and theoretical Pneumocystis concern in immunocompromised people. The CNS review also reports GI complaints, headache, anxiety, excitation, sleep disruption, behavioral changes, mania/hypomania, and oral-formulation variability.^[raw/articles/nccih-2017-same-in-depth.md]^[raw/articles/pmc-11435340-same-cns-systematic-review.md]

Boundary with [[l-methionine]]: L-methionine is the dietary/supplement precursor, but it should not be treated as interchangeable with SAMe. Human methionine-loading work shows movement of SAMe/SAH/homocysteine markers, and supplement-tolerance work uses homocysteine as a safety boundary. That makes methionine a lab-gated pathway perturbation, not a cleaner nootropic substitute.^[raw/articles/pubmed-8774264-methionine-loading-same-homocysteine.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-28637772-methionine-tolerance-healthy-older-adults.md]

Boundary with [[homocysteine]] / SAH: homocysteine and S-adenosylhomocysteine are downstream pathway readouts, not cognition outcomes. Their main value here is attribution control: SAMe, methionine, TMG, folate/B12/B6/B2, choline, and creatine claims should be interpreted through measured status rather than forum labels such as "overmethylation" or "undermethylation."^[raw/articles/pubchem-2026-homocysteine-identity.md]^[raw/articles/pubchem-2026-s-adenosylhomocysteine-identity.md]

Boundary with [[glutathione-products]]: SAMe/methionine claims often drift into transsulfuration, cysteine, NAC, GlyNAC, and "detox" stories. Keep direct reduced/liposomal/micellar/IV-style glutathione products in the redox lane rather than letting them inherit SAMe mood-state or methyl-donor evidence.

Boundary with [[methylsulfonylmethane-msm]]: MSM is a dimethyl-sulfone organosulfur supplement with human brain MRS exposure and cell-model methyl-donor evidence, but it is not SAMe, not methionine, and not proof of one-carbon cognitive support. Keep MSM sulfur/detox/joint-health anecdotes in an attribution-control lane unless objective cognition evidence appears.^[raw/articles/sciencedirect-2001-methylsulfonylmethane-human-brain-mrs.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-36469606-msm-dna-methyl-donor-heparg.md]

Boundary with [[sarcosine]]: sarcosine is N-methylglycine and belongs beside [[glycine]], [[dimethylglycine-dmg]], and [[betaine-tmg]] as a methylated-glycine identity boundary, but its cognition literature mostly routes through NMDA/GlyT1 and [[sodium-benzoate]] combination hypotheses. Do not let sarcosine anecdotes inherit SAMe mood-state or methyl-donor evidence.^[raw/articles/pubchem-2026-sarcosine-identity.md]

## Signal Split

| Layer | Signal | Readout |
|---|---|---|
| Chemical identity | `methylsulfonium pathway molecule` | PubChem identity supports name normalization; it does not establish supplement-form equivalence or efficacy |
| Depression meta-analysis | `low-moderate mood-state` | SAMe monotherapy improved depressive symptoms versus placebo, but adjunctive and antidepressant-comparison questions remain unresolved |
| CNS systematic review | `low CNS-symptom` | 24 of 36 studies positive, mainly depressive symptoms; doses 200-3200 mg/day; mixed placebo and comparator results |
| MDD cognitive symptoms | `low, subjective` | Recall improved in one secondary analysis; word-finding trend; other questionnaire items null |
| AD/MCI phase 2 | `negative/current counterweight` | 400 mg/day for 180 days did not significantly improve p-tau217 or secondary endpoints |
| Multinutrient formula | `formula-level only` | Folate/B12/vitamin E/SAMe/NAC/ALCAR formula improved selected tests, but single-ingredient attribution is invalid |
| Anecdote | `weak-moderate bidirectional` | Focus/mood/brain-fog positives coexist with anxiety, insomnia, depression, memory problems, and depersonalization-like reports |
| Safety | `moderate caution` | Bipolar/mania, serotonergic co-use, levodopa, pregnancy, immunocompromise, GI, sleep, anxiety, and formulation-quality gates |
| L-methionine precursor boundary | `biomarker/safety only` | Methionine loading can move SAMe/SAH/homocysteine; this does not establish direct cognition benefit |
| Homocysteine / SAH boundary | `biomarker/status gate` | Useful for interpreting methyl-donor stacks, but not a cognition endpoint |

## Practical Interpretation

SAMe should be tracked because it is a real endogenous methyl donor with depression RCT/meta-analytic evidence and a small depression-linked cognitive-symptom signal. It should not be ranked as a general nootropic. The best current read is `depression-linked subjective cognitive symptom support, low confidence`, counterweighted by an AD/MCI phase 2 miss and safety/interaction gates.

Any future confidence upgrade needs a trial that directly measures objective cognition, separates mood response from cognitive response, stratifies folate/B12/MMA/homocysteine status, records antidepressant and stimulant co-use, separates [[l-methionine]], [[l-methylfolate-5-mthf]], [[folinic-acid-leucovorin]], [[methylcobalamin-b12]], [[betaine-tmg]], choline, creatine, NAC, and ALCAR co-use, and validates the oral SAMe formulation.
