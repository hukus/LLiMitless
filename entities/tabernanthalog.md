# Tabernanthalog

Tabernanthalog, usually shortened to TBG, is a synthetic ibogaine/tabernanthine-inspired non-hallucinogenic psychoplastogen. It is cognition-relevant because the best rodent paper directly touches cognitive flexibility after chronic stress, but it remains preclinical and should not be treated as a consumer nootropic.

Medicinal cognition signal: `preclinical-only`. The 2021 mouse paper reported rescue of stress-induced sensory-processing and cognitive-flexibility deficits after unpredictable mild stress, plus dendritic-spine effects. A 2025 paper then clarified a more selective plasticity profile: TBG induced cortical neuroplasticity through 5-HT2A / TrkB / mTOR / AMPA-linked pathways without proximate immediate early gene activation. This is mechanistically useful, not human efficacy.^[raw/articles/pubmed-34035476-tabernanthalog-stress-cognitive-flexibility.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40760185-tabernanthalog-neuroplasticity-no-iea.md]

Human/anecdote signal: `absent-to-weak`. Current community traffic is mostly curiosity and mechanism discussion. The checked Reddit trail includes people asking whether TBG has been tried and a 2026 mechanism-focused post, but no stable first-hand cognition corpus.^[raw/articles/reddit-2023-2026-nonhallucinogenic-neuroplastogen-threads.md]
