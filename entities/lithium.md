# Lithium

Lithium is a prescription mood stabilizer, a trace element, and now a cognition-relevant research branch with three different layers that should not be collapsed into one claim: prescription lithium carbonate/citrate, low-dose lithium carbonate repurposing for MCI/AD prevention, and over-the-counter lithium salts such as lithium orotate or lithium aspartate.

## Key Facts

- The 2025 Nature paper shifted the branch by reporting lower endogenous lithium in MCI/AD tissue, plaque sequestration, and a mouse rescue signal with low-dose lithium orotate.^[raw/articles/nature-2025-lithium-deficiency-onset-alzheimers-disease.md]
- The 2025 preclinical review on subtherapeutic lithium concentrations tightened the mechanistic case for AD models, but it did not cross into human efficacy evidence.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40348943-lithium-neuroprotection-subtherapeutic-concentrations-preclinical-ad.md]
- A 2026 Neurotherapeutics paper in ApoE4/E4 patient-derived iPSCs found lithium chloride reduced ferroptosis phenotypes, iron dysregulation, and mitochondrial stress, which strengthens the mechanism story but still stays at the cellular preclinical layer.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41760425-lithium-chloride-ferroptosis-ad-ipsc.md]
- The official 2025 NIH Research Matters summary repeats the same tissue/mouse message and explicitly says the effective and safe human dose range is still unknown.^[raw/articles/nih-2025-lithium-levels-tied-alzheimers-disease-and-dementia.md]
- The 2026 JAMA Neurology pilot trial in MCI was feasibility-positive and safety-positive, but not prespecified-efficacy-positive on its six coprimary outcomes.^[raw/articles/jama-2026-low-dose-lithium-mci-rct.md]
- The 2026 meta-analysis in AD dementia found no consistent global cognition or functional benefit across six RCTs, even though serious adverse events were not clearly increased.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41177743-lithium-ad-meta-analysis.md]
- The 2025 human cognition review found mixed effects across 30 studies and 727 participants, with affective-state and population differences driving much of the apparent signal.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41104528-lithium-cognition-humans-review.md]
- The live [[lithium-orotate]] feasibility study is still not yet recruiting as of 2026-05-04; it is a 40-participant phase 1/2 early-AD trial centered on feasibility, adherence, safety, and CSF lithium target engagement.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct07459959-lio-ad-record-2026-05-04.md]
- The 2020 Springer commentary on the older MCI trial was optimistic but also explicitly flagged sample-size, blinding, and endpoint caveats; it is historical context, not new evidence.^[raw/articles/pubmed-32743689-low-dose-lithium-against-dementia.md]
- Lithium orotate remains a hypothesis and supplement formulation, not a validated cognition intervention. Current evidence does not justify treating it as a superior or safer substitute for monitored prescription lithium.^[raw/articles/pubmed-1260219-lithium-orotate-rat-pharmacokinetics.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-34196467-lithium-orotate-superior-option-review.md]^[raw/articles/sciencedirect-2021-lithium-orotate-toxicology.md]

## Anecdote And Product Identity

- A 2025 OTC-lithium supplement survey gives the strongest anecdote layer: 211 self-selected users, perceived cognition/mood/anxiety benefits, and more side-effect/withdrawal phenomena than expected.^[raw/articles/canadian-jpsychiatry-2025-lithium-supplement-survey.md]
- Forum evidence is mixed: some lithium-orotate users report calm, focus, sleep, or mood benefit, while others report no effect, brain fog, headache, fatigue, grogginess, emotional flatness, crying, and dose confusion.^[raw/articles/reddit-2025-07-13-lithium-orotate-supplements-thread.md]^[raw/articles/reddit-2025-12-13-lithium-orotate-nootropicsdepot-thread.md]
- Keep prescription lithium carbonate/citrate, [[lithium-orotate]], lithium aspartate, ionic lithium, elemental-lithium dose, and trace dietary lithium separate.

## Safety Read

The safety baseline is the prescription label, not supplement folklore. Lithium has a narrow therapeutic range, requires serum monitoring when used therapeutically, and carries kidney, thyroid, sodium-balance, interaction, and serotonin-syndrome concerns.^[raw/articles/dailymed-lithium-oral-solution-medication-guide.md] Do not self-dose from mouse or commentary data.
