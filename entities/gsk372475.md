# GSK372475

GSK372475 is a balanced serotonin, norepinephrine, and dopamine triple reuptake inhibitor that failed major-depression efficacy trials. It is a negative-class anchor for [[triple-reuptake-inhibitor-cognition-watchlist]], [[ansofaxine-toludesvenlafaxine]], [[liafensine]], [[amitifadine]], and [[csti-500]].

## Current Read

Medicinal cognition signal: `negative / risk-first comparator`. The key source describes two randomized placebo- and active-controlled MDD studies. GSK372475 did not significantly differ from placebo on key efficacy endpoints in either study, while active controls showed antidepressant activity.^[raw/articles/sage-2012-gsk372475-mdd-negative-trials.md]

The safety and tolerability layer makes the negative signal more important. Dry mouth, headache, insomnia, nausea, sleep events, anxiety-related events, GI events, and tachycardia were more common with GSK372475 than placebo, and heart-rate / sitting-BP increases were greater than placebo or active controls.^[raw/articles/sage-2012-gsk372475-mdd-negative-trials.md]
