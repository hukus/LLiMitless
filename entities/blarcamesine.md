# Blarcamesine

## Bottom Line

Blarcamesine / ANAVEX2-73 is the most positive-looking published human result in the synaptic-rescue branch, but it remains sponsor-linked and endpoint-mixed. Medicinal signal is `low-moderate but contested`: ADAS-Cog13 and CDR-SB favored treatment in the captured phase IIb/III paper, while ADCS-ADL did not reach significance. Anecdotal signal is absent in the current source corpus.

## What It Is

Blarcamesine is an orally dosed SIGMAR1 activator framed around cellular homeostasis and autophagy. In the wiki it belongs with [[synaptic-rescue-small-molecule-2026-scan]] as a disease-context small molecule, not as a validated healthy-adult enhancer.

## Medicinal Research Signal

The ANAVEX2-73-AD-004 phase IIb/III report was a randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled 48-week early-AD trial. It randomized 508 participants, with 462 in the intent-to-treat population, to oral blarcamesine 30 mg or 50 mg daily versus placebo. The paper reported improvement versus placebo on ADAS-Cog13 and CDR-SB, but ADCS-ADL did not reach significance. It also reported increased plasma Aβ42/40 ratio, reduced whole-brain volume loss, transient dizziness, and numerically higher serious treatment-emergent adverse events in the blarcamesine arm.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39800452-blarcamesine-anavex2-73-ad-004.md]

That package is not null, but it is also not settled. The functional endpoint miss and sponsor-linked publication context keep the rating below high confidence until independent replication or regulator-grade review clarifies the magnitude, missingness handling, and clinical relevance.

## Safety And Status

The captured paper reports transient dizziness as a common treatment-emergent adverse event and numerically higher serious TEAEs in the blarcamesine arm. Any future upgrade should preserve the safety and functional-endpoint caveats rather than quoting only the cognitive endpoint.

## Signal Judgment

Medicinal signal: `low-moderate but contested`. It is stronger than the purely biomarker-positive or primary-negative synaptic-rescue candidates, but not strong enough to rank as settled.

Anecdotal signal: `absent-to-weak`.

Practical route: promising but sponsor-linked AD treatment candidate needing independent confirmation.
