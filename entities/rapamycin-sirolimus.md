# Rapamycin / Sirolimus

Rapamycin, clinically sirolimus, is the mTOR-inhibition and geroscience anchor for the cognitive-aging branch. It has strong preclinical and longevity-community salience, but the current human cognition signal is weak and safety-first.

Medicinal cognition signal: `low / biomarker-heavy but cognition-null so far`. A 2018 placebo-controlled pilot in healthy older adults found no cognitive change. A 2025 open-label phase 1 ADRD trial likewise found no significant cognitive change, no detectable CSF rapamycin, and biomarker movement that was not cleanly disease-modifying.^[raw/articles/pubmed-29408453-rapamycin-older-human-cohort-cognitive-effects.md]^[raw/articles/nature-s43856-025-00904-9-rapamycin-adrd-phase1-clinical-trial.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40394335-rapamycin-adrd-phase1-open-label.md]

Translational signal: `mechanism-positive / human-efficacy unresolved`. Animal AD models remain directionally supportive, and a 2025 early-AD pharmacokinetic study showed that weekly dosing can be monitored and kept below immunosuppressive blood ranges. That supports future trial design, not current cognition efficacy.^[raw/articles/pubmed-38640155-rapamycin-preclinical-ad-meta-analysis.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41046300-rapamycin-intermittent-pk-early-ad.md]

## Anecdote And Community Signal

The clearest community layer is not cognition-specific. A 333-user survey found substantial off-label rapamycin use for healthy longevity / anti-aging, usually weekly dosing. That is a `strong` community-salience signal for longevity use, but it is self-selected and does not establish objective cognitive benefit.^[raw/articles/pubmed-37191826-rapamycin-off-label-healthspan-survey.md]

## Safety And Protocol Caveats

- The sirolimus label carries immunosuppression, infection/malignancy, hyperlipidemia, renal, wound-healing, pneumonitis, embryo-fetal, and infertility warnings.^[raw/articles/dailymed-sirolimus-2026-label.md]
- Low-dose intermittent geroscience use is not the same exposure as transplant dosing, but the safety burden is still prescription-level.
- Biomarker movement, preclinical rescue, and community longevity use should not upgrade the cognition signal without controlled human cognitive endpoints.
- Route rapamycin longevity, dementia-prevention, or "brain aging" anecdotes through [[safety-interaction-matrix-for-multi-modal-stacks]] before treating them as cognition evidence. Intermittent dosing, infection risk, wound healing, lipids, renal markers, mouth ulcers/pneumonitis, fertility/pregnancy, AD/MCI phenotype, blood-level monitoring, and no-detectable-CSF findings are core interpretation gates.
