# Lazabemide

Lazabemide / RO19-6327 is a legacy short-acting reversible MAO-B inhibitor developed for Parkinson disease. It is included so the [[mao-b-propargylamine-cognition-watchlist]] does not collapse reversible MAO-B logic into only [[safinamide]], [[sembragiline]], and [[kds2010-tisolagiline]].

## Current Read

Medicinal cognition signal: `absent/PD-context only`. In a 1993 randomized double-blind trial, 201 early untreated PD patients received lazabemide 100, 200, or 400 mg/day or placebo for 8 weeks. Lazabemide was as well tolerated as placebo and improved the UPDRS activities-of-daily-living component after 4 weeks, but other subscales did not change significantly.^[raw/articles/pubmed-8489205-lazabemide-untreated-pd-rct.md]

The trial did not establish a cognition or brain-fog claim. It is mainly a class-history comparator showing that reversible selective MAO-B inhibition has been explored before KDS2010/tisolagiline.

## Current Route

Route lazabemide through [[mao-b-propargylamine-cognition-watchlist]] as a legacy reversible-MAO-B comparator. It sits near [[rasagiline]], [[safinamide]], [[sembragiline]], and [[selegiline]], but does not warrant a healthy-adult nootropic ranking.
