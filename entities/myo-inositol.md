# Myo-Inositol

Myo-inositol is a cyclohexane-hexol often sold as inositol powder and discussed as a B-vitamin-like signaling/osmolyte supplement. In this wiki it should be separated from D-chiro-inositol, IP6/phytate, phosphatidylinositol signaling, and inositol-stabilized arginine silicate (ASI / Nitrosigine), because the direct healthy-adult cognition evidence belongs mostly to ASI rather than plain myo-inositol.^[raw/articles/pubchem-2026-myo-inositol-identity.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-27869715-asi-cognition-healthy-adults.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-34959823-asi-acute-cognition-healthy-adults.md]

Medicinal cognition signal: `low / product-specific`. Two small randomized double-blind crossover studies report selected healthy-adult cognition improvements after ASI/Nitrosigine, including Trail Making Test-B and RBANS immediate-memory/total-score signals. This is a real direct-cognition clue, but the exposure is an arginine-silicate-inositol complex with nitric-oxide/product identity, not ordinary inositol powder.^[raw/articles/pubmed-27869715-asi-cognition-healthy-adults.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-34959823-asi-acute-cognition-healthy-adults.md]

Plain inositol signal: `mood/anxiety/metabolic-state, not nootropic proof`. Older controlled psychiatry trials summarized benefits in depression, panic disorder, and OCD, but also reported no clear benefit for Alzheimer's disease, ECT-induced memory impairment, schizophrenia, ADHD-like symptoms, or autism. This makes inositol relevant to subjective cognition when anxiety, sleep, or mood is the driver, but weak as a direct healthy-adult cognitive enhancer.^[raw/articles/pubmed-9169302-inositol-controlled-psychiatry-trials.md]

Metabolic/PCOS signal: `status-gated`. A 2024 guideline-informing PCOS meta-analysis included 30 trials and concluded that inositol evidence was limited and inconclusive overall, with possible metabolic or ovulation-related signals and fewer GI adverse events than metformin. This supports PCOS/insulin-resistance routing, not broad cognition claims.^[raw/articles/pubmed-38163998-inositol-pcos-guideline-meta-analysis.md]

## Signal Split

| Layer | Signal | Readout |
|---|---|---|
| ASI / Nitrosigine healthy adults | `low/product-specific positive` | Small crossover studies report TMT-B, RBANS total, and immediate-memory effects |
| Plain myo-inositol psychiatry | `low/mood-anxiety state` | Old small trials suggest depression/panic/OCD benefit, not objective healthy cognition |
| Alzheimer's / memory counterweights | `negative/unclear` | Old summarized AD and ECT-memory trials did not show clear benefit |
| PCOS / insulin resistance | `low/status-gated` | Metabolic/reproductive outcomes uncertain and phenotype-bound |
| Anecdote | `weak-moderate` for calm/sleep/anxiety; `weak` for cognition | Brain-fog reports are mostly indirect through sleep, anxiety, PCOS, or stimulant smoothing |
| Safety / sourcing | `low-moderate caution` | GI symptoms, sleep changes, mood effects, high-dose psychiatry context, and myo:D-chiro ratio confusion |

## Practical Interpretation

Use [[myo-inositol]] as a low-confidence `state and product-identity` branch. It is worth tracking because it is buyable, common in PCOS/anxiety/sleep self-experimentation, and has an ASI-specific direct healthy-adult cognition packet. It should not enter the top cognition tier unless future trials test plain myo-inositol on objective cognition, with anxiety, sleep, PCOS/insulin resistance, dose, D-chiro ratio, caffeine/stimulants, and GI tolerability measured separately.
