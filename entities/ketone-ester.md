# Ketone Ester

Ketone esters are exogenous ketone-body delivery tools, not generic energy supplements. The cognition branch is now stronger than a niche anecdote because a 2026 meta-analysis found a small pooled signal, but the actual trial pattern is still dose-, phenotype-, formulation-, and state-dependent.

## Medicinal Signal

- Formal signal: `low-moderate` for cognition under metabolic or high-load contexts.
- The 2026 systematic review/meta-analysis found exogenous ketone bodies improved cognition versus placebo with SMD 0.29 across 38 studies / 41 protocols / 1,602 participants.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41001501-exogenous-ketone-bodies-cognition-systematic-review-meta-analysis.md]
- The strongest phenotype signal remains adults with obesity, where ketone monoester improved cerebral blood flow and DSST performance.^[raw/articles/pubmed-34605026-ketone-monoester-obesity-cognition-cbf.md]
- High-load trials are mixed: trained-female postexercise cognition improved selectively, simulated soccer mental-fatigue decrement was attenuated, resting hypoxia looked positive, but hypoxia plus exercise went cognition-null.^[raw/articles/pubmed-38051034-ketone-monoester-carbohydrate-postexercise-trained-females.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-36297060-ketone-ester-simulated-soccer-mental-fatigue.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39190580-ketone-monoester-hypoxic-exposure-resting.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40315254-ketone-ester-hypoxia-null.md]
- Healthy-young-adult CBF dose-response data complicate the mechanism because acute ketone monoester lowered resting CBF and end-tidal CO2.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40349325-acute-ketone-monoester-ingestion-lowers-resting-cerebral-blood-flow.md]
- Direct sleep-restriction evidence is still `protocol-only`: STAK-Sleep is a recruiting crossover trial of twice-daily C6 ketone diester during four nights of 50% sleep restriction, with cognitive, operational, mood, glucose/ketone, and blood-marker endpoints but no posted outcomes.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct05519644-stak-sleep-ketone-ester-record-2026-05-04.md]

## Signal Strength Ledger

| Evidence layer | Signal strength | Read |
|---|---|---|
| Pooled human cognition | `low-moderate` medicinal | Small positive pooled effect, but heterogeneous products, states, and endpoints |
| Obesity / metabolic vulnerability | `low-moderate` medicinal | Best clean positive phenotype signal via DSST and CBF |
| Direct sleep restriction | `protocol-only` medicinal | STAK-Sleep is designed for the key state but has no posted outcome data |
| High-load exercise / mental fatigue | `low` medicinal | Selective postexercise and soccer-task positives without broad performance transfer |
| Hypoxia and CBF mechanism | `low/contested` medicinal | Resting-hypoxia rescue conflicts with hypoxia-plus-exercise null and acute CBF lowering |
| Safety / tolerability | `low-moderate` confidence | Short controlled exposure looks tolerable; GI burden, taste, cost, and protocol burden remain practical limits |
| Anecdote / market | `weak-to-moderate` mixed salience | Endurance/focus reports coexist with no-effect reports and product-identity objections |

## Safety And Protocol Burden

- Safety evidence is better than many experimental supplements: 28-day and 12-week pilot data support tolerability, with GI burden still relevant.^[raw/articles/pubmed-31655093-ketone-monoester-28d-safety.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39137624-ketone-ester-older-adults-safety.md]
- Formulation matters. Ketone monoester, C6 ketone diester, ketone diol products, ketone salts, dose, carbohydrate co-ingestion, ketogenic diets, and trial state should not be treated as interchangeable.
- The direct sleep-restriction and kCBF registry records remain live result-watch items rather than efficacy sources.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct05519644-stak-sleep-ketone-ester-record-2026-05-04.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct06032156-kcbf-ketone-dose-record-2026-05-04.md]

## Anecdote And Product Identity

- Anecdote signal: `weak-to-moderate`, mixed, and mostly endurance/focus-state rather than objective cognition.
- The captured Ketone-IQ ultramarathon thread contains sustained-energy/focus reports, no-effect reports, taste/cost objections, and explicit product-identity pushback.^[raw/articles/reddit-2023-11-02-ketone-iq-ultramarathon-thread.md]
- Keep Ketone-IQ / R-1,3-butanediol / ketone-diol marketing separate from ketone monoester or diester trial evidence unless the product identity is explicit.
