# Daridorexant

## Bottom Line

Daridorexant is a dual orexin receptor antagonist (DORA) cognition-safety and sleep-restoration comparator. Medicinal signal is `low` for cognition: healthy-adult nighttime safety data suggest limited cognitive impairment at tested overnight doses, delirium work is early and diagnosis-specific, and a healthy-volunteer executive/emotional-processing trial is recruiting with no results posted. Anecdotal cognition signal is `absent-to-weak` in the current corpus.

## What It Is

Daridorexant is a DORA sleep drug. In this wiki it belongs with [[orexin-hypocretin-axis-interventions-2026-scan]], [[orexin-axis-molecule-watchlist]], [[sleep-and-circadian-interventions-for-cognition]], and [[negative-evidence-watchlist-for-cognition-interventions]] as a sleep-axis comparator where non-impairment and sleep restoration must not be mistaken for enhancement.

## Medicinal Research Signal

The healthy-adult nighttime safety anchor is a randomized placebo-controlled crossover study in 36 healthy adults evaluating daridorexant 25 mg and 50 mg overnight. It did not meaningfully alter auditory awakening threshold or most cognitive measures, produced only a modest increase in body sway, and preserved practical nighttime function overall. This supports low residual impairment in the tested context, not cognitive enhancement.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39641404-daridorexant-nighttime-safety-cognitive-function.md]

The delirium anchor is a scoping review plus small randomized feasibility trial after cardiac surgery. Daridorexant 50 mg was given for the first three nights after surgery; the pilot was too small for efficacy, though symptoms were numerically lower, and the paper emphasized balancing possible benefit against next-morning sedation. The broader DORA delirium meta-analysis found lower incident delirium but very low certainty and no clear secondary-outcome win.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40374039-daridorexant-delirium-feasibility-trial.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41401488-dora-delirium-meta-analysis.md]

The active cognition gate is NCT07267559, a recruiting University of Oxford healthy-volunteer trial testing single-dose daridorexant versus placebo on emotional processing, aversive learning, and executive-function tasks. No results are posted in the captured registry note.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct07267559-dorea-daridorexant-executive-function.md]

Class-level context matters: a 2026 pairwise meta-analysis across 77 DORA trials found subjective total sleep time improved by about 19 minutes and somnolence risk increased, with sparse data outside insomnia. That reinforces the sleep-restoration framing.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41610582-dora-sleep-pairwise-meta-analysis.md]

## Safety And Status

Daridorexant should be treated as a bedtime sleep drug, not a daytime enhancer. The key practical risk is somnolence or next-day impairment if dose, timing, or individual sensitivity are mismatched.

## Signal Judgment

Medicinal signal: `low` for cognition; useful as non-impairment/sleep-restoration comparator and live registry watch item.

Anecdotal signal: `absent-to-weak`.

Practical route: DORA sleep-axis comparator and pending healthy-volunteer cognition protocol.
