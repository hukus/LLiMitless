# 2'-Fucosyllactose

2'-Fucosyllactose, or 2'-FL, is a human milk oligosaccharide used as a prebiotic-style microbiome modulator. For cognition, it belongs beside [[galacto-oligosaccharides]] and the broader [[gut-microbiome-and-cognition]] responder-phenotype branch, not in generic probiotic or dairy categories.

Medicinal signal: `low / Bifidobacterium-responder visual-memory signal`. A 2025 randomized controlled trial in 89 healthy older adults tested 2'-FL for 6 weeks. The primary cytokine-response endpoint was negative, but 2'-FL increased gut Bifidobacterium and shifted several metabolic markers. Participants who responded with Bifidobacterium expansion also performed better on a visual-memory cognitive test.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40738103-2-fucosyllactose-older-adults-cognition.md]

Interpretation: 2'-FL is a responder-gated prebiotic lead, not a broad cognitive enhancer. The primary endpoint miss matters, and the cognitive signal is anchored to a subgroup defined by microbiome response. Older-adult probiotic/prebiotic meta-analytic evidence remains mixed or low-certainty, so 2'-FL should stay in the exploratory product watchlist until a cognition-primary replication appears.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40483001-probiotics-cognitive-performance-older-adults-meta-analysis.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41291638-probiotic-supplements-cognitive-function-meta-analysis.md]

Product and protocol gates:

- Active unit: 2'-FL, not generic HMO blends unless the exact oligosaccharide profile is reported.
- Population: healthy older adults, mean age around late 60s in the captured RCT.
- Endpoint: visual memory in Bifidobacterium responders, not global cognition.
- Responder gate: baseline absence of Bifidobacterium may limit response, but this is not a validated consumer test.
- Safety/tolerability: GI symptoms, dose ramp, diabetes/metabolic context, infant-formula extrapolation, and commercial ingredient purity.
- Replication need: cognition-primary RCT with prespecified responder stratification.
