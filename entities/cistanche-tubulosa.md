# Cistanche Tubulosa

Cistanche tubulosa is a traditional Chinese medicinal herb, often grouped with Cistanche deserticola / Rou Cong Rong in supplement marketing. It enters this wiki through a 2026 healthy-adult memory RCT of a Cistanche plus [[ginkgo]] product and through nootropic-community anecdotes. The key rule is that the strongest human cognition result is a combination product, not isolated Cistanche.

## Bottom Line

Medicinal cognition signal: `low, combination-limited`. A 2026 randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial tested a Cistanche tubulosa plus Ginkgo biloba product in 117 healthy adults aged 30-65 for 30 days. The product improved multiple memory scores and showed MRI/fMRI correlations, but the design cannot isolate Cistanche from Ginkgo or from the specific commercial formulation.^[raw/articles/frontiers-2026-cistanche-ginkgo-memory-rct.md]

Anecdote signal: `weak-moderate, mixed`. Community reports emphasize sleep quality, vitality, libido/testosterone-adjacent quality of life, and long-term use more than objective cognition. Adverse reports include lethargy, emotionality, lightheadedness, low-BP-like feelings, dazed state, and brain fog, sometimes confounded by Tongkat Ali.^[raw/articles/reddit-2022-cistanche-anecdotes-thread.md]^[raw/articles/reddit-2025-cistanche-brainfog-adverse-thread.md]

Healthy-adult relevance: `interesting but not clean`. The RCT population was healthy adults, which is unusually relevant for this wiki, but the combination-product and sponsor/product identity caveats keep confidence low.

## Formal Evidence

The 2026 trial randomized 117 healthy adults to Cistanche/Ginkgo tablets or placebo for 30 days. Reported gains included directional memory, associative learning, graphic free recall, portrait memory, total memory, and Memory Quotient. Imaging analyses linked memory improvement with cerebellar activation and motor-region gray-matter changes. Authors disclosed Amway affiliations.^[raw/articles/frontiers-2026-cistanche-ginkgo-memory-rct.md]

The practical interpretation is product-specific: do not generalize to generic Cistanche powder, isolated echinacoside/acteoside, Cistanche deserticola, or Cistanche stacks without matching chemistry and dose.

## Safety And Status

The formal RCT supports short-term tolerability for the tested Cistanche/Ginkgo product, not broad safety for all Cistanche products. Practical gates include product identity, adulteration/contamination testing, blood-pressure/autonomic symptoms, sleep effects, hormone-adjacent claims, and interaction with Ginkgo if using combination products.

Route Cistanche / Rou Cong Rong reports through [[safety-interaction-matrix-for-multi-modal-stacks]] before treating vitality, libido, sleep, blood-pressure/autonomic, brain-fog, or memory reports as direct cognition. The first gates are isolated Cistanche versus Cistanche-plus-Ginkgo product, echinacoside/acteoside marker profile, dose, sponsor/product identity, Ginkgo interaction layer, Tongkat Ali or hormone-stack co-use, sleep timing, and adverse dechallenge.

## Signal Judgment

Cistanche is worth standalone coverage because it now has a healthy-adult memory trial and real community salience. It should be ranked below cleaner standardized single-extract candidates until isolated Cistanche products replicate the effect and adverse-autonomic anecdotes are better characterized.
