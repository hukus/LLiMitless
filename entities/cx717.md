# CX717

CX717 is the main low-impact AMPAkine / AMPAR positive allosteric modulator in the wiki. It is relevant because it has strong nonhuman-primate sleep-loss rescue evidence, older mixed-to-negative healthy-human sleep-loss evidence, and a newer adult-ADHD disease-state signal. It is not a broad healthy-adult cognitive enhancer.

Medicinal cognition signal: `low-moderate for adult ADHD; low for healthy cognition`. The strongest old translational signal was in nonhuman primates, where CX717 improved task performance and reversed sleep-deprivation impairment.^[raw/articles/pubmed-16104830-cx717-nonhuman-primate-sleep-deprivation.md] Human sleep-loss translation was weaker: an acute sleep-deprivation study found no consistent prespecified cognitive or alertness benefit, and a simulated night-shift study did not reverse performance decline.^[raw/articles/pubmed-21940760-cx717-sleep-deprivation-human.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-17955941-cx717-night-shift-work.md] The important current correction is the 2025 adult-ADHD phase 2A publication: CX717 800 mg BID missed the primary paired t-test threshold as a trend, but a secondary repeated-measures endpoint analysis favored CX717 over placebo on total ADHD-RS and inattentive / hyperactivity subscales.^[raw/articles/sciencedirect-2025-cx717-adhd-phase2a.md] That is disease-state symptom evidence, not proof of general enhancement.

Safety and dose signal: `human-exposure present but still investigational`. A 2025 safety/PK study reported tolerability up to 1600 mg single dose and 800 mg BID, with headache, dizziness, and nausea as prominent adverse effects and an 8-12 hour half-life.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39892449-cx717-safety-pharmacokinetics.md] The ADHD study used 200 mg BID and 800 mg BID, with sleep disturbance and headache most prominent and no significant cardiovascular signal reported in the captured abstract.^[raw/articles/sciencedirect-2025-cx717-adhd-phase2a.md] The class still needs an excitatory-ceiling safety gate because high-impact AMPAkines have mechanistic hyperexcitability / convulsion concerns, even though low-impact CX717 is part of the safer branch.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39304968-high-impact-ampakines-toxic-calcium-release.md]

Interpretation signal: `analysis-sensitive`. CX717 now deserves standalone tracking because it is no longer just an animal / failed-sleep-loss name. The confidence remains low because the newer positive signal is in adult ADHD, male-only, sponsor-linked, and secondary-analysis-dependent; the older healthy-human sleep-loss studies did not provide a clean cognition win.

June 2026 low-impact AMPAkine attribution gate: CX717 claims should separate adult-ADHD symptom response, sleep-loss rescue, night-shift performance, respiratory-development exposure, and rested healthy-adult enhancement. Capture diagnosis or state challenge, baseline sleep and stimulant exposure, dose and BID timing, trial versus gray-market status, primary versus secondary endpoint, washout/crossover order, sleep disturbance, headache, dizziness, nausea, HR/BP where available, co-use of caffeine, modafinil-class drugs, stimulants, racetams, [[tak-653]], [[idra-21]], or plasticity-stack agents, and objective attention/executive/PVT or ADHD-RS endpoints before ranking changes.
