# Pentoxifylline

Pentoxifylline is a methylxanthine hemorheologic drug with PDE, cAMP, inflammatory, vascular, and platelet/fibrinogen effects. It sits in the same broad xanthine/PDE neighborhood as [[ibudilast]] and [[propentofylline]], but its practical identity is vascular/inflammatory medicine rather than nootropic supplement.

## Bottom Line

Brain-fog clearance signal: `protocol-only plus weak anecdote`. RECLAIM has completed a pentoxifylline long-COVID arm with brain fog among the listed symptom targets, but official efficacy results are not yet posted in the captured source. One Reddit long-COVID medication thread mentions Trental in a stack that cleared brain fog, which is not enough for attribution.^[raw/articles/reclaim-2026-ibudilast-pentoxifylline-long-covid.md]^[raw/articles/reddit-2023-longcovid-brainfog-medication-thread.md]

Mechanism signal: `plausible but nonspecific`. A 2024 review describes PDE3/PDE4 inhibition, cAMP increase, adenosine A2 receptor antagonism, and effects on erythrocytes, leukocytes, platelets, fibrinogen, cytokines, and blood vessels. That creates a long-COVID rationale, not a brain-fog efficacy result.^[raw/articles/springer-2024-pentoxifylline-covid-longcovid-review.md]

Practical route: pentoxifylline should be watched through long-COVID trial results and retraction-aware psychiatric-cognition literature. It should not be upgraded from mechanism/rationale to treatment without controlled symptom data.

## Formal Evidence

The main direct long-COVID source is RECLAIM, which lists completed ibudilast and pentoxifylline drug arms against placebo and says results are being analyzed. Because brain fog is among the trial's listed long-COVID symptoms, pentoxifylline is directly relevant to this category but not yet outcome-positive.^[raw/articles/reclaim-2026-ibudilast-pentoxifylline-long-covid.md]

Schizophrenia literature provides a cognition-adjacent but fragile signal. A 52-patient chronic-schizophrenia trial reported subset cognitive and symptom improvements with pentoxifylline 400 mg twice daily over 8 weeks. The nearby negative-symptom pentoxifylline publication was retracted, and the registry record is needed to keep endpoint interpretation conservative.^[raw/articles/pubmed-37466276-pentoxifylline-schizophrenia-cognition.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-38465461-pentoxifylline-negative-symptoms-retraction.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct04094207-pentoxifylline-negative-symptoms-schizophrenia.md]

## Safety And Status

DailyMed frames pentoxifylline as a prescription drug for intermittent claudication symptoms. Practical gates include bleeding risk, recent retinal or cerebral hemorrhage, anticoagulant/antiplatelet context, hypotension, GI effects, dizziness, headache, methylxanthine sensitivity, hepatic or renal impairment, and interaction context.^[raw/articles/dailymed-2026-pentoxifylline-label.md]

## Signal Judgment

Pentoxifylline belongs in the neuroimmune brain-fog category because it has direct long-COVID trial relevance and a plausible vascular-inflammatory mechanism. Current confidence is low because the long-COVID trial results are pending, the schizophrenia cognition signal is adjacent and fragile, and the anecdotal brain-fog signal is a single stack-confounded Trental mention.
