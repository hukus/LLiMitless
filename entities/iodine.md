# Iodine

Iodine is an essential mineral required for thyroid hormone production. In this wiki it is a thyroid-status gate for brain fog, not a general cognition enhancer. It should not be collapsed with kelp, seaweed, thyroid hormone, desiccated thyroid, [[selenium]], [[zinc]], or broad "mineral energy" stacks.

Medicinal cognition signal: `low and indirect in adults`. ODS anchors iodine's importance through thyroid hormone biology and deficiency risk. Adult supplementation evidence captured here is mainly status/thyroid-response evidence, not cognitive-endpoint evidence. The practical claim is therefore narrow: iodine can matter when iodine status or thyroid function is a limiting variable, but it is not a nootropic for euthyroid iodine-replete adults.^[raw/articles/ods-2025-iodine-health-professional-fact-sheet.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-26891118-iodine-mild-deficiency-adults-rct.md]

Safety/status signal: `high thyroid gate burden`. Adult recommended intake is 150 mcg/day and the adult tolerable upper intake level is 1,100 mcg/day. Both deficiency and excess can cause thyroid dysfunction, and kelp/seaweed products can produce highly variable exposure. Thyroid disease, pregnancy/lactation, amiodarone, lithium, antithyroid drugs, and high-dose kelp make casual experimentation high-risk.^[raw/articles/ods-2025-iodine-health-professional-fact-sheet.md]

## Signal Split

| Layer | Signal | Readout |
|---|---|---|
| Iodine deficiency / thyroid dysfunction | `clinically plausible` | Brain fog can be mediated through hypothyroid or hyperthyroid physiology |
| Adult supplementation with cognition endpoints | `not established` | Captured adult RCT source is status-oriented rather than cognition-positive |
| Euthyroid replete adults | `not established` | No healthy-adult enhancement signal |
| Anecdote | `weak-moderate thyroid-status salience` | Improvement claims are tightly confounded by thyroid status and dose variability |
| Safety | `high` | Both under- and over-supplementation can worsen thyroid state |

## Practical Interpretation

Route iodine through [[mineral-status-brain-fog-cognition-watchlist]] only when diet, iodized-salt exposure, seaweed/kelp use, thyroid labs, thyroid antibodies, pregnancy/lactation status, and medication context are visible. In a replete euthyroid adult, iodine belongs in the safety/status checklist rather than an enhancer ranking.
