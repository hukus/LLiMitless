# Metadoxine

Metadoxine is a pyridoxine / pyrrolidone-carboxylate compound with older acute-alcohol-intoxication use in some countries and an extended-release ADHD-development history under the MDX / MG01CI label. In this wiki it is worth tracking because it has human attention-task signals and hangover-adjacent anecdotes, but the later ADHD phase 3 failure keeps it firmly low-confidence.

## Current Read

Medicinal cognition signal: `low/contested`. A 6-week adult ADHD phase 2 trial and an acute ADHD-PI crossover study both reported attention or symptom benefits, but a later phase 3 MEASURE trial failed its primary endpoint.^[raw/articles/pubmed-23290324-metadoxine-er-adult-adhd.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-25295645-metadoxine-single-dose-adhd-pi.md]^[raw/articles/genengnews-2017-metadoxine-phase3-failure.md]

Brain-fog / hangover relevance: `state-bound`. Metadoxine has a clinical acute-alcohol-intoxication evidence trail and weak community hangover-prevention reports, so it belongs in state-restoration / alcohol-aftereffect discussion rather than everyday nootropic ranking.^[raw/articles/pubmed-11923586-metadoxine-acute-alcohol-intoxication.md]^[raw/articles/reddit-2023-2026-centanafadine-metadoxine-attention-threads.md]

Anecdote signal: `weak`. Reddit discussion includes mechanism curiosity, sourcing chatter, paper-sharing, and a single direct claim that taking metadoxine before alcohol prevented hangover-like symptoms for one user. That is not a robust cognition corpus.^[raw/articles/reddit-2023-2026-centanafadine-metadoxine-attention-threads.md]

## Human Evidence

| Evidence layer | What it says | Wiki interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| 6-week adult ADHD phase 2 | Metadoxine ER 1400 mg/day improved CAARS-INV, response thresholds, TOVA, and quality-of-life scores versus placebo in 120 adults.^[raw/articles/pubmed-23290324-metadoxine-er-adult-adhd.md] | Interesting ADHD-state signal |
| Single-dose ADHD-PI crossover | In 36 adults with predominantly inattentive ADHD, 1400 mg improved TOVA ADHD score 3-5 hours after dosing; 700 mg did not, and CANTAB exploratory analyses were not significant.^[raw/articles/pubmed-25295645-metadoxine-single-dose-adhd-pi.md] | Acute attention signal, dose- and phenotype-bound |
| Acute alcohol intoxication | A placebo-controlled 58-patient trial found faster ethanol elimination and faster recovery from intoxication after IV metadoxine.^[raw/articles/pubmed-11923586-metadoxine-acute-alcohol-intoxication.md] | Alcohol-state support, not general cognitive enhancement |
| Phase 3 MEASURE | Later phase 3 failed to show a significant difference from placebo on CAARS primary endpoint; full-analysis set was 283 randomized subjects with post-baseline data.^[raw/articles/genengnews-2017-metadoxine-phase3-failure.md] | Major downgrade |

## Safety And Interpretation Gates

- Do not treat a hangover-prevention claim as a healthy-adult cognition claim. Alcohol elimination, sleep disruption, dehydration, acetaldehyde biology, inflammation, and expectancy all confound the subjective endpoint.
- Common ADHD-trial adverse events included nausea, fatigue, and headache; the development trail also included an FDA clinical-hold episode tied to preclinical neurological findings.^[raw/articles/pubmed-23290324-metadoxine-er-adult-adhd.md]^[raw/articles/genengnews-2017-metadoxine-phase3-failure.md]
- Form matters. The ADHD program used extended-release high-dose MDX; acute alcohol studies used IV metadoxine. Do not collapse those with supplement-style pyridoxine, P5P, or pyroglutamate stacks.
- The most defensible route is "attention-state / alcohol-aftereffect hypothesis with failed pivotal ADHD translation," not "safe nootropic."
