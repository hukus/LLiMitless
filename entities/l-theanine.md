# L-Theanine

L-theanine is a green-tea amino acid that should be interpreted as a calm-attention / caffeine-combination candidate rather than a broad standalone nootropic. Its best use in the wiki is to separate theanine-alone evidence from tea, matcha, and [[caffeine]] synergy.

Medicinal signal: `low-moderate`. A 2025 placebo-controlled RCT meta-analysis in healthy adults found a dose-dependent signal on rapid visual information processing / recognition visual reaction time, but no confirmed effect across simple reaction time or Stroop outcomes. A five-child ADHD proof-of-concept trial suggested that l-theanine plus caffeine can improve total cognition composite and Go/NoGo d-prime acutely, but the sample is far too small to drive ranking.^[raw/articles/mdpi-2025-l-theanine-cognition-meta-analysis.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-32753637-l-theanine-caffeine-adhd-proof-of-concept.md]

## Signal Split

| Layer | Signal | Readout |
|---|---|---|
| Healthy-adult theanine alone | `low-moderate` medicinal | Some visual information-processing support, inconsistent across tasks |
| Theanine plus caffeine | `low` medicinal, plausible synergy | Very small ADHD proof-of-concept and broader tea/matcha inference |
| Anxiety / calm focus | `moderate` anecdote salience | Stronger subjective anxiety-reduction story than objective memory story |
| Broad cognition | `weak` | Not a durable memory or IQ-enhancement claim |

## Practical Interpretation

Rank L-theanine as a low-risk attention-state modifier, especially when caffeine timing and dose are controlled. Do not let tea or matcha studies automatically upgrade purified-theanine evidence, and do not let calm-focus anecdotes override the mixed endpoint pattern in the 2025 meta-analysis.

Route L-theanine claims through [[safety-interaction-matrix-for-multi-modal-stacks]] when paired with caffeine, tea/matcha, stimulants, sleep restriction, anxiety states, or sedating/calming stacks; calm focus can be anxiolysis or caffeine smoothing rather than task-level cognition.
