# Sodium Oligomannate (GV-971)

## Overview

Sodium oligomannate, also called GV-971, is a marine-derived oligosaccharide mixture developed as an Alzheimer's disease treatment. The clearest official approval statement in the source set is China-specific: the NMPA announced market launch and cognitive-improvement use for mild-to-moderate AD in 2019.^[raw/articles/nmpa-2019-gv971-approval.md]

The branch should be read as an AD drug with a contested replication story, not as a broad cognitive enhancer for healthy adults. Its human evidence is centered on the China phase 2 and phase 3 studies, while the global trial status remains unresolved because the multinational phase 3 was suspended and later described as early-terminated.^[raw/articles/pubmed-32928279-sodium-oligomannate-phase2.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-33731209-sodium-oligomannate-phase3-china.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct04520412-gv971-green-memory-global.md]^[raw/articles/greenvalley-2022-gv971-global-phase3-termination.md]

## Key Facts And Dates

- 2019-11-02: China approval / market launch announcement for mild-to-moderate AD.^[raw/articles/nmpa-2019-gv971-approval.md]
- 2020-09-14: phase 2 report published; primary ADAS-Cog12 result was not significant, but the 900 mg arm had a favorable CIBIC-Plus responder signal.^[raw/articles/pubmed-32928279-sodium-oligomannate-phase2.md]
- 2021-03-17: phase 3 report published; ADAS-Cog12 difference favored GV-971 by -2.15 points at 36 weeks, p < 0.0001.^[raw/articles/pubmed-33731209-sodium-oligomannate-phase3-china.md]
- 2021-09-27: global phase 3 registry record posted on ClinicalTrials.gov; later marked suspended with no results posted.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct04520412-gv971-green-memory-global.md]
- 2025-01-24: long-term safety registry still showed active-not-recruiting with 2500 enrolled in China.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct05058040-gv971-postmarketing-safety.md]
- 2024: mechanistic mouse papers extended the microbiome and gut-brain narratives but did not add human replication.^[raw/articles/pubmed-38365827-gv971-mouse-replication.md]^[raw/articles/nature-2024-gv971-enteroendocrine-vagal-afferent.md]

## Evidence Layers

### Human efficacy

The phase 2 trial is mixed: the primary cognitive endpoint was negative, but the 900 mg arm had a better CIBIC-Plus responder rate and a PET subgroup with smaller glucose-metabolism decline in AD-relevant regions.^[raw/articles/pubmed-32928279-sodium-oligomannate-phase2.md]

The phase 3 China trial is the main positive efficacy claim: 818 participants, 36 weeks, ADAS-Cog12 difference of -2.15 points, and similar treatment-emergent AE rates versus placebo.^[raw/articles/pubmed-33731209-sodium-oligomannate-phase3-china.md]

### Geography and registry status

The source set supports China approval and post-marketing surveillance, but not a completed multinational replication. The global GREEN MEMORY study was initiated across multiple regions, later suspended, and still has no posted results in the registry record.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct04520412-gv971-green-memory-global.md]

### Mechanism

The original mechanism story was gut microbiota remodeling plus downstream inflammatory effects, with direct notes about peripheral and central inflammation, A-beta deposition, and tau hyperphosphorylation in the sponsor's trial-rationale material.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct04520412-gv971-green-memory-global.md] The 2019 Cell Research paper adds a microbiota-metabolite-neuroinflammation model, while the 2024 mouse papers narrow the story to sex-specific microbiota effects and an enteroendocrine-vagal afferent pathway.^[raw/articles/nature-2019-gv971-gut-microbiota-neuroinflammation.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-38365827-gv971-mouse-replication.md]^[raw/articles/nature-2024-gv971-enteroendocrine-vagal-afferent.md]

## Replication Uncertainty

There is no completed, publicly posted multinational human efficacy replication in the current source set. That leaves the branch in a narrower status than the China phase 3 paper alone would suggest. The most defensible reading is: clinically active in China, mechanistically plausible, but externally unresolved.

## Safety

The phase 3 paper reported similar treatment-emergent adverse event rates between GV-971 and placebo and said the drug was well tolerated.^[raw/articles/pubmed-33731209-sodium-oligomannate-phase3-china.md] The phase 4 registry exists specifically to monitor long-term safety in real-world use.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct05058040-gv971-postmarketing-safety.md]
