# Bryostatin-1

## Bottom Line

Bryostatin-1 is a PKCepsilon-activating synaptogenesis candidate with mixed advanced-AD trial results. Medicinal signal is `low`: cohort-specific and completer/exploratory signals remain interesting, but the full-analysis primary endpoint problem prevents a clean cognition-efficacy rating. Anecdotal signal is absent in the current wiki corpus.

## What It Is

Bryostatin-1 is a marine-derived macrolide that activates protein kinase C epsilon in the synaptic-rescue narrative. In the cognition wiki it belongs with [[synaptic-rescue-small-molecule-2026-scan]] and [[small-molecule-biomarker-behavior-failure-library]], not with acute nootropic stimulants.

## Medicinal Research Signal

The 2023 six-month paper reported a 122-patient advanced-AD trial with moderate and moderately severe cohorts. The moderately severe cohort showed significant SIB improvement over placebo from weeks 13 through 42, including persistence after dosing ended, while the moderate cohort did not clearly carry the same signal. This is phenotype-dependent and sponsor-linked rather than broad efficacy proof.^[raw/articles/pubmed-37742657-bryostatin-6-month-dlb.md]

The NCT04538066 ClinicalTrials.gov record anchors the trial-status and endpoint read. It describes a completed 122-participant moderately severe AD study with 20 ug IV dosing and a repeated-course design. The associated journal report states that the full-analysis-set primary endpoint was not significant, while completer and exploratory analyses favored 20 ug bryostatin. That is enough to keep bryostatin on the map, but not enough to call it a replicated cognition win.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct04538066-bryostatin-ad.md]

## Safety And Status

The 2023 note reported no DSMB-noted safety events, while the registry note keeps broader tolerability context visible: earlier dosing suggested 40 ug had more tolerability and dropout issues. The more important gate is still efficacy clarity.

## Signal Judgment

Medicinal signal: `low`, with a moderately severe AD subgroup/completer signal but unresolved primary-analysis weakness.

Anecdotal signal: `absent-to-weak`.

Practical route: phenotype-specific advanced-AD research candidate and mixed-evidence comparator.
