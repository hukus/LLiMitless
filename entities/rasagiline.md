# Rasagiline

Rasagiline is a prescription MAO-B inhibitor approved for Parkinson disease, closely related in practical wiki routing to [[selegiline]] and [[safinamide]]. It belongs in [[mao-b-propargylamine-cognition-watchlist]], not in the casual nootropic tier.

## Current Read

Medicinal cognition signal: `low/state-bound`. The most current class synthesis found rasagiline was the only MAO-B inhibitor with a significant global-cognition signal versus placebo in Parkinson disease, but no MAO-B inhibitor showed significant domain-specific effects on attention, executive function, memory, language, or visuospatial ability.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41524120-maob-inhibitors-pd-cognition-network-meta.md]

The best direct trial is still disease-context: in 55 randomized nondemented PD patients with cognitive impairment, rasagiline 1 mg/day for 3 months improved digit span-backward, verbal fluency total, and the attentional Z score, while other tests and domain scores were not significant.^[raw/articles/pubmed-21500280-rasagiline-pd-cognition-rct.md]

Alzheimer signal: `biomarker-positive but cognition-not-proven`. A 50-person phase II AD proof-of-concept trial met an FDG-PET metabolism endpoint and improved QoL, while Digit Span/verbal fluency/NPI were only directional and ADAS-Cog / activities of daily living did not move.^[raw/articles/pubmed-33614888-rasagiline-ad-fdg-pet-cognition.md]

## Safety Gates

DailyMed lists rasagiline as a Parkinson disease drug, not a supplement. Contraindications include several opioids, dextromethorphan, St. John's wort, cyclobenzaprine, and other MAO inhibitors; warnings include hypertension, serotonin syndrome, sleep attacks, orthostatic hypotension, dyskinesia, hallucinations, impulse-control behaviors, and withdrawal-emergent hyperpyrexia/confusion.^[raw/articles/dailymed-2025-rasagiline-label.md]

The label also caps dosing with CYP1A2 inhibitors such as ciprofloxacin and warns against very high tyramine exposure. That safety profile makes rasagiline a prescription-interaction comparator, not a casual brain-fog tool.^[raw/articles/dailymed-2025-rasagiline-label.md]

## Current Route

Use rasagiline as a [[mao-b-propargylamine-cognition-watchlist]] comparator beside [[selegiline]], [[safinamide]], [[ladostigil]], [[sembragiline]], [[kds2010-tisolagiline]], and [[lazabemide]]. Current verdict: real PD-context attention/global-cognition hints, but low-confidence and prescription-gated for healthy adults.
