# Ginseng

Ginseng here means Panax ginseng and standardized ginseng products, including sprout extract formulations, not generic "energy tonic" branding. The current human cognition signal is modest but real enough to justify a node in the wiki: a 2024 product-specific randomized trial in subjective memory impairment plus a 2026 meta-analysis in older impaired adults both point toward a small phenotype-specific benefit rather than broad healthy-adult enhancement.

## Why It Matters

- The 2026 meta-analysis pooled nine randomized studies in 617 older adults with MCI, Alzheimer's disease, or dementia and found modest MMSE and ADAS-Cog gains; the authors described the effect as biologically plausible but small and of uncertain clinical significance.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41863264-ginseng-cognitive-function-meta-analysis.md]
- The 2024 Panax ginseng sprout extract RCT in subjective memory impairment reported better verbal learning, figure recall, MoCA-K, sleep quality, and acetylcholinesterase versus placebo over 12 weeks.^[raw/articles/pubmed-38931306-panax-ginseng-sprout-extract-smi-rct.md]
- The 2026 systematic review of non-clinical randomized studies found 14 eligible articles, mostly standardized extracts, with acute studies tending to favor secondary-memory accuracy and attention; Panax quinquefolius looked somewhat more favorable for attention and working memory/executive function, and studies that did not specify ginsenoside constituents generally did not report benefits. ^[raw/articles/springer-2026-panax-ginseng-cognitive-function-systematic-review.md]
- Taken together, the evidence supports ginseng as a candidate for narrow older-adult or memory-complaint phenotypes, not as a general-purpose nootropic. The new review reinforces that product identity and ginsenoside characterization are not optional details.

## Relationship To Cognition

The most important interpretive point is product identity. Root extracts, sprout extracts, and red ginseng are not interchangeable exposures, and ginsenoside composition likely matters. That makes the branch more reproducible than folklore, but less uniform than a single-molecule intervention.

The current read is "adjunctive and modest" rather than "high-yield enhancement." If the effect is real, it looks more likely to help when baseline cognitive vulnerability is already present.

## Safety

The evidence base is not large enough to treat ginseng as automatically low risk. Insomnia, blood-pressure changes, GI effects, and interaction risk with stimulant or anticoagulant stacks still need to stay visible in this branch.
