# MFGM Phospholipids

Milk fat globule membrane (MFGM) phospholipids are dairy-derived polar-lipid fractions from the membrane surrounding milk fat globules. In this wiki the branch covers MFGM / milk phospholipid interventions relevant to cognition, not ordinary milk, butter, whey protein, phosphatidylserine alone, phosphatidylcholine, lecithin, krill oil, plasmalogens, [[dha-epa]], or [[uridine]] stacks.

Medicinal cognition signal: `low/mostly primary-endpoint null, with alertness and subgroup hints`. The governing human source is a 2025 randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial in 263 older adults with subjective memory complaints. Participants used fortified powdered milk delivering 1.7 g/day or 4.0 g/day MFGM phospholipids for 16 weeks. RBANS and COMPASS did not show significant treatment effects, while the high-dose arm better maintained subjective alertness during cognitive testing and moved TNF-alpha, GABA, serotonin, LDL/cholesterol, active B12, and homocysteine markers in potentially favorable directions.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40939691-mfgm-phospholipid-cognition-rct.md]^[raw/articles/sciencedirect-2025-mfgm-phospholipid-cognition-rct.md]

The positive side is narrower. A 2023 pilot in older adults using an MFGM-enriched milk drink reported improved episodic memory in female participants after 14 weeks, but other cognitive domains were not broadly improved and the sample was small. A 2019 stress trial in high-perfectionist men found better post-stress attention-switching reaction time after 2.7 g/day bovine milk phospholipids for 6 weeks, but no working-memory or cortisol benefit, and blood-pressure responses were augmented. These sources justify tracking MFGM as a state- and matrix-specific lipid lead, not ranking it as a general memory enhancer.^[raw/articles/sciencedirect-2023-mfgm-enriched-milk-episodic-memory-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-30172995-milk-phospholipids-stress-cognition-rct.md]

The mechanism case is plausible but broad. MFGM contains phospholipids, sphingolipids such as sphingomyelin, gangliosides, cholesterol, proteins, and glycoproteins. The 2024 review is optimistic about neurodevelopment and aging biology but explicitly says sufficiently large randomized trials are needed before health claims. That review also reinforces the product-identity problem: different commercial or lab-made MFGM products do not have identical lipid composition.^[raw/articles/rsc-2024-mfgm-polar-lipids-cognition-review.md]

Anecdote signal: `weak/mostly absent`. Sampled community sources surface research-sharing, ingredient-identification, and product-availability questions, but not a stable MFGM nootropic experience corpus. That is materially weaker than the community signal for [[phosphatidylserine]], [[dha-epa]], [[uridine]], or choline-donor stacks.^[raw/articles/reddit-2026-mfgm-phospholipids-scientificnutrition-thread.md]

Safety and product signal: `low-moderate dairy-matrix caution`. The 2025 trial did not create a major safety warning in the captured abstract, and the 2023 pilot reported minimal liver, kidney, blood-pressure, and biochemical safety changes. Practical caveats are dairy allergy, lactose intolerance, bloating/constipation, dose, fat/lipid matrix, and whether the product is fortified milk, powdered milk, whey protein phospholipid concentrate, buttermilk-derived fraction, or an adult supplement ingredient. NZMP's ingredient page shows that commercial variants differ materially in phospholipid percentage, lactose, protein, and fat.^[raw/articles/sciencedirect-2023-mfgm-enriched-milk-episodic-memory-rct.md]^[raw/articles/nzmp-2026-milk-phospholipids-product-identity.md]

## Product And Protocol Gates

- Separate fortified powdered milk, MFGM-enriched milk drinks, whey protein phospholipid concentrate, buttermilk-derived milk phospholipids, infant-formula MFGM lipids, and isolated phosphatidylserine / phosphatidylcholine supplements.
- Record phospholipid dose, percent phospholipids, lactose, protein, fat, source stream, and whether the intervention is a food matrix or capsule/powder ingredient.
- Stratify by age, sex, subjective memory complaint, MCI status, stress challenge, baseline dairy intake, B12/homocysteine status, and cardiometabolic risk.
- Keep alertness, stress reaction time, episodic memory, executive function, RBANS global cognition, and biomarker outcomes separate.
- Track dairy allergy, lactose intolerance, gastrointestinal symptoms, blood pressure during stress, lipid markers, and medication/diet confounds.

## Practical Interpretation

MFGM phospholipids deserve standalone tracking because they have several human cognition-relevant trials and a clear product-identity problem. The current best route is `dairy-matrix polar-lipid / alertness-stress / aging-biomarker watch`, not broad nootropic enhancement. The 2025 primary-endpoint null should govern ranking until longer or better-targeted trials replicate a memory or executive-function benefit.

Confidence would improve with independent long-duration trials that use a reproducible ingredient, pre-register memory/executive endpoints, test whether female/older/MCI/stress subgroups replicate, and disclose phospholipid composition and dairy-matrix matching.
