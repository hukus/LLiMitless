# Sailuotong

Sailuotong is a standardized Chinese herbal medicine formula built from [[ginseng]], [[ginkgo]], and saffron / Crocus sativus constituents. It belongs in the formula-level branch of [[traditional-medicine-cognition-2026-scan]], not as interchangeable evidence for any one ingredient.

## Bottom Line

Medicinal cognition signal: `low-moderate for formula-level impaired cognition`, `low for healthy-adult enhancement`. A small healthy-adult crossover pilot reported working-memory and auditory ERP improvements after one week, and a later 12-week phase 2 pilot in older adults with MCI reported gains on delayed recall, executive switching, and functional-activity measures. Both signals are formula-specific and should not be collapsed into generic ginkgo, ginseng, or saffron claims.^[raw/articles/bmc-2016-sailuotong-healthy-adults-crossover-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-2023-sailuotong-mci-pilot-rct.md]

Product-identity gate: high. The healthy-adult trial used capsules containing a 60 mg standardized mix of 27.27 mg ginsenosides, 27.27 mg total ginkgo flavone glycosides, and 5.46 mg crocins, taken as two capsules daily. The MCI trial used Sailuotong 180 mg/day. Those are product-level exposures, not herb-category exposures.^[raw/articles/bmc-2016-sailuotong-healthy-adults-crossover-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-2023-sailuotong-mci-pilot-rct.md]

## Formal Evidence

The healthy-adult trial was randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled, and crossover, but only 16 participants completed it and each treatment period lasted one week. That makes it a useful proof-of-concept for acute/short-course cognitive electrophysiology, not a durable enhancement result.^[raw/articles/bmc-2016-sailuotong-healthy-adults-crossover-rct.md]

The MCI phase 2 pilot randomized 78 community-dwelling adults aged at least 60 years, with 65 included in the final analysis. Sailuotong 180 mg/day for 12 weeks showed between-group advantages for Logical Memory delayed recall, Delis-Kaplan Trail Making switching and contrast scores, Rey Auditory Verbal Learning Test delayed recall, and Functional Activities Questionnaire. This is a stronger cognition-relevant anchor than the healthy-adult pilot, but it is still small and impairment-context.^[raw/articles/pubmed-2023-sailuotong-mci-pilot-rct.md]

## Safety And Status

The captured notes do not establish broad safety. Practical gates include ginkgo-related bleeding and interaction review, ginseng stimulant/sleep/glucose effects, saffron dose identity, anticoagulants/antiplatelets, surgery timing, and formula-specific quality control.

## Signal Judgment

Sailuotong is one of the cleaner traditional-formula candidates because the exposure is standardized and there are human cognition endpoints in both healthy-adult and MCI settings. It remains below cleaner single-extract candidates until larger independent trials clarify durability, endpoint robustness, and whether the effect is additive formula biology or mostly driven by one constituent.
