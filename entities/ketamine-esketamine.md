# Ketamine / Esketamine

Ketamine and esketamine are NMDA-antagonist rapid-antidepressant chemicals with unusually high public salience around neuroplasticity, depression relief, and cognitive recovery. In this wiki they are not healthy-adult nootropics; they are psychiatric-state / session-bound interventions that belong in [[glutamatergic-rapid-antidepressant-cognition-watchlist]], beside [[memantine]], [[amantadine]], and [[methylene-blue]] as glutamate-adjacent comparators with very different risk profiles.

## Current Read

Medicinal cognition signal: `low/state-bound`. The best case is not direct enhancement, but cognitive safety and possible recovery of attention/processing speed when treatment-resistant depression improves. A 2022 RCT meta-analysis across psychiatric disorders found antidepressant, anxiety, and social-function signals, but no cognition difference versus comparators.^[raw/articles/frontiers-2022-ketamine-cognition-anxiety-social-function-meta.md] A 2026 esketamine-specific systematic review in TRD found no cognitive deterioration and the most consistent improvement in attention/processing speed, but still called the evidence limited and TRD-specific.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41873584-esketamine-cognition-systematic-review.md]

Same-day cognition signal: `negative/risk`. SPRAVATO's label warns that esketamine can impair attention, judgment, thinking, reaction speed, and motor skills, requires observation in a certified setting, and instructs patients not to drive or operate machinery until the next day after restful sleep.^[raw/articles/dailymed-2026-spravato-esketamine-label.md]

Anecdote signal: `moderate but polarized`. Threads contain improved memory, focus, task initiation, and brain-fog reports, but also word-finding, short-term-memory, chronic spaciness, feeling less sharp after sessions, and persistent-cognitive-concern reports.^[raw/articles/reddit-2024-2026-ketamine-auvelity-cognition-brainfog-threads.md]

## Formal Evidence

- Depression treatment: ketamine can produce rapid antidepressant effects, and esketamine has regulatory approval for adult TRD contexts; that does not convert it into an enhancer for healthy adults.^[raw/articles/pubmed-35416105-ketamine-esketamine-long-term-safety.md]^[raw/articles/dailymed-2026-spravato-esketamine-label.md]
- Cognition in psychiatric RCTs: the 2022 meta-analysis found no cognition advantage over comparators despite mood/anxiety/social-function movement.^[raw/articles/frontiers-2022-ketamine-cognition-anxiety-social-function-meta.md]
- Esketamine cognitive safety: the 2026 systematic review suggests no domain deterioration and possible attention/processing-speed recovery in TRD, with memory/executive effects more variable and follow-up dependent.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41873584-esketamine-cognition-systematic-review.md]
- Long-term safety: high-dose ketamine exposure carries a stronger cognitive-impairment concern, while esketamine trials generally reported stable or improved cognition when used in protocolized depression settings.^[raw/articles/pubmed-35416105-ketamine-esketamine-long-term-safety.md]

## Safety And Status Gates

- SPRAVATO is a REMS-restricted Schedule III drug, not a take-home casual nootropic. Sedation, dissociation, respiratory depression, abuse/misuse, BP increases, cystitis/lower urinary symptoms, fetal risk, driving restriction, and session monitoring are first-order variables.^[raw/articles/dailymed-2026-spravato-esketamine-label.md]
- Do not compare same-day postdose performance with long-term mood-state recovery. The wiki should ask whether cognition was measured predose, outside the dissociative window, and with practice effects controlled.
- Do not borrow recreational high-dose cognitive-harm data to condemn protocolized medical use, but do not borrow protocolized esketamine cognitive-safety data to validate unsupervised or escalating use.
