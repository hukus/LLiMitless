# Magnesium L-Threonate

Magnesium L-threonate, often branded as Magtein, is a product-specific magnesium form positioned for brain magnesium delivery, sleep, and cognition. It should not be collapsed into generic magnesium supplementation.

Medicinal signal: `low-moderate`. The 2026 randomized double-blind placebo-controlled trial in 100 adults aged 18-45 with self-reported dissatisfied sleep used 2 g/day Magtein for six weeks. The active group improved more on NIH Total Cognition Composite, working / episodic memory signals, reaction time, estimated cognitive age, heart rate, and HRV, but Raven's Progressive Matrices was null and several sleep outcomes did not separate.^[raw/articles/frontiers-2026-magnesium-l-threonate-cognition-sleep-rct.md]
