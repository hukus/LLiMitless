# Glutathione Products

Glutathione products include reduced glutathione capsules, liposomal or micellar oral products, sublingual products, S-acetyl-glutathione-style labels, and injection/IV clinic claims. This page is for direct glutathione-product attribution, not [[n-acetylcysteine]], [[glynac]], [[glycine]], cysteine, generic antioxidants, or "detox" stacks.^[raw/articles/pubchem-2026-glutathione-identity.md]

Medicinal cognition signal: `very low direct, plausible biomarker`. The strongest human product data here show peripheral glutathione bioavailability or body-store movement in healthy adults, not cognition. A 6-month oral glutathione RCT increased blood/tissue glutathione markers and changed oxidative-stress/immune markers; a small liposomal pilot found similar biomarker movement; a 2026 crossover study found a micellar product gave higher systemic glutathione exposure than standard glutathione. None of these tested objective cognition.^[raw/articles/pubmed-24791752-oral-glutathione-body-stores-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-28853742-liposomal-glutathione-body-stores-pilot.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41897500-micellar-glutathione-bioavailability-rct.md]

Brain-biomarker signal: `mechanistic, not supplement proof`. A 2026 MRS study in young and older adults found age-, region-, and state-dependent relationships among brain glutathione, memory tasks, sustained cognitive effort, and GABA/Glx measures. It supports cognition relevance for glutathione physiology, but it also argues against a simple "more glutathione is better" rule.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41836095-brain-glutathione-cognition-mrs.md]

Anecdote signal: `moderate illness-state salience, weak healthy cognition`. Reddit and adjacent forum trails show glutathione products in long-COVID, ME/CFS, mold/MCAS-style, detox, fatigue, and brain-fog stacks. Positive reports are usually clearer-head or less-fatigue narratives; counterreports include headache, sulfur/methylation intolerance claims, insomnia, anxiety, GI effects, no effect, or crash-like reactions. Route and cointervention confounding dominate.^[raw/articles/reddit-2021-2026-glutathione-brainfog-threads.md]

## Signal Split

| Layer | Signal | Readout |
|---|---|---|
| Standard oral reduced glutathione | `biomarker positive` | 250-1000 mg/day increased body-store markers in healthy adults, no cognition endpoint |
| Liposomal glutathione | `small biomarker positive` | 500-1000 mg/day pilot raised glutathione and immune/oxidative markers, no cognition endpoint |
| Micellar glutathione | `PK/formulation positive` | 2026 crossover shows higher exposure than standard product, no cognition endpoint |
| Brain glutathione MRS | `mechanistic mixed` | Age-, region-, and task-state dependent cognition relationships |
| Anecdote | `moderate illness-state, weak healthy cognition` | Brain-fog reports are mainly long-COVID/CFS/detox/MCAS-style |
| Safety / sourcing | `moderate product-route caution` | Route, sterile injection/IV risks, sulfur sensitivity claims, insomnia/anxiety, GI, and stack confounding |

## Practical Interpretation

[[glutathione-products]] belongs in the redox lane as an attribution-control and route-specific biomarker branch. It is lower-confidence than [[glynac]] for cognition because GlyNAC at least has small older-adult cognition pilots, while direct glutathione products mainly have peripheral biomarkers and illness-state anecdotes. Any future upgrade requires a randomized cognitive endpoint, product form verification, baseline oxidative-stress or glutathione status, route safety, and separation from NAC, glycine, methyl donors, antihistamines, binders, pacing, and recovery trajectory.
