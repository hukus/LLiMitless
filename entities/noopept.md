# Noopept

Noopept is a Russian nootropic / neuroprotective agent, often marketed as racetam-adjacent even though it is not a simple piracetam analogue in the usual consumer sense. The branch is real but thin: the formal human evidence lives in vascular and post-traumatic cognitive impairment, while the preclinical literature is much more mechanism-heavy than the human bridge. This is a sourcing-sensitive branch, not a validated healthy-adult enhancer.

June 2026 anecdote read: `moderate classic-nootropic salience, low healthy-adult confidence`. Noopept deserves to stay in the high-visibility nootropic map because it appears in the 2017 community survey dose table and has a captured Reddit experience thread with many replies, but the direction is mixed: short-lived focus, mood, productivity, memory, and brain-fog reports sit beside sublingual brain fog, irritability, insomnia, no-effect reports, and source-quality doubts.^[raw/articles/darktka-2017-nootropics-survey-on-r-nootropics.md]^[raw/articles/reddit-noopept-racetams-experiences-2023.md]

## Overview

- The 2002 review presents noopept as a piracetam-inspired nootropic with stronger preclinical mnemotropic and neuroprotective claims than the human package can currently support.
- The 2008 comparative study places noopept in mild to moderate cognitive impairment from vascular and traumatic brain disease.
- The 2008 EEG study reports nootropic-like activation in patients with cognitive disturbance, with the strongest signal in vascular disease.
- The animal memory-blockade study supports antiamnesic plausibility, but it does not resolve transportability into healthy humans.
- The branch is therefore best read as a rescue / impairment-context lead with formulation and product-identity sensitivity.

## Key Facts

- Human evidence is concentrated in cerebrovascular and post-traumatic impairment, not baseline healthy enhancement.
- The older review notes tablet development for 5 mg and 10 mg formulations, but that is not the same thing as a modern dosing standard.
- The most defensible interpretation is "human-light but real," not "folk nootropic."

## Safety And Sourcing

- Human safety data are sparse relative to the enthusiasm around the branch.
- Product identity, salt/form, and route matter. This is a supply-chain-sensitive molecule, not a commodity supplement.
- The comparator logic matters too: the branch should be interpreted against vascular / post-traumatic impairment and related rescue states, not against rested healthy adults.
- The market story is noisier than the molecule story, so source QA is part of the safety profile.
- Route Noopept anecdotes through [[safety-interaction-matrix-for-multi-modal-stacks]] when they appear in racetam, stimulant, sleep, or mood stacks. Product identity, route, timing, sleep, irritability, insomnia, and source quality need separation before treating focus or brain-fog reports as molecule-specific.
- June 2026 noopept/racetam-adjacent gate: Noopept reports should capture exact product identity and source, COA where available, capsule/tablet/powder/sublingual/intranasal route, dose and timing, acute versus repeated use, baseline vascular/post-traumatic impairment versus healthy status, sleep and fatigue state, stimulant/caffeine/nicotine/racetam/choline/[[phenylpiracetam]]/[[bromantane]]/[[semax]] co-use, mood or anxiety baseline, irritability/insomnia/brain-fog adverse events, washout and dechallenge/rechallenge, and objective memory, attention, work-output, or symptom-scale endpoints before ranking movement.
