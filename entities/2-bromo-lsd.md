# 2-Bromo-LSD

2-Bromo-LSD, also called 2-Br-LSD or BOL-148, is a non-hallucinogenic LSD analog and lysergamide psychoplastogen comparator. It belongs in the wiki because it helps separate "non-hallucinogenic 5-HT2A-adjacent plasticity" from classic psychedelic microdosing and from newer clinical neuroplastogens such as [[zalsupindole-dlx-001]].

Medicinal cognition signal: `preclinical / non-cognition`. The captured 2023 paper profiled 2-Br-LSD across aminergic GPCRs, found a non-head-twitch mouse profile, and described mood-disorder therapeutic potential. The 2025 5-HT2A efficacy paper cautions that receptor signaling efficacy may matter more than a simple biased-agonism explanation. Neither source establishes human cognition or healthy-adult enhancement.^[raw/articles/pubmed-36884348-2-br-lsd-nonhallucinogenic-lsd-analog.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40545270-5ht2a-efficacy-nonpsychedelic-drugs.md]

Anecdote signal: `weak and non-cognition`. The checked community trail is mostly chemistry, headache, and psychedelic-analog discussion. It does not contain a structured nootropic or brain-fog self-experiment corpus for 2-Br-LSD.^[raw/articles/reddit-2023-2026-nonhallucinogenic-neuroplastogen-threads.md]
