# Diethylpropion

Diethylpropion, also called amfepramone, is a prescription sympathomimetic anorectic sibling in the classic diet-pill stimulant branch. In this wiki it belongs beside [[phentermine]], [[phendimetrazine]], [[benzphetamine]], and [[fenfluramine]] as a risk-first comparator, not as a validated nootropic.

## Current Read

Medicinal cognition signal: `risk-first / absent`. The captured label packet frames diethylpropion as a weight-reduction anorectic, not as a cognitive enhancer. No direct healthy-adult cognition trial or strong molecule-specific brain-fog anecdote packet is captured here.^[raw/articles/dailymed-2026-sympathomimetic-anorectic-labels.md]

Class relevance: `similar to tesofensine only at the appetite/stimulant interpretation layer`. Diethylpropion is closer to [[phentermine]] and the classic sympathomimetic anorectic labels than to [[tesofensine]]'s long-half-life triple-reuptake profile. The shared problem is interpretive: appetite suppression, stimulation, and reduced food noise can be mistaken for cognition.

## Label And Safety Layer

The DailyMed label cluster checked diethylpropion immediate-release and extended-release labels alongside phendimetrazine and benzphetamine. Shared cautions included monotherapy or avoidance of combination with other anorectic products, primary pulmonary hypertension, valvular-heart-disease concern, cardiovascular stimulation, hypertension caution, alcohol / CNS-active-drug caution, abuse/dependence, tolerance, and withdrawal after prolonged high-dose use.^[raw/articles/dailymed-2026-sympathomimetic-anorectic-labels.md]

That makes diethylpropion useful as a class boundary page. It should keep [[phentermine]] anecdotes from being overgeneralized to every anorectic stimulant, and it should keep [[tesofensine]] interest from turning into a broad "diet-pill nootropic" category.

## Interpretation Gates

- Do not infer cognition from appetite suppression, weight loss, less food noise, or short-term stimulation.
- Separate diethylpropion from [[phentermine]] because the current wiki has phentermine-specific sleep-deprivation and old acute-task signals, but no equivalent diethylpropion cognition packet.
- Watch BP/HR, insomnia, anxiety, tolerance, dependence, MAOI or other stimulant stacking, alcohol/CNS-active co-use, cardiovascular history, pulmonary hypertension / valvular-disease concern, eating-disorder context, and caloric-deficit brain fog.
