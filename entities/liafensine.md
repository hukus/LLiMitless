# Liafensine

Liafensine / DB104 / BMS-820836 is a biomarker-guided treatment-resistant-depression SNDRI candidate and triple reuptake inhibitor. It belongs in [[triple-reuptake-inhibitor-cognition-watchlist]] beside [[ansofaxine-toludesvenlafaxine]], [[amitifadine]], [[gsk372475]], and [[csti-500]].

## Current Read

Medicinal cognition signal: `low / biomarker-gated mood-state`. The important modern result is ENLIGHTEN: an ANK3-positive, biomarker-guided phase 2b TRD trial. That makes liafensine a serious psychiatric-state signal, especially for anhedonia and functional recovery, but not a healthy-adult cognition proof.

ENLIGHTEN assessed 1,967 patients for eligibility and randomized 189 ANK3-positive TRD patients to liafensine 1 mg, liafensine 2 mg, or placebo for 6 weeks. The combined liafensine groups improved MADRS more than placebo at day 42, with treatment difference -4.4, p = 0.006.^[raw/articles/jama-2025-liafensine-enlighten-ank3-trd.md]

The caveat is central: the JAMA article notes liafensine failed in two prior large phase 2b trials in non-biomarker-selected TRD populations. The positive signal therefore belongs to an ANK3-positive responder hypothesis, not to generic SNDRI effects.^[raw/articles/jama-2025-liafensine-enlighten-ank3-trd.md]

Development status: Denovo announced FDA Fast Track designation for DB104 / liafensine in biomarker-guided TRD. This is status evidence, not efficacy evidence, but it keeps the branch live.^[raw/articles/prnewswire-2024-liafensine-fast-track-trd.md]

## Interpretation Gates

- Do not generalize from ANK3-positive TRD to healthy adults.
- Keep previous non-selected TRD trial failures in the same paragraph as the ENLIGHTEN positive result.
- Separate MADRS/anhedonia response from attention, working memory, processing speed, and brain-fog endpoints.
- Watch serotonin/norepinephrine/dopamine reuptake stacking, BP/HR, insomnia, anxiety/activation, appetite, nausea, medication washout, and suicidality monitoring.

Current verdict: live biomarker-guided TRD candidate with cognition-adjacent mood/function relevance; not a nootropic candidate.
