# N-Acetylcysteine

N-acetylcysteine (NAC) is an acetylated cysteine derivative used clinically as a glutathione precursor, mucolytic, and acetaminophen-toxicity antidote, while also appearing in supplement and nootropic stacks. For cognition, it belongs in the redox / glutathione / glutamate-modulation lane of [[redox-mitochondrial-cofactor-cognition-watchlist]], not in the broad healthy-enhancement tier.

Medicinal cognition signal: `low`. The freshest controlled cognition anchor is a 2026 vascular-MCI cardiac-rehabilitation RCT: executive function improved over time across the cohort, but NAC 2400 mg/day added no benefit over placebo on executive function or other cognitive domains. This is important because it tested NAC on top of a strong behavioral/recovery background and found no additive cognition signal.^[raw/articles/springer-2026-nac-vascular-mci-cardiac-rehab-rct.md]

Psychiatric cognition signal: `low/mixed-to-negative`. In early-phase schizophrenia spectrum disorders, NAC 3600 mg/day improved PANSS total, negative, and disorganized-thought symptoms but did not improve BACS cognition. In clozapine-treated schizophrenia, NAC 2 g/day did not improve overall cognition or quality of life. Treat psychiatric symptom shifts and cognitive performance as separable endpoints.^[raw/articles/pubmed-29588126-nac-early-schizophrenia-cognition.md]^[raw/articles/pmc-9673271-nac-clozapine-schizophrenia-rct.md]

Recovery-state signal: `low/provisional`. Post-concussion work keeps NAC relevant to injury/recovery and functional-connectivity hypotheses, but it should not be generalized to healthy nootropic use without endpoint-specific replication.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39531327-nac-post-concussion-connectivity-cognition.md]

GlyNAC distinction: `do not collapse`. [[glynac]] combines glycine with NAC and has pilot aging data reporting cognition and mechanism improvements, but that is a combination and aging-metabolism signal, not NAC-alone proof. Glycine dose, glutathione repletion, and aging phenotype are part of the exposure.^[raw/articles/pmc-11689385-glynac-aging-cognition-pilot.md]

Regulatory / product context: `caution`. FDA's 2022 guidance describes NAC's contested dietary-supplement status and enforcement-discretion posture. That matters because NAC anecdotes often come from supplement products, while NAC is also a drug-associated molecule.^[raw/articles/fda-2022-nac-enforcement-discretion-guidance.md]

## Signal Split

| Layer | Signal | Readout |
|---|---|---|
| Vascular MCI plus rehab | `low/negative additive` | Current controlled trial found no NAC advantage over placebo |
| Schizophrenia cognition | `low/mixed-to-negative` | Symptom effects can occur without BACS or global cognition gains |
| Post-concussion recovery | `low/provisional` | Relevant recovery-state work, not healthy enhancement |
| GlyNAC aging branch | `low but mechanistically interesting` | Combination pilot should not be collapsed into NAC alone |
| Healthy enhancement | `unclear/unsupported` | No clean healthy-adult cognitive enhancement packet captured |
| Anecdote | `moderate-strong` | Repeated brain-fog relief, adverse fog/anhedonia, and stimulant-smoothing reports |
| Safety / sourcing | `moderate caution` | Product status, medication context, oxidative/redox phenotype, anhedonia/fog, and GI/tolerability should be monitored |

## Practical Interpretation

NAC should be routed through [[molecule-intake-triage-table]] as a `redox / glutamate / recovery-state responder hypothesis`. It is inclusion-worthy because community signal is strong and formal trials exist, but the best controlled cognition evidence is negative or phenotype-bound. For cognitive-improvement ranking, separate symptom relief, stimulant side-effect smoothing, post-concussion recovery, and objective cognitive performance before scoring benefit.
