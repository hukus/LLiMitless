# Oxytocin

## Bottom Line

Oxytocin is a social-behavior and apathy-domain peptide, not a broad cognitive enhancer. Medicinal signal is `low-moderate` for FTD apathy after the 2025 FOXY trial, but `low` for general cognition: broad social-cognition batteries remain inconsistent or null, and newer imaging/preclinical work mostly supports context-dependent target engagement. Anecdotal cognition signal is `absent-to-weak` in the current captured corpus.

## What It Is

Oxytocin is a neuropeptide and hormone involved in social behavior, reproductive physiology, and network-level social-emotional modulation. In this wiki it belongs with [[oxytocin-vasopressin-desmopressin-social-memory-2026-scan]], [[vasopressin]], [[desmopressin]], [[peptide-safety-and-sourcing-risk-map]], and [[cognitive-fidelity-split]] as a social/apathy-domain molecule.

## Medicinal Research Signal

The strongest current clinical signal is FOXY, a multicenter adaptive crossover phase 2a/2b trial in probable FTD with apathy. Intranasal oxytocin every third day, with 72 IU selected as the best dose schedule, improved apathy versus placebo by an estimated -1.32 NPI points. No adverse events were attributed to oxytocin; headache and upper respiratory tract infection were the main observed adverse events. This is behavioral and phenotype-bound, not broad cognition.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39862881-oxytocin-foxy-ftd-apathy.md]

The older FTD dose-finding study used 24, 48, or 72 IU intranasal oxytocin twice daily for one week in bvFTD and semantic dementia. All doses were reported safe and well tolerated, with serum sodium, blood pressure, and heart rate monitored, but overall NPI did not significantly change. That makes it a tolerability and dosing context source, not an efficacy proof.^[raw/articles/pubmed-25503617-oxytocin-ftd-dose-finding-safety-tolerability.md]

The broad social-cognition counterweight is a randomized controlled trial testing oxytocin and vasopressin across multiple social-cognitive and behavioral paradigms. The result was broadly null, which is a strong guardrail against simple "social hormone = cognition enhancer" claims.^[raw/articles/pubmed-31128568-oxytocin-vasopressin-null-social-paradigms.md]

The newer human neuroimaging layer supports context-dependence. A 2026 PRISMA review of 27 fMRI studies concluded that intranasal oxytocin effects vary by individual traits, sex, dosage, and context through amygdala-centered network changes. A 2026 randomized fMRI CAP study found that a single 24 IU dose altered dynamic brain-state metrics in younger and older adults, but did not test cognitive improvement.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41554388-oxytocin-social-brain-review-2026.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41876708-oxytocin-dynamic-brain-connectivity-2026.md]

The preclinical layer is mechanistically useful but not directly translational. A hamster study suggests oxytocin can regulate social communication through vasopressin V1a receptors, and a mouse sleep-deprivation study tied reduced oxytocin release to social-memory deficits. These papers support receptor cross-talk and state-dependent social-memory biology, not human enhancement.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41249098-oxytocin-v1a-social-communication-hamster.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41660638-oxytocin-sleep-deprivation-social-memory-deficits.md]

## Anecdote Signal

No oxytocin-specific nootropic anecdote raw note is currently indexed. Public claims about bonding, trust, empathy, libido, attachment, or social confidence should be separated from objective cognition and social-memory endpoints. Anecdotal cognition signal is therefore `absent-to-weak`.

## Safety And Status

For cognition purposes, the main issue is overgeneralization. Intranasal dose, context, sex, baseline traits, diagnosis, and behavioral target can change direction or magnitude. FTD trial tolerability does not create a general self-experimentation protocol, and receptor cross-talk with vasopressin pathways makes peptide-label reasoning brittle.

## Signal Judgment

Medicinal signal: `low-moderate` for FTD apathy/social-behavior context; `low` for general cognition.

Anecdotal signal: `absent-to-weak`.

Practical route: social/apathy-domain comparator; require task, phenotype, and delivery details before ranking any claim.
