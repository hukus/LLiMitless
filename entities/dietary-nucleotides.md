# Dietary Nucleotides

Dietary nucleotides are food- or supplement-derived nucleotide mixtures, usually built from 5'-monophosphate nucleotides or nucleotide-rich yeast extracts. In this wiki the branch means the older-adult RiboDiet / RiboCARE Neuro evidence lane, not [[uridine]] alone, NMN/NR, ATP, yeast extract generally, infant-formula nucleotide history, DNA/RNA powders, or generic "nucleotide support" marketing.

Medicinal cognition signal: `low-moderate/preliminary and product-specific older-adult`. The strongest direct source is a 2025 randomized, triple-blind, controlled trial in 69 physically independent adults aged 60-75. Over 10 weeks, both nucleotide arms improved Trail Making Test B versus placebo while participants maintained spontaneous physical activity and did not add structured exercise. The study also reported improvements in physical performance, oxidative-stress biomarkers, inflammatory cytokines, body-composition stability, and quality-of-life dimensions.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40362739-dietary-nucleotides-older-adults-rct.md]^[raw/articles/mdpi-2025-dietary-nucleotides-older-adults-rct-fulltext.md]

The signal is cleaner than many nutraceutical leads because the study names products and doses. The yeast-nucleotide arm used 250 mg/day RiboDiet, a yeast extract standardized to a high free-nucleotide content and containing related yeast-cell nutrients. The neuro-based arm used 150 mg/day RiboCARE Neuro, a defined blend of 5'-AMP, 5'-CMP, 5'-UMP, and 5'-GMP. That product identity makes the result more transportable than a vague "nucleotides" label, but it also means the result should stay product-specific.^[raw/articles/mdpi-2025-dietary-nucleotides-older-adults-rct-fulltext.md]^[raw/articles/prosol-2026-ribodiet-ribocare-product-identity.md]

The main medicinal caveat is replication. TMT-B is a useful executive-function / processing-speed endpoint, and the BDNF correlation is biologically interesting, but the trial is small, short, older-adult-only, activity-context dependent, and partly industry supplied. A separate TALENTs nucleotide RCT in 121 older adults supports an adjacent healthy-aging and safety story at 1.2 g/day, with epigenetic-age and insulin-sensitivity findings, but it does not provide a direct cognition-efficacy upgrade.^[raw/articles/pmc-12412603-talents-nucleotides-aging-rct.md]

Anecdote signal: `weak/mostly absent and uridine-adjacent`. Sampled community searches did not surface a durable RiboDiet / RiboCARE / dietary-nucleotide nootropic experience corpus. The nearest nootropic community lane is [[uridine]] / UMP / triacetyluridine stacking, but that is a different active-unit and stack-attribution problem. It should inform boundary checks, not efficacy ranking for mixed dietary nucleotide products.^[raw/articles/reddit-2021-dietary-nucleotides-community-absence-thread.md]^[raw/articles/reddit-2025-uridine-nootropics-must-thread.md]

Safety and product signal: `low-moderate product-identity caution`. The direct 10-week trial did not surface a major acute safety signal in the captured abstract, and the full text states the tested doses aligned with a Spanish maximum-intake recommendation for this supplement type. TALENTs also reported no severe adverse events, but it noted a transient uric-acid increase at midpoint that returned to baseline. Practical interpretation should therefore record gout / hyperuricemia context, yeast sensitivity, product composition, dose, duration, and whether the exposure is a yeast pool or defined monophosphate blend.^[raw/articles/mdpi-2025-dietary-nucleotides-older-adults-rct-fulltext.md]^[raw/articles/pmc-12412603-talents-nucleotides-aging-rct.md]

## Product And Protocol Gates

- Separate RiboDiet yeast-extract nucleotide pool from RiboCARE Neuro monophosphate blend.
- Record dose, duration, meal timing, activity context, baseline cognitive status, baseline physical activity, and whether structured exercise was added.
- Do not collapse dietary nucleotides into [[uridine]], NMN/NR, ATP, RNA powders, infant-formula nucleotide fortification, or generic yeast extract.
- Track purine load, uric acid / gout history, kidney disease context, yeast allergy or intolerance, and concurrent supplements that change energy, methylation, choline, or sleep.
- Treat BDNF, oxidative stress, inflammation, physical performance, and quality-of-life improvements as supporting context until replicated cognitive endpoints show durable transfer.

## Practical Interpretation

Dietary nucleotides deserve standalone tracking because the 2025 older-adult RCT is a clean product-specific surprise positive and because product identity is unusually explicit for a nutraceutical. They do not yet deserve broad nootropic ranking. The best current route is `older-adult healthy-aging / executive-function proxy / product-specific nutraceutical`, with formal evidence carrying the page and anecdote kept weak.

Confidence would improve with an independent replication that pre-registers cognition domains, includes longer washout or durability follow-up, separates RiboDiet from RiboCARE Neuro adequately, stratifies baseline activity and cognition, monitors uric acid, and discloses batch-level product data.
