# Kisspeptin

## Bottom Line

Kisspeptin is a CNS-active endocrine peptide, but current cognition signal is `preclinical/physiology-only` rather than efficacy. Human work shows reproductive-hormone activity, sexual/emotional brain-processing effects, and a 2025 anxiety-null RCT; a 2025 rodent anatomy paper maps broad behavioral-state circuitry. None of this is a direct cognition endpoint. Anecdotal cognition signal is `absent-to-weak` in the current captured corpus.

## What It Is

Kisspeptin is an upstream reproductive-axis peptide linked to GnRH regulation and extra-hypothalamic brain signaling. In this wiki it belongs with [[neurosteroid-and-endocrine-state-cognition-2026-scan]], [[gnrh]], [[peptide-safety-and-sourcing-risk-map]], and [[target-specificity-gate-for-plasticity-molecules]] as a mechanism-routing node, not a demonstrated cognitive enhancer.

## Medicinal Research Signal

The current human intranasal study confirms kisspeptin can be a biologically active endocrine intervention, but its endpoints were reproductive hormone profiles in healthy volunteers and patients with reproductive disorders. It does not test cognition.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40215751-intranasal-kisspeptin-hormone-release.md]

An earlier randomized human imaging study found that kisspeptin modulated brain processing linked to sexual and emotional functions. This supports CNS activity and affective/reproductive relevance, but it is not memory, attention, executive function, or learning efficacy.^[raw/articles/pubmed-28112678-kisspeptin-sexual-emotional-brain-processing.md]

The 2025 anxiety RCT is useful as a negative-claim guardrail. IV kisspeptin-54 produced the expected LH rise in 95 healthy participants, but did not significantly change state anxiety, cortisol, blood pressure, or heart rate. That is reassuring for the measured acute physiology but does not support an anxiolytic or cognition claim.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40036336-kisspeptin-anxiety-rct.md]

The 2025 anatomy paper mapped kisspeptin fibers in 118 rodent brain regions, especially extra-hypothalamic sensorial-processing and behavioral-state-control regions. This is a mechanism clue for future cognition studies, not human cognition evidence.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40065551-kisspeptin-state-control-anatomy.md]

June 2026 attribution gate: kisspeptin should stay an upstream reproductive/endocrine routing node until direct cognition evidence appears. Future reports need exact kisspeptin form, source/prescription status, route/formulation, dose timing, reproductive-hormone baseline and response, sex/age and fertility context, libido or sexual/emotional task separation, anxiety/autonomic measures, co-interventions, adverse endocrine or mood effects, washout, and objective cognition endpoints before any ranking comparison with [[gnrh]], [[semax]], [[selank]], [[cerebrolysin]], recovery-bound [[bpc-157]], or [[bromantane]].

## Safety And Status

The practical safety issue is over-translation. Human hormone-release and anxiety-null results do not establish a cognition protocol, and reproductive-axis dosing should not be imported into nootropic use without cognition endpoints and endocrine monitoring.
