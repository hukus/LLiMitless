# Ivabradine

Ivabradine / Corlanor is a prescription sinus-node I_f-current inhibitor that lowers heart rate without being a beta blocker. In this wiki it belongs in the autonomic / POTS / long-COVID brain-fog lane, not in the normal nootropic lane.

## Current Read

Medicinal cognition signal: `low/state-bound`. Ivabradine has meaningful POTS evidence, including a small hyperadrenergic-POTS crossover RCT and a 2026 ivabradine-versus-propranolol crossover trial, but those studies are about tachycardia, symptoms, and quality of life rather than direct cognition.^[raw/articles/acc-2021-ivabradine-hyperadrenergic-pots-rct.md]^[raw/articles/acc-2026-ivabradine-propranolol-pots-rct.md]

Long-COVID/PASC signal: `contested`. RECOVER-AUTONOMIC is the critical caution: in 181 adults with Long COVID POTS, ivabradine significantly lowered heart rate versus placebo but did not significantly improve participant-survey POTS symptoms. Coordinated care plus ivabradine looked better than ivabradine plus usual care, suggesting rate control alone is not enough for many PASC patients.^[raw/articles/recover-2026-autonomic-ivabradine-long-covid-results.md]

Anecdote signal: `moderate but confounded`. Reddit POTS/dysautonomia/long-COVID reports include reduced brain fog, work-capacity gains, and better exertional tolerance, but also no benefit, worse brain fog or headaches, exhaustion, dizziness, bradycardia, and visual phenomena. Most positive long-COVID reports include co-interventions such as compression, electrolytes, antihistamines, guanfacine, propranolol, LDN, sleep drugs, pacing, or supplements.^[raw/articles/reddit-2024-2026-ivabradine-pots-brainfog-threads.md]

| 2026 POTS crossover RCT | 28 female POTS patients received ivabradine, propranolol, and placebo phases | Ivabradine and propranolol reduced orthostatic tachycardia and symptoms versus placebo | Stronger class comparator; still orthostatic symptom support, not nootropic proof |

## Safety And Interpretation Gates

DailyMed labels Corlanor for heart failure, not POTS or cognition. Key gates include fetal toxicity, atrial fibrillation monitoring, bradycardia and conduction disturbance, luminous phenomena/phosphenes, hypertension, CYP3A4 inhibitor/inducer interactions, additive bradycardia with negative chronotropes, and breastfeeding not recommended.^[raw/articles/dailymed-2026-corlanor-ivabradine-label.md]

Do not interpret "clearer head" as direct cognitive enhancement unless orthostatic physiology has been measured. In the current evidence, the plausible mechanism is less tachycardia / orthostatic stress, not improved memory, attention, or executive function in healthy rested adults.

## Current Route

Use ivabradine through [[autonomic-pots-brain-fog-chemical-watchlist]] when brain fog tracks standing, tachycardia, inappropriate sinus tachycardia, POTS, or long-COVID dysautonomia. Compare against [[pyridostigmine]], [[desmopressin]], midodrine, fludrocortisone, droxidopa, and low-dose propranolol before treating it as a general stimulant alternative.
