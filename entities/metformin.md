# Metformin

Metformin is a prescription biguanide glucose-lowering drug with a long-COVID prevention signal from acute-infection treatment. In this wiki it belongs in metabolic/post-viral prevention routing, not as a treatment for established brain fog or as a healthy-adult nootropic.

## Bottom Line

Long-COVID signal: `moderate for prevention during acute COVID, low/indirect for brain fog`. In COVID-OUT, early outpatient metformin in adults with overweight or obesity reduced clinician-diagnosed long COVID over 10 months versus matched placebo. This was not a trial of established long COVID and did not prove treatment of existing cognitive symptoms.^[raw/articles/sciencedirect-2023-covidout-metformin-long-covid-prevention-rct.md]

Healthy-adult cognition signal: `not established`. Any relevance to cognition is indirect: reducing long-COVID incidence may prevent downstream fatigue/brain-fog states, but metformin should not be ranked as a cognitive enhancer from this evidence.

Practical route: prescription and risk-gated. The label has a boxed warning for lactic acidosis and highlights renal impairment, carbonic anhydrase inhibitors, older age, iodinated contrast, surgery/procedures, hypoxic states, excess alcohol, hepatic impairment, vitamin B12 deficiency, and hypoglycemia risk when combined with insulin or insulin secretagogues.^[raw/articles/dailymed-2026-metformin-hcl-er-label.md]

## Formal Evidence

COVID-OUT randomized 1,126 adults aged 30-85 with overweight or obesity, acute symptomatic SARS-CoV-2 infection, and early outpatient enrollment. Metformin was titrated to 1,500 mg/day over six days and continued for 14 days. By day 300, long COVID diagnosis was reported in 6.3% of metformin participants versus 10.4% of matched-placebo participants; ivermectin and fluvoxamine did not reduce long-COVID incidence.^[raw/articles/sciencedirect-2023-covidout-metformin-long-covid-prevention-rct.md]

## Signal Judgment

Metformin is one of the cleaner prevention leads for post-COVID condition, but it should stay outside the "treat my current brain fog" bucket until direct established-long-COVID trials show benefit.
