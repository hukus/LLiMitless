# Uridine

Uridine, often discussed as uridine monophosphate / UMP in nootropic stacks, is a membrane-phospholipid and synapse-support hypothesis node. The current captured cognition source is preclinical, not human efficacy evidence.

Medicinal signal: `preclinical-only`. The captured gerbil study tested uridine monophosphate with DHA and choline and framed the combination around brain phosphatides, synaptic proteins, dendritic spines, neurotransmitter release, learning, and memory. That is mechanistically relevant to [[choline-donor-cognition-signal-map]], but it does not prove human cognitive improvement.^[raw/articles/pmc-2574024-uridine-dha-gerbil-learning-memory.md]
