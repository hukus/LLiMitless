# Uridine

Uridine, often discussed as uridine monophosphate / UMP or triacetyluridine in nootropic stacks, is a membrane-phospholipid and synapse-support hypothesis node. In this wiki it is now a stack- and formulation-gated node: human evidence exists for uridine-containing multinutrient formulas, but not for standalone uridine as a healthy-adult cognitive enhancer.

Medicinal signal: `low/stack-formulation-gated`. The mechanistic anchor remains preclinical UMP plus DHA and choline. Human combination-product evidence comes mostly from Souvenaid / Fortasyn Connect, a multinutrient formula containing UMP, choline, phospholipids, omega-3 fatty acids, B vitamins, antioxidants, and selenium. That branch has mild/prodromal Alzheimer's disease signals, an add-on mild-to-moderate Alzheimer's null trial, and a Cochrane-level clinically modest or little/no-effect counterweight, so it cannot be converted into a uridine-alone or healthy-adult claim.^[raw/articles/pmc-2574024-uridine-dha-gerbil-learning-memory.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-20129316-souvenaid-mild-ad-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-24280255-sconnect-souvenaid-mild-moderate-ad-null.md]^[raw/articles/pmc-7821311-lipididiet-fortasyn-36mo-prodromal-ad.md]^[raw/articles/cochrane-2020-souvenaid-ad-review.md]

Healthy-adult cognition signal: `negative/acute stack-null` for the cleanest captured nootropic-stack trial. In 20 college-aged trained men, acute alpha-GPC 500 mg plus UMP 250 mg plus DHA 1500 mg did not improve the tested cognition outcomes versus placebo. Human UMP exposure work shows circulating uridine and food-intake/metabolic effects, and triacetyluridine work shows form-dependent bioavailability, but neither is a cognition-efficacy result.^[raw/articles/pubmed-28222577-agpc-uridine-dha-acute-null.md]^[raw/articles/pmc-9873946-ump-food-intake-healthy-humans.md]^[raw/articles/pmc-3040752-triacetyluridine-bioavailability.md]

Anecdote signal: `moderate but stack-confounded`. The direct nootropic-community thread has intense positive reports around anxiety, executive function, sleep inertia, and "normal function," but uridine is entangled with [[citicoline]], [[alpha-gpc]], [[dha-epa]], baseline choline status, brand/form differences, and expectancy.^[raw/articles/reddit-2025-uridine-nootropics-must-thread.md]

## Signal Split

| Layer | Signal | Readout |
|---|---|---|
| Preclinical combination | `preclinical/mechanistic` | Gerbil UMP + DHA + choline learning-memory anchor |
| Souvenaid / Fortasyn Connect | `low-moderate but disease-context and contested` | Mild/prodromal AD formula signals counterweighted by mild-to-moderate AD null and Cochrane caution |
| Healthy young acute stack | `negative` | Alpha-GPC + UMP + DHA failed to improve cognition acutely in trained young men |
| UMP / triacetyluridine exposure | `form/PK only` | UMP can move circulating uridine; triacetyluridine differs from UMP and prescription uridine triacetate identity matters |
| Anecdote | `moderate` salience, stack-confounded | Direct thread reports intense executive-function and anxiety claims, but in a choline / omega-3 stack context |
| Safety / attribution | `moderate` caution | Multi-compound stacks obscure attribution; TAU, UMP, Souvenaid/Fortasyn, and prescription uridine triacetate are not interchangeable |

## Practical Interpretation

Route uridine through [[common-nootropic-gray-zone-molecule-watchlist]], [[gray-zone-nootropic-community-signal-ledger]], [[choline-donor-cognition-signal-map]], [[nutrient-supplement-cognition-signal-map]], and [[food-derived-cognition-leads-2026-scan]]. It should no longer be labeled simply preclinical-only, but it should stay below healthy-adult cognition-positive ranking until a direct uridine-alone or well-controlled chronic UMP / TAU stack trial shows objective benefit.

Do not interchange [[uridine]], [[dietary-nucleotides]], [[dha-epa]], [[alpha-gpc]], [[citicoline]], Fortasyn/Souvenaid, and prescription uridine triacetate. DailyMed labels prescription Vistogard as uridine triacetate 10,000 mg oral granules for fluorouracil/capecitabine overdose or early severe toxicity, which is a drug-identity anchor rather than a nootropic rationale.^[raw/articles/dailymed-vistogard-uridine-triacetate-label.md] The useful current role is boundary control: identify whether a claim is a disease-context multinutrient formula, an acute healthy-adult stack, a PK/form claim, or an anecdote.

Route uridine / UMP / triacetyluridine stack reports through [[safety-interaction-matrix-for-multi-modal-stacks]] before treating mood, executive-function, sleep-inertia, or "normal function" claims as cognition. The first gates are form identity, choline donor, DHA/omega-3 status, caffeine/stimulants, sleep timing, disease-formula versus supplement-stack context, and whether dechallenge separates uridine from the rest of the stack.
