# Viloxazine extended-release adult ADHD phase III trial

## Captured takeaways

- Phase III randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled adult ADHD trial of viloxazine extended-release capsules.
- Adults aged 18-65 with ADHD were randomized to flexible-dose viloxazine ER 200-600 mg/day or placebo for 6 weeks.
- The primary endpoint was change from baseline in Adult ADHD Investigator Symptom Rating Scale total score at end of study.
- Viloxazine ER produced greater improvement than placebo on ADHD symptoms, including inattentive and hyperactive/impulsive components, and the paper reports executive-function related secondary measures.
- Common adverse events in the trial included insomnia, fatigue, and nausea; discontinuation due to adverse events was higher with viloxazine than placebo.
- Use in wiki: adult ADHD clinical-state support for viloxazine, not healthy-adult cognition evidence.
