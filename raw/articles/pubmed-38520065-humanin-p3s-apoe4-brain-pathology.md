# Humanin variant P3S is associated with longevity in APOE4 carriers and resists APOE4-induced brain pathology

- PMID: 38520065
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38520065/
- Ingested: 2026-04-29

## Summary

Humanin variant P3S was enriched in APOE4 centenarians, bound APOE4 strongly, and reduced amyloid-beta accumulation in an APP/PS1/APOE4 mouse model. The transcriptomic signal pointed toward increased amyloid-beta phagocytosis as a likely mechanism.

## Notes

- Sharpens the APOE4-resilience framing for humanin.
- No human intervention trial was reported; this remains a variant/resilience paper plus mouse validation.
