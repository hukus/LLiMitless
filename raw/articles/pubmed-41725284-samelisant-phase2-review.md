# Samelisant (SUVN-G3031), a histamine H3 receptor inverse agonist, as a potential treatment for narcolepsy: a phase-2 drug evaluation

- PMID: 41725284
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41725284/
- ClinicalTrials.gov: NCT04072380

## Summary

2026 review of the nonclinical and clinical profile of samelisant, an investigational H3 inverse agonist developed for hypersomnia disorders. The paper emphasizes wakefulness rationale, potential cardiovascular and drug-interaction advantages, and pairing with orexin-based therapies, but it does not provide a direct cognition efficacy signal.
