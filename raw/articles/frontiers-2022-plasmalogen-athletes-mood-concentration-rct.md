# Scallop-derived plasmalogen athlete mood and concentration RCT

2022 Frontiers in Cell and Developmental Biology randomized placebo-controlled trial in 40 male Japanese college athletes aged 18-22. The intervention was scallop-derived purified plasmalogen, 2 mg/day for four weeks, with placebo control.

The primary outcome was Profile of Mood States 2 total mood disturbance. The plasmalogen group improved more than placebo, but the primary between-group difference was marginal, p = 0.07. Secondary outcomes showed significant between-group differences for anger-hostility, fatigue-inertia, and the Uchida-Kraepelin mental-concentration performance marker. The authors also reported near-significant sleep improvement, no between-group differences in physical/lab measures, funding by the Japanese Plasmalogen Society, and no declared commercial conflict.

Use in the wiki: direct but small psychobehavioral/concentration human trial. It supports low-confidence tracking, not broad nootropic ranking.
