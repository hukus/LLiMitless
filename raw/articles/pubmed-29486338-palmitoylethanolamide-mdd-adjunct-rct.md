# Source Digest

Type: randomized double-blind placebo-controlled adjunctive major-depressive-disorder trial.

Publication date: 2018-02-21 online; journal issue 2018-05.

Key extraction:
- Intervention: 600 mg palmitoylethanolamide twice daily or placebo added to citalopram for 6 weeks.
- Population: 58 DSM-5 major depressive disorder patients with baseline HAM-D score at least 19; 54 completed the trial.
- Primary psychiatric result: PEA group had greater HAM-D reduction by week 2 and greater improvement across the 6-week trial than placebo.
- Response rate result: at trial end, response was reported in 100% of PEA patients versus 74% of placebo patients.
- Safety readout: baseline parameters and side-effect frequency were not significantly different between groups.
- Limitation: small sample, short follow-up, and predominantly male population.

Wiki signal:
- Medicinal signal: mood-adjacent low signal; useful for mental-state confound tracking, not direct cognition.
- Confidence limiter: depression scale rather than objective cognition; adjunct to citalopram rather than standalone PEA.
