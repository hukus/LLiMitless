# Exploring the effects of faecal microbiota transplantation on cognitive function: A review of clinical trials

- PMID: 40686934
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40686934/
- Ingested: 2026-04-30

## Summary

2025 review of clinical trials on fecal microbiota transplantation (FMT) and cognition. The review identified 14 studies across neurological patient cohorts, including hepatic encephalopathy, Parkinson's disease, dementia, and mild cognitive impairment, with three randomized placebo-controlled or blinded trials among them. The human literature remains small and heterogeneous, and the review reports no clinical trials yet in cognitively healthy individuals.
