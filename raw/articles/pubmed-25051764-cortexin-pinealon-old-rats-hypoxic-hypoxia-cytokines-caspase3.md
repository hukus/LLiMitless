# [Regulation of content of cytokines in blood serum and of caspase-3 activity in brains of old rats in model of sharp hypoxic hypoxia with Cortexin and Pinealon]

- PMID: 25051764
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25051764/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

This old-rat hypoxic-hypoxia study compared Cortexin and Pinealon on cytokines and brain caspase-3. The abstract suggests Pinealon may normalize neurogenesis-related and neuroinflammatory readouts under acute hypoxic stress. The evidence remains preclinical and mechanistic.
