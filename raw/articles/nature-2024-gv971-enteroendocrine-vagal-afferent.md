# Sodium oligomannate activates the enteroendocrine-vagal afferent pathways in APP/PS1 mice

- Journal: Acta Pharmacologica Sinica
- URL: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41401-024-01293-w

## Summary

This 2024 mouse paper proposes an enteroendocrine-to-vagal afferent route for GV-971. The abstract reports TRPA1- and sweet-receptor-linked effects in enteroendocrine cells, increased serotonin and CCK, increased vagal afferent activity, and improved cognition in APP/PS1 mice after 12 weeks. It strengthens a gut-brain mechanism narrative, but it remains preclinical.

## Key Takeaways

- The mechanism is more specific than the original broad "gut microbiome" story.
- It still does not answer whether human cognitive benefit depends on this pathway.
- It is best treated as mechanism support, not efficacy validation.
