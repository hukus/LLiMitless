# Pentoxifylline in COVID-19 and long-COVID considerations

2024 Inflammation Research review on pentoxifylline in COVID-19 and considerations for long-COVID research. The review describes pentoxifylline as a synthetic methylxanthine with pharmacologic effects including PDE3/PDE4 inhibition, cAMP increase, adenosine A2 receptor antagonism, and effects on erythrocytes, leukocytes, platelets, fibrinogen, inflammatory cytokines, and blood vessels.

The review frames COVID-19 and long-COVID research as mechanistically plausible because disease severity and persistence can involve hematological and inflammatory markers. It is rationale/review evidence, not direct brain-fog efficacy.
