# PubChem 2026 glutathione identity note

PubChem identifies glutathione as CID 124886, molecular formula C10H17N3O6S, molecular weight about 307.33 g/mol, and IUPAC name (2S)-2-amino-5-[[(2R)-1-(carboxymethylamino)-1-oxo-3-sulfanylpropan-2-yl]amino]-5-oxopentanoic acid.
