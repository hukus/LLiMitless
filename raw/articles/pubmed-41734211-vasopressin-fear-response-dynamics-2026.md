# Vasopressin and angiotensin II pathways differentially modulate human fear response dynamics to looming threats

- PMID: 41734211
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41734211/
- Publication date: 2026-02-24

## Summary

Randomized, placebo-controlled eye-tracking study of vasopressin during looming-threat processing. AVP increased time overestimation and produced a distinct pupil-response profile, while losartan reduced state anxiety and produced a different threat-dynamics profile.

## Notes

- Source type: randomized human challenge study.
- Sample size: 111 healthy volunteers.
- ClinicalTrials.gov IDs: NCT06329076 and NCT06329063.
- Interpretation: this is strong evidence for threat-processing modulation, not direct cognition enhancement.
