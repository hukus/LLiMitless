# Plasmalogen in the brain: Effects on cognitive functions and behaviors attributable to its properties

- PMID: 35970332
- Source: PubMed review
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

Review of plasmalogen biology in the brain. The paper summarizes evidence that altered ether phospholipid composition is seen in AD and other neuropsychiatric disorders and that plasmalogen deficiency may affect myelin structure, synaptic neurotransmission, intracellular signaling, apoptosis, and neuroinflammation. It also discusses replacement therapy as a possible future direction.

## Notes

- This is a mechanistic/review source, not a cognition trial.
- Useful for linking membrane biology to cognition hypotheses.
- The review supports plausibility, not transportable efficacy.
