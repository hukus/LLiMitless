# Cordyceps militaris subjective-cognitive-decline sleep cognition trial

Garuda index record for a 2025 Eureka Herba Indonesia article titled "Modulation of the Cognition-Sleep Nexus in Subjective Cognitive Decline: A 12-Week, Randomized, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled Trial of a Standardized Cordyceps militaris Extract."

The abstract says the single-center Palembang trial randomized 120 adults aged 45-65 with subjective cognitive decline to Cordyceps militaris mycelial extract 300 mg/day standardized to 3% cordycepin or placebo for 12 weeks. The primary outcome was change in MoCA-INA. Secondary outcomes included PSQI, RAVLT delayed recall, and polysomnography-derived sleep efficiency.

The abstract reports a significant MoCA-INA group-by-time interaction with adjusted mean difference +1.95, 95% CI 1.10 to 2.80, p < 0.001. It also reports improvements in PSQI, RAVLT delayed recall, sleep efficiency, processing speed, percent REM sleep, serum BDNF, and hs-CRP, with good tolerability.

Interpretation for the wiki: this is a direct cognition-sleep signal for Cordyceps militaris, but confidence should stay low until publication quality, indexing, registry status, methods, and independent replication are audited. Treat it as a watch item, not a settled result.
