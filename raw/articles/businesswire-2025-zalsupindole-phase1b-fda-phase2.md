# BusinessWire 2025: Zalsupindole phase 1b and phase 2 design clearance

Delix Therapeutics announced on October 28, 2025 that zalsupindole / DLX-001 is a first-in-class non-hallucinogenic neuroplastogen and that a phase 1b trial in adults with major depressive disorder had produced positive company-reported data. The release also says FDA cleared an IND-backed phase 2 trial design with at-home self-administration.

The phase 1b study enrolled 18 adults with MDD and compared a seven-day daily dosing cohort with a two-dose intermittent cohort during the first week. The company reported qEEG and polysomnography biomarker effects, rapid MADRS reductions, apparent durability through day 36, similar outcomes across regimens, no serious adverse events, all adverse events mild and resolved without intervention, and no hallucinatory, dissociative, or psychotomimetic effects.

Interpretation for the wiki: this is sponsor/company-reported clinical development evidence for mood and biomarkers, not a peer-reviewed cognition result. It upgrades zalsupindole from preclinical-only to clinical-stage neuroplastogen, but the cognition/brain-fog claim remains unproven.
