# SHLP6: a novel NLRP3 and Cav1 modulating agent in Cu-induced oxidative stress and neurodegeneration

- PMID: 40292418
- PMCID: PMC12023277
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40292418/
- Ingested: 2026-04-30

## Summary

2025 zebrafish-larvae paper on SHLP6, a small humanin-like peptide. It reports reduced CuSO4 toxicity, less ROS, improved survival and heart rate, lower lipid peroxidation, improved AChE and locomotion, and changes in NLRP3/Cav1-related inflammatory signaling. The result is cognition-adjacent preclinical evidence only, not a human cognition trial.

## Notes

- Source type: peer-reviewed preclinical animal study.
- Date checked: 2026-04-30.
- Signal size: one zebrafish toxicity model.
- Signal strength: weak.
- Direction: positive for neuroprotection and locomotion under copper stress.
- Caveat: no human exposure, no dose-optimized cognition endpoint, and no direct healthy-adult enhancement evidence.
