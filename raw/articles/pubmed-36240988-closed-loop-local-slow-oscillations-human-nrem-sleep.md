# Closed-loop modulation of local slow oscillations in human NREM sleep

NeuroImage 2022 paper on the TOPOSO algorithm. The authors used topographic EEG analyses to target local UP- and DOWN-states of slow oscillations during napping. Compared with a conventional single-channel approach, TOPOSO targeted local states more specifically and reliably, and auditory stimulation temporarily enhanced the targeted local state.

The main limitation is that the stimulation then elicited a standard frontal slow oscillation rather than a purely local one. This makes the paper a useful mechanistic baseline for closed-loop sleep stimulation, but not a direct cognitive-performance study.
