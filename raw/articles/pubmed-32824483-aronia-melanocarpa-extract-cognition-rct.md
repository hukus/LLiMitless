# The Effect of Long-Term Aronia melanocarpa Extract Supplementation on Cognitive Performance, Mood, and Vascular Function: A Randomized Controlled Trial in Healthy, Middle-Aged Individuals

- PMID: 32824483
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32824483/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

24-week randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled parallel trial in 101 healthy, middle-aged overweight adults. Participants received 90 mg/day AME, 150 mg/day AME, or placebo. The 90 mg/day arm improved psychomotor speed versus placebo. Attention, cognitive flexibility, BDNF, and most vascular measures did not change. This is a narrow domain signal, not a broad cognition effect.
