# Sleep and the recovery from stress

- PMID: 40409251
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40409251/
- Published: 2025-09-17

## Summary

2025 Neuron review on the bidirectional relationship between stress and sleep. The key framing for the wiki is that post-stress sleep may facilitate recovery, reduce anxiety, and improve resilience, but the field still does not know when sleep versus wakefulness is the better post-stress adaptation strategy.

## Notes

Useful as a conceptual anchor for the "protect" side of plasticity budgeting: stress can fragment sleep, but sleep after stress may support recovery rather than simply represent downtime.
