# Efficacy of a medical food in mild Alzheimer's disease

- PMID: 20129316
- Journal: Alzheimer's & Dementia (2010)

## Captured takeaways

- Randomized, double-blind controlled trial in 225 drug-naive patients with mild Alzheimer's disease.
- Participants were assigned to Souvenaid or an iso-caloric control drink once daily for 12 weeks.
- Souvenaid / Fortasyn Connect contains multiple nutrients and precursors, including uridine monophosphate, choline, phospholipids, omega-3 fatty acids, B vitamins, antioxidant vitamins, and selenium.
- Primary outcomes were delayed verbal recall from the Wechsler Memory Scale-revised and modified ADAS-Cog at week 12.
- The PubMed abstract reports improved memory on delayed verbal recall in mild AD patients.
- Interpretation for the wiki: this is human combination-product disease-context evidence for a uridine-containing formula, not uridine-alone or healthy-adult cognition proof.
