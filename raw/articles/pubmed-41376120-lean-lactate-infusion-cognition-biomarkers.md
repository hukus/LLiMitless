# Acute effects of lactate infusion on metabolism, AD biomarkers, and cognition: The LEAN study

- PMID: 41376120
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41376120/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Single-arm lactate-clamp study in cognitively healthy older adults and cognitively impaired participants. The published abstract reports that lactate clearance was similar across groups, global cognition improved during infusion, and multiple AD fluid biomarkers fell acutely. The result is encouraging but still provisional because it is not a randomized placebo-controlled cognition trial.
