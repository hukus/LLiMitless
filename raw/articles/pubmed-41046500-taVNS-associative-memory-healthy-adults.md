# Effects of transcutaneous auricular vagus nerve stimulation on associative memory, event-related potential P300 and P600: a single-blind pilot experiment on healthy adults

- PMID: 41046500
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41046500/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Single-blind crossover pilot in 14 healthy adults. taVNS produced an interaction effect on associative memory performance across timepoints, but did not change P300 or P600 amplitudes. The sample is small, but this is a direct healthy-adult cognition signal that is more specific than the older broad review claims.

## Notes

- Active-versus-sham design with taVNS during the 10 minute rest between repeated associative-memory task blocks.
- Washout was one week.
- The result supports a cautious "possible memory signal" rather than a settled electrophysiology mechanism.
