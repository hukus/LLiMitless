# The effect of milk fat globule membrane phospholipid supplementation on cognitive function in older adults with subjective memory complaints

- PMID: 40939691
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40939691/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

Randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial in 263 adults aged 55-75 with subjective memory complaints. Two MFGM phospholipid doses were compared with placebo for 16 weeks. Primary cognitive outcomes were null on RBANS and COMPASS, but the high-dose group showed better-maintained alertness during testing and favorable biomarker changes.
