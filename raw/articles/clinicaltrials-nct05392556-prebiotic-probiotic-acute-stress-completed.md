# Prebiotic and Probiotic Modulation of the Gut Microbiota-gut-brain Axis During Acute Stress

- ClinicalTrials.gov ID: NCT05392556
- Source: https://www.clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05392556
- Ingested: 2026-04-30

## Summary

Completed randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled parallel-arm trial in healthy young adults testing probiotic vs prebiotic vs placebo under acute military-relevant physical and cognitive stress. The registry currently shows no results posted.

## Notes

The record lists primary completion and study completion on 2025-10-28, enrollment of 73, and a stress battery that includes reaction time, go/no-go, n-back, emotional interference, ambiguity decision-making, HRV, metabolomics, and cortisol. This is a protocol/freshness signal, not a cognition result.
