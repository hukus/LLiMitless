# PubMed 27869715 ASI cognition healthy-adult note

2016 Nutrients paper titled "Randomized Prospective Double-Blind Studies to Evaluate the Cognitive Effects of Inositol-Stabilized Arginine Silicate in Healthy Physically Active Adults."

The paper reports two double-blind placebo-controlled crossover studies of inositol-stabilized arginine silicate (ASI; Nitrosigine) at 1500 mg/day in healthy males, using Trail Making Test A/B as the main cognition task. Study 1 tested 14 days of dosing; Study 2 tested shorter 3-day exposure and a 10-minute post-dose period.

Reported results: after 14 days, TMT-B time decreased significantly from baseline, reported as 28% improvement. In the shorter study, TMT-B time decreased significantly versus placebo in a 10-minute period, reported as 33% improvement, and decreased from baseline after 3 days, reported as 35% improvement.

Interpretation for the wiki: this is a direct healthy-adult cognition signal, but the exposure is ASI, not plain myo-inositol. It is product-specific, small, male/physically-active, Trail-Making-focused, and sponsor/product-linked. Route it beside [[myo-inositol]], [[dietary-nitrate-beetroot]], [[arginine]], nitric-oxide hypotheses, and [[cognitive-improvement-molecule-signal-atlas]], without transferring the signal to ordinary inositol powder.
