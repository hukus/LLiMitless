# DailyMed Vistogard uridine triacetate label

## Captured takeaways

- Vistogard is a prescription uridine triacetate oral granule product.
- DailyMed identifies the product as uridine triacetate 10,000 mg oral granules.
- The label describes uridine triacetate as an acetylated pro-drug of uridine.
- The product is labeled for medical use around fluorouracil/capecitabine overdose or severe early-onset toxicity, not nootropic use.
- Interpretation for the wiki: triacetyluridine / uridine triacetate has prescription-drug identity in at least one context; supplement anecdotes should distinguish TAU, UMP, Souvenaid/Fortasyn, and prescription uridine triacetate.
