# Rhodiola Rosea for Physical and Mental Fatigue

- PMID: 22643043
- PMCID: PMC3541197
- PubMed Central: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3541197/
- DOI: 10.1186/1472-6882-12-70
- Ingested: 2026-05-03

## Summary

2012 systematic review of Rhodiola rosea for physical and mental fatigue. Eleven studies met inclusion criteria, ten described as RCTs and one as a controlled clinical trial. Two of six trials in healthy physical-fatigue settings and three of five RCTs in mental-fatigue settings reported benefit, but all included studies had high risk of bias or reporting flaws that made validity hard to assess.

## Interpretation

Rhodiola has a plausible fatigue / stress-resilience signal, but the formal cognition signal remains low and bias-sensitive. Route it as mental-fatigue support rather than a clean memory or executive-function enhancer.
