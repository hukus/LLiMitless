# The effects of cannabidiol on subjective states, cognition, and psychomotor function in healthy adults

PubMed-indexed randomized, parallel-group, double-blind feasibility trial in healthy college students. Participants received 300 mg CBD oil or placebo and repeated sleepiness, mood, digit-symbol substitution, trail-making, psychomotor vigilance, and reaction-time tests after 120 minutes.

The trial is useful because it tests acute CBD in healthy adults with cognition and psychomotor endpoints instead of relying on anxiety or sleep claims. The source should be interpreted as cognitive-safety / acute-effects evidence, not as a strong nootropic efficacy result.

Relevance: CBD belongs in the gray-zone molecule ledger because community users often describe calm/sleep effects as cognitive support, while formal cognitive benefits remain weak.
