# Effects of 4-aminopyridine on attention and executive functions of patients with multiple sclerosis: Randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled clinical trial. Preliminary report

- PMID: 30593980
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30593980/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Small RRMS trial with 21 completers. Over 20 weeks, 4-aminopyridine improved several cognitive measures versus placebo, including attention span, verbal fluency, planning, and constructive/graphomotor tasks. Adverse events were mild to moderate and did not stop treatment. This is disease-context evidence, not healthy-adult enhancement evidence.

## Notes

- Randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled design.
- 11 participants received 4-aminopyridine and 10 received placebo.
- The paper supports a symptom-restoration framing in MS.
