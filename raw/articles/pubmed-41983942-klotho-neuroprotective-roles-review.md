# Neuroprotective roles of klotho: Molecular pathways and therapeutic implications for cognitive health in neurological and psychiatric diseases

- PMID: 41983942
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41983942/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

2026 review linking Klotho to oxidative stress control, neuroinflammation, synaptic plasticity, and myelination. The authors summarize clinical associations with slower cognitive decline in Alzheimer's and Parkinson's disease, better outcomes in multiple sclerosis, and better stroke recovery, with the strongest apparent protection in APOE e4 carriers. The review also highlights biomarker assay variability, blood-brain-barrier delivery problems, and the need for precision-based interventions rather than generic Klotho supplementation claims.
