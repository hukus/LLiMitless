# KLOTHO-VS heterozygosity, α-klotho protein levels and cognitive performance in Alzheimer's disease

- PMID: 41163083
- PMCID: PMC12574126
- DOI: 10.1186/s13195-025-01878-5
- Published: 2025-10-29
- Ingested: 2026-04-29

## Summary

Cross-sectional study from the Czech Brain Aging Study using 296 participants, with a KL-VS haplotype subsample of 196 and a CSF/serum α-Klotho protein subsample of 147. KL-VS heterozygotes trended toward lower odds of aMCI/dementia due to AD, but the associations were not statistically significant after adjustment. The clearest signal was in aMCI due to AD, where heterozygotes showed better memory performance overall (β = 0.61, p = .008) and a stronger APOE ε4-stratified effect (β = 0.64, p = .042). CSF and serum soluble α-Klotho levels did not differ by diagnostic group and did not associate with memory performance. This supports a genotype-stage interaction hypothesis, not a circulating-protein biomarker or intervention claim.
