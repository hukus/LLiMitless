# FDA approval of Wellcovorin for FOLR1-related cerebral folate transport deficiency

- Source type: FDA news release / regulatory status
- Published: 2026-03-10
- Checked: 2026-05-16
- Product / ingredient: Wellcovorin, leucovorin calcium tablets

## Summary

FDA approved expanded use of Wellcovorin for adult and pediatric patients with cerebral folate deficiency due to a confirmed `FOLR1` gene variant, also described as CFD-FOLR1 or cerebral folate transport deficiency. FDA described leucovorin as the first approved treatment for this rare genetic condition.

The approval was based on a systematic review of published literature, including patient-level case reports and mechanistic data, not a large new randomized cognition trial. FDA described the condition as impaired folate transport into the brain with severe developmental delay, movement disorders, seizures, and other neurological complications.

## Cognition / Brain-Fog Relevance

This is a narrow medical-status signal, not evidence for healthy-adult cognitive enhancement. It makes leucovorin / folinic acid important in the wiki because it can treat a genetically confirmed CNS folate-transport disorder, and because public discussion may generalize the approval into broader autism, MTHFR, or brain-fog claims.

FDA noted that the approval may benefit some people with FOLR1-related cerebral folate transport deficiency who have developmental delays with autistic features. That language should not be widened into a general autism or nootropic claim.

## Safety / Adverse Effects

FDA listed possible side effects including pruritus, rash, urticaria, dyspnea, rigors, impaired thermoregulation, and serious anaphylaxis requiring immediate medical attention.
