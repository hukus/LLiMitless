# Methylliberine ingestion improves affect but not cognitive function

2023 randomized, double-blind, within-subject crossover trial of methylliberine / Dynamine in 25 healthy men and women. Participants took 100 mg methylliberine or placebo for three days, then were tested before and after the fourth dose.

The trial found improvements in subjective concentration, motivation, mood, well-being, stress tolerance, and sustained energy in selected time windows, with no adverse events reported and negligible effects on vitals. It did not improve objective cognitive-function outcomes on the test battery.

Use in the wiki: direct formal counterweight to Dynamine focus/euphoria anecdotes. Methylliberine is best classified as a subjective affect / energy / concentration modifier rather than a proven objective cognitive enhancer.
