# Effects of Anserine/Carnosine Supplementation on Mild Cognitive Impairment with APOE4

- PMID: 31319510
- PMCID: PMC6683059
- Published: 2019
- Ingested: 2026-05-11

## Summary

Randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled 12-week trial in 54 subjects with mild cognitive impairment. The active arm received 750 mg anserine plus 250 mg carnosine daily, while the control arm received placebo.

The active group improved more than placebo on global Clinical Dementia Rating, p = 0.023. The broader test battery did not show active-arm benefit on MMSE, Wechsler Memory Scale, or ADAS-Cog. In APOE4-positive subgroup analysis, MMSE and global CDR improved more in the active arm.

## Relevance

This is a disease-risk / MCI-context source for [[anserine-carnosine]]. It strengthens the rationale for APOE4 or early-impairment stratification, but its main result is not a broad psychometric battery win.

## Caveats

- MCI cohort, not healthy adults.
- APOE4 subgroup should be treated as hypothesis-generating.
- Most cognitive tests did not show active-arm advantage.
- Duration was short, and replication is needed.
