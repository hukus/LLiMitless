# Daily Mucuna pruriens intake pilot in Parkinson's disease

2018 clinical pilot testing daily Mucuna pruriens use in Parkinson's disease. The study belongs in the wiki as a tolerability and clinical-state source, not as evidence that casual supplement use improves cognition in healthy adults.

The practical signal is mixed: Mucuna can deliver dopaminergic activity, but chronic use raises dose, tolerability, standardization, and clinical-supervision questions that are much larger than ordinary botanical-nootropic concerns.

Use in the wiki: clinical-state tolerability and dosing-context support.
