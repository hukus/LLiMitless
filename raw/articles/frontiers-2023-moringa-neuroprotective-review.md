# An insight into the neuroprotective and anti-neuroinflammatory effects and mechanisms of Moringa oleifera

- Published: 2023-01-05
- Journal: Frontiers in Pharmacology
- DOI: 10.3389/fphar.2022.1035220
- Evidence layer: review / preclinical neuroprotection mechanism

## Summary

This Frontiers review summarizes Moringa oleifera neuroprotective and anti-neuroinflammatory mechanisms, including antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, anti-amyloid, and neuronal-survival pathways in cellular and animal models. It is useful for mapping why Moringa enters cognition-adjacent discourse.

The review does not establish a human cognition effect. The right wiki read is preclinical/mechanistic plausibility plus broader metabolic/nutritional human evidence, not a direct healthy-adult cognitive enhancer.
