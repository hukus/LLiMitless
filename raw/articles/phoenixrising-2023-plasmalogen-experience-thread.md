# Phoenix Rising plasmalogen product experience thread

Phoenix Rising ME/CFS forum thread from November 2023 about plasmalogen supplements. The original poster reported trying ProdromeGlia for two months, with weaker brain fog, perceived greater mental acuity, and improved restless-legs/periodic-limb-movement symptoms. The same poster reported switching to NeuroREGAIN and returning to worse brain fog/restless legs, then returning to ProdromeGlia and seeing the prior pattern again.

Use in the wiki: direct N=1 product-specific anecdote. It is useful for product-identity and symptom-state hypotheses, but it is uncontrolled, illness-contextual, and cannot establish objective cognition.
