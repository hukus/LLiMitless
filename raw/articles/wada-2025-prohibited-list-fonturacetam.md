## Summary

WADA's 2025 Prohibited List places `Fonturacetam [4-phenylpiracetam (carphedon)]` in S6.A non-specified stimulants. The entry is officially in force from 1 January 2025, which makes phenylpiracetam sports-visible even when the cognition evidence is still phenotype-bound and mostly rescue-oriented.
