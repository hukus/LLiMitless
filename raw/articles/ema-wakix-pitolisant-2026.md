# EMA Wakix / pitolisant

- Wakix is a prescription medicine for narcolepsy in adults, adolescents, and children from 6 years of age.
- The active substance is pitolisant and the EU marketing authorization dates to 2016-03-31.
- The EMA page was updated in 2026 and still frames Wakix as a sleep-wake disorder treatment, not a cognition drug.
