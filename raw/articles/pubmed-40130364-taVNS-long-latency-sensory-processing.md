# Continuous Transcutaneous Auricular Vagus Nerve Stimulation Increases Long-Latency Neural Processing in Multiple Sensory Modalities

- PMID: 40130364
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40130364/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Sham-controlled crossover study in 45 healthy adults testing continuous taVNS at the cymba concha versus sham at the earlobe across auditory, respiratory, and somatosensory modalities. P2 amplitudes increased in auditory and somatosensory blocks, but P50/N1, neural gating, salivary alpha-amylase, and resting alpha power did not change.

## Notes

- This is a mechanistic arousal/processing signal, not a direct cognition outcome.
- Self-reported arousal increased during taVNS, which fits a state-regulation interpretation.
- The study argues for mechanistic plausibility while also showing that common LC/NA proxies remain mixed.
