# Machine learning based prediction of cognitive metrics using major biomarkers in SuperAgers

- PMID: 40436987
- Published: 2025-05-28
- Study type: observational biomarker / machine-learning analysis
- Key finding: 55 blood biomarkers in 39 SuperAgers and 42 typical agers were reduced to 15 key biomarkers by feature selection. A predictive model built from those biomarkers reached 76% accuracy for cognitive-domain prediction.
- Why it matters: this is a concrete example that a multi-feature biomarker panel can outperform one-biomarker thinking when the goal is to estimate cognitive phenotype.
- Caveat: small sample, cross-sectional design, and LLM-based augmentation make it a proof-of-concept rather than a validated clinical scorecard.
