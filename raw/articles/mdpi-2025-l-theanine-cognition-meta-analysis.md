# Promising, but Not Completely Conclusive - The Effect of l-Theanine on Cognitive Performance

- Journal: Journal of Clinical Medicine
- Published: 2025-10-30
- DOI: 10.3390/jcm14217710
- Source: https://www.mdpi.com/2077-0383/14/21/7710
- Ingested: 2026-05-03

## Summary

2025 systematic review and meta-analysis of randomized placebo-controlled l-theanine trials in healthy adults. The authors searched PubMed, CENTRAL, Embase, and Web of Science and registered the review in PROSPERO. Five RCTs with 148 healthy adults were included in the quantitative analysis. L-theanine showed a dose-dependent benefit on rapid visual information processing / recognition visual reaction time, but effects were non-significant for simple reaction time and Stroop outcomes. Doses in included studies ranged roughly from 50 mg to 500 mg.

## Interpretation

This is a low-to-moderate formal signal for selected attention-processing endpoints, not a confirmed broad cognitive enhancer. The review's own conclusion emphasizes inconsistent test-method support, low trial count, heterogeneous preparations, and the need for larger standardized-product trials.
