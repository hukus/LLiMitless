# Phenethylamine is a substrate of MAO-B in the paraventricular thalamic nucleus

Obata and colleagues used global and brain-specific Maob knockout mice to identify candidate MAO-B substrates in the paraventricular thalamic nucleus. Among noradrenaline, dopamine, 3-methoxytyramine, serotonin, and phenethylamine, only phenethylamine was markedly elevated in the PVT of Maob knockout mice.

The authors also found elevated plasma phenethylamine in global knockout mice but not brain-specific knockout mice, and they note that phenethylamine crosses the blood-brain barrier. Their interpretation is that phenethylamine is a substrate of MAO-B in the PVT as well as other tissues.
