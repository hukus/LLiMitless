# Effects of natural extracts in cognitive function of healthy adults: a systematic review and network meta-analysis

- PMID: 40213691
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40213691/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

2025 network meta-analysis of 27 randomized trials in 2,334 healthy adults. The clearest current signal is not a universal nootropic effect but a small set of extract-specific wins: Polygala tenuifolia for overall cognition, and Cistanche tubulosa plus Ginkgo biloba for memory, executive function, and cognitive flexibility. Attention did not show a consistent placebo-separated winner.
