# PubChem: Emoxypine / Mexidol identity

PubChem lists Mexidol as CID 122298, molecular formula C12H17NO5, molecular weight 255.27, and IUPAC name `butanedioic acid;2-ethyl-6-methylpyridin-3-ol`. The identity is the succinate salt/complex commonly called Mexidol or emoxypine succinate.

PubChem separately lists emoxypine / 2-ethyl-3-hydroxy-6-methylpyridine as CID 114681, molecular formula C8H11NO, molecular weight 137.18, and IUPAC name `2-ethyl-6-methylpyridin-3-ol`.

Interpretation for the wiki: keep base emoxypine, Mexidol / emoxypine succinate, and ordinary vitamin B6 / pyridoxine claims separate. Structural similarity to pyridoxine is not the same as vitamin replacement.
