# CoQ10 post-COVID condition crossover randomized trial

2023 Lancet Regional Health - Europe randomized double-blind 2 x 2 crossover trial of high-dose coenzyme Q10 in post-COVID condition.

The trial randomized 121 participants in Denmark; 119 completed both dosing periods. Participants received CoQ10 500 mg/day or placebo for six weeks, crossed over after a four-week washout, and completed EQ-5D and a post-COVID symptom questionnaire. The primary endpoint was change in the number and/or severity of post-COVID symptoms after CoQ10 versus placebo.

CoQ10 did not significantly improve EQ-5D health index or post-COVID symptom score versus placebo. Both scores improved spontaneously during the 20-week observation period regardless of treatment.

Interpretation for the wiki: CoQ10 has a direct post-COVID RCT, but the result is negative for symptom burden. It should stay a mitochondrial-cofactor hypothesis and low-confidence supplement candidate rather than a supported brain-fog treatment.
