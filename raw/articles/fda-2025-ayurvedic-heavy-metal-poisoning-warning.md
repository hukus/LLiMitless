# FDA 2025: Heavy metal poisoning warning for certain Ayurvedic drug products

FDA updated this warning on 2025-12-02. It warned consumers not to use certain unapproved Ayurvedic drug products because FDA testing found elevated levels of lead and mercury.

Scope and interpretation: this is not a Shilajit-specific warning, but it is directly relevant to Ayurvedic and mineral-containing products marketed online. It supports the wiki rule that product testing and regulatory status must be checked before interpreting subjective effects from Shilajit-like preparations.

Relevance: official heavy-metal risk context for [[shilajit]] and other Ayurvedic/mineral product claims.
