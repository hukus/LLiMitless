# Blarcamesine for the treatment of Early Alzheimer's Disease: Results from the ANAVEX2-73-AD-004 Phase IIB/III trial

- PMID: 39800452
- Journal: Journal of Prevention of Alzheimer's Disease, 2025
- Evidence tier: human phase 2b/3 / sponsor-linked publication

## Summary

This paper reports the ANAVEX2-73-AD-004 phase IIb/III trial in early AD.

Key design details:

- Randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled, 48-week trial.
- 508 participants randomized, 462 in the intent-to-treat population.
- Oral blarcamesine 30 mg or 50 mg daily vs placebo.
- Co-primary outcomes: ADAS-Cog13 and ADCS-ADL.
- Secondary outcome: CDR-SB.

Main findings reported by the paper:

- ADAS-Cog13 improved vs placebo.
- CDR-SB improved vs placebo.
- ADCS-ADL did not reach significance.
- Plasma Aβ42/40 ratio increased and whole-brain volume loss decreased.
- Common TEAEs included transient dizziness.
- Serious TEAEs were numerically higher in the blarcamesine arm than placebo.

Interpretation for the wiki:

- This is a sponsor-heavy positive paper with mixed endpoint support.
