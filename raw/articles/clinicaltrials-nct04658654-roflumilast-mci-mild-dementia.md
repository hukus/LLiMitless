# Cognitive Effects of Roflumilast in (a)MCI and Mild Dementia Patients (ROMEMA)

- ClinicalTrials.gov ID: NCT04658654
- ClinicalTrials.gov: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04658654
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Phase 2 randomized placebo-controlled roflumilast study in amnestic MCI and mild dementia. The registry is important because it targets the phenotype most likely to benefit if PDE4 inhibition truly supports episodic memory, but it was still listed without posted results in the record reviewed here.

## Key points

- Population: adults 50-90 years old with MCI or mild dementia.
- Dose: roflumilast in a 24-week proof-of-concept design.
- Outcomes: episodic memory, attention, processing speed, executive function, mood, and participation.
- Status: registry showed recruiting/unknown status language and no public results in the record used here.
