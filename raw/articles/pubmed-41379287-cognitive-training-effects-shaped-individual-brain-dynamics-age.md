# Cognitive training effects are shaped more by individual brain dynamics than age-evidence from younger and older women

- PMID: 41379287
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41379287/
- Published: 2025-12-11

## Summary

2025 Geroscience study of 39 younger and 40 older women on individualized 1-hour cognitive training sessions with EEG connectivity. Age explained only a modest share of variance; individual neural profiles and self-similarity were more important, and training effects were small but person-specific.

## Notes

Useful for the responder-phenotype side of plasticity budgeting: the same training dose does not appear to spend the same way in all brains.
