# Cerebrolysin in Patients with TBI: Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis

- PMID: 36979317
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36979317/
- Ingested: 2026-04-29

## Summary

Meta-analysis of 10 clinical studies with 8,749 patients. Cerebrolysin was associated with statistically significant improvement in GCS and GOS, but not mortality or length of stay. This supports a recovery-phase interpretation for TBI rather than a generic healthy-adult cognition claim.
