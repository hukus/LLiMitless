# Continuous Glucose Monitoring: A Review of Successes, Challenges, and Opportunities

- PMID: 26784127
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26784127/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

2016 review of CGM as a tool for continuous glucose and glucose-variability measurement. The important methodological point is that CGM captures instantaneous level and rate-of-change data that fingerstick sampling misses, which makes it useful when the intervention may interact with metabolic state.

## Notes

CGM is optional for a practical stack, but it becomes valuable when testing metabolic interventions or when glucose stability is a hypothesized mediator of cognitive change.
