# DailyMed 2026 SPRAVATO esketamine label

- DailyMed label PDF checked 2026-05-15; label revision shown as 2026-03.
- SPRAVATO is esketamine nasal spray, Schedule III, indicated for adult treatment-resistant depression as monotherapy or with an oral antidepressant, and for depressive symptoms in adults with MDD with acute suicidal ideation or behavior when used with an oral antidepressant.
- The boxed warning centers sedation, dissociation, respiratory depression, abuse/misuse, and suicidal thoughts/behaviors.
- Because of sedation, dissociation, respiratory depression, abuse, and misuse risks, administration is restricted through the SPRAVATO REMS; patients are monitored in certified healthcare settings for at least 2 hours after each session.
- The label warns that SPRAVATO may impair attention, judgment, thinking, reaction speed, and motor skills.
- The driving/operating-machinery instruction is to avoid hazardous activities until the next day after restful sleep, and patients need transportation home after treatment.
- Other practical gates include blood-pressure increases, respiratory-status monitoring, abuse/diversion risk, lower urinary tract symptoms / cystitis monitoring, embryo-fetal toxicity, CNS-depressant interactions, psychostimulant/MAOI interaction caution, and post-session observation requirements.
- Interpretation for the wiki: esketamine has an evidence-backed psychiatric-treatment role, but the label itself makes acute cognitive impairment and session logistics central. Brain-fog or cognition claims should distinguish same-day impairment from longer-term depression-state recovery.
