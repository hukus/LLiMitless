# Quinolinic acid is associated with cognitive deficits in schizophrenia but not major depressive disorder

- PMCID: PMC8113521
- PMID: 33976271
- DOI: 10.1038/s41598-021-89335-9
- Journal: Scientific Reports (2021)
- Study type: cross-sectional clinical biomarker study

## Captured takeaways

- Sample: 19 healthy controls, 45 patients with schizophrenia, and 43 patients with major depressive disorder.
- Measured plasma tryptophan, kynurenine, kynurenic acid, 3-hydroxykynurenine, and quinolinic acid.
- Cognition was assessed with the Brief Neurocognitive Assessment, a composite using Letter-Number Span and Symbol Coding.
- After covariate adjustment, plasma quinolinic acid correlated negatively with composite cognition in schizophrenia, but not in major depressive disorder.
- The paper interpreted quinolinic acid as specifically relevant to cognitive deficits in schizophrenia, while emphasizing that causality remains unresolved.
- Interpretation for the wiki: useful human disease-biomarker support for [[quinolinic-acid]], but not an intervention or nootropic-efficacy signal.
