# Notes
- Source: randomized, placebo-controlled intranasal arginine vasopressin study in 48 healthy men.
- Dose: 20 IU intranasal AVP.
- Outcome: AVP increased remembering of happy and angry faces as "known" relative to neutral faces.
- Interpretation: this is a social-memory effect, not broad cognition, and it also linked positive and negative social cues to stronger encoding.
- Safety: abstract does not emphasize major acute safety issues.
