# Neuroprotective and neurorestorative effects of thymosin β4 treatment initiated 6 hours after traumatic brain injury in rats

- PMID: 22324420
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22324420/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Delayed thymosin β4 treatment after experimental traumatic brain injury in rats reduced hippocampal cell loss, increased angiogenesis, neurogenesis, and oligodendrogenesis, and improved sensorimotor function and spatial learning.

## Notes

This is directly relevant to recovery-adjacent cognition, but it remains a rat study with no human proof of cognitive benefit.
