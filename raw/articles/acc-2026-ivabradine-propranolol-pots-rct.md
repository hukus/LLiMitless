# ACC 2026 ivabradine versus propranolol POTS randomized crossover trial summary

## Captured takeaways

- ACC summarized Uppal, Raj, et al.'s 2026 randomized double-blind crossover trial comparing ivabradine, propranolol, and placebo for POTS.
- The trial enrolled 28 female POTS patients with mean age around 33 years.
- Participants received 4-week phases of ivabradine 5 mg twice daily, propranolol 10 mg four times daily, and placebo, with 7-day washouts between phases.
- During 10-minute head-up tilt testing, both ivabradine and propranolol reduced orthostatic tachycardia below the POTS diagnostic threshold compared with placebo.
- ACC's summary states that both active drugs reduced POTS symptoms versus placebo.
- Interpretation for the wiki: this strengthens the heart-rate-control branch and provides a direct propranolol comparator, but it still does not establish direct cognitive enhancement. Brain-fog relevance remains phenotype-gated to orthostatic intolerance, tachycardia, and functional symptoms.
