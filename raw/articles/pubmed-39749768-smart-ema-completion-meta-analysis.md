# Completion Rates of Smart Technology Ecological Momentary Assessment (EMA) in Populations With a Higher Likelihood of Cognitive Impairment: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis

- PMID: 39749768
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39749768/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

2024 systematic review and meta-analysis of smartphone EMA completion rates in cohorts with a higher likelihood of cognitive impairment. Overall completion was about three quarters, but protocol characteristics influenced completion and participants with cognitive impairment completed less often.

## Notes

This supports using sparse, low-burden EMA prompts rather than dense symptom sampling if adherence is a priority.
