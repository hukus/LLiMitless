# The Effect of Peptide Semax, an ACTH(4-10) Analogue, on Intracellular Calcium Dynamics in Rat Brain Neurons

- PMID: 41171324
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41171324/
- DOI: 10.1007/s10517-025-06501-z
- Published: 2025 Aug; epublished 2025 Oct 31

## Summary

Preclinical slice study in rat hippocampal CA1 pyramidal neurons and cerebellar granule cells. Semax at 1 μM increased the frequency of spontaneous intracellular Ca2+ fluctuations in hippocampal pyramidal neurons, but did not significantly alter proton-stimulated Ca2+ increases in cerebellar granule cells. The paper supports a localized network mechanism for Semax and does not provide human cognition evidence.
