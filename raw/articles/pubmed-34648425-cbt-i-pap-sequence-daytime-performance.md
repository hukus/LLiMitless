# A randomized controlled trial of cognitive behavioral therapy for insomnia and PAP for obstructive sleep apnea and comorbid insomnia: effects on nocturnal sleep and daytime performance

- PMID: 34648425
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34648425/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Partial factorial trial in comorbid insomnia and obstructive sleep apnea with treatment pathways that included CBT-I followed by PAP, CBT-I concurrent with PAP, or PAP alone. Outcomes included nocturnal sleep, daytime functioning, and cognitive-emotional measures.

## Notes

Not a neuromodulation study, but it is a direct sequencing study showing that sleep-first pathway design is already being tested in adjacent cognition-relevant care.
