# Pyritinol treatment of SDAT patients

PubMed-indexed double-blind crossover trial in 26 patients with mild-to-moderate senile dementia of Alzheimer type. The trial used psychiatric/neurological exam, psychometric testing, and regional cerebral blood-flow measurements.

The abstract reports improved cognitive performance and a more normalized regional-cerebral-blood-flow activation pattern during pyritinol treatment. The source is relevant as old clinical-state evidence, not healthy-adult proof.

Relevance: supports a legacy clinical-memory branch for pyritinol while keeping modern confidence limited by age, size, and replication context.
