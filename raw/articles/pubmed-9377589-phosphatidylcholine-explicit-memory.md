# Effect of phosphatidylcholine on explicit memory

- PMID: 9377589
- DOI: 10.1097/00002826-199312000-00007
- Journal: Clinical Neuropharmacology
- Year: 1993
- Ingested: 2026-05-04

## Summary

Double-blind mixed-design study in 80 college students testing phosphatidylcholine against placebo. The experiment compared 10 g and 25 g dosing and assessed explicit memory through serial learning at 60 and 90 minutes after ingestion. The 25 g phosphatidylcholine dose, supplying about 3.75 g choline, improved explicit memory at 90 minutes and showed a smaller signal at 60 minutes. Further analyses suggested the effect may have been concentrated among slow learners.

## Notes

- Use as a weak positive acute phosphatidylcholine signal in normal young adults.
- The dose is very high by supplement standards and the endpoint is narrow.
- The slow-learner finding supports responder-gated interpretation rather than broad enhancement.
