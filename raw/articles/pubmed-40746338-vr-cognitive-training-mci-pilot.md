# The Effectiveness of VR-Based Cognitive Training Program for Mild Cognitive Impairment: A Pilot Study

- PMID: 40746338
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40746338/
- Ingested: 2026-04-29

## Summary

Pilot study in 32 MCI patients testing a 12-week VR-based cognitive training program with twice-weekly 50-minute sessions. Twenty-eight completed the intervention.

Post-intervention, the abstract reports significant improvement in total composite and memory-specific neuropsychological scores, no significant changes in depression, anxiety, dizziness, neurobehavioral symptoms, or daily living activities, and increased functional connectivity in bilateral hippocampus, parahippocampal gyrus, and amygdala.

## Notes

This is a feasibility-positive, no-control pilot, so practice effects and natural change remain live explanations. The imaging signal is interesting, but it does not by itself establish durable transfer.
