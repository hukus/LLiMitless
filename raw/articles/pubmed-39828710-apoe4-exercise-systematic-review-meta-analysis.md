Title: The effect of the APOE4 genotype on physiological and cognitive health in randomised controlled trials with an exercise intervention: a systematic review and meta-analysis

This review synthesizes RCTs that analyzed exercise outcomes by APOE4 carrier status.

The main takeaway is mixed and low-certainty. Across the included trials, some cognitive and physiologic outcomes favored APOE4 carriers, while others favored non-carriers. The meta-analysis was limited by sparse data and very low to moderate quality evidence, so it does not support a simple rule that APOE4 enrichment always improves exercise effect sizes.

For the wiki, this is the strongest contradiction source against any overly optimistic enrichment claim.
