# Elamipretide (SS-31) improves mitochondrial dysfunction, synaptic and memory impairment induced by lipopolysaccharide in mice

- PMCID: PMC6865061
- PMC: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6865061/
- Ingested: 2026-04-29

## Summary

SS-31 reduced LPS-induced learning and memory impairment in mice, with improvements in mitochondrial function, oxidative stress, BDNF signaling, synaptic proteins, and synaptic structural complexity.

## Notes

- Best available preclinical cognition signal for elamipretide/SS-31.
- The model is inflammatory and does not establish human cognitive benefit.
