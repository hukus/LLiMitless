# GEMINI phase 3 trial of AXS-05 dextromethorphan-bupropion in MDD

- Citation: Iosifescu et al., Journal of Clinical Psychiatry, 2022.
- DOI: 10.4088/JCP.21m14345
- Trial registration: NCT04019704.
- Design: double-blind, randomized, placebo-controlled phase 3 trial in major depressive disorder, conducted June-December 2019 at 40 U.S. centers.

## Captured takeaways

- 327 adults with MDD were randomized to dextromethorphan-bupropion 45 mg/105 mg or placebo for 6 weeks.
- Dosing was once daily for days 1-3 and twice daily thereafter.
- Primary endpoint was change in Montgomery-Asberg Depression Rating Scale total score at week 6.
- Other endpoints included week 1 and week 2 MADRS changes, remission/response, CGI, PGI, QIDS-SR-16, Sheehan Disability Scale, and quality-of-life measures.
- Week 6 MADRS improvement favored dextromethorphan-bupropion over placebo, and the abstract reports superiority at week 1 and week 2.
- Week 6 remission and clinical response rates were higher with dextromethorphan-bupropion than placebo.
- Common adverse events in the treatment group included dizziness, nausea, headache, somnolence, and dry mouth.
- No dedicated cognitive-performance endpoint was listed among the captured endpoints; disability and quality-of-life endpoints are not substitutes for cognition testing.
- Interpretation for the wiki: GEMINI supports a rapid-antidepressant / mood-state entry for AUVELITY, but not a direct cognition or healthy-adult nootropic claim.
