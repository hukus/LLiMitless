# Methionine loading, SAMe, SAH, folate, and homocysteine in healthy humans

PubMed 8774264 reports an acute methionine-loading study in healthy human subjects that measured 5-methyltetrahydrofolate, S-adenosylmethionine / SAMe, S-adenosylhomocysteine / SAH, methionine, and total homocysteine in plasma.

The important cognition-wiki point is mechanistic rather than nootropic: oral methionine loading can move plasma SAMe and SAH while also raising homocysteine-pathway markers. That makes L-methionine a real one-carbon-pathway perturbation, but not an objective cognition intervention.

Wiki use: use this source to separate [[l-methionine]] from [[s-adenosyl-l-methionine]]. Methionine can feed SAMe biology, but the same loading logic also raises homocysteine / SAH concerns, so subjective "methylation" anecdotes require lab and safety gates.
