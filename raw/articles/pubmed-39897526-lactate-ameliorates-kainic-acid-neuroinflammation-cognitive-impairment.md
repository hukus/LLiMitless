# Lactate Ameliorates Kainic Acid-Induced Neuroinflammation and Cognitive Impairment via the Chemokine Signaling Pathway in Mice

- PMID: 39897526
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39897526/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Acute mouse epilepsy model showing that lactate reduced neuroinflammation, neuronal damage, anxiety-like behavior, and cognitive impairment. The study points to chemokine signaling as a possible mechanism and adds a second 2025 preclinical cognition-positive branch.
