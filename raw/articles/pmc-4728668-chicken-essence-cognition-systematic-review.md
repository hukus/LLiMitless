# Chicken Essence for Cognitive Function Improvement: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis

- PMCID: PMC4728668
- DOI: 10.3390/nu8010057
- Published: 2016-01-20
- Ingested: 2026-05-11

## Summary

Systematic review and meta-analysis of randomized controlled trials of chicken essence for cognitive function. Seven trials were included: six in healthy subjects and one in subjects with poorer cognitive function. One trial had unclear risk of bias and the rest had high risk of bias.

Executive-function analyses were mixed, with one pooled analysis favoring chicken essence and another not significant. Short-term memory was not significant. The authors concluded that convincing evidence for cognitive enhancement was lacking at that time.

## Relevance

This is the main quality-control counterweight for [[essence-of-chicken]]. It prevents over-ranking older, sponsor-linked, or high-risk-of-bias trials.

## Caveats

- Review predates the larger 2018 RCT and the later 2019 meta-analysis.
- Many included studies had high risk of bias.
- Essence of chicken products include peptides, amino acids, and other components, so attribution to anserine/carnosine alone is unsafe.
