# MND Association 2024 TUDCA-ALS phase 3 negative summary

The MND Association's TUDCA page, last updated March 27, 2024, summarizes the European phase 3 TUDCA-ALS trial.

The page describes TUDCA as a bile-acid substance naturally produced in small trace amounts in bile and as a candidate neurodegenerative-disease therapy. It states that the phase 3 TUDCA-ALS trial tested TUDCA with riluzole over 18 months in 334 participants across 26 centers in Italy, Germany, the UK, France, Belgium, the Netherlands, and Ireland.

Trial outcome: the phase 3 trial did not meet its primary endpoint. A greater than 20% reduction in ALSFRS-R slope after 18 months was not observed, and statistically significant differences were not seen in secondary endpoints such as survival and neurofilament light chain.

Interpretation for the wiki: this is a major negative disease-state guardrail for the TUDCA neuroprotection story. It does not directly test cognition, but it prevents the wiki from treating TUDCA's preclinical and phase 2 promise as confirmed neuroprotective efficacy.
