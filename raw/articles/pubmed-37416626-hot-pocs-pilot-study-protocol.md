# Hyperbaric Oxygen Therapy versus placebo for post-concussion syndrome (HOT-POCS): a randomized, double-blinded controlled pilot study.

- PMID: 37416626
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37416626/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Protocol for a civilian PCS trial using a true placebo gas at 2.0 ATA, designed to answer the sham-control question more cleanly than older HBOT studies.

## Notes

The protocol planned 100 adults with persistent PCS 3-12 months after injury, 20 sessions of 100% oxygen vs a placebo gas mixture of 10.5% oxygen and 89.5% nitrogen, and outcomes spanning symptom burden, cognition, quality of life, MRI perfusion, and safety. The registry record later showed the study as completed with no results posted and actual enrollment far below the planned sample.
