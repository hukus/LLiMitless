# A randomized control trial to establish the feasibility and safety of rapamycin treatment in an older human cohort: Immunological, physical performance, and cognitive effects

- PMID: 29408453
- DOI: 10.1016/j.exger.2017.12.026
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

2018 placebo-controlled pilot in 25 generally healthy older adults aged 70-95. Subjects received 1 mg rapamycin or placebo daily; 11 rapamycin and 14 controls completed at least 8 weeks.

No changes were noted in cognitive tests, physical performance, or self-perceived health over the study period. The main safety signal was reduced erythrocyte parameters in the rapamycin arm, without clinically significant short-term effects.
