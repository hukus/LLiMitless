# Melissa officinalis acute cognition and mood RCT in healthy young adults

PubMed-indexed 2002 randomized placebo-controlled double-blind balanced-crossover study of a standardized Melissa officinalis extract in 20 healthy young participants.

Doses: 300 mg, 600 mg, and 900 mg M. officinalis extract or placebo at 7-day intervals. Cognition and mood were assessed before dosing and at 1, 2.5, 4, and 6 hours after dosing with the Cognitive Drug Research battery, serial subtraction tasks, and Bond-Lader mood scales.

Results: 600 mg produced sustained improvement in Accuracy of Attention. The study also reported time- and dose-specific reductions in Secondary Memory and Working Memory factors. Self-rated calmness rose at early time points after the lowest dose, while alertness was reduced after the highest dose.

Relevance: direct healthy-adult acute cognition/mood source showing a mixed calm-focus profile, not clean broad memory enhancement.
