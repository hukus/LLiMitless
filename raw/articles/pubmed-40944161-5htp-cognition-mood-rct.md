# The Impact of 5-Hydroxytryptophan Supplementation on Cognitive Function and Mood in Singapore Older Adults

- PMID: 40944161
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40944161/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

Single-blind randomized controlled trial in 30 older adults in Singapore. Participants received 100 mg/day 5-HTP for 12 weeks or no supplementation. The study reported a small MoCA increase, improved GDS score, and higher serum serotonin in the 5-HTP group, but no clear effects on anxiety or the other measured biomarkers.
