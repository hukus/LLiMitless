# FDA bulk-drug-substances page on epitalon

- Content current as of: 2026-04-22
- Source: https://www.fda.gov/drugs/human-drug-compounding/certain-bulk-drug-substances-use-compounding-may-present-significant-safety-risks

## Key points

FDA's current compounding-risk page explicitly names epitalon in the withdrawn-nomination section. FDA says compounded epitalon may pose risk for immunogenicity due to aggregation and peptide-related impurities, and that FDA has not identified safety-related information for the proposed route of administration. FDA therefore lacks sufficient information to know whether epitalon would cause harm if administered to humans.

## Research-use read

For the wiki, this is a current U.S. regulatory warning, not efficacy proof. Epitalon remains investigational bulk peptide material with unresolved route, purity, and supplier-quality questions.
