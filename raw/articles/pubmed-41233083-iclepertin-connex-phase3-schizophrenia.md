# Efficacy and safety of iclepertin for cognitive impairment associated with schizophrenia (CONNEX programme): results from three phase 3 randomised controlled trials

- PMID: 41233083
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41233083/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

The 2025 CONNEX phase 3 program tested iclepertin 10 mg once daily for 26 weeks in 1,835 treated adults with schizophrenia across three multinational RCTs. The program was negative on the primary MCCB overall composite T-score endpoint and on key secondary cognition/function measures. The adjusted mean difference on MCCB was 0.127 (95% CI -0.396 to 0.650; p=0.63), and tolerability looked acceptable.
