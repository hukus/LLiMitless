# Spatially Resolved EEG Reveals Theta-Band Network Modulation Following iTBS in Aging and Mild Cognitive Impairment

- PMID: 41971354
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41971354/
- Ingested: 2026-04-30

## Summary

Sham-controlled iTBS study in aging and MCI using spatially resolved EEG to reconstruct whole-brain theta-band network modulation. The paper is mainly a target-engagement and mechanistic-network source, useful for responder modeling but not yet a predictive biomarker result.

## Notes

- Supports the idea that EEG can capture stimulation-linked network change in older and MCI cohorts.
- More target-engagement than responder-prediction evidence.
- Useful context for the biomarker and digital-phenotype responder layer.
