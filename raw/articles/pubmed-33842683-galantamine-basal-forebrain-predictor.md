# Structural MRI of the basal forebrain as predictor of cognitive response to galantamine in healthy older adults-A randomized controlled double-blinded crossover study

- PMID: 33842683
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33842683/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Single-dose galantamine in healthy older adults did not produce a uniform cognitive response. The positive signal was concentrated in participants with larger basal forebrain cholinergic volume, where galantamine improved attention-dependent filtering during working-memory encoding. The study is a responder-enrichment lead, not a broad enhancement claim.
