# High Impact AMPAkines Induce a Gq-Protein Coupled Endoplasmic Calcium Release in Cortical Neurons: A Possible Mechanism for Explaining the Toxicity of High Impact AMPAkines

- PMID: 39304968
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39304968/
- Ingested: 2026-04-30

## Summary

2024 mechanistic paper showing why high-impact AMPAkines are harder to develop: compounds such as cyclothiazide and CX614 triggered a Gq/PLCbeta/InsP3-linked endoplasmic-reticulum calcium-release pathway and neuronal hyperexcitability, helping explain toxicity and convulsion liability. The same linkage was not seen with low-impact CX516 or CX717, and CX1739 was described as safe at very high doses. This paper supports the class pivot toward low-impact chemistry, but it does not establish cognition efficacy.
