# 2023 amino-acid upper-limit review: methionine

This review of upper-limit evidence for individual amino-acid supplements summarizes methionine tolerability by leaning on human supplemental methionine studies, including the Deutz healthy-older-adult work that used homocysteine and questionnaires as safety/tolerance indicators.

For methionine, the review preserves the practical signal that homocysteine is the key safety readout and that a human NOAEL/LOAEL can be estimated from supplemented free L-methionine exposure. It does not establish cognitive benefit.

Wiki use: use as a second safety/context source for [[l-methionine]], especially where a page needs to avoid overreading a single small tolerance trial.
