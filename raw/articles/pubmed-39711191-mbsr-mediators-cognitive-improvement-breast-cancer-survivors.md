# A Randomized Clinical Trial of Mindfulness-Based Stress Reduction Program Among Breast Cancer Survivors Post-Treatment: Evaluating Mediators of Cognitive Improvement

- PMID: 39711191
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39711191/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

Randomized trial in post-treatment breast cancer survivors that explicitly tested whether cognitive improvement was mediated through increased mindfulness, decreased rumination, and lower perceived stress. It is a useful behavioral analog for the wiki's mediation ladder because it separates outcome change from the proposed mechanism of change.
