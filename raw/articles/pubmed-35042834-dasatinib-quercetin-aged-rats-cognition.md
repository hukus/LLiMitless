# Combination of dasatinib and quercetin improves cognitive abilities in aged male Wistar rats

- PMID: 35042834
- PMCID: PMC8833137
- DOI: 10.18632/aging.203835

## Summary

This rat study is the clearest direct cognition-positive preclinical result in the senolytic branch. Eight weeks of dasatinib plus quercetin improved spatial memory and learning in aged male Wistar rats, with effects persisting at least 5 weeks after treatment stopped.

## Key Data

- Task: active allothetic place avoidance task.
- Findings: improved learning and memory in aged rats versus vehicle.
- Mechanistic correlates: reduced peripheral inflammation, changes in dendritic spine morphology in CA1, and changes in hippocampal H3K9/H3K27 methylation.
- Durability: benefit persisted after cessation.

## Caveats

- Species translation is uncertain.
- The result is strong for mechanism generation, not sufficient for human efficacy inference.
