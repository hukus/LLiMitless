# The Effect of Closed-Loop Auditory Stimulation on Memory Consolidation and Sleep Physiology in an Ecological Setting

Journal of Sleep Research 2025 study in 34 participants at home. Closed-loop auditory stimulation successfully enhanced slow-oscillation amplitude during sleep, but it did not produce measurable benefits on declarative memory performance or vigilance in this ecological design.

This is the cleanest counterweight to the more optimistic laboratory CLAS reports. It suggests that physiology can move without behavior, and that real-world delivery may be harder than controlled sleep-lab delivery.
