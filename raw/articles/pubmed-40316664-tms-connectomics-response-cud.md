# Brain connectomics markers for response prediction to transcranial magnetic stimulation in cocaine use disorder

- PMID: 40316664
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40316664/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

Randomized controlled trial in cocaine use disorder testing whether resting-state connectomics and baseline craving predict response to add-on rTMS on the left DLPFC.

## Notes

- Forty-four participants were randomized into sham or active acute phases, then a subset continued maintenance rTMS.
- Combining baseline craving with connectivity change improved prediction versus craving alone.
- This is not a healthy-adult nootropic study, but it is a clean example of mechanistic biomarkers outperforming symptom-only screening for stimulation response.
