# Young adult microglial deletion of C1q reduces engulfment of synapses and prevents cognitive impairment in an aggressive Alzheimer's disease mouse model

- PMID: 41000995
- PMCID: PMC12458416
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41000995/
- Retrieved: 2026-04-30

## Summary

2025 preprint in an aggressive AD mouse model showing that deleting C1q from young adult microglia reduced synapse engulfment and prevented cognitive impairment. This strengthens the complement-synapse-loss model and the idea that timing matters, but it remains preclinical.

## Notes

- Useful as a causal target-validation anchor for the C1q branch.
- Not evidence for a human cognition intervention.
