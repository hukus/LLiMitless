# Iodine supplementation in mildly iodine-deficient adults

- PMID: 26891118
- Published: 2016
- Source type: randomized controlled trial
- Checked: 2026-05-11
- Ingested: 2026-05-11

## Summary

This adult trial is useful mainly as a status and thyroid-response anchor, not as a cognition-efficacy anchor. It shows that iodine supplementation can change iodine and thyroid-related measures in mildly deficient adults, but it does not establish reliable cognitive improvement in healthy adults.

Use in the wiki: [[iodine]] should be routed through iodine status, thyroid function, diet, pregnancy/lactation status, and excess-dose risk before any brain-fog interpretation.

## Notes

- Adult cognition claims remain indirect unless a trial measures cognitive endpoints.
- Iodine anecdotes often mix true deficiency, thyroid disease, kelp dose variability, placebo, and hyperthyroid-like activation.
