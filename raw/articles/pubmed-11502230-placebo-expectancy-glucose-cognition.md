# Placebo expectancy effects in the relationship between glucose and cognition

- PMID: 11502230
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11502230/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

Within-subject balanced-placebo study testing whether glucose benefits on cognition depend on what participants were told they were drinking. The results showed expectancy-sensitive effects on some cognitive measures, which makes this a direct example of a cognitive enhancement claim being confounded by instruction and belief.
