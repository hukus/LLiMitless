# DailyMed minocycline label safety context

DailyMed minocycline labels identify minocycline as a prescription tetracycline antibiotic. Common label-level concerns include hypersensitivity, intracranial hypertension/pseudotumor cerebri, vestibular effects such as dizziness or vertigo, autoimmune reactions including lupus-like syndrome and hepatitis, skin/tissue pigmentation, photosensitivity, gastrointestinal effects, and antimicrobial-resistance stewardship.

Interpretation for the wiki: minocycline is not a supplement-style intervention. Any off-label brain-fog use carries antibiotic, vestibular, autoimmune, hepatic, pregnancy/teratogenicity, microbiome, and resistance considerations.
