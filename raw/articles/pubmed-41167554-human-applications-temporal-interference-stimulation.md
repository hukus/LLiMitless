# Human applications of transcranial temporal interference stimulation: A systematic review

- PMID: 41167554
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41167554/
- Published: 2025-10-28 (epub)
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary
Systematic review of 48 records (20 published studies, 28 ongoing trials).
Human tTIS appears safe and well tolerated in phase 1 work.
Most published studies use beta or gamma envelope frequencies and show deep-target engagement in motor cortex, basal ganglia, and hippocampus.
Cognition efficacy is still mostly indirect or preliminary.
