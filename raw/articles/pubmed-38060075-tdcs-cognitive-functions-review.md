# The Effects of Transcranial Direct Current Stimulation (tDCS) on the Cognitive Functions: A Systematic Review and Meta-analysis

- PMID: 38060075
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38060075/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

2025 systematic review/meta-analysis of tDCS cognitive outcomes across 69 studies and 5,545 participants. The review reports significant effects on cognitive function overall, with moderation by timing, population, duration, intensity, stimulation site, and age.

## Notes

This is the broadest recent review anchor for the tDCS branch. It is useful as a complement to the dosage-parameter meta-analysis because it keeps the umbrella question visible while still reinforcing that the signal is parameter-sensitive rather than flat.
