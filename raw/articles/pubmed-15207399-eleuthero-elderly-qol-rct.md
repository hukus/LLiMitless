# Eleuthero elderly quality-of-life randomized trial

PubMed record for a randomized clinical trial of Eleutherococcus senticosus, also called Siberian ginseng, in elderly participants.

The abstract reports no baseline demographic or SF-36v2 differences. After 4 weeks, the Eleuthero group had higher social-functioning scores, but the difference did not persist at 8 weeks. No adverse event was observed, and blood pressure and digitalis/digoxin monitoring did not differ significantly between groups. Subjects receiving Eleuthero were more likely to guess they were receiving active treatment.

Interpretation for the wiki: this is not a cognition trial, but it is useful as human exposure and a weak mental-health/social-functioning comparator. It supports conservative safety/product framing for Eleuthero, not nootropic efficacy.
