# Acute effects of caffeine on attention: a comparison of non-consumers and withdrawn consumers

- PMID: 22992376
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22992376/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Randomized controlled trial in 35 non-consumers and 35 withdrawn consumers found that a week of withdrawal did not normalize away all effects, but acute caffeine still improved reaction time, target detection, and information encoding. The study is useful because it separates acute caffeine response from simple withdrawal reversal.

## Notes

This is one of the clearest primary papers for the interpretation problem around caffeine schedules: some apparent "benefit" may be withdrawal correction, but the acute challenge still has measurable cognitive effects.
