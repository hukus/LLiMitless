# Ingenious Ingredients self-affirms GRAS status for enfinity paraxanthine

- Source type: trade/regulatory-status report
- Published: 2021-11-23
- Ingested: 2026-05-03

## Summary

Trade report that Ingenious Ingredients announced self-affirmed GRAS status for its enfinity-branded paraxanthine ingredient for use in food and beverages. The reported use cases include energy beverages, decaffeinated coffee beverages and powders, and nutrition / cereal bars at levels up to 300 mg.

Use in the wiki: product/status context, not FDA approval. This is self-affirmed GRAS reporting and should not be confused with an FDA no-questions GRAS letter, clinical efficacy, or universal label accuracy.
