# CX614 stimulates BDNF release and dendritic mRNA translation

Open-access Journal of Neuroscience paper on CX614, BDNF release, and dendritic protein-synthesis signaling.

Key extracted points:

- CX614 is described as one of the best-studied positive AMPA receptor modulators, also known as AMPAkines.
- The paper links CX614 to increased BDNF mRNA/protein and facilitation of LTP induction in earlier work.
- CX614 rapidly stimulated BDNF release and increased dendritic mRNA translation in cultured neurons and acute hippocampal-slice preparations.
- The translation effect was blocked by TrkB, AMPA/NMDA, activity, and mTOR-related inhibitors or scavengers in the experimental setup, supporting a BDNF-dependent signaling interpretation.
- Interpretation for the wiki: this is a strong mechanistic synaptic-plasticity source for CX614, but it does not provide human cognition, dose, or chronic safety evidence.
