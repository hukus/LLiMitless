# Senolytic therapy ameliorates high-fat diet-induced hippocampal senescence and cognitive decline in mice

- PMID: 41895394
- DOI: 10.1016/j.expneurol.2026.115745
- Journal: Experimental Neurology (2026; online ahead of print)

## Summary

This is a 2026 mouse study showing that a high-fat diet and palmitate drive hippocampal senescence and cognitive deficits, and that the senolytic cocktail dasatinib plus quercetin reduced senescent cell burden and restored hippocampal-dependent cognitive function and synaptic protein expression.

## Key Data

- Model: male C57BL/6J mice exposed to high-fat diet or palmitate-enriched diet.
- Behavioral domains: hippocampal-dependent spatial learning and memory, novel object recognition, novel location, Y-maze spatial memory, nesting behavior.
- Senescence readouts: SA-β-gal-positive cells, p16/p21, SASP factors, Lamin B1.
- Effect direction: HFD and palmitate induced senescence/cognitive deficits; D+Q reduced senescent burden and normalized cognition/synaptic proteins.

## Caveats

- This is a preclinical obesity-associated cognitive-decline model, not a human cognition trial.
- The finding supports a metabolic-state rescue hypothesis more than a general healthy-enhancement claim.
