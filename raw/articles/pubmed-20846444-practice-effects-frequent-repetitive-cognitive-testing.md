# Practice effects in healthy adults: a longitudinal study on frequent repetitive cognitive testing

- PMID: 20846444
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20846444/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

2010 longitudinal study showing that frequent repeated cognitive testing produces clear practice effects early on, with many measures stabilizing after repeated exposure. The practical lesson for an endpoint dictionary is that "learning slope" and test-retest noise are real and need to be separated from intervention benefit.

## Notes

This source is a useful anchor for distinguishing true acquisition gains from repeated-exposure artifacts.
