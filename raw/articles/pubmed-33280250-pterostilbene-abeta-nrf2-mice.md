# Pterostilbene alleviates Abeta-induced cognitive dysfunction via Nrf2 in mice

- Source type: PubMed-indexed preclinical paper.
- PMID: 33280250
- DOI: 10.1002/mnfr.202000711
- Publication: Molecular Nutrition & Food Research, 2021.
- Date checked: 2026-05-04.

Mouse study of pterostilbene in beta-amyloid 1-42 induced cognitive impairment. The abstract reports improvement across Y-maze, novel object recognition, Morris water maze, and passive avoidance tasks, with reduced neuronal loss and reactive oxygen species. Mechanistically, pterostilbene promoted Nrf2 nuclear translocation and antioxidant-gene expression, and Nrf2 inhibition reversed antioxidant effects in SH-SY5Y cells.

Use in the wiki: strongest direct cognition preclinical anchor for pterostilbene. It supports a mechanistic hypothesis, not human efficacy.
