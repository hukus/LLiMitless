# The effects of targeted reactivation on memories cued once or multiple times during a nap

J Sleep Res 2025 study in 31 analyzed participants after exclusions from 47 recruits. During a 90-minute nap, sounds linked to object locations were cued once or five times. There was no significant behavioral advantage for once versus multiple cues, and neither TMR condition beat the non-cued condition after interference. Sigma power tracked improvement only in the multiple-cue condition, while the authors argued that context and interference may have masked behavioral benefit.

This is a useful negative result for dose-response intuition: more cues were not enough to produce a clear behavioral win.
