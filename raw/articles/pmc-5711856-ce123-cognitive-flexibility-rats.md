# CE-123 cognitive flexibility in healthy male rats

- Frontiers in Behavioral Neuroscience paper on CE-123, a newly synthesized compound with higher specificity for dopamine transporter inhibition.
- The study tested male rats in an attentional set-shifting task for cognitive flexibility and a 5-choice serial-reaction-time task for visuospatial attention and impulsivity.
- The S-enantiomer enhanced cognitive flexibility at very low doses and did not produce impulsive responding even at higher doses in this assay.
- The paper positions CE-123 as suitable for further testing of cognition-enhancing properties in additional behavioral paradigms.
- Interpretation for the wiki: useful preclinical support for CE-123 as a dopamine-transporter / executive-flexibility lead, but still no human cognition or safety packet.
