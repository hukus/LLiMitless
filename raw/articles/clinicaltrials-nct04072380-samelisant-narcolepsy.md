# A Study to Evaluate Safety, and Efficacy of SUVN-G3031 (Samelisant) in Patients With Narcolepsy With and Without Cataplexy (SUVN-G3031)

- ClinicalTrials.gov ID: NCT04072380
- Sponsor: Suven Life Sciences Limited
- ClinicalTrials.gov: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04072380

## Summary

Completed phase 2 randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled narcolepsy study of samelisant. The protocol randomized participants to 2 mg, 4 mg, or placebo once daily for 14 days, with 190 enrolled and study completion in June 2023.
