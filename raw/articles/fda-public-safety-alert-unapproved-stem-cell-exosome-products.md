# FDA public safety alert due to marketing of unapproved stem cell and exosome products

- Posted: 2019-12-09
- Source: https://www.fda.gov/safety/medical-product-safety-information/public-safety-alert-due-marketing-unapproved-stem-cell-and-exosome-products

## Key points

FDA reported serious adverse events in Nebraska tied to unapproved exosome products and warned that clinics were marketing these products with unsubstantiated disease claims. FDA reiterated that there are currently no FDA-approved exosome products.

## Research-use read

This belongs in the branch as a real-world harm and regulatory-mismatch signal, not as evidence of therapeutic benefit.
