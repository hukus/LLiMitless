Title: APOE4, Blood Neurodegenerative Biomarkers, and Cognitive Decline in Community-Dwelling Older Adults

This is observational, not interventional. It shows APOE4 carriers experienced faster cognitive decline when t-tau, NfL, and GFAP were higher, with effect modification stronger in carriers than noncarriers. The value for enrichment is that APOE4 plus blood biomarkers may identify higher-risk cohorts, but it does not prove an intervention effect-size multiplier.

Caveat: cohort association, not treatment response; useful for enrichment hypotheses only.
