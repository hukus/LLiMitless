# Anatomical Characteristics Predict Response to Transcranial Direct Current Stimulation (tDCS): Development of a Computational Pipeline for Optimizing tDCS Protocols

- PMID: 40564472
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40564472/
- Ingested: 2026-04-30

## Summary

Computational tDCS study showing that fixed-dose stimulation ignores major inter-subject anatomical variation. In a 23-subject MRI model set, skull thickness, CSF volume, and tissue composition were associated with electric-field distribution, and the percentage of brain tissue above an electric-field threshold was the main factor linked to responsiveness.

## Notes

- The paper argues for personalized tDCS dosing based on head morphology.
- This is a mechanistic responder model, not a cognition endpoint trial.
- Useful as a gate for whether anatomy should be treated as a stratifier before tDCS use.
