# PubMed 33549685 - Methylene Blue and Photobiomodulation in Hepatic Encephalopathy

## Citation

- Mendez M, Fidalgo C, Arias JL, Arias N. "Methylene blue and photobiomodulation recover cognitive impairment in hepatic encephalopathy through different effects on cytochrome c-oxidase." Behavioural Brain Research. 2021;403:113164. DOI: 10.1016/j.bbr.2021.113164.
- Source type: peer-reviewed animal study.

## Extracted Claims

- The study used a rat hepatic-encephalopathy model and treated animals with intracranial methylene blue or transcranial photobiomodulation.
- Photobiomodulation used 670 +/- 10 nm LED light at 9 J/cm2 once daily for 7 days.
- Methylene blue was injected into the accumbens at 2.2 micrograms / 0.5 microliters in the experimental design.
- Cognitive dysfunction was evaluated using a striatal stimulus-response task in the Morris water maze.
- Both methylene blue and photobiomodulation improved the hepatic-encephalopathy memory deficit, but their cytochrome-c-oxidase effects differed across brain regions.

## Cognition-Relevant Extraction

- The source supports a shared mitochondrial/cytochrome-c-oxidase neighborhood for MB and PBM, but it does not test a combined MB + PBM arm.
- The cognition signal is animal, disease-model, route-specific, and not a healthy-human synergy claim.
- It should be used as a mechanistic comparator for MB plus light, not as evidence that red/NIR light and oral MB are a validated stack.
