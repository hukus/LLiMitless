# NCT02125032 Transcranial Low Voltage Pulsed Electromagnetic Fields in Parkinson Disease

Source type: ClinicalTrials.gov record.

Core design:
- Completed interventional Parkinson's disease trial.
- Sponsor: Odense University Hospital.
- Intervention: transcranial low-voltage pulsed electromagnetic fields versus placebo T-PEMF.
- Actual enrollment: 98.
- Study start: May 2014; primary completion: October 2017; study completion: March 2018.
- Protocol listed one 30-minute daily home treatment for eight weeks.
- ClinicalTrials.gov record lists publications including the Parkinson's quality-of-life paper.

Safety / exclusion gates:
- Exclusions included prior T-PEMF exposure, recent anti-Parkinson medication changes, dementia/brain injury affecting consent or assessment, psychosis, substance abuse, deep brain stimulation, pregnancy or nursing, epilepsy, active implants such as pacemakers or cochlear implants, active medical devices such as insulin or baclofen pumps, other trial participation, selected malignancy histories, autoimmune disease, and scalp wounds.

Wiki takeaway:
- Trial-grade T-PEMF protocols treat active implants, epilepsy, pregnancy, DBS, active implanted devices, and scalp conditions as protocol-level exclusions.
- These gates should be carried into any PEMF/structured-EMF cognition page before discussing self-experimentation or consumer devices.

Limitations:
- Registry page has no posted tabular results.
- Parkinson's disease context does not establish healthy-adult cognition benefit.
