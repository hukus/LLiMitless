# Convolvulus pluricaulis tauopathy Drosophila model

2019 Journal of Chemical Neuroanatomy preclinical study of Convolvulus pluricaulis / Shankhapushpi aqueous extract in a Drosophila tauopathy model expressing human MAP tau.

The study reported that C. pluricaulis supplementation offset tau-induced early death, extended lifespan, reduced tau protein level, improved locomotor activity, reduced oxidative stress markers, and restored antioxidant and acetylcholinesterase-related measures in the model.

Interpretation for the wiki: this supports a preclinical neuroprotective mechanism hypothesis for Shankhpushpi, but it is not human cognition evidence and should not upgrade human confidence.
