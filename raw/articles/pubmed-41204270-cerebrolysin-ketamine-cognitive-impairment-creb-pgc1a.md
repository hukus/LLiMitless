# Cerebrolysin ameliorates ketamine-mediated anxiety and cognitive impairments via modulation of mitochondrial function and CREB/PGC-1alpha pathway

- PMID: 41204270
- PMCID: PMC12595893
- DOI: 10.1186/s13041-025-01255-1
- Journal: Molecular Brain, 2025
- Source type: preclinical cognition/anxiety mechanism study; ketamine mouse model
- Ingested: 2026-05-04

## Summary

2025 mouse study using repeated ketamine to induce schizophrenia-like anxiety and cognitive impairment. Male BALB/c mice received ketamine for 14 days and Cerebrolysin during days 8-14. The study used novel object recognition and elevated-plus-maze behavior plus hippocampal ATP, ROS, CREB, p-CREB, and PGC-1alpha measures. The abstract reports that Cerebrolysin reversed ketamine-linked recognition-memory and anxiety-like changes, restored hippocampal ATP/ROS directionally, and upregulated CREB/p-CREB/PGC-1alpha signaling.

## Signal Use

- Medicinal / formal signal: preclinical psychiatric-state cognition rescue and mitochondrial/CREB mechanism support.
- Translation limit: ketamine mouse model only; does not establish schizophrenia, mood, addiction, or healthy-human cognitive benefit.
- Anecdotal signal: none.
- Wiki implication: route this into the mechanism/preclinical lane and keep it separate from human psychiatry claims.
