# Effect of CX516, an AMPA-modulating compound, on cognition and behavior in fragile X syndrome: a controlled trial

- PMID: 17069542
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17069542/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

2006 phase II randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial in fragile X syndrome. CX516 was well tolerated overall but did not significantly improve memory, language, attention/executive function, behavior, or overall functioning.
