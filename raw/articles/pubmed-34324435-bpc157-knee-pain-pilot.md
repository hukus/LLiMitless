# Intra-Articular Injection of BPC 157 for Multiple Types of Knee Pain

- PMID: 34324435
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34324435/

## Summary

Retrospective 1-year chart review of 16 patients with chronic knee pain who received intra-articular BPC-157, alone or with thymosin beta-4. Most patients reported improvement, but the study was uncontrolled, subjective, and not a safety-quality trial.
