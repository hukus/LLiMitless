# FDA warning letters for higenamine, hordenine, and octopamine supplements

2022 JAMA news item reporting FDA warning letters to companies selling adulterated dietary supplements. The named ingredients included higenamine, higenamine HCl, hordenine, hordenine HCl, and octopamine, among others.

Relevance: higenamine/hordenine/octopamine are stimulant-adjacent preworkout ingredients with regulatory and safety visibility. They belong in a risk-first supplement branch unless direct cognition evidence appears.
