# Otsuka 2026 centanafadine NDA acceptance and priority review

- Source: Otsuka US press release
- Date: 2026-01-27
- Status checked: 2026-05-15

## Captured takeaways

- Otsuka announced that FDA accepted the New Drug Application for centanafadine and granted priority review for ADHD in children, adolescents, and adults.
- Centanafadine was described as an investigational once-daily extended-release capsule and a first-in-class norepinephrine, dopamine, and serotonin reuptake inhibitor.
- The stated PDUFA target action date is 2026-07-24.
- The NDA was said to be supported by four pivotal phase 3 trials across children, adolescents, and adults.
- Otsuka reported statistically significant ADHD symptom improvement versus placebo across those trials, measured with ADHD-RS-5 in children/adolescents and AISRS in adults.
- Adult adverse events highlighted by the sponsor were decreased appetite and headache; pediatric/adolescent adverse events included decreased appetite, nausea, rash, fatigue, abdominal pain, and somnolence.
- The release describes low abuse-potential signals as suggested by clinical and preclinical data, but this remains sponsor framing until the regulatory review is complete.
- Interpretation for the wiki: as of 2026-05-15, centanafadine is a live investigational ADHD drug under FDA priority review, not an approved nootropic or established healthy-adult cognition treatment.
