# Cognitive training and brain stimulation in patients with cognitive impairment: a randomized controlled trial

- PMID: 38212815
- PMC: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10782634/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

Randomized sham-controlled trial in cognitive impairment using 9 training sessions over 3 weeks with concurrent anodal tDCS. The primary trained-task endpoint was negative, but per-protocol analysis showed near-transfer working-memory benefit, 7-month frontoparietal connectivity increases, and a dose-response signal with electric-field magnitude. Strong example of the gap between trained performance and downstream transfer.
