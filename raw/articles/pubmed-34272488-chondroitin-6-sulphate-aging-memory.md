# PubMed 34272488 - Chondroitin 6-Sulphate, Aging, and Memory

## Citation

- Yang S, Hilton S, Alves JN, et al. "Chondroitin 6-sulphate is required for neuroplasticity and memory in ageing." Molecular Psychiatry. 2021. DOI: 10.1038/s41380-021-01208-9. PMCID: PMC8758471.
- Source type: peer-reviewed preclinical study.

## Extracted Claims

- The study links age-related changes in PNN chondroitin sulfation to neuroplasticity and memory.
- Reduced chondroitin 6-sulphate was framed as making PNNs more inhibitory in aged animals.
- Aged mice showed memory and plasticity deficits, while manipulations that retained or restored permissive sulfation were associated with cognitive rescue.
- Chst3 deletion reduced permissive C6S and produced early memory impairment.
- Restoring C6S levels in aged animals rescued memory deficits and restored cortical LTP.

## Cognition-Relevant Extraction

- Medicinal cognition signal: positive animal aging-memory and LTP rescue signal through PNN sulfation chemistry.
- Translation status: no human cognition trial and no supplement-style dose conclusion.
- Signal-strength read: mechanistically important preclinical support for ECM chemistry, not proof that generic PNN degradation is beneficial.
- Safety interpretation for the wiki: sulfation-specific data argue against oversimplifying PNNs into a one-direction plasticity target.
