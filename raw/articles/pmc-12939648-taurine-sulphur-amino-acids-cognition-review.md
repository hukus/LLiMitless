# Cognitive Effects of Taurine and Related Sulphur-Containing Amino Acids

- PMID: 41750826
- PMCID: PMC12939648
- PubMed Central: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12939648/
- Published: 2026
- Ingested: 2026-05-03

## Summary

2026 systematic review of randomized controlled trials on sulphur-containing amino acid supplementation and cognition. Eight RCTs with 244 healthy participants met criteria, and all tested taurine. Acute taurine doses, typically 1-3 g and up to about 50 mg/kg, produced at best small and inconsistent cognitive effects, with most outcomes showing no effect. Trials combining taurine with caffeine showed more reliable performance benefits but did not isolate taurine's independent effects.

## Interpretation

Taurine is a low-confidence cognition candidate. The current signal is mostly null or caffeine-combination confounded, and the review emphasizes the need for longer, better-designed studies that measure baseline diet and nutrient status.
