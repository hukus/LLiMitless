# The kynurenine pathway in Alzheimer's disease: a meta-analysis of central and peripheral levels

- PMID: 36754068
- DOI: 10.47626/1516-4446-2022-2962
- Journal: Brazilian Journal of Psychiatry (2023)

## Captured takeaways

- Meta-analysis of 22 studies, 1,356 participants total.
- Peripheral tryptophan was lower in AD.
- Peripheral kynurenine-to-tryptophan ratio was higher in AD.
- CSF 3-hydroxykynurenine was lower in AD.
- CSF kynurenic acid was higher, while peripheral kynurenic acid was lower.
- Quinolinic acid and kynurenine were not consistently changed.
- Interpretation for the wiki: AD looks like a mixed central/peripheral TRYCAT shift, not a universal neurotoxic-quinolinic state.
