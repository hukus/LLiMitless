# [Dose-dependent effects of cortexin in chronic cerebral ischemia (results of a multicenter randomized controlled study)]

## Summary

This multicenter randomized study tested cortexin in chronic cerebral ischemia with 10 mg and 20 mg arms against baseline treatment. The paper is clinically relevant for the peptide cluster, but it still sits in a regional ischemia/recovery lane rather than a modern cognition-enhancement lane.
