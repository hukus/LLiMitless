# MIDAS DHA age-related cognitive decline RCT

Randomized double-blind placebo-controlled multicenter trial at 19 US sites testing DHA in healthy older adults with age-related cognitive decline. The study randomized 485 adults aged 55 or older with MMSE above 26 and Logical Memory at least 1 standard deviation below younger-adult norms.

Intervention: 900 mg/day DHA orally for 24 weeks versus matching placebo. Primary outcome was CANTAB Paired Associate Learning, a visuospatial learning and episodic-memory test.

Results: intention-to-treat analysis found fewer PAL six-pattern errors with DHA at 24 weeks (difference score about -1.63, 95% CI -3.1 to -0.14, p = 0.03). DHA also improved immediate and delayed Verbal Recognition Memory scores, but not working memory or executive-function tests. Plasma DHA approximately doubled and correlated with PAL improvement. DHA was well tolerated with no reported treatment-related serious adverse events.

Use in wiki: this is the main positive single-ingredient DHA cognition RCT. It is memory/learning-specific and ARCD-enriched rather than a broad healthy-adult nootropic result.
