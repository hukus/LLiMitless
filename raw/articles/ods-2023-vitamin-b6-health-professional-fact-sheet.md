# Vitamin B6 fact sheet for health professionals

- Publisher: NIH Office of Dietary Supplements

- Checked: 2026-05-03

## Summary

ODS describes vitamin B6 as six vitamers with PLP and PMP as active coenzyme forms. B6 is involved in more than 100 enzyme reactions, amino-acid metabolism, one-carbon metabolism, neurotransmitter biosynthesis, immune function, hemoglobin formation, and maintaining normal homocysteine levels. Plasma PLP is the most common status marker.

## Safety Notes

- The US adult tolerable upper intake level is 100 mg/day, while EFSA's 2023 adult upper limit is 12 mg/day.
- Chronic high-dose pyridoxine can cause severe sensory neuropathy with ataxia; other adverse effects include dermatological lesions, photosensitivity, nausea, and heartburn.
- B6 is a meaningful safety gate in high-dose B-complex or homocysteine-lowering stacks even when folate/B12 dominate the cognition story.
