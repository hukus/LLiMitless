# Long-term resveratrol cognition and cerebrovascular trial in postmenopausal women

- Source type: PubMed-indexed randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled crossover trial abstract / Clinical Nutrition record.
- Publication: Clinical Nutrition, 2021.
- DOI: 10.1016/j.clnu.2020.08.025.
- Date checked: 2026-05-03.

The RESHAW trial tested low-dose trans-resveratrol in healthy postmenopausal women. In the full 24-month crossover analysis, 125 women aged 45-85 completed 12 months of 75 mg trans-resveratrol twice daily and 12 months of placebo in alternating order.

Compared with placebo, resveratrol improved overall cognitive performance with a small effect size and improved cerebrovascular secondary measures: resting middle-cerebral-artery blood-flow velocity, cerebrovascular responsiveness to hypercapnia, cerebrovascular responsiveness to cognitive stimuli, fasting insulin, and insulin-resistance index. The older subgroup, especially women at least 65 years old, showed verbal-memory improvement.

Use in the wiki: strongest positive resveratrol cognition anchor, but phenotype-specific. It supports a low vascular/metabolic/postmenopausal signal rather than broad nootropic efficacy.
