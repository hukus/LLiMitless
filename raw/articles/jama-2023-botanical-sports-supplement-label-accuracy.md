# Botanical sports supplement label accuracy case series

2023 JAMA Network Open case series by Cohen and colleagues testing 57 sports supplements labeled as containing Rauwolfia vomitoria, methylliberine, turkesterone, halostachine, or octopamine. The paper frames octopamine as a norepinephrine-like botanical ingredient promoted for stimulant or anabolic effects.

Twenty-three of 57 products did not contain a detectable amount of the labeled ingredient. Among products with detectable labeled ingredients, actual quantity ranged from 0.02% to 334% of the labeled quantity, and only 6 of 57 were within 10% of the labeled amount. The table included octopamine-labeled products, some with no detectable octopamine and others with measured quantities near or above labeled amounts.
