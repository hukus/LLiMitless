# Lion's Mane Mushroom (Hericium erinaceus): A Neuroprotective Fungus with Antioxidant, Anti-Inflammatory, and Antimicrobial Potential-A Narrative Review

- PMID: 40284172
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40284172/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

2025 narrative review of Hericium erinaceus positioning lion's mane as a neuroprotective functional food. It emphasizes polysaccharides, hericenones, erinacines, antioxidant and anti-inflammatory mechanisms, and NGF-related biology, but it also says clinical validation remains limited and larger, standardized trials are still needed. Useful for mechanism mapping, not as new human proof.
