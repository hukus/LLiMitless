# Results of the Solriamfetol's Effect on Cognitive Health in Apnea Participants During a Randomized Placebo-Controlled Study (SHARP)

- PMID: 39528111
- DOI: 10.1016/j.chest.2024.10.050
- Journal: Chest (2025 Mar;167(3):863-875)
- Trial registration: NCT04789174; EudraCT 2020-004243-92

## Captured takeaways

- Phase IV, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled crossover study in adults with obstructive sleep apnea, excessive daytime sleepiness, and cognitive impairment.
- 59 participants were randomized to placebo or solriamfetol 75 mg/day for 3 days then 150 mg/day for 2 weeks, with a 1-week washout between periods.
- Primary endpoint: average post-dose Coding/DSST-like RBANS score.
- Solriamfetol improved the primary objective cognition endpoint versus placebo (P = .009; Cohen's d = 0.37).
- Benefits were time-locked: significant at 2, 6, and 8 hours post-dose on the repeated timepoint analysis.
- Subjective cognition and sleepiness also improved: BC-CCI (P = .002; d = 0.45), PGI-S (P = .034; d = 0.29), and ESS (P = .004; d = 0.40).
- Most common treatment-emergent adverse events were nausea (7%) and anxiety (3%).
- Interpretation for the wiki: this is disorder-context wakefulness/cognition evidence, not healthy-adult enhancement evidence.
