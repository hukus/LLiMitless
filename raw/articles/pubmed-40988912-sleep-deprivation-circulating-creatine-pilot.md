# Sleep deprivation elevates circulating creatine levels in healthy adults: a pilot study

- PMID: 40988912
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40988912/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

2025 pilot study of 23 healthy adults after 24 h sleep deprivation found serum creatine rose significantly post-deprivation, while guanidinoacetic acid and creatinine remained unchanged. This suggests sleep loss itself perturbs creatine metabolism and may complicate simple assumptions about baseline deficiency versus stress response.

## Notes

Biomarker-only study; no cognitive outcomes were reported.
