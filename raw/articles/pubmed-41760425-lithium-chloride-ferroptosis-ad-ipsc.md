# Lithium chloride suppresses ferroptosis of induced pluripotent stem cells with ApoE4/E4 from a sporadic Alzheimer's disease patient

- PMID: 41760425
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41760425/
- Ingested: 2026-04-30

## Summary

This 2026 Neurotherapeutics paper is a mechanistic, preclinical AD study in human iPSCs derived from a sporadic AD patient with ApoE4/E4, compared with isogenic ApoE3/E3 controls. Lithium chloride improved cell viability and reduced ferroptosis-related phenotypes, including iron dysregulation, lipid peroxidation, ROS, GPX4 suppression, and mitochondrial stress, while also lowering InsP3R-1 expression. The paper supports an AD-relevant mechanistic hypothesis for lithium, but it does not test cognition in humans and should not be read as a self-dosing recommendation.
