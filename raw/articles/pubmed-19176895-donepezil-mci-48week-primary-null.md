# Donepezil in MCI 48-week randomized trial

Forty-eight-week multicenter randomized placebo-controlled trial of donepezil in mild cognitive impairment. Participants completed a placebo run-in, then received donepezil 5 mg/day for 6 weeks followed by 10 mg/day for 42 weeks, or placebo.

The captured PubMed abstract reports the study as another major MCI test of donepezil. In the wiki this source is used conservatively as part of the MCI counterweight: longer MCI trials do not turn donepezil into a general healthy-adult enhancer, and any signal must be kept separate from the healthy-adult and sleep-loss evidence.

Interpretation for the wiki: chronic MCI exposure belongs in the clinical-state bucket, not the healthy-adult nootropic bucket.
