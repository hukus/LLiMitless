# Lutein and zeaxanthin cognition RCT in younger healthy adults

- Source type: randomized double-masked placebo-controlled trial.
- Publication: Nutrients, 2017.
- Date checked: 2026-05-11.

This trial randomized 51 healthy adults aged 18-30 as part of a larger xanthophyll / cognition study. The active arm received lutein plus zeaxanthin supplementation for 12 months, and cognition was measured with CNS Vital Signs every 4 months. Macular pigment optical density was used as a retinal / CNS xanthophyll exposure marker.

The active supplement increased MPOD versus placebo. Cognitive results were selective rather than global: spatial / visual memory, reasoning, and complex attention improved beyond practice-effect estimates, especially among participants whose MPOD increased by at least 0.10.

Use in the wiki: this is a direct healthy-young-adult cognition anchor for [[lutein-zeaxanthin]]. The signal should remain low-confidence because the sample was small, randomization was imbalanced, endpoints were selective, and the intervention needs a long exposure window rather than an acute nootropic readout.
