# PubChem 2026: Pramiracetam identity

PubChem identifies pramiracetam as CID 51712, molecular formula C14H27N3O2, molecular weight 269.38 g/mol, and synonyms including pramiracetam, Pramiracetamum, CI-879 free base, and N-[2-[bis(1-methylethyl)amino]ethyl]-2-oxo-1-pyrrolidineacetamide.

Status and pharmacology framing from source summaries: pramiracetam has been previously approved in some eastern European countries under brand names including Pramistar, Neupramir, and Remen. It has also had U.S. orphan-drug designation historically. PubChem-linked hazard data list acute oral toxicity classification H302 from ECHA notifications, which is a chemical-safety flag rather than a clinical adverse-event estimate.

Interpretation for the wiki: pramiracetam is a distinct racetam-class molecule and should not be collapsed into piracetam, oxiracetam, aniracetam, noopept, or generic racetam-stack anecdotes.
