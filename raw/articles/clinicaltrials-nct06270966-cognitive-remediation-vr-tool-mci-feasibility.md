# Cognitive Remediation VR Tool for People With MCI: a Feasibility RCT

- ClinicalTrials.gov ID: NCT06270966
- Study page: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06270966
- Ingested: 2026-04-29

## Summary

Feasibility randomized controlled trial of a fully immersive VR cognitive remediation program in 30 adults over 65 with mild cognitive impairment, versus treatment as usual. The intervention runs 3 months with two 60-minute sessions per week.

The record frames tolerability, dropout, acceptability, and side effects as the key feasibility outcomes, with cognitive function, quality of life, biological and social rhythms, anxiety, and depressive symptoms as preliminary effectiveness measures.

## Notes

This is a useful delivery-validation anchor for VR cognitive remediation in MCI because it makes feasibility, rather than efficacy, the first gate.
