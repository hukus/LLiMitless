# Controlled trial of lazabemide in untreated Parkinson disease

- PMID: 8489205
- DOI: 10.1002/ana.410330404
- Journal: Annals of Neurology (1993)
- Design: randomized, double-blind clinical trial.

## Captured takeaways

- Lazabemide / RO19-6327 is described as a short-acting, reversible, highly selective MAO-B inhibitor and unlike deprenyl/selegiline is not metabolized to active compounds.
- The study randomized 201 early untreated Parkinson disease patients at 14 centers to lazabemide 100, 200, or 400 mg/day or placebo.
- Follow-up lasted 8 weeks, including randomized double-blind withdrawal for 2 or 4 weeks.
- The primary measure was tolerability: the proportion completing the study on assigned treatment.
- Lazabemide was as well tolerated as placebo and had no serious adverse experiences in this short trial.
- Activities of daily living improved significantly after 4 weeks, but other UPDRS subscales did not change significantly.
- Interpretation for the wiki: lazabemide is a legacy reversible MAO-B comparator with PD motor/ADL context, not a cognition or brain-fog enhancer.
