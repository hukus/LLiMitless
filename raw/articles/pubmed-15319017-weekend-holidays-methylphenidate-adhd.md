# Weekend holidays during methylphenidate use in ADHD children: a randomized clinical trial

- PMID: 15319017
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15319017/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

In a 28-day double-blind study of 40 boys with ADHD, weekday-only methylphenidate with weekend placebo reduced insomnia and trended toward less appetite interference, without a significant symptom rebound on weekends or the first school day after them. This is the cleanest human drug-holiday signal in the backlog.

## Notes

The study supports schedule breaks mainly as a tolerability strategy. It does not prove anti-tolerance in healthy cognitive enhancement, but it shows short drug holidays can be feasible without obvious immediate loss of benefit in a pediatric ADHD setting.
