# FDA consumer alert on regenerative medicine products including stem cells and exosomes

- Date: 2020-07-22
- Source: https://www.fda.gov/vaccines-blood-biologics/consumers-biologics/consumer-alert-regenerative-medicine-products-including-stem-cells-and-exosomes

## Key points

FDA says there are no FDA-approved exosome products and warns that stem cell and exosome marketing can mislead patients about unproven disease claims. The alert explicitly says these products are not approved for neurological disorders including Alzheimer's disease.

## Research-use read

This is a direct regulatory safety source. It does not address efficacy, but it is the cleanest consumer-facing warning that the branch should stay safety-first.
