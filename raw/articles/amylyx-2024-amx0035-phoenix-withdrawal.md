# Amylyx 2024 AMX0035 PHOENIX withdrawal note

Amylyx announced on April 4, 2024 that, based on topline results from the phase 3 PHOENIX trial of AMX0035 in ALS, it had started a process with FDA and Health Canada to voluntarily discontinue marketing authorizations for RELYVRIO / ALBRIOZA and remove the product from the U.S. and Canadian markets.

The announcement identifies RELYVRIO / ALBRIOZA as sodium phenylbutyrate plus taurursodiol, also known as AMX0035. It says the product would no longer be available for new patients as of April 4, 2024, while existing patients could transition to a free-drug program if they and their physicians wished to continue.

Amylyx describes AMX0035 as a fixed oral combination designed to target endoplasmic-reticulum stress and mitochondrial dysfunction. The company said the withdrawal decision was informed by PHOENIX results, regulatory engagement, and discussions with the ALS community.
