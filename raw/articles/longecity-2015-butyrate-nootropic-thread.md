# LongeCity butyrate nootropic and bioavailability thread

- Source type: LongeCity forum anecdote / mechanistic speculation thread
- Thread started: 2015
- Checked: 2026-05-11

## Summary

This older forum thread treats butyrate as a possible nootropic, metabolic, epigenetic, and gut-health compound. The useful wiki signal is not efficacy; it is the shape of community reasoning. Participants discuss resistant starch versus direct butyrate, butter/food sources, tributyrin as a potentially better-tolerated or more bioavailable route, HDAC inhibition, BDNF, TET/methylation speculation, and practical taste/smell issues.

The anecdote layer is mixed. One participant reported that magnesium butyrate reduced alcohol craving without clear cognitive testing. Another reported no mental or gastrointestinal effect even at multigram sodium-butyrate doses. The thread also highlights the recurring bioavailability concern: direct butyrate may be rapidly metabolized and may not behave like colon-delivered SCFAs or tributyrin.

## Notes

- Use this as an older nootropic-community context source, not formal evidence.
- The thread is especially useful for separating mechanism enthusiasm from measured human cognition.
