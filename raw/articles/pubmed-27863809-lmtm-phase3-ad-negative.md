# Efficacy and safety of tau-aggregation inhibitor therapy in patients with mild or moderate Alzheimer's disease: a randomised, controlled, double-blind, parallel-arm, phase 3 trial

- PMID: 27863809
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27863809/
- Ingested: 2026-04-29

## Summary

Large phase 3 randomized trial of leuco-methylthioninium bis(hydromethanesulfonate) in 891 people with mild to moderate Alzheimer's disease across 115 sites in 16 countries. Participants received 75 mg twice daily, 125 mg twice daily, or a 4 mg twice daily control meant to preserve masking. The prespecified primary analyses were negative for both coprimary outcomes, and gastrointestinal plus urinary adverse effects were the most common. Small dose-dependent hemoglobin reductions were the main lab abnormality.

## Notes

- Trial registration: NCT01689246 and EudraCT 2012-002866-11.
- Coprimary outcomes: ADAS-Cog and ADCS-ADL at week 65.
- The paper explicitly notes exclusion of concomitant medicines carrying warnings for methaemoglobinaemia.
