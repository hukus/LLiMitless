# DailyMed rivastigmine transdermal-system label safety context

DailyMed rivastigmine transdermal-system label, revised October 2024 and shown as updated January 2025. Rivastigmine is an acetylcholinesterase inhibitor indicated for mild, moderate, and severe dementia of the Alzheimer's type and mild-to-moderate dementia associated with Parkinson's disease.

Key safety issues include serious medication errors from wearing multiple patches at the same time, with hospitalization and rare deaths reported; significant nausea, vomiting, diarrhea, anorexia / decreased appetite, weight loss, dehydration risk, and treatment interruption; allergic contact dermatitis and other application-site reactions; and interaction cautions with metoclopramide, beta-blockers, cholinomimetics, and anticholinergic medications.

Interpretation for the wiki: rivastigmine is a prescription dementia / Parkinson's-dementia drug. The patch route reduces some dosing complexity but adds medication-error and skin-reaction risks that matter for off-label cognition claims.
