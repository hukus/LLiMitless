# Thymosin beta 4 reverses phenotypic polarization of glial cells and cognitive impairment via negative regulation of NF-κB signaling axis in APP/PS1 mice

- PMID: 34183019
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34183019/
- Ingested: 2026-04-30

## Summary

2021 APP/PS1 mouse study. Tβ4 overexpression alleviated AD-like phenotypes, reduced amyloid accumulation, shifted glia away from inflammatory phenotypes, improved neuronal function and cognitive behavior, and unexpectedly showed an antidepressant-like effect. The mechanism was linked to TLR4/MyD88/NF-κB p65 and p52 signaling, but this is still preclinical and does not establish human cognition benefit.
