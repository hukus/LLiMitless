# Sodium Lactate in Severe TBI

- ClinicalTrials.gov ID: NCT06701760
- Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06701760
- Ingested: 2026-04-30

## Summary

Not-yet-recruiting phase 2 safety study of intravenous sodium lactate in severe traumatic brain injury. The record frames sodium lactate as a proof-of-safety and biomarker-response intervention in the ICU setting, with a stated goal of improving brain metabolism and preventing ongoing energy failure after injury. This is not a cognition-outcome trial, but it is a current freshness signal for the lactate branch.
