# Lipidomic Analysis of Plasma Extracellular Vesicles Derived from Alzheimer's Disease Patients

- PMID: 38667317
- PMCID: PMC11049154
- Source: PubMed / Cells 2024
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

Targeted lipidomics study of plasma extracellular vesicles from AD patients and healthy controls after lipoprotein removal. The paper reports group differences across lipid families and EV subtypes, supporting the broader idea that AD is associated with altered circulating lipid biology. The study is biomarker-oriented rather than interventional.

## Notes

- Supports lipidomic remodeling in AD, but not plasmalogen replacement efficacy.
- Useful for the "deficiency signal exists" side of the branch.
- The EV methodology makes it a biomarker paper rather than a direct treatment paper.
