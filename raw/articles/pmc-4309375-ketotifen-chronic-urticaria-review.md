# Ketotifen in chronic urticaria review

- PMID: 24267353
- PMCID: PMC4309375
- Published: 2013
- Source type: review with older clinical-study summary
- Checked: 2026-05-11
- Ingested: 2026-05-11

## Summary

This review frames oral ketotifen as an antiallergic drug with H1-antihistamine and mast-cell/basophil mediator-inhibition properties. It summarizes older urticaria studies and notes that ketotifen may take 4 to 6 weeks for full prophylactic value in some conditions.

The review reports a retrospective chronic urticaria experience in which 18 of 24 patients improved after starting ketotifen and 22 of 24 reported that ketotifen made symptoms better. Sedation was the most frequent side effect. The source is relevant for mast-cell and allergic-disease routing but does not provide brain-fog or healthy-adult cognition evidence.

## Interpretation

Use in the wiki: formal background for [[ketotifen]] as an oral mast-cell / H1 candidate. It should be treated as low-confidence and symptom-state evidence, not as a direct cognitive intervention.
