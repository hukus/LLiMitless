# WHO-UMC standardized case causality assessment

- WHO notes that most suspected ADRs are not "certain" or "unlikely"; many remain in the middle categories.
- The document emphasizes that rechallenge is rarely ethically justified in routine pharmacovigilance.
- Relevance for this wiki: a diary should collect enough structured evidence for later possible/probable/unlikely review without assuming a rechallenge will be available.
