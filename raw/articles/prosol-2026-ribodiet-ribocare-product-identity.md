# Prosol RiboDiet And RiboCARE Product Identity

- Primary source: https://www.prosol.it/ribodiet/
- Related source: https://www.prosol.it/ribocare/
- Source type: manufacturer product pages.
- Date checked: 2026-05-03.

## Summary

Prosol describes RiboDiet as a human-nutrition nucleotide ingredient extracted from yeast cells through a standardized, highly controlled process that is free of organic solvents. The page describes it as a source of nucleotides, nucleosides, oligonucleotides, ribonucleic-acid fragments, amino acids, minerals, and B vitamins. The suggested dose range shown on the product page is 50-350 mg/day, and the listed applications include capsules, tablets, sachets, and liquids.

The RiboCARE page describes the RiboCARE brand as individual natural nucleotides and customized premixes for infant milk or dietary supplements. It lists 5'-AMP, 5'-CMP, 5'-UMP, 5'-GMP, and 5'-IMP forms, and says customized premixes are manufactured in high-care clean rooms under strict procedures intended to guarantee safety standards and profile accuracy.

Product-identity interpretation: the 2025 dietary-nucleotide RCT is unusually transportable for a nutraceutical because it names RiboDiet and RiboCARE Neuro and gives daily doses. Still, RiboDiet is a yeast-extract nucleotide pool while RiboCARE Neuro is a defined monophosphate-nucleotide blend, so they should not be collapsed into generic "nucleotides" or into a single pure molecule.
