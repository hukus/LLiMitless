ClinicalTrials.gov record for SHAPE, a recruiting Phase 2a open-label single-arm pilot in healthy older adults ages 65 to 80 using daily subcutaneous elamipretide for 4 weeks. The primary objective is safety and tolerability. Secondary objectives include inflammatory biomarkers (BDNF, VEGF, IGF-1, IL-6, TNF-a), 6-minute walk performance, cognitive function, and skeletal muscle strength. The record lists a study start date of 2025-11-26, estimated primary completion on 2026-03-15, estimated completion on 2026-04-15, and an estimated enrollment of 30.
