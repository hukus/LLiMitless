## Summary

2025 review summarizing BPC-157 literature, patents, mechanisms, and toxicity framing. The abstract presents BPC-157 as a pleiotropic peptide with broad preclinical activity across tissue injury, bowel disease, and CNS-adjacent models, while also noting the lack of sufficient clinical studies for regulatory approval.

## Key points

- The review says BPC-157 showed beneficial effects across many preclinical models and has generated enough interest to sustain a large patent and gray-market presence.
- It describes the safety profile in the literature as relatively favorable in the preclinical record, but still insufficient for FDA or other mainstream approvals because comprehensive human studies are missing.
- It explicitly notes that BPC-157 is offered for sale on many websites, which makes mechanism and toxicity questions practically relevant rather than academic.

## Signal Use

- Formal signal: useful high-level support that BPC-157 is not just a single-use tendon anecdote; the biology and claimed use cases are broad.
- Caveat: the same review underscores the huge human-trial gap, so it does not justify a clinical-efficacy upgrade.
- Cognition signal: CNS relevance remains preclinical / indirect in this source.
