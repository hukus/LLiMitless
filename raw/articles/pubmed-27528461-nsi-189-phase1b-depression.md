# NSI-189 Phase 1B Placebo-Controlled Depression Study

- PMID: 27528461
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27528461/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

This randomized placebo-controlled study is the main human anchor for NSI-189 in the wiki. It is a depression-development study, not a healthy-adult enhancement trial, and the cognitive material is secondary to the antidepressant program. The source is still useful because it keeps NSI-189 out of the "mystery brain repair" bucket and into the more constrained translational antidepressant lane.

## Notes

- Source type: human clinical trial.
- Evidence tier: human randomized trial, but disease-context and not cognition-specific.
- Claimed domain: depression symptoms with some secondary cognitive subtests.
- Interpretation: no broad healthy-enhancement proof surfaced from this source.
- Safety / status: investigational compound; forum enthusiasm should be treated as anecdote only.
