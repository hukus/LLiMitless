# Vinpocetine in Dietary Supplements

- Source: FDA
- Page: https://www.fda.gov/food/information-select-dietary-supplement-ingredients-and-other-substances/vinpocetine-dietary-supplements
- Ingested: 2026-05-03

## Summary

FDA describes vinpocetine as a synthetically produced compound derived from vincamine chemistry rather than a natural plant constituent. The page notes that products containing vinpocetine are marketed for brain function, energy, visual acuity, memory, and focus, but FDA has not evaluated whether vinpocetine is effective for those uses. FDA also states that it issued a 2019 safety warning for pregnant women and women who could become pregnant because data reviewed by FDA associated vinpocetine with adverse reproductive effects, including possible miscarriage or fetal-development harm.

## Interpretation

Use this as the regulatory and safety anchor for vinpocetine. The nootropic-memory marketing signal should be downgraded by the synthetic-ingredient status, pregnancy warning, and lack of FDA efficacy evaluation.
