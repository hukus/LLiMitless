# PubMed 41543854 berberine obesity and MASLD RCT note

This 2026 JAMA Network Open multicenter double-blind randomized clinical trial enrolled 337 diabetes-free adults with obesity and metabolic dysfunction-associated steatotic liver disease at 11 hospitals in China. Participants received oral berberine 1 g/day or matching placebo for 6 months.

The primary outcomes were relative change in visceral adipose tissue area and absolute change in liver fat content by computed tomography. Berberine did not significantly reduce either primary outcome. Secondary signals favored berberine for LDL cholesterol, apolipoprotein B, and high-sensitivity CRP, while adverse-event incidence was similar between groups.

Interpretation for the wiki: this is a useful counterweight to overbroad metabolic and weight-loss claims. Berberine can still have lipid/inflammation or glucose-related signals, but the 2026 obesity/MASLD trial argues against treating it as a reliable body-fat or liver-fat intervention in diabetes-free adults, and it did not test cognition.
