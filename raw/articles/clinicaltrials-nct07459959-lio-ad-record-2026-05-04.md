# LiO-AD lithium orotate feasibility registry record snapshot

- ClinicalTrials.gov ID: NCT07459959
- Official title: LiO-AD: Lithium Orotate in Alzheimers Disease Feasibility, Biomarker Engagement, and Clinical Response
- Sponsor: Johns Hopkins University
- Collaborator: National Institutes of Health
- Date checked: 2026-05-04

## Summary

The ClinicalTrials.gov record remains a not-yet-recruiting phase 1/2 lithium-orotate feasibility, safety, tolerability, and target-engagement study in adults with biomarker-confirmed early Alzheimer's disease. The record lists no posted results, estimated enrollment of 40, estimated study start 2026-06-01, estimated primary completion 2029-06-01, estimated study completion 2029-08-31, and last update posted 2026-03-10.

The active arm uses oral lithium orotate capsules titrated from 240 mg/day in week 1 to 480 mg/day in week 2 and 720 mg/day in week 3 if tolerated, with option to down-titrate for side effects. Primary outcomes are feasibility, adherence, safety adverse events, renal function, thyroid function, tolerability dose modification, and discontinuation. Secondary outcomes include CSF lithium target engagement, CSF neurofilament light chain, NPI-Q, and delayed recall.

## Safety And Interpretation

The record excludes clinically significant kidney or thyroid problems, thiazide diuretics during dosing unless stopped before baseline, chronic daily non-aspirin NSAID use, new anti-amyloid monoclonal antibody treatment during active treatment, and unstable neurologic or psychiatric illness. This remains a protocol and target-engagement signal, not efficacy evidence for lithium orotate as a cognition intervention.
