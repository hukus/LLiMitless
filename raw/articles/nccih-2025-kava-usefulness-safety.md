# NCCIH Kava Usefulness and Safety

NCCIH kava information page, last updated April 2025. The page states that kava has a fair amount of human research for anxiety, few studies for other conditions, and substantial investigation of rare but sometimes severe liver injury.

NCCIH summarizes that kava supplements may help anxiety but may require several weeks, do not appear helpful for generalized anxiety disorder, and lack enough evidence for other conditions. Safety concerns include rare serious or fatal liver injury, digestive upset, headache, dizziness, dermopathy with high-dose long-term use, sedative interactions, and pregnancy/breastfeeding risks.

Relevance: kava should be treated as an anxiolytic/sedative-adjacent branch with liver and interaction constraints, not a direct cognitive enhancer.
