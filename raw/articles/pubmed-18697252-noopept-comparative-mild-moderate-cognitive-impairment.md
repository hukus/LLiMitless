# A comparative study of noopept and piracetam in the treatment of mild and moderate cognitive impairment in patients with organic brain diseases of vascular and traumatic origin

- PMID: 18697252
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18697252/
- Ingested: 2026-04-30

## Summary

This 2008 Russian comparative study is one of the few human noopept sources that sits above the pure-mechanism layer. The PubMed record has no abstract, but the title and indexing place it in mild to moderate cognitive impairment from vascular and traumatic brain disease. It supports the branch as phenotype-bound and comparator-dependent, not as a modern healthy-adult enhancer.
