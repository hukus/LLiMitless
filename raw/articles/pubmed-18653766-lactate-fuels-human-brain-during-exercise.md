# Lactate fuels the human brain during exercise

- PMID: 18653766
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18653766/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Review of human and physiologic data arguing that the brain can take up lactate during exercise and other activation states, with lactate partly substituting for glucose as an oxidation substrate.
