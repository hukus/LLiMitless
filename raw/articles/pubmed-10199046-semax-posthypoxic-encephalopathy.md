# [Use of semax at a follow-up of patients with posthypoxic encephalopathy]

- PMID: 10199046
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10199046/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Older human follow-up study in 73 patients with remote posthypoxic encephalopathy, including a subgroup in persistent vegetative state. It supports Semax as a recovery-oriented neuropeptide in severe neurologic injury contexts, not as a healthy-adult enhancer.
