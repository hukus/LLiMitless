# Dietary blueberry improves cognition among older adults in a randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial

- Source type: randomized double-blind placebo-controlled trial.
- Publication: European Journal of Nutrition, 2018.
- Date checked: 2026-05-03.

This trial recruited 37 adults aged 60-75 years and randomized them to 90 days of freeze-dried blueberry powder or blueberry placebo. The blueberry dose was 24 g/day, roughly equivalent to 1 cup of fresh blueberries.

The study reported supplement group by visit interactions on executive-function measures: fewer repetition errors on the California Verbal Learning Test and reduced switch cost on a task-switching test. Gait and balance did not improve.

Use in the wiki: healthy-older-adult food-dose blueberry anchor. The signal is selected-domain cognition, not broad motor or whole-cognition enhancement.
