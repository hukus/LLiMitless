# Undercarboxylated osteocalcin is associated with cognition in women with and without HIV

- PMID: 36094482
- DOI: 10.1097/QAI.0000000000003043
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36094482/
- Ingested: 2026-05-04

## Summary

Brief JAIDS 2022 human biomarker study in midlife women with HIV and demographically similar HIV-seronegative women. Plasma undercarboxylated osteocalcin (ucOCN) and sclerostin were related to a longitudinal neuropsychological battery. Neither biomarker predicted cognitive change over time. ucOCN was positively associated with executive function in the full sample and in women living with HIV, positively associated with motor skills in women living with HIV, and negatively associated with attention/working memory in the full sample; sclerostin was not significantly associated with performance. This supports osteocalcin as a human association signal, not a treatment effect.
