# Pomegranate Juice Augments Memory and fMRI Activity in Middle-Aged and Older Adults with Mild Memory Complaints

- PMCID: PMC3736548
- PMID: 23970941
- Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3736548/
- Ingested: 2026-05-03

## Summary

Preliminary randomized, placebo-controlled, double-blind trial in 32 middle-aged and older adults with mild memory complaints; 28 completed the study. Participants drank 8 oz/day pomegranate juice or a flavor-matched placebo for 4 weeks.

The pomegranate group improved on the Buschke selective reminding verbal-memory measure and showed increased plasma antioxidant capacity and urolithin A-glucuronide. Compared with placebo, the pomegranate group also showed greater task-related fMRI activity during verbal and visual memory tasks.

Use in wiki: direct but small juice-specific memory and fMRI signal. This supports pomegranate as a low-confidence product-specific cognition candidate, not as evidence that every pomegranate extract or supplement is interchangeable with juice.
