## Summary

The oral ketotifen monograph lists adverse effects and overdose symptoms relevant to self-experimentation and MCAS discussions. More common or notable effects include weight gain, drowsiness, dry mouth, increased appetite, irritability, nervousness, trouble sleeping, unusual tiredness or weakness, dizziness, headache, GI symptoms, and rare severe reactions such as convulsions or liver-related signs.

Overdose symptoms listed include blurred vision, confusion, convulsions, disorientation, severe drowsiness, faintness or lightheadedness on standing, fast or irregular heartbeat, hyperexcitability, loss of consciousness, palpitations, sweating, and unusual tiredness or weakness.
