# NCCIH Bitter Orange

NCCIH page on bitter orange / Citrus aurantium. Bitter orange contains p-synephrine and is promoted for weight loss, sports performance, energy, and related uses. NCCIH notes that synephrine is structurally similar to ephedrine but pharmacologically distinct, and that NCAA lists synephrine as a banned stimulant.

NCCIH concludes bitter orange has not been clearly shown helpful for any health-related purpose, evidence on cardiovascular effects is inconclusive, and FDA testing found label inaccuracies and adulteration with synthetic amines in some products.

Relevance: bitter orange / synephrine belongs in a sports-visibility and stimulant-safety branch, not a cognition-positive branch.
