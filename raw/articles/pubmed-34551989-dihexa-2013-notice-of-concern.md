# Notice of Concern for McCoy et al. 2013 Dihexa JPET paper

PubMed indexes a 2021 Journal of Pharmacology and Experimental Therapeutics Notice of Concern for McCoy et al. 2013, "Evaluation of Metabolically Stabilized Angiotensin IV Analogs as Procognitive/Antidementia Agents" (DOI 10.1124/jpet.112.199497; PMID 23055539). The notice matters for Dihexa because that 2013 paper is the branch's main source for oral and intraperitoneal rodent procognitive efficacy.

Use in the wiki: keep the oral rodent efficacy signal visible, but mark it with evidence-integrity caution. It should not be treated as clean clinical proof or as a validated human route comparison.
