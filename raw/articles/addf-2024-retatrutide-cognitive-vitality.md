# ADDF Cognitive Vitality 2024 retatrutide report

Alzheimer's Drug Discovery Foundation Cognitive Vitality report last updated 2024-11-18.

## Captured takeaways

- The report frames retatrutide as a triple GIP/GLP-1/glucagon receptor agonist in development for type 2 diabetes, obesity, and MASLD.
- It states that retatrutide has a theoretical neuroprotection rationale, especially in metabolic disease, because related GLP-1 biology has dementia and brain-health hypotheses.
- It states that no clinical or observational studies had assessed whether retatrutide prevents dementia or cognitive decline or improves cognitive function.
- It also states that no clinical or observational studies had tested retatrutide in patients with dementia.
- Mechanistic discussion includes GLP-1, GIP, glucagon, insulin signaling, metabolic improvement, possible inflammatory effects, possible brain receptor expression, and uncertainty about whether retatrutide crosses the blood-brain barrier.
- Interpretation for the wiki: this is the cleanest formal cognition-oriented counterweight. Retatrutide has plausible brain-health mechanisms, but no direct human cognition evidence as of this report.
