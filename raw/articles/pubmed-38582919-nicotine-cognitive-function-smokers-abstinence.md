# An exploratory, randomised, crossover study to investigate the effect of nicotine on cognitive function in healthy adult smokers who use an electronic cigarette after a period of smoking abstinence

- PMID: 38582919
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38582919/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Exploratory crossover study in adult smokers after abstinence. The nicotine arm was aimed at separating withdrawal relief from direct cognitive effects, making it more relevant as a comparator for state restoration than for clean nootropic enhancement.
