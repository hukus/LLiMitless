# FDA warning letter to GenoGenix LLC on Epithalon labeling

- Content current as of: 2026-01-20
- Source: https://www.fda.gov/inspections-compliance-enforcement-and-criminal-investigations/warning-letters/genogenix-llc-718739-01202026

## Key points

FDA's warning letter to GenoGenix LLC cites Epithalon as an example in multiple compounded-product labeling deficiencies, including missing the "this is a compounded drug" statement, the outsourcing facility's name/address/phone number, dosage form, storage and handling instructions, the "Not for resale" / "Office Use Only" statement, and active/inactive ingredient listings. The letter also says the facility failed sterility-assurance and CGMP expectations and had already initiated a voluntary recall of sterile products within expiry.

## Research-use read

For the wiki, this is a compounding-quality and sourcing signal, not proof that Epithalon itself is intrinsically toxic. It does show that Epithalon appears in current FDA enforcement language as a real-world compounding product, with labeling and quality-control problems that make vendor branding a weak safety proxy.
