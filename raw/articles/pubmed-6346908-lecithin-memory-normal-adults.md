# Effect of lecithin on memory in normal adults

- PMID: 6346908
- DOI: 10.1176/ajp.140.8.1010
- Journal: American Journal of Psychiatry
- Year: 1983
- Ingested: 2026-05-04

## Summary

Double-blind, placebo-controlled crossover study in 9 normal paid volunteers aged 22-55. Participants took a single 20 g oral lecithin dose or placebo 5 hours before cognitive testing. Lecithin nearly doubled plasma choline compared with placebo, but categorized serial learning, word recognition, paired-associates learning, category retrieval, low-imagery word memory, and psychomotor speed did not significantly improve.

## Notes

- Use as a direct negative healthy-adult lecithin / phosphatidylcholine datapoint.
- The sample is tiny and old, so it lowers confidence in broad claims rather than proving absence of effect.
- Important interpretation: plasma choline movement did not translate into acute memory improvement.
