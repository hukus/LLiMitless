# The effect of hyperbaric oxygen on persistent postconcussion symptoms.

- PMID: 24052094
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24052094/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Sham-controlled military PCS trial with no evidence of efficacy on symptoms, cognitive measures, or behavioral sequelae.

## Notes

Sixty service members with at least one combat-related mTBI received 40 once-daily 60-minute chamber compressions at 2.0 ATA, with three blinded oxygen fractions that mapped to surface-air sham, 100% oxygen at 1.5 ATA, or 100% oxygen at 2.0 ATA. The abstract reports no significant time-by-intervention interaction for the RPQ-16 or for functional, cognitive, and psychomotor secondary outcomes.
