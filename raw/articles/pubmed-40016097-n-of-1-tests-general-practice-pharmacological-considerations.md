# N-of-1 Tests in General Practice: Pharmacological Considerations

- PMID: 40016097
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40016097/
- Ingested: 2026-04-30

## Summary

2025 practice-focused article on when N-of-1 testing is pharmacologically feasible. It treats N-of-1 as a randomized double-blind multi-period crossover in a single patient, then emphasizes seven feasibility criteria: onset of action, washout, titration, withdrawal symptoms, reversible efficacy, treatment identifiability, and galenic formulation.

The article explicitly notes that there is no fixed maximum period length. Period duration should be driven by half-life, time to efficacy, and clinical context.

## Notes

This is a strong template source for deciding whether a low-risk self-study fits a 30-day structure or needs longer periods.
