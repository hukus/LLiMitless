# Donepezil in MCI 24-week randomized trial

Twenty-four-week multicenter randomized double-blind placebo-controlled trial of donepezil in 270 patients with mild cognitive impairment. Donepezil was started at 5 mg/day for 42 days and escalated to 10 mg/day; primary outcomes included delayed paragraph recall and ADCS CGIC-MCI.

The primary efficacy measures did not show significant treatment effects in the intention-to-treat population. Some secondary measures favored donepezil, including ADAS-cog total score, attention / psychomotor-speed tests, and patient global assessment. Adverse events were more common with donepezil.

Interpretation for the wiki: MCI evidence is not a clean proof line for [[donepezil]] cognition enhancement. Primary-null, secondary-positive patterns should route through phenotype and endpoint-fragility pages.
