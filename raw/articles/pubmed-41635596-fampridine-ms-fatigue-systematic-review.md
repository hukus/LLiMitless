# The effects of fampridine on MS-related fatigue: a systematic review

- PMID: 41635596
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41635596/
- Ingested: 2026-04-30

## Summary

Systematic review of fampridine for fatigue in multiple sclerosis. The review found 33 eligible studies: observational and non-randomized reports were mostly positive, but only 3 of 13 randomized placebo-controlled studies showed benefit over placebo. The authors conclude that the randomized evidence is inconsistent and that better blinded trials are still needed.

## Notes

- Search covered PubMed, Scopus, EMBASE, Web of Science, Google Scholar, and ProQuest.
- The paper is helpful for separating placebo-prone fatigue reports from stronger randomized evidence.
- This supports keeping fampridine in a narrow symptom-restoration lane rather than a broad enhancer lane.
