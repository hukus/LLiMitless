# DailyMed midodrine hydrochloride label

## Captured takeaways

- DailyMed labels midodrine hydrochloride for symptomatic orthostatic hypotension, with the indication based primarily on increased 1-minute standing systolic blood pressure.
- The label states that clinical benefits such as improved ability to perform life activities had not been established in the cited label text, and that treatment should continue only for patients reporting significant symptomatic improvement.
- Contraindications include severe organic heart disease, acute renal disease, urinary retention, pheochromocytoma, thyrotoxicosis, and persistent excessive supine hypertension.
- The central safety issue is supine hypertension; the label emphasizes monitoring supine and sitting blood pressure and warns about cardiovascular-event risk from uncontrolled hypertension.
- Additional cautions include urinary retention, renal impairment, hepatic impairment, visual/glaucoma context with fludrocortisone, and concomitant agents that raise blood pressure or slow heart rate.
- Interpretation for the wiki: midodrine is relevant to low-BP / venous-pooling / orthostatic brain-fog phenotypes, but the safety frame is BP monitoring and supine-hypertension avoidance, not direct cognitive enhancement.
