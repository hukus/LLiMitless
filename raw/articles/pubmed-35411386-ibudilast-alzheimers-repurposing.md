# Ibudilast Alzheimer's disease repurposing model

2023 Brain paper using a multi-scale predictive modelling framework to screen drugs for Alzheimer's disease from patient tissue and systems pharmacology. The framework identified ibudilast as a repurposing candidate.

The abstract frames ibudilast as a multi-target drug through phosphodiesterase inhibition and TLR4 antagonism, with additional predicted off-target kinase effects. This is target-prioritization and preclinical/translational modelling, not human cognition efficacy.

Relevance: AD inflammation hypothesis source for [[ibudilast]].
