# FDA approval of Cobenfy for schizophrenia

- Source: https://www.fda.gov/news-events/press-announcements/fda-approves-drug-new-mechanism-action-treatment-schizophrenia
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

The FDA approved Cobenfy (xanomeline and trospium chloride) on 2024-09-26 for adult schizophrenia, making it the first schizophrenia drug in the US to target cholinergic receptors rather than dopamine receptors. The approval was based on two 5-week randomized placebo-controlled studies. The FDA highlighted common adverse effects of nausea, indigestion, constipation, vomiting, hypertension, abdominal pain, diarrhea, tachycardia, dizziness, and reflux, plus urinary retention, elevated heart rate, decreased gastric motility, angioedema, and liver/kidney restrictions in the label.
