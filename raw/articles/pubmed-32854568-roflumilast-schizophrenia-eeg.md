# The effects of roflumilast, a phosphodiesterase type-4 inhibitor, on EEG biomarkers in schizophrenia: A randomised controlled trial

- PMID: 32854568
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32854568/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

2021 randomized placebo-controlled crossover trial in schizophrenia. Roflumilast 250 micrograms improved mismatch negativity and working-memory-related theta oscillations, which supports target engagement at intermediate cognitive-processing stages rather than broad late-stage performance gains.

## Key points

- Design: 18 schizophrenia patients, placebo and two roflumilast doses.
- Outcome: EEG biomarkers plus auditory steady-state response and P300.
- Signal: mismatch negativity and theta oscillations improved at 250 micrograms.
- Caveat: the study did not show a broad, domain-general cognition win.
