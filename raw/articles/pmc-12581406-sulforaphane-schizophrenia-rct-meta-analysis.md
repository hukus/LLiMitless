# Sulforaphane schizophrenia RCT systematic review and meta-analysis

- Source type: open-access systematic review and meta-analysis of randomized controlled trials.
- Publication: BMC Psychiatry, 2025.
- PMID: 41184790.
- PMCID: PMC12581406.
- DOI: 10.1186/s12888-025-07515-7.
- Date checked: 2026-05-03.

This review searched through April 2025 and included four randomized controlled trials with 369 schizophrenia patients. Primary outcomes were PANSS total and subscales; secondary outcomes included cognitive measures, metabolic markers, discontinuation, and safety.

Main result: sulforaphane did not significantly improve PANSS total or positive symptoms at latest follow-up or 12 weeks. There was a modest 12-week negative-symptom signal that did not persist at latest follow-up, a general-psychopathology signal, metabolic improvements in LDL/triglycerides/cholesterol, and lower discontinuation in the sulforaphane group.

Cognition result: no pooled cognitive benefits were observed. MCCB total, spatial working memory, verbal learning, and reasoning/problem-solving were not significantly improved versus placebo.

Use in the wiki: high-value counterweight for sulforaphane cognition. It lowers confidence despite earlier secondary-domain signals in an individual early-episode trial.
