# Cognitive and Alzheimer's disease biomarker effects of oral nicotinamide riboside supplementation in older adults with subjective cognitive decline and mild cognitive impairment

- PMID: 39817194
- PMCID: PMC11733434
- DOI: 10.1002/trc2.70023
- Trial registry: NCT04078178
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39817194/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

2025 Alzheimer's & Dementia: Translational Research & Clinical Interventions trial of oral nicotinamide riboside in older adults with subjective cognitive decline and mild cognitive impairment. Forty-six participants older than 55 were randomized to NR-placebo or placebo-NR sequences; 37 completed the trial. Active treatment was 1 g/day NR for 8 weeks.

The primary efficacy outcome was RBANS. Secondary and exploratory outcomes included plasma pTau217, GFAP, NfL, Lumosity gameplay scores, and wearable step counts.

Result shape: safe and biomarker-positive but cognition-null. Adverse events did not differ between NR and placebo phases. Plasma pTau217 decreased with NR relative to placebo in both between-group and within-individual comparisons, but RBANS, other plasma biomarkers, Lumosity scores, and step counts did not show significant between-group cognitive or functional separation. The authors concluded that NR lowered pTau217 but did not alter cognition by conventional or digital assessments.

Use in the wiki: second completed human cognition anchor for [[nicotinamide-riboside]]. Together with PMID 37994989, this makes NR a clear biomarker-cognition mismatch rather than a nootropic win.
