# DailyMed XADAGO safinamide label

- Label source: DailyMed.
- Label update shown on page: 2025-10-22.
- Initial U.S. approval: 2017.
- Product class: MAO-B inhibitor.

## Captured takeaways

- XADAGO / safinamide is indicated as adjunctive treatment to levodopa/carbidopa in patients with Parkinson disease experiencing "off" episodes.
- Recommended starting dosage is 50 mg once daily at the same time of day; after two weeks, dose may increase to 100 mg once daily based on need and tolerability.
- Doses above 100 mg have not shown additional benefit and increase adverse-reaction risk.
- XADAGO is effective only in combination with levodopa/carbidopa in the label.
- Contraindications include other MAOIs or potent MAO inhibitors such as linezolid, opioid drugs including meperidine/methadone/propoxyphene/tramadol, SNRIs, tricyclic/tetracyclic/triazolopyridine antidepressants, cyclobenzaprine, methylphenidate, amphetamine and derivatives, St. John's wort, dextromethorphan, hypersensitivity, and severe hepatic impairment.
- Warnings include hypertension, serotonin syndrome, falling asleep during activities of daily living, dyskinesia, hallucinations/psychotic behavior, impulse-control/compulsive behaviors, withdrawal-emergent hyperpyrexia/confusion, and retinal pathology monitoring.
- The label states safinamide is selective for MAO-B at 50 or 100 mg daily and that selectivity decreases above recommended doses.
- Dietary tyramine restriction is not required at recommended doses, but the label advises avoiding very high tyramine amounts above 150 mg because severe hypertension can occur.
- Interpretation for the wiki: safinamide is a disease-state add-on PD drug with a broad interaction and dopaminergic-risk burden; nootropic forum use should be routed through safety-first comparator logic.
