# COVID-OUT metformin long-COVID prevention randomized trial

2023 Lancet Infectious Diseases COVID-OUT multicenter randomized quadruple-blind phase 3 trial testing early outpatient metformin, ivermectin, or fluvoxamine after acute SARS-CoV-2 infection, with post-COVID condition follow-up over 10 months.

The trial included 1,126 adults aged 30-85 with overweight or obesity, COVID-19 symptoms for fewer than seven days, and a documented positive SARS-CoV-2 test within three days before enrollment. Metformin was immediate-release and titrated over six days to 1,500 mg/day for a total 14-day course.

By day 300, long COVID diagnosis was reported by 6.3% of participants assigned metformin versus 10.4% assigned matched placebo, with hazard ratio 0.59. Starting metformin within three days of symptom onset was associated with a stronger effect estimate. Ivermectin and fluvoxamine did not reduce long-COVID incidence in this trial.

Interpretation for the wiki: this is a prevention signal during acute COVID, not evidence that metformin treats established long COVID or brain fog. It is still relevant because preventing post-COVID condition plausibly prevents downstream fatigue/cognitive symptoms.
