# Source Digest

Type: FDA warning letter.

Publication date: 2025-04-15; content current as of 2025-05-20.

Key extraction:
- FDA warning letter concerned "Sulbutiamine Plus Brain Support," labeled as a dietary supplement.
- FDA stated that the product violated the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act because it was a misbranded dietary supplement.
- The agency stated that sulbutiamine does not qualify as a dietary ingredient under section 201(ff)(1) because it is not a vitamin, mineral, herb/botanical, amino acid, dietary substance for increasing total dietary intake, or a concentrate/metabolite/constituent/extract/combination of those categories.
- Declaring sulbutiamine as a dietary ingredient was characterized as false or misleading labeling.
- Footnote caveat: the letter did not address whether sulbutiamine could be lawfully marketed as a non-dietary ingredient; non-dietary ingredients would need a food-additive regulation, GRAS status for intended use, or another exception.

Wiki signal:
- Regulatory/safety signal: risk-first in the US dietary-supplement channel.
- Confidence limiter: warning letter concerns a specific product, but the statutory dietary-ingredient reasoning applies to sulbutiamine labeling logic.
