# Stimulation of the growth hormone (GH)-insulin-like growth factor I axis by daily oral administration of a GH secretogogue (MK-677) in healthy elderly subjects

- PubMed PMID 8954023, J Clin Endocrinol Metab 1996.
- Randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled study in 32 healthy adults ages 64-81.
- Participants received placebo or 2, 10, or 25 mg MK-677 orally once daily for two separate study periods of 14 and 28 days.
- MK-677 increased mean 24-hour GH concentration in a dose-dependent manner; 25 mg/day increased mean 24-hour GH by 97%.
- 25 mg/day also raised IGF-I into the normal young-adult range.
- Fasting glucose rose, IGF-binding protein-3 increased, cortisol did not change, and prolactin rose but stayed within the normal range.
- This is physiology/target-engagement evidence, not a cognition or function trial.
