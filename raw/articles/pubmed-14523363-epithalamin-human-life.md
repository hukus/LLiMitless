# Peptides of pineal gland and thymus prolong human life

- PMID: 14523363
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/14523363/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

Clinical follow-up of 266 elderly and older persons over 6-8 years. The pineal peptide arm (Epithalamin) was associated with lower mortality and fewer age-related clinical problems, but the paper does not provide a direct cognition endpoint.
