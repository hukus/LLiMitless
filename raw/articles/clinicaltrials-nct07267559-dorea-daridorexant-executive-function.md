# Dual Orexin Antagonism and Emotion and Affective Processing Study

- ClinicalTrials.gov: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07267559
- NCT: NCT07267559
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Recruiting healthy-volunteer trial from the University of Oxford. Participants are randomized to single-dose daridorexant or placebo and then complete behavioral and cognitive tasks covering emotional processing, aversive learning, and executive function. No results are posted yet, but it is a live human readout for the DORA branch in a non-insomnia population.
