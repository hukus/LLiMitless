# Closed-Loop Auditory Stimulation (CLAS) During Sleep Augments Language and Discovery Learning

Brain Sciences 2024 study in 20 analyzed healthy adults (28 recruited) using a crossed, randomized, double-blind lab design over six overnight sessions. Verum CLAS was compared with control CLAS while participants completed language learning and discovery learning tasks across two weeks.

Captured takeaways:

- Language learning recall was 35% better with verum CLAS than with control.
- Discovery learning performance was 26% better with verum CLAS than with control.
- EEG showed increased slow-oscillation amplitude and entrainment, plus stronger slow-oscillation-spindle coupling.
- The prototype system was provided by NeuroGeneces / NeuroGevity, and the paper discloses advisor-level conflict-of-interest ties, so the result should be treated as promising but interpretation-sensitive.

Wiki use:

This is the strongest positive CLAS learning paper currently in the branch because it extends beyond rote recall into a discovery-learning task, but it still looks task-specific rather than like a broad cognition enhancer.
