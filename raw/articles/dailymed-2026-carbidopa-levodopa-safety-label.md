# DailyMed carbidopa / levodopa safety label context

DailyMed label family for prescription carbidopa / levodopa products. The label context matters for Mucuna because the relevant pharmacology is L-DOPA exposure, even when delivered through a plant supplement rather than a prescription tablet.

Practical safety themes for wiki interpretation include dyskinesia, hallucinations or psychosis, impulse-control problems, sleepiness, orthostatic/blood-pressure effects, nausea, abrupt-discontinuation concerns, and interaction risk with MAO inhibitors or dopaminergic/psychiatric medications.

Use in the wiki: official safety-context anchor, not a claim that Mucuna products are equivalent to labeled prescription dosing.
