# Ruoxinlin ansofaxine approval in China

- Source: Luye Pharma press release dated 2022-11-03.
- The release states that Ruoxinlin, toludesvenlafaxine hydrochloride extended-release tablets, was approved for launch in China by the National Medical Products Administration for major depressive disorder.
- It frames Ruoxinlin as a Class 1 innovative antidepressant and notes the program's phase 2 and phase 3 clinical-trial background.
- Interpretation for the wiki: regulatory status matters because ansofaxine is not just a research chemical. It is an approved antidepressant in China, but that status is for MDD, not for cognition enhancement.
