# PEALUT long-COVID brain-fog retrospective real-world analysis

- Source type: retrospective real-world analysis by general practitioners.
- Publication: Nutrients, 2023.
- Date checked: 2026-05-11.

Nine general practitioners in the Rome area retrospectively analyzed long-COVID patients who received palmitoylethanolamide co-ultramicronized with luteolin. The paper reports improvement across patient-reported neurological and clinical symptoms, especially pain, anxiety and depression, fatigue, brain fog, anosmia, and dysgeusia.

The detailed symptom section reports that the average number of symptoms decreased during treatment and that brain fog fell from 63.8% of patients at baseline to 17.4% at 60 days and 11.1% at 90 days. Fatigue, difficulty multitasking, memory loss, and word-finding symptoms also decreased over time.

Use in the wiki: this is a low-confidence clinical-recovery signal for [[palmitoylethanolamide-luteolin]] because it is retrospective, uncontrolled, symptom-report based, and illness-state specific. It is still important because it directly names brain fog and related cognitive symptoms.
