# Bright light exposure reduces negative affect and modulates EEG activity in sleep-deprived and well-rested adolescents.

- PMID: 41425945
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41425945/
- Ingested: 2026-04-29

## Summary

2025 study in 47 adolescents (15-21 years) assigned to well-rested or 4-hour sleep-deprived conditions. A single 30-minute morning bright-light session reduced negative affect, improved Digit Span Forward, and altered EEG activity in cingulate, insular, and fronto-parietal regions. Subjective sleepiness and Digit Span Backward did not change.

## Notes

- Acute state-modulation evidence, not a durable cognition trial.
- The sleep-deprived subgroup showed the clearest EEG-affect coupling.
- Useful as a morning-light timing anchor for resilience-stack hypotheses.
