# Picamilon outpatient use in stage I chronic cerebral ischemia

2024 Zhurnal nevrologii i psikhiatrii imeni S.S. Korsakova article with English abstract, PMID 39113452, DOI 10.17116/jnevro2024124071119.

Design: open randomized comparative clinical trial in 44 patients aged 46-67 years with stage I chronic cerebral ischemia. Group 1 (n=23) received Picamilon 50 mg orally 3 times/day for 60 days. Group 2 (n=21) received Picamilon 100 mg intramuscularly for 10 days followed by Picamilon 50 mg orally 3 times/day for 50 days. Total therapy duration was 60 days.

Endpoints: MoCA for cognitive status, A.M. Wayne scale for vegetative disorders, A.I. Fedin scale for neurological disorders, Ya.I. Levin scale for sleep, Dopplerography of intracranial vessels, and endothelial-function markers.

Results: total sample MoCA scores showed positive trend over visits (24.9/26.5/28.3; p=0.022 and p<0.001). Sleep quality improved in 50% by visit 3 and 84% by visit 4. Fedin scale values decreased from 11.9+/-8.3 to 6+/-6.1 and then 2.77+/-4.43. The abstract reports up to 89% clinical efficacy, 98% tolerability, and adverse events under 8.6%.

Interpretation: regional clinical-state signal for chronic cerebral ischemia, but open-label, small, no placebo, and not healthy cognitive enhancement. Useful for not dismissing all clinical evidence; not enough for recommendation upgrade.
