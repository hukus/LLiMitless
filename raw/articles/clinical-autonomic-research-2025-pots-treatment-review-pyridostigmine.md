# Clinical Autonomic Research 2025 POTS treatment review: pyridostigmine

## Captured takeaways

- The 2025 systematic literature review of POTS treatment includes a pyridostigmine section under acetylcholinesterase inhibitors.
- Three pyridostigmine POTS studies were included, only one of which was an RCT.
- The Raj et al. crossover RCT in 15 adults with POTS found significant standing-heart-rate change two hours after a single 30 mg pyridostigmine dose compared with placebo, but the review states symptom burden on VOSS was not significantly changed.
- A non-RCT found reduced heart rate during head-up tilt after one-time 60 mg pyridostigmine, without structured symptom scores.
- A retrospective long-term series of 172 POTS patients using 60-180 mg/day reported lower standing heart rate after about 12 months and patient-reported improvement in selected symptoms such as fatigue or palpitations, but without formal questionnaire assessment.
- The review states that it found no study specifically addressing pyridostigmine in patients with both POTS and ME/CFS.
- Interpretation for the wiki: pyridostigmine has an autonomic / heart-rate evidence trail in POTS, but symptom and cognition transfer are weaker than hemodynamic movement.
