# EDR Peptide: Possible Mechanism of Gene Expression and Protein Synthesis Regulation Involved in the Pathogenesis of Alzheimer's Disease

- PMID: 33396470
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33396470/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

This review identifies EDR peptide as Glu-Asp-Arg, i.e. Pinealon, and collects preclinical neuroprotective mechanisms: MAPK/ERK modulation, apoptosis suppression, antioxidant signaling, and dendritic-spine preservation. It also repeats a human claim that Pinealon improved memory issues in elderly patients and helped post-TBI symptoms, but those claims are review-level here rather than independently validated in this paper.
