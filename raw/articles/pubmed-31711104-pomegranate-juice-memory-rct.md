# Randomized Placebo-Controlled Study of the Memory Effects of Pomegranate Juice in Middle-Aged and Older Adults

- PMID: 31711104
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31711104/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

12-month randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial in 261 nondemented adults aged 50-75 years. Participants consumed 8 oz/day pomegranate juice or placebo. The main between-group effect was preservation of BVMT-R learning versus decline in placebo; other memory outcomes were not significantly different. This looks like a stabilization effect rather than a broad enhancer effect.
