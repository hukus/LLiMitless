# Letter to Industry on the Use of Amanita Muscaria or its Constituents in Food

- Agency: U.S. Food and Drug Administration
- Source type: official industry letter / Not-GRAS determination context
- URL: https://www.fda.gov/food/post-market-determinations-use-substance-not-gras/letter-industry-use-amanita-muscaria-or-its-constituents-food
- Ingested: 2026-05-03

## Summary

FDA informed industry that Amanita muscaria, extracts, muscimol, ibotenic acid, and muscarine do not meet the GRAS standard and are unapproved food additives. FDA said its review included more than 600 unique publications and that the available information was insufficient to show safe use in food; the information also indicated possible harm.

The letter notes well-known toxic consequences of A. muscaria ingestion including gastrointestinal symptoms, delirium, sleepiness, alternating drowsiness and excitement, and severe neurological effects such as convulsions and coma after large exposures. FDA also noted adverse event reports involving central nervous system depression, drowsiness, seizures, and hospitalizations associated with products containing A. muscaria or its constituents.
