# Obesity-associated gut microbiome influences diet-induced metabolic and cognitive outcomes in older adults

- PMID: 41907517
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41907517/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

2025 sub-analysis of a 10-week randomized dietary intervention in 31 adults aged 55-85, stratified by obesity and baseline MCI status. Obesity tracked with lower microbial diversity, worse memory/executive function, and stronger links between fat loss, metabolite shifts, and cognitive gains. The signal is exploratory but useful because it points to obesity and body-composition change as modifiers of microbiome-cognition response.
