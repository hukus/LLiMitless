# Effect of sibutramine on cardiovascular outcomes in overweight and obese subjects

- PMID: 20818901
- DOI: 10.1056/NEJMoa1003114
- Journal: New England Journal of Medicine (2010)

## Captured takeaways

- SCOUT enrolled 10,744 overweight or obese adults age 55 or older with preexisting cardiovascular disease, type 2 diabetes, or both.
- The trial tested weight management with sibutramine versus placebo in a high-cardiovascular-risk population.
- The primary composite endpoint was nonfatal myocardial infarction, nonfatal stroke, resuscitation after cardiac arrest, or cardiovascular death.
- The primary outcome occurred in 11.4% of sibutramine participants versus 10.0% of placebo participants, with hazard ratio 1.16, 95% CI 1.03-1.31, p=0.02.
- Interpretation for the wiki: sibutramine is a key class-warning comparator for monoamine-reuptake anorectics; even real weight-loss pharmacology can carry enough cardiovascular risk to dominate any subjective energy or focus interpretation.
