# BMC Chemistry 2025: Inorganic anion variability in Himalayan Shilajit

This 2025 BMC Chemistry study measured inorganic anions in Himalayan Shilajit from Altai, Caucasus, Pamir, and Tibet sources.

Methods: the authors used ultra high performance liquid chromatography with a high resolution Q-Orbitrap mass spectrometer. Reported means for measured anions were fluoride 47.74 ppm, chloride 861.66 ppm, nitrite 43.34 ppm, nitrate 8.46 ppm, sulfate 1,059.17 ppm, and phosphate 180.72 ppm.

Interpretation for the wiki: this supports the identity problem. Shilajit is a geographically variable phytomineral matrix, not a single molecule, and different sources can differ chemically in ways that matter for safety and interpretation.
