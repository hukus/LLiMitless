# Ladasten multicenter asthenia study

- PMID: 21322821
- Russian multicenter clinical trial across 28 centers.
- 728 patients with psychoautonomic syndrome / asthenic disorders were available for analysis.
- Ladasten was given at 50-100 mg/day for 28 days, with follow-up one month after withdrawal.
- Reported responder rates were 76.0% on CGI-S and 90.8% on CGI-I.
- Reported benefits included antiasthenic effects by day 3, plus improvements in anxiety-depressive spectrum symptoms, autonomic dystonia, sleep disorders, and quality of life.
- Safety signal was favorable in the paper: adverse effects in 3%, discontinuation in 0.8%, and no serious adverse effects.
- Best read: strong rescue / clinical-asthenia signal, not a healthy-adult cognition trial.
