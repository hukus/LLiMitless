# ClinicalTrials.gov NCT07011745 - KarXT ADAGIO-2

- ClinicalTrials.gov: NCT07011745
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

Phase 3 randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial of KarXT + KarX-EC for agitation associated with Alzheimer's disease. The record is recruiting, lists no results posted, and was last update posted 2026-04-01. It uses xanomeline/trospium chloride plus xanomeline enteric capsules and is a jurisdiction-sensitive example: the same drug family is FDA-approved in the U.S. for schizophrenia, but its AD use remains investigational.
