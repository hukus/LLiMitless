# Enhanced neural activation with blueberry supplementation in mild cognitive impairment

- Source type: randomized double-blind placebo-controlled trial with fMRI.
- Publication: Nutritional Neuroscience, 2018.
- Date checked: 2026-05-03.

This 16-week trial studied blueberry supplementation in older adults with mild cognitive impairment, using functional MRI during a working-memory task before and after intervention.

Blueberry-treated participants showed increased BOLD activation in left pre-central gyrus, left middle frontal gyrus, and left inferior parietal lobe during working-memory load conditions. The abstract states there was no clear indication of working-memory enhancement.

Use in the wiki: mechanistic / neural-activation support for the blueberry branch, but not behavioral proof by itself. It strengthens biological plausibility while keeping the cognitive-outcome confidence low.
