# ADFound: A Foundation Model for Diagnosis and Prognosis of Alzheimer's Disease

PMID: 40460008
Published: 2025-11

Abstract focus: ADFound is a multimodal foundation model for AD diagnosis and prognosis built from unlabeled paired and unpaired 3D neuroimaging. The paper positions self-supervised multimodal learning as a way to improve downstream disease tasks, but it still reads as a prediction paper, not a validation of biologically stable intervention-selection biomarkers.
