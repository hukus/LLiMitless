# Delayed administration of type II ampakines enhance synaptic plasticity and cognitive recovery in juvenile global cerebral ischemia

- PMID: 41612671
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41612671/
- Ingested: 2026-04-30

## Summary

2026 preclinical rescue paper in a juvenile mouse global cerebral ischemia model. Delayed administration of type II ampakines 14 days after injury increased hippocampal BDNF, restored LTP, and improved hippocampal-dependent learning and memory. This is the clearest recent reason to keep a rescue-first niche alive for the class, but it is still animal recovery biology rather than human cognition evidence.
