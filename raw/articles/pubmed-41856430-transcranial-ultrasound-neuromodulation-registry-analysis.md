# Transcranial ultrasound neuromodulation: registry-based analysis of global clinical trials

- PMID: 41856430
- DOI: 10.1016/j.brs.2026.103076
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41856430/
- Ingested: 2026-05-12

## Summary

Brain Stimulation 2026 registry analysis of global clinical trials using low-intensity ultrasound for brain neuromodulation. The authors searched ClinicalTrials.gov, WHO ICTRP, EUCTR, ChiCTR, CRiS, ANZCTR, JPRN, ISRCTN, and CTRI through September 2025. They identified 177 unique interventional trials. Registrations rose sharply: 27 trials were registered from 2014-2019 and 150 in the last five years. The United States accounted for 52% of trials and China for 18%. Psychiatric indications were most common at 32%, followed by healthy volunteer studies at 16%, pain at 11%, cognitive studies at 11%, and movement disorders at 9%. Among trials specifying a target, 51% targeted subcortical regions. Forty percent were randomized, multi-arm, and double-blinded. Device information was reported in only 32% of trials, identifying 11 commercial or prototype systems. Only 37% of completed trials were linked to peer-reviewed publications.
