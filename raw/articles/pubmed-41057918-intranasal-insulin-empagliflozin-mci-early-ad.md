# A phase 2A/B randomized trial of metabolic modulators intranasal insulin and empagliflozin for MCI and early AD

- PMID: 41057918
- DOI: 10.1002/alz.70704
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41057918/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

Phase 2A/B factorial RCT in participants with MCI, early AD, or amyloid positivity. Intranasal insulin 40 IU QID, empagliflozin 10 mg daily, both, or placebo were given for 4 weeks. TRAEs were mild and similar across groups; INI improved mPACC5 and some fluid/imaging biomarkers, while empagliflozin shifted CSF tau and vascular/neurodegenerative markers.

## Notes

This trial is more biomarker-rich and more phenotype-enriched than the pooled AD/MCI meta-analysis, so it should be read as a short-duration positive signal rather than a durability proof. It also strengthens the case that metabolic-brain interventions may be more useful as state-specific or combination strategies than as generic enhancers.
