# Paraxanthine provides greater improvement in cognitive function than caffeine after performing a 10-km run

- PMID: 38725238
- PMCID: PMC11089923
- DOI: 10.1080/15502783.2024.2352779
- Ingested: 2026-05-03

## Summary

2024 randomized, double-blind, crossover trial in 12 trained runners. Participants received placebo, 200 mg caffeine, 200 mg ENFINITY paraxanthine, or 200 mg paraxanthine plus 200 mg caffeine with 7-14 day washouts, then completed cognitive and psychomotor testing before and after a 10-km run.

The paper frames paraxanthine as a caffeine metabolite with potential nootropic properties and reports greater post-exercise cognitive-function improvement for paraxanthine than caffeine in this small fatigue/exercise context.

Use in the wiki: direct but state-bound human cognition source. It is relevant to fatigue / exercise / post-load cognition, not proof of durable baseline enhancement. Sample size, sponsor/product links, and exercise-state specificity keep confidence low.
