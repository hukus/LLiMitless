# How Much Does Education Improve Intelligence? A Meta-Analysis

- Source: PNAS / PMC full text
- DOI: 10.1177/0956797618774253
- Ingested: 2026-05-03

## Summary

Meta-analysis of quasi-experimental education evidence finding that additional education is associated with intelligence gains. The paper reports effects around 1 to 5 IQ points per additional year of education depending on design and category, with benefits persisting across the lifespan. This is the strongest durable human lever for broad intelligence-like scores, but it is slow and not a short nootropic-style intervention.

## Notes

For the one-standard-deviation goal, this source argues that true durable gains probably require education-scale exposure. A one-year bootcamp should not be expected to produce a full 15-point gain by itself unless the outcome is a trained or education-loaded score rather than latent general intelligence.
