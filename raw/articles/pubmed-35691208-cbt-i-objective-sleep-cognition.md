# Effects of cognitive behavioral therapy for insomnia on subjective and objective measures of sleep and cognition

- PMID: 35691208
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35691208/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Randomized controlled trial in chronic insomnia with 8 weekly CBT-I sessions versus wait-list control. The study assessed sleep-state misperception and objective cognitive performance alongside sleep outcomes.

## Notes

Useful because it explicitly treats cognition as a CBT-I endpoint, not just a downstream quality-of-life measure.
