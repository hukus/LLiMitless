# ACD856 multiple-dose qEEG healthy-subject study

2023 Journal of Prevention of Alzheimer's Disease open-access paper on multiple ascending oral doses of ACD856 in healthy subjects.

## Captured takeaways

- Study type: prospective phase I, double-blind, parallel-group, placebo-controlled, randomized study in a First-in-Human unit in Sweden.
- Participants: 24 healthy male and female subjects.
- Dosing: ACD856 or placebo oral solution once daily for 7 days, with three ascending dose cohorts of 10 mg, 30 mg, and 90 mg. Within each cohort, six subjects received ACD856 and two received placebo.
- Objective: assess safety/tolerability, pharmacokinetics, and exploratory pharmacodynamics using quantitative EEG as an indicator of central target engagement.
- The paper reports no serious adverse events and no treatment-emergent or dose-related trends in safety assessments.
- ACD856 was rapidly absorbed, reached peak plasma exposure at about 30 to 45 minutes, reached steady state before day 6, and had a steady-state elimination half-life of about 20 hours.
- Cerebrospinal-fluid levels increased with dose, supporting CNS exposure. qEEG changed in a dose-dependent pattern, including increased relative theta power, decreased fast alpha/beta power, and higher theta/beta ratio.

## Wiki interpretation

- This is the main ACD856 human CNS exposure / target-engagement source.
- It still does not establish healthy-adult cognitive enhancement, memory improvement, or brain-fog benefit.
- The qEEG signal should be interpreted as pharmacodynamic support, not as a validated behavioral endpoint.
