# D-phenylalanine chronic pain crossover trial

1986 double-blind crossover trial of D-phenylalanine in 30 chronic pain patients. The abstract reports no significant analgesic effect compared with placebo.

Use in the wiki: D-phenylalanine / enkephalinase-adjacent lore is not cognition evidence. It also keeps DLPA claims separated from L-phenylalanine catecholamine-precursor claims.
