# AI-Supported Cognitive Rehabilitation for Older Adults With Mild Cognitive Impairment

- ClinicalTrials.gov ID: NCT07474038
- Study page: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07474038
- Ingested: 2026-04-29

## Summary

Randomized controlled trial of a 12-month AI-supported cognitive rehabilitation program for older adults with MCI. Participants are randomized 1:1 to AI-supported cognitive rehabilitation or usual care.

The intervention combines clinician-delivered telehealth cognitive rehabilitation with daily AI-guided conversational cognitive exercises and education. The primary outcome is change in MoCA-BLIND at 6 months, with secondary outcomes including ACE-III, subjective memory, mood, loneliness, goal attainment, engagement, and AI-related safety events.
