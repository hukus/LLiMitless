Title: Polygenicity and APOE ε4 shape response to intervention in mild cognitive impairment

This paper re-evaluates the SUPERBRAIN-MEET multidomain intervention in MCI using both polygenic risk scores for Alzheimer's disease and APOE ε4 status.

The key relevance for the wiki is that the intervention effect was not flat across the cohort. The genetic-risk strata showed different relative gains on the RBANS total index, which supports the idea that biomarker/ genotype enrichment can surface a larger intervention signal than an unstratified average would show.

Important caveat: this is a post hoc genetic reanalysis of an existing randomized intervention, not a prospective enriched-enrollment trial. The finding is useful for responder mapping, but it does not by itself prove that enrichment will reliably improve effect sizes in future studies.
