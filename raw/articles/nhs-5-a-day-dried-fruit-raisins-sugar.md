# NHS 5 A Day dried fruit portion guidance

- Source type: public health guidance.
- Publisher: UK National Health Service.
- Date checked: 2026-05-03.

NHS 5 A Day portion guidance lists a dried-fruit portion as around 30 g, including about one heaped tablespoon of raisins, currants, or sultanas. It also cautions that dried fruit can be high in sugar and can be bad for teeth, and advises eating dried fruit as part of a meal rather than as a between-meal snack to reduce tooth-decay risk.

Use in the wiki: practical food-dose safety context for raisin/grape-food products. It does not decide cognitive efficacy, but it helps prevent a 50 g/day raisin trial dose from being treated as a universally low-friction snack dose.
