# Cocoa Flavanols Protect Cognitive Function During Severe Hypoxia

- PMID: 37471213
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37471213/
- Ingested: 2026-05-03

## Summary

Placebo-controlled double-blind trial in 12 participants testing whether cocoa flavanols could protect cognition during acute hypoxia equivalent to 5,500 m altitude. Participants ingested 15 mg/kg cocoa flavanols 90 minutes before cognitive testing during normoxia and hypoxia conditions.

Poikilocapnic hypoxia impaired overall cognition. Cocoa flavanols improved overall cognitive performance by about 4% compared with placebo during hypoxia, with cerebral oxygenation measured by functional near-infrared spectroscopy.

Use in wiki: acute state-challenge signal. This supports cocoa flavanols as a possible vascular/oxygenation resilience candidate under hypoxia, not as a rested-baseline general nootropic.
