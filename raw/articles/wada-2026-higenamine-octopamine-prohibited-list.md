# WADA 2026 prohibited-list entries for higenamine and octopamine

WADA's current prohibited-list page says the list is updated annually and indicates what is prohibited in sport and when. In the 2026 list, S3 beta-2 agonists are prohibited at all times, and higenamine is named among the included beta-2 agonists.

The same page lists S6 stimulants as prohibited in competition. Octopamine is named in S6.B specified stimulants. In this pass, hordenine was not captured as an explicitly named entry on the WADA page, so athlete-risk language for hordenine should be framed through supplement contamination, product-label, and related-stimulant caution rather than a direct named-list claim.

Use in the wiki: sports-status anchor. Higenamine has high anti-doping visibility at all times; octopamine has in-competition stimulant visibility; hordenine needs product-specific athlete checks.
