# Betaine supplementation decreases plasma homocysteine in healthy adult participants: a meta-analysis

2013 meta-analysis of randomized placebo-controlled trials testing daily betaine supplementation in healthy adults. The abstract reports five trials from 2002 to 2010, with at least 4 g/day betaine for 6 to 24 weeks.

The pooled estimate for plasma homocysteine was a statistically significant reduction of 1.23 micromol/L (95% CI -1.61 to -0.85; p = 0.01). The abstract notes that 6 g/day betaine lowered plasma homocysteine by roughly 5% to 20% in healthy volunteers.

No cognitive endpoint is part of this meta-analysis. The signal is biochemical remethylation capacity, not cognition.

Wiki use: this anchors [[betaine-tmg]] and [[one-carbon-methyl-donor-cognition-watchlist]] as biomarker/status routes. It should not upgrade brain-fog or focus anecdotes without measured homocysteine, folate/B12 status, cognitive endpoints, and safety context.
