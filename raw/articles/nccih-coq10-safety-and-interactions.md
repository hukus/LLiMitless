# NCCIH Coenzyme Q10 Safety and Interactions

- Source: NIH National Center for Complementary and Integrative Health.
- Page crawled: 2026-05-03.
- Ingested: 2026-05-03.

## Summary

NCCIH summarizes coenzyme Q10 as an antioxidant-like compound made by the body and used as a dietary supplement. The page says CoQ10 has been studied in multiple conditions, but evidence is often limited or not supportive, including NIH-funded Parkinson's disease work that did not improve early Parkinson's symptoms.

Safety section: no serious side effects have been reported; mild side effects such as insomnia or digestive upset can occur. NCCIH notes possible interaction with warfarin, insulin, and some types of cancer treatment.

## Interpretation

Use this as the public-health safety and interaction anchor for CoQ10 / ubiquinol. It supports generally good tolerability, while keeping anticoagulant, diabetes-medication, and oncology-treatment contexts visible.
