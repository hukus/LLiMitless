# A systematic review and meta-analysis of the effects of resistance exercise on cognitive function in older adults

- PMID: 41503279
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41503279/
- Ingested: 2026-04-29

## Summary

2025 systematic review and meta-analysis of 17 RCTs (n=739) found resistance exercise improved overall cognition, working memory, verbal learning and memory, and spatial memory span in older adults, while processing speed, executive function, and attention were not significant.

## Notes

This strengthens the exercise component of the triad, but it does not provide any direct evidence for a creatine + metabolic-peptide combination or for a triad-level protocol.
