# Integrated meta-analysis, network pharmacology, and molecular docking to investigate the efficacy and potential pharmacological mechanism of Kai-Xin-San on Alzheimer's disease

- Published: 2020-09-21
- Journal: Pharmaceutical Biology
- DOI: 10.1080/13880209.2020.1817103
- Evidence layer: preclinical meta-analysis plus network pharmacology

## Summary

This open-access paper evaluated Kai-Xin-San in Alzheimer's disease models and paired the animal meta-analysis with network pharmacology and molecular docking. Ten animal studies were included after screening 80 studies. In Morris water maze outcomes, KXS reduced escape latency and increased platform crossings and time in the target quadrant in AD animal models.

The network-pharmacology layer identified 25 active compounds and 44 AD-related target genes. The paper highlighted DRD2, MAOA, ACHE, ADRA2A, and CHRM2 as core targets, and mapped pathways including cholinergic synapses, cGMP/PKG signaling, and calcium signaling. This is useful for mechanism routing, but it remains an animal-model and in-silico signal rather than human cognition evidence.
