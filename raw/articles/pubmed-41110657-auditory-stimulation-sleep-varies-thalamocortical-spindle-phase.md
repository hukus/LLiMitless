# The effectiveness of auditory stimulation in sleep varies with thalamocortical spindle phase

NeuroImage 2025 study in 16 healthy adults. Auditory stimulation timed to the slow-oscillation up-state enhanced slow-wave activity with minimal phase dependence, but coordinated spindle activity emerged only at the rising and peak spindle phases. Trough stimulation delayed spindle activity, and stimulation during the falling phase did not enhance spindle activity.

This is physiology-first evidence rather than a memory-performance study. It shows the control loop can hit the intended event, but the spindle-phase window is part of the dose.
