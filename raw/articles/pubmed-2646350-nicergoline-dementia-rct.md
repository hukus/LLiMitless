# Nicergoline in senile dementia of Alzheimer type and multi-infarct dementia

PubMed-indexed 1989 multicenter placebo-controlled double-blind study of nicergoline in 105 patients with mild-to-moderate senile dementia of Alzheimer type or multi-infarct dementia. Nicergoline 30 mg twice daily was tested for three months.

The abstract reports that psychometric testing and clinical observations showed efficacy in improving cognitive and affective symptoms. The age and phenotype keep this as clinical-state evidence, not broad nootropic evidence.

Relevance: primary formal anchor for nicergoline as a legacy vasoactive cognition candidate.
