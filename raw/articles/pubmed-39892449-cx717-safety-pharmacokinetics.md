# Safety, tolerability and pharmacokinetic profile of the low-impact ampakine CX717 in young healthy male subjects and elderly healthy male and female subjects

- PMID: 39892449
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39892449/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

2025 first-in-human safety and PK paper for low-impact ampakine CX717. The study reports tolerability up to 1600 mg single dose and 800 mg BID, with headache, dizziness, and nausea as the main adverse effects and with PK suitable for further development.
