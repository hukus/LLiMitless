# PubChem 2026 TUDCA / UDCA identity note

PubChem identifies tauroursodeoxycholic acid as CID 9848818, formula C26H45NO6S, and lists synonyms including tauroursodeoxycholic acid and taurursodiol / ursodoxicoltaurine-style naming. PubChem's linked drug-information fields describe tauroursodeoxycholic acid as a bile acid used for gallstone-related indications and as a component, with phenylbutyric acid, in the former ALS product AMX0035.

PubChem identifies ursodeoxycholic acid / ursodiol as CID 31401, formula C24H40O4, with synonyms including UDCA, ursodiol, and Actigall. It describes UDCA as a naturally occurring bile acid used therapeutically for cholesterol gallstones and cholestatic liver diseases.

Interpretation for the wiki: TUDCA / taurursodiol and UDCA / ursodiol are closely related hydrophilic bile-acid chemicals, but product, conjugation state, indication, and regulatory status must be kept separate. TUDCA supplement anecdotes should not be treated as equivalent to prescription ursodiol labeling or to AMX0035 fixed-combination evidence.
