# Growth hormone-releasing activity of hexarelin in humans. A dose-response study

- PubMed PMID 7957536, Eur J Clin Pharmacol 1994.
- Double-blind, placebo-controlled rising-dose study in 12 adult male volunteers.
- Hexarelin increased GH dose-dependently after single intravenous boluses.
- This is human GH secretagogue pharmacology, not cognition evidence.
- Helpful mainly because hexarelin appears often in forum stacks with CJC/ipamorelin/tesamorelin.
