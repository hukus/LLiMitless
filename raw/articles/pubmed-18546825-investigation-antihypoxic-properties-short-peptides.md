# [Investigation of antihypoxic properties of short peptides]

- PMID: 18546825
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18546825/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

This review describes short regulatory peptides, including pinealon, as antihypoxic in hypobaric hypoxia models. For pinealon, the abstract emphasizes increased neuronal resistance to hypoxic stress, stimulation of endogenous antioxidant systems, and a possible anti-excitotoxic NMDA-related effect. It is mechanistic and review-level, not a direct human cognition trial.
