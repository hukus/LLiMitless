# Retraction notice for hyperbaric oxygen for severe traumatic brain injury: a randomized trial.

- PMID: 38217421
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38217421/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Retraction notice for the 2020 severe-TBI HBOT trial that reported improved neurologic outcomes.

## Notes

The original trial (PMID 33050752) was later retracted in January 2024. Any evidence synthesis that treated the 2020 study as valid needs to exclude it or explicitly mark it as retracted.
