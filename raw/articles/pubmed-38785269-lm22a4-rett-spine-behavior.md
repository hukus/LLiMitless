# A small-molecule TrkB ligand improves dendritic spine phenotypes and atypical behaviors in female Rett syndrome mice

- PMID: 38785269
- PMCID: PMC11139040
- DOI: 10.1242/dmm.050612
- Journal: Disease Models & Mechanisms
- Year: 2024
- Ingested: 2026-05-04

## Summary

Preclinical Rett syndrome mouse study testing LM22A-4, described as a brain-penetrant small-molecule ligand of TrkB. In vitro LM22A-4 increased dendritic spine density in cultured hippocampal slices from male Mecp2 knockout mice, with effects blocked by TrkB inhibitors. In female Mecp2 heterozygous mice, 4 weeks of systemic LM22A-4 restored spine-volume measures in MeCP2-expressing neurons toward wild-type levels and reduced elevated aggressive behaviors. The paper supports disease-model spine and behavioral rescue, not human cognition efficacy.

## Notes

- Use as fresh LM22A-4 disease-model support and safety/translation caution.
- Behavioral outcomes are atypical social/aggressive behaviors rather than objective human cognition.
- Keep separate from 7,8-DHF, R13, deoxygedunin, and ZEB85.
