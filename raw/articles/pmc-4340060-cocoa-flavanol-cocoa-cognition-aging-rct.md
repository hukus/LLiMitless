# Cocoa Flavanol Consumption Improves Cognitive Function in Elderly Subjects: CoCoA Study

- PMCID: PMC4340060
- Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4340060/
- Publication: American Journal of Clinical Nutrition, 2015 issue; online 2014-12-17
- Ingested: 2026-05-03

## Summary

Double-blind controlled parallel-arm randomized trial in 90 cognitively intact elderly adults. Participants consumed a dairy-based cocoa drink once daily for 8 weeks with high (993 mg), intermediate (520 mg), or low (48 mg) cocoa flavanol content. Cognitive outcomes included MMSE, Trail Making Test A/B, and verbal fluency.

The global MMSE change did not differ by treatment, but specific cognitive measures improved in the intermediate- and high-flavanol groups, alongside blood pressure, insulin resistance, and lipid peroxidation changes. The trial used carefully designed drinks intended to match calories, nutrients, and alkaloids.

Use in wiki: small but direct positive elderly-adult cocoa-flavanol signal. Interpret as product-specific, short-duration, and partly vascular-metabolic, not as proof that commercial chocolate products are interchangeable.
