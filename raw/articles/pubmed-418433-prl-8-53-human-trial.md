# PRL-8-53: enhanced learning and subsequent retention in humans as a result of low oral doses of new psychotropic agent

- PMID: 418433
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/418433/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

Single double-blind human study of PRL-8-53 reported slight acquisition improvement and statistically significant verbal-retention benefit, with no meaningful change in visual reaction time or motor control versus placebo. This is the core reason PRL-8-53 remains a replication-worthy curiosity rather than pure folklore.
