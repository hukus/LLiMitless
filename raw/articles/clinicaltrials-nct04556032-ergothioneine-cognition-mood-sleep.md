# Effects of Ergothioneine on Cognition, Mood, and Sleep in Healthy Adult Men and Women

- ClinicalTrials.gov: NCT04556032
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

Completed placebo-controlled, randomized, double-blind, parallel-arm ergothioneine trial in healthy adults. The public registry shows tiny enrollment, no posted results, and outcomes centered on cognition, mood, and sleep, so this remains an uninformative direct-cognition lead rather than a demonstrated positive study.

## Registry Details

- Actual enrollment: 4
- Interventions: L-Ergothioneine 10 mg/day, L-Ergothioneine 25 mg/day, or placebo
- Study design: placebo-controlled, randomized, double-blind, parallel-arm trial
- Primary outcome: change in composite memory standard score from CNS Vital Signs from baseline to week 16
- Secondary outcomes: memory, attention, executive function, sleep quality, and mood across weeks 4, 8, and 16
- Study start: 2020-09-09
- Primary completion: 2020-10-30
- Study completion: 2020-10-30
- Last update posted: 2020-11-04
