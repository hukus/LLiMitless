# Cerebrolysin for acute ischaemic stroke

- Citation: Ziganshina LE, Abakumova T, Nurkhametova D, Ivanchenko K. Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews 2023, Issue 10, CD007026.
- DOI: 10.1002/14651858.CD007026.pub7
- Published: 2023-10-11
- Source type: Cochrane systematic review of acute ischemic stroke RCTs
- Ingested: 2026-05-04

## Summary

2023 Cochrane update of Cerebrolysin or Cerebrolysin-like peptide mixtures for acute ischemic stroke. The review included seven randomized trials with 1,773 participants, adding one Cortexin trial to the previous evidence base. It concluded with moderate certainty that Cerebrolysin/Cortexin probably does not reduce all-cause mortality after acute ischemic stroke. It also found little or no difference in the total number of people with serious adverse events, but a probable increase in non-fatal serious adverse events with Cerebrolysin. Several key patient-centered endpoints, including poor functional outcome, early death, quality of life, and return to work, were not adequately reported.

## Signal Use

- Medicinal / formal signal: negative or cautionary for acute-stroke hard outcomes and safety, despite later observational recovery-positive reports.
- Safety signal: moderate-certainty concern for increased non-fatal serious adverse events in the Cerebrolysin subgroup.
- Anecdotal signal: none. This is systematic trial evidence.
- Wiki implication: the 2025 observational stroke-recovery signal should be held beside this Cochrane safety/endpoint caution rather than promoted as settled stroke efficacy.
