# Persistent blood glucose reduction upon repeated transcranial electric stimulation in men

- PMID: 28392373
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28392373/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Sham-controlled crossover study in healthy men showing that repeated anodal tDCS lowered circulating glucose compared with sham and did so without changing serum insulin.

## Notes

The useful interaction-level takeaway is that brain stimulation can shift systemic glucose handling through an insulin-independent route, which makes it a plausible metabolic confounder or partner for intranasal-insulin studies.
