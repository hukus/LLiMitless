# Efficacy of xanomeline and xanomeline trospium in the treatment of cognitive impairment: A systematic review of preclinical and clinical trials

- PMID: 41418563
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41418563/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

This 2026 systematic review argues that xanomeline and xanomeline-trospium have cognitive relevance in schizophrenia, bipolar disorder, and possibly AD, with reported benefits in verbal learning, delayed memory, and reaction time. It is a synthesis rather than a primary cognition trial, and the author group includes prominent industry-adjacent conflict-of-interest disclosures, so the cognition claim should be treated as hypothesis-strengthening rather than definitive proof.
