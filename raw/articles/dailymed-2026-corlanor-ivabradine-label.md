# DailyMed Corlanor / ivabradine label

## Captured takeaways

- DailyMed's Corlanor label is the current safety/status anchor for ivabradine.
- The labeled indication is heart failure in adult and pediatric patients, not POTS or cognition.
- Major warnings and precautions include fetal toxicity, atrial fibrillation monitoring, heart-rate decreases, bradycardia symptoms, and caution around conduction disturbance.
- Common adverse reactions listed in the label include bradycardia, hypertension, atrial fibrillation, and luminous phenomena / phosphenes.
- Drug-interaction gates include avoiding CYP3A4 inhibitors or inducers and monitoring bradycardia risk with negative chronotropes such as beta-blockers, digoxin, or amiodarone.
- Pregnancy/lactation gates matter: the label warns of fetal harm based on animal findings and says breastfeeding is not recommended.
- Interpretation for the wiki: ivabradine is a prescription cardiac drug with real off-label POTS evidence but serious rhythm, pregnancy, interaction, and visual-effect gates. It should not be routed as a casual nootropic.
