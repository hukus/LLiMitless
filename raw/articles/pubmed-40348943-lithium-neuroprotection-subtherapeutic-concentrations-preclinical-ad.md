# Lithium and neuroprotection: a review of molecular targets and biological effects at subtherapeutic concentrations in preclinical models of Alzheimer's disease

- PMID: 40348943
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40348943/
- Ingested: 2026-04-30

## Summary

This 2025 review synthesizes animal and cellular evidence that subtherapeutic lithium can modulate GSK-3β, tau phosphorylation, amyloid burden, neurotrophic signaling, and inflammation in Alzheimer's disease models. It strengthens the mechanistic rationale for low-dose lithium, but it does not provide human cognition efficacy evidence or a validated dosing claim.
