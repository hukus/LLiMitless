# A prospective, randomized, placebo-controlled, double-blind trial about safety and efficacy of combined treatment with alteplase (rt-PA) and Cerebrolysin in acute ischaemic hemispheric stroke

- PMID: 23009193
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23009193/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Randomized acute-stroke trial of Cerebrolysin plus alteplase. The combination was safe and improved early NIHSS responder rates during treatment, but did not improve the primary day-90 mRS outcome.
