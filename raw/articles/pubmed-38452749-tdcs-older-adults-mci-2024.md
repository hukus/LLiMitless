# Effects of Transcranial Direct Current Stimulation on Cognitive Function in Older Adults with and without Mild Cognitive Impairment: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis of Randomized Controlled Trials

- PMID: 38452749
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38452749/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

2024 tDCS meta-analysis in older adults and MCI, with small immediate effects and no durable 1-month signal.

## Notes

Useful comparator for the newer dose-parameter meta-analysis because it shows where tDCS still looks weak on memory and executive function.
