# NCCIH Yohimbe usefulness and safety

NCCIH Yohimbe page, last updated May 2025. It says yohimbe bark contains yohimbine, is promoted for erectile dysfunction, athletic performance, weight loss, mood, and other uses, but there is very little human research on yohimbe as a dietary supplement and not enough evidence for definite effectiveness conclusions.

Safety and product-quality points are central: NCCIH links yohimbine to arrhythmia, blood-pressure problems, heart attacks, seizures, stomach problems, tachycardia, anxiety, and high blood pressure; notes inaccurate labeling and variable yohimbine content; says many products fail to list yohimbine amount or side effects; and warns against use with MAO inhibitors or tricyclic antidepressants.

Use in the wiki: official safety/product-identity anchor for keeping yohimbine risk-first.
