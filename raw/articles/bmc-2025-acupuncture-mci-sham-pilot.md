# Acupuncture for patients with mild cognitive impairment: a randomized, patient-assessor-blinded, sham-controlled pilot study

- Published: 2025-07-19
- Journal: BMC Complementary Medicine and Therapies
- DOI: 10.1186/s12906-025-05023-5
- Evidence layer: randomized patient- and assessor-blinded sham-controlled pilot

## Summary

This Korean pilot trial randomized 30 MCI participants to acupuncture or sham acupuncture. Twenty-eight completed the study. Participants received 24 sessions across 12 weeks. The primary endpoint was ADAS-cog change at 12 weeks, with secondary outcomes including the Seoul Neuropsychological Screening Battery.

Both groups improved clinically from baseline, but the between-group ADAS-cog difference was not significant. The memory-domain SNSB-II result had a moderate effect size favoring acupuncture (Cohen's d = 0.57) but did not reach statistical significance. No intervention-related adverse events were reported, and blinding was adequate.
