# Foundation model for cancer imaging biomarkers

PMID: 38523679
PMCID: PMC10957482
Published: 2024-03-15

Abstract focus: a self-supervised convolutional foundation model trained on 11,467 radiographic lesions improved biomarker learning on downstream tasks, especially with limited data. The model was reported to be more stable to input variations and to show stronger associations with underlying biology than supervised alternatives, making it a useful analog for biomarker robustness work in neuroscience.
