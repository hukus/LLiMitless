# Mexican Application for Tesofensine Not Yet Approved

## Captured takeaways

- Saniona's 2024-11-06 press release says Medix had not received Cofepris approval for tesofensine for obesity in Mexico.
- The release says Medix would enter dialogue with the agency about the path forward.
- The application was based on a phase 3 program involving 372 patients conducted by Medix.
- Saniona reported average weight loss of about 10% in 24 weeks, with more than half of patients losing more than 10%.
- Saniona reported an approximately 1600-patient clinical safety database.
- Interpretation for the wiki: this is sponsor-reported status, not regulator confirmation of approval; use it only as a regulatory-status caution.
