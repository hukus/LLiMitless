# Game-Based Cognitive Aging Assessment: Toward a Digital Biomarker of Cognitive Health

- PMID: 41322085
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41322085/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

Home-based cognitive game study showing that game metrics can produce a scalable digital biomarker of cognitive aging.

## Notes

- N = 871 adults aged 18-75 completed the Tunnel Runner game.
- The best stacked ensemble predicted chronological age with MAE 6.97 years, correlation 0.626, and concordance 0.698.
- Cognitive aging scores reflected known patterns, including lower scores in self-reported ADHD and different patterns by gamer identity.
- This is a useful digital biomarker paper, but it is a state estimator rather than an intervention-response study.
