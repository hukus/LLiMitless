# Lower Plasma Klotho Concentrations Are Associated with Vascular Dementia but Not Late-Onset Alzheimer's Disease

- PMID: 29768278
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29768278/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Memory-clinic case-control style study in 320 elderly patients. Lower plasma Klotho was associated with vascular dementia, lacunar infarcts, leukoaraiosis, coronary disease, stroke, and inflammatory/renal markers, but not with late-onset Alzheimer's disease. This points toward vascular and systemic-health confounding rather than a broad dementia biomarker.
