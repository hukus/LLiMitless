# FDA public notification: Superior contains hidden drug ingredient - sibutramine

## Captured takeaways

- FDA warned consumers in 2015 not to purchase or use a weight-loss product called Superior after lab analysis found sibutramine.
- FDA states sibutramine is a controlled substance that was removed from the U.S. market in October 2010 for safety reasons.
- FDA says sibutramine can substantially increase blood pressure and/or pulse rate in some patients and may present significant risk in people with coronary artery disease, congestive heart failure, arrhythmias, or stroke.
- FDA also warns that products like this may interact in life-threatening ways with other medications.
- Interpretation for the wiki: sibutramine is a key cautionary comparator for monoamine-reuptake anorectic stimulants and contaminated weight-loss products.
