# Prevention of Early Alzheimer's Disease by Erinacine A-Enriched Hericium erinaceus Mycelia Pilot Double-Blind Placebo-Controlled Study

- Source: https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/aging-neuroscience/articles/10.3389/fnagi.2020.00155/full
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

Pilot randomized double-blind placebo-controlled trial in mild early AD using three 350 mg erinacine A-enriched Hericium erinaceus mycelia capsules per day for 49 weeks. The abstract reports MMSE improvement in the active arm, a placebo-group CASI decline, and a better IADL signal for the active group.
