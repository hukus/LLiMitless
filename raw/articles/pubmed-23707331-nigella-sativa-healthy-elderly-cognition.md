# Nigella sativa seed and cognition in healthy elderly volunteers

2013 Journal of Ethnopharmacology randomized controlled trial, PMID 23707331, DOI 10.1016/j.jep.2013.05.004.

Population/design: 40 healthy elderly male volunteers, randomized into two groups of 20. The active group received 500 mg Nigella sativa seed capsules twice daily for 9 weeks; placebo group received matching placebo. Assessments were done at baseline and after 9 weeks.

Cognitive endpoints: logical memory test, digit span, Rey-Osterrieth complex figure test, letter cancellation, Trail Making Test A/B, and Stroop. Safety labs covered cardiac, liver, and kidney markers.

Results: the abstract reports significant differences after Nigella sativa ingestion for logical memory I and II, total digit span, 30-minute delayed recall, Rey-Osterrieth percent score, letter-cancellation completion time, Trail Making Test A/B time, and Stroop part C. It reports no statistically significant changes in measured cardiac, liver, or kidney biochemical markers during the 9-week period.

Interpretation: direct human cognition signal, but small, older, male-only, single-research-stream, and not product-normalized beyond seed capsules. It supports low-confidence standalone tracking, not broad recommendation strength.
