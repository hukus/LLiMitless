# Effects of Cerebrolysin on Language Ability in Non-fluent Aphasia Patients After Stroke

- ClinicalTrials.gov: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06897176
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Recruiting randomized, placebo-controlled post-stroke aphasia study. The endpoint is language ability after Cerebrolysin plus speech therapy, so it is a cognition-adjacent rehabilitation lead rather than a pure memory study.
