# A pilot study of senolytics to improve cognition and mobility in older adults at risk for Alzheimer's disease

- PMID: 40010154
- DOI: 10.1016/j.ebiom.2025.105612
- Journal: eBioMedicine (2025)

## Summary

STAMINA was a 12-week, single-arm, open-label pre-post pilot of intermittent dasatinib plus quercetin in older adults at risk for Alzheimer's disease. The study enrolled 12 completers from roughly 115 telephone-screened individuals and found no serious adverse events related to the intervention.

## Key Data

- Dose: 100 mg dasatinib + 1250 mg quercetin, orally for 2 consecutive days every 2 weeks.
- Population: adults aged 65+ with slow gait speed and MCI / low telephone MoCA.
- Functional outcomes: MoCA, trail making, gait speed, SPPB, TUG, grip strength, dual-task walking.
- Biomarker signal: mean MoCA increased by 1.0 point overall and by 2.0 points in the lowest baseline MoCA subgroup; TNF-α decreased about 3.0%.
- Correlation: TNF-α change correlated inversely with MoCA change (r = -0.65, p = 0.02).

## Caveats

- No control group for the functional outcomes.
- Results are hypothesis-generating, not efficacy proof.
- Recruitment and eligibility burden were high, which limits transportability.
