# N100 as a response prediction biomarker for accelerated 1 Hz right DLPFC-rTMS in major depression

- PMID: 39033822
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39033822/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Small 2024 study in 23 major-depression patients testing baseline TMS-EEG as a response predictor for accelerated right DLPFC rTMS. Baseline N100 amplitude at F4 was strongly associated with treatment outcome.

## Notes

- Responders had larger baseline F4 N100 amplitude than non-responders.
- The study supports a low-cost EEG biomarker candidate for rTMS personalization.
- Lack of control group and auditory masking limit the certainty.
