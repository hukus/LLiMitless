# Rasagiline cognition trial in Parkinson disease patients without dementia

- PMID: 21500280
- DOI: 10.1002/mds.23738
- Journal: Movement Disorders (2011)
- Design: randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled, multicenter prospective study.

## Captured takeaways

- The trial studied rasagiline 1 mg/day versus placebo for 3 months in Parkinson disease patients receiving stable dopaminergic treatment.
- Patients were cognitively impaired in at least two of four domains but did not meet criteria for Parkinson disease dementia.
- Fifty-five patients were randomized and 48 completed the study.
- Rasagiline improved digit span-backward versus placebo with p = 0.04.
- Verbal fluency total score favored rasagiline with p = 0.038.
- Composite attentional Z score favored rasagiline with p < 0.005.
- Other cognitive tests and domain Z scores did not differ significantly.
- Interpretation for the wiki: this is a real but small disease-context attention/executive signal, not evidence for broad healthy-adult cognitive enhancement.
