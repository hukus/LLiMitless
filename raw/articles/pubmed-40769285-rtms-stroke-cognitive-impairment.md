# Impact of repetitive transcranial magnetic stimulation on cognitive impairment in stroke patients: A meta-analysis of randomized controlled trials

- PMID: 40769285
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40769285/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Meta-analysis of 14 RCTs in stroke patients with cognitive impairment. The abstract reports a significant improvement in global cognition overall (SMD 0.54) with the strongest subgroup signals for high-intensity rTMS and intermittent theta burst stimulation (iTBS), which supports a protocol-sensitive rather than monolithic view of the modality.
