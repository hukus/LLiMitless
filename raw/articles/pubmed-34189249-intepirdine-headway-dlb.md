# An international, randomized, placebo-controlled, phase 2b clinical trial of intepirdine for dementia with Lewy bodies (HEADWAY-DLB)

- PMID: 34189249
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34189249/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

Randomized phase 2b trial of intepirdine in dementia with Lewy bodies. Neither dose improved the primary UPDRS-III endpoint, and the other prespecified endpoints were also negative. The study was well tolerated apart from a slightly higher rate of gastrointestinal adverse events, so the main conclusion is efficacy failure.
