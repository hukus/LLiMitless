# FDA Alerts Industry and Consumers about the Use of Amanita Muscaria or its Constituents in Food

- Agency: U.S. Food and Drug Administration
- Published: 2024-12-18
- Source type: official food-safety / regulatory alert
- URL: https://www.fda.gov/food/hfp-constituent-updates/fda-alerts-industry-and-consumers-about-use-amanita-muscaria-or-its-constituents-food
- Ingested: 2026-05-03

## Summary

FDA alerted industry and consumers that Amanita muscaria, its extracts, and certain constituents including muscimol, ibotenic acid, and muscarine are not authorized for use as ingredients in conventional food. FDA concluded that they do not meet the GRAS standard and are unapproved food additives, and recommended that people avoid eating foods with these ingredients.

FDA said the agency reviewed safety information because of adverse event reports, news reports, and state/local regulatory inquiries. FDA also pointed to the Bad Bug Book's toxic-agent description of A. muscaria poisoning after ingestion.

## Notes

- This is the central regulatory anchor for Amanita / muscimol edible claims.
- Any "legal mushroom nootropic" framing should route through this safety-first source before anecdote or product marketing is considered.
- The FDA alert concerns food use and conventional food ingredients; dietary-supplement assessment was described as a broader ongoing evaluation.
