# EMA 2013: Restrictions on ergot-derivative medicines

EMA's Committee for Medicinal Products for Human Use recommended restrictions on medicines containing ergot derivatives on 2013-06-27.

Scope: the referral covered substances including dihydroergocristine, dihydroergotamine, dihydroergotoxine, nicergoline, and combinations of dihydroergocryptine with caffeine.

Safety conclusion: the committee recommended that these medicines should no longer be used for several blood-circulation, memory/sensation, or migraine-prevention indications because the risks were greater than benefits, based on increased risk of fibrosis and ergotism. EMA noted that in some EU member states some ergot derivatives were also authorized for other indications such as dementia treatment or acute migraine treatment.

Interpretation for the wiki: this is a major safety/regulatory gate for nicergoline and adjacent ergot-derived nootropics. It does not erase old dementia evidence, but it prevents casual use as a broad nootropic or blood-flow supplement.
