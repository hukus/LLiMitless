# Ginkgo biloba leaf extract EGb 761® for the treatment of various diseases: Overview of systematic reviews

- PMID: 40121884
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40121884/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

2025 overview of systematic reviews for the proprietary ginkgo extract EGb 761. Neurocognitive reviews were mostly favorable for cognition and behavioral symptoms, but the overview repeatedly flags poor AMSTAR-2 quality and heavy overlap across reviews, so the safety/efficacy signal should be treated cautiously despite the standardized extract.
