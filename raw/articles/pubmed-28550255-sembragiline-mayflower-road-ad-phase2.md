# Sembragiline in moderate Alzheimer's disease: MAyflOwer RoAD phase 2 trial

- PMID: 28550255
- PMCID: PMC5523913
- DOI: 10.3233/JAD-161309
- Journal: Journal of Alzheimer's Disease (2017)
- Trial registration: NCT01677754

## Captured takeaways

- Sembragiline is described as a potent, selective, long-acting reversible MAO-B inhibitor.
- The phase 2 trial enrolled 542 patients with moderate Alzheimer's disease, MMSE 13 to 20, on background acetylcholinesterase inhibitor with or without memantine.
- Patients received sembragiline 1 mg, sembragiline 5 mg, or placebo for 52 weeks.
- The primary endpoint, ADAS-Cog11, was not met.
- ADAS-Cog11 differences at Week 52 were -0.15 with p = 0.865 for 1 mg and 0.90 with p = 0.312 for 5 mg.
- ADCS-ADL was also not significant.
- Neuropsychiatric symptoms on BEHAVE-AD-FW improved at Week 52 in both active arms, but this was not a primary cognition win.
- The drug was generally well tolerated.
- Interpretation for the wiki: sembragiline is an important target-engagement-to-efficacy failure case for MAO-B Alzheimer's cognition claims.
