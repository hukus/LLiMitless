# PubMed 24791752 oral glutathione body-stores RCT note

2015 European Journal of Nutrition randomized double-blind placebo-controlled trial titled "Randomized controlled trial of oral glutathione supplementation on body stores of glutathione."

Fifty-four non-smoking healthy adults received oral glutathione at 250 mg/day or 1000 mg/day, or placebo, for 6 months. Outcomes included glutathione levels in blood, erythrocytes, plasma, lymphocytes, and buccal cells; a subset also had immune markers.

Reported results: blood glutathione rose after 1, 3, and 6 months versus baseline at both doses. At 6 months, the high-dose group showed roughly 30-35% increases in erythrocytes, plasma, and lymphocytes, and a larger buccal-cell increase; lower-dose blood and erythrocyte increases were also reported. The oxidized-to-reduced glutathione ratio decreased after 6 months, and natural killer cytotoxicity increased in the high-dose group versus placebo at 3 months. Levels returned to baseline after washout.

Interpretation for the wiki: oral reduced glutathione can move peripheral glutathione/body-store biomarkers in healthy adults, but this was not a cognition trial. Do not convert peripheral redox movement into brain-fog or cognition efficacy without separate evidence.
