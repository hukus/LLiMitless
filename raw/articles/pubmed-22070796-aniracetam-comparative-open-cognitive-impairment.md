# Clinical efficacy of aniracetam, either as monotherapy or combined with cholinesterase inhibitors, in patients with cognitive impairment: a comparative open study

- PMID: 22070796
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22070796/
- Ingested: 2026-04-30

## Summary

2012 prospective open-label comparative study in 276 patients with cognitive disorders, including no-treatment, aniracetam monotherapy, cholinesterase-inhibitor monotherapy, and combination groups. The primary text describes aniracetam as a fixed 1500 mg/day dose taken from prescribing information. Aniracetam-maintained neuropsychological parameters at 6 and 12 months, improved emotional state at 3 months, and outperformed the comparator groups in some mild-dementia subgroup analyses. Because the study was open-label and nonrandomized, it is supportive but weak as efficacy proof.
