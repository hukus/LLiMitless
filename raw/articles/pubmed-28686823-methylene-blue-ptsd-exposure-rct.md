# Enhancing Extinction Learning in Posttraumatic Stress Disorder With Brief Daily Imaginal Exposure and Methylene Blue: A Randomized Controlled Trial

- PMID: 28686823
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28686823/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Randomized PTSD exposure-therapy trial in 42 chronic PTSD patients. After five daily 50-minute imaginal exposure sessions, 260 mg oral methylene blue or placebo was given. MB-augmented exposure improved independent-evaluator-rated response and quality of life versus placebo, with NNT 7.5 and effect size d = 0.58, and the authors reported that better working memory helped MB's benefit. The finding supports a memory-consolidation use case, not broad baseline enhancement.
