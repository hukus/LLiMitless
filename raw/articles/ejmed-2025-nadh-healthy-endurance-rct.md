# EJMED 2025 oral NADH healthy endurance trial

This 2025 European Journal of Medical and Health Sciences article reported a double-blind, placebo-controlled randomized study of oral NADH in healthy untrained young adults. The authors framed NADH as a coenzyme involved in cellular energy production and redox regulation and stated that direct effects on exercise endurance and biochemical markers in healthy individuals were understudied.

Design: 16 healthy male medical students aged 20-22 years were assigned to sublingual NADH 40 mg/day or placebo for 3 weeks. Physical endurance was tested with the PWC170 cycle ergometer protocol to estimate VO2max. Heart rate, blood pressure, pulse pressure, and leukocyte NADH, NAD+, ATP, and phospho-AMPKalpha1 were measured.

Result: the NADH group reportedly improved PWC170 by 25.6% and VO2max by 23.9% versus baseline and placebo (p<0.05), with improved heart-rate recovery and lower systolic blood pressure. Leukocyte NADH, NAD+, ATP, and phospho-AMPKalpha1 did not significantly change.

Interpretation for the wiki: this is a small healthy-adult physical-endurance proxy, not a cognition endpoint. It is useful for state-reserve hypotheses but should not upgrade cognitive confidence.
