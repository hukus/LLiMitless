# FDA GRN No. 791: Urolithin A

- FDA GRAS notice: GRN No. 791
- Notifier: Amazentis SA
- Date of filing: 2018-06-15
- Date of closure: 2018-12-20
- Page last updated: 2026-04-30
- Ingested: 2026-05-03

## Summary

FDA GRAS inventory entry for urolithin A. The intended uses are ingredient use in protein shakes, beverages, meal-replacement beverages, instant oatmeal, protein/nutrition bars, and yogurts at typical levels of 250 mg or 500 mg per serving, up to 500 mg or 1000 mg per serving depending on food category.

The notifier is Amazentis SA, and the basis is scientific procedures. FDA's response letter says the agency has no questions about the notifier's GRAS conclusion for the specified uses.

Use in the wiki: food-ingredient safety/status anchor for urolithin A under specified use conditions. It is not FDA approval for cognition, anti-aging efficacy, or all commercial supplement products.
