# Retraction: Effects of folic acid combined with DHA supplementation on cognitive function and amyloid-beta-related biomarkers in older adults with MCI

- DOI: 10.3233/JAD-239002
- Published online: 2023-04-25
- Source type: retraction notice
- Original publication: Journal of Alzheimer's Disease 81(1), 2021, 155-167, DOI 10.3233/JAD-200997
- PubMed: 37125556
- Ingested: 2026-05-03

## Summary

Retraction notice for a folic acid plus DHA MCI trial publication. The notice states that ethical approval and clinical-trial registry issues were not of sufficient standard, and that the authors were given the opportunity to resubmit with clarification and possible subject-level or flow-sheet checks.

## Notes

- This does not automatically invalidate every folic acid plus DHA claim, but it raises the credibility gate for the FA+DHA lineage.
- Any wiki confidence upgrade for folic acid plus DHA should verify trial registration, ethics approval, flow sheet, and independence from earlier studies.
- Keep the 2026 NMA folic-acid/DHA signal lower-confidence until the source lineage is reconciled.
