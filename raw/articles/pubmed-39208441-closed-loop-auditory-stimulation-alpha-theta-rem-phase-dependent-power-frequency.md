# Closed-loop auditory stimulation targeting alpha and theta oscillations during rapid eye movement sleep induces phase-dependent power and frequency changes

- PMID: 39208441
- Published: 2024-12-11
- Study type: human sleep physiology experiment
- Population: 18 healthy young adults

## Captured takeaways

- CLAS during REM sleep was phase-locked to alpha or theta oscillations detected in a frontal electrode.
- The stimulation produced phase-dependent changes in power and frequency at the target location.
- Effects were observed in both phasic and tonic REM sleep.
- This supports the broader claim that within-sleep phase matters for brain-state targeting, even when the outcome is electrophysiologic rather than behavioral.
- For the wiki, this is a strong timing/phase anchor but not a direct cognition-efficacy result.
