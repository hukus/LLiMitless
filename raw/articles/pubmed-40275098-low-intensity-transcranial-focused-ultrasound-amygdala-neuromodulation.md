# Low-intensity transcranial focused ultrasound amygdala neuromodulation: a double-blind sham-controlled target engagement study and unblinded single-arm clinical trial

- PMID: 40275098
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40275098/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

MRI-guided tFUS to the left amygdala was tested in a sham-controlled target engagement study and then in a short unblinded treatment trial. The report shows reduced left amygdala BOLD, patient-level differences in related regions, symptom improvement in the open treatment phase, and no serious adverse events. Useful as a clean example of target engagement plus biomarker response, though it is not a cognition trial.
