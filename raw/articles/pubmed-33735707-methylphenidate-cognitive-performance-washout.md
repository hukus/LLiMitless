# Do effects of methylphenidate on cognitive performance last beyond treatment? A randomized placebo-controlled trial in boys and men with ADHD

- PMID: 33735707
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33735707/
- Ingested: 2026-04-29

## Summary

Randomized placebo-controlled trial in boys and adult men with ADHD evaluated cognition after 16 weeks of methylphenidate treatment and a 1-week washout. Outcomes included working memory, response inhibition, response speed, episodic memory, and delay aversion.

MPH improved working memory and response speed during treatment, but the benefits did not persist after washout. The authors concluded that the cognitive effects were transient and did not extend beyond treatment.

## Notes

- This is one of the cleanest direct rebuttals to the idea that repeated stimulant treatment necessarily leaves a durable cognitive residue after stopping.
- It also supports treating washout as a distinct state rather than an inert control.
