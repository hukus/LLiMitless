# Tinospora cordifolia (Giloy)-Induced Liver Injury During the COVID-19 Pandemic: Multicenter Nationwide Study From India

- Published: 2022
- Journal: Hepatology Communications
- Evidence layer: multicenter retrospective case series / safety

## Summary

This multicenter Indian retrospective study examined liver injury temporally associated with Giloy/Tinospora cordifolia use during the COVID-19 pandemic. The final analysis included 43 patients from 13 centers at nine locations. Patients presented with acute hepatitis, acute worsening of chronic liver disease, or acute liver failure. Causality assessment judged liver injury as probable in 67.4% of cases.

The study reported autoimmune-like features in some patients and performed toxicological analysis on retrieved samples. It concluded that Giloy-induced liver injury was a clinically important signal during the COVID-era immune-booster surge. This makes Tinospora a safety-first traditional-medicine branch.
