# Effects of a single dose of tablets containing lactononadecapeptide on cognitive function in healthy adults

- DOI: 10.1093/bbb/zbaa117
- Published: 2021
- Ingested: 2026-05-11

## Summary

Randomized, double-blind, crossover, placebo-controlled trial of tablets containing lactononadecapeptide, sequence NIPPLTQTPVVVPPFLQPE, in healthy Japanese men aged 30-59. The Uchida-Kraepelin test was used to induce cognitive load, and cognition was evaluated with the Japanese CNS Vital Signs battery.

Composite memory and verbal memory were significantly higher after lactononadecapeptide than placebo. Because carryover effects were observed in attention and concentration domains, period-1 data were analyzed; in that analysis, processing speed, executive function, and cognitive flexibility were higher after lactononadecapeptide.

## Relevance

Adds an acute tablet-format source to [[fermented-milk-lactononadecapeptide]], but the carryover issue makes it a supporting source rather than a high-confidence upgrade.

## Caveats

- Healthy Japanese male sample.
- Carryover effects complicate interpretation.
- Tablet exposure may not match fermented milk exposure.
- Manufacturer involvement and product identity require disclosure.
