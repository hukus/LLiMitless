# Rescue of cognitive aging by administration of a neurogenic and/or neurotrophic compound

- PMID: 24702821
- DOI: 10.1016/j.neurobiolaging.2014.02.017
- Journal: Neurobiology of Aging (2014)
- Model: 22- to 24-month-old Fischer rats

## Summary

Chronic oral P021 treatment reduced age-associated learning and memory deficits in old rats. The study also reported increased BDNF, improved neurogenesis and synaptic-marker expression, and lower hippocampal myo-inositol relative to untreated aged animals.

## Evidence boundary

This is an animal cognitive-aging study, not evidence of human efficacy, oral bioavailability, or a human dose. The work comes from the same core P021 research program represented in most of the Alzheimer-model literature.
