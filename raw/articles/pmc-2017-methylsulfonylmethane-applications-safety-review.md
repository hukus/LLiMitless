# Methylsulfonylmethane applications and safety review

2017 open-access Nutrients review of methylsulfonylmethane / MSM applications and safety.

Key capture:

- MSM is described as a popular dietary supplement used most commonly as an anti-inflammatory agent, especially in joint, muscle, oxidative-stress, and antioxidant-capacity contexts.
- The review summarizes animal, human clinical, and experimental literature, with the best practical evidence centered on pain, inflammation, oxidative stress, and antioxidant-capacity proxies rather than cognition.
- The abstract and review frame MSM as generally well tolerated at up to about 4 g/day, with few known and usually mild side effects, while also emphasizing that dose/time-course optimization still needs work.
- The paper is useful but not neutral for product interpretation: at least one author affiliation / support trail is tied to the MSM ingredient industry, so claims should be treated as supportive background rather than a high-confidence cognition proof.

Wiki use: use this as broad human-safety and mechanism context for [[methylsulfonylmethane-msm]]. It does not establish a brain-fog or healthy-adult cognition benefit.
