# GlyNAC older-adults open-label cognition pilot

Open-label 36-week pilot clinical trial in eight older adults and eight young-adult comparators. Older adults were assessed before supplementation, after 12 and 24 weeks of GlyNAC, and after 12 weeks of withdrawal. The exposure was the combination of glycine and N-acetylcysteine as glutathione precursors.

Key cognition details: cognitive testing included MoCA, Trails A, Trails B, verbal fluency, and digit-symbol substitution. The abstract reports that 24 weeks of GlyNAC corrected RBC glutathione deficiency, oxidative stress, and mitochondrial dysfunction, and improved inflammation, endothelial dysfunction, insulin resistance, genomic damage, cognition, strength, gait speed, exercise capacity, body fat, and waist circumference. Benefits declined after 12 weeks off GlyNAC.

Useful numeric anchors from the paper table: MoCA in older adults was about 26.0 at baseline, 28.6 after 12 weeks, 28.8 after 24 weeks, and 27.5 after withdrawal. Trails A was about 45.2 seconds at baseline, 37.3 seconds after 12 weeks, 32.1 seconds after 24 weeks, and 33.4 seconds after withdrawal.

Use in wiki: this is the main direct human cognition anchor for GlyNAC, but it is small, open-label, older-adult-only, and not a blinded efficacy trial.
