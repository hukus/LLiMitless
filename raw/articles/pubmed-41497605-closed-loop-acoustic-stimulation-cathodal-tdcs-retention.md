# Closed-loop acoustic stimulation modified by cathodal tDCS is beneficial for retention

bioRxiv preprint from 2025 in 23 young adults. The study compared closed-loop acoustic stimulation alone with acoustic stimulation combined with cathodal tDCS during slow-wave sleep. The combined condition improved overnight retention on a figural paired-associate task, whereas CLAS alone did not.

This is important because it suggests closed-loop sleep stimulation may become more effective when the cortical state is altered on purpose. The caveat is that it is still a small preprint, and the effect is task-specific rather than a broad cognition claim.
