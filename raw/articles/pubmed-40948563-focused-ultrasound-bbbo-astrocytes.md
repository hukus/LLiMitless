# Focused ultrasound-induced blood-brain barrier opening promotes glioprotective phenotypes in ACSA-II+ murine astrocytes

- PMID: 40948563
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40948563/
- PMCID: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12432449/
- Published: 2025-07-22
- Ingested: 2026-04-30

## Summary

Single-cell RNA sequencing and flow cytometry in aged wild-type and Alzheimer's disease mice found that FUS-BBBO shifts hippocampal astrocytes toward a glioprotective and repair-associated transcriptional program over days 2-7 after exposure. The study is mechanistic and preclinical, but it adds biological plausibility to the idea that BBB opening may alter local glial state in ways that could support downstream delivery or recovery. It does not show human cognition benefit.
