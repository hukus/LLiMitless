# Non-hallucinogenic psychedelics for mood and anxiety disorders: A systematic review

- PMID: 40354769
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40354769/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

2025 systematic review of non-hallucinogenic psychedelics for mood and anxiety disorders. The review found five animal studies with antidepressant-like effects without head-twitch response plus one case report in which trazodone coadministration appeared to suppress psychedelic effects while preserving antidepressant benefit. No human randomized cognition or mood trials of a bona fide non-hallucinogenic psychoplastogen were identified in the review.
