# PubMed 9169302 controlled inositol psychiatry trials note

1997 European Neuropsychopharmacology review/article titled "Controlled trials of inositol in psychiatry." It summarizes small controlled inositol trials across depression, panic disorder, OCD, schizophrenia, Alzheimer's disease, ADHD-like symptoms, autism, and ECT-induced memory impairment.

Key positive psychiatric signals: 12 g/day inositol for 4 weeks showed benefit on Hamilton Depression Scale in 28 depressed patients; 12 g/day for 4 weeks reduced panic attack frequency/severity and agoraphobia severity in 21 panic-disorder patients; 18 g/day improved OCD symptoms in 13 OCD patients.

Key cognition-relevant counterweights: 6 g/day in 11 Alzheimer patients showed no clearly significant therapeutic effect, and 6 g/day around ECT did not improve ECT-induced memory impairment. Inositol also did not help schizophrenia, ADHD-like symptoms, or autism in the summarized small controlled trials.

Interpretation for the wiki: plain myo-inositol has a mood/anxiety/OCD-state signal in old small studies, but this is not a direct healthy-adult nootropic packet. It can be relevant to brain fog when anxiety, panic, sleep, PCOS/insulin signaling, or mood state dominates subjective cognition, but direct cognition claims should be gated.
