# Does repetitive transcranial magnetic stimulation improve cognitive function in age-related neurodegenerative diseases? A systematic review and meta-analysis

- PMID: 37526325
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37526325/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Systematic review/meta-analysis of DLPFC-targeted, high-frequency rTMS in age-related neurodegenerative disease. The abstract reports a significant improvement in global cognition with a large pooled effect, but also notes high between-study heterogeneity and no clear subgroup separation for individual cognitive domains.
