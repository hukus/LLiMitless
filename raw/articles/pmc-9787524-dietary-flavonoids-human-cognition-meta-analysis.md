# Dietary Flavonoids and Human Cognition: A Meta-Analysis

- Source type: open-access meta-analysis of randomized controlled trials.
- Publication: Molecular Nutrition & Food Research, 2022.
- Date checked: 2026-05-03.

This meta-analysis evaluated randomized controlled trials of dietary flavonoids and cognition across the lifespan. Eighty studies with 5,519 participants were included. The global analysis found a small significant cognitive benefit, with cocoa, ginkgo, and berries among the stronger source subgroups.

The authors reported sensitivity in long-term memory, processing speed, and mood, with larger effects in chronic studies, middle-aged and older adults, and low-to-medium dose ranges. The effect size was small, and the source/product subgroup matters.

Use in the wiki: broad flavonoid anchor for cocoa and berry branches. It supports watchlist visibility, not broad nootropic ranking.
