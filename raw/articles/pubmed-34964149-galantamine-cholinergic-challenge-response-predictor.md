Title: Acute response to cholinergic challenge predicts long-term response to galantamine treatment in patients with Alzheimer's disease

This is the strongest small-scale responder-biomarker paper in the galantamine branch. A single-dose galantamine challenge produced acute CNS and EEG changes, and those acute pharmacodynamic shifts distinguished 6-month responders from non-responders. It is not prospective enrichment, but it is a concrete example of a biomarker-like response marker linked to later cognitive benefit.

Caveat: the sample is small, the challenge design is specific to galantamine, and the responder definition combined MMSE, NPI, and DAD rather than a pure cognition-only endpoint.
