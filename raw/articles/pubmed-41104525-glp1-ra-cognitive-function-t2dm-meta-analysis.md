# Effects of GLP-1 receptor agonists on cognitive function in patients with type 2 diabetes: A systematic review and meta-analysis based on randomized controlled trials

- PMID: 41104525
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41104525/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

2026 meta-analysis of 18 RCTs in T2DM found small but statistically significant improvements in MMSE and MoCA with GLP-1 receptor agonists. The signal was stronger with longer treatment duration, which makes it one of the clearest direct human cognition signals in the incretin branch.
