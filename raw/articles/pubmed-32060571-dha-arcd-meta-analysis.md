# DHA age-related cognitive decline meta-analysis

2020 systematic review and meta-analysis of DHA supplementation for age-related cognitive decline. The review included 10 studies and 2,327 elderly individuals.

Main result: pooled analyses showed minimal or no incremental effects of DHA supplementation on memory, attention, working memory, or executive function. Standardized between-group results were similarly small or nonsignificant. Sensitivity and subgroup analyses did not materially change the conclusions.

Conclusion reported by the abstract: current evidence did not support DHA supplementation for preventing or retarding age-related cognitive decline across memory, executive function, attention, and working memory.

Use in wiki: counterweight to MIDAS and the 2026 nutrient NMA. DHA/EPA should remain low-confidence and domain-/phenotype-bound.
