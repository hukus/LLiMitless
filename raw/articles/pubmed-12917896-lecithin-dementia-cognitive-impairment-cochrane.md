# Lecithin for dementia and cognitive impairment

- PMID: 12917896
- DOI: 10.1002/14651858.CD001015
- Journal: Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews
- Year: 2003
- Ingested: 2026-05-04

## Summary

Cochrane review of randomized trials comparing lecithin with placebo for dementia or cognitive impairment. The review identified 12 randomized trials involving Alzheimer's disease, Parkinsonian dementia, and subjective memory problems. No clear clinical benefit was found for Alzheimer's disease or Parkinsonian dementia. A dramatic result was reported in one small subjective-memory-problem trial, but the authors concluded that randomized evidence did not support lecithin as a dementia treatment and did not make lecithin a high-priority target for a large trial.

## Notes

- Use as the impairment-context counterweight for lecithin claims.
- The subjective-memory-problem outlier should be treated as hypothesis-generating only.
- Do not infer dementia benefit from phosphatidylcholine mechanism or choline bioavailability.
