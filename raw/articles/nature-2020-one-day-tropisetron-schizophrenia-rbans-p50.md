# One-day tropisetron schizophrenia RBANS / P50 trial

2020 Neuropsychopharmacology randomized double-blind study of one-day tropisetron treatment in schizophrenia.

## Captured takeaways

- Participants: 40 non-smoking schizophrenia patients with P50 ratios above 0.5, taking risperidone 3-6 mg/day before the experiment.
- Design: placebo, 5 mg/day, 10 mg/day, or 20 mg/day oral tropisetron groups.
- Outcomes: Repeatable Battery for the Assessment of Neuropsychological Status and P50 inhibition measured before and one day after treatment.
- After one day, RBANS total scores were higher in the 20 mg and 5 mg tropisetron groups than placebo, and immediate memory was higher in the 10 mg group.
- P50 ratio was lower in the 5 mg and 10 mg groups than placebo after treatment.
- The study linked cognitive improvement and P50 changes, but it was a one-day disease-state experiment rather than an enhancement trial.

## Wiki interpretation

- Use as a second schizophrenia-specific cognition/P50 anchor for [[tropisetron]].
- The dose-response pattern is not a simple "more is better" story.
- Do not generalize the one-day result to rested healthy adults.
