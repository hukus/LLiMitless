# The human brain utilizes lactate via the tricarboxylic acid cycle: a 13C-labelled microdialysis and high-resolution nuclear magnetic resonance study

- PMID: 19700417
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19700417/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Direct human TBI study showing labeled lactate could be traced into brain metabolites consistent with TCA-cycle utilization. This is one of the clearest human demonstrations that the brain can oxidize lactate.
