# WISH long-term soy isoflavone cognition RCT full-text details

The 2012 Women's Isoflavone Soy Health trial randomized 350 healthy postmenopausal women to 25 g/day isoflavone-rich soy protein or milk-protein placebo for 2.5 years. The soy product delivered 91 mg/day aglycone-weight isoflavones: 52 mg genistein, 36 mg daidzein, and 3 mg glycitein. The trial was double-blind, placebo-controlled, and had high adherence.

Primary cognitive endpoint: no significant between-group difference in change in global cognition. Secondary analyses suggested greater improvement on a visual-memory factor, but not on the other cognitive factors or individual tests. The authors classified the evidence as Class I that long-term isoflavone-rich soy protein did not improve global cognition in healthy postmenopausal women.
