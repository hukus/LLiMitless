# Prebiotic and Probiotic Modulation of the Gut Microbiota-gut-brain Axis During Acute Stress

- ClinicalTrials.gov ID: NCT05392556
- Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05392556
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Completed double-blind randomized parallel-arm study in healthy young adults comparing placebo, probiotic, and prebiotic during military-relevant acute stress. The registry is useful for protocol design and indicates the field is still testing stress-resilience and cognition together, but no results were posted in the source record.
