# Naftidrofuryl vascular or mixed dementia randomized trial

PubMed record for Emeriau, Lehert, and Mosnier, "Efficacy of naftidrofuryl in patients with vascular or mixed dementia: results of a multicenter, double-blind trial," Clinical Therapeutics, 2000 Jul;22(7):834-44, DOI 10.1016/S0149-2918(00)80056-9.

The trial tested naftidrofuryl 600 mg/day versus placebo for 1 year in patients with vascular or mixed dementia. It used a 2-month washout period to select compliant patients. Endpoints included change on the Alzheimer Disease Assessment Scale cognitive subscale and the Mini-Mental State Examination.

Eighty-four patients were assessable on an intent-to-treat basis, and 74 were assessable in the per-protocol analysis. The abstract reports statistically significant improvements in cognitive and global function in patients receiving naftidrofuryl. Naftidrofuryl was described as well tolerated, with no clinically significant laboratory abnormalities.

Relevance: a positive but small old dementia-state RCT. It supports legacy vascular/mixed-dementia tracking, not broad healthy-adult enhancement.
