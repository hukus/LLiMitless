# Vitamin B6 cognition Cochrane review

- PMID: 14584010
- Title: The effect of vitamin B6 on cognition
- Journal: Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews
- Publication year: 2003
- Source type: systematic review / cognition counterweight
- Checked: 2026-05-16
- Ingested: 2026-05-16

## Summary

This Cochrane review asked whether vitamin B6 supplementation reduces the risk of cognitive impairment in healthy older people or improves cognitive function in people with cognitive decline or dementia.

The review found no eligible trials in people with cognitive impairment or dementia. It included two double-blind randomized placebo-controlled trials in healthy older adults, totaling 109 older participants in the review's cognition-relevant comparison.

For older women in the Bryan 2002 arm, 75 mg/day vitamin B6 for 5 weeks did not show statistically significant mood or cognition benefit versus placebo. For older men in the Deijen 1992 study, 20 mg/day pyridoxine hydrochloride for 12 weeks also did not show statistically significant mood or cognition differences versus placebo.

The Deijen trial improved biochemical vitamin B6 indices, including plasma pyridoxal-5'-phosphate, but neither trial measured homocysteine. No adverse effects were reported in the included trials.
