# Improved human visuomotor performance and pupil constriction after choline supplementation in a placebo-controlled double-blind study

- DOI: 10.1038/srep13188
- Journal: Scientific Reports
- Year: 2015
- Ingested: 2026-05-04

## Summary

Placebo-controlled double-blind study in 28 healthy individuals. Participants ingested 2 g choline bitartrate or placebo in separate sessions. About 70 minutes later they performed a visuomotor aiming task requiring rapid target hits. Choline supplementation improved target precision, reduced pupil size, and shifted performance toward action precision rather than speed. Reaction-time interpretation was not a broad cognition win; the signal was a narrow visuomotor precision / cholinergic biomarker result.

## Notes

- Use as the best direct positive acute choline-bitartrate signal.
- The endpoint is motor-control precision, not memory, executive function, or durable cognitive enhancement.
- Pupil-size change supports cholinergic engagement but does not prove central memory benefit.
