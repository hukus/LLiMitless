# Hydergine for dementia

PubMed-indexed Cochrane-style review of Hydergine / ergoloid mesylates for dementia. The abstract explains that Hydergine was widely used in some countries to treat cognitive symptoms of aging and dementia and that earlier reviews suggested a modest effect in a subset of patients.

For the wiki, this is legacy dementia evidence rather than modern nootropic proof. The source is relevant because hydergine appears in older smart-drug communities and older dementia pharmacology.

Relevance: route hydergine as an old clinical-state / vasoactive comparator with low modern confidence.
