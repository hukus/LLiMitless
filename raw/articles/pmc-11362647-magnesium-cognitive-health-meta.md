# Magnesium and cognitive health in adults

- PMCID: PMC11362647
- Published: 2024
- Source type: systematic review and meta-analysis
- Checked: 2026-05-11
- Ingested: 2026-05-11

## Summary

This systematic review and meta-analysis evaluated magnesium exposure and adult cognitive health. The useful wiki interpretation is conservative: observational studies tend to support an association between higher magnesium intake or status and better cognitive outcomes, but the limited randomized trial evidence is not enough to infer a reliable supplement effect.

Use this as the generic-magnesium counterweight to the product-specific [[magnesium-l-threonate]] page. It supports tracking magnesium as a status/sleep/metabolic gate, while blocking broad claims that any magnesium form improves cognition.

## Notes

- Separate dietary magnesium, serum magnesium, supplemental magnesium, and product-specific Magtein.
- Track sleep, migraine, muscle symptoms, bowel effects, kidney function, and medication interactions before interpreting subjective brain-fog changes.
