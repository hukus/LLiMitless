# Allopregnanolone imaging follow-up
- PMID: 35310526
- Exploratory MRI analysis of the phase 1b/2a allopregnanolone AD cohort (24 participants total).
- Key result: hippocampal atrophy slowed or reversed in the allopregnanolone group, with gains most evident at 4 mg and in APOE epsilon4 carriers.
- White-matter integrity and functional-connectivity measures also shifted in the favorable direction, but this remained exploratory.
- Branch readout: helpful structural support for phase 2, not proof of cognitive efficacy.
