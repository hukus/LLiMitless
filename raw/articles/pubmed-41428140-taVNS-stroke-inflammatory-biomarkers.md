# Transcutaneous Auricular Vagus Nerve Stimulation Reduces Inflammatory Biomarkers after Large Vessel Occlusion Stroke: Results of a Prospective Randomized Open-Label Blinded Endpoint Trial

- PMID: 41428140
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41428140/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Randomized sham-controlled stroke trial in anterior circulation large-vessel occlusion AIS, with twice-daily taVNS for five days or until discharge. Primary endpoints were inflammatory biomarkers including IL-1β, IL-6, IL-10, IL-17α, and TNFα.

## Notes

- This is strong disease-context support for the anti-inflammatory branch of taVNS.
- It does not generalize to healthy-adult cognitive enhancement, but it does make the inflammation mechanism more credible than the cognition mechanism.
- The paper is a published follow-up to the earlier preprint result.
