# Geriatric Patients Are at a High Risk of Hypokalemia Associated with Yokukansan Preparation: A Retrospective Cohort Study

- PMID: 33132320
- Published: 2020
- Evidence layer: retrospective cohort / safety

## Summary

This retrospective cohort study examined hypokalemia associated with yokukansan preparation in 665 patients with a median age of 78 years. Hypokalemia, defined as serum potassium below 3.0 mEq/L, occurred in 55 patients (8.3%). Risk factors in the PubMed abstract included older age, lower serum albumin, and daily yokukansan preparation dose of at least 7.5 g.

The mechanism is relevant because yokukansan contains Glycyrrhiza/licorice, whose glycyrrhizin metabolites can produce pseudoaldosteronism-like mineralocorticoid effects. This safety signal makes potassium, blood pressure, edema/weakness symptoms, dose, age, and co-use of other licorice-containing products important gates for any yokukansan cognition discussion.
