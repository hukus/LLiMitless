# FDA BMPEA dietary-supplement status note

FDA's BMPEA dietary-supplement page states that BMPEA does not meet the statutory definition of a dietary ingredient and that products declaring BMPEA as a dietary supplement are misbranded. FDA lists synonyms including beta-methylphenethylamine, R-beta-methylphenethylamine, beta-methylphenylethylamine, and 2-phenylpropan-1-amine.

Wiki use: BMPEA is not identical to [[phenethylamine]], but it is an important nearby supplement-market boundary. PEA-like and Acacia-rigidula-style stimulant products need identity checks because substituted phenethylamine analogs can appear in dietary-supplement contexts without the evidence or regulatory status of ordinary nutrients.
