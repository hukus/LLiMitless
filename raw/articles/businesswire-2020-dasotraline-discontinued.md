# Sunovion discontinues dasotraline program

- Source: Business Wire / Sunovion press release
- Date: 2020-05-13

## Captured takeaways

- Sunovion announced withdrawal of New Drug Applications for dasotraline in moderate-to-severe binge eating disorder and ADHD.
- The company described dasotraline as a dopamine and norepinephrine reuptake inhibitor.
- Sunovion stated that further clinical studies would be needed to support regulatory approval for those indications.
- The company said it would not pursue further development of dasotraline at that time.
- Interpretation for the wiki: dasotraline should be filed as a discontinued development comparator, not a live clinical option or community-access nootropic.
