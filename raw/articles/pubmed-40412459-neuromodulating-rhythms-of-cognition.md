# Neuromodulating the rhythms of cognition

Neurosci Biobehav Rev 2025 review of rhythmic non-invasive brain stimulation. The paper distinguishes oscillatory synchronization from frequency-shifting and separates online entrainment effects from offline plasticity. It argues that protocol details and individual differences are central to interpreting cognition results.

This is not an efficacy trial, but it provides the conceptual vocabulary for reading closed-loop and adaptive stimulation studies without collapsing rhythm control into generic enhancement claims.
