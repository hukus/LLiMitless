# Efficacy and Safety of Xanomeline-Trospium Chloride in Schizophrenia: A Randomized Clinical Trial

- PMID: 38691387
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38691387/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

Phase 3 EMERGENT-3 in acute schizophrenia. Xanomeline-trospium produced an 8.4-point greater reduction in PANSS total score than placebo at week 5, with a Cohen d of 0.60. The adverse-event profile was mainly cholinergic and gastrointestinal: nausea, dyspepsia, vomiting, constipation, hypertension, and transient blood-pressure/heart-rate increases were more common than placebo, but no dose-limiting toxicity or QTc signal emerged in the report.
