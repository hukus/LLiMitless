# Ritalin methylphenidate DailyMed label

DailyMed label snapshot for Ritalin / methylphenidate hydrochloride tablets, updated 2025-02-05 and revised 2025-02.

Safety-relevant label points for cognition routing: Ritalin is a Schedule II CNS stimulant indicated for ADHD and narcolepsy. The boxed warning emphasizes abuse, misuse, addiction, overdose, and death risk. The label warns about serious cardiac disease, increased blood pressure and heart rate, psychiatric adverse reactions including psychotic or manic symptoms, priapism, peripheral vasculopathy/Raynaud's phenomenon, glaucoma and intraocular pressure concerns, and tic/Tourette worsening.

Interpretation for the wiki: methylphenidate can be a legitimate prescription comparator or supervised post-COVID symptom-support candidate, but its label-level risk and misuse burden make it inappropriate as a casual nootropic recommendation.
