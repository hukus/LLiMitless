# Impact of Pomegranate Extract Supplementation on Physical and Cognitive Function in Community-Dwelling Older Adults Aged 55-70 Years: A Randomised Double-Blind Clinical Trial

- PMID: 39997528
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39997528/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

12-week randomized, double-blind trial in 86 community-dwelling adults aged 55-70 years. Participants received 740 mg/day pomegranate extract or placebo. The only clear between-group cognitive signal was a time-by-treatment effect on WCST performance; digit span, Tower of Hanoi, and Stroop showed favorable trends. The extract was well tolerated. This is promising but still thin and domain-limited.
