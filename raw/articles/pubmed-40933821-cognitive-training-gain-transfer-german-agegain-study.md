# Cognitive training gain transfer in cognitively healthy aging: per protocol results of the German AgeGain study

- PMID: 40933821
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40933821/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

Per-protocol analysis of a cognitive training intervention in healthy aging focused on transfer rather than only practice gain. The study explicitly aims to demonstrate training transfer and identify neurobiological modulators of successful transfer, making it a good functional-transfer anchor for the ladder.
