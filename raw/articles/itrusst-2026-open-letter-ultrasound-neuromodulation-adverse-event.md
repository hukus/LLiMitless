# ITRUSST open letter on intervention regimes and adverse events in focused ultrasound neuromodulation

- Source: https://www.itrusst.com/open-letter
- Ingested: 2026-05-12

## Summary

ITRUSST 2026 open letter page responding to a recently reported adverse event in a clinical trial that used low-frequency ultrasound outside the low-intensity domain. The page states that the letter aims to provide shared expert perspective on distinguishing low-, intermediate-, and high-intensity focused-ultrasound regimes, assessing cavitation risk across those regimes, and improving collaboration and detailed reporting for safe development. The page lists 160 signees at time of capture.
