# Acute donepezil in healthy young adults and executive function

Double-blind, placebo-controlled independent-groups study in 42 young healthy male participants randomized to placebo, donepezil 5 mg, or donepezil 7.5 mg. The test battery separated shifting, updating, inhibition, dual-task performance, planning, long-term-memory access, arousal, vigilance, visuomotor performance, and working-memory subsystems.

Donepezil improved sustained attention, reaction times, dual-task performance, and the executive component of digit span. The dual-task effect could not be explained by arousal, vigilance, visuomotor performance, or working-memory slave-system changes in the authors' interpretation.

Interpretation for the wiki: this is a small acute healthy-young signal for selective executive components, but it does not resolve chronic tolerance, adverse effects, or broad enhancement.
