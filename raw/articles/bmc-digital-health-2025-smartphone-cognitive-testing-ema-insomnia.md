# Feasibility of cognitive testing and ecological momentary assessments using smartphones in middle aged and older adults with insomnia

- DOI: 10.1186/s44247-025-00158-4
- Article: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s44247-025-00158-4
- Published: 2025-07-01
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

2025 feasibility study in 20 adults aged 55 and older with insomnia. Daily drowsiness EMA plus weekly smartphone digit span and verbal paired-associate testing was feasible over 28 days, but completion was imperfect and some participants used strategies that could invalidate results. This is measurement-layer evidence, not cognition efficacy evidence.
