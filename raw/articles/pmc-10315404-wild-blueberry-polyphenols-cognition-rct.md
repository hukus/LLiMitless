# Wild blueberry polyphenols can improve vascular function and cognitive performance in healthy older individuals

- Source type: open-access randomized double-blind placebo-controlled trial.
- Publication: American Journal of Clinical Nutrition, 2023.
- Date checked: 2026-05-03.

This 12-week trial randomized 61 healthy adults aged 65-80 to daily freeze-dried wild blueberry powder or placebo. The blueberry intervention provided 302 mg anthocyanins, equivalent to about 178 g fresh wild blueberries.

The study reported improved flow-mediated dilation, reduced 24-hour ambulatory systolic blood pressure, enhanced immediate recall, and better task-switch accuracy versus placebo. Cerebral blood flow and gut microbiota composition did not change.
