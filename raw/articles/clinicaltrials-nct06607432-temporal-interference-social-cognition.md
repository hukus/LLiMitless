# Temporal Interference Stimulation for Social Cognition (TISSC)

- ClinicalTrials.gov ID: NCT06607432
- Registry: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06607432
- Updated: 2025-08-20
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary
Columbia University is running the first FDA-regulated US human tIS study.
The protocol tests safety and tolerability in healthy volunteers, with fMRI, MRS, EEG, and face-emotion recognition used to assess target engagement and feasibility.
The long-term rationale is treatment of social-cognitive impairment in schizophrenia, but the current stage is still basic validation.

## Notes
This is the clearest US registry signal that TI is moving toward psychiatric cognition applications.
