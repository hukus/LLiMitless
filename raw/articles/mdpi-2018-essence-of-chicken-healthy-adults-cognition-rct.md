# Effectiveness of Essence of Chicken on Cognitive Function Improvement: A Randomized Controlled Clinical Trial

- DOI: 10.3390/nu10070845
- Published: 2018-06-29
- Ingested: 2026-05-11

## Summary

Randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial in healthy adults aged 18-45. The study evaluated essence of chicken for short-term memory, working memory, and selective/sustained attention over 14 days. The main analysis used 235 participants on a per-protocol basis.

Digit Span Forward showed a significant three-way interaction for treatment, stress, and visit, p = 0.020. Further analysis found improvement in moderate-stress participants and a near-significant effect in severe-stress participants, but not normal or mild stress. Digit Span Backward improved more with essence of chicken than placebo, p = 0.028, with about 0.60 more digits recalled on day 7. Selective and sustained attention tests did not show treatment effects.

## Relevance

This is the cleanest larger healthy-adult source for [[essence-of-chicken]]. It supports a stress-modulated working-memory / short-term-memory read rather than broad attention enhancement.

## Caveats

- Sponsored product supplied by Cerebos / BRAND'S Suntory Asia.
- One anomalous trial arm was excluded from reporting.
- Short duration and practice effects matter.
- Effect appears memory-task and stress-subgroup specific.
