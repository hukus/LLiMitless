# Hyperbaric oxygen therapy for cognitive impairments in patients with traumatic brain injury: A systematic review.

- PMID: 35213282
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35213282/
- Ingested: 2026-04-29

## Summary

Systematic review of HBOT for post-TBI cognitive impairments. The review found heterogeneous results and judged the evidence limited.

## Notes

The review searched PubMed, SCOPUS, PEDro, REHABDATA, MIDLINE, CHINAL, EMBASE, and Web of Science through June 2021. Ten studies met inclusion criteria: six randomized controlled trials and four pilot studies. PEDro scores ranged from 2 to 9, with a median of 7. The authors concluded that the evidence for cognitive benefit after TBI was limited and that larger randomized trials were needed.
