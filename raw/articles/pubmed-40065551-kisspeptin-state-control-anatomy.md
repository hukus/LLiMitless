# Kisspeptin state-control anatomy paper
- PMID: 40065551.
- 2025 rat/mouse mapping study of kisspeptin fibers and Kiss1r expression.
- Main result: kisspeptin fibers were mapped in 118 brain regions, primarily in extra-hypothalamic sensorial-processing and behavioral-state-control regions.
- Branch readout: this is a mechanistic routing clue, not human cognition efficacy evidence.
