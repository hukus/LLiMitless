# Blood-brain barrier opening in Alzheimer's disease using MR-guided focused ultrasound

- PMID: 30046032
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30046032/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

The 2018 phase I safety trial in 5 early-to-moderate AD patients reported safe, reversible, repeated BBB opening in targeted volumes. The abstract notes no serious clinical or radiographic adverse events and no clinically significant cognitive worsening at 3 months, with exploratory amyloid analysis showing no group-wise change.
