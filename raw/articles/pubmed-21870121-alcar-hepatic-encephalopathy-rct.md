# Acetyl-L-Carnitine in Severe Hepatic Encephalopathy RCT

- PMID: 21870121.
- DOI: 10.1007/s11011-011-9260-z.
- Journal: Metabolic Brain Disease, 2011.
- Ingested: 2026-05-03.

## Summary

Randomized double-blind placebo-controlled trial in severe hepatic encephalopathy. The study recruited 61 patients and compared ALCAR 2 g twice daily with placebo for 90 days. Outcomes included clinical/laboratory measures, psychometric tests, and automated EEG.

At 90 days, the ALCAR group showed better changes than placebo on Everyday Memory Questionnaire, logical memory, Trail Making A and B, verbal fluency, several visuospatial/attention measures, ammonia, and EEG improvement rate. The authors interpreted the cognitive, ammonia, and EEG changes as support for ALCAR as a possible tool in severe hepatic encephalopathy.

## Interpretation

Use this as a disease-state / gut-liver-brain axis cognition signal, not as healthy nootropic evidence. The dose is high, the phenotype is severe hepatic encephalopathy, and the route is metabolic/toxin-state restoration.
