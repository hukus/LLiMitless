# Effect of Choline Alfoscerate on Cognitive Dysfunction After Mild Traumatic Brain Injury: A Single-Center Prospective Case-Control Study

- PMID: 40353278
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40353278/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

Single-center prospective case-control study in adults with mild traumatic brain injury. Choline alfoscerate 400 mg twice daily for 8 weeks was associated with greater K-MMSE improvement than control, making this a small recovery-phenotype signal rather than healthy-adult enhancement evidence.
