# Sodium oligomannate phase 3 trial in mild-to-moderate Alzheimer's dementia

- PMID: 33731209
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33731209/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

China phase 3 randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial of sodium oligomannate / GV-971 in mild-to-moderate Alzheimer's dementia. The study randomized 818 participants to GV-971 900 mg/day or placebo for 36 weeks. The primary endpoint was ADAS-Cog12 change from baseline.

The reported week-36 drug-placebo difference favored GV-971 by -2.15 ADAS-Cog12 points (95% CI -3.07 to -1.23; p < 0.0001; effect size 0.531). The abstract reports sustained separation from week 4 onward and comparable treatment-emergent adverse-event incidence between GV-971 and placebo.

## Wiki Relevance

This is the central positive clinical evidence for GV-971 and the basis for the China approval narrative. It is also geography-bound and sponsor-linked, so the wiki should not treat it as globally replicated evidence until an independent or multinational program reports clean results.
