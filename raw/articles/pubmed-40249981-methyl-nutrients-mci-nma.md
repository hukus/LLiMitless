# Methyl nutrients for cognition and one-carbon metabolism in MCI

- Source type: PubMed abstract / systematic review and network meta-analysis
- PMID: 40249981
- Journal: Geriatric Nursing
- Published: 2025-05/06
- Checked: 2026-05-16 via PubMed E-utilities

## Summary

Zhao et al. reviewed methyl nutrient supplementation in older adults with mild cognitive impairment, including folate plus vitamin B12, folate alone, and vitamin B12 alone. The review aimed to evaluate cognitive function and one-carbon metabolism. The abstract reports that combined folate plus vitamin B12 was the most effective intervention type for slowing cognitive decline in older adults with MCI, reducing serum homocysteine, and increasing serum folate and B12 concentrations.

## Use In Wiki

- Supports keeping B12 plus folate in the MCI / high-homocysteine / one-carbon status lane.
- Does not isolate methylcobalamin from folic acid or other B12 forms.
- Useful as a 2025 update to the folate/B12/MCI evidence packet, not as healthy-adult enhancement proof.

## Caveats

- Population is older adults with MCI, not healthy adults.
- The abstract emphasizes combination folate plus B12; single-molecule attribution is limited.
- Network-meta-analysis conclusions depend on included study quality, dose, status, and endpoint heterogeneity.
