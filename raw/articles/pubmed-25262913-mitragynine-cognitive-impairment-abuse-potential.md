# Abuse potential and adverse cognitive effects of mitragynine (kratom)

PubMed-indexed animal study of mitragynine, the major psychoactive alkaloid in kratom. The abstract reports addictive-profile findings and cognitive impairments from acute and chronic mitragynine administration in rodents, with similarities to morphine.

The study reports that acute mitragynine impaired passive-avoidance learning, memory consolidation, and retrieval, and chronic administration impaired passive avoidance and object-recognition learning. Withdrawal signs and anxiety-like effects were also reported.

Relevance: formal preclinical cognition evidence is adverse, not nootropic-positive; human anecdotes should be interpreted through mood, analgesia, opioid-like effects, and dependence risk.
