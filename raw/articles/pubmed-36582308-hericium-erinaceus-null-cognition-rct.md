# Four Weeks of Hericium erinaceus Supplementation Does Not Impact Markers of Metabolic Flexibility or Cognition

- PMID: 36582308
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36582308/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

Single-blind, placebo-controlled parallel study in 24 college-age adults. Participants received 10 g/day of Hericium erinaceus or placebo for 4 weeks around a fatiguing exercise protocol. The study found no significant effects on cognition or metabolic flexibility.
