# Efficacy and Safety of Lithium for Behavioral and Cognitive Symptoms in Alzheimer's Disease Dementia: A Systematic Review With Frequentist and Bayesian Meta-Analysis

- PMID: 41177743
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41177743/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

Six RCTs with 394 participants were pooled. Lithium did not show consistent global cognition or functional benefit in AD dementia, memory effects were mixed, and serious adverse events were not increased, although drug-related adverse events were more frequent and heterogeneous across trials.
