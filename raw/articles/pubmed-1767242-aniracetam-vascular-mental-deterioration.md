# Efficacy and tolerance of aniracetam in elderly patients with primary or secondary mental deterioration

- PMID: 1767242
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/1767242/
- Ingested: 2026-04-30

## Summary

1991 randomized clinical trial in 60 elderly patients with slight-to-moderate brain pathology, primary or secondary to vascular forms. Patients received aniracetam 1500 mg/day orally or placebo and were assessed with multiple psychometric tests over two and four months. The aniracetam group showed significant improvement at both controls, while placebo did not show a statistically significant change. This is one of the more favorable older vascular/rescue signals.
