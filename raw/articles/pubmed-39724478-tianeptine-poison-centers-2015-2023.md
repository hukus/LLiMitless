# Tianeptine Exposures Reported to United States Poison Centers, 2015-2023

- PMID: 39724478
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39724478/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

National Poison Data System analysis of tianeptine exposures from 2015 to 2023. The paper reports 892 single-substance exposures, a 1,400% increase over the period, with 51.5% moderate effects, 12.0% major effects, and 40.1% requiring medical admission. Withdrawal accounted for 22.5% of exposures, and abuse accounted for 40.1%.
