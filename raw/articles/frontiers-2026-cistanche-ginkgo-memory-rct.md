# Cistanche tubulosa plus Ginkgo biloba memory randomized trial

2026 Frontiers in Pharmacology randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial of a botanical product containing Cistanche tubulosa and Ginkgo biloba extracts. One hundred seventeen healthy participants aged 30 to 65 years were randomized to CG tablets or placebo for 30 days. Clinical memory assessments and MRI/fMRI measures were performed at baseline and after the intervention. The trial was registered as ChiCTR2400084102.

The CG product improved multiple memory dimensions versus placebo, including directional memory, associative learning, graphic free recall, portrait memory, total memory, and Memory Quotient. Effects were reported across age and sex subgroups. Imaging analyses reported slight gray-matter-volume reductions in precentral gyrus and supplementary motor area and increased cerebellar culmen activity correlated with memory gains. The paper describes the intervention as Cistanche tubulosa plus Ginkgo biloba; authors disclosed Amway affiliations.

Interpretation for the wiki: this is a notable healthy-adult traditional-medicine memory signal, but it is a combination product. It supports Cistanche as a page-worthy candidate only with a strict caveat that the strongest human evidence cannot isolate Cistanche from Ginkgo or product-specific formulation.
