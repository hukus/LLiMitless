# Sleep Disorders and Complementary Health Approaches: 5-HTP / L-tryptophan Safety Note

- Publisher: National Center for Complementary and Integrative Health, NIH
- Source type: official health information and safety overview
- Checked: 2026-05-03
- Ingested: 2026-05-03

## Summary

NCCIH's sleep-disorders overview includes a safety paragraph on L-tryptophan and 5-HTP. It states that L-tryptophan supplement use was discontinued in 1989 because an eosinophilia-myalgia syndrome outbreak was traced to contaminated synthetic L-tryptophan from a single manufacturer. Because 5-HTP is chemically and biochemically related to L-tryptophan, NCCIH says it has been under close safety watch.

NCCIH also notes that extensive analyses of several 5-HTP sources did not find the toxic contaminants associated with L-tryptophan or other significant impurities. The practical interpretation is balanced: the historic outbreak matters for product-quality vigilance, but it does not prove that every modern 5-HTP product carries the same contaminant burden.

## Safety Notes

- Use as an official-product-quality caution for serotonin-precursor supplements.
- Keep 5-HTP sleep or mood claims separate from direct cognition claims.
- For consumer products, route interpretation through supplement QA, contaminant screening, dosing, and medication interactions.
