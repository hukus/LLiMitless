# Noradrenaline might enhance assertive human social behaviours

## Captured takeaways

- Randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled crossover study in ten same-sex healthy-volunteer flatmate pairs.
- One member of each pair received reboxetine 4 mg twice daily or placebo for two weeks, with washout and crossover.
- The study used questionnaires and a cooperative Tangrams game to probe social behavior with a familiar person.
- Reboxetine showed a statistical trend toward increased commands in the cooperative game, and the authors interpreted the result as preliminary evidence that noradrenaline enhancement can increase self-confident/assertive behavior.
- Use in wiki: social/affective noradrenergic probe only. Do not treat this as objective cognition enhancement.
