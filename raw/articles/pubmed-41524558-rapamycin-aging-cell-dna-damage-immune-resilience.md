# Rapamycin Exerts Its Geroprotective Effects in the Ageing Human Immune System by Enhancing Resilience Against DNA Damage

- PMID: 41524558
- DOI: 10.1111/acel.70364
- Published: 2026-01-12
- Ingested: 2026-04-30

## Summary

2026 placebo-controlled experimental-medicine study in older adults, using low-dose rapamycin for 4 months. The paper reports reduced p21 in immune cells, supporting a geroprotective / genome-stability signal.

The result strengthens the human biomarker plausibility for low-dose mTOR inhibition, but it does not include a cognition endpoint and therefore cannot upgrade the cognitive-aging claim on its own.
