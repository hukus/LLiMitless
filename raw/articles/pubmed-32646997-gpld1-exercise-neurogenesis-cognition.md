# Blood factors transfer beneficial effects of exercise on neurogenesis and cognition to the aged brain

- PMID: 32646997
- DOI: 10.1126/science.aaw2622
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32646997/
- Ingested: 2026-05-04

## Summary

Science 2020 paper identifying GPLD1 as a liver-derived exercise-responsive blood factor. Plasma from exercised aged mice transferred neurogenesis and cognition benefits to sedentary aged mice. GPLD1 rose after exercise, correlated with improved cognition in aged mice, was higher in active older humans, and systemic GPLD1 elevation improved age-related regenerative and cognitive impairments in aged mice. This is strong preclinical and human-correlational exercise biology, not a human GPLD1 treatment trial.
