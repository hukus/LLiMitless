# Effects of cardiorespiratory fitness and exercise training on cerebrovascular blood flow and reactivity: a systematic review with meta-analyses

- PMID: 34018848
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34018848/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary
Higher cardiorespiratory fitness is associated with lower cerebrovascular resistance and higher cerebrovascular reactivity. Exercise training itself did not reliably increase global cerebral blood flow or MCA velocity, though cerebrovascular reactivity showed a small favorable trend. This is the cleanest reserve-mechanism synthesis for the exercise lane.
