# A phase II randomized trial of sodium oligomannate in Alzheimer's dementia

- PMID: 32928279
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32928279/
- Trial registration: NCT01453569

## Summary

This was a 24-week, multicenter, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled phase 2 trial in China with 1:1:1 allocation to GV-971 900 mg, GV-971 600 mg, or placebo. The primary endpoint, ADAS-cog12, was not significantly different from placebo at either dose. The 900 mg arm did better than placebo on the CIBIC-Plus responder outcome, and a PET subgroup showed smaller glucose-metabolism decline in several AD-relevant regions. Treatment-related adverse events were reported at 5.9% for placebo, 14.3% for 600 mg, and 3.5% for 900 mg.

## Key Takeaways

- Human cognition signal was weak on the primary endpoint and should not be read as a clean phase 2 win.
- The paper is still important because it selected 900 mg bid for phase 3.
- The mechanistic PET subgroup is supportive but not decisive proof of benefit.
- This trial is the cleanest evidence that the branch should be framed as AD treatment, not healthy-adult enhancement.
