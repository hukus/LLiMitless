# PubMed 26029050 - Low-Dose Methylene Blue and Near-Infrared Light Opinion

## Citation

- Gonzalez-Lima F, Auchter A. "Protection against neurodegeneration with low-dose methylene blue and near-infrared light." Frontiers in Cellular Neuroscience. 2015;9:179. DOI: 10.3389/fncel.2015.00179. PMCID: PMC4428125.
- Source type: peer-reviewed opinion article.

## Extracted Claims

- The article argues that low-dose methylene blue and near-infrared light can both metabolically protect neurons through mitochondrial electron-transport-chain mechanisms.
- It frames methylene blue as a redox-cycling electron carrier that can donate electrons to mitochondrial electron transport.
- It frames near-infrared light as acting through mitochondrial targets, especially cytochrome oxidase / complex IV.
- The article is explicitly neuroprotection-oriented and hypothesis-driven.

## Cognition-Relevant Extraction

- This is a plausibility source for why MB and NIR light are discussed together.
- It does not establish a human cognition protocol, dose, or safety profile for combining oral MB with red/NIR light.
- In the wiki it should be marked as mechanism / opinion, not as efficacy evidence.
