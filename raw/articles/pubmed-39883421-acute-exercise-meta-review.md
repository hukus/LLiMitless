# Effects of acute exercise on cognitive function: A meta-review of 30 systematic reviews with meta-analyses

- PMID: 39883421
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39883421/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary
Acute exercise shows a small-to-medium overall cognitive benefit across 383 unique studies and 18,347 participants. Effects are present for attention, executive function, memory, and information processing, with the strongest readout when cognition is assessed after exercise rather than during it. This is the strongest broad human cognition signal in the reserve branch.
