# The Effects of Kynurenine Aminotransferase Inhibition in People With Schizophrenia (TrypNAC-II)

- ClinicalTrials.gov ID: NCT04013555
- Sponsor: University of Maryland, Baltimore
- Status: completed
- Primary completion: 2024-08-14
- Results status: posted on ClinicalTrials.gov

## Captured takeaways

- Double-blind, placebo-controlled, randomized crossover challenge study in schizophrenia / schizoaffective / schizophreniform disorder.
- Participants received NAC 140 mg/kg up to 15 g or placebo, then tryptophan 6 g.
- Primary readouts included serum kynurenine, KYNA, cerebral blood flow, glutamate, glutathione, and white-matter FA.
- Secondary readouts included cognition, specifically attention, verbal memory, visual memory, and working memory.
- Baseline KYNA and KMO activity were part of the responder logic.
- Interpretation for the wiki: the cleanest direct human test of KAT II inhibition logic in this branch, but still a registry-level evidence item until the results are published in a journal.
