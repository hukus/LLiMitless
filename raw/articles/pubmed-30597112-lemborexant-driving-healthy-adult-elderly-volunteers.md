# On-the-road driving performance the morning after bedtime administration of lemborexant in healthy adult and elderly volunteers

- PMID: 30597112
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30597112/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Randomized crossover highway-driving study in healthy adult and elderly volunteers. Lemborexant up to 10 mg did not cause clinically meaningful next-morning driving impairment when assessed about 9 hours after bedtime dosing.
