# Hyperbaric oxygen therapy can improve post concussion syndrome years after mild traumatic brain injury - randomized prospective trial.

- PMID: 24260334
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24260334/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Randomized crossover PCS trial reporting significant improvements after HBOT relative to a no-treatment control period, but without a sham comparator.

## Notes

Fifty-six mTBI patients 1-5 years after injury were analyzed after 40 sessions at 1.5 ATA. Cognitive function and quality of life improved after HBOT, and SPECT activity increased, but the design is vulnerable to expectancy and period effects because the control condition was no treatment rather than sham.
