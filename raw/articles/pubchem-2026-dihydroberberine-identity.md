# PubChem 2026 dihydroberberine identity note

PubChem identifies dihydroberberine as CID 10217, with molecular formula C20H19NO4 and molecular weight about 337.4 g/mol. It is chemically distinct from the cationic berberine entry and should be handled as a reduced berberine-related compound rather than a synonym.

Interpretation for the wiki: dihydroberberine is a form/analog and pharmacokinetic branch inside berberine coverage. It should not inherit all berberine clinical evidence automatically, and berberine studies should not be used to prove dihydroberberine cognition effects unless the product actually contained dihydroberberine.
