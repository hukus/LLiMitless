# FDA dietary supplements regulatory baseline

- FDA does not approve dietary supplements for safety, effectiveness, or labeling before they are sold.
- Under DSHEA, supplement companies carry the primary responsibility for safety and labeling.
- Supplements can have strong biological effects, can interact with medications, and can cause adverse events.
