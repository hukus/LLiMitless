# Cerebrovascular reactivity measured by functional magnetic resonance imaging during breath-hold challenge: A systematic review

- PMID: 28487157
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28487157/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary
Breath-hold challenge is a noninvasive way to measure cerebrovascular reactivity, but the review is about physiology and method standardization rather than cognition outcomes. Healthy controls generally show higher CVR than several pathologic groups, which makes the paper useful as a reserve-measurement anchor but not as proof of cognitive enhancement.
