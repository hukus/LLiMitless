# A phase 2, randomized, placebo-controlled study of the efficacy and safety of TAK-063 in subjects with an acute exacerbation of schizophrenia

- PMID: 30190165
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30190165/
- Ingested: 2026-04-29

## Summary

2019 phase II schizophrenia trial of the PDE10A inhibitor TAK-063. The primary PANSS endpoint was not met, although secondary endpoints were somewhat suggestive of antipsychotic activity. This is a PDE10 pipeline study, not a cognition-positive result.

## Key points

- Randomization: 83 TAK-063 and 81 placebo for 6 weeks.
- Dosing: TAK-063 20 mg daily, with blinded de-escalation to 10 mg for intolerance.
- Primary endpoint: change in PANSS total score.
- Result: no significant primary efficacy win.
- Safety: mostly mild or moderate AEs, but akathisia and dystonia were more frequent on TAK-063.
