# Systematic review and meta-analysis of repetitive transcranial magnetic stimulation (rTMS) for activities of daily living in Alzheimer's disease

- PMID: 39044102
- Published: 2025-01
- Source: PubMed

## Summary

Meta-analysis of rTMS for AD activities of daily living.

## Notes

- 2024 literature cut-off; 10 databases searched.
- Adds a functioning/rehabilitation endpoint layer alongside cognition.
- Relevant because protocol choices in AD trials often track cognitive and ADL outcomes together.
