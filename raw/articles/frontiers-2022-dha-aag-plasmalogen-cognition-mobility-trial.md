# Targeted DHA-AAG plasmalogen precursor cognition and mobility study

2022 Frontiers in Cell and Developmental Biology investigational clinical trial in 22 cognitively impaired persons. The intervention was an escalating oral dosing regimen of DHA-containing alkyl-acylglycerol plasmalogen precursors, from 900 to 3,600 mg/day over four months.

The study reported dose-dependent increases in DHA plasmalogens, oxidative-stress biomarker changes, and mixed cognition/mobility outcomes: cognition improved in nine participants, was unchanged in nine, and declined in four. The paper itself cautions that the study was not designed to critically evaluate clinical response, had a small sample, short duration, and no sufficient placebo or crossover design. Conflict notes include Prodrome-related employment/shareholder interests.

Use in the wiki: biomarker/exposure and hypothesis-generation source for plasmalogen precursors, but not a controlled cognition-efficacy anchor.
