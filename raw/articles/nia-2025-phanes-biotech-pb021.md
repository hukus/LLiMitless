# NIA Small Business Showcase: Phanes Biotech PB021

- Publisher: U.S. National Institute on Aging
- Source last updated: 2025-06-06
- Evidence type: NIA-hosted company development profile

## Summary

The profile identifies PB021 as Phanes Biotech's neuro-regenerative small-molecule peptide program for Alzheimer disease and related neurodegenerative disorders. It says the Research Foundation for Mental Hygiene granted Phanes an exclusive worldwide license in 2021.

The milestone table projects GMP and pre-IND work in 2024-2025, Phase 1 trials in 2026, and Phase 2 trials in 2028. These are company development milestones and forecasts, not proof that a trial began or that an IND was cleared.

## Evidence boundary

An exact ClinicalTrials.gov intervention search for P021 returned zero records on 2026-07-21. The projected 2026 Phase 1 milestone is therefore not publicly corroborated by that registry check.
