# Source Digest

Type: open-access narrative review.

Publication date: 2020.

Key extraction:
- Sulbutiamine is described as a synthetic thiamine derivative developed in Japan in the mid-1960s as a beriberi-treatment drug.
- The review states that reported potential applications include anti-fatigue, nootropic, antioxidant, sport-supplement, and possibly masking/doping-related contexts.
- The review references earlier asthenia studies, diabetic polyneuropathy work, post-traumatic asthenia, and sulbutiamine-in-sport analytical findings.
- Interpretation for cognition: the review supports branch identity and mechanistic plausibility, but it does not create modern healthy-adult cognitive efficacy.

Wiki signal:
- Medicinal signal: low review-level support for anti-fatigue / nootropic hypothesis.
- Confidence limiter: narrative review, mixed indications, old or indirect studies, and sport-supplement/doping-channel ambiguity.
