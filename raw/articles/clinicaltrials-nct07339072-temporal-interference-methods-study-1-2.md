# Temporal Interference Methods for Non-invasive Deep Brain Stimulation, Study 1.2

- ClinicalTrials.gov ID: NCT07339072
- Registry: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07339072
- Updated: 2026-02-10
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary
Indiana University is running a randomized crossover study to understand the causal role of deep brain structures in cognition.
Study 1.2 tests 5 Hz and 10 Hz TI at nucleus accumbens target settings during fMRI, with active and sham blocks counterbalanced.
The protocol is designed to validate focality and brain-activity effects before any stronger efficacy claims.

## Notes
This is an active cognition-relevant registry entry, but it is still basic-science / target-validation work.
