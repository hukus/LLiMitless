# Finnrick peptide products dashboard

## Summary

Public Finnrick products dashboard for peptide testing. On 2026-05-06, the page reported 7,164 samples from 205 vendors across 15 products, with samples checked for purity and quantity in commercial labs. The listed peptide products included BPC-157, CJC-1295, PT-141, ipamorelin, tesamorelin, TB-500, and several GLP-1-adjacent compounds.

## Key points

- This is one of the larger visible public market-monitoring datasets for gray-market peptides.
- The dashboard is product-level and score-level, not a peer-reviewed paper and not a medicinal-grade release program.
- It is useful mainly as a live market signal that peptide testing volume is now large enough to say something about active-market quality spread.
- The methodology page says purity and quantity are measured in commercial labs and used for scoring.

## Signal Use

- Class-level signal: strong evidence that an active third-party peptide-testing ecosystem now exists at meaningful scale.
- Caveat: the dataset is commercial, continuously updated, and not a published epidemiologic or regulatory-quality survey.
- Best use: support a more balanced sourcing read where some gray-market peptide supply may test well, but vendor spread and non-purity risks remain substantial.
