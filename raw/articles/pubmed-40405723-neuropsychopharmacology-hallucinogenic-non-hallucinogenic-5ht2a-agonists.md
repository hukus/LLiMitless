# Neuropsychopharmacology of hallucinogenic and non-hallucinogenic 5-HT2A receptor agonists

- PMID: 40405723
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40405723/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

2025 review of hallucinogenic and non-hallucinogenic 5-HT2A agonists. The paper argues that a newer class of compounds may retain therapeutic potential without disorienting hallucinatory experiences, with candidate explanations including partial agonism, biased signaling, non-selectivity, and downstream neuroplasticity. This is a mechanistic review, not a cognition trial.
