# Cerebrolysin for vascular dementia

- PMID: 31710397
- PMCID: PMC6844361
- DOI: 10.1002/14651858.CD008900.pub3
- Review: Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews, 2019 update
- Source type: systematic review/meta-analysis of randomized trials in vascular dementia
- Ingested: 2026-05-04

## Summary

2019 Cochrane update of Cerebrolysin for vascular dementia. The review found six randomized trials with 597 participants and no new eligible studies since the previous review. Combining cognitive outcomes from three studies with 420 participants suggested benefit on cognition with SMD 0.36, but the certainty was very low. Global-function response also favored Cerebrolysin in two studies with 379 participants, but again with very-low-quality evidence. The authors emphasized heterogeneity, high risk of bias, possible modest clinical importance, industry support where funding was available, and the need for larger methodologically robust trials.

## Signal Use

- Medicinal / formal signal: positive but very-low-certainty dementia-cognition meta-analytic signal.
- Safety signal: no clear adverse-effect increase in the included vascular-dementia trials, but the evidence quality is very low and does not erase later anaphylaxis or stroke-safety concerns.
- Anecdotal signal: none. This is controlled-trial synthesis, not community evidence.
- Wiki implication: vascular dementia deserves its own low-moderate medicinal signal row, separate from AD RCTs, aMCI, and stroke-recovery studies.
