# Semaglutide attenuates Alzheimer's disease model progression by targeting microglial NLRP3 inflammasome-mediated neuroinflammation and ferroptosis

- PMID: 41173224
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41173224/
- Ingested: 2026-04-29

## Summary

2026 preclinical study in APP/PS1 mice and BV2 microglia. Semaglutide reduced cognitive impairment, Aβ aggregation, tau hyperphosphorylation, NLRP3 activation, microglial M2 skew, and ferroptosis-related signaling. This is useful as a mechanistic bridge between incretin biology and the NLRP3 branch, but it is not human cognition evidence.

## Notes

- Strong mechanistic relevance, weak translational certainty.
- Best treated as a target-validation paper, not as proof that semaglutide is a direct nootropic.
