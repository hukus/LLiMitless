# WADA 2025 bromantan entry

- The 2025 WADA Prohibited List explicitly names Bromantan under S6.A non-specified stimulants.
- The 2025 list took effect on 2025-01-01.
- Practical interpretation for the wiki: bromantane is entangled with anti-doping / sports-visibility concerns even when users frame it as a nootropic or actoprotector.
