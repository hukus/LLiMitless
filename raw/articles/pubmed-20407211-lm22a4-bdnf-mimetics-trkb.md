# Small molecule BDNF mimetics activate TrkB signaling and prevent neuronal degeneration in rodents

- PMID: 20407211
- PMCID: PMC2860903
- DOI: 10.1172/JCI41356
- Publication: Journal of Clinical Investigation, 2010
- Evidence tier: preclinical discovery / TrkB-mimetic chemistry

## Summary

This paper describes in silico screening based on a BDNF loop-domain pharmacophore, followed by in vitro screening, to identify small molecules with neurotrophic activity reported as TrkB-specific versus other Trk family members. The selected prototype showed extracellular-domain binding, TrkB-dependent downstream activity, neuroprotection in vitro, hippocampal and striatal TrkB activation in mice, and motor-learning improvement after traumatic brain injury in rats.

The compound family includes the LM22A-4 lane in later literature. For the cognition wiki, this supports LM22A-4 as a BDNF-mimetic lead, but the behavioral evidence is still rodent recovery / motor-learning oriented, not human cognition.

## Wiki Use

- Use as the primary LM22A-4 / loop-domain BDNF-mimetic source.
- Compare to 7,8-DHF and deoxygedunin under a shared `preclinical TrkB agonist` class.
- Do not treat as a validated human neuroenhancement candidate.
