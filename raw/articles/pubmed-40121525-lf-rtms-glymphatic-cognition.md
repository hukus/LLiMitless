# Enhancement of glymphatic function and cognition in chronic insomnia using low-frequency rTMS

- PMID: 40121525
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40121525/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

2025 human study in chronic insomnia. The paper reports that 10 sessions of low-frequency rTMS over 2 weeks improved sleep, glymphatic clearance as indexed by DTI-ALPS, and cognitive performance, with improvement in DTI-ALPS positively correlated with improvement in MoCA and digit span backward at 3 months. This is the clearest direct cognition-outcome signal in the current glymphatic-targeting set.
