# Evaluating the Neurophysiologic and Clinical Effects of Single Dose Drug Challenge

- ClinicalTrials.gov ID: NCT05418049
- Study link: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05418049
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Active, not recruiting Fragile X syndrome challenge study testing single-dose baclofen, roflumilast, memantine, and placebo with neurophysiologic, behavioral, and clinical measures. The record is relevant because it extends the roflumilast branch into a neurodevelopmental phenotype where the question is target engagement and short-term behavioral change rather than general healthy-adult enhancement.

## Key points

- Population: Fragile X syndrome.
- Design: single-dose challenge with multiple pharmacology arms.
- Relevance: adds an ongoing human roflumilast program outside the stroke/MCI/AD track.
- Status: active, not recruiting; no results posted in the record.
