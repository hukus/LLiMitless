# The Relationship Between Glymphatic Function, White Matter Hyperintensity and Cognition: A Structural Equation Model MRI Study

- PMID: 40538144
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40538144/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

2025 MRI structural-equation-model study linking glymphatic function, white matter hyperintensity burden, and cognition in adults. The paper is observational rather than interventional, but it adds a newer human-pathway layer that supports the idea that glymphatic impairment sits in the same causal neighborhood as vascular white-matter injury and cognitive decline.
