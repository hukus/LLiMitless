# Effect of ashwagandha (Withania somnifera) extract with Sominone (Somin-On™) to improve memory in adults with mild cognitive impairment: A randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled study

- PMID: 40099725
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40099725/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

2025 randomized pilot in 40 adults with MCI using 250 mg/day of a standardized ashwagandha extract for 60 days. The active arm improved memory-related scores, MoCA/MMSE, and mental rotation relative to placebo, but the study is small enough that it should be treated as an encouraging proof-of-concept, not a settled recommendation.
