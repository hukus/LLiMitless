# AMPYRA (dalfampridine) label

- FDA label PDF: https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2021/022250s019lbl.pdf
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Current FDA labeling for AMPYRA/dalfampridine. The drug is indicated to improve walking in MS, the recommended dose is 10 mg twice daily, and the label contraindicates use with a seizure history or moderate-to-severe renal impairment (CrCl <= 50 mL/min). Mild renal impairment can raise exposure into a higher-risk range, and the label warns against combining AMPYRA with other 4-aminopyridine products.

## Notes

- Seizure risk is the key practical safety issue.
- Renal monitoring is required before and during treatment.
- This is the main regulatory source for the safety layer of the fampridine branch.
