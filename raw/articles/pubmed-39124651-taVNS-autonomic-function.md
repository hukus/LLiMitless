# The Effect of In-Ear and Behind-Ear Transcutaneous Auricular Vagus Nerve Stimulation on Autonomic Function: A Randomized, Single-Blind, Sham-Controlled Study

- PMID: 39124651
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39124651/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Randomized study in 76 healthy participants comparing in-ear and behind-ear taVNS variants against sham. The protocol used bilateral auricular stimulation for 20 min at 25 Hz with 250 microsecond pulses. The main contribution is that electrode placement and stimulation geometry matter for autonomic readouts; cognition was not directly tested.

## Notes

- Four-arm design: in-ear taVNS, behind-ear taVNS, in-ear sham, behind-ear sham.
- Outcomes included HR, blood pressure, and HRV.
- Helpful as a parameter and sham-design reference for future cognition studies.
