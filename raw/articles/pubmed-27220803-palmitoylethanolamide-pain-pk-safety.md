# Source Digest

Type: pharmacokinetics, safety, and efficacy review focused on pain.

Publication date: 2016-06-29 online; journal issue 2016-10.

Key extraction:
- Scope: clinical trial, case-report, pilot-study, meta-analysis, pharmacokinetic, and formulation evidence for PEA as an analgesic.
- Safety readout: for treatment times up to 49 days, the available data argued against serious adverse drug reactions at incidence of 1/200 or greater.
- Long-duration caveat: for treatment lasting more than 60 days, the number of patients was insufficient to rule out adverse reaction frequency below 1/100.
- Evidence-quality caveat: the review identified variable trial quality, missing data-spread reporting, and missing intermediate timepoint reporting.
- Formulation caveat: no head-to-head clinical comparison was available between unmicronized and micronized PEA, so superiority of one formulation over another was not established in that review.

Wiki signal:
- Safety signal: moderate short-term tolerability support, but not a clean long-term safety guarantee.
- Confidence limiter: pain-focused evidence, older review, and formulation comparisons remain incomplete.
