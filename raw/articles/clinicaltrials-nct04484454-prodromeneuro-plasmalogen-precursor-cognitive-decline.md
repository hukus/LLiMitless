# Open-Label Study of Omega 3 Oil Supplementation for Aging-Related Cognitive Decline

- ClinicalTrials.gov: NCT04484454
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

Completed open-label registry study of ProdromeNeuro omega-3 oil supplementation in aging-related cognitive decline. The registry describes the product as algae-derived omega-3 oil with naturally occurring alkylglycerol and fatty acids that function as a natural plasmalogen precursor. Enrollment was tiny and no results were posted, so this is mainly a product-identity and commercial-translation reference, not efficacy evidence.

## Registry Details

- Actual enrollment: 20
- Design: open-label supplementation study
- Population: aging-related cognitive decline
- Cognitive measures included MoCA and QDRS/CDR-derived function/cognition readouts
- Study completion: 2021-03-13
- Results posted: none

## Notes

- Important because it shows how commercial plasmalogen-adjacent products can differ from purified scallop-derived plasmalogen.
- The registry wording is precursor-oriented, not a direct identity match to the 2017 RCT material.
