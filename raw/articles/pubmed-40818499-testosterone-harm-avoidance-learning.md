# Testosterone harm-avoidance learning study
- PMID: 40818499.
- Double-blind placebo-controlled study in 120 healthy men.
- Intervention: single testosterone dose; task battery administered about 3 hours later.
- Key result: testosterone shifted harm-avoidance learning, enhancing learning from negative outcomes and suppressing positive-outcome learning in the self condition, while not affecting learning in the other condition.
- Additional signal: testosterone slowed prosocial learning from negative outcomes and interacted with trait anxiety.
- Branch readout: acute testosterone changes specific learning parameters, but this is not evidence for broad cognitive enhancement.
