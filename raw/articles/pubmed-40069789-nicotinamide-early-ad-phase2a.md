# Nicotinamide NEAT pharmacokinetic / pharmacodynamic assessment

PubMed 40069789: pharmacokinetic and pharmacodynamic assessment of oral nicotinamide in the NEAT clinical trial for early Alzheimer disease.

Key capture:

- Context: companion PK/PD analysis for the NEAT early-Alzheimer nicotinamide trial.
- Intervention context: oral nicotinamide in a high-dose early-AD protocol, tied to the same overall clinical program captured in the NEAT phase 2A trial.
- Main result shape: public abstracts report systemic nicotinamide exposure and metabolite movement, but only a small subset of participants with early AD had measurable nicotinamide in CSF, the presumed compartment for CSF p-tau231 lowering.
- Interpretation: this helps explain why oral high-dose nicotinamide can be biologically active yet still fail to deliver a clear cognitive or tau-phosphorylation efficacy signal in the clinical trial.

Interpretation for the wiki:

This is a mechanistic transport/exposure source for [[niacin-nicotinamide-b3]]. It supports the caution that NAD / nicotinamide biology can be real while brain exposure and cognition translation remain weak.
