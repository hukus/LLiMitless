# NCCIH DMSO and MSM osteoarthritis safety note

NCCIH consumer-health page on dimethyl sulfoxide / DMSO and methylsulfonylmethane / MSM for osteoarthritis.

Key capture:

- NCCIH states that only a small amount of research has been conducted on DMSO or MSM for osteoarthritis and that no conclusion can be reached about whether either substance is helpful.
- The safety section says MSM safety is uncertain because little research has been done, and lists possible allergic reactions, digestive upsets, and skin rashes.
- The page advises people considering DMSO or MSM for osteoarthritis to consult a health care provider.

Wiki use: this is the conservative safety and evidence-quality boundary for [[methylsulfonylmethane-msm]]. It prevents joint-health popularity from being treated as cognition evidence.
