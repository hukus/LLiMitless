# KDS2010 phase 2a Alzheimer's disease trial

- Registry: ClinicalTrials.gov
- NCT ID: NCT07027072
- Sponsor: NeuroBiogen Co., Ltd.
- Study ID: NB_KDS_ADP2_23
- Secondary registry ID: KCT0010589
- ClinicalTrials.gov derived version holder: 2026-05-14.

## Captured takeaways

- Trial title: Study to Evaluate the Efficacy and Safety of KDS2010 in Patients With Alzheimer's Disease With Mild Cognitive Impairment and Mild Dementia Due to Alzheimer's Disease.
- Official design: randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled, dose-finding phase 2a clinical trial.
- Status on registry: recruiting, verified 2025-08.
- Actual start date: 2025-08-06.
- Estimated primary completion: 2027-06-30.
- Estimated study completion: 2027-12-31.
- First posted: 2025-06-18; last update posted: 2025-08-08.
- Enrollment target: 114 participants, randomized 1:1:1 to placebo, KDS2010 60 mg, or KDS2010 120 mg.
- Dosing: oral once daily for 24 weeks.
- Population: age 50 to 85, MCI or mild Alzheimer's disease due to AD, amyloid PET positive, CDR 0.5 to 1.0, MMSE 21 to 30.
- Primary outcomes include CDR-SB change, time to at least 0.5-point CDR-SB increase, MMSE, ADAS-Cog13, and A-IADL-Q-SV.
- Secondary biomarker measures include MAO-B specific activity, GFAP, p-tau181, p-tau217, A-beta 40/42, NfL, BDNF, IL-1beta, and TNF-alpha.
- Exclusion criteria include recent MAOIs/linezolid, opioids such as pethidine/tramadol/tapentadol, cyclobenzaprine, St. John's wort, serotonergic drugs, sympathomimetics such as methylphenidate/amphetamine/methamphetamine/lisdexamfetamine, and dextromethorphan.
- Interpretation for the wiki: KDS2010 is the live reversible-MAO-B Alzheimer's candidate, but as of 2026-05-15 the registry has no posted efficacy results.
