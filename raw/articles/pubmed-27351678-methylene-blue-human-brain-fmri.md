# Multimodal Randomized Functional MR Imaging of the Effects of Methylene Blue in the Human Brain

- PMID: 27351678
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27351678/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Randomized double-blind crossover-style healthy-adult study of a single oral 280 mg methylene blue dose. Compared with placebo, MB increased task-related fMRI activity during psychomotor vigilance and delayed-match-to-sample tasks and improved short-term memory retrieval by 7%. The study excluded G6PD deficiency, serotonergic medications, major medical/psychiatric disease, pregnancy, colorblindness, and MRI contraindications.
