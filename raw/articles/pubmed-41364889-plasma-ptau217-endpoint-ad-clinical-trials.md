Title: Evaluating Plasma p-tau217 as an Endpoint for Alzheimer Disease Clinical Trials

This is a trial-design and endpoint-efficiency paper, not a cognition trial. It estimates that selecting Aβ-positive participants increases biomarker endpoint effect size, and selecting intermediate Aβ levels (Centiloid 20-40) increases p-tau217 effect size further and lowers the estimated sample size needed for a 25% drug effect. Use it as evidence that enrichment can improve surrogate endpoint efficiency, but not as proof of better cognitive effect sizes.

Caveat: surrogate endpoint modeling; cognition outcomes are not the primary readout.
