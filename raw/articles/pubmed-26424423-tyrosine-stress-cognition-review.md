# Effect of Tyrosine Supplementation on Clinical and Healthy Populations Under Stress or Cognitive Demands

- PMID: 26424423
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26424423/
- DOI: 10.1016/j.jpsychires.2015.08.014
- Ingested: 2026-05-03

## Summary

2015 review of tyrosine supplementation in clinical and healthy populations under stress or cognitive demands. The abstract concludes that tyrosine seems to enhance cognitive performance particularly in short-term stressful and cognitively demanding situations, and only when dopamine / norepinephrine function is intact and temporarily depleted.

## Interpretation

This is a state-challenge nutrient signal, not a daily cognition-enhancement claim. Route tyrosine through stress, cold, sleep-loss, and high-demand tasks rather than rested-baseline ranking.
