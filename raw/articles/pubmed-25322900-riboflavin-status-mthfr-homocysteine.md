# Riboflavin status, MTHFR/MTRR polymorphisms, and homocysteine

- PMID: 25322900
- PMCID: PMC4235830
- Title: Riboflavin status modifies the effects of methylenetetrahydrofolate reductase (MTHFR) and methionine synthase reductase (MTRR) polymorphisms on homocysteine
- Journal: Genes & Nutrition
- Publication date: 2014-11
- Source type: human observational genotype/status study
- Checked: 2026-05-16
- Ingested: 2026-05-16

## Summary

This study examined 771 adults aged 18-75 years and tested whether riboflavin status modified the effect of MTHFR 677C>T, MTRR 66A>G, and MTRR 524C>T polymorphisms on fasting total homocysteine.

The authors used erythrocyte glutathione reductase activation coefficient as a riboflavin-status marker. MTHFR 677T allele carriers with lower plasma folate had higher total homocysteine than 677CC carriers; this effect was eliminated when riboflavin status was optimal.

The study also found riboflavin/cobalamin context around MTRR 66A>G and homocysteine, while MTRR 524C>T depended mainly on cobalamin status.
