# Magnesium L-Threonate Cognitive Performance and Sleep RCT

- Journal: Frontiers in Nutrition
- Published online: 2026-01-12
- DOI: 10.3389/fnut.2025.1729164
- Source: https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/nutrition/articles/10.3389/fnut.2025.1729164/full
- Ingested: 2026-05-03

## Summary

Two-arm 6-week randomized double-blind placebo-controlled trial in 100 adults aged 18-45 with self-reported dissatisfied sleep. Participants received 2 g/day Magtein magnesium L-threonate or placebo. The active arm showed greater improvement in NIH Total Cognition Composite, working and episodic memory signals, reaction time, and estimated cognitive age, while Raven's Progressive Matrices did not differ. Self-report sleep-related impairment improved, but objective sleep-tracker outcomes did not separate, except for lower resting heart rate and higher HRV. The source reports good tolerability and no significant adverse reactions.

## Interpretation

This is a product-specific low-to-moderate signal for adults with sleep dissatisfaction. It should not be generalized to every magnesium form or treated as an intelligence-enhancement result because Raven's was null and several sleep outcomes did not separate.
