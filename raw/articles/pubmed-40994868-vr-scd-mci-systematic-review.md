# Cognitive rehabilitation using virtual reality in subjective cognitive decline and mild cognitive impairment: a systematic review

- PMID: 40994868
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40994868/
- Ingested: 2026-04-29

## Summary

Systematic review of VR-based cognitive interventions in subjective cognitive decline and mild cognitive impairment, covering studies published from 2019 to 2025.

The review included 19 studies total: 14 RCTs, 2 usability studies, and 3 experimental studies. Most studies reported improvements in memory, attention, and executive function, with high adherence and engagement, but protocol, outcome, and participant heterogeneity remained large and the SCD evidence base was still thin.

## Notes

This is the clearest higher-level synthesis for the SCD/MCI branch, but it still supports a "promising and heterogeneous" read rather than a mature standardized intervention class.
