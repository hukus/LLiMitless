# U.S. Psychedelic Use and Microdosing in 2025

- Organization: RAND
- Published: 2026-01-21
- Source type: nationally representative survey report
- URL: https://www.rand.org/pubs/research_reports/RRA4334-1.html
- Ingested: 2026-05-03

## Summary

RAND report based on a September 2025 probability-based nationally representative survey of 10,122 U.S. adults. Amanita muscaria mushrooms were among the top five psychedelic substances used by U.S. adults in the past year. RAND estimated past-year Amanita muscaria use at 1.34 percent, about 3.5 million U.S. adults.

The report's detailed microdosing questions focused on psilocybin, LSD, and MDMA, not Amanita, but it notes that Amanita is among other substances reported to be microdosed.

## Notes

- Useful as population visibility evidence, not efficacy evidence.
- Supports a `strong` public-salience signal for Amanita, but not a cognition-benefit signal.
- Combine with FDA/CDC sources before interpreting forum microdosing claims.
