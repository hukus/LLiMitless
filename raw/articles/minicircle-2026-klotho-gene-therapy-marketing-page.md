# Minicircle Klotho gene therapy marketing and waitlist page

- Source: https://minicircle.io/our-therapies/klotho/
- Ingested: 2026-05-04

## Summary

Current sponsor/marketing page for Minicircle's Klotho gene-therapy offering. The page describes Klotho as "coming 2026", asks users to join a waitlist, and presents anticipated benefits around longevity, cognitive function, cardiovascular health, and kidney health. It says the therapy uses a subcutaneous fat injection of plasmid gene therapy and that effects are expected to be temporary rather than genome-integrating.

The page also contains an important status caveat: Minicircle describes its therapies as investigational, not reviewed by the FDA, and administered by licensed clinicians at independent international partner clinics under non-U.S. regulatory frameworks. The page says clinical-trial data are being prepared for publication and asks users to follow updates rather than providing posted efficacy results.

## Signal Use

- Market/anecdote signal: weak prospective salience for cognitive-enhancement demand, but not a user-outcome report.
- Medicinal signal: none; marketing claims do not upgrade clinical confidence.
- Safety/regulatory signal: relevant because consumer-facing availability/waitlist claims exist before peer-reviewed human cognition results.
- Attribution caveat: marketing combines longevity, physical, cognition, kidney, and cardiovascular claims, so it should route through safety and trial-watch pages rather than molecule efficacy.
