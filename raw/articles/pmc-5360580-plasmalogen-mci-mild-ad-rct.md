# Efficacy and Blood Plasmalogen Changes by Oral Administration of Plasmalogen in Patients with Mild Alzheimer's Disease and Mild Cognitive Impairment

- PMCID: PMC5360580
- Source: PubMed Central / J Alzheimers Dis trial report
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

Multicenter randomized double-blind placebo-controlled trial in 328 Japanese participants with mild AD or MCI. The intervention was 1 mg/day of scallop-derived purified plasmalogen for 24 weeks, followed by a 4-week off-treatment period. The primary MMSE-J endpoint did not separate clearly from placebo in the intention-to-treat analysis. Secondary memory outcomes were more favorable in the mild-AD subgroup, especially in women and in participants age 77 or younger, but the signal is subgroup-driven rather than a clean primary-endpoint win.

## Notes

- The trial is the key human efficacy source for the branch.
- Blood plasmalogen measures moved differently between arms, but the cognitive interpretation remains limited by the null primary endpoint.
- The tested material was a scallop-derived purified plasmalogen jelly-like product, not a generic commercial "plasmalogen" label.
- This should be read as weak-to-moderate phenotype-specific recovery evidence, not a general enhancement claim.
