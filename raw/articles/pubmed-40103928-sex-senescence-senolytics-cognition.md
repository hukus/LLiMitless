# Sex, senescence, senolytics, and cognition

- PMID: 40103928
- PMCID: PMC11913825
- DOI: 10.3389/fnagi.2025.1555872
- Journal: Frontiers in Aging Neuroscience (2025)

## Summary

This is a review, not a trial result, but it matters for branch framing because it explicitly links senescence biology to age-related cognitive decline and adds a sex-differences caution to senolytic interpretation.

## Key Data

- Focus: sexual dimorphism in cellular senescence and senolytic treatment in relation to brain health and age-related cognitive decline.
- Core claim: peripheral senescence and systemic inflammation may contribute to neuroinflammation and cognitive decline.
- Branch implication: senolytic responses may differ by sex, senescent-cell burden, age at treatment, and hormonal context.

## Caveats

- No direct cognition efficacy data.
- Review-level synthesis only.
- The sex-difference framing is mechanistic and hypothesis-generating, not a dosing or recommendation guide.
