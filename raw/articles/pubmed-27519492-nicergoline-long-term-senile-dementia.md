# PubMed 27519492: Long-term nicergoline treatment in mild-to-moderate senile dementia

This multicentre double-blind placebo-controlled study evaluated nicergoline in mild-to-moderate senile dementia.

Design: 108 outpatients with DSM-III-R mild-to-moderate senile dementia of degenerative, vascular, or mixed origin were randomized to nicergoline 30 mg twice daily or placebo for 12 months.

Results from the abstract: 101 completers were analyzed. SCAG total score and clusters improved significantly versus placebo at study end; MMSE total score was maintained with nicergoline. Physician and patient/caregiver global evaluations favored nicergoline. Adverse events were reported in 7% of nicergoline and 2% of placebo participants, with withdrawals and hemodynamic changes comparable to placebo.

Interpretation for the wiki: reinforces low-moderate legacy clinical-state support, but still not direct healthy enhancement.
