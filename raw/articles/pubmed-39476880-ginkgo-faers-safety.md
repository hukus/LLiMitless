# Ginkgo biloba extract safety: Insights from a real-world pharmacovigilance study of FDA adverse event reporting system (FAERS) events

- PMID: 39476880
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39476880/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

FAERS pharmacovigilance study of Ginkgo biloba extract reports from 2004-2023. The authors identified 700 reports, 88 disproportionality signals, and a median onset time of 7 days. The signal does not prove causality, but it is a useful safety counterweight for cognition-oriented ginkgo use.
