# 5-HT6 receptors: Contemporary views on their neurobiological and pharmacological relevance in neuropsychiatric disorders

- PMID: 40347153
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40347153/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

2025 review of 5-HT6 biology in neuropsychiatric disorders. It emphasizes that the receptor remains a plausible target for cognition-related effects in AD and schizophrenia because of its network interactions with mTOR, neurofibromin, Fyn, and GPRIN1, but the review is still a mechanistic synthesis rather than clinical proof.
