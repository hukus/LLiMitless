# Acute effects of Salvia supplementation on cognitive function in athletes during fatiguing cycling exercise

- Source type: randomized double-blind placebo-controlled crossover trial.
- Publication: Frontiers in Nutrition, 2021.
- Date checked: 2026-05-03.

This study tested acute 600 mg Cognivia, a Salvia officinalis plus Salvia lavandulaefolia combination, in athletes during a fatiguing cycling task. Twenty-six volunteers were enrolled, but the final analysis included 17 after COVID-19-related losses.

Sage reduced rating of perceived exertion versus placebo, shortened simple reaction time, and improved backward digit span. Stroop results were less convincing, and taste/reflux made blinding imperfect for a small number of participants.

Use in the wiki: fatigue-state / sport-cognition anchor. It supports a state-bound working-memory and reaction-time hypothesis, not general daily cognitive enhancement.
