# Mazindol in the treatment of narcolepsy

- PMID: 525256

## Captured takeaways

- Older narcolepsy treatment study of mazindol.
- PubMed summary reports sustained improvement in narcolepsy but no effect on cataplexy or sleep paralysis.
- The abstract summary says mazindol 6 mg had the same effect on narcolepsy as d-amphetamine 50 mg and caused fewer side effects.
- Interpretation for the wiki: mazindol is relevant as an old anorectic / wakefulness-adjacent monoamine-reuptake comparator, but this is sleep-disorder evidence rather than healthy-adult cognition evidence.
