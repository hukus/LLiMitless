# Creatine supplementation associated or not with strength training upon emotional and cognitive measures in older women

- PMID: 24098469
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24098469/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

24-week randomized double-blind placebo-controlled trial in older women. Creatine and/or strength training improved muscle strength and emotional state, but neither altered cognitive performance.

## Notes

This is the closest direct exercise-plus-creatine cognition trial in the current search and it was null for cognition.
