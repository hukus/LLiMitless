# Safety, Efficacy and Clinical Applications of Focused Ultrasound-Mediated Blood Brain Barrier Opening in Alzheimer's Disease: A Systematic Review

- PMID: 39044508
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39044508/

## Summary

This 2024 review frames FUS-mediated BBB opening in AD as a way to improve permeability for drug delivery without visible tissue damage in the small early-clinical literature. It emphasizes safety, efficacy, and biological effects, but the evidence base remains heterogeneous and still exploratory.
