# Temporal interference electrical neurostimulation at 20 Hz beat frequency leads to increased fMRI BOLD activation in orbitofrontal cortex in humans

- PMID: 39059712
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39059712/
- Published: 2024-07-24
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary
Resting-state TI aimed at the left caudate increased BOLD in orbitofrontal cortex and parahippocampal gyrus.
The paper argues that TI can engage the region with the highest amplitude-modulation field in humans.
Safety and tolerability were good, but the study also highlights off-target effects and spatial-precision limits.

## Notes
Useful target-engagement evidence, not a cognition efficacy trial.
