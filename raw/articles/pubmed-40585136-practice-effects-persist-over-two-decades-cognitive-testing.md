# Practice Effects Persist Over Two Decades of Cognitive Testing: Implications for Longitudinal Research

- PMID: 40585136
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40585136/
- Ingested: 2026-04-30

## Summary

2025 preprint showing that practice effects persist across repeated cognitive assessments over roughly 20 years. In the VETSA sample, 7-12 of 30 measures showed significant practice effects across follow-up waves, especially in episodic memory and visuospatial domains, and adjusting for practice effects increased the apparent amount of cognitive decline and MCI prevalence.

## Notes

This strengthens the case for a run-in phase, alternate forms, and cautious interpretation of repeated cognitive testing even when the study is long rather than short.
