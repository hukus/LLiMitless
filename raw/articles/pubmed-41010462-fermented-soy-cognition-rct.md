# Effects of Fermented Soy on Cognition in Older Adults: Outcomes of a Randomized, Controlled Trial
2025 randomized trial in 61 adults aged 65+ tested 12 weeks of non-probiotic fermented soy powder versus energy-matched placebo. The fermented-soy group improved memory versus placebo, with post hoc stronger findings in women 70+ for global cognition and memory. Global cognition and memory changes were small, sex- and age-sensitive, and exploratory rather than definitive.
