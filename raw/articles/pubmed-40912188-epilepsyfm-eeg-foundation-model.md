# EpilepsyFM: A domain-specific foundation model for epileptic representation learning using EEG signals

PMID: 40912188
Published: 2026-01
Journal: Neural Networks
Authors: Zhuoyi Li, Ning Zhu, Yifan Chen, Beibei Chen, Qiufeng Dong, Lin Gan, Shijie Zhao, Zhiqiang Yan, Tuo Zhang

Abstract: EpilepsyFM is a domain-specific foundation model built to learn generalized epilepsy representations from EEG. It combines clinical EEG with large public datasets such as the TUH EEG Corpus through self-supervised pretraining. The abstract describes a discrete neural tokenizer, a domain-specific neural codebook, a seizure-inspired brain-region masking strategy, and temporal, spectral, and spatial encoders. The reported downstream tasks include seizure detection, seizure typing, short- and long-term forecasting, frequency-phase forecasting, antiseizure medication efficacy analysis, and radiofrequency thermocoagulation surgery analysis.

Research note: The immediate utility is clinical EEG representation learning and decision support. For this wiki, it is most useful as a proof that foundation-model methods are moving into domain-specific neural signal workflows, not as a cognition intervention by itself.
