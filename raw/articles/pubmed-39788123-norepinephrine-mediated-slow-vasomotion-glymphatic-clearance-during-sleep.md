# Norepinephrine-mediated slow vasomotion drives glymphatic clearance during sleep

- PMID: 39788123
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39788123/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

2025 Cell study in mice showing tightly synchronized norepinephrine, cerebral blood volume, and CSF oscillations during NREM sleep were the strongest predictors of glymphatic clearance. Optogenetic locus coeruleus stimulation altered vasomotion and CSF signal, and zolpidem suppressed norepinephrine oscillations and glymphatic flow. This is mechanistic support for the sleep-pressure branch, not a human cognition trial.
