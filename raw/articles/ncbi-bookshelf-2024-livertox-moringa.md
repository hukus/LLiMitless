# Moringa - LiverTox

- Publisher: NCBI Bookshelf / LiverTox
- Last update: 2024-07-15
- Evidence layer: official hepatotoxicity monograph

## Summary

LiverTox describes Moringa oleifera as a widely consumed food and traditional medicine plant. It states that moringa has generally been well tolerated in small clinical trials and has not usually been linked to serum enzyme elevations, but it has recently been implicated in a rare case of acute anicteric hepatitis with positive rechallenge.

The case described a 60-year-old woman who developed fatigue, nausea, and elevated aminotransferases after starting a Moringa oleifera product for energy, improved after stopping, and worsened again after rechallenge. LiverTox rates moringa as a possible rare cause of clinically apparent liver injury.
