# Sulforaphane effects on cognition and symptoms in first and early episode schizophrenia

- Source type: open-access randomized double-blind clinical trial.
- Publication: Schizophrenia Bulletin Open, 2022.
- Date checked: 2026-05-03.

This 22-week randomized double-blind trial tested sulforaphane in patients with first-episode schizophrenia. The primary cognitive outcome was change in the MATRICS composite score.

Sulforaphane did not significantly improve the primary MATRICS composite outcome. Secondary domain findings favored spatial working memory and verbal learning, and the treatment was described as well tolerated.

Use in the wiki: clinical Nrf2 / sulforaphane signal that is interesting but not primary-endpoint positive. Route through disease-context cognition and antioxidant / inflammation mechanisms, not healthy enhancement.
