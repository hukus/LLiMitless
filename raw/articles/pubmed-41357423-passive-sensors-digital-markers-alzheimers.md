# Operationalizing passive sensors into scalable, reproducible, neurobehavioral digital markers for Alzheimer's disease: Lessons learned over 10 years

- PMID: 41357423
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41357423/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

Perspective from a long-running AD passive-sensing program arguing that digital markers can complement molecular biomarkers if they are interpretable, reproducible, and governed well.

## Notes

- The article frames passive sensing as a low-burden complement to invasive or costly AD biomarkers.
- It emphasizes interpretability, time handling, versioning, missing-data policy, and vendor-agnostic pipelines.
- The main signal is operational: passive sensors are useful only if they are reproducible enough to support clinical translation.
