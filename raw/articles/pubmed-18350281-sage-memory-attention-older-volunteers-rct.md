# An extract of Salvia with anticholinesterase properties improves memory and attention in healthy older volunteers

- Source type: randomized double-blind placebo-controlled five-period crossover trial.
- Publication: Psychopharmacology, 2008.
- Date checked: 2026-05-03.

This older-adult crossover trial enrolled 20 volunteers over age 65 and tested standardized Salvia officinalis extract doses of 167, 333, 666, and 1332 mg against placebo, with a 7-day washout between visits.

The 333 mg dose showed the clearest signal: secondary memory performance improved at all testing times, and accuracy of attention also improved. Other doses had smaller or less consistent effects. In vitro work confirmed cholinesterase-inhibiting properties for the extract.
