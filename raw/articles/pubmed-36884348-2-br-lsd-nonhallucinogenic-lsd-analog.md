# A non-hallucinogenic LSD analog with therapeutic potential for mood disorders

- PMID: 36884348
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36884348/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

2023 preclinical paper on 2-bromo-LSD (2-Br-LSD). The compound showed partial agonism at 5-HT2A, no head-twitch response in mice, weak beta-arrestin recruitment, no 5-HT2B agonism, and no tolerance in vivo after repeated administration. It induced dendritogenesis/spinogenesis and improved active coping in mice, but the paper does not provide human cognition data.
