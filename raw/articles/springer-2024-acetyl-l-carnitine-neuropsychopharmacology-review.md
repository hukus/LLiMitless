# Neuropsychopharmacology of Acetyl-L-Carnitine Review

- Journal: Discover Mental Health.
- Published: 2024-01-02.
- DOI: 10.1007/s44192-023-00056-z.
- Ingested: 2026-05-03.

## Summary

Open-access review framing acetyl-L-carnitine / LAC as a mitochondrial metabolite involved in fatty acid oxidation, acetyl-group handling, histone acetylation, glutamatergic plasticity, inflammation, insulin resistance, depression phenotypes, and Alzheimer's disease hypotheses. The review notes that oral LAC can increase plasma and CSF levels, supporting brain exposure, and discusses rodent evidence for rapid antidepressant-like and plasticity effects.

For cognition, the review is mainly mechanistic and translational. It links LAC metabolism to stress-related disorders, depression, brain insulin resistance, and AD, and notes prior AD/MCI trial signals, but it does not supply a modern healthy-adult cognition efficacy packet.

## Interpretation

Use this as a mechanism and phenotype-enrichment source. It supports why ALCAR belongs in the mitochondrial / plasticity / mood-cognition neighborhood, but it should not upgrade efficacy beyond controlled human outcome evidence.
