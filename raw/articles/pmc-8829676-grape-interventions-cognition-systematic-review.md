# Grape interventions and cognitive / mental performance systematic review

- PMID: 34041549
- PMCID: PMC8829676
- Source type: systematic review of randomized controlled trials.
- Date checked: 2026-05-03.

This systematic review evaluated randomized controlled trials of grape interventions for cognition and mood in healthy participants and people with mild cognitive impairment. Eight studies met inclusion criteria: one acute intervention, six chronic interventions, and one that assessed both acute and chronic effects.

The review summarized chronic grape studies as showing improvements in some cognitive domains, including memory, motor skills, or executive function. Acute studies did not show a consistent memory effect, though reaction-time improvements appeared in some data. The review supports the grape-food branch as plausible but heterogeneous, because products, phenolic profiles, durations, endpoints, and populations differ.

Use in the wiki: family-level grape-food evidence source. Do not use it to merge raisins, Concord grape juice, whole grapes, grape seed, standardized grape extract, and mixed grape-blueberry extract into one product.
