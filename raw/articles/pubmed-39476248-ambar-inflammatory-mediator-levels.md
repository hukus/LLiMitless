# Plasma exchange with albumin replacement for Alzheimer's disease treatment induced changes in serum and cerebrospinal fluid inflammatory mediator levels

- PMID: 39476248
- PMCID: PMC11651178
- Source: Annals of Clinical and Translational Neurology 2024
- Ingested: 2026-04-29

## Summary

Biomarker follow-up from the AMBAR trial showing that plasma exchange with albumin replacement changes inflammatory mediators in serum and CSF and that some mediator shifts relate to clinical outcome trajectories. This strengthens the mechanism story, but it is still not a new efficacy trial.

## Notes

- The analysis used serum and CSF from 142 AMBAR participants.
- 19 inflammatory mediators were tracked: cytokines, chemokines, and vascular injury markers.
- PE-Alb reduced IFN-gamma, eotaxin, MIP-1a, and ICAM-1 in serum and eotaxin-3 and MIP-1b in CSF at selected time points.
- Vascular injury markers moved most clearly around the procedure itself, which suggests an acute exchange effect as well as longer-horizon changes.
- In the placebo group, higher serum MIP-1a tracked with worse ADAS-Cog, CDR-SB, and ADCS-CGIC scores; that association was not seen in PE-Alb-treated patients.
- The paper is useful for the blood-factor branch because it links peripheral intervention to both peripheral and central inflammatory readouts.
