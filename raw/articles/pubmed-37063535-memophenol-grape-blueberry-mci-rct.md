# Effects of a polyphenol-rich grape and blueberry extract on cognitive function in older adults with mild cognitive impairment

- Source type: randomized double-blind placebo-controlled trial.
- Publication: Frontiers in Psychology, 2023.
- Date checked: 2026-05-03.

This 24-week trial randomized 143 adults aged 60-80 years with mild cognitive impairment to Memophenol, a standardized grape and wild-blueberry extract, or placebo. The active arm used 150 mg twice daily, for 300 mg/day total.

Compared with placebo, Memophenol was associated with greater improvements in speed of information processing, visuospatial learning, and BRIEF-A global score. Other assessed cognitive tests and self-report questionnaires did not show significant between-group differences. The supplement was reported as well tolerated, with no significant adverse reactions in the abstract.
