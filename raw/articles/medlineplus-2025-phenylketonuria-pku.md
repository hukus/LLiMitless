# MedlinePlus phenylketonuria safety context

MedlinePlus PKU page, last updated September 17, 2025. It explains that phenylketonuria is an inherited amino-acid metabolism disorder in which the body cannot process phenylalanine, and high phenylalanine can damage the brain and cause severe intellectual disability. The page also says staying on the low-phenylalanine diet for life is important.

Use in the wiki: official safety and metabolic-vulnerability context. It is not a nootropic-efficacy source, but it is central to why phenylalanine / DLPA should not be treated as an ordinary focus supplement.
