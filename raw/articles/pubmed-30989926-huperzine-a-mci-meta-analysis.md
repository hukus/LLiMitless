# Efficacy and Safety of Huperzine A in Treating Patients With Mild Cognitive Impairment

- PMID: 30989926
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30989926/
- DOI: 10.19540/j.cnki.cjcmm.20180925.008
- Ingested: 2026-05-03

## Summary

2019 Chinese-language systematic review and meta-analysis of huperzine A for mild cognitive impairment. The abstract reports nine randomized controlled trials and says huperzine A improved memory quotient and MMSE scores compared with placebo, with mild side effects. The authors caution that original-study quality was low and that higher-quality studies are needed.

## Interpretation

This supports a low-to-moderate impairment-context signal, not healthy-adult enhancement. Keep the cholinesterase-inhibitor mechanism and low study quality visible.
