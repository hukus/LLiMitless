# Huperzine A for Alzheimer's Disease Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis

- PMID: 24086396
- PMCID: PMC3781107
- PubMed Central: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3781107/
- DOI: 10.1371/journal.pone.0074916
- Ingested: 2026-05-03

## Summary

2013 systematic review and meta-analysis of randomized clinical trials of huperzine A for Alzheimer's disease. The review included 20 RCTs with 1,823 participants. It reported improvements on several cognitive and daily-living measures, including MMSE and ADL measures, but also stated that most included trials had high risk of bias and that findings should be interpreted cautiously.

## Interpretation

This is disease-context evidence with major methodological caveats. It should not be imported into healthy nootropic rankings without a safety and bias downgrade.
