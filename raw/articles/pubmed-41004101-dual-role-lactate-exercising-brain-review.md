# The Dual Role of Lactate in the Exercising Brain: Fueling Energy and Signaling Fatigue

- PMID: 41004101
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41004101/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

2025 review emphasizing that exercise-related lactate is not just a fuel surrogate; it is also tied to fatigue signaling. For the cognition branch, this is a useful caution against reading every lactate rise as a performance gain, especially outside metabolically stressed or AD-like phenotypes.
