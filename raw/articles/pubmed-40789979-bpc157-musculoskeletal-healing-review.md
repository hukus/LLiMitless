# Regeneration or Risk? A Narrative Review of BPC-157 for Musculoskeletal Healing

- PMID: 40789979
- PMCID: PMC12446177
- DOI: 10.1007/s12178-025-09990-7
- Source type: narrative/scoping review
- Ingested: 2026-05-06

## Summary

2025 review focused on BPC-157 in musculoskeletal healing. The abstract describes broad preclinical regenerative effects across tendon, muscle, vascular, and inflammatory pathways, but says human data remain extremely limited.

## Key points

- The review highlights VEGFR2 / Akt-eNOS, ERK1/2, angiogenesis, fibroblast activity, and neuromuscular-stabilization mechanisms as the main repair story.
- It says only three human pilot studies were identified in the human literature at the time of review: intra-articular knee pain, interstitial cystitis, and intravenous safety/pharmacokinetics.
- The authors frame BPC-157 as potentially valuable for musculoskeletal medicine, but still investigational and in need of proper controlled human trials.

## Signal Use

- Formal signal: positive review-level support for a real repair hypothesis and strong demand for better human trials.
- Safety/regulatory signal: explicitly notes rising popularity and non-regulated-source availability without treating that as validation.
- Cognition signal: indirect only; useful mainly as a recovery / injury-state bridge rather than direct nootropic evidence.
