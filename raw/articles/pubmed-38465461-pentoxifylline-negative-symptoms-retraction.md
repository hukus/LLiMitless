# Retraction: Abdallah MS, Mosalam EM, Hassan A, et al. Pentoxifylline as an adjunctive in treatment of negative symptoms in chronic schizophrenia: A double-blind, randomized, placebo-controlled trial.

- PMID: 38465461
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38465461/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

This is the PubMed retraction notice for the 2023 pentoxifylline adjunctive trial in chronic schizophrenia. The notice retracts the trial that had claimed benefit on negative symptoms and inflammatory markers. The retraction is the key credibility record for the pentoxifylline / PDE-cAMP-CREB branch.

## Notes

- The retracted paper is PMID 36341700 / DOI 10.1111/cns.14010.
- PubMed shows the retraction notice as a 2024 CNS Neurosci Ther item.
- Any downstream summary of pentoxifylline in schizophrenia should treat the negative-symptom paper as invalidated evidence, not a live positive signal.
