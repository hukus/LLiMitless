# Efficacy and cognitive effect of sarcosine (N-methylglycine) in patients with schizophrenia: A systematic review and meta-analysis of double-blind randomised controlled trials

- PMID: 32122256
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32122256/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

This 2020 meta-analysis found sarcosine helped overall schizophrenia symptoms but not cognition. The pooled cognitive effect was positive but insignificant (SMD 0.27, 95% CI -0.06 to 0.60), so sarcosine remains a weak comparator unless a phenotype or combination effect is specified.
