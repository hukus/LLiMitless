# Daily consumption of essence of chicken improves cognitive function: a systematically searched meta-analysis of randomized controlled trials

- PMID: 31131735
- DOI: 10.1080/1028415X.2019.1619984
- Published: 2019
- Ingested: 2026-05-11

## Summary

Meta-analysis of randomized controlled trials testing essence of chicken on cognitive function. Six databases were searched, yielding 1,760 articles and 8 eligible articles with a pooled population of 794 subjects.

The abstract reports generally favorable cognitive effects after daily essence of chicken intake, specifically in the working-memory domain with standardized mean difference 0.31, 95% CI 0.16 to 0.46. Working memory is presented as a core component of executive function.

## Relevance

This is the more favorable meta-analytic source for [[essence-of-chicken]], and helps explain why the branch deserves its own product-level entity. It should be balanced with the earlier high-risk-of-bias review.

## Caveats

- Product sponsorship and trial quality remain important.
- Working-memory signal should not be generalized to all domains.
- Essence of chicken remains a complex food extract, not a pure anserine/carnosine exposure.
