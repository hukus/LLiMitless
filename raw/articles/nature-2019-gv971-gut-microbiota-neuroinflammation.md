# Sodium oligomannate remodels gut microbiota and suppresses neuroinflammation

- Source: Cell Research / Nature: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41422-019-0216-x
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

Mechanistic Cell Research paper proposing that Alzheimer's progression involves gut dysbiosis, phenylalanine and isoleucine accumulation, peripheral Th1 immune activation, and brain neuroinflammation. The paper reports that GV-971 remodels gut microbiota, reduces amino-acid-linked inflammatory signaling, and improves cognition-related phenotypes in AD mouse models.

The paper also reports small human observational comparisons involving MCI due to AD and healthy controls for amino acids and Th1-cell markers. The human material is mechanistic and observational, not an intervention trial showing cognitive benefit.

## Wiki Relevance

This is the main gut-brain mechanism source for GV-971, but it should not be treated as clinical proof. The wiki should keep the preclinical and small-human-biomarker mechanism separate from the China phase 3 cognitive endpoint.
