# Effects and mechanisms of Apelin in treating central nervous system diseases

- PMID: 39681256
- DOI: 10.1016/j.neuroscience.2024.12.025
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39681256/
- Ingested: 2026-05-04

## Summary

Neuroscience 2025 review of apelin/APJ signaling in CNS diseases. The review describes apelin as an endogenous APJ ligand distributed in the CNS and discusses protective mechanisms across apoptosis, autophagy, oxidative stress, angiogenesis, inflammation, neurotrauma, stroke, spinal cord injury, tumors, neurodegenerative diseases, psychiatric disease, epilepsy, and pain. It is useful for CNS mechanism mapping, but it is not evidence that apelin improves cognition in humans.
