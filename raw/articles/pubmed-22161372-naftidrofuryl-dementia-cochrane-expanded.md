# Naftidrofuryl for dementia - expanded Cochrane abstract capture

PubMed record for Lu et al., "Naftidrofuryl for dementia," Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews, 2011 Dec 7, DOI 10.1002/14651858.CD002955.pub4.

The review searched the Cochrane Dementia and Cognitive Improvement Group register on 11 January 2011. Eligible studies were randomized placebo-controlled trials in dementia patients treated with naftidrofuryl.

The review identified nine randomized controlled trials with 847 patients with Alzheimer's disease, vascular dementia, mixed dementia, senile dementia, or unspecified dementia. Functional performance and behavior favored naftidrofuryl with a standardized mean difference of -1.04, 95% CI -1.73 to -0.35, but heterogeneity was high. Mood favored naftidrofuryl with SMD -0.80, 95% CI -1.26 to -0.34. Cognitive function also favored naftidrofuryl with SMD -0.36, 95% CI -0.71 to -0.02.

The abstract states that the effect was not confirmed by clinical global measures. The authors concluded that naftidrofuryl was well tolerated and that low-quality evidence suggested possible benefits for performance, behavior, cognition, and mood, but the global-impression benefit was inconsistent and unconvincing.

Relevance: use as the main dementia/cognition evidence anchor, but keep the signal low-confidence, clinical-state-bound, and not transportable to healthy-adult cognitive enhancement.
