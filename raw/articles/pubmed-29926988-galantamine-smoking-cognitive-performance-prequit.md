# Effects of galantamine on smoking behavior and cognitive performance in treatment-seeking smokers prior to a quit attempt

- PMID: 29926988
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29926988/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Randomized trial in 60 treatment-seeking smokers. Galantamine ER 8 or 16 mg/day reduced smoking choice and urine cotinine during the pre-quit period, but had minimal effect on cognitive performance.
