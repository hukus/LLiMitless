# Intranasal insulin enhances resting-state functional connectivity in Type 2 Diabetes

- PMID: 40392946
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40392946/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

MRI sub-study from MemAID showing that intranasal insulin altered resting-state functional connectivity in T2DM.

## Notes

The key mechanistic point is that better connectivity tracked lower HOMA-IR, which makes insulin resistance look like a plausible effect modifier rather than just a background comorbidity.
