# Efficacy and safety of choline alphoscerate for amnestic mild cognitive impairment: a randomized double-blind placebo-controlled trial

- PMID: 39300341
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39300341/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

In 100 adults with amnestic mild cognitive impairment, 12 weeks of choline alphoscerate (600 mg) improved ADAS-cog more than placebo and did not produce serious adverse events or excess discontinuation. The result is a phenotype-bound positive signal for a choline-donor strategy, not a healthy-adult enhancement claim.
