# PubMed 11305684 DMG autism / PDD randomized trial note

Kern et al. tested N,N-dimethylglycine in a 4-week double-blind placebo-controlled trial in 37 children aged 3-11 with autism and/or pervasive developmental disorder. Behavioral outcomes included the Vineland Maladaptive Behavior Domain and Aberrant Behavior Checklist, with neurologic exams before and after treatment in 33 children.

Both placebo and DMG groups improved overall, but DMG was not statistically different from placebo on the measured behavioral outcomes. The authors noted heterogeneous apparent responses and fewer negative changes in the DMG arm, but the quantitative result did not establish efficacy.

Interpretation for the wiki: this is a small pediatric neurodevelopmental negative / non-significant trial. It does not support a healthy-adult cognition claim, but it matters because DMG has a long supplement-lore history around speech, behavior, methylation, and brain function.
