# First-in-human positron emission tomography study of intranasal insulin in aging and MCI

- PMID: 40703476
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40703476/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

First-in-human PET validation study showing that intranasally delivered insulin reaches multiple brain regions in humans, with uptake patterns that vary by cognitive status and sex.

## Notes

A specialized Aptar Cartridge Pump System delivered [68Ga]Ga-NOTA-insulin to cognitively normal adults and people with MCI. Brain uptake was measurable in olfactory cortex, hippocampus, parahippocampus, amygdala, temporal pole, insula, caudate, putamen, thalamus, and anterior cingulum. Baseline pulse pressure, plasma insulin, and p-tau217 correlated with uptake in subgroup analyses, which makes delivery device and vascular/metabolic state part of the responder story.
