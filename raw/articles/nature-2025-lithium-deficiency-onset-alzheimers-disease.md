# Lithium deficiency and the onset of Alzheimer disease

- PMID: 40770094
- Nature: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-09335-x
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

The paper reports lower endogenous lithium in prefrontal cortex tissue from MCI and AD brains, lithium sequestration in amyloid plaques, and worsening pathology when dietary lithium is reduced in mouse AD models. In mice, low-dose lithium orotate improved pathology and memory while lithium carbonate did not show the same effect. The authors frame lithium replacement as a plausible preventive and therapeutic direction but explicitly stop short of a human recommendation.
