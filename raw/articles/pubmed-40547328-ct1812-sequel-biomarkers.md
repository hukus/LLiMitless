# Identification of cerebrospinal fluid pharmacodynamic biomarkers and molecular correlates of brain activity in a Phase 2 clinical trial of the Alzheimer's disease drug candidate CT1812

- PMID: 40547328
- PMCID: PMC12178945
- Publication: Alzheimer's & Dementia: Translational Research & Clinical Interventions, 2025
- Evidence tier: human phase 2 biomarker / qEEG analysis

## Summary

SEQUEL was a completed 4-week randomized, placebo-controlled crossover trial in adults with mild-to-moderate AD. CT1812 (zervimesine) was given orally once daily at 300 mg.

The paper reports improved quantitative EEG markers of spontaneous brain activity and identifies CSF proteomic changes that support pathway engagement, synaptic biology, vesicle trafficking, and lipoprotein biology. The biomarker set also correlated with the previously reported functional-connectivity signal.

This is a target-engagement and pharmacodynamic paper, not a direct cognition-efficacy win. It strengthens the mechanism but does not settle whether the branch improves patient-relevant outcomes over longer durations.
