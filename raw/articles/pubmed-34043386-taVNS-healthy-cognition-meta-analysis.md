# The effects of transcutaneous auricular vagal nerve stimulation on cognition in healthy individuals: A meta-analysis

- PMID: 34043386
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34043386/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

2021 meta-analysis of 19 eligible healthy-adult studies. The pooled overall effect size was small but positive, with stronger signals for executive function and accuracy than for response time. Parameter meta-analysis suggested stimulation site mattered, with tragus stimulation showing a much larger executive-function estimate than cymba concha, but the review is now best treated as a historical baseline rather than a settled contemporary estimate.

## Notes

- Weighted overall effect size reported by the authors: 0.21.
- The authors emphasized protocol sensitivity and outcome-domain dependence.
- Useful as a baseline for interpreting newer taVNS studies, not as the last word on the field.
