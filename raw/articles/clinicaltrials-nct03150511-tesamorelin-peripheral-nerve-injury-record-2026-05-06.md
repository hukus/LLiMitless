ClinicalTrials.gov record for NCT03150511, a recruiting Phase 2 randomized blinded tesamorelin study in peripheral nerve injury. The record describes tesamorelin 2 mg in 36 participants with repaired ulnar nerve lacerations, with motor and sensory recovery, electrodiagnostic measures, and functional outcomes followed for 12 months. Study start is listed as 2018-06-01, primary completion is estimated for 2027-12, and the last update posted is 2025-11-12.
