# NLRP3 inflammasome in Alzheimer's disease: molecular mechanisms and emerging therapies

- PMID: 40260242
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40260242/
- Ingested: 2026-04-29

## Summary

2025 review of NLRP3 in AD. Frames NLRP3 as a microglial inflammasome linked to Aβ, tau, ROS, pyroptosis, autophagy, gut microbiota, and candidate therapies. Useful as the umbrella review anchor, but it is not direct human cognition-intervention evidence.

## Notes

- Good source for mechanism scope and intervention classes.
- Does not establish clinical efficacy for any NLRP3-targeted cognition intervention.
