# Intranasal insulin diminishes postoperative delirium and elevated osteocalcin and brain derived neurotrophic factor in older patients undergoing joint replacement: A randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial

- PMID: 39911448
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39911448/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Randomized trial in older orthopedic surgery patients showing reduced delirium and biomarker changes with 40 IU intranasal insulin.

## Notes

This is the most direct recent biomarker-linked perioperative neurocognitive signal outside the MCI/AD space. It strengthens the inflammation and insulin-sensitivity interpretation.
