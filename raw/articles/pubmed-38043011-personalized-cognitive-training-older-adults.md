# Toward Personalized Cognitive Training in Older Adults: A Pilot Investigation of the Effects of Baseline Performance and Age on Cognitive Training Outcomes

- PMID: 38043011
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38043011/
- Ingested: 2026-04-29

## Summary

Pilot study of a six-month BrainHQ training regimen in 31 healthy aging adults who completed the intervention, designed to test whether baseline performance and age predict training and transfer benefits.

All completers improved on the training regimen. Age was positively associated with training improvement, and baseline performance moderated transfer patterns by domain: lower baseline performers gained more in inhibitory control, while age and baseline level interacted differently across processing speed, inhibitory control, and episodic memory tracks.

## Notes

This is an important personalization anchor because it shows that "who benefits" can be at least as interesting as "does the average sample benefit."
