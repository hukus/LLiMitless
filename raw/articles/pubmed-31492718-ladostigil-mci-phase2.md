# Low-dose ladostigil for mild cognitive impairment phase 2 trial

- PMID: 31492718
- PMCID: PMC7010322
- DOI: 10.1212/WNL.0000000000008239
- Journal: Neurology (2019)
- Design: 3-year randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled phase 2 trial.

## Captured takeaways

- Ladostigil 10 mg/day was tested in patients aged 55 to 85 with mild cognitive impairment and medial temporal lobe atrophy.
- A total of 210 participants were randomized: 107 placebo and 103 ladostigil.
- After 36 months, 21/103 placebo participants and 14/99 ladostigil participants progressed to Alzheimer's disease; p = 0.162.
- Ladostigil had no significant effects on Neuropsychological Test Battery composite score, Disability Assessment for Dementia, or Geriatric Depression Scale.
- Whole-brain and hippocampal volume decreased more in the placebo group than the ladostigil group, with p = 0.025 and p = 0.043.
- Serious adverse events were similar: 28/107 on placebo and 26/103 on ladostigil.
- The authors concluded low-dose ladostigil was safe and tolerated but did not significantly delay conversion to Alzheimer's disease.
- Interpretation for the wiki: ladostigil belongs in the MAO-B/propargylamine watchlist as a negative/atrophy-hint MCI program, not a cognition-positive nootropic.
