# Bright light exposure during simulated night work improves cognitive flexibility.

- PMID: 35343353
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35343353/
- Ingested: 2026-04-29

## Summary

2022 counterbalanced crossover study in young healthy individuals during three simulated night shifts. A full-spectrum bright-light condition (~900 lx) improved reversal-learning performance toward the end of the night shift compared with standard light (~90 lx), suggesting that bright light can mitigate fatigue-related cognitive-flexibility decline during night work.

## Notes

- Direct cognition signal under a sleep-disruption and night-work state.
- More specific to cognitive flexibility than general wakefulness.
- Useful as the bright-light performance anchor for the stack.
