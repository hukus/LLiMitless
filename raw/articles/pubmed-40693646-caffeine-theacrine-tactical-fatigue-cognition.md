The source is relevant to the wiki because it tests a caffeine plus theacrine stack in a fatigue / tactical-performance context rather than rested baseline cognition.

## Interpretation

Use this as a state-challenge signal for theacrine only in combination with caffeine. The formal signal should not be assigned to theacrine alone unless standalone theacrine cognition trials are captured.
