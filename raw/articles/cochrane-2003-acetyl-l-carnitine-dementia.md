# Cochrane Review: Acetyl-L-Carnitine for Dementia

- Citation: Hudson SA, Tabet N. Acetyl-l-carnitine for dementia. Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews 2003, Issue 2. CD003158.
- DOI: 10.1002/14651858.CD003158.
- Search update noted by Cochrane page: November 2007 found no new studies.
- Ingested: 2026-05-03.

## Summary

The review included 16 double-blind randomized placebo-controlled trials in mild-to-moderate dementia or cognitive decline. It found some nominal signals: categorical clinical global impression favored ALCAR at 12 and 24 weeks, and MMSE favored ALCAR at 24 weeks. However, the review found no evidence of benefit on cognition, dementia severity, functional ability, or continuous clinical global impression as objective outcome areas.

The authors concluded that the significant categorical and MMSE findings could be due to chance given multiple comparisons and heterogeneity, and that ALCAR should not be recommended for routine clinical practice in dementia.

## Interpretation

Use this as the conservative dementia gate. It does not erase older positive MCI / early-AD analyses, but it keeps the medicinal signal contested and below recommendation-grade.
