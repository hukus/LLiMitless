# Task activation results in regional 13C-lactate signal increase in the human brain

- PMID: 39862181
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39862181/
- Ingested: 2026-04-30

## Summary

Healthy-volunteer hyperpolarized 13C MRI study showing that a visual stimulus increased regional 13C-lactate signal in activated cortex compared with the rest of the brain, while pyruvate and bicarbonate did not show the same activation-linked increase. This is not a cognition trial, but it is strong human evidence that task activation is coupled to measurable regional lactate production.
