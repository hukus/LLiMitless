# What is the clinical evidence to support off-label rapamycin therapy in healthy adults?

- PMID: 40778880
- DOI: 10.18632/aging.206300
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

2025 review of low-dose rapamycin and rapalogs in healthy adults. The authors conclude that human evidence is still insufficient to establish rapamycin as a proven senotherapeutic or aging-delay intervention.

The review is useful for framing community longevity use, but it does not provide cognition-specific efficacy evidence.
