# Metadoxine single-dose attention study in inattentive ADHD

- PMID: 25295645
- DOI: 10.3810/pgm.2014.09.2795
- Journal: Postgraduate Medicine (2014)
- Trial registration: NCT01685281

## Captured takeaways

- Double-blind, placebo-controlled crossover study in 36 adults with predominantly inattentive ADHD.
- Participants received single doses of metadoxine ER 1400 mg, metadoxine ER 700 mg, and placebo in randomized order.
- The primary endpoint was change in Test of Variables of Attention ADHD score from baseline to 3-5 hours after dosing.
- Metadoxine ER 1400 mg significantly improved TOVA ADHD score versus placebo; the 700 mg dose did not show significant benefit.
- Response-time variability and TOVA responder rate favored the 1400 mg dose.
- Exploratory Cambridge Neuropsychological Test Automated Battery measures did not yield significant findings.
- Fatigue and headache were the most frequently reported adverse events; no clinically significant vital-sign, lab, ECG, or C-SSRS abnormalities were reported in the abstract.
- Interpretation for the wiki: the acute attention signal is interesting but small, ADHD-PI-specific, dose-specific, and not enough to overcome later phase 3 failure.
