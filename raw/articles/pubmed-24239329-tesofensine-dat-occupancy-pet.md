# Tesofensine, a novel triple monoamine re-uptake inhibitor with anti-obesity effects: dopamine transporter occupancy as measured by PET

- PMID: 24239329
- DOI: 10.1016/j.euroneuro.2013.10.007
- Journal: European Neuropsychopharmacology (2014)

## Captured takeaways

- PET study using [11C]beta-CIT-FE to estimate striatal dopamine transporter occupancy after repeated oral tesofensine dosing.
- Tesofensine was described as blocking reuptake of dopamine, norepinephrine, and serotonin.
- Multiple oral doses of 0.125-1 mg over 8-12 days produced dose-dependent DAT blockade.
- Mean striatal DAT occupancy ranged from 18% to 77% across doses.
- The modeled maximum achievable DAT occupancy was about 80%, and approximately half of that effect was estimated at roughly 0.25 mg tesofensine / 4 ng/mL plasma concentration.
- The authors interpreted the DAT effect as one mechanism contributing to appetite and weight effects.
- Interpretation for the wiki: this is a target-engagement anchor for why tesofensine anecdotes can feel stimulant-like, but it is not a direct cognitive-efficacy endpoint.
