# Mazdutide, a dual agonist targeting GLP-1R and GCGR, mitigates diabetes-associated cognitive dysfunction

- Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12205698/
- Captured via: PubMed/PMC index

## Summary

2025 preclinical study in male db/db mice showing that mazdutide (IBI362) improved cognitive performance versus dulaglutide in a diabetes-associated cognitive dysfunction model. The paper combined behavioral testing with transcriptomic, proteomic, and metabolomic analyses and reported improved neuronal integrity, reduced demyelination, and pathway signals consistent with neuroprotection, energy metabolism, and synaptic plasticity. This is animal evidence only, but it strengthens the mechanistic rationale behind the LIGHT-COG registry trial.
