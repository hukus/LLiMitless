# Source Digest

Type: open-access healthy-adult cognitive study.

Publication date: 2020.

Key extraction:
- Study question: whether dopamine precursor L-tyrosine affects working-memory gating and updating.
- Main interpretation from title and abstract metadata: effects were baseline-dependent for working-memory gating, not updating.
- Wiki relevance: this supports the idea that tyrosine is response-state dependent and may help selected dopamine-gating phenotypes, not uniformly improve every cognitive domain.
- Caveat: not a broad stress-rescue trial and not a direct daily nootropic recommendation.

Wiki signal:
- Medicinal signal: low mechanistic / phenotype-dependent human cognition signal.
- Confidence limiter: task-specific working-memory-control endpoint and baseline-dependent moderation.
