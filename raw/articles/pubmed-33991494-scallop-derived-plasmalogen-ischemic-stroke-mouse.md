# Neuroprotective effects of Scallop-derived plasmalogen in a mouse model of ischemic stroke

- PMID: 33991494
- Source: PubMed / Brain Research 2021
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

Mouse study reporting neuroprotective effects for scallop-derived plasmalogen in an ischemic stroke model. This provides additional preclinical support for the same source material used in the human AD/MCI trial, but it still remains animal evidence.

## Notes

- Supports a recovery / neuroprotection framing.
- Does not resolve the human cognition question.
