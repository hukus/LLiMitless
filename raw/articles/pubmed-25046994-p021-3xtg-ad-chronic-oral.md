# Disease-modifying effect of chronic oral P021 in 3xTg-AD mice

- PMID: 25046994
- DOI: 10.1016/j.nbd.2014.07.001
- Journal: Neurobiology of Disease (2014)

## Summary

Female 3xTg-AD and wild-type mice received P021 or vehicle in feed for 12 months beginning at 9-10 months of age. P021-treated transgenic mice showed improved cognitive, neurogenesis, and synaptic-plasticity measures; lower tau hyperphosphorylation and accumulation; lower soluble amyloid-beta; and only a trend toward reduced hippocampal plaque burden.

The proposed pathway was increased BDNF expression followed by lower GSK3-beta activity. The soluble-versus-aggregated amyloid distinction matters: the paper did not show broad plaque clearance.

## Evidence boundary

The study used a transgenic mouse model, very long exposure, and a treatment start before advanced tau pathology. It does not establish human disease modification, safety, dose, or route.
