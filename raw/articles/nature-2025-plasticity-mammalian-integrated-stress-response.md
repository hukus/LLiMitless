# Plasticity of the mammalian integrated stress response

## Summary

2025 Nature mechanistic paper showing that mammalian ISR biology is more plastic than the simple canonical eIF2alpha-phosphorylation model. The paper describes a split ISR triggered by reduced eIF2B activity in the absence of eIF2alpha phosphorylation, with distinct translation and transcription programs and an eIF4E-ATF4-PCK2 axis that helps maintain cellular bioenergetics.

## Notes

- This source is not a cognition intervention paper, but it is important for interpreting eIF2B activators and ISR modulators.
- It argues against treating all ISR suppression or activation as one uniform mechanism.
- The molecule atlas should treat eIF2B activators, PERK-pathway agents, and viral PPP1R15B-like tools as separate mechanistic routes until human endpoint data exist.
