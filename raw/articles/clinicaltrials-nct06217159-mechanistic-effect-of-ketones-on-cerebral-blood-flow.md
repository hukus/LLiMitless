- Status: completed as of the current scrape; no results posted
- Sponsor: McMaster University
- Last update posted: 2025-04-23
- Summary: single-dose ketone monoester pilot in young adults that tests whether manipulating end-tidal CO2 changes ketone-linked cerebral blood flow and cerebrovascular reactivity after ingestion.
