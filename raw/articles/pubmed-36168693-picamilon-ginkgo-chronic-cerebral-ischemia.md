# Picamilon Ginkgo versus ginkgo in chronic cerebral ischemia with cognitive impairment

2022 Zhurnal nevrologii i psikhiatrii imeni S.S. Korsakova randomized multicenter comparative study with English abstract, PMID 36168693, DOI 10.17116/jnevro202212209195.

Design: open multicenter randomized comparative study in 278 patients older than 45 with chronic cerebral ischemia and cognitive impairment. Picamilon Ginkgo combination group n=139; ginkgo biloba extract monotherapy group n=139; treatment duration 90 days.

Endpoints: MoCA, MMSE, Hamilton depression scale, EQ-5D quality of life, and patient/doctor subjective effectiveness.

Results: the abstract reports greater regression of cognitive impairment with combination therapy than with ginkgo monotherapy. End-of-study between-group differences were significant for MMSE (p=0.007) and MoCA (p=0.0003), with MoCA differences evident from day 30. EQ-5D dynamics also differed from day 30. Hamilton depression did not differ significantly. Both arms were reported safe and not accompanied by significant adverse events.

Interpretation: disease-state, open-label, combination-confounded evidence. It supports a regional vascular-cognitive-impairment lane for Picamilon-containing therapy, not standalone picamilon efficacy in healthy adults.
