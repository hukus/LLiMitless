# An orexin agonist promotes wakefulness and inhibits cataplexy through distinct brain regions

- PMID: 40233754
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40233754/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Preclinical mapping study showing an orexin agonist can support wakefulness and suppress cataplexy through different neural circuits. Useful mechanism support for the axis, but still not human cognition evidence.
