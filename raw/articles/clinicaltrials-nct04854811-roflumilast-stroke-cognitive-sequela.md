# Roflumilast to Treat Cognitive Sequela After Stroke (ROSTMEMA)

- ClinicalTrials.gov ID: NCT04854811
- ClinicalTrials.gov: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04854811
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Phase 2 randomized trial of chronic roflumilast in people with persistent cognitive complaints after stroke. The registry frames the question as episodic-memory recovery with secondary daily-function outcomes, making it one of the most relevant translational tests for PDE4 inhibition in a vascular-cognitive phenotype.

## Key points

- Population: adults 41-70 years old with cognitive complaints at least 1 year after stroke.
- Dose: roflumilast 100 micrograms daily for 12 weeks.
- Primary outcome: delayed recall on a 15-word verbal learning test.
- Status: study completion was recorded, but no results were posted in the registry record used here.
