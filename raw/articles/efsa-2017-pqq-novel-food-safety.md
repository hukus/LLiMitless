# EFSA BioPQQ Novel Food Safety Opinion

- Source: EFSA Journal.
- Published: 2017-11-20.
- DOI: 10.2903/j.efsa.2017.5058.
- Ingested: 2026-05-03.

## Summary

EFSA safety opinion on pyrroloquinoline quinone disodium salt, trade name BioPQQ, as a novel food for food supplements. The intended population was healthy adults excluding pregnant and lactating women, at a maximum proposed intake of 20 mg/day. EFSA noted that this proposed intake is far higher than estimated background dietary PQQ intake, and that human absorption, distribution, metabolism, and excretion data were limited.

The panel concluded that BioPQQ was safe under the intended conditions of use, based partly on composition/specification data, genotoxicity studies, a 90-day rat NOAEL, and human clinical studies up to 100 mg/day that did not raise safety concerns. EFSA also noted that the human studies were not designed to comprehensively assess renal function.

## Interpretation

Use this as a product-identity and safety gate for PQQ. It supports BioPQQ 20 mg/day supplement safety under specified conditions, but it does not establish cognitive efficacy or transportability to all PQQ-containing products.
