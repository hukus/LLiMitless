# Correlation Between Irisin and Cognitive Functions in Alzheimer Dementia

- PMID: 40560607
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40560607/
- Ingested: 2026-04-29

## Summary

2025 observational cohort in biomarker-confirmed dementia-related symptomatology. CSF and serum irisin were lower in AD and MCI than in subjective memory complaint controls, and both compartments correlated with global cognition plus multiple domains. The paper reads as a biomarker/association study, not an intervention trial.

## Notes

- Cohort included AD (n=82), MCI (n=44), and SMC (n=20).
- Cognitive readouts included MMSE and a multidomain psychometric battery.
- Reported correlations covered memory, executive function, attention, visuospatial ability, and language.
- Main interpretation: irisin may be a cognitive biomarker of AD progression.
- Causal direction and treatment relevance remain unresolved.
