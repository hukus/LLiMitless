# A Conversational Platform (Okaya) for Multimodal Digital Biomarkers of Fatigue, Cognition, and Mental Health: Feasibility Observational Study

- PMID: 41921090
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41921090/
- Ingested: 2026-04-30

## Summary

Feasibility study showing that conversational platforms can collect multimodal digital biomarker data from video, audio, and text for fatigue, cognition, and mental health. The key limitation is that this is a platform feasibility paper, not a validated responder model.

## Notes

- Useful as a direct digital-phenotype source.
- Supports the idea that multimodal passive/interactive capture can track state at low burden.
- Does not prove response prediction for nootropic or stimulation interventions.
