# Multivariate prediction of cognitive performance from the sleep electroencephalogram

- PMID: 37574121
- Published: 2023-10-01
- Study type: cohort / multivariate prediction
- Key finding: cross-validated regularized regression in MrOS found sleep-EEG features explain 2.5-10% of variance in cognitive performance in independent validation samples, depending on covariates.
- Signal pattern: NREM sigma power and REM beta power were associated with better cognition; REM theta power was associated with worse performance.
- Why it matters: sleep physiology is a real trait/state input for the scorecard, but effect size is modest and sensitive to demographic and health covariates.
