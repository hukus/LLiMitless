# Tau PET imaging as a mediator between glymphatic dysfunction and cognitive decline: a cross-sectional and longitudinal study

- PMID: 41578064
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41578064/
- Ingested: 2026-04-30

## Summary

2026 human cross-sectional plus 2-year longitudinal study in MCI, AD dementia, other dementia, and cognitively normal participants. Lower DTI-ALPS was associated with higher tau burden and worse cognition; tau burden mediated about 21-27% of the DTI-ALPS/cognition association, and baseline lower DTI-ALPS predicted faster cognitive decline over time. This is not an intervention trial, but it is strong mediation evidence that glymphatic dysfunction may sit upstream of tau-linked cognitive deterioration.
