# Circadian stage-dependent and stimulation duration effects of transcutaneous auricular vagus nerve stimulation on heart rate variability

- PMID: 36327249
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36327249/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Healthy-volunteer HRV study showing taVNS effects depend on circadian stage. Morning stimulation increased vagal HRV markers more reliably than evening stimulation, and lower baseline vagal activity predicted larger autonomic shifts. Stimulation duration did not add a clear benefit beyond the early minutes, so timing appears more important than simply lengthening the session.

## Notes

- Acute physiology study, not a cognition trial.
- Suggests circadian-state gating for taVNS.
- Supports a morning-light + morning-taVNS hypothesis, but does not test the combination.
