# Frontiers 2024 TUDCA Alzheimer's disease review

This 2024 Frontiers in Neuroscience review discusses bile-acid metabolism and tauroursodeoxycholic acid as a possible prevention or treatment strategy for Alzheimer's disease.

The review frames TUDCA as an endogenous bile acid with neuroprotective actions in models of neurodegenerative disease. It discusses possible mechanisms including reduced amyloid-beta deposition, regulation of apoptotic pathways, prevention of tau hyperphosphorylation and aggregation, synaptic protection, anti-inflammatory effects, and metabolic modulation.

The review's experimental table includes preclinical AD models where TUDCA reduced amyloid pathology, altered Akt/GSK3-beta activity, reduced glial activation, and improved memory-like tasks such as Morris water maze, social recognition, and passive avoidance in animal models.

Interpretation for the wiki: this is a useful mechanism and preclinical AD anchor, not a human cognition-efficacy source. It supports keeping [[tudca-ursodiol]] on the neuroprotection watchlist while preserving a low-confidence rating for healthy adults.
