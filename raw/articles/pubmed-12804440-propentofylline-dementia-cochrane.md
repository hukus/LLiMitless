# Propentofylline for dementia

PubMed-indexed Cochrane review of propentofylline for dementia. Propentofylline is described as a xanthine derivative with potential glial/adenosine-related and microcirculatory mechanisms that was investigated for Alzheimer's and vascular dementia.

For the wiki, this is a legacy clinical-dementia program rather than a modern healthy-adult cognitive enhancer. It belongs as a comparator because it overlaps with adenosine/PDE/xanthine biology and appears in older nootropic literature.
