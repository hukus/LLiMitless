# Oral Chinese herbal medicine combined with donepezil for mild cognitive impairment: A systematic review and meta-analysis

- PMID: 39134455
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39134455/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

2024 systematic review and meta-analysis of 20 studies with 1,611 participants found that oral Chinese herbal medicine combined with donepezil improved MMSE and MoCA versus donepezil alone in MCI, with gastrointestinal symptoms and insomnia the most common adverse events. The review is supportive but still limited by "some concerns" risk-of-bias ratings across all studies and by formula-level heterogeneity; the frequently used formulas were Kai xin san and Si wu decoction.
