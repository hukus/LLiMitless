# Are TrkB receptor agonists the right tool to fulfill the promises for a therapeutic value of BDNF?

- PMID: 37488840
- PMCID: PMC10479861
- DOI: 10.4103/1673-5374.374138
- Publication: Neural Regeneration Research, 2024
- Evidence tier: review / translational caution

## Summary

This review argues that BDNF/TrkB signaling remains therapeutically relevant but that TrkB agonist approaches should be interpreted carefully. It distinguishes BDNF's broad pleiotropic biology and p75NTR-related liabilities from more selective TrkB-targeting tools. It also notes that several small-molecule TrkB agonist claims have not always been supported by sufficiently specific activation assays, and that observed benefits may sometimes reflect broader neuroprotection rather than clean TrkB agonism.

The review highlights TrkB agonist antibodies, including ZEB85-like biologic approaches, as an important next lane because they may avoid p75NTR binding and offer better target specificity.
