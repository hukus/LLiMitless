# Lutein and astaxanthin RCT systematic review in healthy adults

- Source type: systematic review of randomized controlled trials.
- Publication: Nutrients, 2020.
- Date checked: 2026-05-11.

This review searched for oral carotenoid RCTs in healthy young, middle-aged, and older adults with cognitive-function outcomes. Five lutein studies and two astaxanthin studies met inclusion criteria.

The authors concluded that lutein and its isomer showed consistent selective improvement in visual episodic memory in young and middle-aged adults, and an inhibition signal in middle-aged and older adults. They summarized 10 mg/day lutein for 12 months as the most interpretable dosing pattern. Astaxanthin had too few studies for a clear conclusion, with one included study reporting verbal episodic-memory improvement.

Use in the wiki: this is the cleanest review-level support for a [[lutein-zeaxanthin]] / carotenoid watchlist. It also argues against ranking astaxanthin as a separate cognition entity until more direct human evidence or strong anecdotes are captured.
