# GnRH replacement rescues cognition in Down syndrome
- PMID: 36048943
- 2022 Science paper combining Ts65Dn mouse work with a small adult DS human study.
- Key result: pulsatile GnRH improved cognition and brain connectivity in adult DS patients; the paper also reports mouse rescue of olfaction and novel-object recognition.
- Branch readout: strongest direct cognition signal on the GnRH axis, but it is disease-specific and not an enhancement study.
