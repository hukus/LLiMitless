# Sleep duration, chronotype, health and lifestyle factors affect cognition: a UK Biobank cross-sectional study

- PMID: 40018197
- Published: 2024-05
- Study type: cross-sectional UK Biobank analysis
- Sample: 26,820 adults aged 53-86 years
- Key finding: cognition was analyzed against sleep duration, chronotype, sleep quality, and health/lifestyle factors, including sex, age, vascular and cardiac conditions, diabetes, alcohol, smoking, and BMI.
- Why it matters: chronotype should be treated as part of a broader state/risk bundle rather than as an isolated device-response biomarker.
