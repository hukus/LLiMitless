# Notes
- Source: 2022 review of intranasal oxytocin and vasopressin in social cognition and delivery routes/mechanisms.
- Main claim: these neuropeptides have been studied for social cognition, but effects are inconsistent.
- Value: useful branch-level synthesis for route and mechanism caveats, especially the uncertainty around nasal-to-brain delivery.
- Classification: review, not efficacy proof.
- Caveat: mechanistic claims should stay separate from controlled human endpoint evidence.
