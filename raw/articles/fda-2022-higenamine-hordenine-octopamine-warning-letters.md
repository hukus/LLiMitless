# FDA 2022 warning-letter update for higenamine, hordenine, and octopamine

FDA's May 2022 constituent update announced warning letters to multiple companies selling adulterated dietary supplements. The named ingredients included higenamine, higenamine HCl, hordenine, hordenine HCl, and octopamine, alongside 5-alpha-hydroxy-laxogenin.

FDA said some products contained new dietary ingredients without the required premarket notification, some contained unsafe food additives, and some were also unapproved new drugs because of disease claims. FDA also noted prior concern about potential cardiovascular effects of higenamine and said the agency had not evaluated the unapproved products for effectiveness, proper dosage, interactions, or dangerous side effects.
