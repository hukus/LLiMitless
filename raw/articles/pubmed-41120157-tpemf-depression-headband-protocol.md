# Active Versus Sham Transcranial Pulsed Electromagnetic Field Headband Treatment for Major Depression

Source type: PubMed-indexed BMJ Open randomized-trial protocol.

Core design:
- Published 2025.
- Double-blind randomized active versus sham T-PEMF trial protocol for moderate to severe major depressive disorder.
- Planned N=96.
- Device: MoodHeadBand transcranial pulsed electromagnetic-field treatment.
- Treatment: daily 30-minute at-home sessions for eight weeks.
- Primary endpoint is depressive symptomatology.
- Cognitive functions are planned secondary endpoints at baseline, after one week, and at treatment completion.

Regulatory / protocol notes:
- The abstract reports Danish Medicines Agency and medical ethics approval.
- The project is described as aligning with EU medical device regulation and ISO 14155.

Wiki takeaway:
- The post-2024 clinical pipeline is mood-treatment first, cognition-secondary.
- This supports adding PEMF/T-PEMF to the device-dose ontology, but it does not establish cognitive enhancement.
- The branch should track sham headband design, field pattern, treatment adherence, and cognition battery results when posted.

Limitations:
- Protocol only; no outcome results yet.
- Depression phenotype; any cognition changes may be mood/sleep mediated.
- At-home use is in a regulated trial setting, not equivalent to uncontrolled consumer device use.
