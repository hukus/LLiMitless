# Transcranial Photobiomodulation for Alzheimer's Disease (TRAP-AD)

- ClinicalTrials.gov ID: NCT04784416
- Study URL: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04784416
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Large randomized phase 2 trial in aMCI and early AD. The design includes dose-dependent tPBM vs sham, imaging target engagement, 31P-MRS bioenergetics, and 8-week treatment blocks. This is the main scale-up study to watch for decisive evidence.
