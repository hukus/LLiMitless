## Summary

DailyMed label for dextroamphetamine sulfate tablets. The label indicates the product for narcolepsy and for attention-deficit disorder with hyperactivity as part of a total treatment program in pediatric patients. This is a regulatory and safety source, not evidence for healthy-adult cognitive enhancement.

The label carries a boxed warning for abuse, misuse, and addiction. It lists the product as a Schedule CII CNS stimulant and warns that misuse and abuse can lead to overdose and death, with higher risk at higher doses or unapproved routes.

## Safety Notes

- Assess abuse, misuse, and addiction risk before prescribing and monitor during treatment.
- Avoid use in patients with serious structural cardiac disease, cardiomyopathy, serious arrhythmia, coronary artery disease, or other serious cardiac disease.
- Monitor blood pressure and heart rate.
- Psychiatric warnings include worsening psychosis, induction of manic episodes, and new psychotic or manic symptoms.
- Other warnings include seizures, peripheral vasculopathy / Raynaud's phenomenon, serotonin syndrome with serotonergic drugs or CYP2D6 inhibitors, and motor or verbal tics / Tourette's syndrome worsening.
- Monoamine oxidase inhibitor use during or within 14 days is contraindicated because hypertensive crisis can occur.
