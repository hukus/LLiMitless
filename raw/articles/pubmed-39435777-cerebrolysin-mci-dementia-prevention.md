# Cerebrolysin in the preventive therapy of dementia in elderly patients with mild cognitive impairment: a three-year prospective comparative study

- PMID: 39435777
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39435777/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Open comparative study in 100 patients with amnestic MCI. Patients assigned to annual Cerebrolysin courses for 3 years had less cognitive decline and a lower conversion rate to dementia than the non-treated comparison group, but the design was not blinded and therefore remains vulnerable to selection and expectancy bias.
