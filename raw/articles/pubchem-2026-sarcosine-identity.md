# PubChem sarcosine identity note

PubChem identifies sarcosine as the compound also called N-methylglycine, methylglycine, and N-methylaminoacetic acid. The identity record is useful for keeping sarcosine separate from [[glycine]], [[dimethylglycine-dmg]] / DMG, [[betaine-tmg]] / TMG, [[s-adenosyl-l-methionine]] / SAMe, and generic "methylation support" labels.

Wiki interpretation: sarcosine belongs in two different maps. It is an NMDA/glycine-transporter-adjacent comparator because clinical cognition literature usually routes it through glutamatergic state restoration, and it is also a methylated-glycine identity boundary in the one-carbon branch. Its name alone should not make it inherit SAMe, TMG, DMG, glycine, sodium benzoate, or B-vitamin evidence.
