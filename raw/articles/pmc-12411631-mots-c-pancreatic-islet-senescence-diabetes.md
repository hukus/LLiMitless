# Mitochondrial-encoded peptide MOTS-c prevents pancreatic islet cell senescence to delay diabetes

- PMID: 40855115
- PMCID: PMC12411631
- DOI: 10.1038/s12276-025-01521-1
- Journal: Experimental & Molecular Medicine, 2025
- Source type: metabolic translational study; human biomarker plus mouse/cell intervention data
- Ingested: 2026-05-04

## Summary

2025 study linking MOTS-c to pancreatic beta-cell senescence and diabetes biology. The paper reports that MOTS-c levels decline with aging/senescence in islet-cell contexts, that MOTS-c treatment reduced senescence-linked signatures in aged mouse islets and improved glucose intolerance in mouse diabetes models, and that circulating MOTS-c was lower in people with type 2 diabetes than in healthy controls. The study strengthens the metabolic and senescence rationale for MOTS-c but does not include a human MOTS-c treatment arm or any cognition endpoint.

## Signal Use

- Medicinal / formal signal: human biomarker plus preclinical metabolic intervention support.
- Cognition signal: absent.
- Translation limit: lower endogenous MOTS-c in type 2 diabetes does not prove exogenous MOTS-c treatment improves cognition, metabolism, or safety in humans.
- Anecdotal signal: none.
