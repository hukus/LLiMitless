# Effects of Baduanjin exercise on cognitive function in older adults with mild cognitive impairment: a systematic review and meta-analysis

- Published: 2026-03-08
- Journal: Sport Sciences for Health
- DOI: 10.1007/s11332-025-01639-9
- Evidence layer: systematic review / preliminary meta-analysis

## Summary

This 2026 review took a narrower approach to Baduanjin in older adults with MCI and included three studies with 190 participants. The pooled Hedges g standardized mean difference for MoCA scores favored Baduanjin, with SMD 1.09 and 95% CI 0.49 to 1.68. Heterogeneity was substantial at I2 = 71%.

The authors concluded that Baduanjin may improve global cognitive function in older adults with MCI but stressed that the evidence is limited by the small number of studies, substantial heterogeneity, and the need for larger, more rigorous RCTs. This is the more conservative current anchor for the branch.
