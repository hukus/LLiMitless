# Low-intensity Hippocampal Transcranial Ultrasound Stimulation for Managing Cognitive and Sleep Dysfunction in Preclinical Alzheimer's Disease

- ClinicalTrials.gov: NCT07298876
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

Recruiting randomized, triple-blind, sham-controlled trial from the Chinese University of Hong Kong testing 2-week 500 kHz low-intensity hippocampal transcranial ultrasound stimulation in older adults with preclinical AD and sleep disturbance. Primary outcomes are PSQI and delayed word recall; secondary outcomes include objective sleep quality, plasma A beta and tau, and glymphatic function. No results are posted yet, so the branch remains protocol-level rather than outcome-positive evidence.
