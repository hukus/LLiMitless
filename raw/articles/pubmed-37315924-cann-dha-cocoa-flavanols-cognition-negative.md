# CANN DHA-Rich Fish Oil Plus Cocoa Flavanols Trial in Older Adults With Memory Complaints

- PMID: 37315924
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37315924/
- Ingested: 2026-05-03

## Summary

Randomized placebo-controlled parallel-design trial in 259 older adults with subjective cognitive impairment or mild cognitive impairment. The intervention combined DHA-rich fish oil (1.1 g/day DHA and 0.4 g/day EPA) with flavanol-rich dark chocolate supplying 500 mg/day flavan-3-ols. Follow-up occurred at 3 and 12 months; 197 participants completed the study.

The combined intervention did not improve the primary picture-recognition outcome or most cognitive, mood, BDNF, glucose, or brain-structure outcomes. Some secondary outcomes moved unfavorably in the intervention group, including reaction-time variability, alertness, executive function, and cortical volume.

Use in wiki: negative / attribution-limited counterweight. It does not isolate cocoa flavanols from omega-3s and dark chocolate, but it blocks broad claims that adding cocoa flavanols to a cognition stack reliably improves older-adult cognition.
