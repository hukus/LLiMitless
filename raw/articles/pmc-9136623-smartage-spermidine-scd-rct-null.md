# SmartAge spermidine SCD RCT null full-text details

2022 JAMA Network Open SmartAge phase 2b randomized, double-masked, placebo-controlled trial in 100 older adults aged 60-90 with subjective cognitive decline. Participants received 12 months of wheat-germ-derived spermidine-rich supplement providing about 0.9 mg/day spermidine, or microcrystalline-cellulose placebo. Eighty-nine participants completed the intervention with high compliance.

Primary outcome: no significant benefit on mnemonic discrimination performance versus placebo (between-group difference -0.03, 95% CI -0.11 to 0.05; p = 0.47). Secondary neuropsychological, behavioral, and physiological outcomes were also not meaningfully improved in intention-to-treat analyses. Exploratory analyses suggested possible verbal-memory, inflammation, and subgroup signals, but these were not confirmatory and did not overcome the null primary endpoint.

Safety: adverse events and serious adverse events were balanced, hematologic safety parameters were not concerning, and tolerability was good. Important caveats include low dose, product-specific wheat-germ extract, possible underdosing, limited amyloid/tau biomarker enrichment, and exclusion of participants with malignant neoplasms.
