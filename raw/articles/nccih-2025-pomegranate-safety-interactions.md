# NCCIH Pomegranate Usefulness and Safety

- Source: NCCIH Herbs at a Glance, Pomegranate
- URL: https://www.nccih.nih.gov/health/pomegranate
- Last updated by source: April 2025
- Ingested: 2026-05-03

## Summary

NCCIH says human evidence exists for a few pomegranate health questions, with many studies using juice rather than supplements. NCCIH states that limited research does not allow firm conclusions for most other health conditions.

Safety read: pomegranate juice is believed to be safe, and pomegranate extract may also be safe. Large amounts of root, stem, or peel may not be safe. Digestive symptoms and allergic reactions have been reported. Juice during pregnancy or breastfeeding may be safe, but safety of other forms or plant parts is less clear.

Interaction read: NCCIH's general herbal-product caution applies to pomegranate users taking medications; people using any medicine should discuss herbal products with a health care provider. Supplement forms are not preapproved by FDA before marketing.

Use in wiki: safety and product-form caveat. Treat juice, extract, peel/root/stem preparations, and supplement products separately, and avoid strong cognition claims without product-specific trials.
