# Lactobacillus helveticus-fermented milk containing lactononadecapeptide improves cognitive function in healthy middle-aged adults

- PMID: 28819993
- DOI: 10.1080/09637486.2017.1365824
- Published: 2018
- Ingested: 2026-05-11

## Summary

Randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial of a Lactobacillus helveticus-fermented milk drink containing lactononadecapeptide, sequence NIPPLTQTPVVVPPFLQPE. Healthy middle-aged adults received 190 g/day fermented milk drink or an equivalent placebo drink for eight weeks.

Cognition was assessed using the Japanese RBANS. The test group improved in total score, attention score, and delayed memory score. Attention differed significantly between placebo and test groups after eight weeks, p = 0.028.

## Relevance

This source supports a low-confidence [[fermented-milk-lactononadecapeptide]] branch: a food-derived peptide intervention with direct healthy-middle-aged-adult cognitive endpoints.

## Caveats

- Product and funding ties to Asahi-affiliated authors/manufacturer.
- Single product and Japanese healthy middle-aged cohort.
- Fermented milk, isolated peptide tablets, probiotic effects, and ordinary dairy should not be collapsed.
