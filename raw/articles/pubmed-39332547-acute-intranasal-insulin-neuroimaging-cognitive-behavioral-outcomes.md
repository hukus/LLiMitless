# Central effects of acute intranasal insulin on neuroimaging, cognitive, and behavioural outcomes: A systematic review

- PMID: 39332547
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39332547/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Systematic review of acute intranasal-insulin studies in adults reporting that age, sex, BMI, and insulin resistance moderate effects on brain activity, cerebral blood flow, functional connectivity, cognition, behavior, and stress responses.

## Notes

This review is the broadest single-source justification for treating central insulin action as phenotype dependent rather than uniform, which matters for any attempt to combine insulin with stimulation.
