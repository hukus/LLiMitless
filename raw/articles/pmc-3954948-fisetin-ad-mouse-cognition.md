# Fisetin maintains cognitive function in Alzheimer's disease transgenic mice

- Source type: open-access preclinical Alzheimer's disease mouse study.
- Publication: Aging Cell, 2014.
- PMID: 24341874.
- PMCID: PMC3954948.
- DOI: 10.1111/acel.12185.
- Date checked: 2026-05-03.

This study administered fisetin orally to APPswe/PS1dE9 Alzheimer's disease model mice from 3 to 12 months of age. Fisetin prevented development of learning and memory deficits and was associated with increased ERK phosphorylation, reduced protein carbonylation, reduced p25/p35 dysregulation, anti-inflammatory eicosanoid changes, and preservation of synaptic markers.

Use in the wiki: preclinical cognition anchor for fisetin. It supports mechanistic plausibility but does not establish human cognition efficacy.
