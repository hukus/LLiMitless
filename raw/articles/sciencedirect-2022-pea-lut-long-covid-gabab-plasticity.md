# PEA-LUT and cortical GABAB activity in long COVID

- Source type: clinical neurophysiology intervention study.
- Publication: Clinical Neurophysiology, 2022.
- Date checked: 2026-05-11.

This long-COVID study focused on patients with fatigue and cognitive problems. The mechanistic target was impaired cortical GABA(B)-ergic activity and reduced plasticity measured with TMS-derived long-interval intracortical inhibition and related plasticity measures.

The intervention was co-ultramicronized palmitoylethanolamide plus luteolin, described in the abstract as 700 mg + 70 mg twice daily for 8 weeks. The paper reports normalization or restoration of GABA(B)-ergic neurotransmission and cortical plasticity measures after PEA-LUT.

Use in the wiki: this supports a mechanistic post-viral / neuroimmune brain-fog watch item for [[palmitoylethanolamide-luteolin]]. It does not establish healthy-adult cognitive enhancement, and it should not be collapsed into generic PEA or generic luteolin claims.
