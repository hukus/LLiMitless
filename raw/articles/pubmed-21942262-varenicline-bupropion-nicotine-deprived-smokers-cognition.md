# Effects of varenicline and bupropion on cognitive processes among nicotine-deprived smokers

- PMID: 21942262
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21942262/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Randomized trial in 58 daily smokers after overnight abstinence. Varenicline 2 mg/day sped CPT reaction time but reduced accuracy versus placebo; bupropion showed sex-moderated effects on working memory and delay discounting.
