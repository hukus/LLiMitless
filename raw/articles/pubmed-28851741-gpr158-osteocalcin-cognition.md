# Gpr158 mediates osteocalcin's regulation of cognition

- PMID: 28851741
- PMCID: PMC5626410
- DOI: 10.1084/jem.20171320
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28851741/
- Ingested: 2026-05-04

## Summary

Journal of Experimental Medicine 2017 mechanistic mouse paper. Osteocalcin was necessary for the memory benefit of young plasma in older mice, and peripheral osteocalcin delivery improved hippocampal-dependent memory and reduced anxiety-like behavior in aged mice. The work identifies Gpr158 in CA3 hippocampal neurons as a receptor mediating osteocalcin's memory effects through signaling that includes IP3 and BDNF. This is a strong preclinical bone-brain mechanism anchor, but it is not human osteocalcin treatment evidence.
