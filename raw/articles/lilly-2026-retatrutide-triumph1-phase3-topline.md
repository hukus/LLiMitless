# Lilly 2026 TRIUMPH-1 retatrutide Phase 3 topline

Official Lilly release dated 2026-05-21 for TRIUMPH-1, NCT05929066.

## Captured takeaways

- TRIUMPH-1 was an 80-week randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled Phase 3 master trial in adults with obesity or overweight and at least one weight-related comorbidity, without diabetes.
- Retatrutide is described as a once-weekly investigational triple hormone receptor agonist activating GIP, GLP-1, and glucagon receptors.
- Weight-loss results at 80 weeks were large: -19.0% at 4 mg, -25.9% at 9 mg, -28.3% at 12 mg, and -2.2% with placebo using the efficacy estimand.
- Lilly also reported waist-circumference reduction and improvements in non-HDL cholesterol, triglycerides, systolic blood pressure, and hsCRP.
- The published topline release did not report a cognitive endpoint, cognitive test, dementia endpoint, brain-fog endpoint, mood endpoint, or cognition-specific adverse event.
- The adverse-event list was gastrointestinal-dominant and included nausea, diarrhea, constipation, vomiting, upper respiratory tract infection, dysesthesia, urinary tract infection, and dose-related discontinuation due to adverse events.
- Dysesthesia occurred more often on retatrutide than placebo and was generally mild to moderate, with most participants continuing treatment.
- Interpretation for the wiki: TRIUMPH-1 strengthens the metabolic and cardiometabolic evidence but does not establish a direct cognitive benefit or cognitive harm signal.
