# Transcutaneous auricular vagus stimulation (taVNS) improves human working memory performance under sleep deprivation stress

- PMID: 36473677
- Published: 2023-02
- Study type: within-subject sleep-deprivation experiment
- Key finding: after 24 h of sustained wakefulness, taVNS improved spatial 3-back accuracy versus active ear control, but did not specifically improve PVT.
- Why it matters: this is a high-load rescue signal, not a proof of broad wakefulness restoration or chronotype matching.
