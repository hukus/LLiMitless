# DailyMed 2026 Wellbutrin XL label

## Captured takeaways

- DailyMed label for Wellbutrin XL / bupropion hydrochloride extended-release tablets.
- The label frames seizure risk as dose-related and emphasizes gradual titration and dose ceilings.
- Warning sections include hypertension, activation of mania or hypomania, neuropsychiatric adverse events during smoking cessation, and suicidality language carried by antidepressant labeling.
- Bupropion exposure and interactions can be affected by CYP2B6 inhibitors or inducers; bupropion also inhibits CYP2D6 and can alter exposure of CYP2D6 substrates.
- The label makes bupropion a prescription risk-managed drug, not a casual nootropic. Eating-disorder history, seizure risk, alcohol/sedative withdrawal, other seizure-threshold-lowering drugs, BP/HR, insomnia/anxiety, and psychiatric activation are practical gates.
