# Modafinil Subjectively Does Not Impair Sleep in Aviators After a Period of Extended Wakefulness

- PMID: 38790126
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38790126/
- Ingested: 2026-04-29

## Summary

Randomized placebo-controlled aviation study in 32 subjects after a prolonged wake period. Participants received 300 mg caffeine, 200 mg modafinil, or placebo at midnight after a normal day and extended wakefulness.

Modafinil did not clearly worsen objective post-test sleep and subjectively did not negatively affect recovery sleep; recovery sleep was actually shorter than placebo. The paper is about sleep carryover rather than cognition, but it matters for schedule logic around wake-promoters.

## Notes

- Useful as a counterweight to the assumption that modafinil simply pushes sleep later in an obvious way.
- It does not establish anti-tolerance, but it does show that one night of wakefulness countermeasure use does not necessarily map to worse next-day sleep metrics.
