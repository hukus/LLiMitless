# Donepezil improves episodic memory in sleep-deprivation-vulnerable healthy adults

Randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled crossover Sleep study in 26 young healthy volunteers across rested and 24-hour sleep-deprivation conditions. Participants took donepezil 5 mg once daily for about 17 days or placebo before fMRI and delayed word-recognition testing.

Donepezil did not help when participants were well rested. Under sleep deprivation, the benefit depended on vulnerability: participants whose performance dropped more under untreated sleep deprivation improved with donepezil, while resistant participants showed little benefit or possible marginal decline. The behavioral effect aligned with task-relevant fMRI modulation.

Interpretation for the wiki: this supports a state-rescue / responder-vulnerability reading for [[donepezil]], not a broad rested-baseline cognition-enhancement claim.
