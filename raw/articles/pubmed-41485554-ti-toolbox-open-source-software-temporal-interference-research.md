# TI-Toolbox: An open-source software for temporal interference stimulation research

- PMID: 41485554
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41485554/
- Published: 2026-01-02
- Ingested: 2026-04-29

## Summary
Open-source software platform for the TI workflow, from MRI preprocessing through montage optimization, field simulation, and region-of-interest analysis.
The paper emphasizes Docker-based reproducibility, HD-EEG mapping, and individualized modeling for exposure assessment.

## Notes
This is infrastructure evidence rather than efficacy evidence, but it materially raises the quality ceiling for later TI studies.
