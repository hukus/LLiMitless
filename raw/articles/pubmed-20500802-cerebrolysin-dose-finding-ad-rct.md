# Efficacy and safety of Cerebrolysin in moderate to moderately severe Alzheimer's disease: results of a randomized, double-blind, controlled trial investigating three dosages of Cerebrolysin

- PMID: 20500802
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20500802/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Dose-finding randomized AD trial in moderate to moderately severe disease. Cerebrolysin improved global clinical function and cognition at week 24, with dose-specific effects and no major safety signal in the abstracted results.
