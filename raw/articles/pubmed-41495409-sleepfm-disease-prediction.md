# A multimodal sleep foundation model for disease prediction

PMID: 41495409
Published: 2026-02
Journal: Nature Medicine
Authors: Rahul Thapa, Magnus Ruud Kjaer, Bryan He, Ian Covert, Hyatt Moore, Umaer Hanif, Gauri Ganjoo, M Brandon Westover, Poul Jennum, Andreas Brink-Kjaer, Emmanuel Mignot, James Zou

Abstract: SleepFM is a multimodal sleep foundation model trained on over 585,000 hours of sleep recordings from about 65,000 participants. It is designed to work with any PSG montage and uses contrastive learning to generate embeddings that predict future disease risk. The PubMed abstract reports prediction of 130 future diseases, including dementia, stroke, death, heart failure, chronic kidney disease, atrial fibrillation, and myocardial infarction, with strong held-out performance. The model also performs well on standard sleep staging and sleep apnea tasks and generalizes to the unseen Sleep Heart Health Study dataset.

Research note: This is immediately useful as a disease-risk and enrichment tool. It is more relevant to responder stratification and endpoint selection than to any direct cognition-enhancement claim.
