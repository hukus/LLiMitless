# Source Digest

Type: preclinical rodent mechanistic study.

Publication date: 2018-07-11.

Key extraction:
- Molecule: thiamine tetrahydrofurfuryl disulfide, a thiamine derivative also known as TTFD.
- Acute administration enhanced locomotor activity in rats.
- In vivo microdialysis showed that enhanced locomotor activity synchronized with dopamine release in medial prefrontal cortex.
- D1 receptor antagonism in medial prefrontal cortex suppressed TTFD-enhanced locomotor activity, while D2 antagonism did not.
- TTFD dose-dependently increased voluntary wheel running.
- Interpretation: this is motivation / activity biology, not direct human cognition.

Wiki signal:
- Medicinal signal: preclinical-only for TTFD as a motivation / activity thiamine-derivative comparator.
- Confidence limiter: rodent locomotor and dopamine endpoints; not a cognitive-performance trial.
