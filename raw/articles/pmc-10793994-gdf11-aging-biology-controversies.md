## Summary

2023 Journal of Cardiovascular Aging review of GDF11 biology and controversies. The review emphasizes that GDF11 is a TGF-beta superfamily member closely related to GDF8 / myostatin, that GDF11 and GDF8 are biochemically distinct, and that GDF11 biology has been controversial because of assay specificity, recombinant-protein quality, dose, and experimental-design issues. It describes brain, heart, muscle, stroke, metabolism, and aging biology, notes that high-dose GDF11 can cause cachexia and premature death in mice, and argues that simple "GDF11 declines with aging and can be replaced" framing is incorrect. This is the main caution source for translating GDF11 rejuvenation claims.
