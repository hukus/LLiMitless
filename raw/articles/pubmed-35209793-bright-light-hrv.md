# Influence of daytime blue-enriched bright light on heart rate variability in healthy subjects

- PMID: 35209793
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35209793/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Healthy-young-adult crossover study (N=20) testing 5 hours of afternoon blue-enriched light at 1200 lx versus 200 lx. The intervention changed time-domain HRV, with higher SDNN and RMSSD under the dimmer condition and no clear frequency-domain effect with eyes open. The main relevance here is that bright light measurably shifts autonomic state, making it a plausible timing lever rather than a pure cognition booster.

## Notes

- Exposure was in the afternoon.
- The study supports a light-autonomic link in healthy adults.
