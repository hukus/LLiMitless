# FDA 2026 GLP-1 503B bulks proposal

- Source: https://www.fda.gov/news-events/press-announcements/fda-proposes-exclude-semaglutide-tirzepatide-and-liraglutide-503b-bulks-list
- FDA news release date: 2026-04-30
- Content current as of: 2026-04-30

## Summary

FDA announced that it is proposing to exclude semaglutide, tirzepatide, and liraglutide from the 503B bulks list. The agency said it did not identify a clinical need for outsourcing facilities to compound these drugs from bulk drug substances after evaluating nominations for the three substances.

The release explains the relevant 503B rule context: outsourcing facilities generally cannot compound drugs using bulk drug substances unless the substance appears on the 503B bulks list or the compounded drug is on FDA's drug shortage list at the time of compounding, distribution, and dispensing.

FDA said it will accept docket comments through 2026-06-29 before making a final determination.

## Research-use read

This is a compounding-policy signal, not a cognition-efficacy result. For the wiki's peptide and metabolic-drug branches, it matters because GLP-1 / GIP agonists are often discussed alongside peptide vendors and weight-loss compounding channels. The proposal strengthens the rule that approved-drug availability, shortage status, and clinical need must be separated from wellness, longevity, or cognition-adjacent marketing.
