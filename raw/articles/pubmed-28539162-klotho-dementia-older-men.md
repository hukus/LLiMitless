# Longevity Klotho gene polymorphism and the risk of dementia in older men

- PMID: 28539162
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28539162/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Prospective cohort of 527 men aged 71-87 who were dementia-free at baseline. KL-VS genotype was associated with higher dementia incidence in a dose-dependent pattern, with heterozygous and homozygous carriers showing higher risk than TT. This does not support a simple "more Klotho is better" story in humans.
