# Colored Light Exposure Ensues Chronotype-Based Responses: Evidence From QEEG Analysis

- PMID: 40709491
- Published: 2025-06-01
- Study type: chronotype-by-light exposure experiment
- Population: healthy participants grouped by morning, intermediate, and evening chronotype

## Captured takeaways

- The paper argues that light spectrum can produce chronotype-dependent electrophysiologic responses.
- It is relevant to chronotype because the authors explicitly frame light wavelength as a zeitgeber whose effect may vary by morning/intermediate/evening type.
- The result is closer to a physiology/timing anchor than a cognition-efficacy proof.
- For the wiki, this is useful as a color/light chronotype modifier signal, not as evidence that colored light improves cognition directly.
