# Predictive and mechanistic biomarkers of treatment response to Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation (TMS) in Psychiatric and Neurocognitive Disorders, identified via TMS-Electroencephalography (EEG) and Resting-State EEG: A systematic review

- PMID: 40925485
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40925485/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Systematic review of TMS-EEG and resting-state EEG biomarkers of TMS response in psychiatric and neurocognitive disorders. The review found the strongest predictive signal for greater baseline N100 amplitude before DLPFC rTMS or iTBS in unipolar depression, plus a smaller mechanistic signal for post-treatment N100 reductions and theta-connectivity reductions.

## Notes

- Conditions in scope included unipolar depression, Alzheimer's disease, bipolar depression, and schizophrenia.
- The review emphasizes that depression has the clearest biomarker signal so far.
- This is the best umbrella source for the EEG biomarker branch.
