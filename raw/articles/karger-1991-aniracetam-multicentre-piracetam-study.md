# Aniracetam (Ro 13-5057) for the Treatment of Senile Dementia of Alzheimer Type: Results of a Multicentre Clinical Study

- Source page: https://karger.com/dti/article-abstract/2/5/262/118128/Aniracetam-Ro-13-5057-for-the-Treatment-of-Senile
- Ingested: 2026-04-30

## Summary

1991 double-blind randomized multicentre study in 115 patients with probable Alzheimer-type dementia, comparing aniracetam against piracetam for 6 months. The abstract reports that the aniracetam group improved on the majority of psychobehavioural parameters, while the piracetam group improved to a lesser extent. Tolerability was described as excellent in both groups. Dose details were not exposed in the abstract snippet I captured.
