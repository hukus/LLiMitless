# Effects of virtual reality-based cognitive training on cognition, IADL, and depression in MCI

- DOI: 10.3389/fneur.2026.1728495
- Source: https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/neurology/articles/10.3389/fneur.2026.1728495/full
- Ingested: 2026-05-12

## Summary

Frontiers in Neurology 2026 systematic review and meta-analysis of randomized controlled trials testing virtual-reality-based cognitive training in older people with mild cognitive impairment. The review searched PubMed, EBSCOhost, CINAHL Complete, Cochrane, ProQuest, Scopus, and Web of Science through March 16, 2026 and registered the protocol in PROSPERO as CRD42024558108. Nineteen studies met inclusion criteria. Pooled analyses reported significant effects on cognitive function, including Montreal Cognitive Assessment and Trail Making Test B, plus instrumental activities of daily living and depressive symptoms on the Geriatric Depression Scale. The authors cautioned that findings should be interpreted carefully because of intervention heterogeneity, mixed risk of bias, and moderate certainty of evidence.

## Wiki Relevance

This is the freshest quantitative anchor for the VR cognitive-training sub-branch. It upgrades confidence that VRCT can move MCI-relevant cognition and function in controlled studies, but it does not convert the branch into a healthy-adult enhancer because the population is MCI, the protocols are heterogeneous, and durability/generalization remain open.
