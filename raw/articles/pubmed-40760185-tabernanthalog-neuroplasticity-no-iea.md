# The psychoplastogen tabernanthalog induces neuroplasticity without proximate immediate early gene activation

- PMID: 40760185
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40760185/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

2025 rodent study of tabernanthalog (TBG). The paper reports cortical neuroplasticity through the 5-HT2A, TrkB, mTOR, and AMPA pathway, but unlike classic psychedelics TBG did not trigger an immediate glutamate burst or immediate early gene activation. The work supports a non-hallucinogenic psychoplastogen mechanism, but it is still preclinical and does not establish human cognition benefit.
