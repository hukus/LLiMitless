# Notes
- Source: double-blind study in 40 healthy volunteers comparing lysine vasopressin, desmopressin, and placebo.
- Doses: nasal lysine vasopressin 23 IU/day or desmopressin 20 micrograms/day for 15 days.
- Outcome: no significant change in verbal or visual memory scores versus placebo.
- Use case: this is a direct negative comparator for old vasopressin/desmopressin nootropic claims.
- Safety: not highlighted as problematic in the abstract, but the efficacy signal was absent.
