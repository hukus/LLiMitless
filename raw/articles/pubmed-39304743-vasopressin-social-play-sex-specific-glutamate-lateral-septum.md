# Vasopressin regulates social play behavior in sex-specific ways through glutamate modulation in the lateral septum

- PMID: 39304743
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39304743/
- Ingested: 2026-04-29

## Summary

This 2025 rat study shows that vasopressin signaling in the lateral septum regulates social play in a sex-specific way through glutamate, not GABA. Blocking V1a receptors changed glutamate release and social play behavior differently in male and female juvenile rats, which makes vasopressin biology look much more conditional than a simple pro-social switch.

## Notes

- Source type: preclinical animal study.
- Main mechanistic point: AVP/V1aR signaling in the lateral septum affects extracellular glutamate/GABA balance, with sex-specific behavioral effects.
- Relevance: supports a sex- and circuit-dependent vasopressin mechanism for social behavior.
- Caveat: juvenile rat social play is not the same endpoint as adult human memory or cognition.
