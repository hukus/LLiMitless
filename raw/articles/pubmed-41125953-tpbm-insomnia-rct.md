# Transcranial photobiomodulation improves sleep quality, reduces daytime sleepiness, and modulates delta power in chronic insomnia: a randomized controlled trial

- PMID: 41125953
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41125953/
- Published: 2025-10
- Study type: randomized controlled trial in chronic insomnia
- Key finding: three sessions of frontal 810 nm tPBM improved PSQI, ESS, and frontal delta power versus sham, with no serious adverse events reported.

## Notes

- This is a sleep/wakefulness anchor for tPBM rather than a chronotype paper.
- It supports the idea that tPBM may be state-sensitive in insomnia, even though the study does not stratify by chronotype.
