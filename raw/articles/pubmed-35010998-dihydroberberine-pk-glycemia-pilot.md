# PubMed 35010998 dihydroberberine pharmacokinetics and glycemia pilot note

This Nutrients randomized, double-blind, crossover pilot trial compared placebo, 500 mg berberine, 100 mg dihydroberberine, and 200 mg dihydroberberine in five healthy male volunteers. Participants took three doses the day before testing and a fourth dose with a standardized test meal; plasma berberine, glucose, and insulin were measured over 120 minutes.

The abstract reports that dihydroberberine produced greater plasma berberine exposure than 500 mg berberine in this small sample. It did not show significant glucose or insulin differences during the short protocol, which the authors attributed to short exposure and insulin-responsive participants.

Interpretation for the wiki: dihydroberberine is mainly a bioavailability/formulation branch at this point. It should not be upgraded to a cognitive enhancer or glucose-control treatment from this pilot alone, especially because the sample was n=5, all male, short-duration, and not metabolically impaired.
