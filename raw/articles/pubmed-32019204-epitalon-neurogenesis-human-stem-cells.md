# AEDG Peptide (Epitalon) Stimulates Gene Expression and Protein Synthesis during Neurogenesis: Possible Epigenetic Mechanism

- PMID: 32019204
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32019204/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

Human gingival mesenchymal stem cells exposed to AEDG/Epitalon upregulated neurogenic markers such as Nestin, GAP43, beta Tubulin III, and Doublecortin. This is a mechanistic stem-cell paper, not a human cognition or safety study.
