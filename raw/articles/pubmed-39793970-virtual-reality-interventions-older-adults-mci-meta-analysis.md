# Virtual Reality Interventions for Older Adults With Mild Cognitive Impairment: Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis of Randomized Controlled Trials

- PMID: 39793970
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39793970/
- Ingested: 2026-04-29

## Summary

Systematic review and meta-analysis of RCTs of VR interventions in older adults with MCI. The review reports small but positive effects on attention and information-processing speed, with more modest effects on global cognition and noncognitive outcomes.

The abstract also reports low-to-moderate certainty across outcomes, which matters because VR enthusiasm can otherwise outrun the evidence quality.

## Notes

This is the best synthesis anchor for the MCI branch, but the effect is not broad enough to treat VR as a general cognitive enhancer.
