# Tadalafil Treatment in Patients With Cerebral Small Vessel Disease: The ETLAS-2 Randomized Clinical Trial

- PMID: 40718899
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40718899/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

2025 randomized placebo-controlled trial of tadalafil 20 mg daily for 3 months in cerebral small vessel disease after stroke/TIA. Adherence was worse on tadalafil, adverse events were much more frequent, and cognition did not differ overall despite a small imaging trend.

## Key points

- Population: 76 participants with CSVD; 71 started treatment.
- Outcome: feasibility, cognition, imaging markers, blood pressure, and adverse events.
- Signal: no cognition difference overall; a nonsignificant trend toward lower WMH volume appeared in per-protocol analysis.
- Safety: adverse events occurred in 76% on tadalafil versus 36% on placebo.
