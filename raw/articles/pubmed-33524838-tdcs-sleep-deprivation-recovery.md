# Effects of transcranial direct current stimulation on performance and recovery sleep during acute sleep deprivation: a pilot study

- PMID: 33524838
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33524838/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Pilot study in healthy participants with at least 30 h of sleep deprivation. Active tDCS during sleep deprivation tested cognitive performance and recovery sleep against sham.

## Notes

Useful as a mechanistic comparator showing that stimulation during a sleep-loss state is a different experiment from stimulation after sleep has been restored.
