# Low-dose naltrexone long-COVID pre-post study

2022 Brain, Behavior, & Immunity - Health interventional pre-post study of low-dose naltrexone in long COVID. Patients from a post-COVID clinic received two months of LDN, 1 mg in month one and 2 mg in month two, and completed quality-of-life questionnaires before and after treatment.

The study enrolled 52 participants; 40 (76.9%) were female and median age was 43.5 years. The design focused on safety and symptom/QOL change and did not include a placebo control.

Interpretation for the wiki: supports tolerability and hypothesis generation for LDN in long COVID, but the uncontrolled pre-post design cannot establish causality.
