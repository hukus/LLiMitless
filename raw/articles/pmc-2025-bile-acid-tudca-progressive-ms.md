# Progressive MS bile-acid and TUDCA trial summary

This 2025 Med paper reports an observational bile-acid metabolomics analysis in multiple sclerosis and a randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled phase 1/2a TUDCA supplementation trial in progressive MS.

The trial randomized people with progressive MS to TUDCA 1 g twice daily or placebo for 16 weeks. The source reports that 47 participants were included in analyses, with 21 in placebo and 26 in TUDCA. Adverse events did not significantly differ between arms, and TUDCA increased serum levels of multiple bile acids.

No significant differences were noted in clinical or fluid biomarker outcomes. The TUDCA arm showed measurable biological changes in immune-cell subsets and gut-microbiome composition/function compared with placebo.

Interpretation for the wiki: this supports TUDCA as a biologically active and apparently tolerable disease-state metabolic intervention, but it does not establish cognition benefit. It is a target-engagement and safety/tolerability source, not a nootropic-efficacy source.
