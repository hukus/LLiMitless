# PMID 40917653: A foundation model for learning genetic associations from brain imaging phenotypes

- Study type: 2025 Bioinformatics Advances primary research.
- Core utility: COMICAL uses contrastive multimodal learning to pair SNPs with brain-imaging-derived phenotypes and surface many-to-many associations.
- Scale / data: UK Biobank brain imaging phenotypes and genetic markers, with pretrained weights and code available.
- Reported output: significant genetic-marker to imaging-phenotype associations across neurological disorders, plus disease and unseen clinical-outcome prediction from learned representations.
- Wiki read: useful as a cross-modal phenotype-transfer and biomarker discovery tool, not as a cognition intervention.
