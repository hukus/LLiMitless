# Short Peptides Protect Fibroblast-Derived Induced Neurons from Age-Related Changes

- PMID: 39518916
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39518916/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

This human-cell aging model tested EDR, KED, and AEDG peptides on induced cortical neurons derived from elderly donors. The peptides increased dendritic arborization and primary processes, showing that EDR/Pinealon still has a living mechanistic signal in modern human-cell work. The result remains preclinical and does not establish human cognitive benefit.
