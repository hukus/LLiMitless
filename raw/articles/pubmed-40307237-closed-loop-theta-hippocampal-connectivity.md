# Closed-loop control of theta oscillations enhances human hippocampal network connectivity

Nature Communications 2025 study in seven neurosurgical patients. The team phase-locked direct electrical stimulation of lateral temporal cortex to ongoing hippocampal theta. Theta-synchronized stimulation increased hippocampal theta during delivery and produced persistent post-stimulation increases in hippocampal-neocortical connectivity relative to phase-blind control.

This is a physiology-first closed-loop cognition paper, not a direct healthy-adult performance trial. It supports the mechanistic claim that timing stimulation to endogenous rhythms matters for hippocampal network communication, but it does not establish a general cognitive enhancement effect in healthy adults.
