# [Effectiveness of cortexin in the complex treatment of patients with chronic alcohol encephalopathy and polyneuropathy]

- PMID: 20517210
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20517210/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

This open randomized study reported cortexin use in chronic alcohol encephalopathy and polyneuropathy. It is another human recovery-context signal, but it does not establish healthy-adult cognitive enhancement.
