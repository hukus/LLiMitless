# ACS 2025: Zalsupindole neuroplastogen preclinical profile

ACS Chemical Neuroscience published the peer-reviewed article "Zalsupindole is a Nondissociative, Nonhallucinogenic Neuroplastogen with Therapeutic Effects Comparable to Ketamine and Psychedelics." Search/open snippets describe zalsupindole, also called AAZ-A-154, as a brain-penetrant nonhallucinogenic neuroplastogen that promoted neuroplasticity in rats to an extent compared with ketamine, psilocybin, and DMT.

The source is a preclinical neuroplasticity and therapeutic-model anchor. It supports the claim that zalsupindole is more than a marketing label, but it does not establish human cognition benefit, healthy-adult enhancement, or brain-fog treatment.
