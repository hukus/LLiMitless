# A Study to Evaluate the Safety and Efficacy of CT1812 in Early Alzheimer's Disease

- ClinicalTrials.gov ID: NCT05531656
- Sponsor: Cognition Therapeutics
- Status: Active, not recruiting
- Last update posted: 2025-09-22
- Evidence tier: registry / protocol record

## Summary

This is the long-duration early-AD phase 2 CT1812 study. It is a multicenter randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial designed for approximately 18 months.

Participants are randomized 1:1:1 to CT1812 100 mg, CT1812 200 mg, or placebo, given orally once daily as two capsules. The study aims to enroll about 540 participants with MCI due to AD or mild AD dementia.

The primary endpoint is change in CDR-SB at 18 months. Secondary outcomes include ADAS-Cog 13, ADCS-ADL-MCI, CSF and plasma biomarkers, and volumetric MRI.
