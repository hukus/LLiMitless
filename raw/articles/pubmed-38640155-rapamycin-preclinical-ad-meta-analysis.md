# Effect and Mechanism of Rapamycin on Cognitive Deficits in Animal Models of Alzheimer's Disease: A Systematic Review and Meta-analysis of Preclinical Studies

- PMID: 38640155
- DOI: 10.3233/JAD-231249
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

2024 systematic review/meta-analysis of preclinical AD models. The paper retrieved 4,277 records and synthesized behavioral and pathological outcomes.

Directionally, the animal literature supports improved cognitive deficits and AD pathology with rapamycin, but the evidence is still preclinical and does not establish human cognition benefit.
