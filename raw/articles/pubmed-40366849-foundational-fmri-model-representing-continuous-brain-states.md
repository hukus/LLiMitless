# PMID 40366849: A Foundational fMRI Model for Representing Continuous Brain States

- Study type: 2025 IEEE Journal of Biomedical and Health Informatics primary research.
- Core utility: BrainSN represents continuous brain-state information rather than only fixed windows, which is closer to a state-tracking backbone than a static classifier.
- Scale / data: 1,256 hours of resting-state and naturalistic-stimulus fMRI.
- Reported output: 75.23% autism diagnosis accuracy and 75.82% attention-disorder diagnosis accuracy without fine-tuning, 95.31% mental-state decoding accuracy without fine-tuning, and sequence-sensitive embeddings for movie-stimulus content.
- Wiki read: useful for clinical diagnosis, treatment evaluation, and cognitive neuroscience research, but still a prediction/state-representation tool rather than an intervention study.
