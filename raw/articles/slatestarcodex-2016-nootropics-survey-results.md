# 2016 Nootropics Survey Results

- Source type: open-label community survey
- Published: 2016-03-01
- Respondents: 850 in the 2016 run, up from 150 in the earlier survey

## Summary

Scott Alexander's 2016 survey is the older public anchor for this branch. The survey asked 850 respondents to rate nootropics on a 0-10 scale and reported materially less noisy results than the earlier 150-person version.

The survey is informative for ranking and hypothesis generation, but it is still open-label, self-selected, and expectation-heavy. The most useful output is relative ordering, not absolute efficacy.

## Key signals

- The survey reported that PRL-8-53 rated above picamilon in one comparison, and ashwagandha beat rhodiola by about 0.9 points.
- The narrative singled out phenibut, modafinil, tianeptine, Semax, and nicotine as strong or notable signals.
- Nicotine remained a mixed comparator: overall addiction concerns were significant, and the smoking versus gum/lozenge split mattered.

## Caveats

- Self-selected respondents.
- No placebo control.
- No blinding.
- Product identity and use-context confounds remain substantial.
