# Source Digest

Type: rapid evidence assessment of tyrosine for stress and performance in healthy adults.

Publication date: 2015-07-01.

Key extraction:
- Scope: supplemental tyrosine for mitigating physical or cognitive performance decrements under high physical or psychological stress.
- Evidence base: 10 randomized controlled trials and 4 controlled clinical trials met inclusion criteria.
- Assessment framework: Samueli Institute rapid evidence assessment methodology, Scottish Intercollegiate Guidelines 50 criteria, and GRADE framework.
- Physical performance conclusion: no recommendation could be made for physical performance under stressful physical conditions.
- Cognitive stress conclusion: a weak recommendation in favor of tyrosine was made because all cognitive-stress studies showed a positive effect.
- Overall conclusion: evidence was insufficient for confident recommendations, but tyrosine may benefit cognitive performance under stress and deserves further study.

Wiki signal:
- Medicinal signal: low-moderate for stress-state cognitive protection; not broad enhancement.
- Confidence limiter: weak recommendation, stress-state specificity, and evidence confidence limits.
