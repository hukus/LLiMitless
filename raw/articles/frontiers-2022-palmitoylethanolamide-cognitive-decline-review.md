# Source Digest

Type: systematic review and preliminary meta-analysis of preclinical and clinical evidence.

Publication date: 2022-10-28.

Key extraction:
- Scope: palmitoylethanolamide and bio-behavioral correlates in neurocognitive disorders and cognitive decline.
- Included outputs: 33 eligible studies, mostly preclinical; the review describes 4 human in vivo PEA-treatment studies plus related human biological studies.
- Preclinical readout: animal models generally supported memory/learning and neuroprotection effects through oxidative-stress, neuroinflammation, BDNF, glutamatergic, PPAR-alpha, mast-cell, astrocyte, amyloid, and tau-related pathways.
- Human readout: clinical evidence was limited and heterogeneous. The review reported possible improvements in fatigue, cognitive impairment, executive function, working memory, language deficits, daily living activities, and cortical oscillatory/GABAergic measures.
- Meta-analysis detail: 3 human studies using MMSE contributed 282 patients. The pooled change-from-baseline estimate was 3.80 points with 95% CI -0.16 to 7.75 and very high heterogeneity, I-squared 98.40%.
- Tolerability statement: the review reported that PEA was well tolerated across the included human studies, with no relevant side effects noted in those studies.

Wiki signal:
- Medicinal signal: low for clinical-state cognitive decline; promising but too heterogeneous for strong inference.
- Confidence limiter: human studies were few, often uncontrolled, condition-specific, and highly heterogeneous; preclinical disease-modification logic must not be converted into healthy enhancement claims.
