# Emraclidine, a novel positive allosteric modulator of cholinergic M4 receptors, for the treatment of schizophrenia: a two-part, randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled, phase 1b trial

- PMID: 36528376
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36528376/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

Phase 1b schizophrenia study of emraclidine, a selective M4 positive allosteric modulator. The trial enrolled stable and acutely ill participants, found acceptable tolerability, and reported a clinically meaningful PANSS signal in the acute-schizophrenia part of the study. It is a psychiatry efficacy anchor, not a direct cognition trial, and the reported outcome set is symptom-first rather than neurocognitive.
