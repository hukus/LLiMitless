# Influence of a 2-week transcutaneous auricular vagus nerve stimulation on memory: findings from a randomized placebo controlled trial in non-clinical adults

- PMID: 39039354
- Springer: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10286-024-01053-0
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Single-blind randomized placebo-wait-list trial in 76 non-clinical adults. Two weeks of daily 4-hour auricular taVNS improved immediate recall and short-term memory score versus sham, with the strongest signal in immediate recall; delayed recall was not statistically different. A 2025 correction was published for this article, so any downstream summary should treat the result as corrected and version-aware.

## Notes

- Early and late active/sham groups let the authors test timing and replication across windows.
- The task used was the Rey Auditory Verbal Learning Test.
- The paper supports a narrow memory-enhancement claim, not a general cognition claim.
