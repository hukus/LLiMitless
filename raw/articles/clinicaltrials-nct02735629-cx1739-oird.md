# CX1739 antagonism of opioid-induced respiratory depression

- ClinicalTrials.gov: NCT02735629
- Sponsor: RespireRx
- Collaborator: Duke University
- Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02735629
- Ingested: 2026-05-04

## Summary

ClinicalTrials.gov registry record for a phase 2 crossover study of CX1739 in healthy male volunteers to test whether ascending doses of CX1739 could antagonize remifentanil-induced respiratory depression while preserving analgesia and sedation measures. The registry lists 300 mg, 600 mg, and 900 mg CX1739 dose arms, placebo, actual enrollment of 18, and unknown status with last known status active-not-recruiting in 2016.

This is a respiratory-drive / safety-development record, not a cognition trial. It supports the status of CX1739 as a low-impact ampakine with human exposure outside cognition, while keeping cognitive-enhancement confidence low.
