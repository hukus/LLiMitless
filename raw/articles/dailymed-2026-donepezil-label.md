# DailyMed donepezil label safety context

DailyMed January 2026 donepezil hydrochloride tablet label. Donepezil is an acetylcholinesterase inhibitor indicated for dementia of the Alzheimer's type, with demonstrated efficacy in mild, moderate, and severe Alzheimer's disease. Labeled dosing begins at 5 mg once daily and may rise to 10 mg/day for mild-to-moderate Alzheimer's disease or 23 mg/day in moderate-to-severe Alzheimer's disease after prior 10 mg/day exposure.

Key label safety issues include vagotonic effects causing bradycardia or heart block, reported syncope, nausea, diarrhea, vomiting, GI bleeding / peptic-ulcer monitoring, weight loss at higher dose, possible bladder outflow obstruction, potential generalized convulsions, pulmonary caution in asthma or obstructive pulmonary disease, anesthesia interaction with succinylcholine-type muscle relaxation, and interaction with anticholinergic or other cholinergic drugs.

Interpretation for the wiki: donepezil is a prescription dementia drug, not a casual nootropic. Safety burden and off-label medical supervision dominate healthy-adult experimentation claims.
