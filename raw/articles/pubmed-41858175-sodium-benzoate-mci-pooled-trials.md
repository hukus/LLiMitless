# Sodium benzoate, a D-amino acids oxidase inhibitor, for the treatment of mild cognitive impairment: Pooled data from three randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trials

- PMID: 41858175
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41858175/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

This 2026 pooled analysis combined three aMCI trials (n=133) using 250-1500 mg/day sodium benzoate for 24 weeks. ADAS-cog improved versus placebo at weeks 16 and 24, with a stronger signal in women and an IADL benefit in the female subgroup. Safety remained similar to placebo.
