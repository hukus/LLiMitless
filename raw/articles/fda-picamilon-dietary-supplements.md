# FDA Picamilon in Dietary Supplements

FDA page explaining the agency's view that picamilon does not meet the statutory definition of a dietary ingredient. Picamilon is a synthetic chemical entity made from niacin and GABA; FDA says products declaring it as a dietary ingredient are misbranded.

The page notes that picamilon is used as a prescription drug in Russia for neurological conditions but is not approved as a drug in the United States. It also lists synonyms including pikatropin, pikamilon, nicotinyl-GABA, and nicotinoyl-GABA.

Relevance: picamilon should be routed as a regulatory gray-zone / unapproved drug-like nootropic, not a lawful ordinary supplement in the U.S.
