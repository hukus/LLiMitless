# DailyMed guanfacine extended-release label

DailyMed label updated September 30, 2025 for guanfacine extended-release tablets. The label identifies guanfacine ER as a central alpha-2A adrenergic receptor agonist indicated for ADHD as monotherapy or adjunctive treatment.

Safety gates relevant to cognition and brain-fog self-experimentation include hypotension, bradycardia, syncope, sedation, somnolence, cardiac conduction abnormalities, rebound hypertension after abrupt discontinuation, high-fat-meal exposure changes, and CYP3A4 inhibitor/inducer interactions. The label advises measuring heart rate and blood pressure before treatment, after dose increases, and periodically during therapy. It also warns against crushing or chewing ER tablets and recommends tapering by no more than 1 mg every 3 to 7 days when discontinuing.

Interpretation for the wiki: guanfacine has a plausible prefrontal-cortex and brain-fog rationale, but the label-level safety pattern overlaps directly with long-COVID dysautonomia, POTS, fatigue, and dizziness phenotypes. Any cognition page must lead with medical supervision, BP/HR monitoring, sedation, and tapering constraints.
