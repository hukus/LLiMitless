# Osteocalcin and GPR158: linking bone and brain function

## Summary

Frontiers in Cell and Developmental Biology 2025 review of osteocalcin, GPR158, and the bone-brain axis. The review frames osteocalcin as an osteoblast-derived endocrine factor that can regulate CNS biology through GPR158, including neuronal survival, plasticity, energy metabolism, mood/stress, and possible links to Alzheimer's and Parkinson's disease. The review is mechanism-rich and useful for target context, but it does not provide a direct human cognition intervention result.
