# Daily consumption of ketone ester, bis-octanoyl (R)-1,3-butanediol, is safe and tolerable in healthy older adults

- PMID: 39137624
- Study type: randomized parallel-arm double-blind placebo-controlled pilot
- Population: 30 older adults randomized, 23 completed
- Intervention: 25 g bis-octanoyl (R)-1,3-butanediol daily for 12 weeks
- Main finding: tolerability/safety study rather than cognition efficacy study
- Usefulness: extends the safety picture into older adults over a longer period

## Summary

This extends the safety signal beyond the 28-day ketone-monoester study, but it does not yet answer the cognition question directly.
