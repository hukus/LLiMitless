# Ginseng for Cognitive Function in Elderly Adults with Mild Cognitive Impairment, Alzheimer's Disease, or Dementia: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis

- PMID: 41863264
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41863264/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

2026 meta-analysis of nine randomized studies in 617 older adults with MCI, AD, or dementia found modest MMSE and ADAS-Cog gains for ginseng, with a subgroup signal around 4.5 g/day. The authors themselves note that the effect is biologically plausible but small and of uncertain clinical significance.
