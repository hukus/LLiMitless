# Minocycline postoperative cognitive dysfunction randomized trial

2023 Anesthesiology randomized double-blind placebo-controlled trial in older adults undergoing total knee arthroplasty. Patients were randomized to minocycline 100 mg twice daily from the day before surgery through postoperative day 7 or placebo. Cognitive testing occurred before surgery, at 1 week, and at 3 months.

There was no significant reduction in postoperative cognitive dysfunction with minocycline. At 1 week, POCD occurred in 8/90 minocycline patients versus 4/95 placebo patients; at 3 months, the imputed rates were 17.0% versus 16.1%. Postoperative delirium and pain also did not differ.

Interpretation for the wiki: perioperative neuroinflammation is a plausible mechanism, but this clinical test did not support minocycline as a postoperative cognitive-protection intervention.
