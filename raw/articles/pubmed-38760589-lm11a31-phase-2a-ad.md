# p75 neurotrophin receptor modulation in mild to moderate Alzheimer disease: a randomized, placebo-controlled phase 2a trial

- PMID: 38760589
- Publication: Nature Medicine, 2024
- Evidence tier: human phase 2a randomized trial

## Summary

This 26-week multicenter, randomized, placebo-controlled, double-blind phase 2a trial tested LM11A-31 in 242 participants with mild-to-moderate AD. Participants received placebo, 200 mg LM11A-31, or 400 mg LM11A-31 twice daily by oral capsules.

The trial met its primary safety/tolerability endpoint. It found significant drug-placebo differences in prespecified biomarker and imaging domains, including structural MRI, FDG-PET, and CSF biomarkers, but no significant cognitive benefit on active treatment.

## Safety

- Most frequent AEs: nasopharyngitis, diarrhea, headache, eosinophilia.
- The 400 mg group had more GI-related AEs and more discontinuations.
- A single gastrointestinal bleeding SAE was considered possibly related; MRI did not raise new safety concerns.

## Wiki Use

- Use as the biomarker-positive / cognition-negative anchor for p75NTR modulation.
- Keep future follow-up trials distinct from this completed phase 2a readout.
