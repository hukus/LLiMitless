# Semax copper ROS and Abeta cell-model paper

- Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12151629/
- Article: Semax, a Copper Chelator Peptide, Decreases the Cu(II)-Catalyzed ROS Production and Cytotoxicity of Abeta by Metal Ion Stripping and Redox Silencing
- Journal: Bioinorganic Chemistry and Applications, 2025
- DOI: 10.1155/bca/4226220
- PMID: 40496623
- PMCID: PMC12151629

## Summary

Tomasello and colleagues studied Semax as a copper-binding peptide in amyloid-beta / copper model systems relevant to Alzheimer's disease chemistry. The paper reports that Semax can extract Cu(II) from Abeta-copper species, reduce copper-linked redox cycling and reactive oxygen species production, and protect SH-SY5Y cells against oxidative stress induced by copper-catalyzed Abeta oxidation.

## Research-use read

This is a cell / bioinorganic chemistry mechanism source. It supports an Abeta-copper / oxidative-stress plausibility lane for Semax, but it does not test behavioral cognition, human outcomes, intranasal delivery, or clinical safety.
