# Effect of acute cardiovascular exercise on cerebral blood flow: A systematic review

- PMID: 37003561
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37003561/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary
Across 52 studies, acute cardiovascular exercise generally follows an inverted-U effect on cerebral blood flow: it rises early and then falls with longer duration or higher load. This is useful physiology for the reserve branch, but it does not by itself prove cognition enhancement.
