# ClinicalTrials.gov NCT06366724 LIFT pyridostigmine and LDN in ME/CFS

## Captured takeaways

- NCT06366724 is the LIFT: Life Improvement Trial sponsored by Brigham and Women's Hospital.
- The study targets people aged 18-70 meeting Canadian Consensus Criteria for ME/CFS and specifically having orthostatic intolerance; the condition fields include ME/CFS, long COVID, and PASC.
- The intervention set is pyridostigmine, low-dose naltrexone, and placebo in a randomized factorial double-blind placebo-controlled phase 2 design.
- Estimated enrollment is 160.
- The record lists actual study start as 2024-09-10, estimated primary completion as 2026-09, and estimated study completion as 2026-11.
- Pyridostigmine is planned as a liquid suspension three times daily, scaling from 20 mg TID to 40 mg TID and then 60 mg TID during weeks 5-13.
- Low-dose naltrexone is planned as a once-daily suspension scaling to 4.5 mg/day.
- The ClinicalTrials.gov record was recruiting and had no posted results in the captured search record.
- Interpretation for the wiki: LIFT is the key controlled follow-up for pyridostigmine plus LDN in ME/CFS / long-COVID-like orthostatic-intolerance phenotypes; do not treat it as positive until results are posted or published.
