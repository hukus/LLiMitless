# Transcranial Blood-Brain Barrier Opening in Alzheimer's Disease Patients Using a Portable Focused Ultrasound System with Real-Time 2-D Cavitation Mapping

- PMID: 39113808
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39113808/
- PMC: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11303073/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

This 2024 AD clinical trial report pairs transcranial BBB opening with a portable focused ultrasound system and real-time 2-D cavitation mapping. It strengthens the operational case for monitored BBB opening in humans, but it remains a feasibility/targeting paper rather than evidence that drug delivery improved cognition.
