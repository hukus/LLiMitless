# Alpha-lipoic acid in Alzheimer's disease review

2021 Life Sciences review on alpha-lipoic acid as an Alzheimer's disease candidate. The review frames alpha-lipoic acid around antioxidant, mitochondrial, anti-inflammatory, and metal-chelation mechanisms.

Use in wiki: mechanism plausibility for alpha-lipoic acid is stronger than direct cognition proof. Keep clinical interpretation anchored to small or combination trials and do not promote as general enhancement.
