# Pterostilbene natural neuroprotective stilbene AD review

- Source type: PubMed-indexed review / Journal of Pharmaceutical Analysis open-access review.
- PMID: 40291020
- PMCID: PMC12032911
- DOI: 10.1016/j.jpha.2024.101043
- Published: 2025 Apr; epub 2024-07-17.
- Date checked: 2026-05-04.

The review summarizes pterostilbene as a dimethylated resveratrol analogue with preclinical anti-Alzheimer's disease evidence. It frames the current evidence as in vivo and in vitro work, not human cognition efficacy. Mechanisms discussed include oxidative-stress reduction, anti-neuroinflammation, anti-apoptosis, cholinesterase activity inhibition, beta-amyloid and tau-pathology attenuation, PPAR-alpha, MAO-B, SIRT1, PDE4A, network pharmacology, and molecular docking.

Use in the wiki: current-review anchor for pterostilbene as a preclinical AD / Nrf2 / SIRT1 / inflammation hypothesis. Do not use it as human cognition proof.
