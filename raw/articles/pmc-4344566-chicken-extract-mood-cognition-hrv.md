# The Effect of Chicken Extract on Mood, Cognition and Heart Rate Variability

- PMCID: PMC4344566
- DOI: 10.3390/nu7020887
- Published: 2015
- Ingested: 2026-05-11

## Summary

Randomized supplement study of BRAND'S Essence of Chicken or placebo in 46 young adults, using a psychological stressor with mood, cognition, heart-rate variability, and cortisol measures at baseline and after 10 days of supplementation.

The captured abstract reports that essence of chicken produced lower anxiety, depression, confusion, and higher agreeableness and clearheadedness. HRV decreased after essence of chicken only in females. Cognition and cortisol were not influenced.

## Relevance

Useful mood / clearheadedness and stress-state context for [[essence-of-chicken]]. It should not be counted as a cognition-positive trial because objective cognition did not move.

## Caveats

- Small sample.
- Objective cognition was null.
- Mood and clearheadedness are subjective state outcomes.
- Product and comparator identity matter.
