# Effects of cholinesterase-inhibiting sage on mood, anxiety, and performance on a psychological stressor battery

- Source type: double-blind placebo-controlled crossover study.
- Publication: Neuropsychopharmacology, 2006.
- Date checked: 2026-05-03.

This trial tested 300 mg and 600 mg dried Salvia officinalis leaf in 30 healthy participants across three lab days. Mood was assessed before and after a Defined Intensity Stress Simulator computerized multitasking battery.

Both doses improved mood ratings before the stressor. The 300 mg dose reduced anxiety before the stressor but that effect disappeared after the task and was associated with lower alertness during performance. The 600 mg dose increased alertness, calmness, and contentedness and improved task performance at both post-dose sessions.

Use in the wiki: stressor-battery and dose-direction anchor. Sage may have state- and dose-dependent effects rather than a monotonic nootropic profile.
