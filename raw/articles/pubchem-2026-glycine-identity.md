# PubChem 2026 glycine identity note

PubChem identifies glycine as compound CID 750, formula C2H5NO2, molecular weight 75.07 g/mol, CAS 56-40-6, and IUPAC name 2-aminoacetic acid. Synonyms include glycine, aminoacetic acid, and aminoethanoic acid.

PubChem/ChEBI describe glycine as the simplest proteinogenic amino acid and the only achiral one, with a hydrogen side chain. The entry also classifies it as a neurotransmitter, nutrient/nutraceutical, NMDA receptor agonist, and fundamental metabolite.

Wiki use: glycine should have its own entity rather than being hidden inside [[glynac]], magnesium glycinate, collagen, [[dimethylglycine-dmg]], [[sarcosine]], [[betaine-tmg]], or [[d-serine]]. It is chemically adjacent to the one-carbon / methylated-glycine branch, but its main current cognition-relevant evidence is sleep-state support and negative/null acute healthy-cognition testing.
