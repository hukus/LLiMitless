# Effects of Creatine Supplementation on Cognitive Measures and Markers of Acute Kidney Injury After Exercise in the Heat.

- ClinicalTrials.gov ID: NCT07472426
- ClinicalTrials.gov: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07472426
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Recruiting crossover trial in active healthy adults aged 18-45 testing seven days of creatine monohydrate before exertional heat stress. Cognitive measures are assessed before and after exercise in the heat, alongside inflammation and kidney-injury markers.

## Notes

This is a useful high-demand healthy-adult model because it combines heat stress, fatigue, and cognition in a short supplementation window.
