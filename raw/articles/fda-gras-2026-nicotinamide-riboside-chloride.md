# FDA GRAS Notice 000635 For Nicotinamide Riboside Chloride

- FDA agency response letter: GRN 000635
- Original FDA response date: 2016-08-03
- Checked: 2026-05-03

## Summary

FDA response letter for ChromaDex's GRAS notice on nicotinamide riboside chloride (NR). ChromaDex notified FDA that NR is a source of vitamin B3 intended for use in vitamin waters, protein shakes, nutrition bars, gum, chews, and powdered beverages, excluding products under USDA jurisdiction and foods for infants/toddlers.

Identity and QA details: NR is described as a water-soluble off-white powder, precursor of NAD+, source of vitamin B3, and ChromaDex product containing at least 95% NR and water. The manufacturing description uses chemical synthesis to produce nicotinamide riboside chloride, with food-grade raw materials and cGMP. Specifications include 95-102% purity, residual solvent limits, microbial controls, and heavy-metal limits.

Safety/regulatory details: the notice summarized animal toxicology, genotoxicity assays, stability, and estimated intake. FDA stated it had no questions at that time about ChromaDex's GRAS conclusion under the intended conditions of use, while also saying FDA had not made its own GRAS determination and that ChromaDex remains responsible for compliance. FDA also did not evaluate whether health-benefit labeling claims would be supported.

Use in the wiki: product-identity and food-ingredient safety context for [[nicotinamide-riboside]]. This supports a cleaner ingredient identity than vague NAD+ booster labels, but it does not establish cognition efficacy.
