# Ketone Ester Supplementation Improves Some Aspects of Cognitive Function during a Simulated Soccer Match after Induced Mental Fatigue

- PMID: 36297060
- Study type: double-blind balanced crossover trial
- Population: 9 recreationally active men
- Intervention: ketone monoester 25 g vs non-caloric placebo
- Challenge: 40-minute mental fatiguing task followed by 45-minute simulated soccer match
- Signal: attenuated decrement in choice reaction task performance; no other clear cognitive differences

## Summary

This is the clearest state-dependent high-load signal: ketone ester did not broadly improve cognition, but it slightly reduced performance drop during repeated intermittent exercise after mental fatigue.
