# Effects of sodium benzoate on cognitive function in neuropsychiatric disorders: a systematic review and meta-analysis

- PMID: 39315325
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39315325/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

This 2024 meta-analysis found a small-to-moderate positive effect of sodium benzoate on global cognition (SMD 0.40, 95% CI 0.20 to 0.60). Effects were more pronounced in women, at doses above 500 mg/day, and in early-phase AD, chronic schizophrenia, and MDD, which makes the branch look phenotype-sensitive rather than universal.
