# Rasagiline effects on glucose metabolism, cognition, and tau in Alzheimer's dementia

- PMID: 33614888
- PMCID: PMC7882538
- DOI: 10.1002/trc2.12106
- Journal: Alzheimer's & Dementia (New York) (2021)
- Trial registration: NCT02359552

## Captured takeaways

- Phase II proof-of-concept randomized trial tested rasagiline 1 mg daily versus placebo for 24 weeks in mild to moderate Alzheimer's disease.
- Fifty participants were randomized 1:1 and 43 completed treatment.
- The primary endpoint was regional brain metabolism on FDG-PET, not a primary cognitive score.
- The trial met the primary endpoint with favorable FDG-PET differences in middle frontal, anterior cingulate, and striatal regions.
- Quality of life improved with p < 0.04.
- Digit Span, verbal fluency, and Neuropsychiatric Inventory directionally favored rasagiline but were not statistically significant.
- No effects were observed on ADAS-Cog or activities of daily living.
- Rasagiline was generally well tolerated in this small trial.
- Interpretation for the wiki: this is a biomarker-positive AD repurposing signal with no robust cognition or function efficacy, useful for hypothesis tracking but not for healthy-adult ranking.
