# Hydrophone Measurements for Biomedical Ultrasound Applications: A Review

- PMID: 36215339
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36215339/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

This review covers the basic principles of hydrophone measurements for biomedical ultrasound, including mechanisms of action for different hydrophone designs, sensitivity and directivity calibration procedures, practical measurement considerations, signal processing to correct frequency-dependent sensitivity and spatial averaging, measurement uncertainty, special considerations for high-intensity therapeutic ultrasound, and guidance on choosing an appropriate hydrophone. It explicitly recommends what should be included in hydrophone measurement reporting.
