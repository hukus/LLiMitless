# NCT04777409: Semaglutide in Early Alzheimer's Disease (EVOKE Plus)

- ClinicalTrials.gov ID: NCT04777409
- Sponsor: Novo Nordisk A/S
- Status: Completed
- Last update posted: 2026-03-27
- Results posted: No

## Summary

Phase 3 placebo-controlled study of oral semaglutide in early symptomatic AD with small vessel pathology enrichment. The registry lists CDR-SB change at week 104 as the primary endpoint and shows no posted results.

## Notes

Like EVOKE, the completed registry record remains results-free while the published report is negative on the primary clinical endpoint.
