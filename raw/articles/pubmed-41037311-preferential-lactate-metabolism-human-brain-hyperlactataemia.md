# Preferential lactate metabolism in the human brain during exogenous and endogenous hyperlactataemia

- PMID: 41037311
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41037311/
- Ingested: 2026-04-30

## Summary

Human crossover physiology study in 13 healthy adults comparing intravenous sodium lactate infusion with cycling exercise at matched arterial lactate levels. Both routes increased cerebral lactate oxidation and reduced cerebral glucose use; at an arterial lactate concentration of about 8 mmol/L, circulating lactate accounted for roughly 24% of total cerebral oxidative metabolism. The practical read-through is that the brain appears to prefer lactate as a fuel when it is available, regardless of whether the lactate came from infusion or endogenous exercise.
