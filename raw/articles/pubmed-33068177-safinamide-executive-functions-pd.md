# Safinamide improves executive functions in fluctuating Parkinson disease: exploratory study

- PMID: 33068177
- DOI: 10.1007/s00702-020-02259-y
- Journal: Journal of Neural Transmission (2021)
- Design: exploratory study.

## Captured takeaways

- Thirty-two fluctuating Parkinson disease patients were tested at the end of levodopa dose before and after adding safinamide 100 mg/day for 12 weeks.
- Measures included UPDRS-III, Frontal Assessment Battery, and Stroop Word Color Test.
- Safinamide was associated with significant increases in total FAB score, Stroop interference-time score, and UPDRS-III score.
- FAB subdomains that improved included motor programming, mental flexibility, and inhibitory control.
- The authors suggested attention and inhibition of cognitive interference may improve in fluctuating PD, but explicitly framed the result as exploratory and needing larger controlled confirmation.
- Interpretation for the wiki: this is a small PD wearing-off/executive-control signal, not healthy-adult cognition proof.
