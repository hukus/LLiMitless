# AI-driven cognitive telerehabilitation for stroke: a randomized controlled trial

- PMID: 40895105
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40895105/
- Ingested: 2026-04-29

## Summary

Randomized non-inferiority trial in 63 subacute stroke patients with cognitive impairment comparing self-guided AI-driven cognitive telerehabilitation against therapist-supervised rehabilitation. Both groups completed 24 sessions in 6 weeks on the same mobile platform.

The AI-guided arm improved across the primary cognitive measures and was non-inferior to therapist supervision on K-MMSE2 change. The abstract also reports no serious adverse events and high usability.
