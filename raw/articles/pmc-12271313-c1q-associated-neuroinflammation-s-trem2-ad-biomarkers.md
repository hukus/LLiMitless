# Complement C1q is associated with neuroinflammation and mediates the association between amyloid-β and tau pathology in Alzheimer's disease

- PMID: 40675947
- PMCID: PMC12271313
- PubMed Central: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12271313/
- Ingested: 2026-04-29

## Summary

2025 biomarker and mediation study in ADNI and CABLE participants. CSF C1q associated with sTREM2, GFAP, Aβ42, p-tau, and t-tau; longitudinally it also tracked T-tau and ADAS-Cog 13. Mediation analysis suggested that Aβ partly mediates the C1q -> sTREM2 -> tau pathway.

## Notes

- Strongest human bridge in this branch between complement activation and cognitive trajectory.
- Supports a pathway claim, not a therapy claim.
