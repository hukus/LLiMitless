# Early postnatal P021 treatment in 3xTg-AD mice

- PMID: 34057082
- DOI: 10.3233/JAD-201599
- Journal: Journal of Alzheimer's Disease (2021)

## Summary

Female 3xTg-AD and background-matched wild-type mice received P021 or vehicle from birth through postnatal day 120. The P021 group showed better novel-object recognition and more time in the target quadrant of the Morris water maze, plus higher pCREB and BDNF and improved synaptic-protein measures.

Not every behavioral endpoint moved: treatment did not significantly change water-maze escape latency or target crossings. That makes the result more mixed than a simple claim that P021 normalized cognition.

## Evidence boundary

Starting exposure at birth is highly remote from routine treatment of adult cognitive decline. The study remains an animal developmental-prevention experiment.
