# Benefits of Nicotinamide Riboside Upon Cognition and Sleep

- ClinicalTrials.gov: NCT05500170
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

ClinicalTrials.gov registry for "The Benefits of Nicotinamide Riboside Upon Cognition and Sleep in Older Veterans." Current public record, checked 2026-05-03, lists the study as recruiting / not-yet-recruiting by site, with no posted results. The last update was posted 2025-08-22, and estimated primary completion and study completion are both 2027-08-30.

Design: randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled, parallel assignment in 50 adults ages 65-85 with poor sleep quality. Inclusion requires PSQI global score >5; dementia is excluded by VA-SLUMS <20.

Intervention: 500 mg nicotinamide riboside twice daily versus placebo for 12 weeks.

Endpoint hierarchy: primary outcome is sleep quality by Pittsburgh Sleep Quality Index. Secondary outcome is cognitive function by the Veterans Affairs St. Louis University Mental Status questionnaire. Other outcomes include serum BDNF, NfL, Aβ42/Aβ40 ratio, and p-tau as sleep/cognition biomarkers.

Use in the wiki: active NR sleep/cognition watch item for [[nicotinamide-riboside]], but not evidence of benefit. Because sleep quality is the primary endpoint and cognition is secondary, even a positive result will need endpoint discipline before upgrading the cognition signal.
