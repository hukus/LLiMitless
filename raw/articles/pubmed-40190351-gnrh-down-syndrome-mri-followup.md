# GnRH Down syndrome MRI and cognition follow-up
- PMID: 40190351.
- Pilot longitudinal MRI study of 6-month pulsatile GnRH therapy in adults with Down syndrome, with healthy controls for comparison.
- Sample: cross-sectional DS/control n=11/27; longitudinal DS/control n=8/13; white matter analyses n=7/12.
- Cognitive readout: MoCA improved from 11.4 +/- 5.5 at baseline to 15.1 +/- 5.6 after 6 months (paired p=0.006).
- Imaging readout: enduring differential patterns of neuronal loss and myelination were reported across the treatment interval.
- Branch readout: strengthens the disease-specific GnRH cognition signal, but the sample is still very small.
