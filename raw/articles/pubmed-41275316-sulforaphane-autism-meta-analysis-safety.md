# Sulforaphane autism spectrum disorder meta-analysis and safety

- Source type: PubMed-indexed meta-analysis plus network pharmacology / computational biology paper.
- Publication: BMC Pharmacology and Toxicology, 2025.
- PMID: 41275316.
- PMCID: PMC12751817.
- DOI: 10.1186/s40360-025-01052-5.
- Date checked: 2026-05-03.

This paper evaluated sulforaphane as an adjunctive dietary supplement for autism spectrum disorder. It included six randomized trials with 333 participants and analyzed Social Responsiveness Scale and Aberrant Behavior Checklist outcomes, adverse events, and mechanistic target hypotheses.

The pooled ASD result favored sulforaphane on Social Responsiveness Scale scores at 4-5 weeks and 8-10 weeks, with no significant difference in adverse-event incidence. The mechanism analysis proposed oxidative-stress, inflammation, autophagy, PI3K-Akt, MAPK, TNF, Th17, mTOR, and IL-17 pathway links.

Use in the wiki: ASD/social-function support and safety context, not direct cognitive enhancement proof. It explains community/autism salience but should not upgrade general cognition claims.
