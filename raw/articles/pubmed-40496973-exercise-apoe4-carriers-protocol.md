Title: Protocol of a randomized, single-blind, controlled trial of an 18-month, home-based, high-intensity, exercise intervention in older, cognitively unimpaired APOE ε4 carriers

This protocol defines CYCLE-AD, a randomized exercise trial restricted to older cognitively unimpaired APOE ε4 carriers.

It matters here because it is a concrete example of prospective enrichment design in the cognition-prevention space. The design assumes the biomarker matters enough to justify restricting enrollment before the outcome readout exists.

Because it is still a protocol, it should be treated as design evidence, not efficacy evidence.
