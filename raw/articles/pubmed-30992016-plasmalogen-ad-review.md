# Plasmalogens and Alzheimer's disease: a review

- PMID: 30992016
- Source: PubMed review
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

Review linking ethanolamine plasmalogen deficiency with Alzheimer's disease. The paper argues that lower plasmalogen levels are commonly reported in AD and may correlate with cognitive impairment and disease severity. It also outlines speculative mechanisms, including reduced gamma-secretase activity, membrane effects, GPCR-linked AKT/ERK signaling, and anti-apoptotic pathways.

## Notes

- Useful as mechanistic background and biomarker framing.
- The review is explicitly cautious: clinical intervention is still in its infancy.
- It does not resolve whether plasmalogen loss is causal, compensatory, or a downstream marker.
