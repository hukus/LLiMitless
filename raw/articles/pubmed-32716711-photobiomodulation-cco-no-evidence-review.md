# PubMed 32716711 - Photobiomodulation, CCO, and Nitric Oxide Mechanism Critique

## Citation

- Quirk BJ, Whelan HT. "What Lies at the Heart of Photobiomodulation: Light, Cytochrome C Oxidase, and Nitric Oxide-Review of the Evidence." Photobiomodulation, Photomedicine, and Laser Surgery. 2020;38(9):527-530. DOI: 10.1089/photob.2020.4905. PMCID: PMC7495914.
- Source type: peer-reviewed review / mechanism critique.

## Extracted Claims

- The review says PBM mechanisms remain elusive.
- It identifies cytochrome c oxidase and cellular energetics as attractive hypotheses, but says reliable demonstration of PBM-related light-induced effects on CCO had not been shown.
- The authors argue that several mechanistic studies are nonreproducible, questionably relevant, or use wavelengths unlikely to be operative in vivo.

## Cognition-Relevant Extraction

- This is a counterweight to simple MB + red/NIR synergy claims built only on a shared CCO narrative.
- It supports downgrading any MB + tPBM mechanism claim unless direct target engagement and behavioral outcomes are measured.
- It does not negate tPBM clinical findings; it cautions against overclaiming a specific CCO mechanism.
