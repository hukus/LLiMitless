# Effects of Acacia catechu and Scutellaria baicalensis extract on cognitive function in a healthy adult population: a double-blind, randomized, placebo-controlled trial

- PMID: 39701593
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39701593/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Healthy-adult randomized trial of standardized UP326 extract containing Scutellaria baicalensis and Acacia catechu. The study found strong time effects across cognition but no clear between-group difference, so it reads as a weak or null controlled signal despite good tolerability.
