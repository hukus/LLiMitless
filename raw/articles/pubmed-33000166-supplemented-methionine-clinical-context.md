# Metabolic consequences of supplemented methionine in a clinical context

This 2020 clinical-context review frames methionine as a sulfur-containing amino acid with roles in protein synthesis, transmethylation, remethylation, transsulfuration, cysteine and glutathione metabolism, and homocysteine biology.

The review is useful because it treats methionine supplementation as a metabolic intervention rather than a generic amino-acid nootropic. It highlights the tension between potential clinical uses of supplemental methionine and the need to account for homocysteine, cardiovascular-risk biology, B-vitamin context, and dose/tolerance boundaries.

Wiki use: use as a class-level source for [[l-methionine]], not as direct cognition efficacy evidence. It supports the route "SAMe precursor / one-carbon-pathway perturbation / homocysteine-risk gate" rather than "healthy-adult cognition enhancer."
