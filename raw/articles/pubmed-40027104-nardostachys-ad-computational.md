# Integration of phytochemical profiling and computational approaches to evaluate the neuroprotective potential of Nardostachys jatamansi in Alzheimer's disease

- PMID: 40027104
- Published: 2025-02-08
- DOI: 10.1016/j.btre.2025.e00881
- Evidence layer: phytochemical profiling / computational AD-target analysis

## Summary

This 2025 paper combined phytochemical profiling and computational approaches to evaluate Nardostachys jatamansi neuroprotective potential in Alzheimer's disease target biology. It is another mechanism-source rather than a clinical cognition trial.

The wiki should treat this as target-generation evidence. It supports tracking Nardostachys/Jatamansi in a long-tail Ayurvedic cognition watchlist, but it does not support a practical nootropic claim.
