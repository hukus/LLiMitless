# Longevity factor klotho enhances cognition in aged nonhuman primates

- PMID: 37400721
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37400721/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

2023 Nature Aging study in aged nonhuman primates. Single low-dose klotho improved memory, while high-dose did not, after prior mouse work showing enhanced plasticity and cognition. This is the strongest intervention-like signal, but it remains preclinical and dose-sensitive.
