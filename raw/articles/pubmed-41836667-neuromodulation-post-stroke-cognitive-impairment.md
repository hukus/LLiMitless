# Neuromodulation and rehabilitation of post-stroke cognitive impairment: challenges and prospects.

## Summary

Review of post-stroke cognitive impairment highlighting neuromodulation strategies. It notes that transcranial photobiomodulation has shown preliminary cognitive-performance benefits, while transcranial ultrasound stimulation still lacks strong scientific evidence.
