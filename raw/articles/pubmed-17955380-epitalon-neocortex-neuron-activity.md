# Effects of intranasal administration of epitalon on neuron activity in the rat neocortex

- PMID: 17955380
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17955380/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

Intranasal epitalon increased spontaneous neuron firing in the rat neocortex within minutes, with a multiphasic response in some recordings. The result shows direct CNS activity after intranasal delivery, but it is still an animal electrophysiology study rather than a human cognition trial.
