# PubMed 38158878 - PNN Remodeling in Learning and Memory

## Citation

- Sanchez B, Kraszewski P, Lee S, Cope EC. "From molecules to behavior: Implications for perineuronal net remodeling in learning and memory." Journal of Neurochemistry. 2024 Sep; epub 2023 Dec 30. DOI: 10.1111/jnc.16036. PMCID: PMC12303236.
- Source type: peer-reviewed review.
- Conflict statement: the authors reported no conflict.

## Extracted Claims

- Perineuronal nets are condensed extracellular-matrix structures throughout the CNS, commonly organized around cell bodies and proximal dendrites.
- PNN formation and maturation are linked to closure of critical periods.
- In adult brain, PNNs promote synaptic stabilization and restrict formation of new synaptic connections.
- The review connects PNN remodeling to higher cognitive processes, learning, memory, experience, and memory disorders.
- The review emphasizes that PNN effects depend on structural components, architecture, remodeling enzymes, and disease or experience context.

## Cognition-Relevant Extraction

- The source supports a plasticity-brake model: PNNs can restrain new plasticity while preserving mature-circuit stability.
- The source does not support a simple "remove PNNs to improve cognition" rule.
- Human enhancement evidence: none in this source.
- The reference network points to key animal-memory studies, including Romberg et al. 2013, Anderson et al. 2020, Yang et al. 2021, and Yang et al. 2017.
