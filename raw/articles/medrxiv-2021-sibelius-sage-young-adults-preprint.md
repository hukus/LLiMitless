# Assessment of the effects of a sage extract on cognitive performance in adolescents and young adults

- Source type: medRxiv preprint / acute double-blind placebo-controlled study.
- Posted: 2021-05-30.
- Date checked: 2026-05-03.

This preprint tested Sibelius Sage, a standardized aqueous-ethanolic Salvia officinalis extract, in adolescents aged 12-14 and young adults aged 18-25. Young adults received 150 mg, 300 mg, or placebo; adolescents received 150 mg or placebo.

The preprint reports a significant treatment effect on Immediate Word Recall Accuracy in young adults at 1 hour post-dose, with limited evidence for physiological effects on cortisol, oxytocin, blood pressure, or heart rate. It reports no adverse events or side effects linked to study-product intake.

Important caveat: the study was sponsored by Sibelius Limited, and the sponsor was involved in study-design discussion and writing. Treat this as supportive but lower-weight than peer-reviewed RCTs.

Use in the wiki: product-specific preprint support for young-adult episodic-memory claims; do not let it upgrade confidence beyond low.
