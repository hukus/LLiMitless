# Milk Phospholipids Stress-Cognition RCT

- Article: Effects of milk-based phospholipids on cognitive performance and subjective responses to psychosocial stress: A randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial in high-perfectionist men.
- Journal: Nutrition, 2019;57:183-193.
- DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.nut.2018.05.002
- PMID: 30172995.
- Date checked: 2026-05-03.

## Summary

Randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial in 54 high-perfectionist men testing 2.7 g/day bovine milk-derived phospholipids for 6 weeks versus placebo. The study examined working memory, executive control, and physiological/subjective responses to an acute psychosocial stressor.

The milk-phospholipid arm improved post-stress reaction-time performance on an attention-switching task. It did not significantly attenuate the salivary cortisol stress response and did not show a working-memory benefit. Energetic arousal increased during stress, anticipatory subjective stress trended down, and systolic and diastolic blood-pressure responses were significantly augmented in the phospholipid condition.

Interpretation: this is a stress-state / executive-control reaction-time signal, not evidence for broad memory enhancement. The blood-pressure response makes it relevant to safety and state-challenge routing.
