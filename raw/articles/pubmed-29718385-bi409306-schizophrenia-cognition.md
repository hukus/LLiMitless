# Evaluation of the Efficacy, Safety, and Tolerability of BI 409306, a Novel Phosphodiesterase 9 Inhibitor, in Cognitive Impairment in Schizophrenia: A Randomized, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled, Phase II Trial

- PMID: 29718385
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29718385/
- Ingested: 2026-04-29

## Summary

2019 learn-and-confirm phase II schizophrenia cognition trial. BI 409306 failed to show a significant cognitive benefit on the MCCB composite score or the SCoRS total score after the stage-1 CANTAB screen did not identify a convincing endpoint. Adverse events rose with dose.

## Key points

- Randomization: 518 patients, placebo or BI 409306 10, 25, 50, or 100 mg once daily for 12 weeks.
- Primary endpoint: MCCB composite score after stage-1 endpoint selection.
- Result: no significant difference versus placebo on MCCB or SCoRS.
- Safety: treatment-emergent adverse events increased from 33.3 percent at 10 mg to 53.5 percent at 100 mg, vs 36.4 percent on placebo.
- Relevance: negative cognition result, but useful for the PDE9 translator map.
