# Beetroot Juice Supplementation as a Healthy Aging Strategy Through Improving Physical Performance and Cognitive Functions: A Systematic Review

- PMID: 41470898
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41470898/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary
This 2025 systematic review finds stronger and more consistent ergogenic evidence for beetroot-derived nitrates than for cognition. The cognitive literature is still limited and inconsistent, so beetroot remains a plausible physiology-supportive option rather than a proven nootropic.
