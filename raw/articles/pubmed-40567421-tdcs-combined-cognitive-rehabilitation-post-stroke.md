# Effect of transcranial direct current stimulation combined with cognitive rehabilitation on cognitive function and activities of daily living in patients with post-stroke cognitive impairment: a systematic review and meta-analysis

- PMID: 40567421
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40567421/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

2025 meta-analysis finding that tDCS plus cognitive rehabilitation improved cognition and ADL in post-stroke cognitive impairment.

## Notes

This source is useful as a counterexample to the older-impaired meta-analysis that favored tDCS alone over tDCS plus training.
