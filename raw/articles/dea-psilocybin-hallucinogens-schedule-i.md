# DEA hallucinogens fact sheet and psilocybin Schedule I status

- Source: https://www.dea.gov/factsheets/psilocybin
- Ingested: 2026-04-29

## Summary

DEA lists psilocybin as a Schedule I hallucinogen, which keeps it in the high-abuse-potential / no-accepted-medical-use category under federal controlled-substance law. The DEA hallucinogen materials also note acute physiological effects such as elevated heart rate, elevated blood pressure, nausea, vomiting, and possible long-lasting fear, paranoia, or psychosis-like reactions.
