# Sailuotong MCI phase 2 pilot randomized trial

2023 Alzheimer's & Dementia: Translational Research & Clinical Interventions randomized double-blind placebo-controlled pilot phase 2 trial of Sailuotong for cognition in older adults with mild cognitive impairment.

The study randomized 78 community-dwelling people with MCI aged at least 60 years to Sailuotong 180 mg/day or placebo for 12 weeks; 65 were included in the final analysis. The trial reported between-group advantages for Logical Memory delayed recall, Delis-Kaplan Trail Making switching and contrast scaled scores, Rey Auditory Verbal Learning Test delayed recall, and Functional Activities Questionnaire.

Interpretation for the wiki: the MCI trial strengthens Sailuotong as a formula-level cognition candidate, especially for delayed memory retrieval and executive function. It does not prove broad healthy-adult enhancement, and it should not be collapsed into generic ginkgo, ginseng, or saffron exposure.
