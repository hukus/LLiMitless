## Summary

2025 narrative overview of nootropics and smart drugs that separates treatment of disorder-related cognitive impairment from healthy-user pursuit of improved mental performance. It is useful as a safety, misuse, and taxonomy anchor across popular nootropics, prescription stimulants, and newer smart-drug markets, but it is not a quantitative efficacy review.

## Notes

- Use this as a broad framing source rather than as a signal-strength source.
- It supports the wiki rule that noticeable subjective effects, nonmedical use, and community popularity should not be treated as controlled evidence of cognitive benefit.
