# PubMed 34959823 acute ASI cognition healthy-adult note

2021 Nutrients randomized double-blind crossover study titled "Acute Inositol-Stabilized Arginine Silicate Improves Cognitive Outcomes in Healthy Adults."

Nineteen young adults, mean age about 20.9 years, consumed ASI (1.5 g ASI plus 12 g dextrose) and placebo (12 g dextrose) in crossover fashion. Participants completed the RBANS and two digital cognitive assessments before supplementation and 60 minutes afterward.

Reported results: ASI significantly improved total RBANS and immediate-memory scores compared with placebo. Other cognitive domains did not significantly differ between trials.

Interpretation for the wiki: this strengthens ASI as a low-confidence healthy-adult cognition branch because it is a controlled acute crossover study with a direct cognitive endpoint. It still should not be generalized to myo-inositol alone. Dextrose control, small n, acute design, product identity, immediate-memory selectivity, and sponsor/product context keep the branch below cleaner nootropic comparators.
