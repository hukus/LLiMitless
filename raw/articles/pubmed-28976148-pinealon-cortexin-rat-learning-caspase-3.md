# [Effect of peptide geroprotectors on the navigation system learning and caspase-3 in brain structures in rats of different age]

- PMID: 28976148
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28976148/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

This rat study tested Cortexin and Pinealon across young and old animals, looking at navigation learning and caspase-3 in brain structures. The abstract links peptide exposure to regional caspase-3 changes and suggests those changes may help explain learning differences. It is preclinical support for the broader neuroprotective cluster, not human efficacy evidence.
