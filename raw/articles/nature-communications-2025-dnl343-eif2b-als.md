# Investigational eIF2B activator DNL343 modulates the integrated stress response in preclinical models of TDP-43 pathology and individuals with ALS in a randomized clinical trial

- DOI: 10.1038/s41467-025-63031-y
- Published: 2025-08-18
- Journal: Nature Communications
- Source type: preclinical plus phase 1 / phase 1b randomized clinical safety and target-engagement report
- URL: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-025-63031-y
- Ingested: 2026-05-03

## Summary

2025 Nature Communications paper on DNL343, a CNS-penetrant small-molecule eIF2B activator. The paper reports ISR inhibition in cellular ALS models, brain ISR reduction in an inducible TDP-43 mouse model, and phase 1 / phase 1b randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trials in healthy participants and ALS participants.

The clinical trials were designed primarily for safety and tolerability. DNL343 showed a half-life compatible with once-daily dosing, extensive CSF distribution, general tolerability, and reduced ISR biomarkers in peripheral blood mononuclear cells and CSF of ALS participants.

## Notes

- This is the key human target-engagement anchor for ISR inhibition, but it is not a cognition-efficacy trial.
- ALS and TDP-43 pathology make the translational context disease-modifying / neurodegenerative rather than healthy enhancement.
- Keep DNL343 below any cognition recommendation threshold until human cognition or functional endpoints are positive.
