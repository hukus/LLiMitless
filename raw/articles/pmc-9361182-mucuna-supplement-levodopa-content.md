# Levodopa content in Mucuna pruriens supplements

Analytical study of Mucuna pruriens dietary supplements showing that consumer products can contain pharmacologically meaningful levodopa amounts and that label transparency is a central interpretation problem. This source is important because nootropic users often treat Mucuna like an ordinary herb despite its L-DOPA payload.

Use in the wiki: product-quality and regulatory-risk anchor. It supports keeping extract percentage, actual L-DOPA amount, label claims, and product testing separate from the plant name.
