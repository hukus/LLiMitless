# The effects of the phosphodiesterase type 5 inhibitor vardenafil on cognitive performance in healthy adults: a behavioral-electroencephalography study

- PMID: 23427190
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23427190/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

2013 healthy-volunteer study of vardenafil. The paper did not find a convincing cognitive benefit, and the only clear behavioral effect was worse performance on a reaction-time task at 20 mg, with some incidental ERP changes and more headache/weakness.

## Key points

- Design: healthy young adults, placebo and 10/20 mg oral vardenafil.
- Outcome: memory, executive function, reaction time, and EEG.
- Signal: no prominent cognition enhancement.
- Safety: headache and weakness were more common than with placebo.
