# Broad repetitive transcranial magnetic stimulation (rTMS) of the precuneus in Alzheimer's disease: A rationale and study design

- PMID: 40065917
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40065917/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Protocol paper for a two-stage precuneus rTMS trial in 54 participants with mild to moderate Alzheimer's clinical syndrome. The design emphasizes tolerability and feasibility, uses a larger coil, and consolidates the induction phase into an off-the-shelf protocol with short-term memory and biomarker endpoints.
