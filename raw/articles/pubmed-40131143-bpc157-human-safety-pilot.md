# Safety of Intravenous Infusion of BPC157 in Humans: A Pilot Study

- PMID: 40131143
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40131143/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Small 2025 pilot of intravenous BPC-157 in two healthy adults. The report found no measurable biomarker changes in the tested panels and no side effects, but the sample is far too small to establish general safety.

## Notes

Important as a human safety anchor, not as efficacy evidence.
