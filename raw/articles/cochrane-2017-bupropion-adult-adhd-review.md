# Cochrane 2017 bupropion for adult ADHD

## Captured takeaways

- Cochrane review of bupropion versus placebo for attention deficit hyperactivity disorder in adults.
- Searches covered CENTRAL, MEDLINE, Embase, and other databases through February 2017.
- The review found possible benefit of bupropion for adult ADHD symptoms versus placebo, but the evidence was limited and more research was needed.
- PubMed-indexed meta-analysis context also reports greater ADHD-RS improvement and response with bupropion than placebo, but this remains a clinical ADHD evidence layer rather than healthy-adult enhancement proof.
