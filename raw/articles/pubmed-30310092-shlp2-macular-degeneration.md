PubMed abstract for the 2018 Scientific Reports paper on SHLP2 in macular degeneration. The paper characterizes protective effects of SHLP2 in macular-degeneration models and helps anchor SHLP2 as a mitochondria-derived peptide with tissue-protective biology. It does not provide a cognition endpoint.
