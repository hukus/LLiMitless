# Elamipretide: The first cardiolipin-directed mitochondrial therapeutic for Barth syndrome approved under accelerated approval

- PMID: 41260682

## Summary

2026 review summarizing the FDA approval and the Barth syndrome development program. It notes that the randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled crossover trial did not show significant improvement in 6-minute walk test or fatigue scores, but a 168-week open-label extension reported sustained benefits. This is a translational note, not cognition evidence.
