# MOTS-c for Improving Insulin Sensitivity in Adults With Prediabetes and Overweight/Obesity

- ClinicalTrials.gov ID: NCT07505745
- Registry: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07505745
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Phase 2a randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial of subcutaneous MOTS-c in adults with prediabetes and overweight/obesity. Primary endpoints are insulin sensitivity and safety; the record includes adverse-event, vital-sign, ECG, and lab monitoring, but no cognition endpoint.

## Notes

This is the clearest current human translational signal for MOTS-c, but it is metabolic rather than cognitive.
