# GnRH therapy on cognition in Down syndrome
- ClinicalTrials.gov ID: NCT04390646 (Pulse-UP); recruiting with last update posted 2024-02-12.
- Randomized, double-blind phase 2/3 study of pulsatile GnRH pump vs saline in people with Down syndrome.
- Primary endpoint: MoCA total score; secondary endpoints include DCCS, PAL, Token test, Corsi block tapping, and non-word repetition.
- Registry publications link to the 2022 Science paper showing that GnRH replacement rescued cognition and brain connectivity in a small adult DS sample.
- Branch readout: this is the strongest current human cognition signal on the GnRH axis, but it is disease-specific and still small.
