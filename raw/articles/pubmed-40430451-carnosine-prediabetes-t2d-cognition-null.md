# Effects of Carnosine Supplementation on Cognitive Outcomes in Prediabetes and Well-Controlled Type 2 Diabetes

- PMID: 40430451
- PMCID: PMC12114902
- DOI: 10.3390/ph18050630
- Published: 2025-04-26
- Ingested: 2026-05-11

## Summary

Secondary cognitive analysis of a double-blind randomized placebo-controlled trial. Forty-nine adults with prediabetes or early-stage well-controlled type 2 diabetes were randomized to 2 g/day carnosine or placebo for 14 weeks, and 42 completed the trial.

Cognitive outcomes included Digit Symbol Substitution, Stroop, Trail Making Tests A and B, and CANTAB tasks. No between-group differences were found in change or follow-up values for any cognitive measure, and adjustments for baseline cognition, diabetic status, education, age, or sex interactions did not change the result.

## Relevance

Important negative counterweight for [[anserine-carnosine]]. It argues against assuming purified carnosine improves cognition in relatively young and well-controlled metabolic-risk adults.

## Caveats

- Cognition was a secondary outcome.
- Cohort had prediabetes or well-controlled T2D, not healthy adults or MCI.
- The authors suggest higher-risk or impaired cohorts may be more informative.
