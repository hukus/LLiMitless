## Summary

The current U.S. label keeps selegiline in prescription-drug territory rather than the supplement or research-chemical shelf. The label frames it as an MAO inhibitor used in Parkinson disease, warns that higher doses lose MAO-B selectivity, and highlights interaction and tyramine-related risks. That makes selegiline a state-restoration comparator with real safety constraints, not a casual nootropic.

## Notes

- Source type: current human prescription label.
- Regulatory status: U.S. prescription drug, not an OTC enhancer.
- Safety flags: MAOI interaction risk, tyramine risk at higher doses, and washout requirements.
- Interpretation: useful comparator for dopamine-state restoration, not a general cognition enhancer.
