# DHEA DAWN trial
- PMID: 18482290
- Double-blind randomized trial in 225 healthy older adults (110 men, 115 women), oral DHEA 50 mg/day vs placebo for 1 year.
- Outcomes included six cognitive tests plus quality-of-life and mood/sexual-function measures.
- Key result: no difference in cognitive function over time; no durable benefit on wellbeing or sexual-function measures either.
- Branch readout: DHEA did not help cognition in the general healthy older adult population.
