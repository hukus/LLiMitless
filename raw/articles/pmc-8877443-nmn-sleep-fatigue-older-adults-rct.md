# Effect of 12-Week NMN Intake on Sleep Quality, Fatigue, and Physical Performance in Older Japanese Adults

- PMCID: PMC8877443
- DOI: 10.3390/nu14040755
- Published: 2022-02-11
- Checked: 2026-05-03

## Summary

2022 Nutrients randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled study of NMN timing in 108 older Japanese adults. Participants were assigned to NMN in the morning, NMN in the afternoon, placebo in the morning, or placebo in the afternoon. NMN dose was 250 mg once daily for 12 weeks.

Outcomes were sleep quality by Pittsburgh Sleep Quality Index, fatigue/drowsiness by the Jikaku-sho shirabe questionnaire, and physical performance by grip strength, 5-times sit-to-stand, timed up and go, and 5-meter habitual walk.

The clearest finding was not cognition. Significant interactions appeared for 5-times sit-to-stand and drowsiness, and the NMN afternoon group had the largest effect sizes for 5-STS and drowsiness. The authors interpreted afternoon NMN as potentially improving lower-limb function and reducing drowsiness in older adults.

Use in the wiki: sleep/fatigue/physical-performance proxy for [[nicotinamide-mononucleotide]]. It is relevant to alertness-state claims but does not establish memory, attention, processing-speed, or executive-function benefit.
