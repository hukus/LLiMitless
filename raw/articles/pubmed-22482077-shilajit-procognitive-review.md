# Shilajit: a natural phytocomplex with potential procognitive activity

PubMed-indexed 2012 review of shilajit as a natural phytocomplex with possible procognitive activity. The abstract emphasizes fulvic acid as a main active principle and describes in vitro tau self-aggregation inhibition as a possible Alzheimer's-disease research angle.

The review calls for more basic and clinical investigation. For the wiki, this is hypothesis-level and mechanistic, not a human cognition trial.

Relevance: shilajit should be treated as a product-quality and contamination-sensitive hypothesis node with weak formal cognition support.
