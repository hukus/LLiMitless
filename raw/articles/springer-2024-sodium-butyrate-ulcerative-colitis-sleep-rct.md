# Sodium butyrate in active ulcerative colitis: clock genes, inflammation, sleep quality, and QoL

- Published: 2024
- Source type: double-blind randomized placebo-controlled trial
- Checked: 2026-05-11
- Population: 36 active ulcerative-colitis patients

## Summary

This trial randomized active ulcerative-colitis patients to sodium butyrate or placebo for 12 weeks. The intervention was reported as 600 mg/kg. Outcomes included whole-blood circadian-clock gene expression, fecal calprotectin, serum hs-CRP, Pittsburgh Sleep Quality Index, and IBD quality of life.

Compared with placebo, sodium butyrate decreased fecal calprotectin and hs-CRP, upregulated several circadian-clock genes, improved PSQI sleep-quality score, and improved IBD quality of life.

Use this as an illness-state inflammation/sleep anchor for butyrate, not as healthy-adult cognition evidence.

## Notes

- The disease state is active UC, so transportability to healthy adults or nonspecific brain fog is weak.
- Sleep and inflammatory markers can plausibly mediate subjective cognition, but no objective cognitive endpoint was captured.
