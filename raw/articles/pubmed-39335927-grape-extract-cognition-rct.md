# Standardized Grape (Vitis vinifera L.) Extract Improves Short- and Long-Term Cognitive Performances in Healthy Older Adults: A Randomized, Double-Blind, and Placebo-Controlled Trial

- PMID: 39335927
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39335927/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Healthy older adults received once-daily 250 mg standardized grape extract (Cognigrape®) for 12 weeks. The trial reported improvement in immediate and delayed memory, visuospatial ability, language, attention, and MMSE/RBANS/ENB-2 scores, with some effects reported as early as 14 days. This is one of the cleaner recent positive signals for a standardized botanical extract.
