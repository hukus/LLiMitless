# The Effect of Beta-Alanine Supplementation on Performance, Cognitive Function and Resiliency in Soldiers

- PMID: 36839397
- PMCID: PMC9961614
- DOI: 10.3390/nu15041039
- Published: 2023-02-19
- Ingested: 2026-05-11

## Summary

Narrative review of beta-alanine supplementation in soldiers and tactical athletes. The review emphasizes beta-alanine's established role in raising carnosine and supporting anaerobic performance, then reviews possible effects on soldier performance, resilience, PTSD, mild TBI, heat stress, and cognitive function.

The review's cognition conclusion is cautious: cognitive evidence is inconclusive and may depend on whether a stressor is applied during assessment. The authors call for additional human-model research.

## Relevance

Useful framing source for [[beta-alanine]] and [[histidine-dipeptide-cognition-watchlist]]. It keeps beta-alanine in the state-challenge / resiliency bucket rather than the direct-nootropic bucket.

## Caveats

- Review, not a new RCT.
- Military/tactical stressors do not map cleanly to rested healthy adults.
- Some resilience claims are preclinical or indirect.
