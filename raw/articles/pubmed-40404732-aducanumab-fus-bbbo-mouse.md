# Aducanumab delivery via focused ultrasound-induced transient blood-brain barrier opening in vivo

- PMID: 40404732
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40404732/
- PMC: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12098756/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

This 2025 mouse study used MRI-guided focused ultrasound to transiently open the BBB and increase aducanumab delivery into brain tissue. It reported approximately 7-fold higher delivery at 0.25 MPa and 60-fold higher delivery at 0.42 MPa versus sham, without substantial tissue damage, supporting FUS-BBBO as a drug-delivery platform.
