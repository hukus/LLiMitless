# FDA phosphatidylserine qualified health claim status

## Source snapshot

- FDA page: Qualified Health Claims: Letters of Enforcement Discretion.
- The page lists a 2003 phosphatidylserine and cognitive dysfunction / dementia letter and a 2004 update letter.
- FDA's qualified-health-claim framework means the claim is supported by some scientific evidence but does not meet the significant-scientific-agreement standard required for an authorized health claim.

## Claim interpretation

- FDA material and downstream FDA-linked summaries allow a qualified, heavily caveated dementia / cognitive dysfunction risk claim for qualifying phosphatidylserine labeling.
- The key interpretation is the disclaimer: the science is very limited and preliminary, not settled.
- This is regulatory-language evidence, not clinical proof that PS improves cognition in healthy adults.

## Wiki interpretation

- Use this as a status caveat for supplement marketing.
- The qualified claim should not be read as FDA approval of efficacy, a disease-treatment claim, or a general nootropic endorsement.
- It is relevant to product labels, advertising, and source-quality interpretation for phosphatidylserine pages.
