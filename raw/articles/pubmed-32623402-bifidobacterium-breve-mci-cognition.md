# Probiotic Bifidobacterium breve in Improving Cognitive Functions of Older Adults with Suspected Mild Cognitive Impairment: A Randomized, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled Trial

- PMID: 32623402
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32623402/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

2020 placebo-controlled MCI trial of Bifidobacterium breve A1 at 2x10^10 CFU/day for 16 weeks. RBANS total score improved by 11.3 points versus placebo and memory subdomains improved, with no adverse events reported; a 2026 corrigendum corrected a JMCIS table value.
