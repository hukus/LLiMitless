# Nature Communications 2025 FOXO4-DRI p53 TAD2 mechanism paper

- Source type: Peer-reviewed structural/mechanistic paper.
- Publication: Nature Communications, 2025-07-01; volume 16, article 5672.
- DOI: 10.1038/s41467-025-60844-9.
- Date checked: 2026-05-06.

This paper maps the FOXO4/FOXO4-DRI interaction with p53 using solution NMR and related biophysical approaches. It reports that FOXO4-DRI binds the intrinsically disordered p53 transactivation domain, especially p53 TAD2, and forms a transiently folded complex. The authors also report that p53 phosphorylation enhances binding affinity for both FOXO4 and FOXO4-DRI.

The paper strengthens molecular understanding of the FOXO4-p53 axis and FOXO4-DRI mode of action. It is not a human intervention study and does not validate any self-experiment protocol.

Use in the wiki: mechanism upgrade for FOXO4-DRI. It supports target-level plausibility and future inhibitor design, not clinical efficacy.
