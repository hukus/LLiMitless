# MOTS-c improves intrinsic muscle mitochondrial bioenergetic health and efficiency in a PGC-1alpha/AMPK-dependent manner

- PMID: 41520850
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41520850/
- Ingested: 2026-04-29

## Summary

2026 mechanistic study found exogenous MOTS-c improved intrinsic muscle mitochondrial bioenergetics in a PGC-1alpha/AMPK-dependent manner, adding a more direct metabolic mechanism to the peptide's exercise-linked biology.

## Notes

This deepens the metabolism bridge, but it still does not add a human cognition endpoint or a combined exercise + creatine + MOTS-c trial.
