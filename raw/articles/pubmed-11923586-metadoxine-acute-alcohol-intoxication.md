# Metadoxine in acute alcohol intoxication

- PMID: 11923586
- Journal: Alcoholism: Clinical and Experimental Research (2002)

## Captured takeaways

- Double-blind, randomized, multicenter, placebo-controlled trial in 58 patients with acute ethanol intoxication.
- Participants received a single 900 mg intravenous dose of metadoxine or placebo.
- Metadoxine significantly decreased the half-life of ethanol in blood and accelerated ethanol elimination.
- Median time to onset of recovery from intoxication was 0.95 hours with metadoxine versus 2.34 hours with placebo.
- Toxic clinical symptom ratings improved more quickly with metadoxine at the 2-hour assessment.
- The authors concluded that metadoxine could help manage acute ethanol intoxication.
- Interpretation for the wiki: relevant to intoxication / hangover-aftereffect brain-fog hypotheses, but not evidence for routine healthy-adult cognitive enhancement.
