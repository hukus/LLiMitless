# AL002 phase 2 early Alzheimer's disease study result

- Source: ClinicalTrials.gov NCT04592874
- Retrieved: 2026-04-30

## Summary

The phase 2 randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled AL002 study in early Alzheimer's disease was completed and later marked as terminated. The registry result page states that the parent study failed to meet the primary endpoint and that there were no treatment effects favoring AL002 on secondary clinical and functional endpoints.

## Notes

- This is the strongest current human evidence in the TREM2 agonist lane and it is negative.
- It argues against upgrading TREM2 agonism into a cognition-enhancement claim on the basis of AL002.
- Best used as a comparator for why target biology does not automatically translate into clinical benefit.
