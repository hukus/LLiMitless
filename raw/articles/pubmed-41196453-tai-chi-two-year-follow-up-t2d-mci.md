# Two-year follow-up assessment of a randomized controlled trial evaluating the durability of Tai Chi on cognitive function in older adults with type 2 diabetes and mild cognitive impairment

- PMID: 41196453
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41196453/
- Ingested: 2026-04-29

## Summary

Two-year follow-up of a 328-participant randomized trial in older adults with type 2 diabetes and mild cognitive impairment. The parent trial delivered 24 weeks of Tai Chi, fitness walking, or health education; cognition was reassessed at baseline, 24 weeks, 36 weeks, and 2 years. Tai Chi showed better 2-year MoCA preservation than health education, with nearly half of participants reaching a clinically meaningful MoCA improvement threshold and selective benefit on executive and visuospatial outcomes. This is a durable mind-body counterexample to the idea that only device-based interventions can show retention.
