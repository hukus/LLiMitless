# Regionally specific picture naming benefits of focal tDCS are dependent on baseline performance in older adults

- PMID: 40347355
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40347355/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

2025 preregistered, double-blind sham-controlled study in 72 older and 72 younger adults. Focal anodal tDCS to the left inferior frontal gyrus helped older adults with poorer baseline naming performance, while the effect was regionally specific and absent as a broad group-level win.

## Notes

Useful as a responder-phenotype paper for the tDCS branch. The signal is not "tDCS works" in general; it is that baseline performance and cortical target shape whether the older-adult naming effect appears.
