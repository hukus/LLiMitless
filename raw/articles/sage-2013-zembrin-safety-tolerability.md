# A Randomized, Double-Blind, Parallel-Group, Placebo-Controlled Trial of Extract Sceletium tortuosum (Zembrin) in Healthy Adults

- DOI: 10.1089/acm.2012.0185
- Published: 2013
- Source type: randomized safety and tolerability trial
- URL: https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/full/10.1089/acm.2012.0185
- Ingested: 2026-05-03

## Summary

Three-month randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled safety study of standardized extract Sceletium tortuosum, trademarked Zembrin, in 37 healthy adults. Participants received 8 mg/day, 25 mg/day, or placebo. No efficacy variables were assessed. Safety variables included vital signs, physical examination, ECG, laboratory measures, and adverse events.

Both Zembrin doses were well tolerated. There were no clinically significant differences between treatment groups in vital signs, ECG, physical examination, or laboratory safety tests. The most common adverse event was headache, and adverse events were more frequent in placebo than Zembrin arms. Some unsolicited diary comments in the Zembrin groups described improved stress coping and sleep.

## Notes

- Useful safety anchor, not an efficacy paper.
- The trial used a defined Zembrin lot and standardized alkaloid identity checks, so it supports product-specific interpretation.
- Three months is still not enough to establish long-term safety.
