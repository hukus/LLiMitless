# ADDF Cognitive Vitality Dihexa route and safety note

The Alzheimer's Drug Discovery Foundation Cognitive Vitality report says Dihexa had no published human studies as of its 2021 update, no established dose, and no clinical-trial protocol. It lists rat-route exposure as oral 2 mg/kg, intracerebroventricular up to 1 nmol, intravenous up to 10 mg/kg, and intraperitoneal up to 20 mg/kg. The report also notes rat half-life estimates of about 12 days after IV administration and 8.8 days after IP administration, with human half-life unknown.

The report summarizes the key rodent cognition signal as intraperitoneal and oral routes both being effective in the scopolamine water-maze model at the higher tested doses, and orally delivered Dihexa 2 mg/kg/day improving Morris-water-maze performance in aged rats. It also flags no long-term safety studies in animals or humans and theoretical c-Met / tumorigenesis concerns.

Use in the wiki: route discussion should say Dihexa's best formal cognition route is oral in rodents, with i.p. and i.c.v. animal evidence and IV/IP pharmacokinetic work. It should not convert those routes into a human protocol.
