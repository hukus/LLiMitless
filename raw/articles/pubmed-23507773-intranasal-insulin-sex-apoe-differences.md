# Sex and ApoE genotype differences in treatment response to two doses of intranasal insulin in adults with mild cognitive impairment or Alzheimer's disease

- PMID: 23507773
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23507773/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Responder analysis from an older MCI/AD trial showing that sex, APOE status, and dose can change the apparent cognitive response to intranasal insulin.

## Notes

Key stratifier source for the wiki: 20 IU helped both sexes on delayed story memory, while 40 IU favored men and looked especially favorable in APOE-ε4-negative men.
