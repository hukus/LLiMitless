# FDA bulk-drug-substances peptide risk list

- Content current as of: 2026-04-22
- Source: https://www.fda.gov/drugs/human-drug-compounding/certain-bulk-drug-substances-use-compounding-may-present-significant-safety-risks

## Key points

FDA's current compounding-risk page places several cognition-adjacent peptides in its risk framework:
- BPC-157: limited human safety data; potential immunogenicity and peptide-related impurity/API-characterization issues.
- MOTS-c: significant-risk listing; no identified human exposure data on drug products containing MOTS-c and insufficient information to know whether it would harm humans.
- Selank acetate: potential immunogenicity risk and limited safety information.
- Semax (heptapeptide): potential immunogenicity risk and limited safety information.
- Dihexa acetate: no identified human exposure data.
- Thymosin beta-4 fragment (LKKTETQ), also called TB-500: no identified human exposure data.

## Research-use read

The page is best read as a current U.S. regulatory/sourcing warning, not as proof of clinical toxicity. It does, however, make clear that these peptides should be treated as investigational bulk substances with unresolved quality and route-of-administration questions.
