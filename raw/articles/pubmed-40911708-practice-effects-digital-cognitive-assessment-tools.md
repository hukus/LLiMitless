# Practice effects on digital cognitive assessment tools: insights from the defense automated neurobehavioral assessment battery

- PMID: 40911708
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40911708/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

2025 digital-battery paper showing modest practice effects across two DANA sessions about 93 days apart. The main takeaway is that digital or app-based tests still need alternate forms, baseline stabilization, and explicit retest-interval tracking.

## Notes

The paper is useful for QC because it frames practice effects as a measurement issue even in digital cognitive monitoring, not just in legacy neuropsychological batteries.
