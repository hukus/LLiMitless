# The Effect of Exogenous Ketone Bodies on Cognition in Patients with Mild Cognitive Impairment, Alzheimer's Disease and in Healthy Adults: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis

- DOI: 10.1101/2025.09.17.25335999
- Version posted: 2025-09-18
- Study type: preprint systematic review and meta-analysis
- Scope: 29 studies, 18 protocols in meta-analysis, 1,347 participants

## Summary

2025 medRxiv preprint found exogenous ketone bodies were associated with a modest but statistically significant improvement in cognition versus placebo (SMD 0.26, 95% CI 0.11-0.40, p = 0.0007). Subgroup analyses did not show clear differences by study duration, ketone form, population type, or acute cognitive stressor status.
