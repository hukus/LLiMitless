# Effect of homocysteine lowering treatment on cognitive function: a systematic review and meta-analysis of randomized controlled trials

- Review publication year: 2012
- Source type: DARE quality-assessed review summary of RCT meta-analysis
- Intervention class: vitamin B12, vitamin B6, and folic acid homocysteine-lowering treatment
- Ingested: 2026-05-03

## Summary

DARE summary of a systematic review and meta-analysis of 19 randomized placebo-controlled trials. The review concluded that vitamins B12, B6, or folic acid supplementation did not improve cognitive function in older adults with or without cognitive impairment. DARE judged the conclusion broadly reflective of the evidence, while noting potential review bias and heterogeneity across small trials.

## Notes

- Important broad counterweight to the VITACOG subgroup-positive story.
- Helps keep folate/B-vitamin confidence low and contested.
- Best interpretation: homocysteine lowering is biologically coherent but does not reliably translate to cognitive improvement across unselected older adults.
