# PubMed 32414940 - ChABC mPFC Infusion and TUNL Working Memory

## Citation

- Anderson EM, Moench KM, Boyce-Rustay JM, Holmes A. "ChABC infusions into medial prefrontal cortex, but not posterior parietal cortex, improve the performance of rats tested on a novel, challenging delay in the touchscreen TUNL task." Learning & Memory. 2020. DOI: 10.1101/lm.050245.119. PMCID: PMC7233150.
- Source type: peer-reviewed animal study.

## Extracted Claims

- PNNs are described as extracellular structures containing chondroitin sulfate proteoglycans, hyaluronan, tenascin-R, and link proteins.
- Chondroitinase ABC was used to degrade PNNs in medial prefrontal cortex and posterior parietal cortex of Long-Evans rats.
- Medial-prefrontal ChABC initially improved accuracy, selection trials, and correction-trial burden during a novel 20-second-delay TUNL challenge.
- Posterior-parietal ChABC did not produce a significant improvement.
- Immunohistochemistry confirmed degradation markers and reduced WFA intensity after medial-prefrontal infusion; the authors interpreted the effect as subtle and condition-specific rather than essential to spatial working-memory function.

## Cognition-Relevant Extraction

- Medicinal cognition signal: narrow animal working-memory challenge signal, region-dependent and time-limited.
- Translation status: no human cognition trial; route is local brain infusion.
- Signal-strength read: weak-to-moderate preclinical signal because the effect is constrained to a challenging novel delay and does not generalize across brain regions.
- Safety interpretation for the wiki: this source supports circuit-specific target engagement, not broad systemic enhancement.
