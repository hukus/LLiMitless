# EDQM MSSIP007 nootropics surveillance and IDRA-21

June 2025 EDQM Official Medicines Control Laboratories summary report on market surveillance of suspected illegal nootropic products.

Key extracted points:

- The study covered January 2020 through September 2024 across 10 countries and 12 participating laboratories.
- The scope included medicines, herbal medicines, dietary supplements, raw materials, or other products labelled as nootropic or containing illicit nootropic substances, unauthorized novel foods, or purported nootropic activity.
- The report analyzed 159 samples and identified 34 unique molecules across 166 molecule-identification entries.
- IDRA-21 was identified once, with CAS 22503-72-6, presented with no information on status in the summary table.
- The conclusion emphasizes that illegal nootropics, unapproved drugs, banned substances, unauthorized novel foods, and research chemicals with limited clinical or toxicological data are circulating in Europe and Australia.
- Interpretation for the wiki: this is a regulatory/market surveillance anchor for IDRA-21 and adjacent research chemicals; it supports a sourcing-risk lane, not efficacy.
