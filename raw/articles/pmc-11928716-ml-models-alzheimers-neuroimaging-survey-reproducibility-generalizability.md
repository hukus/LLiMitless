# Machine-learning models for Alzheimer's disease diagnosis using neuroimaging data: survey, reproducibility, and generalizability evaluation

PMCID: PMC11928716
PMID: 40117001
Published: 2025

Abstract focus: the survey maps ML/DL methods for AD diagnosis from sMRI, fMRI, and PET, then explicitly evaluates reproducibility and generalizability of open-source models. The key result is that existing models lose generalizability when cohorts or modalities shift, even after controlling computational factors. The paper frames reproducibility and generalizability as core barriers to biomarker discovery and clinical translation.
