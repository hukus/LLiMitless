# Epitalon-activated telomerase enhance bovine oocyte maturation rate and post-thawed embryo development

PubMed indexed a 2025 Life Sciences paper showing that Epitalon-activated telomerase improved bovine oocyte maturation rate and post-thawed embryo development in vitro. The abstract frames the work as telomerase activation during in vitro embryo production and reports improved maturation and embryo-development outcomes in that animal-reproduction setting.

For the wiki, this is a fresh 2025 mechanistic data point that keeps Epitalon in the telomerase and oxidative-stress biology lane, but it does not add any human cognition endpoint or clinical safety signal.
