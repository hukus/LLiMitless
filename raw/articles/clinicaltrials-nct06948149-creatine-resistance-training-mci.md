# Creatine and Resistance Training in Older Adults With Mild Cognitive Impairment.

- ClinicalTrials.gov ID: NCT06948149
- ClinicalTrials.gov: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06948149
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Ongoing 26-week factorial RCT testing creatine supplementation and resistance training, alone and together, in community-dwelling older adults with MCI. Primary outcome is visuospatial working memory; secondary outcomes include executive function, mobility, strength, cerebral blood flow, and biomarkers.

## Notes

This is one of the most relevant active trials for the older clinical cohort side of the creatine question.
