# PubChem 2026 L-methionine identity note

PubChem identifies L-methionine as compound CID 6137, formula C5H11NO2S, molecular weight 149.21 g/mol, CAS 63-68-3, and IUPAC name (2S)-2-amino-4-methylsulfanylbutanoic acid.

L-methionine is the biologically relevant L-enantiomer of methionine, a sulfur-containing essential proteinogenic amino acid. It is upstream of S-adenosyl-L-methionine / SAMe, S-adenosylhomocysteine, homocysteine, cysteine, taurine, glutathione, and polyamine chemistry.

Wiki use: L-methionine deserves its own entity because it is the direct dietary/supplement precursor often blurred with [[s-adenosyl-l-methionine]], [[betaine-tmg]], [[folate-b-vitamins]], [[glycine]], [[dimethylglycine-dmg]], and generic protein or essential-amino-acid claims. Identity does not imply a direct cognition-enhancement signal.
