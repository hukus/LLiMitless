# Virtual Reality-Enhanced Rehabilitation for Cognitive Recovery in Acute Post-Stroke Patients: Pilot Study

- ClinicalTrials.gov ID: NCT07381452
- Study page: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07381452
- Ingested: 2026-04-29

## Summary

Completed single-blind pilot trial in acute post-stroke patients testing VR-enhanced rehabilitation versus conventional rehabilitation. The study targets cognitive function and activities of daily living over a two-week, ten-session program.

The record reports 36 enrolled participants, actual completion in 2025, and a rehabilitation dose that pairs VR with conventional therapy rather than replacing it. That makes it a task-ecology and recovery-context source, not a healthy enhancement study.

## Notes

The intervention includes a memory task and a meal-preparation task, which is useful for the wiki's ecological-validity language.
