# The effect of probiotics on select cognitive domains in mild cognitive impairment and Alzheimer's disease: a systematic review and meta-analysis

- PMID: 40034358
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40034358/
- Ingested: 2026-04-30

## Summary

2024 systematic review and meta-analysis of 10 randomized controlled trials in MCI and Alzheimer's disease. Pooled effects were directionally mixed across cognitive domains but all were non-significant, which keeps the probiotic branch phenotype-sensitive and still not practice-ready.

## Notes

The abstract reports MMSE SMD 0.28, MoCA SMD 0.51, language SMD -0.12, naming SMD 0.02, visuospatial SMD 0.38, memory SMD 0.20, and attention SMD -0.07, with all confidence intervals crossing zero.
