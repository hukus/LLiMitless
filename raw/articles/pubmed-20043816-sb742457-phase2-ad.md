# Double-blind, controlled phase II study of a 5-HT6 receptor antagonist, SB-742457, in Alzheimer's disease

- PMID: 20043816
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20043816/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

Phase 2 randomized study of SB-742457 in mild-to-moderate AD. The 35 mg arm showed a nominal signal on global impression but not on ADAS-cog, so the paper reads as an early hint rather than a confirmed cognition win.
