# Pharmacokinetic analysis of intermittent rapamycin administration in early-stage Alzheimer's Disease

- PMID: 41046300
- DOI: 10.1007/s11357-025-01911-3
- ClinicalTrials.gov: NCT06022068
- EudraCT: 2023-000127-36
- Ingested: 2026-04-30

## Summary

2025 open-label pilot in 13 early-stage AD participants receiving weekly oral rapamycin, mostly 7 mg/week, for 26 weeks. Blood concentrations were sampled at baseline and during week 13.

Interindividual variability was moderate, the 48-hour post-dose sample correlated best with prior trough, and measured blood levels stayed below immunosuppressive ranges. The paper is useful for dosing/monitoring logic, but it does not establish a cognition benefit.
