# Tinospora - LiverTox

- Publisher: NCBI Bookshelf / LiverTox
- Last update: 2025-06-27
- Evidence layer: official hepatotoxicity monograph

## Summary

LiverTox describes Tinospora cordifolia as an Ayurvedic herb also known as Giloy or Guduchi, traditionally used for conditions including diabetes, arthritis, liver diseases, malaria, pain, urinary tract infection, and COVID-19 prevention or amelioration. It notes that several recent reports have documented clinically apparent liver injury with long-term use.

The monograph is especially important because Tinospora is often marketed as a rejuvenative or immune-support herb. For cognition and brain-fog coverage, the safety read should come first: immune modulation, autoimmune-like hepatitis reports, product identity, duration, baseline liver disease, and co-use with other hepatotoxic supplements or drugs are central.
