# Fenfluramine-phentermine valvular heart disease case series

- PMID: 9271479
- DOI: 10.1056/NEJM199708283370901
- Journal: New England Journal of Medicine (1997)

## Captured takeaways

- Case-series report of valvular heart disease associated with fenfluramine-phentermine therapy.
- The PubMed abstract describes fenfluramine and phentermine as FDA-approved anorectic agents at the time.
- Histopathological features were reported as identical to those seen in carcinoid or ergotamine-induced valve disease.
- The abstract states candidates for fenfluramine-phentermine therapy should be informed about serious potential adverse effects, including pulmonary hypertension and valvular heart disease.
- Interpretation for the wiki: fen-phen history should be a class-level safety caution, but it should not be used to imply that phentermine alone has the same 5-HT2B valvulopathy mechanism as fenfluramine.
