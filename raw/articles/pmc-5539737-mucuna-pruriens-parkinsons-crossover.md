# Mucuna pruriens versus dispersible levodopa in Parkinson's disease

Randomized, double-blind crossover Parkinson's disease trial comparing single-dose Mucuna pruriens preparations against dispersible levodopa/benserazide in patients with advanced PD. The study is relevant to the wiki because Mucuna is a natural L-DOPA source and is often repackaged as a motivation / dopamine nootropic.

The trial supports a clinical-state dopaminergic interpretation, not healthy-adult cognition enhancement. Its outcomes are motor-state and levodopa-response anchored, and any cognitive/nootropic extrapolation would be a category error.

Use in the wiki: formal human PD comparator and identity anchor for Mucuna as L-DOPA exposure.
