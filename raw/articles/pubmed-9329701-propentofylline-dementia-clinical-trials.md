# PubMed 9329701: Clinical trials in dementia with propentofylline

This 1997 Annals of the New York Academy of Sciences review is titled "Clinical trials in dementia with propentofylline."

The abstract reports four double-blind, placebo-controlled, randomized studies in mild-to-moderate Alzheimer's disease and mild-to-moderate vascular dementia. It describes 901 Alzheimer's disease patients and 359 vascular dementia patients treated for up to 12 months with propentofylline 300 mg three times daily, taken one hour before food.

Efficacy variables included cognitive and global functions and activities of daily living. The abstract reports statistically significant, clinically relevant improvements over placebo in both Alzheimer's disease and vascular dementia.

Safety summary from the abstract: propentofylline was well tolerated, had no significant effects on laboratory findings, and related adverse events were mostly minor, transient, and affected the digestive and nervous systems.

Interpretation for the wiki: this is the strongest positive legacy clinical-program summary, but it should be read alongside Cochrane and DARE limitations.
