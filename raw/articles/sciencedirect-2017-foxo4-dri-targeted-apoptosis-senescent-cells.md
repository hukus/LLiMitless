# Cell 2017 FOXO4-DRI targeted apoptosis of senescent cells

- Source type: Peer-reviewed preclinical senolytic proof-of-concept paper.
- Publication: Cell, 2017-03-23; 169(1):132-147.e16.
- PMID: 28340339.
- DOI: 10.1016/j.cell.2017.02.031.
- Date checked: 2026-05-06.

This is the main FOXO4-DRI anchor. The paper identifies FOXO4 as a senescent-cell viability node and designs a modified FOXO4-p53 interfering peptide. In senescent cells, the peptide perturbs FOXO4-p53 interaction, drives p53 nuclear exclusion, and induces cell-intrinsic apoptosis.

In vivo, the source reports tolerability under tested conditions, doxorubicin chemotoxicity rescue, and improved fitness, fur density, and renal function in fast-aging and naturally aged mice. It does not provide human dosing, human safety, or human cognitive efficacy.

Use in the wiki: proof-of-concept source for FOXO4-DRI as a preclinical senolytic peptide. It supports mechanism and mouse aging-model salience, not a human anti-aging or cognition recommendation.
