# Saniona Annual Report 2025 - Tesomet / tesofensine status

## Captured takeaways

- Saniona's 2025 annual report describes Tesomet as an investigational once-daily oral fixed-dose combination of tesofensine and metoprolol for hypothalamic obesity and Prader-Willi syndrome.
- The report says tesofensine is a presynaptic reuptake inhibitor with appetite-suppressing properties and that metoprolol is included as a cardioselective beta-1 receptor blocker.
- It reports FDA feedback that Tesomet may proceed through a 505(b)(2) pathway for hypothalamic obesity and Prader-Willi syndrome, with orphan drug designation for both indications.
- For hypothalamic obesity, the report summarizes a 24-week randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled phase 2 trial with 21 adult patients; the primary safety/tolerability endpoint was achieved, and placebo-adjusted weight loss was 6.28%.
- For Prader-Willi syndrome, the report summarizes a two-center randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled phase 2 study with nine adults and nine adolescents, followed by open-label extensions in adolescents; adult Tesomet users had 5.4% body-weight reduction and an 8.1 percentage-point hyperphagia reduction by HQ-CT.
- Interpretation for the wiki: current sponsor status supports an investigational rare-obesity branch, not a cognition indication; metoprolol coadministration highlights the cardiovascular mitigation issue.
