# WADA 2026 monitoring program

- WADA monitors substances that are not on the Prohibited List but may be misused in sport.
- The 2026 Monitoring Program includes caffeine, nicotine, phenylephrine, and synephrine for in-competition monitoring.
- The monitoring program is not a prohibition list, but it is a useful sports-visibility signal for wakefulness and supplement branches.
