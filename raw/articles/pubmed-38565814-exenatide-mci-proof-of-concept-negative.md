# Long-acting exenatide does not prevent cognitive decline in mild cognitive impairment: a proof-of-concept clinical trial

- PMID: 38565814
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38565814/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

2024 proof-of-concept MCI trial of weekly exenatide was negative on ADAS-Cog11. Glucose and weight improved, but cognition did not, which is a useful counterweight to the more optimistic T2DM meta-analysis.
