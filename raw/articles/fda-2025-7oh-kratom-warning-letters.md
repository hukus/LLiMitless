# FDA warning letters for products containing 7-hydroxymitragynine

- Source: FDA press announcement
- Date: 2025-07-15
- Evidence tier: regulatory / safety

## Summary

The FDA announced warning letters to firms marketing products containing 7-hydroxymitragynine, also called 7-OH, 7-OH-mitragynine, 7-OHMG, or 7-HMG. The agency described concentrated 7-OH products as novel potent opioid products sold online and in retail channels, distinct from trace 7-OH occurring naturally in kratom leaves.

The FDA stated that 7-OH is not lawful in dietary supplements, cannot be lawfully added to conventional foods, and is not an ingredient in any FDA-approved drug. The warning focused on products with added or enhanced 7-OH, including tablets, gummies, drink mixes, and shots, and described them as unproven for safety or effectiveness.
