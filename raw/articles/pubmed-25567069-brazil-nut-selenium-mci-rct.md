# Brazil nut selenium status and cognitive performance in mild cognitive impairment

- PMID: 25567069
- Published: 2015
- Source type: randomized controlled pilot trial
- Checked: 2026-05-11
- Ingested: 2026-05-11

## Summary

This pilot trial tested Brazil nut consumption as a selenium intervention in older adults with mild cognitive impairment. It is relevant because it links selenium status movement with cognitive measures such as verbal fluency and constructional praxis in a clinical-risk population.

Use in the wiki: this is not healthy-adult enhancement proof. It supports a low-confidence, phenotype-gated selenium watch item where low selenium status, diet, thyroid/redox context, and Brazil-nut dose variability are explicitly tracked.

## Notes

- Brazil nuts are a food matrix and a variable selenium exposure, not a precise selenium dose.
- The evidence is too small and clinical-state specific to justify blind selenium supplementation for cognition.
