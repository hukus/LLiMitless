# FDA 101: Dietary supplements

- Source: FDA consumer guidance
- URL: https://www.fda.gov/consumers/consumer-updates/fda-101-dietary-supplements
- Ingested: 2026-04-30

## Summary

FDA states that it does not approve dietary supplements for safety and effectiveness before marketing. Under DSHEA, manufacturers and distributors are responsible for safety and labeling, and FDA can act after products reach the market if they are adulterated or misbranded. For consumer aniracetam products, that means a hidden-drug recall should be read as a sourcing-credibility warning rather than an isolated oddity.
