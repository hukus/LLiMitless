# 36-month LipiDiDiet Fortasyn Connect trial in prodromal Alzheimer's disease

- PMID: 32920957
- PMCID: PMC7821311
- Journal: Alzheimer's & Dementia (2021)

## Captured takeaways

- LipiDiDiet investigated Fortasyn Connect, the multinutrient combination used in Souvenaid, in prodromal Alzheimer's disease.
- Three hundred eleven participants were randomized; 162 completed 36 months, and 81 had 36-month data eligible for efficacy analysis in the reported extension.
- The paper reports significant between-group differences favoring active intervention over 36 months for the NTB 5-item composite, NTB memory domain, CDR-SB, and brain-atrophy measures.
- The paper reports no suggestion of tolerability or health concerns related to active product use over 36 months.
- Funding included Danone Nutricia Research support for the intervention period from 25 to 96 months.
- Interpretation for the wiki: this is the strongest human uridine-containing formula signal, but it is prodromal-AD, multinutrient, long-duration, and product-specific.
