# ScienceDirect 2008: Centella asiatica healthy elderly cognition and mood RCT

This Journal of Ethnopharmacology randomized, placebo-controlled, double-blind study tested Centella asiatica extract in healthy elderly volunteers.

Design: 28 healthy elderly participants received plant extract doses of 250, 500, or 750 mg once daily for 2 months. Cognitive performance was assessed with a computerized cognitive test battery and event-related potentials; mood was assessed with Bond-Lader visual analogue scales before treatment and after single-dose, 1-month, and 2-month treatment timepoints.

Results from the abstract: the high-dose extract enhanced working memory and increased N100 event-related-potential amplitude. Self-rated mood improvements were also reported after Centella asiatica treatment.

Interpretation for the wiki: this is the main small healthy-elderly positive trial, but it is very small, dose-specific, and older. It supports tracking Centella, not broad enhancer confidence.
