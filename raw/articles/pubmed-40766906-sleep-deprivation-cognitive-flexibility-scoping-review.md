# The effects of sleep deprivation on cognitive flexibility: a scoping review of outcomes and biological mechanisms

- PMID: 40766906
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40766906/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

2025 scoping review of 6 randomized controlled trials and 11 non-randomized studies on sleep deprivation and cognitive flexibility. Total sleep deprivation consistently reduced task-switching accuracy and cognitive flexibility; partial sleep deprivation was less consistent. The review frames accuracy as a more sensitive signal than reaction time for this endpoint.

## Notes

Useful for the resilience bucket: it treats sleep loss as a challenge state that can unmask executive-control failure, especially on task-switching style measures.
