# ClinicalTrials.gov NCT06221826: Mexidol acute cerebral failure rehabilitation results

ClinicalTrials.gov record NCT06221826 is a Pharmasoft-sponsored phase 4 randomized open-label parallel trial titled `MEXIDOL in the Rehabilitation Treatment of Patients With Acute Cerebral Failure`. The registry lists actual enrollment of 60, study start 2023-04-17, completion 2023-09-12, results first posted 2025-04-27, and last update posted 2025-04-27.

Intervention arm: Mexidol IV 500 mg for 10 days, then Mexidol FORTE 250 mg orally three times daily for 8 weeks, plus standard rehabilitation. Control: standard rehabilitation only. The standard program included kinesitherapy, occupational therapy, speech therapy, clinical psychology, electrotherapy, rehabilitation environment therapy, and at least 3 hours/day rehabilitation load for 12 days.

Primary and reported outcomes included attentiveness/performance, cognitive status, depression, anxiety, post-intensive-care syndrome, mobility, quality of life, muscle strength, and TCD measures. For cognitive status, the day 66 registry value was MoCA 23.8 (SD 2.6) in the Mexidol+standard arm versus 22.9 (SD 3.0) in control. The attentiveness/performance values were baseline-imbalanced at day 1, with 94.9 in the Mexidol arm and 71.2 in control, then 71.7 vs 63.0 by day 66.

Safety: the registry reports 0 deaths, 0 serious adverse events, and 0 other adverse events in both 30-person arms.

Interpretation for the wiki: this is rehabilitation-context support only. It is open-label, small, baseline-imbalanced for at least one cognitive-performance measure, and not a healthy-adult cognition trial.
