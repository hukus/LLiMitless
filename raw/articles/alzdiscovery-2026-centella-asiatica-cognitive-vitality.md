# Cognitive Vitality Centella asiatica / Gotu Kola

Alzheimer's Drug Discovery Foundation Cognitive Vitality page for Centella asiatica, updated April 30, 2026. The page summarizes Centella as an Ayurvedic/traditional-medicine plant with modest signals in a few cognitive functions but no strong meta-analytic cognition benefit.

Key interpretation: small clinical trials suggest possible benefits in selected cognitive functions, but a meta-analysis of trials did not show significant cognitive benefits; a post-stroke vascular cognitive impairment study did not improve overall cognition compared with folic acid but did improve delayed recall. Safety is generally mild, but liver-toxicity cases have been reported.

Relevance: current expert-synthesis anchor for keeping gotu kola in the product-specific low-confidence botanical lane.
