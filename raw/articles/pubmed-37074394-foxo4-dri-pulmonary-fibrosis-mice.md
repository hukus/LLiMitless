# PubMed 37074394 FOXO4-DRI pulmonary fibrosis mouse study

- Source type: PubMed-indexed preclinical pulmonary fibrosis paper.
- Publication: Naunyn-Schmiedeberg's Archives of Pharmacology, 2023-10; 396(10):2393-2403.
- PMID: 37074394.
- DOI: 10.1007/s00210-023-02452-2.
- Date checked: 2026-05-06.

This paper evaluates FOXO4-D-Retro-Inverso in a bleomycin-induced pulmonary fibrosis mouse model and activated fibroblast models. The authors report milder pathology and less collagen deposition in FOXO4-DRI-treated animals compared with the bleomycin-induced group.

The mechanistic framing is p53 nuclear redistribution plus decreased extracellular matrix protein content. The paper concludes that FOXO4-DRI may be promising for pulmonary fibrosis, but it remains preclinical.

Use in the wiki: disease-model extension source. It supports anti-fibrotic preclinical plausibility, not human pulmonary fibrosis treatment or cognition evidence.
