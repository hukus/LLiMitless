# Evaluation of Metabolically Stabilized Angiotensin IV Analogs as Procognitive/Antidementia Agents.

- PMID: 23055539
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23055539/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Foundational AngIV analog paper that identifies dihexa (N-hexanoic-Tyr-Ile-(6) aminohexanoic amide) as an orally active, blood-brain-barrier-permeant compound with antidementia activity in scopolamine and aged rat models and marked synaptogenic activity.
