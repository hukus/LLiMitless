# PubMed 8997547: Nicergoline in multi-infarct dementia

This 1997 randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial tested nicergoline in multi-infarct dementia.

Design: patients aged 55-85 with mild to moderate multi-infarct dementia by DSM-III entered a 6-month double-blind phase after a 3-week single-blind placebo washout/run-in. Nicergoline was dosed at 30 mg twice daily. A total of 136 patients entered the double-blind phase and were evaluated as intent-to-treat patients.

Endpoints: primary endpoints were changes in Sandoz Clinical Assessment Geriatric Scale and Mini-Mental State Examination at treatment end versus baseline. Secondary endpoints included Clinical Global Impression, Wechsler Adult Intelligence Scale subtests, Blessed A activities-of-daily-living scale, and interval assessments.

Interpretation for the wiki: a vascular-dementia clinical-state anchor for [[nicergoline]], not a healthy-adult enhancement trial.
