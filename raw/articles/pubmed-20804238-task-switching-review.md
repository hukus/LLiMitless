# Control and interference in task switching - a review

- PMID: 20804238
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20804238/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

2010 review of task-switching paradigms as measures of cognitive control and interference. It shows why switch cost is a useful executive-function readout, but also why task-switch results are sensitive to cueing, block structure, and general task design.

## Notes

Task switching belongs in the battery as a compact measure of executive flexibility, not as a stand-alone claim about "general intelligence" or broad cognition.
