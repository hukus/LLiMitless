# ChEBI 2026 NAD+ identity note

ChEBI identifies NAD+ as CHEBI:15846, with ASCII name NAD(+), formula C21H28N7O14P2, average mass 664.438, and CAS 53-84-9. Synonyms shown on the ChEBI record include NAD, NAD+, DPN, nadide, diphosphopyridine nucleotide, beta-NAD, and nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide.

ChEBI defines NAD+ as the oxidised form of nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide found in all living cells. The record describes NAD+ as involved in redox reactions and electron transfer, with roles including cofactor, coenzyme, and fundamental metabolite.

Interpretation for the wiki: use this as the identity anchor for direct NAD+ products. NAD+ should be separated from reduced NADH, nicotinamide riboside, nicotinamide mononucleotide, niacin, nicotinamide, and vague "NAD booster" blends. Product pages should specify whether the exposure is IV NAD+, IM/injectable NAD+, patch/transdermal NAD+ marketing, lozenge/oral NAD+, or a precursor.
