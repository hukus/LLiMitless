# Paraxanthine safety and comparison to caffeine

- Journal: Frontiers in Toxicology
- DOI: 10.3389/ftox.2023.1117729
- Published: 2023-02-02
- Ingested: 2026-05-03

## Summary

2023 toxicology paper evaluating paraxanthine relative to caffeine using bacterial reverse mutation, mammalian chromosomal aberration, mammalian cell gene mutation, acute oral toxicity, 14-day repeat-dose oral toxicity, and 90-day subchronic oral toxicity studies.

The paper reports no evidence of genetic toxicity or mutagenicity in the in vitro studies, an acute oral LD50 of 829.20 mg/kg body weight in rats, no mortality or treatment-related adverse effects in the 14-day rat study at 50-150 mg/kg, and a 90-day NOAEL of 185 mg/kg body weight for paraxanthine compared with 150 mg/kg for caffeine in Sprague Dawley rats. The paper notes mortality in two high-dose caffeine-treated animals, not the paraxanthine groups.

Use in the wiki: safety/toxicology anchor and caffeine-comparison source. It is not human long-term safety proof and does not remove stimulant cautions around blood pressure, heart rate, anxiety, sleep, pregnancy, medication interactions, or high-dose consumer use.
