# Brain energy homeostasis: the evolution of the astrocyte-neuron lactate shuttle hypothesis

- PMID: 39725609
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39725609/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

2025 review of the astrocyte-neuron lactate shuttle literature. It frames lactate as both an energy substrate and a signaling molecule, but it is still a mechanism paper rather than human cognition evidence. The useful take-away for the wiki is that the branch remains biologically plausible while the exact direction of lactate flux and its behavioral relevance stay debated.
