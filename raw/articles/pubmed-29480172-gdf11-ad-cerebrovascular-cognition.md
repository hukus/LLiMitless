# GDF11 rejuvenates cerebrovascular structure and function in an animal model of Alzheimer's disease

- PMID: 29480172
- DOI: 10.3233/JAD-170474
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29480172/
- Ingested: 2026-05-04

## Summary

Journal of Alzheimer's Disease 2018 animal-model study. Daily intravenous GDF11 was administered to 12-month-old A-betaPP/PS1 mice. The study reports rescued Morris-water-maze cognitive function, improved cerebrovascular structure/function, higher vascular-related protein expression, and lower Iba-1 / GFAP inflammatory markers with a lower A-beta40/42 ratio. This is a disease-model signal for cognition and vasculature, but it remains mouse evidence.
