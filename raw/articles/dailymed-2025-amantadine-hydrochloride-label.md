# Amantadine hydrochloride DailyMed label

DailyMed label snapshot for amantadine hydrochloride 100 mg capsules, updated 2025-07-09 and revised 2025-06.

Safety-relevant label points for cognition routing: amantadine has antiparkinsonian and antiviral indications and is primarily renally excreted. The label warns about overdose deaths, accumulation in renal impairment, suicide attempts/ideation, psychiatric worsening, hallucinations/paranoia/confusion/agitation, seizure caution, blurred vision/CNS effects, congestive heart failure/peripheral edema, anticholinergic-like effects, untreated angle-closure glaucoma, corneal edema, and abrupt-discontinuation neuropsychiatric syndromes.

Commonly reported adverse reactions include nausea, dizziness/lightheadedness, and insomnia; less frequent events include depression, anxiety/irritability, hallucinations, confusion, dry mouth, constipation, ataxia, peripheral edema, orthostatic hypotension, headache, somnolence, nervousness, dream abnormalities, agitation, diarrhea, and fatigue.

Interpretation for the wiki: amantadine should be a prescription medical candidate with renal, psychiatric, sleep, vision, cardiovascular/orthostatic, and discontinuation gates, not a casual fatigue or brain-fog supplement.
