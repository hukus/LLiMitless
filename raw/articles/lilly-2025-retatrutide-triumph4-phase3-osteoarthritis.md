# Lilly 2025 TRIUMPH-4 retatrutide Phase 3 knee osteoarthritis topline

Official Lilly release dated 2025-12-11 for TRIUMPH-4, NCT05931367.

## Captured takeaways

- TRIUMPH-4 was a 68-week randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled Phase 3 study in adults with obesity or overweight and knee osteoarthritis, without diabetes.
- Retatrutide 9 mg and 12 mg met co-primary endpoints for body-weight reduction and WOMAC pain-subscale improvement.
- Weight loss reached -26.4% at 9 mg and -28.7% at 12 mg versus -2.1% with placebo using the efficacy estimand.
- WOMAC pain score improved by about 75% in the retatrutide groups versus about 40% with placebo; WOMAC physical-function scores also improved.
- Lilly reported improvements in non-HDL cholesterol, triglycerides, hsCRP, and systolic blood pressure.
- The topline release did not report cognition, attention, memory, brain-fog, mood, or neuropsychiatric endpoints.
- Common adverse events included nausea, diarrhea, constipation, vomiting, and decreased appetite. Dysesthesia was reported, especially at 12 mg.
- Interpretation for the wiki: the OA readout is important for pain and function, which can indirectly affect perceived cognition through sleep, mobility, mood, and inflammation, but it is not direct cognitive evidence.
