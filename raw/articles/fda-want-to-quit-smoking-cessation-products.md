# Want to Quit Smoking? FDA-Approved and FDA-Cleared Cessation Products Can Help

- Source: https://www.fda.gov/consumers/consumer-updates/want-quit-smoking-fda-approved-products-can-help
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

FDA guidance frames nicotine replacement therapy as a cessation aid that reduces withdrawal by delivering measured nicotine without smoke toxins. It also notes that nicotine is highly addictive and that nicotine-containing products can increase heart rate and blood pressure. Varenicline is a non-nicotine cessation drug; the FDA summary highlights nausea, constipation, gas, vomiting, trouble sleeping, vivid dreams, and alcohol-tolerance changes as common concerns.
