# Minocycline at two dosages versus placebo in mild Alzheimer disease

2020 JAMA Neurology randomized clinical trial in 544 eligible participants with mild Alzheimer disease. Participants received minocycline 400 mg/day, minocycline 200 mg/day, or placebo for 24 months.

Minocycline did not significantly slow cognitive or functional decline compared with placebo. The combined minocycline group had a 24-month sMMSE decline similar to placebo, and BADLS worsening was also similar. The 400 mg/day arm was poorly tolerated: fewer participants completed treatment, with more gastrointestinal symptoms, dermatologic adverse effects, and dizziness.

Interpretation for the wiki: this is a major negative disease-modification counterweight for the minocycline neuroinflammation story. It is not a brain-fog study, but it argues against broad anti-inflammatory cognition claims.

Relevance: high-value negative clinical trial for [[minocycline]].
