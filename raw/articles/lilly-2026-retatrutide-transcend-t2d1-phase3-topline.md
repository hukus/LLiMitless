# Lilly 2026 TRANSCEND-T2D-1 retatrutide Phase 3 topline

Official Lilly release dated 2026-03-19 for TRANSCEND-T2D-1.

## Captured takeaways

- TRANSCEND-T2D-1 was a Phase 3 trial of once-weekly retatrutide in adults with type 2 diabetes, inadequate glycemic control with diet and exercise alone, and mean diabetes duration of 2.5 years.
- Retatrutide met the primary and key secondary endpoints at 40 weeks.
- A1C reductions were -1.7% to -2.0% across 4 mg, 9 mg, and 12 mg versus -0.8% with placebo.
- Body-weight reductions reached -16.8% at 12 mg versus -2.5% with placebo using the efficacy estimand.
- The release did not report cognition, brain fog, mood, attention, memory, dementia, or neuropsychiatric endpoints.
- The release reported common adverse events and dysesthesia but did not foreground a cognitive adverse-event signal.
- Interpretation for the wiki: the diabetes Phase 3 readout supports glycemic and weight efficacy but does not answer whether retatrutide has cognitive effects beyond metabolic-state changes.
