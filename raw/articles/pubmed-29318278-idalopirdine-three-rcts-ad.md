# Effect of Idalopirdine as Adjunct to Cholinesterase Inhibitors on Change in Cognition in Patients With Alzheimer Disease: Three Randomized Clinical Trials

- PMID: 29318278
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29318278/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

Large phase 3 development program of idalopirdine in mild-to-moderate AD across three randomized clinical trials. Idalopirdine added to cholinesterase inhibitors did not reduce cognitive decline over 24 weeks, and the JAMA report states the findings do not support use for AD. This is the class-defining negative readout for idalopirdine.
