# Relation of the kynurenine pathway with normal age

- PMID: 38056721
- DOI: 10.1016/j.mad.2023.111890
- Journal: Mechanisms of Ageing and Development (2024)

## Captured takeaways

- Systematic review covered 55 studies published through October 2023.
- Blood tryptophan tends to decrease with age.
- Blood and CSF kynurenine and kynurenine/tryptophan ratio tend to increase with age.
- CSF and blood kynurenic acid / quinolinic acid findings were mixed or non-significant.
- Usefulness for the wiki: age correction matters, but the review does not itself establish a cognition intervention target.
