# Source Digest

Type: systematic review and meta-analysis of double-blind randomized controlled trials for chronic pain.

Publication date: 2023-03-10.

Key extraction:
- Scope: double-blind randomized controlled trials comparing PEA with placebo or active comparators in chronic pain.
- Included evidence: 11 articles in narrative synthesis and meta-analysis, combined sample size 774 patients.
- Primary pain result: pooled standard mean difference for pain reduction was 1.68 with 95% CI 1.05 to 2.31.
- Safety readout: no major side effects were attributed to PEA in any included study.
- Interpretation for cognition wiki: safety and tolerability context is useful, but this is not a cognition-efficacy source.

Wiki signal:
- Safety signal: moderate support for tolerability in pain RCTs.
- Confidence limiter: pain endpoints do not imply nootropic benefit, and optimal dosing/administration still needed further study.
