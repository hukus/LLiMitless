# Interindividual differences in digital phenotypes of major depressive disorder: A passive sensing study using smartphone and wearable sensor data

- PMID: 41707474
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41707474/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Passive-sensing study in 297 people with major depressive disorder that clustered participants into digital phenotypes using sleep, activity, screen time, social engagement, and heart-rate-variability features.

## Notes

- One profile was characterized by deficient sleep, chronically low HRV, and low social engagement.
- That profile had poorer social and occupational functioning than the average profile, though the difference weakened after correction.
- This supports digital phenotype as a state stratifier, even though it is not a direct intervention-response paper.
