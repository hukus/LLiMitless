# Pharmacological enhancement of glymphatic function in humans increases the clearance of Alzheimer’s disease-related proteins

- medRxiv: https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.03.10.26348048v2
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

2026 human preprint in healthy older adults. Two crossover clinical trials tested a fixed-dose combination of intravenous dexmedetomidine and oral midodrine (ACX-02) designed to suppress central noradrenergic tone while maintaining arterial pressure. The intervention increased EEG slow waves, cerebrovascular pulsatility, and reduced parenchymal resistance to perivascular fluid flow, with plasma mass-balance indices suggesting about a 9-10% increase in A beta and tau clearance during a single 4h 15m sleep opportunity. No direct cognition endpoint is reported, so this is strong human glymphatic mechanistic evidence rather than enhancement proof.
