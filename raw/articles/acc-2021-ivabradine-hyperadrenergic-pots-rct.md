# ACC 2021 ivabradine hyperadrenergic POTS RCT summary

## Captured takeaways

- ACC's trial summary covers Taub et al.'s randomized, blinded crossover trial of ivabradine versus placebo in hyperadrenergic postural orthostatic tachycardia syndrome (POTS).
- Twenty-two patients with hyperadrenergic POTS entered 1-month ivabradine and placebo periods separated by a 1-week washout.
- The primary heart-rate outcome favored ivabradine: standing-minus-supine heart-rate change at 1 month was lower on ivabradine than placebo.
- Quality-of-life measures for physical functioning and social functioning also improved versus placebo in the summary.
- The study excluded people with resting heart rate below 60 bpm, atrial fibrillation, supraventricular tachycardia, and pregnancy or breastfeeding.
- Interpretation for the wiki: this is a real small RCT for POTS physiology and quality-of-life state, not direct evidence of healthy-adult cognitive enhancement or a cognition endpoint.
