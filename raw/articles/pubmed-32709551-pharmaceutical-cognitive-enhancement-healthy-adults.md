# How effective are pharmaceuticals for cognitive enhancement in healthy adults? A series of meta-analyses of cognitive performance during acute administration of modafinil, methylphenidate and D-amphetamine

- PMID: 32709551
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32709551/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Meta-analysis in healthy non-sleep-deprived adults. Modafinil had a small overall benefit and improved memory updating. Methylphenidate had a somewhat larger small effect, driven by recall, sustained attention, and inhibitory control. D-amphetamine showed no overall effect in this dataset.
