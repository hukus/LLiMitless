# PubMed 17109576 D-ribose CFS / fibromyalgia pilot

This 2006 Journal of Alternative and Complementary Medicine pilot study evaluated D-ribose in chronic fatigue syndrome (CFS) and fibromyalgia syndrome (FMS). The paper framed CFS/FMS around impaired cellular energy metabolism and tested whether D-ribose could improve symptoms.

Design: open-label, uncontrolled pilot study. Participants with CFS and/or FMS received D-ribose powder, commonly described as 5 g three times daily, for roughly three weeks. Outcomes included visual-analog scales for energy, sleep, mental clarity, pain, and overall well-being.

Result: the PubMed abstract reports that D-ribose was associated with significant improvement across all five visual-analog categories. About 66% of patients experienced significant improvement while on D-ribose, with average energy increase of about 45% and overall well-being increase of about 30%.

Interpretation for the wiki: this is the main D-ribose brain-fog-adjacent formal source because "mental clarity" was explicitly measured. However, the study is open-label, short, uncontrolled, illness-state only, and not an objective cognition trial.
