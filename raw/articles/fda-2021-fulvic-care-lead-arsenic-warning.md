# FDA 2021: Fulvic Care powder and tablets lead/arsenic warning

FDA warned consumers not to use Fulvic Care powder and tablets distributed by Black Oxygen Organics after finding elevated levels of lead and arsenic in product samples. FDA said the products could pose serious health risks if taken routinely.

Interpretation for the wiki: this is not Shilajit, but it is a fulvic/humic-mineral product contamination precedent. It strengthens the requirement that [[shilajit]] and fulvic-acid claims use product-specific contaminant testing instead of generic "natural mineral" arguments.
