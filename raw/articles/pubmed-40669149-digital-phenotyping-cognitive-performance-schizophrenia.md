# Digital phenotyping correlates of cognitive performance in schizophrenia spectrum disorders from a longitudinal study

- PMID: 40669149
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40669149/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Longitudinal study in 50 participants using MindLAMP passive sensing over 12 months. Home-time, screen duration, and entropy were related to social and nonsocial cognitive performance in schizophrenia spectrum disorders.

## Notes

- Greater baseline and six-month home-time was associated with poorer social cognition.
- Baseline or first-month home-time predicted later faux-pas recognition.
- This is a state-marker paper, not a treatment-response study, but it supports digital phenotype as a cognition-enrichment signal.
