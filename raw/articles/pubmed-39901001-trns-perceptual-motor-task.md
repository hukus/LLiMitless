# The effect of transcranial random noise stimulation on the movement time and components of noise, co-variation, and tolerance in a perceptual-motor task

- PMID: 39901001
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39901001/
- Ingested: 2026-04-30

## Summary

Randomized controlled trial in 30 healthy men using tRNS during training on a perceptual-motor task. The paper reported significant improvements in movement time, noise cost, co-variation, tolerance, and the sensorimotor wave measure. This is performance-adjacent evidence rather than direct cognition evidence, but it adds another positive stochastic-resonance signal around motor learning.

## Notes

Keep this source in the branch as an adjacent-performance boundary case, not as proof of general executive-function enhancement.
