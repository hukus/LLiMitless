# Randomized, placebo-controlled, double-blind, pilot trial to investigate safety and efficacy of Cerebrolysin in patients with aneurysmal subarachnoid hemorrhage

- PMID: 33143640
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33143640/
- PMCID: PMC7607674
- Ingested: 2026-04-30

## Summary

Prospective randomized double-blind placebo-controlled pilot in 50 aneurysmal SAH patients. Cerebrolysin 30 mL/day or saline for 14 days. No significant difference in 6-month GOSE, mRS, or MoCA; no severe Cerebrolysin-attributable adverse effects or mortality. Cognition signal is neutral, but this is one of the few blinded human data points with a direct cognitive readout.

## Notes

Clinical trial registration: NCT01787123.
