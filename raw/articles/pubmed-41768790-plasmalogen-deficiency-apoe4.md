# Plasmalogen deficiency and the Alzheimer's disease risk of apolipoprotein E4

- PMID: 41768790
- PMCID: PMC12946155
- Source: PubMed / Brain Communications 2026
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

CSF lipidomics study in cognitively intact, amyloid-negative individuals with known APOE status. The authors report a biological gradient in ethanolamine plasmalogen association with apolipoprotein E, with E4E4 lower than E3E3 and E3E4 intermediate. They argue that this supports a plausible causal role for plasmalogen deficiency in AD risk rather than a purely downstream disease effect.

## Notes

- This is strong hypothesis support for the deficiency story.
- It is not a cognition-intervention result.
- The sample is small, so the paper should be treated as mechanistically important but not decisive.
