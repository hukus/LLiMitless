# Repeated Administration Effects on Psychomotor Vigilance Test Performance

- PMID: 29126328
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29126328/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

Study of a validated 3-minute PVT repeated 16 times found measurable administration effects, mostly early, with the largest shifts on fastest and slowest response distributions and fewer false starts. The practical lesson is that PVT is a good anchor but not a magic practice-free baseline.

## Notes

For short self-study blocks, at least one or two familiarization runs are still sensible before treating PVT drift as an intervention effect.
