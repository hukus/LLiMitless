# Impact of creatine supplementation and exercise training in older adults: a systematic review and meta-analysis

- PMID: 41062952
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41062952/
- Ingested: 2026-04-29

## Summary

2025 systematic review and meta-analysis of 20 studies (n=1093) found creatine plus exercise improved 1RM and reduced body-fat percentage in older adults, but did not improve total-body bone mineral density.

## Notes

This is useful for the synergy question, but it is still a physical-performance/body-composition result rather than a cognition trial.
