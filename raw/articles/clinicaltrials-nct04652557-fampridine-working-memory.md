# Influence of Fampridine on Working Memory in Healthy Young Subjects

- ClinicalTrials.gov ID: NCT04652557
- Study title: Randomized Placebo-controlled Phase II Cross-over Study on the Influence of Fampridine on Working Memory in Healthy Subjects
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Completed healthy-volunteer crossover study from the University of Basel. The protocol used fampridine SR 10 mg twice daily for 3.5 days and measured 3-back working memory, 0-back attention, SDMT, digit span, matrix reasoning, and resting motor threshold. The registry also documents the main safety exclusions: seizure history, renal impairment, and OCT2 interaction risk.

## Notes

- Enrollment was 44; the published paper analyzed 43 for the primary outcome.
- Trial includes acute and repeated administration periods.
- This is the cleanest protocol source for dose and endpoint details.
