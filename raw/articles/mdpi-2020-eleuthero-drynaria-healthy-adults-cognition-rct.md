# Eleutherococcus senticosus plus Drynaria fortunei healthy-adult cognition RCT

2020 Nutrients placebo-controlled randomized double-blind trial of combined Eleutherococcus senticosus leaf water extract and Drynaria fortunei rhizome water extract in healthy adults.

The study analyzed 31 healthy adults after 12 weeks. The daily dose was 203 mg Eleutherococcus senticosus extract plus 20 mg Drynaria fortunei extract, delivered as three capsules once per day. The primary neurocognitive assessment was the Japanese version of RBANS, with MMSE and stress-checklist measures as additional outcomes.

The abstract and article report a significant intergroup improvement in RBANS figure recall, with intragroup movement in language, semantic fluency, and figure recall. Anxiety/uncertainty improved only in intragroup comparison. No adverse effects were observed in the trial.

Interpretation for the wiki: this is a small but relevant healthy-adult cognition signal for a fixed Eleuthero-plus-Drynaria formula. It should not be generalized to Eleuthero alone, Drynaria alone, or generic "Siberian ginseng" supplements.
