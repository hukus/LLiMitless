# Pulse-UP GnRH Down syndrome 2026 status
- ClinicalTrials.gov ID: NCT04390646.
- Status: recruiting; estimated enrollment 56; primary completion estimated 2028-12.
- Design: randomized, double-blind pulsatile GnRH pump vs saline in people with Down syndrome.
- Historical registry record tracks cognition, olfaction, MRI, and biomarker endpoints over 24 weeks of therapy.
- Branch readout: this remains the key ongoing GnRH cognition trial and is now larger and longer than the early registry snapshot.
