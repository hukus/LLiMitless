# The relationship between serum klotho and cognitive performance in a nationally representative sample of US adults

- PMID: 36819720
- PMC: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9932504/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

NHANES 2011-2014 cross-sectional study of 2171 US adults aged 60-79. Higher serum Klotho was associated with better DSST and CERAD performance after adjustment, with a nonlinear dose-response that appeared to plateau around the upper range of observed values. This supports Klotho as a cognitive-health marker, not as proof of causality.
