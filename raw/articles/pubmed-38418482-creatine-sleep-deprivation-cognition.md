# Single dose creatine improves cognitive performance and induces changes in cerebral high energy phosphates during sleep deprivation.

- PMID: 38418482
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38418482/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

2024 randomized crossover sleep-deprivation study in healthy adults used a single high oral dose (0.35 g/kg) and reported improved cognitive performance and processing speed, alongside changes in cerebral phosphocreatine/ATP measures.

## Notes

The study suggests creatine can partially buffer fatigue-related cognitive decline under acute sleep loss, rather than acting as a broad baseline enhancer.
