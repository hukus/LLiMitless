# Effectiveness of VR-based cognitive training and games on cognitive rehabilitation in patients with MCI: a systematic review and meta-analysis

- PMCID: PMC12634598
- PMC: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12634598/
- Ingested: 2026-04-29

## Summary

Frontiers meta-analysis of 11 RCTs in MCI found that VR-based interventions improved cognitive rehabilitation overall, with a pooled Hedges' g of 0.6.

The abstract reports that VR games showed Hedges' g of 0.68 and VR cognitive training Hedges' g of 0.52, and that immersion level was a significant moderator of heterogeneity. Evidence quality was rated moderate overall, with lower certainty for the games subgroup.

## Notes

This is the strongest quantitative anchor in the current VR branch. The key caveat is that immersion and delivery details appear to matter, so the category is not interchangeable with generic "brain games."
