# Safety and tolerability of sustained exogenous ketosis using ketone monoester drinks for 28 days in healthy adults

- PMID: 31655093
- Study type: 28-day human safety trial
- Population: 24 healthy adults aged 18-70
- Intervention: 25 mL ketone monoester three times daily
- Main finding: no meaningful changes in weight, glucose, lipids, electrolytes, blood gases, or kidney function
- Adverse events: mild nausea after 6 of 2,016 drinks

## Summary

This is the main short-term safety anchor for sustained ketone-monoester use in healthy adults.
