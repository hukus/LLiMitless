# Choline - Health Professional Fact Sheet

NIH Office of Dietary Supplements health-professional fact sheet for choline, checked 2026-05-15. ODS describes choline as an essential nutrient present in foods and available as a dietary supplement.

Choline is a source of methyl groups needed for metabolism. It is required to synthesize phosphatidylcholine and sphingomyelin, major cell-membrane phospholipids, and is also needed to produce acetylcholine, a neurotransmitter involved in memory, mood, muscle control, and other nervous-system functions.

ODS notes that when folate is deficient, the need for dietary choline rises because choline becomes the primary methyl donor. Choline requirement is influenced by dietary methionine, betaine, and folate, sex, pregnancy, lactation, endogenous choline synthesis, and genetic variants affecting choline needs.
