# Artificial Intelligence-Guided Mobile Telerehabilitation for Individuals With Cognitive Impairment: A Feasibility Study

- PMID: 41492723
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41492723/
- Ingested: 2026-04-29

## Summary

Feasibility study of an AI-guided mobile cognitive telerehabilitation program (Zenicog) in 13 participants with cognitive impairment, including nine with stroke and four with mild cognitive impairment. Participants were asked to complete 24 sessions within 6 weeks.

Eleven participants completed the study and 10 met adherence criteria. MMSE improved from 24.00 to 27.50 after intervention, usability was rated fairly high, and the lowest usability domain was tolerance for error.

## Notes

This is not an efficacy-definitive trial, but it is useful for the delivery-validation layer because it exposes adherence and usability constraints.
