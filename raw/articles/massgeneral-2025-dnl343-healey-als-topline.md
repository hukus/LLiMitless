# DNL343 HEALEY ALS Regimen G topline results

- Source: Mass General / Sean M. Healey & AMG Center for ALS
- Date: 2025-01-06
- Source URL: https://www.massgeneral.org/neurology/als/news/healeyamg-announces-platform-trial-update-dnl343

## Summary

Topline results from HEALEY ALS Platform Trial Regimen G evaluating DNL343, an investigational eIF2B agonist / integrated-stress-response modulator, in adults with amyotrophic lateral sclerosis. The primary endpoint of overall function measured by ALSFRS-R and survival from baseline through 24 weeks was not met. Key secondary endpoints measuring muscle strength and respiratory function also were not statistically different between active and placebo groups at week 24.

The primary analysis compared 186 participants randomized to DNL343 against 139 placebo participants, including regimen-specific and shared concurrent placebo participants. DNL343 was reported as safe and well tolerated. Additional analyses, including neurofilament light, other fluid biomarkers, prespecified subgroup analyses, active-extension data, and target-engagement data, were expected later in 2025.

This source changes the DNL343 wiki interpretation: DNL343 remains a human target-engagement molecule, but it now also has a negative ALS phase 2/3 functional readout and still no cognition endpoint.
