# Shortened Spadin Analogs Display Better TREK-1 Inhibition, In Vivo Stability and Antidepressant Activity

- PMID: 28955242
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28955242/
- Ingested: 2026-04-29

## Summary

Preclinical optimization paper that produced the 7-amino-acid peptide PE-22-28 from spadin degradation products. The work reports stronger TREK-1 affinity than spadin, shorter-term rodent antidepressant-like behavior, rapid neurogenesis, and synaptogenesis signals, but no human exposure or human cognition validation.
