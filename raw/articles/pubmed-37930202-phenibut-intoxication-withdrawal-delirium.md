# Toxidrome of an Easily Obtainable Nootropic: A Case Report of Phenibut Intoxication and Withdrawal Delirium

- PMID: 37930202
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37930202/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

Case report describing phenibut intoxication followed by withdrawal delirium. The article frames phenibut as an easily obtainable nootropic with central nervous system depressant and anxiolytic properties, but the clinical issue here is the intoxication/withdrawal toxidrome rather than any cognition benefit.
