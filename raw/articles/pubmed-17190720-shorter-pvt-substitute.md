# Can a shorter psychomotor vigilance task be used as a reasonable substitute for the ten-minute psychomotor vigilance task?

- PMID: 17190720
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17190720/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

2006 paper comparing abbreviated PVT formats against the 10-minute reference. The study supports shorter testing for field studies, with the usual tradeoff that the shortest versions are less stable than the full task and should be interpreted as a screening-style vigilance readout.

## Notes

Useful for choosing a PVT duration that can be repeated daily without making the battery too long.
