# A methodological review of randomised n-of-1 trials

- PMID: 38622638
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38622638/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

2024 methodological review of randomized n-of-1 trials. The review identified 74 studies and found a median of six periods per trial, 77% with blinding, 66% placebo-controlled, 43% with washout, and 66% using patient-reported primary outcomes. The review is useful because it shows that current n-of-1 work commonly uses several periods, frequent blinding, and washout when needed, while still lacking standardized guidance on design and analysis.

## Notes

The reported mix of blinding, placebo control, period count, and washout makes this a practical template source for low-risk self-study planning.
