# Safety of phosphatidylserine containing omega-3 fatty acids in non-demented elderly

## Bibliographic snapshot

- BMC Neurology 2011 safety paper for PS-DHA in nondemented elderly people with memory complaints.
- PubMed ID: 21711517.
- PMCID: PMC3136416.
- DOI: 10.1186/1471-2377-11-79.
- Registry: NCT00437983.

## Study design

- Single-center randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled 15-week study, followed by a 15-week open-label extension.
- Participants: 157 nondemented elderly people with memory complaints.
- Double-blind arms: PS-DHA 300 mg PS/day versus placebo.
- Open-label extension: 100 mg PS/day PS-DHA for participants who continued.
- Standard safety labs, blood pressure, heart rate, weight, and adverse events were monitored.

## Results captured

- 131 participants completed the double-blind phase.
- 122 entered the open-label phase and 121 completed it.
- The paper reports no significant safety-parameter differences between groups during the double-blind phase.
- The open-label phase showed reduced resting diastolic blood pressure and slight weight gain.
- Authors concluded PS-DHA at 300 mg PS/day for 15 weeks or 100 mg PS/day for 30 weeks was safe and well tolerated in this tested population.

## Wiki interpretation

- Useful safety anchor for PS-DHA in older nondemented participants with memory complaints.
- It is not an efficacy paper by itself, but it points to the companion efficacy program and helps define dose / duration / tolerability boundaries.
- It does not establish healthy-adult cognition benefit or long-term safety beyond the studied windows.
