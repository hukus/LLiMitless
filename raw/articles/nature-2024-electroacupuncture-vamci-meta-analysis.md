# Meta-analysis with trial sequential analysis investigating the impact of adjunctive electroacupuncture therapy on vascular mild cognitive impairment

- Published: 2024-08-30
- Journal: Translational Psychiatry
- DOI: 10.1038/s41398-024-03052-1
- Evidence layer: systematic review / meta-analysis / trial sequential analysis

## Summary

This review evaluated electroacupuncture as an adjunct to conventional therapy for vascular mild cognitive impairment. The search covered eight databases through 2023-08-31. Fifteen RCTs with 1,033 participants were included. Pooled results favored adjunctive electroacupuncture for MoCA (overall SMD 0.72), MMSE (overall SMD 0.73), and ADL (SMD 0.83) versus conventional therapy.

The review also noted important limitations: bias concerns, limited clarity in randomization and allocation concealment, limited treatment durations, and heterogeneous protocols for point selection, stimulation, needle retention time, and treatment duration. This makes the branch interesting for vascular/MCI rehabilitation, but not a general healthy-adult enhancer.
