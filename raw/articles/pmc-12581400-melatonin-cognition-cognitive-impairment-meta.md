# Effect of melatonin on cognitive function in adults with cognitive impairment: a multi-dimensional meta-analysis of randomized trials

- PMID: 41185054
- PMCID: PMC12581400
- DOI: 10.1186/s13195-025-01881-w
- Published: 2025-11-03
- Source type: systematic review and meta-analysis of randomized trials
- Population: adults with MCI, Alzheimer disease, dementia, or mixed cognitive impairment
- Ingested: 2026-05-03

## Summary

Open-access 2025 review including 8 randomized trials and 518 participants. Pooled MMSE results favored melatonin with mean difference 1.08, while ADAS-Cog did not significantly improve. Subgroup findings were strongest for 13-24 week duration, evening administration around 20:30-21:00, and MCI rather than established Alzheimer disease or mixed dementia.

## Notes

- This source upgrades melatonin from a nutrient-map placeholder to a low-confidence entity candidate, but only for cognitive-impairment phenotypes.
- The same paper contains the main downgrade: small samples, heterogeneous duration/timing/dose, MMSE-driven signal, and no ADAS-Cog benefit.
- Do not transfer the MCI subgroup signal to healthy users, shift workers, or acute study-focus claims.
