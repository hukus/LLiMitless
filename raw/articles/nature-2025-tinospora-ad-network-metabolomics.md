# Network pharmacology and metabolomics analysis of Tinospora cordifolia reveals BACE1 and MAOB as potential therapeutic targets for neuroprotection in Alzheimer's disease

- PMID: 40057579
- Published: 2025-03-08
- Journal: Scientific Reports
- DOI: 10.1038/s41598-025-92756-5
- Evidence layer: metabolomics / network pharmacology / target hypothesis

## Summary

This Scientific Reports paper used LC-MS/MS untargeted metabolomics plus network pharmacology to map Tinospora cordifolia stem powder metabolites against Alzheimer's disease target biology. The LC-MS/MS analysis identified 1,186 metabolites, and network analysis mapped 591 enriched human proteins with pathways including cAMP, mTOR, MAPK, PI3K-Akt, apoptosis, and cholinergic, dopaminergic, serotonergic, and glutamatergic synapse pathways. Docking suggested Tinospora metabolites could interact with BACE1 and MAO-B.

This is a target-generation source, not a human cognition trial. It supports tracking Tinospora/Guduchi as a neuroprotection hypothesis, but it does not establish brain-fog or cognition benefit in healthy adults.
