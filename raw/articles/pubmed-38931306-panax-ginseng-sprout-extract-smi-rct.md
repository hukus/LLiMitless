# Efficacy and Safety of Panax ginseng Sprout Extract in Subjective Memory Impairment: A Randomized, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled Clinical Trial

- PMID: 38931306
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38931306/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

2024 randomized trial of a standardized sprout ginseng product (ThinkGIN™) in 80 adults with subjective memory impairment. At 12 weeks, the active arm improved verbal learning, figure recall, MoCA-K, sleep quality, and acetylcholinesterase versus placebo, with no clinically meaningful safety signal reported.
