# Phenibut in Dietary Supplements

- Source page: https://www.fda.gov/food/information-select-dietary-supplement-ingredients-and-other-substances/phenibut-dietary-supplements
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

FDA says phenibut does not meet the statutory definition of a dietary ingredient under the FD&C Act, so any dietary supplements listing it as an ingredient are misbranded. The page also lists common aliases and notes FDA warning letters and enforcement action against products containing phenibut.
