# A toxicological evaluation of lithium orotate

- Journal: Regulatory Toxicology and Pharmacology (2021)
- Source URL: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0273230021001136
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

The preclinical package reported no in vitro or in vivo genotoxicity and no treatment-related adverse effects in a 28-day rat study up to the highest tested dose. The paper is supportive of a lack of an obvious short-term animal toxicology signal, but it does not establish long-term human safety or efficacy for cognition.
