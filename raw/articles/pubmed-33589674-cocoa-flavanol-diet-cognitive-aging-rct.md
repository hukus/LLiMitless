# Insights Into the Role of Diet and Dietary Flavanols in Cognitive Aging

- PMID: 33589674
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33589674/
- Ingested: 2026-05-03

## Summary

Randomized controlled parallel-arm dietary intervention in 211 healthy adults aged 50-75 years. Participants received placebo or 260, 510, or 770 mg/day cocoa flavanols for 12 weeks, followed by 8 weeks washout. Outcomes included an object-recognition task localized to dentate gyrus function, list learning, list sorting, diet quality, a flavanol-intake biomarker, and an MRI substudy of hippocampal cerebral blood volume.

Object-recognition and list-sorting performance did not correlate with baseline diet quality and did not improve after flavanol intake. Hippocampal-dependent list-learning performance was associated with baseline diet quality and improved after flavanol intake, especially in participants with the lowest baseline diet quality.

Use in wiki: diet-quality responder signal. Cocoa flavanols may matter more as a low-flavanol-diet correction than as broad enhancement.
