# DailyMed Qsymia phentermine/topiramate cognitive impairment label

- Source: DailyMed PDF label for QSYMIA, phentermine and topiramate extended-release capsules
- Revision captured: 2025-09 label shown by DailyMed

## Captured takeaways

- Qsymia combines phentermine with topiramate extended release and is labeled for chronic weight management rather than cognitive enhancement.
- The label warns that Qsymia may cause cognitive impairment, including disturbances in attention, memory, or speech/language problems.
- Patients are cautioned about operating automobiles or hazardous machinery when starting treatment.
- Adult one-year controlled trials reported cognitive-related adverse reactions in 5.0% of Qsymia 7.5 mg/46 mg and 7.6% of Qsymia 15 mg/92 mg users, compared with 1.5% for placebo.
- These cognitive adverse reactions were mainly attention/concentration, memory, and language / word-finding problems.
- Other relevant warnings include mood and sleep disorders, metabolic acidosis, decreased renal function, seizure risk with abrupt withdrawal, kidney stones, hypokalemia, increased creatinine, and hyperammonemia with or without encephalopathy from topiramate.
- Interpretation for the wiki: phentermine anecdotes must not be generalized to phentermine/topiramate, because topiramate adds a direct cognitive adverse-event gate.
