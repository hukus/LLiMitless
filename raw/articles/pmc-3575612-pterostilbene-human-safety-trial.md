# Human safety trial of pterostilbene

- Source type: PMC full-text randomized double-blind placebo-controlled human trial.
- PMID: 23431291
- PMCID: PMC3575612
- ClinicalTrials.gov: NCT01267227
- Date checked: 2026-05-04.

Eighty adults with hypercholesterolemia were randomized to pterostilbene 125 mg twice daily, pterostilbene 50 mg twice daily, pterostilbene 50 mg plus grape extract 100 mg twice daily, or placebo for 6-8 weeks. Safety markers included biochemical and subjective measures. The study reported no adverse drug reactions on liver, kidney, or glucose markers, no statistically significant self-reported or major adverse drug reactions, and concluded that pterostilbene was generally safe up to 250 mg/day in this short-term setting.

Use in the wiki: human safety and product-dose anchor only. This trial did not test cognition and should not upgrade pterostilbene's medicinal cognition signal.
