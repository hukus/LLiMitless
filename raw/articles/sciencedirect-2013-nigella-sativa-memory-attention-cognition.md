# Nigella sativa seed and memory, attention, and cognition in healthy volunteers

2013 Journal of Ethnopharmacology randomized placebo-controlled study of Nigella sativa seed in healthy elderly volunteers. Forty older volunteers were divided into active and placebo groups; the active group received 500 mg Nigella sativa capsules twice daily for nine weeks.

The abstract describes neuropsychological testing across logical memory, digit span, Rey-Osterrieth complex figure, letter cancellation, trail-making, and Stroop tasks, with safety labs for cardiac, liver, and kidney markers. Search-result summaries report improvements in several memory, attention, and executive-function tasks.

Relevance: black seed / thymoquinone has a small direct human cognition signal but remains low-confidence until replicated and product-normalized.
