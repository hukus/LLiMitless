# A 2-Period Crossover Study of BPN14770 in Adult Males With Fragile X Syndrome

- ClinicalTrials.gov ID: NCT03569631
- Study link: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03569631?tab=results
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

Completed phase 2 crossover trial of the PDE4D inhibitor BPN14770 in adult males with fragile X syndrome. The posted results show a positive language-and-function signal with good tolerability, making this a real human cognition win for a PDE4D-selective strategy rather than just roflumilast.
The ClinicalTrials.gov results table reports a small crystallized-cognition advantage, with NIH Toolbox Crystallized Cognition Composite change of 0.1 in the BPN14770 arm versus -0.9 on placebo, plus VAS daily functioning change of 9.4 versus 6.8 and VAS language change of 18.2 versus 15.4.

## Key points

- Design: 24-week randomized, placebo-controlled two-period crossover.
- Population: 30 adult males with fragile X syndrome.
- Dose: BPN14770 25 mg twice daily.
- Cognitive signal: oral reading recognition, picture vocabulary, and crystallized cognition improved versus placebo.
- Functional signal: caregiver-rated language and daily functioning improved.
- Safety: study met the primary tolerability objective with no meaningful active-versus-placebo difference.
- Registry outcome table: the NIH-TCB CCC change was 0.1 vs -0.9; VAS daily functioning was 9.4 vs 6.8; VAS language was 18.2 vs 15.4.
- Adverse events: 16.7% vs 20.0% overall AEs, 3.3% vs 0% SAEs, and no deaths.
- Status: results were posted to ClinicalTrials.gov in December 2024.
