# Safety and efficacy of Citrus aurantium and p-synephrine

2022 systematic review and meta-analysis of Citrus aurantium / bitter orange extracts and p-synephrine. The paper includes double-blind prospective, crossover, parallel, within-subject, and counterbalanced trials, mostly for weight loss, performance, body composition, metabolism, and safety endpoints.

The review concludes that evidence for beneficial effects is inconclusive and that synephrine can influence blood pressure and heart rate. This is not direct cognition evidence.

Relevance: safety and efficacy anchor for the synephrine branch.
