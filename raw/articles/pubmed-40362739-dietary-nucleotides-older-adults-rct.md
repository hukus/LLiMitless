# Dietary Nucleotides Enhance Neurogenesis, Cognitive Capacity, Muscle Function, and Body Composition in Older Adults

- PMID: 40362739
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40362739/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

Randomized, triple-blind controlled trial in 69 physically independent older adults over 10 weeks. Two nucleotide formulations were compared with placebo while spontaneous physical activity continued. Both nucleotide groups improved Trail Making Test B, physical performance, oxidative-stress biomarkers, inflammatory cytokines, and quality-of-life measures versus placebo.
