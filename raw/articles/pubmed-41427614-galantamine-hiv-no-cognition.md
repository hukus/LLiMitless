# Galantamine for 12 weeks does not improve neurocognition or immune activation in ART-suppressed people with HIV

- PMID: 41427614
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41427614/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

In ART-suppressed people with HIV, 12 weeks of galantamine did not improve composite neurocognition versus placebo, including in smokers. The trial did move one monocyte marker and increased several pro-inflammatory cytokines, which weakens the idea that cholinergic boosting reliably translates into cognitive or anti-inflammatory benefit in this phenotype.
