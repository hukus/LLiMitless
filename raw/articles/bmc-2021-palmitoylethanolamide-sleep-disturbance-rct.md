# Source Digest

Type: open-access double-blind randomized placebo-controlled interventional study.

Publication date: 2021-09-10.

Key extraction:
- Intervention: 350 mg Levagen+ palmitoylethanolamide nightly for 8 weeks.
- Population: adults with disturbed sleeping pattern; 103 completed the study.
- Main sleep outcome: after 8 weeks both groups improved sleep quality/quantity similarly, and there was no between-group difference on sleep quantity or quality by actigraphy or sleep diaries.
- Subgroup outcome: among participants with sleep-onset latency above 10 minutes at baseline, PEA reduced sleep-onset latency at weeks 4 and 8.
- Cognitive-on-waking outcome: PEA reduced time to feel completely awake and improved the sleep-inertia questionnaire cognition domain at week 8.
- Safety/tolerability: six participants dropped out due to adverse events, two in PEA and four in placebo. Reported PEA-group dropout reasons were loss of taste and reduced sleep quality; placebo dropout reasons included stomach pain, diarrhea, dizziness, and worse sleep.
- Limitation: the COVID-19 pandemic limited blood sampling; the study was underpowered for inflammatory and biochemical markers.

Wiki signal:
- Medicinal signal: low for sleep-inertia and cognition-on-waking support, not direct broad cognition enhancement.
- Confidence limiter: sleep-primary study, mixed sleep outcomes, product-specific formulation, and self-report sleep-inertia domain rather than a full objective cognitive battery.
