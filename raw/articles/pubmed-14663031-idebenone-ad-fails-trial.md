# Idebenone treatment fails to slow cognitive decline in Alzheimer's disease

PubMed-indexed multicenter randomized placebo-controlled trial of idebenone in probable Alzheimer's disease. The study enrolled 536 subjects and compared idebenone 120, 240, or 360 mg three times daily against placebo for one year.

The title and abstract framing report a failure to slow cognitive decline in AD. For the wiki, this later negative result prevents older positive idebenone trials from being treated as a clean cognition-efficacy packet.

Relevance: negative clinical anchor for idebenone and for the broader redox/mitochondrial analog branch.
