# Life extension factor klotho enhances cognition

- PMID: 24813892
- PMC: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4176932/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Foundational 2014 human-plus-mouse paper. The KL-VS variant was associated with enhanced cognition in heterozygous carriers across three aging cohorts without dementia, and systemic klotho overexpression improved learning, memory, LTP, and synaptic GluN2B in mice. The paper supports klotho as a plausible resilience target, but not as proof of a clinically deployable intervention.
