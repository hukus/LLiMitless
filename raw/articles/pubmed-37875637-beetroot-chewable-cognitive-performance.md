# Acute effects of a chewable beetroot-based supplement on cognitive performance: a double-blind randomized placebo-controlled crossover clinical trial

- PMID: 37875637
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37875637/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary
Acute chewable beetroot supplement in healthy adults improved immediate and delayed verbal recall, frontal-lobe function, and cognitive flexibility after 90 min. Working memory and processing speed did not change. This is one of the clearest direct nitrate cognition signals but remains a single small crossover trial.
