# Ladasten dopamine / serotonin mechanism study

- PMID: 20369592
- Rat study of receptor and monoamine effects.
- No direct effect was found on dopamine D1, D2, or D3 receptors, or on serotonin 5-HT1A / 5-HT2A receptors in vitro.
- A single 50 mg/kg dose stimulated ex vivo dopamine biosynthesis and release in striatum, without a serotonergic biosynthesis change.
- Both ladasten and sydnocarb inhibited dopamine transport in striatal synaptosomes, with ladasten far weaker than sydnocarb.
- Best read: mechanism claims are mainly preclinical and indirect, so human dopamine-proof language would be overstated.
