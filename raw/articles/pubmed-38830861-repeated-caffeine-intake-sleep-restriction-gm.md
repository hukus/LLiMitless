# Repeated caffeine intake suppresses cerebral grey matter responses to chronic sleep restriction in an A1 adenosine receptor-dependent manner

- PMID: 38830861
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38830861/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Double-blind randomized controlled study in 36 healthy adults undergoing 5-day chronic sleep restriction. Daily caffeine changed grey matter responses and the effect depended on ADORA1/adenosine-receptor biology. The paper is relevant to anti-tolerance discussions because it shows repeated caffeine does not simply act as an unchanged acute enhancer across days of exposure.

## Notes

Mechanistic rather than a cognition-only paper, but it is a good reminder that chronic caffeine exposure can reshape the state being measured.
