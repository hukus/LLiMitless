# Lactate uptake by the injured human brain: evidence from an arteriovenous gradient and cerebral microdialysis study

- PMID: 23968221
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23968221/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

TBI microdialysis/AV-gradient study showing that the injured human brain sometimes takes up lactate rather than exporting it, most often several days after injury. This supports state-dependent lactate uptake in human brain tissue.
