# Acute administration of roflumilast enhances immediate recall of verbal word memory in healthy young adults

- PMID: 29241652
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29241652/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

2018 randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled crossover study in healthy young adults. Single-dose roflumilast improved immediate recall of verbal word memory, with the clearest signal at 250 micrograms, but the study was small and narrow in scope.

## Key points

- Design: healthy-volunteer crossover with placebo and acute roflumilast dosing.
- Outcome: verbal word-memory immediate recall, not a broad cognition battery.
- Signal: positive on verbal recall; no strong generalization to working memory or executive function.
- Safety: acute tolerability was acceptable in this small study, but the class remains dose-sensitive.
