# Effects of 90 days of resveratrol supplementation on cognitive function in elders

- Source type: open-access randomized placebo-controlled pilot study.
- Publication: Journal of Alternative and Complementary Medicine, 2018.
- Date checked: 2026-05-03.

This phase IIa pilot randomized sedentary overweight older adults to placebo, 300 mg/day resveratrol, or 1000 mg/day resveratrol for 90 days. The cognitive battery included Trail Making, digit span, Eriksen-Flanker, word fluency, verbal learning, and task switching.

The 1000 mg/day group improved on Trail Making Test part A psychomotor speed versus the 300 mg/day and placebo groups, but other cognitive domains were not significantly affected.

Use in the wiki: modest resveratrol human signal. It should be treated as narrow and pilot-level rather than broad enhancement proof.
