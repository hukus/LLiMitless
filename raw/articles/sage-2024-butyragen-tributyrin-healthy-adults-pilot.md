# ButyraGen tributyrin pilot in healthy adults

- Published: 2024
- Source type: single-blind randomized pilot human study
- Checked: 2026-05-11
- Population: healthy adults; 29 randomized, 24 completed

## Summary

This pilot tested a tributyrin complex, ButyraGen, in healthy adults. Participants first took placebo capsules for 7 days, then 200 mg or 400 mg ButyraGen daily for 21 days. The study focused on tolerability, microbiome composition, gut permeability, inflammatory markers, metabolic markers, and SCFA concentrations.

The product was generally tolerated, with fewer than 10% reporting gastrointestinal discomfort. The paper reported decreased fecal acetate and propionate after supplementation, a triglyceride increase in the 400 mg arm, and trend-level decreases in hs-CRP and glucose for selected dose comparisons. No cognition or brain-fog endpoint was tested.

Use this as a modern healthy-adult tributyrin safety/biomarker pilot, not evidence of cognitive benefit.

## Notes

- The study's own interpretation was that biological effects suggest butyrate generation and absorption in the small intestine with possible liver activity.
- Product identity matters: ButyraGen is a specific tributyrin complex, not generic sodium butyrate.
