# MSM half-marathon oxidative stress / pain RCT

2017 double-blind randomized placebo-controlled trial of methylsulfonylmethane / MSM for exercise-induced oxidative stress, muscle damage, and pain after a half-marathon.

Key capture:

- Twenty-two healthy adult runners completed the study. The active group used 3 g/day MSM for 21 days before the race and two days afterward.
- The primary endpoint was the oxidative-stress marker 8-OHdG. Secondary outcomes included MDA, CK, LDH, muscle pain, and joint pain.
- The half-marathon increased oxidative-stress, muscle-damage, and pain markers, but time-by-treatment results did not reach statistical significance for any outcome.
- MSM showed clinically meaningful but not statistically significant reductions in muscle and joint pain.
- No cognitive endpoints were tested.

Wiki use: this keeps MSM in a recovery/redox/proxy lane rather than a cognition lane. For [[methylsulfonylmethane-msm]], it is a human RCT counterweight against extrapolating exercise, pain, or oxidative-stress claims into brain-fog relief.
