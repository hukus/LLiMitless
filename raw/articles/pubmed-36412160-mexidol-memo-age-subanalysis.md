# PubMed 36412160: Mexidol MEMO chronic cerebral ischemia age subanalysis

PubMed 36412160 is a 2022 English/Russian abstract titled `Efficacy of Mexidol in patients with chronic brain ischemia and cognitive impairment of different age groups (results of sub-analysis of the international multicenter, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled study of sequential therapy in patients with chronic brain ischemia MEMO)`.

The subanalysis used data from the MEMO randomized double-blind placebo-controlled sequential-therapy trial in chronic brain ischemia. It included 318 participants aged 40-90 years, subdivided into 40-60 years (n=163), 61-75 years (n=141), and 76-90 years (n=13). The primary efficacy endpoint was change in total MoCA score at day 75 versus pretreatment values; secondary endpoints included Digit Symbol Substitution Test, SF-36, MFI-20, Vane questionnaire, Beck anxiety scale, and Tinetti scale.

The abstract reports positive cognitive, emotional, and motor changes in both Mexidol and placebo groups for the 40-60 and 61-75 subgroups, with more prominent changes in the Mexidol group. The conclusion states that cognitive and motor function, quality of life, vegetative impairment, asthenia, and anxiety improved more with Mexidol than placebo after 75 days.

Interpretation for the wiki: this supports the MEMO clinical-state signal, but it is still chronic cerebral ischemia with cognitive impairment, not healthy-adult enhancement. The 76-90 subgroup is very small and should not be overread.
