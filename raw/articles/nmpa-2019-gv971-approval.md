# Treatment for Alzheimer's available by end of 2019

- Source: National Medical Products Administration news page republished from China Daily
- URL: https://english.nmpa.gov.cn/2019-11/04/c_422037.htm

## Summary

The NMPA page states that GV-971 was approved for market launch in China and would be available to Chinese patients by the end of 2019. The page says the drug is capable of treating mild-to-moderate AD and improving cognition, and notes the launch schedule in China. This is the clearest official approval statement in the sources I checked.

## Key Takeaways

- China approval is documented here.
- The page does not establish marketing approval outside China.
- For the wiki, this should be treated as geography-specific approval status, not global validation.
