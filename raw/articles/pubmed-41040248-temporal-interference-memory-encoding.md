# Temporal Interference Stimulation Can Enhance or Disrupt Human Memory Encoding as a Function of Brain Location and Frequency

- PMID: 41040248
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41040248/
- Preprint: bioRxiv 2025
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary
In 70 healthy participants, TI during figure-memory encoding was bidirectional rather than uniformly enhancing.
High-frequency envelopes (130 Hz offset) targeting bilateral hippocampi and temporal cortex impaired recall.
Low-frequency envelopes (5 Hz offset) targeting bilateral hippocampi improved recall.
Functional MRI also showed frequency-dependent differences in hippocampal BOLD, connectivity, DMN, and attention networks.

## Notes
This is the clearest current human cognition signal in the TI branch, but it is still preprint-level.
