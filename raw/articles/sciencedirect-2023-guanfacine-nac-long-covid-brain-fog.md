# Guanfacine plus NAC for long-COVID brain fog case series

2023 Neuroimmunology Reports open-label clinical-experience report on guanfacine plus N-acetylcysteine for long-COVID cognitive deficits. The authors framed long-COVID brain fog as executive-function and working-memory impairment tied to vulnerable prefrontal cortex circuits.

The treated case series included 12 patients with brain fog and executive-function difficulty. The protocol was guanfacine 1 mg by mouth at bedtime for the first month, increased to 2 mg after one month if tolerated, plus 600 mg NAC daily. Eight of 12 patients reported improved cognition, including working memory, concentration, executive function, and ability to resume normal workloads. Four discontinued or were lost to follow-up; two stopped because of hypotension and/or dizziness. One patient reported return of deficits after temporarily stopping guanfacine because of hypotension, with improvement after resuming.

Interpretation for the wiki: this is one of the most direct chemical signals for post-COVID brain fog, but it is uncontrolled, small, combination-treated, and guanfacine-side-effect limited. It should sit in a medical off-label / post-viral brain-fog lane, not in healthy-adult nootropic rankings.
