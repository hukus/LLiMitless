# Thymosin Beta 4 Protects Hippocampal Neuronal Cells against PrP (106-126) via Neurotrophic Factor Signaling

- PMID: 37175330
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37175330/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Cell-model work in hippocampal neuronal cells found thymosin beta-4 protection against PrP(106-126)-induced injury, with the mechanism linked to neurotrophic factor signaling.

## Notes

Useful as a mechanistic CNS signal, but still preclinical and indirect for cognition.
