# Apelin controls emotional behavior in age- and metabolic state-dependent manner

- PMID: 35305406
- DOI: 10.1016/j.psyneuen.2022.105711
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35305406/
- Ingested: 2026-05-04

## Summary

Psychoneuroendocrinology 2022 study combining older-adult association and mouse knockout work. Higher plasma apelin was associated with higher depressive symptoms in older adults. Twelve-month-old apelin knockout mice showed an antidepressant-like phenotype under standard diet, while high-fat-diet apelin knockout mice showed insulin resistance, anxiety-like behavior, and altered central serotonergic and dopaminergic activity; metformin reversed the anxiety phenotype without normalizing insulin resistance. This supports age- and metabolic-state-dependent affective biology, not a direct cognition-enhancement claim.
