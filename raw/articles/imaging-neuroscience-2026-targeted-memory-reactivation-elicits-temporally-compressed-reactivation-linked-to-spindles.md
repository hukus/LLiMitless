# Targeted Memory Reactivation Elicits Temporally Compressed Reactivation Linked to Spindles

- Imaging Neuroscience 2026; 48 participants.
- Design: slow-wave sleep TMR on a serial reaction time task, with wake/sleep-adapted classification of reactivation.
- Result: reactivation after TMR cues was detectable and occurred about 3 to 20 times faster than waking activity.
- Reactivation detectability correlated positively with memory improvement.
- Spindles were linked to reactivation, reinforcing the phase and timing dependence of cueing.
- Interpretation: useful mechanistic support for TMR, but still task-specific and not a broad cognition claim.
