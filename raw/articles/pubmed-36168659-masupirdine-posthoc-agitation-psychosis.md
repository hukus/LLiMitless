# Potential beneficial effects of masupirdine (SUVN-502) on agitation/aggression and psychosis in patients with moderate Alzheimer's disease: Exploratory post hoc analyses

- PMID: 36168659
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36168659/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

Post hoc subgroup analysis of the masupirdine phase 2 AD study focused on agitation/aggression and psychosis domains in patients with moderate AD. The original cognition trial was negative, but symptom-domain signals appeared in enriched subgroups. This is hypothesis-generating only and should not be treated as a validated cognition win.
