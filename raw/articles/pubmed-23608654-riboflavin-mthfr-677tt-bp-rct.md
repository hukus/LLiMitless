# Riboflavin MTHFR 677TT blood-pressure randomized trial

- PMID: 23608654
- Title: Blood pressure in treated hypertensive individuals with the MTHFR 677TT genotype is responsive to intervention with riboflavin
- Journal: Hypertension
- Publication date: 2013-06
- Source type: targeted randomized trial / genotype-status proxy
- Checked: 2026-05-16
- Ingested: 2026-05-16

## Summary

This targeted randomized trial identified 157 people with hypertension and the MTHFR 677TT genotype from a larger sample of 1,427 hypertensive patients. Ninety-one agreed to participate and were randomized to placebo or riboflavin 1.6 mg/day for 16 weeks.

Riboflavin improved riboflavin biomarker status. The study reported an overall systolic blood-pressure treatment effect of 5.6 +/- 2.6 mm Hg (p = 0.033), with systolic blood pressure falling in the riboflavin group and not improving in placebo. The diastolic blood-pressure effect was not significant.

## Wiki Use
