# From physical activity patterns to cognitive status: development and validation of novel digital biomarkers for cognitive assessment in older adults

- PMID: 39833903
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39833903/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

Large cross-cohort study using wrist accelerometry to derive digital biomarkers of cognitive status in older adults.

## Notes

- Data came from 3,363 older adults across NHATS and NHANES.
- The best explainable models reached AUC 0.84 and 0.80 in the two cohorts.
- Activity variability and rhythmicity features, especially change quantiles and FFT coefficients, were the most informative signals.
- This supports digital phenotype as a cognition-state marker, not as proof of intervention response.
