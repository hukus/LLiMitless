# High phenylalanine intake and cognition in adults with phenylketonuria

PubMed-indexed 2024 randomized controlled trial of high phenylalanine intake in adults with phenylketonuria. The study tested four weeks of oral phenylalanine administration versus placebo in early-treated adults with classical PKU, with cognition, mood, and depression outcomes.

This is not DLPA nootropic evidence. It is a cautionary human source showing that phenylalanine has clinically meaningful cognitive relevance in a vulnerable metabolic phenotype and that "more precursor" can be the wrong direction in the wrong person.

Relevance: phenylalanine / DLPA should stay low-confidence and phenotype-sensitive, especially for users with PKU risk or unknown metabolic status.
