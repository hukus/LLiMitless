# ADDF Cognitive Vitality fisetin summary

- Source type: Alzheimer's Drug Discovery Foundation Cognitive Vitality supplement summary.
- Page update noted by source: 2016-08-09.
- Date checked: 2026-05-03.

The ADDF Cognitive Vitality page describes fisetin as a dietary flavonol found in foods such as strawberries, apples, persimmons, onions, and cucumbers. It reports preclinical cognition / Alzheimer's-disease interest but no human clinical or observational evidence for cognition at the time of review.

Evidence readout: fewer than 10 preclinical studies were identified as suggesting benefit for cognition or Alzheimer's protection, with no human cognition data. Safety readout emphasizes that dietary fisetin is safe, while supplemental-dose human safety and long-term safety were not established.

Use in the wiki: conservative human-evidence governor for fisetin. It supports preclinical-watch status and blocks a cognition-efficacy upgrade.
