# Vitamin-mineral complex with guarana before fasted exercise

- Source type: placebo-controlled double-blind randomized balanced crossover trial.
- Publication: Nutrients, 2015.
- Date checked: 2026-05-03.

This study enrolled 40 active young men who consumed either a vitamin/mineral complex with guarana or placebo before a 30-minute morning run at 60% VO2max. Cognitive performance, affect, exertion, and substrate metabolism were measured.

The active product reduced rating of perceived exertion during exercise and improved selected cognitive measures: numeric working memory accuracy and picture-recognition speed. There were no significant effects on other cognitive or mood measures or on substrate metabolism.
