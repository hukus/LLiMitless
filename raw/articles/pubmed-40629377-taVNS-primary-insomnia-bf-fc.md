# Baseline functional connectivity of the basal forebrain-cortical circuit predict taVNS treatment response in primary insomnia: a randomized controlled trial and fMRI study

- PMID: 40629377
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40629377/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Randomized trial in 70 primary insomnia patients, with 67 completers, comparing 4 weeks of real taVNS with sham. Real taVNS reduced PSQI, ISI, and SAS scores more than sham and altered basal forebrain connectivity.

## Notes

- Baseline basal forebrain-visual connectivity predicted treatment response in the real-taVNS arm.
- The imaging signal matters because it points to a potential responder biomarker rather than a broad healthy-adult enhancer claim.
- This is disease-context evidence, but it strengthens the stress/sleep/responder branch of the taVNS map.
