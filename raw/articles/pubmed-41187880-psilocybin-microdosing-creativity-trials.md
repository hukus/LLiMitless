# Microdosing psilocybin and its effect on creativity: Lessons learned from three double-blind placebo controlled longitudinal trials

- PMID: 41187880
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41187880/
- Ingested: 2026-04-29

## Summary

2026 mega-analysis pooling three double-blind placebo-controlled longitudinal trials (N = 171). Active microdosing increased the originality/fluency ratio and unadjusted originality when relative dose was considered, but it did not improve other divergent-thinking scores or convergent thinking. The authors report that the effect survived controls for dose guess and demographic biases, so the signal is narrow rather than a general creativity boost.
