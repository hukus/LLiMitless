# Spearmint extract reactive-agility trial in healthy active adults

- Source type: randomized double-blind placebo-controlled parallel trial.
- Publication: Journal of the International Society of Sports Nutrition, 2018.
- Date checked: 2026-05-11.

This trial randomized 142 healthy recreationally active adults aged 18-50 years to 900 mg/day proprietary spearmint extract or placebo for 90 days. The product contained at least 14.5% rosmarinic acid and 24% total phenolics. Reactive agility was measured on the Makoto Arena II, an audio-visual choice-reaction device with stationary, lateral, and multidirectional tasks.

The trial reported an overall treatment effect for more hits on stationary footplate testing, with between-group differences at days 30 and 90. Average reaction time improved at day 7 and day 30 on the same task. Multidirectional footplate hits also improved at days 30 and 90. No effects were seen in several other Makoto tests. Blood safety panels did not show a concerning signal.

The authors emphasized that active choice-reaction tasks can differ from passive cognitive tests and may translate better to athletic settings. For the wiki, this trial is a psychomotor/attention bridge rather than proof of broad nootropic efficacy.
