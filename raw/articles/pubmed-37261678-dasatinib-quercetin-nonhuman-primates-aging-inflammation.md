# Long-term dasatinib plus quercetin effects on aging outcomes and inflammation in nonhuman primates

- PMID: 37261678
- PMCID: PMC10643765
- DOI: 10.1007/s11357-023-00830-5

## Summary

This was the first preclinical trial of intermittent dasatinib plus quercetin in a relevant middle-aged nonhuman primate model. The study focused on systemic aging, inflammaging, and metabolic health rather than cognition.

## Key Data

- Group sizes: D+Q n=9; vehicle n=7.
- Regimen: 5 mg/kg dasatinib + 50 mg/kg quercetin, 2 consecutive days monthly for 6 months.
- Add-on: 10% caloric restriction in month 6 for both groups.
- Findings: reduced senescence marker gene expression in adipose tissue, lower circulating PAI-1 and MMP-9, anti-inflammatory immune shifts, reduced microbial translocation biomarkers, improved blood urea nitrogen, and improved glycosylated hemoglobin with the CR interaction.

## Caveats

- No cognition endpoint was reported.
- This supports translational plausibility for inflammaging and metabolic effects, not direct cognitive benefit.
