# Epitalon protects against post-ovulatory aging-related damage of mouse oocytes in vitro

PubMed indexed a 2022 Aging paper showing that Epitalon reduced intracellular ROS, decreased spindle defects and abnormal cortical granule distribution, improved mitochondrial membrane potential and mtDNA copy number, and lowered apoptosis in post-ovulatory aging mouse oocytes in vitro.

For the wiki, this is another longevity/mechanism data point for Epitalon. It strengthens the antioxidant and mitochondrial-aging lane, but it does not create a human cognition endpoint or clinical safety signal.
