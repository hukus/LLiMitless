# Dihydrogen-PQQ mixture in MCI, 2024 RCT

2024 randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial in 34 elderly individuals with mild cognitive impairment. Intervention was a commercial dihydrogen-producing minerals plus PQQ mixture for 6 weeks.

Primary endpoint was serum BDNF; secondary outcomes included cognitive indices, brain metabolites, oxygenation, and side effects. Because the active product combined hydrogen and PQQ, attribution to PQQ alone is limited.

Use in wiki: supports PQQ as a low-confidence mitochondrial/redox product candidate, but route it as combination-product evidence rather than single-molecule proof.
