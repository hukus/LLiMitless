# Fosgonimeton in mild-to-moderate Alzheimer's disease

- Trial: LIFT-AD
- Evidence tier: human phase 2/3 randomized trial
- Registry: NCT04488419

## Summary

LIFT-AD was a randomized, placebo-controlled 26-week trial of subcutaneous fosgonimeton in mild-to-moderate AD. The primary analysis set included participants not receiving concomitant acetylcholinesterase inhibitors and compared fosgonimeton 40 mg daily with placebo.

The trial missed its primary Global Statistical Test and its secondary ADAS-Cog11 and ADCS-ADL23 endpoints. The published paper reports a small placebo-vs-treatment separation that did not reach statistical significance and discusses subgroup patterns, especially around disease severity and APOE4 status, as exploratory only.

## Safety

- Safety population included participants on 40 mg and 70 mg dosing.
- Fosgonimeton was generally well tolerated.
- Injection-site reactions drove more treatment-emergent adverse events, and the 70 mg arm had lower tolerability.
