# Thymosin beta 4 ophthalmic solution for dry eye: randomized phase II trial

- PMID: 26056426
- PMCID: PMC4445951
- DOI: 10.2147/OPTH.S80954
- Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4445951/
- Ingested: 2026-05-04

## Summary

Randomized, double-masked, placebo-controlled phase II trial of 0.1% thymosin beta 4 ophthalmic solution (RGN-259) in 72 subjects with moderate-to-severe dry eye using a controlled adverse environment model. The co-primary endpoints, ocular discomfort and inferior corneal staining at visit 5, did not significantly separate from placebo, but several secondary endpoints improved, including CAE discomfort on day 28 and central/superior corneal staining. No adverse events were reported in the enrolled subjects.

## Signal Use

- Medicinal signal: full-length/topical thymosin beta 4 has human ophthalmic exposure and some tissue-repair symptom/sign signals.
- Cognition signal: absent; dry-eye and corneal endpoints do not establish neurocognitive benefit.
- Safety signal: useful as route-specific topical ophthalmic tolerability context only, not as injected TB-500 fragment safety.
