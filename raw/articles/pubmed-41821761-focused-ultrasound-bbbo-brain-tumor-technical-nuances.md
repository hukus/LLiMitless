# Focused ultrasound for blood-brain barrier opening in brain tumor patients: Technical nuances

- PMID: 41821761
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41821761/
- PMC: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12978305/
- Published: 2026-01-31

## Summary

This 2026 Neuro-Oncology Advances paper is not a cognition study. It reports technical nuances from brain-tumor BBBO workflows: an optimized protocol improved patient comfort and reduced procedure times; a large treatment envelope with a 64-spot grid allowed near-total brain coverage; same-session MRI updates and in-transducer acquisition reduced transport burden; and the procedures completed without clinical complications. It is useful as a professional-workflow reference for BBBO implementation, not as evidence of cognitive benefit.
