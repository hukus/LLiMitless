# The effect of exogenous ketone bodies on cognition across health and disease: a systematic review and meta-analysis

- DOI: 10.3389/fnut.2026.1802531
- Published: 2026-04-15
- Study type: systematic review and meta-analysis
- Scope: 38 studies, 41 protocols, 1,602 participants; 29 protocols in meta-analysis

## Summary

2026 systematic review/meta-analysis found exogenous ketone bodies were associated with a modest but statistically significant improvement in cognition versus placebo (SMD 0.29, 95% CI 0.16-0.41). Meta-regression found a positive association with higher daily dose. Subgroup analyses did not show clear differences by ketone type, study duration, population type, or acute cognitive stressor status.

## Notes

This materially upgrades the ketone branch from a pure niche-state-rescue hypothesis to a small-but-real pooled human signal, but it does not erase the mixed trial record under hypoxia and repeated high-load exercise.
