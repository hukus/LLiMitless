# Bryostatin Treatment of Moderately Severe Alzheimer's Disease

- ClinicalTrials.gov ID: NCT04538066
- Sponsor: Neurotrope Bioscience, Inc.
- Study type: randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled phase 2
- Status: completed; results posted July 2024
- Enrollment: 122 randomized, 117 treated

## Summary

The registry describes a 122-participant bryostatin trial in moderately severe AD, with 20 ug IV dosing and a repeated-course design.

Design details:

- Participants were randomized to bryostatin 1 or placebo.
- The protocol used 7 infusions over the first 12 weeks, followed by a second identical course beginning 30 days later.
- The primary endpoint was SIB at Week 28 after both courses.
- The trial was run without memantine.

Result-status notes:

- ClinicalTrials.gov shows the study as completed with results posted.
- The associated journal report states that the FAS primary endpoint was not significant, while completer and exploratory analyses favored 20 ug bryostatin.
- Safety was broadly acceptable, but the 40 ug arm had more tolerability and dropout issues in the earlier paper.

Interpretation for the wiki:

- This is a mixed or partially positive trial, not a clean replicated win.
- The safest read is that bryostatin remains interesting for phenotype and dose exploration, but the branch should stay below high-confidence efficacy status.
