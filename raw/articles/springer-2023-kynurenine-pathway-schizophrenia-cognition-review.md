# Importance of the dysregulation of the kynurenine pathway on cognition in schizophrenia

- Journal: European Archives of Psychiatry and Clinical Neuroscience
- Publication date: 2023-12-02
- Study type: systematic review of clinical studies

## Captured takeaways

- Review concluded that kynurenine-pathway activation affects cognition in schizophrenia.
- The evidence was still limited and the authors called for more studies.
- Usefulness for the wiki: this is target-validity support, not proof of a broad cognitive enhancer.
- The branch remains most relevant for KYNA-sensitive memory, attention, and CBF hypotheses.
