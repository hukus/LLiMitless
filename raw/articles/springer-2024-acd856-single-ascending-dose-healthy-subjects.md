# ACD856 single ascending dose healthy-subject safety / PK study

2024 European Journal of Clinical Pharmacology open-access paper on ACD856, a novel positive allosteric modulator of Trk receptors.

## Captured takeaways

- The sponsor-developed NeuroRestore compound ACD856 is positioned for Alzheimer's disease, depression, psychiatric conditions, and disorders where cognition is impaired.
- Human work included a phase 0 intravenous microdose study with 0.100 mg ACD856 in six healthy male subjects and a randomized, placebo-controlled, double-blind phase I oral single-ascending-dose study from 1 to 150 mg in 56 healthy subjects.
- The primary evidence is safety, tolerability, and pharmacokinetics, not cognition efficacy.
- The paper reports ACD856 was well tolerated with no treatment-emergent or dose-related adverse events or other safety-signal trends in the captured abstract.
- Pharmacokinetic points: approximately 20-hour plasma half-life after microdose, rapid oral absorption, almost complete bioavailability, dose-proportional exposure, low impact of food on half-life and overall bioavailability, and no renal elimination of unchanged ACD856.
- Trial registrations listed in the source: NCT05783830 for the microdose study and NCT05077631 for the single ascending dose study.
