# ACD856 Trk-PAM neuroprotective / neuroplasticity preclinical paper

2023 International Journal of Molecular Sciences paper characterizing triazinetrione ACD856 as a pan-Trk positive allosteric modulator in preclinical systems.

## Captured takeaways

- The study tested ACD856 in recombinant cell lines, primary cortical neurons, and animals.
- Reported biology includes enhanced NGF-induced neurite outgrowth, increased SNAP25 in PC12 cells, increased phosphorylated TrkB in SH-SY5Y cells, increased phospho-ERK1/2 in primary cortical neurons, neuroprotection against amyloid-beta or energy-deprivation-induced neurotoxicity, and increased BDNF levels.
- In old mice, ACD856 administration reportedly increased brain BDNF levels.
- Repeated administration produced a sustained antidepressant-like effect in the reported animal model.
- The paper frames ACD856 as a cognitive enhancer and potential disease-modifying AD candidate, but the evidence is preclinical and sponsor-linked.
- Conflict statement: the authors were employed by AlzeCure Pharma AB at the time of the experiments.
