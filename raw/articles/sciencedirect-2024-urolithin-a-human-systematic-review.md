# Targeting aging with urolithin A in humans: A systematic review

- DOI: 10.1016/j.arr.2024.102406
- PMID: 39002645
- Journal: Ageing Research Reviews
- Ingested: 2026-05-03

## Summary

2024 systematic review of human urolithin A intervention studies. The review summarizes five studies including 250 healthy individuals, with doses from 10 to 1000 mg/day and durations from 28 days to 4 months.

The review reports dose-dependent anti-inflammatory effects and upregulation of some mitochondrial genes, autophagy markers, and fatty-acid oxidation markers. It also reports increased muscle strength and endurance. It did not find effects on mitochondrial maximal ATP production, biogenesis, dynamics, gut microbiota composition, anthropometrics, cardiovascular outcomes, or physical function. Adverse events judged unrelated were mild or moderate, and the authors call for broader and longer studies.

Use in the wiki: summary-level human geroprotector/proxy evidence. It strengthens the classification of urolithin A as a human mitochondrial / muscle proxy candidate, but it does not establish cognitive benefit.
