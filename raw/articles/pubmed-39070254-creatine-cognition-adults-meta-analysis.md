# The effects of creatine supplementation on cognitive function in adults: a systematic review and meta-analysis.

- PMID: 39070254
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39070254/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

2024 meta-analysis of 16 RCTs (492 participants, age range 20.8-76.4) found creatine monohydrate improved memory, attention time, and processing-speed time, but not overall cognition or executive function.

## Notes

Signal looked stronger in diseases, in ages 18-60, and in females; certainty was moderate for memory and low-to-moderate for the other domains.
