# PubChem 2026 N,N-dimethylglycine identity note

PubChem identifies N,N-dimethylglycine as compound CID 673 with formula C4H9NO2 and molecular weight about 103.12 g/mol. It is glycine carrying two N-methyl substituents and is also described as dimethylglycine, DMG, N-methylsarcosine, or 2-(dimethylamino)acetic acid.
