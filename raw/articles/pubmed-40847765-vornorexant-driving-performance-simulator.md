# Driving performance in the morning after bedtime vornorexant administration

- PMID: 40847765
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40847765/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Randomized crossover driving-simulator trial in healthy Japanese participants. Bedtime vornorexant at 10 mg and 20 mg did not produce clinically meaningful next-morning driving impairment versus placebo at the tested doses and timing.
