# Sleep deprivation: sensitivity comparison between a visuomotor tracking task and the psychomotor vigilance test (PVT)

- PMID: 41999492
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41999492/
- Preprint: 2025 DOI 10.21203/rs.3.rs-7231113/v1
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

2026 paper in 25 healthy young adults comparing a continuous visuomotor tracking task with a 10-minute PVT after total sleep deprivation. Both tasks detected decline, but the PVT was more sensitive overall; tracking sensitivity looked limited to severe sleep-loss states. This reinforces PVT as the arousal anchor and tracking as a supplementary resilience assay rather than a replacement.

## Notes

Useful caution that continuous monitoring tasks may capture a different slice of impairment, but they do not automatically supersede PVT for sleep-loss sensitivity.
