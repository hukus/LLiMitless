# Safety of creatine supplementation: analysis of the prevalence of reported side effects in clinical trials and adverse event reports

- PMID: 40198156
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40198156/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

2025 review of reported side effects in creatine clinical trials and adverse-event reports. The abstract states creatine is generally well tolerated and that clinically significant adverse effects are not supported by the available report set.

## Notes

Useful for separating common anecdotal complaints from a true safety signal.
