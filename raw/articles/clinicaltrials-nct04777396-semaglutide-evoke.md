# NCT04777396: Semaglutide in Early Alzheimer's Disease (EVOKE)

- ClinicalTrials.gov ID: NCT04777396
- Sponsor: Novo Nordisk A/S
- Status: Completed
- Last update posted: 2026-04-09
- Results posted: No

## Summary

Phase 3 placebo-controlled study of oral semaglutide in early symptomatic AD. The registry lists CDR-SB change at week 104 as the primary endpoint and shows no posted results.

## Notes

The paper later reported a negative primary endpoint and stated that both EVOKE trials were discontinued because of negative clinical outcome.
