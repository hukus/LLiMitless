# Caffeine, TeaCrine, and Dynamine in adult male egamers

PubMed-indexed randomized crossover study in 50 young male egamers comparing caffeine 125 mg, caffeine 125 mg plus Dynamine 75 mg plus TeaCrine 50 mg, and placebo. The study tested cognitive performance, reaction time, mood, arousal biomarkers, EEG power, and CYP1A2 genotype context.

The paper reports that the caffeine + Dynamine + TeaCrine stack improved cognitive performance and reaction time without interfering with mood, but the design does not isolate methylliberine from caffeine and theacrine.

Use in the wiki: stack-context support and e-gamer performance visibility. Do not count it as methylliberine monotherapy cognition proof.
