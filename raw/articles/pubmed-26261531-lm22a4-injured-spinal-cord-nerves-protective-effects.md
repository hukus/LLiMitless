# Protective effects of LM22A-4 on injured spinal cord nerves

- PMID: 26261531
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26261531/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Notes

2015 mouse spinal-cord injury study. LM22A-4 was given systemically after injury and was associated with reduced tissue injury, lower apoptosis markers, and better recovery of limb function in the animal model.

## Relevance

- Adds a second LM22A-4 preclinical rescue model outside the oral-dentistry literature.
- Still not human cognition evidence.
- Useful for the rescue-versus-enhancement distinction in the wiki.
