# Nefopam abuse case series

- Source: Villier and Mallaret, Annals of Pharmacotherapy 2002.
- The indexed abstract reports three patients who abused nefopam.
- It describes nefopam as a central analgesic that inhibits serotonin, norepinephrine, and dopamine reuptake.
- Interpretation for the wiki: this source supports keeping nefopam in the SNDRI / triple-reuptake-adjacent safety map, but as an abuse/safety comparator rather than a cognition lead.
