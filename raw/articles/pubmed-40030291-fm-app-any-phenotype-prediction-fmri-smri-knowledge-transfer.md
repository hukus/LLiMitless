# FM-APP: Foundation Model for Any Phenotype Prediction via fMRI to sMRI Knowledge Transfer

PMID: 40030291
Published: 2025-10
Journal: IEEE Transactions on Medical Imaging 44(10):4010-4022
Authors: Zhibin He, Wuyang Li, Yifan Liu, Xinyu Liu, Junwei Han, Tuo Zhang, Yixuan Yuan

Abstract: Predicting individual-level non-neuroimaging phenotypes using brain imaging data is a fundamental goal of neuroscience. Existing methods often focus only on seen phenotypes and do not support zero-shot inference for unseen phenotypes. They also miss the opportunity to transfer information from fMRI to cost-effective structural MRI. To address this, FM-APP combines a phenotype text memory bank, an any-phenotype prediction module, and an fMRI-to-sMRI knowledge-transfer module. The model generates regressor parameters rather than relying on standard fine-tuning, and experiments on HCP and HCP Aging show strong zero-shot inference and improved prediction on downstream phenotypes.

Research note: This is highly relevant to the wiki's responder-biomarker question because it frames foundation models as phenotype predictors and cost-transfer systems rather than generic classifiers.
