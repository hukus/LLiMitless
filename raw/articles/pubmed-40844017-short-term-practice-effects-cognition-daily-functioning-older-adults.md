# Short-term practice effects on measures of cognition and daily functioning in older adults

- PMID: 40844017
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40844017/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

2025 study in 194 older adults showing significant one-week practice gains on neuropsychological scores, especially memory tests, plus gains on functional measures. The practical takeaway is that short-block protocols need a stabilization phase even when the outcome is not a pure memory test.

## Notes

Delayed recall improved by about 29-34% across one week in the sample, so block-level change over short intervals can reflect repeated exposure rather than intervention effect.
