# Relationship of Klotho with cognition and dementia: A systematic review and meta-analysis

- PMID: 40048920
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40048920/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

2025 systematic review/meta-analysis pooling 8 studies and 6645 subjects. Higher Klotho levels were associated with better cognitive scores and dementia-related outcomes, but the evidence base was mostly observational, heterogenous, and included only two small single-center RCTs with unclear risk of bias.
