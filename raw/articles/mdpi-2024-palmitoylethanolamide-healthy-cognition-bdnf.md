# Source Digest

Type: peer-reviewed human randomized double-blind placebo-controlled crossover trial.

Publication date: 2024-02-08.

Key extraction:
- Intervention: formulated palmitoylethanolamide, Levagen+, 700 mg/day for 6 weeks versus placebo.
- Population: 39 healthy young adult university students; exclusions included chronic illness, neurological disorder, smoking, medication, supplement use, obesity, pregnancy, and high alcohol intake.
- Cognitive battery: CANTAB Core Cognition, including Rapid Visual Information Processing, Paired Associates Learning, and Spatial Working Memory.
- Primary wiki-relevant result: serum BDNF increased versus placebo with p = 0.0057 and d = 0.62.
- Memory result: Paired Associates Learning showed fewer total errors with p = 0.0287 and d = -0.47, and a first-attempt success result with d = 0.54 but p = 0.142.
- Interpretation: direct healthy-adult memory signal exists, but the trial is small, product-specific, short, and not a broad cognition-domain win.
- Dose/product caveat: the tested material was Levagen+, not all bulk or generic palmitoylethanolamide products.

Wiki signal:
- Medicinal signal: low-moderate for formulated PEA as a memory/BDNF candidate in healthy young adults.
- Confidence limiter: single small trial, narrow memory endpoint, likely product-identity dependence, and no durable or independent replication captured yet.
