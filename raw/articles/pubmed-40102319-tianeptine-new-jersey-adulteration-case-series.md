# An Outbreak of Synthetic Cannabinoid-Adulterated Tianeptine Products in New Jersey - Case Series

- PMID: 40102319
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40102319/
- Ingested: 2026-04-30

## Summary

Case series describing a New Jersey outbreak involving tianeptine products adulterated with synthetic cannabinoids. The relevance here is product-identity and contamination risk: even when consumers think they are buying tianeptine, the actual exposure may include other psychoactive ingredients that change both toxicity and interpretation of self-reported effects.
