# Dose-response of paraxanthine on cognitive function

2021 double-blind, placebo-controlled, crossover, counterbalanced dose-response study of paraxanthine in 12 healthy adults. Doses of 50 mg, 100 mg, and 200 mg ENFINITY paraxanthine were compared with placebo across acute and 7-day repeated-dose testing.

The trial reports cognition, executive-function, memory, reaction-time, side-effect, blood-marker, clinical chemistry, and hemodynamic endpoints. The abstract reports that acute 100 mg and 200 mg paraxanthine affected selected measures of cognition, memory, reasoning, response time, and sustained attention, and that acute plus daily 7-day ingestion did not show clinically significant side effects in the tested panel.

Use in the wiki: direct human cognition signal, but small, short, and product/sponsor-sensitive. It supports paraxanthine as a low-confidence caffeine-adjacent candidate, not as a caffeine benchmark replacement.
