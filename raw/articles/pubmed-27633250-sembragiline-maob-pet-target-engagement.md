# PET measurement of brain MAO-B inhibition after sembragiline

- PMID: 27633250
- PMCID: PMC5281649
- DOI: 10.1007/s00259-016-3510-6
- Journal: European Journal of Nuclear Medicine and Molecular Imaging (2017)

## Captured takeaways

- PET with [11C]-L-deprenyl-D2 measured brain MAO-B inhibition in Alzheimer's disease patients and elderly controls after oral sembragiline.
- The study included 10 AD patients and 6 healthy elderly controls.
- Treatment duration was 6 to 15 days.
- Estimated maximum inhibition was approximately 80% to 90% across brain regions.
- Estimated EC50 was approximately 1 to 2 ng/mL.
- Near-maximal inhibition was achieved with 1 mg daily dosing.
- Interpretation for the wiki: sembragiline reached the target, so the later phase 2 cognition miss is a translational efficacy problem rather than simply lack of MAO-B engagement.
