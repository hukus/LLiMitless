# The TREM2 agonistic antibody AL002 in early Alzheimer's disease: a phase 2 randomized trial

Published: 2026-03-05 in Nature Medicine.

DOI: 10.1038/s41591-026-04273-1.

PubMed: 41787076.

Study type:
- Phase 2 randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial in early Alzheimer's disease.
- AL002 is a humanized TREM2 agonistic monoclonal antibody.
- Participants were assigned to AL002 15 mg/kg, 40 mg/kg, 60 mg/kg, or placebo by IV infusion every 4 weeks for 48-96 weeks.
- ClinicalTrials.gov identifier: NCT04592874.

Key result:
- AL002 produced target engagement and pharmacodynamic movement in the central nervous system.
- The reported pharmacodynamic pattern included lower CSF soluble TREM2 and higher CSF osteopontin.
- The study did not meet the primary endpoint: change from baseline in Clinical Dementia Rating-Sum of Boxes versus placebo.
- Week 96 least-squares mean differences versus placebo on CDR-SB were:
  - 15 mg/kg: -0.31, 95% CI -1.61 to 0.98.
  - 40 mg/kg: 0.13, 95% CI -1.18 to 1.43.
  - 60 mg/kg: -0.17, 95% CI -1.49 to 1.15.
  - All p values were greater than 0.05.

Safety / adverse events:
- The most frequent treatment-emergent adverse events were MRI changes resembling amyloid-related imaging abnormalities (ARIA).
- This makes AL002 useful not only as a TREM2 efficacy failure anchor, but also as a safety signal for immune-modulating AD antibodies.

Wiki interpretation:
- This is the paper-level primary source for the AL002/TREM2 human-intervention result.
- It strengthens the precision-neuroinflammation branch because it proves CNS target engagement and pharmacodynamic movement are possible.
- It also downgrades simple TREM2-activation claims because clinical progression and cognition/function endpoints did not improve.
- Route AL002 through target-engagement-without-clinical-benefit and ARIA-like safety gates, not through healthy-adult enhancement or generic anti-inflammatory nootropic claims.

Caveats:
- Early AD disease-modification trials do not directly answer healthy-adult cognition questions.
- The study was industry funded, and many authors were employees or consultants of Alector or other AD-therapy companies.
- The negative result does not prove TREM2 biology is irrelevant; it argues that agonistic antibody dosing, timing, phenotype, downstream biology, and safety must be treated as hard translation gates.
