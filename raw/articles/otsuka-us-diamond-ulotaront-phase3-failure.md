# Notes
- Source: July 31, 2023 Otsuka / Sumitomo Pharma topline press release.
- DIAMOND 1: 435 participants; 50 mg/day and 75 mg/day over 6 weeks did not beat placebo on PANSS total at week 6.
- DIAMOND 2: 464 participants; 75 mg/day and 100 mg/day also did not show statistically significant improvement on the primary endpoint.
- Interpretation: large placebo response may have masked effect; drug was generally safe and well tolerated.
