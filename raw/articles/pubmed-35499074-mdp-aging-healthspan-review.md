# Mitochondria-derived peptides in aging and healthspan

- PMID: 35499074
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35499074/
- Ingested: 2026-04-29

## Summary

2022 review defining the MDP family: humanin, MOTS-c, and SHLPs 1-6. It is the cleanest source for the family taxonomy and for separating biomarker/aging biology from cognition claims.

## Notes

- Notes that humanin has been linked to cognition and AD pathology in rodents and humans.
- Notes that SHLP2 is more biomarker/protection oriented and that MOTS-c is metabolism-first.
