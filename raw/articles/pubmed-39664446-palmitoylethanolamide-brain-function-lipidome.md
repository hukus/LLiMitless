# Source Digest

Type: preclinical rat MRI and lipidomics study.

Publication date: 2024-11-27.

Key extraction:
- Intervention: awake rats were given 3, 10, or 30 mg/kg PEA or vehicle during MRI scanning.
- Brain-function readout: PEA caused dose-related changes in BOLD signal and functional connectivity across prefrontal cortex, sensorimotor cortices, basal ganglia, thalamus, and related regions.
- Lipidomics readout: CNS PEA increased after 30 mg/kg, while anandamide and at least 20 other endolipids were significantly lower across CNS regions; 51 of 78 detected CNS endolipids were modulated in at least one region.
- Interpretation: PEA is not behaviorally inert in the brain; acute dosing can shift functional-connectivity and lipid-tone measures.

Wiki signal:
- Medicinal signal: preclinical mechanism only.
- Confidence limiter: acute rat study, intraperitoneal-style exposure, imaging/lipid endpoints rather than human cognitive performance.
