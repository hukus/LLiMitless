# Correction to the 2025 Pomegranate Extract Older-Adult Cognition Trial

- Article: Correction: Farhat et al. Impact of Pomegranate Extract Supplementation on Physical and Cognitive Function in Community-Dwelling Older Adults Aged 55-70 Years: A Randomised Double-Blind Clinical Trial.
- Journal: Geriatrics 2025;10(6):159
- DOI: 10.3390/geriatrics10060159
- Source: https://www.mdpi.com/2308-3417/10/6/159
- Ingested: 2026-05-03

## Summary

Correction notice for the 2025 randomized double-blind pomegranate extract trial. The original paper incorrectly specified punicalagin content; the correction changes the capsule composition from 75% punicalagins to 36% punicalagins. The corrected methods statement says each pomegranate-extract capsule contained 36% punicalagins and 1.3% ellagic acid by HPLC.

The authors state that the scientific conclusions are unaffected.

Use in wiki: product-identity correction. The 2025 extract trial remains relevant, but any dose or product-transportability claim should use the corrected 36% punicalagin composition rather than the original mistaken value.
