# Cognitive Vitality Shankhpushpi evidence review

Alzheimer's Drug Discovery Foundation Cognitive Vitality review of Shankhpushpi, also spelled Shankhapushpi, an Ayurvedic memory and nerve-tonic herb usually linked to Convolvulus pluricaulis and sometimes to other regional plant identities.

The review states that human data from well-controlled clinical trials are lacking. It identifies two open-label clinical studies, reviews, and numerous preclinical studies. In one open-label study of 102 young healthy volunteers, Shankhpushpi tablets 500 mg twice daily for two months were compared with yoga and appeared to improve long-term memory measures, but the design had no placebo control. A second open-label schoolchild study used a multi-herb formula, preventing attribution to Shankhpushpi alone.

Safety notes in the review include possible blood-pressure lowering and potential interaction with phenytoin that could reduce seizure control.

Interpretation for the wiki: Shankhpushpi is high-salience traditional cognition lore, but current human evidence is open-label and low-confidence. It should be an anecdote/tradition-plus-preclinical node, not a ranked nootropic.
