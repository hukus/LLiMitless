# Fermented soy cognition RCT full-text details

2025 Nutrients randomized controlled trial in 61 adults aged 65+ tested 12 weeks of non-probiotic fermented soy powder added to the usual diet versus an energy-matched placebo. Forty-seven participants completed the study. The fermented-soy group improved memory versus placebo in the main adjusted analysis, and post hoc analysis in women aged 70+ found stronger global-cognition and memory signals.

Important caveats: the memory effect was small, the strongest findings were post hoc and female/age-subgroup based, and attrition was nontrivial. Five fermented-soy participants dropped out because of dyspepsia, and palatability was a recurring adherence issue. The study supports a fermented-soy memory hypothesis in older adults, not a broad soy-isoflavone nootropic claim.
