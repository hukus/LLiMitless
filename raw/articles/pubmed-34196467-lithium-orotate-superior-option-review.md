# Lithium orotate: A superior option for lithium therapy?

- PMID: 34196467
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34196467/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

This narrative review summarizes the history and theory around lithium orotate as a lithium salt. It argues that orotate may have theoretical cellular or membrane-transport advantages, but it does not establish human superiority or a validated cognition indication. The review also acknowledges uncertainty around toxicity and the need for more research.
