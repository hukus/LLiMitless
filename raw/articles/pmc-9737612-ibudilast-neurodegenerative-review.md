# Ibudilast neurodegenerative disease review

2022 Molecules review on ibudilast as a PDE inhibitor for neurodegenerative diseases. The review summarizes preclinical evidence that ibudilast may be neuroprotective by suppressing neuroinflammation, inhibiting apoptosis, regulating mitochondrial function, affecting ubiquitin-proteasome and autophagosome-lysosome pathways, and attenuating oxidative stress.

The clinical evidence highlighted includes progressive multiple sclerosis and ALS as promising but still incomplete translational contexts.
