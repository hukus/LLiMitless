# Short-term Aronia melanocarpa Extract Supplementation Improves Cognitive Performance: A Randomized, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled Crossover Study in Healthy Young Adults

- PMID: 38656355
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38656355/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

1-week crossover trial in 35 healthy young adults. Aronia melanocarpa extract delivered 180 mg anthocyanins/day. Compared with placebo, movement time improved on the five-choice reaction time task and serum BDNF increased, but memory and executive function did not change. The product was well tolerated and vascular markers were unchanged.
