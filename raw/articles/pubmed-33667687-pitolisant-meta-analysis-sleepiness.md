# Pitolisant versus placebo for excessive daytime sleepiness in narcolepsy and obstructive sleep apnea: A meta-analysis from randomized controlled trials

- PMID: 33667687
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33667687/

## Summary

2021 meta-analysis pooling four RCTs and 678 patients with narcolepsy or OSA. Pitolisant reduced Epworth Sleepiness Scale scores, increased mean sleep latency, and improved EQ-5D versus placebo, but the evidence base is still disorder-context wakefulness rather than direct cognitive enhancement.
