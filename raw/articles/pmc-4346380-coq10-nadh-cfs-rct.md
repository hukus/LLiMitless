# PMC 4346380 CoQ10 plus NADH chronic fatigue syndrome RCT

This 2015 randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial tested oral coenzyme Q10 plus NADH in chronic fatigue syndrome. The trial was registered as NCT02063126 and included 73 Spanish CFS patients.

Design: participants received CoQ10 200 mg/day plus NADH 20 mg/day or placebo for 8 weeks. The study evaluated fatigue and biochemical parameters related to oxidative stress, bioenergetics, and mitochondrial function.

Result: the treated group showed a significant reduction in Fatigue Impact Scale total score versus placebo (p<0.05). Blood mononuclear-cell markers also moved in the active group: NAD+/NADH ratio (p<0.001), CoQ10 (p<0.05), ATP (p<0.05), and citrate synthase (p<0.05) were significantly higher, while lipoperoxides were lower (p<0.05).

Interpretation for the wiki: this supports a CoQ10 plus NADH mitochondrial/fatigue route in CFS, not NADH-alone cognition. The combination design prevents attribution to NADH, and the main clinical endpoint is fatigue rather than objective cognition.
