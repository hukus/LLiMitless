# The Effects of Pulsed Low-Level EM Fields on Memory Processes

Source type: PubMed-indexed German-language pilot study with English abstract.

Core design:
- Published 2004 in Zeitschrift fur Medizinische Physik.
- N=33 volunteers.
- Tested pulsed electromagnetic fields using a GSM-standard exposure.
- Exposure occurred for 30 minutes during a relaxation period in an acoustically shielded room.
- Cognitive performance was assessed with an auditory discrimination / order-threshold task.

Main findings:
- The abstract reports reduced mental-regeneration performance in 21 of 33 participants after pulsed-field exposure, reflected by increased order threshold.

Wiki takeaway:
- Historical human caution signal that pulsed EM exposure can worsen a cognitive/perceptual timing measure in some participants.
- Supports keeping PEMF/EMF in a parameter-sensitive disruption/modulation branch rather than a simple enhancement lane.

Limitations:
- Older pilot study.
- German-language article; abstract-level capture only.
- GSM-standard exposure is not interchangeable with modern transcranial PEMF devices, solenoid arrays, tACS, or rTMS.
