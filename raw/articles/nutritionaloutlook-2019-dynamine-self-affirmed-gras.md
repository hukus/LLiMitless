# Dynamine self-affirmed GRAS status

- Source type: trade/regulatory-status report
- Published: 2019-07-12
- Ingested: 2026-05-03

## Summary

Trade report that Compound Solutions announced self-affirmed GRAS status for Dynamine, described as a nature-identical purine alkaloid positioned for energy, mood, and focus products. The report says self-affirmed GRAS status opened the ingredient for use in foods and beverages and mentions pre-workout, gaming, nootropic, and biohacking formulas.

Use in the wiki: status context, not FDA approval. Self-affirmed GRAS reporting should be distinguished from an FDA no-questions letter and from cognition efficacy.
