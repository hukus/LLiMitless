# PubMed 41897500 micellar glutathione bioavailability RCT note

2026 Antioxidants randomized crossover clinical trial titled "A Targeted Metabolomic Assessment of Oral Glutathione Bioavailability and Safety in Humans."

The first phase randomized 14 healthy adults in double-blind crossover fashion to single oral doses of a novel micellar glutathione formulation (LipoMicel), standard glutathione, and liposomal glutathione, measuring whole-blood glutathione PK and related metabolites. A second 30-day single-arm follow-up assessed safety/tolerability of daily LipoMicel.

Reported results: the micellar glutathione formulation produced higher baseline-adjusted systemic glutathione exposure and peak response than standard 500 mg glutathione. Dose-normalized exposure and Cmax were reported as up to about 4-fold higher for micellar glutathione than standard glutathione. No significant changes in clinical safety markers such as ALT, AST, ALP, or creatinine were observed after 30 days of 600 mg/day LipoMicel.

Interpretation for the wiki: this is a modern formulation/bioavailability and short safety/tolerability anchor, not a cognition study. It makes route/product identity important for [[glutathione-products]] and helps explain why standard, liposomal, micellar, S-acetyl, sublingual, and injection claims should not be collapsed.
