# MOTS-c modulates skeletal muscle function by directly binding and activating CK2

- PMID: 39559755
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39559755/

## Summary

Mechanistic paper showing MOTS-c directly binds CK2 and improves muscle glucose uptake in mice. This strengthens metabolic plausibility, but it remains preclinical and does not add a cognition endpoint.
