# Safety of transcranial focused ultrasound stimulation: A systematic review of the state of knowledge from both human and animal studies

- PMID: 31401074
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31401074/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

Low-intensity transcranial focused ultrasound stimulation had a limited safety literature in 2019. The review found adverse effects were rare, but at high stimulation intensity and/or rate TFUS could cause hemorrhage, cell death or damage, unintentional blood-brain barrier opening, and unintended long-term neural and behavioral effects. Most included studies reported no adverse effects even when at least one FDA safety index was exceeded, but the authors still called for a stronger safety framework and more standardized parameter reporting.
