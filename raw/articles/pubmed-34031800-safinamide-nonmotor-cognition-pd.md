# Safinamide non-motor, cognitive, and behavioral symptoms in fluctuating Parkinson disease

- PMID: 34031800
- PMCID: PMC8724100
- DOI: 10.1007/s10072-021-05324-w
- Journal: Neurological Sciences (2022)
- Design: prospective longitudinal study.

## Captured takeaways

- The study followed 20 fluctuating Parkinson disease patients treated with safinamide 50 mg for 6 months.
- It evaluated non-motor, cognitive, and behavioral symptoms using validated PD-specific scales and a neuropsychological assessment across five cognitive domains.
- At 6 months, patients improved on interest, motivation, urinary disturbances, fatigue, apathy, and total wake-up time spent in OFF state.
- Follow-up neuropsychological evaluation did not reveal cognitive change compared with baseline.
- Interpretation for the wiki: safinamide may help non-motor burden and fatigue/apathy in fluctuating PD, but this study is cognition-neutral and disease-state-specific.
