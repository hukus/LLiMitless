# Hydrolyzed Chicken Extract (ProBeptigen) on Cognitive Function in Healthy Middle-Aged People

- PMID: 32397609
- PMCID: PMC7284526
- DOI: 10.3390/nu12051362
- Published: 2020-05-10
- Ingested: 2026-05-11

## Summary

Randomized double-blind trial of ProBeptigen, a hydrolyzed peptide product derived from essence of chicken, in healthy middle-aged people under mild stress. Ninety healthy subjects were randomized to ProBeptigen or placebo for eight weeks. Outcomes included neurocognitive assessment, event-related potentials, and blood tests.

The ProBeptigen group outperformed placebo on Wechsler Memory Scale Logical Memory subtests and a CANTAB Spatial Working Memory task. High-sensitivity CRP declined progressively, supporting an anti-inflammatory signal. The authors suggest possible benefit for verbal short- and long-term memory and spatial working memory in stressed middle-aged adults.

## Relevance

This is a product-specific bridge between [[essence-of-chicken]] and peptide-standardized chicken-extract products. It strengthens the stress / inflammation / memory route while preserving product-specificity.

## Caveats

- Product and funding ties include BRAND'S Suntory Asia.
- Mild-stress, middle-aged cohort limits broad transfer.
- The product is hydrolyzed chicken extract, not generic essence of chicken and not isolated anserine/carnosine.
