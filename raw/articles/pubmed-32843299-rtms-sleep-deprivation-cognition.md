# Effect of repetitive transcranial magnetic stimulation on the cognitive impairment induced by sleep deprivation: a randomized trial

- PMID: 32843299
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32843299/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Randomized trial in healthy people undergoing 24 h sleep deprivation. High-frequency rTMS was tested during sleep deprivation to see whether it could reverse sleep-loss cognitive impairment.

## Notes

This is one of the strongest state-dependent stimulation comparators for the current question. It supports the idea that untreated sleep loss is not a neutral baseline for neuromodulation experiments.
