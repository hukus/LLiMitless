# Mucuna pruriens in untreated Parkinson's disease in sub-Saharan Africa

PubMed-indexed 2026 12-month multicenter randomized controlled trial of Mucuna pruriens in untreated Parkinson's disease in sub-Saharan Africa. The trial is relevant because Mucuna is a natural L-DOPA source and often appears in dopamine/motivation nootropic stacks.

For this wiki, Parkinson's disease treatment evidence does not translate into healthy-adult cognition enhancement. The source supports clinical-state visibility and L-DOPA safety/dosing caution, especially when paired with product-assay and prescription levodopa label context.

Relevance: Mucuna should be a dopaminergic clinical-state / risk-first supplement branch rather than a casual motivation enhancer.
