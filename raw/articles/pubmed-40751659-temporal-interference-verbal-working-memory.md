# Temporal interference stimulation modulates verbal working memory: an EEG study on hemispheric lateralization and neural dynamics

- PMID: 40751659
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40751659/
- Published: 2025-08-01
- Journal: Cerebral Cortex

## Summary
This 2025 human EEG study tested TIS on verbal working memory with left/right-hemisphere stimulation and sham control.
The effect appeared under high-load conditions, where right-hemisphere stimulation showed a right-parietal N200 increase and P300 decrease.
Low-load conditions were null, so the signal is load-dependent and likely parameter-sensitive.

## Notes
This paper strengthens the case that TI can modulate cognition, but only under specific load and lateralization conditions.
It is still an acute laboratory effect, not a general healthy-adult enhancement result.
