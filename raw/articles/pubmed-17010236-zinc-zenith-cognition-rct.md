# ZENITH zinc supplementation and cognitive function in older adults

- PMID: 17010236
- Published: 2006
- Source type: randomized controlled trial
- Checked: 2026-05-11
- Ingested: 2026-05-11

## Summary

The ZENITH study tested zinc supplementation in older adults and included cognitive outcomes. It is a useful human cognition anchor for [[zinc]], but the result should be interpreted conservatively: endpoint, age, baseline status, geography, duration, and dose matter, and the study does not make zinc a broad nootropic.

Use in the wiki: zinc sits in the mineral status watchlist as low-confidence, possibly status-sensitive cognition support with a strong copper-deficiency safety gate.

## Notes

- Route through baseline zinc status, diet, inflammation, immune context, age, and copper status.
- Zinc is not interchangeable with multivitamin/mineral mixtures or cold-remedy lozenges.
