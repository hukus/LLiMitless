# Soy-based phosphatidylserine / phosphatidic acid complex and stress reactivity

## Bibliographic snapshot

- Randomized, placebo-controlled study of soy-based phosphatidylserine / phosphatidic acid complex (PAS) in chronically stressed male subjects.
- PMCID: PMC4237891.
- Trial context: HPA-axis stress reactivity and Trier Social Stress Test.

## Study design

- Healthy but chronically stressed male participants.
- Treatment arms included daily PAS dosing and placebo before a laboratory psychosocial stressor.
- Outcomes included hypothalamus-pituitary-adrenal axis reactivity, cortisol / ACTH-style stress markers, and subjective stress response.

## Results captured

- The article title and abstract frame PAS as normalizing HPA-axis stress reactivity in chronically stressed male subjects.
- The intervention is a phosphatidylserine plus phosphatidic acid complex, not isolated phosphatidylserine.
- The outcome domain is stress physiology, not direct cognitive performance.

## Wiki interpretation

- This is relevant because many community PS reports are really stress, cortisol, sleep, or stimulant-smoothing reports.
- It should route phosphatidylserine through a stress-state / cortisol-response branch, not directly into a memory-enhancement rank.
- PAS results should not be assigned to isolated PS or to sunflower/soy PS products without matching product identity.
