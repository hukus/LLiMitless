# Estimation of the Orexin Receptor Occupancy From Human Plasma Pharmacokinetics of Vornorexant, a Novel Dual Orexin 1/2 Receptor Antagonist for the Treatment of Insomnia

- PMID: 41622624
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41622624/
- PMCID: PMC12862099
- Ingested: 2026-04-30

## Summary

Human PK/RO modeling study in healthy phase I subjects (n = 67 total across two studies, with 10 mg fasting PK data used for model development) estimated that vornorexant produces strong OX1 and OX2 receptor occupancy after oral dosing. OX2 occupancy exceeded 70% at 30 minutes after 5 mg and 10 mg doses, and remained roughly 50% and 60% at 6 hours, respectively. OX1 occupancy was estimated to be higher than OX2 occupancy. This is a target-engagement and sleep-architecture anchor for the DORA branch, not a cognition-enhancement result.
