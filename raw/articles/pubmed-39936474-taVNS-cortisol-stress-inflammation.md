# Transcutaneous auricular vagus nerve stimulation inhibits mental stress-induced cortisol release-Potential implications for inflammatory conditions

- PMID: 39936474
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39936474/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Healthy adults (n = 12) completed taVNS and sham-taVNS sessions separated by at least four days. During a mental arithmetic stress test, salivary cortisol rose less under taVNS than sham, while heart rate and HRV changes still confirmed sympathetic activation.

## Notes

- A psoriasis patient on daily taVNS for 3 months showed lower diurnal salivary cortisol, but that is a single-patient observation.
- The authors framed the result as potentially relevant to inflammatory conditions, but the design does not prove a central PVN mechanism.
- This is a useful stress-endocrine signal for the taVNS state-regulation branch.
