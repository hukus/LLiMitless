# Huperzine A for Mild Cognitive Impairment

- PMID: 23235666

## Summary

2012 Cochrane review of huperzine A for mild cognitive impairment. The review looked for randomized, parallel-group, placebo-controlled trials comparing huperzine A with placebo in MCI and found no eligible trials. The authors concluded that available evidence was insufficient to assess huperzine A for MCI.

## Interpretation

Use this as a contradiction anchor against later lower-quality or differently filtered meta-analyses. The huperzine branch should stay contested unless a larger high-quality placebo-controlled MCI trial appears.
