# Effect of Cocoa Extract Supplementation on Cognitive Function: COSMOS Clinic Subcohort

- Article: Effect of cocoa extract supplementation on cognitive function: results from the clinic subcohort of the COSMOS trial
- Authors: Chirag M. Vyas, JoAnn E. Manson, Howard D. Sesso, Pamela M. Rist, Alison Weinberg, Eunjung Kim, M. Vinayaga Moorthy, Nancy R. Cook, Olivia I. Okereke
- Journal: American Journal of Clinical Nutrition, 2024;119(1):39-48
- DOI: 10.1016/j.ajcnut.2023.10.031
- Source: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0002916523662756
- Ingested: 2026-05-03

## Summary

COSMOS clinic subcohort randomized trial using detailed in-person neuropsychological testing over 2 years. The subcohort included 573 older adults; the cocoa extract intervention supplied 500 mg/day cocoa flavanols.

The reported conclusion was no overall benefit of daily cocoa extract supplementation versus placebo for global or domain-specific cognitive function. Secondary analyses suggested possible cognitive benefit among participants with lower baseline diet quality, while people with healthier baseline dietary patterns did not show cognitive benefit.

Use in wiki: large, longer-duration counterweight to smaller positive cocoa-flavanol trials. This keeps cocoa flavanols as low-confidence and phenotype/diet-quality dependent.
