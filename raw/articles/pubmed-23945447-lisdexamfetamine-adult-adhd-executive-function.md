# Lisdexamfetamine adult ADHD executive-function RCT

- PMID: 23945447
- DOI: 10.4088/JCP.12m08144
- Journal: Journal of Clinical Psychiatry (2013)
- Trial registration: NCT01101022

## Captured takeaways

- Randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled study in adults aged 18-55 with primary ADHD and clinically significant executive-function deficits.
- Participants received optimized lisdexamfetamine 30, 50, or 70 mg/day, or placebo, for a 10-week double-blind period.
- The primary objective was efficacy on executive-function deficits using self-reported Behavior Rating Inventory of Executive Function-Adult version Global Executive Composite scores.
- PubMed abstract reports significant improvement in self-reported executive-function ratings among adults with ADHD and clinically significant executive-function deficits.
- Related publications from the same program report clinician / informant ADHD symptom improvement and quality-of-life movement.
- Interpretation for the wiki: this is clinical ADHD executive-function behavior evidence, not healthy-adult enhancement proof.
