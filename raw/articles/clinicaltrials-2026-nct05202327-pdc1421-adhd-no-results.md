# ClinicalTrials.gov NCT05202327: PDC-1421 adult ADHD completed, no posted results

Official ClinicalTrials.gov record for "PDC-1421 Treatment in Adult Patients With ADHD." Sponsor: BioLite, Inc.; collaborator ABVC BioPharma, Inc. Overall status: completed, verified 2024-08. The record version checked in this pass was dated 2026-05-01.

Design: randomized, parallel, placebo-controlled, quadruple-masked phase 2 study in adults with ADHD. Actual enrollment was 70. The trial started on 2022-04-07 and completed on 2023-12-06.

Intervention: PDC-1421 Capsule, described as a botanical investigational new drug containing extract of Radix Polygalae (Polygala tenuifolia Willd.) as the active ingredient. Arms were high dose (two PDC-1421 capsules TID for 8 weeks), low dose (one PDC-1421 capsule plus one placebo TID for 8 weeks), and placebo (two placebo capsules TID).

Endpoints: primary outcome was number of participants with at least 40% improvement in ADHD-RS-IV from baseline up to 8 weeks. Secondary outcomes included CAARS self-report subscales, Sluggish Cognitive Tempo score, ADHD-RS-IV inattention/hyperactivity subscores, symptom remission, and CGI response.

Results status: ClinicalTrials.gov reported `hasResults: false` in the checked API response. The derived tracking field listed an estimated results-first-submission date of 2026-04-15, but no results were posted as of the 2026-05-03 wiki pass.

Relevance: current watch item for PDC-1421 attention/cognition-adjacent evidence. It should not be used as positive efficacy evidence until results post.
