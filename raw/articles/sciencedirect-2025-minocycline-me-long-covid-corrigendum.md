# Corrigendum to oral minocycline ME and long COVID pilot

2025 corrigendum to the eNeurologicalSci minocycline ME/long-COVID pilot. The correction changed Table 4 values for long-COVID symptom counts after minocycline.

Corrected long-COVID symptom table: fatigue 18/18 before and 3/18 after, post-exertional malaise 18/18 before and 3/18 after, unrefreshing sleep 16/18 before and 3/18 after, brain fog 8/18 before and 3/18 after, orthostatic intolerance 3/18 before and 1/18 after, and disequilibrium 5/18 before and 2/18 after.

Interpretation for the wiki: the corrigendum preserves the direction of the brain-fog signal but slightly changes the long-COVID brain-fog count from 8/18 to 3/18 after treatment. Any wiki claim about the pilot should cite the corrected figures.
