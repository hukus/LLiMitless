# Pitolisant for Daytime Sleepiness in Patients with Obstructive Sleep Apnea Who Refuse Continuous Positive Airway Pressure Treatment. A Randomized Trial

- PMID: 31917607
- ClinicalTrials.gov: NCT01072968
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31917607/

## Summary

2020 randomized trial in 268 patients with moderate-to-severe OSA who refused CPAP. Pitolisant reduced Epworth Sleepiness Scale scores more than placebo and improved fatigue and patient-reported outcomes, but wake-maintenance testing was not improved. Adverse events were similar overall between groups.
