# Desmopressin Acetate Spray label

- DailyMed: https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=1ac423b8-27a6-4cef-4a82-c50bf81b4f49

## Summary

Current U.S. prescribing label for desmopressin acetate nasal spray. The approved indication is central diabetes insipidus, not cognition. The label contraindicates use in patients with renal impairment or hyponatremia/history of hyponatremia, warns about fluid restriction and sodium monitoring, and notes altered absorption with nasal mucosa abnormalities.

## Notes

- Source type: FDA label / DailyMed.
- Updated label date: 2025-08-22.
- Key safety flags: hyponatremia, water intoxication, unpredictable absorption with nasal mucosa abnormalities, and blood-pressure interaction with other vasoconstrictors.
- Use case: safety-first comparator for desmopressin self-experimentation or nootropic claims.
