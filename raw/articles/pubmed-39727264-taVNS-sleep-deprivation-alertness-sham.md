- PMID: 39727264
- Title: Can earlobe stimulation serve as a sham for transcutaneous auricular vagus stimulation? Evidence from an alertness study following sleep deprivation.
- Design: within-subject randomized study in 56 participants after 24 h of sleep deprivation.
- Comparison: taVNS versus earlobe stimulation (ES) as the control condition.
- Main readout: both taVNS and ES improved alertness after sleep deprivation.
- Key sham caution: ES increased HR and reduced HRV, while taVNS increased salivary alpha-amylase, so the control condition was not physiologically inert.
- Why it matters: this is an active-sham caution for sleep-loss taVNS work and a reminder that sham controls can still shift arousal-state readouts.
