# Cognitive benefits of folic acid, docosahexaenoic acid, and a combination of both nutrients in mild cognitive impairment

- DOI: 10.1159/000540021
- Published online: 2024-07-17
- Source type: randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial
- Population: 280 adults age 60+ with mild cognitive impairment
- Intervention: folic acid 800 mcg/day, DHA 800 mg/day, both, or placebo for 12 months
- Ingested: 2026-05-03

## Summary

Four-arm MCI trial comparing folic acid, DHA, combined folic acid plus DHA, and placebo over 12 months. FA, DHA, and the combined intervention improved full IQ, verbal IQ, and selected WAIS-RC subtests versus placebo, and the combined intervention was superior to either intervention alone. The intervention groups also decreased 8-OHdG and increased mitochondrial DNA copy number.

## Notes

- This is a direct anchor for the folic acid plus DHA memory signal reported in the 2026 nutrient NMA.
- Combination attribution remains difficult because FA and DHA both moved outcomes, and DHA already has its own entity.
- Related earlier publication history includes a Journal of Alzheimer's Disease retraction for a folic acid plus DHA MCI paper; source-integrity and registry details should be checked before upgrading confidence.
