# FDA compounder supplier-quality warning

- Content current as of: 2026-04-27
- Source: https://www.fda.gov/drugs/human-drug-compounding/fda-compounders-know-your-bulks-and-excipients-suppliers

## Key points

FDA says compounded-drug quality depends on the quality of bulk drug substances and excipients. The agency urges compounders to know their bulk supplier, verify the quality testing performed by the supplier, and comply with the bulk-substance requirements in sections 503A and 503B.

## Research-use read

For peptide "research chemical" markets, this is the relevant caution: the presence of a peptide name on a vendor page does not establish human-grade quality, validated sourcing, or a safe route of administration.
