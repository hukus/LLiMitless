# Metformin hydrochloride ER DailyMed label

DailyMed label snapshot for metformin hydrochloride extended-release tablets, updated 2026-05-05.

Safety-relevant label points for cognition routing: metformin ER is a prescription type-2-diabetes drug with a boxed warning for lactic acidosis. The label lists risk factors including renal impairment, carbonic anhydrase inhibitors such as topiramate, age 65 or older, iodinated contrast, surgery/procedures, hypoxic states such as acute congestive heart failure, excessive alcohol intake, and hepatic impairment.

Other label points relevant to self-experimentation: assess renal function before use and more often in higher-risk groups, temporarily discontinue around certain iodinated contrast procedures, monitor for vitamin B12 deficiency, and consider hypoglycemia risk when used with insulin or insulin secretagogues.

Interpretation for the wiki: metformin may have a long-COVID prevention signal, but prescription-label renal, hepatic, hypoxia, contrast-procedure, alcohol, B12, and drug-interaction gates are not optional.
