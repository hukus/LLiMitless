# Quantity of phenibut in dietary supplements before and after FDA warnings

- PMID: 34550038
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34550038/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

Analytical study of over-the-counter phenibut supplements before and after FDA warnings. Four brands met inclusion criteria. Before the warnings, two brands contained phenibut at 484 mg and 487 mg per serving. After the warnings, all four products contained phenibut at 21 mg to 1,164 mg per serving, and the quantity increased in 3 of 4 products.
