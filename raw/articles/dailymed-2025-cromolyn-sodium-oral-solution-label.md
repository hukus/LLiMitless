## Summary

Cromolyn sodium oral solution concentrate is a prescription mast-cell stabilizer labeled for oral use. Each 5 mL ampule contains 100 mg cromolyn sodium. The label frames the product as a human prescription drug with no DEA schedule and an ANDA marketing status.

The label's adverse-event section reports that in mastocytosis clinical studies, headache and diarrhea each occurred in 4 of 87 patients. Pruritus, nausea, and myalgia each occurred in 3 patients, and abdominal pain, rash, and irritability each in 2 patients. One malaise report was recorded. Additional postmarketing reports are mostly gastrointestinal, including diarrhea, nausea, abdominal pain, constipation, dyspepsia, flatulence, glossitis, stomatitis, vomiting, dysphagia, and esophagospasm.
