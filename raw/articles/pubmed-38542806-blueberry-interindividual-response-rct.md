# Inter-individual responses to a blueberry intervention across multiple endpoints

- Source type: randomized cross-over controlled trial / responder analysis.
- Publication: Nutrients, 2024.
- Date checked: 2026-05-03.

This 1-week single-blinded cross-over RCT compared 160 g whole fresh blueberry, 20 g freeze-dried blueberry powder, and placebo control in 37 healthy participants. It evaluated vascular and cognitive endpoints and attempted to characterize responders.

The study found extensive inter-individual variation across vascular and cognitive endpoints, but no consistent response pattern across interventions or participants. It also did not identify a useful urinary-metabolite predictor of responder status.

Use in the wiki: heterogeneity counterweight. It supports the view that blueberry response is variable and product-/phenotype-sensitive rather than a uniform acute nootropic effect.
