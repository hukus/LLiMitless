# High-dose intranasal insulin in an adaptive dose-escalation study in healthy human participants

- PMID: 39558506
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39558506/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

Phase I adaptive dose-escalation safety study in healthy adults. Intranasal insulin from 0 to 1000 U was generally tolerated, but isolated severe and mild hypoglycemia occurred after 600 U, so systemic absorption is not zero at high doses.

## Notes

Twenty-four participants (12 female; median age 53) received repeated weekly intranasal doses. Mean glucose stayed largely stable, serum insulin rose slightly in a dose-dependent manner, and C-peptide fell. The study is relevant as a safety ceiling and target-engagement caution for any future high-dose intranasal-insulin protocol, not as efficacy evidence for cognition.
