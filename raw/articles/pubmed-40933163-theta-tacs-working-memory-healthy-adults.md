# Effect of theta-transcranial alternating current stimulation on working memory performance among healthy adults: A systematic review and meta-analysis

- PMID: 40933163
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40933163/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

2025 theta-tACS meta-analysis in healthy adults. The uncorrected working-memory signal is modest, but the trim-and-fill adjusted estimate drops close to null. N-back tasks and several protocol variables appear to drive most of the positive signal.
