## Summary

DrugBank article summary for sulbutiamine, a lipophilic thiamine derivative reviewed as an antiasthenic compound. The abstract frames asthenia as a common reason for medical consultation and describes sulbutiamine as crossing the blood-brain barrier and acting on brain structures involved in asthenia. The review covers animal models and human asthenia contexts.

## Interpretation

Use this as branch identity and fatigue/asthenia context, not as modern healthy-adult cognition proof. Sulbutiamine belongs in a fatigue and anecdote watchlist unless higher-quality cognition trials are captured.
