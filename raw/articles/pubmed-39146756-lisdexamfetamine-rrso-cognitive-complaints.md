# Lisdexamfetamine for new-onset cognitive complaints after risk-reducing salpingo-oophorectomy

- PMID: 39146756
- DOI: 10.1097/GME.0000000000002443
- Journal: Menopause (2024)
- Trial registration: NCT03187353

## Captured takeaways

- Randomized controlled crossover trial in 69 participants with new-onset cognitive complaints after risk-reducing salpingo-oophorectomy.
- Participants completed 6-week active medication and placebo periods separated by a minimum 2-week washout.
- Active medication was lisdexamfetamine.
- The abstract frames the trial as treatment of new-onset cognitive complaints rather than ADHD.
- Interpretation for the wiki: this is a rare non-ADHD cognitive-complaint clinical context for lisdexamfetamine, but it is a specific surgical/menopause-risk phenotype and not general healthy-adult enhancement.
