# Neural signature underlying the effect of intranasal vasopressin on emotional responses to spontaneous social comparison

- PMID: 41765123
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41765123/
- Publication date: 2026-04

## Summary

Randomized human neuroimaging study of intranasal vasopressin during spontaneous social comparison. The core result was not broad cognition, but self-protective social-emotional modulation: AVP intensified contrastive emotions toward social evaluations, with effects linked to mPFC activity, neural patterns, and connectivity.

## Notes

- Source type: randomized human neuroimaging trial.
- Sample size: 111 healthy volunteers, split across treatment groups.
- Interpretation: this strengthens the idea that vasopressin modulates self-image defense and social-comparison threat processing.
- Relevance to cognition: mechanistic and context-bound, not a general nootropic signal.
