# Pharmacological characterization of DM232 and DM235, new potent cognition enhancers

- Source type: open-access preclinical pharmacology review / characterization paper.
- Publication: CNS Drug Reviews, 2006.
- Date checked: 2026-05-03.

This paper characterizes DM232 / unifiram and DM235 / sunifiram as potent preclinical cognition-enhancing compounds. The abstract states that they are far more potent than piracetam in the tested preclinical models, do not show affinity for major central receptors or channels in binding studies, prevent amnesia induced by modulation of several neurotransmission systems, increase acetylcholine release from rat cerebral cortex, and likely involve AMPA-receptor-related mechanisms.
