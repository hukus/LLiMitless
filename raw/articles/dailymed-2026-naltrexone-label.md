# DailyMed naltrexone label safety context

DailyMed naltrexone hydrochloride labels identify naltrexone as an opioid antagonist indicated for alcohol dependence and blockade of exogenously administered opioids. Labels emphasize that naltrexone is contraindicated in patients receiving opioid analgesics, opioid-dependent patients, patients in acute opioid withdrawal, people with a failed naloxone challenge or positive opioid urine screen, hypersensitivity, and acute hepatitis or liver failure.

Warnings include precipitated opioid withdrawal, problems with opioid analgesia while blocked, hepatotoxicity or clinically significant liver dysfunction, nausea, headache, dizziness, nervousness, fatigue, insomnia, vomiting, anxiety, and somnolence in addiction-treatment experience. Low-dose off-label use is different from 50 mg addiction dosing but does not remove the opioid-interaction and liver-monitoring logic.
