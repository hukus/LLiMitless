# The Antioxidant Tetrapeptide Epitalon Enhances Delayed Wound Healing in an In Vitro Model of Diabetic Retinopathy

PubMed indexed a 2025 Stem Cell Reviews and Reports paper showing that Epitalon improved delayed wound healing in an in vitro diabetic-retinopathy model. The paper frames the effect as antioxidant and wound-repair support in a cell-model context.

For the wiki, this is another fresh non-neural mechanistic signal for Epitalon. It supports the broader telomere and oxidative-stress biology lane, but it still does not create a human cognition or safety endpoint.
