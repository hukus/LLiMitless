# Oral curcumin for Alzheimer's disease: tolerability and efficacy in a 24-week randomized, double blind, placebo-controlled study

- PMID: 23107780
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23107780/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Thirty-six people with mild-to-moderate AD were randomized to placebo, 2 g/day, or 4 g/day Curcumin C3 Complex for 24 weeks. The trial found no clinical or biomarker efficacy signal and noted limited bioavailability plus GI-related withdrawals, making it a useful negative comparator for the curcumin branch.
