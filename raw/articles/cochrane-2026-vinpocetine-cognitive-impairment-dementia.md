# Cochrane vinpocetine for cognitive impairment and dementia

- Source: Cochrane evidence page
- Review: Vinpocetine for cognitive impairment and dementia
- Ingested: 2026-05-15

## Summary

Cochrane framed vinpocetine as a treatment question for cognitive impairment due to vascular disease, Alzheimer's disease, mixed vascular/Alzheimer's disease, and other dementias. The eligible evidence was human, unconfounded, double-blind randomized trials using vinpocetine for more than one day compared with control.

The review page states that the identified studies were all performed before and in the early 1990s, used heterogeneous terms and criteria for cognitive decline and dementia, and included three studies with 583 people with dementia treated with vinpocetine or placebo. The reports did not allow the reviewers to separate effects by degenerative versus vascular dementia.

## Interpretation

Use this as old disease-context evidence, not healthy-adult enhancement evidence. The right wiki read is not "vinpocetine is proven for cognition"; it is "vinpocetine has a historical dementia/cognitive-impairment evidence trail, but the public synthesis is old, heterogeneous, and insufficient for modern healthy-adult cognition ranking."
