# Blood-Brain Barrier Opening with MRI-guided Focused Ultrasound Elicits Meningeal Venous Permeability in Humans with Early Alzheimer Disease

- PMID: 33399511
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33399511/
- PMC: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7924515/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

This 2021 Radiology study in 3 women with early AD showed repeated hippocampal and entorhinal BBB opening with no intracranial hemorrhage or other adverse effect. It also identified transient perivenous and later perivenular enhancement patterns, suggesting a downstream meningeal/blood-meningeal permeability response after BBB opening.
