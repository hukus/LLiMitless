# Saniona 2026 tesofensine pipeline status

## Captured takeaways

- Saniona's current tesofensine pipeline page describes tesofensine as a triple monoamine reuptake inhibitor for obesity.
- The page says the TIPO-1 phase 2 obesity trial indicated 10% or greater weight loss at the expected recommended dose of 0.50 mg/day over 24 weeks.
- It states that partner Medix completed a phase 3 program studying 0.25 and 0.50 mg daily tesofensine in obesity.
- The page says Medix submitted a new drug application to COFEPRIS in Mexico for obesity and that the technical committee expressed a favorable opinion in February 2023.
- The page does not present tesofensine as a cognition treatment.
- Interpretation for the wiki: current sponsor status supports obesity-program salience and oral triple-reuptake identity, but does not upgrade the cognition signal.
