# Piracetam for dementia or cognitive impairment

- Source type: Cochrane systematic review, PMC full text.
- Publication: Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews, 2004 update.
- Date checked: 2026-05-03.

This Cochrane review evaluated piracetam for dementia or cognitive impairment. It included 24 randomized double-blind studies with 11,959 participants, but many trials were old, cross-over first-phase data were often unavailable, and unpublished/untraceable manufacturer data raised publication-bias concerns.

The pooled global-impression-of-change signal favored piracetam, but the review found no significant differences for specific cognitive outcomes in the limited data available. The authors concluded that published evidence did not support piracetam use for dementia or cognitive impairment, while still justifying further evaluation.

Use in the wiki: negative / insufficient formal-evidence anchor for piracetam. Do not treat piracetam as a proven healthy-adult cognitive enhancer.
