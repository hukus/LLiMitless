# Acute ketone monoester ingestion lowers resting cerebral blood flow: a randomized cross-over trial

- PMID: 40349325
- DOI: 10.1113/JP287320
- Study type: randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled crossover trial
- Population: 20 healthy young adults (10 female; age 23 ± 3 years)
- Intervention: low-dose ketone monoester (0.3 g/kg β-OHB) vs high-dose ketone monoester (0.6 g/kg β-OHB) vs placebo
- Main finding: ketone monoester lowered resting cerebral blood flow in a dose-dependent manner; higher dose lowered CBF at both 45 and 120 min, low dose lowered CBF at 45 min
- Physiologic correlates: dose-dependent reductions in end-tidal CO2, increased heart rate, no mean arterial pressure change
- Practical read: this complicates any simple "ketones raise CBF" story in healthy young adults and suggests the CBF mechanism is context- and dose-sensitive
