# Predicting the Beneficial Effects of Cognitive Stimulation and Transcranial Direct Current Stimulation in Amnestic Mild Cognitive Impairment with Clinical, Inflammation, and Human Microglia Exposed to Serum as Potential Markers: A Double-Blind Placebo-Controlled Randomized Clinical Trial

- PMID: 40004217
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40004217/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Small double-blind placebo-controlled RCT in amnestic MCI. Cognitive stimulation improved cognition and inflammatory/BDNF markers, but adding active tDCS did not improve cognition or modulate the biomarkers beyond sham.

## Notes

Good negative-evidence example for combination neuromodulation: the ancillary stimulation arm did not add measurable benefit over the training arm.
