# Effect of intranasal insulin on perioperative cognitive function in older adults: a randomized, placebo-controlled, double-blind clinical trial

- PMID: 39216470
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39216470/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Perioperative RCT in older surgical patients showing better short-term cognitive scores and lower postoperative delirium with intranasal insulin.

## Notes

Useful as a modern cognition-adjacent translation case: 40 IU/400 μL once daily around surgery improved MMSE and MoCA-B and reduced POD, with lower IL-6 and S100-β.
