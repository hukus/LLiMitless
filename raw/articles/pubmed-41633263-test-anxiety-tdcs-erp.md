# Modulating inhibitory control in test-anxious individuals via tDCS: An ERP study

- PMID: 41633263
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41633263/
- Ingested: 2026-04-30

## Summary

ERP study in 42 participants with high or low test anxiety. Active left DLPFC tDCS reduced the P3 amplitude during a Flanker task in the high-anxiety group but not in the low-anxiety group, suggesting that anxiety phenotype modulates the neural response to stimulation.

## Notes

- This is a phenotype-sensitive responder result, not a broad healthy-adult tDCS effect.
- The result is electrophysiological rather than a direct durable cognition endpoint.
- Useful for separating stress/anxiety state from generic stimulation claims.
