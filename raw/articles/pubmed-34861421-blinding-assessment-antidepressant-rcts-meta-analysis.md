# A systematic review and meta-analysis of the success of blinding in antidepressant RCTs

- PMID: 34861421
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34861421/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

Systematic review and meta-analysis showing that blinding is rarely assessed in antidepressant RCTs and often fails when it is checked. The paper also reports that participants and investigators can make above-chance treatment guesses, which is exactly the kind of failure mode a self-study detector should flag.

## Notes

- Broadly applicable to intervention trials, not just psychiatry.
- Good evidence that the label "double blind" is not enough without an actual assessment.
