# The Effects of 8-Week Creatine Hydrochloride and Creatine Ethyl Ester Supplementation on Cognition, Clinical Outcomes, and Brain Creatine Levels in Perimenopausal and Menopausal Women (CONCRET-MENOPA): A Randomized Controlled Trial.

- PMID: 40854087
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40854087/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

2026 double-blind RCT in apparently healthy perimenopausal and menopausal women tested low-dose creatine hydrochloride regimens, alone or with creatine ethyl ester, with cognition, clinical outcomes, brain creatine, and biochemical markers as outcomes.

## Notes

This is important mainly as a phenotype-specific older-adult/female cohort signal; it does not yet establish a general healthy-adult enhancement effect.
