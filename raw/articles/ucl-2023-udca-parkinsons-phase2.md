# UCL 2023 UDCA Parkinson's phase 2 trial

UCL Discovery hosts the open-access record for a 2023 Movement Disorders article titled "A Double-Blind, Randomized, Placebo-Controlled Trial of Ursodeoxycholic Acid (UDCA) in Parkinson's Disease."

The UP study tested high-dose UDCA, 30 mg/kg daily, in 30 participants with Parkinson's disease for 48 weeks, randomized 2:1 UDCA versus placebo. The primary outcome was safety and tolerability. Secondary outcomes included 31-phosphorus magnetic resonance spectroscopy for midbrain target engagement and motor-progression measures.

The abstract reports that UDCA was safe and well tolerated, with mild transient gastrointestinal adverse events more frequent in the UDCA group. Midbrain spectroscopy suggested improved ATP hydrolysis, while sensor-based gait measures suggested possible cadence and gait-parameter improvement. Subjective MDS-UPDRS-III assessment did not detect a between-group difference.

Interpretation for the wiki: UDCA has a real disease-state mitochondrial target-engagement signal in Parkinson's disease, but this trial does not establish cognitive benefit or healthy-adult nootropic value.
