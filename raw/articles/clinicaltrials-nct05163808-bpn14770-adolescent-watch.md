# A Randomized Study of BPN14770 in Male Adolescents With Fragile X Syndrome

- ClinicalTrials.gov ID: NCT05163808
- Other study ID: BPN14770-CNS-204
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

ClinicalTrials.gov currently shows this BPN14770 / zatolmilast fragile X adolescent study as active, not recruiting, with no results posted. The estimated completion dates are already in the past relative to this sweep, so the record belongs in the registry watchlist rather than the active recruitment queue.

## Key points

- Sponsor: Tetra Discovery Partners.
- Population: male adolescents aged 9 to <18 years with fragile X syndrome.
- Design: two-part study with an open-label single-dose PK part and a randomized double-blind placebo-controlled part.
- Intervention: zatolmilast (BPN14770) versus placebo.
- Sample size: 163 enrolled.
- Primary endpoint: NIH Toolbox Cognitive Battery cognition crystallized composite score at week 13.
- Secondary endpoints: patient-specific daily-function / language / academic ratings, caregiver impression of improvement, investigator impression of improvement, NIH Toolbox subdomains, Vineland-3 adaptive behavior, and Stanford-Binet verbal knowledge.
- Registry status: active, not recruiting; no results posted.
- Timing: last update posted 2025-08-17; estimated primary completion 2025-09-30; estimated study completion 2025-12-31.
