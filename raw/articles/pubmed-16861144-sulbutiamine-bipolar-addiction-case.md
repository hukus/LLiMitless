# Source Digest

Type: case report.

Publication date: 2006.

Key extraction:
- Case report presented a patient with bipolar disorder, hospitalization history, and addiction-like escalating sulbutiamine use.
- Sulbutiamine was described as a thiamine precursor that crosses the blood-brain barrier and is widely available without prescription in many countries or online.
- The patient's need to consume increasing quantities compromised psychiatric care through defaulting appointments and frequent psychiatrist changes.
- Authors argued clinicians should be aware of misuse potential from over-the-counter or internet-available medications and their harmful influence.

Wiki signal:
- Safety signal: risk-first for bipolar disorder / psychiatric vulnerability, escalating use, and disrupted care.
- Confidence limiter: single case report; does not quantify population-level risk.
