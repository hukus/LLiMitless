# The impact of transcranial random noise stimulation (tRNS) on alpha coherence and verbal divergent thinking

- PMID: 40487366
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40487366/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

Within-subject active/sham study in 40 participants. High-frequency tRNS over the DLPFC was associated with better originality and flexibility on verbal divergent-thinking tasks, and those behavioral changes tracked with bilateral DLPFC alpha coherence changes on EEG. The result is interesting because it ties a cognitive task signal to a measurable network change, but the sample is small and the effect is task-specific.

## Notes

This is the cleanest positive tRNS cognition-adjacent signal in the current branch, but it should be treated as a narrow creativity/network result rather than a broad enhancement claim.
