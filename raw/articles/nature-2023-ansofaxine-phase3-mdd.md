# Ansofaxine LY03005 phase 3 MDD trial

- Source: Mi et al., Translational Psychiatry 2023; phase 3, multicenter, double-blind, randomized, placebo-controlled trial in China.
- Compound identity: ansofaxine / LY03005 / toludesvenlafaxine hydrochloride extended-release tablet was described as a potential triple reuptake inhibitor of serotonin, norepinephrine, and dopamine.
- Design: 588 eligible adults with major depressive disorder were randomized 1:1:1 to 8 weeks of ansofaxine 80 mg/day, ansofaxine 160 mg/day, or placebo.
- Primary endpoint: Montgomery-Asberg Depression Rating Scale total-score change from baseline to study end.
- Main result: week-8 mean MADRS change was -20.0 for 80 mg and -19.9 for 160 mg versus -14.6 for placebo, with p < 0.0001 for both active doses versus placebo.
- Anhedonia/function clues: the paper reported superiority on MADRS anhedonia factor score and greater Sheehan Disability Scale improvement versus placebo. These are depression-domain and function-domain signals, not objective healthy-adult cognition endpoints.
- Safety: treatment-emergent adverse events were reported in 74.46% at 80 mg, 78.26% at 160 mg, and 67.93% on placebo; treatment-related adverse events were higher in active arms than placebo.
- Interpretation for the wiki: ansofaxine is a real approved/late-stage SNDRI antidepressant signal for MDD symptoms and anhedonia, but the captured trial does not prove brain-fog relief or cognition enhancement in healthy adults.
