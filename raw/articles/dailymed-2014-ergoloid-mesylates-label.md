# DailyMed 2014: Ergoloid mesylates tablet label

DailyMed label for ergoloid mesylates tablets USP, oral, 1 mg. The product contains equal listed portions of dihydroergocristine mesylate, dihydroergocornine mesylate, and dihydroergocryptine mesylate isomers.

Clinical pharmacology: the label says there is no specific evidence clearly establishing the mechanism by which ergoloid mesylates produce mental effects, and no conclusive evidence that the drug particularly affects cerebral arteriosclerosis or cerebrovascular insufficiency.

Indication framing: the label says a proportion of people over 60 with signs and symptoms of idiopathic mental-capacity decline may experience symptomatic relief. It emphasizes careful diagnosis, excluding delirium, systemic disease, neurologic disease, or mood disturbance, and continually reviewing whether initial benefit persists.

Trial/endpoint note: efficacy was evaluated using the Sandoz Clinical Assessment-Geriatric scale. Modest statistically significant changes after 12 weeks were observed across items including mental alertness, confusion, recent memory, orientation, mood/self-care/social items, fatigue, and overall clinical status.

Contraindications and adverse reactions: contraindicated with hypersensitivity and acute or chronic psychosis. Transient nausea and gastric disturbances are reported. Dose listed as 1 mg three times daily, with gradual alleviation and results often not observed for 3-4 weeks.
