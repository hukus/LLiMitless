# Nootropics Survey 2020 Results

- Source type: open-label community survey
- Published: 2021-04-28
- Respondents: 852, mostly from the SSC audience

## Summary

This is the later SSC/ACX survey update. It used an ad hoc Bayesian adjustment on open-label ratings, with some substances resting on only 5-10 data points. The survey is best used as a modern community-signal anchor, not as causal evidence.

The headline value for this branch is the Zembrin/kanna signal, which stood out above many more familiar substances. Modafinil remained a strong comparator, and the survey also exposed useful tolerance and use-pattern details.

## Key signals

- Zembrin was the standout newer substance.
- Of 37 kanna users, 20 used Zembrin and 17 used another kanna product.
- The Zembrin subgroup was rated at 6.88 raw and 6.72 after adjustment.
- Sublingual modafinil was split roughly evenly: 13 thought it was better, 12 worse, among 25 users who had tried it.
- Theacrine had only weak support for reduced tolerance.

## Caveats

- 95% of respondents were SSC readers, so the sample is not representative of the broader nootropics community.
- Open-label ratings remain highly expectation-sensitive.
- Several items were based on very small counts.
