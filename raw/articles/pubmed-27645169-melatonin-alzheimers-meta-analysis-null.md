# Meta-analysis of randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trials of melatonin in Alzheimer's disease

- PMID: 27645169
- Published: 2016
- Source type: meta-analysis of randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trials
- Population: Alzheimer disease
- Ingested: 2026-05-03

## Summary

Meta-analysis of 7 studies with 462 Alzheimer disease patients. Melatonin prolonged total sleep time at night with small effect size, but did not improve cognitive abilities assessed by MMSE or ADAS-Cog. Discontinuation rates were similar between melatonin and placebo.

## Notes

- Important counterweight to newer positive MMSE/global-cognition summaries.
- The practical read is sleep-support signal without reliable Alzheimer cognition improvement.
- This source helps keep the melatonin entity contested rather than upgraded on the basis of one newer positive synthesis.
