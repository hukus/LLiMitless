# Flavonoid derivative 7,8-DHF attenuates TBI pathology via TrkB activation

- PMID: 25661191
- PMCID: PMC4754355
- DOI: 10.1016/j.bbadis.2015.01.018
- Publication: Biochimica et Biophysica Acta, 2015
- Evidence tier: preclinical injury / rodent cognition

## Summary

Rat traumatic brain injury work tested 7,8-DHF at 5 mg/kg intraperitoneally once daily for seven days after fluid percussion injury. The intervention improved Barnes maze memory performance and restored injury-disrupted TrkB phosphorylation, CREB phosphorylation, GAP-43, syntaxin-3, and multiple energy-homeostasis markers including PGC-1alpha, TFAM, COII, AMPK, and SIRT1. A TrkB antagonist counteracted the memory and signaling effects, supporting a TrkB-mediated interpretation within this model.

The most important wiki interpretation is state dependence. This is a recovery-from-injury result, not evidence that a healthy rested adult will gain baseline cognition.
