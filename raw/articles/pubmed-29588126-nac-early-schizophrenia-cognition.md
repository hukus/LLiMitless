# NAC in early phase schizophrenia spectrum disorders

2018 Schizophrenia Research randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled 52-week trial of N-acetylcysteine 3600 mg/day in early phase schizophrenia spectrum disorders, N=60.

NAC improved PANSS total, negative, and disorganized-thought symptom scores, but failed to improve PANSS positive symptoms or BACS cognitive scores. Brain morphology outcomes did not show significant longitudinal treatment effects.

Use in wiki: NAC has formal psychiatric-state data, but the cognition endpoint is negative in this trial. Treat as symptom / glutathione / glutamate-modulation evidence, not as direct cognitive enhancement proof.
