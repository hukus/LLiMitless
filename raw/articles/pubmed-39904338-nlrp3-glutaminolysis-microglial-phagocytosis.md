# NLRP3-mediated glutaminolysis controls microglial phagocytosis to promote Alzheimer's disease progression

- PMID: 39904338
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39904338/
- Ingested: 2026-04-29

## Summary

2025 Immunity primary paper showing that Aβ triggers NLRP3 in APP/PS1 mice, and that chronic NLRP3 inhibition shifts glutamine/glutamate metabolism, increases alpha-ketoglutarate, and improves microglial clearance of Aβ. The mechanism was conserved in human cells. This is the strongest current source for the NLRP3/phagocytosis sub-branch, but it remains preclinical.

## Notes

- Important because it moves NLRP3 from a generic inflammatory switch to a metabolic-phagocytic control point.
- The pharmacologic effect required chronic inhibition, which matters for translation and safety.
