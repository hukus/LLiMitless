# EMA methylthioninium chloride / methylene blue

- Proveblue is a prescription injection containing methylthioninium chloride, the active substance also known as methylene blue.
- EMA positions it as a treatment for methaemoglobinaemia, not a cognition intervention.
- The product remains a medical antidote/diagnostic-adjacent medicine, which matters when readers confuse oral nootropic use with the clinical label.
