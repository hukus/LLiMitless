# BioPQQ cognitive function RCT

2016 randomized, placebo-controlled, double-blind study of pyrroloquinoline quinone disodium salt, 20 mg/day for 12 weeks, in 41 healthy elderly subjects.

Endpoints included Stroop/reverse Stroop selective attention and visual-spatial cognition. The abstract reports a significantly smaller change in Stroop interference ratios in the PQQ group versus placebo and a stratified Touch M improvement only among lower-baseline PQQ participants.

Use in wiki: PQQ has a small product-specific human cognition signal, but sample size, stratification, and sponsor/product identity keep confidence low.
