# The effect of anthocyanins and anthocyanin-rich foods on cognitive function: a meta-analysis of randomized controlled trials

- Source type: systematic review and meta-analysis of randomized controlled trials.
- Publication: GeroScience, online ahead of print 2025.
- Date checked: 2026-05-03.

This meta-analysis evaluated randomized controlled trials of anthocyanin-rich interventions and cognitive outcomes. The abstract reports 59 eligible studies and an overall significant improvement in global cognition versus controls, with SMD 0.46 and 95% CI 0.30 to 0.63.

The abstract frames anthocyanin-rich interventions as relevant to prevention and cognitive-performance support, but the wiki should treat this as a family-level signal. It does not prove that all blueberry products, juices, powders, extracts, capsules, bilberry products, aronia products, tart cherry products, and mixed grape-blueberry products are interchangeable.

Use in the wiki: current broad anthocyanin anchor for the berry branch. It upgrades the family-level visibility while preserving product-specific confidence limits.
