# Acute effects of essential oil blend containing phlai oil on mood among healthy male volunteers: Randomized controlled trial

- PMID: 31618177
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31618177/
- Ingested: 2026-04-30

## Summary

2019 randomized pilot in 38 healthy men tested a single 15-minute hot-steam inhalation of an essential oil blend containing phlai oil versus olive oil. Both groups improved on positive mood and reduced negative mood after inhalation, but the phlai blend only clearly beat olive oil on a modified Rorschach stress measure. The paper supports an acute aromatherapy/stress branch rather than a durable cognition branch.
