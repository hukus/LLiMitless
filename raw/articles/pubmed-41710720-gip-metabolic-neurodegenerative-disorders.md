# Revitalizing GIP: Therapeutic Potential in Metabolic and Neurodegenerative Disorders.

- PMID: 41710720
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41710720/
- Ingested: 2026-04-26

## Summary

Review arguing that GIP is gaining attention for metabolic and neurodegenerative applications; useful as an incretin-axis source.

## Notes

This review highlights the renewed interest in GIP, driven by a deeper understanding of its pleiotropic actions. Beyond the pancreas, GIP promotes lipid storage in adipose tissue, reduces ectopic fat deposition, modulates bone remodeling, influences behavior and brain-related biology, and may matter for metabolic and neurodegenerative disorders.
