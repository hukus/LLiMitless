# Ergogenic Effects of 8 Days of Sceletium Tortuosum Supplementation on Mood, Visual Tracking, and Reaction in Recreationally Trained Men and Women

- DOI: 10.1519/JSC.0000000000003693
- Published: 2020-09
- Journal: Journal of Strength and Conditioning Research
- Source type: randomized controlled trial in healthy young adults
- URL: https://journals.lww.com/nsca-jscr/fulltext/2020/09000/ergogenic_effects_of_8_days_of_sceletium_tortuosum.10.aspx
- Ingested: 2026-05-03

## Summary

Randomized trial in 60 recreationally trained adults aged 20-35. Participants received 25 mg/day Sceletium tortuosum extract or placebo for eight days. The trial reported significantly better complex reactive performance requiring responses to repeated visual stimuli with cognitive load, and better reactive agility, but no benefit on visual tracking performance, simple motor/visual/physical reaction time, mood, alertness, or energy.

## Notes

- Supports a narrow high-load / complex-reactive-performance signal.
- Does not support a broad cognition or mood-enhancement claim.
- No side effects associated with supplementation were reported during the study, but duration was only eight days.
