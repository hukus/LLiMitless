# Kisspeptin sexual and emotional brain processing
- PMID: 28112678
- Randomized controlled human imaging study of kisspeptin in sexual and emotional brain processing.
- Key result: kisspeptin modulated brain processing linked to sexual and emotional functions, not direct cognitive testing.
- Branch readout: supports the view that kisspeptin is a CNS-active endocrine axis, but not yet a cognition enhancer.
