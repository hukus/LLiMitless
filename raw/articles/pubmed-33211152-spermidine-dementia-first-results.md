# The positive effect of spermidine in older adults suffering from dementia: First results of a 3-month trial

- PMID: 33211152
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33211152/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

2021 multicenter preliminary trial in nursing-home residents with mild to moderate dementia. The higher-dose group showed better MMSE performance than the lower-dose group, but the product was a spermidine-rich extract rather than isolated spermidine and the study is still small.
