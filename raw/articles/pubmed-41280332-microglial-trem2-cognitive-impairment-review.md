# Microglial TREM2 and cognitive impairment: insights from Alzheimer's disease with implications for spinal cord injury and AI-assisted therapeutics

- PMID: 41280332
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41280332/
- Ingested: 2026-04-29

## Summary

2025 review arguing that TREM2 is a microglial gatekeeper for neuroinflammation, pathological protein clearance, and disease-associated microglia formation in AD. The paper extends the discussion to spinal cord injury and AI-assisted precision approaches, but the cognition section is still mechanistic and translational rather than interventional.

## Notes

- Useful as the current overview anchor for TREM2 in the cognition branch.
- Does not itself establish a human TREM2-targeted cognition treatment effect.
