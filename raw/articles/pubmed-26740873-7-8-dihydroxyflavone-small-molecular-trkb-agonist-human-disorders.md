# 7,8-dihydroxyflavone, a small molecular TrkB agonist, is useful for treating various BDNF-implicated human disorders

- PMID: 26740873
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26740873/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Notes

2016 review that frames 7,8-dihydroxyflavone (7,8-DHF) as a small-molecule TrkB agonist with potential relevance across BDNF-linked neurologic and psychiatric disorders. It is a mechanistic and translational position paper, not a human cognition trial.

## Relevance

- Useful as a branch overview for how the 7,8-DHF literature was being marketed by 2016.
- Still preclinical / translational rather than evidence of human cognitive efficacy.
- Supports keeping the compound in a comparator lane, not a validated enhancer lane.
