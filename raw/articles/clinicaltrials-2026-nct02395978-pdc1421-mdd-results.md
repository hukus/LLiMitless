# ClinicalTrials.gov NCT02395978: PDC-1421 phase 2 major depressive disorder results

Official ClinicalTrials.gov record for "A Phase II Study of PDC-1421 Capsule to Evaluate the Safety and Efficacy in Patients With Major Depressive Disorder." Sponsor: BioLite, Inc. Overall status: completed. Results were posted in 2020 and the last update posted was 2021-07-22; the record version checked in this pass was dated 2026-05-01.

Design: 72 adults with major depressive disorder. Part I was open-label dose escalation: 1 or 2 PDC-1421 capsules three times daily for 28 days, 6 participants per dose. Part II was randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled, parallel-group: 2 PDC-1421 capsules TID, 1 PDC-1421 plus 1 placebo TID, or 2 placebo capsules TID for 42 days. PDC-1421 is described in the record as a botanical investigational new drug containing extract of Radix Polygalae (Polygala tenuifolia Willd.) as active ingredient.

Mood efficacy signal: in the Part II ITT population, MADRS total score change from baseline to week 6 was -13.19 (SD 9.68; n=21) for 2 PDC-1421 capsules, -9.21 (SD 8.32; n=19) for 1 PDC-1421 plus placebo, and -9.20 (SD 7.98; n=20) for placebo. Week-6 MADRS responders were 11/21, 6/19, and 7/20 respectively.

Safety signal: no deaths and no serious adverse events were posted in the five study groups. Other adverse events affected 11/21 in the high-dose blinded PDC-1421 group, 12/19 in the low-dose blinded PDC-1421 group, and 9/20 in placebo. PDC-1421-associated event terms in the posted table include dry mouth, epigastric discomfort, dizziness, alanine aminotransferase increased, aspartate aminotransferase increased, and blood potassium increased; placebo also had gastrointestinal and palpitations entries.

Relevance: neuropsychiatric exposure and mood-adjacent signal for PDC-1421. It does not test healthy-adult cognition or establish Polygala as a nootropic.
