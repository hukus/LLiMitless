# Epidiolex Cannabidiol Prescribing Information

Prescribing-information PDF for Epidiolex, a purified cannabidiol oral solution. The label indicates Epidiolex for seizures associated with Lennox-Gastaut syndrome or Dravet syndrome in patients 2 years and older in this captured version.

Safety points relevant to cognition/nootropic routing: the label requires liver-enzyme and bilirubin assessment because of hepatocellular-injury risk; somnolence and sedation were materially more common with Epidiolex than placebo in controlled epilepsy studies; CNS depressants and alcohol can potentiate sedation.

Relevance: CBD is not a benign calm-focus supplement by default. Formal safety routing should consider liver monitoring, sedation, drug interactions, and seizure-medication context.
