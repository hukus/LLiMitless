# Omega-3, carotenoid, and vitamin E supplementation in older adults

- Source type: randomized double-blind placebo-controlled clinical trial.
- Publication: Clinical Nutrition, 2022.
- Date checked: 2026-05-11.

This trial enrolled cognitively healthy adults aged at least 65 years and tested a 24-month multi-nutrient supplement containing fish oil, carotenoids, and vitamin E. The active daily dose included 430 mg DHA, 90 mg EPA, 10 mg lutein, 10 mg meso-zeaxanthin, 2 mg zeaxanthin, and 15 mg vitamin E.

After 24 months, the active group made fewer working-memory errors than placebo, with small point-estimate effect sizes. Because the product combined omega-3 fatty acids, xanthophyll carotenoids, and vitamin E, attribution to any one ingredient is not justified.

Use in the wiki: this is useful for the carotenoid watchlist and nutrient-combination layer. It supports tracking xanthophylls but should not be used as isolated [[lutein-zeaxanthin]] proof.
