# PubMed 449002 - Operational assessment of adverse drug reactions

- JAMA 1979 paper on making adverse-drug-reaction attribution more reproducible.
- The key diagnostic axes are previous experience with the drug, alternative etiologies, timing of events, drug levels / overdose evidence, dechallenge, and rechallenge.
- The paper explicitly frames spontaneous ADR identification as nonreproducible subjective judgment unless the decision process is operationalized.
- Relevance for this wiki: a self-study diary should preserve timing, dose change, stop/restart behavior, and competing causes rather than only storing a final causal label.
