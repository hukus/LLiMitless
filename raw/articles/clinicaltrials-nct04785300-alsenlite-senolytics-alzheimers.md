# ALSENLITE: Senolytics for Alzheimer's Disease

- ClinicalTrials.gov ID: NCT04785300
- Sponsor: Mayo Clinic
- Status: active, not recruiting
- Last update posted: 2026-01-22

## Summary

This pilot study evaluates intermittent dasatinib plus quercetin in older adults with MCI or Alzheimer's disease and tau-PET positivity. It is the clearest current randomized clinical pipeline item for the senolytic branch.

## Key Data

- Design: open-label pilot study.
- Population: adults over 55 with probable Alzheimer's disease and Alzheimer's biomarker positivity by tau-PET.
- Intervention: dasatinib 100 mg daily for 2 consecutive days plus quercetin 1000 mg daily for the same 2 days, repeated every 15 days for 6 cycles, totaling 77 days.
- Branch relevance: this is still feasibility-oriented, but it is closer to disease-specific cognition testing than the mixed-phenotype psychiatry protocol.

## Caveats

- No posted results in the registry record.
- Open-label pilot format limits efficacy interpretation.
- Biomarker enrichment improves specificity, but not proof of cognitive benefit.
