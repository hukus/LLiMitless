# Phase-dependent closed-loop deep brain stimulation of the fornix provides bidirectional manipulation of hippocampal theta oscillations

Brain Stimulation 2025 study in five male Lister Hooded rats using the OscillTrack closed-loop algorithm. While animals traversed a linear track, fornix stimulation was triggered at four theta phases and compared with no stimulation plus open-loop replay of the same trains. The stimulation trains were 15 minutes long, delivered biphasically at 50-100 µA and 200 µs.

The key finding is mechanistic and phase-dependent: trough/ascending-phase stimulation amplified hippocampal theta, while peak/descending-phase stimulation suppressed it, and both effects were larger than replayed open-loop stimulation. This is a clear closed-loop control result for theta modulation, but it is still an animal physiology paper, not a human cognitive-outcome study.
