# PubMed 28890301 - C4S Antibody and Tauopathy Memory

## Citation

- Yang S, Cacquevel M, Saksida LM, et al. "Antibody recognizing 4-sulfated chondroitin sulfate proteoglycans restores memory in tauopathy-induced neurodegeneration." Neurobiology of Aging. 2017. DOI: 10.1016/j.neurobiolaging.2017.08.002.
- Source type: peer-reviewed preclinical study.

## Extracted Claims

- Chondroitin sulfate proteoglycans are presented as the main active components of perineuronal nets relevant to plasticity restriction.
- The Cat316 antibody recognizes 4-sulfated chondroitin sulfate proteoglycans.
- Cat316 reduced inhibitory axon-growth effects, partly blocked semaphorin-3A binding, and attenuated PNN formation in the experimental context.
- Perirhinal-cortex Cat316 injection extended long-term object-recognition memory in wild-type mice to 24 hours, similar to ChABC or PNN attenuation.
- In P301S tau mice, Cat316 normalized object-recognition performance at the tested time point.

## Cognition-Relevant Extraction

- Medicinal cognition signal: positive preclinical memory rescue and extension through C4S-targeted antibody manipulation.
- Translation status: no human cognition trial; local antibody manipulation is not a consumer route.
- Signal-strength read: useful specificity comparator for the PNN branch, still low medicinal confidence for human cognition.
- Safety interpretation for the wiki: antibody/sulfation targeting reinforces that PNN intervention effects depend on chemistry, region, and disease model.
