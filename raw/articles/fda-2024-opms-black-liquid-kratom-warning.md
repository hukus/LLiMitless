# FDA Warning on OPMS Black Liquid Kratom

FDA safety alert from July 26, 2024 advising consumers not to consume OPMS Black Liquid Kratom after serious adverse health effects, including a death report. The page states the product label indicates kratom alkaloids mitragynine and 7-hydroxymitragynine.

The FDA states it has not approved prescription or OTC drug products containing kratom, mitragynine, or 7-hydroxymitragynine; says it has serious safety concerns about kratom in dietary supplements and conventional foods; and warns about risks including liver toxicity, seizures, and substance use disorder.

Relevance: kratom/mitragynine is risk-first in a cognitive-improvement molecule wiki even when users describe energy, mood, pain relief, or focus.
