# RGN-259 thymosin beta 4 ophthalmic solution in neurotrophic keratopathy phase III

- PMID: 36613994
- PMCID: PMC9820614
- DOI: 10.3390/ijms24010554
- Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9820614/
- Ingested: 2026-05-04

## Summary

Randomized, placebo-controlled, double-masked phase III trial of 0.1% RGN-259 ophthalmic solution containing thymosin beta 4 in patients with stage 2 or 3 neurotrophic keratopathy. Complete healing after 4 weeks occurred in 6 of 10 RGN-259-treated subjects versus 1 of 8 placebo-treated subjects, a trend rather than conventional statistical significance. Additional healing and symptom measures favored RGN-259 at some time points, and the report states that no significant adverse effects were observed.

## Signal Use

- Medicinal signal: human disease-context ophthalmic repair signal for full-length thymosin beta 4, not the TB-500 fragment.
- Cognition signal: absent; corneal epithelial healing and ocular comfort do not test cognition.
- Safety signal: small, route-specific topical trial; cannot be generalized to injected gray-market TB-500.
