# GlyNAC old-mice brain cognition study

Preclinical aging-brain study testing whether age-associated cognitive decline in old mice is linked to glutathione deficiency, oxidative stress, mitochondrial dysfunction, glucose dysmetabolism, inflammation, and neurotrophic-factor changes.

Design: young 20-week mice were compared with old 90-week mice. Old mice received regular diet or GlyNAC-supplemented diet for 8 weeks.

Findings: old-control mice had cognitive impairment and multiple brain defects. GlyNAC supplementation improved or corrected brain glutathione, oxidative stress, mitochondrial energetics, autophagy/mitophagy, glucose transporter, inflammation, genomic damage, and neurotrophic-factor measures, and improved cognitive function.

Use in wiki: mechanistic preclinical support for the aging-brain hypothesis. It should not be treated as human cognition proof.
