# Kynurenine pathway dysregulation in cognitive impairment and dementia

- Journal: GeroScience
- Publication date: 2025-05-08
- Study type: systematic review and meta-analysis

## Captured takeaways

- Reviewed 98 eligible studies up to October 2023 across subjective cognitive decline, MCI, and dementia.
- AD dementia meta-analyses showed lower CSF tryptophan, 3-hydroxykynurenine, anthranilic acid, and quinolinic acid, with higher CSF kynurenic acid.
- Blood meta-analyses showed lower tryptophan, kynurenic acid, xanthurenic acid, and 3-hydroxyanthranilic acid in AD dementia.
- MCI showed similar directions for some metabolites, but the evidence base was smaller.
- Associations with cognitive test scores were mostly inconclusive and generally non-significant.
- Interpretation for the wiki: the dementia story is compartment-specific and does not support a simple "more quinolinic acid = more cognitive decline" rule.
