# NCT04228666 - Hope Biosciences autologous MSC therapy for Alzheimer's disease

- Registry source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04228666
- Sponsor: Hope Biosciences LLC
- Condition: Alzheimer's disease
- Design: phase 1/2a, open-label, non-randomized

## Summary

Stem-cell rather than exosome protocol, but relevant as a live regenerative-medicine comparator in AD. The study uses four IV infusions of autologous adipose-derived MSCs.

## Notes

This helps keep the wiki's stem-cell lane distinct from exosome and secretome claims while still recognizing that all three often get marketed together.
