# Inhalation of phlai-containing essential oil enhances cognition: a comparison with olive oil in healthy adults

- PMID: 41614069
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41614069/
- Ingested: 2026-04-30

## Summary

2026 double-blind randomized trial in 40 healthy men tested a single 15-minute inhalation of a phlai-containing essential-oil blend versus olive oil. The blend was GC-MS characterized, with major constituents including alpha-terpinolene, p-cymene, alpha-terpinene, sabinene, terpinene-4-ol, and 1,8-cineole. Compared with olive oil, the phlai blend produced greater gains in working memory and executive function after acute inhalation, with broader within-group gains on MoCA, digit span, verbal fluency, and word-list learning.
