# Federal Register 2023 Cylert pemoline approval withdrawal

## Captured takeaways

- The Federal Register notice says FDA withdrew approval of NDA 016832 for CYLERT pemoline tablets and NDA 017703 for CYLERT pemoline chewable tablets, effective May 23, 2023.
- The notice records the original FDA approvals: January 27, 1975 for CYLERT tablets and January 30, 1976 for CYLERT chewable tablets, both for ADHD treatment.
- The notice states that FDA issued a 2005 Postmarket Drug Safety Information communication concluding that the overall liver-toxicity risk of CYLERT and generic pemoline products outweighed their benefits.
- It also states that all holders of approved pemoline applications ceased marketing the products at that time.
- FDA contacted Abbott in 2021 and requested withdrawal of the CYLERT approvals due to the risk of liver toxicity.
- The notice states that distribution of CYLERT without an approved application is illegal and subject to regulatory action.
- Interpretation for the wiki: pemoline's current status is not merely "old drug." It has a formal FDA approval-withdrawal trail tied to liver-toxicity risk.
