# Hydergine in multi-infarct dementia

PubMed-indexed placebo-controlled double-blind trial of Hydergine in multi-infarct dementia. The study is relevant because it anchors hydergine in vascular/clinical impairment rather than healthy enhancement.

The abstract and title frame the compound as a dementia intervention, not a general cognitive-improvement compound for unimpaired users.

Relevance: older vascular-dementia source for the legacy vasoactive branch.
