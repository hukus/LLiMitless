# PubMed 7249508 - Naranjo adverse drug reactions probability scale

- 1981 Clinical Pharmacology & Therapeutics paper that standardizes ADR causality with a 10-question score.
- The core diary-relevant items are temporal sequence, improvement on discontinuation, recurrence on readministration, alternative causes, placebo response, dose-response, prior similar reaction, and objective confirmation.
- The scale was designed to improve consistency, not to act as a universal gold standard for every setting.
- Relevance for this wiki: the raw event log should stay detailed enough that a later reviewer can reconstruct those 10 questions.
