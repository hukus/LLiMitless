# Does tDCS over prefrontal cortex improve episodic memory retrieval? Potential importance of time of day

- PMID: 30035659
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30035659/
- Published: 2018-08
- Study type: human tDCS timing / episodic memory experiment
- Key finding: post-hoc analyses across four experiments suggested that anodal prefrontal tDCS improved recollection accuracy during morning sessions, with weaker or null effects in other timing conditions.

## Notes

- The authors explicitly framed time of day as potentially important for replication of the tDCS effect.
- Experiment 4 randomized participants to morning or afternoon sessions and again favored morning stimulation.
- This is timing evidence, not chronotype-stratified evidence, but it supports the same response-window hypothesis.
