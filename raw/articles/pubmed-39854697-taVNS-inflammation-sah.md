# Auricular vagus nerve stimulation for mitigation of inflammation and vasospasm in subarachnoid hemorrhage: a single-institution randomized controlled trial

- PMID: 39854697
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39854697/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Randomized trial in subarachnoid hemorrhage suggesting auricular vagus nerve stimulation may reduce inflammation and vasospasm, with potential outcome benefits. This is disease-context evidence only, but it matters for the inflammation branch of the taVNS graph.

## Notes

- Single-institution randomized controlled trial.
- The healthy-cognition relevance is indirect.
- Useful mainly as a mechanistic inflammation and outcome signal.
