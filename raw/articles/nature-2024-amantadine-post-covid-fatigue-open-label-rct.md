# Amantadine for post-COVID fatigue open-label randomized trial

2024 Scientific Reports randomized open-label trial of amantadine for post-COVID-19 fatigue.

The study randomized 66 adults with post-COVID fatigue to amantadine or no treatment. Sixty-two completed the two-week study. The intervention was amantadine 100 mg twice daily. Fatigue was measured using the Visual Analog Fatigue Scale and Fatigue Severity Scale before and after treatment.

Both fatigue scales improved more in the amantadine group than in the no-treatment control group after two weeks, with reported between-group differences favoring amantadine. The trial did not use placebo or blinding, did not center a dedicated cognition endpoint, and included a short follow-up window. One participant stopped amantadine because of severe nausea and abdominal pain; other reported side effects were described as transient/tolerable.

Interpretation for the wiki: amantadine belongs in the post-viral fatigue/brain-fog watchlist as a fatigue-state and arousal/NMDA-dopamine hypothesis, not as proven direct cognitive repair. The open-label no-placebo design makes expectancy and regression-to-mean serious caveats.
