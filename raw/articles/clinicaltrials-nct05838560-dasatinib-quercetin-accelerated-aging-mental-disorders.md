# Dasatinib Plus Quercetin for Accelerated Aging in Mental Disorders

- ClinicalTrials.gov ID: NCT05838560
- Sponsor: Washington University School of Medicine
- Status: recruiting
- Last update posted: 2025-04-02

## Summary

This is a pilot open-label study of dasatinib plus quercetin in older adults with schizophrenia, schizoaffective disorder, or treatment-resistant depression. It is relevant because it moves the senolytic branch into an accelerated-aging psychiatry phenotype and now has a 1-year protocol with MRI and repeated blood sampling.

## Key Data

- Design: pilot open-label study.
- Population: older adults with schizophrenia, schizoaffective disorder, or treatment-resistant depression.
- Aim: test safety/feasibility and examine SASP changes.
- Protocol shape: about 1 year total participation, with 8 total medication doses across weeks 1 to 4, baseline MRI, week-10 MRI, and blood draws at baseline, weeks 1, 2, 3, 4, 10, and endpoint.
- Branch relevance: age-related cognitive decline is a target concept, and the protocol now explicitly includes neuropsychological functioning, functional status, brain aging markers, and clinical symptoms.

## Caveats

- Open-label and pilot-sized.
- No posted results in the registry record.
- This remains a pipeline signal rather than an efficacy result.
