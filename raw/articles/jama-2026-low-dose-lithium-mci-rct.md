# Low-Dose Lithium for Mild Cognitive Impairment: A Pilot Randomized Clinical Trial

- DOI: 10.1001/jamaneurol.2026.0072
- JAMA Neurology: https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jamaneurology/fullarticle/2845746
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

This 80-participant 2-year pilot RCT in older adults with MCI tested low-dose lithium carbonate versus placebo. None of the six coprimary outcomes met the prespecified significance threshold, but verbal memory declined more slowly in the lithium arm and exploratory analyses suggested larger effects in amyloid-positive participants. The trial supports feasibility and safety, not a settled efficacy claim.
