# Cathepsin B in Neurodegeneration of Alzheimer’s Disease, Traumatic Brain Injury, and Related Brain Disorders

- PMCID: PMC7261628
- PMC: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7261628/
- Ingested: 2026-04-29

## Summary

2020 review mapping cathepsin B across AD, TBI, ischemia, and related neurodegeneration. It argues that cathepsin B participates in cognitive and behavioral deficits and may be a therapeutic target, but the human exercise-mediator story is still mixed rather than settled.

## Notes

- The review spans human biomarker findings plus animal knockout and inhibitor studies.
- It links lysosomal leakage and cytosolic cathepsin B to cell death, inflammation, and behavioral deficits.
- For exercise biology, the review treats cathepsin B as a plausible mediator of cognitive benefit.
- The paper supports mechanism generation, not a direct intervention recommendation.
