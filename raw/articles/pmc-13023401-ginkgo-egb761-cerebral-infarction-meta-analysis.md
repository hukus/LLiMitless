# Ginkgo biloba extract EGb 761 in patients with dementia and a history of cerebral infarction - meta-analysis of pooled data from randomised clinical trials

- PMID: 41908799
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41908799/
- PMC: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13023401/
- Ingested: 2026-04-30

## Summary

2026 pooled-data meta-analysis of four randomized placebo-controlled trials in mild-to-moderate dementia after imaging-confirmed cerebral infarction. Across 488 patients, EGb 761 at 240 mg/day for 22-24 weeks significantly improved cognition, activities of daily living, and global clinical impression versus placebo, with adverse-event and adverse-drug-reaction rates similar to placebo. The benefit is clinically meaningful in a cerebrovascular-dementia phenotype, but the evidence comes from a sponsor-homogeneous trial set.
