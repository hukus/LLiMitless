# Stem Cell, Granulocyte-Colony Stimulating Factor and/or Dihexa to Promote Limb Function Recovery in a Rat Sciatic Nerve Damage-Repair Model: Experimental Animal Studies.

- PMID: 34703584
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34703584/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Preclinical peripheral-nerve repair study in rats where Dihexa was part of a combination strategy; motor recovery improved in some MSC + Dihexa groups, but this is injury-repair evidence rather than direct cognition evidence.
