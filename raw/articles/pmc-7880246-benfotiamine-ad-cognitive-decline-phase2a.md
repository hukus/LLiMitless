# Source Digest

Type: randomized placebo-controlled phase IIa clinical trial.

Publication date: 2020-10-16 online.

Key extraction:
- Intervention: oral benfotiamine for 12 months versus placebo.
- Population: 70 participants randomized, with amnestic mild cognitive impairment or mild dementia due to Alzheimer disease.
- Primary clinical outcome: ADAS-Cog.
- Main result: ADAS-Cog worsening was 43% lower in the benfotiamine group than placebo, p = 0.125, so the primary cognitive signal was directionally positive but not conventionally significant.
- Secondary outcomes: CDR worsening was 77% lower in the benfotiamine group, p = 0.034, with stronger effect in APOE epsilon 4 non-carriers.
- Biomarkers: benfotiamine reduced increases in AGE, p = 0.044, and an exploratory FDG-PET pattern score showed a treatment effect at one year, p = 0.002.
- Safety: benfotiamine was reported safe in the trial.

Wiki signal:
- Medicinal signal: low-moderate for disease-context thiamine-derivative support, not healthy enhancement.
- Confidence limiter: phase IIa, small sample, primary endpoint only near-significant, exploratory biomarker analyses.
