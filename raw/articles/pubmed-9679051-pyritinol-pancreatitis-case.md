# Acute pancreatitis due to pyritinol

PubMed-indexed case report of recurrent acute pancreatitis associated with pyritinol. A 23-year-old man had three episodes after pyritinol intake, and controlled rechallenge with a single dose triggered a fourth episode.

The authors interpreted the case as consistent with an immune-mediated drug reaction based on clinical timing and immunologic testing.

Relevance: pyritinol is not a trivial vitamin-like nootropic. Safety routing should include rare but serious immune-mediated reactions.
