# Actions of the TrkB Agonist Antibody ZEB85 in Regulating the Architecture and Synaptic Plasticity in Hippocampal Neurons

- PMID: 35845610
- PMCID: PMC9280622
- DOI: 10.3389/fnmol.2022.945348

## Notes

2022 Frontiers in Molecular Neuroscience paper on a fully human TrkB agonist antibody. The abstract reports that ZEB85 modulates hippocampal neuron architecture, activity, and synaptic plasticity under physiological conditions, and that it prevents beta-amyloid toxicity in cultured hippocampal neurons in a manner comparable to BDNF. The same abstract explicitly notes that recent side-by-side comparative studies failed to show specificity for several small-molecule TrkB agonists, which is why the antibody lane matters.

## Relevance

- Strongest source for the biologic TrkB-agonist comparator lane.
- Useful as a specificity-control example, not a cognition lead.
