# Vinpocetine, cognition, and epilepsy

- Source: PubMed
- PMID: 33957389
- DOI: 10.1016/j.yebeh.2021.107988
- Ingested: 2026-05-15

## Summary

This 2021 paper directly tested whether vinpocetine could enhance cognition in healthy adult volunteers or people with focal epilepsy. The healthy-volunteer arm used a double-blind randomized crossover design in eight adults with single oral doses of placebo, 10 mg, 20 mg, and 60 mg. The epilepsy arm used a double-blind randomized crossover single-dose test in eight patients with focal epilepsy, followed by one month of open-label 20 mg three-times-daily dosing.

The neuropsychological battery included computerized and non-computerized tests. The authors reported no significant cognitive benefits in healthy volunteers or in patients with epilepsy. Vinpocetine was well tolerated in this small study, but blood levels of vinpocetine and its active metabolite were substantially lower in humans than levels reached in animal models at effective doses.

## Interpretation

Use this as the direct healthy-volunteer counterweight for vinpocetine. It does not prove vinpocetine can never help cognition, because the study was small and pharmacokinetic exposure may have been low, but it does prevent the wiki from treating animal memory data, older dementia use, or supplement anecdotes as confirmed healthy-adult cognitive enhancement.
