# Caffeine and Taurine in Elite Boxers RCT

- PMID: 36297081.
- PMCID: PMC9610400.
- DOI: 10.3390/nu14204399.
- Journal: Nutrients, 2022.
- Ingested: 2026-05-03.

## Summary

Double-blind randomized crossover study in 20 elite male boxers. Conditions were caffeine 6 mg/kg, taurine 3 g, caffeine plus taurine, or placebo, followed by Wingate, balance, agility, and Stroop cognitive measures after fatigue.

The combined caffeine-plus-taurine condition improved several power, agility, balance, and neutral reaction-time measures compared with placebo and single-agent conditions. Taurine alone had some physical-performance signals but the main cognitive result was combination-led.

## Interpretation

Use this as a caffeine-combination / fatigue-after-exercise source. It does not establish independent taurine cognition benefit, but it explains why taurine remains salient in energy-drink and preworkout contexts.
