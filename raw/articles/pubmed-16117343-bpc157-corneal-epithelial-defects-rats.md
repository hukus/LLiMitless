# Gastric pentadecapeptide BPC 157 promotes corneal epithelial defects healing in rats

- PMID: 16117343
- Journal: Coll Antropol, 2005
- Source type: preclinical rat ophthalmology study
- Ingested: 2026-05-06

## Summary

Rat corneal-injury study testing topical BPC-157 eye drops after epithelial debridement. Forty-eight rats were assigned to control or three topical BPC-157 concentrations. The abstract reports faster closure of corneal epithelial defects in the ng and microg treatment groups, with lesions disappearing by 40-48 hours versus persistence in controls at 48 hours.

## Key points

- Model: unilateral corneal epithelial defects in rats.
- Intervention: topical BPC-157 at 2 pg/mL, 2 ng/mL, or 2 ug/mL, started immediately and repeated every 8 hours up to 40 hours.
- Signal: accelerated epithelial healing in the 2 ng/mL and 2 ug/mL groups.
- Limit: animal corneal-healing model only; no human ocular data and no cognition endpoint.

## Signal Use

- Ocular-health signal: specific support for a corneal epithelial-healing hypothesis.
- Breadth signal: shows that the BPC-157 tissue-repair literature includes an ophthalmic lane, not just tendon or gut claims.
- Use limit: preclinical only; does not establish human ophthalmic efficacy, retinal benefit, or vision enhancement.
