# Fisetin senotherapeutic healthspan and lifespan study

- Source type: PubMed-indexed preclinical senotherapeutic study.
- Publication: EBioMedicine, 2018.
- PMID: 30279143.
- DOI: 10.1016/j.ebiom.2018.09.015.
- Date checked: 2026-05-03.

This study screened flavonoids for senolytic activity in senescent murine and human fibroblasts and identified fisetin as a top senotherapeutic candidate. Fisetin was then tested in progeroid and aged wild-type mice for senescence markers, age-related pathology, healthspan, and lifespan.

Use in the wiki: senolytic mechanism anchor for fisetin. It helps explain longevity-community salience but does not provide human cognitive efficacy.
