# High-Frequency Repetitive Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation on Overall Cognition in Patients With Poststroke Cognitive Impairment: A Systematic Review and Meta-analysis

- PMID: 38113027
- Published: 2024-05-01
- Source: PubMed

## Summary

Systematic review/meta-analysis of high-frequency rTMS for post-stroke cognitive impairment.

## Notes

- 61 RCTs with 4,012 patients were included.
- MoCA, MMSE, P300 amplitude, LOTCA, and total effective rate favored high-frequency rTMS.
- Fifty studies selected the DLPFC as the stimulation site.
- Useful as a high-volume protocol reference point for cognition-relevant rTMS in rehabilitation populations.
