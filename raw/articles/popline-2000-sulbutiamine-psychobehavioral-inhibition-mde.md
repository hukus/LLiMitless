# Source Digest

Type: multicenter randomized double-blind placebo-controlled trial abstract record.

Publication date: 2000.

Key extraction:
- Intervention: sulbutiamine / Arcalion 200, 600 mg per day, added to adjusted clomipramine 75-150 mg/day for 8 weeks.
- Population: inpatients with DSM-III-R major depressive episode and psycho-behavioral inhibition.
- Main depression scales: MADRS, HAM-A, and CGI showed comparable major-depressive-episode improvement between sulbutiamine and placebo at 4 weeks.
- Psycho-behavioral inhibition scales: Sheehan Disability Scale, Norris visual analog scale, and Depressive Psychomotor Retardation Scale were used to track social, professional, family, emotional, affective, cognitive, and behavioral impairment.
- Main wiki-relevant result: at 4 weeks, sulbutiamine-treated patients were reported as significantly less incapacitated than placebo across facets of psycho-behavioral inhibition, despite no antidepressant effect.
- Safety statement: treatment groups were comparable, and the abstract states sulbutiamine had not induced inappropriate behavior including suicide attempts or mania in that trial.

Wiki signal:
- Medicinal signal: low clinical-state signal for depressive psycho-behavioral inhibition / functional activation.
- Confidence limiter: older inpatient depression adjunct trial, not healthy-adult cognition; full methods and exact effect sizes were not captured.
