# NCCIH 2026 berberine weight-loss and safety context note

NCCIH frames berberine as an internet-visible supplement promoted for weight loss, while cautioning that preliminary human studies do not yet provide enough rigorous clinical-trial evidence to determine weight-loss effectiveness.

Interpretation for the wiki: this is a useful official guardrail against converting berberine's metabolic popularity into a cognition or brain-fog recommendation. Berberine can belong in a glucose-stability / metabolic-state watchlist, but it should not be promoted as a proven "natural GLP-1," weight-loss drug, or healthy-adult cognitive enhancer from social-media visibility.
