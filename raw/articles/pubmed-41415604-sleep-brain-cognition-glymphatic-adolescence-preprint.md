# Sleep effects on brain, cognition, and mental health during adolescence are mediated by the glymphatic system

- PMID: 41415604
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41415604/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

2025 arXiv preprint in the ABCD cohort. Insufficient sleep was associated with greater perivascular space burden, poorer cognition, and worse mental health, and PVS burden partially mediated the sleep-to-cognition link. This strengthens the glymphatic mechanism story but is observational rather than interventional.
