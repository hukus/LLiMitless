- NCT04299009: Bright Light Therapy for Residual Daytime Symptoms Associated With Obstructive Sleep Apnea.
- Status: completed; results posted on 2025-02-03.
- Sponsor: VA Office of Research and Development.
- Design: randomized, crossover, single-blind participant masking; active bright-light therapy glasses versus red-spectrum sham glasses.
- Sample: 15 veterans with CPAP- or BiPAP-adherent OSA plus residual sleepiness and depressive symptoms.
- Primary outcomes: feasibility/retention and Epworth Sleepiness Scale.
- Secondary outcome: FOSQ-10.
- Publication linked by registry: Soreca I, Arnold N, Dombrovski AY. Bright light therapy for CPAP-resistant OSA symptoms. J Clin Sleep Med. 2024;20(2):211-219.
- Why it matters: direct state-rescue evidence for bright light in residual sleepiness rather than rested-baseline enhancement, and a concrete registry anchor for the sleep-loss / circadian side of the stack.
