# SAGE 2024: Centella aged Tg2576 and wild-type mouse cognition/metabolome

This 2024 Journal of Alzheimer's Disease Reports open-access study tested Centella asiatica water extract in aged Tg2576 and wild-type mice.

Design: 20-month-old female Tg2576 mice and wild-type littermates received Centella asiatica herb water extract at 0, 200, or 1000 mg/kg/day by mouth for 3-5 weeks. The study compared cognitive function, hippocampal amyloid beta plaque burden, and hippocampal metabolite profile.

The abstract states that prior work had reported attenuation of cognitive decline in murine AD/aging models and that this study explored hippocampal metabolome changes associated with cognitive modulation and amyloid plaque load.

Interpretation for the wiki: this supports biological plausibility and metabolomic target tracking, but it is preclinical and should not raise the human cognition signal above low.

Relevance: preclinical mechanism support for [[centella-asiatica]].
