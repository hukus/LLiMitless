# Macular pigment lutein, zeaxanthin, meso-zeaxanthin and cognition review

- Source type: literature review with clinical-trial emphasis.
- Publication: Journal of Alzheimer's Disease, 2022.
- Date checked: 2026-05-11.

This review examined whether lutein, zeaxanthin, and meso-zeaxanthin supplementation affects adult cognition. The review included eight clinical trials as the strongest evidence layer and summarized observational associations between macular pigment / blood carotenoids and cognitive performance.

The review reported that macular pigment measures are often positively associated with adult cognitive performance, that higher blood or macular pigment levels tend to associate with better cognition, and that clinical trials of L + Z or L + Z + MZ are associated with improved cognition in adults. The authors also suggested that vision-related effects may mediate part of the cognition relationship.

Use in the wiki: this supports a macular-pigment / visual-cognition framing for [[lutein-zeaxanthin]], not a fast stimulant-like nootropic framing.
