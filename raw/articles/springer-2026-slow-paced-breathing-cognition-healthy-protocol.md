# A systematic review protocol for slow-paced breathing in healthy populations

- Source: Systematic Reviews
- Published: 2025-12-07
- Version of record: 2026-01-12
- DOI: 10.1186/s13643-025-03004-w
- PROSPERO: CRD42024615253
- Ingested: 2026-05-12

## Summary

Protocol for a systematic review of slow-paced breathing in healthy adult populations, focused on cognition and mechanisms. Planned searches include MEDLINE, Embase, Cochrane Central, PsycINFO, CINAHL, Web of Science, gray literature, clinical-trial registries, and citation tracking.

Eligible studies must test slow-paced breathing or breath-pacing interventions against a control or comparator group and measure cognitive-task performance. The protocol excludes pediatric and animal studies and excludes combined interventions such as breathing plus exercise, meditation, cognitive training, or yoga when those combinations would confound attribution.

## Cognitive-Relevance Extraction

- Evidence layer: review protocol, not efficacy result.
