# Higenamine exercise-performance randomized trial

2021 Frontiers in Psychology randomized double-blind placebo-controlled trial of a one-component higenamine supplement in 12 recreational female basketball players. Participants received 75 mg/day higenamine for 21 days or placebo.

The study assessed body composition, resting metabolic rate, exercise testing, free fatty acids, blood pressure, safety labs, and cardiopulmonary/echocardiography parameters. It did not find improved cardiopulmonary exercise fitness or weight-loss outcomes. Reported adverse events were transient headache in both groups and dry mouth in one higenamine participant.

Use in the wiki: direct human single-ingredient higenamine trial anchor, but it is exercise/safety oriented rather than cognition oriented. It lowers confidence in broad stimulant/ergogenic claims and does not establish nootropic efficacy.
