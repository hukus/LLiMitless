# Effects of taVNS on physiological responses and cognitive performance during a mental stressor

- PMID: 40908377
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40908377/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Randomized study in 41 healthy volunteers. taVNS increased RMSSD and sympathetic electrodermal activity during stimulation but did not change PASAT performance, self-reported stress or anxiety, or the physiological stress response. The study suggests autonomic modulation without acute behavioral cognitive benefit.

## Notes

- 15 min left cymba conchae stimulation before and during a socially evaluated PASAT.
- Sham stimulation used the ear lobe.
- Cognitive endpoint was working memory and divided attention under acute stress.
- Null for behavioral performance, despite autonomic changes.
