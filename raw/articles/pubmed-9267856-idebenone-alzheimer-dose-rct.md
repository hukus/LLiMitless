# A controlled study of 2 doses of idebenone in the treatment of Alzheimer's disease

PubMed-indexed prospective randomized double-blind placebo-controlled multicenter study of idebenone in 300 patients with mild-to-moderate dementia of the Alzheimer type. Patients were randomized to placebo, idebenone 30 mg three times daily, or idebenone 90 mg three times daily for six months.

The abstract reports superiority of the 90 mg three-times-daily dose on ADAS-Total and ADAS-Cog at month 6, with inconspicuous safety findings. The result is an older clinical-dementia signal for a CoQ analog, not healthy-adult cognitive enhancement evidence.

Relevance: positive legacy idebenone signal that must be balanced against later negative AD data.
