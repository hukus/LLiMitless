# Epitalon in illegal pharmaceutical preparations

- PMID: 25535022
- Year: 2015
- Journal: Drug Test Anal.
- Evidence type: forensic / analytical letter

## Key points

PubMed indexes a 2015 letter describing Epitalon detected by LC-MS in two illegal pharmaceutical preparations, framed as a supposed treatment for cancer, old age, and retinitis pigmentosa. This is a sourcing/adulteration signal: the peptide appears in illicit products, but the paper does not establish approved clinical use, modern safety, or efficacy.

## Wiki read

For the wiki, this is useful because it shows Epitalon is present in unregulated or illegal product channels, supporting the sourcing-risk narrative alongside FDA's compounding-risk pages. It does not add a cognition endpoint.
