# Intranasal insulin for improving cognitive function in multiple sclerosis

- PMID: 40253245
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40253245/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Phase Ib/II randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled MS cognition trial. Intranasal insulin was safe and well tolerated, but it did not beat placebo on SDMT or secondary cognitive outcomes.

## Notes

The study randomized 105 people with MS to 10 IU BID, 20 IU BID, or placebo for 24 weeks using the ViaNase controlled particle dispersion system. The null result matters because it is a modern, cautionary extension of the intranasal-insulin story into another neurocognitive phenotype.
