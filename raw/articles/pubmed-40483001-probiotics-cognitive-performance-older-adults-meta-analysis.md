# The role of probiotics in the improvement of cognitive performance of older adults: a meta-analysis

- PMID: 40483001
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40483001/
- Ingested: 2026-04-30

## Summary

2025 meta-analysis of 20 randomized controlled trials in older adults. Pooled effects trended positive for cognitive performance and inflammation reduction, but neither reached significance and heterogeneity was high.

## Notes

The abstract reports a pooled cognitive effect size of SMD 0.19 (95% CI -0.13 to 0.52) and an inflammation effect size of SMD -0.44 (95% CI -0.94 to 0.06). This is a broad older-adult signal, not a validated responder-specific effect.
