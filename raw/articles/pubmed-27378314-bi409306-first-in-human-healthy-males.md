# First-in-human study assessing safety, tolerability and pharmacokinetics of BI 409306, a selective phosphodiesterase 9A inhibitor, in healthy males

- PMID: 27378314
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27378314/
- Ingested: 2026-04-29

## Summary

2016 randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled single-center phase 1 study in healthy male volunteers. BI 409306 showed rapid absorption, short elimination, and generally acceptable tolerability across single rising doses. The study is a PK and safety bridge, not a cognition efficacy trial.

## Key points

- Doses: single rising oral doses from 0.5 mg to 500 mg, tablet or oral solution.
- Population: healthy male extensive metabolizers and poor metabolizers of CYP2C19.
- Safety: adverse events were mostly mild to moderate; no serious adverse events occurred.
- Exposure: poor metabolizers had higher exposure at the same dose.
- Relevance: establishes early human PK and safety for later PDE9 cognition trials.
