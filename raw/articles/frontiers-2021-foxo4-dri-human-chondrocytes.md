# Frontiers 2021 FOXO4-DRI human chondrocytes

- Source type: Peer-reviewed in vitro human cell study.
- Publication: Frontiers in Bioengineering and Biotechnology, 2021-04-29; 9:677576.
- DOI: 10.3389/fbioe.2021.677576.
- Date checked: 2026-05-06.

This brief research report tests FOXO4-DRI in expanded human chondrocytes, a cartilage-repair-relevant cell context where in vitro expansion can increase senescence. The paper reports that FOXO4-DRI selectively removes senescent cells from expanded human chondrocyte cultures.

The important limitation is that clearance of senescent cells did not automatically restore the cartilage-forming capacity of the expanded chondrocytes. This makes the paper useful as a counterweight against assuming that senolysis alone repairs tissue function.

Use in the wiki: human-cell translational source and limitation anchor. It supports selective senescent-cell clearance in vitro, but not clinical cartilage repair, anti-aging, or cognition efficacy.
