# Successful closed-loop neurofeedback alpha frequency modulation enhances the temporal dynamics of attention

NeuroImage 2026 study in 108 healthy adults. Participants completed five EEG-based individual alpha frequency neurofeedback sessions or an active placebo control and then performed the Attention Network Test after each session. Learners who successfully increased alpha frequency showed faster responses, better attentional efficiency, stronger cue facilitation, and shorter alpha event-related desynchronization latency.

The behavioral effect is real but conditional: the paper reports benefit in NF learners, not across the full neurofeedback group. This makes it a useful adaptive-neuromodulation signal for attention, but not a blanket closed-loop win.
