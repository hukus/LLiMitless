# Symptomatic and cognitive effects of D-amino acid oxidase inhibitors in patients with schizophrenia: a meta-analysis of double-blind randomized controlled trials

- PMID: 40328785
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40328785/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

This 2025 meta-analysis pooled five double-blind randomized trials of DAO inhibitors in schizophrenia, mostly sodium benzoate plus one luvadaxistat trial. It reported modest benefits on PANSS and a positive pooled cognitive signal, but the class was heterogeneous and the apparent signal likely depends on compound, dose, and phenotype.
