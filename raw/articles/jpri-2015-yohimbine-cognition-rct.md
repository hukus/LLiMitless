# Yohimbine cognitive function and working-memory RCT

2015 randomized controlled clinical trial of yohimbine in 20 healthy young volunteers, published in Journal of Pharmaceutical Research International. The article tested cognitive function, psychomotor performance, and working memory.

The source is small and not enough to treat yohimbine as a robust enhancer. It is useful because it shows why yohimbine appears in nootropic discourse, while other evidence keeps the route adrenergic and anxiety-risk constrained.

Relevance: low-confidence formal cognition anchor for yohimbine.
