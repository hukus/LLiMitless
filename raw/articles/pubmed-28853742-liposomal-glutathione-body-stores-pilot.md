# PubMed 28853742 liposomal glutathione body-stores pilot note

2018 European Journal of Clinical Nutrition pilot study titled "Oral supplementation with liposomal glutathione elevates body stores of glutathione and markers of immune function."

Twelve healthy adults took oral liposomal glutathione at 500 or 1000 mg/day for 1 month. The study measured glutathione levels in whole blood, erythrocytes, plasma, and PBMCs, plus oxidative-stress and immune-function markers.

Reported results: glutathione levels increased after 1 week, with maximum reported increases around 40% in whole blood, 25% in erythrocytes, 28% in plasma, and 100% in PBMCs after 2 weeks. Oxidative-stress markers decreased, and immune-function markers such as NK-cell cytotoxicity and lymphocyte proliferation increased. The small sample limited dose comparisons.

Interpretation for the wiki: this supports product/form-specific oral liposomal glutathione biomarker movement, not cognitive efficacy. Route it through [[glutathione-products]] and [[redox-mitochondrial-cofactor-cognition-watchlist]], and keep it separate from NAC or GlyNAC precursor evidence.
