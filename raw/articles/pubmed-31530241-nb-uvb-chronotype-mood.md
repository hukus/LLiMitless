# Narrow-band ultraviolet B exposures improve mood in healthy individuals differently depending on chronotype

- PMID: 31530241
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31530241/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Evening preference amplified mood improvement after repeated NB UV-B exposure in a small healthy-women pilot. The result is not a cognition trial, but it is a useful chronotype-sensitive light-response datapoint.

## Notes

- Eleven healthy women received four afternoon full-body NB UV-B exposures.
- Mood improved over the five days after exposure.
- The more evening-oriented the participant, the greater the improvement in wellbeing.
- IL-6 fell and 25(OH)D3 rose during the exposure period.
- This is a small pilot and should be treated as hypothesis-generating.
