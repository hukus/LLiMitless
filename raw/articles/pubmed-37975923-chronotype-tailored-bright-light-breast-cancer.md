# Effects of chronotype-tailored bright light intervention on post-treatment symptoms and quality of life in breast cancer survivors

- PMID: 37975923
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37975923/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Chronotype-tailored bright light did not separate cleanly from control at the between-group level, but within-group symptom improvements were observed. The study is useful mostly as a reminder that chronotype-tailored light is not automatically a large-effect intervention.

## Notes

- 30 post-treatment breast cancer survivors were randomized.
- Light was 30 minutes daily at 12,000 lx or dim red light at 5 lx.
- Timing was either morning or evening based on chronotype.
- There were no significant between-group differences on symptoms or quality of life.
- Within each group, sleep disturbance, fatigue, depressive mood, cognitive dysfunction, and function improved over time.
