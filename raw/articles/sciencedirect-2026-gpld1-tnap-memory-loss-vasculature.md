# Liver exerkine reverses aging- and Alzheimer's-related memory loss via vasculature

- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41713415/

- Source: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S009286742600111X

## Summary

Cell 2026 GPLD1 mechanism paper. The authors identify brain vasculature as a mediator of GPLD1's cognitive benefits and name tissue-nonspecific alkaline phosphatase (TNAP) on brain endothelial cells as a GPLD1 substrate. Increased cerebrovascular TNAP impaired blood-brain transport and cognition in young mice, while GPLD1 elevation or TNAP inhibition improved BBB function and cognition in aging and Alzheimer's disease mouse models. This is an important mechanistic advance for the liver-to-brain exercise axis, but it remains animal/intervention biology plus translational rationale rather than human cognition efficacy.
