# Effect of ashwagandha (Withania somnifera) extract with Sominone (Somin-On™) to improve memory in adults with mild cognitive impairment: a randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled study

- PMID: 40099725
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40099725/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Pilot RCT in adults with MCI. Subjects received standardized ashwagandha extract with Sominone (250 mg daily) or placebo for 60 days. The active arm improved immediate memory, general memory, working memory, and visuospatial processing on WMS-III-related outcomes at 30 and 60 days. This is one of the cleaner recent cognition-positive signals for a standardized traditional-medicine extract, but the sample was small.
