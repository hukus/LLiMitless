# Non-invasive Blood-brain Barrier Opening in Alzheimer's Disease Patients Using Focused Ultrasound

- ClinicalTrials.gov: NCT04118764
- Registry: https://clinicaltrials.gov/ct2/show/NCT04118764
- Sponsor: Columbia University
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

This completed Columbia feasibility study enrolled 6 AD participants and tested neuronavigation-guided focused ultrasound with Definity microbubbles. The registry states the team would assess BBB opening, safety, amyloid change, and cognitive function; results were posted in 2024, underscoring that the translational goal was drug delivery plus biomarker/cognitive readout, not enhancement alone.
