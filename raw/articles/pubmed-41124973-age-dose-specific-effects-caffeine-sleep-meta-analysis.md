# Age- and dose-specific effects of caffeine on sleep: A meta-analysis of controlled crossover trials

- PMID: 41124973
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41124973/
- Published: 2025-12

## Summary

2025 meta-analysis of 22 controlled crossover trials (956 participants) on acute caffeine and nighttime polysomnography in healthy adults. Pooled effects show shorter total sleep time, lower sleep efficiency, less slow-wave sleep, and longer sleep-onset latency. The authors did not find a significant linear age or dose gradient, but younger and high-dose consumers showed the clearest disruption.

## Notes

This is a stronger sleep-protection anchor than the single-dose trial alone because it quantifies the average sleep cost across many studies.
