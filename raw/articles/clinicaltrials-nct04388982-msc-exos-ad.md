# NCT04388982 - Allogenic adipose MSC exosomes for Alzheimer's disease

- Status at capture: last known recruiting / unknown status on ClinicalTrials.gov
- Sponsor: Ruijin Hospital
- Condition: Alzheimer's disease
- Intervention: intranasal allogeneic adipose MSC-derived exosomes

## Summary

Phase I/II, open-label, single-center AD trial with three intranasal dose arms. The trial was registered in 2020 and lists no posted results in the captured record.

## Notes

This is the registry complement to the 2023 Gen Psychiatr publication and the cleanest way to keep the human exosome branch separate from marketing claims.
