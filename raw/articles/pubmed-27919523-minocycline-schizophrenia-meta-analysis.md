# Adjunctive minocycline for schizophrenia meta-analysis

2017 European Neuropsychopharmacology meta-analysis of randomized controlled trials of adjunctive minocycline in schizophrenia. Eight RCTs with 548 patients were included; average completed treatment duration was about 18.5 weeks and mean minocycline dose was about 172 mg/day.

The meta-analysis reported significant symptom benefits on PANSS/BPRS total and several symptom subscales, but no significant difference in neurocognitive function, discontinuation rate, or adverse drug reactions. The authors emphasized heterogeneity and the need for larger trials.

Interpretation for the wiki: this is a classically mixed minocycline signal: psychiatric symptom endpoints may move, but cognitive improvement is not robust.
