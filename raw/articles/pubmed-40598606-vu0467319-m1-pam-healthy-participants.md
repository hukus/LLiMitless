# Safety, tolerability, pharmacokinetic and pharmacodynamic effects of the muscarinic M1 positive allosteric modulator VU0467319 for Alzheimer's disease: a single ascending-dose study in healthy participants

- PMID: 40598606
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40598606/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

Phase 1 healthy-volunteer dose-escalation and food-effect study of VU0467319 (VU319), a muscarinic M1 positive allosteric modulator. Tolerability was good with no dose-limiting side effects, and exploratory cognitive and event-related-potential readouts showed CNS target engagement at higher doses. This is a mechanistic anchor, not efficacy proof.
