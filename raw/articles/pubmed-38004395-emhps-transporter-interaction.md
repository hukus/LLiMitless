# PubMed 38004395: EMHPS transporter interaction study

This 2023 Pharmaceuticals paper studied 2-ethyl-6-methyl-3-hydroxypyridine succinate / EMHPS / Mexidol as an ABCB1 and SLCO1B1 transporter-interaction question.

The PubMed abstract states that EMHPS is used as an antioxidant and anti-ischemic drug in complex therapy for oxidative-stress and ischemia-related diseases such as ischemic stroke, chronic cerebral ischemia, and chronic heart failure.

In vitro results: EMHPS was not a substrate of ABCB1 in Caco-2 cells and did not affect ABCB1 expression, but inhibited ABCB1 activity less strongly than verapamil. In HEK293 / HEK293-SLCO1B1 and HepG2 systems, EMHPS was not a substrate of SLCO1B1, inhibited transporter activity less strongly than rifampicin, and did not affect SLCO1B1 expression.

Clinical-significance interpretation from the abstract: systemic SLCO1B1 inhibition and systemic ABCB1 inhibition by EMHPS were not judged clinically significant, but gastrointestinal ABCB1 inhibition should be tested in vivo.

Relevance: safety and drug-interaction caution for [[emoxypine-mexidol]], especially where users stack it with other drugs or nootropics.
