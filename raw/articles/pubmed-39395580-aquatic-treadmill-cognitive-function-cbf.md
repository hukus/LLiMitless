# Effect of moderate-intensity aquatic treadmill exercise on cognitive function and cerebral blood flow for healthy older adults

- PMID: 39395580
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39395580/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary
In healthy older adults, both aquatic and land treadmill exercise improved DSST after a single session. The aquatic condition showed a larger cerebral blood-flow increase, but the study was small and the between-group cognition interaction was not clearly decisive. Useful as a physiologic exercise/CBF anchor, not a definitive modality winner.
