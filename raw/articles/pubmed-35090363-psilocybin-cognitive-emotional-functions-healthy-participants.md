# The effects of psilocybin on cognitive and emotional functions in healthy participants

- PMID: 35090363
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35090363/
- Ingested: 2026-04-29

## Summary

2022 phase 1 randomized placebo-controlled trial in healthy volunteers given 10 mg or 25 mg psilocybin with psychological support. There were no clinically relevant between-group differences in CANTAB global cognition, domain scores, or emotional processing, and no serious adverse events. The paper supports supervised tolerability, not a broad cognition-enhancement claim.
