# Effectiveness and Safety of Dual Orexin Receptor Antagonists for the Prevention of Delirium: A Systematic Review and Meta-analysis

- PMID: 41401488
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41401488/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Systematic review and meta-analysis of DORAs for delirium prevention. Across 10 studies, including 3 RCTs and 4,342 patients, DORA prophylaxis was associated with lower incident delirium, pooled OR 0.23, but certainty remained very low and secondary outcomes did not clearly improve. Mild falls and hypnogogic/hypnopompic hallucinations were reported more often with suvorexant in one RCT.
