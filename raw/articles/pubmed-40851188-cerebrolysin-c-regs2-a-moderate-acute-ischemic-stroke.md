# C-REGS2-A multinational, high-quality comparative effectiveness study of Cerebrolysin in moderate acute ischemic stroke

- PMID: 40851188
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40851188/
- Ingested: 2026-04-30

## Summary

This 2025 comparative-effectiveness study analyzed 1,769 moderate ischemic stroke patients after target-trial-emulation style standardization. Cerebrolysin was associated with better 90-day mRS, NIHSS, and ordinal MoCA outcomes than standard therapy, with a stronger MoCA signal in patients who already had cognitive impairment at baseline. The design is observational, but this is the clearest post-2024 human cognition-linked signal in the Cerebrolysin branch.
