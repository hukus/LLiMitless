# Supplementation of spermidine at 40 mg/day has minimal effects on circulating polyamines

- PMID: 39405978
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39405978/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

2024 double-blind randomized controlled safety study in healthy older men. High-purity spermidine trihydrochloride at 40 mg/day for up to 28 days was well tolerated with no product-related adverse events, but it did not produce major changes in circulating polyamines.
