# Acupuncture for mild cognitive impairment: A systematic review with meta-analysis and trial sequential analysis

- Published: 2023-01-06
- Journal: Frontiers in Neurology
- DOI: 10.3389/fneur.2022.1091125
- Evidence layer: systematic review / meta-analysis / trial sequential analysis

## Summary

This systematic review searched English and Chinese databases through 2022-08-01 and included 11 randomized controlled trials with 602 patients with mild cognitive impairment. Low-quality evidence suggested acupuncture improved overall cognitive function on MMSE and MoCA, both with mean differences around 1.22 points versus controls. Subgroup analyses favored acupuncture versus conventional medicine or sham acupuncture for overall cognition.

The same review found no clear statistical difference for memory function or activities of daily living between acupuncture and conventional medicine, and GRADE evidence for memory and ADL ranged from low to critically low. The authors concluded that acupuncture appeared promising for MCI overall cognition but that higher-quality research was still required.
