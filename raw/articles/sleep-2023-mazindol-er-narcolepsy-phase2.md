# Four-week randomized phase 2 study of mazindol ER in narcolepsy

- Abstract number: 0585
- DOI: 10.1093/sleep/zsad077.0585
- Venue: SLEEP 2023 / Sleep supplement

## Captured takeaways

- The abstract describes mazindol ER / NLS-2 as a partial orexin 2 receptor agonist and triple monoamine reuptake inhibitor in development for narcolepsy.
- NLS-1021 was a four-week, double-blind, randomized, placebo-controlled phase 2 study in adults with narcolepsy type 1 or type 2.
- Participants were randomized 1:1 to placebo or mazindol ER 2 mg once daily for one week followed by 3 mg once daily for three weeks.
- The primary endpoint was change from baseline to week 4 in Epworth Sleepiness Scale.
- Sixty-seven participants were randomized; the least-squares mean difference versus placebo on ESS was -3.6 points at week 4, p=0.0081.
- In the narcolepsy type 1 subgroup, weekly cataplexy episodes fell from 14.1 to 4.1 with mazindol ER and from 15.0 to 8.5 with placebo.
- The abstract reported no severe or serious adverse events and no discontinuations due to adverse events.
- Interpretation for the wiki: modern mazindol ER evidence is a sleep-disorder / wakefulness-development signal, not healthy-adult cognition proof; its orexin and monoamine profile makes it a useful comparator for tesofensine, solriamfetol, and orexin agonist pages.
