# INTERACT: a randomized phase 2 study of the DAAO inhibitor luvadaxistat in adults with schizophrenia

- PMID: 38943928
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38943928/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

INTERACT randomized 256 adults with schizophrenia to luvadaxistat 50, 125, or 500 mg versus placebo for 12 weeks. The primary negative-symptom endpoint was negative, but the 50 mg arm met nominal secondary signals on BACS and SCoRS. The drug was well tolerated with no new safety signals.
