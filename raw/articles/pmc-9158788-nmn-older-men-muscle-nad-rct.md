# Chronic NMN Supplementation Elevates Blood NAD Levels and Alters Muscle Function in Healthy Older Men

- PMCID: PMC9158788
- Published: 2022
- Checked: 2026-05-03

## Summary

2022 placebo-controlled, randomized, double-blind, parallel-group trial in healthy older men. Participants received 250 mg/day oral NMN for 6 or 12 weeks versus placebo.

Result shape: well tolerated, blood NAD+ target engagement, and muscle-function hints. Whole-blood metabolomics showed significant increases in NAD+ and NAD+ metabolites. The paper reported nominal improvements in gait speed and left-grip performance, but no significant body-composition effect and a need for larger validation.

Use in the wiki: human NAD/muscle proxy source for [[nicotinamide-mononucleotide]]. It supports plausibility and physical-function endpoints, not direct cognition.
