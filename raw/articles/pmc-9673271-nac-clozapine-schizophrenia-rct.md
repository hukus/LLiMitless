# NAC adjunctive trial in clozapine-resistant schizophrenia

Randomized, double-blind, multicenter placebo-controlled trial in clozapine-treated schizophrenia with enduring symptoms, N=84. NAC dose was 2 g/day, with outcomes assessed at 8, 24, and 52 weeks.

NAC did not significantly improve negative symptoms, overall cognition, or quality of life at any assessed time point. Side-effect reporting did not differ meaningfully from placebo.

Use in wiki: another controlled negative cognition anchor for NAC in schizophrenia. It counters broad claims that NAC reliably improves cognitive symptoms across psychiatric phenotypes.
