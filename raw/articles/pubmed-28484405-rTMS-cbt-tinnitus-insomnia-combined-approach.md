# Management of Chronic Tinnitus and Insomnia with Repetitive Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation and Cognitive Behavioral Therapy - a Combined Approach

- PMID: 28484405
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28484405/
- Ingested: 2026-04-29

## Summary

Single case report in a 53-year-old man with tinnitus and comorbid insomnia. The treatment plan used 10 sessions of rTMS followed by 10 sessions of CBT, with improvements in tinnitus severity, depressive symptoms, and well-being.

## Notes

This is the closest explicit order-comparison analog in the current scan. It is not a CBT-I cognition trial, but it is direct evidence that clinicians have already used a stimulation-first, psychotherapy-second sequence in an insomnia-adjacent setting.
