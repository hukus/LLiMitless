# Sex-specific adverse event profiles of PDE4 inhibitors: a comparative big-data pharmacovigilance study of apremilast, crisaborole, and roflumilast in FAERS (2004-2025)

- PMID: 41988533
- DOI: 10.3389/fphar.2026.1761303
- Published: 2026-03-31

## Summary

Retrospective FAERS pharmacovigilance study of PDE4 inhibitors using reports from Q1-2004 through Q1-2025. The roflumilast cohort included 2,142 reports. The analysis found sex-specific and drug-specific safety heterogeneity across apremilast, crisaborole, and roflumilast, with roflumilast showing male-skewed signals, especially for malignancy- and metabolism-related adverse events. Median time-to-onset for roflumilast events was 42 days, with an IQR of 14 to 128 days. The paper is safety-context only; it does not add efficacy data for cognition.

## Key Points

- Data source: FDA FAERS.
- Analysis period: Q1-2004 to Q1-2025.
- Roflumilast reports included: 2,142.
- Main roflumilast pattern: male-skewed disproportionality signals, especially malignancy- and metabolism-related preferred terms.
- Median time-to-onset for roflumilast: 42 days.
- Relevance: safety and monitoring signal for the PDE4 branch, not cognition efficacy.
