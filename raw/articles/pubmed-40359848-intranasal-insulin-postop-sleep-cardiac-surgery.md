# Intranasal insulin enhances postoperative sleep quality and delirium in middle-aged cardiac surgery patients: A randomized controlled trial

- PMID: 40359848
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40359848/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Randomized controlled trial in middle-aged on-pump cardiac surgery patients. Intranasal insulin 20 IU pre-op plus postoperative days 1-2 reduced postoperative delirium, improved postoperative sleep efficiency and total sleep time, and increased postoperative MMSE.

## Notes

This is a perioperative neurocognitive-protection signal, not a healthy-adult enhancement result. The sleep-mediated mechanism makes it especially relevant to the sleep/consolidation cluster.
