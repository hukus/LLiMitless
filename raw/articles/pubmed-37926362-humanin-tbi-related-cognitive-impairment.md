# Humanin ameliorates TBI-related cognitive impairment by attenuating mitochondrial dysfunction and inflammation

- PMID: 37926362
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37926362/
- Ingested: 2026-04-29

## Summary

Humanin reduced traumatic-brain-injury-related cognitive impairment in a preclinical model, with the effect linked to improved mitochondrial function and lower inflammatory signaling.

## Notes

- Useful as a recovery / neuroprotection anchor for the humanin branch.
- Does not establish human enhancement efficacy or a usable protocol.
