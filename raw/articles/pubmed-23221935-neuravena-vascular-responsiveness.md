# Chronic wild green-oat extract and vascular responsiveness in older adults

- Source type: randomized double-blind placebo-controlled two-way crossover dietary intervention.
- Publication: Journal of Hypertension, 2013.
- Date checked: 2026-05-03.

This companion older-adult study tested 1500 mg/day wild green-oat extract / Neuravena for 12-week arms in 37 healthy adults over age 60. It measured brachial flow-mediated dilation and hypercapnia-induced middle-cerebral-artery blood-flow responsiveness.

Compared with placebo, wild green-oat extract increased cerebrovascular responsiveness and brachial flow-mediated dilation by about 42% and 41%, respectively. This vascular signal is relevant because the companion cognition trial in older adults did not show sustained cognitive improvement despite the same 1500 mg/day chronic dosing frame.

Use in the wiki: mechanism / vascular counterpoint. It supports physiological activity without upgrading cognition when behavior is null.
