# A placebo-controlled study of sildenafil effects on cognition in schizophrenia

- PMID: 18716761
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18716761/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

2009 placebo-controlled study of sildenafil in schizophrenia. Neither 50 mg nor 100 mg improved cognition or symptom ratings, and one participant developed irritability severe enough to require hospitalization after the 100 mg dose.

## Key points

- Design: 17 outpatients; 15 completed all conditions.
- Outcome: cognitive battery plus 48-hour recall.
- Signal: no significant cognitive benefit over placebo.
- Safety: the 100 mg condition produced a serious psychiatric adverse event in one participant.
