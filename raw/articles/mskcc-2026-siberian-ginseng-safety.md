# MSKCC Siberian ginseng / Eleuthero safety monograph

Memorial Sloan Kettering Cancer Center Siberian ginseng monograph checked in 2026.

MSKCC describes Siberian ginseng / Eleutherococcus senticosus as a traditional Chinese medicine herb used as an adaptogen, performance enhancer, and immunostimulant. It emphasizes that Siberian ginseng is not the same as Asian ginseng, American ginseng, or Panax notoginseng.

Safety interpretation for the wiki: MSKCC warns people taking digoxin to consult clinicians because Siberian ginseng can increase side-effect risk or affect serum digoxin assays. It also notes possible CYP450 substrate concerns, case-report context for subarachnoid hemorrhage with a multi-herb supplement containing Siberian ginseng, and insufficient breastfeeding safety information.
