# The Accuracy of Acquiring Heart Rate Variability from Portable Devices: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis

- PMID: 30706234
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30706234/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

2019 meta-analysis comparing portable-device HRV estimates with ECG. The main takeaway is that portable HRV can be useful, but accuracy depends on metric, device, and conditions, so resting morning HRV is more defensible than highly dynamic or exercise-adjacent readings.

## Notes

For a daily research stack, HRV should be treated as a state marker, not as a precise mechanistic endpoint unless collection conditions are standardized.
