# Tau aggregation inhibitor therapy: an exploratory phase 2 study in mild or moderate Alzheimer's disease

- PMID: 25550228
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25550228/
- Ingested: 2026-04-29

## Summary

Exploratory double-blind, randomized, placebo-controlled dose-finding trial of methylthioninium in 321 people with mild or moderate Alzheimer's disease. Doses were 69, 138, and 228 mg/day over 24 weeks, with a primary ADAS-cog endpoint and a regional cerebral blood flow substudy in 135 participants. The 138 mg/day arm showed the clearest signal: better cognitive performance in the moderate subgroup and a small rCBF benefit in the mild subgroup. The highest dose had delivery limitations related to dissolution and absorption.

## Notes

- Trial registration: NCT00515333.
- Follow-up extensions: sequential 6- and 12-month blinded extensions after week 24.
- The paper treats methylthioninium as a tau aggregation inhibitor and frames imaging as a functional readout, not just a biomarker side quest.
