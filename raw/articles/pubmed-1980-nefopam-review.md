# Nefopam pharmacology and analgesic review

- Source: Heel et al., Drugs 1980 review.
- The PubMed abstract describes nefopam as a non-narcotic analgesic not structurally related to other analgesic drugs.
- It reports oral and parenteral analgesic efficacy in short-term studies compared with aspirin, dextropropoxyphene, pentazocine, and moderate parenteral opioid doses, while noting that higher opioid dose ratios were usually more effective and that nefopam may have an analgesic ceiling.
- Interpretation for the wiki: nefopam's main clinical identity is analgesic, not cognition treatment. Pain relief may indirectly change cognition or brain fog in pain-state users, but that is state restoration and not nootropic evidence.
