# PubChem 2026 berberine identity note

PubChem identifies berberine as CID 2353, a cationic alkaloid with molecular formula C20H18NO4+ and molecular weight about 336.4 g/mol. The PubChem IUPAC name describes the quaternary azonia scaffold rather than a neutral amine.

Interpretation for the wiki: berberine should be treated as a specific isoquinoline alkaloid / supplement chemical, not as generic "goldenseal," generic barberry, generic traditional medicine, or a stand-in for prescription glucose-lowering drugs such as [[metformin]]. Any cognition claim needs product identity, dose, metabolic phenotype, and safety context before it is compared with [[creatine]], [[ketone-ester]], [[d-ribose]], or caffeine in the energy-substrate branch.
