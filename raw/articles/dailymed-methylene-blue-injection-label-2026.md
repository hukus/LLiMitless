# Methylene Blue Injection label

## Summary

Current U.S. prescribing label for injectable methylene blue. The approved indication is acquired methemoglobinemia, the standard dose is 1 mg/kg IV over 5-30 minutes with possible repeat once after 1 hour, and the boxed warning highlights serious or fatal serotonin syndrome with SSRIs, SNRIs, MAOIs, and opioids. The label also contraindicates G6PD deficiency because of hemolytic risk.
