# Behavioral and Neurobiological Effects of Cogmed Working Memory Training: A Meta-Analysis

- PMID: 40581145
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40581145/
- Ingested: 2026-05-03

## Summary

2025 meta-analysis of Cogmed working-memory training. The PubMed abstract reports large effects on directly trained working-memory tasks, moderate-to-large effects on non-trained working-memory tasks, and small effects on far-transfer measures. The pattern reinforces the transfer ladder: cognitive training can improve trained and nearby tasks, but broad far transfer remains much smaller.

## Notes

This newer source does not overturn the far-transfer caution. It is useful because it gives a more current split between trained-task gain, near transfer, and small far-transfer effects.
