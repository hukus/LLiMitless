# Transcranial photobiomodulation increases cognition and serum BDNF levels in adults over 50 years: A randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial

- PMID: 39423445
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39423445/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Randomized placebo-controlled trial in older adults with MCI. Reported greater MoCA improvement with tPBM than placebo, persistence to follow-up, and increased serum BDNF in the active arm. Sample size was modest and the result should be treated as encouraging but not definitive.
