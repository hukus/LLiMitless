# Effect of Guarana on Cognitive Performance: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis

- Source type: systematic review and meta-analysis.
- Publication: Nutrients, 2023.
- Date checked: 2026-05-03.

This review/meta-analysis evaluated acute guarana / Paullinia cupana ingestion for cognitive performance. Eight placebo-controlled studies met inclusion criteria, providing data from 328 participants and 272 cognitive dependent variables.

Guarana doses ranged from 37.5 to 500 mg, and reported caffeine content ranged from 4.3 to 100 mg. The overall pooled effect across cognitive measures was less than trivial and not significant: Hedges g = 0.076, p = 0.14. Subgroup analysis separated a small faster-response-time signal from non-significant accuracy results: response time g = 0.202, p = 0.005; accuracy g = -0.077, p = 0.4.

The authors concluded that acute guarana has a small response-time effect without accuracy improvement, and that it remains unknown whether the signal is due to caffeine or other guarana bioactives. They called for studies directly comparing matched caffeine and guarana doses.

Use in the wiki: primary guarana formal-evidence anchor. Treat medicinal signal as low and caffeine-confounded; do not generalize to durable memory or broad nootropic benefit.
