# Results of N = 1 randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled, cross-over discontinuation trials embedded in clinical practice after longer term methylphenidate use: a pilot study

- PMID: 39601808
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39601808/
- Ingested: 2026-04-29

## Summary

Pilot embedded N-of-1 discontinuation trials in 26 children/adolescents who had been on methylphenidate for at least a year. Clinicians ran randomized placebo-controlled cross-over discontinuation trials to decide whether ongoing treatment still mattered.

Teacher-rated hyperactivity and impulsivity were significantly lower on methylphenidate than placebo. No other major differences emerged, and 61.5% continued methylphenidate after the discontinuation trial.

## Notes

- This is useful as a real-world discontinuation design rather than a claim that holidays improve cognition.
- The main scheduling message is that long-term treatment review can reveal phenotype- and setting-specific benefit, especially in school-rated behavior.
