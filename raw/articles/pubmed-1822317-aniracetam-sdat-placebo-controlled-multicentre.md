# Aniracetam (Ro 13-5057) in the treatment of senile dementia of Alzheimer type (SDAT): results of a placebo controlled multicentre clinical study

- PMID: 1822317
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/1822317/
- Ingested: 2026-04-30

## Summary

1991 randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled multicentre study in 109 elderly patients with mild to moderate probable Alzheimer-type dementia. The PubMed abstract reports that the aniracetam group differed significantly from placebo by study end and maintained psychobehavioural parameters better than placebo, which steadily deteriorated. Tolerability was described as excellent. The abstract snippet I captured did not expose the dose.
