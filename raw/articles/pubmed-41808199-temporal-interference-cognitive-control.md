# The Distinct Roles of the Dorsolateral Prefrontal Cortex and Dorsal Anterior Cingulate Cortex in Cognitive Control: Evidence From Transcranial Temporal Interference Stimulation

- PMID: 41808199
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41808199/
- Published: 2026-03
- Journal: Psychophysiology

## Summary
This 2026 human TI study compared dlPFC, dACC, and sham stimulation on Stroop conflict effect.
dACC stimulation improved cognitive control regardless of working-memory capacity.
dlPFC stimulation improved control only in low-WMC individuals.
The result supports target-specific and phenotype-specific effects rather than a uniform enhancement claim.

## Notes
This is the strongest direct cognition-facing TI paper in the current wiki set.
It still looks like acute, task-specific modulation, not proof of durable broad enhancement.
