# Soluble TREM2 in CSF and its association with other biomarkers and cognition in autosomal-dominant Alzheimer's disease: a longitudinal observational study

- PMID: 35305339
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35305339/
- Ingested: 2026-04-29

## Summary

2022 DIAN longitudinal observational study. CSF sTREM2 tracked with amyloid, tau, imaging markers, and cognitive decline in autosomal-dominant AD. This is a stronger human anchor than a cross-sectional biomarker paper, but it still does not show that raising or lowering TREM2 improves cognition.

## Notes

- Good for phenotype and longitudinal staging.
- Helpful for separating biomarker association from target-modifying treatment evidence.
