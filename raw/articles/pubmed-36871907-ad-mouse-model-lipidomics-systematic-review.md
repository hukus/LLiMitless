# Systematic review of brain and blood lipidomics in Alzheimer's disease mouse models

- PMID: 36871907
- Source: PubMed review
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

Systematic review of lipidomic changes in Alzheimer's disease mouse models. The review is useful for showing that lipid remodeling is a recurring feature across models, but the models are heterogeneous and do not by themselves validate a plasmalogen-replacement intervention in humans.

## Notes

- Supports preclinical plausibility for lipid-based AD hypotheses.
- Translation to human cognition remains uncertain.
