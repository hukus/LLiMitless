# Noradrenergic enhancement of motor learning, attention, and working memory in humans

## Captured takeaways

- 2021 International Journal of Neuropsychopharmacology open-access study of acute reboxetine in healthy humans.
- Twenty-five healthy participants completed a crossover design comparing a single dose of the noradrenaline reuptake inhibitor reboxetine with placebo.
- The tasks included motor sequence learning, Stroop attention/interference, and n-back working memory.
- The paper reports improved motor learning, attentional processes, and working-memory performance after reboxetine compared with placebo.
- This is a stronger direct healthy-adult cognition anchor than most reboxetine lore, but it is still a small single-study pharmacology probe, not a replicated nootropic evidence base.
- Use in wiki: direct but low-confidence healthy-human noradrenergic enhancement signal for the class watchlist.
