# ADDF Cognitive Vitality evidence review: P021

- Publisher: Alzheimer's Drug Discovery Foundation
- Last updated by source: 2025-05-12
- Evidence type: independent secondary review

## Summary

The review rates P021 as a laboratory-only neurotrophic candidate with repeated 3xTg-AD, aging, and Down-syndrome rodent signals but no human studies, clinical trials, established human dose, or human safety evidence. It notes up to 18 months of rodent exposure without reported weight loss, tumors, or pain-like signs associated with full-length CNTF.

The review also highlights a pharmacology uncertainty: the molecule was modified to improve lipophilicity and blood-brain-barrier penetration, but direct measurement of P021 reaching mouse brain had not been documented. It lists a mouse plasma half-life over 3 hours while marking BBB evidence as not documented.

## Evidence boundary

The review predates the 2026 mouse diffusion-MRI paper. Its human-evidence and registry status was refreshed separately in the wiki on 2026-07-21.
