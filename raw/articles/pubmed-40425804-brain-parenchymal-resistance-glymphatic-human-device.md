# A wireless device for continuous measurement of brain parenchymal resistance tracks glymphatic function in humans

- PMID: 40425804
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40425804/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

2025 human validation study of a non-invasive multimodal wireless device that uses repeated electrical impedance spectroscopy plus EEG and cardiovascular sensors to track sleep-active changes in brain parenchymal resistance. The device measurements paralleled sleep-associated extracellular-volume changes, predicted glymphatic solute exchange measured by contrast-enhanced MRI, and tracked glymphatic-relevant sleep physiology. This is not an intervention efficacy paper, but it materially improves target-engagement measurement for future glymphatic-modulation studies.
