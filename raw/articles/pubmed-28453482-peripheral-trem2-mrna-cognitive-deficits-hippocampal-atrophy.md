# Higher Peripheral TREM2 mRNA Levels Relate to Cognitive Deficits and Hippocampal Atrophy in Alzheimer's Disease and Amnestic Mild Cognitive Impairment

- PMID: 28453482
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28453482/
- Ingested: 2026-04-29

## Summary

2017 human observational study in AD, aMCI, and controls. Peripheral TREM2 mRNA was higher in AD and correlated with worse MMSE, MoCA, episodic memory, and hippocampal atrophy. This is a biomarker association, not an intervention study.

## Notes

- Useful as an early human cognition correlate for TREM2.
- Directionality is ambiguous: higher TREM2 may reflect compensatory microglial activation rather than a causal driver.
