# Nardostachys jatamansi: Phytochemistry, ethnomedicinal uses, and pharmacological activities: A comprehensive review

- PMID: 38042505
- Published: 2024-01
- Journal: Fitoterapia
- DOI: 10.1016/j.fitote.2023.105764
- Evidence layer: phytochemistry / ethnomedicine / pharmacology review

## Summary

This review describes Nardostachys jatamansi as a rhizomatous Himalayan plant used in Ayurveda, homeopathy, ethnomedicine, and other traditional systems. Traditional use includes bitter tonic, stimulant, antispasmodic, epilepsy treatment, and hysteria contexts. The review summarizes pharmacological reports including central nervous system activity, anticonvulsant, neuroprotective, antiparkinson's, antioxidant, antidiabetic, tranquilizing, and hepatoprotective activities.

For cognition, this supports a traditional CNS/neuroprotection hypothesis but not a human cognition signal. It also raises product-identity and conservation concerns because the plant is high-altitude and supply-chain constrained.
