## Summary

Review of modafinil, its prodrug adrafinil, and armodafinil, focused on chemical, pharmacokinetic, pharmacodynamic, toxicological, clinical, and forensic aspects. The abstract frames modafinil as a nonamphetamine nootropic drug of therapeutic interest and explicitly includes adrafinil as the prodrug in the review scope, with concern about unconscious and unregulated use.

## Interpretation

Use this as the formal anchor that adrafinil should be interpreted through the modafinil / armodafinil wakefulness-promoter family, with extra regulatory and safety uncertainty rather than a separate positive cognition evidence base.
