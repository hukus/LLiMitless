# Metadoxine ER adult ADHD phase 2 trial

- PMID: 23290324
- DOI: 10.4088/JCP.12m07767
- Journal: Journal of Clinical Psychiatry (2012)
- Trial registration: NCT01243242

## Captured takeaways

- Randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled, parallel-design phase 2 study of metadoxine ER 1400 mg/day for 6 weeks in 120 adults with DSM-IV ADHD.
- Efficacy measures included Conners' Adult ADHD Rating Scale-Investigator Rated total ADHD symptoms score, response rates, Test of Variables of Attention (TOVA), and Adult ADHD Quality of Life score.
- Intent-to-treat analysis favored metadoxine ER over placebo on CAARS-INV total ADHD symptom score, response thresholds, TOVA score, and quality-of-life score.
- Separation from placebo on CAARS-INV was reported as early as 2 weeks after treatment initiation.
- Frequently reported adverse effects included nausea, fatigue, and headache. Headache was also common in placebo.
- The authors concluded that metadoxine ER was well tolerated and effective for adults with ADHD.
- Interpretation for the wiki: this is an ADHD clinical-state and attention-task signal, not a healthy-adult cognition result; later phase 3 failure prevents promotion.
