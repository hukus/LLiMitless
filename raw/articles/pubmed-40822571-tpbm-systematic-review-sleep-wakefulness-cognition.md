# Enhancing sleep, wakefulness, and cognition with transcranial photobiomodulation: a systematic review

- PMID: 40822571
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40822571/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

2025 systematic review summarizing tPBM literature across clinical and healthy populations. The review frames tPBM as a candidate for sleep, wakefulness, glymphatic, and cognition effects, but it is still a narrative synthesis rather than a decisive pooled meta-analysis.
