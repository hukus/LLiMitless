# Intranasal kisspeptin human hormone study
- PMID: 40215751
- 2025 randomized double-blind crossover clinical study in healthy volunteers and patients with reproductive disorders.
- Endpoint focus: reproductive hormone profiles after intranasal kisspeptin administration.
- Branch readout: a current human kisspeptin lead, but still an endocrine/reproductive study rather than a cognition trial.
