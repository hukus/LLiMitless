# The role of the kynurenine pathway in cognitive functioning after stroke: A prospective clinical study

- PMID: 37852105
- DOI: 10.1016/j.jns.2023.120819
- Journal: Journal of the Neurological Sciences (2023)

## Captured takeaways

- Prospective cohort of 198 stroke patients followed for 3 years.
- No evidence that baseline plasma kynurenines predicted odds of post-stroke cognitive impairment.
- Higher anthranilic acid was associated with better episodic memory at baseline.
- The kynurenic acid / quinolinic acid ratio tracked episodic memory.
- Higher kynurenic acid was associated with better processing speed in women only.
- Interpretation for the wiki: the branch is domain-specific and exploratory, not a stable global PSCI biomarker.
