# WADA 2026 stimulant list: afinils, pemoline, and tesofensine

## Captured takeaways

- The 2026 WADA Prohibited List came into effect on January 1, 2026.
- S0 says any pharmacological substance that is not addressed elsewhere and has no current approval by any governmental regulatory health authority for human therapeutic use is prohibited at all times.
- S6 says all stimulants, including relevant optical isomers, are prohibited in competition.
- S6 also says a stimulant not expressly listed in the section is a Specified Substance.
- The S6.A non-specified stimulant list names adrafinil, fladrafinil, flmodafinil, hydrafinil / fluorenol, and modafinil.
- The S6.B specified stimulant list names methylphenidate, pemoline, sibutramine, solriamfetol, and tesofensine.
- The 2026 list does not need to name every gray-market stimulant to create sports risk: unapproved pharmacological substances can route through S0, and unlisted stimulants can still be covered by S6 logic.
- Interpretation for the wiki: afinil analogs, pemoline, tesofensine, and pemoline-adjacent research stimulants should be treated as sports-visible before any cognition interpretation.
