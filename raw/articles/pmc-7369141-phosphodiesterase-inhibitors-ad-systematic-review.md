# Phosphodiesterase Inhibitors for Alzheimer’s Disease: A Systematic Review of Clinical Trials and Epidemiology with a Mechanistic Rationale

- PMID: 32715279
- PMC: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7369141/
- Ingested: 2026-04-29

## Summary

2020 systematic review of PDE inhibitors in AD. The review is useful as a branch map because it explicitly concludes that PF-04447943 and BI 409306 were ineffective in AD, while several older PDE-related drugs remain mixed, preliminary, or historically interesting rather than definitive.

## Key points

- Mechanistic framing: PDE inhibition may raise cAMP/cGMP and support CREB-linked plasticity.
- PDE9 conclusion: PF-04447943 and BI 409306 are ineffective in AD in the cited clinical trials.
- PDE5 conclusion: sildenafil and other PDE5 agents were promising but still inconclusive in the literature the review covered.
- Historical comparators: propentofylline, cilostazol, selegiline, and vinpocetine are discussed, but they do not convert the PDE9/PDE10 branch into a positive cognition class.
- Relevance: branch-level synthesis, not primary efficacy evidence.
