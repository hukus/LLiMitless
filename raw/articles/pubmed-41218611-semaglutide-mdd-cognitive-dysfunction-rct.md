# Semaglutide for the treatment of cognitive dysfunction in major depressive disorder: A randomized clinical trial

- PMID: 41218611
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41218611/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

First randomized clinical trial of oral semaglutide for cognitive dysfunction in MDD. The primary executive-function outcome was negative, but a prespecified secondary global-cognition composite favored semaglutide.
