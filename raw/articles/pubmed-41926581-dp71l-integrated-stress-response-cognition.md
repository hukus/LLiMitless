## Summary

2026 Science paper on persistent integrated-stress-response activation and cognition. The study used a PPP1R15B R658C mouse model linked to intellectual disability, found persistent ISR activation with impaired protein synthesis and long-term memory deficits, and characterized DP71L, a viral ortholog of PPP1R15B, as a potent pan-ISR inhibitor.

The central cognition-relevant claim is preclinical: DP71L reversed cognitive and synaptic deficits across mouse models of Down syndrome, Alzheimer disease, and aging, and enhanced synaptic plasticity and memory in healthy mice.

## Notes
