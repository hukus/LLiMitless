# Taurine From Tumour Niche Drives Leukaemogenesis

- PMID: 40369079.
- DOI: 10.1038/s41586-025-09018-7.
- Journal: Nature, 2025.
- Ingested: 2026-05-03.

## Summary

Nature study identifying taurine from the bone-marrow tumor niche as a metabolic signal supporting aggressive myeloid leukemia progression. The study integrated single-cell RNA-seq, leukemia stem-cell transcriptomics, and in vivo CRISPR dependency screening, identifying the taurine / taurine-transporter axis as important for myeloid leukemias. Blocking taurine uptake or niche-cell taurine synthesis reduced leukemia progression in experimental systems.

## Interpretation

This is not evidence that taurine supplementation causes leukemia in healthy people. It is a mechanistic cancer-context caution that should be surfaced when high-dose taurine anecdotes mention leukemia risk or when users have active myeloid malignancy concerns.
