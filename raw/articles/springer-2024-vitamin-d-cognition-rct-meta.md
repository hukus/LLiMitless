# Effects of vitamin D supplementation on cognitive outcomes: a systematic review and meta-analysis

- DOI: 10.1007/s11065-023-09598-z
- Published online: 2023-07-07
- Journal issue: Neuropsychology Review, 2024
- Source type: systematic review and meta-analysis of randomized controlled trials
- Registration: PROSPERO CRD42021249908
- Ingested: 2026-05-03

## Summary

Systematic review and meta-analysis of 24 trials with 7,557 participants. Vitamin D supplementation had a small significant effect on global cognition (Hedges' g 0.128, p = 0.008) but not on specific cognitive domains. Subgroups suggested larger effects in vulnerable populations and in people with baseline vitamin D deficiency. The authors suggest that intervention models should correct baseline deficiency.

## Notes

- Useful broad formal anchor for a low but status-gated vitamin D cognition signal.
- The effect size is small and domain-specific outcomes were not significant, so this does not support broad nootropic ranking.
- Baseline 25(OH)D status is a first-class moderator.
