PubMed abstract for the 2023 Nature Communications paper showing that SHLP2 regulates energy homeostasis through activation of hypothalamic neurons. The paper reports circulating SHLP2 in healthy, obese, and diabetic men, daily intraperitoneal SHLP2 in high-fat-diet-fed male mice for 3 weeks, reduced body weight, altered body composition, improved glucose and insulin tolerance, and a hypothalamic-neuron mechanism. This is metabolic and hypothalamic biology, not cognition evidence.
