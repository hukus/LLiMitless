# Quantitative Electroencephalographic Biomarkers for Repetitive Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation Treatment Response Prediction in Mild Cognitive Impairment: A Pilot Study Protocol for Multi-Center, Assessor-Blinded, Open-Label Clinical Trial

- PMID: 41171882
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41171882/
- Ingested: 2026-04-30

## Summary

Protocol for a 25-patient MCI study that will use baseline QEEG to classify rTMS responders and non-responders. Participants receive 10 left-DLPFC rTMS sessions over 2 weeks; the primary outcome is responder proportion, with baseline QEEG features as the response-prediction layer.

## Notes

- This is protocol-stage evidence, not outcomes evidence.
- Useful because it explicitly formalizes biomarker-enriched enrollment in MCI rTMS.
- The protocol endpoint is cognition change after treatment, not passive physiology alone.
