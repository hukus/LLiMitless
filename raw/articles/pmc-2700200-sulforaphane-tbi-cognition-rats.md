# Sulforaphane improves cognitive function administered following traumatic brain injury

- Source type: open-access preclinical traumatic-brain-injury study.
- Publication: Neuroscience Letters, 2009.
- Date checked: 2026-05-03.

This rat study tested whether sulforaphane could improve cognitive deficits after cortical impact injury. Treatment initiated one hour after injury improved Morris water maze performance and reduced working-memory dysfunction, while later treatment windows were less effective.

Use in the wiki: preclinical rescue-context anchor for sulforaphane. It supports Nrf2/TBI plausibility but does not establish a supplement cognition claim.
