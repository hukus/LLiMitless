# A human milk oligosaccharide alters the microbiome, circulating hormones, and metabolites in a randomized controlled trial of older adults

- PMID: 40738103
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40738103/
- Ingested: 2026-04-30

## Summary

2025 randomized controlled trial in 89 healthy older adults (mean age 67.3) testing 2'-fucosyllactose (2'-FL) for 6 weeks. The primary cytokine-response endpoint was negative, but 2'-FL increased gut Bifidobacterium and shifted several metabolic markers. Participants who responded with Bifidobacterium expansion also performed better on a visual-memory cognitive test, making this a phenotype-gated prebiotic signal rather than a broad enhancer result.
