# The mitochondrial derived peptide humanin is a regulator of lifespan and healthspan

- PMCID: PMC7343442
- PMC: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7343442/
- Ingested: 2026-04-29

## Summary

Humanin overexpression increased lifespan in worms, and humanin/HNG improved metabolic healthspan markers and inflammatory readouts in mice. Humanin levels generally decline with age and are higher in children of centenarians.

## Notes

- Strong aging/healthspan source for humanin as an endogenous signal.
- Decreased levels were reported in AD and MELAS cohorts, which is relevant to disease framing but not a cognition intervention.
