# Memorial Sloan Kettering nattokinase safety monograph

Memorial Sloan Kettering About Herbs monograph on nattokinase safety, interactions, and evidence limitations.

The monograph describes nattokinase as an enzyme from natto, a traditional fermented soybean food. It says lab studies suggest fibrin/clot and amyloid-plaque mechanisms, but human studies are limited and do not establish broad clinical efficacy. It warns that nattokinase may increase bleeding risk with blood-thinning drugs and highlights caution with anticoagulants, antiplatelets, fibrinolytics, daily aspirin, coagulation disorders, and deep-vein-thrombosis history.

The monograph also lists case reports including mechanical-valve thrombosis after replacing warfarin with nattokinase, fatal internal bleeding in an elderly person using over-the-counter nattokinase, allergic reactions to natto/fermented soybean products, and severe harm after injection of an oral enzyme supplement containing serrapeptase and nattokinase.

Interpretation for the wiki: nattokinase should be treated as a supplement with real hemostatic plausibility and real safety caveats, not as a harmless long-COVID brain-fog experiment. Aspirin, anticoagulant/antiplatelet drugs, surgery, clot history, and product identity are primary gates.
