# NZMP Milk Phospholipids Product Identity

- Source type: manufacturer ingredient page.
- Date checked: 2026-05-03.

## Summary

NZMP/Fonterra describes milk phospholipids as complex lipids naturally present in milk as part of the milk fat globule membrane. The ingredient page positions Milk Phospholipids 70 and Milk Phospholipids 100 as adult-focused ingredients for mood and cognitive performance under stress.

Product identity details from the page: Milk Phospholipids 70 is sourced from high-phospholipid whey protein concentrate and is listed as 5-7% phospholipids, 2% lactose, 73% whey protein, and 16% fat. Milk Phospholipids 100 is sourced from buttermilk and listed as 7-9% phospholipids, 40% lactose, 31% milk protein, and 20% fat. The page also notes clean neutral flavor, solubility, non-GMO status, halal/kosher availability, and New Zealand/Europe origin options.

Identity interpretation: commercial milk phospholipid ingredients are dairy-matrix fractions, not pure phospholipid molecules. They can differ by source stream, lactose, protein, fat, phospholipid percentage, and whether they are used in adult foods, infant formula, milk drinks, or powders.
