# Effects of Combined Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation and Photobiomodulation in Mild Cognitive Impairment: A Randomized Controlled Trial

- PMID: 40254536
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40254536/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Randomized controlled trial in 88 MCI participants testing combined TMS and tPBM versus monostimulation and sham. The combination outperformed the comparators on a complex working-memory task and showed supporting fNIRS connectivity changes. This is not a pure tPBM monotherapy result, but it matters because it suggests tPBM may be more potent as part of a dual-neuromodulation stack.
