# Enhancement of vasoreactivity and cognition by intranasal insulin in type 2 diabetes

- PMID: 24101698
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24101698/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Acute proof-of-concept RCT in older adults with type 2 diabetes and controls showing regional perfusion changes and small cognitive benefits.

## Notes

This is the clearest early bridge between insulin resistance, cerebral blood flow, and cognition. It supports the mechanistic claim that peripheral metabolic state can alter the intranasal-insulin response.
