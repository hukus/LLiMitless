# ClinicalTrials.gov NCT01774045: PDC-1421 phase 1 safety in healthy volunteers

Official ClinicalTrials.gov record for "A Dose Escalation Phase I Study of PDC-1421 Capsule to Evaluate the Safety in Healthy Volunteers." Sponsor: BioLite, Inc. Overall status: completed. Results were posted in 2014 and the record version checked in this pass was dated 2026-05-01.

Design: randomized, parallel, triple-masked phase 1 study in 30 healthy volunteers aged 20-65. PDC-1421 capsules contained 380 mg per capsule. Single-dose cohorts received 1, 3, 6, or 10 capsules once after a meal and were observed for 72 hours. The PDC-1421 arm had 23 participants; placebo had 7.

Primary safety outcomes: dose-limiting toxicity on physical exam, ECG, vital signs, laboratory values, and C-SSRS from baseline to 72 hours. Posted results showed 0 dose-limiting toxicities for physical examination, ECG, vital signs, and C-SSRS in both arms. Laboratory dose-limiting toxicity was 0/23 with PDC-1421 and 2/7 with placebo.

Adverse events: 0 serious adverse events in both arms. Other adverse events affected 3/23 PDC-1421 participants and 2/7 placebo participants. PDC-1421-listed non-serious events included abdominal fullness, constipation, sleepiness, and oral ulcer; all were described as mild and recovered during or after the trial.

Relevance: short-term human exposure and safety anchor for PDC-1421, not evidence of cognitive efficacy.
