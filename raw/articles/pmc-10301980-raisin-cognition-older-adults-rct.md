# Raisin supplement and cognition in healthy older adults

- PMID: 37375715
- PMCID: PMC10301980
- DOI: 10.3390/nu15122811
- Source type: randomized controlled clinical trial.
- Publication: Nutrients, 2023.
- Date checked: 2026-05-03.

This parallel randomized clinical trial tested 50 g/day Malaga muscatel raisins for 6 months in 80 adults over 70 years of age. The intervention group consumed raisins added to the usual diet, while the control group received no supplement. The trial reported mean adherence of about 89%.

Cognitive performance favored the raisin group after 6 months. The Montreal Cognitive Assessment difference versus control was 3.27 points, with significant subdomain differences for visuospatial/executive capacity, language, and orientation. Immediate and delayed recall on the Rey Auditory Verbal Learning Test also improved in the intervention group. The intervention group also showed better quality of life and instrumental activities of daily living; most other variables were not significantly changed.

Product details matter: the 50 g raisin dose contained a reported total polyphenol content of 9.48 mg in the article's table. The comparator was no supplement rather than a matched raisin-like placebo, so expectation, diet-change, and food-matrix confounds remain important.

Use in the wiki: direct raisin food-matrix cognition anchor. Treat as low-confidence product-specific evidence until placebo-controlled replication or stronger diet-control data appear.
