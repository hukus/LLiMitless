# Strategies to Augment Ketosis: Ketone Conferred Resiliency Against Sleep Restriction

- ClinicalTrials.gov ID: NCT05519644
- Status: recruiting as of 2025-05-29
- Population: healthy adults 18-40
- Design: double-blind, placebo-controlled, two-period crossover
- Main question: whether twice-daily ketone ester can improve cognitive and physical performance during short-term sleep restriction
- Key endpoints include cognitive performance, marksmanship, reaction time, mood, sleep, heart rate, CGM/CKM, CRP, ghrelin, insulin, and BDNF

## Summary

This is the direct sleep-loss trial for the current backlog item. It is still registry-level evidence, but it is important because it explicitly targets sleep restriction rather than general wellness.
