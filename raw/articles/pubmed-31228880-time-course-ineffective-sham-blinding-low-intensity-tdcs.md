# The time course of ineffective sham-blinding during low-intensity (1 mA) transcranial direct current stimulation

- PMID: 31228880
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31228880/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

Preregistered crossover study showing that even low-intensity 1 mA tDCS can fail as a placebo control because participants detect the active-versus-sham timing pattern above chance. The paper also tracked certainty repeatedly during stimulation, which makes it especially relevant for real-time blinding-failure detection.

## Notes

- Strong evidence that timing differences are a side-channel.
- Particularly relevant to home self-study devices that use a shortened sham ramp.
