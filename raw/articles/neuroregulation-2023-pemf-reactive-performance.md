# Effects of Pulsed Electromagnetic Field on Reactive Performance

Source type: NeuroRegulation human performance study.

Core design:
- Published 2023.
- N=20 healthy subjects, 10 experimental and 10 sham.
- Participants wore a Bellabee wearable PEMF device.
- Stimulation was described as 40 minutes of beta stimulation at FpZ / Brodmann areas 10, 11, and 32.
- Reactive performance was measured with six Dynavision D2 trials before and after stimulation.

Main findings:
- Within the experimental condition, visual and physical components improved after stimulation.
- Between-condition post-reaction-time analysis reported a significant motor-component difference.

Wiki takeaway:
- This is the small positive healthy-performance counterweight for the PEMF branch.
- It is not enough to classify PEMF as a broad cognitive enhancer.
- The endpoint is reactive/perceptual-motor performance, not working memory, delayed recall, or brain-fog recovery.

Limitations:
- Small sample.
- Single-session design.
- Specific wearable, site, stimulation band, and task.
- Needs replication in larger sham-controlled cognition protocols with better endpoint breadth.
