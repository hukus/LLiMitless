# Notes
- Source: three-month intranasal vasopressin study in healthy older humans.
- Outcome: no general effect on memory, but recall of an interfering word list improved; primacy improved and recency worsened.
- Role in branch: useful as a mixed comparator showing the literature was not purely negative.
- Caveat: older study with limited modern reporting detail.
- Classification: human comparator evidence, not a nootropic endorsement.
