# NCT07512362 - Human mesenchymal stem cells plus anti-amyloid monoclonal antibodies in MCI / early AD

- Registry source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07512362
- Status: not yet recruiting
- Sponsor: Bernard (Barry) Baumel
- Condition: MCI or early Alzheimer's disease

## Summary

Phase 2 pilot that adds one hMSC infusion on top of standard anti-amyloid monoclonal antibody treatment. The primary goal is stabilization of cognitive and functional decline, with ADAS-Cog and ADCS-MCI-ADL tracked.

## Notes

This is not exosome therapy, but it is a current human stem-cell comparator in the same disease space and belongs in the branch's registry context.
