# Investigating the efficacy of ergothioneine to delay cognitive decline in mild cognitively impaired subjects: A pilot study

- PMID: 39544014
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39544014/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

Randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled pilot in 19 adults aged 60+ with MCI. Participants received 25 mg ergothioneine three times per week for one year. The study reported better learning performance and stabilized plasma neurofilament light chain in the ergothioneine group, with no toxicity on blood counts, kidney function, or liver function.
