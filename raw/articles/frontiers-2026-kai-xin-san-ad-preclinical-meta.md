# Efficacy and possible mechanism of Kai-xin-san in animal models of Alzheimer's disease: a systematic review and meta-analysis of preclinical studies

- Published: 2026-04-23
- Journal: Frontiers in Pharmacology
- DOI: 10.3389/fphar.2026.1806181
- Evidence layer: preclinical systematic review / meta-analysis

## Summary

This 2026 preclinical systematic review searched seven databases through October 2025 and included 44 animal studies with 2,681 animals. The review reported that Kai-Xin-San improved cognitive performance across behavioral tests in Alzheimer's disease animal models and moved multiple mechanistic markers: lower amyloid and tau measures, lower inflammatory cytokines including IL-6, IL-8, IL-1beta, and TNF-alpha, lower MDA, higher SOD activity, lower acetylcholinesterase, and higher acetylcholine.

The review's own caveat is important for wiki use: heterogeneity was significant, SYRCLE risk-of-bias domains were often unclear, and GRADE certainty was low or very low. This source supports a mechanistic and preclinical signal for KXS, not a healthy-adult cognition recommendation.
