# Effect of peptide preparation epithalamin on circadian rhythm of epiphyseal melatonin-producing function in elderly people

- PMID: 15452611
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15452611/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

Course treatment with Epithalamin in healthy elderly subjects modulated nighttime melatonin secretion, increasing dark-period melatonin in those with low baseline pineal activity. This is circadian/endocrine evidence, not a cognition trial.
