# The n-of-1 randomized controlled trial: clinical usefulness. Our three-year experience

- PMID: 2297206
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/2297206/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Classic early report showing that n-of-1 trials can be run as double-blind, randomized, multiple-crossover trials in clinical practice. In the three-year series, 57 of 70 started trials were completed and 50 of those yielded a definite clinical or statistical answer, with management changes in 39% of cases where data were available. The paper is useful here because it anchors the core template logic: one person, repeated periods, randomized order, and an explicit management decision at the end.

## Notes

This source is the historical foundation for treating an individual self-study as a structured trial rather than an informal diary.
