# Retraction notice for Benoist et al. 2014 Dihexa / HGF-cMet paper

PubMed indexes a 2025 retraction notice for Benoist et al. 2014, "The Procognitive and Synaptogenic Effects of Angiotensin IV-Derived Peptides Are Dependent on Activation of the Hepatocyte Growth Factor/c-Met System" (DOI 10.1124/jpet.114.218735; PMID 25187433). The retracted paper had been the clearest mechanistic support for Dihexa and related AngIV peptides acting through HGF/c-Met.

Use in the wiki: downgrade the HGF/c-Met mechanistic anchor for Dihexa. The independent APP/PS1 and sciatic-nerve papers still belong in the branch, but the mechanism should not be presented as settled.
