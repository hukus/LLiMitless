# Differing effectiveness of transcranial random noise stimulation and transcranial direct current stimulation for enhancing working memory in healthy individuals: a randomized controlled trial

- PMID: 39402554
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39402554/
- Ingested: 2026-04-30

## Summary

Randomized, double-blind, sham-controlled four-arm trial in 117 healthy right-handed adults. Left DLPFC stimulation used 2 mA for 20 min during dual n-back, with arms for tRNS, tRNS + DC offset, tDCS, and sham. tRNS improved d-prime during stimulation compared with sham, while response time did not improve. tRNS and tDCS both showed baseline-dependent slopes, but no other pairwise interaction clearly separated the active conditions from one another.

## Notes

This is the most important healthy-adult working-memory signal in the current tRNS branch. It supports a narrow online WM effect, but the benefit is task-specific, stimulation-dependent, and not a broad enhancement claim.
