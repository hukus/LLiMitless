# PubMed 26395129: Purified Shilajit testosterone trial in healthy volunteers

This 2016 Andrologia randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial tested purified Shilajit in healthy male volunteers aged 45-55 years.

Design: participants received 250 mg purified Shilajit twice daily or placebo for 90 consecutive days.

Results from the abstract: purified Shilajit increased total testosterone, free testosterone, and dehydroepiandrosterone compared with placebo after 90 days. Gonadotropic hormones were maintained.

Interpretation for the wiki: this is a human exposure and endocrine signal, not a cognition trial. It should not be used as proof of memory, focus, or nootropic benefit. It does make endocrine and product-identity claims relevant to [[shilajit]] safety and evidence routing.
