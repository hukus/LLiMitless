# Long-acting intranasal insulin detemir improves cognition for adults with mild cognitive impairment or early-stage Alzheimer's disease dementia

- PMID: 25374101
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25374101/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Randomized trial of intranasal insulin detemir in MCI/early AD with a dose- and genotype-sensitive memory signal.

## Notes

Important because it adds both APOE and baseline insulin-resistance moderation: 40 IU looked more favorable for APOE-ε4 carriers and for participants with higher baseline insulin resistance.
