# [Comparative analysis of different methods of geroprotective]

- PMID: 28539017
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28539017/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

This comparative human study in 110 people of different ages examined geroprotective techniques including Pinealon and Vezugen. The abstract says the combined oligopeptide complexes had the clearest positive impact on biological-age indicators and were among the safest options by biochemical, immunological, and clinical measures. It does not report a direct cognitive endpoint.
