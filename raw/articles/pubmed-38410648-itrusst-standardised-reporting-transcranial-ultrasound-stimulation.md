# ITRUSST Consensus on Standardised Reporting for Transcranial Ultrasound Stimulation

- PMID: 38410648
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38410648/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Reporting checklist for low-intensity TUS. The consensus calls for standardized reporting of the transducer and drive system, drive settings, free-field acoustic parameters, pulse timing parameters, in situ exposure estimates in brain, and intensity parameters. The point is reproducibility and comparability across studies, not consumer-use simplification.
