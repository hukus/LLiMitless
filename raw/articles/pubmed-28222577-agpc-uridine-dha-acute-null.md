# Acute alpha-GPC plus uridine plus DHA supplement null in trained young men

- PMID: 28222577
- DOI: 10.23736/S0022-4707.17.07022-0
- Journal: Journal of Sports Medicine and Physical Fitness (2018)

## Captured takeaways

- Randomized crossover study in 20 college-aged trained males.
- Active supplement contained 500 mg alpha-glycerophosphocholine, 250 mg uridine-5'-monophosphate, and 1500 mg DHA.
- The supplement was compared with placebo for anaerobic performance and cognition.
- The PubMed abstract reports no benefit from acute ingestion of the DHA / uridine / choline supplement versus placebo on anaerobic performance or cognition.
- Interpretation for the wiki: this is the cleanest healthy-young-adult counterweight for the common alpha-GPC + UMP + DHA nootropic-stack claim; acute dosing did not move cognition.
