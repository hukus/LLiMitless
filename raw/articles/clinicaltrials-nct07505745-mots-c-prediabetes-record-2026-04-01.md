## Summary

Current ClinicalTrials.gov record for a Phase 2a randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled, multicenter MOTS-c trial in adults with prediabetes and overweight/obesity. The record lists recruiting status, estimated enrollment of 120, actual study start on 2026-02-02, estimated primary completion on 2027-02-14, and estimated study completion on 2028-05-17. Participants receive subcutaneous MOTS-c or placebo once daily for 12 weeks plus lifestyle counseling. Primary outcomes are change in OGTT-derived insulin sensitivity by Matsuda Index at 12 weeks and incidence of treatment-emergent adverse events through 16 weeks. Secondary endpoints include HbA1c, fasting glucose, 2-hour OGTT glucose, and immunogenicity. No cognition, mood, fatigue, or neuropsychological endpoint is listed in the current record.
