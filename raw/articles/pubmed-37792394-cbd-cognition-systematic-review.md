# Effects of the Acute and Chronic Administration of Cannabidiol on Cognition in Humans and Animals

PubMed-indexed 2023 systematic review of acute and chronic CBD effects on cognition in humans and animals. The review searched PubMed, Web of Science, PsycINFO, and Scopus and included 59 articles: 36 preclinical studies and 23 clinical trials.

For wiki purposes, the review supports a mixed / low-confidence interpretation: CBD has cognition-relevant clinical and preclinical literature, but the evidence base spans heterogeneous diagnoses, doses, endpoints, and acute-versus-chronic designs.

Relevance: formal counterweight to CBD nootropic anecdotes and product marketing.
