# Modern Warrior recalls Modern Warrior Ready due to undeclared 1,4-DMAA and aniracetam

- Source: FDA company announcement / recall notice
- URL: https://www.fda.gov/safety/recalls-market-withdrawals-safety-alerts/modern-warrior-recalls-modern-warrior-ready-dietary-supplement-due-undeclared-14-dmaa-and-aniracetam
- Ingested: 2026-04-30

## Summary

FDA posted a January 9, 2026 recall notice for Modern Warrior Ready, a dietary supplement marketed for metabolism boosting, improving brain function, and reducing cravings. The company said regulatory testing found undeclared 1,4-DMAA, aniracetam, and tianeptine. FDA labels the notice as a public service announcement, not an FDA endorsement of the product or company. The notice is a concrete product-quality enforcement signal that aniracetam appeared as an undeclared ingredient in a consumer supplement, not as a transparently labeled ingredient with verified sourcing.
