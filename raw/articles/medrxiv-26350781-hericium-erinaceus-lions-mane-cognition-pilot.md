# A randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled study evaluating the impact of Hericium erinaceus (Lion's Mane) on cognitive performance and subjective wellbeing

- medRxiv DOI: 10.64898/2026.04.13.26350781
- Source: https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.04.13.26350781v1
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

Preprint randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial in 109 adults aged 40-75 with self-reported cognitive difficulty. Eight weeks of 2 g/day Hericium erinaceus were associated with better visual attention and working memory plus better subjective sleep, restedness, and mood; no adverse events were reported. Sponsor involvement is material, so the result should be treated as promising but preliminary.
