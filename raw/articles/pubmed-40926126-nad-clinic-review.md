# PubMed 40926126 NAD+ clinical strategies review

Zhang et al. 2025, Nature Aging: "Emerging strategies, applications and challenges of targeting NAD+ in the clinic." DOI 10.1038/s43587-025-00947-6.

This review summarizes safety, bioavailability, and efficacy data from NAD+-related clinical trials, with emphasis on aging and neurodegenerative diseases. It covers established NAD+ precursors such as nicotinic acid and nicotinamide, newer compounds such as nicotinamide riboside and nicotinamide mononucleotide, and emerging precursors.

The abstract frames NAD and NADP as redox cofactors with broader signaling, epigenetic-regulation, and energy-homeostasis roles. It also emphasizes that large-scale studies are still needed to determine optimal dose, administration route, frequency, long-term safety, and interindividual response variability.

Interpretation for the wiki: use this as a current broad-clinic caution source. NAD biology is a plausible clinical target, but route, dose, frequency, and long-term safety are unresolved; direct IV/IM/patch products should not inherit oral precursor or preclinical neurodegeneration claims.
