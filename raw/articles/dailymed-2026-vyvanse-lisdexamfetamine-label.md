# DailyMed Vyvanse lisdexamfetamine label

- Source: DailyMed label for VYVANSE, lisdexamfetamine dimesylate capsules and chewable tablets
- Label updated: 2026-04-30

## Captured takeaways

- Vyvanse / lisdexamfetamine is a central nervous system stimulant and prodrug of amphetamine.
- It is indicated for ADHD in adults and pediatric patients 6 years and older, and for moderate to severe binge eating disorder in adults.
- It is not indicated or recommended for weight loss; the label notes that sympathomimetic weight-loss drugs have been associated with serious cardiovascular adverse events.
- The boxed warning is abuse, misuse, and addiction. Misuse and abuse of CNS stimulants including Vyvanse can result in overdose and death, especially with higher doses or unapproved routes such as snorting or injection.
- Vyvanse contains lisdexamfetamine, a Schedule II controlled substance.
- Main warning categories include serious cardiac disease, increased blood pressure and heart rate, psychiatric adverse reactions, growth suppression in pediatric patients, peripheral vasculopathy / Raynaud's phenomenon, serotonin syndrome, tics / Tourette's, pregnancy/lactation cautions, renal dose limitations, dependence, tolerance, and overdose.
- Morning dosing is recommended; afternoon doses are avoided because of insomnia potential.
- The abuse-liability section reports that 100 mg oral Vyvanse produced lower drug-liking than 40 mg immediate-release d-amphetamine, while 150 mg Vyvanse produced similar drug-liking to 40 mg d-amphetamine and 200 mg diethylpropion in a study of people with drug-abuse history.
- Interpretation for the wiki: lisdexamfetamine is a prescription ADHD/BED stimulant and prodrug comparator, not a nootropic supplement or general cognition treatment.
