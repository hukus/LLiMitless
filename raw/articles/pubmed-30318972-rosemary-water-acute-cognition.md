# Rosemary water acute cognition and cerebrovascular study

- Source type: randomized controlled acute beverage study.
- Publication: Journal of Psychopharmacology, 2018.
- Date checked: 2026-05-11.

This study randomized 80 healthy adults to consume 250 mL rosemary water, a commercially available water containing rosemary extract and hydrolat, or plain mineral water. Participants completed computerized cognitive tasks and subjective alertness/fatigue measures, while near-infrared spectroscopy monitored cerebrovascular responses during testing.

The abstract reported several statistically significant, small beneficial effects of rosemary water on cognition, broadly consistent with prior rosemary aroma work. It also reported cerebrovascular effects measured by near-infrared spectroscopy.

Use in the wiki: supports a route-specific rosemary water / hydrolat branch, but the effects are acute and small, and product identity matters.
