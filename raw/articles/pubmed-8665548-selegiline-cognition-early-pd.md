# Effects of Selegiline in Early Parkinson's Disease

- PMID: 8665548
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8665548/

## Summary

Eight-week randomized placebo-controlled double-blind trial of selegiline 5 mg twice daily in 20 levodopa-naive early Parkinson's disease patients. The paper reports cognitive testing at baseline and 8 weeks and suggests some short-term cognitive effects in a disease-context population, but the sample is tiny and the result is not a healthy-adult enhancement signal.

## Notes

- Source type: human randomized trial.
- Evidence tier: disease-context cognition rescue, not baseline enhancement.
- Claimed domain: cognition in early PD.
- Interpretation: supports selegiline as a state-restoration comparator, not a general nootropic.
- Safety / status: prescription MAOI with interaction and washout constraints.
