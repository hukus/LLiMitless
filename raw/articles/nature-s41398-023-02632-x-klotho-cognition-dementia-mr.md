# Relationship of Klotho with cognition and dementia: Results from the NHANES 2011-2014 and Mendelian randomization study

- PMID: 37914711
- Nature: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41398-023-02632-x
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

2023 Translational Psychiatry paper combining NHANES with Mendelian randomization. In NHANES, higher serum Klotho tracked with better DSST performance; in MR, genetically predicted Klotho showed no significant causal association with any dementia subtype. This is the clearest human evidence split: observational signal without causal confirmation.
