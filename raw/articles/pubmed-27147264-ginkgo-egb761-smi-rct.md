# Effects of Ginkgo biloba extract EGb 761® on cognitive control functions, mental activity of the prefrontal cortex and stress reactivity in elderly adults with subjective memory impairment - a randomized double-blind placebo-controlled trial

- PMID: 27147264
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27147264/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Pilot RCT in older adults with subjective memory impairment. EGb 761 at 240 mg daily improved task-switch cost and trended toward better response inhibition, but delayed response, prospective memory, and fMRI outcomes were not clearly improved. This is a mixed but mechanism-relevant signal for standardized ginkgo.
