# Association Between Irisin Level and Cognitive Function: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis

- PMCID: PMC12284321
- PMID: 40696815
- PMC: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12284321/
- Ingested: 2026-04-29

## Summary

2025 meta-analysis of human correlation studies linking irisin and cognition. The pooled association with global cognition was positive and modest, but the subdomain analysis was mostly null, so the paper supports biomarker association more than domain-specific therapeutic optimism.

## Notes

- Search included five databases and found 11 global-cognition articles.
- Pooled global-cognition correlation: r = 0.26 (95% CI 0.10-0.41).
- Positive subgroup signals appeared in non-Asians, smaller studies, serum and CSF measurements, MMSE-based studies, and disease populations.
- No significant correlations were found for immediate memory, short-term memory, executive function, attention, language, or visuospatial ability.
- BDNF showed a positive correlation with global cognition and with irisin level.
