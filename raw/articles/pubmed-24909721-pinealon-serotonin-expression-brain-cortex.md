# Short peptides stimulate serotonin expression in cells of brain cortex

- PMID: 24909721
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24909721/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

Glu-Asp-Arg (Pinealon/EDR) and Lys-Glu-Asp stimulate serotonin expression in aging brain-cortex cell cultures. The paper is mechanistic and preclinical; it uses molecular docking to argue for gene-level interaction with tryptophan hydroxylase, not a human cognition endpoint.
