# Recombinant human thymosin beta 4 improves ischemic cardiac dysfunction in mice and patients with acute ST-segment elevation myocardial infarction after reperfusion

- PMID: 41229390
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41229390/

## Summary

Human translational paper reporting that recombinant human thymosin beta 4 improved ischemic cardiac dysfunction in mice and in patients with acute STEMI after reperfusion. This adds a human disease-context signal for full thymosin beta 4, but it still does not provide any cognition endpoint.
