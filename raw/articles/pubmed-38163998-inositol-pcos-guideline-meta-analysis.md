# PubMed 38163998 inositol PCOS guideline meta-analysis note

2024 Journal of Clinical Endocrinology and Metabolism systematic review and meta-analysis titled "Inositol for Polycystic Ovary Syndrome: A Systematic Review and Meta-analysis to Inform the 2023 Update of the International Evidence-based PCOS Guidelines."

The review included 30 PCOS trials (n = 2230; 1093 intervention, 1137 control), with 19 pooled in meta-analyses. It assessed hormonal, metabolic, lipid, psychological, anthropometric, reproductive, and adverse-effect outcomes.

Reported synthesis: evidence suggested benefits for myo-inositol or D-chiro-inositol on some metabolic measures and possible ovulation benefit from D-chiro-inositol, but inositol may have no effect on other outcomes. Compared with metformin, myo-inositol likely caused fewer gastrointestinal adverse events, but evidence supporting inositol for PCOS management was limited and inconclusive.

Interpretation for the wiki: this is a metabolic/insulin-resistance and PCOS-state anchor, not cognition evidence. It matters because inositol anecdotes often come from PCOS, insulin-resistance, anxiety, and sleep contexts; the route should remain phenotype/status-gated rather than broad healthy-adult enhancement.
