# Clinical safety and efficacy of allogenic human adipose mesenchymal stromal cells-derived exosomes in patients with mild to moderate Alzheimer's disease: a phase I/II clinical trial

- PMID: 37859748
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37859748/
- Trial registration: NCT04388982

## Summary

Open-label, three-arm phase I/II trial of intranasal allogeneic human adipose MSC-derived exosomes in mild to moderate AD. The medium-dose arm showed the clearest short-term cognitive movement, with no adverse events reported and no significant amyloid/tau differences across arms.

## Notes

The study is small and uncontrolled enough that the cognition signal is still fragile, but it is the main human efficacy anchor for the exosome branch. The conclusion supports further study at a dose of at least 4e8 particles.
