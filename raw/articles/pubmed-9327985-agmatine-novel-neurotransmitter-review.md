# Agmatine: a novel neurotransmitter?

PubMed-indexed review describing agmatine as a putative neurotransmitter / neuromodulator. The abstract says evidence is consistent with agmatine being synthesized, stored, and released in brain, contained in neurons and axon terminals, interacting with receptors, and producing CNS actions.

The review also notes that agmatine's role in normal brain function had not yet been established, partly because of limited selective tools affecting its biosynthesis or degradation.

Relevance: mechanism anchor for agmatine without upgrading it beyond preclinical / speculative cognition status.
