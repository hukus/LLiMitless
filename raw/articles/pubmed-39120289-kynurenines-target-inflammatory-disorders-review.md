# Kynurenines as a Novel Target for the Treatment of Inflammatory Disorders

- PMID: 39120289
- DOI: 10.3390/cells13151259
- Journal: Cells (2024)

## Captured takeaways

- The review presents kynurenine-pathway modulation as a therapeutic concept in inflammatory disorders.
- IDO1, IDO2, TDO, and KMO are named as promising targets.
- The authors emphasize that both inhibition and activation may be beneficial depending on the disorder, which makes the pathway context-sensitive rather than uniformly suppressive.
- Interpretation for the wiki: target review for immunometabolic context, not a cognition endpoint paper.
