# The gain-of-function TREM2-T96K mutation increases risk for Alzheimer's disease by impairing microglial function

- PMID: 41109213
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41109213/
- Retrieved: 2026-04-30

## Summary

2026 Neuron paper showing that the TREM2-T96K mutation increases Alzheimer's disease risk even though it behaves as a gain-of-function variant in some respects. In mouse and human microglial systems, the mutation reduced soluble TREM2, lowered surface TREM2, and impaired Aβ42 uptake. The paper is a strong warning that more TREM2 signaling is not automatically better.

## Notes

- Human genetics plus functional microglial data make this a high-value precision-target anchor.
- Supports a dose/timing/specificity view of TREM2 rather than a simple "activate TREM2" framing.
