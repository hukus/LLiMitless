# Application of down-phase targeted auditory stimulation during sleep in a home setting: A feasibility study across seven consecutive nights

Sleep Medicine 2025 study in five healthy adults using the MHSL Sleepband Version 3 in an unsupervised home setting over seven consecutive nights. Participants received STIM and SHAM conditions with tones delivered shortly before the negative peak of slow waves during N2/N3 sleep in alternating 10-second ON/OFF windows.

Captured takeaways:

- Data quality was sufficient for analysis in 87% of recordings, with more than 1500 correctly delivered stimuli per recording on average.
- The protocol did not alter sleep architecture or subjective sleep quality, depth, or sleepiness.
- Down-phase PTAS reduced low slow-wave activity, especially during the first sleep cycle and in OFF windows.
- The stimulation elicited a K-complex-like auditory evoked response.
- The paper's claim is feasibility and tolerability, not behavioral efficacy.

Wiki use:

This is a translational home-delivery proof that the control loop can run repeatedly outside the lab, but it does not yet show cognitive or mood benefit.
