# Experience of using Actitropil in chronic cerebral ischemia: clinical follow-up results

- Journal page: https://www.rusmedreview.com/en/articles/nevrologiya/Opyt_primeneniya_preparata_Aktitropil_pri_hronicheskoy_ishemii_golovnogo_mozga_rezulytaty_nablyudatelynoy_klinicheskoy_programmy/
- DOI: https://doi.org/10.32364/2587-6821-2023-7-10-2
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

2023 observational clinical follow-up in 30 patients with chronic cerebral ischemia. Actitropil (fonturacetam) was given at 200 mg/day for 30 days. The abstract reports statistically significant improvement in MoCA, EQ-5D, MFI-20, and neurologic symptom severity, with no serious adverse events or dose-limiting adverse events reported. The phenotype is recovery / cerebrovascular impairment, not healthy-adult enhancement.
