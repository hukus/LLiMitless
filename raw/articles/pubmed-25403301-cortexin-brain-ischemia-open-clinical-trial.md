# [An open clinical trial of cortexin in treatment of brain ischemia]

- PMID: 25403301
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25403301/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

This open clinical trial reported cortexin use in brain ischemia with before/after changes in cognitive and affective scales. The design is not blinded, but it is direct human exposure evidence for the cortexin branch.
