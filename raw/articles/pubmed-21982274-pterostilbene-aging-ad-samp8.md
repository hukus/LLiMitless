# Low-dose pterostilbene SAMP8 aging and Alzheimer's disease model

- Source type: PubMed-indexed preclinical paper.
- PMID: 21982274
- DOI: 10.1016/j.neurobiolaging.2011.08.015
- Publication: Neurobiology of Aging, 2012.
- Date checked: 2026-05-04.

Preclinical paper comparing diet-achievable pterostilbene and resveratrol in SAMP8 mice, a model of accelerated aging and sporadic / age-related Alzheimer's disease. The source is titled "Low-dose pterostilbene, but not resveratrol, is a potent neuromodulator in aging and Alzheimer's disease" and is relevant as a resveratrol-boundary paper because it argues that the two stilbenes should not be treated as interchangeable.

Use in the wiki: preclinical aging / AD-model boundary source. It supports standalone pterostilbene tracking but does not establish human cognition efficacy.
