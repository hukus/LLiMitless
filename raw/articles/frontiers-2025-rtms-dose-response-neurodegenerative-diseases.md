# Repetitive transcranial magnetic stimulation across neurodegenerative diseases: a systematic review and dose-response meta-analysis.

- DOI: 10.3389/fnagi.2025.1615734
- Published: 2025-07-10
- Source: Frontiers in Aging Neuroscience

## Summary

Dose-response meta-analysis across PD and AD found non-linear pulse-response relationships rather than a monotonic "more is better" pattern.

## Notes

- Included 51 publications and 1,938 subjects.
- Average sessions: 14; range: 3 to 32.
- Average total pulses: 23,300; range: 500 to 80,000.
- 55% of studies used 1-10 Hz, 35% used >10 Hz, and 10% used <=1 Hz.
- For AD, MMSE and MoCA showed bell-shaped curves with peaks around 28,793 pulses and 25,201 pulses, respectively.
- For PD, several motor and affective outcomes showed bell-shaped dose-response curves, supporting a narrow optimal-dose idea.
