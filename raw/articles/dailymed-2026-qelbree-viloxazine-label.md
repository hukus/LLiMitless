# DailyMed 2026 Qelbree viloxazine label

## Captured takeaways

- DailyMed label for Qelbree / viloxazine hydrochloride extended-release capsules.
- Qelbree is described as a selective norepinephrine reuptake inhibitor indicated for ADHD in adults and pediatric patients aged 6 years and older.
- Label warnings include suicidal thoughts and behaviors, blood pressure and heart rate increases, activation of mania or hypomania, somnolence and fatigue, and clinically important drug interactions.
- DailyMed notes that coadministration with moderate sensitive CYP1A2 substrates is not recommended.
