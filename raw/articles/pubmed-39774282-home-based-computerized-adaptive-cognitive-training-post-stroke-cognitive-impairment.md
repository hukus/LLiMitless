# Efficacy of home based computerized adaptive cognitive training in patients with post stroke cognitive impairment: a randomized controlled trial

- PMID: 39774282
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39774282/
- Ingested: 2026-04-29

## Summary

Randomized controlled trial of a 4-week remotely delivered multi-domain computer-adaptive cognitive retraining program in post-stroke cognitive impairment. The intervention was tailored to individual performance levels and compared against standard care.

The abstract frames the study as supporting improved neuropsychological function and daily living capabilities in stroke-related cognitive impairment, but it remains a phenotype-bound rehabilitation result rather than general enhancement evidence.

## Notes

Useful as a home-based adaptive training comparator, especially because it makes the adaptive loop explicit rather than hiding it inside a generic digital cognitive app.
