# Agmatine Improves Cognitive Dysfunction and Prevents Cell Death in a Streptozotocin-Induced Alzheimer Rat Model

2014 Yonsei Medical Journal preclinical study of agmatine in an intracerebroventricular streptozotocin Alzheimer-like rat model. Rats received agmatine 100 mg/kg daily for up to two weeks after STZ injection.

The paper reports reduced amyloid-beta accumulation, improved insulin signaling, antioxidant pathway activation, reduced apoptosis markers, and improved Morris-water-maze performance in the STZ-agmatine group versus disease-model controls.

Relevance: agmatine has a cognition-relevant mechanism and animal signal, but this is not human nootropic evidence.
