# Natural choline from egg yolk phospholipids is more efficiently absorbed compared with choline bitartrate

- PMCID: PMC6893749
- DOI: 10.3390/nu11112758
- Journal: Nutrients
- Year: 2019
- Ingested: 2026-05-04

## Summary

Randomized double-blind crossover bioavailability trial in 18 apparently healthy adults aged 30-70. Participants consumed an egg-yolk phospholipid drink containing 3 g phospholipid-bound choline, mainly phosphatidylcholine, or a control drink containing 3 g choline as choline bitartrate. Plasma choline, betaine, and dimethylglycine were measured for 6 hours. The egg-yolk phospholipid drink produced a roughly fourfold higher plasma choline incremental area-under-the-curve than choline bitartrate, with similar direction for betaine and dimethylglycine.

## Notes

- This is a biomarker / absorption study, not a cognition trial.
- Use to separate phospholipid-bound choline from choline salt exposure.
- The intervention also carried egg-yolk phospholipids and DHA-bound phosphatidylcholine, so do not generalize to all lecithin products.
