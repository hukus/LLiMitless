# Theobromine plus low-calorie diet in overweight and obese adults with metabolic syndrome

- Journal: Food & Function (2023)
- PubMed anchor: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37615657/
- DOI visible from RSC article landing page: 10.1039/D3FO00555K

## Captured takeaways

- Randomized controlled trial testing pure theobromine supplementation with a low-calorie diet in overweight and obese adults with metabolic syndrome.
- The intervention was 450 mg/day theobromine for 12 weeks combined with a low-calorie diet.
- PubMed / RSC snippets report the trial focused on anthropometric and metabolic-syndrome indices, not cognitive endpoints.
- PubMed snippet reports no significant between-group differences for body weight, BMI, hip circumference, hip-to-waist ratio, systolic or diastolic blood pressure, fasting total cholesterol, triglycerides, LDL-C, fasting glucose, insulin, HOMA-IR, or beta-cell function.
- Interpretation for the wiki: this is a metabolic/safety-context trial, not a cognition trial. It helps bound chronic exposure and prevents extrapolating cocoa-product benefits to pure theobromine cognition.
