# Energy Drink Ingredients Beyond Caffeine Review

- PMID: 23206286.
- DOI: 10.1111/j.1753-4887.2012.00525.x.
- Journal: Nutrition Reviews, 2012.
- Ingested: 2026-05-03.

## Summary

Review asking whether energy-drink ingredients other than caffeine contribute to physical or cognitive performance. The review considered 32 articles on energy-drink ingredients alone or combined with caffeine.

The authors concluded that, aside from weak evidence for glucose and guarana extract, evidence overwhelmingly did not substantiate claims that other energy-drink components such as taurine contribute to physical or cognitive performance beyond caffeine.

## Interpretation

Use this as a product-attribution gate. Taurine in energy drinks should not be credited with caffeine-like performance effects unless the trial isolates taurine.
