# Lithium Oral Solution Medication Guide

## Summary

The label emphasizes a narrow therapeutic range, serum monitoring, and clinically important toxicity risk near therapeutic concentrations. It flags kidney problems, thyroid problems, sodium depletion, antipsychotic interactions, serotonergic drug interactions, and serotonin syndrome risk. This is the safety anchor for any low-dose-lithium discussion.
