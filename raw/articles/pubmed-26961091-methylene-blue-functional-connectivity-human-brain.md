# Methylene blue modulates functional connectivity in the human brain

- PMID: 26961091
- PMC: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5018244/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Healthy-adult randomized double-blind placebo-controlled imaging study of a single low oral dose of methylene blue. MB increased resting-state functional connectivity in networks linking perception and memory, and changed task-related deactivation/CBF patterns during a visuomotor task. The exclusion profile again centered on G6PD deficiency, serotonergic drugs, MRI contraindications, renal/hepatic disease, and pregnancy.
