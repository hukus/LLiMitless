# Do We Really Become Smarter When Our Fluid-Intelligence Test Scores Improve?

- PMID: 25395695
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25395695/
- Ingested: 2026-05-03

## Summary

Strategy-training study showing that scores on a fluid-intelligence-style matrix task can increase after short explicit instruction. The abstract reports a training gain of d = 0.56, while the control group showed no gain. The authors argue that some apparent fluid-intelligence improvement can reflect acquired task strategies rather than a broad rise in intelligence.

## Notes

This source makes the distinction between measured matrix-test improvement and latent g explicit. It supports using strategy instruction if the target is an IQ-test score, and it warns against interpreting that score gain as broad cognitive enhancement.
