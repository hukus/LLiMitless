# Neuroenhancement by repetitive transcranial magnetic stimulation (rTMS) on DLPFC in healthy adults

- PMID: 39866659
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39866659/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Review/meta-analysis of healthy-adult rTMS studies centered on DLPFC and theta-burst protocols. The abstract says the field may show cognitive benefit after excluding outliers, but heterogeneity remains high and target selection/navigation are major sources of variance. The review covers working memory, attention, cognitive control, learning, processing speed, and decision-making.
