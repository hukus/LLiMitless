# Long-acting intranasal insulin for the treatment of delirium-a randomised clinical trial

- PMID: 41091693
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41091693/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Randomized delirium-treatment trial in older hospitalized patients using long-acting intranasal insulin.

## Notes

Primary delirium-duration endpoint was not significant overall, but length of stay shortened and a younger-old subgroup looked more responsive. This is adjacent evidence, not a clean cognition win.
