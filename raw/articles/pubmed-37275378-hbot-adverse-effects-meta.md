# Adverse effects of hyperbaric oxygen therapy: a systematic review and meta-analysis.

- PMID: 37275378
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37275378/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Broad HBOT safety meta-analysis showing more adverse effects than control, with ear discomfort the most common signal.

## Notes

Across 24 RCTs and 1,497 participants, adverse effects were more common with HBOT than control (30.11% vs 10.43%). Risk rose with more than 10 sessions and with chamber pressure above 2.0 ATA. This is relevant background for any cognition-focused HBOT protocol.
