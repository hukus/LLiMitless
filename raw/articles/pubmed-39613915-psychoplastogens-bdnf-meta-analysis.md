# Effects of psychoplastogens on blood levels of brain-derived neurotrophic factor (BDNF) in humans: a systematic review and meta-analysis

- PMID: 39613915
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39613915/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

2025 meta-analysis of human peripheral BDNF after psychoplastogens. The dataset included ketamine, esketamine, LSD, psilocybin, ayahuasca, DMT, MDMA, scopolamine, and rapastinel, and the authors reported mixed human BDNF results overall. This is useful as a biomarker context page, but it does not establish a non-hallucinogenic psychoplastogen cognition effect.
