# EFSA 2018 D-ribose novel food safety opinion

EFSA's 2018 Novel Food safety opinion evaluated D-ribose as a food ingredient for adults excluding pregnant and lactating women. The panel reviewed identity, production, toxicology, human tolerance, proposed use levels, and intake estimates.

Safety interpretation: EFSA concluded that D-ribose was safe for the proposed uses and use levels, with an adult intake limit in the opinion. The safety context is important because ribose is a sugar and high acute exposures can have gastrointestinal and glycemic effects; dose and population matter.

Interpretation for the wiki: EFSA supports that D-ribose has a consumer-food safety packet, but safety review is not efficacy evidence. The wiki should still keep GI intolerance, glycemic symptoms, diabetes/hypoglycemia context, pregnancy/lactation exclusion, and product-dose accuracy visible.
