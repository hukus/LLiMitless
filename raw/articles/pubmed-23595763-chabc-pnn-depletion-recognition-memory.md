# PubMed 23595763 - ChABC PNN Depletion and Recognition Memory

## Citation

- Romberg C, Yang S, Melani R, et al. "Depletion of perineuronal nets enhances recognition memory and long-term depression in the perirhinal cortex." Journal of Neuroscience. 2013. DOI: 10.1523/JNEUROSCI.6267-11.2013. PMCID: PMC3655688.
- Source type: peer-reviewed animal study.
- Chemical indexing includes chondroitin ABC lyase.

## Extracted Claims

- PNNs are presented as regulators of critical-period closure and adult plasticity.
- Genetic attenuation of PNNs in adult Crtl1 knockout mice enhanced long-term object-recognition memory and facilitated long-term depression in the perirhinal cortex.
- Local digestion of PNNs with chondroitinase ABC produced a similar prolongation of object-recognition memory.
- The memory-enhancing effect attenuated over time, consistent with PNN regeneration restoring constraints on plasticity.

## Cognition-Relevant Extraction

- Medicinal cognition signal: positive animal recognition-memory signal after local PNN depletion.
- Translation status: no human cognition trial; route and target are invasive/local in the relevant experiment.
- Signal-strength read: moderate preclinical mechanistic signal, low medicinal confidence for human cognition.
- Safety interpretation for the wiki: reversibility/regeneration matters, but the intervention manipulates mature-circuit plasticity rather than supplying a standard systemic dose.
