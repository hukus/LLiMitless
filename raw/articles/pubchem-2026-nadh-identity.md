# PubChem 2026 NADH identity note

PubChem identifies NADH as CID 439153 with molecular formula C21H29N7O14P2 and molecular weight 665.4 g/mol. Synonyms include NADH, DPNH, beta-NADH, 1,4-dihydronicotinamide adenine dinucleotide, coenzyme-I, ENADA, and nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide reduced.

PubChem describes NADH as the reduced form of NAD+, while NAD+ is the oxidized form of NADH. It is a coenzyme found in living cells and is involved in enzymatic redox reactions as an electron carrier. PubChem-linked drug-information fields mention possible therapeutic relevance in Parkinson's disease, chronic fatigue syndrome, Alzheimer's disease, and cardiovascular disease, but this identity record is not an efficacy assessment.

Interpretation for the wiki: NADH is a named redox cofactor and should be separated from NAD+, nicotinamide riboside, nicotinamide mononucleotide, niacin, nicotinamide, IV NAD+ products, and broad "NAD booster" blends. Any cognition claim must specify reduced NADH form, dose, route, product stabilization, and whether it is NADH alone or a CoQ10 plus NADH combination.
