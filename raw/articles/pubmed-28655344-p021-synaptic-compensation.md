# Prevention of dendritic, synaptic, and cognitive deficits with P021

- PMID: 28655344
- DOI: 10.1186/s13195-017-0273-7
- Journal: Alzheimer's Research & Therapy (2017)

## Summary

P021 was administered in feed to female 3xTg-AD mice beginning at 3 months of age, before overt amyloid-beta or tau pathology, and continued as long as 21 months. The study reported improved dendritic and synaptic markers, increased neurogenesis, and better performance on Morris water maze and object-location tasks compared with vehicle-treated transgenic mice.

The paper describes P021 as derived from the active CNTF tetrapeptide region with a C-terminal adamantylated glycine intended to improve stability and brain penetration. It gives the formulated-feed concentration as 60 nmol/g and estimated intake as about 162 nmol per mouse per day.

## Evidence boundary

Treatment began far earlier than a typical symptomatic human intervention and continued for much of the animals' lives. Several tissue-marker comparisons used small group sizes. The results support a preclinical prevention hypothesis, not a human treatment protocol.
