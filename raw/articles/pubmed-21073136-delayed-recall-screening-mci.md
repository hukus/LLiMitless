# A delayed recall battery as a sensitive screening for mild cognitive impairment: follow-up study of memory clinic patients after 10 years

- PMID: 21073136
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21073136/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

2010 follow-up study arguing that a delayed-recall battery can sensitively screen for MCI and probable AD over long follow-up. The main practical implication is that delayed recall is a high-value endpoint when the research question includes memory consolidation rather than just immediate performance.

## Notes

Delayed recall should be scheduled after a fixed delay and standardized encoding, otherwise the signal becomes hard to compare across sessions.
