# Digital Physiological Biomarkers Predict Within-Person Symptom Changes in Complex Chronic Illness

- PMID: 41876851
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41876851/
- Ingested: 2026-04-30

## Summary

Digital physiological signals, including morning wearable metrics such as HR and HRV, improved prediction of same-day symptom worsening in complex chronic illness. The value here is the near-real-time state-prediction layer rather than a cognition-specific effect.

## Notes

- Relevant as a state marker for fatigue, brain fog, and intervention timing.
- Supports the claim that passive physiology can add signal beyond prior-day symptom reports.
- Not a responder trial for cognition interventions by itself.
