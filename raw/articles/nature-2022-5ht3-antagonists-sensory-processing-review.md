# 5-HT3 antagonists and sensory processing systematic review

2022 Neuropsychopharmacology systematic review of ondansetron and other 5-HT3 antagonists on sensory gating and sensory processing.

## Captured takeaways

- The review included 11 eligible human studies.
- Eight studies were in patients with schizophrenia who were chronically stable on antipsychotic medication.
- Five studies measured P50 suppression response to repeated auditory stimuli.
- Three studies were in healthy participants and used visuoperceptual or sensorimotor tests.
- The review found a consistent and robust finding that ondansetron and tropisetron improved sensory gating in patients with schizophrenia.
- Tropisetron also improved sustained visual attention in non-smoking patients in the reviewed schizophrenia literature.
- Evidence for other measures of sensory processing was inconsistent, and interpretation was limited by small study count, methodological heterogeneity, and concomitant medication confounding.
- The review notes no clear healthy-participant performance gain in the reviewed non-schizophrenia sensory-processing studies.

## Wiki interpretation

- Use as the class-level caution source for [[tropisetron]] and other setron-style cognition claims.
- The clearest signal is P50/sensory gating in schizophrenia, not broad healthy cognition.
