# Methionine tolerance in healthy older adults

Deutz et al. tested supplemental oral free L-methionine in 15 healthy older adults using 4-week dose periods with washouts. Average supplemental intakes were 9.2, 22.5, 46.3, and 91 mg/kg/day.

The study used plasma homocysteine as the primary determinant for tolerance, while also tracking blood chemistry, body composition, tolerance, quality of life, and questionnaire-based cognitive function. Plasma homocysteine was elevated at the highest dose. The estimated no-observed-adverse-effect level was 46.3 mg/kg/day, and the estimated lowest-observed-adverse-effect level was 91 mg/kg/day, based on homocysteine.

No meaningful health, wellbeing, or body-composition effects were reported at the tested boundary, but this was a small tolerance study, not a cognition-efficacy trial.

Wiki use: use as the main human supplement-dose safety anchor for [[l-methionine]]. It argues for homocysteine monitoring and dose caution, while providing no support for healthy-adult cognitive enhancement.
