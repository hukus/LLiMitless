# Focused ultrasound-mediated blood-brain barrier opening in Alzheimer's disease: long-term safety, imaging, and cognitive outcomes

- PMID: 36334289
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36334289/
- Published: 2023-07-01
- Ingested: 2026-04-30

## Summary

This open-label AD follow-up expanded the treatment field beyond the hippocampus and entorhinal cortex to include frontal and parietal targets. Ten mild AD participants completed 30 BBB-opening treatments across two institutions with 6-12 months of follow-up, all with immediate BBB opening and closure within 24-48 hours, no serious procedure-related adverse events, and cognitive change comparable to ADNI controls. The paper supports repeated, supervised feasibility and suggests no apparent cognitive worsening up to 1 year, but it does not establish a controlled cognition benefit.
