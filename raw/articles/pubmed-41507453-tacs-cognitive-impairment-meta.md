# Efficacy and safety of tACS in older adults with cognitive impairment: a systematic review and meta-analysis

- PMID: 41507453
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41507453/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

2026 systematic review and meta-analysis of tACS in older adults with cognitive impairment. Across 6 publications and 888 subjects, tACS improved memory and overall cognition, did not clearly help attention, and was reported as safe. This is the clearest newer human signal that tACS may have a disease-context cognition effect, even though it still does not establish a broad healthy-adult enhancement claim.

## Notes

Useful for the tACS scan because it gives an explicit impairment-cohort effect split rather than only a working-memory-specific summary.
