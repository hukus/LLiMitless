# NCT06632470 - Human umbilical cord MSC secretome in moderate dementia

- Registry source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06632470
- Status: not yet recruiting
- Sponsor: Baermed
- Condition: moderate dementia

## Summary

Open-label, non-randomized secretome trial in moderate dementia. The protocol uses intramuscular secretome injections every two weeks for four months, with blood markers and MMSE / mini-cog / AD8 as outcomes.

## Notes

This is useful for separating secretome therapy from exosomes and for tracking which cognition-adjacent outcomes are being used in current regenerative-medicine protocols.
