# Clinical and pharmacologic study of tributyrin as an oral butyrate prodrug

- PMID: 12736763
- Published: 2003
- Source type: clinical pharmacology / oncology trial
- Checked: 2026-05-11
- Population: 20 advanced solid-tumor patients

## Summary

This clinical pharmacology study tested oral tributyrin three times daily in advanced solid-tumor patients. Doses ranged from 150 to 200 mg/kg three times daily. No dose-limiting toxicity was reported, but escalation stopped at 200 mg/kg three times daily because of capsule burden. Median butyrate concentration was about 52 micromolar with substantial interpatient variability. No objective tumor responses were seen.

Use this as a human PK and tolerability anchor for tributyrin, especially the short exposure window, interindividual variability, and capsule-burden problem. It is not a cognition study.

## Notes

- This is much higher exposure than typical supplement-discussion doses.
- The oncology context limits safety generalization to healthy users.
