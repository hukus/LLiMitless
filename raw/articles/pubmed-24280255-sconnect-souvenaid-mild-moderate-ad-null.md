# S-Connect randomized trial of Souvenaid in mild-to-moderate Alzheimer's disease

- PMID: 24280255
- PMCID: PMC3978853
- Journal: Alzheimer's Research & Therapy (2013)

## Captured takeaways

- Twenty-four-week double-masked trial at 48 clinical centers.
- Enrolled 527 people with mild-to-moderate Alzheimer's disease who were already taking stable AD medications.
- Participants were randomized 1:1 to daily 125 mL Souvenaid or an isocaloric control product.
- The PubMed abstract reports that add-on Souvenaid did not slow cognitive decline in people treated for mild-to-moderate AD.
- Interpretation for the wiki: Fortasyn / Souvenaid evidence is stage- and context-sensitive; the add-on mild-to-moderate AD trial is a direct null counterweight to early mild/prodromal signals.
