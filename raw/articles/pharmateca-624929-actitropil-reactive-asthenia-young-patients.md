# Efficacy and safety of the Actitropil in the treatment of reactive asthenia in young patients

- Journal page: https://journals.eco-vector.com/2073-4034/article/view/624929/en_US
- DOI: https://doi.org/10.18565/pharmateca.2023.9-10.159-166
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

2023 open-label, non-randomized observational study in 50 young adults aged 18-30 with reactive asthenia. Patients received Actitropil (fonturacetam) 200 mg/day for 30 days with 60 days of follow-up. The abstract reports decreases in MFI-20 and HADS scores and better sleep on the Spiegel scale, with no side effects reported. This is a rescue / fatigue context, not a healthy-adult enhancement trial.
