# FDA ingredient directory entries for higenamine, hordenine, and octopamine

FDA's dietary supplement ingredient directory lists higenamine, hordenine, and octopamine as substances with agency actions tied to the May 2022 constituent update and warning letters. The directory says it is an FDA action/communication directory and reminds readers that FDA does not approve dietary supplements for safety and effectiveness.

The relevant entries list higenamine and hordenine as new dietary ingredients requiring a premarket safety notification for which no notification has been submitted. Higenamine HCl and hordenine HCl are listed separately as not qualifying as dietary ingredients. Octopamine is listed as a new dietary ingredient requiring premarket safety notification. FDA's directory dates these entries to 2023/03.

Use in the wiki: current official regulatory-status anchor for keeping higenamine, hordenine, and octopamine risk-first rather than nootropic-ranked.
