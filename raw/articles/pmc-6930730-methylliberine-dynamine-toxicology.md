# A Toxicological Evaluation of Methylliberine (Dynamine)

- PMID: 31911801
- PMCID: PMC6930730
- DOI: 10.1155/2019/4981420
- Ingested: 2026-05-03

## Summary

2019 toxicological evaluation of Dynamine methylliberine. The test item was a synthetic >=98% pure methylliberine ingredient from Compound Solutions, with the lot used in testing reported at 99.6% purity and produced to food-grade specifications.

The paper reports standard genotoxicity / mutagenicity and repeat-dose toxicology work. The 90-day rat study concluded a NOAEL of 150 mg/kg body weight/day in males and 225 mg/kg body weight/day in females. Funding was provided by Compound Solutions.

Use in the wiki: product-specific preclinical safety and identity source for Dynamine. It supports short-term consumer-product plausibility but does not establish long-term human safety or cognitive efficacy.
