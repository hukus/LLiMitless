# Kynurenic Acid and Promotion of Activity-Dependent Synapse Elimination in Schizophrenia

- PMID: 40165559
- DOI: 10.1176/appi.ajp.20240048
- Journal: American Journal of Psychiatry (2025)

## Captured takeaways

- Patient-derived neuron/microglia models were used to test how KYNA influences synaptic activity and microglial uptake.
- KYNA increased microglial uptake of synaptic structures, while inhibition of endogenous KYNA production reduced synapse internalization.
- Large-scale transcriptomic and genetic datasets linked KYNA-producing KATs to synaptic activity genes and schizophrenia risk variants.
- Interpretation for the wiki: this is a newer mechanistic delta for schizophrenia cognition, but it still stops short of a human intervention or cognition outcome.
