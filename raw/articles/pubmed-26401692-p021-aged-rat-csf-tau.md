# Elevated tau in aged-rat CSF reduced by P021

- PMID: 26401692
- DOI: 10.3233/JAD-142799
- Journal: Journal of Alzheimer's Disease (2015)

## Summary

In aged Fischer rats, chronic oral P021 reduced total tau in brain and cerebrospinal fluid without reducing CSF amyloid-beta/A-beta-precursor-protein measures. The authors described P021 as blood-brain-barrier permeable and reported no detectable immune reaction in the rats.

## Evidence boundary

The abstract does not provide a human exposure or safety bridge. A later independent ADDF evidence review noted that direct measurement of P021 reaching mouse brain had not been documented, so the BBB claim should be treated as preclinical and incompletely characterized rather than as settled pharmacokinetics.
