# Pentoxifylline as an Adjunctive in Treatment of Negative Symptoms in Chronic Schizophrenia

- ClinicalTrials.gov ID: NCT04094207
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

ClinicalTrials.gov shows this pentoxifylline schizophrenia study as completed with no results posted. The registry lists a PANSS total-score primary outcome and cytokine secondary outcomes. The study is therefore useful as an endpoint-framing record, but it does not validate the published positive interpretation on its own.

## Key points

- Sponsor: Sadat City University.
- Population: adults with chronic schizophrenia stable on risperidone.
- Design: randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled parallel trial.
- Enrollment: 80 actual.
- Study start: 2019-01-01.
- Primary completion: 2021-09-30.
- Study completion: 2022-09-30.
- Last update posted: 2022-10-25.
- Results posting: no results posted.
- Primary endpoint: treatment-induced change in total PANSS score.
- Secondary endpoint: plasma cytokine level change.
- Publications list the 2023 pentoxifylline cognition/negative-symptom paper, which was later retracted.
