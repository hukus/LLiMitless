# Neurophysiological effects of targeting sleep spindles with closed-loop auditory stimulation

Sleep Advances 2025 study in 10 healthy adults across six nights each. The algorithm detected sleep spindles and delivered auditory stimulation before spindle offset in most detections without disturbing sleep. Compared with sham, stimulation increased sigma activity after the cue, but if stimulation occurred at the beginning of the spindle it could also terminate the spindle early.

This is validation of a spindle-targeting closed-loop method rather than a memory-performance study. It matters because it shows the control loop can hit the intended event, but it also shows that the same intervention can disrupt the spindle it is trying to engage.
