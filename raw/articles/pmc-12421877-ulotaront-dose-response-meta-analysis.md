# Trajectory of efficacy and safety across ulotaront dose levels in schizophrenia

- Source type: dose-response meta-analysis of randomized clinical trials.
- Included studies: 3 randomized trials, 1144 participants.
- Main efficacy snapshot: 100 mg had the largest PANSS total improvement, with a small effect size, and PANSS negative symptoms also favored higher doses.
- Global severity: CGI-S did not show a clear dose-response relationship.
- Safety snapshot: most safety outcomes did not show a clear dose-response relationship, but anxiety-related adverse events were higher than placebo at 50 mg and 75 mg.
- Interpretation: the meta-analysis softens the most pessimistic reading of ulotaront by showing a small dose-linked symptom signal, but it still does not establish healthy-adult cognition benefit or durable nootropic value.
