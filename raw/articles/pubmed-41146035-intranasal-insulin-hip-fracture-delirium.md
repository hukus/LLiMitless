# Effect of perioperative intranasal insulin on postoperative delirium in elderly hip fracture patients undergoing general anesthesia: a randomized controlled study

- PMID: 41146035
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41146035/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Randomized controlled study in elderly hip-fracture surgery patients under general anesthesia. Intranasal insulin 20 units twice daily, starting two days preoperatively and continuing five days, reduced postoperative delirium and lowered IL-6 and CRP.

## Notes

This is perioperative protection evidence with an inflammatory readout, not broad cognition enhancement.
