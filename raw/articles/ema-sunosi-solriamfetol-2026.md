# EMA Sunosi / solriamfetol

- Sunosi is a prescription medicine used to improve wakefulness and reduce excessive daytime sleepiness in narcolepsy and obstructive sleep apnoea.
- The active substance is solriamfetol and the EU marketing authorization dates to 2020-01-16.
- The EMA page keeps Sunosi in the disorder-context wakefulness lane, not the healthy-enhancement lane.
