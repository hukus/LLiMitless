## Summary

ClinicalTrials.gov registry record for a planned randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled phase 2 study of TAK-653 in treatment-resistant depression after ketamine response assessment. The registry lists TAK-653 6 mg daily and placebo arms, depression outcomes such as MADRS, and safety endpoints. Actual enrollment is listed as 0, with no results posted.

This record matters for development-status context only. It does not provide cognition evidence and should not be counted as a positive clinical signal.
