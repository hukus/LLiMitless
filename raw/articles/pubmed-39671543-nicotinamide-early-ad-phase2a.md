# Nicotinamide in early Alzheimer disease phase 2A

PubMed 39671543: phase 2A proof-of-concept randomized placebo-controlled trial of nicotinamide in early Alzheimer disease.

Key capture:

- Population: early Alzheimer disease; 47 participants randomized.
- Intervention: oral nicotinamide 1,500 mg twice daily, or 3 g/day total, for 48 weeks versus placebo.
- Primary endpoint: CSF tau phosphorylated at threonine 231 / p-tau231.
- Prespecified secondary outcomes included CSF biomarkers and clinical measures including ADAS-Cog13, ADCS-ADL-MCI, and CDR-SB.
- Main result shape: nicotinamide treatment did not reduce tau phosphorylation in early AD. The study does not establish cognitive efficacy for nicotinamide. Exploratory or unadjusted clinical/imaging signals should be treated as hypothesis-generating rather than a cognition-positive result.

Interpretation for the wiki:

This is the main high-dose nicotinamide clinical counterweight for [[niacin-nicotinamide-b3]]. It keeps ordinary niacinamide / nicotinamide below the NR/NMN/NADH hype layer and below cognition-positive ranking. The trial is disease-context, high-dose, and not a healthy-adult nootropic result.
