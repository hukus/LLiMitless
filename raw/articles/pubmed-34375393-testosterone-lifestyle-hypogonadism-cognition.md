# Testosterone plus lifestyle in obese hypogonadal older men
- PMID: 34375393
- LITROS secondary analysis: 83 older obese hypogonadal men with frailty randomized to lifestyle intervention plus testosterone vs lifestyle plus placebo for 6 months.
- Primary outcome: global cognition composite z score; secondary outcomes included attention/information processing, memory, executive function, and language.
- Key result: global cognition improved more in the testosterone arm, with additional gains in attention/information processing and memory.
- Branch readout: this is a phenotype-sensitive positive signal, but it is tied to lifestyle co-intervention and a high-risk endocrine/metabolic cohort.
