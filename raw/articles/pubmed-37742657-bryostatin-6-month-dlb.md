# Advanced Alzheimer's Disease Patients Show Safe, Significant, and Persistent Benefit in 6-Month Bryostatin Trial

- PMID: 37742657
- Journal: Journal of Alzheimer's Disease, 2023
- Evidence tier: human phase 2 / sponsor-linked follow-up

## Summary

This paper reports a 6-month bryostatin trial in advanced AD with 122 patients and two prespecified severity cohorts.

Key design details:

- Two cohorts: moderate MMSE 15-18 and moderately severe MMSE 10-14.
- Bryostatin was given intravenously in repeated cycles across the treatment period.
- Randomization was stratified by baseline SIB.

Main findings:

- The moderately severe cohort showed significant improvement over placebo from Weeks 13 through 42 on SIB, including persistence after dosing ended.
- The paper reports no safety events noted by the DSMB.
- The moderate cohort did not clearly carry the same signal.

Interpretation for the wiki:

- This is a phenotype-dependent positive signal, not a broad class victory.
- The post hoc / cohort-specific framing should stay visible when the branch is summarized.
- Use this paper together with the ClinicalTrials.gov results record for NCT04538066, not instead of it.
