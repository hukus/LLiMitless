# Transcranial Photobiomodulation Improves Cognitive Function, Post-Concussion, and PTSD Symptoms in Mild Traumatic Brain Injury

- PMID: 40485299
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40485299/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Randomized placebo-controlled crossover trial in 17 adults with mild TBI. After real tPBM, participants showed better visual working memory, improved verbal-learning performance, better subjective sleep quality, fewer post-concussion symptoms, less pain, and lower PTSD symptoms. Sham did not produce the same change pattern. The sample is very small, but this is the newest direct cognition-positive human RCT in the tPBM cluster.
