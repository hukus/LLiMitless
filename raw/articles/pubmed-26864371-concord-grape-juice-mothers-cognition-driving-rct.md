# Concord grape juice, cognition, and driving in working mothers

- PMID: 26864371
- DOI: 10.3945/ajcn.115.114553
- Source type: randomized placebo-controlled crossover trial.
- Publication: American Journal of Clinical Nutrition, 2016.
- Date checked: 2026-05-03.

This 12-week randomized crossover trial tested daily Concord grape juice in 25 healthy working mothers aged 40-50 years with demanding lifestyles. Participants consumed 12 oz / 355 ml/day Concord grape juice containing 777 mg total polyphenols or an energy-, taste-, and appearance-matched placebo, with a 4-week washout. A subsample of 17 completed a driving-simulator task.

The abstract reports significant improvements in immediate spatial memory and driving performance after Concord grape juice relative to placebo. It also reports evidence of an enduring effect: participants who received grape juice in the first arm maintained better performance into the placebo arm. That carryover is interesting but complicates interpretation of the crossover design and washout length.

Use in the wiki: adult grape-juice cognition and everyday-task anchor. Treat as low-confidence, product-specific, and potentially carryover-confounded.
