# Positron Emission Tomography Assessments of Phosphodiesterase 10A in Patients With Schizophrenia

- PMID: 36458958
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36458958/
- Ingested: 2026-04-29

## Summary

2023 PET/MRS study of PDE10A in schizophrenia. The paper found altered striatal PDE10A availability and a relationship between sensorimotor-striatal PDE10A and Tower of London performance in controls, supporting biology around the target but not a clinical efficacy claim.

## Key points

- Sample: 27 schizophrenia patients and 27 healthy controls.
- Method: [18F]MNI-659 PET plus regional glutamate measures.
- Result: diagnosis affected PDE10A availability; control sensorimotor-striatal PDE10A correlated negatively with Tower of London scores.
- Relevance: biomarker bridge only, not proof that PDE10 inhibition improves cognition in patients.
