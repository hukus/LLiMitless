# Impaired hippocampal plasticity associated with loss of recycling endosomal SLC9A6/NHE6 is ameliorated by the TrkB agonist 7,8-dihydroxyflavone

- PMID: 39341363
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39341363/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Notes

2025 mouse and hippocampal-neuron study linking NHE6 loss to impaired spine density, AMPAR expression, and LTP. 7,8-DHF restored structural and functional plasticity measures in the knockout system, which makes the compound relevant to Christianson-syndrome-like and broader plasticity-rescue hypotheses.

## Relevance

- Updates the branch with a newer mechanistic phenotype.
- Still preclinical; no human cognition endpoint.
- Helps explain why the compound continues to attract disease-rescue interest.
