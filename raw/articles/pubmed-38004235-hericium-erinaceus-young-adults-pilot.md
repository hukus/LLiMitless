# The Acute and Chronic Effects of Lion's Mane Mushroom Supplementation on Cognitive Function, Stress and Mood in Young Adults

- PMID: 38004235
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38004235/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

Randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled parallel-groups pilot in 41 healthy adults aged 18-45. The study used 1.8 g Hericium erinaceus and reported faster Stroop performance 60 minutes after a single dose, with only a trend toward lower stress after 28 days and several null outcomes.
