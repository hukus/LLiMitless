# [Systematic review: Effects of cholinergic signaling on cognition in human pharmacological studies]

- PMID: 41076097
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41076097/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

Broad 2025 systematic review of human pharmacological cholinergic studies. The abstract concludes that acetylcholine is involved in sustained attention, perceptual detection, cue-response updating, and information-processing speed, with different effects for muscarinic and nicotinic modulators. The authors also caution that pharmacologic methods are limited by nonspecificity and an inability to reproduce signaling dynamics cleanly.

## Notes

- This is a synthesis source, not a primary RCT.
- It strengthens the branch by clarifying which cognitive domains cholinergic modulation most plausibly affects.
