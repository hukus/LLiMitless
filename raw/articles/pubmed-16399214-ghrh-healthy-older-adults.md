# Growth hormone releasing hormone improves the cognition of healthy older adults

- PubMed PMID 16399214, Neurobiol Aging 2006.
- Randomized placebo-controlled 6-month trial in 89 healthy older adults.
- Daily GHRH improved performance IQ, picture arrangement, finding A's, verbal sets, and single-dual task.
- The abstract reports the benefit as independent of gender, estrogen status, and baseline cognition.
- This is the strongest early positive cognition signal in the GH-axis branch, but the sample is modest and the result is old.
