# PMC 9389226: Emoxypine and succinate derivative review

This 2022 open-access review covers emoxypine and its succinate derivative, emphasizing a shared hydroxypyridine structure and proposed antihypoxic, neuroprotective, cardioprotective, antioxidant, and membrane-modulating properties.

The review describes emoxypine succinate / Mexidol as a Russian-origin compound with neurological, vascular, ophthalmic, cardiovascular, and renal research lines. It frames the molecule as crossing the blood-brain barrier because of small size and low molecular weight, and summarizes preclinical and regional clinical studies, including chronic cerebral ischemia, ischemic stroke, panic disorder, glaucoma, and autonomic dysfunction.

The review also states that the compound is used most widely in Russia and that U.S. or European approval was not obtained for usage. It calls for more studies under regulatory guidelines and more work on pharmacokinetics, pharmacodynamics, interactions, adverse effects, and formulation/delivery.

Interpretation for the wiki: this is a mechanism and literature-map source, not itself a decisive cognition efficacy source. It supports plausibility and geography/regulatory caution while the MEMO/MIR trial records carry the stronger clinical-state evidence.
