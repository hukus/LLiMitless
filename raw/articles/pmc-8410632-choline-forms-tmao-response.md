# Dietary choline supplements, but not eggs, raise fasting TMAO levels in participants with normal renal function

- PMCID: PMC8410632
- PMID: 33872583
- DOI: 10.1016/j.amjmed.2021.03.016
- Journal: American Journal of Medicine
- Year: 2021
- Ingested: 2026-05-04

## Summary

Randomized clinical trial in healthy volunteers with normal renal function. Participants were assigned for 4 weeks to hardboiled eggs, choline bitartrate tablets, eggs plus choline bitartrate, egg whites plus choline bitartrate, or phosphatidylcholine capsules. All choline-bitartrate-containing arms significantly increased plasma TMAO and platelet reactivity. Four whole eggs daily and phosphatidylcholine capsules providing comparable total choline did not significantly increase plasma TMAO or platelet reactivity. Plasma choline increased across arms, supporting compliance.

## Notes

- Use as a safety and product-form caveat for free choline salts.
- This is not a cognition trial, but it matters for risk-benefit interpretation.
- Do not collapse egg yolk, phosphatidylcholine capsules, and choline bitartrate tablets into one safety profile.
