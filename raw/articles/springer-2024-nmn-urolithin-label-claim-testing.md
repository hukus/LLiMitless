# Testing NMN and Urolithin A Supplements Against Label Claims

- PMID: 38935229
- DOI: 10.1007/s11357-024-01257-2
- Published: 2024-06-27
- Checked: 2026-05-03

## Summary

2024 GeroScience quality-control study testing nicotinamide mononucleotide (NMN) and urolithin A supplements against label claims. Eighteen NMN supplements and five urolithin A supplements were purchased online, in pharmacies, or from manufacturers, blinded, and tested by HPLC-triple-quadrupole MS.

The active-ingredient amounts deviated substantially from labels. Across NMN and urolithin A products, deviations ranged from +28.6% to -100%. For NMN specifically, three products had non-detectable NMN, and the difference from adjusted label claim ranged from -100% to +11.2%. The authors also referenced a previous ChromaDex report where many tested NMN products contained less than 1% of the claimed amount.

Use in the wiki: product-quality and transportability warning for [[nicotinamide-mononucleotide]] and urolithin A. Even if an NMN trial is biologically active, a consumer product labeled "NMN" may not contain the claimed dose unless independent COA/assay evidence is available.
