# Rapamycin treatment for Alzheimer’s disease and related dementias: a pilot phase 1 clinical trial

- DOI: 10.1038/s43856-025-00904-9
- Published: 2025-05-20
- Trial: NCT04200911
- Ingested: 2026-04-29

## Summary

Open-label phase 1 clinical trial in 10 participants aged 55-85 with MCI or early-stage dementia. Rapamycin 1 mg/day for 8 weeks.

Rapamycin was not detectable in CSF before or after treatment. There were 20 mostly mild adverse events; systolic blood pressure and HbA1c increased, erythrocyte indices fell, and no significant cognitive changes were observed.

CSF p-tau181, GFAP, and NfL increased, as did plasma IFN-gamma, IL-5, VEGF-D, sFlt-1, and PlGF. The pattern is biomarker-active but not cognitively convincing, and the open-label design prevents causal attribution.
