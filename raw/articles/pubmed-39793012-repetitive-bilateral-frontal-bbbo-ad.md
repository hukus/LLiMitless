# Repetitive and extensive focused ultrasound-mediated bilateral frontal blood-brain barrier opening for Alzheimer's disease

- PMID: 39793012
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39793012/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

This 2025 J Neurosurg report describes repetitive, extensive bilateral frontal BBB opening in AD and frames the procedure as safe and potentially beneficial for amyloid-beta plaque removal. It adds to the human feasibility signal, but the cognition story remains indirect and early.
