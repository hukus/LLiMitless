# LongeCity 2013-2017 P21 human-use discussion

- Source type: two-page LongeCity Brain Health forum thread.
- Thread opened: 2013-08-09; direct-use posts visible from 2014 through 2017.
- Page 2: https://www.longecity.org/forum/topic/65066-cntf-based-peptide-p21/page-2
- Checked: 2026-07-21.

## Visible firsthand signal

The 32-reply thread contains multiple independent, mixed direct-use accounts of products sold as P21:

- A 2014 commenter described a subtle increase in psychological presence and a vivid dream after several days, while also using Noopept and Selank.
- Another early user said cognitive change was unclear but stress and sleep seemed better during the first week.
- One person stopped an injection almost immediately because it felt intensely burning; later discussion could not distinguish compound, batch, diluent, route, technique, or individual reaction.
- A subcutaneous user reported no real effect across a stated 500-microgram to 2-mg daily range.
- A separate quoted report described eight weeks of nasal use with no clear long-term effect.
- A user who finished a nasal-spray bottle described intermittent clarity, calm, and focus, but also variable stimulation, fatigue, and sleepiness in the context of sleep apnea and idiopathic narcolepsy.
- A repeat user described mild short-term acuity and possible learning/retention support, while tying the response to training, diet, and sleep and reporting that calm awareness could become sleepiness.

All amounts and routes above are descriptive forum evidence only, not protocol guidance.

## Interpretation boundaries

Signal size / strength: at least six distinct firsthand accounts, mixed in direction, across one long-running forum thread; `moderate` as salience and `weak` as efficacy or safety evidence.

The reports span different years, batches, vendors, routes, amounts, health states, and stacks. There is no denominator, chemical verification, blinding, standardized endpoint, medical adjudication, or reliable follow-up. Some mechanistic explanations in replies are speculative. The thread supports the existence of human self-experimentation and recurring clarity/calm/sleep/fatigue themes, not clinical benefit or safety.
