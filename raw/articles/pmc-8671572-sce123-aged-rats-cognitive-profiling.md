# S-CE-123 cognitive profiling and proteomics in experienced aged rats

- Scientific Reports 2021 paper on cognitive profiling and proteomic analysis of S-CE-123, a modafinil analogue, in 27 male Lister Hooded rats aged 26 months.
- The study used a randomized blinded experiment in experienced aged rats that had learned several cognitive tasks.
- CE-123 markedly enhanced motivation, which improved performance in a new-to-learn operant discrimination task and a social-cognition cooperation assay, and mildly increased impulsivity.
- CE-123 did not affect attention, spatial learning, or motor learning in the captured abstract.
- Proteomic analysis of the prefrontal-cortex synaptosomal fraction found 182 protein groups differing between treatment groups, with clusters involving synaptic vesicle recycling, synapse organization, and antioxidant activity.
- Interpretation for the wiki: CE-123 has a serious preclinical cognition/motivation profile, but it is animal evidence, not human cognitive-enhancement evidence.
