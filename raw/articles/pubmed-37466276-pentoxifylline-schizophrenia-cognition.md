# Pentoxifylline as adjunctive therapy in cognitive deficits and symptoms of schizophrenia: A randomized double-blind placebo-controlled clinical trial

- PMID: 37466276
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37466276/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

2023 randomized placebo-controlled schizophrenia trial. Pentoxifylline improved a subset of cognitive and symptom measures and increased cAMP while lowering TNF-alpha and IL-6, but the effect pattern was partial rather than domain-wide and the adjacent pentoxifylline schizophrenia follow-on paper later carried a retraction notice in PubMed.

## Key points

- Design: 52 chronic schizophrenia patients, 8 weeks, pentoxifylline 400 mg twice daily.
- Outcome: WCST, digit span, Stroop, Rey Auditory and Verbal Learning Test, PANSS.
- Signal: some positive findings on WCST and Stroop, but many endpoints were null.
- Caveat: interpret cautiously because the nearby pentoxifylline literature has integrity problems.
