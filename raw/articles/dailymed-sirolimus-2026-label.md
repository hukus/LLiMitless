# Sirolimus Tablets Prescribing Information

- Initial U.S. approval: 1999
- Ingested: 2026-04-30

## Summary

Official label for oral sirolimus. The boxed warning is immunosuppression with increased infection and malignancy risk in transplant settings.

Other major warnings include hyperlipidemia, decline in renal function, proteinuria, impaired wound healing / fluid accumulation, latent viral infections, interstitial lung disease / non-infectious pneumonitis, embryo-fetal toxicity, and possible male infertility.
