# L-Lactate Administration Improved Synaptic Plasticity and Cognition in Early 3xTg-AD Mice

- PMID: 40003952
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40003952/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Early-stage AD mouse study in which systemic L-lactate restored hippocampal lactate levels, improved spatial learning and memory, and enhanced LTP and synaptic structure. The result is preclinical, but it strengthens the case that lactate can act as more than a passive byproduct in cognition-relevant circuits.
