# Aniracetam tested in chronic psychosyndrome after long-term exposure to organic solvents

- PMID: 2188276
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/2188276/
- Ingested: 2026-04-30

## Summary

1990 randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled crossover study in 44 subjects with chronic psychosyndrome after long-term exposure to organic solvents. Aniracetam 1 g/day was given for 3 months and compared with 3 months of placebo. The doctors' and subjects' overall-condition ratings did not show a trial effect, no significant neuropsychological change emerged overall, one of 19 tests favored aniracetam, and another favored placebo. The best read is negative or at most mixed.
