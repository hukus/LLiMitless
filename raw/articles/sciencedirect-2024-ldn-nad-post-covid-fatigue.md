# Low-dose naltrexone plus NAD+ for persistent fatigue after COVID-19

2024 Brain, Behavior, & Immunity - Health pilot study of low-dose naltrexone 4.5 mg/day plus NAD+ patches in 36 patients with persistent moderate to severe fatigue after COVID-19.

The abstract reports that treatment was well tolerated and improved SF-36 quality-of-life and fatigue scores, with a subset of clinical responders. Because LDN was combined with NAD+ and the study was a pilot, it does not isolate the effect of LDN on brain fog or cognition.

Relevance: combination pilot source for [[low-dose-naltrexone]], kept separate from LDN monotherapy.
