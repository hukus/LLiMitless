# Effects of Tesamorelin on Neurocognitive Impairment in Persons With HIV and Abdominal Obesity

- PubMed PMID 39813152, J Infect Dis 2025.
- Phase 2 randomized open-label trial in 73 virally suppressed people with HIV and abdominal obesity.
- Tesamorelin 2 mg daily reduced waist circumference and increased IGF-1.
- Neurocognitive performance trended better within the tesamorelin arm, but the between-group difference was not significant.
- The abstract's own conclusion is cautious: no clear short-term benefit on NCI, with the limitation of no placebo arm.
