# Yohimbine effects on cerebral blood flow and physiological functions

PubMed-indexed PET study in nine healthy humans comparing intravenous yohimbine with placebo. The study used yohimbine as an alpha-2 adrenergic antagonist to probe central adrenergic activation, cerebral blood flow, symptoms, and physiology.

For the wiki, yohimbine is a stress/adrenergic probe rather than a clean nootropic. Any cognitive benefit claim needs to be weighed against anxiety, blood-pressure, and sympathetic activation.

Relevance: formal mechanism/safety anchor for yohimbine.
