# Taurine Supplementation and Cognitive Function Meta-Analysis

- Journal: International Journal of Food Sciences and Nutrition.
- Published online: 2025-05-04.
- DOI: 10.1080/09637486.2025.2499044.
- PMID surfaced by secondary index: 40320621.
- Ingested: 2026-05-03.

## Summary

Systematic review and meta-analysis of randomized controlled trials evaluating taurine and cognition in cognitively impaired and non-impaired populations. The review searched through January 2025 and included 7 RCTs / 9 intervention trials with 402 individuals.

Overall, taurine alone or taurine combined with exercise did not show significant cognitive-score benefits. Taurine plus therapeutic drugs did not outperform therapeutic drugs alone overall, although one subgroup analysis found a Mini-Mental State Examination improvement. The authors concluded that the evidence was insufficient to support taurine supplementation for enhancing cognitive function.
