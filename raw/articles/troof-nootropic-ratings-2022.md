# What I learned gathering thousands of nootropic ratings

- Source URL: https://troof.blog/posts/nootropics/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

Troof analyzed 36,163 nootropic ratings from 2,802 people, combining a recommender-system dataset with the 2016 SlateStarCodex survey foundation. The post argues that unblinded self-report is noisy but still useful for search-space reduction. Key takeaways for the mismatch map: phenylpiracetam stands out among racetams with a higher community rating and an estimated 10-20% tolerance probability; tianeptine was rated unusually high among antidepressant-like substances; and phenibut carried a material tolerance/addiction signal, with the author estimating about 5.5% addiction and 20% tolerance probability, while cautioning that the numbers depend heavily on use frequency and model assumptions.
