# Acute and chronic Cognitaven green oat extract during a laboratory stressor

- Source type: randomized double-blind placebo-controlled parallel-group dose-ranging study.
- Publication: Nutrients, 2020.
- Date checked: 2026-05-03.

This trial randomized 132 healthy adults aged 35-65 to 430 mg, 860 mg, or 1290 mg Cognitaven green oat extract, or placebo, for 29 days. Cognitive function, mood, and psychological-state changes during an observed multitasking stressor were assessed at baseline and 2 and 4 hours post-dose on Day 1 and Day 29.

The study reported that a single 1290 mg dose, and more strongly four weeks of 430 mg and 1290 mg supplementation, improved a computerized Corsi Blocks working-memory task and a multitasking task involving verbal serial subtractions and computerized tracking. After four weeks, the 1290 mg dose also reduced electrodermal response to the stressor. The study did not find treatment-related effects on mood.

Important product note: Cognitaven is not the same product as Neuravena. The finished product used 430 mg capsules, each corresponding to 300 mg native Avena sativa extract, with daily finished-product doses of 430, 860, or 1290 mg.
