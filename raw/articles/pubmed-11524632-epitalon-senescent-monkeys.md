# Synthetic tetrapeptide epitalon restores disturbed neuroendocrine regulation in senescent monkeys

- PMID: 11524632
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11524632/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

Female senescent macaques given Epitalon showed restored melatonin and cortisol secretion patterns in the abstracted results. This is a primate circadian/endocrine study, not a cognition trial.
