# GV-971 long-term postmarketing safety registry

- ClinicalTrials.gov: NCT05058040
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

ClinicalTrials.gov record for a phase 4 long-term safety study of sodium oligomannate capsules in patients receiving GV-971 in clinical practice in China. The record lists actual enrollment of 2500 participants, active-not-recruiting status, and no posted results as of this sweep.

The registry states that sodium oligomannate capsules received China's NMPA approval on 2019-11-02 for mild-to-moderate Alzheimer's disease and improving cognitive function, with a recommended dose of 450 mg twice daily by mouth. The study is intended to monitor adverse reactions, adverse events, serious adverse events, and special populations over 48 and 96 weeks.
