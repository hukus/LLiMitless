# Sustained cerebrovascular and cognitive benefits of resveratrol in postmenopausal women

- Source type: open-access randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial interim analysis.
- Publication: Nutrients, 2020.
- PMID: 32244933.
- PMCID: PMC7146200.
- Date checked: 2026-05-03.

This RESHAW interim analysis randomized postmenopausal women aged 45-85 to 75 mg trans-resveratrol twice daily or placebo for 12 months. Outcomes included a neuropsychological cognition battery, cerebral blood flow, cerebrovascular responsiveness, blood pressure, diabetes markers, and fasting lipids.

Compared with placebo, resveratrol improved overall cognitive performance and attenuated decline in cerebrovascular responsiveness to cognitive stimuli. The cerebrovascular-response result was associated with fasting-blood-glucose reduction.
