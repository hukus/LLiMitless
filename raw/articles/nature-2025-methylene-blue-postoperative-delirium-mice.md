# Methylene blue reduces postoperative delirium in mice through neuroinflammation suppression and BBB repair

- DOI: 10.1038/s41598-025-26108-8
- Journal: Scientific Reports, 2025
- Source: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-025-26108-8
- Ingested: 2026-05-04

## Summary

Open-access mouse postoperative-delirium model paper. Adult mice underwent laparotomy anesthesia/surgery, with behavioral tests at baseline, 6 hours, and 24 hours after surgery. Methylene blue at 5 mg/kg attenuated neuroinflammation and microglial activation in hippocampus and prefrontal cortex, improved blood-brain-barrier integrity through tight-junction/transcytosis markers and EMMPRIN/MMP9 downregulation, and ameliorated delirium-like behavior, especially at the 6-hour postoperative window.

## Signal Use

- Medicinal / preclinical signal: postoperative-delirium and BBB/neuroinflammation recovery-state model.
- Cognition signal: animal delirium-like behavior only; not a human prevention trial and not healthy enhancement.
- Caveat: young male mouse model; authors note need for broader sex, age, and dosing work.
