# Brasofensine treatment for Parkinson's disease in combination with levodopa/carbidopa

- PMID: 11847938
- DOI: 10.1345/aph.1A152
- Journal: Annals of Pharmacotherapy (2002)

## Captured takeaways

- Clinical crossover study in 8 men with moderate Parkinson disease receiving levodopa/carbidopa.
- Brasofensine / BMS-204756 was described as a dopamine transporter antagonist.
- Participants received single oral doses of 0.5, 1, 2, and 4 mg coadministered with usual levodopa/carbidopa.
- Exposure increased more than proportionally with dose.
- Motor performance on the UPDRS motor subscale did not change at any dose.
- Brasofensine was reported safe and well tolerated in this small cohort up to 4 mg; mild adverse events included headache, insomnia, phlebitis, dizziness, ecchymosis, and vomiting.
- Interpretation for the wiki: adjacent phenyltropane / dopamine-reuptake development history exists, but this is not a cognition-positive human signal.
