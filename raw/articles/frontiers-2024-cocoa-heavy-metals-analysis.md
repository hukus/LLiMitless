# Multi-Year Heavy Metal Analysis of Dark Chocolate and Cocoa Products in the USA

- Source: Frontiers in Nutrition 2024;11:1366231
- DOI: 10.3389/fnut.2024.1366231
- URL: https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/nutrition/articles/10.3389/fnut.2024.1366231/full

## Summary

Multi-year analysis of 72 consumer dark chocolate and cocoa-containing products purchased in the United States and analyzed for lead, cadmium, and arsenic across 2014, 2016, 2019, and 2022 cohorts. The authors used California Proposition 65 maximum allowable daily levels as a conservative threshold.

The analysis found that 43% of products exceeded the lead threshold and 35% exceeded the cadmium threshold, while none exceeded the arsenic threshold. The authors also noted that median lead and cadmium concentrations were below the conservative Prop 65 thresholds and that most products fell below FDA interim reference levels for lead, implying a product-outlier and cumulative-exposure problem rather than a uniform single-serving hazard.

Use in wiki: cocoa product-quality and safety caution. Heavy metal burden, source region, processing, serving size, and third-party testing matter before cocoa powder, dark chocolate, or cocoa extract is treated as a routine cognition supplement.
