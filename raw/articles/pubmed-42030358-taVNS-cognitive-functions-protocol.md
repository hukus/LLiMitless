# Sorting the mind: A systematic review and meta-analysis protocol of transcutaneous auricular vagus nerve stimulation on cognitive functions

- PMID: 42030358
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42030358/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

2026 meta-analysis protocol for taVNS and cognition. The authors plan a PRISMA-P review of randomized controlled trials across clinical and non-clinical populations, with pooled Hedges g estimates, heterogeneity testing, and moderator/meta-regression analyses. The existence of the protocol is a reminder that the evidence base is still fragmented rather than conclusively pooled.

## Notes

- Databases listed: PubMed, EMBASE, PsycINFO, Web of Science, CENTRAL, and Scopus.
- Primary outcomes are validated cognitive measures after taVNS versus sham or active controls.
- No pooled results yet; this is a planning paper, not an effect-size source.
