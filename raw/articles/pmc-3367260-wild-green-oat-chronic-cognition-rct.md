# Chronic effects of a wild green oat extract supplementation on cognitive performance in older adults

- Source type: open-access randomized double-blind placebo-controlled crossover trial.
- Publication: Nutrients, 2012.
- Date checked: 2026-05-03.

This 12-week crossover trial tested wild green oat extract supplementation at 1500 mg/day versus placebo in 37 healthy older adults. Cognitive testing was performed after chronic supplementation rather than immediately after dosing.

The authors reported no effect on measured cognition, concluding that any acute cognitive benefit of wild green oat extract did not persist with chronic treatment in older adults with normal cognition.

Use in the wiki: null chronic comparator for green-oat product claims.
