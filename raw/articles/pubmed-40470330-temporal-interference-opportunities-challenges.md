# Temporal interference stimulation for human brain: Opportunities and challenges

- PMID: 40470330
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40470330/
- Published: 2025-01-15
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary
Narrative review of TI as a human neurostimulation platform.
The paper frames TI as a deep-target method that may reach hippocampal and striatal circuits, with current human work spanning memory, spatial navigation, motor learning, and Parkinsonian symptom control.
It emphasizes that the main bottleneck is still translation from target engagement to robust behavioral benefit.
