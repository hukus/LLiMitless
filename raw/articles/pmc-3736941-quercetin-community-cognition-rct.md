# Quercetin supplementation and cognition in a community sample

- Source type: open-access randomized placebo-controlled trial.
- Publication: Therapeutic Advances in Psychopharmacology, 2012.
- PMID: 23983966.
- PMCID: PMC3736941.
- DOI: 10.1177/2045125312445894.
- Date checked: 2026-05-03.

This large community trial randomized 941 completers to 500 mg/day quercetin, 1000 mg/day quercetin, or placebo for 12 weeks. The CNS Vital Signs battery assessed memory, psychomotor speed, reaction time, complex attention, and cognitive flexibility.

Main result: quercetin did not significantly improve memory, psychomotor speed, reaction time, attention, or cognitive flexibility, despite increased plasma quercetin in the treatment groups.

Use in the wiki: major negative human cognition anchor for quercetin supplementation. It prevents upgrading quercetin based on mechanistic, dietary, or senolytic enthusiasm.
