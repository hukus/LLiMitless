# SAHRANG: Subarachnoid Hemorrhage Recovery and Galantamine: A Pilot Multicenter Randomized Placebo-Controlled Trial

- PMID: 40877496
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40877496/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

Pilot multicenter double-masked randomized placebo-controlled study in aneurysmal subarachnoid hemorrhage. Galantamine 8 mg or 12 mg every 12 h was started within 36 h after aneurysm securing and continued for 90 days. The readout is mainly tolerability and safety: no difference versus placebo on discontinuation or mortality, with bradycardia the most common adverse event.
