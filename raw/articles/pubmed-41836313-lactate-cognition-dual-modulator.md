# Lactate and cognition: a dual modulator

- PMID: 41836313
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41836313/
- Ingested: 2026-04-30

## Summary

Review article describing lactate as a context-sensitive, double-edged regulator of cognition. It argues that moderate elevations can support resilience and enhancement, while excessive accumulation or impaired transport may contribute to dysfunction, making lactate a biomarker and therapeutic target only in phenotype-specific contexts.
