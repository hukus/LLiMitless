# The validity of psychomotor vigilance tasks of less than 10-minute duration

- PMID: 15354700
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15354700/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

2004 validation paper testing shorter PVT variants against the standard 10-minute task during sustained wakefulness. Shorter versions captured the same sleep-loss degradation pattern, supporting use of shorter PVTs when repeated field testing has to fit into a practical battery.

## Notes

This source is the main justification for using a brief PVT as the vigilance anchor in a daily measurement stack, while still preserving sensitivity to sleep pressure.
