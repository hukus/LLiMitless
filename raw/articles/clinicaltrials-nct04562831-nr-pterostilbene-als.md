# NCT04562831 nicotinamide riboside pterostilbene ALS trial

- Source type: ClinicalTrials.gov registry record.
- NCT ID: NCT04562831
- Last update posted: 2026-01-12.
- Status at capture: active, not recruiting; no results posted in the captured registry view.
- Date checked: 2026-05-04.

Registry record for the NO-ALS study testing EH301, a nicotinamide riboside plus pterostilbene dietary supplement, in ALS. The study arms include high-dose EH301 with 1500 mg nicotinamide riboside / 300 mg pterostilbene, single-dose EH301 with 1000 mg nicotinamide riboside / 200 mg pterostilbene, and placebo. The trial is disease-modification oriented in ALS and should be treated as a combination-product program, not isolated pterostilbene cognition evidence.

Use in the wiki: current translational-status anchor. It shows that pterostilbene appears in active clinical combination programs, but no cognition result and no isolated pterostilbene efficacy can be inferred.
