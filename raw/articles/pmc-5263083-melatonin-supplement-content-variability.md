# Melatonin natural health products and supplements: presence of serotonin and significant variability of melatonin content

- PMID: 27855744
- PMCID: PMC5263083
- DOI: 10.5664/jcsm.6462
- Published: 2017-02-15
- Source type: analytical chemistry study of commercial supplements
- Ingested: 2026-05-03

## Summary

Analysis of 31 commercial melatonin supplements found large product-content variability. Measured melatonin ranged from 83 percent below to 478 percent above label claim, lot-to-lot variability reached 465 percent in one product, more than 71 percent did not meet label claim within a 10 percent margin, and 26 percent contained serotonin.

## Notes

- Product quality is a core interpretation gate for melatonin anecdotes and self-experiments.
- Dose-response claims based on label dose can be wrong if products are not independently tested.
- This is especially relevant for next-day grogginess and adverse reports, where actual dose and contaminants may be unknown.
