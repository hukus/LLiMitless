# REGEN-BRAIN allopregnanolone phase 2
- ClinicalTrials.gov ID: NCT04838301; recruiting as of 2026-03-13.
- Phase 2, double-blind, randomized, placebo-controlled trial in mild AD dementia.
- Eligibility includes probable AD, MMSE 20-26, p-Tau217 positive, age 55-80.
- Intervention: 4 mg allopregnanolone IV over 30 minutes once per week for 6 months, then placebo crossover/open-label follow-up.
- Primary endpoint: hippocampal volume; secondary cognition endpoints include CANTAB PAL, CANTAB composite, and ADAS-Cog 11.
- Branch readout: the first direct efficacy test for allopregnanolone in AD remains in progress.
