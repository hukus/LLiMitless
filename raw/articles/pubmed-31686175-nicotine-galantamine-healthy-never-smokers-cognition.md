# Evidence for positive allosteric modulation of cognitive-enhancing effects of nicotine in healthy human subjects

- PMID: 31686175
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31686175/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Randomized, counterbalanced study in 26 adult never-smokers. Nicotine 7 mg/24 h improved reaction time and signal detection in visuospatial attention and RVIP tasks; galantamine 4 mg alone did not modulate those effects, but the nicotine + galantamine combination synergistically reduced reaction time on one change-detection task.
