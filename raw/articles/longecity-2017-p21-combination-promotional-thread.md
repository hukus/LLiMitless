# LongeCity 2017 P21 combination and promotional-context thread

- Source type: LongeCity Retailer/Product Discussion forum thread.
- Original post date: 2017-02-02.
- Checked: 2026-07-21.

## Visible firsthand signal

The original poster attributed rapid, large improvements in mood, wakefulness, focus, sociability, anxiety, fatigue, and anhedonia to one week of combined P21 and orexin-A use. A second user said a P21 plus NA-Semax-amidate combination had greatly helped short- and long-term memory compared with other experimental compounds.

The thread also contains explicit community criticism of commercial framing. A LongeCity moderator later described the original poster's contributions as spam-like and said promotional or advertising content belonged in the retailer/product forum.

## Interpretation boundaries

Signal size / strength: two dramatic combination reports; `weak` after promotional and stack down-weighting.

Neither account isolates P21. Co-administered orexin-A or NA-Semax-amidate, strong expectancy, retailer enthusiasm, lack of objective endpoints, short follow-up, unknown product identity, and explicit moderator concern make the thread unsuitable for efficacy inference. It is retained to prevent dramatic promotional anecdotes from being silently counted as clean P21 reports.
