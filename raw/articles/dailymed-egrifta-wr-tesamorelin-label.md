# EGRIFTA WR tesamorelin label

- DailyMed label updated 2025-03-31; EGRIFTA SV consumer page and WR professional label both describe tesamorelin as a GHRH analog.
- Indication is reduction of excess abdominal fat in HIV-infected adult patients with lipodystrophy.
- Label warnings include increased risk of neoplasms, elevated IGF-1, fluid retention, glucose intolerance/diabetes mellitus, hypersensitivity reactions, and injection-site reactions.
- Common adverse reactions include arthralgia, peripheral edema, myalgia, hyperglycemia, palpitations, and carpal tunnel syndrome.
- The label explicitly says to evaluate glucose before and during therapy.
