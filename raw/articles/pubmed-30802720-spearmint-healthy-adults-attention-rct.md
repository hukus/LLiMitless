# Spearmint extract attention trial in healthy active adults

- Source type: randomized double-blind placebo-controlled parallel trial.
- Publication: Nutrition Research, 2019.
- Date checked: 2026-05-11.

This trial tested 900 mg/day proprietary spearmint extract in healthy recreationally active adults aged 18-50 years. The supplement was standardized around high-polyphenol / high-rosmarinic-acid spearmint lines rather than ordinary mint tea.

The study reported significant treatment effects for sustained attention, with spearmint outperforming placebo at day 30 and day 90. A treatment-by-visit interaction was also reported for complex attention, with an advantage at day 7. Two individual CNS Vital Signs tasks, shifting attention and the 4-part continuous performance test, contributed to the signal. Mood, sleep, and quality-of-life outcomes did not clearly differ.

Use in the wiki: this is the cleanest healthy-adult support for spearmint extract as an attention-focused product. It should remain low-confidence because the study used one proprietary product and the signal is domain-specific rather than broad cognitive enhancement.
