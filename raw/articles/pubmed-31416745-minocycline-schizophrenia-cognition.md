# Minocycline schizophrenia cognition and cytokine trial

2019 Schizophrenia Research trial titled "The effect of minocycline on amelioration of cognitive deficits and pro-inflammatory cytokines levels in patients with schizophrenia." The trial tested minocycline as an adjunct in schizophrenia, measuring cognitive deficits and inflammatory cytokines.

The abstract-level signal is positive enough to keep minocycline on the neuroimmune-cognition map, but the schizophrenia literature is mixed. Earlier small trials and meta-analyses suggested symptom and executive-function benefits, while later larger trials reported no improvement in symptoms or BACS cognition.

Interpretation for the wiki: schizophrenia results are phenotype-bound and contested. They should not be generalized to healthy nootropic use or long-COVID brain fog.
