# Kisspeptin anxiety RCT
- PMID: 40036336.
- 2025 randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled crossover study in 95 healthy participants.
- Intervention: IV kisspeptin-54 1 nmol/kg/h for 75 minutes vs rate-matched placebo.
- Main result: biological activity confirmed by LH rise; state anxiety, cortisol, blood pressure, and heart rate were not significantly changed.
- Branch readout: kisspeptin is biologically active but does not support an anxiolytic claim in humans, and it still does not establish cognition benefit.
