# Effects of Saffron on Cognition, Anxiety, and Depression in Patients Undergoing Coronary Artery Bypass Grafting: A Randomized Double-Blind Placebo-Controlled Trial

- PMID: 29185780
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29185780/

## Summary

Randomized trial in CABG patients using saffron capsules 15 mg twice daily for 12 weeks. Cognition, anxiety, and depression were assessed, making this a phenotype-specific traditional-medicine signal rather than a healthy-adult enhancer claim.
