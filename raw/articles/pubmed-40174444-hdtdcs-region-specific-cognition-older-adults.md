# Region-specific cognitive effects of HD-tDCS in older adults: M1, DLPFC, and cerebellum

- PMID: 40174444
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40174444/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

2025 randomized, double-blind HD-tDCS trial in older adults. M1 and DLPFC improved cognitive flexibility and processing speed, while cerebellar stimulation selectively improved working memory.

## Notes

Useful for the tDCS branch because it separates target effects instead of treating frontal stimulation as a single bucket. It also gives the comparison page a stronger region-specific example.
