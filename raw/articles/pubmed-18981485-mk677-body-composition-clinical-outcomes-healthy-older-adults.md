# Effects of an Oral Ghrelin Mimetic on Body Composition and Clinical Outcomes in Healthy Older Adults: A Randomized Trial

- PubMed PMID 18981485, Ann Intern Med 2008.
- Two-year, double-blind, randomized, placebo-controlled modified-crossover trial in 65 healthy adults ages 60-81.
- Participants received oral MK-677 25 mg once daily or placebo.
- Primary endpoints were fat-free mass and abdominal visceral fat; secondary endpoints included strength, function, lipids, and quality of life.
- MK-677 increased GH and IGF-I to youthful levels and increased fat-free mass, but did not improve strength or function.
- Weight increased, insulin sensitivity declined, appetite rose transiently, and mild edema/muscle pain were reported.
- This is the main older-adult body-composition anchor and a caution against treating lean-mass gain as functional gain.
