# Do small molecules activate the TrkB receptor in the same manner as BDNF?

- DOI: 10.3390/ph14080704
- Publication: Pharmaceuticals, 2021
- Article: Do Small Molecules Activate the TrkB Receptor in the Same Manner as BDNF? Limitations of Published TrkB Low Molecular Agonists and Screening for Novel TrkB Orthosteric Agonists
- Evidence tier: mechanistic / assay-specificity counterpoint

## Summary

This paper is a direct counterpoint to optimistic small-molecule TrkB agonist claims. The authors developed assays for direct interaction, dimerization, downstream signaling, cytoprotection, physicochemical properties, and ADME profiling to evaluate published reference compounds and screen for new TrkB agonists. They report that 7,8-DHF bound TrkB weakly in their system but did not produce the expected TrkB receptor activity in tested cell lines. Mouse pharmacokinetic / pharmacodynamic work also did not support 7,8-DHF as a strong in vivo TrkB-activation tool, and a broad target panel suggested limited selectivity.

For the wiki, this does not erase the animal literature, but it sharply lowers confidence in any simple statement that 7,8-DHF, LM22A-4, or deoxygedunin are clean BDNF substitutes. It supports a target-specificity gate before promoting any plasticity molecule.
