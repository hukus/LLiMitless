# Effects of Sleep Deprivation and Adrenergic Inhibition on Glymphatic Flow in Humans

- ClinicalTrials.gov: NCT03576664
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Completed randomized crossover study in 22 healthy volunteers. The protocol used carvedilol vs placebo around sleep deprivation/napping in the MRI and explicitly measured cognitive performance before and after the scan, including PVT, visual attention, paired-associative memory, working memory, emotional recognition, and mood. The registry has no posted results, so it remains protocol-level evidence.
