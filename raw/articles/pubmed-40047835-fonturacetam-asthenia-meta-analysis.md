# Efficacy and safety of fonturacetam in asthenia

- PMID: 40047835
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40047835/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

2025 Russian meta-analysis of 11 studies covering 549 patients with asthenia. Fonturacetam was assessed at 200 mg/day, usually over about 30 days. The pooled result reported a 16.3-point reduction in MFI-20 (95% CI 8.85-23.85; p<0.0001), with additional reported improvements in emotional background, sleep, cognitive functions, and quality of life. Side effects averaged 5.5% and were described as transient. The evidence is phenotype-bound to asthenia/fatigue rather than healthy-adult enhancement.
