# Two-Arm Crossover Randomized Controlled Trial Versus Meta-Analysis of N-of-1 Studies: Comparison of Statistical Efficiency in Determining an Intervention Effect

- PMID: 40071868
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40071868/
- Ingested: 2026-04-30

## Summary

2025 simulation-based comparison of a traditional two-arm randomized crossover RCT versus an aggregated meta-analysis of N-of-1 studies. In the reported digital-health cardiovascular setting, the aggregating N-of-1 procedure reached 80% power earlier and needed fewer participants under some heterogeneity and carry-over settings.

## Notes

This is useful as a design-selection source, not as evidence for a specific therapeutic effect. The main practical point is that multi-period single-person data can be statistically efficient when the intervention has manageable carry-over and the aggregation rule is planned in advance.
