# Platelet-derived exerkine CXCL4/platelet factor 4 rejuvenates hippocampal neurogenesis and restores cognitive function in aged mice

- Source: Nature Communications
- PMID: 37587147
- DOI: 10.1038/s41467-023-39873-9
- Nature: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-023-39873-9
- Ingested: 2026-04-29

## Summary

2023 mouse study showing that exercise activates platelets, PF4 is a platelet-derived exerkine, and systemic PF4 can mimic exercise-linked rejuvenation of hippocampal neurogenesis and cognition in aged mice. This is a strong preclinical transfer story, but still not a human cognition intervention.

## Notes

- Exercise was required for the hippocampal precursor-cell proliferation effect in aged mice.
- Systemic PF4 increased adult hippocampal neurogenesis and improved cognitive function in aged animals.
- The paper positions PF4 as sufficient and necessary in the exercise-response pathway.
- Human efficacy, dosing, and safety remain unvalidated.
