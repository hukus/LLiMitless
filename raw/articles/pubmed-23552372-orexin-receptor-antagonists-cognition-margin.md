# Orexin receptor antagonists differ from standard sleep drugs by promoting sleep at doses that do not disrupt cognition

- PMID: 23552372
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23552372/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Preclinical rat and rhesus-monkey work showing a DORA had a wider sleep-versus-cognition therapeutic margin than zolpidem, eszopiclone, or diazepam. Useful as a mechanistic safety-and-residual-impairment anchor, but not a human enhancement result.
