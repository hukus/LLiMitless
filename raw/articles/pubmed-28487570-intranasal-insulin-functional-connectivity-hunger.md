# Intranasal insulin enhances brain functional connectivity mediating the relationship between adiposity and subjective feeling of hunger

- PMID: 28487570
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28487570/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Acute intranasal insulin increased resting-state functional connectivity in healthy adults, with the hypothalamic connectivity response depending on peripheral insulin sensitivity.

## Notes

This is useful proxy evidence for any insulin-plus-stimulation hypothesis because it shows that central insulin effects are state dependent rather than uniform across participants.
