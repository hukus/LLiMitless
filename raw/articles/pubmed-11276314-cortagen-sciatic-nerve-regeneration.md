# Effect of tetrapeptide cortagen on regeneration of sciatic nerve

- PMID: 11276314
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11276314/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

This 2000 rat study found that cortagen accelerated sciatic-nerve regeneration after transection and suturing. It is useful for the identity trail behind Kortagen/Cortagen, but it is still preclinical.
