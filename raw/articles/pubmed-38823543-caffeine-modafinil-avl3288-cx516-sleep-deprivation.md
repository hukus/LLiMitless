# Chronic administration of caffeine, modafinil, AVL-3288 and CX516 induces time-dependent complex effects on cognition and mood in an animal model of sleep deprivation

- PMID: 38823543
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38823543/
- Ingested: 2026-04-29

## Summary

Mouse model of chronic sleep deprivation with daily caffeine, modafinil, AVL-3288, or CX516. Cognitive and mood tests were repeated every six days across a 24-day deprivation protocol.

Caffeine showed a short-term anxiolytic effect that disappeared with chronic administration, while memory effects evolved over time. AVL-3288 improved memory short term, but that effect also faded, and the authors explicitly described tolerance developing over time.

## Notes

- Animal data only, but it is one of the clearest schedule/tolerance papers in the current search set.
- It supports the broader idea that repeated administration can change effect direction and magnitude over time rather than preserving the first-block response.
