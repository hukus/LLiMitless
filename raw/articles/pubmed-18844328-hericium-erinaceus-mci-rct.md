# Improving effects of the mushroom Yamabushitake (Hericium erinaceus) on mild cognitive impairment

- PMID: 18844328
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18844328/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

Randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial in 30 Japanese adults aged 50-80 with MCI. The active arm received 3 g/day of powdered Hericium erinaceus for 16 weeks. Cognitive symptom scales based on HDS-R improved with Hericium versus placebo, with only mild gastrointestinal adverse effects reported.
