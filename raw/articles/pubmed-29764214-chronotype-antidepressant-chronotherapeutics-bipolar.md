# Chronotype influences response to antidepressant chronotherapeutics in bipolar patients

- PMID: 29764214
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29764214/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

In bipolar depression, chronotype influenced response to sleep deprivation plus light therapy. Evening-type patients showed higher response rates, supporting chronotype as a chronotherapy moderator.

## Notes

- 194 bipolar depressed patients were considered.
- The intervention was total sleep deprivation plus light therapy.
- More than 60% responded overall.
- Evening-type subjects had higher response rates.
- This is one of the stronger direct chronotype-response papers in the chronotherapy literature.
