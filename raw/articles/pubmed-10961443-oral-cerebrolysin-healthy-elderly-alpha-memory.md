# Oral Cerebrolysin enhances brain alpha activity and improves cognitive performance in elderly control subjects

- PMID: 10961443
- DOI: 10.1007/978-3-7091-6781-6_33
- Journal: Journal of Neural Transmission Supplementum, 2000
- Source type: human cognition study; healthy elderly; single oral dose
- Ingested: 2026-05-04

## Summary

Old human study in healthy elderly volunteers testing a single oral 30 mL Cerebrolysin solution dose. The abstract reports increased relative EEG alpha power over 1-6 hours, decreased slow delta activity, and improved ADAS-cog memory-item performance, especially recognition more than recall. This is the only direct healthy-elderly cognition signal surfaced in the current Cerebrolysin audit, but it is old, acute, oral-route, and not a modern replicated enhancement trial.

## Signal Use

- Medicinal / formal signal: direct human cognition-adjacent signal in healthy elderly people, but low confidence because the design details, sample size, blinding, route relevance, and replication status are weak from the abstract-level capture.
- Anecdotal signal: none. This source is formal literature, not community experience.
- Wiki implication: Cerebrolysin should no longer be described as having no healthy-human signal at all, but this study is too weak and old to move it out of the recovery-first / medical-supervision lane.
