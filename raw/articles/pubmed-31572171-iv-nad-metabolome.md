# PubMed 31572171 IV NAD+ plasma and urine metabolome pilot

Grant et al. 2019, Frontiers in Aging Neuroscience: "A Pilot Study Investigating Changes in the Human Plasma and Urine NAD+ Metabolome During a 6 Hour Intravenous Infusion of NAD." DOI 10.3389/fnagi.2019.00257.

The paper measured plasma and urine NAD+ and related metabolites during and after a 6-hour IV NAD+ infusion at 3 micromol/min. The abstract reports that plasma NAD+ and measured metabolites did not change until after 2 hours. Urinary methyl-nicotinamide and NAD+ excretion rose by 6 hours, without a significant urinary nicotinamide rise.

The authors interpreted the findings as showing that directly infused NAD+ was rapidly and completely removed from plasma during at least the first 2 hours, with metabolite patterns consistent with NAD+ glycohydrolase and pyrophosphatase activity. This is a pharmacokinetic/metabolome pilot, not a cognition, fatigue, or wellness efficacy trial.

Interpretation for the wiki: direct IV NAD+ has a human metabolite-handling signal, but this does not establish brain uptake, brain-fog improvement, or cognitive enhancement. It is useful for route logic and for keeping IV NAD+ separate from oral NR/NMN/NADH evidence.
