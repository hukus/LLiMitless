# Omega-3 plus alpha-lipoic acid pilot RCT in Alzheimer's disease

2014 randomized placebo-controlled pilot trial in 39 Alzheimer's disease participants. Groups received placebo, omega-3 fatty acids, or omega-3 plus alpha-lipoic acid for 12 months.

The primary oxidative-stress endpoint did not differ between groups. ADAS-cog and ADL were not significantly different from placebo, but omega-3 plus alpha-lipoic acid showed less decline in MMSE and IADL in this small pilot.

Use in wiki: alpha-lipoic acid has combination-product clinical signal but not isolated broad cognition proof. Treat as low-confidence and disease-context only.
