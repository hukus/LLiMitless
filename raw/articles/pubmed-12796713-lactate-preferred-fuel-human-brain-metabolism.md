# Lactate: a preferred fuel for human brain metabolism in vivo

- PMID: 12796713
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12796713/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Healthy-men FDG-PET crossover study. Lactate infusion reduced whole-brain glucose uptake, supporting the claim that circulating lactate can be used by the human brain as a metabolic substrate under euglycemic conditions.
