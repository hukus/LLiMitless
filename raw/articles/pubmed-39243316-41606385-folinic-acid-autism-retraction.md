# Retracted 2024 folinic-acid autism RCT and 2026 retraction notice

## Summary

The 2024 European Journal of Pediatrics record for Panda et al. is now marked in PubMed as a retracted publication. The abstract describes an 80-child randomized double-blind placebo-controlled trial of oral folinic acid as add-on therapy in children with autism spectrum disorder aged 2-10 years, alongside standard care. It reported larger improvements in CARS and CBCL outcomes in the folinic-acid group than placebo and more pronounced benefit in children with high anti-folate-receptor-autoantibody titers.

PubMed lists a 2025 correspondence about the article and a 2026 retraction notice. The 2026 notice is titled "Retraction Note: Efficacy of oral folinic acid supplementation in children with autism spectrum disorder: a randomized double-blind, placebo-controlled trial" and is indexed as a retraction notice of PMID 39243316.

## Interpretation Boundaries

This paper should not be used as efficacy support. It is still worth capturing because secondary summaries, forums, and supplement discussions may continue to cite the original positive result without noticing the 2026 retraction.
