# Pyritinol / Encephabol placebo-controlled dementia trial

PubMed-indexed 1980 double-blind placebo-controlled trial of pyritinol (Encephabol) in 40 patients with moderately advanced dementia.

Design: participants were randomized to pyritinol 800 mg daily or placebo for 3 months. A modified Crichton Geriatric Behavioural Rating Scale was assessed before treatment, monthly up to 3 months, and at 6-month follow-up.

Results: patients on pyritinol showed significantly higher improvement than placebo. Laboratory tests reportedly remained within normal limits in both groups.

Relevance: old dementia-context clinical signal. It supports inclusion as a legacy cognitive drug but does not establish modern healthy-adult enhancement.
