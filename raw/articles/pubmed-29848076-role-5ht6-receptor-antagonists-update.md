# The role of 5 HT6-receptor antagonists in Alzheimer's disease: an update

- PMID: 29848076
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29848076/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

2018 review of 5-HT6 receptor antagonists in AD. It treats the class as mechanistically plausible and summarizes preclinical plus phase I-III clinical work on idalopirdine, intepirdine, and SUVN-502/masupirdine. The review already flags that later phase III programs for idalopirdine and intepirdine failed to show statistically significant cognition benefit, while suggesting partial or inverse agonists as future directions.
