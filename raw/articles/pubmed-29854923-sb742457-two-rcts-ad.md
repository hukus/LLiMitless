# Two randomized controlled trials of SB742457 in mild-to-moderate Alzheimer's disease

- PMID: 29854923
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29854923/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

Two randomized controlled trials tested SB742457 in mild-to-moderate AD, one as monotherapy and one with donepezil. Neither study met overall success criteria; the adjunctive study had some timepoint-specific signals, but the program did not deliver a durable positive outcome. This is the later-stage SB742457 failure that matters for the negative-class map.
