# Peptide-based Anxiolytics: The Molecular Aspects of Heptapeptide Selank Biological Activity

- PMID: 30255741
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30255741/
- Ingested: 2026-04-29

## Summary

Review-style molecular paper that frames Selank as a prolonged anti-anxiety and nootropic peptide. The main mechanistic claim is positive allosteric modulation of GABA binding; it helps explain why Selank is often discussed as calming rather than stimulating, but it does not add a modern human cognition trial.
