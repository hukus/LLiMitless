# Safety of TeaCrine over eight weeks of continuous use

- PMCID: PMC4711067
- DOI: 10.1186/s12970-016-0113-3
- Journal: Journal of the International Society of Sports Nutrition (2016)

## Captured takeaways

- Randomized, placebo-controlled, double-blind 8-week study in apparently healthy, recreationally active, regular caffeine-consuming adults.
- Sixty men and women participated; the trial compared placebo, 200 mg TeaCrine, and 300 mg TeaCrine once daily.
- Outcomes included clinical safety markers, heart rate, blood pressure, ECG, questionnaires, and body composition.
- The authors reported that low- and high-dose TeaCrine appeared safe over the 8-week protocol and did not alter hemodynamic measures or clinical-safety blood markers.
- The authors also reported no evidence of a tachyphylactic / habituation response typical of caffeine-like stimulants, but this is not the same as long-term cognition efficacy.
- Interpretation for the wiki: useful short-term product-specific safety anchor for TeaCrine up to 300 mg/day, with sponsor/product-context caution and no direct objective cognition upgrade.
