# Vortioxetine for post-COVID condition randomized trial

2023 Brain randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial of vortioxetine for post-COVID-19 condition. The paper was published online in November 2023 and appeared in Brain volume 147 in 2024.

The trial enrolled Canadian adults with WHO-defined post-COVID condition and randomized 149 participants to vortioxetine 5-20 mg/day or placebo for eight weeks. The primary cognitive outcome was Digit Symbol Substitution Test change. In the overall unadjusted analysis, vortioxetine did not significantly improve cognition versus placebo.

The important subgroup/moderator signal was inflammation-linked: in an adjusted model, baseline C-reactive protein moderated treatment response, and participants with CRP above the sample mean showed a significant DSST advantage for vortioxetine. Vortioxetine also improved depressive symptoms and health-related quality of life versus placebo.

Interpretation for the wiki: vortioxetine is one of the more formal post-COVID pharmacologic cognition leads, but the headline should be "overall cognition miss with CRP-moderated signal and mood/quality-of-life benefits," not broad nootropic efficacy.
