# Safety and Efficacy of Transcranial Random Noise Stimulation in Psychiatric Disorders: A Systematic Review

- PMID: 41582468
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41582468/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

Systematic review of tRNS in psychiatric disorders. The review covered studies through 2025-04-30 and included 22 studies with 642 participants across ADHD, depression, schizophrenia, dyslexia, and related conditions. Most studies used 20-minute sessions over 1-4 weeks. ADHD and dyslexia showed the most consistent functional gains, schizophrenia studies reported symptom reductions, and depression results were mixed. Adverse effects were generally mild and transient.

## Notes

This is the strongest broad human source for the tRNS branch in the current wiki. It supports a signal in selected psychiatric phenotypes, but it does not establish tRNS as a general healthy-adult cognitive enhancer.
