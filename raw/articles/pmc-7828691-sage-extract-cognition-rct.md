# The acute and chronic cognitive effects of a sage extract in healthy humans

- Source type: open-access randomized placebo-controlled human study.
- Publication: Nutrients, 2021.
- Date checked: 2026-05-03.

This study evaluated a sage extract in healthy humans across acute and chronic time windows. Sage is relevant because older Salvia officinalis / Salvia lavandulaefolia work is frequently cited for cholinergic and mood/cognition effects, but the product identity and extract chemistry need normalization.

Use in the wiki: salvia / sage product-specific cognition anchor. Keep it in the plant-product watchlist until extract identity, dose, and endpoint replication are clearer.
