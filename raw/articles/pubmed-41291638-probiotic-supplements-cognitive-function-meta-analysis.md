# Efficacy and safety of probiotic supplements on cognitive function: a systematic review and meta-analysis of randomized clinical trials

- PMID: 41291638
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41291638/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

2025 meta-analysis of 34 randomized clinical trials (2,390 participants). Pooled results suggest small improvements at 12 weeks on MMSE, MoCA, and categorical fluency, but certainty is low to very low and adverse-event reduction was not shown.
