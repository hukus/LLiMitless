# Ibudilast pharmacology, efficacy, and safety review

2009 Expert Opinion on Pharmacotherapy review of ibudilast. The abstract describes ibudilast as a relatively nonselective phosphodiesterase inhibitor marketed in Japan for asthma, with more recent interest in anti-inflammatory activity in the peripheral immune system and CNS through glial-cell attenuation.

The review frames ibudilast as a potential treatment for multiple sclerosis, neuropathic pain, and opioid-tolerance/withdrawal/reinforcement modulation. It also notes suitable pharmacokinetics and generally good tolerability.
