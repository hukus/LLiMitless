# DailyMed FINTEPLA fenfluramine REMS label

- Source: DailyMed label for FINTEPLA, fenfluramine oral solution
- Revision captured: 2026-era DailyMed label page

## Captured takeaways

- Fenfluramine is now marketed as FINTEPLA for seizure indications, not as a cognition or weight-loss nootropic.
- The label warns that FINTEPLA can cause valvular heart disease and pulmonary arterial hypertension.
- DailyMed describes a known association between serotonergic drugs with 5-HT2B receptor agonist activity, including fenfluramine, and valvular heart disease / pulmonary arterial hypertension.
- Echocardiographic monitoring is required before treatment, every 6 months during treatment, and 3-6 months after treatment concludes.
- FINTEPLA is only available through a restricted REMS program because of the VHD/PAH risk.
- Interpretation for the wiki: fenfluramine belongs as a safety precedent for old anorectic combinations and serotonergic 5-HT2B risk, not as a cognitive-enhancement lead.
