# Cognitive and emotional effects of bilateral prefrontal anodal tDCS and high-frequency tRNS in schizophrenia: a randomized sham-controlled study

- PMCID: PMC12949120
- Nature: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41537-025-00720-z
- Ingested: 2026-04-30

## Summary

Randomized crossover sham-controlled study in 36 male patients with schizophrenia. Bilateral DLPFC high-frequency tRNS used 2 mA and 100-640 Hz for 20 min with extracephalic returns. Compared with sham, tRNS improved planning and also improved working-memory accuracy and strategy score. Positive affect increased and negative affect decreased after stimulation; side effects were minimal and sham blinding was effective.

## Notes

This is the clearest psychiatric-context tRNS study in the current branch. It supports adjunctive use research in schizophrenia, not healthy-adult enhancement.
