# Comparative bioavailability and utilization of B12 supplement forms

- PMID: 28223907
- Published: 2017
- Source type: review / hypothesis and form-comparison discussion
- Checked: 2026-05-18

## Summary

This review compares cyanocobalamin with methylcobalamin, hydroxycobalamin, and adenosylcobalamin as supplemental vitamin B12 forms. It argues that different cobalamin forms may behave differently in absorption, retention, conversion, or genetic-polymorphism contexts, while also acknowledging that many people may respond adequately to more than one form.

The source is useful because it represents the form-specific argument often invoked in supplement communities without itself proving healthy-adult cognitive enhancement. In this wiki, it should be read as a mechanistic and bioavailability rationale, not as a cognition trial.

- Use as the pro-form-specific counterpoint beside raw/articles/pmc-4692085-coenzyme-b12-forms-not-superior.md.
