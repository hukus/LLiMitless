# MSM as methyl-group donor in human liver HepaRG cells

PubMed-indexed study titled "Methylsulfonylmethane Serves as a Donor of Methyl Groups for Methylation of DNA in Human Liver HepaRG Cells."

Key capture:

- The study tested whether methylsulfonylmethane / MSM can donate methyl groups for DNA methylation in a human liver HepaRG cell model.
- The result is relevant to methyl-donor lore because MSM contains methyl groups and can contribute to DNA methylation chemistry in this cell context.
- This is not a human cognition study, not a brain study, and not proof that oral MSM improves methylation status or cognitive symptoms in people.
