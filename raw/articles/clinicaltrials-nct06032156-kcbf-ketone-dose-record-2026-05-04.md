# NCT06032156 kCBF ketone dose and cerebral blood flow registry snapshot

## Summary

Current ClinicalTrials.gov record for "The Acute Effects of a High Versus Low Dose of a Ketone Monoester Supplement on Cerebral Blood Flow and Cognition." The registry status is unknown, with last known status recruiting, last update posted 2023-12-07, and verification in 2023-12. The record shows no posted results. Estimated enrollment is 20 young adults, with actual study start 2023-11-08, estimated primary completion 2024-05, and estimated study completion 2024-08.

The study compares low-dose beta-hydroxybutyrate, high-dose beta-hydroxybutyrate, and placebo to characterize dose-dependent effects on cerebral blood flow, circulating blood markers, and cognition. It also includes an exploratory exercise oxygen-uptake kinetics aim two hours after ketone ingestion.

## Signal Use

- Medicinal signal: registry context for the published acute CBF dose-response paper and for no-results surveillance.
- Cognition signal: no posted registry results; use the published trial raw note for actual CBF/cognition interpretation.
- Caveat: unknown registry status and no posted results mean this should stay in the watch queue, not be treated as an independent positive or negative result.
