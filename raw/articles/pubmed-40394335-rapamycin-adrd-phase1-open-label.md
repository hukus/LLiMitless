# Rapamycin treatment for Alzheimer's disease and related dementias: a pilot phase 1 clinical trial

- PMID: 40394335
- ClinicalTrials.gov: NCT04200911
- DOI: 10.1038/s43856-025-00904-9
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

2025 single-site open-label phase 1 trial in 10 participants aged 55-85 with MCI or early-stage dementia. All received rapamycin 1 mg/day for 8 weeks.

Rapamycin was not detectable in CSF before or after treatment and there were no significant cognitive changes. Mild adverse events occurred, with increases in systolic blood pressure and HbA1c and rises in several CSF/plasma AD and inflammatory biomarkers.
