# PubChem 2026 D-ribose identity note

PubChem identifies D-ribose as a pentose sugar / ribose stereoisomer with formula C5H10O5 and molecular weight about 150.13 g/mol. Common labels include D-ribose, ribose, alpha-D-ribose, and beta-D-ribose depending on stereochemical and anomeric representation.

Interpretation for the wiki: D-ribose is a sugar / energy-metabolism substrate and nucleotide-building-block chemical. It should not be collapsed with [[nadh]], [[coenzyme-q10]], [[creatine]], ketone esters, lactate, glucose, ATP supplements, or generic mitochondrial blends. Any cognition claim should state whether the source is D-ribose alone or a combination with NADH, CoQ10, carnitine, magnesium, or other ME/CFS stack components.
