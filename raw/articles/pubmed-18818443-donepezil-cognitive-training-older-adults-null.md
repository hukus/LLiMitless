# Donepezil plus cognitive training in older adults with memory complaints

Randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial in 168 nondemented community-dwelling older adults with memory complaints. Participants received donepezil 5 mg/day for 6 weeks, then 10 mg/day for another 6 weeks, followed by a two-week cognitive-training course, with treatment maintained for the remainder of a year.

Cognitive training improved performance, but donepezil did not produce significant benefits over placebo. The authors raised physiological tolerance / increased acetylcholinesterase as a possible reason why short-term signals in nondemented participants may fail with chronic use.

Interpretation for the wiki: this is an important chronic-use counterweight for [[donepezil]] and argues against treating short acute gains as durable cognitive enhancement.
