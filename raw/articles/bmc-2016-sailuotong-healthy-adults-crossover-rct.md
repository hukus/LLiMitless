# Sailuotong healthy-adult crossover pilot trial

2016 BMC Complementary and Alternative Medicine randomized double-blind placebo-controlled crossover pilot trial of Sailuotong in healthy adults.

Sailuotong is a standardized Chinese herbal medicine formula combining Panax ginseng, Ginkgo biloba, and Crocus sativus / saffron bioactive constituents. The healthy-adult study had 16 completers and tested one week of Sailuotong versus placebo with a one-week washout. Each SLT capsule contained a 60 mg standardized mixture: 27.27 mg ginsenosides, 27.27 mg total ginkgo flavone-glycosides, and 5.46 mg crocins; participants took two capsules daily.

Compared with placebo, SLT produced small improvements in working memory and changes in auditory ERP components, but no resting EEG band or cardiovascular effects. The authors framed the trial as preliminary and underpowered, requiring a larger sample.

Interpretation for the wiki: Sailuotong is one of the few standardized traditional formulas with a healthy-adult cognition pilot, but the evidence remains very small, short-duration, and formula-specific.
