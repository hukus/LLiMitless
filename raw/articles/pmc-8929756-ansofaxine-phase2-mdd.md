# Ansofaxine LY03005 phase 2 dose-finding MDD trial

- Source: phase 2 randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled, dose-finding trial of ansofaxine extended-release tablets for major depressive disorder.
- Public index summary identifies ansofaxine / LY03005 as a potential triple reuptake inhibitor of serotonin, norepinephrine, and dopamine.
- Design summary from indexed source: 260 adults with MDD were studied in a phase 2 dose-finding trial; the MDPI review of the program lists LY03005 40, 80, 120, and 160 mg versus placebo over 6 weeks, after washout.
- Endpoint summary: primary depression endpoint was HAMD-17 change, with MADRS and CGI-I as secondary endpoints.
- Interpretation for the wiki: this source supports a phase-2 clinical-development anchor for ansofaxine before the larger phase-3 trial. It should not be treated as direct cognition evidence.
