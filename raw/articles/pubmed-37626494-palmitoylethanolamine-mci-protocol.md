# Source Digest

Type: peer-reviewed randomized placebo-controlled mild-cognitive-impairment trial protocol.

Publication date: 2023-07-29.

Key extraction:
- Intervention: oral N-palmitoylethanolamine / PEA, 600 mg twice daily, versus placebo.
- Planned population: 50 subjects with mild cognitive impairment and 30 healthy controls, randomized within group.
- Planned duration: 12 months of treatment followed by 6 months of observation.
- Planned cognitive and functional measures: MMSE and CDR for screening; RBANS, HADS, Instrumental Activities of Daily Living, and dual-task protocol at baseline, post-treatment, and follow-up.
- Planned biomarkers: PEA, endocannabinoid congeners, inflammatory markers, and neuronal-damage markers in blood and urine.
- Interpretation: important registry-like anchor that a formal MCI cognition trial exists, but the protocol does not provide outcome results.

Wiki signal:
- Medicinal signal: pending; supports surveillance, not efficacy.
- Confidence limiter: protocol only. Do not count as a positive trial until results are published or posted.
