# Transcutaneous Auricular Vagus Nerve Stimulation (taVNS) for Insomnia Disorder: A Narrative Review of Effectiveness, Mechanisms and Recommendations for Clinical Practice

- PMID: 40535058
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40535058/
- PMCID: PMC12174914
- Published: 2025-06-13
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

2025 narrative review of taVNS for insomnia that frames the method as promising but still methodologically immature.

## Notes

- The review treats taVNS as safe and well tolerated, but emphasizes that evidence quality is limited by trial-method weaknesses.
- Mechanistic discussion centers on brain-network modulation, neurotransmitter-hormone axes, anti-inflammatory effects, and autonomic changes.
- The authors explicitly note that there is not yet consensus on duration of efficacy, optimal stimulation site, or parameters.
- For the ontology, this is less a proof-of-efficacy source than a clear marker that taVNS is still a site/threshold/timing problem.
- Good companion source for protocol headers because it highlights why auricular site and scheduling belong in the dose record.
