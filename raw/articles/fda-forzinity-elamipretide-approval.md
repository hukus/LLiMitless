# FDA Grants Accelerated Approval to First Treatment for Barth Syndrome

- Source: U.S. Food and Drug Administration
- Source URL: https://www.fda.gov/news-events/press-announcements/fda-grants-accelerated-approval-first-treatment-barth-syndrome
- Ingested: 2026-04-29

## Summary

On September 19, 2025, FDA granted accelerated approval to Forzinity (elamipretide) injection as the first treatment for Barth syndrome in patients weighing at least 30 kg. The approval was based on improved knee extensor muscle strength, with once-daily subcutaneous dosing and injection-site reactions as the most common adverse events.

## Notes

- This is an approved disease-specific mitochondrial therapy, not a cognition indication.
- FDA stated that serious reactions have also been reported and required a post-approval confirmatory trial.
