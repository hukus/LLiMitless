# Temporal Interference Methods for Non-invasive Deep Brain Stimulation, Study 1.1

- ClinicalTrials.gov ID: NCT07215299
- Registry: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07215299
- Updated: 2026-02-10
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary
Indiana University is running a randomized crossover study to validate TI focality and brain-activity effects in healthy volunteers.
Study 1.1 is part of a program designed to link TI to the causal role of deep structures in cognition.
The protocol uses active and sham blocks during fMRI and pairs the stimulation with a cognitive-control / decision-making research program.

## Notes
This is still a target-validation study, but it is explicitly cognition-facing rather than purely motor.
