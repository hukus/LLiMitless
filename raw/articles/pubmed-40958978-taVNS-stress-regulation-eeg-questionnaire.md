# Effect of transcutaneous auricular vagus nerve stimulation on stress regulation: an EEG and questionnaire study

- PMID: 40958978
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40958978/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Randomized crossover study in healthy adults using mental arithmetic and 2-back stress induction with taVNS versus sham. Perceived stress rose in sham but not taVNS, and frontal theta/alpha power decreased only in sham.

## Notes

- The study suggests taVNS may blunt stress-linked EEG changes, but it did not establish a direct cognition gain.
- The task battery was used to induce stress, so this is primarily a stress-resilience readout.
- Useful for the autonomic/stress branch, not for a strong memory or executive-function claim.
