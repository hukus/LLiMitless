# FDA hidden-ingredients risk note

- FDA says products that claim to help health issues are not reviewed by the agency and can contain hidden ingredients.
- Hidden ingredients may include prescription drugs, banned substances, or harmful chemicals.
- "Herbal" or "all natural" labels do not guarantee safety or purity.
