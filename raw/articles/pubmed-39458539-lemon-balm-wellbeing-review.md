# Clinical efficacy and tolerability of lemon balm in psychological well-being

PubMed-indexed 2024 review of Melissa officinalis / lemon balm in psychological well-being. The abstract states that lemon balm contains phytochemicals including rosmarinic acid, citral, oleanolic acid, and ursolic acid and may modulate GABAergic, cholinergic, and serotonergic systems.

The review concludes that more robust RCTs are needed, while existing research indicates promise as a calming agent with anxiolytic, antidepressant, cognitive, and sleep-quality signals.

Relevance: mechanistic and clinical-context source for lemon balm, but not enough to upgrade it above low-moderate signal.
