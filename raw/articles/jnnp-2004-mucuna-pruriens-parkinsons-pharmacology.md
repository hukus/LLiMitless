# Mucuna pruriens in Parkinson's disease pharmacological study

2004 JNNP randomized, controlled, double-blind crossover pharmacological study in Parkinson's disease comparing Mucuna pruriens seed powder against standard levodopa/carbidopa. The paper is an early human anchor for rapid-onset Mucuna / L-DOPA exposure rather than a cognition trial.

For cognitive-improvement ranking, the key point is negative by translation: a faster or stronger dopaminergic motor response in PD does not establish safe healthy-adult motivation or cognition enhancement.

Use in the wiki: early formal human Mucuna/Parkinson's evidence and caution against nootropic extrapolation.
