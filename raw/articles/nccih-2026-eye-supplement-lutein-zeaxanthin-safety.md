# NCCIH eye-supplement note on lutein and zeaxanthin

- Source type: U.S. government consumer health summary.
- Publication / page status: NCCIH, checked 2026-05-11.
- Date checked: 2026-05-11.

NCCIH summarizes dietary supplements for eye conditions, including AREDS and AREDS2 findings. The page notes that AREDS2 follow-up favored formulations that include lutein and zeaxanthin over beta-carotene-containing formulations for age-related macular degeneration safety and effectiveness contexts.

For safety framing, NCCIH notes that lutein and zeaxanthin appear safe regardless of smoking status, while beta-carotene has a smoker / former-smoker lung-cancer signal in the eye-supplement literature.
