# GlyNAC healthy-aging glutathione dose RCT

Randomized controlled clinical trial in 114 healthy volunteers with mean age around 65 years. Participants received glycine plus N-acetylcysteine at three dose levels for 2 weeks: 1.2 g/1.2 g, 2.4 g/2.4 g, or 3.6 g/3.6 g of each amino acid.

Primary finding: GlyNAC did not increase circulating glutathione versus placebo on the primary endpoint. Stratified analyses suggested that participants with higher oxidative stress and lower glutathione status may have responded with glutathione generation.

Mechanistic interpretation: healthy aging was associated with lower fasting glycine that could be raised toward young-adult levels with supplementation. Preclinical follow-up suggested glycine depletion as a feature of healthy aging and glycine support for mitochondrial function independent of NAC.

Safety: the abstract describes GlyNAC as safe and well tolerated in this short healthy-aging trial.

Use in wiki: this is a counterweight to overclaiming. It suggests responder selection by oxidative stress / glutathione demand and does not support assuming every older adult or healthy user gets glutathione or cognition benefit.
