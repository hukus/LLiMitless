# RewinD-LB - Clinical Study of Neflamapimod in Patients With Dementia With Lewy Bodies

- ClinicalTrials.gov ID: NCT05869669
- Sponsor: EIP Pharma Inc / CervoMed
- Study type: randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled phase 2b
- Population: early-stage DLB
- Status: not recruiting on external listings; trial later reported as completed

## Summary

The registry record and external listings describe RewinD-LB as the follow-on DLB trial meant to validate the earlier neflamapimod signal in a more enrichment-focused population.

Design details:

- Primary endpoint: change in CDR-SB.
- Secondary endpoints: Timed Up and Go, a cognitive battery, and Clinician's Global Impression of Change.
- Trial size: 159 participants in the top-line reporting.
- Dosing: oral neflamapimod 40 mg TID.
- Enrichment strategy: exclude advanced DLB / likely AD co-pathology using plasma ptau181.

Result-status notes:

- December 2024 topline company reporting said the trial did not meet statistical significance on the primary endpoint or the key secondary endpoints.
- Safety and tolerability were described as favorable, but target plasma concentrations were not achieved in the 16-week double-blind phase.
- A 2025 journal report on the extension phase kept the negative initial-phase result separate from the open-label follow-up.

Interpretation for the wiki:

- This is the main counterweight to the earlier AscenD-LB signal.
- The best reading is phenotype-sensitive but not yet a replicated DLB cognitive win.
