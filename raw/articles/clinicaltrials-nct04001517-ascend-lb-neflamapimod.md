# Cognitive Effects of Oral p38 Alpha Kinase Inhibitor Neflamapimod in Dementia With Lewy Bodies (AscenD-LB)

- ClinicalTrials.gov ID: NCT04001517
- Sponsor: EIP Pharma Inc
- Study type: randomized, quadruple-masked, parallel-assignment phase 2
- Enrollment (actual): 91
- Treatment: neflamapimod vs placebo, 16 weeks
- Population: probable DLB, with eligibility based on consensus criteria and biomarker / imaging rules in the protocol

## Summary

The registry record shows AscenD-LB as a completed DLB phase 2 study with results posted on ClinicalTrials.gov and a last update posted in 2023.

Design details:

- 40 mg neflamapimod capsules were given orally with food.
- Dosing was BID for lower-weight participants and TID for higher-weight participants.
- The primary endpoint was a study-specific Cogstate neuropsychological battery.
- Secondary endpoints included CDR-SB, MMSE, NPI-10, Timed Up and Go, and EEG as a biomarker readout.

Interpretation for the wiki:

- This is the key human DLB proof-of-principle record for neflamapimod.
- Keep the trial design, the posted-results status, and the later ptau181 subgroup analysis separate.
- Use the follow-up paper on AD co-pathology to interpret phenotype enrichment, not as standalone proof of broad efficacy.
