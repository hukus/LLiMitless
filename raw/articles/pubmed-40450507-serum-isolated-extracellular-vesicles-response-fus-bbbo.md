# Response of Serum-Isolated Extracellular Vesicles to Focused Ultrasound-Mediated Blood-Brain Barrier Opening

- PMID: 40450507
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40450507/
- PMCID: PMC12229771
- DOI: 10.1016/j.ultrasmedbio.2025.04.019
- Ingested: 2026-04-30

## Summary

Mouse and AD-patient biomarker study. FUS-BBBO increased serum EV concentration in mice and AD patients, with patient EV changes linked to BBB-opening volume and post-procedure timing. EV isolation also amplified detection of BBB-opening-associated biomarkers such as GFAP, Aβ42, and p-tau181. The paper supports liquid-biopsy / monitoring biology, not cognition efficacy.
