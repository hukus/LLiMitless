# Effectiveness of Essence of Chicken in Improving Cognitive Function in Young People Under Work-Related Stress

- PMCID: PMC4902523
- DOI: 10.1097/MD.0000000000003640
- Published: 2016
- Ingested: 2026-05-11

## Summary

Randomized, double-blind trial in 102 young workers with high work-related stress. Participants received 70 mL/day essence of chicken or placebo for 2 weeks. Blood tests and neurocognitive assessments were performed before treatment, at treatment end, and 2 weeks after treatment.

The captured abstract reports that essence of chicken improved form-color associative memory in participants with high depression scores, but not as a broad cognitive effect. Creatinine and glutamic-pyruvic transaminase rose at week 2 and returned to baseline by week 4, while BUN decreased at week 4.

## Relevance

Supports the stress/mood-subgroup interpretation for [[essence-of-chicken]]. It is also a safety-monitoring reminder for liver/kidney markers in longer or higher-dose use.

## Caveats

- Subgroup-positive rather than broad cognition-positive.
- Young workers under high work stress are not a general healthy-adult cohort.
- Lab changes were transient but should still be recorded as safety context.
