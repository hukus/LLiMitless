# Efficacy and safety of focused ultrasound-mediated blood-brain barrier opening in Alzheimer's disease: A systematic review and meta-analysis

- PMID: 40406679
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40406679/
- PMC: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12095960/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Eight clinical studies (57 AD participants, 2018-2023) were pooled. The meta-analysis reported no deterioration of cognitive function after FUS-mediated BBB opening, with minor temporary adverse events, and framed the method as a promising targeted-delivery and disease-modification platform.
