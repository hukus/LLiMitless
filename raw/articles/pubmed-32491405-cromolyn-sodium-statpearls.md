## Summary

StatPearls describes cromolyn sodium as a mast-cell stabilizer derived from khellin, a natural extract from Ammi visnaga, and first introduced as an inhaled asthma treatment in 1969. It prevents release of inflammatory mediators such as histamine and leukotrienes and is used across asthma, allergic rhinitis, mastocytosis, and allergic eye conditions.

The overview notes that cromolyn differs from antihistamines because it prevents mediator release rather than blocking histamine after release. It also notes that oral solution is poorly absorbed and may have a delayed onset of 2 to 6 weeks.
