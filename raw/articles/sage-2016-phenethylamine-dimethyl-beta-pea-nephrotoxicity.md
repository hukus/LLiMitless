# 2016 in-vitro nephrotoxicity study of 2-phenethylamine plus N,N-dimethyl-beta-phenethylamine

Mossoba and colleagues tested 2-phenethylamine and N,N-dimethyl-beta-phenethylamine in human proximal-tubule cells. The abstract reports that each compound alone produced little or no evidence of toxicity in this system, but the combination at high treatment concentrations consistently produced evidence of potent proximal-tubule cell injury.

The paper frames both compounds as phenethylamines used in dietary supplements and argues that combining phenethylamines may potentiate toxic effects in supplement settings.

Wiki use: safety and stack-boundary anchor for [[phenethylamine]], especially when PEA appears in stimulant blends, preworkouts, fat-loss products, or "eria jarensis"/N,N-DMPEA-adjacent products. It is in-vitro evidence, not clinical kidney-injury incidence.
