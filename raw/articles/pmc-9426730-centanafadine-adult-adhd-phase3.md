# Centanafadine sustained-release adult ADHD phase 3 trials

- Article: "Efficacy, Safety, and Tolerability of Centanafadine Sustained-Release Tablets in Adults With Attention-Deficit/Hyperactivity Disorder"
- Journal: Journal of Clinical Psychopharmacology (2022)
- DOI: 10.1097/JCP.0000000000001575
- Trial IDs: NCT03605680 and NCT03605836

## Captured takeaways

- Two randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled phase 3 studies tested centanafadine sustained-release 200 mg/day or 400 mg/day against placebo in adults aged 18-55 with ADHD.
- The primary endpoint was change from baseline to day 42 in Adult ADHD Investigator Symptom Rating Scale (AISRS) total score; CGI-S was a key secondary endpoint.
- Study 1 randomized 149 participants to 200 mg/day, 149 to 400 mg/day, and 148 to placebo. Study 2 randomized 145, 143, and 142 participants to the same arms.
- AISRS least-squares mean differences favored centanafadine over placebo in both trials: study 1, -3.16 for 200 mg/day and -2.74 for 400 mg/day; study 2, -4.01 for 200 mg/day and -4.47 for 400 mg/day.
- The phase 3 paper reports significant CGI-S movement and improvement in inattentiveness and hyperactivity-impulsivity subscales.
- Common treatment-emergent adverse events included headache and decreased appetite; drug-related events noted in the paper included decreased appetite, headache, dry mouth, and nausea.
- The trials used a single-blind placebo run-in that excluded high placebo responders, which helps reduce placebo noise but also makes comparisons with other ADHD trials less direct.
- Interpretation for the wiki: this is an ADHD symptom-efficacy signal for a nonstimulant triple-reuptake agent, not healthy-adult cognitive enhancement proof.
