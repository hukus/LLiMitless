# IDRA-21 delayed matching performance in young and aged rhesus monkeys

2004 Neuropharmacology paper on IDRA-21 in recombinant AMPA receptor assays and delayed matching-to-sample performance in rhesus monkeys.

Key extracted points:

- IDRA-21 is described as a positive allosteric modulator of the glutamate AMPA receptor.
- In HEK cells expressing human GluR1/2 flip receptors, IDRA-21 inhibited glutamate-induced current inactivation and increased charge transfer, supporting a mechanism of facilitated excitatory neurotransmission.
- Oral IDRA-21 produced significant improvement on a delayed matching-to-sample task in young adult rhesus monkeys.
- The article also tested aged monkeys, making it one of the more relevant nonhuman-primate cognition anchors for the compound.
- Interpretation for the wiki: this is the strongest primate bridge for IDRA-21, but it still stops short of human cognition evidence and does not establish a safe dose for self-experimentation.
