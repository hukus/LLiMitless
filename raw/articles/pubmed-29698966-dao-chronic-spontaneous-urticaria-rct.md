# Diamine oxidase supplementation in chronic spontaneous urticaria

- PMID: 29698966
- Published: 2018
- Source type: randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled crossover study
- Checked: 2026-05-11
- Ingested: 2026-05-11

## Summary

This study tested oral diamine oxidase supplementation in chronic spontaneous urticaria incompletely controlled by first-line antihistamines. Participants took one capsule twice daily, 15 minutes before a meal, for 30 days in a double-blind placebo-controlled crossover design. Twenty patients completed the study.

DAO supplementation reduced the 7-day urticaria activity score by 3.8 points in patients with low baseline serum DAO levels, with a placebo comparison p value of 0.041. Baseline DAO level inversely correlated with improvement, and patients receiving DAO slightly reduced daily antihistamine dose.
