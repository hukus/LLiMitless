# Safety, Tolerability, and Pharmacokinetic Profile of the Low-Impact Ampakine CX1739 in Young Healthy Volunteers

- PMID: 39302241
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39302241/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

2025 healthy-volunteer safety and PK study of the low-impact ampakine CX1739. The paper reports single-dose escalation from 100 to 1200 mg and multiple-dose ascending regimens of 300 to 600 mg BID for 7 to 10 days. CX1739 was generally well tolerated up to 900 mg once daily or 450 mg BID, with headache and nausea as the prominent adverse effects, and PK appeared dose-proportional with a 6 to 9 hour half-life. This is a development-package paper, not an efficacy trial.
