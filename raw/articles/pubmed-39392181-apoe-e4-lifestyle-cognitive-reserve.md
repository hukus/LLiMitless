Title: APOE ε4 carrier status moderates the effect of lifestyle factors on cognitive reserve

This paper is not an intervention trial, but it adds a useful moderator signal: APOE ε4 status changes how lifestyle factors relate to cognitive reserve in older adults.

That makes it relevant as background evidence for enrichment logic. If a biomarker already modifies the lifestyle-cognition relationship, a biomarker-targeted intervention trial has a better chance of finding a sharper signal than a fully unselected cohort.

The limitation is obvious: moderation of baseline associations is not the same as treatment response.
