# The Effect of a Post-Learning Nap on Motor Memory Consolidation in People With Parkinson's Disease: A Randomised Controlled Trial

- Journal Sleep Res. 2025 Sep 26:e70203. PMID 41001836.
- Trial registration: NCT04144283.
- Study size: 32 people with Parkinson's disease and 32 healthy older adults.
- Design: post-learning nap versus wakefulness after finger-tapping sequence learning.
- Outcomes: post-intervention change, 24-hour retention, and dual-task cost for automaticity.
- Result: no beneficial effect of napping on motor retention or automaticity compared with wakefulness in either group.
- In Parkinson's disease, slow-wave amplitude and slope during the nap were positively associated with performance improvement; cross-frequency coupling associations were inconclusive.
- Interpretation: sleep physiology may still track plasticity even when the nap does not outperform wake on the behavioral endpoint.
