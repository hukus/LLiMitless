# Metadoxine ER phase 3 ADHD failure

- Source: Genetic Engineering & Biotechnology News
- Date: 2017-01-17

## Captured takeaways

- Alcobra reported that metadoxine extended release (MDX) failed a phase 3 ADHD trial.
- The MEASURE study did not meet its primary endpoint, defined as a statistically significant difference from placebo in change from baseline on Conners' Adult ADHD Rating Scales.
- The reported full-analysis set included 283 randomized subjects with at least one post-baseline efficacy assessment.
- The failure followed an FDA clinical-hold episode tied to preclinical neurological findings rather than observed clinical safety data from the ongoing trial.
- Interpretation for the wiki: earlier phase 2 and acute attention signals should be downgraded by the later pivotal miss and safety/regulatory uncertainty.
