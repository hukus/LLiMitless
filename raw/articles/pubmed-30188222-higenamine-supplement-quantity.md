# The stimulant higenamine in weight loss and sports supplements

Clinical Toxicology paper by Cohen and colleagues, published in 2019 after online publication in 2018, analyzed 24 U.S. weight-loss and sports/energy supplements listing higenamine or synonyms such as norcoclaurine or demethylcoclaurine.

The paper reports that higenamine ranged from trace amounts to about 62 mg per serving, and following label directions could expose consumers to about 110 mg per day. Only 5 of 24 products listed an amount of higenamine, and none of those were accurately labeled.

Use in the wiki: product-quantity and label-accuracy anchor. It supports a high product-quality burden for higenamine, especially in stimulant or weight-loss products.
