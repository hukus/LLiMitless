# The effects of transcranial magnetic stimulation on cognitive flexibility among undergraduates with insomnia symptoms: A prospective, single-blind, randomized control trial

- PMID: 40276332
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40276332/
- Ingested: 2026-04-29

## Summary

Randomized single-blind trial in 29 undergraduates with insomnia symptoms comparing 1 Hz left DLPFC rTMS versus sham for 2 weeks (10 sessions). The active arm improved sleep quality and cognitive flexibility versus sham, with an 8-week follow-up showing lingering electrophysiologic differences.

## Notes

This is not a CBT-I sequence trial, but it is the clearest 2025 insomnia-phenotype cognition signal for rTMS and supports the idea that sleep state and stimulation state are interacting variables rather than independent levers.
