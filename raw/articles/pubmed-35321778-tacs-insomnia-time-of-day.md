# Transcranial Alternating Current Stimulation (tACS) as a Treatment for Insomnia

- PMID: 35321778
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35321778/
- Published: 2023-05
- Study type: small randomized in-lab sleep study
- Key finding: 0.75 mA, 0.75 Hz, 5-minute bilateral frontal tACS during polysomnography improved several sleep outcomes and recall memory on the stimulation night, but the sample was tiny and the paper did not report chronotype stratification.

## Notes

- This is useful mainly as a sleep-state / timing anchor for the tACS branch.
- The trial suggests the modality may be more sensitive to state and protocol context than to a generic healthy-adult cognition effect.
