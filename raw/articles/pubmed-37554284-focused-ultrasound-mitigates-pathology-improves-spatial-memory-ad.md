# Focused ultrasound mitigates pathology and improves spatial memory in Alzheimer's mice and patients

- PMID: 37554284
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37554284/
- PMCID: PMC10405840
- DOI: 10.7150/thno.79898
- Ingested: 2026-04-30

## Summary

Mixed preclinical/clinical paper. In 3xTg-AD and wild-type mice, repeated bilateral sonications over the hippocampus improved spatial memory, and the magnitude of cognitive improvement correlated with BBB-opening volume. The authors also report a single mild AD patient from NCT04118764 with significant amyloid PET reduction in the treated hemisphere. The human cognition evidence remains extremely thin because the clinical component is effectively one case.
