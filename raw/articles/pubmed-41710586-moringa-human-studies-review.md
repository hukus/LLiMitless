# Moringa oleifera Lamk. as a Promising Adjunct Therapeutic Candidate: A Narrative Review of Human Studies and Published Case Reports

- PMID: 41710586
- Published: 2026-02-10
- DOI: 10.2147/DDDT.S586556
- Evidence layer: narrative review of human studies and case reports

## Summary

This 2026 narrative review mapped human studies and case reports of Moringa oleifera from 2015 to 2025. It included 22 clinical trials and nine case reports. The main clinical themes were immunological and nutritional effects, metabolic and endocrine disorders, inflammatory and oxidative-stress-related diseases, maternal and child health outcomes, and other clinical applications.

The review reported more consistent human signals for immune function, glycemic control, antioxidant status, HIV/prediabetes/malnutrition contexts, and maternal/infant nutrition than for cognition. It also noted generally good tolerability but rare hypersensitivity and thrombotic events. For the wiki, this means Moringa is a metabolic/nutritional adjunct candidate, not a direct cognition lead.
