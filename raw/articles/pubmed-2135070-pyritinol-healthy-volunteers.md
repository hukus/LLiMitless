# Psychopharmacological effects of pyritinol in normal volunteers

PubMed-indexed randomized double-blind crossover study in 12 healthy male volunteers. Participants received pyritinol 600 mg, pyritinol 1200 mg, or placebo for three days and completed critical flicker fusion, choice reaction time, memory, and subjective-drug-effect measures.

The abstract reports significant improvements in critical flicker fusion and choice reaction time after pyritinol, with no significant differences on other tests. For the wiki, this is an old acute arousal/processing-speed signal, not a modern broad memory-enhancement packet.

Relevance: pyritinol deserves inclusion as a legacy nootropic, but signal strength should stay low-moderate and endpoint-specific.
