## Summary

2002 review of aniracetam pharmacology and clinical positioning. The review describes aniracetam as a pyrrolidinone cognition enhancer used clinically for behavioral and psychological symptoms of dementia after stroke and in Alzheimer disease, but it is a review article rather than a primary efficacy trial.
