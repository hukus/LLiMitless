# Pregnenolone schizophrenia cognition trial
- PMID: 24496044
- 60 recent-onset schizophrenia/schizoaffective participants randomized to pregnenolone 50 mg/day vs placebo for 8 weeks, added to antipsychotics.
- Primary cognitive endpoint was MCCB composite score; secondary endpoints included UPSA-B functional capacity and negative symptoms.
- Key result: no significant MCCB cognitive benefit, but functional capacity improved.
- Branch readout: pregnenolone looks more like a functional/symptom lead than a direct cognition enhancer in this controlled study.
