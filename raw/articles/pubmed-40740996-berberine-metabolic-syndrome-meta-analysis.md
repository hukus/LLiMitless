# PubMed 40740996 berberine metabolic syndrome meta-analysis note

This 2025 Frontiers in Pharmacology systematic review and meta-analysis evaluated berberine for components of metabolic syndrome using randomized placebo-controlled trials. The abstract reports significant improvements in triglycerides, fasting plasma glucose, waist circumference, LDL cholesterol, total cholesterol, BMI, and 2-hour oral glucose tolerance, with no significant effects on HDL cholesterol or blood pressure and no significant safety difference versus placebo.

Interpretation for the wiki: this is the strongest formal reason to keep berberine visible in metabolic-state coverage. It supports a metabolic/glucose-lipid hypothesis, not a direct cognition claim. Any brain-fog link should be phrased as indirect: users with postprandial fog, metabolic syndrome, insulin resistance, PCOS-style metabolic symptoms, or glucose excursions might report cognitive-state changes because the underlying metabolic state changed.
