# Efficacy of xanomeline in Alzheimer disease: cognitive improvement measured using the Computerized Neuropsychological Test Battery (CNTB)

- PMID: 9876958
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9876958/
- Companion paper: PMID 9109749
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

This companion analysis of the same xanomeline AD trial found a dose-response trend and a 75 mg TID vs placebo signal for the CNTB summary score in the intent-to-treat analysis, plus benefits on simple reaction time and delayed verbal recall. The paper also suggests the CNTB was more sensitive than ADAS-cog in the studied cohort, and the placebo washout period exposed how unstable the apparent gains could be. It strengthens the view that the branch has a real old cognition signal in AD, but also a large cholinergic tolerability penalty.
