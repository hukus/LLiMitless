# Aging 2020 FOXO4-DRI Leydig cells and testosterone in aged mice

- Source type: Peer-reviewed preclinical endocrine aging paper.
- Publication: Aging, 2020-01-20; 12(2):1272-1284.
- PMID: 31959736.
- PMCID: PMC7053614.
- DOI: 10.18632/aging.102682.
- Date checked: 2026-05-06.

This paper tests whether FOXO4-DRI can target senescent Leydig-cell biology relevant to late-onset hypogonadism. The authors report FOXO4 expression in human Leydig cells and connect nuclear FOXO4 localization in older tissue with reduced testosterone synthesis.

In hydrogen peroxide-induced senescent TM3 Leydig cells, FOXO4-DRI disrupted FOXO4-p53 interaction, caused p53 nuclear exclusion, and selectively promoted apoptosis in senescent Leydig cells. In naturally aged male mice, intraperitoneal FOXO4-DRI treatment every other day for three administrations improved the testicular microenvironment and increased testosterone-related readouts 30 days after treatment.

Use in the wiki: disease-model extension source for FOXO4-DRI. It supports aged-mouse endocrine plausibility, but it is not human testosterone therapy evidence and not cognition evidence.
