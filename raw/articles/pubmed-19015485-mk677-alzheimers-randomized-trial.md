# Growth hormone secretagogue MK-677: no clinical effect on AD progression in a randomized trial

- PubMed PMID 19015485, Neurology 2008.
- 563 patients with mild-to-moderate Alzheimer disease were randomized to MK-677 25 mg or placebo daily for 12 months.
- IGF-1 rose substantially, showing target engagement.
- No significant differences emerged on CIBIC-plus, ADAS-Cog, ADCS-ADL, or CDR-sb.
- This is the main negative comparator for the GH-secretagogue branch.
