# Cerebrolysin and early neurorehabilitation in patients with acute ischemic stroke: a prospective, randomized, placebo-controlled clinical study

- PMID: 29362596
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29362596/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Randomized stroke-rehabilitation study in 60 acute ischemic stroke patients. Cerebrolysin plus early physical rehabilitation improved NIHSS, mRS, and Barthel Index versus placebo during the early recovery window, supporting a recovery signal more than a direct cognition-enhancement claim.
