# Lateralised modulation of posterior alpha oscillations by closed loop auditory stimulation during memory retention

Cerebral Cortex 2025 study in healthy adults. Closed-loop auditory stimulation was used to target opposite alpha phases over opposite cortices during a working-memory retention task. The intervention successfully induced asymmetric alpha-frequency modulation between the visual cortices, and the amount of induced frequency lateralisation tracked behavioral asymmetry in task performance.

This is an important adaptive-neuromodulation result because it links a closed-loop physiological manipulation to a behavioral readout during memory retention. The claim is still narrow: it demonstrates phase- and hemisphere-specific controllability, not a general cognition enhancement effect.
