# Transcranial ultrasound stimulation parameters for neurological diseases: a systematic review

- PMID: 40470501
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40470501/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Systematic review of 35 human studies and 664 participants. Reported fundamental frequencies ranged from 220 to 650 kHz, spatial peak pulse-average intensities from 0.5 to 31 W/cm^2, and frequency, pulse repetition frequency, and mechanical index all showed significant effects. The review says TUS studies were administered in clinical settings by trained health professionals and that large trials with multimodal navigation are still needed to standardize dose-response and spatial targeting.
