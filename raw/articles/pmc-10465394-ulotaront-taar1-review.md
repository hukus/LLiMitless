# Notes
- Source: 2023 review of preliminary evidence for ulotaront / SEP-363856 in schizophrenia.
- Identity: TAAR1 agonist with additional 5-HT1A agonism.
- Phase 2 signal: the 4-week acute schizophrenia RCT reported PANSS total improvement versus placebo (effect size 0.45; p < 0.001), and the open-label extension continued improvement and tolerability.
- Caveat: review is sponsor-authored and predates phase 3 DIAMOND failure.
