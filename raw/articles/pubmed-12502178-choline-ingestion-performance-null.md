# Choline ingestion does not modify physical or cognitive performance

- PMID: 12502178
- Journal: Military Medicine
- Year: 2002
- Ingested: 2026-05-04

## Summary

Double-blind crossover study in 13 healthy men testing whether choline ingestion improves physical and cognitive performance after exhaustive load-carriage exercise. Participants completed load-carriage and no-load sessions after choline or placebo. Choline at 50 mg/kg increased plasma choline, but placebo load carriage did not deplete circulating choline. There were no physical performance benefits and no cognitive effects on reaction time, logical reasoning, vigilance, spatial memory, or working memory.

## Notes

- Use as a stress/load-carriage null for generic choline ingestion.
- The null matters because it tests a plausible depletion/state-restoration hypothesis.
- Small male-only military sample; do not overgeneralize beyond this context.
