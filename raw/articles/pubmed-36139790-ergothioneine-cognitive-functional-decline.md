# Low Plasma Ergothioneine Predicts Cognitive and Functional Decline in an Elderly Cohort Attending Memory Clinics

- PMID: 36139790
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36139790/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

2022 longitudinal cohort from memory clinics found lower baseline plasma ergothioneine predicted poorer baseline cognition and faster decline over up to five years, with mediation largely explained by white-matter hyperintensities and brain atrophy. This is strong biomarker/observational evidence, not supplementation proof.
