# Psychiatric Times 2025: DLX-001 phase 1 healthy-volunteer report

Psychiatric Times reported on January 14, 2025 that DLX-001 phase 1 data had been presented at ACNP 2024. The report says the three-part phase 1 study enrolled 106 healthy volunteers and assessed safety, pharmacokinetics, and pharmacodynamic markers.

The report states that DLX-001 was generally safe and well tolerated in healthy volunteers who received at least one dose. It also states that no psychotomimetic, hallucinatory, or dissociative effects were observed at any dose level, and that some brain effects suggested future depression-study relevance.

Interpretation for the wiki: this is a human safety and target-engagement signal, not a cognition-efficacy source. It is useful because it moves zalsupindole beyond pure animal psychoplastogen status, but it does not justify a healthy-adult cognition ranking.
