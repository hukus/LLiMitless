# Donepezil and flight simulator performance

Randomized, double-blind, parallel-group, placebo-controlled Neurology study of 18 licensed aircraft pilots with mean age about 52 years. Donepezil 5 mg/day for 30 days was tested against placebo after simulator training.

The donepezil group showed better retention of complex flight-simulator task performance after 30 days, with p < 0.05. This is one of the cleaner nondemented-adult donepezil cognition signals, but it is tiny, task-specific, and tied to retention of trained complex skills rather than broad memory or brain-fog clearance.

Interpretation for the wiki: useful as a state/training-retention anchor for [[donepezil]], but not enough to promote prescription cholinesterase inhibitors as general healthy-adult nootropics.
