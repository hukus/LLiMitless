# Liafensine ENLIGHTEN ANK3-positive TRD trial

- Source: Wang et al., JAMA Psychiatry 2025, ENLIGHTEN randomized clinical trial.
- Compound identity: liafensine / DB104 / BMS-820836 was described as a potent triple reuptake inhibitor.
- Design: biomarker-guided, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled phase 2b trial at 58 sites from July 2022 through March 2024.
- Enrollment: 1,967 patients were assessed for eligibility; 189 ANK3-positive patients with treatment-resistant depression were randomized.
- Intervention: once-daily oral liafensine 1 mg, liafensine 2 mg, or placebo for 6 weeks.
- Primary endpoint: MADRS total-score change from baseline to day 42.
- Result: combined liafensine groups improved MADRS by -15.4 versus -11.0 for placebo, treatment difference -4.4, 95% CI -7.6 to -1.3, p = 0.006.
- Secondary domains: the article reports statistically significant improvements in secondary endpoints and exploratory superiority for response, remission, and anhedonia.
- Safety: at least one TEAE occurred in 57.3% of liafensine-treated and 56.3% of placebo patients; TEAE discontinuation occurred in 4.0% on liafensine and 14.1% on placebo. One death in the 2 mg group had unknown cause and undetermined relationship.
- Important caveat: JAMA notes liafensine failed in two prior large phase 2b trials in non-biomarker-selected TRD populations, and the present trial is ANK3-positive, biomarker-gated evidence.
- Interpretation for the wiki: liafensine is one of the strongest modern SNDRI antidepressant signals, but the cognitive relevance is mood/TRD/anhedonia-function restoration rather than healthy-adult cognition enhancement.
