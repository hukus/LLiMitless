# Unmasking three blinding indices for randomized controlled trials: comparison and application

- PMID: 41142043
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41142043/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

Tutorial-style paper comparing three blinding indices and applying them to hypothetical and real trial data. Main point: blinding should be estimated and reported with a method that matches the question being asked, because the common indices do not answer exactly the same thing.

## Notes

- Useful as the methodological anchor for a detector page.
- Reinforces that raw guess data should be preserved instead of collapsing everything into a single yes/no blinding claim.
