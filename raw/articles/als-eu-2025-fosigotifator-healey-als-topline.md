# Fosigotifator / ABBV-CLS-7262 HEALEY ALS Regimen F topline results

## Summary

Topline results from HEALEY ALS Platform Trial Regimen F evaluating fosigotifator, also known as ABBV-CLS-7262, an investigational eIF2B activator developed by Calico in collaboration with AbbVie. The primary-dose comparison did not meet the primary endpoint of ALS disease progression measured by ALSFRS-R and survival from baseline through 24 weeks, and it did not meet key secondary endpoints of respiratory function and muscle strength.

An exploratory high-dose group showed nominal muscle-strength differences versus placebo in upper and lower extremities and a possible signal toward slowing respiratory functional decline, but this was exploratory rather than primary-endpoint success. Fosigotifator was reported safe and well tolerated, with treatment-emergent adverse event rates comparable between placebo and combined fosigotifator groups.

This source makes fosigotifator a relevant eIF2B/ISR comparator for the wiki, but it is not cognition evidence and should not be treated as an enhancer.
