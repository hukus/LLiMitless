# A Phase II study of a histamine H3 receptor antagonist GSK239512 for cognitive impairment in stable schizophrenia subjects on antipsychotic therapy

- PMID: 25728831
- Trial registration: NCT01009060
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25728831/

## Summary

2015 phase II exploratory study in 50 stable schizophrenia outpatients. GSK239512 produced a small positive effect size on the CogState Schizophrenia Battery composite, but the confidence interval crossed zero and most MATRICS outcomes were neutral or placebo-favoring. The authors concluded there was no overall beneficial effect for cognitive impairment in this population.
