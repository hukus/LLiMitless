# GlyNAC aging cognition pilot clinical trial

Pilot human clinical trial of glycine plus N-acetylcysteine supplementation in older adults. The abstract reports that older adults had cognitive decline with glutathione deficiency, oxidative stress, mitochondrial dysfunction, inflammation, insulin resistance, endothelial dysfunction, and BDNF deficiency.

GlyNAC for 24 weeks reportedly improved cognition and multiple mechanistic defects, while stopping for 12 weeks led to redevelopment of defects. The design is proof-of-concept and should not be treated as definitive RCT-level evidence.

Use in wiki: GlyNAC is a higher-plausibility redox/mitochondrial branch than NAC alone, but current cognition confidence remains low until larger blinded trials isolate the effect.
