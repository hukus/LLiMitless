# Ampakine (CX717) effects on performance and alertness during simulated night shift work

- PMID: 17955941
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17955941/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

2007 randomized, double-blind parallel study in 48 volunteers under simulated night shift work. CX717 at 200, 400, or 1000 mg did not reverse the performance or alertness decline from shift work, though it reduced slow-wave sleep and appeared generally well tolerated.
