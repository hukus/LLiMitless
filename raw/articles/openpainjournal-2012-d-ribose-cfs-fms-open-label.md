# Open Pain Journal 2012 D-ribose CFS / fibromyalgia open-label study

This 2012 Open Pain Journal article reported a multicenter open-label D-ribose study in patients with fibromyalgia and/or chronic fatigue syndrome. The article was linked to registered study context and followed the same energy-metabolism rationale as earlier CFS/FMS work.

Design: open-label, no placebo control. The study enrolled several hundred patients and assessed symptoms such as energy, sleep, mental clarity, pain intensity, and well-being after D-ribose supplementation.

Result: the article reported significant average improvements in energy, sleep, mental clarity, pain, and well-being. The reported direction is consistent with the 2006 pilot, but the design remains uncontrolled and subjective.

Interpretation for the wiki: this source makes D-ribose relevant enough for a standalone low-confidence page because mental clarity is part of the measured symptom battery. It does not establish healthy-adult cognition improvement or objective neuropsychological benefit.
