# Effects of L-theanine-Caffeine Combination on Sustained Attention and Inhibitory Control Among Children With ADHD

- PMID: 32753637
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32753637/
- DOI: 10.1038/s41598-020-70037-7
- Ingested: 2026-05-03

## Summary

Proof-of-concept randomized four-way repeated-measures crossover trial in five boys aged 8-15 with ADHD. Acute l-theanine, caffeine, their combination, and placebo were compared with fMRI tasks and NIH Cognition Toolbox testing. L-theanine improved total cognition composite versus placebo; caffeine worsened inhibitory-control reaction time; the l-theanine plus caffeine combination improved total cognition composite and Go/NoGo d-prime, with a trend for improved inhibitory control.

## Interpretation

The result is mechanistically interesting but very small. Use it as a caffeine-combination and ADHD-state clue, not as standalone adult nootropic proof.
