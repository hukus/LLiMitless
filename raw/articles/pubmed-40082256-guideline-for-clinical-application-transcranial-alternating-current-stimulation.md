## Summary

Chinese clinical guideline for transcranial alternating current stimulation (tACS). The key ontology point is not efficacy but protocol formalization: tACS now has a clinical guidance layer that treats frequency, montage, current, duration, and session structure as first-order reporting items rather than optional details.
