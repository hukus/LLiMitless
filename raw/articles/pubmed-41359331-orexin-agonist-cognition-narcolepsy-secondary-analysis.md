# Effects of Oveporexton, an Orexin Receptor 2-Selective Agonist, on Cognition in Narcolepsy Type 1: A Secondary Analysis of a Randomized Clinical Trial

- PMID: 41359331
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41359331/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Secondary analysis of a randomized trial in narcolepsy type 1 that specifically examined cognition. Oveporexton improved attention, memory, and executive-function measures, with placebo-adjusted PVT lapse reductions of roughly 8.7 to 10.8 lapses and sizable CPAL improvements. This is the clearest current human cognition signal for the orexin-agonist class, but it remains disorder-context evidence rather than a general healthy-adult enhancement benchmark.
