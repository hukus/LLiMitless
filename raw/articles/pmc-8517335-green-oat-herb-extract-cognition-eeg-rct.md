# Effects of a green oat herb extract on cognitive performance and neurophysiological activity

- Source type: open-access randomized double-blind placebo-controlled study.
- Publication: Frontiers in Neuroscience, 2021.
- Date checked: 2026-05-03.

This acute study tested 800 mg Neuravena green oat herb extract in 20 healthy participants using EEG and attention / executive-function tasks. The active group showed reduced ERP components linked to performance monitoring and attention, faster performance without loss of accuracy, and protection against task-related tiredness.

Use in the wiki: acute green-oat product signal. It should be balanced against older chronic wild-green-oat work showing no sustained cognitive effect.
