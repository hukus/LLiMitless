# Life-Threatening Anaphylaxis due to Cerebrolysin®

- PMID: 39055722
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39055722/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

2024 case report describing a severe anaphylactic reaction after intravenous cerebrolysin. This is an important counterweight to the generally mild adverse-event profile reported in older dementia and stroke trials.

## Notes

Useful for the peptide safety map because it shows that rare but serious hypersensitivity can occur.
