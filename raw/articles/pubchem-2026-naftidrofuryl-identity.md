# Naftidrofuryl identity and pharmacology

PubChem lists naftidrofuryl as CID 4417 with molecular formula C24H33NO3 and synonyms including nafronyl, naphtidrofuryl, and Tridus. The oxalate salt is separately indexed as naftidrofuryl oxalate / nafronyl oxalate.

The PubChem summary routes naftidrofuryl as a drug used in peripheral and cerebral vascular disorders. It describes claimed enhancement of cellular oxidative capacity, spasmolytic activity, and possible 5-HT2 receptor antagonism.

The toxicology summary describes 5-HT2 blockade with inhibition of serotonin-induced vasoconstriction or platelet aggregation, plus improved aerobic glucose metabolism in ischemic conditions. Overdose risks include atrioventricular-conduction disturbance, ventricular arrhythmia, convulsions, acute renal failure, and acute hepatic necrosis. Listed signs and symptoms include nausea, epigastric pain, rash, hepatitis or hepatic failure, and calcium oxalate stones.

Relevance: identity-normalize naftidrofuryl / nafronyl / naftidrofuryl oxalate as a legacy vasoactive drug and keep mechanism claims separate from cognition efficacy.
