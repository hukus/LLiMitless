# The Effect of Suvorexant on Fear Extinction Recall: A Double-Blind Randomised Controlled Pilot Trial in Healthy Individuals

- PMID: 40087982
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40087982/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

Pilot randomized controlled trial in 30 healthy adults testing suvorexant 20 mg, temazepam 20 mg, and placebo for extinction recall. The drug conditions did not differ meaningfully on REM percentage or extinction recall, although greater REM across the sample correlated with better recall. This is useful as a null human cognition readout for the DORA branch and a reminder that REM modulation alone does not guarantee memory gain.
