# Safety profile of Cerebrolysin: clinical experience from dementia and stroke trials

- PMID: 22514795
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22514795/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

2012 review of Cerebrolysin safety in dementia and stroke trials. The abstract reports that adverse reactions were generally mild and transient, with vertigo, agitation, and feeling hot as the most common events.

## Notes

Useful as the main counterweight to the later anaphylaxis case report.
