# Thymosin beta 4 prevents systemic lipopolysaccharide-induced plaque load in middle-age APP/PS1 mice

- PMID: 36878045
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36878045/
- Ingested: 2026-04-30

## Summary

2023 APP/PS1 mouse study. IV Tβ4 given after systemic LPS attenuated sickness behavior, preserved food burrowing, and prevented the LPS-induced increase in amyloid plaque load. It also produced more reactive microgliosis and astrocytic proliferation in WT mice after LPS, so the signal is context-dependent and still preclinical; it is cognition-adjacent rather than a human enhancement result.
