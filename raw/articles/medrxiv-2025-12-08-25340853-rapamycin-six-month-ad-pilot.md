# Evaluation of rapamycin as a neuroprotective treatment in Alzheimer's disease: a six-month single-arm open-label clinical pilot trial

- medRxiv DOI: 10.64898/2025.12.08.25340853
- Posted: 2025-12-09
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

Open-label pilot in 14 people with early-stage AD; 13 completed 26 weeks of oral rapamycin 7 mg weekly. Primary endpoint was FDG PET in pre-specified AD-vulnerable regions.

No significant metabolic or perfusion changes were seen in the primary regions. Exploratory analyses showed increases in FDG SUVR in putamen, insula, and anterior cingulate cortex and thalamic CBF, with no serious adverse events.
