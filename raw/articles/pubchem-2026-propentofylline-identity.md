# PubChem 2026: Propentofylline identity

PubChem identifies propentofylline as CID 4938, molecular formula C15H22N4O3, molecular weight 306.36 g/mol, InChIKey RBQOQRRFDPXAGN-UHFFFAOYSA-N, and connectivity SMILES CCCN1C=NC2=C1C(=O)N(C(=O)N2C)CCCCC(=O)C.

Synonyms and identifiers include propentofylline, propentophylline, Hextol, HWA 285, and CAS 55242-55-2.

PubChem classifies propentofylline as an oxopurine / xanthine derivative, lists WHO ATC N06BC02 under psychostimulants / nootropics / xanthine derivatives, and describes investigation for Alzheimer's disease. Mechanistic summary material frames it as a phosphodiesterase inhibitor and adenosine-uptake inhibitor with purported neuroprotective effects.

Chemical hazard notifications include H302 harmful if swallowed, H312 harmful in contact with skin, H332 harmful if inhaled, and H373 possible organ-damage warning after prolonged or repeated exposure. These are chemical-handling hazard flags, not clinical-dose adverse-event rates.

Interpretation for the wiki: propentofylline is a distinct legacy xanthine / glial-adenosine / PDE dementia-program molecule, not caffeine, theacrine, paraxanthine, or a modern nootropic supplement.
