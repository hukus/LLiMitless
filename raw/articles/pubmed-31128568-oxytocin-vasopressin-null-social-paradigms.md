# Notes
- Source: randomized controlled trial testing oxytocin and vasopressin across multiple social cognitive and behavioral paradigms.
- Outcome: broad null result across the battery.
- Role in branch: a strong counterweight to simple "social hormone" claims.
- Interpretation: effects are context-dependent and may vanish across broader task batteries.
- Safety: not the main focus of the abstract.
