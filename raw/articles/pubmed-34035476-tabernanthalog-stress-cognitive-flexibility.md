# An analog of psychedelics restores functional neural circuits disrupted by unpredictable stress

- PMID: 34035476
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34035476/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

2021 mouse study showing tabernanthalog (TBG) reduced anxiety-like behavior and rescued stress-induced deficits in sensory processing and cognitive flexibility after unpredictable mild stress. The paper also reported regrowth of excitatory neuron dendritic spines. This is relevant to cognition, but the evidence is still rodent-only and state-dependent.
