# Cognitive and subjective effects of psilocybin microdosing: Results from two double-blind placebo-controlled longitudinal trials

- PMID: 41110634
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41110634/
- Ingested: 2026-04-29

## Summary

2026 double-blind placebo-controlled longitudinal trials of psilocybin truffle microdosing. The pooled report found no significant behavioral or subjective benefits versus placebo; some initial signals in social cognition, mood, and self-reported cognitive flexibility disappeared after correction for multiple comparisons. Active microdosing increased negative bodily feelings, while participants remained effectively blinded.
