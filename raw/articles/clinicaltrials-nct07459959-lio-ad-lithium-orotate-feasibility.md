# LiO-AD: Lithium Orotate in Alzheimer's Disease Feasibility, Biomarker Engagement, and Clinical Response

- ClinicalTrials.gov ID: NCT07459959
- Study URL: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07459959
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

This not-yet-recruiting phase 1/2 trial will randomize adults with biomarker-confirmed early AD to lithium orotate or placebo for 9 weeks. It targets feasibility, tolerability, renal/thyroid safety, CSF lithium engagement, and small cognition/neuropsychiatric readouts, with titration from 240 mg/day to 720 mg/day if tolerated.
