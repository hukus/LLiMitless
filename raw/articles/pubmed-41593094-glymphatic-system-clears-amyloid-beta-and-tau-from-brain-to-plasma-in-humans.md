# The glymphatic system clears amyloid beta and tau from brain to plasma in humans

- PMID: 41593094
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41593094/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

2026 human mechanistic paper showing that sleep-active glymphatic transport contributes to the clearance of A beta and tau from the human brain to plasma. The study does not test a cognition intervention, but it strengthens the causal plausibility of the glymphatic pathway that the intervention branch is trying to manipulate. Useful as a mechanistic anchor, not as an enhancement result.
