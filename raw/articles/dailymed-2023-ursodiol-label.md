# DailyMed 2023 ursodiol capsule label

DailyMed identifies ursodiol capsules as human prescription drug labeling with abbreviated-new-drug-application marketing status, updated December 31, 2023. The label describes ursodiol as a bile acid available as 200 mg and 400 mg oral capsules.

The description says ursodiol, also called ursodesoxycholic acid, is a naturally occurring bile acid found in small quantities in normal human bile and in larger quantities in the bile of some mammals. It gives the chemical name 3-alpha,7-beta-dihydroxy-5-beta-cholan-24-oic acid, molecular formula C24H40O4, and molecular weight 392.57.

The label's special note says gallbladder stone dissolution with ursodiol requires months, complete dissolution does not occur in all patients, and stone recurrence within 5 years has been observed in up to 50% of patients who dissolve stones on bile-acid therapy. The clinical-pharmacology section emphasizes liver, bile, and gut-lumen action after absorption and enterohepatic handling.

Interpretation for the wiki: prescription ursodiol is a real hydrophilic bile-acid drug, but its label is liver/bile/gallstone oriented. It is an identity and safety anchor for the UDCA side of [[tudca-ursodiol]], not evidence for healthy-adult cognitive enhancement.
