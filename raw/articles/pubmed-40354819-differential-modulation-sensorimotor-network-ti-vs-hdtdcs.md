# Differential modulation of sensorimotor network between temporal interference and high-definition direct current stimulation

- PMID: 40354819
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40354819/
- Published: 2025-06-10
- Ingested: 2026-04-29

## Summary
Randomized, double-blind, crossover RCT in 40 healthy adults.
TIS at 20 Hz beat frequency and 2 mA targeting left M1 improved sensorimotor-network connectivity and graph-theory efficiency more than HD-tDCS.

## Notes
This is the strongest recent head-to-head mechanistic comparison in the branch, but it is still a network-metric study, not a cognition-endpoint trial.
