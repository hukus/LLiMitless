# H1-antihistamines for primary mast cell activation syndromes

- PMID: 26095756
- Source type: systematic review
- Checked: 2026-05-11
- Ingested: 2026-05-11

## Summary

This systematic review assessed orally administered H1 antihistamines for primary mast cell activation syndromes. It identified 36 potentially relevant studies but only five crossover trials, totaling 71 patients, met eligibility criteria. The included studies were judged at moderate or high risk of bias. One trial compared H1 and H2 antihistamines with oral cromolyn sodium.
