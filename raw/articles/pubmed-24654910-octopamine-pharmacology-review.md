# Physiological functions and pharmacological effects of p-octopamine

2014 review on p-octopamine / norsynephrine physiology, pharmacology, and toxicology. The PubMed abstract notes that octopamine levels can vary in neurological and hepatic diseases, but its normal neurophysiological role is unclear.

The review says no human studies had demonstrated adverse cardiovascular effects after oral p-octopamine administration and no human studies had examined effects on athletic performance, weight loss, or weight management. It calls for animal and human safety and efficacy studies involving oral p-octopamine.

Use in the wiki: formal-evidence absence anchor. Octopamine may be stimulant-adjacent and sports-visible, but the human oral supplement evidence base is thin and not cognition-supportive.
