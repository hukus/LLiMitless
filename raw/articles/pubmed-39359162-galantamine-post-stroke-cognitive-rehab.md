# Galantamine combined with cognitive rehabilitation on post-stroke cognitive impairment: a proof-of-concept study

- PMID: 39359162
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39359162/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Twelve-week randomized, double-blind proof-of-concept study in post-stroke cognitive impairment. Both groups improved with cognitive rehabilitation, but galantamine did not clearly separate from placebo on the primary conversion outcome, so this is best treated as a null-or-ambiguous cholinesterase-inhibitor signal in a clinical recovery phenotype rather than a general enhancement win.

## Notes

- Primary outcome: proportion crossing from vascular cognitive disorder to no-VCD at 12 weeks.
- Secondary outcomes: cognition, mood, quality of life, and N-back fMRI.
- Sample size was very small, so the study is mainly useful as a phenotype-specific comparator and not as decisive efficacy evidence.
