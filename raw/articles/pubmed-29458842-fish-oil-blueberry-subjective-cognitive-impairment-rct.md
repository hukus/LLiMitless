# Cognitive response to fish oil, blueberry, and combined supplementation in older adults with subjective cognitive impairment

- Source type: randomized double-blind placebo-controlled trial.
- Publication: Neurobiology of Aging, 2018.
- Date checked: 2026-05-03.

This 24-week trial studied older adults with cognitive complaints assigned to fish oil, blueberry, both, or control-style comparison. Diet records indicated reduced background EPA/DHA and anthocyanin intake as prescribed, and urinary markers suggested recent blueberry intake in blueberry-supplemented participants.

Fish oil and blueberry groups reported fewer cognitive symptoms. The blueberry group showed improved memory discrimination, and this benefit was associated with urinary anthocyanins reflecting recent blueberry intake. The combined fish-oil plus blueberry condition did not improve cognition as expected.

Use in the wiki: subjective-cognitive-impairment blueberry support and a combination-counterexample. Do not assume omega-3 plus blueberry is additive.
