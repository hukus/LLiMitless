# Effects of an 8-week intake of lysolecithin on cognitive function and concentrations of blood choline and lysophosphatidylcholine: a randomized, double-blinded, placebo-controlled trial

- PMID: 39896163
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39896163/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

Healthy adults aged 40-74 received 480 mg LPC lysolecithin or placebo for 8 weeks. Memory was the primary outcome. The trial found no significant between-group difference in memory or subjective mental function, but lysolecithin slightly increased plasma choline and LPC18:2 without changing inflammatory LPC fractions, reactive oxygen metabolites, or adverse-event rates. This supports tolerability and a limited choline-bioavailability effect, not a broad cognition win.

## Notes

- 23 participants completed a crossover-style comparison.
- All authors were employees of Otsuka Pharmaceutical.
- Use this as a choline-source comparator, not as evidence for nicotine-like acute attention effects.
