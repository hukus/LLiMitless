# Tart cherries and sleep systematic review / meta-analysis

- Source type: open-access systematic review and meta-analysis.
- Publication: Current Sleep Medicine Reports, 2023.
- DOI: 10.1007/s40675-023-00261-w.
- Date checked: 2026-05-03.

This review synthesized clinical studies of Prunus cerasus / tart cherry products for sleep. The authors reported that objective sleep efficiency was significantly higher for cherry versus placebo in the studies suitable for meta-analysis, with effect size 0.63 and low heterogeneity. Objective total sleep time was also higher, with pooled effect size 1.21, but heterogeneity was high.

The review stressed that objective improvements were not consistently mirrored by subjective sleep reports. It also noted small sample sizes, short-term trials, lack of long-term response data, and unresolved mechanism. The authors argued that the melatonin and tryptophan content in typical cherry doses is much lower than common clinical dosing ranges, so those compounds alone are unlikely to explain the effect.

Use in the wiki: sleep-confound and mechanism-context source for tart cherry. This supports a sleep/recovery route, not a direct cognition upgrade.
