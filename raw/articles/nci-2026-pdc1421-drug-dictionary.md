# NCI Drug Dictionary: Radix polygalae extract PDC-1421

NCI Drug Dictionary entry for PDC-1421, also called BLI-1005 / PDC 1421 / PDC-1421 / PDC1421.

The entry defines PDC-1421 as a botanical formulation containing extract of Radix polygalae, the dry root of Polygala tenuifolia Willd. It frames the product as having potential antidepressant and sedative activities and gives a proposed norepinephrine transporter inhibition mechanism.

Relevance: identity-normalization source for separating PDC-1421 from generic Polygala powder, BT-11, tenuifolin, and broader Polygalae Radix traditional-medicine claims.
