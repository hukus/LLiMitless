# Notes
- Source: randomized dose-finding oxytocin study in bvFTD and semantic dementia.
- Doses: 24, 48, or 72 IU intranasal oxytocin twice daily for 1 week.
- Outcome: all doses were reported safe and well tolerated; overall NPI did not significantly change.
- Safety: serum sodium, BP, and heart rate were monitored; no serious AEs were reported.
- Interpretation: short-term safety looked acceptable, but efficacy remained unproven.
