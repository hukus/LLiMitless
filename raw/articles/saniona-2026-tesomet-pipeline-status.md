# Saniona 2026 Tesomet pipeline status

## Captured takeaways

- Saniona's Tesomet page describes Tesomet as an investigational fixed-dose combination of tesofensine and metoprolol.
- The page states that Tesomet phase 2b trials in hypothalamic obesity and Prader-Willi syndrome have been voluntarily paused because of funding limitations, not because of safety or efficacy.
- The page says Saniona holds worldwide rights to Tesomet and is evaluating opportunities to advance it.
- It explains the metoprolol rationale as cardiovascular mitigation for tesofensine-associated heart-rate or blood-pressure increases seen at some doses.
- The page does not present Tesomet as a cognition treatment or nootropic stack.
- Interpretation for the wiki: Tesomet is useful as a status and safety-mitigation clue for tesofensine, but its current paused rare-obesity program does not validate nootropic use.
