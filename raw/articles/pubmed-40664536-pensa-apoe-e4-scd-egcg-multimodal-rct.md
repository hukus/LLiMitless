Title: A multimodal lifestyle intervention complemented with epigallocatechin gallate to prevent cognitive decline in APOE- ɛ4 carriers with Subjective Cognitive Decline: a randomized, double-blinded clinical trial (PENSA study)

PENSA enrolled APOE-ε4 carriers with subjective cognitive decline and tested whether adding EGCG to a multimodal lifestyle intervention improved cognition.

The main result was that the prespecified primary endpoint was not met at 12 months, but exploratory analyses favored the EGCG arm after washout and the trial still showed a meaningful reliability signal for improvement. That makes PENSA a useful example of a biomarker-enriched cohort where the primary average effect was modest, yet the enriched phenotype remains plausibly responsive.

The page is also useful because it warns against overreading enrichment: even when a cohort is biomarker-selected, the primary outcome can still be null.
