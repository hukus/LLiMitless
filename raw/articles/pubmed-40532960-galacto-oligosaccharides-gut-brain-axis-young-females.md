# A randomised controlled trial of the effects of Galacto-Oligosaccharides on the gut brain-axis of young females

- PMID: 40532960
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40532960/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

2025 randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial in 83 healthy females aged 17-25. GOS transiently increased Bifidobacterium and produced brain-chemistry shifts, but trait anxiety did not change at endline. Cognitive effects were narrow: faster reaction times showed up mainly in the high-anxious subgroup, which makes this a phenotype-gated signal rather than a broad prebiotic enhancer.
