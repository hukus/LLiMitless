# NAC in post-concussion syndrome, connectivity and cognitive-affective symptoms

2025 Journal of Head Trauma Rehabilitation randomized controlled trial of N-acetylcysteine in post-concussion syndrome, indexed ahead of print in 2024.

The paper examines resting-state functional connectivity and cognitive-affective symptoms after NAC treatment in a post-concussion population. It is relevant as recovery-state NAC evidence rather than healthy enhancement evidence.

Use in wiki: keep NAC in a recovery / injury-context lane until direct endpoint details are extracted from full text. Do not generalize post-concussion cognitive-affective findings to healthy-adult nootropic use.
