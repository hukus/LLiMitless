# Adjunctive sarcosine plus benzoate improved cognitive function in chronic schizophrenia patients with constant clinical symptoms: A randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial

- PMID: 26691576
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26691576/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

This 12-week trial compared sarcosine 2 g/day plus benzoate 1 g/day against sarcosine alone in chronic schizophrenia. The combination improved cognitive and global functioning, while sarcosine alone did not. The result supports a combination or double-hit glutamatergic hypothesis rather than a sarcosine monotherapy claim.
