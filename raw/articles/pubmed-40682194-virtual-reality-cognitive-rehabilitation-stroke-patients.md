# Randomised control trial of virtual reality in cognitive rehabilitation: effectiveness and near-transfer effect for stroke patients

- PMID: 40682194
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40682194/
- Ingested: 2026-04-29

## Summary

Randomized controlled trial in 132 stroke patients comparing egocentric and allocentric VR-based cognitive training against conventional rehabilitation. The training targeted selective visual attention and short-term visual memory using perspective manipulation.

The trial reports near-transfer effects and broader cognitive gains, with the egocentric training approach intended to test whether a more immersive perspective improves transfer beyond conventional rehab.
