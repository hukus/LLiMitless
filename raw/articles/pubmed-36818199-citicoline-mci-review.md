# Role of Citicoline in Patients With Mild Cognitive Impairment

- PMID: 36818199
- PMCID: PMC9936398
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36818199/
- DOI: 10.1177/26331055231152496
- Ingested: 2026-05-03

## Summary

2023 review focused on citicoline / CDP-choline in mild cognitive impairment, especially vascular cognitive impairment, and related neurodegenerative or vascular conditions. The abstract states that citicoline has shown consistent cognitive-function improvement in MCI, especially vascular-origin MCI, and reports long-term treatment as well tolerated without severe adverse events in the discussed literature.

## Interpretation

Use this as a review-level context source, not as a pooled efficacy estimate. It supports keeping citicoline separate from alpha-GPC, lysolecithin, and generic choline salts, but the strongest direct cognition claims still need trial-level anchoring.
