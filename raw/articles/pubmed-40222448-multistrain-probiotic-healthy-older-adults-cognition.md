# Exploring the acute and chronic effects of a multistrain probiotic supplement on cognitive function and mood in healthy older adults: a randomized controlled trial

- PMID: 40222448
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40222448/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

2025 randomized crossover trial in 30 healthy older adults. Acute dosing sped reaction time on demanding executive trials by about 65 ms, but chronic dosing did not improve memory and only showed small mood-reactivity changes.
