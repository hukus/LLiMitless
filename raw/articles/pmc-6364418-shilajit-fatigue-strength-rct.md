# PMC 6364418: Shilajit supplementation and fatigue-induced strength loss

This Journal of Medicinal Food randomized study tested Shilajit supplementation in recreationally trained men.

Design: 63 active men were randomized to 500 mg/day Shilajit, 250 mg/day Shilajit, or placebo for 8 weeks. The study evaluated muscular strength and fatigue-induced decline after exercise.

Results: the primary signal was physical-performance oriented. High-dose Shilajit helped maintain maximal voluntary isometric contraction after fatigue compared with placebo, and hydroxyproline-related measures suggested connective-tissue or collagen-turnover relevance.

Interpretation for the wiki: this supports a fatigue/physical-performance exposure lane, not a cognition endpoint. Community claims about energy or stamina may map better to fatigue physiology than to direct cognitive enhancement.
