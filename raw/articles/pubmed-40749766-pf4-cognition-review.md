# Research progress on platelet factor 4 in improving cognition

- PMID: 40749766
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40749766/

## Summary

2025 review of platelet factor 4 (PF4) as a cognition-relevant exerkine. The paper frames PF4 as a coagulation-linked chemokine with mouse cognitive benefits and mechanistic plausibility, but it does not present a human PF4 dosing trial.

## Notes

- Best read as a translational review, not intervention proof.
- The cognition signal remains preclinical and should stay in a comparator lane until human dosing data exist.
