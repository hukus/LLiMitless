# Broccoli sprout beverage thyroid safety trial

- Source type: PubMed-indexed randomized clinical safety trial.
- Publication: Food and Chemical Toxicology, 2019.
- PMID: 30735751.
- DOI: 10.1016/j.fct.2019.02.004.
- Date checked: 2026-05-03.

This 12-week randomized trial examined whether a broccoli sprout beverage enriched with sulforaphane and glucoraphanin affected thyroid function or thyroid autoimmunity in 45 female participants.

The rationale was that broccoli sprout-derived products are promising oxidative-stress interventions but have raised questions about thyroidal toxicity and long-term Nrf2 modulation. The trial measured biochemical thyroid function and thyroid autoimmunity before and after 84 days of administration.

Use in the wiki: safety and product-form anchor. Sulforaphane interpretation should include thyroid-context monitoring and distinguish food, glucoraphanin/myrosinase products, and direct sulforaphane-yielding products.
