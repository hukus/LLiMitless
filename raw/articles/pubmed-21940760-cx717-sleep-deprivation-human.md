# Acute sleep deprivation: the effects of the AMPAKINE compound CX717 on human cognitive performance, alertness and recovery sleep

- PMID: 21940760
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21940760/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

2012 randomized crossover in 16 healthy men under extended wakefulness. Prespecified analyses did not show consistent improvement in cognition or alertness at 100, 300, or 1000 mg, though exploratory analysis suggested attention benefit at 1000 mg and recovery sleep was altered.
