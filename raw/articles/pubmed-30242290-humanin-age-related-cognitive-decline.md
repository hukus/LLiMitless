# Humanin Prevents Age-Related Cognitive Decline in Mice and is Associated with Improved Cognitive Age in Humans

- PMID: 30242290
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30242290/
- Ingested: 2026-04-29

## Summary

Humanin improved cognition in aged mice and neuroprotection in cell culture models. In a large older-adult cohort, mitochondrial SNP rs2854128 in the humanin-coding region was associated with lower circulating humanin and accelerated cognitive aging.

## Notes

- Best humanin paper for connecting the peptide to cognition rather than only aging biomarkers.
- Still an association plus mouse-intervention paper, not a human treatment trial.
