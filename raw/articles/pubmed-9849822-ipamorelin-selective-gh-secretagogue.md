# Ipamorelin, the first selective growth hormone secretagogue

- PubMed PMID 9849822, Eur J Endocrinol 1998.
- The paper is mainly a development and pharmacology description, with rat and pituitary data.
- Ipamorelin shows GH-releasing potency and receptor selectivity in preclinical systems.
- This is a mechanism/target-engagement source, not a cognition trial.
- It belongs in the branch because the community often treats ipamorelin as if it were a cognition or recovery enhancer.
