# Cognitive function in a traumatic brain injury hyperbaric oxygen randomized trial.

- PMID: 26403017
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26403017/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Sham-controlled TBI trial with no significant overall cognitive difference between HBOT and sham, though both arms improved.

## Notes

Fifty randomized subjects completed 30 exposures at 2.4 ATA vs 1.3 ATA air sham. Overall cognitive and PTSD scores did not differ significantly between groups, but subgroup analyses suggested possible responder patterns by concussion history and select test components.
