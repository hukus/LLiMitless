# The effects of digital cognitive behavioral therapy for insomnia on cognitive function: a randomized controlled trial.

- PMID: 32128593
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32128593/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Randomized online trial of dCBT-I vs wait-list control in adults with insomnia and cognitive complaints. Primary outcome was self-reported cognitive impairment; secondary outcomes included objective cognitive performance and insomnia symptoms.

## Notes

This is the cleanest direct CBT-I cognition trial in the current scan. It supports the claim that sleep treatment can be evaluated against cognition endpoints, even if it does not establish sequence with neuromodulation.
