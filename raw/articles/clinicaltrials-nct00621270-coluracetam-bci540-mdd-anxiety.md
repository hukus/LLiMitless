# Safety and Effectiveness Study of BCI-540 Versus Placebo in Major Depressive Disorder With Concomitant Anxiety

- Source type: ClinicalTrials.gov registry record.
- ClinicalTrials.gov ID: NCT00621270.
- Date checked: 2026-05-03.

This completed phase 2 trial evaluated BCI-540, also known as coluracetam, in adults with major depressive disorder and concomitant anxiety. The design was randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled, and parallel-group, testing BCI-540 80 mg once daily or 80 mg three times daily versus placebo over six weeks.

Enrollment was 115 participants. The primary outcomes were changes in IDS-C30 depression score and HAM-A anxiety score at week 6. The record shows no posted results in the captured ClinicalTrials.gov view.
