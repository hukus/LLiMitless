# Baduanjin Exercise Improves Cognitive Function in Older Adults With Mild Cognitive Impairment: A Systematic Review and Meta-analysis

- PMID: 39207292
- Published: 2024-09-01
- Journal: Journal of Nervous and Mental Disease
- Evidence layer: systematic review / meta-analysis

## Summary

This meta-analysis evaluated Baduanjin exercise for older adults with mild cognitive impairment. Fourteen randomized controlled studies with 994 participants aged at least 60 years were included. The abstract reported improvements in MoCA global cognition, Wechsler Memory Scale memory decline, Trail Making Test executive function, and Edmonton Frailty Scale frailty, all favoring Baduanjin.

The large pooled effects should be interpreted cautiously because Baduanjin studies often vary in protocol, comparator, and outcome reporting. This source is useful for the traditional mind-body treatment lane, not for claiming a generic healthy-adult nootropic effect.
