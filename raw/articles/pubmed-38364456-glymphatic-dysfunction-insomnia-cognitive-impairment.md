# Glymphatic system dysfunction in middle-aged and elderly chronic insomnia patients with cognitive impairment evidenced by DTI-ALPS

- PMID: 38364456
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38364456/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

2024 cross-sectional insomnia study showing lower DTI-ALPS in insomnia patients with cognitive impairment than in insomnia patients without impairment and healthy controls. MMSE tracked positively with DTI-ALPS (r = 0.78 after adjustment). This supports the biomarker-cognition link but does not test an intervention.
