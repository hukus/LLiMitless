# Kynurenine-3-monooxygenase: a review of structure, mechanism, and inhibitors

- PMID: 26589832
- DOI: 10.1016/j.drudis.2015.11.001
- Journal: Drug Discovery Today (2016)

## Captured takeaways

- KMO is a branch-point enzyme in the kynurenine pathway that converts kynurenine toward 3-hydroxykynurenine.
- The review frames KMO as an attractive drug target for neurodegenerative and neuroinflammatory disease, especially Huntington's disease, Alzheimer's disease, and Parkinson's disease.
- Interpretation for the wiki: useful for target biology and inhibitor landscape, but not itself evidence of cognition benefit in humans.
