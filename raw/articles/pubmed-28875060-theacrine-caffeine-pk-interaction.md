# Assessment of drug-drug interaction potential between theacrine and caffeine in humans

- PMID: 28875060
- PMCID: PMC5582588
- DOI: 10.1089/jcr.2017.0006
- Journal: Journal of Caffeine Research (2017)

## Captured takeaways

- Eight healthy adults received theacrine as TeaCrine 25 or 125 mg, caffeine 150 mg, or theacrine 125 mg plus caffeine 150 mg in a randomized, double-blind crossover study.
- Blood samples were collected over 24 hours and analyzed for theacrine, caffeine, and paraxanthine.
- Caffeine coadministration increased theacrine Cmax and AUC without altering theacrine half-life.
- Theacrine did not affect caffeine or paraxanthine pharmacokinetics.
- Heart rate and systolic/diastolic blood pressure did not differ between treatment groups in this small study.
- The paper reports Compound Solutions funding and author funding relationships.
- Interpretation for the wiki: caffeine can increase theacrine exposure, so stack claims should track caffeine co-use as a pharmacokinetic confound even when acute hemodynamics look unchanged at the tested doses.
