# CE-123 and modafinil CNS distribution and metabolism

- International Journal of Molecular Sciences 2023 paper comparing tissue distribution, central nervous system distribution, and metabolism of S-CE-123 and R-modafinil.
- The abstract and search-indexed summary report substantial hepatic metabolic-stability differences, with S-CE-123 metabolized 9.3-fold faster than R-modafinil in the relevant in vitro comparison.
- The source focuses on pharmacokinetics, CNS distribution, and metabolism rather than cognition endpoints.
- Interpretation for the wiki: CE-123 should not be dose- or duration-equated with modafinil, armodafinil, flmodafinil, or adrafinil. PK/form identity is a first-order gate for any anecdotal comparison.
