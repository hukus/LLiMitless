# The effects of lithium on cognition in humans: A systematic review

- PMID: 41104528
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41104528/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

The review included 30 studies / 727 participants across affective disorders, neurocognitive disorders, and healthy people. Cognitive effects were heterogeneous: some studies favored lithium, others showed decline or mixed results. The authors conclude that definitive isolated cognitive effects remain elusive and that population and affective-state differences matter.
