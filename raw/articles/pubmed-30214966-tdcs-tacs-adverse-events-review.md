# Adverse events of tDCS and tACS: A review

- PMID: 30214966
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30214966/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Review of tDCS and tACS adverse events. Most effects were mild and transient, but the review noted occasional persistent skin lesions with tDCS, rare mania or hypomania in depression, and one pediatric seizure report with unclear causality.

## Notes

Useful for the stimulation safety layer because it distinguishes common local discomfort from rarer serious events.
