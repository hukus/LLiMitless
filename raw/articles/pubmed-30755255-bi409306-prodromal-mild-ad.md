# Evaluation of the efficacy, safety and tolerability of orally administered BI 409306, a novel phosphodiesterase type 9 inhibitor, in two randomised controlled phase II studies in patients with prodromal and mild Alzheimer's disease

- PMID: 30755255
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30755255/
- Ingested: 2026-04-29

## Summary

2019 pair of phase II AD studies in prodromal and mild disease. BI 409306 did not improve the NTB total z-score or the secondary cognitive and functional outcomes, despite a plausible cGMP rationale. The compound was generally well tolerated.

## Key points

- Studies: prodromal AD (N = 128) and mild AD (N = 329).
- Dosing: 10, 25, or 50 mg daily, or 25 mg twice daily, for 12 weeks.
- Primary endpoint: NTB total z-score.
- Result: no significant benefit on the primary endpoint or on secondary outcomes including CDR-SB, ADAS-Cog11, and ADCS-ADL measures.
- Relevance: the cleanest PDE9 AD failure and a strong negative anchor for the branch.
