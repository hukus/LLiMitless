# Chronotype, cognitive outcomes, and neural dynamics: recent evidence and potential mechanisms with implications for perioperative period

- PMID: 41113437
- Published: 2025-10
- Study type: review
- Key point: the review frames chronotype as a modifier of cognitive outcomes and neural dynamics, with perioperative circadian misalignment and postoperative cognitive dysfunction as a central application area.
- Why it matters: this is a synthesis source, not a direct intervention trial, but it reinforces chronotype as a plausible timing variable for perioperative and sleep-sensitive cognition work.
