# The Efficacy and Safety of Transcranial Photobiomodulation for Mild Cognitive Impairment Due to Alzheimer's Disease: A Randomized, Double-Blind, Sham-Controlled Study

- PMID: 40939031
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40939031/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Small sham-controlled home-use study in MCI due to AD. Reported statistically significant K-MoCA improvement at week 13 and no serious adverse events. The result is promising but still limited by sample size and short follow-up.
