# TAK-925 (Danavorexton), an Orexin Receptor 2 Agonist, Reduces Opioid-induced Respiratory Depression and Sedation without Affecting Analgesia in Healthy Men

- PMID: 39804333
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39804333/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Single-center randomized crossover phase 1 trial in healthy men using a remifentanil respiratory-depression model. Danavorexton reduced opioid-induced respiratory depression and sedation without reducing analgesia. The result supports orexin-agonist arousal rescue, but it is not a direct healthy-cognition enhancement trial.
