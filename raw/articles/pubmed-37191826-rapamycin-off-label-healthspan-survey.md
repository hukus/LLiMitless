# Evaluation of off-label rapamycin use to promote healthspan in 333 adults

- PMID: 37191826
- DOI: 10.1007/s11357-023-00818-1
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

Survey of 333 adults with off-label rapamycin use and 172 non-users. Most users reported healthy longevity / anti-aging as the reason for taking rapamycin, and weekly dosing was the dominant pattern.

This is a strong community-signal source for off-label longevity use, but it is still self-selected survey data and not cognition efficacy evidence.
