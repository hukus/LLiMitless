# Nocebo expectations rather than placebo expectations affect topical pain relief: A randomized clinical trial

- PMID: 38733772
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38733772/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

Randomized clinical trial in cancer survivors showing that high nocebo expectations reduced pain improvement and increased unwanted adverse events, regardless of treatment arm. This is a strong reminder that expectation can shape both efficacy and tolerability reports, and that adverse-event logs need to include belief context.

## Notes

- Not a cognition trial, but highly relevant to self-study side-channel detection.
- Supports separating true pharmacology from expectation-driven symptom amplification.
