# Cognitive outcomes after tadalafil treatment in patients with cerebral small vessel disease: ETLAS-2 sub-study

- PMID: 41332889
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41332889/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

2025 preregistered ETLAS-2 sub-study focused specifically on cognition in cerebral small vessel disease. The report found a slower simple reaction time in the tadalafil group, but did not establish a broad cognitive benefit.

## Key points

- Design: randomized placebo-controlled double-blind parallel sub-study.
- Outcomes: processing speed, attention, memory, executive function.
- Signal: simple reaction time was slower in the tadalafil group at follow-up.
- Interpretation: the most recent PDE5 cognition readout is not a clean enhancement signal.
