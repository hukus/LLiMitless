# Prolonged stimulation of growth hormone and IGF-I secretion by CJC-1295 in healthy adults

- PubMed PMID 16352683, J Clin Endocrinol Metab 2006.
- Two randomized, placebo-controlled, double-blind ascending-dose trials in healthy adults.
- CJC-1295 produced sustained dose-dependent increases in GH and IGF-I for days to weeks.
- The paper supports pharmacology and safety/tolerability, not cognition.
- It is useful as a target-engagement note for long-acting GHRH analogs such as CJC-1295 and Mod-GRF analog logic.
