# Systematic review of clinical trials assessing pharmacological properties of Salvia species on memory and cognitive impairment

- Source type: systematic review of clinical trials.
- Publication: CNS Neuroscience & Therapeutics, 2014.
- Date checked: 2026-05-03.

This review summarized clinical trials of Salvia officinalis and Salvia lavandulaefolia for cognition, memory, and Alzheimer's disease. It included eight clinical studies: six in healthy subjects and two in Alzheimer's disease or cognitive impairment contexts.

The review concluded that Salvia preparations showed beneficial cognitive effects in healthy subjects and dementia/cognitive impairment, but emphasized methodological limitations, heterogeneous preparations, incomplete herbal-product details, and the need for higher-quality trials.
