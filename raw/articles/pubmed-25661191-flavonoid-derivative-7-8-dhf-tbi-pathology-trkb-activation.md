# Flavonoid derivative 7,8-DHF attenuates TBI pathology via TrkB activation

- PMID: 25661191
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25661191/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Notes

2015 rodent traumatic brain injury study. Daily 7,8-DHF at 5 mg/kg i.p. for 7 days improved Barnes-maze memory and plasticity-associated markers, and the effect was blocked by a TrkB antagonist. The paper also reports normalization of several energy-homeostasis markers after injury.

## Relevance

- Strongest cognition-adjacent animal signal I found for 7,8-DHF.
- Still a post-injury recovery model, not healthy-adult enhancement and not human evidence.
- Good comparator for the rescue-vs-enhancement split in the wiki.
