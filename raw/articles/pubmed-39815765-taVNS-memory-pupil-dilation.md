# Stimulate to Remember? The Effects of Short Burst of Transcutaneous Vagus Nerve Stimulation (taVNS) on Memory Performance and Pupil Dilation

- PMID: 39815765
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39815765/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Randomized within-subject study in 24 younger adults using 3 s event-related taVNS bursts during emotional encoding. Real stimulation selectively increased memory for negative events, but sham and real stimulation both increased pupil dilation and memory over time, suggesting a strong sensory and attention confound.

## Notes

- Sham-controlled, single-blind, within-subject, counterbalanced design.
- Real versus sham used 5 mA, 25 Hz in the emotional memory task.
- Short bursts were also tested at 3 mA and 5 mA with 10 Hz and 25 Hz in resting-state blocks.
- The authors interpret the memory effect cautiously because sensory stimulation itself affected pupil dilation and performance.
