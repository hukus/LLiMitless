# GSK372475 negative MDD trials

- Source: Learned et al., Journal of Psychopharmacology 2012.
- Compound identity: GSK372475 was described as a triple reuptake inhibitor with approximately equipotent inhibition of serotonin, norepinephrine, and dopamine transporters.
- Design: two randomized, placebo- and active-controlled, double-blind studies in outpatients aged 18-64 with major depressive disorder.
- Randomization: placebo, GSK372475 1-2 mg/day, or active control. Study 1 active control was venlafaxine XR 150-225 mg/day; Study 2 active control was paroxetine 20-30 mg/day.
- Result: GSK372475 did not significantly differ from placebo on key efficacy endpoints in either study, while active controls showed significant antidepressant activity.
- Safety: common adverse effects included dry mouth, headache, insomnia, and nausea. Sleep, anxiety-related, gastrointestinal, and tachycardia events were more frequent with GSK372475 than placebo.
- Vital signs: heart rate and sitting blood pressure increases were greater for GSK372475 than placebo or active controls.
- Completion: completion rates were lower for GSK372475 than placebo or active controls.
- Interpretation for the wiki: GSK372475 is a central negative-class anchor showing that balanced triple reuptake can fail antidepressant efficacy while adding cardiovascular and tolerability burden.
