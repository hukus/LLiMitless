# Acute endurance exercise stimulates circulating levels of mitochondrial-derived peptides in humans

- PMID: 34351816
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34351816/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Randomized human exercise study found acute endurance exercise increased circulating mitochondrial-derived peptides, especially humanin, and MOTS-c showed a trend toward increase. Resistance exercise did not show the same pattern.

## Notes

This makes MOTS-c a real exercise-responsive human biomarker, but it still does not establish a cognition endpoint or a supplement/intervention protocol.
