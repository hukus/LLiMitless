# Peptide geroprotector from the pituitary gland inhibits rapid aging of elderly people: results of 15-year follow-up

- PMID: 22451889
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22451889/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

Randomized comparative follow-up in coronary patients treated with repeated epithalamin courses reported slower cardiovascular aging, more favorable melatonin and metabolism readouts, and lower mortality. The paper supports a geroprotective claim, but it still does not supply direct cognitive-outcome data.
