# ReNEW: Phase 3 Study of Efficacy, Safety & Pharmacokinetics of Subcutaneous Injections of Elamipretide in Subjects With Dry Age-Related Macular Degeneration

- ClinicalTrials.gov ID: NCT06373731
- Registry: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06373731
- Ingested: 2026-04-29

## Summary

Phase 3 randomized, double-masked, placebo-controlled trial of 40 mg daily subcutaneous elamipretide in dry AMD. The registry was active/not recruiting in the latest snapshot, with no results posted.

## Notes

- Relevant for elamipretide disease scope, not for cognition.
- The primary endpoint is retinal structure/photoreceptor loss, not cognitive performance.
