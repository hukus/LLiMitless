# NIH summary of ibudilast progressive MS trial

2018 NIH/NINDS release summarizing a phase 2 trial of ibudilast in more than 250 participants with progressive multiple sclerosis. The trial found ibudilast was better than placebo in slowing brain shrinkage/atrophy. Main side effects were gastrointestinal symptoms and headaches.

Interpretation for the wiki: this is a serious human neuroinflammation/neurodegeneration signal, but it is an imaging/neurodegenerative-disease outcome rather than direct brain-fog clearance or healthy cognition evidence.
