# Closed-loop modulation of remote hippocampal representations with neurofeedback

bioRxiv preprint later published in Neuron. In rats, the authors built a neurofeedback system that rewarded expression of remote hippocampal representations. Animals learned to generate specific spatial representations that often jumped directly to the experimenter-defined target location.

This is not a direct human cognition enhancement trial, but it is a key proof-of-principle for closed-loop memory-related neurofeedback. It shows that retrieval-related neural activity can be shaped without sensory cues or a behavioral report.
