# Foundation model embeddings for quantitative tumor imaging biomarkers

PMID: 40502795
PMCID: PMC12154149
Published: 2025

Abstract focus: TumorImagingBench evaluates ten medical-imaging foundation models across six public datasets and explicitly compares endpoint prediction, robustness to common variability, saliency-based interpretability, and similarity between learned embeddings. The main lesson is that model choice changes robustness and interpretability, so benchmark accuracy alone is not enough to choose a biomarker extractor.
