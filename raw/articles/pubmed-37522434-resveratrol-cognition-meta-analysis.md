# Effect of resveratrol on cognitive functions in older adults: systematic review and meta-analysis

- Source type: PubMed-indexed systematic review and meta-analysis abstract.
- Publication: Nutricion Hospitalaria, 2023.
- Date checked: 2026-05-03.

This systematic review/meta-analysis included six studies of resveratrol and cognitive function in older adults. It found no significant effect on delayed memory, immediate memory, working memory, or processing speed.

Use in the wiki: negative pooled counterweight for resveratrol. It lowers the signal from "promising pilot" to "low and inconsistent."
