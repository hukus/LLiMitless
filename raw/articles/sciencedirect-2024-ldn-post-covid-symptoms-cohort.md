# Low-dose naltrexone improves post-COVID condition symptoms cohort

2024 Clinical Therapeutics retrospective cohort from a post-COVID clinic comparing low-dose naltrexone, amitriptyline, duloxetine, and physical therapy. The study included 108 consecutive patients and evaluated improvements in fatigue, pain, dyspnea, and brain fog from the electronic health record.

The adjusted relative hazard of improvement for low-dose naltrexone was 5.04 versus physical therapy alone (95% CI 1.22-20.77; p = 0.02). Fatigue and pain improved in patients taking LDN; only fatigue improved in patients taking amitriptyline. The abstract does not establish a controlled brain-fog-specific effect.

Interpretation for the wiki: useful observational signal for post-COVID symptoms, but confounding and non-randomized treatment assignment keep confidence low.
