## Summary

WADA's prohibited-list pages explicitly name MOTS-c under S4.4.1 metabolic modulators and thymosin-beta-4 derivatives such as TB-500 under S3 growth factors and related substances. That makes both compounds a sports-regulatory concern even when the clinical evidence base is thin.
