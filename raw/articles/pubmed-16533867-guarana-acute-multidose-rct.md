# Multi-dose acute behavioral effects of guarana in humans

- Source type: double-blind counterbalanced placebo-controlled human study.
- Publication: Journal of Psychopharmacology, 2007.
- Date checked: 2026-05-03.

This n=26 acute study tested four doses of a standardized guarana extract, PC-102: 37.5 mg, 75 mg, 150 mg, and 300 mg. Outcomes included the Cognitive Drug Research computerized battery and Bond-Lader mood scales.

The study reported improved secondary memory performance and higher alert/content mood ratings. Lower doses, especially 37.5 mg and 75 mg, produced more positive cognitive effects than higher doses, which argues against a simple monotonic caffeine-dose explanation.

Use in the wiki: older positive guarana-alone anchor. Treat as small, acute, product-specific evidence; it supports tracking guarana but does not defeat later caffeine-confounding and pooled-effect cautions.
