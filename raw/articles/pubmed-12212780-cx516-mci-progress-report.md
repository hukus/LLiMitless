# Randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled international clinical trial of the Ampakine CX516 in elderly participants with mild cognitive impairment: a progress report

- PMID: 12212780
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12212780/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

2002 progress report for an international MCI trial of CX516. The paper describes the rationale, design, and outcome measures, but it is not a final efficacy report and does not establish a cognitive benefit.
