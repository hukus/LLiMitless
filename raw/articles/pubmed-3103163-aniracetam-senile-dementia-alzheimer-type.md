# Senile dementia of the Alzheimer type treated with aniracetam: a new nootropic agent

- PMID: 3103163
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/3103163/
- Ingested: 2026-04-30

## Summary

1987 randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial in 44 patients with senile dementia of the Alzheimer type. Aniracetam 1 g/day was given for 3 months. Cognitive tests improved in both arms, but the improvement occurred in placebo as well as aniracetam-treated patients, and clinical evaluation found no efficacy difference between groups. Confusion interrupted treatment in 4 aniracetam patients and 1 placebo patient.
