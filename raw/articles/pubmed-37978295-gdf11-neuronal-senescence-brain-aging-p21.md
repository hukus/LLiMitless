# GDF11 slows excitatory neuronal senescence and brain ageing by repressing p21

- PMID: 37978295
- DOI: 10.1038/s41467-023-43292-1
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37978295/
- Ingested: 2026-05-04

## Summary

Nature Communications 2023 mechanistic study. GDF11 was predominantly expressed in excitatory neurons in adult mouse, marmoset, and human brain tissue. Selective deletion of GDF11 in mouse post-mitotic excitatory neurons induced senescence and hyperexcitability, reduced dendritic structure and synaptic input, impaired object recognition memory, and shortened lifespan. Mechanistically, GDF11 deletion induced neuronal senescence through Smad2-driven p21 expression. This supports endogenous GDF11 as a brake on excitatory-neuron senescence, but it is not an exogenous human cognition intervention.
