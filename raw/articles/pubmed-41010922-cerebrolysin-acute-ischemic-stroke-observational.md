# Evaluating the Effect of Cerebrolysin as an Adjuvant to Standard Therapy in Patients with Acute Ischemic Stroke: A Prospective Observational Study

- PMID: 41010922
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41010922/
- Ingested: 2026-04-30

## Summary

This prospective observational study included 143 adults with acute ischemic stroke. Cerebrolysin 30 mL IV daily for 14 days was associated with larger NIHSS improvement and better Barthel Index gains than standard therapy alone at day 14, but the design was non-randomized and the follow-up was short, so it supports recovery-first plausibility rather than causal proof of cognition benefit.
