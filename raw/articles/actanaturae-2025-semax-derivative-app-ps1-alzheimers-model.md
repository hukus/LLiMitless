# Acta Naturae 2025 Semax and derivative in APP/PS1 Alzheimer's model

- Source: https://actanaturae.ru/2075-8251/article/view/27808
- Article: The potential of the peptide drug Semax and its derivative for correcting pathological impairments in the animal model of Alzheimer's disease
- Journal: Acta Naturae, Vol. 17, No. 4, 2025, pages 110-120
- Published: 2025-12-04
- DOI: 10.32607/actanaturae.27808

## Summary

Radchenko and colleagues tested Semax and a related heptapeptide derivative in APPswe/PS1dE9/Blg transgenic mice. The animals received intranasal Semax or derivative at 50 ug/kg every other day for one month, followed by a one-month washout before behavioral testing in the second subgroup.

The study used open field, novel object recognition, and Barnes maze behavioral tests, plus histological analysis of amyloid inclusions in cortex and hippocampus. The article reports that Semax and its derivative improved cognitive-function measures in mice and reduced amyloid inclusions in brain tissue.

## Research-use read

This is a modern preclinical Semax cognition / Alzheimer's-model source. It strengthens the animal and disease-model branch, but it does not provide human cognition efficacy, healthy-adult enhancement evidence, or dosing guidance for people.
