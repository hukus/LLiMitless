# Communications Biology 2025 FOXO4-DRI keloid senescent fibroblasts

- Source type: Peer-reviewed human tissue / cell-model disease-mechanism paper.
- Publication: Communications Biology, 2025-02-24; 8:299.
- PMCID: PMC11850796.
- DOI: 10.1038/s42003-025-07738-0.
- Date checked: 2026-05-06.

This paper studies senescence and apoptosis resistance in keloid fibroblast biology. It reports more senescent fibroblast features in keloids, increased p16 and beta-galactosidase-positive cells, and upregulated p53-serine15 phosphorylation.

FOXO4-DRI promoted apoptosis and reduced G0/G1 phase cells in pro-senescence keloid organ culture and fibroblast models, accompanied by nuclear exclusion of p53-pS15. The paper frames FOXO4-DRI as a potential way to target senescence and apoptosis resistance in keloids.

Use in the wiki: human tissue/cell disease-model extension. It supports a keloid mechanistic hypothesis, not validated clinical treatment or cognition evidence.
