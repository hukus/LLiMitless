# Nighttime Safety of Daridorexant: Evaluation of Responsiveness to an External Noise Stimulus, Postural Stability, Walking, and Cognitive Function

- PMID: 39641404
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39641404/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

Randomized, placebo-controlled crossover study in 36 healthy adults evaluating daridorexant 25 mg and 50 mg overnight. Daridorexant did not meaningfully alter auditory awakening threshold or most cognitive measures, produced only a modest increase in body sway, and preserved practical nighttime function overall. This supports the idea that some DORAs can be bedtime sleep aids without obvious next-night cognitive crash, but it does not support enhancement.
