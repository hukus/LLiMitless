# Adverse effects of oral niacin and nicotinamide

PubMed 40999226: 2025 overview of reviews on adverse effects of oral niacin and nicotinamide.

Key capture:

- Scope: overview of review evidence on oral niacin and nicotinamide adverse effects.
- Result shape: the review identified adverse-effect categories including skin flushing, skin rash, gastrointestinal symptoms, liver dysfunction, fatigue, sedation, and energy abnormalities.
- Practical split: nicotinic acid / niacin is more strongly associated with flushing, while nicotinamide / niacinamide is often used to avoid flushing, but high-dose nicotinamide is not safety-free.
- Interpretation is dose- and indication-sensitive. Pharmacologic lipid doses, high-dose dermatology / oncology / Alzheimer protocols, ordinary dietary intakes, fortified-food exposure, and low-dose multivitamin exposure should not be collapsed.
