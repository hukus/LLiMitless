# ClinicalTrials.gov NCT06437626: Mexidol MIR ischemic stroke results

ClinicalTrials.gov record NCT06437626 is the MIR study: a Pharmasoft-sponsored international multicenter phase 3 randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled, parallel trial of sequential Mexidol therapy in acute and early recovery ischemic stroke. The registry lists 304 actual participants, study start 2019-11-18, completion 2023-08-18, results first posted 2025-12-08, and last update posted 2025-12-30.

Intervention: Mexidol IV 500 mg twice daily for 10 days, then Mexidol FORTE 250 mg orally three times daily for 60 days. Comparator: matched placebo IV and tablets.

Primary outcome: change from baseline in modified Rankin Scale at day 71. The registry reports -2.45 for Mexidol versus -2.01 for placebo. Secondary disability outcomes also favored Mexidol in the registry tables, including mRS 0-1 at day 71 reported as 85 versus 57 and mRS >3 reported as 22 versus 39.

Secondary cognition/state outcomes: MoCA change from baseline at day 71 was +4 (range/interval 2 to 6) for Mexidol and +3 (1 to 6) for placebo. NIHSS change was -7 in both groups; Rivermead Mobility Index was 10 versus 9; HADS anxiety and depression changes were -2 versus -1.

Safety: adverse-event tables report deaths in 2/152 Mexidol participants and 1/152 placebo participants, serious adverse events in 6/152 versus 4/152, and other adverse events in 36/152 versus 39/152. Serious events included pneumonia and recurrent/related cerebrovascular events; ordinary events included GI, blood-pressure, liver-enzyme, headache, insomnia, tachycardia, and other system events.

Interpretation for the wiki: MIR is a stroke-recovery and disability trial, not a cognitive enhancement trial. The small MoCA difference is secondary and should be treated as clinical-state support, not broad nootropic evidence.
