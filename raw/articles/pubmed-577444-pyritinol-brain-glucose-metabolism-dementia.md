# Pyritinol brain blood flow and oxidative metabolism in dementia

PubMed-indexed 1977 study of pyritinol-HCl effects on cerebral blood flow and oxidative metabolism in 87 patients with organic brain disorders.

Design: 45 participants received pyritinol 900 or 1000 mg/day; 42 controls received intravenous 5% laevulose. Average study duration was about 3 weeks. Outcomes included cerebral blood flow, oxygen and glucose consumption, and CO2/lactate output.

Results: the abstract reports that pyritinol-HCl usually significantly improved previously disturbed cerebral glucose metabolism. It did not find an effect on disturbed cerebral blood flow or pathologically changed cerebral oxygen consumption.

Relevance: mechanism-adjacent legacy clinical physiology source, not a direct cognition-efficacy trial.
