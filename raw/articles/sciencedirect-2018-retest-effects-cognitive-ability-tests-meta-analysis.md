# Retest Effects in Cognitive Ability Tests: A Meta-Analysis

- Source: Intelligence, 2018
- DOI: 10.1016/j.intell.2018.01.003
- Ingested: 2026-05-03

## Summary

Meta-analysis of retest effects in cognitive-ability tests. The key practical result is that retesting can produce meaningful score gains, with larger gains on the first retest and stronger effects for shorter intervals and more practice-like conditions. The publisher summary reports increases of up to about half a standard deviation on the third test administration.

## Notes

This is the central evidence for a measured-IQ proposal: one can plausibly move an IQ score substantially through repeated exposure and test familiarity. It is not evidence that latent general intelligence has increased by the same amount.
