# Efficacy of Cerebrolysin Treatment as an Add-On Therapy to Mechanical Thrombectomy in Patients with Acute Ischemic Stroke Due to Large Vessel Occlusion in Anterior Circulation: Results of a 3-Month Follow-up of a Prospective, Open Label, Single-Center Study

- PMID: 40325343
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40325343/
- Ingested: 2026-04-30

## Summary

This open-label single-center study compared 50 Cerebrolysin-treated thrombectomy patients with 50 historical controls. Cerebrolysin 30 mL IV within 8 hours of onset and continued through day 21, with a second cycle in the recovery window, was associated with higher 90-day functional independence, lower symptomatic intracranial hemorrhage, and better Barthel and NIHSS outcomes. The design is not blinded and the comparator is historical, so the signal is supportive but not definitive.
