# Safety and Efficacy of Repeated Low-Dose LSD for ADHD Treatment in Adults: A Randomized Clinical Trial

- PMID: 40105807
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40105807/
- Ingested: 2026-04-30

## Summary

2025 multicenter, double-blind, placebo-controlled phase 2A RCT in 53 adults with ADHD. LSD 20 micrograms twice weekly for 6 weeks was physically safe and generally well tolerated, but it was not better than placebo on the primary ADHD symptom outcome (AISRS). This is a disorder-context null, not a general cognition-enhancement win.
