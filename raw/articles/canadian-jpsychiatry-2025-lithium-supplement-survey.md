# A Survey Exploring People's Experiences With Lithium Bought as a Supplement

- DOI: 10.1177/07067437251328282
- Journal page: https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/07067437251328282
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

This opportunistic cross-sectional survey included 211 adults taking over-the-counter lithium supplements. The most common form was lithium aspartate, but lithium orotate and ionic lithium were also common. Respondents most often reported perceived benefits for cognition, anxiety, and mood, while side effects and withdrawal phenomena were more common than expected. The study is useful as a community-signal map, not as efficacy proof.
