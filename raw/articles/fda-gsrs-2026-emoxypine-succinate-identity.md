# FDA GSRS: Emoxypine succinate identity

FDA's Global Substance Registration System / UNII record lists EMOXYPINE SUCCINATE with UNII 2R985002CT, formula C8H11NO.C4H6O4, and preferred substance name EMOXYPINE SUCCINATE. Synonyms and mappings include Mexidol, Mexicor, Mexiprim, Emicidin / Emicidine, methylethylpiridinol succinate, and the succinate salt chemical names.

The related FDA GSRS emoxypine base record lists EMOXYPINE with UNII V247P5H4E1, formula C8H11NO, and synonyms including emoxipin, emoxipine, emoxypin, ethylmethylhydroxypyridine, and methylethylpiridinol.

Regulatory interpretation: the GSRS page explicitly notes that UNII availability does not imply regulatory review or approval. For the wiki, GSRS supports identity mapping only; it does not prove U.S. drug approval, supplement legality, or clinical efficacy.
