# Oligodendrocyte-encoded lactate dehydrogenase A couples glycolysis to remyelination via protein lactylation

- PMID: 41844160
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41844160/
- Ingested: 2026-04-30

## Summary

Neuron paper showing that oligodendrocyte LDHA and lactate production support remyelination, and that lactate or LDHA overexpression can improve remyelination in lesion models. Conditional LDHA loss worsened dysmyelination, and the mechanism involved lactylation-linked gene silencing and myelin restoration. This is still preclinical, but it strengthens the lactate-shuttle story beyond simple fuel supply into white-matter repair and epigenetic signaling.
