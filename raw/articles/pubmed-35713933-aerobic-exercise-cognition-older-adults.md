# Aerobic exercise training and neurocognitive function in cognitively normal older adults

- PMID: 35713933
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35713933/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

One-year randomized trial in sedentary but otherwise healthy older adults. Aerobic exercise increased fitness, cognitive composite scores rose over time in both exercise and stretching groups, and there was no between-group cognitive difference.

## Notes

The main takeaway is that exercise may help state-dependent performance and fitness, but the active-control design weakens a simple exercise-causes-cognition claim.
