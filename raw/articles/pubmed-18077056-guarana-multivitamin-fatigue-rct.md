# Multivitamin-mineral supplement with guarana during sustained mental demand

- Source type: double-blind randomized placebo-controlled parallel-group trial.
- Publication: Appetite, 2008.
- Date checked: 2026-05-03.

This study tested an acute vitamin/mineral/guarana supplement versus placebo in 129 healthy young adults aged 18-24. Participants completed a 10-minute Cognitive Demand Battery with Serial 3s, Serial 7s, Rapid Visual Information Processing, and a mental-fatigue rating, repeated six times after dosing.

The active vitamin/mineral/guarana product improved RVIP speed and accuracy throughout the post-dose sustained-demand assessment and attenuated the rise in mental fatigue. Because the product combined micronutrients with guarana, it is not a clean guarana-alone result.

Use in the wiki: sustained-mental-demand combination-product anchor. It supports guarana visibility in energy/fatigue products but should not be treated as isolated guarana efficacy.
