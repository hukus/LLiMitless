# Mesenchymal stem cell-derived exosomes: Shaping the next era of Alzheimer's disease treatment

- PMID: 40951706
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40951706/
- Publication date: 2025-08-26

## Summary

Review of MSC-derived exosomes for AD. The paper highlights proposed mechanisms including amyloid clearance, neuroinflammation modulation, and neural regeneration, while also flagging production standardization, safety, and long-term efficacy as major barriers.

## Notes

This review is useful for mechanistic claims and translational caveats, not for upgrading the branch to evidence of clinical benefit.
