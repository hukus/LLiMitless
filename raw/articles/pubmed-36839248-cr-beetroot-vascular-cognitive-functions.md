# Caloric Restriction (CR) Plus High-Nitrate Beetroot Juice Does Not Amplify CR-Induced Metabolic Adaptation and Improves Vascular and Cognitive Functions in Overweight Adults: A 14-Day Pilot Randomised Trial

- PMID: 36839248
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36839248/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary
In overweight and obese adults, CR plus beetroot juice improved microvascular flux, NO-dependent endothelial activity, and Trail-Making Test B versus CR alone over 14 days. Body composition, resting energy expenditure, and blood pressure did not differ. Small pilot; signal looks vascular plus executive-function-specific rather than broadly cognitive.
