# The neuro- and psychophysiological effects of bromantane

- PMID: 10998997
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10998997/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

Small healthy-volunteer study of bromantane found EEG and psychophysiological changes consistent with a more vigilant action state, but not a dramatic subjective or broad cognitive shift in untired participants. The paper fits bromantane better as a state-adjacent stimulant/actoprotective lead than as a proven healthy-adult nootropic.
