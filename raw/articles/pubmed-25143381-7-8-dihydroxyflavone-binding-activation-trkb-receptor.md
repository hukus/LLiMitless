# Biochemical and biophysical investigation of the brain-derived neurotrophic factor mimetic 7,8-dihydroxyflavone in the binding and activation of the TrkB receptor

- PMID: 25143381
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25143381/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Notes

2014 biochemical study showing direct TrkB binding and activation by 7,8-DHF, with prolonged receptor phosphorylation relative to BDNF in the assay system. It strengthens the mechanistic case that 7,8-DHF is not just a loose phenotypic mimic but a bona fide TrkB ligand in preclinical systems.

## Relevance

- Key mechanism paper for the branch.
- Highlights that neuronal maturation and receptor glycosylation may matter for the response.
- Useful when separating signal plausibility from actual human efficacy.
