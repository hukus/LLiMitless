# Testosterone TEAAM cognition trial
- PMID: 27377542
- 308 older men with low or low-to-normal testosterone were randomized to 7.5 g of 1% testosterone gel daily vs placebo for 3 years.
- Cognitive testing covered visuospatial ability, verbal fluency, verbal memory, manual dexterity, and attention/executive function.
- Key result: no between-group differences on any major cognitive domain; cognitive change also did not track testosterone or estradiol change.
- Branch readout: long-duration TRT did not improve cognition in this phenotype.
