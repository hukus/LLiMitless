# Five-year effects of cognitive training in individuals with mild cognitive impairment

- PMID: 39246830
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39246830/
- Ingested: 2026-04-29

## Summary

Randomized single-blind follow-up of 145 older adults with mild cognitive impairment. Participants had previously been assigned to MEMO+ cognitive training, an active psychosocial control, or no-contact. At the 5-year follow-up, the cognitive-training group showed smaller decline in delayed memory and maintained MoCA scores versus controls, with no clear group differences in strategy use or daily living difficulties. This is a strong long-horizon durability signal for structured cognitive training.
