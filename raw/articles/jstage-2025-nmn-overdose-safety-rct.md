# Placebo-Controlled Trial of Safety of Overdose NMN Intake

- Journal: Fundamental Toxicological Sciences
- Published: 2025
- Checked: 2026-05-03

## Summary

2025 placebo-controlled randomized double-blind parallel-group safety study of Teijin-manufactured NMN in 30 healthy Japanese volunteers aged 20-64. Participants received 1500 mg/day, 750 mg/day, or placebo for 4 weeks.

Safety monitoring included medical interviews, subjective symptoms, weight, BMI, blood pressure, pulse, hematology, biochemistry, and urine testing at 0, 2, and 4 weeks. The study reported no problematic findings or test-result changes, and blood NAD+ increased significantly. NMN did not affect telomere length.

Use in the wiki: high-dose short-term safety source for [[nicotinamide-mononucleotide]]. It supports tolerability over 4 weeks but has no cognition endpoint and does not settle long-term high-dose use.
