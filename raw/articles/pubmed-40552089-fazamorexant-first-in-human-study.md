# Pharmacokinetics, Pharmacodynamic, Safety and Tolerability of Fazamorexant, a Novel Dual Orexin Receptor Antagonist: Report of the First-in-Human Study

- PMID: 40552089
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40552089/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

First-in-human randomized, double-blind placebo-controlled study of a new DORA in healthy adults. Single ascending doses were 2-80 mg and multiple ascending doses were 10-60 mg. The drug showed quick absorption and elimination, dose-dependent sleepiness, no meaningful accumulation by day 7, and no serious dose-dependent adverse events, supporting continued insomnia development rather than any cognition-enhancement claim.
