# MSKCC Cordyceps safety monograph

Memorial Sloan Kettering Cancer Center Cordyceps monograph checked in 2026.

The monograph describes Cordyceps as a traditional Chinese medicine used for fatigue, sexual dysfunction, cough, adaptogen claims, and immune stimulation. MSKCC notes that doctors have not studied several claimed uses enough to know if they work.

Safety interpretation for the wiki: MSKCC says no major side effects have been reported, but warns people taking insulin or other glucose-lowering medications to talk with a clinician because Cordyceps can lower blood sugar. It also warns about blood thinners such as warfarin because Cordyceps may increase bleeding risk.
