# RECLAIM ibudilast and pentoxifylline long-COVID drug arms

RECLAIM is a Canadian adaptive randomized platform trial for lingering symptoms of COVID-19. Symptoms listed by the trial include fatigue, shortness of breath with exertion, tachycardia, brain fog, insomnia or sleep disturbance, gastrointestinal disorders, skin rashes, anxiety, and depression.

The RECLAIM site says the completed drug arm intervention trials examined ibudilast or pentoxifylline compared with placebo and that recruitment is closed. As of the page crawl, RECLAIM says the information is being analyzed and findings will be reported soon. The listed registry link is NCT05513560.

Interpretation for the wiki: ibudilast and pentoxifylline have direct long-COVID trial relevance, but posted efficacy results are not yet available from the official trial page.
