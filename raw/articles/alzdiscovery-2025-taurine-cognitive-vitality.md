# Cognitive Vitality Taurine Review

- Source: Alzheimer's Drug Discovery Foundation Cognitive Vitality.
- Updated: 2025-12-18.
- Ingested: 2026-05-03.

## Summary

Cognitive Vitality summarizes taurine as a synthesized and diet-derived amino acid involved in physiological and metabolic functions. It reports that higher circulating taurine has been associated with lower dementia risk, but that clinical trials of taurine interventions have not shown cognitive benefits so far.

The evidence scan lists one RCT meta-analysis, six RCTs, one prospective controlled trial, one open-label trial, one cohort biomarker study, one postmortem study, and preclinical mechanism literature. Safety discussion says clinical trials generally used 1 to 6 g/day and were generally well tolerated, but notes possible medication, alcohol, caffeine, energy-drink, blood-pressure, heart-rate, and QTc considerations.

## Interpretation

Use this as a 2025 synthesis and safety bridge. It supports the wiki's split between biomarker/preclinical plausibility and intervention-trial weakness.
