# A review of caffeine's effects on cognitive, physical and occupational performance

- PMID: 27612937
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27612937/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Review of caffeine showing low-to-moderate doses improve alertness, vigilance, attention, and reaction time, with less consistent effects on memory and executive function. The review also notes repeated dosing can help maintain performance under restricted sleep, but sleep disruption is a real tradeoff.
