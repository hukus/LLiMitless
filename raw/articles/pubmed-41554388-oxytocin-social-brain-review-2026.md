# Oxytocin's impact on the social brain: Individual differences and context shape a core amygdala-mediated mechanism

- PMID: 41554388
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41554388/
- Publication date: 2026-04

## Summary

PRISMA review of 27 oxytocin fMRI studies from 2014-2024. The review concludes that intranasal oxytocin effects are context-dependent and mediated by amygdala-centered network changes, with direction and magnitude varying by individual traits, sex, and dosage.

## Notes

- Source type: systematic review / neuroimaging synthesis.
- Coverage: resting-state and task-based fMRI studies on the human social brain.
- Interpretation: a moderator map, not proof of universal social enhancement.
