# Personalized temporal interference stimulation targeting striatum reduces functional stability and dynamic connectivity variability in the sensorimotor network

- PMID: 41079480
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41079480/
- Published: 2025-09-26
- Ingested: 2026-04-29

## Summary
Randomized, double-blind, sham-controlled crossover study in 26 healthy men.
Personalized TI targeting the right striatum at 10 mA and 20 Hz reduced SMA functional stability and striatal dFC variability, with no adverse cognitive effects or side effects reported.

## Notes
This strengthens the case that target choice and individualized modeling matter, but the outcome layer is still mechanistic and motor-network centered.
