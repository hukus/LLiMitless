# The gut microbiome, mild cognitive impairment, and probiotics: A randomized clinical trial in middle-aged and older adults

- PMID: 36228569
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36228569/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

2022 randomized clinical trial of Lactobacillus rhamnosus GG in 169 middle-aged and older adults. Microbiome shifts tracked with MCI status, and improved cognitive score correlated with decreased Prevotella/Dehalobacterium in the MCI subgroup, but this was not a simple broad cognition win.
