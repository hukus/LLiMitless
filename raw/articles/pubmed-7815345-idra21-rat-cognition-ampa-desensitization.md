# IDRA-21 rat cognition and AMPA desensitization

1995 JPET paper on IDRA-21, formally 7-chloro-3-methyl-3,4-dihydro-2H-1,2,4-benzothiadiazine S,S-dioxide.

Key extracted points:

- IDRA-21 attenuates rapid desensitization of AMPA-selective glutamate receptors and increases excitatory synaptic strength.
- In rats, oral IDRA-21 improved performance in water-maze and passive-avoidance learning/memory tests.
- It reduced performance impairment caused by an AMPA receptor antagonist, alprazolam, and scopolamine in the cited behavioral procedures.
- The paper reports IDRA-21 as 20- to 30-fold more potent than aniracetam in those tests, and only the positive stereoisomer was behaviorally active.
- Interpretation for the wiki: this is a strong historical animal-cognition anchor for IDRA-21, but it remains preclinical and cannot be treated as human nootropic efficacy.
