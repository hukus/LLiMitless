# WADA 2026 prohibited list: peptide and GH-axis expansion

- Source: https://www.wada-ama.org/en/prohibited-list
- Checked: 2026-05-04
- List status: 2026 Prohibited List, in force from 2026-01-01.

## Summary

WADA's 2026 prohibited-list page places non-approved pharmacologic substances in S0 and gives BPC-157 as an example. This matters for gray-market peptide discussions because lack of approval can be a sports-rule problem even before molecule-specific clinical efficacy is settled.

The S2 peptide-hormone / growth-factor section names growth-hormone-releasing hormone analogues including CJC-1295, growth-hormone secretagogues and mimetics including ibutamoren and ipamorelin, and GH-releasing peptides including hexarelin, GHRP-2, and GHRP-6. It also lists thymosin-beta-4 derivatives, including TB-500, under growth factors / growth-factor modulators.

The S4 metabolic-modulator section lists activators of AMPK and gives MOTS-c as an example.

## Research-use read

For the wiki, this is a sports-regulatory status layer, not a cognition-efficacy result. It means GH-axis peptide anecdotes, BPC-157 recovery claims, TB-500 repair claims, and MOTS-c metabolic claims must be interpreted with anti-doping visibility and source-quality risk visible beside any medicinal or community signal.
