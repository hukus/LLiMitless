# Green Valley announcement on early termination of GV-971 global phase 3 trial

- Source: Green Valley announcement, 2022-05-13
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

Green Valley announced early termination of the GV-971 global multicenter phase 3 trial. The announcement says GV-971 was approved by China's NMPA on 2019-11-02 and that the US FDA approved the IND for the global phase 3 trial on 2020-04-08.

The sponsor attributed early termination to COVID-19 effects, financing constraints, operational burden, dropout risk, and trial-quality concerns. The announcement says that by 2022-04-26 the trial had screened 1308 patients and randomized 439 participants across North America, Europe, and China. It also says a DSMB reviewed safety at the 10% enrollment milestone on 2022-01-21 and recommended continuation without modifications because no safety risk was identified.

## Wiki Relevance

This source is sponsor-authored and should be read as status/context rather than independent efficacy evidence. It is still useful because it explains why the global confirmatory program did not deliver the planned full phase 3 result.
