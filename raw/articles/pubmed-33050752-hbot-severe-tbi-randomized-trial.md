# Hyperbaric oxygen for severe traumatic brain injury: a randomized trial

- PMID: 33050752
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33050752/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

Randomized severe-TBI HBOT trial that originally reported benefit but was later retracted. It should not be used as valid efficacy evidence.

## Notes

The later retraction notice is the important credibility record for this paper.
