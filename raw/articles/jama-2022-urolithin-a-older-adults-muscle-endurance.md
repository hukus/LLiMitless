# Effect of Urolithin A Supplementation on Muscle Endurance and Mitochondrial Health in Older Adults: A Randomized Clinical Trial

- Citation: Liu S, D'Amico D, Shankland E, et al. JAMA Network Open. 2022;5(1):e2144279.
- DOI: 10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2021.44279
- ClinicalTrials.gov: NCT03283462
- Ingested: 2026-05-03

## Summary

Randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial in 66 adults aged 65-90 years. Participants received 1000 mg/day oral urolithin A or placebo for 4 months. The primary endpoints were change in 6-minute walk distance and maximal ATP production in hand skeletal muscle; secondary endpoints included muscle endurance in hand and leg muscles and plasma metabolomic biomarkers.

The trial found significant improvement in muscle endurance for hand and leg skeletal muscles and lower plasma acylcarnitines, ceramides, and C-reactive protein with urolithin A. However, 6-minute walk distance and maximal ATP production did not significantly improve versus placebo. No statistical differences in adverse events were reported.

Use in the wiki: strong human proxy source for muscle endurance / mitochondrial biomarkers and short-term safety, but not a cognition trial. It should not upgrade urolithin A as a cognitive enhancer before cognitive endpoints are positive.
