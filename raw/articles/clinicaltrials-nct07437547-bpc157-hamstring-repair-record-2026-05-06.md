# ClinicalTrials.gov record: BPC 157 for Acute Hamstring Muscle Strain Repair

- Registry: ClinicalTrials.gov
- Trial ID: NCT07437547
- Sponsor: Hudson Biotech
- Last Update Posted: 2026-02-27
- Ingested: 2026-05-06

## Summary

Recruiting randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled Phase 2 trial of pentadecapeptide BPC 157 for MRI-confirmed acute grade II hamstring strain. The record says study start was 2026-02-02, planned enrollment is 120, and dosing is subcutaneous once daily for 14 days plus standardized rehabilitation.

## Key points

- Co-primary endpoints are time to return to unrestricted sport and change in MRI-assessed injury volume at Day 14.
- Secondary endpoints include pain during activity, hamstring strength symmetry, and Lower Extremity Functional Scale scores.
- The record is sponsor-provided registry data, not published results, but it is the clearest signal in the current wiki corpus that BPC-157 has moved into a larger controlled human study.

## Signal Use

- Formal signal: meaningful upgrade in trial-readiness compared with the earlier pilot-only human record.
- Cognition signal: none directly; this is a musculoskeletal recovery trial.
- Safety/regulatory signal: still investigational and sports-rule-sensitive despite the more serious study design.
