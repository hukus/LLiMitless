# DL-phenylalanine versus imipramine in depression

1979 double-blind controlled study comparing DL-phenylalanine with imipramine in 40 depressed patients, with 27 completers. The paper reported no statistical difference between groups on depression scales and concluded DL-phenylalanine might have antidepressant properties, while explicitly requiring careful interpretation.

Use in the wiki: old mood-state evidence only. It does not test cognition, healthy-adult focus, or durable nootropic benefit.
