# The effect of transcutaneous auricular vagus nerve stimulation on vigilance, cognition, and mood during military exercise with acute sleep deprivation.

- PMID: 41973311
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41973311/
- Ingested: 2026-04-29

## Summary

2026 field experiment in 48 male conscripts during a 24-hour training period that included total sleep deprivation. A 4-minute taVNS session with individually adjusted intensity (12-26 mA) modestly attenuated vigilance decrements in the early morning and reduced the shift toward negative mood valence, but did not improve response inhibition.

## Notes

- Sleep-loss rescue, not rested enhancement.
- Strongest signal was on vigilance and psychomotor speed in the early morning hours.
- No physiological biomarkers were collected, so mechanism remains speculative.
- Useful as a real-world sleep-deprivation comparator for the taVNS branch.
