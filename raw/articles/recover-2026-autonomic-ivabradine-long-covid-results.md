# RECOVER-AUTONOMIC 2026 ivabradine Long COVID POTS results announcement

## Captured takeaways

- RECOVER announced first results from the RECOVER-AUTONOMIC ivabradine clinical trial at the ACC Annual Conference on 2026-03-28.
- The study included 181 adults with Long COVID POTS across the United States.
- Participants were randomized to ivabradine or placebo for 3 months, with 3 months of follow-up, and were also assigned to coordinated care or usual care.
- RECOVER reports that ivabradine significantly lowered heart rate compared with placebo but did not significantly improve participant-survey POTS symptoms in adults with Long COVID POTS.
- Participants receiving coordinated care in addition to ivabradine reported symptom improvement compared with those receiving ivabradine and usual care.
- More detailed results were still pending journal publication at capture time.
- Interpretation for the wiki: this is a major downgrade signal for "heart-rate lowering alone fixes Long COVID POTS brain fog." Ivabradine may remain useful for selected tachycardia phenotypes, but symptom recovery appears to require more than rate control in this large PASC sample.
