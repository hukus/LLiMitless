# Prolonged oral treatment with MK-677, a novel growth hormone secretagogue, improves sleep quality in man

- PubMed PMID 9349662, Neuroendocrinology 1997.
- Double-blind placebo-controlled crossover work in healthy young and older adults.
- Bedtime MK-677 increased slow-wave sleep and REM-related measures and reduced sleep deviations.
- The signal is mainly sleep/restoration and somatotropic-axis correction, not direct cognition.
- Useful as state-restoration evidence because several peptide threads frame these compounds as sleep aids.
