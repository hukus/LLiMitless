# Cognitive Vitality Centrophenoxine

Alzheimer's Drug Discovery Foundation Cognitive Vitality page for centrophenoxine / meclofenoxate, updated December 18, 2017. The page summarizes centrophenoxine as a supplement marketed for memory support with acetylcholine-like properties.

ADDF's interpretation is weak and inconclusive: multiple trials exist, but study quality is suboptimal; the best healthy-elderly trial found one delayed-free-recall signal without effects on five other memory measures; another smaller study found no significant memory or concentration effects; and a DMAE MCI trial found no significant memory, executive-function, or attention benefit after 24 weeks.

Safety notes include generally mild adverse events in centrophenoxine studies, but DMAE-related concerns including serious adverse events in one clinical trial and neural-tube-defect signals in preclinical work.

Relevance: formal anchor for keeping DMAE/centrophenoxine as legacy gray-zone rather than strong nootropic evidence.
