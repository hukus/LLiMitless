# Baseline cognition and stimulation site shape the effects of tdcs on verbal fluency in older and younger adults

- PMID: 41870727
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41870727/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

2026 preregistered, double-blind sham-controlled study in 72 younger and 72 older adults. Focal anodal tDCS to left IFG or left TPJ during semantic and phonemic fluency showed age- and site-dependent effects, with the strongest signal in the left IFG condition and a time-dependent tradeoff in sustained phonemic output.

## Notes

This source adds a more granular phenotype layer to the tDCS map: site choice matters, the age group matters, and the effect is not uniform across the time course of verbal output.
