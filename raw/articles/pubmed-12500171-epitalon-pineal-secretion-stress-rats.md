# Epitalon influences pineal secretion in stress-exposed rats in the daytime

- PMID: 12500171
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12500171/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

Intranasal epitalon increased C-Fos in stress-exposed rat pinealocytes and partially reduced stress-related pineal structural changes. The paper supports a stress-state pineal mechanism, not a cognition endpoint.
