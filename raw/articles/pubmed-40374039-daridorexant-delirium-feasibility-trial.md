# Dual Orexin Receptor Antagonists for Delirium: A Scoping Review and Feasibility Trial of Daridorexant

- PMID: 40374039
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40374039/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Scoping review plus small randomized feasibility trial in post-cardiac-surgery patients. The review found mostly Japanese DORA-delirium reports and the feasibility arm gave daridorexant 50 mg for the first three nights after surgery. The pilot was too small for efficacy, but daridorexant subjects had numerically lower delirium symptom burden and the paper emphasizes balancing benefit against next-morning sedation.
