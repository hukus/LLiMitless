# Central nervous system effects of TAK-653, an investigational alpha-amino-3-hydroxy-5-methyl-4-isoxazole receptor (AMPAR) positive allosteric modulator in healthy volunteers

- PMID: 36153330
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36153330/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

2022 randomized double-blind crossover study in 24 healthy volunteers using TMS and NeuroCart endpoints. TAK-653 showed a psychostimulant-like pharmacodynamic profile, with increased cortical excitability and improvements on some NeuroCart measures, but it was a CNS pharmacodynamic study rather than a direct cognition-efficacy trial. It is the clearest modern human signal that the low-impact AMPAR PAM strategy still produces measurable CNS effects.
