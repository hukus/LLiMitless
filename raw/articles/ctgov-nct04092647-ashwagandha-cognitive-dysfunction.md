# Ashwagandha for Cognitive Dysfunction

- ClinicalTrials.gov ID: NCT04092647
- Source: https://www.clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04092647
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Randomized double-blind placebo-controlled trial in chemotherapy-related cognitive dysfunction. The study tests ashwagandha for memory/thinking problems in patients undergoing or recently exposed to chemotherapy, with cognitive dysfunction as the target phenotype rather than healthy enhancement.
