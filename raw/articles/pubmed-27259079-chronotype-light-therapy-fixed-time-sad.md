# The relation between chronotype and treatment outcome with light therapy on a fixed time schedule

- PMID: 27259079
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27259079/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

In this retrospective SAD analysis, morning light at a fixed time worked well regardless of chronotype. That makes it an important counterexample to overly rigid chronotype-matching rules.

## Notes

- 132 patients were pooled from four studies.
- Everyone received morning light at 8:00 AM.
- No significant relationship was found between MEQ score and therapy success.
- The authors argued the antidepressant effect might not depend on a phase shift alone.
- This study is useful as a contradiction to chronotype-personalization claims.
