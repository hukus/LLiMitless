# Efficacy and safety of iclepertin (BI 425809) with adjunctive computerized cognitive training in patients with schizophrenia

- PMID: 39759424
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39759424/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

This double-blind phase 2 trial paired iclepertin 10 mg with at-home computerized cognitive training for 12 weeks in schizophrenia. Of 200 randomized patients, 154 completed the study, but no between-group differences were seen at the efficacy endpoint. Safety looked acceptable and no new risks were identified.
