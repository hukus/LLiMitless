# GREEN MEMORY global GV-971 phase 3 registry

- ClinicalTrials.gov: NCT04520412
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

ClinicalTrials.gov record for GREEN MEMORY, a planned phase 3 multicenter randomized double-blind placebo-controlled trial of GV-971 in mild-to-moderate Alzheimer's disease. The registered target enrollment is 2046 participants, with a 52-week double-blind period and follow-up extension logic in sponsor-facing materials.

As checked in this sweep, the ClinicalTrials.gov record lists the study as suspended and says study quality was seriously affected by COVID-19, so the sponsor decided to suspend the trial and restart at the right time. No results are posted in the registry record.

## Wiki Relevance

The global registry is the key replication-status source. It means the China phase 3 result has not yet been confirmed by a completed global phase 3 readout, and any confidence upgrade should wait for results rather than enrollment or approval plans.
