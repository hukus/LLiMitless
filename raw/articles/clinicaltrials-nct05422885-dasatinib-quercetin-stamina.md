# Safety and Feasibility of Dasatinib and Quercetin in Adults at Risk for Alzheimer's Disease

- ClinicalTrials.gov ID: NCT05422885
- Study name: STAMINA

## Summary

This registry entry describes the 12-week single-arm pilot that later published in eBioMedicine. It targets older adults with slow gait and mild cognitive impairment and uses intermittent D+Q dosing.

## Key Data

- Design: single-arm, open-label, pre-post pilot.
- Population: adults aged 65+ with slow gait speed and MCI.
- Dose: 100 mg dasatinib + 1250 mg quercetin for 2 consecutive days every 2 weeks over 12 weeks.
- Outcomes: safety, feasibility, cognition, gait speed, cerebral blood flow regulation, biomarkers of senescence.
- Update status: last update posted 2025-03-17.

## Caveats

- The trial was not randomized.
- Cognitive and mobility outcomes should be read as preliminary.
