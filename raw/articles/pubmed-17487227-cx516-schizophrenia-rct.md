# A placebo-controlled add-on trial of the Ampakine, CX516, for cognitive deficits in schizophrenia

- PMID: 17487227
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17487227/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

2008 placebo-controlled add-on RCT in stable schizophrenia. CX516 900 mg TID for 4 weeks did not improve the composite cognitive score or any individual cognitive test versus placebo, and caused fatigue, insomnia, and epigastric discomfort.
