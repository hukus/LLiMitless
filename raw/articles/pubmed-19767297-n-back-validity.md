# Is the n-back task a valid neuropsychological measure for assessing working memory?

- PMID: 19767297
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19767297/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

2009 validation paper examining convergent validity between the n-back and established working-memory measures such as digit span backward. It supports n-back as a practical working-memory probe, but not as a complete substitute for broader executive-function assessment.

## Notes

This is the best fit for the measurement stack when the goal is a brief computerized working-memory anchor rather than a full neuropsychological battery.
