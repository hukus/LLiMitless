# CX717 in the Treatment of Adult ADHD

- ClinicalTrials.gov: NCT03375021
- Sponsor: RespireRx
- Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03375021
- Ingested: 2026-05-04

## Summary

ClinicalTrials.gov registry record for a completed randomized, double-blind, two-period crossover phase 2 study of CX717 in adult men with ADHD. The study compared CX717 200 mg BID, CX717 800 mg BID, and placebo across 3-week periods separated by a 2-week washout. Enrollment was 68, and the primary outcome was ADHD-RS at 3 weeks, with secondary outcomes including ADHD-RS subscales, CGI-I, sleepiness, sleep quality, Stroop, trail making, continuous performance, and digit span measures.

The registry record was first posted in 2017 for a trial that ran in 2005-2006. It showed no posted results on the registry page at the time captured, but the later 2025 European Journal of Pharmacology paper reports the trial outcomes. The registry is useful for protocol, dose, endpoint, and population identity.
