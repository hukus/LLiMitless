# A Systematic Review to Evaluate the Effect of Neflamapimod on Cognitive Function and Progression of Dementia

- PMID: 41510854
- DOI: 10.4103/neurol-india.Neurol-India-D-25-00227
- Publication: Neurology India, 2026
- Evidence tier: systematic review

## Summary

This 2026 systematic review evaluated neflamapimod / VX-745, a p38alpha kinase inhibitor, across dementia studies including Alzheimer's disease and Lewy body dementia. The review searched PubMed, Embase, ProQuest, Cochrane Library, and Web of Science through 2024-05-05 and identified only two eligible clinical studies, so no meta-analysis was possible.

The review's interpretation was mixed. Primary cognitive outcomes such as NTB and HVLT-R were not statistically different, while selected episodic memory, executive-function, attention, gait, motor, and biomarker signals were described as favorable, especially in participants with elevated plasma ptau181. The review concluded that cognitive effects remain uncertain, while disease-biomarker effects justify further study.
