# FDA warns consumers not to purchase or use any tianeptine product due to serious risks

- Source page: https://www.fda.gov/drugs/drug-safety-and-availability/fda-warns-consumers-not-purchase-or-use-neptunes-fix-or-any-tianeptine-product-due-serious-risks
- Dated on page: 2025-05-08
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

FDA says tianeptine is not approved in the U.S., is not generally recognized as safe for food, and does not meet the definition of a dietary ingredient. The page says it is still marketed as a research chemical, nootropic, or dietary supplement, often at convenience stores, gas stations, vape shops, and online. FDA also says poison control center cases have increased to about 350 in 2024 and that case reports include doses far above labeled foreign-drug doses.
