# NCT05519644 STAK-Sleep ketone ester sleep-restriction registry snapshot

- ClinicalTrials.gov ID: NCT05519644
- Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05519644
- Ingested: 2026-05-04

## Summary

Current ClinicalTrials.gov record for STAK-Sleep, "Strategies to Augment Ketosis: Ketone Conferred Resiliency Against Sleep Restriction." The study remains recruiting with last update posted 2025-05-29 and no posted results. The sponsor is Ohio State University. Estimated enrollment is 60 healthy adults aged 18 to 40. Actual study start is listed as 2023-06-20, with primary completion and study completion estimated for 2026-08.

The design is a randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled, two-period crossover trial. Participants reduce sleep by 50% of normal for four consecutive nights. During one intervention period they take a ketone supplement, described as a diester of hexanoic acid and (R)-1,3-butanediol, with a two-week washout before crossing over to placebo or the reverse sequence.

Primary cognition/performance endpoints include ANAM, CANTAB, Gradual-onset Continuous Performance Task, Face-Name Task, marksmanship metrics with a VirTra simulator, Quick Board response-time measures, and physical performance. The protocol also tracks sleep, heart rate, diet, exercise performance, mood/pain/sleep questionnaires, continuous glucose/ketone monitoring, blood markers including CRP, insulin, ghrelin, and BDNF, and controlled feeding/caffeine context.

## Signal Use

- Medicinal signal: direct sleep-restriction protocol anchor, but still no-results.
- Cognition signal: high-value because it tests sleep restriction and operational-style endpoints rather than rested baseline.
- Upgrade condition: posted results or peer-reviewed paper with cognitive endpoint statistics.
- Caveat: the intervention is a C6 ketone diester, so it should not be collapsed into ketone salts, ketone monoester, or Ketone-IQ without formulation-specific logic.
