# Proof-of-Concept Randomized Controlled Study of Cognition Effects of the Proprietary Extract Sceletium tortuosum (Zembrin) Targeting Phosphodiesterase-4 in Cognitively Healthy Subjects: Implications for Alzheimer's Dementia

- PMID: 25389443
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25389443/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

2014 randomized crossover study in cognitively healthy adults. Zembrin, a Sceletium tortuosum extract marketed as PDE4-targeting, improved set flexibility and executive function in a very small sample and was well tolerated, but the study is proof-of-concept rather than a stable clinical signal.

## Key points

- Design: 21 healthy subjects in a 3-week crossover.
- Outcome: CNS Vital Signs battery and mood ratings.
- Signal: improved cognitive set flexibility and executive function.
- Interpretation: interesting CREB-pathway-adjacent lead, but low-confidence and not a mainstream comparator.
