# Exercise plasma boosts memory and dampens brain inflammation via clusterin

- Source: Nature
- PMID: 34880498
- DOI: 10.1038/s41586-021-04183-x
- Nature: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-021-04183-x
- Ingested: 2026-04-29

## Summary

2021 mouse study showing that runner plasma reduces neuroinflammatory gene expression and that clusterin is a key transferable exercise-associated factor. The paper also reports higher plasma clusterin in humans with cognitive impairment after 6 months of structured exercise, which is biomarker support rather than direct treatment proof.

## Notes

- Runner plasma counteracted LPS-induced brain inflammation in mice.
- Proteomics showed increased complement cascade inhibitors, including clusterin.
- Intravenous clusterin bound brain endothelial cells and reduced inflammatory gene expression in mouse inflammation and AD models.
- The human arm is exercise biomarker support, not a direct clusterin administration trial.
