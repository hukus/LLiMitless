# Vinpocetine supplement label accuracy

- Source: USDA Agricultural Research Service publication record
- Article: Identification and quantification of vinpocetine from dietary supplements
- Journal: Drug Testing and Analysis
- DOI: 10.1002/dta.1853
- Ingested: 2026-05-15

## Summary

This USDA ARS record summarizes a peer-reviewed analytical study of vinpocetine and picamilon products sold as dietary supplements in the United States. The authors developed and validated an ultra-high-performance liquid chromatography-photodiode-array method to quantify the compounds and compare product contents with label claims.

For vinpocetine-labeled products, 23 supplements were available at two large supplement retail chains. Seventeen contained vinpocetine, with quantities ranging from 0.3 mg to 32 mg per recommended daily serving. No vinpocetine was detected in 26% of sampled products, and 17% of supplement brands implied that vinpocetine was an extract of lesser periwinkle.

The record states that consumers could not obtain accurate information from supplement labels about the presence or quantity of vinpocetine and picamilon, two drugs that had never been approved by FDA.

## Interpretation
