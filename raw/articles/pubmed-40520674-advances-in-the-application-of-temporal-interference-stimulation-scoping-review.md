# Advances in the application of temporal interference stimulation: a scoping review

- PMID: 40520674
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40520674/
- Published: 2025-05-30
- Ingested: 2026-04-29

## Summary
Scoping review of 708 records, with 33 studies ultimately included.
The review concludes that TIS can reach deep brain regions and that the literature is still mostly about development and application rather than settled efficacy.

## Notes
Useful for branch orientation because it shows the field has moved beyond theory, but it does not establish cognition efficacy on its own.
