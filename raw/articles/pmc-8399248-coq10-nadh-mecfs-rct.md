# PMC 8399248 CoQ10 plus NADH ME/CFS randomized trial

This 2021 Nutrients study tested oral coenzyme Q10 plus NADH in myalgic encephalomyelitis / chronic fatigue syndrome (ME/CFS). The authors describe ME/CFS as a multisystem neuroimmune disease with fatigue, post-exertional malaise, concentration and memory alterations, nonrestorative sleep, orthostatic intolerance, and chronic pain.

Design: 207 ME/CFS patients were randomized to 200 mg CoQ10 plus 20 mg NADH (n=104) or placebo (n=103) once daily for 12 weeks. Validated patient-reported outcome measures were assessed at baseline, 4 weeks, 8 weeks, and 4 weeks after treatment cessation.

Result: the abstract reports that CoQ10 plus NADH co-supplementation was examined for perceived fatigue, unrefreshing sleep, and health-related quality of life. The authors concluded that the combination may be a safe therapeutic option for reducing perceived cognitive fatigue and improving health-related quality of life in ME/CFS. Comment-level discussion in patient forums notes that effect-size interpretation is modest and questionnaire-based.

Interpretation for the wiki: this is directly relevant to brain-fog/cognitive-fatigue routing, but it remains ME/CFS-state evidence and a CoQ10 plus NADH combination. It should not be interpreted as NADH-alone or healthy-adult cognitive enhancement.
