# Increasing nitric oxide availability via ingestion of nitrate-rich beetroot juice improves vascular responsiveness in individuals with Alzheimer's Disease

- PMID: 40089052
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40089052/
- Ingested: 2026-04-30

## Summary
Acute nitrate-rich beetroot juice increased plasma nitrate and nitrite and improved vascular responsiveness (ΔPLM) in individuals with Alzheimer's disease, healthy older adults, and young controls over 4 hours. The AD group still showed lower vascular responsiveness than the comparison groups, and the authors conclude that acute inorganic nitrate was not enough to restore chronically adapted vascular properties in AD. No cognition endpoint was reported in the abstract, so this is reserve/vascular-response evidence rather than direct nootropic evidence.

## Notes
- Participants: AD `n=10`, healthy older adults `n=10`, young controls `n=10`.
- Design: acute, placebo-controlled, hourly measurements over 4 hours.
- Signal: biomarker movement only; cognition remains untested in the abstract.
