# MKC-231, a choline-uptake enhancer: long-lasting cognitive improvement after repeated administration in AF64A-treated rats

- Source type: PubMed-indexed preclinical study, abstract captured via indexed citation summary.
- Publication: Journal of Neural Transmission, 2008.
- Date checked: 2026-05-03.

MKC-231 / coluracetam was tested as a high-affinity choline-uptake enhancer in AF64A-treated rats. Repeated oral administration at 1 or 3 mg/kg for 8 days improved water-maze learning impairment for about 24 hours after the last dose, even though brain drug concentration was not detected at that time.

The study also reported increased high-affinity choline uptake on a time course similar to the cognitive improvement, supporting a cholinergic transporter-regulation hypothesis.

Use in the wiki: preclinical cholinergic-rescue anchor for coluracetam. It does not establish human cognition efficacy.
