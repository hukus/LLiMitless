# 2017 Nootropics Survey on /r/Nootropics

- Source type: public survey mirror / aggregated subjective ratings
- Published: survey page documents 2017 /r/Nootropics results
- Respondents: 380
- Substances rated: 63
- Scale: seven-point perceived-effect ratings plus dose reporting

## Summary

This page mirrors a 2017 /r/Nootropics survey. It is useful as a quantified anecdote layer because it separates perceived focus, anxiety, memory, motivation, and mood from dose reporting, but it is still open-label, self-selected, and not causal evidence.

The strongest focus signals were adrafinil, caffeine, phenylpiracetam, modafinil, armodafinil, methylphenidate, and Adderall. Anxiety reduction was strongest for L-theanine, tianeptine, CBD, kava, and phenibut. Memory gains were small across the board, and the mood signal clustered around LSD, tianeptine, phenibut, and Adderall. The dose table gives useful medians for compounds that show up in `RC-36`, including phenylpiracetam, Semax, Selank, BPC-157, NSI-189, PRL-8-53, noopept, nicotine, modafinil, phenibut, and tianeptine.

## Candidate Notes

- Phenylpiracetam: strong focus signal; median reported dose 160 mg.
- Modafinil: strong focus signal; median reported dose 125 mg.
- Nicotine: notable focus/motivation signal; median reported dose 4 mg.
- Semax: reported dose median 0.6 mg; use concentrated in the low-dose peptide lane.
- Selank: reported dose median 0.25 mg; mostly anxiety/apathy-adjacent rather than broad enhancement.
- BPC-157: reported dose median 0.25 mg; included as a community-signal source, not cognition proof.
- NSI-189: median reported dose 40 mg; anecdote lane only.
- PRL-8-53: median reported dose 20 mg; anecdote lane only.
- Noopept: median reported dose 15 mg; legacy racetam-adjacent signal.
- Phenibut: anxiety reduction and mood signal with median reported dose 1000 mg; risk-first because tolerance and dependence dominate.
- Tianeptine: anxiety reduction and mood signal with median reported dose 33 mg; risk-first because reinforcement and withdrawal confounds dominate.

## Caveats

- Self-selected Reddit sample.
- Open-label and expectation-sensitive.
- Dose reports are noisy and should not be used as dosing guidance.
- No blinding, randomization, or product-identity control.
