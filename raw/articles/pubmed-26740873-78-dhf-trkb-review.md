# 7,8-Dihydroxyflavone, a small molecular TrkB agonist, is useful for treating various BDNF-implicated human disorders

- PMID: 26740873
- PMCID: PMC4702337
- DOI: 10.1186/s40035-015-0048-7
- Publication: Translational Neurodegeneration, 2016
- Evidence tier: review / preclinical drug-development synthesis

## Summary

This review positions 7,8-dihydroxyflavone (7,8-DHF) as a small-molecule TrkB agonist and BDNF mimetic. It summarizes work claiming extracellular-domain binding, TrkB dimerization, downstream signaling, oral bioactivity, blood-brain barrier penetration, pharmacokinetic limitations, structure-activity work, and broad animal-model efficacy across BDNF-implicated disorders.

For the cognition wiki, this is a mechanism and candidate-prioritization source, not a human efficacy source. The review's strongest relevance is that it makes 7,8-DHF a named TrkB / BDNF-mimetic lead worth tracking. It should not be used to infer healthy-adult cognitive enhancement or a validated clinical protocol.
