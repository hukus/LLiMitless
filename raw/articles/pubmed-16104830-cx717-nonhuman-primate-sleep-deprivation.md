# Facilitation of task performance and removal of the effects of sleep deprivation by an ampakine (CX717) in nonhuman primates

- PMID: 16104830
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16104830/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

2005 nonhuman-primate study showing dose-dependent performance enhancement under normal alert conditions and reversal of sleep-deprivation impairment. This paper anchored the original translational promise for CX717, but the human studies have not matched it cleanly.
