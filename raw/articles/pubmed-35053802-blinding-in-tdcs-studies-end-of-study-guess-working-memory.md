# Blinding in tDCS Studies: Correct End-of-Study Guess Does Not Moderate the Effects on Associative and Working Memory

- PMID: 35053802
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35053802/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

Within-subject tDCS memory experiments examined whether correct end-of-study guesses about sham condition moderated associative-memory or working-memory outcomes. The study found no evidence that sham guessing explained the tDCS effects, which is a useful caution against overinterpreting guess data as a direct causal moderator.

## Notes

- Important nuance: a blinding metric can be informative without being outcome-determining.
- Works well as a counterweight to the stronger claim that any correct guess necessarily invalidates the cognitive result.
