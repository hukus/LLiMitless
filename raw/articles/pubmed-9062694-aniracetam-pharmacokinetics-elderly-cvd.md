# Pharmacokinetic study of aniracetam in elderly patients with cerebrovascular disease

- PMID: 9062694
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9062694/
- Ingested: 2026-04-30

## Summary

1997 pharmacokinetic study of oral aniracetam 200 mg in six hospitalized elderly women with cerebrovascular disease, compared with young healthy volunteers. Peak levels of the main metabolites appeared at 2 hours and returned to baseline by 6 hours, while metabolite half-life rose 4- to 7-fold in the elderly. No clinical side effects were observed, but the data support age- and renal-function-sensitive exposure handling.
