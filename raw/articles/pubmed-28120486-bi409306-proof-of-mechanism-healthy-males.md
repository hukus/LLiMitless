# A phase I, randomized, proof-of-clinical-mechanism study assessing the pharmacokinetics and pharmacodynamics of the oral PDE9A inhibitor BI 409306 in healthy male volunteers

- PMID: 28120486
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28120486/
- Ingested: 2026-04-29

## Summary

2017 healthy-volunteer phase I study showing that BI 409306 reached CSF quickly and increased CSF cGMP in a dose-dependent manner. This is the clearest target-engagement source for the compound, but it does not demonstrate cognition benefit.

## Key points

- Doses: single oral doses of 25, 50, 100, and 200 mg.
- Population: 20 healthy male volunteers.
- PK/PD: plasma levels rose rapidly, CSF exposure followed, and CSF cGMP increased dose-dependently.
- Safety: BI 409306 was safe and well tolerated; most AEs were mild to moderate and procedure-related.
- Relevance: proof that BI 409306 crosses into CSF and moves the intended second messenger.
