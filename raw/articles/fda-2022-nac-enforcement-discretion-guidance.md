# FDA NAC Enforcement Discretion Guidance

- Source: FDA guidance for industry.
- Final guidance date: August 2022.
- Ingested: 2026-05-03.

## Summary

FDA guidance addressing products labeled as dietary supplements that contain N-acetyl-L-cysteine. The agency explains that it previously concluded NAC-containing products were excluded from the dietary-supplement definition because NAC had been approved as a drug before being marketed as a supplement or food, but FDA also stated it was considering rulemaking and intended to exercise enforcement discretion for certain NAC-containing dietary-supplement products while the rulemaking question remained open.

The guidance does not establish cognitive efficacy or general safety. It is a regulatory-status and sourcing-context source for NAC products, especially when community anecdotes discuss availability, supplement status, or product trust.

## Interpretation

Use this as a regulatory caveat. NAC is both a drug-associated molecule and a supplement-market molecule, so cognitive anecdotes must keep product legality, labeling, and sourcing separate from efficacy.
