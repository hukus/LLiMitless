# Combination of exercise and GLP-1 receptor agonist treatment reduces severity of metabolic syndrome, abdominal obesity, and inflammation

- PMID: 36841762
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36841762/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Randomized trial in adults with obesity after a weight-loss run-in. Exercise plus liraglutide improved metabolic syndrome severity, abdominal fat, and inflammation more than placebo; cognition was not an endpoint.

## Notes

This is the strongest direct exercise-plus-metabolic-peptide combination evidence I found, but it is cardiometabolic rather than cognitive.
