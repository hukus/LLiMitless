# Evaluation of exploratory fluid biomarker results from a phase 1 senolytic trial in mild Alzheimer's disease

- PMID: 40274471
- Journal: Neurotherapeutics (2025)
- ClinicalTrials.gov: NCT04063124

## Summary

This paper analyzes blood, CSF, and urine biomarkers from the first open-label senolytic trial in mild Alzheimer's disease. The parent study involved 5 older adults and reported favorable safety/tolerability with CNS penetration of dasatinib.

## Key Data

- Biomarkers assessed: immunoassays, mass spectrometry, transcriptomics.
- Reported changes: plasma fractalkine and MMP-7 increased; CSF IL-6 increased.
- Stability: amyloid beta and tau species remained stable.
- Transcriptome: inflammatory genes including FOS, FOSB, IL1β, IL8, JUN, JUNB, and PTGS2 were downregulated.

## Caveats

- This is exploratory biomarker work from a very small open-label trial.
- The biomarker pattern is mixed, so it does not cleanly validate one direction of effect.
- No robust cognition efficacy estimate is established here.
