# β-Amyloid and mitochondrial-derived peptide-c are additive predictors of adverse outcome to high-on-treatment platelet reactivity in type 2 diabetics with revascularized coronary artery disease

- PMID: 32052315
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32052315/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Human cohort study in type 2 diabetes and coronary artery disease found that lower circulating MOTS-c predicted worse cardiovascular outcomes and interacted with beta-amyloid and platelet reactivity. This is a human biomarker bridge, not a cognition trial.
