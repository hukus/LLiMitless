# Acute mental effects of phentermine, ephedrine, fenfluramine, and prolintane

- PMID: 6471970
- Journal: Arzneimittel-Forschung (1984)

## Captured takeaways

- Double-blind, placebo-controlled study in healthy volunteer medical students.
- Compared acute oral ephedrine HCl 30 or 40 mg, fenfluramine HCl 15 or 20 mg, phentermine 7.5 or 11.25 mg, and prolintane HCl 10 or 20 mg.
- Each group included 16-43 participants.
- The abstract reports that ephedrine had the most pronounced cardiovascular effects.
- Phentermine and prolintane appeared most active on performance of some mental tasks at the doses tested.
- Interpretation for the wiki: this is an old acute healthy-volunteer mental-task signal for phentermine, but it is not enough to support chronic use, broad cognition, or modern nootropic ranking.
