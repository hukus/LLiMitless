# Cordyceps militaris beverage immune RCT in healthy adults

2024 Scientific Reports randomized controlled trial of a fermented Cordyceps militaris beverage in healthy adults.

The study enrolled 40 healthy Thai volunteers, separated by sex, and assigned 10 men and 10 women to fermented C. militaris beverage and matched placebo groups. The beverage contained 2.85 mg cordycepin per 75 mL serving and was taken once daily after breakfast for 60 days. The C. militaris powder was characterized by HPLC-DAD and contained cordycepin and adenosine.

The primary readout was immune function rather than cognition. NK cell activity increased in men after 4 weeks within group and in women after 8 weeks relative to placebo. The paper also reports selected cytokine and monocyte changes, with no liver, kidney, blood-component, glucose, or lipid toxicity signal in the measured safety indices.

Interpretation for the wiki: this supports product-characterized human exposure and immune-modulation plausibility for Cordyceps militaris, but it is not a cognition or brain-fog trial. It belongs as mechanism/safety context, not efficacy proof.
