# A randomized trial of the long-term, continued efficacy and safety of modafinil in narcolepsy

- PMID: 10767651
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10767651/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Sixty-nine narcolepsy patients who had already completed an earlier crossover study stayed on modafinil for 16 weeks, then were randomized for 2 more weeks to continue modafinil or switch to placebo. Continued modafinil still improved wakefulness metrics, with no clear tolerance signal in this window.

## Notes

This is a disorder-context comparator, not healthy enhancement evidence. It is still useful because it suggests at least one wake-promoter can retain benefit over months without an obvious need for built-in cycling.
