# ANSM NAFTILUX 200 mg naftidrofuryl oxalate product characteristics

French ANSM public medicines database RCP for NAFTILUX 200 mg capsules, updated 7 May 2025.

The listed therapeutic indication is symptomatic treatment of intermittent claudication in chronic obliterative lower-limb arterial disease, stage 2. The dose is one 200 mg capsule three times daily, or 600 mg/day. ANSM describes the effect as symptomatic only, allowing a moderate improvement of walking distance in the six months after treatment initiation, and only as a complement to other recommended PAD measures such as cardiovascular risk-factor management, regular exercise, and smoking cessation. Response should be reassessed after six months.

Administration instructions say to take doses with meals and swallow capsules with a large glass of water. The contraindications include hypersensitivity, known hyperoxaluria, and history of recurrent calcium renal stones.

Safety warnings describe reported severe acute cytolytic liver injury, urinary-composition changes favoring calcium oxalate renal stones, 38 mg oxalate per 200 mg capsule, and local esophagitis when the capsule is taken without drink at bedtime. Listed adverse effects include digestive symptoms, severe liver injury, oxalocalcium renal lithiasis, oral mucosal ulcerations, and rash. Massive overdose may cause confusion and convulsions. The product is listed as Liste II.

Relevance: current regulatory safety and dosing context. It makes naftidrofuryl a prescription-style PAD drug with oxalate/hepatic constraints, not a supplement-style cognition candidate.
