# The effects of transcranial magnetic stimulation on patients with chronic insomnia: a prospective functional near-infrared spectroscopy study

- PMID: 40267527
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40267527/
- Ingested: 2026-04-29

## Summary

Prospective study of 60 participants including 30 chronic-insomnia patients and 30 healthy controls. Chronic-insomnia patients received 1 Hz rTMS to the right DLPFC for 2 weeks; symptom scores improved and fNIRS showed stronger prefrontal activation after rTMS.

## Notes

This is not a CBT-I sequence study, but it strengthens the state-dependence argument: insomnia is linked to altered prefrontal task responses, and rTMS appears to shift both symptoms and activation patterns.
