# Phase 2 study of zervimesine (CT1812) in participants with mild-to-moderate dementia with Lewy bodies (DLB)

- PMID: 41416579
- PMCID: PMC12715698
- DOI: 10.1002/alz.71004
- Publication: Alzheimers Dement, 2025
- Evidence tier: human phase 2a randomized trial

## Summary

This was a multi-center, double-blind, placebo-controlled 26-week phase 2a study in probable DLB. Participants aged 50 to 85 years with MMSE 18 to 27 were randomized to zervimesine 100 mg, zervimesine 300 mg, or placebo.

The abstract reports that the study was safe and well tolerated and that exploratory outcomes favored zervimesine, supporting larger follow-up studies. The published abstract does not provide the full endpoint table, so the exact magnitude of the exploratory signal should not be overstated from the abstract alone.

## Safety

- 130 randomized; 109 completed.
- AE discontinuations: 4.5% at 100 mg, 16.3% at 300 mg, 4.8% on placebo.
- The abstract does not describe a major new safety signal beyond dose-related tolerability concerns.

## Wiki Use

- Use as the DLB exploratory readout for CT1812.
- Treat as hypothesis-positive, not definitive efficacy.
- Keep dose-related AE burden visible when comparing against other synaptic-rescue candidates.
