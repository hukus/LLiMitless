# Emerging Role of Mesenchymal Stromal Cell and Exosome Therapies in Treating Cognitive Impairment

- PMID: 40142948
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40142948/
- Publication date: 2025-02-20

## Summary

Review of MSC and exosome approaches for cognitive impairment, especially AD and PD. The paper frames both as promising regenerative strategies but emphasizes that source variability, dosing, and delivery remain unresolved.

## Notes

This is a mechanism-and-translation review, not a human efficacy result. It is useful for framing why the branch exists, but it does not raise confidence above the level set by the small phase I/II trial.
