# PubMed 33767223: ECa 233 Centella extract pharmacokinetics in healthy volunteers

This 2021 Scientific Reports clinical trial studied an orally modified formula of standardized Centella asiatica extract ECa 233 in healthy Thai volunteers.

Design: open-label phase I study with two dosage sequences, 250 and 500 mg, in 12 healthy Thai volunteers. The modified formula was administered in single and multiple doses to assess disposition kinetics and metabolomic changes.

Key pharmacokinetic finding from the abstract: the major parent compounds madecassoside and asiaticoside were rarely absorbed. They underwent biotransformation into active metabolites, madecassic acid and asiatic acid, with possible fecal elimination.

Interpretation for the wiki: this is not an efficacy trial, but it is important for product/delivery normalization. Claims about Centella extract should specify extract standardization, parent glycosides, and metabolite exposure rather than treating all gotu kola products as equivalent.
