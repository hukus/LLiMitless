# Antipsychotic-like pharmacological profile of the low impact ampakine CX691 (farampator): Implications for the use of low impact ampakines in the treatment of schizophrenia

- PMID: 40245529
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40245529/
- Ingested: 2026-04-30

## Summary

2025 preclinical paper on low-impact ampakine CX691 (farampator). The compound penetrated the BBB, partially offset AMPAR desensitization, reduced amphetamine- and methamphetamine-driven locomotor activity in rats, preserved antipsychotic activity under chronic amphetamine, lacked catalepsy at supratherapeutic doses, and improved eight-arm radial-maze performance. It is still preclinical and company-linked, but it adds a new low-impact branch oriented toward schizophrenia/rescue rather than broad healthy-adult enhancement.
