ClinicalTrials.gov record for NCT06554717, TRIUMPH, a recruiting Phase 2 randomized placebo-controlled tesamorelin study in adults aging with HIV, frailty or impaired physical function, and abdominal obesity. The record describes tesamorelin WR 1.28 mg subcutaneously daily plus supervised home-based exercise versus placebo plus exercise, with primary completion estimated for 2028-01-01, study completion estimated for 2028-06-30, actual study start on 2025-07-07, estimated enrollment of 100, and last update posted on 2025-07-29.
