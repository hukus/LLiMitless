# Systematic review of experimental studies in humans on transcranial temporal interference stimulation

- PMID: 40925398
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40925398/
- Published: 2025-09-22
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary
Systematic review of experimental human TI studies.
This review helps separate direct experimental human evidence from theory and animal work.
It reinforces that the field is still early and that cognition claims depend on small, heterogeneous studies.

## Notes
Useful as an evidence-structure check rather than a single-behavioral-endpoint source.
