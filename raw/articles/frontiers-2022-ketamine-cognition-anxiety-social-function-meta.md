# Ketamine effects on cognition, anxiety, and social functioning in psychiatric disorders

- Citation: Marchi et al., Frontiers in Neuroscience, 2022.
- DOI: 10.3389/fnins.2022.1011103
- Design: systematic review and meta-analysis of RCTs of ketamine or esketamine in adults with psychiatric disorders, searched through 2022-04.
- Included evidence: 22 reports; 20 trials / 1,298 participants in quantitative synthesis.

## Captured takeaways

- The review confirmed an antidepressant effect in the pooled psychiatric-disorder trial set.
- Anxiety and social-functioning outcomes moved in a favorable direction, though anxiety estimates were heterogeneous and social functioning relied on only two studies.
- The authors found no difference versus comparators for cognition or quality of life in the pooled analysis.
- The introduction distinguishes acute single-dose cognitive impairment from longer-term impairment reported mainly in higher-dose abuse contexts, while noting that depression itself carries cognitive dysfunction.
- Interpretation for the wiki: ketamine/esketamine may improve cognitive experience indirectly through mood/anxiety/social-function recovery in some patients, but the controlled RCT meta-analysis did not show a broad direct cognition benefit over comparators.
