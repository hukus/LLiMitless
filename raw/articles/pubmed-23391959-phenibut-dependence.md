# Phenibut dependence

- PMID: 23391959
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23391959/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

Case report of phenibut initially helping with anxiety, insomnia, and alcohol cravings, followed by tolerance, dependence, and withdrawal within hours of last use. This is safety-relevant for the mismatch map because phenibut often reads like a useful community nootropic until dependence becomes the dominant outcome.
