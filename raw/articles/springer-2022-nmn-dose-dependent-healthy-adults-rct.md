# Dose-Dependent NMN Supplementation in Healthy Middle-Aged Adults

- DOI: 10.1007/s11357-022-00705-1
- Published: 2022-12-08
- Checked: 2026-05-03

## Summary

GeroScience randomized, multicenter, double-blind, placebo-controlled, parallel-group dose-ranging study in 80 healthy adults aged 40-65. Participants received placebo, 300 mg, 600 mg, or 900 mg NMN once daily for 60 days.

Blood NAD concentrations increased significantly in all NMN-treated groups by days 30 and 60 versus placebo and baseline, with the highest levels in the 600 mg and 900 mg groups. No safety issues were found based on adverse events, laboratory measures, or clinical monitoring, and the trial reported tolerability up to 900 mg/day over 60 days.

Secondary proxy outcomes included 6-minute walking distance, Aging.Ai blood biological age, HOMA-IR, and SF-36 general-health scores. Walking distance improved versus placebo, while HOMA-IR did not clearly improve. The trial did not test direct cognition.

Use in the wiki: human NAD target-engagement, short-term tolerability, and physical-performance proxy source for [[nicotinamide-mononucleotide]]. It should not be used as cognitive efficacy evidence.
