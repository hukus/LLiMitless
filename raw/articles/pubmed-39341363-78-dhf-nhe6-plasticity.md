# Impaired hippocampal plasticity associated with loss of recycling endosomal SLC9A6/NHE6 is ameliorated by the TrkB agonist 7,8-dihydroxyflavone

- PMID: 39341363
- DOI: 10.1016/j.bbadis.2024.167529
- Publication: Biochimica et Biophysica Acta Molecular Basis of Disease, 2025 issue; epub 2024-09-27
- Evidence tier: preclinical mechanism / neurodevelopmental plasticity model

## Summary

This 2025 issue paper used Nhe6 knockout mice to study synaptic dysfunction relevant to SLC9A6/NHE6 loss, Christianson syndrome, and broader conditions where NHE6 expression may be altered. Knockout neurons showed reduced dendritic spine density, lower AMPA receptor expression, weaker AMPAR-mediated neurotransmission, and impaired functional and structural long-term potentiation. 7,8-DHF restored spine density and LTP readouts in the model.

This is one of the fresher TrkB / BDNF-mimetic hits in the branch. It does not provide human cognition evidence, but it points to an under-discussed responder phenotype: impaired endosomal trafficking / synaptic-plasticity machinery rather than generic low performance.
