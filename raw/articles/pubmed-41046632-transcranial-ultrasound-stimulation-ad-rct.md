# Safety and efficacy of transcranial ultrasound stimulation for the treatment of Alzheimer's disease: A randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial

- PMID: 41046632
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41046632/
- Ingested: 2026-04-29

## Summary

Pilot randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial of repeated clinic-based transcranial ultrasound stimulation in mild Alzheimer disease. Nine of 30 screened patients enrolled (six active, three placebo) and all completed 30 sessions over 6 weeks. No serious clinical or radiographic adverse events were reported. The cognitive signal was preliminary: ADAS-cog was relatively stable in the active group versus worsening in placebo at 52 weeks, and MMSE favored active treatment at 24 and 52 weeks. The study supports feasibility and short-term safety in a supervised clinical setting, but it is too small to justify any at-home protocol inference.
