# Systemic GDF11 attenuates depression-like phenotype in aged mice via stimulation of neuronal autophagy

- DOI: 10.1038/s43587-022-00352-3
- Source: https://www.nature.com/articles/s43587-022-00352-3
- Ingested: 2026-05-04

## Summary

Nature Aging 2023 mouse and biomarker study. Systemic recombinant GDF11 in aged mice improved memory measures and reduced depression-like phenotypes in a neurogenesis-independent manner. The proposed mechanism was direct hippocampal neuronal action through autophagy stimulation and reduced mTOR activity. The study also reported lower GDF11 levels in sera from young adults with major depressive disorder. This supports a pro-memory / mood-state preclinical hypothesis and a human biomarker clue, not human treatment efficacy.
