# Spadin, a sortilin-derived peptide, targeting rodent TREK-1 channels: a new concept in the antidepressant drug design

- PMID: 20405001
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20405001/
- Ingested: 2026-04-29

## Summary

Foundational preclinical paper that identified spadin as a sortilin-derived peptide acting through TREK-1 inhibition. Mouse data showed antidepressant-like behavior, increased CREB phosphorylation, and hippocampal neurogenesis after brief treatment, but there is no human cognition trial here. This is mainly the parent-mechanism anchor for PE-22-28 / mini-spadin.
