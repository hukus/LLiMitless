# CX614 increases neurotrophin expression in hippocampal and cortical neurons

Open-access Journal of Neuroscience paper on positive AMPA receptor modulators and neurotrophin expression.

Key extracted points:

- The study tested whether positive modulators of AMPA-type glutamate receptors influence neurotrophin expression in forebrain neurons.
- Treatment with the AMPAkine CX614 markedly and reversibly increased BDNF mRNA and protein in cultured rat entorhinal/hippocampal slices.
- The paper also used CX546 as a related AMPA receptor-modulating comparator.
- The authors frame positive modulation of excitatory transmission as a potential strategy for increasing neurotrophin expression in adult brain.
- Interpretation for the wiki: CX614 has a strong mechanistic BDNF/plasticity rationale, but this source is cell/slice biology rather than human cognitive efficacy.
