# MSM accumulation in the human brain by MRS

2001 Toxicology Letters paper on methylsulfonylmethane / MSM detection in human brain by multinuclear magnetic resonance spectroscopy.

Key capture:

- In vivo MRS detected and quantified MSM in the brains of four patients with memory loss and three normal volunteers who had ingested MSM at recommended doses of 1-3 g/day.
- Brain MSM concentrations were reported around 0.42-3.40 mmol/kg brain, with similar gray and white matter distribution.
- MSM was not detected in larger groups without known MSM exposure, including drug-naive normal subjects, toxic-exposure screens, and probable Alzheimer disease MRS examinations.
- The authors reported no adverse clinical or neurochemical effects in these exposed subjects, while also concluding that appearance of MSM in significant brain concentrations indicates ready transfer across an intact blood-brain barrier for a compound without established medical benefit.

Wiki use: this is a bridge-and-attribution source for [[methylsulfonylmethane-msm]]. It supports brain exposure and MRS detectability, not cognitive efficacy. It is especially useful when forum claims imply that MSM is only a joint or peripheral sulfur supplement.
