# Selegiline for Alzheimer's Disease Meta-analysis

- PMID: 11813282
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11813282/

## Summary

The 2002 meta-analysis pooled randomized placebo-controlled trials of selegiline in Alzheimer's disease. It found statistically significant short-term cognitive differences at 4-6 weeks and 8-17 weeks, but the effect disappeared at later assessments and was judged unlikely to be clinically important. The review therefore supports a short-lived rescue signal, not durable enhancement.

## Notes

- Source type: systematic review / meta-analysis.
- Evidence tier: disease-context cognition rescue.
- Claimed domain: cognition, activities of daily living, mood, and global response in AD.
- Interpretation: short-term benefit with no durable clinically meaningful effect.
- Safety / status: prescription MAOI with interaction and washout constraints.
