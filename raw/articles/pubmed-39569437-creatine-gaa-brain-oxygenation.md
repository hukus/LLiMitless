# Creatine with guanidinoacetic acid improves prefrontal brain oxygenation before, during, and after a cognitive task

- PMID: 39569437
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39569437/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Randomized crossover pilot in healthy young adults. Seven days of creatine plus guanidinoacetic acid improved prefrontal oxygen saturation indices at rest, during mindful breathing, and during recovery around a cognitive task.

## Notes

This is a biomarker-positive energy/oxygenation proxy, not a direct cognition win, but it is relevant to stack logic.
