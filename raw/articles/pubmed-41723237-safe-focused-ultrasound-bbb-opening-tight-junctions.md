# Safe focused ultrasound-mediated blood-brain barrier opening is driven primarily by transient reorganization of tight junctions

- PMID: 41723237
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41723237/
- Preprint PMID: 40502796
- Published: 2026-02-21
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

This 2026 mouse study sharpens the BBB-opening safety model: low-pressure FUS with microbubbles (450 kPa) produces transient tight-junction reorganization in arterioles and capillaries, while high pressure (750 kPa) causes persistent tight-junction obliteration, fibrinogen passage, and microglial activation at 72 hours. The result strengthens the argument that the clinically relevant question is pressure-controlled delivery safety, not just whether BBB opening occurs.
