# Role of beta-alanine supplementation on cognitive function, mood, and physical function in older adults

- PMID: 36839281
- PMCID: PMC9960300
- DOI: 10.3390/nu15040923
- Published: 2023-02-12
- Ingested: 2026-05-11

## Summary

Double-blind randomized controlled study in 100 older adults, mean age about 70.6 years, testing 10 weeks of beta-alanine supplementation at 2.4 g/day versus placebo. Participants completed MoCA, Stroop pattern-recognition testing, mood questionnaires, and physical-function testing.

Across all participants, MoCA scores did not differ between groups. In participants whose baseline MoCA scores were at or below normal, beta-alanine improved MoCA at midpoint and week 10 versus placebo. No differences were noted in mood scores, anxiety, or physical performance, while geriatric depression score decreased in the beta-alanine group.

## Relevance

This is the most direct beta-alanine cognition signal for [[beta-alanine]]. It is subgroup-limited and older-adult-biased, so it should not be generalized to healthy young preworkout users.

## Caveats

- Overall MoCA group effect was null.
- Positive result depends on baseline MoCA <= 26 subgroup.
- Funded by Natural Alternatives Inc.
- MoCA is a screening measure, not a high-resolution enhancement endpoint.
