# FDA 2025 Citizen Petition Response on NMN Dietary Supplement Status

- Docket/source: FDA-2023-P-0872-2754 attachment
- Date referenced in public reporting: 2025-09-29
- Checked: 2026-05-03

## Summary

FDA citizen-petition response concerning beta-nicotinamide mononucleotide (NMN) and the dietary supplement definition. The public PDF states that, under FDA's revised interpretation of the race-to-market clause, FDA now concludes that NMN is not excluded from the definition of dietary supplement under section 201(ff)(3)(B).

The key reasoning is timing: although NMN was authorized for investigation as a new drug and substantial clinical investigations had been instituted and made public, FDA found evidence that NMN was marketed as a dietary supplement in the United States before that authorization, as early as 2017. FDA also stated its earlier position that the product had to have been lawfully marketed was not the best reading of the statute.

Use in the wiki: regulatory status source for [[nicotinamide-mononucleotide]]. It reverses the earlier 2022 drug-preclusion concern for supplement status, but it does not mean FDA has approved NMN for safety, effectiveness, disease treatment, or cognition claims. Standard dietary-supplement quality, labeling, and adulteration cautions still apply.
