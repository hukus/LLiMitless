# Anserine and carnosine supplementation in the elderly: effects on cognitive functioning and physical capacity

- PMID: 24880197
- DOI: 10.1016/j.archger.2014.04.008
- Published: 2014
- Ingested: 2026-05-11

## Summary

Double-blind trial in 56 adults aged 65+ allocated 1:1 to chicken meat extract containing anserine and carnosine or placebo. The active product was 2.5 g/day chicken meat extract containing 40% anserine/carnosine components in a 2:1 anserine:carnosine ratio, supplying about 1 g/day of dipeptides, for 13 weeks. Fifty-one subjects participated at follow-up.

The abstract reports significant improvement only in the active group on Short Test of Mental Status mean scores, especially construction/copying, abstraction, and recall subscores. The active group also improved in two of six Senior Fitness Test measures, had lower BMI versus placebo, and had lower perceived exertion.

## Relevance

This is an early human anserine/carnosine cognition signal in older adults. It supports tracking purified or meat-extract histidine dipeptides as an aging / memory-stabilization lead, but it does not establish broad healthy-young-adult enhancement.

## Caveats

- Small trial and older-adult cohort.
- The intervention was a chicken meat extract, not pure carnosine, pure anserine, or beta-alanine.
- Cognitive signal is limited to selected STMS/subscore changes.
- Product composition and ratio matter.
