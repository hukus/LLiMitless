# Effects of psychedelic microdosing on cognitive functions: A systematic review and meta-analysis

- PMID: 41314362
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41314362/
- Ingested: 2026-04-29

## Summary

2026 preregistered meta-analysis of 14 studies (N = 1614) and 59 effect sizes. The pooled result showed a significant decrease in cognitive control, with no detectable effects on other cognitive domains or overall cognition. Neither substance type, dose, duration, nor on-drug vs off-drug timing emerged as significant moderators.
