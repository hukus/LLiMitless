# Montmorency tart-cherry powder sleep and inflammation null trial

- PMID: 39683518
- PMCID: PMC11644614
- DOI: 10.3390/nu16234125
- Source type: randomized controlled crossover trial.
- Publication: Nutrients, 2024.
- Date checked: 2026-05-03.

This randomized crossover trial recruited 34 adults with sleep issues and overweight or obesity. Participants took 500 mg Montmorency tart-cherry capsules or placebo one hour before bed for 14 days. Sleep was measured with Zmachine and Fitbit devices plus subjective sleep questionnaires, and inflammatory markers included CRP, TNF-alpha, and several interleukins.

No significant effects of Montmorency tart-cherry supplementation were observed for the sleep or inflammation measures tested. The authors concluded that studies in overweight/obesity populations may need to test higher doses than common recommendations.

Use in the wiki: product/formulation null counterweight. Do not transport juice or concentrate cognition/sleep findings to low-dose powder capsules without product and dose normalization.
