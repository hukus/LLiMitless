# Cerebrospinal fluid lactate levels along the Alzheimer's disease continuum and associations with blood-brain barrier integrity, age, cognition, and biomarkers

- PMID: 35473756
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35473756/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Large observational AD-continuum study. CSF lactate was higher in MCI-AD than in healthy controls or dementia after some adjustment, but it did not behave as a simple severity marker and tracked age and blood-brain barrier integrity more strongly than clinical stage.
