# Auditory N1 event-related potential amplitude is predictive of serum concentration of BPN14770 in fragile X syndrome

- PMID: 39488698
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39488698/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

2024 follow-up biomarker paper from the BPN14770 fragile X program. It links auditory N1 ERP amplitude to serum concentration in participants who received BPN14770, strengthening the original trial's interpretation that the drug was moving a disease-relevant neurophysiologic marker rather than only producing a noisy behavioral result.

## Key points

- Design: secondary analysis of the original fragile X trial data.
- Biomarker: auditory N1 event-related potential amplitude.
- Signal: N1 amplitude tracked with serum BPN14770 concentration in the active-drug period.
- Interpretation: supports neural hyperexcitability modulation as a plausible mechanism for the clinical signal.
- Caveat: this is biomarker validation, not a new efficacy trial.
