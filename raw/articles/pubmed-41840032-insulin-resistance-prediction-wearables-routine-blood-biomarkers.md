# Insulin Resistance Prediction from Wearables and Routine Blood Biomarkers

- PMID: 41840032
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41840032/
- Ingested: 2026-04-30

## Summary

WEAR-ME is a large remotely conducted study (n = 1,165) that used wearable time-series data plus routine blood biomarkers to train models against HOMA-IR. The main value for this wiki is state enrichment: consumer-grade physiology plus basic labs can predict insulin-resistance phenotype at scale.

## Notes

- This is not a cognition trial.
- It strengthens the case for metabolic state stratification before metabolic-brain interventions.
- Relevant to intranasal insulin, incretin, and sleep-loss state work.
