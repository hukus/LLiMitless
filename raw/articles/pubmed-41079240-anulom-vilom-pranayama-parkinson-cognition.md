# Effect of 12-week Practice of Anulom Vilom Pranayama as Adjunctive Therapy on the Cardiac Autonomic Balance, Cognition, Psychological Status, and Quality of Life in Individuals with Mild-to-Moderate Parkinson's Disease: A Randomized Controlled Trial

- PMID: 41079240
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41079240/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary
In Parkinson's disease, 12 weeks of alternate nostril breathing improved HRV, cognition, affect, stress, anxiety, and QoL versus control. This is disease-context evidence, not healthy-adult enhancement proof, but it is the clearest direct breath-protocol cognition signal in this branch.
