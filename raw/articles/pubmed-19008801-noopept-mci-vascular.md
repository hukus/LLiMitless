# Noopept in Patients With Mild Cognitive Impairment

- PMID: 19008801
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19008801/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

This Russian-language clinical trial is one of the few human sources that keeps noopept above the pure-animal layer. The abstract reports EEG changes and a nootropic-like activation pattern in patients with cognitive disturbances of posttraumatic or vascular origin, with the strongest signal in vascular disease. The result is still phenotype-bound and does not translate cleanly into healthy-adult enhancement.

## Notes

- Source type: human clinical trial.
- Evidence tier: human, but recovery / impairment-context rather than healthy enhancement.
- Claimed domain: EEG activation, cognitive disturbance, and asthenia-like symptoms.
- Interpretation: supportive of a rescue / impairment branch, not a broad nootropic recommendation.
- Safety / status: product identity and formulation quality still matter for downstream interpretation.
