# Low-impact ampakine CX717 exhibits promising therapeutic profile in adults with ADHD - A phase 2A clinical trial

- DOI: 10.1016/j.ejphar.2025.178047
- Journal: European Journal of Pharmacology
- Published: 2025-10-15
- Source: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0014299925008015
- Ingested: 2026-05-04

## Summary

2025 publication of the completed CX717 adult-ADHD phase 2A crossover trial. The trial randomized 68 adult male participants with moderate-to-severe ADHD to CX717 200 mg BID, CX717 800 mg BID, or placebo across two 3-week treatment periods with a 2-week washout. The primary paired t-test analysis showed only a trend for the 800 mg BID group on ADHD-RS total score, but a secondary repeated-measures endpoint analysis favored 800 mg BID over placebo on total ADHD-RS and inattentive and hyperactivity subscales. The 200 mg BID dose was not significant. Treatment was reported as well tolerated, with sleep disturbance and headache the most frequent adverse events and no significant cardiovascular changes.

This is the strongest newer human signal for CX717, but it is an adult-ADHD symptom study, not healthy-adult cognitive enhancement. The interpretation remains sponsor-linked and analysis-sensitive because the prespecified primary paired t-test did not reach significance while the secondary repeated-measures analysis did.
