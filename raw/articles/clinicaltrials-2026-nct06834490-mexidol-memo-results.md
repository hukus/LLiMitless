# ClinicalTrials.gov NCT06834490: Mexidol MEMO chronic cerebral ischemia results

ClinicalTrials.gov record NCT06834490 is the MEMO study: a Pharmasoft-sponsored international multicenter phase 3 randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled parallel trial of sequential Mexidol therapy in chronic cerebral ischemia. The registry lists 318 randomized participants, study start 2019-11-05, completion 2020-12-08, results first posted 2025-10-06, and last update posted 2025-10-06.

Intervention: Mexidol IV 500 mg once daily for 14 days, then Mexidol FORTE 250 mg orally three times daily for 60 days. Comparator: matched placebo IV and tablets on the same schedule.

Primary cognitive outcome: MoCA at Visit 5 / day 75+2. The registry reports MoCA 25.72 (SD 2.47) for Mexidol and 23.95 (SD 2.58) for placebo, with change from baseline 4.22 (SD 2.59) vs 2.17 (SD 2.20). Secondary MoCA changes at Visit 2 and Visit 4 also favored Mexidol: +2.30 vs +1.31 and +3.54 vs +1.98.

Other cognition and state outcomes: DSST change at Visit 5 was +9.84 (SD 10.50) for Mexidol versus +5.40 (SD 10.15) for placebo. Fatigue, Beck anxiety, SF-36 mental health, autonomic questionnaire, Tinetti, and CGI-style global improvement also numerically favored Mexidol in the registry tables.

Safety: the registry reports no deaths and no serious adverse events in either group. Other adverse events affected 19/159 Mexidol participants and 32/159 placebo participants; Mexidol-group events included dyspepsia, itchy throat/nose, rash, arthralgia, dizziness, leukocyturia, nausea, and dysgeusia.

Interpretation for the wiki: this is the main modern formal signal, but it is a regional, industry-sponsored clinical-state CCI trial, not a healthy-adult cognitive-enhancement study.
