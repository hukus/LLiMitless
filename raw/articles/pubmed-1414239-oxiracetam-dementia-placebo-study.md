# Oxiracetam in dementia: a double-blind, placebo-controlled study

- Source type: PubMed abstract for multicentre double-blind placebo-controlled dementia trial.
- Publication: Acta Neurologica Scandinavica, 1992.
- Date checked: 2026-05-03.

This multicentre study tested oxiracetam 800 mg twice daily versus placebo for 12 weeks in patients with primary degenerative, multi-infarct, or mixed dementia. Sixty-five patients were enrolled and 58 completed treatment.

The abstract reports a significant effect favoring oxiracetam on quality of life and selected neuropsychological tests, including controlled associations and short-story measures. Adverse events were reported by four oxiracetam patients and one placebo patient; some withdrawals occurred for poor tolerability, compliance, a transient ischemic attack judged unrelated, and administrative reasons.

Use in the wiki: supportive but old disease-context oxiracetam evidence. It does not establish healthy-adult enhancement.
