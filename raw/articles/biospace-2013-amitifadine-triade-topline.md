# Amitifadine TRIADE phase 2b/3a topline failure

- Source: BioSpace republication of Euthymics Bioscience top-line TRIADE announcement, dated 2013-05-29.
- TRIADE was a phase 2b/3a clinical study of amitifadine in MDD patients who failed to respond to one course of first-line antidepressants.
- Result: amitifadine at 50 mg or 100 mg did not show a statistically significant difference from placebo on the primary endpoint of change in MADRS.
- The release states that the active comparator arm suggested the study worked, and that the tested amitifadine doses may have been too low.
- Interpretation for the wiki: this is the main counterweight to the 2012 positive proof-of-concept signal. Amitifadine should not be treated as a validated antidepressant or cognition lead.
