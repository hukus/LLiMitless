# Effects on nonverbal numerical acuity performance after a single-session transient random noise stimulation over the intraparietal sulcus or dorsolateral prefrontal cortex

- DOI: 10.1038/s41598-025-21890-x
- Nature: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-025-21890-x
- Ingested: 2026-04-30

## Summary

Double-blind crossover RCT in 39 healthy university students after preregistered exclusions. The study tested single-session tRNS over the intraparietal sulcus or dorsolateral prefrontal cortex against sham on a multidirectional number-line estimation task. The main analysis found no significant treatment effect on accuracy or reaction time after covariate adjustment, so the branch should treat this as a null on nonverbal numerical acuity rather than a positive cognition result.

## Notes

This is a useful counterweight for the numerical-cognition sub-branch. The study also included working-memory and math-ability screening, but the primary numerical-acuity result stayed null.
