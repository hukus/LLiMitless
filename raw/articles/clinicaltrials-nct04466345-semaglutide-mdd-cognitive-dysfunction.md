# NCT04466345: Semaglutide for the Treatment of Cognitive Dysfunction in Major Depressive Disorder

- ClinicalTrials.gov ID: NCT04466345
- Sponsor: University Health Network, Toronto
- Status: Completed
- Last update posted: 2025-10-02
- Results posted: No

## Summary

Randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled phase 2 trial of adjunctive oral semaglutide in overweight adults with MDD and baseline cognitive impairment. The registry prespecified an executive-function composite as the primary outcome and reported completion without posted results.

## Notes

The registry matches the published paper's primary executive-function framing, so the main credibility issue is endpoint interpretation rather than a registry mismatch.
