# Psychopharmacology of theobromine in healthy volunteers

- PMID: 23420115
- DOI: 10.1007/s00213-013-3021-0
- Journal: Psychopharmacology (2013)

## Captured takeaways

- Placebo-controlled, within-subjects, double-blind study of oral theobromine in healthy adult volunteers.
- The study tested 250 mg, 500 mg, and 1000 mg theobromine against placebo and an active 200 mg caffeine control over five 6-hour sessions separated by at least 48 hours.
- PubMed / open snippets report 84 healthy adult volunteers participated, with 80 included in the search-result summary.
- The paper characterized mood, cognitive performance, and physiological measures.
- Theobromine showed dose-dependent subjective effects: limited positive subjective effects at 250 mg, but more negative mood effects at higher intakes.
- Interpretation for the wiki: this is the direct human theobromine-alone anchor. It does not make theobromine a caffeine-equivalent nootropic; it supports low-confidence tracking and dose/tolerability gates.
