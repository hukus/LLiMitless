# Effects of caffeine, methylliberine, and theacrine on vigilance, marksmanship, and hemodynamic responses in tactical personnel

- DOI: 10.1080/15502783.2022.2113339
- Journal: Journal of the International Society of Sports Nutrition
- Ingested: 2026-05-03

## Summary

2022 double-blind, randomized, placebo-controlled tactical-personnel trial testing placebo, 300 mg caffeine, or a combination of 150 mg caffeine, 100 mg methylliberine, and 50 mg theacrine. The study evaluated reaction time, vigilance, marksmanship, hemodynamics, and arousal during a 150-minute protocol with sustained vigilance and movement/marksmanship tasks.

Use in the wiki: combination-product and state-challenge evidence. It supports the real-world tactical-performance relevance of caffeine + methylliberine + theacrine stacks, but it cannot attribute effects to methylliberine alone.
