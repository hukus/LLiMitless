# MemAID: Memory advancement with intranasal insulin vs. placebo in type 2 diabetes and control participants: a randomized clinical trial

- PMID: 35482079
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35482079/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Longer phase 2 trial in older adults with and without type 2 diabetes that found phenotype-separated cognitive and gait benefits.

## Notes

Best current trial for the insulin-resistance layer. T2DM participants improved gait and showed lower HOMA-IR, while non-diabetic controls improved executive function and verbal memory.
