# MFGM Polar Lipids Cognition Review

- Article: Milk fat globule membrane and its polar lipids: reviewing preclinical and clinical trials on cognition.
- Journal: Food & Function, 2024;15:6783-6797.
- DOI: https://doi.org/10.1039/D4FO00659C
- PMID: 38828877.
- Date checked: 2026-05-03.

## Summary

Narrative review of milk fat globule membrane (MFGM) and polar lipids for cognition across neurodevelopment and aging. The review frames MFGM as an abundant source of polar lipids, especially phospholipids and sphingolipids, from dairy streams such as buttermilk and whey-related byproducts.

The review is favorable to MFGM as a bioactive lipid source, but it repeatedly emphasizes that preclinical and clinical evidence remains insufficient for firm health claims. It concludes that large, adequately sized randomized controlled trials of extended duration are needed before health-related statements can be made.

Mechanistic and identity notes: MFGM is not one molecule. It is a complex tri-layer membrane fraction with phospholipids, sphingolipids such as sphingomyelin, gangliosides, cholesterol, proteins, and glycoproteins. The review notes that different commercial or lab-made products do not have identical lipid composition, making product identity and intervention duration important.
