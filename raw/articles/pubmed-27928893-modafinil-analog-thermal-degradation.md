# Thermal degradation and forensic detection of modafinil analogs

- PubMed record for a 2017 Drug Testing and Analysis paper on GC-injector thermal degradation of modafinil, modafinic acid, adrafinil, CRL-40,940, and CRL-40,941.
- The paper is relevant because CRL-40,940 and CRL-40,941 are the flmodafinil / fladrafinil-style analogs that appear in nootropic and research-chemical markets.
- The title reports formation of 1,1,2,2-tetraphenylethane and a tetra-fluoro analog during thermal degradation, which matters for forensic and product-identity interpretation.
- Interpretation for the wiki: this is an identity / analytical-chemistry anchor, not human cognition evidence. It supports keeping unapproved modafinil analog claims separate from prescription [[modafinil]] and [[adrafinil]].
