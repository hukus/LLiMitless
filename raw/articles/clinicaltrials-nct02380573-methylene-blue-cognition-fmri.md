# Effects of Methylene Blue in Healthy Aging, Mild Cognitive Impairment and Alzheimer's Disease

- ClinicalTrials.gov ID: NCT02380573
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Phase 2 double-blind placebo-controlled study of oral USP methylene blue in healthy aging, MCI, and mild AD. The registry specifies 282 mg daily dosing over 2-week and 12-week periods, with fMRI, working-memory, face-name, PVT, CBF, resting-state connectivity, MMSE, and CLOX endpoints. Exclusions included G6PD deficiency, serotonergic medications, pregnancy/breastfeeding, renal/hepatic disease, MRI contraindications, and hypersensitivity to MB or related dye classes. Results were posted in 2024, but this capture records the design and eligibility layer.
