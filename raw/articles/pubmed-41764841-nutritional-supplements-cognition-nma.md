# Nutritional supplements and cognition in healthy aging and mild cognitive impairment patients: a systematic review and network meta-analysis

- PMID: 41764841
- PMCID: PMC12966656
- DOI: 10.1016/j.tjpad.2026.100518
- Published: 2026-02-27
- Source type: systematic review and network meta-analysis of RCTs
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41764841/
- Ingested: 2026-05-03

## Summary

2026 network meta-analysis of randomized trials in healthy aging and mild cognitive impairment. The authors report domain-specific rather than universal effects: DHA+EPA+vitamin E+tryptophan+melatonin and melatonin alone improved global cognition; DHA, folic acid+DHA, and the multi-nutrient combination improved memory; DHA improved processing speed; and vitamin D3 improved visuospatial function. Sensitivity analyses found that some rankings were sensitive to study duration and sample size.

## Notes

- This source broadens the molecule atlas beyond classic nootropics into nutrient and sleep-linked molecules.
- It should not upgrade any single supplement to a broad enhancer; the authors emphasize baseline cognitive status, age, intervention duration, and the need for higher-quality longitudinal trials.
