# The D-amino acid oxidase inhibitor luvadaxistat improves mismatch negativity in patients with schizophrenia in a randomized trial

- PMID: 36928351
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36928351/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

This 8-day crossover biomarker study in 31 schizophrenia patients found no effect on eyeblink conditioning, but 50 mg luvadaxistat showed nominal improvement in mismatch negativity and a trend on 40 Hz ASSR. The result supports target engagement, not a general cognition claim.
