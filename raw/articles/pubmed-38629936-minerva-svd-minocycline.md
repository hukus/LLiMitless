# MINERVA minocycline cerebral small vessel disease trial

2024 phase II randomized double-blind placebo-controlled experimental medicine trial in 44 participants with moderate-to-severe cerebral small vessel disease. Participants took minocycline or placebo for 3 months. Co-primary outcomes were microglial signal by 11C-PK11195 PET and blood-brain-barrier permeability by dynamic contrast-enhanced MRI.

Minocycline did not reduce microglial PET signal, blood-brain-barrier permeability, or serum inflammatory markers. The paper concluded that these imaging measures are feasible in small-vessel-disease trials but minocycline did not modify the targeted processes.

Interpretation for the wiki: this is a disease-relevant mechanistic counterweight. It does not address long-COVID brain fog directly, but it weakens the assumption that oral minocycline reliably quiets human microglial/BBB neuroinflammation.

Relevance: negative human mechanism trial for [[minocycline]] and [[neuroinflammation-and-cognition]].
