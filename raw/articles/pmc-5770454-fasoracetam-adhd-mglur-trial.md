# Fasoracetam in adolescents with ADHD and glutamatergic gene network variants

- Source type: open-access Nature Communications clinical study.
- Publication date: 2018-01-16.
- Date checked: 2026-05-03.

This five-week study tested fasoracetam / NFC-1 in 30 adolescents aged 12-17 with ADHD and disruptive copy-number variants in metabotropic glutamate receptor network genes. It was open-label after a fixed single-blind placebo week, with pharmacokinetic profiling from 50-800 mg and dose escalation up to 400 mg twice daily.

The study reported significant improvement on Clinical Global Impressions measures and parent Vanderbilt scores in subjects with Tier 1 mGluR variants. Adverse events were generally mild, and the authors reported no difference in adverse-event incidence between the placebo week and active-drug weeks.

Use in the wiki: genotype-enriched ADHD / mGluR signal, not evidence for broad healthy-adult cognition enhancement. The design is useful but not a definitive randomized efficacy trial.
