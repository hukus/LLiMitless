# Deoxygedunin, a natural product with potent neurotrophic activity in mice

- DOI: 10.1371/journal.pone.0011528
- Publication: PLOS ONE, 2010
- Evidence tier: preclinical discovery / natural-product TrkB agonist claim

## Summary

This open-access PLOS ONE paper reports deoxygedunin, a gedunin-family natural product from neem-related chemistry, as a TrkB-activating BDNF mimetic. The authors describe TrkB and downstream signaling activation, oral activity in mouse brain, protection of neurons from cell death in a TrkB-dependent manner, and neuroprotective, antidepressant-like, and learning-enhancement effects in mice.

The important wiki interpretation is that deoxygedunin is a named molecule in the TrkB-mimetic family, but its cognition relevance is preclinical and heavily dependent on the specificity of the TrkB-agonism claim.
