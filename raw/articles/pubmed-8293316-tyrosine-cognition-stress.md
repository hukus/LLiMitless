# Source Digest

Type: randomized placebo-controlled crossover-style clinical trial in healthy young adults.

Publication date: 1994.

Key extraction:
- Intervention: 100 mg/kg tyrosine versus placebo, tested on separate days.
- Population: 16 healthy young subjects.
- Stressor: 90 dB noise during stress-sensitive cognitive tasks.
- Result: tyrosine improved performance on two cognitive tasks characterized as highly stress-sensitive, measured one hour after administration.
- Cardiovascular result: tyrosine decreased diastolic blood pressure 15 minutes after ingestion; one hour after ingestion diastolic blood pressure did not differ from placebo. No mood, systolic blood pressure, or heart-rate effects were found.

Wiki signal:
- Medicinal signal: low-moderate for acute stress-state cognition support.
- Confidence limiter: small old study, acute high dose, stress/noise condition, and task-specific effects.
