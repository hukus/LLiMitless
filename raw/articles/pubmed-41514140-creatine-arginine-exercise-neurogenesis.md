# The Emerging Role of L-Arginine and Creatine Supplementation Combined with Exercise on Hippocampal Neurogenesis, Spatial Learning, and Cognitive Flexibility in Aging and Neurodegenerative Diseases.

- PMID: 41514140
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41514140/
- Ingested: 2026-04-26

## Summary

Review on creatine + L-arginine + exercise and potential hippocampal neurogenesis/cognitive effects.

## Notes

Hippocampal neurogenesis is a pivotal process for cognitive adaptability, memory consolidation, and the regulation of emotions, all of which exhibit a decline as individuals age and contribute to the onset of neurodegenerative and mood disorders. Recent empirical studies underscore the promising effects of creatine and L-arginine supplementation, in conjunction with physical exercise, in enhancing hippocampal neuroplasticity and fostering neurogenesis.
