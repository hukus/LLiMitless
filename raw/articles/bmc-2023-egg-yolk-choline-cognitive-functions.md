# Effects of egg yolk choline intake on cognitive functions and plasma choline levels in healthy middle-aged and older Japanese

- DOI: 10.1186/s12944-023-01844-w
- Journal: Lipids in Health and Disease
- Year: 2023
- Ingested: 2026-05-04

## Summary

Twelve-week randomized double-blind placebo-controlled parallel-group trial in healthy Japanese adults aged 60-80 without dementia but with forgetfulness concerns. The choline group received 300 mg/day egg-yolk choline, a food matrix rich in phosphatidylcholine, lysophosphatidylcholine, and Alpha-GPC forms. Forty-one participants were analyzed after exclusions. Verbal-memory scores and delayed correct hits were significantly higher in the choline group than placebo at 6 and 12 weeks, and plasma free choline was higher at 6 weeks. However, processing-speed scores, symbol-digit-coding correct responses, and SF-36 physical quality-of-life summary scores were lower in the choline group at 6 weeks. TMT-A and TMT-B did not differ.

## Notes

- Use as a product-matrix signal for egg-yolk phospholipid choline, not as proof that generic lecithin or choline bitartrate improves cognition.
- The study was funded by Kewpie Corporation; sponsor/product identity matters.
- Signal is narrow verbal memory in older adults with forgetfulness concerns, with mixed secondary outcomes.
