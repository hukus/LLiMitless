# A vitamin/nutriceutical formulation improves memory and cognitive performance in community-dwelling adults without dementia

2010 Journal of Nutrition, Health & Aging paper testing a multinutrient formulation in community-dwelling adults without dementia. The formulation included folic acid, vitamin B12, vitamin E, S-adenosylmethionine, N-acetylcysteine, and acetyl-L-carnitine.

The abstract reports that participants receiving the formulation, but not placebo, improved statistically and clinically on the California Verbal Learning Test II and Trail-Making Test. Both groups improved during an open-label extension. A withdrawal/resumption sequence suggested performance declined toward baseline after stopping the formulation and improved again after resuming it. Shorter-term testing also showed improvements on Trail-Making and Digit-Memory tests.

This is not a SAMe monotherapy trial. It is a formulation result combining one-carbon, antioxidant, mitochondrial, and vitamin components. It can justify a methyl-donor / redox / mitochondrial combination watch item but cannot attribute benefit to SAMe, B12, folate, NAC, ALCAR, or vitamin E alone.
