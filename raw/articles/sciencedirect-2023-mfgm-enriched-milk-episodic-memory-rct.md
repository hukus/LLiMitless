# MFGM-Enriched Milk Episodic Memory Pilot RCT

- Article: Milk fat globule membrane-enriched milk improves episodic memory: A randomized, parallel, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial in older adults.
- Journal: Journal of Functional Foods, 2023;111:105849.
- DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jff.2023.105849
- Date checked: 2026-05-03.

## Summary

Small randomized, parallel, double-blind, placebo-controlled pilot trial of an MFGM-enriched milk drink in older adults. Forty-four adults older than 65 years, either cognitively healthy or mildly cognitively impaired, received MFGM-enriched milk or control milk for 14 weeks; 40 completed the study.

The abstract and full-text summary report an improvement in episodic memory among female participants, specifically short-delay cued-recall episodic verbal memory. Other cognitive domains, including attention, processing speed, visuospatial abilities, verbal fluency, executive function, and working memory, did not show broad benefit.

Safety/tolerability notes: the study reported minimal changes in liver enzymes, creatinine, blood pressure, and related safety measures. Constipation and bloating were common recorded symptoms during the intervention period, while other side effects were uncommon. Product acceptability was rated positively by most volunteers.

Interpretation: this is a small positive pilot and sex-subgroup signal, not a broad cognition proof. It provides a reason to keep MFGM phospholipids visible, but the newer 2025 larger RCT with null RBANS and COMPASS outcomes should govern the overall confidence rating.
