# Ladasten neurasthenia EEG phenotype study

- PMID: 22834121
- Randomized controlled trial in neurasthenia comparing ladasten with placebo.
- The study focused on how subjective and objective effects varied by EEG alpha rhythm pattern and MMPI traits.
- Reduced alpha activity plus emotional lability / inertness looked like the more responsive phenotype.
- Self-assessment of tolerability and desire to continue treatment was less phenotype-dependent than the efficacy signal.
- Best read: a possible responder-phenotype clue, but still in a symptom-state setting with strong placebo sensitivity.
