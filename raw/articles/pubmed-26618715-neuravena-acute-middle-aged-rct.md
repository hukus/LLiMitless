# Acute effects of wild green-oat extract on cognitive function in middle-aged adults

- Source type: double-blind placebo-controlled within-subjects trial.
- Publication: Nutritional Neuroscience, 2017.
- Date checked: 2026-05-03.

This study tested single doses of wild green-oat extract / Neuravena in healthy adults aged 40-65 who self-reported age-related memory decline. Participants attended six occasions and received placebo, 800 mg, or 1600 mg green-oat extract in a counterbalanced repeated crossover schedule.

The 800 mg dose increased speed of performance across timed tasks, reduced errors on delayed word recall, improved an executive-function Peg and Ball task by reducing thinking and completion time, and improved Corsi Blocks working-memory span only on the second exposure. The abstract interpretation suggested that the optimal acute dose may be at or below 800 mg.

Use in the wiki: product-specific acute Neuravena cognition anchor. It supports acute processing-speed / executive-task interest, not durable or generic oat-straw benefit.
