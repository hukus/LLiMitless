# Trintellix vortioxetine DailyMed label

DailyMed label snapshot for Trintellix / vortioxetine tablets, updated 2026-02-17.

Safety-relevant label points for cognition routing: Trintellix is a prescription antidepressant indicated for major depressive disorder in adults. The boxed warning covers suicidal thoughts and behaviors in pediatric and young-adult patients taking antidepressants and emphasizes monitoring. The label warns about serotonin syndrome, especially with serotonergic agents but also alone; increased bleeding risk with NSAIDs, aspirin, anticoagulants, or other agents affecting coagulation; activation of mania/hypomania; angle-closure glaucoma; hyponatremia/SIADH; sexual dysfunction; and pregnancy/newborn concerns.

The label lists common adverse reactions as nausea, constipation, and vomiting, and notes dose adjustments for strong CYP2D6 inhibitors, CYP inducers, and known CYP2D6 poor metabolizers.
