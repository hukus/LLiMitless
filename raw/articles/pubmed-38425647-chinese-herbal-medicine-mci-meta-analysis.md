# The efficacy and safety of Chinese herbal medicine for mild cognitive impairment: a systematic review and meta-analysis of randomized placebo-controlled trials

- PMID: 38425647
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38425647/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

2024 meta-analysis of 13 placebo-controlled trials in MCI found moderate-to-low certainty MMSE and MoCA improvements for Chinese herbal medicine overall, with gastrointestinal symptoms the most common adverse events. The branch is promising but heterogeneous, and the paper still points to ginkgo leaf tablets as the most reproducible subgroup signal.
