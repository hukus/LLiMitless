# The Lactate Nexus: A Molecular Bridge Linking Physical Activity, Sleep, and Cognitive Enhancement

- PMID: 41595786
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41595786/
- Ingested: 2026-04-30

## Summary

Review article that reframes lactate as a mediator connecting exercise and sleep to cognitive resilience. It argues for roles in NMDA signaling, HCAR1 signaling, and histone lactylation, and it specifically links lactate to slow-wave sleep, memory consolidation, and glymphatic clearance. This is a mechanistic synthesis, not a direct cognition trial.
