# ADAPT-232 acute cognition trial in stressed healthy adults

2010 Phytomedicine double-blind placebo-controlled randomized pilot trial of ADAPT-232 in stressed/tired healthy women.

ADAPT-232 is a standardized fixed combination of Rhodiola rosea, Schisandra chinensis, and Eleutherococcus senticosus extracts. The pilot trial enrolled 40 healthy females aged 20-68 who reported chronic psychological stress. Participants received a single ADAPT-232 tablet, 270 mg, or placebo. A Stroop Color-Word task was used to induce/exhaust cognitive stress before the d2 Test of Attention.

The abstract reports a significant treatment advantage on d2 attention, speed, and accuracy two hours after dosing, with a tendency toward fewer errors. No serious side effects were reported, though sleepiness and cold extremities appeared as minor adverse events in both groups.

Interpretation for the wiki: ADAPT-232 supports an acute stress/tiredness-state cognition signal for a fixed adaptogen formula. It does not prove that isolated Schisandra, isolated Eleuthero, or isolated Rhodiola produces the same effect in rested healthy adults.
