# Bright light therapy influences glymphatic system function in individuals with subthreshold depression: a randomized clinical trial

- PMID: 41672157
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41672157/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

2026 randomized placebo-controlled trial in subthreshold depression (N=110). Morning bright light therapy for 8 weeks improved depressive symptoms and increased glymphatic markers (DTI-ALPS) while reducing free water. The abstract does not report a direct cognition endpoint, so this is glymphatic biomarker evidence rather than cognition proof.
