PubMed abstract for the 2024 Molecular Psychiatry paper on SHLP2 in Parkinson's disease. The study identifies the mtSNP m.2158 T>C within the SHLP2 open reading frame, shows that the variant produces SHLP2 K4R, detects SHLP2 fragments in neuronal cells, demonstrates binding to mitochondrial complex I, and reports association with reduced PD risk. This is brain-relevant translational biology, but it is not a cognition intervention study.
