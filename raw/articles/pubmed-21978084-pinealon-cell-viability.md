# Pinealon increases cell viability by suppression of free radical levels and activating proliferative processes

- PMID: 21978084
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21978084/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

Pinealon reduced ROS accumulation and necrotic cell death in cerebellar granule cells, neutrophils, and PC12 cells under oxidative stress. The signal is mechanistic and preclinical.
