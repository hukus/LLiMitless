# Enhanced uridine bioavailability following triacetyluridine-rich supplement administration

- PMCID: PMC3040752
- Journal: PLOS ONE (2011)

## Captured takeaways

- Single- and multi-dose pharmacokinetic studies of NucleomaxX were compared with single-dose studies of equimolar pure uridine in healthy volunteers.
- Product analysis found that more than 90% of the nucleoside component of NucleomaxX was triacetyluridine.
- The paper reports enhanced plasma uridine exposure after the triacetyluridine-rich product compared with expectations from pure uridine, while noting differences in analytic techniques and study designs as interpretation caveats.
- Interpretation for the wiki: triacetyluridine / TAU and uridine monophosphate / UMP should not be treated as interchangeable in anecdote or dosing discussions; this is a form and PK anchor, not cognition efficacy evidence.
