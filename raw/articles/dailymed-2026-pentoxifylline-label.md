# DailyMed pentoxifylline label safety context

DailyMed pentoxifylline extended-release labels identify pentoxifylline as a prescription hemorrheologic xanthine derivative indicated for intermittent claudication due to chronic occlusive arterial disease of the limbs.

Contraindications include recent cerebral and/or retinal hemorrhage and previous intolerance to pentoxifylline or methylxanthines such as caffeine, theophylline, and theobromine. Precautions include anaphylactic/anaphylactoid reactions, occasional angina/hypotension/arrhythmia reports, bleeding monitoring with warfarin or other hemorrhage risk factors, increased exposure in hepatic or renal impairment, drug interactions with anticoagulants, theophylline, CYP1A2 inhibitors, and cimetidine. Usual labeled dosing is 400 mg extended-release three times daily with meals, with dose reduction or discontinuation for dose-related digestive and CNS side effects.
