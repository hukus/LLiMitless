# REGEN-BRAIN allopregnanolone 2026 status
- ClinicalTrials.gov ID: NCT04838301.
- Status: recruiting; last update posted 2026-03-13.
- Design: phase 2, double-blind, randomized, placebo-controlled trial in mild AD dementia.
- Enrollment: 100 estimated participants, ages 55-80, plasma p-tau217 positive.
- Intervention: allopregnanolone 4 mg IV once weekly for 6 months vs placebo, then placebo crossover to open-label allopregnanolone for 3 months.
- Primary focus: restore structural integrity and cognitive function; registry notes brain imaging at baseline, 3 months, and 6 months.
- Branch readout: the program is still active and is now the main allopregnanolone efficacy test.
