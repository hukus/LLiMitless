# Increasing target engagement via customized electrode positioning for personalized transcranial electrical stimulation: A biophysical modeling study

- PMID: 40228684
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40228684/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

In silico biophysical modeling study showing that digitally designed and 3D-printed TES caps with flexible electrode positioning can increase target engagement and allow tunable dose delivery to a cortical target. This is a pre-efficacy example of the "target reached" rung, not a human cognition result.
