# WADA 2026 prohibited list

- The 2026 List took effect on 2026-01-01 and is updated annually.
- S0 covers non-approved substances with no current approval by a governmental health authority for human therapeutic use.
- S6.B explicitly names stimulants including methylphenidate and solriamfetol.
