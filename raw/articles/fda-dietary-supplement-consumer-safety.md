# FDA consumer information on dietary supplements

FDA consumer page explaining that under DSHEA, FDA is not authorized to approve dietary supplements for safety and effectiveness before they are marketed. Firms are responsible for ensuring safety and truthful labeling, while FDA can act against unsafe or illegal products after market entry.

Relevance: general regulatory anchor for supplement branches such as shilajit, black seed, gotu kola, and other product-quality-sensitive extracts. It should not be used as a molecule-specific safety proof.
