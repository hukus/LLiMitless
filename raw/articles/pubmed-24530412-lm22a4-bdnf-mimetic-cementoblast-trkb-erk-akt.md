# BDNF mimetic compound LM22A-4 regulates cementoblast differentiation via the TrkB-ERK/Akt signaling cascade

- PMID: 24530412
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24530412/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Notes

2014 preclinical paper describing LM22A-4 as a BDNF mimetic that facilitates TrkB signaling in hippocampal-neuron assays and regulates cementoblast differentiation via TrkB-ERK/Akt signaling. This is a tool-compound / mechanism paper, not cognition efficacy evidence.

## Relevance

- Supports the idea that LM22A-4 is a real TrkB-pathway probe.
- No human cognition or cognition-adjacent trial evidence in this paper.
- Helpful comparator for the broader BDNF-mimetic branch.
