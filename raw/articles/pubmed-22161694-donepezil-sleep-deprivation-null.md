# Donepezil cognitive performance after sleep deprivation null study

Human Psychopharmacology study of 30 healthy volunteers given donepezil 5 mg/day or placebo for 14-17 days, then tested after either 24 hours sleep deprivation or a normal night in a non-blinded crossover state challenge.

Sleep deprivation impaired several memory-sensitive tasks. Donepezil partially reversed subjective-alertness decline but did not significantly reverse cognitive-performance decline on the tested tasks.

Interpretation for the wiki: direct counterweight to the positive sleep-deprivation-vulnerability study. For [[donepezil]], sleep-loss rescue remains inconsistent and task/responder-sensitive.
