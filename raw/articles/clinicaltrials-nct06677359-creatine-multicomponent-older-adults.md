# Impact of Creatine Monohydrate Micronized Supplementation With or Without Multicomponent Training in Older Adults.

- ClinicalTrials.gov ID: NCT06677359
- ClinicalTrials.gov: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06677359
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Recruiting study in adults over 75 testing creatine supplementation with and without multicomponent training for functional and cognitive capacity.

## Notes

This is an older-adult comparator trial that may help separate creatine-alone effects from exercise synergy.
