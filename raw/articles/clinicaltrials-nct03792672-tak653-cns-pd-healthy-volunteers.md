# TAK-653 CNS pharmacodynamic activity in healthy volunteers using TMS

- ClinicalTrials.gov: NCT03792672
- Sponsor: Neurocrine Biosciences
- Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03792672
- Ingested: 2026-05-04

## Summary

ClinicalTrials.gov registry record for a completed randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled, three-period crossover phase 1 study of TAK-653 in 24 healthy volunteers, followed by an open-label ketamine comparator period. The study tested TAK-653 0.5 mg and 6 mg single doses and used transcranial magnetic stimulation to assess CNS excitability, with NeuroCart measures in the associated publication.

This is a target-engagement / CNS pharmacodynamic study. It supports the claim that TAK-653 can move measurable human CNS physiology, but it does not establish clinical or healthy-adult cognition efficacy.
