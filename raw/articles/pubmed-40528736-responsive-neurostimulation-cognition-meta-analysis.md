# Neuropsychological and neurobehavioral outcomes of responsive neurostimulation in epilepsy: A systematic review and meta-analysis

This 2025 systematic review/meta-analysis treats responsive neurostimulation (RNS) as a deployed closed-loop epilepsy device and asks what happens to cognition, mood, and quality of life over time. The key value for the wiki is that it puts formal neuropsychological outcomes on a real closed-loop system rather than a laboratory prototype.

The evidence is still disease-context and heterogeneous, so it is better read as a safety-and-trajectory source than as proof of healthy cognitive enhancement. It nevertheless matters because it shows that closed-loop neuromodulation can be evaluated with memory, attention, and executive-function endpoints in humans.
