# Augmenting Cognitive Function in the Elderly with Mild Cognitive Impairment Using Probiotic Lacticaseibacillus rhamnosus CBT-LR5: A 12-Week Randomized, Double-Blind, Parallel-Group Non-Comparative Study

- PMID: 40005019
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40005019/

## Summary

2025 small MCI trial with only 20 participants. Low-dose LR5 improved MOCA-K naming and delayed recall, while the high-dose arm did not show the same signal; the sample is too small for a durable efficacy claim.
