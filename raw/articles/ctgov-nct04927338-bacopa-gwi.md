# Study of Bacopa in Gulf War Illness Patients

- ClinicalTrials.gov ID: NCT04927338
- Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04927338
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Phase II randomized placebo-controlled trial of Bacopa for Gulf War Illness. Cognitive function is an efficacy endpoint alongside safety, making this a near-term translational signal for a standardized traditional-medicine extract in a phenotype with fatigue, sleep, and cognitive complaints.
