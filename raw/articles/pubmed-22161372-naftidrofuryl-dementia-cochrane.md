# Naftidrofuryl for dementia

PubMed-indexed Cochrane review of naftidrofuryl for dementia. Naftidrofuryl is a 5-HT2 antagonist and vasoactive drug once studied for dementia and cognitive impairment.

The source is useful as a legacy comparator but does not establish modern cognitive enhancement. The interpretation should remain low-confidence and clinical-state-bound.

Relevance: route naftidrofuryl as an old vasoactive dementia lead, not a nootropic recommendation.
