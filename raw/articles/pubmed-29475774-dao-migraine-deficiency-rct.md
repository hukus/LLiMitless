# DAO supplement in episodic migraine patients with DAO deficiency

- PMID: 29475774
- Published online: 2018; journal issue 2019
- Source type: randomized double-blind trial
- Checked: 2026-05-11
- Ingested: 2026-05-11

## Summary

This trial tested a diamine oxidase supplement in episodic migraine patients with DAO deficiency. The abstract frames histamine intolerance as reduced intestinal degradation of histamine and notes headache as a common histamine-related symptom. Current strategies discussed include low-histamine diet and exogenous DAO supplementation.

Use in the wiki: this source supports the DAO/histamine headache and deficiency phenotype, which can be adjacent to brain-fog self-reports. It does not establish direct cognition benefit.

## Interpretation

DAO evidence should be routed through histamine-intolerance symptoms, headache/migraine phenotype, meal timing, and measured DAO where available. It should not be treated as a general focus supplement.
