# Transcranial focused ultrasound in the human brain

- PMID: 41610842
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41610842/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

This 2026 review separates transcranial focused ultrasound into three human applications: high-intensity lesioning, low-intensity blood-brain barrier opening, and low-intensity circuit stimulation. It frames the modality as a set of distinct clinical tools rather than one uniform intervention and emphasizes the translational potential of each branch.
