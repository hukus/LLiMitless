# Luye LY03005 phase 3 endpoint announcement

- Source: Luye Life Sciences / Luye Pharma announcement that LY03005 met predefined endpoints in a phase 3 trial for major depressive disorder.
- The announcement describes LY03005 as an SNDRI: a serotonin, norepinephrine, and dopamine reuptake inhibitor.
- It states that both 80 mg and 160 mg LY03005 groups showed statistically significant changes versus placebo on the primary MADRS endpoint.
- It also reports secondary endpoint improvements including HAMD-17, CGI, Hamilton Anxiety Scale, HAMD-17 anxiety/somatization, HAMD-17 cognitive-impairment factor, HAMD-17 blocking factor, MADRS anhedonia factor, and Sheehan Disability Scale total score.
- Interpretation for the wiki: the HAMD cognitive-impairment-factor note is cognition-adjacent, but it is a depression-scale symptom factor reported in a sponsor announcement, not an independent objective cognitive battery or healthy-adult nootropic endpoint.
