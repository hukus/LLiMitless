# Ginkgo biloba extract EGb 761 is safe and effective in the treatment of mild dementia - a meta-analysis of patient subgroups in randomised controlled trials

- PMID: 39895346
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39895346/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

2025 patient-subgroup meta-analysis pooled four randomized placebo-controlled trials in mild dementia (782 patients). EGb 761 at 240 mg/day beat placebo on cognition, global assessment, activities of daily living, and quality of life, with similar adverse-event frequency.
