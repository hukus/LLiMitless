# mPFC-rTMS for patients with insomnia disorder using resting-state functional magnetic resonance imaging: a protocol for a randomized controlled trial

- PMID: 36510305
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36510305/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Protocol for a randomized controlled trial of mPFC-targeted rTMS in insomnia, with resting-state fMRI as a mechanistic readout and clinical sleep outcomes as the main endpoints.

## Notes

This is a protocol rather than outcome evidence, but it shows the stimulation branch is actively being designed around insomnia-state change, not just cognition in the abstract.
