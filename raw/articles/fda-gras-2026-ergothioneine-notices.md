# FDA GRAS notices for ergothioneine

- Publisher: US FDA Human Foods Program / GRAS Notices inventory
- Primary notice checked: GRN No. 734, ergothioneine
- Additional notices checked: GRN No. 1191 and GRN No. 1270 for fermentation-produced ergothioneine ingredients
- Checked: 2026-05-03

## Summary

FDA's GRAS Notice inventory lists ergothioneine under GRN No. 734, with Blue California as notifier and a May 7, 2018 closure date. The intended food use was as a nutrient supplement in foods and drinks at levels intended to provide 5 mg per serving, and FDA's letter says it had no questions.

The inventory also lists later notices for fermentation-produced ergothioneine ingredients: GRN No. 1191 closed January 30, 2025 and GRN No. 1270 closed January 22, 2026, both with FDA no-questions letters. These are food-ingredient safety/regulatory signals, not cognition-efficacy evidence.

## Interpretation For Cognition Claims

- GRAS status supports a better-than-average supplement acceptability profile, but it does not prove cognitive efficacy.
- Different production routes and purity claims remain product-identity variables.
- The cognition wiki should keep food-use levels, supplement capsule doses, and clinical-study doses separate.
