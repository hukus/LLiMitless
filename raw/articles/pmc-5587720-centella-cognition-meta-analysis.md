# Effects of Centella asiatica on cognitive function and mood related outcomes

2017 systematic review and meta-analysis of Centella asiatica for cognition and mood-related outcomes. The review included five randomized controlled trials of Centella alone and six trials of Centella-containing products.

The abstract reports no significant differences versus placebo across cognitive-function domains, but mood-related findings suggested increased alert scores and reduced anger scores acutely.

Relevance: formal counterweight to gotu kola memory/nootropic anecdotes.
