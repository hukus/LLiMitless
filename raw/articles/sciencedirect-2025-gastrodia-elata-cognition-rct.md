# Gastrodia elata cognition randomized trial in middle-aged adults

2025 Journal of Functional Foods randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial of Gastrodia elata Blume / Gastrodiae Rhizoma / Tianma extract for cognitive enhancement in 100 participants aged 40 to 65 years with normal or impaired cognition. Participants received daily 930 mg tablets for 12 weeks. The trial was registered in the Korean Clinical Research Information Service as KCT0006605.

The primary outcome was ADAS-cog total score. Secondary outcomes included K-MMSE, BDNF, amyloid beta, and total antioxidant status. The G. elata group improved ADAS-cog total score by -2.15 +/- 2.22 versus -0.92 +/- 2.03 in placebo. K-MMSE total score, BDNF, and total antioxidant status also improved, while amyloid beta did not. No serious adverse events were reported. The paper notes HPLC confirmation of gastrodin in the extract and states that the product was recognized as a Korean health functional food in 2024.
