# Anserine, HClO-scavenger, protected against cognitive decline in individuals with mild cognitive impairment

- PMID: 33472172
- PMCID: PMC7880346
- DOI: 10.18632/aging.202535
- Published: 2021
- Ingested: 2026-05-11

## Summary

Randomized controlled trial in elderly people with mild cognitive impairment. Thirty-six MCI individuals were assigned to 500 mg/day anserine or placebo for 12 weeks, and MMSE data were analyzed for 30 completers.

The change in MMSE differed between active and placebo arms: active +1.9 +/- 2.0, n = 15, versus placebo 0 +/- 2.8, n = 15, p = 0.036. After correction for daily anserine intake, the reported significance improved to p = 0.0176. The mechanistic frame was hypochlorous-acid scavenging and myeloperoxidase-mediated neuroinflammation.

## Relevance

Supports an anserine-specific MCI / neuroimmune branch inside [[anserine-carnosine]]. It should inform disease-risk and brain-fog hypothesis generation, not healthy-adult nootropic ranking.

## Caveats

- Very small completer sample.
- MCI endpoint and MMSE do not map cleanly to healthy-adult executive function or attention.
- Product and dose are not interchangeable with chicken essence or beta-alanine.
