# The mitophagy activator urolithin A is safe and induces a molecular signature of improved mitochondrial and cellular health in humans

- PMID: 32694802
- DOI: 10.1038/s42255-019-0073-4
- Ingested: 2026-05-03

## Summary

2019 first-in-human Nature Metabolism study of urolithin A in healthy, sedentary elderly individuals. Participants received urolithin A as single doses or repeated dosing over 4 weeks. The paper reports safety / tolerability and molecular signatures consistent with improved mitochondrial and cellular health.

Use in the wiki: human target-engagement and safety anchor for urolithin A, not cognition evidence. It supports the biological plausibility of the mitophagy / mitochondrial-health route but does not show memory, attention, executive-function, or processing-speed benefit.
