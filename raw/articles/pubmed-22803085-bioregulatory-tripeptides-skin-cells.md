# Effect of bioregulatory tripeptides on the culture of skin cells from young and old rats

- PMID: 22803085
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22803085/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

The study tested bioregulatory tripeptides in rat skin cultures, including T-33 (Glu-Asp-Arg, i.e. Pinealon). T-33 stimulated proliferation in young-rat skin explants, while T-38 was the stronger signal in old-rat explants. This is regenerative-cell biology, not a cognition endpoint.
