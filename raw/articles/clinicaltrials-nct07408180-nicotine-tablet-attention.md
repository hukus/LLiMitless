# Exploratory Randomized, Controlled, Dose-response Study on the Impact of Nicotine From Five Variants of a Nicotine Tablet on Attention

- ClinicalTrials.gov: NCT07408180
- Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07408180
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Active, not recruiting 2026 trial of an orally delivered nicotine tablet in adult occasional nicotine-pouch users. The study is directly relevant to the branch because it is explicitly testing attention and dose response in humans, but results are not yet posted. Sponsor involvement from Philip Morris means the eventual readout should be treated with extra bias scrutiny.
