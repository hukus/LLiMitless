# Ergothioneine for cognitive health, longevity and healthy ageing: where are we now?

- PMID: 40968729
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40968729/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

2025 review arguing that ergothioneine is a promising cognitive-health and healthy-ageing nutraceutical. It cites observational links between low blood ergothioneine and poorer cognitive/health outcomes, small interventional trials suggesting possible benefit on cognition, memory, sleep, and neurodegeneration biomarkers, and mechanistic work on oxidative stress, inflammation, mitochondrial function, neurogenesis, and NAD+ metabolism. The review is directionally positive, but one author is affiliated with Blue California and the paper itself says larger, longer trials are still needed.
