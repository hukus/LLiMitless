# HPLC analysis of levodopa in commercial Mucuna pruriens products

Analytical paper measuring levodopa in commercial Mucuna pruriens products. The study supports the wiki's product-identity gate: Mucuna preparations are not interchangeable, and commercial extracts can vary in actual L-DOPA delivery.

Use in the wiki: formulation, assay, and label-risk evidence. A nootropic anecdote using a 15% L-DOPA extract should not be equated with seed powder, clinical preparations, or unassayed capsules.
