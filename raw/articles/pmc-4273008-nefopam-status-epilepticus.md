# Nefopam status epilepticus and neuropsychiatric safety

- Source: open-access case report / safety discussion on status epilepticus caused by nefopam.
- The article states that serious side effects related to nefopam are neuropsychiatric and cardiovascular.
- It cites serious unexpected neuropsychiatric adverse drug reactions including confusion, hallucinations, convulsions, and delirium, and notes a case of fatal convulsions in older literature.
- It reports that nefopam has a prominent anticholinergic effect.
- Interpretation for the wiki: nefopam should be treated as risk-first for cognition. Stimulant-like or mood-like effects do not override seizure, delirium/confusion, anticholinergic, and cardiovascular cautions.
