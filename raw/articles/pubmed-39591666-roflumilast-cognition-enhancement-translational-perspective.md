# Roflumilast and cognition enhancement: A translational perspective

- PMID: 39591666
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39591666/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

2024 translational review arguing that low-dose roflumilast has a favorable therapeutic window for cognition, citing positive clinical studies in healthy adults, healthy elderly adults, MCI, and schizophrenia. The review also notes dose-limited tolerability at the approved COPD dose and frames the branch as mechanistically plausible rather than settled.

## Key points

- Mechanistic framing: pro-neuroplastic and anti-inflammatory effects.
- Clinical framing: positive human studies at 100-250 micrograms.
- Ongoing trials: early AD, post-stroke cognitive impairment, and Fragile X.
- Conflict: the authors disclose a proprietary interest in roflumilast for cognitive impairment.
