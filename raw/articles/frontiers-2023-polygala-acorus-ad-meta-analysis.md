# Polygala tenuifolia and Acorus tatarinowii in the treatment of Alzheimer's disease: a systematic review and meta-analysis

- Published: 2023
- Journal: Frontiers in Pharmacology
- DOI: 10.3389/fphar.2023.1268000
- Evidence layer: clinical systematic review / meta-analysis, formula-family rather than exact KXS-only evidence

## Summary

This systematic review examined randomized trials of formulas that included Polygala tenuifolia and Acorus tatarinowii, two Kai-Xin-San constituents, in Alzheimer's disease. Sixteen studies with 1,103 participants were included. Polygala plus Acorus alone was not clearly superior to conventional therapy for MMSE, ADL, or ADAS-cog. When formulas containing Polygala plus Acorus were added to conventional treatment, pooled estimates favored the add-on group for MMSE, ADL, and ADAS-cog, but certainty was mostly low or very low and heterogeneity was high for some endpoints.

The paper is useful as a human formula-family signal, not as clean product identity evidence for Kai-Xin-San itself. It mentions individual Chinese clinical studies including a Kaixin San plus donepezil trial in references, but the meta-analytic unit is the Polygala plus Acorus formula family.

## Wiki Use

- Use as a cautious human disease-state adjunct context for [[kai-xin-san]].

- Do not treat this as evidence for generic Polygala, Acorus, or KXS supplements in healthy adults.
