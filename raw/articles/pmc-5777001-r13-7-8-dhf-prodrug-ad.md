# The prodrug of 7,8-dihydroxyflavone development and therapeutic efficacy for treating Alzheimer's disease

- PMID: 29295929
- PMC: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5777001/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Notes

2018 PNAS preclinical paper on the 7,8-DHF prodrug R13. The authors used prodrug chemistry to improve oral exposure and brain delivery of 7,8-DHF, then reported that R13 increased 7,8-DHF half-life, oral bioavailability, and brain exposure in mice. In 5XFAD AD mice, chronic oral R13 activated TrkB signaling, reduced Abeta deposition, inhibited APP and Tau cleavage by AEP, preserved hippocampal synapses, and improved memory deficits in a dose-dependent manner.

## Relevance

- Main follow-up paper for the 7,8-DHF branch.
- Stronger for druggability and target-engagement than for human cognition.
- Useful comparator against ZEB85 and against the specificity-caution literature.
