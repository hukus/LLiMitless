# Effect of masupirdine (SUVN-502) on cognition in patients with moderate Alzheimer's disease

- PMID: 35662833
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35662833/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

Randomized, double-blind, phase 2 proof-of-concept adjunct-to-donepezil/memantine trial in moderate AD. The primary ADAS-Cog 11 endpoint was not significant at either 50 mg or 100 mg, and secondary efficacy measures were also negative. Safety and tolerability were acceptable, so the result is a clean efficacy miss rather than a safety stop.
