# Creatine and Cognition in Aging: A Systematic Review of Evidence in Older Adults.

- PMID: 40971619
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40971619/
- Ingested: 2026-04-29

## Summary

2025 systematic review of creatine and cognition in adults aged 55+ included six studies with 1542 participants. Five of six studies reported a positive relationship, mostly in memory and attention domains, but the evidence base was small and mixed in quality.

## Notes

Two of the six studies were double-blind creatine supplementation trials and four were cross-sectional dietary-intake studies. The review is useful for older-adult framing, but it does not yet establish a clean causal effect in healthy aging.
