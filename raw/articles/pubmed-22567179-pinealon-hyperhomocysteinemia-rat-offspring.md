# Pinealon protects the rat offspring from prenatal hyperhomocysteinemia

- PMID: 22567179
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22567179/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

In rats, pinealon improved offspring spatial orientation and learning after prenatal hyperhomocysteinemia and reduced oxidative-stress readouts. This is the clearest pinealon cognition-adjacent animal result in the current sweep.
