# Dietary Nucleotides Older-Adult RCT Full Text

- Article: Dietary Nucleotides Enhance Neurogenesis, Cognitive Capacity, Muscle Function, and Body Composition in Older Adults: A Randomized, Triple-Blind, Controlled Clinical Trial.
- Journal: Nutrients, 2025;17(9):1431.
- DOI: https://doi.org/10.3390/nu17091431
- PMID: 40362739.
- Date checked: 2026-05-03.

## Summary

Full-text source for the 2025 dietary-nucleotide RCT already represented by the PubMed raw note. The trial enrolled 69 physically independent older adults aged 60-75 years into three arms: yeast nucleotide formulation, neuro-based formulation, or placebo. Participants continued spontaneous physical activity but did not perform a structured exercise program during the 10-week intervention.

The yeast-nucleotide arm used RiboDiet, 250 mg/day, described as a yeast extract from a standardized, controlled, organic-solvent-free process and a source of at least 40% nucleotides, including 5'-AMP, 5'-CMP, 5'-UMP, and 5'-GMP, plus nucleosides, oligonucleotides, RNA fragments, amino acids, minerals, and B vitamins. The neuro formulation used RiboCARE Neuro, 150 mg/day, containing four monophosphate nucleotides: 5'-AMP, 5'-CMP, 5'-UMP, and 5'-GMP.

Both nucleotide arms improved Trail Making Test B versus placebo, with reported TMT-B percent changes of -19.35% for yeast nucleotides, -18.21% for the neuro formulation, and -4.31% for placebo. TMT-A improved within groups but did not show the same between-group signal. TMT-B change correlated with BDNF. The paper interprets the result as executive-function / processing-speed / mental-flexibility support in older adults maintaining spontaneous physical activity.

The same trial reported improvements versus placebo in physical performance, oxidative stress biomarkers, inflammatory cytokines, body-composition stability, and quality-of-life parameters. The authors also note that the sample is narrow, the duration is short, serum biomarkers may not map directly to central nervous system changes, and more work is needed on dose, duration, and external validity.

Product and bias notes: RiboDiet and RiboCARE Neuro are named Prosol products. Prosol provided reagents for the study, and two authors served as advisers to nutraceutical companies, while the paper states conflicted authors were not involved in data collection or entry and there were no restrictions on analysis, writing, or publication.

Use in the wiki: strongest direct formal source for [[dietary-nucleotides]], especially the product-specific TMT-B / BDNF / older-adult spontaneous-activity signal. Do not generalize it to uridine alone, NMN/NR, ATP, yeast extract generally, infant-formula nucleotides, or unrestricted healthy-adult nootropic use.
