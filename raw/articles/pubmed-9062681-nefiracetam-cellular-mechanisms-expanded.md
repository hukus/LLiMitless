# PubMed 9062681: Expanded nefiracetam cellular mechanisms note

This 1997 Behavioural Brain Research article is titled "Cellular mechanisms underlying cognition-enhancing actions of nefiracetam (DM-9384)."

The abstract describes biochemical experiments on cholinergic and GABAergic transmission plus electrophysiological work on neuronal calcium channels. In rat behavioral experiments, nefiracetam 3 mg/kg ameliorated amnesia induced by basal-forebrain lesion or scopolamine.

Mechanistic findings from the abstract: nefiracetam increased uptake and release of transmitters in both cholinergic and GABAergic systems in rat brain. In NG108-15 cells, 1 uM nefiracetam increased long-lasting N/L-type calcium-channel currents, and the calcium-channel effect was blocked by pertussis toxin.

The authors interpreted the results as suggesting that nefiracetam improves impaired memory by facilitating cholinergic and GABAergic transmission, with PTX-sensitive G-proteins and associated calcium channels contributing to neurotransmission effects.

Interpretation for the wiki: this is a useful mechanism/preclinical anchor, not human healthy-adult efficacy evidence.
