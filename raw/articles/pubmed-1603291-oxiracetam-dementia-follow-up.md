# Clinical studies with oxiracetam in patients with dementia of Alzheimer type and multi-infarct dementia of mild to moderate degree

- Source type: PubMed abstract for placebo-controlled dementia trial plus follow-up.
- Publication: Drug Development Research / dementia clinical study abstract indexed in PubMed, 1992.
- Date checked: 2026-05-03.

This study assessed oxiracetam in mild-to-moderate senile dementia of Alzheimer type and multi-infarct dementia. The randomized double-blind placebo-controlled phase compared oxiracetam 800 mg twice daily with placebo over 90 days in 60 outpatients, followed by longer follow-up.

The abstract reports significant improvements favoring oxiracetam on Mini-Mental State Examination, Auditory Continuous Performance Test, Rey 15 Words Test, Block Tapping Test, Mattis Word Fluency, Luria Alternating Series, and Instrumental Activities of Daily Living at the end of therapy.

Use in the wiki: second old disease-context oxiracetam signal. Keep the confidence low because the trial is small, old, and not a healthy-adult enhancement test.
