# Evidence that 5-HT2A receptor signalling efficacy and not biased agonism differentiates serotonergic psychedelic from non-psychedelic drugs

- PMID: 40545270
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40545270/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

2025 mechanistic study arguing that 5-HT2A receptor signaling efficacy, rather than biased agonism, better separates serotonergic psychedelic drugs from non-psychedelic drugs. The result matters for non-hallucinogenic psychoplastogen design because it suggests human target-engagement and efficacy thresholds may be more important than a simple biased-agonism story.
