# A Wearable, Steerable, Transcranial Low-Intensity Focused Ultrasound System

- PMID: 39449176
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39449176/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Prototype wearable LIFU system for anterior brain targets. Despite the wearable form factor, the workflow still depends on neuronavigation, LIFU planning, MRI-based skull assessment, simulations and water experiments to establish safe steering range, and safety/usability screening in healthy volunteers. It is a research prototype, not evidence of a validated consumer home protocol.
