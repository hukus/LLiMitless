# A 28-week, double-blind, placebo-controlled study with Cerebrolysin in patients with mild to moderate Alzheimer's disease

- PMID: 11552768
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11552768/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled AD trial with 149 patients. Repeated 30 mL Cerebrolysin infusions improved CGI responder rate and ADAS-cog versus placebo, and the benefit was still visible 3 months after treatment stopped.
