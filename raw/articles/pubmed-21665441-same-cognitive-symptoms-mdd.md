# Effects of S-adenosylmethionine augmentation of serotonin-reuptake inhibitor antidepressants on cognitive symptoms of major depressive disorder

2012 secondary analysis of a 6-week double-blind randomized trial of adjunctive oral SAMe in 46 serotonin-reuptake-inhibitor nonresponders with major depressive disorder. Cognitive symptoms were assessed with the self-rated Cognitive and Physical Symptoms Questionnaire.

The abstract reports greater improvement in the ability to recall information for adjunctive SAMe versus placebo (p = 0.04), and a trend toward better word-finding (p = 0.09). The other five questionnaire items were not significant.

The authors framed the finding as preliminary and in need of replication. The signal is subjective MDD cognitive-symptom improvement, not objective healthy-adult cognition enhancement.

Wiki use: this is the cleanest direct cognition-adjacent SAMe human result in the current scan. It belongs on [[s-adenosyl-l-methionine]] as `low, depression-state, subjective cognition`.
