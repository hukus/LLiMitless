# Short-term ketone monoester supplementation improves cerebral blood flow and cognition in obesity

- PMID: 34605026
- Study type: placebo-controlled double-blind crossover trial
- Population: 14 adults with obesity, age 56 +/- 12 years
- Intervention: 30 mL (12 g) beta-hydroxybutyrate thrice daily for 14 days
- Cognitive tasks: digit-symbol substitution, Stroop, task switching
- Main signal: improved extracranial cerebral blood flow and a small DSST gain
- Main caveat: phenotype is obesity, not healthy sleep-loss challenge

## Summary

This is the strongest positive human cognition signal in the current set, but the mechanism and phenotype are metabolic rather than sleep-deprived high-load workers.
