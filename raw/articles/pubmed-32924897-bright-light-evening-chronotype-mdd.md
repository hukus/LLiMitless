# Adjunctive bright light treatment with gradual advance in unipolar major depressive disorder with evening chronotype - A randomized controlled trial

- PMID: 32924897
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32924897/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Chronotype-tailored bright white light with gradual advance improved remission speed in unipolar depression with evening chronotype. This is one of the clearest chronotype-aware intervention signals in the current sweep.

## Notes

- 93 participants were randomized.
- Bright white light was compared with dim red light.
- Time to remission was shorter in the bright-light group.
- Adherent participants showed greater improvement in HAM-D17 over time.
- The design supports chronotype-aware light timing, but it is still a depression trial rather than a direct cognition study.
