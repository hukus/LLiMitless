# The effect of the triple monoamine reuptake inhibitor tesofensine on energy metabolism and appetite in overweight and moderately obese men

- PMID: 20479765
- DOI: 10.1038/ijo.2010.87
- Journal: International Journal of Obesity (2010)

## Captured takeaways

- Randomized controlled trial in 32 healthy overweight or moderately obese men.
- Participants received tesofensine 2.0 mg daily for 7 days followed by 1.0 mg daily for another 7 days, or corresponding placebo.
- Participants were instructed to maintain habitual food intake and physical activity.
- Tesofensine produced 1.8 kg more weight loss than placebo after 2 weeks.
- Tesofensine increased ratings of satiety and fullness and lowered prospective food intake.
- The study measured energy expenditure, fat oxidation, spontaneous physical activity, body composition, and appetite sensations rather than cognition.
- Interpretation for the wiki: appetite/satiety and body-state effects can plausibly be misread as energy or focus changes in community reports, but this trial does not establish cognitive enhancement.
