# Extruded wheat bran increases serum SCFAs but does not modulate psychobiological functions

- PMID: 35694161
- Published: 2022
- Source type: randomized triple-blind placebo-controlled trial
- Checked: 2026-05-11
- Population: 69 healthy male participants

## Summary

This trial tested 28 days of extruded wheat bran cereal against placebo in healthy men. The intervention increased serum acetate and butyrate, but did not affect the primary cortisol-stress outcome or secondary psychobiological outcomes, including subjective stress, fear-task response, cortisol awakening response, heart-rate variability, retrospective mood, BDNF, intestinal permeability, fecal SCFAs, or stool consistency.

Use this as an important null counterweight for SCFA-mediated brain claims. Raising serum SCFA markers modestly was not enough to change measured psychobiological function in this trial.

## Notes

- The intervention was food/fiber-mediated SCFA change, not direct butyrate or tributyrin.
- The study explicitly discussed SCFAs as candidate mediators of mood and cognition links, but the measured outcome pattern was null.
