# A Phase 1b Study of Thymosin Beta 4 in Healthy Volunteers

- ClinicalTrials.gov ID: NCT04555850
- ClinicalTrials.gov: https://www.clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04555850
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Completed phase 1b randomized, double-blind healthy-volunteer study of intravenous recombinant human thymosin beta 4 (NL005). The stated goals were safety, tolerability, pharmacokinetics, and potential immunologic reaction after multiple daily doses over 10 days. No cognition endpoint surfaced in the registry record.

## Notes

This is the cleanest human safety signal I found for thymosin beta-4, but it is still a drug-development record, not cognition evidence.
