# Effects of Panax ginseng and Panax quinquefolius on Cognitive Function: A Systematic Review

- DOI: 10.1007/s41465-026-00355-7
- Springer: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s41465-026-00355-7
- Ingested: 2026-04-30

## Summary

2026 systematic review of randomized placebo-controlled cognitive studies in non-clinical populations found 14 eligible articles, most using standardized Panax ginseng extracts and a smaller set using standardized Panax quinquefolius. Most studies were acute (1-6 hours), with a smaller chronic subset (8 days to 12 weeks). Overall, Panax ginseng tended to improve secondary-memory accuracy and some attention measures, while Panax quinquefolius appeared more favorable for attention and working memory/executive function. The review emphasizes that extract characterization matters: studies that did not specify ginsenoside constituents did not report benefits.
