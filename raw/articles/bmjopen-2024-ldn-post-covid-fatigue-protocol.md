# Low-dose naltrexone post-COVID fatigue syndrome randomized trial protocol

2024 BMJ Open protocol for a double-blind randomized placebo-controlled phase II trial of low-dose naltrexone for post-COVID fatigue syndrome in British Columbia. The trial planned 160 participants, randomized to LDN or placebo, with titration from 1 mg daily to 4.5 mg daily or maximum tolerated dose.

Primary outcome is Fatigue Severity Scale at 16 weeks. Secondary outcomes include pain, overall symptom severity, 7-day step count, and health-related quality of life. The protocol notes that LDN has emerged as an off-label treatment for fatigue and other PCC symptoms, but only small non-controlled studies had assessed it.
