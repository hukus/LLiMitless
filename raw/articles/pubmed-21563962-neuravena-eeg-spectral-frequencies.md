# Ingested oat herb extract changes EEG spectral frequencies in healthy subjects

- Source type: double-blind randomized placebo-controlled crossover EEG study.
- Publication: Journal of Alternative and Complementary Medicine, 2011.
- Date checked: 2026-05-03.

This trial compared acute 1250 mg and 2500 mg Neuravena with placebo while healthy participants completed eyes-open/eyes-closed recording, a d2 concentration test, and mental arithmetic. Quantitative EEG was the main endpoint.

The source is useful as an early neurophysiology anchor: it reported EEG spectral-frequency changes during mental work after oat herb extract ingestion. Later Neuravena and Cognitaven trials are more directly useful for behavior, but this study helps explain why the green-oat branch is often framed around attention, concentration, and electrophysiology.

Use in the wiki: supporting neurophysiology source only. Do not treat EEG shifts alone as proof of objective cognitive enhancement.
