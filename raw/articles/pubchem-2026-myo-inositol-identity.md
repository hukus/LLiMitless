# PubChem 2026 myo-inositol identity note

PubChem identifies inositol as CID 892, molecular formula C6H12O6, molecular weight about 180.16 g/mol, and IUPAC name cyclohexane-1,2,3,4,5,6-hexol.

Wiki use: this establishes the molecule identity for [[myo-inositol]] and separates plain inositol from inositol-stabilized arginine silicate, D-chiro-inositol, IP6/phytate, phosphatidylinositol signaling, and generic B-vitamin-like supplement claims.
