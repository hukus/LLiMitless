# ITRUSST consensus on biophysical safety for transcranial ultrasound stimulation

- PMID: 41072763
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41072763/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Consensus safety paper for transcranial ultrasound stimulation (TUS). It assumes use in people without contraindications, compromised thermoregulation, vascular vulnerabilities, or administered ultrasound contrast agents. It defines a nonsignificant-risk frame at mechanical index or transcranial mechanical index <= 1.9 and, for thermal effects, peak temperature rise <= 2 C, peak absolute temperature <= 39 C, or dose-based equivalents. The paper emphasizes that these are not safety limits and do not replace regulatory standards or ethical review.
