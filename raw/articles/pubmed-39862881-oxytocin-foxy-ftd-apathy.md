# Notes
- Source: FOXY multicentre adaptive crossover phase 2a/2b trial in probable FTD.
- Population: adults 30-80 with apathy and stable meds; study partner required.
- Intervention: intranasal oxytocin every third day, with 72 IU selected as the best dose schedule.
- Outcome: apathy improved versus placebo by NPI estimate -1.32 points.
- Safety: no adverse events attributed to oxytocin; headache and URTI were the main observed AEs.
