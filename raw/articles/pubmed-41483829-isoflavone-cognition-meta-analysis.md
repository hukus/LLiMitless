# Systematic review and meta-analysis of isoflavone interventions on cognitive function
2026 meta-analysis of 37 studies with 16 RCTs meta-analyzed. The review found a modest positive effect on composite cognition (0.25 [0.04, 0.45]) and memory (0.33 [0.04, 0.62]), while executive function, psychomotor speed, and processing speed were not significant. The data support a memory-leaning soy/isoflavone branch, but the effect is modest and domain-specific.
