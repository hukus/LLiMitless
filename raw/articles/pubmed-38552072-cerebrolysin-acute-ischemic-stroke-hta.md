# Efficacy, safety, and cost-effectiveness analysis of Cerebrolysin in acute ischemic stroke: A rapid health technology assessment

- PMID: 38552072
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38552072/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Rapid HTA synthesizing 14 studies on Cerebrolysin in acute ischemic stroke. The review reports advantages for total efficacy, neurological function, upper-limb impairment, and activities of daily living, with the clearest read in moderate to severe stroke, but it is a secondary synthesis of older trials rather than a new primary RCT.
