# Epitalon increases telomere length in human cell lines through telomerase upregulation or ALT activity

- PMID: 40908429
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40908429/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

2025 Brunel study in human cell lines found dose-dependent telomere extension in normal cells and ALT-linked telomere changes in cancer lines. The paper strengthens the telomere biology story, but it remains a cell-culture study with no cognition endpoint.
