## Summary

ClinicalTrials.gov API snapshot for MB2, a phase 2 double-blind placebo-controlled trial of oral USP methylene blue in healthy aging, mild cognitive impairment, and mild Alzheimer disease. The study is completed with actual enrollment of 117, primary completion on 2022-04-21, study completion in 2023-07, and results first posted on 2024-09-19.

The intervention was methylene blue 282 mg orally daily over 2-week and 12-week windows, with placebo comparators and phenazopyridine hydrochloride used to preserve blinding. Posted outcomes include fMRI task activation, Wechsler Memory Scale, FNAME, psychomotor vigilance, Logical Memory, MMSE, CLOX, cerebral blood flow, functional connectivity, and CO2 challenge measures.

## Results Read

- The registry posts descriptive outcome means by subgroup, but this raw capture does not establish a peer-reviewed statistical interpretation.
- Denominators are small for imaging outcomes: commonly 12-16 per middle-age/healthy-aging/MCI arm, and only 1-2 participants in the mild-AD arms for several clinical outcomes.
- No deaths or serious adverse events were reported in any arm.
- Non-serious adverse events were common and expected for dye/urinary effects: blue/orange urine, green stool, urinary frequency/urgency, nausea/vomiting, diarrhea/loose stool, bladder irritation, and leg cramps.

## Signal Use

- Medicinal signal: registry-result anchor that MB2 is no longer protocol-only; it remains descriptive and needs peer-reviewed analysis before upgrading confidence.
- Safety signal: no deaths or SAEs in this posted registry snapshot, but nuisance urinary/GI events were common and the trial excluded major safety-risk groups.
- Cognition signal: useful for tracking outcome domains and denominators; do not treat as a clean positive trial without paper-level statistics.
