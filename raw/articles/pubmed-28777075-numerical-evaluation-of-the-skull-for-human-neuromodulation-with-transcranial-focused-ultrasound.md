# Numerical evaluation of the skull for human neuromodulation with transcranial focused ultrasound

- PMID: 28777075
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28777075/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

This modeling study uses CT and MR scans to estimate how individual skull differences alter intracranial pressure maps under transcranial focused ultrasound. It shows that skull thickness, composition, transducer placement, and frequency all matter for beam propagation, so target dose cannot be assumed from device settings alone.
