# Circulating uridine and food intake in healthy humans

- PMCID: PMC9873946
- Journal: Cell Reports Medicine (2023)

## Captured takeaways

- Two placebo-controlled studies administered single-dose uridine monophosphate to healthy participants to test food-behavior effects.
- Doses included 0.5 g and 1 g UMP.
- In the 0.5 g study, oral UMP significantly increased circulating uridine and prevented the initial post-prandial decline in serum uridine concentrations.
- The study focus was circulating uridine and food intake, not cognition.
- Interpretation for the wiki: human UMP exposure can acutely move circulating uridine and behaviorally relevant metabolism; do not convert this into a cognitive-enhancement claim without cognitive endpoints.
