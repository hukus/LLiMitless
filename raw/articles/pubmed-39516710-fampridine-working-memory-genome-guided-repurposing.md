# The effect of fampridine on working memory: a randomized controlled trial based on a genome-guided repurposing approach

- PMID: 39516710
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39516710/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Genome-guided repurposing study in 43 healthy young adults. Fampridine 10 mg twice daily for 3.5 days did not improve the primary 3-back outcome overall, but lower baseline performers did better on repeated fampridine than placebo. The paper also reports lower resting motor threshold, which fits an excitability mechanism rather than a universal healthy-adult enhancement effect.

## Notes

- Double-blind, randomized, placebo-controlled crossover design.
- Primary WM endpoint was 3-back d-prime.
- Main effect was null, but baseline performance moderated response.
- Useful for the genotype-guided repurposing story, but not yet proof of general enhancement.
