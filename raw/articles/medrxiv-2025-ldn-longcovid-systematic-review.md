# Low-dose naltrexone for long COVID systematic review preprint

2025 medRxiv systematic review preprint on low-dose naltrexone for long COVID. The review reported no randomized controlled trials in the evidence base at the time of analysis, but four observational studies reported LDN outcomes.

The preprint summarized moderate-to-large pre-post improvements in fatigue, brain fog, pain, sleep quality, and daily functioning, with standardized mean difference around 0.53 for brain fog and 0.93 for daily functioning. No serious adverse events were reported. The authors rated certainty as low because all four studies were non-randomized.

Interpretation for the wiki: this is the best synthesis for the LDN brain-fog anecdote/observational lane, but as a preprint with nonrandomized inputs it should not upgrade confidence above low.
