# Notes
- Source: Phase 2 randomized placebo-controlled PRESENCE trial in Lewy body dementia.
- Population: 344 participants with PDD or DLB, treated for 12 weeks.
- Intervention: mevidalen 10, 30, or 75 mg daily versus placebo.
- Outcome: primary continuity-of-attention and ADAS-cog13 cognition endpoints were negative; MDS-UPDRS total and ADCS-CGIC improved at 30 mg and 75 mg.
- Safety: blood pressure increases, adverse events, and cardiovascular serious adverse events were most pronounced at 75 mg.
