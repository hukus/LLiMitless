# PubMed 1786500: Pramiracetam in young males with memory problems after head injury and anoxia

This 1991 Brain Injury placebo-controlled study tested pramiracetam sulfate in young males with memory and cognitive problems after brain injury and anoxia.

Design from the abstract: pramiracetam sulfate 400 mg three times daily was evaluated under double-blind placebo-controlled conditions.

Results from the abstract: memory measures, especially delayed recall, showed clinically significant improvement with pramiracetam compared with placebo. Improvement was maintained during an 18-month open-trial period on medication and during a 1-month follow-up period after discontinuation.

Interpretation for the wiki: this is a stronger recovery-state human cognition signal than generic racetam anecdotes, but it applies to young males with head-injury/anoxia cognitive problems rather than healthy adults.
