# Colon-delivered short-chain fatty acids attenuate the cortisol response to psychosocial stress in healthy men

- Published: 2020
- Source type: triple-blind randomized placebo-controlled trial
- Checked: 2026-05-11
- Population: 66 healthy men

## Summary

This trial delivered a colon-targeted mixture of short-chain fatty acids for one week at doses designed to approximate fermentation of 10 g or 20 g of arabinoxylan oligosaccharides. Both SCFA doses increased serum SCFA levels and attenuated cortisol response to psychosocial stress compared with placebo.

The trial did not show broad psychobiological or cognition effects. It did not change fecal SCFAs, serum BDNF, cortisol awakening response, fear learning/extinction, or subjective mood ratings.

Use this as the strongest captured healthy-human SCFA gut-brain anchor: an HPA-axis stress-reactivity signal, not direct cognitive enhancement or brain-fog clearance.

## Notes

- Route matters: this was colon delivery of an SCFA mixture, not ordinary sodium butyrate capsules or dietary fiber alone.
- The result supports gut-brain communication plausibility, but endpoint translation to focus, memory, fatigue, or brain fog remains unproven.
