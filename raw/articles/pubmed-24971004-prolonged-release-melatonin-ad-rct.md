# Add-on prolonged-release melatonin for cognitive function and sleep in mild to moderate Alzheimer's disease

- PMID: 24971004
- PMCID: PMC4069047
- DOI: 10.2147/CIA.S65625
- Published: 2014-06-18
- Source type: randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled multicenter trial
- Intervention: prolonged-release melatonin 2 mg nightly for 24 weeks
- Population: 80 people with mild to moderate Alzheimer disease on standard therapy
- Ingested: 2026-05-03

## Summary

The trial reported better IADL and MMSE performance with prolonged-release melatonin than placebo after 24 weeks, and better sleep-efficiency on the PSQI component. Mean ADAS-Cog did not differ between groups. The comorbid-insomnia subgroup had stronger cognitive and sleep signals, including MMSE and median ADAS-Cog differences.

## Notes

- Useful positive RCT anchor, especially for the sleep-mediated interpretation.
- The study was stopped after about half of the intended sample was recruited, making results exploratory.
- Product identity matters: 2 mg prolonged-release melatonin is not equivalent to high-dose immediate-release gummies or mixed sleep stacks.
