# ClinicalTrials.gov NCT06223360 BenfoTeam benfotiamine early AD trial

ClinicalTrials.gov record for "A Trial to Evaluate the Safety and Efficacy of Benfotiamine in Patients With Early Alzheimer's Disease (BenfoTeam)", a recruiting Alzheimer's Disease Cooperative Study trial.

Key details captured 2026-05-03:

- Status: recruiting.
- Last update posted: 2026-02-23; last verified February 2026.
- Design: seamless phase 2A to phase 2B randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial.
- Population: early Alzheimer's disease, including MCI due to AD or mild dementia due to AD; age 50 to 89; biomarker-positive AD signature.
- Planned enrollment: 406 participants.
- Doses: low-dose benfotiamine 300 mg twice daily, high-dose benfotiamine 600 mg twice daily, or placebo.
- Duration / endpoint window: 72 weeks.
- Phase 2A primary objective: choose the highest safe and tolerated dose by tolerability events.
- Phase 2B primary cognitive endpoint: change in ADAS-Cog13 from baseline to 72 weeks.
- Phase 2B primary functional endpoint: change in CDR-SB from baseline to 72 weeks.
- Estimated primary completion and study completion: 2027-12-01.

Wiki interpretation:

- This is the most important current formal-source update for the benfotiamine cognition branch.
- It strengthens inclusion and watch status, but because no results are posted, it does not upgrade efficacy beyond the completed phase IIa signal.
- The trial is disease-context early AD, not healthy-adult enhancement.
