# Long-term soy isoflavone supplementation and cognition in women
2012 Women's Isoflavone Soy Health trial randomized 350 healthy postmenopausal women to 25 g/day soy protein with 91 mg aglycone-weight isoflavones versus milk protein placebo for 2.5 years. There was no significant between-group difference on global cognition; the long-duration null result is an important counterweight to newer positive memory-leaning signals.
