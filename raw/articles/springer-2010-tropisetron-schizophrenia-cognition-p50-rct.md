# Tropisetron schizophrenia cognition / P50 randomized trial

2010 Annals of General Psychiatry randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial of tropisetron in schizophrenia.

## Captured takeaways

- Participants: 40 patients with chronic schizophrenia taking risperidone 2-6 mg/day.
- Design: 8-week randomized, double-blind adjunctive trial comparing tropisetron 10 mg/day with placebo.
- Outcomes included auditory P50 sensory gating, Quality of Life Scale, CANTAB, and PANSS.
- Thirty-three patients completed the trial.
- Tropisetron, but not placebo, significantly improved P50 deficits in non-smoking patients.
- The rapid visual information processing task of CANTAB, interpreted as sustained visual attention, significantly improved with tropisetron.
- PANSS total and subscale scores did not change in this trial.
- The authors reported no adverse side effects associated with the trial and noted that tropisetron was already used as an antiemetic outside the United States.

## Wiki interpretation

- This is a real human cognition-adjacent signal for [[tropisetron]], but it is small, schizophrenia-specific, adjunctive to risperidone, and smoking-status dependent.
- It supports a disease-state alpha7/5-HT3 hypothesis, not healthy-adult cognitive enhancement.
