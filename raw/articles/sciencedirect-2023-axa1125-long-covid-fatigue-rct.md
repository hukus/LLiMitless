# AXA1125 fatigue-predominant long-COVID randomized trial

2023 eClinicalMedicine single-center double-blind randomized phase 2a pilot trial of AXA1125 in fatigue-predominant long COVID.

AXA1125 is an endogenous metabolic modulator composition made from five amino acids plus N-acetylcysteine. The trial randomized 41 adults in the UK to AXA1125 33.9 g twice daily or placebo for four weeks with two-week follow-up. The primary endpoint was phosphocreatine recovery after moderate exercise by 31P magnetic resonance spectroscopy.

The trial did not significantly improve the primary mitochondrial-respiration endpoint or six-minute walk distance. It did significantly reduce Chalder Fatigue Questionnaire fatigue score at day 28 versus placebo. Adverse events occurred in 52.4% of AXA1125 participants and 20.0% of placebo participants, with no serious adverse events or discontinuations.

Interpretation for the wiki: AXA1125 is relevant to post-viral cognitive fatigue because the trial explicitly included mental/cognitive fatigue in the symptom framing, but it is not an objective cognition trial and the primary mechanistic endpoint was negative.
