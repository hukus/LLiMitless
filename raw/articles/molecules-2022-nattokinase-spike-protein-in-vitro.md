# Nattokinase degradation of SARS-CoV-2 spike protein in vitro

2022 Molecules in-vitro study of nattokinase against SARS-CoV-2 spike protein.

The authors incubated spike-protein-transfected cell lysates with nattokinase and reported dose- and time-dependent degradation of spike protein. Immunofluorescence work also suggested degradation of spike protein on the cell surface after nattokinase was added to culture medium.

Interpretation for the wiki: this is a mechanistic/in-vitro source, not clinical evidence for treating COVID, long COVID, brain fog, or microclots. It explains why nattokinase became popular in spike-protein and long-COVID self-experiment circles, but it cannot establish oral dosing efficacy, tissue exposure, symptom improvement, or safety.
