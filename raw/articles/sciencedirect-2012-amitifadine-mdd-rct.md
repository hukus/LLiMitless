# Amitifadine MDD proof-of-concept RCT

- Source: Tran et al., Journal of Psychiatric Research 2012; open-access ScienceDirect abstract.
- Compound identity: amitifadine / EB-1010 / DOV 21,947 was described as a serotonin-preferring triple reuptake inhibitor with relative potency for serotonin, norepinephrine, and dopamine uptake of about 1:2:8.
- Design: 6-week, multicenter, randomized, double-blind, parallel, placebo-controlled study in 63 patients with major depressive disorder.
- Dosing: amitifadine 25 mg twice daily for 2 weeks, then 50 mg twice daily for 4 weeks, versus placebo.
- Modified intent-to-treat population: n = 56.
- Main result: estimated least-squares MADRS change was statistically significantly superior for amitifadine versus placebo, with p = 0.028 and Cohen's d around -0.601.
- Cognition-adjacent factor: a MADRS anhedonia factor grouping included concentration difficulties, lassitude, and inability to feel, and favored amitifadine with p = 0.049.
- Safety: two patients in each group discontinued early because of adverse events; no serious adverse events were reported.
- Interpretation for the wiki: amitifadine had an early mood/anhedonia signal but no objective cognition trial and later development uncertainty.
