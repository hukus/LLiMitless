# PubChem 2026: Nicergoline identity

PubChem identifies nicergoline as CID 34040, molecular formula C24H26BrN3O3, molecular weight 484.4 g/mol, and synonyms including nicergoline, Nimergoline, Sermion, and Nicotergoline.

Identity and pharmacology framing: PubChem/linked source summaries describe nicergoline as an ergot derivative used as a cerebral vasodilator and in peripheral vascular disease, with suggested use for cognitive deficits in cerebrovascular disease. It is classified under ergot alkaloid/peripheral vasodilator ATC context and MeSH pharmacology classes including adrenergic alpha-antagonists, vasodilator agents, and nootropic agents.

Interpretation for the wiki: nicergoline is a distinct ergot derivative and should not be collapsed into [[hydergine-ergoloid-mesylates]], ergotamine migraine drugs, or generic blood-flow supplements.
