# Identification of nine smart drugs in Japan from 2020 to 2022

Open-access Emerging Trends in Drugs, Addictions, and Health paper identifying compounds sold online in Japan as reagents or supplements.

Key extracted points:

- Six powder products and three capsule products purchased online in Japan between April 2020 and June 2022 were analyzed by LC-PDA-MS, GC-MS, and NMR where needed.
- Nine "smart drug" compounds were isolated and identified from products sold as reagents or supplements.
- Seven unknown substances were identified by comparison with authentic substances as IDRA-21, bromantane, sunifiram, 9-MBC, fasoracetam, CE-123, and GTS-21.
- The paper warns that some detected compounds are analogs of pharmaceuticals or development compounds with CNS pharmacology, and excessive intake may cause health problems.
- Interpretation for the wiki: IDRA-21 has real gray-market / online-product presence, so anecdotal reports must be interpreted with identity, dose, purity, and legal-status uncertainty.
