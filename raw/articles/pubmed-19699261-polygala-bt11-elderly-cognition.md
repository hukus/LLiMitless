# BT-11 is effective for enhancing cognitive functions in the elderly humans

PubMed-indexed randomized double-blind placebo-controlled comparison study of BT-11, an extract from Polygala tenuifolia root, in elderly humans. The study used CERAD and MMSE endpoints with 28 treated and 25 placebo participants.

The abstract reports larger total CERAD improvement with BT-11, especially word-list recognition, constructional recall and praxis, and modified Boston naming test. The source is small and product-specific but directly cognition-relevant.

Relevance: Polygala / BT-11 deserves watchlist visibility as a standardized traditional-medicine cognition lead with low-moderate medicinal signal.
