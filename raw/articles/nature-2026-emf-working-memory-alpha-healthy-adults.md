# Electromagnetic Field Stimulation Modulates Working Memory and Cortical Alpha Oscillations in Healthy Adults

Source type: open-access Scientific Reports article.

Core design:
- Published 2026-02-26 in Scientific Reports.
- N=98 healthy volunteers with mean age about 24 years.
- Between-subjects design comparing sham, Theta-Burst EMF, Theta-Gamma EMF, and 40 Hz gamma stimulation.
- Exposure lasted 30 minutes and used solenoid arrays placed in left-hemisphere, right-hemisphere, or bilateral temporal configurations.
- Outcomes included WAIS-IV Digit Span Forward, Backward, Sequencing, Arithmetic, Letter-Number Sequencing, Working Memory Index, and EEG/source-localization measures.

Main cognition findings:
- Theta-Burst EMF significantly reduced Digit Span Backward performance versus sham/control, with t(41)=2.47, p=0.018, Cohen d=0.76.
- The paper reports lower DSB standardized scores after Theta-Burst EMF than sham.
- Theta-Gamma EMF showed a lower Digit Span Forward post-hoc comparison versus sham, but the omnibus ANOVA was p=0.06, so this is exploratory rather than confirmatory.
- Neither effect appeared in the WAIS composite scores.

Main EEG findings:
- Theta-Burst EMF increased high-alpha activity in frontal regions, including left inferior frontal gyrus ROI and a whole-brain source-localized effect centered in right superior frontal gyrus.
- No significant EMF-placement-by-type interaction was detected for WAIS or EEG outcomes.
- The authors caution that the low-density EEG source projections should be interpreted as regional summaries, not precise anatomical localization.

Wiki takeaway:
- This is the current caution anchor for structured EMF / PEMF cognition claims.
- The direct healthy-adult signal is cognitive disruption, not clean enhancement.
- Any wiki page about EMF-based cognitive enhancement needs to preserve field pattern, frequency structure, coil/solenoid placement, sham generation, and endpoint specificity.

Limitations:
- No formal a priori power analysis.
- Between-subjects exposure design.
- Immediate post-exposure endpoints only.
- Low-density EEG and exploratory source-localization limits.
- Field pattern and placement should not be generalized to consumer PEMF devices without device-specific exposure validation.
