# Urolithin A improves muscle strength, exercise performance, and biomarkers of mitochondrial health in a randomized trial in middle-aged adults

- DOI: 10.1016/j.xcrm.2022.100633
- PMID: 35584623
- Journal: Cell Reports Medicine
- Ingested: 2026-05-03

## Summary

2022 randomized, placebo-controlled middle-aged adult trial of Mitopure urolithin A at two doses for 4 months. The paper reports increased circulating urolithin A, increased muscle strength, high-dose effects on VO2 and 6-minute-walk performance, lower plasma acylcarnitines and C-reactive protein, and increased mitophagy / mitochondrial-metabolism proteins in skeletal muscle.

The primary peak-power-output outcome was not significantly improved. The study is a muscle / mitochondrial-health trial, not a cognition trial.

Use in the wiki: human target-engagement and physical-performance proxy support for the urolithin A branch. Do not treat exercise-performance or skeletal-muscle mitophagy markers as direct cognition evidence.
