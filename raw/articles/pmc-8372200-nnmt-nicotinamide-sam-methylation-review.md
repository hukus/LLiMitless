# NNMT, nicotinamide, and SAM-dependent methylation

Open-access review on mechanisms and inhibitors of nicotinamide N-methyltransferase (NNMT).

Key capture:

- NNMT catalyzes transfer of a methyl group from S-adenosyl methionine / SAM to nicotinamide / NAM and related pyridine compounds.
- The reaction produces S-adenosyl-L-homocysteine / SAH and N1-methylnicotinamide / 1-MNA.
- NAM is a vitamin B3 form and a major NAD+ precursor in salvage metabolism, so NNMT links vitamin B3 handling, NAD biology, and one-carbon methylation state.
- The review is mainly enzyme/mechanism and inhibitor biology, not a cognition trial and not a supplement recommendation.

Interpretation for the wiki:
