# Hemispheric asymmetry in high-energy phosphate consumption during sleep-deprivation is balanced by creatine

- PMID: 40520504
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40520504/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

2025 open-access Frontiers study re-analysed a prior 21 h sleep-deprivation cohort of 15 healthy adults using 31P-MRS and cognitive testing. It found greater right- than left-hemisphere ATP decline under sleep deprivation, and high-dose creatine monohydrate (0.35 g/kg) partially balanced that asymmetry, aligning with the earlier cognitive rescue signal.

## Notes

This is a mechanistic target-state paper rather than a new standalone cognition RCT, but it strengthens the high-demand state model for creatine.
