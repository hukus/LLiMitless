# Coenzyme Q10 and cognition, 2025 review

2025 Nutrients review of CoQ10 supplementation and cognition across animal studies and human clinical trials.

The abstract reports that out of eight human clinical trials, four showed evidence of cognitive benefit and two showed increased cerebral blood flow, but results are inconsistent due to heterogeneous cognitive testing, populations, and CoQ10 formulation/bioavailability.

Use in wiki: CoQ10 is relevant enough to include as a redox/mitochondrial cofactor, but confidence should stay low because the human evidence is mixed and preparation-sensitive.
