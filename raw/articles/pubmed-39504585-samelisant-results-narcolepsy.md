# Samelisant (SUVN-G3031), a histamine 3 receptor inverse agonist: Results from the phase 2 double-blind randomized placebo-controlled study for the treatment of excessive daytime sleepiness in adult patients with narcolepsy

- PMID: 39504585
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39504585/
- ClinicalTrials.gov: NCT04072380

## Summary

Phase 2 double-blind placebo-controlled narcolepsy study of samelisant. The linked ClinicalTrials.gov record indicates 190 randomized participants and a 2 mg / 4 mg once-daily, 14-day design. The paper reports a statistically significant improvement on the primary Epworth Sleepiness Scale endpoint and supportive patient/clinician perspective scales. The most common adverse events were insomnia, abnormal dreams, nausea, and hot flush. This is a wakefulness result, not a direct cognition trial, but it materially upgrades the class from protocol-only to published phase-2 evidence.
