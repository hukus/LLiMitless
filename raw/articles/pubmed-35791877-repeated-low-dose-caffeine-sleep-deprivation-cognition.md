# Repeated low-dose caffeine ingestion during a night of total sleep deprivation improves endurance performance and cognitive function in young recreational runners

- PMID: 35791877
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35791877/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled crossover study in 12 non-habitual caffeine users. Three 2 mg/kg caffeine doses during total sleep deprivation improved reaction time and correct detections versus placebo, and still helped after a baseline night. This is one of the clearest examples that repeated acute dosing can preserve performance under strain rather than obviously collapsing from tolerance.

## Notes

Useful as a schedule-design example, but the participants were not habitual users and the setting was short-term sleep loss rather than baseline enhancement.
