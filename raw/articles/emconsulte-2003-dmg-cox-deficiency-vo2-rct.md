# EM Consulte 2003 DMG cytochrome-c oxidase deficiency crossover trial note

Liet et al. studied short-term dimethylglycine in children with Saguenay-Lac-Saint-Jean cytochrome-c oxidase deficiency. The design was a randomized double-blind crossover trial in 5 children, with 3 days of placebo or DMG, a 2-week washout, and crossover to the alternate treatment. The primary metabolic readout was oxygen consumption measured by indirect calorimetry.

DMG increased blood DMG exposure and was described as well tolerated, but oxygen consumption did not change significantly. The study also did not detect an effect on lactate, pyruvate, bicarbonate, or pH.

Interpretation for the wiki: this is not a cognition study, but it is a useful mechanistic counterweight to DMG oxygen-utilization and mitochondrial-energy claims. It should keep DMG below stronger energy-substrate comparators unless direct cognition or fatigue outcomes improve in better trials.
