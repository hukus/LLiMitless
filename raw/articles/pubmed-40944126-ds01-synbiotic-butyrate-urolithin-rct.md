# DS-01 synbiotic increased microbial diversity, urolithin A, butyrate production, and inflammation markers in healthy adults

- PMID: 40944126
- Published: 2025
- Source type: randomized placebo-controlled trial
- Checked: 2026-05-11
- Population: 32 healthy adults

## Summary

This trial tested a multi-species synbiotic, DS-01, in healthy adults for 91 days. The product combined 24 probiotic strains, a polyphenol-based prebiotic, capsule-in-capsule delivery, and 400 mg Indian pomegranate extract. Endpoints included microbiome composition, urinary urolithin A, fecal butyrate, serum CRP, and safety.

The captured abstract reports increased Bifidobacterium and Lactobacillus diversity, increased native beneficial microbes, urolithin A and butyrate-related movement, and inflammation-marker changes. It did not test cognition or brain fog.

Use this as a postbiotic-output / synbiotic-product identity source, not as evidence that butyrate itself improves cognition.

## Notes

- The formulation includes probiotic, prebiotic, and pomegranate-polyphenol elements, so attribution to butyrate alone is not possible.
- This source bridges [[urolithin-a]], psychobiotic products, and SCFA-output claims.
