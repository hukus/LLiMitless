# Oveporexton, an Oral Orexin Receptor 2-Selective Agonist, in Narcolepsy Type 1

- PMID: 40367374
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40367374/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

Phase 2 randomized placebo-controlled trial of oveporexton (TAK-861) in narcolepsy type 1. The parent trial showed large improvements in wakefulness and cataplexy over 8 weeks in 112 randomized participants, and it is the basis for the later cognition secondary analysis. This is the cleanest human proof-of-mechanism for orexin agonism so far, but it remains disorder-context evidence rather than healthy-adult enhancement evidence.
