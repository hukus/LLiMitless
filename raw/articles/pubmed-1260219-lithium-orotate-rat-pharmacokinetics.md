# Lithium orotate, carbonate and chloride: pharmacokinetics, polyuria in rats

- PMID: 1260219
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/1260219/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

This rat study found no difference in lithium uptake, distribution, or excretion between lithium orotate, carbonate, and chloride under the tested conditions. It is a useful counterpoint to newer claims that orotate is intrinsically superior as a lithium carrier.
