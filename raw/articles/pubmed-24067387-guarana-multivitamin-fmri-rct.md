# Acute multivitamin preparations with and without guarana: cognition and fMRI

- Source type: double-blind placebo-controlled randomized balanced crossover trial plus small fMRI substudy.
- Publication: Nutrients, 2013.
- Date checked: 2026-05-03.

This trial tested multivitamin preparations with and without guarana against placebo in 20 healthy adults, with five additional participants undergoing fMRI during rapid visual information processing and inspection-time tasks.

The multivitamin-with-guarana condition improved Serial Threes performance and self-rated contentment. fMRI showed that both multivitamin preparations increased activation in regions associated with working memory and attentional processing, with greater activation in the multivitamin-with-guarana condition.

Important caveat: this was not isolated guarana. The study was funded by Bayer Healthcare, and several authors reported supplement-industry research funding or consultancy. Treat it as supportive but product-combination and sponsor-sensitive.

Use in the wiki: combination-product and mechanistic plausibility anchor, not proof that guarana alone improves cognition.
