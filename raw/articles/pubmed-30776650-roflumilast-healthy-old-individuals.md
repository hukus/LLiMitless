# Acute treatment with the PDE4 inhibitor roflumilast improves verbal word memory in healthy old individuals: a double-blind placebo-controlled study

- PMID: 30776650
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30776650/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

2019 double-blind placebo-controlled 4-way crossover study in healthy older adults (60-80 years). Low-dose roflumilast improved delayed verbal recall, with Cohen's d about 0.69 at 100 micrograms; the highest dose brought more adverse effects and no added cognitive value.

## Key points

- Design: acute dose-ranging crossover after baseline screening for normal verbal memory.
- Outcome: verbal memory and spatial memory.
- Signal: delayed verbal recall improved at 100 micrograms, but not spatial memory.
- Safety: headache, dizziness, insomnia, and diarrhea were reported more often at 1000 micrograms.
