# A multicenter, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial of the PDE9A inhibitor, PF-04447943, in Alzheimer's disease

- PMID: 24801218
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24801218/
- Ingested: 2026-04-29

## Summary

2014 phase II AD trial of PF-04447943. The drug did not improve cognition, behavior, or global change compared with placebo, despite the PDE9/cGMP rationale. Gastrointestinal adverse events and discontinuations were somewhat higher in the active arm.

## Key points

- Randomization: 91 PF-04447943 and 100 placebo, 12 weeks.
- Dosing: PF-04447943 25 mg every 12 hours.
- Primary endpoint: ADAS-cog.
- Result: no significant treatment difference on ADAS-cog, NPI, or CGI-I.
- Safety: diarrhea, nausea, and discontinuation due to AEs were more frequent with PF-04447943.
