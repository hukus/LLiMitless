# The effect of plant active substances on cognitive function in healthy older adults: a systematic review and network meta-analysis of randomized controlled trials

## Summary

2026 Frontiers network meta-analysis of 25 randomized trials with 1,861 healthy older adults across 10 plant active substances. Raisin and tart cherry ranked highest for learning and memory; a Bacopa monnieri compound, curcumin, and tart cherry ranked highest for executive function; the Bacopa compound and raisin ranked highest for language; and guarana ranked highest for the perceptual-motor domain. Complex attention effects were generally limited.

## Notes

- The authors treat the rankings as probabilistic rather than definitive, with especially small evidence bases for language and perceptual-motor outcomes.
- The source expands the plant/extract lane beyond the current standalone pages to raisin/grape, tart cherry, guarana, salvia, wild green oat, and blueberry.
- Long-term safety, dosage optimization, and interactions with conventional medications remain unresolved.
