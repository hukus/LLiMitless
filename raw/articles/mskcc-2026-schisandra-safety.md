# MSKCC Schisandra safety monograph

Memorial Sloan Kettering Cancer Center Schisandra monograph checked in 2026.

MSKCC describes Schisandra as a fruit extract used in traditional Chinese medicine, including as a tonic for vitality and in formulas for fatigue and sleep. It says only a small number of human studies have been conducted and they are too limited to draw conclusions.

Safety interpretation for the wiki: MSKCC says no serious side effects have been reported but emphasizes that Schisandra is not well studied in humans. It warns that Schisandra can affect drugs that are CYP1A2, CYP3A4, CYP3A5, or P-glycoprotein substrates, and may reduce liver-enzyme values on lab tests. It also notes tacrolimus interaction evidence in liver-transplant patients.
