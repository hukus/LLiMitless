# Rasagiline for dysexecutive symptoms during wearing-off in Parkinson disease

- PMID: 28956175
- DOI: 10.1007/s10072-017-3123-2
- Journal: Neurological Sciences (2018)
- Design: pilot study.

## Captured takeaways

- The study examined executive function at the end of levodopa dose in Parkinson disease patients with wearing-off symptoms.
- Rasagiline was reported as well tolerated.
- Rasagiline produced significant improvement on the Frontal Assessment Battery and improved motor symptoms at the end of levodopa dose.
- The authors interpreted the result as support for optimizing dopamine replacement therapy in fluctuating PD.
- Interpretation for the wiki: this strengthens a PD wearing-off / executive-state hypothesis, but the evidence remains a pilot and does not generalize to healthy-adult nootropic use.
