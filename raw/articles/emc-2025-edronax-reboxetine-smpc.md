# Edronax reboxetine Summary of Product Characteristics

## Captured takeaways

- UK electronic Medicines Compendium Summary of Product Characteristics for Edronax 4 mg tablets / reboxetine.
- Reboxetine is a selective norepinephrine reuptake inhibitor marketed as an antidepressant in some jurisdictions; it is not an FDA-approved U.S. drug.
- The SmPC warns about cardiovascular caution, hypotension contexts, drug-history review, and interactions.
- In short-term depression studies where heart rate was measured by ECG, reboxetine was associated with mean heart-rate increases versus placebo.
- Common practical adverse effects include dry mouth, constipation, insomnia, sweating, tachycardia, dizziness/vertigo, urinary difficulty or retention, and sexual adverse effects.
- Use in wiki: safety/status source for reboxetine as a non-U.S.-approved comparator in the nonstimulant noradrenergic attention class.
