# Growth Differentiation Factor 11 treatment leads to neuronal and vascular improvements in the hippocampus of aged mice

- PMID: 30470794
- PMCID: PMC6251885
- DOI: 10.1038/s41598-018-35716-6
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30470794/
- Ingested: 2026-05-04

## Summary

Scientific Reports 2018 mouse study. Systemic GDF11 treatment in old mice enhanced hippocampal neurogenesis, improved vasculature, and increased neuronal activity / plasticity markers in the hippocampus and cortex. The authors argue that systemically delivered GDF11 acts at least partly through brain endothelial cells rather than by crossing the BBB. This is preclinical vascular-neurogenesis support, not a human cognition trial.
