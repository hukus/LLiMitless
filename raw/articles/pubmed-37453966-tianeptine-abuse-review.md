# Tianeptine, an Antidepressant with Opioid Agonist Effects: Pharmacology and Abuse Potential, a Narrative Review

- PMID: 37453966
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37453966/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

Review of tianeptine pharmacology and abuse potential emphasizing opioid-agonist effects, retail availability outside prescription channels, tolerance, dependence, overdose, and suicide-attempt cases. For the mismatch map, the salient point is that the strong subjective appeal of tianeptine is inseparable from a serious misuse and dependency profile.
