# Effect of Montmorency tart cherry juice on cognitive performance in older adults

- Source type: open-access randomized controlled trial.
- Publication: Food & Function, 2019.
- Date checked: 2026-05-03.

This 12-week trial randomized older adults aged 65-80 with normal cognition to consume two cups of Montmorency tart cherry juice daily or a control drink. Thirty-seven participants were enrolled and 34 completed the study.

The tart cherry group showed better contentment-with-memory scores, movement time, and paired-associates-learning performance versus control, with within-group improvements in visual sustained attention and a trend in spatial working memory. The authors described no adverse events, but the study was small and industry-supported.

Use in the wiki: tart-cherry product anchor. Treat as low-confidence product-specific evidence until replicated.
