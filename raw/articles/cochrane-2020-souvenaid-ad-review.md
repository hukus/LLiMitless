# Cochrane plain-language summary: Souvenaid for Alzheimer's disease

## Captured takeaways

- Cochrane summarizes evidence for Souvenaid, a patented Fortasyn Connect multinutrient mixture intended for people with Alzheimer's disease.
- The review page says Souvenaid probably has little or no effect on memory or other thinking-skill measures in prodromal AD after two years or mild-to-moderate AD dementia after 24 weeks.
- It also says Souvenaid probably produces a slight improvement below estimates of meaningful change in prodromal AD after 24 months, while having little or no effect in mild-to-moderate AD dementia after 24 weeks.
- The review notes manufacturer funding for two studies.
- Interpretation for the wiki: even the stronger Fortasyn/Souvenaid human branch is contested and clinically modest; do not treat it as proof for standalone uridine or healthy-adult enhancement.
