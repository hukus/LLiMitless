# Exploratory Randomized, Controlled, Dose-response Study on the Impact of Nicotine From Five Variants of a Nicotine Tablet on Attention

- ClinicalTrials.gov: NCT07408180
- Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07408180
- Ingested: 2026-04-30
- Last update posted: 2026-02-18
- Status: active, not recruiting
- Sponsor: Philip Morris Products S.A.

## Summary

This is a 40-participant, randomized, double-blind, crossover study in adult occasional nicotine-pouch users. It tests five nicotine-tablet variants (0, 0.5, 1.0, 1.5, 2.0 mg) with attention measured by Conners Continuous Performance Test at 15 minutes and 2 hours, plus craving as a secondary outcome. Results are not posted, so it remains a pending translational readout rather than an efficacy signal.

## Notes

- The study is directly relevant to the branch because it is explicitly attention-focused and dose-response focused.
- Sponsor involvement means the eventual readout should be interpreted cautiously for bias.
- No results were posted as of the 2026-02-18 record update.
