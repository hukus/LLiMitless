# Is Cerebrolysin Useful in Psychiatry Disorders?

- PMID: 40722733
- PMCID: PMC12292664
- DOI: 10.3390/biomedicines13071661
- Journal: Biomedicines, 2025
- Source type: scoping review of psychiatry uses
- Ingested: 2026-05-04

## Summary

2025 scoping review of Cerebrolysin in psychiatric disorders. The review searched Embase, Scopus, and Web of Science using psychiatry, depression, mood, bipolar, schizophrenia, and addiction terms. The authors report that eligible psychiatry literature was sparse, with six included articles after duplicate removal and screening. Their conclusion is cautious: Cerebrolysin may have limited relevance as support in depression, autism spectrum disorders, or adverse effects during neuroleptic treatment, but the evidence base is small and difficult to use for firm psychiatric indications.

## Signal Use

- Medicinal / formal signal: low-confidence review-level context for psychiatry; not a direct cognition efficacy result.
- Anecdotal signal: none.
