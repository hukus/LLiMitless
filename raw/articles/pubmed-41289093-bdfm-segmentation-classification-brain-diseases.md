# BDFM: Foundation Model for Segmentation and Classification Tasks of Brain Diseases

PMID: 41289093
Published: 2025-11-25
Journal: IEEE Transactions on Biomedical Engineering
Authors: Jiatian Zhang, Chunxiao Xu, Han Zhong, Yiheng Cao, Lingxiao Zhao

Abstract: The lack of high-quality annotated images and the limited transferability of task-specific models hamper practical AI-assisted diagnosis for brain diseases. BDFM is a masked-image-modeling foundation model built on a pretraining database of more than ten brain diseases. The model uses a spatial-frequency dual-domain decoder and spatial mean masking to improve lesion feature extraction. Results on three downstream tasks show that BDFM generalizes well to segmentation and classification with small annotated datasets.

Research note: This is useful as an annotation-sparse disease-analysis backbone, but it is still a clinical-imaging tool rather than a cognition-outcome tool.
