# Pterostilbene biomedical applications review

- Source type: PubMed-indexed narrative review abstract.
- Publication: Critical Reviews in Clinical Laboratory Sciences, 2013.
- DOI: 10.3109/10408363.2013.805182.
- Date checked: 2026-05-03.

Pterostilbene is a naturally dimethylated analogue of resveratrol. The review frames it as having overlapping biological activity with resveratrol and higher in-vivo bioavailability, and discusses inflammatory, metabolic, cardiovascular, aging, memory, and cognition applications.

Use in the wiki: boundary source only. Pterostilbene should not be treated as a separate cognition-efficacy entity until human cognition trial evidence is captured; for now it stays folded under the resveratrol/stilbene branch.
