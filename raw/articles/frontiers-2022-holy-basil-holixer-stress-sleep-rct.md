# Holixer holy basil stress, mood, and sleep trial

- Source type: randomized double-blind placebo-controlled trial.
- Publication: Frontiers in Nutrition, 2022.
- Date checked: 2026-05-11.

This 8-week trial randomized 100 stressed adults aged 18-65 years to 125 mg Ocimum tenuiflorum extract twice daily or placebo. The product was Holixer, a hydroalcoholic extract derived from leaf-rich aerial parts and standardized to at least 5% Ocimum Bioactive Complex.

Compared with placebo, holy basil improved Perceived Stress Scale scores and Athens Insomnia Scale scores. At week 8, hair cortisol was lower. The extract also buffered responses to the Maastricht Acute Stress Test, with lower salivary cortisol, salivary amylase, systolic and diastolic blood pressure, and subjective stress ratings. The study reported good tolerability and no major adverse effects.

This is not a direct cognition trial, but it supports a stress/sleep-mediated brain-fog hypothesis for holy basil, especially in people whose cognitive complaints are driven by hyperarousal, stress, or poor sleep.
