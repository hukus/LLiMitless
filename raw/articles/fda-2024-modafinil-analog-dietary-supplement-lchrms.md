# FDA/ORA LC-HRMS method for modafinil analogs in dietary supplements

- FDA/ORA poster titled "Development and Validation of an Analytical Method to Identify and Quantitate Novel Modafinil Analogs in Dietary Supplements."
- The poster notes that drugs boosting concentration or wakefulness are prescribed for specific medical conditions, that nootropics have been used recreationally and found in dietary supplements, and that modafinil is a Schedule IV drug used for narcolepsy and sleep disorders in the United States.
- The method separates modafinil from four unscheduled analogs: adrafinil, bisfluoromodafinil / CRL-40,940, fladrafinil / CRL-40,941, and N-methyl-4,4-difluoromodafinil.
- The poster frames active pharmaceutical ingredients in dietary supplements as adulteration and lists modafinil-style adverse effects such as headache/migraine, nausea, anxiety, dizziness, and difficulty sleeping.
- Interpretation for the wiki: unapproved modafinil analogs are a product-identity and hidden-API risk lane before they are a cognition-efficacy lane. The source is analytical/regulatory, not proof that these analogs improve cognition.
