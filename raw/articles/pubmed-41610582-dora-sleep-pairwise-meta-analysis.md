# The Effect of Dual Orexin Receptor Antagonists on Sleep: A Systematic Review and Pairwise Meta-analysis

- PMID: 41610582
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41610582/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Large 2026 systematic review and pairwise meta-analysis of DORAs across randomized trials through January 2026. Across 77 trials and 16,416 participants, DORAs increased subjective total sleep time by about 19 minutes and raised somnolence risk, while sleep quality did not clearly improve. The authors caution that data outside insomnia are sparse.
