# Low-speed uphill exercise increases lactate and brain-derived neurotrophic factor in brain regions for memory and learning

- PMID: 39933602
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39933602/
- Ingested: 2026-04-30

## Summary

Rat exercise study showing that low-speed uphill exercise raised lactate and BDNF in cortex and hippocampus, with effects depending on exercise duration and grade. It is mechanistic rather than human cognition evidence, but it supports the exercise-lactate-brain-plasticity bridge that the lactate branch has been circling.
