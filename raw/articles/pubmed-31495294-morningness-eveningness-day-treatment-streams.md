# Morningness-eveningness scores predict outcomes differentially for depressed patients attending morning vs. afternoon day treatment streams

- PMID: 31495294
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31495294/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Chronotype interacted strongly with treatment stream in depressed patients. Definite evening chronotypes did better in the morning stream than in the afternoon stream, making this a practical chronotype-by-timing example.

## Notes

- 203 patients with a major depressive episode were analyzed.
- Treatment stream by MEQ interaction predicted change in depression and quality of life.
- Evening types responded best in the morning stream and worst in the afternoon stream.
- Morning-stream patients whose MEQ shifted toward morningness also improved more in depression.
- This is a scheduling / chronotherapy signal, not a device stimulation signal.
