# DNA Methylation: A Potential Mediator of the Memory Regulatory Effects of taVNS.

- PMID: 40940739
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40940739/
- Ingested: 2026-04-26

## Summary

Review of possible epigenetic/memory mechanisms for taVNS.

## Notes

Transcutaneous auricular vagus nerve stimulation (taVNS), an emerging noninvasive neuromodulation technique, has shown promise for improving memory. A better understanding of the epigenetic mechanisms underlying the effects of taVNS would inform the molecular outcomes essential for memory and cognition.
