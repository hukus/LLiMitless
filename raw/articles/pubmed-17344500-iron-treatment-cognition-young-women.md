# Iron treatment normalizes cognitive functioning in young women

- PMID: 17344500
- Published: 2007
- Source type: randomized controlled trial
- Checked: 2026-05-11
- Ingested: 2026-05-11

## Summary

This controlled trial tested whether iron supplementation could improve cognition in women across iron-status categories, including iron deficiency without anemia. The key result for the wiki is status dependence: cognitive improvement tracked iron-status improvement, especially ferritin improvement, rather than behaving like a general stimulant or universal nootropic.

Use this source as the main formal anchor for [[iron]] as a brain-fog / cognition status-correction candidate in menstruating or otherwise iron-deficient adults. It should not be used to support blind iron use in iron-replete people.

## Notes

- Best routed through ferritin, transferrin saturation, hemoglobin, menstruation/blood-loss history, diet, inflammation, and gastrointestinal tolerance.
- The practical claim is "correcting low iron stores can improve cognition in some deficient people," not "iron improves cognition in healthy replete adults."
