# Impaired glymphatic function as a biomarker for subjective cognitive decline: An exploratory dual cohort study

- PMID: 39107995
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39107995/

## Summary

2024 exploratory dual-cohort study linking impaired glymphatic function to subjective cognitive decline. The paper positions DTI-ALPS as a potential biomarker for early cognitive vulnerability, but it does not provide interventional evidence.
