# 2016 trace amines and TAAR1 review note

Pei, Asif-Malik, and Canales reviewed trace amines and trace amine-associated receptor 1. The review frames beta-phenylethylamine, octopamine, and tyramine as trace amines that can act through TAAR1, a G-protein-coupled receptor relevant to monoaminergic modulation, addiction biology, mood disorders, schizophrenia, and Parkinson disease hypotheses.

The review cites experimental work in which beta-phenylethylamine altered monoamine transporter function through TAAR1 and places TAAR1 pharmacology near amphetamine-like and trace-amine signaling rather than ordinary nutrient replacement.

Wiki use: mechanistic support for keeping [[phenethylamine]] in a trace-amine / TAAR1 / monoamine-modulation lane. It does not provide healthy-adult cognition efficacy evidence.
