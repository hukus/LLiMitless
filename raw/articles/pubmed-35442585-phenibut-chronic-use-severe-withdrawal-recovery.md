# Chronic Phenibut Use: Symptoms, Severe Withdrawal, and Recovery

- PMID: 35442585
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35442585/
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

Case report of 3-year phenibut use with dependence, parasomnia-like symptoms, and dysexecutive function at high doses. After abrupt cessation the patient developed severe withdrawal, required hospitalization and intubation, then improved after about a week and remained stable on an extended acamprosate and baclofen taper.
