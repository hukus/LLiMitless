# A generalizable foundation model for analysis of human brain MRI

PMID: 41644653
Published: 2026-04
Journal: Nature Neuroscience
Authors: Divyanshu Tak, Biniam A Garomsa, Tafadzwa L Chaunzwa, Anna Zapaishchykova, Juan Carlos Climent Pardo, Zezhong Ye, John Zielke, Yashwanth Ravipati, Sri Vajapeyam, Maryam Mahootiha, Ceilidh Smith, Ariana M Familiar, Kevin X Liu, Sanjay Prabhu, Pratiti Bandopadhayay, Ali Nabavizadeh, Sabine Mueller, Hugo Jwl Aerts, Raymond Y Huang, Tina Y Poussaint, Benjamin H Kann

Abstract: BrainIAC is presented as a general-purpose foundation model for brain MRI analysis. It was trained and validated on 48,519 brain MRIs across 35 datasets spanning 10 neurological conditions and 4 MRI sequences. The model uses self-supervised contrastive learning to build reusable representations from unlabeled scans, then adapts to downstream tasks such as sequence classification, brain-age prediction, mutation classification, and overall survival. The published abstract says BrainIAC outperforms supervised-from-scratch training and an established pretrained model, especially in low-data and hard tasks, and can serve as a core encoder for imaging pipelines and multimodal frameworks.

Research note: The immediate utility is generalizable low-data imaging backbone support, not direct cognition enhancement. It matters because it can improve biomarker discovery, disease stratification, and transfer across neurological tasks.
