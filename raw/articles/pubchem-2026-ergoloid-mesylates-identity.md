# PubChem 2026: Ergoloid mesylates / dihydroergotoxine identity

PubChem identifies dihydroergotoxine mesylate / ergoloid mesylates as an ergot-alkaloid mixture with synonyms including ergoloid mesylate, co-dergocrine mesylate, dihydroergotoxine methanesulfonate, and dihydroergotoxine mesilate.

Identity details: PubChem CID 592735 is listed with molecular formula C33H45N5O5 and molecular weight 591.7, while the PubChem/MeSH description emphasizes that ergoloid mesylates are a mixture of mesylates of dihydroergocornine, dihydroergocristine, and alpha/beta dihydroergocryptine.

Regulatory/history note from linked source summaries: the mixture was developed by Novartis, FDA approved historically, and specific formulations have changed or been discontinued. Treat product identity and country/formulation status as part of the evidence gate.
