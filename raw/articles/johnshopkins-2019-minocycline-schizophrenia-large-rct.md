# Large minocycline schizophrenia randomized trial

2019 Schizophrenia Research multicenter randomized placebo-controlled trial summarized by Johns Hopkins. Two hundred participants with schizophrenia or schizoaffective disorder were randomized to minocycline 200 mg/day or placebo as an add-on to antipsychotic treatment for 16 weeks.

The trial found no significant difference between minocycline and placebo for PANSS total, PANSS subscales, CGI, or BACS cognition. The publication summary concluded that minocycline did not improve symptoms or cognition in schizophrenia.

Interpretation for the wiki: this larger later RCT is an important counterweight to earlier smaller schizophrenia signals.
