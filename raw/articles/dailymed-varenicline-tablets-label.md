# VARENICLINE- varenicline tartrate tablet, film coated

## Summary

Varenicline is a nicotinic receptor partial agonist indicated for smoking cessation. The label warns about neuropsychiatric events, seizures, alcohol interaction, accidental injury, cardiovascular events in patients with underlying CV disease, somnambulism, and nausea.
