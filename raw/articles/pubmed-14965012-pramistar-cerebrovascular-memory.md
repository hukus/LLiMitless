# Pramistar in memory decline after cerebrovascular accidents or head injuries

PubMed-indexed placebo-controlled multicenter study of pramiracetam / Pramistar in memory and attention problems after cerebrovascular accidents or craniocerebral trauma. The trial is clinical-recovery rather than healthy enhancement.

The source is relevant because pramiracetam has a stronger formal bridge than several racetam long-tail names, but the phenotype is injury/vascular recovery.

Relevance: supports watchlist inclusion and argues against broad enhancement extrapolation.
