# Randomized controlled study of the histamine H3 inverse agonist MK-0249 in adult attention-deficit/hyperactivity disorder

- PMID: 22901359
- Trial registration: NCT00475735
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22901359/

## Summary

2012 randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled crossover trial in 72 adults with ADHD. MK-0249 5-10 mg/day was not different from placebo on AISRS at week 4; OROS methylphenidate was superior to placebo. Insomnia was more common with MK-0249 than placebo.
