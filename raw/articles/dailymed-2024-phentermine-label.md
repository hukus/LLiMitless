# DailyMed phentermine hydrochloride label

- Source: DailyMed label for phentermine hydrochloride tablet
- Revision captured: 2024-05 label shown by DailyMed

## Captured takeaways

- Phentermine hydrochloride is labeled as a sympathomimetic amine anorectic for short-term adjunct use in exogenous obesity management, alongside exercise, behavioral modification, and caloric restriction.
- Indication is weight reduction in patients with BMI at least 30 kg/m2, or at least 27 kg/m2 with risk factors such as controlled hypertension, diabetes, or hyperlipidemia.
- The label warns against coadministration with other drug products for weight loss because safety and efficacy of combination therapy are not established.
- Key warnings include primary pulmonary hypertension, valvular heart disease, tolerance, effect on ability to engage in hazardous tasks, abuse/dependence risk, alcohol interaction, and caution in hypertension.
- Drug interactions include monoamine oxidase inhibitors due to hypertensive-crisis risk, alcohol, altered requirements for insulin or oral hypoglycemics, and reduced effect of adrenergic neuron blocking drugs.
- Adverse event categories include cardiovascular, central nervous system, gastrointestinal, allergic, and endocrine systems.
- The label states phentermine is a Schedule IV controlled substance.
- Interpretation for the wiki: phentermine is a prescription obesity drug and stimulant/anorectic comparator. Any focus or energy report must be separated from weight loss, appetite suppression, sleep loss, caffeine changes, BP/HR, and misuse risk.
