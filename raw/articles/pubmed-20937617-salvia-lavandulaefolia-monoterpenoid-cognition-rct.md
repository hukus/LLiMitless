# Monoterpenoid extract of Salvia lavandulaefolia with cholinesterase-inhibiting properties improves cognitive performance and mood

- Source type: double-blind placebo-controlled crossover study plus in vitro cholinesterase testing.
- Publication: Journal of Psychopharmacology, 2011.
- Date checked: 2026-05-03.

This study tested 50 microliters of Salvia lavandulaefolia essential oil in 36 healthy participants, with placebo on a separate occasion 7 days apart. The essential oil was characterized as a potent human acetylcholinesterase inhibitor and consisted mostly of monoterpenoids.

Oral consumption improved secondary memory and attention, most notably 1 hour post-dose, and reduced mental fatigue while increasing alertness more clearly at 4 hours post-dose.

Use in the wiki: Spanish-sage essential-oil / terpenoid anchor. This is not interchangeable with raw sage leaf, sage tea, Cognivia, Sibelius Sage, or Salvia divinorum.
