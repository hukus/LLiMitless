# N-of-1 Randomized Intervention Trials in Health Psychology: A Systematic Review and Methodology Critique

- PMID: 30124759
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30124759/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

2018 systematic review and methodology critique of n-of-1 randomized intervention trials in health psychology. The review included 54 publications covering 1,193 participants; 90% reported some quantitative way to determine the superior treatment, but only 36% reported enough information to calculate heterogeneity of treatment effect. The paper is useful here because it reinforces that n-of-1 designs need explicit randomization, clear outcomes, and analysis choices that fit the repeated-measures structure.
