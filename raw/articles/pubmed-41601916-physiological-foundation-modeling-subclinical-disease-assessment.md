# Physiological foundation modeling for subclinical disease assessment

PMID: 41601916
PMCID: PMC12832941
Published: 2026-01-24

Abstract focus: Bioprofile is a physiology-based patient representation trained on large routinely collected health data and tested prospectively for steatotic liver disease screening. Retrospective validation beat existing foundation-model and clinical-score comparators, and prospective nominations aligned strongly with observed data, making this a practical example of foundation-model-based candidate selection rather than pure benchmark scoring.
