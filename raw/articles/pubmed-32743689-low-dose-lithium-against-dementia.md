# Low-dose lithium against dementia

- PMID: 32743689
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32743689/
- Letter to the editor / commentary, published 2020-08-03

## Summary

This commentary argues that the 2019 long-term low-dose lithium trial in amnestic MCI was unusually encouraging for dementia prevention. It notes that cognition stayed stable on lithium while placebo declined, and that the open-label extension showed fewer progressions to dementia in the lithium arm. The author also flags major caveats: the trial was smaller than planned, blinding and outcome-shift concerns exist, some p-values are borderline, and the dementia-conversion endpoint missed statistical significance by a narrow margin.

## Key Points

- The letter highlights the 2019 MCI trial as the first low-dose lithium study in MCI.
- It quotes effect sizes around Hedges' g 0.5 to 0.6 for key scales.
- It discusses a relative risk around 0.54 under one dropout assumption and 0.76 under a more conservative assumption.
- It treats the result as promising but uncertain, not settled proof.
