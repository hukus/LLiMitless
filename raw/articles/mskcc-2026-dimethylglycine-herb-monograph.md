# MSKCC 2026 dimethylglycine monograph note

Memorial Sloan Kettering's About Herbs monograph describes dimethylglycine as an amino acid found in plant and animal cells and foods including beans, cereal grains, brown rice, pumpkin seeds, and liver. It notes use claims around energy, immune function, seizures, autism, ADHD, athletic performance, and chronic fatigue syndrome.

MSKCC's interpretation is cautious: clinical confirmation is lacking or mixed for the major promoted uses. The monograph says athletic performance and ADHD uses lack scientific evidence, chronic fatigue syndrome lacks scientific evidence, autism evidence is mixed with no systematic-review support for benefit, and epilepsy case reports have not been confirmed by larger studies.

Interpretation for the wiki: this is a practical safety/status source for keeping DMG in a low-confidence supplement watchlist. It supports tracking DMG as a marketed chemical with cognition/fatigue claims while preventing upgrade beyond the evidence.
