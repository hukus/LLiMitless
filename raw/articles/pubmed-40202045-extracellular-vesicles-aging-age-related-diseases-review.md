# Extracellular Vesicles in Aging and Age-Related Diseases. How Important Are They?

- PMID: 40202045
- Source: Advanced Biology 2025
- Ingested: 2026-04-29

## Summary

Broad review of extracellular vesicles in aging and age-related disease. The article is useful as a cautionary umbrella for the plasma-factor branch because it emphasizes that EV cargo is context-dependent, the literature is heterogeneous, and reproducibility remains a major problem.

## Notes

- EV biology is framed as important for organ crosstalk, nutrient sensing, senescence, mitochondrial function, insulin resistance, and neurodegeneration.
- The review explicitly notes that age, disease state, exercise, nutrition, and sleep can shape EV cargo and function.
- The main limitation is not lack of ideas; it is method heterogeneity, limited reproducibility, and context-dependent effects.
- The paper does not provide a direct cognitive intervention effect size.
- It is a good high-level reminder that EV/plasma-factor claims should not be upgraded from biomarker plausibility to treatment plausibility without controlled human evidence.
