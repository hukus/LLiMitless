# Allopregnanolone phase 1b/2a early AD
- PMID: 33344752
- Clinical trial: NCT02221622; 24 participants with early AD (MCI due to AD or mild AD), weekly IV allopregnanolone or placebo for 12 weeks plus 1-month follow-up.
- Primary endpoint was safety/tolerability; exploratory endpoints included cognitive and imaging biomarkers.
- Key result: allopregnanolone was safe and well tolerated; mild sedation set the MTD; no adverse cognitive or MRI outcomes were evident.
- Branch readout: this is a dosing/safety anchor, not an efficacy win.
