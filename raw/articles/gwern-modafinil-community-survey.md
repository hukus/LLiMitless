# Modafinil community survey

- Source type: online survey of modafinil users
- Survey fielding: 2015-09-26 to 2015-10-27
- QA-passing responses: 3,835
- Respondents with genotype data: 216 for COMT Rs4680
- Scope: dosages, consumption patterns, ratings, purchases, demographics, tolerance, side effects, and SNP moderators

## Summary

This survey is a strong comparator anchor for modafinil because it is large, detailed, and includes both usage and adverse-effect questions. It does not establish causal efficacy, but it does give useful bounds on tolerance, dependency, and subjective use patterns.

## Key signals

- Modafinil use is concentrated in students and professionals, with the sample skewing male and highly educated.
- Generic versus name-brand comparisons were inconclusive for most respondents.
- A proposed COMT Rs4680 moderator did not replicate: the modafinil subjective-experience score was not significantly associated with genotype, and the direction was opposite to the earlier candidate-gene claim.
- Tolerance was common but usually partial: 22.61% reported no tolerance, 31.18% slight tolerance, 33.82% moderate tolerance, 11.08% substantial tolerance, and 1.31% complete loss of effect.
- Median time to tolerance onset was 2 weeks.
- The most common side effects were headaches, smelly urine, insomnia, and anxiety/fast heart rate.

## Caveats

- Self-selected online sample.
- Self-report and recall bias are large.
- Genotype subgroup analysis is observational and not a validation of mechanism.
