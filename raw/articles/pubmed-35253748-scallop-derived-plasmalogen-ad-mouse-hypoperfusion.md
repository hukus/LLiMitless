# Clinical and Pathological Benefits of Scallop-Derived Plasmalogen in a Novel Mouse Model of Alzheimer's Disease with Chronic Cerebral Hypoperfusion

- PMID: 35253748
- Source: PubMed / J Alzheimers Dis 2022
- Ingested: 2026-04-28

## Summary

Mouse study reporting that scallop-derived plasmalogen improved clinical and pathological outcomes in a chronic cerebral hypoperfusion AD model. The paper is preclinical support for the branch and aligns with the human trial material, but it does not establish human efficacy.

## Notes

- More relevant as mechanistic support than as efficacy proof.
- Useful for keeping the product identity consistent with the human trial.
