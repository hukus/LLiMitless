# Non-invasive Stimulation of the Glymphatic System for Slowing Cognitive Decline

- ClinicalTrials.gov: NCT07044596
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

Active University of Wisconsin study using V1 trigeminal stimulation, fNIRS, and MRI to test whether non-invasive stimulation can modulate blood flow, restore CSF flow, and improve waste clearance. The registry lists healthy adults and recent mTBI cohorts, but no results are posted yet. Cognitive benefit remains hypothetical and secondary.
