# Transcranial temporal interference stimulation precisely targets deep brain regions to regulate eye movements

- Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40214945/
- Captured via: PubMed / Neuroscience Bulletin open-access record

## Summary

2025 preclinical mouse study showing that temporal interference stimulation (tTIS) can more focally target the superior colliculus than conventional tACS-like approaches. Computational modeling plus in vivo calcium recordings and eye-movement tracking showed that beat-frequency settings could modulate SC neural activity and induce eye movements, whereas a 0 Hz control did not. This is target-precision and mechanism evidence, not human cognition efficacy.
