# Bedtime Brain State Predicts the Impact of Closed-Loop Auditory Stimulation on Sleep and Cognition

- DOI: 10.1101/2025.11.12.25340010
- Study type: preprint
- Population: 28 healthy adults, ages 19-40, under mild sleep restriction

## Captured takeaways

- Pre-sleep EEG features predicted responsiveness to auditory stimulation.
- Auditory stimulation increased slow-wave activity in more than 90% of participants.
- Pre-sleep alpha and theta power correlated with slow-wave activity enhancement.
- Sleep onset latency also correlated with response.
- The authors reported memory recall and sustained attention assessments the next day, making this a state-response and cognition paper rather than a pure physiology paper.
- Interpretation for the wiki: pre-sleep brain state is a candidate responder marker for CLAS, but the evidence is still preprint-level and does not yet validate chronotype as the causal modifier.
