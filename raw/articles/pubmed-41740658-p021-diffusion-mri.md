# Diffusion MRI after early P021 treatment in 3xTg-AD mice

- PMID: 41740658
- PMCID: PMC12993872
- DOI: 10.1016/j.mri.2026.110641
- Journal: Magnetic Resonance Imaging (2026)

## Summary

The study compared diffusion and diffusional-kurtosis MRI measures in 8-month-old normal-control, vehicle-treated 3xTg-AD, and orally P021-treated 3xTg-AD mice. P021 partially normalized selected white-matter measures in corpus callosum and external capsule and a fractional-anisotropy measure in cingulate cortex.

## Evidence boundary

This is a mouse imaging-biomarker extension, not a human study and not by itself a demonstration of cognitive benefit. The paper disclosed that Khalid Iqbal holds P021-related patents and co-founded Phanes Biotech, which licenses the compound; several authors also disclosed a provisional patent related to P021 for myelin disorders.
