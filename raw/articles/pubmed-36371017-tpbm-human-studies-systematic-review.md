# Can transcranial photobiomodulation improve cognitive function? A systematic review of human studies

- PMID: 36371017
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36371017/
- Ingested: 2026-04-27

## Summary

2023 systematic review of human tPBM studies. Useful as a baseline: it supported biologic plausibility and early cognitive signals, but the field was still small and heterogeneous. The 2025 literature mainly adds stronger task/network data and more active trial registration.
