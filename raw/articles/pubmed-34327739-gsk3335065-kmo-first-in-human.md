# Assessment of the safety, pharmacokinetics and pharmacodynamics of GSK3335065, an inhibitor of kynurenine monooxygenase, in a randomised placebo-controlled first-in-human study in healthy volunteers

- PMID: 34327739
- DOI: 10.1111/bcp.15010
- Journal: British Journal of Clinical Pharmacology (2022)

## Captured takeaways

- Healthy male volunteers received ascending single IV bolus doses of GSK3335065 or placebo.
- The study was designed to assess safety, tolerability, pharmacokinetics, and pharmacodynamics.
- One subject had changes in tryptophan-pathway metabolites consistent with partial KMO inhibition.
- A broad complex ventricular tachycardia was observed in that subject, and the study was terminated early.
- Development was subsequently discontinued.
- Interpretation for the wiki: important for target engagement and safety, but not a cognition-efficacy signal.
