# Methylene Blue Protects Spatial Working Memory after Traumatic Brain Injury in Mice

- PMID: 40879922
- DOI: 10.1007/s10517-025-06458-z
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40879922/
- Ingested: 2026-05-04

## Summary

2025 mouse traumatic-brain-injury paper reporting that inflammatory markers rose in blood plasma and affected brain area 24 days after injury, alongside spatial working-memory impairment. Methylene blue administered during the first 3 days after injury reduced some inflammatory markers, increased expression of genes involved in mitochondrial biogenesis and mitophagy, and partially mitigated TBI-induced cognitive deficits.

## Signal Use

- Medicinal / preclinical signal: recovery-state cognition support in a mouse TBI model.
- Mechanism signal: mitochondrial quality control, mitophagy, and inflammatory-marker modulation.
- Cognition signal: preclinical only; does not upgrade healthy-human enhancement confidence.
