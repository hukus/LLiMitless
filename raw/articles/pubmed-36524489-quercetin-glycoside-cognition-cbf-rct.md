# Quercetin glycosides cognitive function and cerebral blood flow RCT

- Source type: PubMed-indexed randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial.
- Publication: European Review for Medical and Pharmacological Sciences, 2022.
- PMID: 36524489.
- DOI: 10.26355/eurrev_202212_30541.
- Date checked: 2026-05-03.

This trial randomized 80 healthy adults aged 60-75 with ageing-related forgetfulness awareness but no cognitive impairment. Participants consumed 500 mL/day of a beverage containing 110 mg quercetin glycoside as isoquercitrin or placebo for 40 weeks.

Main result: Cognitrax reaction time significantly improved in the quercetin-glycoside group. Cerebral blood flow measures suggested possible suppression of declines in cerebral blood volume, cerebral blood flow, and cerebral activity, but between-group differences for CBF were not significant.

Use in the wiki: narrow positive human quercetin-glycoside anchor. It supports low-confidence reaction-time / vascular-cognition interest, not broad memory or executive-function efficacy.
