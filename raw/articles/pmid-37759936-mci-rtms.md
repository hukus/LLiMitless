# A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis of Cognitive Effects of rTMS in Caucasian Patients with Mild Cognitive Impairment

- PMID: 37759936
- Published: 2023-09-17
- Source: PubMed

## Summary

Systematic review/meta-analysis of rTMS for cognition in MCI.

## Notes

- The review frames high-frequency DLPFC-targeted rTMS as the common research protocol.
- Global cognition improved, but subgroup and individual-domain findings were not clearly separated.
- Useful mainly as a phenotype-specific anchor for the MCI branch rather than a protocol optimizer.
