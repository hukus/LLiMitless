# Cerebrolysin treatment improved short-term memory deficits while simultaneously increasing hippocampal spine density in hypertensive female rats

- PMID: 39826813
- DOI: 10.1016/j.bbr.2025.115436
- Journal: Behavioural Brain Research, 2025
- Source type: preclinical cognition study; spontaneously hypertensive female rats
- Ingested: 2026-05-04

## Summary

2025 preclinical study in spontaneously hypertensive rats and normotensive controls. The abstract reports that Cerebrolysin improved recognition memory in hypertensive female rats in the novel object recognition test, and increased dendritic arborization or spine density in hippocampal and cortical regions depending on brain area and strain context. The authors frame the result around hypertension-related cognitive vulnerability, blood-brain-barrier/cerebrovascular risk, and neurotrophic repair.

## Signal Use

- Medicinal / formal signal: preclinical support for vascular-risk memory rescue and synaptic-structure remodeling.
- Translation limit: animal model only; does not establish human vascular-dementia, stroke, or healthy-adult benefit.
- Anecdotal signal: none.
- Wiki implication: strengthens mechanistic plausibility for vascular-risk responder phenotypes, while staying below human clinical evidence.
