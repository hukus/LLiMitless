# Effects of Growth Hormone-Releasing Hormone on Cognitive Function in Adults With Mild Cognitive Impairment and Healthy Older Adults: Results of a Controlled Trial

- Arch Neurol 2012; PMID 22869065.
- Randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial in 152 adults aged 55-87; 66 had MCI.
- Tesamorelin 1 mg/day was given by bedtime subcutaneous injection for 20 weeks, followed by washout.
- Intent-to-treat cognition was favorable (P=.03) and completer analysis was stronger (P=.002).
- Executive function improved; verbal memory trended favorable.
- IGF-1 rose 117% and body fat fell 7.4%, but adverse events were common and generally mild.
