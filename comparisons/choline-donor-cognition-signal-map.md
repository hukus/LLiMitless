# Choline Donor Cognition Signal Map

This page normalizes the choline-donor branch so alpha-GPC / choline alphoscerate, choline alfoscerate, [[citicoline]], [[lysolecithin]], [[phosphatidylcholine]], and [[choline-bitartrate]] / generic "choline" supplement claims are not treated as interchangeable. The current wiki evidence supports impairment, recovery, product-matrix, or narrow task phenotypes more than healthy-adult broad enhancement.

For choline's methyl-donor / betaine branch, route through [[one-carbon-methyl-donor-cognition-watchlist]] and [[betaine-tmg]] rather than treating acetylcholine, phospholipid, TMAO, and homocysteine-remethylation claims as one choline effect.

## Medicinal Signal

| Candidate or formulation | Main formal signal | Signal strength | Best route | Main caveat |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [[alpha-gpc]] / choline alphoscerate | 100-person amnestic-MCI RCT; 12 weeks of 600 mg improved ADAS-cog versus placebo with no serious safety imbalance in the raw note | `low-moderate` | Impairment / MCI recovery support | This is not healthy-adult enhancement evidence |
| Choline alfoscerate | Small prospective mTBI recovery study; 400 mg twice daily for 8 weeks associated with greater K-MMSE improvement | `low` | Recovery phenotype | Case-control design and K-MMSE outcome limit causal strength |
| [[lysolecithin]] / LPC choline source | Healthy adults aged 40-74; 480 mg for 8 weeks increased plasma choline/LPC18:2 but did not improve memory versus placebo | `negative` for broad healthy-adult cognition | Biomarker-only comparator | Biomarker movement did not translate into the primary cognitive endpoint |
| [[citicoline]] / CDP-choline | 100-person older-adult AAMI RCT with secondary memory improvements plus 2023 MCI review support | `low-moderate` | Older-adult memory / vascular-MCI support | Branded-product and review-level context; not young healthy-adult proof |
| [[phosphatidylcholine]] / lecithin / egg-yolk phospholipid choline | Old healthy-adult PC/lecithin trials are mixed, dementia review is negative, and egg-yolk phospholipid choline has a small verbal-memory signal | `low/contested` | Product-form / older-adult or slow-learner memory hypothesis | Do not collapse lecithin, purified PC capsules, egg-yolk phospholipid choline, and [[lysolecithin]] |
| [[choline-bitartrate]] / free choline salts | Acute 2 g choline bitartrate improved visuomotor precision, but three acute memory experiments and load-carriage cognition were null | `low/narrow`; `negative` for memory | Narrow action precision / cheap choline comparator | Free choline salts raise TMAO / platelet reactivity in a form-comparison trial; not interchangeable with PC or eggs |
| [[uridine]] / UMP + DHA + choline stacks | Preclinical UMP+DHA+choline mechanism, Fortasyn/Souvenaid disease-context multinutrient trials, and an acute healthy-young alpha-GPC+UMP+DHA cognition-null trial | `low/stack-formulation-gated`; `negative` for acute healthy stack | Choline-cofactor / phospholipid-formula boundary | This is not an alpha-GPC, citicoline, or generic choline proof; attribution belongs at formula or stack level |

The current signal is therefore asymmetric: [[alpha-gpc]] deserves tracking in amnestic MCI and mild-TBI recovery, [[citicoline]] deserves its own older-adult / vascular-MCI lane, [[lysolecithin]] is a caution that better choline biomarkers can still miss memory endpoints, [[phosphatidylcholine]] has old mixed-to-negative evidence plus a product-matrix egg-yolk signal, and [[choline-bitartrate]] is narrow visuomotor-positive but memory-null and safety-gated. ## Interpretation Gates

- Do not collapse [[alpha-gpc]], choline alfoscerate, [[citicoline]], [[phosphatidylcholine]], [[choline-bitartrate]], and [[lysolecithin]] into one effect estimate.
- Do not borrow [[betaine-tmg]] or [[s-adenosyl-l-methionine]] methyl-donor anecdotes for choline donor ranking unless the choline form, methylation marker, and cognitive endpoint are isolated.
- Do not treat [[uridine]] / UMP + DHA + choline stack evidence as proof for [[alpha-gpc]], [[citicoline]], or standalone UMP; keep formula, dose, and duration attached.
- Separate impairment / recovery settings from rested healthy adults.
- Require formulation, dose, duration, baseline diet, and cognitive endpoint before upgrading any generic choline claim.
- Treat stack anecdotes as attribution warnings unless the choline donor is isolated by dechallenge/rechallenge or blinded comparison.

## Current Read

Choline donors belong inside [[cholinergic-state-restoration-and-cognition]] as phenotype-bound support candidates. They are weaker than [[nicotine]] for acute attention and weaker than [[methylphenidate]] or [[modafinil]] as general comparators, but they matter because the MCI, older-adult memory, mTBI, product-matrix, and narrow visuomotor signals are specific enough to keep the branch alive. Route methyl-donor claims through [[one-carbon-methyl-donor-cognition-watchlist]]; route acetylcholine / phospholipid claims through [[cholinergic-and-nicotinic-agents-2026-scan]], [[pharmacology-comparator-map]], and [[cognitive-improvement-molecule-signal-atlas]].
