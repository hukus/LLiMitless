# Pharmacology Comparator Map

This page fills the baseline comparator layer that the wiki has been missing. The point is not to call these drugs "good nootropics"; it is to define the reference set that novel interventions should be compared against before anyone claims a breakthrough. For the intervention-level endpoint split that keeps speed, precision, transfer, and delayed retention separate, see [[cognitive-fidelity-split]]. For label, schedule, and sports-visibility context on the same stack, see [[regulatory-and-sports-visibility-map]].

The practical reading rule is simple: compare novel wakefulness or attention interventions against caffeine first, then the prescription wake-promoters, then disorder-context agents such as nicotine withdrawal rescue or narcolepsy drugs. If a novel method cannot beat that stack on either effect size or tolerability, the claim should stay small. But comparator choice is not neutral: caffeine withdrawal, nicotine abstinence, stimulant washout, sleep debt, meal timing, and practice effects can all move the baseline before the real comparison starts. ## Comparator stack

|---|---|---|---|---|
| Caffeine | Healthy adults, sleep restriction, long duty cycles | Reliable acute gains in alertness, vigilance, attention, and reaction time; memory and executive effects are less consistent | Sleep disruption, anxiety, tolerance, and dependence on dose/timing; withdrawal and schedule shifts can masquerade as benefit | First-line baseline comparator for any state-optimization claim; lock timing before comparing blocks |
| Modafinil / armodafinil | Healthy non-sleep-deprived adults and sleepiness disorders | Small overall enhancement signal in healthy adults, with the clearest effect on memory updating; armodafinil is usually treated as the same wakefulness class for benchmarking.^[raw/articles/dailymed-modafinil-tablet-label.md]^[raw/articles/dailymed-armodafinil-tablet-label.md] | Headache, insomnia, appetite loss, psychiatric activation, prescription constraints; modafinil is also a WADA S6.A stimulant | The closest serious prescription benchmark for "clean" wake-promoting comparison |
| [[adrafinil]], [[ce-123]], flmodafinil, fladrafinil, hydrafinil, and other unapproved afinil-style analogs | Gray-market prodrug, preclinical DAT/motivation leads, analytical-chemistry identity sources, supplement-screening sources, and community anecdotes | No captured human cognition-efficacy packet for the analog class; [[ce-123]] is preclinical-positive but human-absent | Product identity, hidden-API/adulteration risk, unknown dose conversion, sleep-state confounding, sourcing, and unapproved pharmacologic status | Risk-first analog watch; use [[modafinil]] and methylphenidate as benchmarks before upgrading any analog claim |
| [[methylphenidate]] | Healthy adults and ADHD/EDS-style attention problems | Small but somewhat larger healthy-adult signal than modafinil in the 2020 meta-analysis, with a 2025 crossover trial in 37 healthy adults showing frontoparietal-state shifts and faster low-demand visuospatial responding after 60 mg | Abuse liability, BP/HR effects, insomnia, appetite suppression | The comparator to beat if a novel intervention claims attention and control benefits |

## What changed since 2024

- The old ranking pages already implied caffeine, modafinil-class drugs, and methylphenidate as the obvious comparators, but they did not make the baseline explicit. The 2025 methylphenidate trial now gives the class a state-dynamics readout, not just a behavioral meta-analysis.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40101961-methylphenidate-frontoparietal-state-cognition.md]
- Caffeine's comparator value was sharpened by repeated-dose sleep-loss studies: acute rescue is still real, but chronic/repeated exposure also changes the state being measured and can distort the next-day comparison if timing is sloppy.^[raw/articles/pubmed-35791877-repeated-low-dose-caffeine-sleep-deprivation-cognition.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-38830861-repeated-caffeine-intake-sleep-restriction-gm.md]
- Solriamfetol now has a direct cognition trial in OSA with EDS, which makes it a better disorder-context comparator than a pure wakefulness drug and a better benchmark for fatigue-rescue claims.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39528111-solriamfetol-sharp-osa-cognition.md]
- Tesofensine now has its own risk-first comparator branch because obesity efficacy, DAT occupancy, and executive-function anecdotes are all visible, but none of them establish healthy-adult cognition. The long half-life and appetite/weight-loss confounds make the class easy to overread.^[raw/articles/pubmed-18950853-tesofensine-obesity-phase2.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-24239329-tesofensine-dat-occupancy-pet.md]^[raw/articles/reddit-2025-2026-tesofensine-focus-energy-threads.md]
- MAO-B/propargylamine drugs now have a dedicated branch. [[rasagiline]] is the only marketed MAO-B inhibitor with a significant PD global-cognition signal in the 2026 network meta-analysis, but the class still lacks healthy-adult cognition proof, and [[sembragiline]] shows why target engagement does not guarantee AD cognition efficacy.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41524120-maob-inhibitors-pd-cognition-network-meta.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-27633250-sembragiline-maob-pet-target-engagement.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-28550255-sembragiline-mayflower-road-ad-phase2.md]
- Pemoline-adjacent gray-market stimulants now have their own risk-first branch. [[cyclazodone]] and [[n-methyl-cyclazodone]] have enough forum focus salience to track, but [[pemoline]]'s liver-toxicity history, NMC's lab-confirmed toxicity case, and the multi-agent DILI/liver-transplant case keep the class below prescription comparator drugs.^[raw/articles/fda-2002-cylert-pemoline-label-liver-warning.md]^[raw/articles/clinical-toxicology-2022-n-methyl-cyclazodone-toxicity.md]^[raw/articles/acg-2022-n-methylcyclazodone-online-hormones-dili.md]^[raw/articles/reddit-2020-2026-cyclazodone-nmc-threads.md]
- Unapproved modafinil analogs now have their own routing layer so flmodafinil / CRL-40,940, fladrafinil / CRL-40,941, hydrafinil / fluorenol, N-methyl-difluoromodafinil, and [[ce-123]] do not inherit prescription [[modafinil]] evidence by analogy. The current signal is identity/regulatory/anecdote first, with CE-123 as a preclinical-only exception rather than a human benchmark.
- Nonstimulant norepinephrine-heavy attention agents now have their own comparator row. [[bupropion]] and [[atomoxetine]] have narrow healthy-adult attention or response-inhibition anchors plus mixed focus/fog anecdotes; [[viloxazine]] is currently ADHD-clinical rather than healthy-cognition-positive; [[reboxetine]] is a replication-worthy NRI probe with one small healthy-human attention/working-memory signal.^[raw/articles/pubmed-18489015-bupropion-attention-healthy-adults.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-16469930-atomoxetine-response-inhibition-healthy-volunteers.md]^[raw/articles/pmc-9328182-viloxazine-er-adult-adhd-phase3.md]^[raw/articles/oxford-2021-reboxetine-motor-learning-attention-working-memory.md]
- Pipeline nonstimulant ADHD-attention chemicals now have a separate row. [[centanafadine]] is a live 2026 FDA priority-review watch item, [[dasotraline]] is a discontinued DNRI with symptom-scale benefit but cognitive-battery null, and [[metadoxine]] is a failed-pivotal ADHD / alcohol-aftereffect hypothesis rather than a general cognition lead.
- SNDRI / triple-reuptake antidepressant and analgesic comparators now have a separate row so [[ansofaxine-toludesvenlafaxine]], [[liafensine]], [[amitifadine]], [[gsk372475]], [[csti-500]], and [[nefopam]] do not get folded into either the [[tesofensine]] anorectic branch or the ADHD pipeline branch. The class read is mood-state or safety comparator first, healthy enhancement second at best.
- Classic sympathomimetic anorectic stimulants now have a separate branch. [[phentermine]] has enough sleep-loss and anecdotal focus signal to track, while [[diethylpropion]], [[phendimetrazine]], [[benzphetamine]], and [[fenfluramine]] now have standalone risk-first pages so sibling labels and fenfluramine's VHD/PAH precedent do not hide inside a generic diet-stimulant row.^[raw/articles/dailymed-2024-phentermine-label.md]^[raw/articles/dailymed-2025-qsymia-cognitive-impairment-label.md]^[raw/articles/dailymed-2026-sympathomimetic-anorectic-labels.md]^[raw/articles/dailymed-2026-fenfluramine-fintepla-rems.md]^[raw/articles/reddit-2021-2026-phentermine-focus-brainfog-threads.md]
- [[lisdexamfetamine]] now closes the prodrug-amphetamine gap: it has direct acute healthy-volunteer task evidence plus ADHD / BED prescription status, but its Schedule II label and high-dose acute study design keep it as a comparator rather than a general enhancement lead.^[raw/articles/pubmed-29511807-lisdexamfetamine-damphetamine-healthy-cognition.md]^[raw/articles/dailymed-2026-vyvanse-lisdexamfetamine-label.md]
- Nicotine now has a more explicit division between abstinence-state restoration and direct enhancement claims, and the newer nicotinic/cholinergic evidence is strongest when phenotype enrichment is built in. The 2026 nicotine-tablet study has now passed its estimated completion date without posted results, which keeps the fresh comparator live but unresolved.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct07408180-nicotine-tablet-attention-2026-04-30.md]
- Tropisetron is now separated from generic nicotinic lore: its formal signal is schizophrenia P50 / attention and one-day RBANS movement, while the nootropic-community signal is mostly alpha7 mechanism extrapolation rather than direct healthy-adult self-testing.
- Mevidalen stayed alive as a D1 PAM comparator despite the LBD cognition miss, and a new AD phase 2 remains active; ulotaront also stayed alive in development despite the DIAMOND 1/2 misses, with the 2025 dose-response meta-analysis only adding a small symptom signal rather than a cognition signal.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct06538116-mevidalen-ad.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct06894212-ulotaront-phase3-schizophrenia.md]^[raw/articles/pmc-12421877-ulotaront-dose-response-meta-analysis.md]
- Cobenfy/KarXT turned the muscarinic branch into a real approved schizophrenia drug, while emraclidine keeps the M4 side of the pipeline alive; neither of those modern programs gives this page a clean cognition benchmark.
- Muscarinic-selective PAMs now have a healthy-volunteer CNS target-engagement readout, and the 2026 xanomeline-trospium review adds hypothesis support, but both remain far from a new benchmark efficacy claim.
- Galantamine now has a post-SAH safety pilot in addition to the older enrichment-bound cognitive work, which pushes it further into the recovery bucket than the healthy-enhancement bucket.
- Donepezil and rivastigmine now have explicit comparator routing: donepezil's healthy-adjacent positives are too inconsistent to outrank caffeine/modafinil-style benchmarks, and rivastigmine remains a clinical dementia/HIV-pilot comparator rather than a healthy-adult enhancer.
- Choline-source evidence now splits more cleanly: Alpha-GPC remains phenotype-bound, [[lysolecithin]] was null in healthy adults, [[phosphatidylcholine]] / lecithin is old mixed-to-negative with a separate egg-yolk product-matrix signal, and [[choline-bitartrate]] is narrow visuomotor-positive but memory-null and TMAO-gated. That keeps the choline-donor lane out of the generic nootropic tier.
- Pitolisant's 2026 label keeps it relevant because it is non-scheduled and still in active use, but the direct cognition story remains thin and the QT/insomnia/anxiety burden stays front and center.^[raw/articles/dailymed-wakix-pitolisant-tablet-label.md]^[raw/articles/medscape-pitolisant-mania-case-report.md]
- Orexin agonists have finally crossed from preclinical promise into human cognition analysis in NT1, but they are still too early to treat as a stable enhancement baseline.
- Histamine H3 inverse agonists sit in the same wakefulness bucket as the orexin agents, but the class has already accumulated enough negative cognition data that it should remain a state-restoration comparator, not an enhancer claim. Samelisant strengthens the wakefulness side of that ledger, not the healthy-adult cognition side.
- DORAs are the mirror-image comparator: the 2026 pairwise meta-analysis confirms insomnia benefit with somnolence risk, the newer healthy-volunteer suvorexant and daridorexant studies show null cognition and low-residual-impairment readouts rather than enhancement, and the vornorexant PK/RO paper adds human target-engagement data without changing the sleep-restoration framing; none of that justifies an enhancement claim.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41622624-vornorexant-orexin-receptor-occupancy-human-pk.md]
- Fampridine now belongs on the map as a niche potassium-channel repurposing candidate: a real healthy-volunteer WM RCT exists, the average effect was null, the 2025 upper-extremity RCT was also null, the 2026 fatigue review was inconsistent in randomized studies, and the 2026 TBI paper adds preclinical axon-rescue support without changing the healthy-adult enhancement reading. The safety ceiling is much tighter than for the standard wake-promoting stack.
- The NMDA/GlyT1/DAOI branch is now better treated as a state-restoration comparator with one phenotype-sensitive outlier ([[sodium-benzoate]]) rather than a class-level enhancement lane: [[iclepertin]] was null in both phase 2 and phase 3, and [[luvadaxistat]] remains secondary-only.

## Synthesis

If the question is "what should a novel cognitive enhancer be compared against?", the ranking is:

1. Caffeine for cheap, acute state control.
2. Modafinil / armodafinil for the prescription wake-promoter benchmark.
3. Methylphenidate for attention and control claims.
4. Lisdexamfetamine when the claim is prodrug amphetamine, ADHD-state task initiation, or smoother long-acting stimulant response.
5. Solriamfetol, pitolisant, and the broader histamine H3 class for disorder-context wakefulness.
6. Bupropion, atomoxetine, viloxazine, and reboxetine as nonstimulant noradrenergic attention comparators when the target is response inhibition or ADHD-like executive control, not broad cognition.
7. Centanafadine, dasotraline, and metadoxine as pipeline / discontinued attention-agent comparators, not current healthy-adult enhancers.
8. SNDRI / triple-reuptake antidepressants as depression/TRD/anhedonia-state comparators, not healthy-adult enhancers.
9. Phentermine-style sympathomimetic anorectics as risk-first diet-pill stimulant comparators when appetite, BED, or ADHD-like focus reports are the claim.
10. Orexin agonists as the frontier class to watch, not the current baseline.
11. DORAs as the sleep-restoration comparator, not a daytime cognitive enhancer.
12. Fampridine as a responder-enrichment lead for working-memory deficits, not a broad enhancer.

MAO-B inhibitors are a separate disease-state comparator lane: useful for PD executive/off-state and AD/MCI translational hypotheses, but not a benchmark enhancer in healthy adults.

Ketamine/esketamine and dextromethorphan-bupropion are also separate from the standard attention stack: they are rapid-antidepressant / mood-state comparators where cognitive recovery, acute impairment, dissociation, and interaction risk must be kept distinct.

The main discipline this map enforces is comparability. A peptide, stimulation protocol, or light-based intervention should not be called a meaningful enhancer unless it is compared with these older, better-known state-shifters under a similar task and sleep context.

For schedule logic and anti-tolerance framing, see [[anti-tolerance-periodization-in-cognitive-enhancement]]. The current practical reading is that cycling is easiest to justify when it reduces insomnia, appetite suppression, or withdrawal confounding; it is much harder to justify as a universal way to produce larger long-run cognitive gains.
