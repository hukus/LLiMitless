# Pharmacology Comparator Map

This page fills the baseline comparator layer that the wiki has been missing. The point is not to call these drugs "good nootropics"; it is to define the reference set that novel interventions should be compared against before anyone claims a breakthrough. For the intervention-level endpoint split that keeps speed, precision, transfer, and delayed retention separate, see [[cognitive-fidelity-split]]. For label, schedule, and sports-visibility context on the same stack, see [[regulatory-and-sports-visibility-map]].

The practical reading rule is simple: compare novel wakefulness or attention interventions against caffeine first, then the prescription wake-promoters, then disorder-context agents such as nicotine withdrawal rescue or narcolepsy drugs. If a novel method cannot beat that stack on either effect size or tolerability, the claim should stay small. But comparator choice is not neutral: caffeine withdrawal, nicotine abstinence, stimulant washout, sleep debt, meal timing, and practice effects can all move the baseline before the real comparison starts. ## Comparator stack

| Comparator | Best evidence context | Human cognition signal | Main downside | Role in the wiki |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Caffeine | Healthy adults, sleep restriction, long duty cycles | Reliable acute gains in alertness, vigilance, attention, and reaction time; memory and executive effects are less consistent | Sleep disruption, anxiety, tolerance, and dependence on dose/timing; withdrawal and schedule shifts can masquerade as benefit | First-line baseline comparator for any state-optimization claim; lock timing before comparing blocks |
| Modafinil / armodafinil | Healthy non-sleep-deprived adults and sleepiness disorders | Small overall enhancement signal in healthy adults, with the clearest effect on memory updating; armodafinil is usually treated as the same wakefulness class for benchmarking.^[raw/articles/dailymed-modafinil-tablet-label.md]^[raw/articles/dailymed-armodafinil-tablet-label.md] | Headache, insomnia, appetite loss, psychiatric activation, prescription constraints; modafinil is also a WADA S6.A stimulant | The closest serious prescription benchmark for "clean" wake-promoting comparison |
| Methylphenidate | Healthy adults and ADHD/EDS-style attention problems | Small but somewhat larger healthy-adult signal than modafinil in the 2020 meta-analysis, with a 2025 crossover trial in 37 healthy adults showing frontoparietal-state shifts and faster low-demand visuospatial responding after 60 mg | Abuse liability, BP/HR effects, insomnia, appetite suppression | The comparator to beat if a novel intervention claims attention and control benefits |
| D-amphetamine | Healthy adults, fatigue, and high-demand tasks | No overall healthy-adult effect in the 2020 meta-analysis, despite task-specific positive findings elsewhere | Higher abuse risk, cardiovascular burden, mood activation, sleep disruption | Useful as a cautionary comparator: strong reputation, weaker average healthy-adult evidence |
| D1 PAM / TAAR1 agonists | Lewy body dementia, Parkinson disease dementia, schizophrenia, and active phase 2/3 programs | Mevidalen missed the primary and secondary cognition endpoints in LBD but improved motor and global-impression measures; ulotaront's phase 2 schizophrenia signal did not survive the DIAMOND 1 / 2 phase 3 primary endpoints, and a 2025 dose-response meta-analysis only found a small PANSS signal at 100 mg | Blood-pressure / cardiovascular burden for mevidalen; psychiatric activation and insomnia concerns for TAAR1-class agents; development uncertainty remains high | Dopamine-state comparator for salience, reward, and psychiatric-symptom claims, not a healthy-adult nootropic tier^[raw/articles/pubmed-34859493-mevidalen-lbd-phase-2.md]^[raw/articles/pmc-10465394-ulotaront-taar1-review.md]^[raw/articles/otsuka-us-diamond-ulotaront-phase3-failure.md]^[raw/articles/pmc-12421877-ulotaront-dose-response-meta-analysis.md] |
| Nicotine | Smokers after abstinence, nonsmokers in acute attention tasks, MCI pilot work | Often looks more like withdrawal relief and attention sharpening than broad enhancement; the 2024 abstinence crossover remains the cleanest direct effect and the 2026 nicotine-tablet study has passed its estimated completion date without posted results, so the live readout is still pending.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct07408180-nicotine-tablet-attention-2026-04-30.md] | Dependence, cardiovascular risk, nausea, sleep effects, public-health baggage | Comparator for state restoration, not a clean enhancement benchmark; abstinence state matters |
| Cholinergic / nicotinic agents | Nicotine abstinence, healthy older adults with cholinergic reserve, donepezil trained-skill or sleep-loss-vulnerability probes, amnestic MCI, mTBI, SAH recovery, schizophrenia, older AD xanomeline studies, and choline-source form comparisons | Nicotine has the clearest acute attention effect; varenicline has a small healthy-nonsmoker memory signal; galantamine is enrichment- and recovery-bound; [[donepezil]] is split between narrow healthy-adjacent positives and direct null/negative counterweights; [[rivastigmine]] is disease-context only; choline donors are phenotype-sensitive, with [[phosphatidylcholine]] / lecithin old mixed-to-negative, egg-yolk choline product-matrix positive, [[lysolecithin]] healthy-memory-null, and [[choline-bitartrate]] visuomotor-positive but memory-null | Dependence, BP/HR effects, nausea, vivid dreams, sleep disruption, bradycardia, cholinergic GI burden, urinary retention, hypertension, free-choline TMAO/platelet caveats, prescription-label risks, rivastigmine patch medication errors, and rapid overgeneralization from small subgroup studies | Keep as a state-restoration / enrichment comparator, not a generic nootropic tier; use [[prescription-cholinesterase-inhibitor-cognition-watchlist]] for donepezil/rivastigmine/galantamine/huperzine routing |
| NMDA co-agonist / GlyT1 / DAAO branch | Schizophrenia CIAS, early AD / aMCI, biomarker and remediation studies | [[iclepertin]] was null in the adjunctive CCT phase 2 study and also negative in phase 3; [[sodium-benzoate]] has the best reproducible cognition signal, especially in early AD / aMCI and women; [[luvadaxistat]] is secondary-only; [[sarcosine]] and [[d-serine]] are weaker standalone comparators | Mostly phenotype-sensitive rather than universal; sodium load / product quality / regulatory status matter more than with standard prescription comparators | Comparator for glutamatergic state-restoration claims, not a broad nootropic benchmark |
| AMPAkines / AMPAR PAMs | Sleep-loss rescue, ADHD attention/symptom state, depression, respiratory drive, juvenile ischemia rescue, and cognition programs | [[cx717]] now has a low-moderate adult-ADHD phase 2A signal at 800 mg BID, but the primary paired t-test was trend-only and older sleep-loss / shift-work studies stayed mixed to negative; [[cx516]] failed schizophrenia and fragile X cognition; [[tak-653]] and [[cx1739]] provide CNS PD and safety/PK rather than cognition wins; [[cx691-farampator]] adds preclinical schizophrenia-adjacent rescue logic; a 2026 juvenile ischemia paper adds a narrow injury-state rescue phenotype | Narrow therapeutic window, insomnia/headache/nausea, sleep-architecture tradeoffs, excitatory-ceiling toxicity at high impact, and a development pivot toward phenotype-bound rescue indications | Comparator for glutamatergic plasticity / rescue claims, not a broad enhancer benchmark^[raw/articles/sciencedirect-2025-cx717-adhd-phase2a.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39304968-high-impact-ampakines-toxic-calcium-release.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40245529-cx691-farampator-antipsychotic-like-low-impact-ampakine.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41612671-type-ii-ampakines-juvenile-global-cerebral-ischemia.md] |
| Roflumilast / PDE4 family | Healthy adults, schizophrenia experimental medicine, post-stroke cognitive impairment, Fragile X syndrome | Repeated low-dose human memory and language signals, plus a modest post-stroke PSCI positive; still phenotype-bound rather than broad enhancement | Low-dose tolerability ceiling, headache/dizziness/insomnia/diarrhea, and sex-skewed FAERS safety heterogeneity | PDE4 comparator for memory/plasticity and recovery-phenotype claims^[raw/articles/pubmed-29241652-roflumilast-healthy-young-adults.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-30776650-roflumilast-healthy-old-individuals.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-30536081-roflumilast-schizophrenia-memory.md]^[raw/articles/sagepub-02698811261436581-roflumilast-post-stroke-cognitive-performance.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41988533-pde4-faers-sex-specific-ae.md] |
| Solriamfetol | Narcolepsy / OSA with excessive daytime sleepiness | Strong wakefulness signal and direct OSA cognition benefit in SHARP, but the evidence is disorder-context rather than healthy enhancement.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39528111-solriamfetol-sharp-osa-cognition.md] | BP/HR effects, insomnia, anxiety, appetite loss, schedule IV; WADA S6.B specified stimulant | Useful as a modern wake-promoting benchmark when the target is EDS rather than general cognition |
| Pitolisant | Narcolepsy with EDS/cataplexy | Strong wakefulness role with indirect cognition relevance; direct cognition evidence is still thin even after the 2026 label update | QT prolongation, insomnia, nausea, anxiety, hepatic and drug-interaction issues; no WADA prohibited-list match found in this pass | A lower-abuse comparator for wakefulness, especially when stimulant risk is a concern |
| Histamine H3 inverse agonists / antagonists | Narcolepsy, OSA, and exploratory psychiatry | Wakefulness improvement is real in hypersomnolence disorders, and samelisant's phase-2 narcolepsy result reinforces that signal; general cognition benefit is still weak or negative outside that context | Insomnia, nausea, anxiety, QT concerns, and rare activation/mood issues | Class comparator for state-restoration claims, not a validated healthy-adult nootropic |
| Orexin receptor 2 agonists | Narcolepsy type 1 and other arousal-deficit states | Emerging cognition signal in NT1 secondary analysis, with visible attention, memory, and executive-function gains after oveporexton in a 2026 human secondary analysis, but still disease-specific | Development-stage uncertainty, safety profile still evolving, not a general-use benchmark | Experimental class; watch it, but do not overstate it as a mature comparator |
| Fampridine / 4-aminopyridine | Healthy adults, MS cognition pilots, MS symptom studies, and preclinical TBI rescue | No main effect in the 2025 healthy-adult crossover, but low-baseline responders, small MS cognition gains, a null upper-extremity RCT, mixed fatigue evidence, and 2026 preclinical axon-rescue data keep it interesting | Seizures, renal dependence, OCT2 interactions, dose sensitivity | Experimental potassium-channel repurposing lead, not a benchmark enhancer |

## What changed since 2024

- The old ranking pages already implied caffeine, modafinil-class drugs, and methylphenidate as the obvious comparators, but they did not make the baseline explicit. The 2025 methylphenidate trial now gives the class a state-dynamics readout, not just a behavioral meta-analysis.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40101961-methylphenidate-frontoparietal-state-cognition.md]
- Caffeine's comparator value was sharpened by repeated-dose sleep-loss studies: acute rescue is still real, but chronic/repeated exposure also changes the state being measured and can distort the next-day comparison if timing is sloppy.^[raw/articles/pubmed-35791877-repeated-low-dose-caffeine-sleep-deprivation-cognition.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-38830861-repeated-caffeine-intake-sleep-restriction-gm.md]
- Solriamfetol now has a direct cognition trial in OSA with EDS, which makes it a better disorder-context comparator than a pure wakefulness drug and a better benchmark for fatigue-rescue claims.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39528111-solriamfetol-sharp-osa-cognition.md]
- Nicotine now has a more explicit division between abstinence-state restoration and direct enhancement claims, and the newer nicotinic/cholinergic evidence is strongest when phenotype enrichment is built in. The 2026 nicotine-tablet study has now passed its estimated completion date without posted results, which keeps the fresh comparator live but unresolved.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct07408180-nicotine-tablet-attention-2026-04-30.md]
- Mevidalen stayed alive as a D1 PAM comparator despite the LBD cognition miss, and a new AD phase 2 remains active; ulotaront also stayed alive in development despite the DIAMOND 1/2 misses, with the 2025 dose-response meta-analysis only adding a small symptom signal rather than a cognition signal.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct06538116-mevidalen-ad.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct06894212-ulotaront-phase3-schizophrenia.md]^[raw/articles/pmc-12421877-ulotaront-dose-response-meta-analysis.md]
- Cobenfy/KarXT turned the muscarinic branch into a real approved schizophrenia drug, while emraclidine keeps the M4 side of the pipeline alive; neither of those modern programs gives this page a clean cognition benchmark.
- Muscarinic-selective PAMs now have a healthy-volunteer CNS target-engagement readout, and the 2026 xanomeline-trospium review adds hypothesis support, but both remain far from a new benchmark efficacy claim.
- Galantamine now has a post-SAH safety pilot in addition to the older enrichment-bound cognitive work, which pushes it further into the recovery bucket than the healthy-enhancement bucket.
- Donepezil and rivastigmine now have explicit comparator routing: donepezil's healthy-adjacent positives are too inconsistent to outrank caffeine/modafinil-style benchmarks, and rivastigmine remains a clinical dementia/HIV-pilot comparator rather than a healthy-adult enhancer.
- Choline-source evidence now splits more cleanly: Alpha-GPC remains phenotype-bound, [[lysolecithin]] was null in healthy adults, [[phosphatidylcholine]] / lecithin is old mixed-to-negative with a separate egg-yolk product-matrix signal, and [[choline-bitartrate]] is narrow visuomotor-positive but memory-null and TMAO-gated. That keeps the choline-donor lane out of the generic nootropic tier.
- Pitolisant's 2026 label keeps it relevant because it is non-scheduled and still in active use, but the direct cognition story remains thin and the QT/insomnia/anxiety burden stays front and center.^[raw/articles/dailymed-wakix-pitolisant-tablet-label.md]^[raw/articles/medscape-pitolisant-mania-case-report.md]
- Orexin agonists have finally crossed from preclinical promise into human cognition analysis in NT1, but they are still too early to treat as a stable enhancement baseline.
- Histamine H3 inverse agonists sit in the same wakefulness bucket as the orexin agents, but the class has already accumulated enough negative cognition data that it should remain a state-restoration comparator, not an enhancer claim. Samelisant strengthens the wakefulness side of that ledger, not the healthy-adult cognition side.
- DORAs are the mirror-image comparator: the 2026 pairwise meta-analysis confirms insomnia benefit with somnolence risk, the newer healthy-volunteer suvorexant and daridorexant studies show null cognition and low-residual-impairment readouts rather than enhancement, and the vornorexant PK/RO paper adds human target-engagement data without changing the sleep-restoration framing; none of that justifies an enhancement claim.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41622624-vornorexant-orexin-receptor-occupancy-human-pk.md]
- Fampridine now belongs on the map as a niche potassium-channel repurposing candidate: a real healthy-volunteer WM RCT exists, the average effect was null, the 2025 upper-extremity RCT was also null, the 2026 fatigue review was inconsistent in randomized studies, and the 2026 TBI paper adds preclinical axon-rescue support without changing the healthy-adult enhancement reading. The safety ceiling is much tighter than for the standard wake-promoting stack.
- The NMDA/GlyT1/DAOI branch is now better treated as a state-restoration comparator with one phenotype-sensitive outlier ([[sodium-benzoate]]) rather than a class-level enhancement lane: [[iclepertin]] was null in both phase 2 and phase 3, and [[luvadaxistat]] remains secondary-only.
- The AMPAkine branch is now better treated as a phenotype-bound rescue comparator: [[cx717]] has a low-moderate adult-ADHD signal but not a clean healthy-cognition win, [[cx516]] remains a failed cognition comparator, [[tak-653]] and [[cx1739]] mainly add CNS PD and safety data, and [[cx691-farampator]] adds a preclinical schizophrenia-adjacent rescue lane rather than a human efficacy bridge.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40650226-ampa-kainate-modulators-cns-review.md]^[raw/articles/sciencedirect-2025-cx717-adhd-phase2a.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-36153330-tak653-tms-cns-effects.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39302241-cx1739-safety-pharmacokinetics.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40245529-cx691-farampator-antipsychotic-like-low-impact-ampakine.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39892449-cx717-safety-pharmacokinetics.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-21940760-cx717-sleep-deprivation-human.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-17955941-cx717-night-shift-work.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-17487227-cx516-schizophrenia-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-17069542-cx516-fragile-x-controlled-trial.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-16104830-cx717-nonhuman-primate-sleep-deprivation.md]
- The 5-HT6 branch now belongs in the failure ledger: SB-742457, idalopirdine, masupirdine, and intepirdine collectively show that a clean serotonergic mechanism can still miss primary cognition endpoints across AD and DLB.

For the fampridine deep dive, see [[fampridine-working-memory-genome-guided-repurposing-2026-scan]] and [[fampridine]].

## Synthesis

If the question is "what should a novel cognitive enhancer be compared against?", the ranking is:

1. Caffeine for cheap, acute state control.
2. Modafinil / armodafinil for the prescription wake-promoter benchmark.
3. Methylphenidate for attention and control claims.
4. Solriamfetol, pitolisant, and the broader histamine H3 class for disorder-context wakefulness.
5. Orexin agonists as the frontier class to watch, not the current baseline.
6. DORAs as the sleep-restoration comparator, not a daytime cognitive enhancer.
7. Fampridine as a responder-enrichment lead for working-memory deficits, not a broad enhancer.

The main discipline this map enforces is comparability. A peptide, stimulation protocol, or light-based intervention should not be called a meaningful enhancer unless it is compared with these older, better-known state-shifters under a similar task and sleep context.

For schedule logic and anti-tolerance framing, see [[anti-tolerance-periodization-in-cognitive-enhancement]]. The current practical reading is that cycling is easiest to justify when it reduces insomnia, appetite suppression, or withdrawal confounding; it is much harder to justify as a universal way to produce larger long-run cognitive gains.

For a dedicated confound audit on caffeine timing, active comparators, and repeated testing, see [[active-comparator-and-caffeine-scheduling-confound-audit]].
