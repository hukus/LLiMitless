# Unapproved Modafinil Analog Eugeroic Watchlist

This watchlist catches the gray-market wakefulness chemicals that orbit [[modafinil]] and [[adrafinil]]: flmodafinil / CRL-40,940, fladrafinil / CRL-40,941, N-methyl-4,4-difluoromodafinil / Modafiendz-like labels, hydrafinil / fluorenol, and [[ce-123]]. The purpose is not to rank them as nootropics. The purpose is to stop community wakefulness anecdotes, supplement adulteration, and animal DAT/cognition data from being collapsed into prescription-modafinil evidence.

## Interpretation Gates

- Do not borrow [[modafinil]]'s prescription evidence for unapproved analogs unless the analog has its own human cognition, PK, safety, and product-quality packet.
- FDA/ORA analytical work places these chemicals in a dietary-supplement hidden-API / adulteration context before it says anything about efficacy.
- Animal DAT or motivation findings are useful for target plausibility, but they do not settle sleep loss, anxiety, abuse liability, skin reactions, psychiatric activation, or cardiovascular tolerability in humans.
- Community comparisons are especially fragile because users change dose, route, sleep debt, caffeine/stimulant co-use, source, and prior modafinil/armodafinil tolerance at the same time.
- For athlete or regulated-performance contexts, WADA 2026 now directly names adrafinil, fladrafinil, flmodafinil, hydrafinil / fluorenol, and modafinil in S6.A; other unapproved pharmacologic analogs remain status-risk chemicals through S0/S6 logic even when a direct line item is not captured.^[raw/articles/wada-2026-stimulant-list-afinils-pemoline-tesofensine.md]

## Practical Read

This class is a risk-first comparator branch. The wiki should keep prescription [[modafinil]] / armodafinil evidence, gray-market [[adrafinil]], CE-123 preclinical data, and unapproved analog anecdotes separate. [[ce-123]] is the only analog in this pass with enough captured preclinical cognition material for a standalone entity; flmodafinil, fladrafinil, N-methyl-difluoromodafinil, hydrafinil, and didesoxymodafinil should stay in this watchlist until direct human evidence or a major safety/regulatory signal justifies promotion.
