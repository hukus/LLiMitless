# Plant Active Substance Product Watchlist

This watchlist now functions as the product-normalization gate for adjacent plant-active substances surfaced by the 2026 healthy-older-adult network meta-analysis. It sits below [[standardized-herbal-extracts-for-cognition]] because many ranked interventions are not interchangeable even after they clear a standalone threshold. [[pomegranate]], [[blueberry-anthocyanins]], [[sage-salvia-extract]], [[guarana]], [[wild-green-oat-extract]], [[tart-cherry]], and [[grape-food-products]] have now graduated from this product-watch layer to low-confidence entities, but they remain model cases for why juice, powder, extract, marker content, species identity, dose, caffeine content, sugar load, and endpoint domain cannot be merged casually. For broader flavonoid, anthocyanin, Nrf2, and longevity-polyphenol routing, use [[polyphenol-nrf2-cognition-watchlist]].

The 2026 NMA is useful as a discovery source, not as a final ranking. It reports probabilistic domain rankings across 25 RCTs and 1,861 healthy older adults, but also notes sparse evidence in some domains and unresolved safety, dose, and interaction questions.^[raw/articles/pmc-12864429-plant-active-substances-healthy-older-adults-nma.md]

## Page Gate

- Keep [[tart-cherry]] low-confidence and product-specific: do not merge Montmorency juice, concentrate, powder capsules, gummies, sweet cherries, or anthocyanin extracts.
- Keep [[guarana]] low-confidence and caffeine-confounded: do not merge seed powder, extract, energy drinks, multivitamin products, or pure caffeine controls.
- Keep [[grape-food-products]] as the food-matrix entity and [[grape-extract]] as the standardized-extract entity until direct product transportability is clear.
- Keep [[blueberry-anthocyanins]] product-specific: do not merge whole blueberries, wild-blueberry powder, juice, bilberry-style extracts, [[aronia-melanocarpa-extract]], and grape-blueberry blends.
- Keep [[sage-salvia-extract]] product-specific: do not merge common sage, Spanish sage, essential oil, Cognivia, Sibelius Sage, culinary sage, or Salvia divinorum.
- Keep [[wild-green-oat-extract]] product-specific: do not merge Neuravena, Cognitaven, generic oat straw, milky oat tincture, oat seed extract, or ordinary oats.
- Keep [[bacopa]], [[curcumin]], [[saffron]], [[ashwagandha]], and [[grape-extract]] as the current entity-addressable botanical set.

## Current Read

The current plant-active product gaps from this NMA pass are now represented as low-confidence entities or stricter standardized-extract pages. [[pomegranate]], [[blueberry-anthocyanins]], [[sage-salvia-extract]], [[guarana]], [[wild-green-oat-extract]], [[tart-cherry]], and [[grape-food-products]] have standalone pages because they have product-specific human cognition anchors, but they should still be read as product-normalization cases rather than broad botanical recommendations. These plant-active products should influence future raw-capture priorities, not ranked recommendations, until product identity and replication are cleaner.
