# Triple Reuptake Inhibitor Cognition Watchlist

This page covers SNDRI / triple-reuptake chemicals that are relevant to brain fog or cognition discourse through serotonin, norepinephrine, and dopamine reuptake inhibition, but are not primarily the tesofensine-style anorectic branch. For that anorectic/appetite/stimulant lane, use [[monoamine-reuptake-anorectic-stimulants-cognition-watchlist]].

The key discipline: depression-scale concentration, anhedonia, and fatigue improvements can be real and still not prove cognitive enhancement in healthy adults.

## Evidence Split

[[ansofaxine-toludesvenlafaxine]] is the clinically most mature member. The phase 3 trial found both 80 mg and 160 mg superior to placebo on MADRS change, with anhedonia and function signals, and Luye states the drug was approved in China for MDD.^[raw/articles/nature-2023-ansofaxine-phase3-mdd.md]^[raw/articles/luye-2022-ruoxinlin-approval-china.md]

[[liafensine]] is the sharpest live precision-psychiatry signal: ENLIGHTEN found superiority in ANK3-positive TRD, but the same source preserves the crucial counterweight that prior non-biomarker-selected phase 2b trials failed.^[raw/articles/jama-2025-liafensine-enlighten-ank3-trd.md]

[[amitifadine]] and [[gsk372475]] are the class guardrails. Amitifadine had a small early positive MDD signal and then failed TRIADE, while GSK372475 missed in two MDD trials and added cardiovascular/tolerability burden.^[raw/articles/sciencedirect-2012-amitifadine-mdd-rct.md]^[raw/articles/biospace-2013-amitifadine-triade-topline.md]^[raw/articles/sage-2012-gsk372475-mdd-negative-trials.md]

[[csti-500]] is not an antidepressant efficacy result yet, but it is useful because the healthy-volunteer PET study directly measured SERT and DAT occupancy and showed a 44-50 hour half-life. That is target engagement, not cognition.^[raw/articles/pmc-12979414-csti500-first-in-human-tri.md]

[[nefopam]] keeps the class safety-honest. Its analgesic and abuse sources place it near SNDRI pharmacology, but confusion, hallucinations, convulsions, delirium, anticholinergic effects, cardiovascular effects, and abuse reports make it a risk-first comparator.^[raw/articles/pubmed-1980-nefopam-review.md]^[raw/articles/pmc-4273008-nefopam-status-epilepticus.md]^[raw/articles/sage-2002-nefopam-abuse.md]

## Interpretation Rules

1. Do not equate "triple reuptake" with cleaner cognition than [[bupropion]], [[methylphenidate]], [[modafinil]], [[solriamfetol]], or [[tesofensine]].
2. Keep depression-scale cognitive symptoms separate from objective cognition tests.
3. For brain-fog claims, ask whether the effect is depression remission, anxiety relief, anhedonia relief, pain relief, stimulant masking, sleep disruption, appetite change, or medication-withdrawal reversal.
4. Safety gates include BP/HR, insomnia, anxiety/activation, nausea/GI effects, dry mouth, appetite/weight change, serotonergic stacking, dopaminergic/noradrenergic stacking, seizure threshold, anticholinergic burden, abuse liability, and washout.
5. Use [[glutamatergic-rapid-antidepressant-cognition-watchlist]] for ketamine/esketamine and dextromethorphan-bupropion rather than mixing rapid-antidepressant claims into this reuptake class.

Current verdict: track the class for mood-state cognitive symptoms and future biomarker/TRD readouts, but keep healthy-adult cognition confidence low.
