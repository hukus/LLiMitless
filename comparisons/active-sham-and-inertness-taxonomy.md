# Active-Sham and Inertness Taxonomy

`Sham` is an operational label. `Inert` is a biological label. A control can hide assignment, move physiology, do both, or do neither. This page names the control classes so future pages do not flatten every comparator into generic placebo language.^[raw/articles/pubmed-31228880-time-course-ineffective-sham-blinding-low-intensity-tdcs.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41008346-auricular-ultrasonic-vagus-nerve-stimulation-blinding-effectiveness.md]

## What Changed Since 2024

- tDCS sham can fail on timing alone, so a `sham` label does not guarantee a clean blind.^[raw/articles/pubmed-31228880-time-course-ineffective-sham-blinding-low-intensity-tdcs.md]
- tACS now has sham-controlled randomized evidence in older-adult and healthy-adult cognition clusters, which reinforces that the protocol and phenotype matter more than the modality label.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41034500-tacs-cognitive-function-older-adults.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40933163-theta-tacs-working-memory-healthy-adults.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40283048-40hz-tacs-dementia-cognitive-exercises.md]
- taVNS now has enough recent work to show that some sham devices are well-blinded, yet ear-level sensory stimulation can still move pupil dilation, HRV, and stress-related readouts.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41008346-auricular-ultrasonic-vagus-nerve-stimulation-blinding-effectiveness.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39815765-taVNS-memory-pupil-dilation.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40130364-taVNS-long-latency-sensory-processing.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40958978-taVNS-stress-regulation-eeg-questionnaire.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39124651-taVNS-autonomic-function.md]
- tPBM has repeated sham-controlled human studies, which makes it one of the cleaner home-sham candidates, but it is still a device-specific comparator rather than a universal inert placebo.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39423445-transcranial-photobiomodulation-cognition-bdnf.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40939031-tpbm-mci-ad-sham-controlled.md]
- temporal interference now has active/sham block designs and multiple human reviews, but the comparator remains field-geometry-dependent and research-grade.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct07339072-temporal-interference-methods-study-1-2.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41167554-human-applications-temporal-interference-stimulation.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40470330-temporal-interference-stimulation-for-human-brain-opportunities-and-challenges.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40925398-systematic-review-of-experimental-studies-in-humans-on-transcranial-temporal-interference-stimulation.md]
- tFUS has moved into sham-controlled target-engagement work, but that sham is calibration- and targeting-dependent rather than a casual placebo.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40275098-low-intensity-transcranial-focused-ultrasound-amygdala-neuromodulation.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41610842-transcranial-focused-ultrasound-in-the-human-brain.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41072763-itrusst-biophysical-safety-transcranial-ultrasound-stimulation.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-38410648-itrusst-standardised-reporting-transcranial-ultrasound-stimulation.md]
- HBOT shams are chamber-active because pressure and gas composition are physiological exposures, not empty placebos.^[raw/articles/pubmed-26403017-hbot-tbi-sham-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-37416626-hot-pocs-pilot-study-protocol.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-24052094-hbot-persistent-postconcussion-symptoms.md]
- Closed-loop sleep cueing can change sleep physiology even when memory does not move, so CLAS/TMR controls are best treated as active sleep controls rather than inert shams.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40102385-phase-specific-enhancement-motor-memory-consolidation-closed-loop-tmr.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41229102-closed-loop-auditory-stimulation-ecological-memory-null.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41497605-closed-loop-acoustic-stimulation-cathodal-tdcs-retention.md]
- Caffeine and nicotine comparator logic often turns into withdrawal-reversal logic, which means the control can be rescuing a deficit rather than testing baseline enhancement.^[raw/articles/pubmed-22992376-caffeine-attention-withdrawn-consumers.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-30861208-expectancy-caffeine-withdrawal-balanced-placebo-design.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-31686175-nicotine-galantamine-healthy-never-smokers-cognition.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-38582919-nicotine-cognitive-function-smokers-abstinence.md]
- Sleep restriction is a challenge paradigm, not a sham, and sleep-banking work makes that load-state framing even clearer.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41892386-sleep-banking-cognitive-motor-impairments-sleep-restriction.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-33524838-tdcs-sleep-deprivation-recovery.md]

## Core Axes

| Axis | Question | If yes | If no |
|---|---|---|---|
| Blinded? | Can the participant or assessor tell active from control? | `sham-capable` | open-label or obvious control |
| Biologically inert? | Does the control avoid moving the phenotype of interest? | `placebo` or inert comparator | `active` or state-active comparator |
| State-restoring? | Is the main effect relief of a deficit or withdrawal? | `rescue` or `withdrawal-reversal` | neutral comparator or enhancement |
| State-challenging? | Does the control intentionally impose a load or perturbation? | `state challenge` | neutral comparator |

The useful distinction is not binary. A control can be blinded and active, or unblinded and inert. The page labels below are the practical names to use in future syntheses.

## Control Classes

| Control class | Blinded? | Inert? | What it is doing | Example families | Best label |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Inert placebo | Usually yes | Yes | Tries to have no intended active exposure | Identical capsules, matched excipients, true placebo pills | `placebo` or `inert comparator` |
| Sensory-matched sham | Yes, but not always perfectly | Often no | Imitates the active condition's feel, sound, or appearance | Short-ramp tDCS/tACS, earlobe taVNS sham, visually matched tPBM sham, sham TMS coils | `sham` or `active sham` |
| Chamber-active sham | Yes | No | Uses an active environment as the comparator | Pressurized-air HBOT, low-oxygen placebo gas | `active sham` |
| Sleep-phase control | Usually yes | No | Alters cue timing or sleep physiology | CLAS, TMR, no-cue, random-cue, or off-phase cueing | `active sleep control` |
| Withdrawal-reversal comparator | Sometimes | No | Relieves abstinence, sleep pressure, or a manufactured deficit | Caffeine in withdrawn consumers, nicotine in abstinent smokers | `rescue comparator` |
| Active comparator | Sometimes | No | Another active intervention used as the reference | Low-dose drug comparator, alternate device setting, or another wake-promoting drug | `active comparator` |
| State challenge | No | No | Deliberately creates a load or deficit | Sleep restriction, postprandial load, exercise fatigue, stress challenge | `state challenge` |

| Temporal interference | Counterbalanced active and sham field blocks | Field geometry, target focality, and operator workflow matter | Control depends on electrical field modeling as much as blinding.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct07339072-temporal-interference-methods-study-1-2.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41167554-human-applications-temporal-interference-stimulation.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40470330-temporal-interference-stimulation-for-human-brain-opportunities-and-challenges.md] |

| HBOT | Pressurized air | Pressure and gas mixture are physiological exposures | `Sham` is chamber-active, not empty.^[raw/articles/pubmed-26403017-hbot-tbi-sham-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-37416626-hot-pocs-pilot-study-protocol.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-24052094-hbot-persistent-postconcussion-symptoms.md] |
| CLAS / TMR | No-cue, random-cue, or off-phase cueing | Sleep physiology can move even when memory does not | Useful for mechanistic sleep work, but not an inert comparator.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40102385-phase-specific-enhancement-motor-memory-consolidation-closed-loop-tmr.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41229102-closed-loop-auditory-stimulation-ecological-memory-null.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41497605-closed-loop-acoustic-stimulation-cathodal-tdcs-retention.md] |
| Caffeine | Placebo or decaf | Withdrawal reversal and expectancy can both contribute | In dependent users, the comparator is often a rescue control.^[raw/articles/pubmed-22992376-caffeine-attention-withdrawn-consumers.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-30861208-expectancy-caffeine-withdrawal-balanced-placebo-design.md] |
| Nicotine | Placebo nicotine or abstinence control | Withdrawal relief can mimic enhancement | Best treated as state restoration rather than broad nootropic evidence.^[raw/articles/pubmed-31686175-nicotine-galantamine-healthy-never-smokers-cognition.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-38582919-nicotine-cognitive-function-smokers-abstinence.md] |

## Labeling Rules

- Use `placebo` only when the control is intended to be biologically inert and there is no strong reason to think it changes the target state.
- Use `sham` when the control hides assignment but may still have sensory or physiological effects.
- Use `active sham` when the comparator is deliberately sensory-matched or chamber-matched but not inert.
- Use `rescue comparator` when the main effect is withdrawal reversal or deficit relief.
- Use `state challenge` when the comparator intentionally imposes sleep loss, stress, or another perturbation.
- When writing future pages, state both `blinded?` and `biologically inert?` in the first paragraph.

## Open Contradictions

- A good sham can still be biologically active.
- A biologically active comparator can still be well blinded.
- Withdrawal reversal can look like enhancement if the baseline state is not tracked.
- State-challenge paradigms are useful but should never be labeled as placebo trials.
