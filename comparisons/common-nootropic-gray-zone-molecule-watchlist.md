# Common Nootropic Gray-Zone Molecule Watchlist

This page catches high-salience nootropic molecules that are too important to omit from the wiki, but not yet clean enough for standalone entity promotion. The rule is simple: a molecule can be relevant without being recommendable. Each row keeps formal medicinal signal, anecdote signal, and safety/regulatory burden separate. Use [[gray-zone-nootropic-community-signal-ledger]] for the direct forum-source layer.

## Watchlist

| Candidate | Formal medicinal signal | Anecdote signal | Current route | Entity threshold |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [[adrafinil]] | `low/risk-first`; formal interpretation should go through [[modafinil]] because adrafinil is a prodrug covered in modafinil/adrafinil review context, not a separate modern cognition-efficacy packet; WADA 2026 names adrafinil in S6.A with several afinil analogs^[raw/articles/sage-2020-modafinil-adrafinil-review.md]^[raw/articles/wada-2026-stimulant-list-afinils-pemoline-tesofensine.md] | `moderate-strong`; 2017 /r/Nootropics survey placed adrafinil among the strongest focus signals^[raw/articles/darktka-2017-nootropics-survey-on-r-nootropics.md] | Wakefulness gray market / prodrug risk | Entity exists; regulatory, WADA, analog/source-quality refresh complete |

## Practical Interpretation

This watchlist keeps the common gray-zone molecules connected to the main graph without prematurely promoting them in ranking. Route [[adrafinil]] through [[modafinil]] and [[regulatory-and-sports-visibility-map]], [[vinpocetine]] through [[safety-interaction-matrix-for-multi-modal-stacks]] and [[supplement-identity-evidence-map]], [[magnesium-l-threonate]] and [[phosphatidylserine]] through [[supplement-identity-evidence-map]], [[theacrine]] and [[theobromine]] through [[caffeine]], [[methylliberine]], and [[state-challenge-matrix-for-cognition-interventions]], and [[uridine]] through [[choline-donor-cognition-signal-map]] plus [[nutrient-supplement-cognition-signal-map]]. [[adrafinil]], [[vinpocetine]], [[phosphatidylserine]], [[theacrine]], [[theobromine]], and [[uridine]] are no longer source-extraction gaps; the remaining adrafinil gap is direct modern healthy-adult cognition/safety evidence independent of modafinil, the remaining vinpocetine gap is any well-powered positive healthy-adult cognition replication after the small null crossover, the remaining PS gap is a modern isolated soy/sunflower PS healthy-adult cognition trial, the remaining theacrine gap is objective theacrine-alone cognition, the remaining theobromine gap is independent objective cognition beyond subjective/mood and cocoa-product confounds, and the remaining uridine gap is direct uridine-alone or well-controlled chronic UMP / TAU cognition data in healthy adults.
