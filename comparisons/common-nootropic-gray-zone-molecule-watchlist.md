# Common Nootropic Gray-Zone Molecule Watchlist

This page catches high-salience nootropic molecules that are too important to omit from the wiki, but not yet clean enough for standalone entity promotion. The rule is simple: a molecule can be relevant without being recommendable. Each row keeps formal medicinal signal, anecdote signal, and safety/regulatory burden separate. Use [[gray-zone-nootropic-community-signal-ledger]] for the direct forum-source layer.

## Watchlist

| Candidate | Formal medicinal signal | Anecdote signal | Current route | Entity threshold |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [[adrafinil]] | `low/risk-first`; formal interpretation should go through [[modafinil]] because adrafinil is a prodrug covered in modafinil/adrafinil review context, not a separate modern cognition-efficacy packet^[raw/articles/sage-2020-modafinil-adrafinil-review.md] | `moderate-strong`; 2017 /r/Nootropics survey placed adrafinil among the strongest focus signals^[raw/articles/darktka-2017-nootropics-survey-on-r-nootropics.md] | Wakefulness gray market / prodrug risk | Entity exists; needs regulatory, liver-safety, and WADA/source-quality refresh |
| [[vinpocetine]] | `risk-first`; FDA says it has not evaluated efficacy for brain-function claims and warns pregnant women / women who could become pregnant not to use it because of reproductive-harm concerns^[raw/articles/fda-2019-vinpocetine-dietary-supplements.md] | `moderate` direct forum salience for acute vascular/brain-fog/headache contexts, not efficacy certainty^[raw/articles/reddit-2022-vinpocetine-impact-thread.md] | Regulatory safety exemplar | Entity exists; formal cognition trials still need refresh |
| [[phosphatidylserine]] | `low-moderate`; ADDF notes modest trial signals but composition variation and long-term-data gaps^[raw/articles/alzdiscovery-2023-phosphatidylserine-cognitive-vitality.md] | `moderate` direct forum salience for stress/cortisol, focus, and memory claims^[raw/articles/reddit-2020-phosphatidylserine-experiences-thread.md] | Older-adult / product-composition support | Entity exists; direct trial/meta source extraction still pending |
| [[magnesium-l-threonate]] / Magtein | `low-moderate`; 2026 RCT found NIH Total Cognition Composite, working/episodic memory, reaction-time, HR, and HRV improvements, but Raven's and several sleep outcomes were null^[raw/articles/frontiers-2026-magnesium-l-threonate-cognition-sleep-rct.md] | `moderate` direct forum salience, with mixed sleep/focus benefit and paradoxical-stimulation reports^[raw/articles/reddit-2021-magnesium-l-threonate-magtein-experience-thread.md] | Product-specific sleep/cognition support | Entity exists; older MMFS-01 and 2022 formula sources should be normalized |
| [[theacrine]] / TeaCrine with caffeine | `low-moderate` for fatigue-state combination, not theacrine alone; 2025 tactical-personnel paper tested caffeine plus theacrine under physically fatiguing conditions^[raw/articles/pubmed-40693646-caffeine-theacrine-tactical-fatigue-cognition.md] | `weak-moderate`; direct forum thread is mostly weak alone, stronger as caffeine/theobromine stack modifier^[raw/articles/reddit-2025-theacrine-experience-thread.md] | Caffeine-stack / state-challenge candidate | Entity exists; standalone theacrine trials still pending |
| [[uridine]] / UMP with DHA and choline | `preclinical-only` for cognition in this source packet; captured anchor is a gerbil DHA/choline/uridine learning-memory study^[raw/articles/pmc-2574024-uridine-dha-gerbil-learning-memory.md] | `moderate` direct forum salience, but heavily stack-confounded^[raw/articles/reddit-2025-uridine-nootropics-must-thread.md] | Mechanism / stack hypothesis | Entity exists; human cognition trial or structured anecdote capture needed |
