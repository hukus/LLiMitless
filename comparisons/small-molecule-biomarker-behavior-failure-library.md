# Small-Molecule Biomarker-Behavior Failure Library

This page is the pattern library for small molecules that looked stronger in biomarkers, subgroup analyses, or secondary endpoints than they ultimately did in primary cognition readouts. The point is not that the mechanisms are fake. The point is that human cognition claims often weaken exactly where the evidence gets harder: a real biomarker moves, an early hint appears, or a post hoc subgroup lights up, but the primary endpoint or the broader replication package does not hold.

Use this page together with [[target-engagement-to-behavior-mediation-audit]], [[endpoint-fragility-ledger]], [[negative-evidence-watchlist-for-cognition-interventions]], and [[evidence-credibility-gate-for-cognition-interventions]]. For the serotonergic subset, see [[5-ht6-antagonist-negative-class-map]].

## What Changed Since 2024

- The wiki now has a clean human example of biomarker movement without a cognition win: donepezil in fragile X left cognition and behavior flat while fMRI activation changed.^[raw/articles/pubmed-31264943-donepezil-fragile-x-biomarker-null.md]
- The 5-HT6 family now looks like a mature failure class rather than a single-program miss. SB-742457, idalopirdine, masupirdine, and intepirdine all failed to produce a durable cognition story across later human programs.^[raw/articles/pubmed-20043816-sb742457-phase2-ad.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-29854923-sb742457-two-rcts-ad.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-25297016-idalopirdine-ladder-moderate-ad.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-29318278-idalopirdine-three-rcts-ad.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-35662833-masupirdine-suvn-502-moderate-ad-cognition.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-34189249-intepirdine-headway-dlb.md]
- GV-971 / sodium oligomannate remains the clearest geography-limited case: phase 2 was primary-endpoint negative but had secondary and PET hints, the China phase 3 was positive, and the planned global program was suspended/terminated without a posted efficacy confirmation.^[raw/articles/pubmed-32928279-sodium-oligomannate-phase-2.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-33731209-sodium-oligomannate-phase3-china.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct04520412-gv971-green-memory-global.md]^[raw/articles/greenvalley-2022-gv971-global-phase3-termination.md]

## Library Rules

1. Do not upgrade a biomarker signal into a cognition win unless an objective cognitive endpoint moved.
2. Do not upgrade a post hoc subgroup into a validated responder phenotype unless it replicates independently.
3. Do not treat a positive single-country program as globally replicated until a multinational or independent confirmation exists.
4. Keep the failure pattern visible even when the same molecule has a separate positive context elsewhere.
5. Use this page as a cautionary comparator, not as proof that a mechanism can never work.

## Case Notes

### Donepezil in Fragile X

This is the cleanest biomarker/behavior split in the current library. The active group showed reduced fMRI activation during gaze discrimination, which implies a real CNS effect, but the cognitive contingency naming test and behavioral scales did not separate from placebo. The useful lesson is that a brain-state shift is not automatically a cognition shift.^[raw/articles/pubmed-31264943-donepezil-fragile-x-biomarker-null.md]

### SB-742457

SB-742457 is the classic "good early shape, weak late package" example. Phase 2 produced a nominal global-impression signal, but the larger trial package did not convert that into a stable cognition win. The follow-on paper is especially useful because it shows how timepoint-specific or adjunctive signals can survive even when overall success criteria are still not met.^[raw/articles/pubmed-20043816-sb742457-phase2-ad.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-29854923-sb742457-two-rcts-ad.md]

### Masupirdine and Idalopirdine

Masupirdine and idalopirdine show two slightly different ways the same class can fail. Masupirdine's primary cognition trial was negative, then the sponsor-led post hoc paper shifted attention to agitation and psychosis subgroups. Idalopirdine's larger program was overall null, but sensitivity analyses kept teasing a higher-dose / moderate-AD story that never became a robust result. These are the cases that most often get misread as "almost worked."^[raw/articles/pubmed-35662833-masupirdine-suvn-502-moderate-ad-cognition.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-36168659-masupirdine-posthoc-agitation-psychosis.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-25297016-idalopirdine-ladder-moderate-ad.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-29318278-idalopirdine-three-rcts-ad.md]

### GV-971 / Sodium Oligomannate

GV-971 is not a simple failure. It is a geography-limited and registry-sensitive branch. Phase 2 missed the primary endpoint but showed a responder and PET subgroup story; the China phase 3 was positive; the planned global confirmation did not deliver a posted efficacy answer before suspension/termination. That combination makes it a strong cautionary comparator whenever a branch depends on one jurisdiction's data package.^[raw/articles/pubmed-32928279-sodium-oligomannate-phase-2.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-33731209-sodium-oligomannate-phase3-china.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct04520412-gv971-green-memory-global.md]^[raw/articles/greenvalley-2022-gv971-global-phase3-termination.md]

## Evidence Tiering

| Tier | What counts here | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Biomarker / brain-state only | Brain imaging, physiology, or related mechanistic movement without a behavior win | Useful for target validation, not enough for enhancement ranking |
| Primary endpoint null with side signals | The main cognition endpoint misses, but a secondary or timepoint signal survives | High risk of overinterpretation |
| Post hoc subgroup only | A signal appears after the main trial, often in enriched or sponsor-led analyses | Hypothesis-generating only |
| Geography-limited replication | One jurisdiction or program is positive while global confirmation is missing | Requires approval- and registry-aware interpretation |

## Open Contradictions

- A compound can move a biomarker and still fail cognition.
- A compound can miss the primary endpoint and still produce an attractive subgroup signal.
- A subgroup signal can be real and still be too weak or too brittle to support a general claim.
- A single-country positive program can coexist with global uncertainty.
- A mechanism can remain plausible long after the human evidence has become cautionary.
