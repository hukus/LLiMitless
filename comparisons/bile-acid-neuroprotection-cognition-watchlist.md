# Bile Acid Neuroprotection Cognition Watchlist

This page routes hydrophilic bile-acid cognition claims, especially [[tudca-ursodiol]], UDCA / ursodiol, TUDCA / taurursodiol / TURSO, and AMX0035. The shared hypothesis is gut-liver-brain signaling, mitochondrial and endoplasmic-reticulum stress, apoptosis, neuroinflammation, bile-acid receptor biology, and disease-state metabolic modulation. The current evidence does not support ranking this branch as a healthy-adult nootropic.

What changed since 2024: the branch became easier to score because the negative translation layer got much stronger. The European TUDCA-ALS phase 3 trial did not meet its primary endpoint or key secondary endpoints, and AMX0035 / RELYVRIO-ALBRIOZA was removed for new U.S. and Canadian ALS patients after PHOENIX failed. Against that, the branch still has a 2023 UDCA Parkinson's target-engagement trial, a 2024 AD mechanism review, a 2025 progressive-MS safety/biomarker trial, and a small but visible TUDCA brain-fog/mood anecdote trail.^[raw/articles/ucl-2023-udca-parkinsons-phase2.md]^[raw/articles/mndassociation-2024-tudca-als-phase3-negative.md]^[raw/articles/amylyx-2024-amx0035-phoenix-withdrawal.md]^[raw/articles/frontiers-2024-tudca-ad-review.md]^[raw/articles/pmc-2025-bile-acid-tudca-progressive-ms.md]^[raw/articles/reddit-2024-2026-tudca-brainfog-mood-threads.md]

## Evidence Notes

UDCA / ursodiol has a cleaner medical identity than consumer TUDCA supplements. DailyMed labels prescription ursodiol as a bile-acid capsule for liver/bile/gallstone contexts, while PubChem separates UDCA and TUDCA structures and names. That helps prevent "TUDCA supplement," "prescription ursodiol," and "AMX0035" from being treated as one exposure.^[raw/articles/pubchem-2026-tudca-udca-identity.md]^[raw/articles/dailymed-2023-ursodiol-label.md]

The Parkinson's UDCA trial is the best human target-engagement anchor: high-dose UDCA for 48 weeks in 30 PD participants was safe and tolerable, moved midbrain spectroscopy markers of ATP hydrolysis, and suggested possible gait movement in sensor metrics, while subjective MDS-UPDRS-III did not separate treatment groups. It is relevant to mitochondrial-rescue logic, not proof of cognition enhancement.^[raw/articles/ucl-2023-udca-parkinsons-phase2.md]

The TUDCA neurodegeneration story is strongest preclinically and weakest when overgeneralized. A 2024 AD review summarizes anti-apoptotic, anti-inflammatory, synaptic, amyloid, tau, and memory-task animal evidence. The progressive-MS trial found safety/tolerability and biological changes without clinical or fluid-biomarker differences. The ALS phase-3 failures make this a watchlist, not a recommendation tier.^[raw/articles/frontiers-2024-tudca-ad-review.md]^[raw/articles/pmc-2025-bile-acid-tudca-progressive-ms.md]^[raw/articles/mndassociation-2024-tudca-als-phase3-negative.md]^[raw/articles/amylyx-2024-amx0035-phoenix-withdrawal.md]

## Inclusion Rules

- Include bile-acid candidates when they have a named chemical identity, formal disease-state evidence, direct brain-fog anecdotes, or a safety/regulatory signal relevant to cognition claims.
- Keep TUDCA, UDCA/ursodiol, AMX0035, ox bile, bile salts, nor-UDCA, and product blends separate unless a source explicitly tests the same exposure.
- Do not upgrade medicinal signal from liver enzymes, digestion, mood, or energy anecdotes unless cognitive endpoints are independently measured.
- Route any strong brain-fog claim through [[molecule-intake-triage-table]], phenotype notes, and [[self-experimentation-stop-rules]] before comparing it to cleaner state-support chemicals.

## Verdict

The bile-acid branch is now covered, but it is a low-confidence, phenotype-gated watch item. It is most useful for hypothesis generation around gut-liver-brain state, bile flow, mitochondrial/ER stress, neuroinflammation, and disease-state translation failures. It should sit below practical buyable candidates such as [[creatine]], [[caffeine]], [[ketone-ester]], [[citicoline]], and [[l-theanine]] until direct human cognitive endpoints appear.
