# Fixed Traditional Formula Cognition Watchlist

This page routes traditional formula claims where the active unit is a named multi-ingredient formula rather than a single herb, molecule, or standardized extract. It exists to keep formula-level signals from being incorrectly assigned to isolated ingredients.

## Verdict

The cleanest current formula-level cognition signal remains [[sailuotong]], because the captured trials specify a standardized ginseng/Ginkgo/crocin formula and measure cognition in both a tiny healthy-adult pilot and an MCI phase 2 pilot. [[kai-xin-san]] is tradition-salient and mechanistically rich but currently strongest in animal AD models and formula-family clinical adjunct evidence, not healthy adults. [[yokukansan]] is a useful negative-cognition comparator: it has dementia behavioral-symptom evidence but no MMSE benefit in meta-analysis and a licorice/hypokalemia safety gate. [[shankhpushpi]] is better treated as an Ayurvedic memory-tonic label with open-label/preclinical evidence and plant-identity ambiguity.^[raw/articles/bmc-2016-sailuotong-healthy-adults-crossover-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-2023-sailuotong-mci-pilot-rct.md]^[raw/articles/frontiers-2026-kai-xin-san-ad-preclinical-meta.md]^[raw/articles/frontiers-2023-polygala-acorus-ad-meta-analysis.md]^[raw/articles/sage-2016-yokukansan-bpsd-updated-meta-analysis.md]^[raw/articles/alzdiscovery-2020-shankhpushpi-cognitive-vitality.md]

## Formula Rows

| Formula | Evidence read | Best-fit phenotype | Main safety / identity gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| [[sailuotong]] | Low-moderate formula-level signal; healthy-adult crossover pilot plus MCI phase 2 pilot | MCI / small healthy-adult proof-of-concept | Standardized ginsenosides, ginkgo flavone glycosides, crocins; bleeding/stimulant/saffron gates |
| [[kai-xin-san]] | Preclinical-to-low disease-state signal; strong animal-model packet, weak exact human formula evidence | AD/MCI research hypothesis, not healthy enhancement | Acorus beta-asarone, Polygala/Acorus formula heterogeneity, product marker chemistry |
| [[yokukansan]] | Behavioral-symptom dementia signal, cognition-neutral by MMSE | BPSD / agitation / sleep-behavior state, not cognition | Licorice/Glycyrrhiza hypokalemia, pseudoaldosteronism, potassium/BP monitoring |
| [[shankhpushpi]] | Open-label/preclinical and tradition-salient; no controlled human cognition proof | Ayurvedic memory-tonic lore / research-priority scan | Plant identity, multi-herb attribution, contaminants, BP/phenytoin cautions |

## Decision Rules

- Use a formula page when the intervention is sold or studied as a fixed combination.
- Use a single-herb entity only when a trial tests that herb or extract directly.
- Do not borrow ingredient anecdotes to upgrade a formula unless the exact formula has direct anecdote or human data.
- Formula trials need product identity, constituent markers, dose, route, comparator, and adverse-event reporting before ranking.

## Relationship To Standardized Extracts

This page is a sibling of [[standardized-herbal-extracts-for-cognition]], not a replacement. Standardized extracts such as [[ginkgo]], [[ginseng]], [[spearmint-extract]], [[sage-salvia-extract]], or [[grape-extract]] can be judged by product chemistry and dose. Formula pages require an extra attribution layer because constituent interactions, dose ratios, processing, and marker profiles can drive the result.

## Open Questions

- Can [[kai-xin-san]] be standardized with a low-asarone Acorus gate and tested against placebo in MCI or subjective cognitive decline?
- Does [[sailuotong]] replicate in a larger independent MCI trial with durable follow-up?
- Is [[yokukansan]] ever cognition-positive in a defined sleep/agitation subgroup, or is the benefit mainly behavioral-state modulation?
- Can [[shankhpushpi]] be pinned to a single verified botanical exposure and tested in a blinded human cognition trial?
