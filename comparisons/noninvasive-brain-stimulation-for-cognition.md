# Noninvasive Brain Stimulation for Cognition

This comparison collects the main noninvasive stimulation modalities that currently have real cognition endpoints: [[repetitive-transcranial-magnetic-stimulation-and-cognition]], [[transcranial-direct-current-stimulation-and-cognition]], [[transcranial-alternating-current-stimulation-and-cognition]], [[transcranial-random-noise-stimulation-and-cognition]], and the broader umbrella work that also touches adjacent methods such as [[transcutaneous-auricular-vagus-nerve-stimulation]]. The shared theme is that these are network-level interventions, not classic nootropics. tRNS now stands out as a separate stochastic-resonance branch with a healthy working-memory signal, an emotion-attention signal, a schizophrenia adjunctive signal, and several null boundary cases.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39910547-nibs-cognitive-impairment-umbrella-review.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41549257-noninvasive-neuromodulation-cognitive-function-adl.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39402554-trns-working-memory-healthy-individuals.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41387754-trns-attention-bias-negative-emotional-stimuli.md]^[raw/articles/pmc-12949120-trns-schizophrenia-bilateral-prefrontal.md]^[raw/articles/nature-s41598-025-21890-x-trns-numerical-acuity.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40669128-trns-motion-processing-null-registered-report.md]

rTMS remains the strongest clinical candidate in this cluster, but it is also the clearest example of why "protocol sensitivity" matters. The newest literature now points to target selection, pulse dose, and add-on context as the main levers, not just whether stimulation was delivered at all. The practical takeaway is that the relevant comparison is no longer "rTMS versus sham" in the abstract, but "which target, which family, and which pulse window?"^[raw/articles/pubmed-40676541-rtms-cognitive-impairment-neurodegenerative-diseases.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41191186-rtms-cognitive-emotional-symptoms-neurodegenerative-diseases.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41192010-rtms-non-pharmacological-interventions-cognitive-disorders.md]

| Modality | Best current use-case | Evidence shape | Main caveat |
|---|---|---|---|
| [[repetitive-transcranial-magnetic-stimulation-and-cognition]] | MCI/AD, cognitive impairment, adjunctive rehab | Strongest and most mature evidence among the three | Highly protocol-dependent; DLPFC is the default, but precuneus, iTBS, and intermediate pulse windows are now part of the real decision space |
| [[transcranial-direct-current-stimulation-and-cognition]] | Cognitive training adjunct, older adults, MCI | Cheap and accessible; modest effects | Dosage and montage sensitivity |
| [[transcranial-alternating-current-stimulation-and-cognition]] | Rhythm/oscillation-focused memory work | Promising but still exploratory | Small and heterogeneous evidence base |
| [[transcranial-random-noise-stimulation-and-cognition]] | Healthy working memory, emotion-attention bias, schizophrenia adjunctive work, selective creativity; ADHD/dyslexia in review data | Mixed but real signal; task- and phenotype-specific | Separate stochastic-resonance branch with one healthy WM RCT, one positive emotion-attention study, one schizophrenia crossover, and null boundary cases |

The synthesis is simple: rTMS is the strongest clinical candidate, tDCS is the most practical low-cost option, tACS is the most mechanistically interesting but least settled, and tRNS is the clearest separate stochastic-resonance branch rather than a subrow of tDCS or tACS. All three belong in the same evidence bucket as [[focused-ultrasound-neuromodulation]] because they are attempts to modulate network function without surgery, but rTMS is the one where target, frequency family, pulse count, and combination context already appear to change the answer. tRNS now adds a sharper rule: target and task matter enough that healthy WM, emotional attention, schizophrenia, and numerical/motion nulls all need to be carried separately.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39910547-nibs-cognitive-impairment-umbrella-review.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41034500-tacs-cognitive-function-older-adults.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41507453-tacs-cognitive-impairment-meta.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40769285-rtms-stroke-cognitive-impairment.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41582468-trns-psychiatric-systematic-review.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39402554-trns-working-memory-healthy-individuals.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41387754-trns-attention-bias-negative-emotional-stimuli.md]^[raw/articles/pmc-12949120-trns-schizophrenia-bilateral-prefrontal.md]^[raw/articles/nature-s41598-025-21890-x-trns-numerical-acuity.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40669128-trns-motion-processing-null-registered-report.md]

Temporal interference stimulation now sits one step beyond the classic surface-stimulation map: it is still early, but it deserves its own deep-target branch rather than being folded into tACS or generic tES. The 2026 prolonged-disorders-of-consciousness pilot widens the clinical pipeline, but it is still not proof of broad cognition enhancement.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41167554-human-applications-temporal-interference-stimulation.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41040248-temporal-interference-memory-encoding.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41654211-temporal-interference-prolonged-disorders-consciousness-pilot.md]

The newer tDCS/tACS scan adds a sharper parameter lesson: for tDCS, current density, duration, session count, target, and broader review-level moderators like timing, population, intensity, and age matter more than montage labels alone; for tACS, theta-band and gamma-band protocols split into different use-cases rather than one generic oscillation story, phase lag now behaves like a dose variable, and the 2026 impairment meta suggests the branch is more treatment-like in older adults with cognitive impairment than enhancement-like in healthy adults. The 2025-2026 language-network studies add one more rule: baseline cognition can gate whether focal tDCS helps, which means the responder phenotype is part of the protocol. The 2026 memory map still suggests site-by-frequency interactions that can flip sign across cortex. tRNS now sits outside that tDCS/tACS pair as a separate stochastic-resonance lane, not an extra parameter row. See [[tdcs-tacs-parameter-response-2025-2026-scan]] for the extracted map.^[raw/articles/pubmed-38060075-tdcs-cognitive-functions-review.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39905569-tdcs-dosage-parameters.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39438426-tacs-working-memory-meta-2025.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40097468-phase-lagged-tacs-executive-default-mode-working-memory.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41461804-bifrontal-tacs-long-term-working-memory-episodic-memory.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41034500-tacs-older-adults-meta-2025.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41507453-tacs-cognitive-impairment-meta.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40933163-theta-tacs-working-memory-healthy-adults.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41784599-tacs-long-term-memory-mapping.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40174444-hdtdcs-region-specific-cognition-older-adults.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40347355-focal-tdcs-picture-naming-baseline-performance.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41870727-tdcs-verbal-fluency-baseline-cognition-site.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41449818-tdcs-auditory-false-perceptions-null.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41582468-trns-psychiatric-systematic-review.md]

tDCS is also a metabolic actor, not just a cognitive one: repeated anodal stimulation lowered blood glucose without changing serum insulin in healthy men, which matters if anyone is trying to pair stimulation with intranasal insulin or other glucose-sensitive interventions.^[raw/articles/pubmed-28392373-tdcs-glucose-reduction.md]
