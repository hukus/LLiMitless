# Nonstimulant ADHD Pipeline Attention Chemicals Watchlist

This page routes attention-agent chemicals that are adjacent to [[nonstimulant-noradrenergic-attention-agent-watchlist]] and [[monoamine-reuptake-anorectic-stimulants-cognition-watchlist]], but do not fit cleanly in either bucket: [[centanafadine]], [[dasotraline]], and [[metadoxine]]. SNDRI / triple-reuptake antidepressant comparators now route through [[triple-reuptake-inhibitor-cognition-watchlist]] instead of this ADHD-pipeline page.

The class lesson is that ADHD symptom relief, reuptake pharmacology, and hangover / alertness anecdotes can look like cognitive improvement, but they need separate gates before being promoted into healthy-adult cognition.

| [[metadoxine]] | Pyridoxine / pyrrolidone-carboxylate compound; ER MDX ADHD program and acute-alcohol-intoxication history | Phase 2 ADHD and single-dose ADHD-PI TOVA signals; alcohol-intoxication recovery evidence | Later phase 3 ADHD failure and preclinical-neurology clinical-hold history | Attention-state / alcohol-aftereffect hypothesis, not nootropic tier |

## Why This Branch Matters

[[tesofensine]] prompted the broader monoamine-reuptake expansion, but tesofensine is mostly an anorectic / obesity-development and self-experimentation story. Centanafadine and dasotraline are closer to clinical ADHD attention-agent development, while metadoxine is a different mechanism with ADHD-PI and alcohol-aftereffect relevance.

The newer [[triple-reuptake-inhibitor-cognition-watchlist]] handles [[ansofaxine-toludesvenlafaxine]], [[liafensine]], [[amitifadine]], [[gsk372475]], [[csti-500]], and [[nefopam]]. That split keeps MDD/TRD anhedonia or pain-state claims from being overread as ADHD-style executive-function proof.

That split prevents three common overreads:

1. A triple-reuptake label does not mean a compound should inherit [[tesofensine]] anecdotes.
2. ADHD symptom-scale improvement does not equal direct improvement on attention, working memory, or episodic-memory tests.
3. Hangover or intoxication recovery does not equal durable cognition improvement.

## Evidence Split

[[centanafadine]] is the active watch item. The adult phase 3 evidence is statistically positive on ADHD symptoms, and the FDA review status makes it worth tracking until the 2026-07-24 PDUFA date. Current community signal is mainly anticipation rather than direct experience.^[raw/articles/pmc-9426730-centanafadine-adult-adhd-phase3.md]^[raw/articles/otsuka-2026-centanafadine-nda-priority-review.md]^[raw/articles/reddit-2023-2026-centanafadine-metadoxine-attention-threads.md]

[[dasotraline]] is the cautionary comparator. It improved adult ADHD symptoms in a proof-of-concept trial, but the same article reports no significant main effects on direct cognitive battery measures, and the sponsor later withdrew the NDAs and stopped development.^[raw/articles/nature-2015-dasotraline-adult-adhd-proof-of-concept.md]^[raw/articles/businesswire-2020-dasotraline-discontinued.md]

[[metadoxine]] is the mixed-signal comparator. It has phase 2 and single-dose attention-task data in adult ADHD / ADHD-PI, plus acute-alcohol-intoxication recovery evidence, but the pivotal ADHD program failed.^[raw/articles/pubmed-23290324-metadoxine-er-adult-adhd.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-25295645-metadoxine-single-dose-adhd-pi.md]^[raw/articles/genengnews-2017-metadoxine-phase3-failure.md]

## Safety And Interpretation Gates

- For reuptake inhibitors, monitor insomnia, appetite/weight loss, BP/HR, anxiety/activation, mood effects, dry mouth, nausea, serotonergic / stimulant stacking, and washout.
- For long-half-life agents such as [[dasotraline]] and [[tesofensine]], accumulation and slow adverse-event resolution are central.
- For [[metadoxine]], keep ER ADHD studies, IV alcohol-intoxication studies, and supplement-style B-vitamin / pyroglutamate claims separate.
- For [[centanafadine]], preserve exact regulatory dates: as of 2026-05-15 the NDA is under priority review, with PDUFA target 2026-07-24.
