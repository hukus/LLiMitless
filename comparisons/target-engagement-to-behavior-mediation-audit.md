# Target Engagement to Behavior Mediation Audit

This page tracks the chain that usually gets collapsed in cognition research: target exposure, biomarker movement, same-day cognition, delayed retention, and functional transfer. The recurring pattern is that the first two links are easier to show than the last three. Use this page together with [[delivery-validation-checklist-for-cognition-interventions]], [[endpoint-dictionary-for-cognition-interventions]], and [[washout-durability-for-cognition-interventions]].

## Summary

The strongest full-chain evidence in this slice comes from [[repetitive-transcranial-magnetic-stimulation-and-cognition]] and the better tPBM studies, where exposure is credible, biomarkers move, cognition moves, and at least some follow-up signal survives beyond the immediate post-session window. Glymphatic-targeting work also has a real mediator story now, with human sleep-linked A beta/tau clearance and better proxy measurement, but it still looks more like a pathway-enriched rescue mechanism than a general enhancement class. By contrast, taVNS and intranasal insulin usually move state or biomarker readouts before they move broad cognition, temporal interference is still target-engagement-heavy, and focused-ultrasound BBB opening is mostly a delivery platform rather than a cognition result, even after the newer astrocyte-response paper added more preclinical plausibility.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40948563-focused-ultrasound-bbbo-astrocytes.md]

## Mediation Ladder

Use this ladder to avoid collapsing the whole chain into one bucket.

1. `Target reached`: the intervention reaches or meaningfully shifts the intended tissue, circuit, or delivery zone.
2. `Biomarker moved`: a mechanistic readout changes in the predicted direction.
3. `Same-day cognition changed`: the immediate behavioral endpoint moves.
4. `Delayed cognition changed`: a follow-up endpoint still shows benefit after the acute window.
5. `Functional transfer changed`: the effect generalizes to untrained tasks, broader ability, or day-to-day function.

A branch can stall at any rung. The wiki should treat lower rungs as prerequisites, not as proof of downstream efficacy.

## Recent Methodological Anchors

- `Target reached`: the 2025 personalized TES modeling paper shows how customized electrode placement can increase cortical target engagement before any efficacy claim is made.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40228684-customized-electrode-positioning-personalized-tes-target-engagement.md]
- `Biomarker moved`: the 2026 human glymphatic crossover study linked sleep-active clearance to morning plasma A beta and tau, and the 2025 parenchymal-resistance device study showed a continuous way to track the same pathway in humans.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41593094-glymphatic-system-clears-amyloid-beta-and-tau-from-brain-to-plasma-in-humans.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40425804-brain-parenchymal-resistance-glymphatic-human-device.md]
- `Biomarker moved`: the 2025 tFUS amygdala trial is explicitly framed as a target-engagement study and reports reduced amygdala BOLD with a subsequent symptom signal in the open phase.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40275098-low-intensity-transcranial-focused-ultrasound-amygdala-neuromodulation.md]
- `Delayed cognition changed`: the 2024 tDCS plus cognitive training trial in cognitive impairment missed the trained-task primary endpoint but still showed near-transfer working-memory benefit and 7-month connectivity change in the more compliant sample.^[raw/articles/pmc-10782634-cognitive-training-and-brain-stimulation-in-patients-with-cognitive-impairment.md]
- `Functional transfer changed`: the 2025 German AgeGain study explicitly centers transfer to untrained abilities and neurobiological modulators of successful transfer in healthy aging.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40933821-cognitive-training-gain-transfer-german-agegain-study.md]
- `Behavioral mediation`: the 2025 MBSR breast-cancer-survivor trial tests whether cognitive gains are mediated by mindfulness, rumination, and perceived stress, which is a useful non-device analog for the same logic.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39711191-mbsr-mediators-cognitive-improvement-breast-cancer-survivors.md]

## What Changed Since 2024

- Acute exercise now has a broad cognition signal across 383 studies, while the reserve mechanism literature still shows that higher cardiorespiratory fitness is more tightly linked to cerebrovascular reactivity than exercise training is to a universal cerebral blood-flow gain.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39883421-acute-exercise-meta-review.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-34018848-cardiorespiratory-fitness-exercise-training-cerebrovascular.md]
- Glymphatic work moved from association to mediation: LF-rTMS in chronic insomnia linked DTI-ALPS improvement to later MoCA and digit-span-backward gains, older-adult TBS linked APOE epsilon4 subgroup DTI-ALPS changes to memory improvement, and a 2026 human sleep-vs-deprivation crossover showed that glymphatic clearance can be tracked directly through brain-to-plasma A beta and tau movement.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40121525-lf-rtms-glymphatic-cognition.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40746364-glymphatic-system-as-therapeutic-target-tms-induced-modulation-older-adults.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41593094-glymphatic-system-clears-amyloid-beta-and-tau-from-brain-to-plasma-in-humans.md]
- tPBM gained a cleaner chain from exposure to biomarkers to behavior: the MCI pilot reported improved MMSE and long-delay recognition alongside higher lactate/pyruvate, lower lactate-to-pyruvate ratio, higher DMN connectivity, and lower IL-6; a 2026 MEG/EEG study added frequency-specific network reorganization, and the combined TMS+tPBM MCI trial added a working-memory plus fNIRS bridge.^[raw/articles/medrxiv-25333989-tpbm-mci-cognitive-metabolic-neuroimaging-pilot.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41584102-transcranial-1064nm-laser-photobiomodulation.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40254536-combined-tms-tpbm-mci-working-memory.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41125953-tpbm-insomnia-rct.md]
- Bright light now has a glymphatic biomarker bridge too: morning BLT improved depressive symptoms and shifted DTI-ALPS and free-water markers in subthreshold depression, which matters because it shows a circadian intervention can move the same pathway this audit watches in stimulation work.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41672157-blt-glymphatic-subthreshold-depression.md]
- Lactate moved from mechanistic substrate to acute biomarker bridge in the LEAN study, where infusion improved global cognition and lowered multiple AD fluid biomarkers despite unchanged clearance rates across phenotype groups.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41376120-lean-lactate-infusion-cognition-biomarkers.md]
- taVNS data shifted toward state regulation rather than broad enhancement: newer work shows sensory leakage, pupil-dilation effects, and autonomic/stress changes that do not cleanly map to a stable cognition win.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39815765-taVNS-memory-pupil-dilation.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40958978-taVNS-stress-regulation-eeg-questionnaire.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39124651-taVNS-autonomic-function.md]
- Intranasal insulin now has direct brain-uptake validation in humans, but the branch still splits between phenotype-sensitive connectivity changes and mostly mixed or null chronic-cognition results in AD/MCI; perioperative protection remains the clearest functional use case.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40703476-intranasal-insulin-pet-aging-mci.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40392946-intranasal-insulin-t2d-rsfc.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40253245-intranasal-insulin-ms-null.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41720686-intranasal-insulin-perioperative-delirium-meta.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40195962-intranasal-insulin-ad-systematic-review-meta-analysis.md]
- Temporal interference now has convincing human target-validation and a bidirectional memory-encoding preprint, but no durable cognition branch yet.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39059712-temporal-interference-orbitofrontal-bold.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41040248-temporal-interference-memory-encoding.md]
- Focused-ultrasound BBB opening now reads as a delivery platform with safety and exposure control, not a cognition-positive branch.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40406679-focused-ultrasound-bbbo-ad-meta-analysis.md]
- 2025-2026 papers now make the chain itself explicit: target-engagement studies, electric-field / biomarker correlations, transfer-specific trials, and mediation analyses provide reusable examples for each rung instead of forcing the wiki to infer the ladder from scattered branch pages.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40228684-customized-electrode-positioning-personalized-tes-target-engagement.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40275098-low-intensity-transcranial-focused-ultrasound-amygdala-neuromodulation.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40933821-cognitive-training-gain-transfer-german-agegain-study.md]^[raw/articles/pmc-10782634-cognitive-training-and-brain-stimulation-in-patients-with-cognitive-impairment.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39711191-mbsr-mediators-cognitive-improvement-breast-cancer-survivors.md]

| Cerebrovascular reserve interventions | Yes; reserve can be measured and modified by exercise and nitrate protocols | Yes; reactivity, resistance, and flow-related markers move | Yes; acute exercise and beetroot improve performance on some tasks | Limited; durable transfer is not established | Limited | Best read as rescue / state support, not durable enhancement |

## Branch Notes

- Glymphatic-targeting interventions now have the clearest mediator story in the sleep/stimulation space because the biomarker move and the cognition move are explicitly linked in LF-rTMS and APOE-stratified TBS work, and a human sleep-vs-deprivation crossover plus a parenchymal-resistance device make the pathway measurable even outside intervention trials; the branch still looks enriched by insomnia, MCI, or genetic state rather than universally portable.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40121525-lf-rtms-glymphatic-cognition.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40746364-glymphatic-system-as-therapeutic-target-tms-induced-modulation-older-adults.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41593094-glymphatic-system-clears-amyloid-beta-and-tau-from-brain-to-plasma-in-humans.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40425804-brain-parenchymal-resistance-glymphatic-human-device.md]
- Cerebrovascular reserve is a better state-rescue branch than a durable enhancement branch. Acute exercise has the cleanest broad cognition signal, and beetroot can move immediate and delayed recall, but the mechanism literature still does not prove that reserve gains themselves are the causal engine for long-horizon transfer.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39883421-acute-exercise-meta-review.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-34018848-cardiorespiratory-fitness-exercise-training-cerebrovascular.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-37875637-beetroot-chewable-cognitive-performance.md]
- tPBM is unusually strong for a low-burden device branch because it already has the sequence exposure -> biomarker -> cognition -> follow-up, and the latest MEG/EEG and combined TMS+tPBM papers extend the mechanistic chain into network reorganization and stacked-neuromodulation transfer, even though the studies are still small and the home/device details matter.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39423445-transcranial-photobiomodulation-cognition-bdnf.md]^[raw/articles/medrxiv-25333989-tpbm-mci-cognitive-metabolic-neuroimaging-pilot.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41584102-transcranial-1064nm-laser-photobiomodulation.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40254536-combined-tms-tpbm-mci-working-memory.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40939031-tpbm-mci-ad-sham-controlled.md]
- Metabolic substrate work is still mostly state-rescue rather than baseline enhancement, but LEAN shows that even a simple infusion can move both cognition and AD fluid biomarkers if the phenotype and timing are right.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41376120-lean-lactate-infusion-cognition-biomarkers.md]
- taVNS sits one layer below that. It has plausible autonomic mediation and some cognition-adjacent findings, but sensory leakage and stress effects make it hard to tell when the intervention is genuinely improving cognition versus simply changing arousal state.^[raw/articles/pubmed-34043386-taVNS-healthy-cognition-meta-analysis.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39815765-taVNS-memory-pupil-dilation.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40958978-taVNS-stress-regulation-eeg-questionnaire.md]
- Intranasal insulin is the clearest example of delivery being part of pharmacology. PET-confirmed uptake means the brain is being reached, but the branch still needs a cleaner line from uptake to durable cognition before it can leave the rescue / phenotype-sensitive lane.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40703476-intranasal-insulin-pet-aging-mci.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40392946-intranasal-insulin-t2d-rsfc.md]
- TMS/TBS is the best-developed mediation chain in this audit because the same branch now has target engagement, biomarker movement, cognition, and follow-up improvement. The glymphatic studies are important because they show one plausible mechanism for delayed retention, not just same-day score changes.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40121525-lf-rtms-glymphatic-cognition.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40746364-glymphatic-system-as-therapeutic-target-tms-induced-modulation-older-adults.md]
- Temporal interference is valuable because it shows the difference between target engagement and behavior. The field can now modulate hippocampal and orbitofrontal signals, but the effect can flip sign with frequency and location, which is exactly why target-validation is not yet efficacy.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41040248-temporal-interference-memory-encoding.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39059712-temporal-interference-orbitofrontal-bold.md]
- FUS-BBBO is the cleanest example of a branch that should not be overread as cognitive enhancement. It is a delivery and safety platform, and the current human literature does not yet justify a cognition-positive label, even though preclinical astrocyte-response work has improved the mechanistic story.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40406679-focused-ultrasound-bbbo-ad-meta-analysis.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40948563-focused-ultrasound-bbbo-astrocytes.md]

## Evidence Tiering

| Tier | What it means in this audit |
|---|---|
| Target validation | Exposure can be delivered to the intended tissue or circuit, but no behavioral claim is yet justified |
| Biomarker mediation | A mechanistic readout moves in the expected direction after exposure |
| Same-day cognition | A behavioral effect appears in the same session or short testing window |
| Delayed retention | A benefit persists after a real gap, not just the immediate post-session window |
| Functional transfer | The signal generalizes into a broader real-world or untrained performance domain |

## Open Contradictions

- Biomarker movement is much easier to prove than meaningful cognition transfer, and some branches may never reach the latter even when the former is real.
- A branch can be both effective and narrow. taVNS and intranasal insulin both move state-linked outcomes, but that does not automatically make them broad enhancers.
- A branch can be target-valid without being efficacy-valid. Temporal interference and FUS-BBBO both live in that gap today.
- Delayed retention is the hardest hurdle for device and state-regulation branches, which is why follow-up windows matter more than immediate effect sizes.
- The main interpretive risk is treating pathway plausibility as if it were proof of durable cognitive improvement.
