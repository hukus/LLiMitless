# Peptide Safety and Sourcing Risk Map

This map separates three questions that often get blurred together:

1. Has the peptide been given to humans at all?
2. Does FDA currently treat the peptide as a low-data or higher-risk bulk compound?
3. Is the sourcing chain plausibly human-grade, or just vendor branding?

The key present-day takeaway is simple: a few peptides in this cluster have real human exposure, but most remain investigational bulk substances with thin safety packages and unresolved quality/impurity concerns. FDA's current bulk-risk page, current as of 2026-04-22, explicitly names BPC-157, MOTS-c, Semax heptapeptide, Selank acetate, Dihexa acetate, and the thymosin beta-4 fragment LKKTETQ/TB-500, which is the strongest current regulatory signal this branch has. Epitalon sits in a separate regulatory bucket: FDA's July 23-24, 2026 PCAC agenda keeps Epitalon-related bulk substances on the policy track for insomnia, a Jan 2026 FDA warning letter uses Epithalon as an example of compounded-product labeling failures, a 2015 forensic paper found Epitalon in illegal pharmaceutical preparations, and WADA's surveillance PDF separately lists EPITALON (EPITHALON) as not approved for human use with anti-aging / injury-healing black-market uses. Pinealon did not surface with a comparable current FDA or WADA listing in this sweep, so its regulatory read is still literature-based rather than regulator-based, even though its anecdote lane now includes TBI recovery, sleep, and alertness reports.^[raw/articles/fda-bulk-drug-substances-peptide-risk-2026.md]^[raw/articles/fda-2026-advisory-epitalon-semax.md]^[raw/articles/fda-compounder-supplier-quality-warning-2026.md]^[raw/articles/fda-warning-letter-genogenix-epithalon-2026.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-25535022-epitalon-illegal-pharmaceutical-preparations.md]^[raw/articles/wada-internet-surveillance-epitalon-2026.md]

## Compound By Compound

- [[bpc-157]]: FDA's current bulk-substance page says compounded BPC-157 may pose immunogenicity and peptide-impurity/API-characterization risks and that FDA has only limited safety-related information. - [[semax]]: older human studies exist in optic-nerve disease, cerebrovascular insufficiency, and posthypoxic recovery, but FDA still classifies compounded semax as a bulk peptide with limited safety-related information and impurity/immunogenicity concerns.^[raw/articles/pubmed-10741256-semax-optic-nerve-disease.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-15792140-semax-cerebrovascular-insufficiency.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-10199046-semax-posthypoxic-encephalopathy.md]^[raw/articles/fda-bulk-drug-substances-peptide-risk-2026.md]
- [[selank]]: the human anchor is an older randomized study in generalized anxiety disorder and neurasthenia, which is real exposure but not a modern safety dossier. FDA still treats selank acetate as a low-data bulk substance with immunogenicity and impurity concerns.^[raw/articles/pubmed-18454096-selank-gad-neurasthenia.md]^[raw/articles/fda-bulk-drug-substances-peptide-risk-2026.md]
- [[cortexin]]: older regional human exposure exists in brain-ischemia, chronic-cerebral-ischemia, and alcohol-encephalopathy studies, but the dossier is still mostly regional and injectable. - [[pinealon]]: older regional human exposure exists in a 110-person comparative geroprotective study, and review-level sources keep repeating memory/TBI claims, but the safety dossier is still thin and not standardized. ## Sourcing Read

The sourcing warning is not subtle: FDA says bulk drug-substance quality depends on the supplier and the component testing behind the supply chain, so a vendor page that says “research use only” or “not for human consumption” does not establish human-grade quality or route safety. WADA's prohibited list adds a separate sports-regulatory problem for MOTS-c and thymosin-beta-4 derivatives.^[raw/articles/fda-compounder-supplier-quality-warning-2026.md]^[raw/articles/wada-prohibited-list-peptide-entries-2026.md]

That matters for the peptide market because the main risk is not only pharmacology. It is also identity, purity, aggregation, peptide-related impurities, and whether the material you are holding is actually what the label says it is.^[raw/articles/fda-bulk-drug-substances-peptide-risk-2026.md]^[raw/articles/fda-compounder-supplier-quality-warning-2026.md]

The new [[peptide-stack-topology-ledger]] adds the adjacent attribution problem: fixed blends, route improvisation, uncertain washouts, and vendor-linked protocol posts can make a safety signal visible while still making molecule-specific benefit impossible to assign. The same rule applies to MOTS-c energy/fat-loss threads, where diet, exercise, GLP-1/GIP/glucagon drugs, SS-31 sequencing, and source quality dominate attribution, and to TB-500/BPC-157 recovery threads, where physical therapy, natural injury history, product identity, and fragment-versus-full-peptide confusion dominate interpretation.^[raw/articles/reddit-2025-12-13-have-anyone-tried-mots-c-thread.md]^[raw/articles/reddit-2026-04-25-bpc157-tb500-meniscus-mri-thread.md]^[raw/articles/reddit-2026-01-12-bpc157-tb500-three-months-mixed-thread.md]

## Practical Verdict

- If a peptide has no human exposure plus an FDA bulk-risk listing, treat it as a research compound, not a self-experiment candidate.
- If a peptide has tiny human exposure, treat that as a safety foothold only, not as permission to assume class safety.
- If a peptide is sold through an opaque research-chemical chain, sourcing risk can dominate the clinical question.
