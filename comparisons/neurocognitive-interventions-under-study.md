# Neurocognitive Interventions Under Study

This comparison groups three “interesting but not settled” intervention families for cognition: [[transcranial-photobiomodulation]], [[focused-ultrasound-neuromodulation]], and [[peptide-neuroprotection]].

Focused ultrasound has a second branch that is not neuromodulation: BBB opening for drug delivery. Keep that branch separate from stimulation claims; see [[focused-ultrasound-bbb-opening-and-cognition-2026-scan]] for the AD feasibility and meta-analysis track.

Relevant raw PubMed notes for the peptide bucket: `raw/articles/pubmed-41460391-cerebrolysin-age-induced-memory-decline.md`, `raw/articles/pubmed-32342318-selank-semax-functional-connectomics.md`, and `raw/articles/pubmed-40816274-thymosin-beta4-ad-candidate.md`.

| Modality | Mechanistic appeal | Current evidence shape | Main limitation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Transcranial photobiomodulation | Non-invasive light-based neuromodulation; measurable cortical effects in early studies | Early clinical and lab signals, especially in post-stroke and healthy-adult contexts | Dosing, skull attenuation, and trial size remain open questions |
| Focused ultrasound neuromodulation | Spatially targeted acoustic stimulation | Promising across several domains, but cognition-specific evidence is still early | Clinical translation and safety/effect-size certainty are incomplete |
| Focused ultrasound BBB opening | Pressure-controlled delivery platform for getting drugs or biologics across the BBB | Small AD feasibility cohorts and pooled reviews suggest reversible opening without clear cognitive worsening | Platform safety, target specificity, and disease-modifying efficacy remain unresolved |
| Peptide neuroprotection | Biologically active peptides may support plasticity or recovery | Strongest in preclinical work, case reports, and mixed translational literature | Healthy cognitive enhancement remains weakly proven |

The common pattern is that each modality has a plausible mechanism and some early wins, but none yet has the kind of large, decisive evidence base that would make it a standard cognitive-enhancement tool. The presence of anecdotal enthusiasm should therefore be treated as a search signal, not a verdict.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41836667-neuromodulation-post-stroke-cognitive-impairment.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41152970-transcranial-focused-ultrasound-neuromodulation.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41460391-cerebrolysin-age-induced-memory-decline.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41584102-transcranial-1064nm-laser-photobiomodulation.md] The focused-ultrasound branch should also stay split in two: neuromodulation versus BBB opening as a delivery platform, because the evidence and claims are not interchangeable.
