# Neurocognitive Interventions Under Study

This comparison groups three "interesting but not settled" intervention families for cognition: [[transcranial-photobiomodulation]], [[focused-ultrasound-neuromodulation]], and [[peptide-neuroprotection]].

June 2026 routing update: the peptide bucket is no longer a single undifferentiated frontier. The strongest current peptide/nootropic anecdote nodes are [[cerebrolysin]], [[semax]], [[selank]], recovery-bound [[bpc-157]], and adjacent [[bromantane]], which is tracked as a non-peptide activation comparator rather than a peptide mechanism. De-ranked but still important frontier labels include [[dihexa]], [[pe-22-28]], [[adamax]], [[epithalon]], and [[pinealon]]. Focused ultrasound has a second branch that is not neuromodulation: BBB opening for drug delivery. Keep that branch separate from stimulation claims; see [[focused-ultrasound-bbb-opening-and-cognition-2026-scan]] for the AD feasibility and meta-analysis track.

Relevant raw PubMed notes for the peptide bucket: `raw/articles/pubmed-41460391-cerebrolysin-age-induced-memory-decline.md`, `raw/articles/pubmed-32342318-selank-semax-functional-connectomics.md`, and `raw/articles/pubmed-40816274-thymosin-beta4-ad-candidate.md`.

| Modality | Mechanistic appeal | Current evidence shape | Main limitation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Transcranial photobiomodulation | Non-invasive light-based neuromodulation; measurable cortical effects in early studies | Early clinical and lab signals, especially in post-stroke and healthy-adult contexts | Dosing, skull attenuation, and trial size remain open questions |
| Focused ultrasound neuromodulation | Spatially targeted acoustic stimulation | Rapid formal trial growth, including healthy-volunteer and cognitive studies, but cognition-specific efficacy is still early | Device reporting is poor in registries, and recent low-frequency adverse-event debate keeps acoustic parameterization central |
| Focused ultrasound BBB opening | Pressure-controlled delivery platform for getting drugs or biologics across the BBB | Small AD feasibility cohorts and pooled reviews suggest reversible opening without clear cognitive worsening | Platform safety, target specificity, and disease-modifying efficacy remain unresolved |
| Peptide/nootropic strongest monitoring cluster | Biologically active peptides may support plasticity, stress-state restoration, or recovery; adjacent activation comparators help separate true peptide effects from stimulant or fatigue-rescue signals | [[cerebrolysin]] has the strongest formal-relative peptide packet; [[semax]]/[[selank]] have older regional anchors plus strong survey/forum salience; [[bpc-157]] is recovery-salient but cognition-weak; [[bromantane]] is a non-peptide fatigue/work-capacity comparator with sports visibility | Healthy cognitive enhancement remains weakly proven; source/COA or medical-product status, route/formulation, stack order, washout, adverse events, regulatory/sports status, objective endpoints, and phenotype attribution dominate |
| Peptide frontier, de-ranked labels | Synaptogenic, pineal-bioregulator, or vendor-defined peptide stories remain hypothesis-generating | [[dihexa]], [[pe-22-28]], [[adamax]], [[epithalon]], [[pinealon]], [[cortexin]], [[kortagen]], [[thymosin-beta-4]], [[mots-c]], and mitochondrial peptides are searchable nodes with uneven evidence | Most lack modern human cognition trials, clean product identity, source/route controls, or direct nootropic endpoints |

The common pattern is that each modality has a plausible mechanism and some early wins, but none yet has the kind of large, decisive evidence base that would make it a standard cognitive-enhancement tool. The presence of anecdotal enthusiasm should therefore be treated as a search signal, not a verdict.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41836667-neuromodulation-post-stroke-cognitive-impairment.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41152970-transcranial-focused-ultrasound-neuromodulation.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41460391-cerebrolysin-age-induced-memory-decline.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41584102-transcranial-1064nm-laser-photobiomodulation.md] The focused-ultrasound branch should also stay split in two: neuromodulation versus BBB opening as a delivery platform, because the evidence and claims are not interchangeable. The 2026 registry and safety sources strengthen that split: tFUS is now a real clinical-trial field, but weak device reporting and low-frequency adverse-event concerns mean exposure details are part of the evidence, not optional methods text.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41856430-transcranial-ultrasound-neuromodulation-registry-analysis.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41916485-low-frequency-ultrasound-sae-response.md]
