# Supplement Identity Evidence Map

This page is a transportability gate, not an efficacy ranking.

The question is whether a cognition result can travel beyond a single paper. That depends on whether the tested item was a named molecule, a standardized extract, a matrix-specific formulation, or a loose ingredient label. A clean outcome on an unclean product is still a weak basis for a broad recommendation.

## What The Gate Checks

- Active unit: the molecule, extract, or matrix actually dosed.
- Source part: mushroom biomass, yeast extract, seed extract, dairy matrix, or pure compound.
- Marker control: assay percentage, named nucleotide mix, phospholipid dose, or similar anchor.
- Product identity: whether the trial named a reproducible commercial product or only a generic class.
- QA / COA signal: solvent-free processing, trademarked blend, clean-room production, GMP claim, or third-party testing.
- Contaminant / variability risk: substrate drift, adulteration, allergen load, matrix variability, or source-species variability.
- Commercial availability: whether the tested exposure exists outside the paper.

## Commercial Cross-Check

- [Hericium-MRL](https://www.mycologyresearch.com/products/hericium) shows that standardized whole-mushroom Hericium biomass exists commercially, but that does not make it interchangeable with erinacine-enriched products or the 96% dry-powder trial tablet.
- [Ribodiet](https://www.prosol.it/ribodiet/) and [RiboCARE](https://www.prosol.it/ribocare/) show a commercial nucleotide ingredient family with explicit assay and profile language.
- [NOW Foods 5-HTP](https://www.nowfoods.com/products/supplements/5-htp-100-mg-veg-capsule) shows a commercial Griffonia-seed 5-HTP product with named source extract.
- [California Gold Nutrition Ergothioneine](https://www.californiagoldnutrition.com/products/california-gold-nutrition-ergothioneine-25-mg-90-veggie-capsules-143936) shows that pure ergothioneine is a commercially sold supplement ingredient.
- [NZMP Milk Phospholipids](https://www.nzmp.com/global/en/products/ingredients/types/specialty/complex-lipids/milk-phospholipids.html) shows a commercial MFGM ingredient line with named phospholipid variants.
- [ClinicalTrials.gov NCT04484454](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04484454) shows a plasmalogen-adjacent omega-3 oil precursor study, but that is not the same as the scallop-derived purified plasmalogen used in the RCT.
- [FDA GRN 000635](https://www.fda.gov/food/gras-notice-inventory/agency-response-letter-gras-notice-no-grn-000635) and [Tru Niagen 300 mg](https://www.truniagen.com/products/tru-niagen-300mg) show a clearer NR chloride / Niagen product identity than generic NAD+ booster labels.
- [FDA's 2025 NMN citizen-petition response](https://downloads.regulations.gov/FDA-2023-P-0872-2754/attachment_1.pdf) clears the dietary-supplement-definition issue under FDA's revised race-to-market interpretation, but it is not an efficacy or safety approval.
- [FDA GRN 000791](https://www.hfpappexternal.fda.gov/scripts/fdcc/index.cfm?id=791&set=grasnotices) and [Mitopure](https://www.mitopure.com/) show a clearer branded urolithin A identity than generic pomegranate extract or marketplace urolithin labels.

## What The Map Says

- The cleanest transportable identities here are the pure molecule [[ergothioneine]], the pure molecule [[5-htp-tryptophan|5-HTP]], and the trademarked [[dietary-nucleotides|dietary nucleotide]] family, with 5-HTP carrying a stronger interaction and QA burden than its clean molecule identity suggests.
- `Hericium erinaceus` is the least transportable because species, substrate, and processing change the active chemistry.
- [[mfgm-phospholipids]] are transportable only at the matrix level; the tested milk formulation matters, and the current efficacy readout is mostly null.
- [[soy-isoflavones]] need an aglycone-equivalent dose and product-form label before trial evidence can travel.
- [[spermidine]] is a dose-label trap: extract mass, active spermidine mass, food matrix, and pure-molecule exposure are different units.
- [[nicotinamide-riboside]] has comparatively clean Niagen/NR chloride identity, but the efficacy result is still biomarker-positive and cognition-null.
- [[nicotinamide-mononucleotide]] is the opposite kind of NAD+ precursor trap: the clinical molecule is understandable, but consumer products can fail label claims badly enough that the assumed exposure may not exist.
- [[urolithin-a]] is transportable only when the exposure is direct assayed urolithin A; pomegranate precursors and cheap generic labels are separate hypotheses.
- [[plasmalogens]] are a transportability trap because the purified scallop RCTs, DHA-AAG precursor study, and registry product use different source materials and likely different active units.
- Commercial availability does not equal reproducibility. It only clears one part of the gate.
