# Standardized Herbal Extracts for Cognition

## Verdict

If the wiki wants a practical traditional-medicine branch, the right unit is not "herbs" in general. It is standardized extracts with named chemistry, fixed dose, and human cognition endpoints. On that definition, the strongest recent healthy-adult signal is standardized grape extract, ginkgo `EGb 761` now has the strongest impairment-phenotype case among the standardized botanicals, ginseng now has both a sprout-extract RCT and a 2026 review package that keeps the branch modest but real, and Aronia melanocarpa extract now has two product-specific cognition RCTs. [[gastrodia-elata]] adds a recent gastrodin-confirmed Tianma extract RCT in middle-aged adults, while [[cistanche-tubulosa]] adds a healthy-adult memory signal only through a Cistanche-plus-Ginkgo product and should not be collapsed into generic Rou Cong Rong claims. [[sailuotong]] now adds the cleanest standardized formula-level signal, [[kai-xin-san]] adds a preclinical/formula-family traditional-memory formula branch, and [[yokukansan]] acts as a cognition-neutral behavioral-symptom formula comparator. [[shankhpushpi]] fills a tradition-salient Ayurvedic memory-tonic gap that remains open-label/preclinical rather than controlled evidence. Ashwagandha has a small but interesting MCI pilot, while [[bacopa]] is now explicitly contested by a 2025 Bacumen trial that missed primary verbal-learning, attention, and working-memory outcomes but kept a stress/fatigue secondary signal. The Lamiaceae branch now has route-normalized additions: [[spearmint-extract]] is the cleanest new oral-extract attention/working-memory candidate, [[holy-basil]] is stress-state plus a small healthy-volunteer cognition signal, and [[peppermint-essential-oil]] / [[rosemary-and-1-8-cineole]] are acute route-specific candidates rather than general herbal extracts. [[pomegranate]], [[blueberry-anthocyanins]], [[grape-food-products]], [[tart-cherry]], [[sage-salvia-extract]], [[guarana]], and [[wild-green-oat-extract]] remain low-confidence product-specific entities rather than broad botanical recommendations. Separately, the phlai essential-oil blend has enough human data to deserve a low-confidence node, but it sits outside the oral standardized-extract ranking because the route is inhaled aromatherapy rather than ingestion. Broader Chinese herbal medicine meta-analyses and non-drug traditional treatments should route through [[fixed-traditional-formula-cognition-watchlist]] and ## New Standalone-Page Candidates

- `[[aronia-melanocarpa-extract]]`: two product-specific cognition RCTs now exist, and both point to a narrow psychomotor-speed / reaction-time signal. The product is standardized enough to track as its own entity, even though the branch is still low-confidence.
- `[[phlai-essential-oil-blend]]`: two human trials now exist, with GC-MS characterization and acute mood/cognition effects, so the exposure is specific enough for its own node even though it lives in an inhaled/aromatherapy lane.
- `[[pomegranate]]`: the cognition literature now has a small juice/fMRI study, a 12-month juice-memory stabilization trial, and a newer extract trial with corrected punicalagin content. It now clears a low-confidence standalone threshold, but remains product-identity constrained.
- `[[blueberry-anthocyanins]]`: broad anthocyanin meta-analysis plus multiple small blueberry RCTs now justify a low-confidence standalone entity, but product identity remains the limiting factor.
- `[[sage-salvia-extract]]`: multiple small Salvia officinalis / Salvia lavandulaefolia RCTs now justify a low-confidence standalone entity, but species, essential-oil/terpenoid fraction, Cognivia, Sibelius Sage, blinding, and thujone exposure remain limiting factors.
- `[[guarana]]`: guarana-specific meta-analysis, older acute RCTs, combination-product studies, and a 2024 caffeine-comparator null now justify a low-confidence standalone entity, but caffeine content, label accuracy, energy-drink stacking, and product form remain limiting factors.
- `[[wild-green-oat-extract]]`: acute Neuravena and Cognitaven trials plus a chronic older-adult null now justify a low-confidence standalone entity, but acute-vs-chronic timing, product identity, electrophysiology-to-behavior transfer, and oatstraw/milky-oat confusion remain limiting factors.
- `[[tart-cherry]]`: Montmorency older-adult juice and middle-aged concentrate RCTs now justify a low-confidence standalone entity, but sleep/recovery confounding, sugar load, dose timing, capsule null data, and whole-fruit versus concentrate transportability remain limiting factors.
- `[[grape-food-products]]`: raisin and Concord grape-juice trials now justify a low-confidence food-matrix entity, but they must stay separate from standardized [[grape-extract]], grape seed, Memophenol-style blends, resveratrol, wine, and whole-grape food claims.
- `[[gastrodia-elata]]`: a 2025 middle-aged-adult RCT now justifies a low-confidence Tianma / Gastrodiae Rhizoma entity, but the branch still needs independent replication and product-marker discipline.
- `[[cistanche-tubulosa]]`: a 2026 healthy-adult Cistanche-plus-Ginkgo RCT plus mixed anecdotes justify a low-confidence entity, but Cistanche-alone claims remain unproven.
- `[[sailuotong]]`: a standardized ginseng/Ginkgo/saffron-constituent formula with one healthy-adult pilot and one MCI phase 2 pilot now deserves a formula-level entity.
- `[[kai-xin-san]]`: the classic KXS memory-formula branch now deserves a low-confidence entity because it has substantial preclinical AD synthesis plus formula-family human adjunct context, but it remains below human-RCT standardized extracts.
- `[[yokukansan]]`: the Kampo / Yi-Gan San branch deserves a low-confidence entity as a behavioral-symptom formula and cognition-neutral comparator, with explicit licorice/hypokalemia safety routing.
- `[[shankhpushpi]]`: an Ayurvedic memory-tonic label now deserves a low-confidence entity because tradition salience and preclinical support are high enough to track, but it remains outside the standardized-extract ranking until controlled human trials exist.
- `[[spearmint-extract]]`: a proprietary high-polyphenol Mentha spicata extract now deserves a low-confidence entity because it has AAMI working-memory, healthy-adult sustained-attention, and reactive-agility trials.
- `[[holy-basil]]`: Tulsi now deserves a low-confidence entity because it has one small healthy-volunteer cognitive-task trial plus a standardized Holixer stress/sleep trial.
- `[[peppermint-essential-oil]]` and `[[rosemary-and-1-8-cineole]]`: these clear standalone thresholds as route-specific aromatic / essential-oil / hydrolat candidates, not as ordinary oral herbal extracts.
- `[[lamiaceae-aromatic-cognition-watchlist]]`: the mint-family aromatic branch now has its own route-normalization page so sage, lemon balm, phlai, spearmint, peppermint, rosemary, and holy basil are not collapsed together.
- `Grape polyphenol products`: the branch now has split coverage across [[grape-extract]], [[grape-food-products]], and grape-blueberry extract anchors, but those products are not interchangeable enough to merge.
- `[[plant-active-substance-product-watchlist]]`: the current tart-cherry and raisin/grape-food product gaps have graduated to low-confidence standalone pages; the page remains a product-normalization gate.

## Why This Is the Right Cut

Traditional-medicine cognition research is easy to overstate because formula names are often treated as though they were fixed molecules. The current evidence set argues for a stricter filter:

- fixed product identity,
- known dose,
- actual cognitive endpoint,
- replicated human trial signal,
- and tolerable safety.

Once that filter is applied, the field becomes much smaller and more useful. The new branch mostly strengthens the case for product-specific scans instead of broad "herbal nootropic" claims.

## Standalone Page Gate

- Already justified as standalone pages: `ginkgo`, `ginseng`, `curcumin`, and now `[[aronia-melanocarpa-extract]]`.
- Best un-split candidates if more replication arrives: `grape extract` and standardized `ashwagandha`.
- Newly standalone but still low-confidence: [[pomegranate]], because juice, extract, punicalagin content, sugar load, and safety context are still not interchangeable.
- Newly standalone but still low-confidence: [[blueberry-anthocyanins]], because juice, powder, whole-food berries, mixed grape-blueberry extract, and isolated anthocyanin capsules are still not interchangeable.
- Newly standalone but still low-confidence: [[grape-food-products]], because raisins, Concord grape juice, whole grapes, grape seed, standardized [[grape-extract]], mixed grape-blueberry extract, resveratrol, pterostilbene, and wine are not interchangeable.
- Newly standalone but still low-confidence: [[sage-salvia-extract]], because common sage, Spanish sage, essential oil, Cognivia, Sibelius Sage, thujone exposure, and Salvia divinorum are not interchangeable.
- Newly standalone but still low-confidence: [[guarana]], because seed powder, extract, caffeine-standardized extract, energy drinks, multivitamin products, and pure caffeine controls are not interchangeable.
- Newly standalone but still low-confidence: [[wild-green-oat-extract]], because Neuravena, Cognitaven, oat straw tea, milky oat tinctures, oat seed extracts, and ordinary oats are not interchangeable.
- Newly standalone but still low-confidence: [[tart-cherry]], because Montmorency juice, concentrate, powder capsules, gummies, whole tart cherries, sweet cherries, anthocyanin extracts, and sleep/recovery claims are not interchangeable.
- Newly standalone but still low-confidence: [[gastrodia-elata]], because the current cognition signal is one recent product-specific RCT and direct anecdotes are sparse.
- Newly standalone but still low-confidence: [[cistanche-tubulosa]], because the controlled cognition signal is combination-product evidence with [[ginkgo]] and the anecdote layer is mixed.
- Newly standalone but still low-confidence: [[sailuotong]], because its signal is formula-level and still small even though the product identity is cleaner than most CHM formulas.
- Newly standalone but still low-confidence: [[kai-xin-san]], because exact human cognition evidence is still weak and Acorus/beta-asarone product safety matters.
- Newly standalone but still low-confidence: [[yokukansan]], because the human signal is behavioral-symptom oriented and MMSE-neutral rather than cognition-positive.
- Newly standalone but still low-confidence: [[shankhpushpi]], because the human evidence is open-label or multi-herb and plant identity varies across products.
- Newly standalone but still low-confidence: [[spearmint-extract]], because Neumentix-style extract has multiple controlled studies but ordinary spearmint products are not equivalent.
- Newly standalone but still low-confidence: [[holy-basil]], because the direct cognition trial is small and the stronger modern data are stress/sleep-state rather than direct cognition.
- Newly standalone but route-specific: [[peppermint-essential-oil]] and [[rosemary-and-1-8-cineole]], because essential oil, aroma, powder, and hydrolat exposures cannot be merged.
- Better kept in the comparison layer for now: `bacopa`, `saffron`, `Acacia + Scutellaria`, and formula-level Chinese herbal medicine. `[[phlai-essential-oil-blend]]` is close enough to standalone that it can sit beside the comparison layer, but its inhaled route keeps it separate from the oral extract ranking.

## Adjacent Formula-Level Evidence

Chinese herbal medicine is not a single standardized extract, so it does not belong inside the main ranking above. It still matters because the 2024 MCI meta-analysis and the 2024 donepezil-adjunct meta-analysis both found MMSE/MoCA improvement with tolerable AE profiles, which makes it a separate but real traditional-medicine lane. [[sailuotong]] is the current best example of a more interpretable CHM formula because the captured trials specify constituent-standardized capsules and cognition endpoints. [[kai-xin-san]] is a preclinical/formula-family research lead, while [[yokukansan]] is a behavioral-symptom comparator whose MMSE signal is neutral. Use [[fixed-traditional-formula-cognition-watchlist]] for this layer rather than treating formula evidence as proof for isolated [[ginkgo]], [[ginseng]], saffron, Polygala, Acorus, or licorice.^[raw/articles/pubmed-38425647-chinese-herbal-medicine-mci-meta-analysis.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39134455-chinese-herbal-medicine-donepezil-mci-meta-analysis.md]^[raw/articles/bmc-2016-sailuotong-healthy-adults-crossover-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-2023-sailuotong-mci-pilot-rct.md]^[raw/articles/frontiers-2026-kai-xin-san-ad-preclinical-meta.md]^[raw/articles/sage-2016-yokukansan-bpsd-updated-meta-analysis.md]

## Open Questions

- Does standardized bacopa become cleaner once extraction and bioavailability are locked down?
- Does standardized ginseng remain modest but reproducible once the phenotype and product are fixed?
- Does ginkgo retain its advantage outside impaired phenotypes, or is the signal mostly phenotype-limited?
- Is the grape extract result robust enough to survive independent replication?
- Are the ashwagandha and bacopa positives real class effects or single-study artifacts?
- Does phlai keep its cognitive advantage once odor/sensory confounding is stressed harder?
- Does Gastrodia replicate outside one middle-aged-adult extract trial?
- Does any Cistanche-alone product improve cognition, or is the current signal mostly a Cistanche-plus-Ginkgo effect?
- Does Sailuotong replicate in a larger, independent trial, and can its effects be separated from constituent additivity?
- Does Kai-Xin-San ever produce a placebo-controlled human cognition signal once Acorus chemistry and dose are standardized?
- Is Yokukansan useful only for behavioral/sleep-state modulation, or can any defined subgroup show objective cognition change?
- Can Shankhpushpi be normalized to one verified plant identity and tested in a placebo-controlled cognition trial?
- Does proprietary spearmint extract replicate outside sponsor-linked or product-linked contexts, and does attention transfer beyond task scores?
- Does holy basil's stress/sleep signal translate into objective cognition in stressed but otherwise healthy adults?
- Can rosemary and peppermint aromatic/essential-oil signals be replicated under stronger sensory and route controls?
- Do curcumin-like products need better formulation and bioavailability before the effect becomes interpretable?
- Can formula-level Chinese herbal medicine be standardized enough to compete with single-extract candidates, or does the heterogeneity always swamp reproducibility?
