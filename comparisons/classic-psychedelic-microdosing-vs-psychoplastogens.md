# Classic Psychedelic Microdosing vs Psychoplastogens

This comparison is meant to stop a common category error: classic psychedelic microdosing is not evidence for non-hallucinogenic psychoplastogens, and non-hallucinogenic psychoplastogens are not validated microdosing substitutes. The former has human data, but the latest controlled evidence is mostly negative for broad cognition; the latter has interesting preclinical plasticity biology, but no human cognition trial signal yet.

For the adjacent concept lane, see [[non-hallucinogenic-psychoplastogens]] and [[non-hallucinogenic-psychoplastogens-2026-scan]]. For the negative-evidence framing, see [[negative-evidence-watchlist-for-cognition-interventions]] and [[anecdote-led-chemical-heat-map]].

## What Changed Since 2024

- The 2026 meta-analysis of classical psychedelic microdosing moved the human cognition story in a clearly negative direction: cognitive control went down, while other cognitive domains and global cognition did not show a detectable benefit.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41314362-effects-of-psychedelic-microdosing-on-cognitive-functions.md]
- The 2026 two-trial psilocybin microdosing report found no significant behavioral or subjective benefit versus placebo, and the small early signals did not survive multiple-comparison correction.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41110634-psilocybin-microdosing-cognitive-subjective-effects.md]
- The 2026 creativity mega-analysis narrowed the signal to a specific originality/fluency measure, with no broad divergent-thinking or convergent-thinking boost.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41187880-psilocybin-microdosing-creativity-trials.md]
- A 2026 Yale registry entry keeps the healthy-microdosing question open as a protocol lead: a not-yet-recruiting, double-blind pilot will test 2.0 mg psilocybin three times weekly for four weeks in 20 healthy psychedelic non-users with cognition, mood, EMA, and MRI endpoints.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct07449351-psilocybin-microdosing-cognition-mood-quality-of-life.md]
- The 2025 low-dose LSD ADHD RCT added a disorder-context null: repeated 20 ug dosing was safe and tolerated, but it was not superior to placebo on the primary symptom outcome.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40105807-repeated-low-dose-lsd-adhd-adults.md]
- The non-hallucinogenic psychoplastogen lane became more mechanistically coherent in rodents, especially for tabernanthalog and 2-Br-LSD, but it still did not cross into human cognition proof.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40760185-tabernanthalog-neuroplasticity-no-iea.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-36884348-2-br-lsd-nonhallucinogenic-lsd-analog.md]
- The biomarker layer did not rescue the translation problem: a 2025 human BDNF meta-analysis found no evidence that psychoplastogens raise peripheral BDNF.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39613915-psychoplastogens-bdnf-meta-analysis.md]
- Regulatory status remains a practical barrier because psilocybin is still treated as a Schedule I hallucinogen in DEA materials.^[raw/articles/dea-psilocybin-hallucinogens-schedule-i.md]
- The anecdote layer is now explicit rather than implied: productivity and creativity reports remain highly visible in Reddit/forum microdosing communities, but the formal evidence still routes the branch as a mismatch case rather than an upgraded cognition signal.^[raw/articles/reddit-2022-2026-psychedelic-microdosing-productivity-threads.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41314362-effects-of-psychedelic-microdosing-on-cognitive-functions.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41110634-psilocybin-microdosing-cognitive-subjective-effects.md]

## Anecdote Signal

Community microdosing salience is `strong`: captured Reddit/forum threads repeatedly claim morning energy, gentle clarity, focus, creativity, flow, reduced anxiety, and better work engagement, with counterreports of no effect, emotionality, worsened focus, dose sensitivity, couch-lock, and intensified depressive symptoms. This is useful for hypothesis generation, but it does not change the controlled-evidence read: the 2026 meta-analysis found lower cognitive control and null broad cognition, while placebo-controlled psilocybin microdosing reports were null or narrow after correction.^[raw/articles/reddit-2022-2026-psychedelic-microdosing-productivity-threads.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41314362-effects-of-psychedelic-microdosing-on-cognitive-functions.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41110634-psilocybin-microdosing-cognitive-subjective-effects.md]

## Evidence Tiering

| Lane | Human evidence | Best-supported signal | What it does not show |
|---|---|---|---|
| Classic psychedelic microdosing | Meta-analysis + placebo-controlled longitudinal trials | A narrow creativity signal and some context-sensitive subjective effects | Broad enhancement of cognition, mood, or general productivity |
| Classic supervised psilocybin, not microdosing | Phase 1 healthy-volunteer safety study | Short- and longer-term cognitive neutrality under supervised conditions | That microdosing is a general nootropic |
| Classic low-dose LSD in ADHD | Phase 2A randomized trial | Safety and tolerability with placebo-equivalent symptom change | That low-dose LSD is an ADHD efficacy signal |
| Non-hallucinogenic psychoplastogens | Mostly preclinical + mechanistic review | Rodent neuroplasticity, stress-rescue, and scaffold plausibility | Human cognition efficacy, human dose-response, or real-world safety |
| Biomarker bridge | Human BDNF meta-analysis | A check on whether plasticity biomarkers move in people | That biomarker movement translates to cognition gains |

## Effect Size Snapshot

- Microdosing meta-analysis: significant decrease in cognitive control, no detectable effect on other domains or overall cognition, and no moderation by substance type, dose, duration, or on- vs off-drug timing.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41314362-effects-of-psychedelic-microdosing-on-cognitive-functions.md]
- Microdosing creativity mega-analysis: originality/fluency ratio and relative-dose-adjusted originality improved, but convergent thinking and most other divergent-thinking outcomes did not.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41187880-psilocybin-microdosing-creativity-trials.md]
- Human BDNF meta-analysis: no evidence of a psychoplastogen-driven peripheral BDNF increase in humans, with SMD 0.024 and p = 0.64.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39613915-psychoplastogens-bdnf-meta-analysis.md]
- Psychoplastogen preclinical effects: TBG and 2-Br-LSD show rodent plasticity / behavior signals, but there is no human effect size to compare against the microdosing literature.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40760185-tabernanthalog-neuroplasticity-no-iea.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-36884348-2-br-lsd-nonhallucinogenic-lsd-analog.md]

## Safety / Adverse Events

- Psilocybin is still treated as a Schedule I hallucinogen in DEA materials, and DEA safety language highlights elevated heart rate, elevated blood pressure, nausea, vomiting, fear, paranoia, and psychosis-like reactions as relevant risks.^[raw/articles/dea-psilocybin-hallucinogens-schedule-i.md]
- The microdosing placebo-controlled trials did not show serious safety signals in the abstracted summaries, but the active arm increased negative bodily feelings.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41110634-psilocybin-microdosing-cognitive-subjective-effects.md]
- The 2022 healthy-participant phase 1 trial found no clinically relevant cognitive harm at 10 mg or 25 mg psilocybin under close supervision, which is reassuring for supervised use but not a license to extrapolate to chronic microdosing.^[raw/articles/pubmed-35090363-psilocybin-cognitive-emotional-functions-healthy-participants.md]
- Non-hallucinogenic psychoplastogens remain safety-uncertain in humans; `non-hallucinogenic` is a pharmacology label, not a completed clinical safety dossier.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40354769-non-hallucinogenic-psychedelics-mood-anxiety-systematic-review.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40405723-neuropsychopharmacology-hallucinogenic-non-hallucinogenic-5ht2a-agonists.md]

## Responder Phenotypes

- Microdosing: no validated responder phenotype emerged in the controlled human literature. Relative dose to body weight mattered in the creativity mega-analysis, but that is not the same as a robust biomarker-defined responder class.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41187880-psilocybin-microdosing-creativity-trials.md]
- Microdosing: the meta-analysis found no moderation by substance, dose, duration, or timing, which weakens the idea that simple schedule tweaks solve the broad enhancement problem.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41314362-effects-of-psychedelic-microdosing-on-cognitive-functions.md]
- Psychoplastogens: preclinical hints suggest stress load, flexibility impairment, and chronic stress exposure may matter, but human responder selection is not yet available.^[raw/articles/pubmed-34035476-tabernanthalog-stress-cognitive-flexibility.md]

## Protocol Parameters

- Microdosing studies in the meta-analysis used approximately 0.1-0.5 g psilocybin mushroom material or 6.5-20 ug LSD over 1-42 days, with both on-drug and off-drug testing included in the analysis.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41314362-effects-of-psychedelic-microdosing-on-cognitive-functions.md]
- The two-trial psilocybin microdosing report used psilocybin truffles in double-blind placebo-controlled longitudinal designs.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41110634-psilocybin-microdosing-cognitive-subjective-effects.md]
- The supervised healthy-participant phase 1 study used a single oral 10 mg or 25 mg psilocybin dose with psychological support and CANTAB-based cognitive follow-up.^[raw/articles/pubmed-35090363-psilocybin-cognitive-emotional-functions-healthy-participants.md]
- TBG and 2-Br-LSD do not yet have validated human cognition protocols in the wiki corpus, so any dose suggestion would be speculative and should not be turned into a consumer protocol.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40760185-tabernanthalog-neuroplasticity-no-iea.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-36884348-2-br-lsd-nonhallucinogenic-lsd-analog.md]
- The Yale microdosing registry lead is still a protocol, not a result: it matters because the field is continuing to test the claim in a controlled design even after the pooled human evidence turned negative.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct07449351-psilocybin-microdosing-cognition-mood-quality-of-life.md]

## Open Contradictions

- The public productivity narrative for microdosing conflicts with the latest controlled cognition meta-analysis.
- A narrow creativity effect does not justify a general cognition or executive-function claim.
- The absence of peripheral BDNF change weakens the simple "more plasticity equals better cognition" shortcut.
- "Non-hallucinogenic" should not be read as "clinically proven safer" or "already enhancement-ready."
- The 5-HT2A field still disagrees on whether low efficacy, biased signaling, or downstream network effects best explain the psychedelic/non-psychedelic split.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40405723-neuropsychopharmacology-hallucinogenic-non-hallucinogenic-5ht2a-agonists.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40545270-5ht2a-efficacy-nonpsychedelic-drugs.md]
