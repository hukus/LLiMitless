# Cognitive Fidelity Split

This page is the intervention-level counterpart to [[endpoint-dictionary-for-cognition-interventions]]. The endpoint dictionary says what the labels mean; this page shows why the labels matter in practice.

The recurring pattern is that many interventions improve one slice of cognition while leaving the others unchanged. Some mostly raise arousal or throughput. Others rescue performance only in a disorder-context or under a challenge state. A smaller set moves delayed retention or transfer, and even those effects are usually narrower than the broad "brain boost" framing people attach to them.

The point is not to dismiss the interventions. It is to keep the wiki from turning a state shift into a universal cognition claim.

## What Changed Since 2024

- The 2025 methylphenidate imaging trial makes the split concrete: a single 60 mg dose shifted frontoparietal brain-state occupancy and speeded one low-demand visuospatial task, but the behavioral benefit was task-limited rather than a broad general enhancement. A separate randomized ADHD washout study also found that treatment-period gains in working memory and response speed did not persist after a week off, which is a direct warning against reading stimulant benefit as durable learning.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40101961-methylphenidate-frontoparietal-state-cognition.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-33735707-methylphenidate-cognitive-performance-washout.md]
- Nicotine now has a clearer state-restoration interpretation in smokers after abstinence, and the healthy-never-smoker study shows the acute signal can sit in reaction time and signal detection rather than in a wide cognitive domain.^[raw/articles/pubmed-38582919-nicotine-cognitive-function-smokers-abstinence.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-31686175-nicotine-galantamine-healthy-never-smokers-cognition.md]
- Nicotine also has a live 2026 dose-response tablet study in occasional pouch users, but the readout is still pending, so it remains a comparator watch item rather than a new efficacy win.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct07408180-nicotine-tablet-attention.md]
- Solriamfetol's SHARP trial shows a clean wakefulness/cognition effect in OSA with excessive daytime sleepiness, but that is still disorder-context evidence, not a healthy-adult enhancement benchmark.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39528111-solriamfetol-sharp-osa-cognition.md]
- Oveporexton gives the orexin-agonist branch its first strong human cognition signal, but the effect sits inside narcolepsy type 1, so the result still belongs to the rescue/state-restoration bucket.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41359331-orexin-agonist-cognition-narcolepsy-secondary-analysis.md]
- Recent tDCS work reinforces phenotype dependence: stress-state rescue can be target-specific, and older adults show larger response variability than younger adults, which is exactly the sort of split that turns a generic "cognition" claim into a much narrower claim.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40292419-tdcs-stress-working-memory-2025.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40633904-tdcs-variability-age-2025.md]
- A 2026 taVNS field experiment under sleep deprivation improved vigilance and mood but did not improve response inhibition, which is a clean reminder that arousal can move while precision stays flat.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41973311-taVNS-military-exercise-sleep-deprivation.md]

## Intervention Split Map

| Intervention class | What it most reliably moves | What it often does not move | How to read the claim |
|---|---|---|---|
| Caffeine | Arousal, vigilance, reaction time, short-horizon throughput | Learning slope, delayed retention, far transfer | Use it as the baseline state-control comparator, not as proof of broad cognition |
| Methylphenidate | Task-limited speed, frontoparietal state, some executive-control or novelty-sensitive performance | Broad transfer, stable learning advantage, universal accuracy gains | A favorable speed readout can coexist with null or mixed higher-order effects |
| Nicotine | Withdrawal relief, attention, reaction time, signal detection | Broad enhancement in non-withdrawn people | Separate abstinence rescue from enhancement in never-smokers |
| Solriamfetol | Wakefulness and cognition in OSA/EDS | Healthy-adult enhancement, durable transfer | Treat as disorder-context rescue unless the phenotype matches the trial |
| Orexin agonists | Wakefulness and, in NT1, attention/memory/executive performance | Healthy-adult generalization | Strong arousal biology does not automatically imply general enhancement |
| tDCS / tACS | State-specific or phenotype-specific rescue, sometimes under stress or load | Universal effect sizes, durable transfer | Protocol and phenotype often matter more than the device label |
| taVNS | Vigilance and mood under sleep loss or stress | Response inhibition, broad rested-state enhancement | Useful as a sleep-loss rescue comparator, not as proof of precision gains |
| Sleep-based interventions | Consolidation, delayed retention, resilience under later load | Immediate arousal boosts | Read them as consolidation/resilience tools, not as stimulant substitutes |

## Reading Rules

1. Name the endpoint bucket before you interpret the result.
2. Do not upgrade arousal or throughput into learning, transfer, or durable enhancement without a separate endpoint.
3. If the participant state changes, state-restoration may be the real mechanism rather than enhancement.
4. If the task is repeated, track practice effects before calling the improvement real.
5. If the effect is phenotype-bound, keep the phenotype in the claim.
6. If the effect is challenge-bound, keep the challenge in the claim.
7. If speed improves but response inhibition does not, keep the effect in throughput or arousal rather than precision.

## Open Questions

- Which interventions can improve precision without just trading speed for errors?
- Which interventions improve transfer after the trained task is stripped away?
- Which interventions survive a delay long enough to count as delayed retention rather than same-session state shift?
- Which apparent enhancer effects disappear once the participant is already well-rested and abstinent from the comparator drug?
