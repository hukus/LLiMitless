# SCFA Postbiotic Brain Fog Cognition Watchlist

This watchlist covers short-chain-fatty-acid and postbiotic-output claims relevant to brain fog and cognition: [[butyrate-tributyrin]], colon-delivered SCFA mixtures, fiber-mediated SCFA increases, and synbiotic products that raise butyrate or urolithin outputs. It is a bridge between [[gut-microbiome-and-cognition]], [[psychobiotic-cognition-product-watchlist]], [[urolithin-a]], and broader inflammation/sleep brain-fog pages. It is not a general healthy-adult enhancement lane.

## Category Verdict

The SCFA branch has a plausible gut-brain mechanism and a real healthy-human stress-axis signal, but no captured direct healthy-adult cognitive-performance win. The most important counterweight is that a fiber intervention increased serum SCFAs without improving measured psychobiological outcomes. The butyrate/tributyrin anecdote layer is explicit but bidirectional, with both clarity reports and adverse fog/anxiety/lethargy reports.^[raw/articles/nature-2020-colon-delivered-scfa-stress-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-35694161-extruded-wheat-bran-scfa-null-rct.md]^[raw/articles/reddit-2024-2026-butyrate-tributyrin-brainfog-threads.md]

## Decision Rules

1. If the claim is healthy-adult focus, memory, or processing speed, keep SCFAs below [[healthy-adult-cognition-signals]] until a direct cognition endpoint appears.
2. If the claim is stress reactivity, use the colon-delivered SCFA trial as the clean anchor, but do not generalize it to supplement capsules.
3. If the claim is gut-inflammation, sleep, or IBD-adjacent brain fog, route through [[butyrate-tributyrin]] and illness-state context rather than nootropic rankings.
4. If the product is a synbiotic or polyphenol-probiotic blend, keep it in [[psychobiotic-cognition-product-watchlist]] and [[supplement-identity-evidence-map]] rather than attributing effects to butyrate alone.
5. If a report mentions dizziness, panic/anxiety, lethargy, diarrhea, or severe fog, record it as a safety/adverse signal before considering mechanism.

## Research Watch

- Randomized butyrate or tributyrin trials with objective cognition, fatigue, sleep, and adverse-event endpoints in healthy adults.
- Trials separating direct sodium butyrate, tributyrin, resistant starch, inulin, and colon-delivered SCFA mixtures.
- Studies that test whether SCFA-mediated cortisol changes translate into attention, working memory, or post-stress cognitive resilience.
- Brain-fog trials in gut-inflammatory, post-infectious, histamine/sulfur-sensitive, or dysbiosis-enriched phenotypes with dechallenge/rechallenge.
