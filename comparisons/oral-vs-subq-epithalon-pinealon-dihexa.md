# Oral vs Subcutaneous Epithalon, Pinealon, and Dihexa

## Bottom Line

The wiki does not have a direct oral-versus-subcutaneous efficacy comparison for [[epithalon]], [[pinealon]], or [[dihexa]]. The evidence is route-fragmented: Epithalon has indirect human Epithalamin physiology plus intranasal animal CNS activity; Pinealon has oral review-level human claims and route-loose anecdotes; Dihexa has the strongest formal oral animal signal, but no human efficacy data and new evidence-integrity caveats around the originating Dihexa literature.^[raw/articles/pubmed-15452611-epithalamin-melatonin-elderly.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-12500171-epitalon-pineal-secretion-stress-rats.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-17955380-epitalon-neocortex-neuron-activity.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-33396470-edr-peptide-mechanism-alzheimers-disease.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-23055539-dihexa-procognitive-antidementia.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-34551989-dihexa-2013-notice-of-concern.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40312093-dihexa-hgf-cmet-retraction.md]

## Interpretation

For efficacy, the most defensible route statement is narrow: Dihexa is the only one in this trio where the formal cognition literature was explicitly designed around oral peripheral delivery. Even there, the evidence stays animal-only, and the 2013 source should now be read with a Notice-of-Concern caveat. Pinealon has oral claims in review-level human/TBI material, but the wiki does not have a modern Pinealon-only cognition RCT. Epithalon's route evidence mostly points away from a cognition claim: intranasal rat CNS activity and human Epithalamin melatonin/longevity physiology do not answer whether oral or subq Epitalon works better.^[raw/articles/pubmed-23055539-dihexa-procognitive-antidementia.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-34827486-dihexa-app-ps1-memory.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-33396470-edr-peptide-mechanism-alzheimers-disease.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-15452611-epithalamin-melatonin-elderly.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-17955380-epitalon-neocortex-neuron-activity.md]

For subcutaneous use, the wiki currently has no controlled efficacy support for these three compounds. FDA's current peptide-compounding warning layer is especially relevant here because Epitalon and Dihexa acetate are listed with unresolved route-specific safety questions or no identified human exposure data. That does not prove harm, but it blocks a casual "subq is stronger" inference from anecdotes or vendor protocol pages.^[raw/articles/fda-bulk-drug-substances-epitalon-2026.md]^[raw/articles/fda-bulk-drug-substances-peptide-risk-2026.md]

## Practical Verdict

No comparative efficacy conclusion is available. If forced into an evidence ranking by route, the wiki's current order is:

1. Oral Dihexa: best formal route-specific animal cognition signal, still no human proof and now integrity-caveated.
2. Oral Pinealon: review-level and anecdotal support for recovery-state cognition, but weak primary human isolation.
3. Subq Epithalon/Pinealon/Dihexa: no controlled route-efficacy evidence captured; mostly anecdote, route improvisation, or non-cognition animal delivery.
4. Oral Epithalon: no meaningful cognition-efficacy support captured; the branch is sleep/circadian/longevity-first.
