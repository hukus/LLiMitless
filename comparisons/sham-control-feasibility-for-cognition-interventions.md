# Sham-Control Feasibility for Cognition Interventions

The key distinction for self-experimentation is not just whether a method has a sham arm in the literature. It is whether a single person can keep the active and control conditions meaningfully hidden while also running the protocol alone. For what the control is actually doing biologically, see [[active-sham-and-inertness-taxonomy]].

That splits the wiki's intervention families into three practical buckets:

1. `Yes` - a single person can randomize and blind the condition with ordinary at-home tools.
2. `Partial` - a single person can self-administer, but sensory leakage or device constraints make the blind weak.
3. `No` - the method needs a clinic, a chamber, or another operator, so a solo blinded trial is not realistic.

The right answer is often about implementation, not mechanism. Oral compounds can be easy to blind but still have withdrawal or side-effect leakage. Device therapies can be sham-controlled in trials and still be poor solo self-experiments.

Sham feasibility is still not the same as ecological robustness. A method can be reasonably blindable and still fail to carry its behavioral effect into home delivery or everyday use; see [[ecological-robustness-table]] for that separate gate.

## What Changed Since 2024

- Home-based tDCS and multiple recent sham-controlled tDCS trials make it clear that a self-run, coded device protocol is realistic for some home settings, even though the sensory blind remains imperfect.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39905569-tdcs-dosage-parameters.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40292419-tdcs-stress-working-memory-2025.md]
- tACS now has sham-controlled cognition trials in both healthy and impairment phenotypes, so it belongs in the same home-feasibility bucket as tDCS while still requiring protocol-level attention to ramp, montage, and stimulus duration.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41034500-tacs-cognitive-function-older-adults.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40933163-theta-tacs-working-memory-healthy-adults.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40283048-40hz-tacs-dementia-cognitive-exercises.md]
- tPBM now has repeated double-blind / sham-controlled human studies, so light-based home use is one of the cleaner device families for solo blinding.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39423445-transcranial-photobiomodulation-cognition-bdnf.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40939031-tpbm-mci-ad-sham-controlled.md]
- temporal interference has active and sham blocks in human protocols, but the equipment and field-modeling burden keep it out of the realistic solo-self-study bucket.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct07339072-temporal-interference-methods-study-1-2.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41167554-human-applications-temporal-interference-stimulation.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40470330-temporal-interference-stimulation-for-human-brain-opportunities-and-challenges.md]
- taVNS has accumulated enough sham-controlled work to be credible, but sensory cues at the ear still make the blind weaker than the phrase "sham-controlled" suggests.^[raw/articles/pubmed-34043386-taVNS-healthy-cognition-meta-analysis.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39815765-taVNS-memory-pupil-dilation.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40958978-taVNS-stress-regulation-eeg-questionnaire.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39124651-taVNS-autonomic-function.md]
- rTMS remains clinic-grade: sham methods exist, but blinding success is still a known weak point, and the current cognition literature is not a good template for solo self-blinding.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40676541-rtms-cognitive-impairment-neurodegenerative-diseases.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39866659-rtms-neuroenhancement-healthy-adults.md]^[raw/articles/pmid-31901449-rtms-guidelines.md]
- HBOT trials can be double-blind and sham-controlled, but they depend on chamber infrastructure and staff, so they do not translate to a one-person home protocol.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41979527-hyperbaric-oxygen-therapy-traumatic-brain-injury.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-26403017-hbot-tbi-sham-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-37416626-hot-pocs-pilot-study-protocol.md]
- Transcranial ultrasound has moved toward clearer safety and reporting standards, and sham-controlled target-engagement work now exists, which strengthens the case that it is research-grade rather than solo-blindable at home.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40275098-low-intensity-transcranial-focused-ultrasound-amygdala-neuromodulation.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41072763-itrusst-biophysical-safety-transcranial-ultrasound-stimulation.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-38410648-itrusst-standardised-reporting-transcranial-ultrasound-stimulation.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40470501-transcranial-ultrasound-stimulation-parameters-neurological-diseases.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39449176-wearable-steerable-transcranial-low-intensity-focused-ultrasound-system.md]

## Active-Sham vs Biological Inertness

This audit treats `blinded` and `biologically inert` as separate axes. [[active-sham-and-inertness-taxonomy]] names the control classes; this page stays focused on whether a single person can run them alone. A control can hide assignment and still change arousal, sleep physiology, pressure exposure, or withdrawal state. That distinction matters most for taVNS, HBOT, CLAS/TMR, caffeine, nicotine, and sleep-loss paradigms.

| Family | Typical control | What can still move | Audit read |
|---|---|---|---|
| tDCS / tACS | Short-ramp sham or low-intensity comparator | Timing cues can unblind; cutaneous sensations can leak assignment | Blinding is fragile; inertness depends on ramp and montage design.^[raw/articles/pubmed-31228880-time-course-ineffective-sham-blinding-low-intensity-tdcs.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41034500-tacs-cognitive-function-older-adults.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40933163-theta-tacs-working-memory-healthy-adults.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40283048-40hz-tacs-dementia-cognitive-exercises.md] |
| Temporal interference | Active vs sham blocks | Field geometry and operator dependence can leak assignment | Not a solo-self-study family; control depends on field modeling as much as on masking.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct07339072-temporal-interference-methods-study-1-2.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41167554-human-applications-temporal-interference-stimulation.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40470330-temporal-interference-stimulation-for-human-brain-opportunities-and-challenges.md] |
| taVNS | Earlobe or behind-ear sham | Arousal, pupil dilation, HRV, and stress reactivity can still shift | Sensory-leaky and often state-active even when labeled sham.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39815765-taVNS-memory-pupil-dilation.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40130364-taVNS-long-latency-sensory-processing.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40958978-taVNS-stress-regulation-eeg-questionnaire.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39124651-taVNS-autonomic-function.md] |
| tPBM | Light-off or visually matched device | Heat, light leakage, and device cues can matter | Better home-sham candidate, but still device-specific and not a universal inert control.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39423445-transcranial-photobiomodulation-cognition-bdnf.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40939031-tpbm-mci-ad-sham-controlled.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40822571-tpbm-systematic-review-sleep-wakefulness-cognition.md] |
| Temporal interference | Active vs sham blocks | Field geometry may still engage tissue | Control is about field validity as much as blinding.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41167554-human-applications-temporal-interference-stimulation.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40925398-temporal-interference-experimental-studies-human-review.md] |
| tFUS / FUS-BBBO | Calibration-matched sham or no-energy control | Acoustic exposure, skull coupling, pressure, and safety margins can still be biologically relevant | Research-grade only; not a casual sham family.^[raw/articles/pubmed-38410648-itrusst-standardised-reporting-transcranial-ultrasound-stimulation.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41072763-itrusst-biophysical-safety-transcranial-ultrasound-stimulation.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40470501-transcranial-ultrasound-stimulation-parameters-neurological-diseases.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41723237-safe-focused-ultrasound-bbb-opening-tight-junctions.md] |
| HBOT | Pressurized air or low-O2 placebo gas | Pressure and gas mixture are active physiological exposures | Sham is chamber-active, not inert.^[raw/articles/pubmed-26403017-hbot-tbi-sham-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-37416626-hot-pocs-pilot-study-protocol.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-24052094-hbot-persistent-postconcussion-symptoms.md] |
| CLAS / TMR | No-cue, random-cue, or off-phase cueing | Sleep physiology can move even when memory does not | Control may perturb sleep while still failing behavior.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40102385-phase-specific-enhancement-motor-memory-consolidation-closed-loop-tmr.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41229102-closed-loop-auditory-stimulation-ecological-memory-null.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41497605-closed-loop-acoustic-stimulation-cathodal-tdcs-retention.md] |
| Caffeine | Placebo or decaf | Withdrawal reversal, expectancy, and state restoration can all contribute | In dependent users, the control is often not inert.^[raw/articles/pubmed-22992376-caffeine-attention-withdrawn-consumers.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-30861208-expectancy-caffeine-withdrawal-balanced-placebo-design.md] |
| Nicotine | Placebo nicotine or abstinence control | Withdrawal relief can mimic enhancement | Best treated as state restoration, not pure nootropic evidence.^[raw/articles/pubmed-31686175-nicotine-galantamine-healthy-never-smokers-cognition.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-38582919-nicotine-cognitive-function-smokers-abstinence.md] |
| Sleep restriction | Rested or partial-sleep-loss comparator | The control state itself is the intervention | Not a sham; it is a state challenge.^[raw/articles/pubmed-33524838-tdcs-sleep-deprivation-recovery.md] |

The practical rule is to record every control on two axes: `blinded?` and `biologically inert?`. A control can be one, both, or neither.

## Practical Matrix

The matrix below is a blinding-feasibility grid, not an inertness grid.

| Intervention family | Single-person blind? | Single-person sham? | Main leak | Bottom line |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Oral / encapsulated compounds | Yes | Yes | Taste, timing, withdrawal, and dose-dependent side effects | Best solo blinding option; use identical capsules or matched powders. Caffeine and creatine are the simplest examples.^[raw/articles/pubmed-22992376-caffeine-attention-withdrawn-consumers.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39070254-creatine-cognition-adults-meta-analysis.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-38418482-creatine-sleep-deprivation-cognition.md] |
| Oral wake-promoters / comparator drugs | Yes | Yes | Noticeable subjective activation or rebound | Still feasible if the goal is a true A/B crossover, but the state shift itself can reveal assignment. |
| Intranasal insulin / device-dependent drug delivery | Partial | Partial | Device feel, nasal sensation, metabolic effects, and regimen complexity | Can be blinded in principle, but the delivery device becomes part of the experiment.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40703476-intranasal-insulin-pet-aging-mci.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40195962-intranasal-insulin-ad-systematic-review-meta-analysis.md] |
| Bright light / circadian timing | Partial | Partial | Brightness, color, and timing cues | Can be controlled and compared, but the blind is weak unless the placebo light is closely matched. |
| tDCS / tACS | Partial to yes | Partial to yes | Tingling, itching, phosphenes, and user expectation | One of the more realistic home-device families for solo sham, especially if the device has a built-in sham mode.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39905569-tdcs-dosage-parameters.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40292419-tdcs-stress-working-memory-2025.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41034500-tacs-cognitive-function-older-adults.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40933163-theta-tacs-working-memory-healthy-adults.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40283048-40hz-tacs-dementia-cognitive-exercises.md] |
| Temporal interference | No | No | Field modeling, hardware complexity, and operator workflow | Counterbalanced active/sham blocks exist in humans, but the method is too setup-heavy for realistic solo blinding.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct07339072-temporal-interference-methods-study-1-2.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41167554-human-applications-temporal-interference-stimulation.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40470330-temporal-interference-stimulation-for-human-brain-opportunities-and-challenges.md] |
| tPBM | Yes | Yes | Heat, light leakage, and device indication lights | Strong home-sham candidate when the active and control devices are visually and thermally matched.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39423445-transcranial-photobiomodulation-cognition-bdnf.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40939031-tpbm-mci-ad-sham-controlled.md] |
| taVNS | Partial | Partial | Skin sensation at the ear, site differences, and current intensity clues | Feasible, but ear-level sensory leakage makes it a weaker blind than tPBM or capsules.^[raw/articles/pubmed-34043386-taVNS-healthy-cognition-meta-analysis.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39815765-taVNS-memory-pupil-dilation.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40958978-taVNS-stress-regulation-eeg-questionnaire.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39124651-taVNS-autonomic-function.md] |
| Closed-loop acoustic stimulation / TMR | Partial | Partial | Sleep disruption, cue audibility, and phase-timing artifacts | Self-run at home is possible, but reliable blinding is only as good as the EEG/audio setup.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40102385-phase-specific-enhancement-motor-memory-consolidation-closed-loop-tmr.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41229102-closed-loop-auditory-stimulation-ecological-memory-null.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41497605-closed-loop-acoustic-stimulation-cathodal-tdcs-retention.md] |
| rTMS | No | No | Coil noise, scalp sensation, and operator dependence | Sham trials exist, but a solo blinded self-experiment is not realistic.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40676541-rtms-cognitive-impairment-neurodegenerative-diseases.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39866659-rtms-neuroenhancement-healthy-adults.md]^[raw/articles/pmid-31901449-rtms-guidelines.md] |
| HBOT | No | No | Chamber logistics, pressure cues, and staff involvement | Sham-control is trial-feasible, but not single-person feasible.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41979527-hyperbaric-oxygen-therapy-traumatic-brain-injury.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-26403017-hbot-tbi-sham-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-37416626-hot-pocs-pilot-study-protocol.md] |
| Transcranial focused ultrasound | No | No | Targeting, calibration, and operator workflow | Research-grade only; not a solo sham protocol.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40275098-low-intensity-transcranial-focused-ultrasound-amygdala-neuromodulation.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41072763-itrusst-biophysical-safety-transcranial-ultrasound-stimulation.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-38410648-itrusst-standardised-reporting-transcranial-ultrasound-stimulation.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40470501-transcranial-ultrasound-stimulation-parameters-neurological-diseases.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39449176-wearable-steerable-transcranial-low-intensity-focused-ultrasound-system.md] |

## Practical Read

If the goal is a true one-person randomized self-study, start with the families that can be masked with identical capsules or a coded home device:

- oral compounds
- tDCS / tACS
- tPBM
- some closed-loop acoustic stimulation or TMR setups

If the goal is a sham-controlled clinical protocol, the set expands to include taVNS, HBOT, rTMS, and tFUS, but only with outside operators, validated equipment, and better blinding than a home experiment can usually provide.

If the intervention is behaviorally obvious by design, do not pretend it is blindable. CBT-I, sleep extension, exercise, and most schedule-based interventions should be treated as randomized but open-label.

## Open Contradictions

- A sham arm can be blinded and still biologically active, especially in taVNS, HBOT, CLAS/TMR, caffeine, nicotine, and sleep-loss paradigms.
- A study can be sham-controlled and still have weak blinding. `Sham` does not automatically mean `blinded`.
- Some sham conditions are not biologically inert, so a null or partial effect may reflect the control design as much as the intervention.
- Sensory leakage matters more than the protocol label. A home device with a coded button is not automatically a good blind if the participant can feel or hear the active condition.
- State-dependent interventions can be especially misleading because withdrawal reversal, alertness, and expectancy can all look like efficacy.
