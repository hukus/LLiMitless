# Racetam Long Tail Watchlist

This page keeps [[piracetam]], [[oxiracetam]], [[fasoracetam]], [[coluracetam]], and [[sunifiram]] visible without flattening them into the stronger existing racetam / actoprotector entities. The common pattern is not "racetams work"; it is a split between old dementia trials, genotype-enriched symptom trials, preclinical cholinergic / AMPA hypotheses, and noisy nootropic-market lore.

## Watchlist

| Candidate | Formal medicinal signal | Anecdote signal | Current route | Entity threshold |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [[piracetam]] | `low/negative`; Cochrane found global-impression hints but no specific cognitive-measure support and concluded published evidence does not support clinical use in dementia / cognitive impairment^[raw/articles/pmc-12016011-piracetam-dementia-cognitive-impairment-cochrane.md] | `moderate` legacy nootropic salience, but broad and denominator-poor | Negative / historical class anchor | Entity exists; keep as negative comparator |
| [[oxiracetam]] | `low`; two old small dementia RCT abstracts report selected cognitive or quality-of-life improvements at 800 mg twice daily^[raw/articles/pubmed-1414239-oxiracetam-dementia-placebo-study.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-1603291-oxiracetam-dementia-follow-up.md] | `weak-moderate`; long-tail forum use but not source-normalized here | Old disease-context racetam | Entity exists; needs modern safety/source-quality scan before any upgrade |
| [[fasoracetam]] / NFC-1 | `low`; one genotype-enriched adolescent ADHD study with open-label active phase and single-blind placebo week, not healthy cognition proof^[raw/articles/pmc-5770454-fasoracetam-adhd-mglur-trial.md] | `weak-moderate`; research-chemical and ADHD-interest salience | mGluR / ADHD genotype-enrichment branch | Entity exists; upgrade only if later randomized ADHD results or safety signals surface |
| [[coluracetam]] / MKC-231 / BCI-540 | `preclinical-only` for cognition; phase 2 human exposure exists in MDD/anxiety but no posted cognition result^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct00621270-coluracetam-bci540-mdd-anxiety.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-18461272-coluracetam-mkc231-af64a-rats.md] | `moderate` visual / mood / focus lore, not formally captured here | Choline-uptake / mood-trial / research-chemical branch | Entity exists; upgrade requires posted human data or structured community extraction |
| [[sunifiram]] / DM235 | `preclinical-only`; animal and slice pharmacology suggests potent antiamnesic / AMPA-linked activity but no human efficacy or safety package^[raw/articles/pmc-6741768-sunifiram-unifiram-pharmacological-characterization.md] | `moderate` potency lore, high sourcing/safety uncertainty | Potent preclinical nootropic / risk-first watch | Entity exists; upgrade only if human toxicology, safety, or clinical data appear |

## Practical Interpretation

Use [[racetam-comparison-2026-scan]] for the better-covered racetam / actoprotector set: [[aniracetam]], [[phenylpiracetam]], [[noopept]], [[bromantane]], and [[prl-8-53]]. Use this page for the long tail. [[piracetam]] is mainly a negative / historical comparator; [[oxiracetam]] is an old disease-context branch; [[fasoracetam]] is a genotype-enriched ADHD hypothesis; [[coluracetam]] is preclinical cognition plus mood-trial exposure; [[sunifiram]] is preclinical potency plus safety uncertainty. Route the whole long-tail racetam lane through [[safety-interaction-matrix-for-multi-modal-stacks]] before upgrading anecdotes, because choline co-use, stimulant history, sleep, tolerance, task practice, cannabis aftereffects, and research-chemical source identity can dominate subjective cognition reports.

June 2026 long-tail gate: piracetam, oxiracetam, fasoracetam, coluracetam, sunifiram, and nearby racetam-adjacent reports should capture exact compound, source/COA or trial status, route/formulation, dose and timing, acute versus repeated use, tolerance/cycling, choline/fat/meal co-use, baseline dementia/ADHD/mood/cannabis-aftereffect/sleep-loss state, stimulant/caffeine/nicotine/racetam/AMPA-stack co-use, adverse events, washout/dechallenge/rechallenge, and objective endpoint tier before ranking changes.
