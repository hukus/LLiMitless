# Neuroimmune Brain-Fog Chemical Guide

This guide covers the chemical candidates in the wiki's neuroimmune / glial-modulating brain-fog lane: [[minocycline]], [[ibudilast]], [[low-dose-naltrexone]], [[pentoxifylline]], [[propentofylline]], [[palmitoylethanolamide]], and [[n-acetylcysteine]]. The shared hypothesis is not stimulation. It is symptom clearance through inflammation, glial tone, redox/glutamate balance, vascular-inflammatory signaling, sleep-inertia recovery, or post-viral illness biology.

## Category Verdict

No member of this category is a proven brain-fog clearer. The best direct formal brain-fog signal is [[minocycline]]'s uncontrolled ME / long-COVID pilot, but that candidate also has the heaviest antibiotic-specific risk and several negative or mixed cognition counterweights. The best community brain-fog signal is [[low-dose-naltrexone]], but controlled post-COVID results are still pending in the captured source set. [[ibudilast]] and [[pentoxifylline]] have the most important near-term trial watch because RECLAIM has completed drug arms targeting long-COVID symptoms including brain fog, but official efficacy results were not posted in the captured page.^[raw/articles/sciencedirect-2024-minocycline-me-long-covid-pilot.md]^[raw/articles/sciencedirect-2025-minocycline-me-long-covid-corrigendum.md]^[raw/articles/reclaim-2026-ibudilast-pentoxifylline-long-covid.md]^[raw/articles/sciencedirect-2024-ldn-post-covid-symptoms-cohort.md]^[raw/articles/bmjopen-2024-ldn-post-covid-fatigue-protocol.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct05430152-ldn-post-covid-fatigue.md]

## Decision Rules

1. If the question is "which one has the strongest brain-fog anecdote?", start with [[low-dose-naltrexone]] and [[n-acetylcysteine]], but keep formal evidence and self-report separate.
2. If the question is "which one has the most direct long-COVID/ME formal brain-fog signal?", [[minocycline]] is the current answer, with an immediate uncontrolled-study and antibiotic-risk caveat.
3. If the question is "which one is most important to watch next?", follow RECLAIM for [[ibudilast]] and [[pentoxifylline]] and the post-COVID fatigue RCT for [[low-dose-naltrexone]].
4. If the question is "which is lowest-friction as a non-prescription adjunct?", [[palmitoylethanolamide]] and [[n-acetylcysteine]] are closer to that lane than the prescription drugs, but both still have mixed and phenotype-sensitive signals.
5. If the question is "which old xanthine/PDE drug proves the category?", none does. [[propentofylline]] is historically relevant but does not carry modern brain-fog evidence.

## Safety Gates

Minocycline is an antibiotic with label-level risks including vestibular effects, intracranial hypertension, autoimmune/hepatic reactions, pregnancy/fetal tooth-bone concerns, antimicrobial resistance, photosensitivity, and microbiome disruption risk.^[raw/articles/dailymed-2026-minocycline-label.md]

Low-dose naltrexone inherits naltrexone's opioid-antagonist gates: opioid analgesics, opioid dependence, opioid withdrawal, failed naloxone challenge or positive opioid screen, acute hepatitis or liver failure, and analgesia-blockade planning.^[raw/articles/dailymed-2026-naltrexone-label.md]

Pentoxifylline is a vascular prescription drug with bleeding/hemorrhage and anticoagulant-context issues, not a generic methylxanthine supplement.^[raw/articles/dailymed-2026-pentoxifylline-label.md]

Ibudilast has meaningful CNS clinical exposure but remains access/status constrained for many users and shows GI, headache, sleep, and tolerability signals in the captured evidence and anecdotes.^[raw/articles/nih-2018-ibudilast-progressive-ms-brain-atrophy.md]^[raw/articles/reddit-2022-ibudilast-longcovid-thread.md]

## Research Watch

- RECLAIM official ibudilast and pentoxifylline outcomes for long-COVID symptoms including brain fog.
- Published outcome data from the 160-person low-dose naltrexone post-COVID fatigue trial.
- Any randomized, blinded replication of minocycline in ME/long COVID with brain fog as a specified endpoint.
- PEA MCI trial results and independent replication of the healthy-adult Levagen+ memory/BDNF signal.
- NAC responder-phenotype studies that separate post-viral, stimulant-smoothing, glutamate/redox, and adverse-anhedonia/fog patterns.
