# Autonomic POTS Brain Fog Chemical Watchlist

This page is the phenotype-first chemical router for brain fog that tracks standing, tachycardia, orthostatic intolerance, low preload, venous pooling, hypovolemia, inappropriate sinus tachycardia, or POTS-like long COVID. It is adjacent to [[post-viral-brain-fog-chemical-watchlist]], but the practical question is narrower: can autonomic state correction reduce fog without mistaking the drug for a general nootropic?

## Current Read

POTS medication evidence is strongest for symptoms and hemodynamics, not cognition. A 2025 oral-medication review found beta-blockers, [[ivabradine]], and midodrine were the most frequently studied POTS medications; ivabradine and midodrine had the highest symptomatic-improvement rates, while beta-blockers showed the largest heart-rate-variability reduction. The same review emphasizes limited PASC evidence and no FDA-approved POTS medication.^[raw/articles/frontiers-2025-oral-medications-pots-systematic-review.md]

The important 2026 update is mixed: [[ivabradine]] now has stronger non-PASC POTS RCT support, but RECOVER-AUTONOMIC found that lowering heart rate with ivabradine did not significantly improve POTS symptom surveys in 181 adults with Long COVID POTS.^[raw/articles/acc-2026-ivabradine-propranolol-pots-rct.md]^[raw/articles/recover-2026-autonomic-ivabradine-long-covid-results.md]

## Decision Rules

1. If brain fog appears only after standing, stairs, showering, heat, meals, exertion, or sustained upright work, route here before generic stimulants.
2. If the dominant problem is high standing heart rate without low blood pressure, [[ivabradine]] or low-dose beta-blocker logic is the rate-control branch, but RECOVER-AUTONOMIC prevents assuming symptom rescue in Long COVID POTS.
3. If the dominant problem is low blood pressure, venous pooling, coat-hanger symptoms, or suspected cerebral hypoperfusion, midodrine, fludrocortisone, droxidopa, and [[desmopressin]] are the pressor/volume branch, each with different monitoring risk.
4. If exertional intolerance, PEM threshold, and ME/CFS physiology dominate, [[pyridostigmine]] remains the better chemical fit than pure heart-rate lowering.
5. If mast-cell symptoms, food triggers, flushing, urticaria, or histamine intolerance dominate, route to [[mast-cell-histamine-brain-fog-chemical-watchlist]] before changing autonomic drugs.
6. If caffeine, amphetamines, [[methylphenidate]], [[modafinil]], or appetite stimulants help fog while worsening tachycardia or crashes, treat that as stimulant masking until orthostatic physiology is measured.

## Research Watch

- Published RECOVER-AUTONOMIC ivabradine full paper with subgroup results, adverse events, coordinated-care interaction, and cognitive/functional endpoints.
- Longer-term randomized POTS trials comparing [[ivabradine]], propranolol, midodrine, and nonpharmacologic care with brain-fog or objective cognition outcomes.
- LIFT results for [[pyridostigmine]], [[low-dose-naltrexone]], and their combination in ME/CFS / Long COVID / PASC with orthostatic intolerance.
- Better phenotype-stratified studies separating tachycardic, hypovolemic, neuropathic, hyperadrenergic, MCAS-overlap, and PEM-heavy POTS.
