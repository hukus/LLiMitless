# Histidine Dipeptide Cognition Watchlist

This page keeps meat-, chicken-, and food-derived peptide cognition leads from being collapsed into one "carnosine" claim. The current route is product-aware: [[anserine-carnosine]] is the purified/formula dipeptide branch, [[essence-of-chicken]] is a traditional food-extract branch, [[beta-alanine]] is a carnosine-precursor / sports-nutrition branch, and [[fermented-milk-lactononadecapeptide]] is an adjacent dairy-peptide branch rather than a histidine-dipeptide intervention.

## Medicinal Signal

| Candidate | Formal signal | Signal strength | Best route | Main caveat |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [[anserine-carnosine]] | 2024 HCD meta-analysis delayed-recall signal; several small older-adult/MCI memory-preservation trials | `low-moderate/domain-specific` | Older-adult delayed recall / neuroimmune-aging watch | Pure carnosine metabolic-risk trial was cognition-null; product ratio matters |
| [[essence-of-chicken]] | Larger 2018 healthy-adult RCT with working-memory and stress-subgroup short-term-memory signal; 2019 meta-analysis favorable for working memory | `low-moderate/quality-gated` | Stress-linked working-memory food extract | Earlier review judged trials high-risk-of-bias; sponsor/product identity central |
| ProBeptigen / hydrolyzed chicken extract | 90-person mildly stressed middle-aged trial with WMS Logical Memory, CANTAB spatial working memory, and hs-CRP signal | `low/product-specific` | Standardized chicken-peptide product | Derived from essence of chicken but not equivalent to bottled EC |
| [[beta-alanine]] | Older-adult MoCA subgroup signal; combat-soldier cognition null; strong ergogenic not cognitive support | `low/subgroup` | State-challenge / fatigue-resilience candidate | Preworkout visibility and paraesthesia do not prove cognition |
| [[fermented-milk-lactononadecapeptide]] | Healthy middle-aged fermented-milk RBANS attention/delayed-memory signal; acute tablet memory signal with carryover caveat | `low/product-specific` | Food-derived peptide watch | Dairy/probiotic/matrix/manufacturer effects unresolved |

The current best interpretation is not "histidine dipeptides improve cognition." It is narrower: delayed recall and working memory show selective signals in older, stressed, or product-specific contexts, while attention, global cognition, and healthy rested-baseline enhancement remain weak or inconsistent.^[raw/articles/pubmed-38013229-histidine-dipeptides-cognition-meta.md]^[raw/articles/mdpi-2018-essence-of-chicken-healthy-adults-cognition-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40430451-carnosine-prediabetes-t2d-cognition-null.md]

## Product And Protocol Gates

- Do not collapse [[anserine-carnosine]], [[essence-of-chicken]], ProBeptigen, chicken meat extract, [[beta-alanine]], ordinary meat intake, and [[fermented-milk-lactononadecapeptide]].
- Record active unit: anserine:carnosine ratio, pure carnosine grams, beta-alanine grams, peptide sequence/dose, chicken-extract serving, fermented-milk grams, and protein/peptide comparator.
- Record phenotype: healthy young, stressed adult, middle-aged mild stress, healthy elderly, MCI, APOE4, metabolic-risk, tactical/soldier stress, and fatigue state.
- Keep delayed recall, short-term memory, working memory, attention, global cognition, mood/clearheadedness, fatigue, and physical performance separate.
- Track sponsor/product ties, dairy/chicken allergy, GI tolerance, high-dose carnosine adverse events, beta-alanine paraesthesia, sodium/protein load, and assay availability.

## Current Read

This lane is worth adding because it fills a real gap between nutrient pages, traditional food-extract medicine, and biohacker chemical anecdotes. The highest-confidence use is not acute focus; it is a low-confidence memory-preservation or stress-linked working-memory branch. [[anserine-carnosine]] and [[essence-of-chicken]] should be discoverable entities, [[beta-alanine]] should remain state-challenge/fatigue-bound, and [[fermented-milk-lactononadecapeptide]] should sit as a food-derived peptide lead awaiting independent replication.
