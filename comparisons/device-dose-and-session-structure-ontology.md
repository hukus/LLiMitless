# Device-Dose and Session-Structure Ontology

This page is the normalization layer underneath the delivery-validation checklist. The goal is not to invent one universal dose unit; the goal is to preserve each family's native exposure variable while making target, timing, session structure, and monitoring burden comparable across pages. A study can be "high dose" in one family and "low dose" in another, but that comparison only works if the page records the target, the unit, and the schedule separately. The recent literature makes that unavoidable: tDCS and tACS are field-configuration problems, tPBM is a wavelength/fluence/contact problem, taVNS is a site-and-sensation problem, temporal interference is a field-geometry problem, tFUS is an acoustic-calibration problem, and CLAS/TMR is a phase-locked timing problem.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39905569-tdcs-dosage-parameters.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-38060075-tdcs-cognitive-functions-review.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39438426-tacs-working-memory-meta-2025.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40933163-theta-tacs-working-memory-healthy-adults.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41034500-tacs-older-adults-meta-2025.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41507453-tacs-cognitive-impairment-meta.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40822571-tpbm-systematic-review-sleep-wakefulness-cognition.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-34043386-taVNS-healthy-cognition-meta-analysis.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39815765-taVNS-memory-pupil-dilation.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41167554-human-applications-temporal-interference-stimulation.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40925398-temporal-interference-experimental-studies-human-review.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-38410648-itrusst-standardised-reporting-transcranial-ultrasound-stimulation.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41072763-itrusst-biophysical-safety-transcranial-ultrasound-stimulation.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40626105-targeting-sleep-spindles-closed-loop-auditory-stimulation.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40575621-tmr-rem-negative-images-lpp.md]

The practical rule is simple: if a page cannot say what the native dose is, how it is delivered, when it is delivered, and what state it is paired with, the page is not describing the intervention yet. That matters especially for home-capable methods, because session structure and monitoring burden can be more important than the label on the device.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39423445-transcranial-photobiomodulation-cognition-bdnf.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40939031-tpbm-mci-ad-sham-controlled.md]^[raw/articles/medrxiv-25333989-tpbm-mci-cognitive-metabolic-neuroimaging-pilot.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39124651-taVNS-autonomic-function.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40470501-transcranial-ultrasound-stimulation-parameters-neurological-diseases.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41610842-transcranial-focused-ultrasound-in-the-human-brain.md]

Structured EMF / PEMF now belongs in this ontology as its own field-configuration family. The 2026 healthy-adult anchor shows that patterned EMF can selectively impair Digit Span endpoints, while small physiology and reactive-performance studies show target engagement and task-specific movement. That makes field pattern, coil/solenoid geometry, placement, sham field generation, and contraindication screening first-order dose variables.^[raw/articles/nature-2026-emf-working-memory-alpha-healthy-adults.md]^[raw/articles/bmc-2018-lf-pemf-cortical-plasticity-healthy-brain.md]^[raw/articles/neuroregulation-2023-pemf-reactive-performance.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct02125032-tpemf-parkinsons.md]

## What Changed Since 2024

- tACS now has a 2025 clinical guideline, so the family has moved from "parameter-sensitive literature" into a more formal clinical reporting layer even though the field still needs montage, current, frequency, and session structure spelled out explicitly.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40082256-guideline-for-clinical-application-transcranial-alternating-current-stimulation.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39438426-tacs-working-memory-meta-2025.md]
- tRNS now has enough human signal to deserve its own family row: the dose is the random-frequency spectrum plus target and session structure, not a hidden tDCS/tACS variant.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41582468-trns-psychiatric-systematic-review.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39402554-trns-working-memory-healthy-individuals.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41387754-trns-attention-bias-negative-emotional-stimuli.md]^[raw/articles/pmc-12949120-trns-schizophrenia-bilateral-prefrontal.md]^[raw/articles/nature-s41598-025-21890-x-trns-numerical-acuity.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40487366-trns-alpha-coherence-divergent-thinking.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40669128-trns-motion-processing-null-registered-report.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39901001-trns-perceptual-motor-task.md]
- tPBM has a 2025 systematic review plus newer home-capable cognitive studies, which reinforces that wavelength, fluence, site, and thermal management are the real exposure variables, not the device label alone.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40822571-tpbm-systematic-review-sleep-wakefulness-cognition.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39423445-transcranial-photobiomodulation-cognition-bdnf.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40939031-tpbm-mci-ad-sham-controlled.md]^[raw/articles/medrxiv-25333989-tpbm-mci-cognitive-metabolic-neuroimaging-pilot.md]
- Intranasal insulin has now crossed into the ontology layer because the delivery device changes the brain exposure profile, so the spray platform belongs in the dose record rather than in a footnote.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40703476-intranasal-insulin-pet-aging-mci.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40195962-intranasal-insulin-ad-systematic-review-meta-analysis.md]
- Structured EMF / PEMF now has a 2026 healthy-adult caution anchor: pattern-specific exposure reduced working-memory or short-term recall measures rather than improving them, so "magnetic stimulation" cannot be treated as a single dose label.^[raw/articles/nature-2026-emf-working-memory-alpha-healthy-adults.md]
- taVNS still behaves like a sensory-and-state problem rather than a simple amplitude problem: sham can leak through sensation and autonomic arousal, so current reporting needs the auricular site, thresholding, and state context.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40535058-tavns-insomnia-clinical-practice-recommendations.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41973311-taVNS-military-exercise-sleep-deprivation.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-34043386-taVNS-healthy-cognition-meta-analysis.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39815765-taVNS-memory-pupil-dilation.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40958978-taVNS-stress-regulation-eeg-questionnaire.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39124651-taVNS-autonomic-function.md]
- tFUS now has both standardized reporting and biophysical safety consensus, so skull modeling, acoustic calibration, thermal bounds, and contraindication screening belong in the protocol description.^[raw/articles/pubmed-38410648-itrusst-standardised-reporting-transcranial-ultrasound-stimulation.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41072763-itrusst-biophysical-safety-transcranial-ultrasound-stimulation.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40470501-transcranial-ultrasound-stimulation-parameters-neurological-diseases.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41610842-transcranial-focused-ultrasound-in-the-human-brain.md]
- The 2026 registry and adverse-event sources add device class and frequency as explicit tFUS dose fields: if the trial record omits the device, frequency, MI/MI_tc, focal geometry, and in situ estimate, the exposure is underdescribed.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41856430-transcranial-ultrasound-neuromodulation-registry-analysis.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41916485-low-frequency-ultrasound-sae-response.md]
- CLAS/TMR now has both event-targeting validation and an ecological home-setting null for behavior, which is the cleanest reminder that phase-correct physiology movement does not guarantee memory or vigilance transfer.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40626105-targeting-sleep-spindles-closed-loop-auditory-stimulation.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41229102-closed-loop-auditory-stimulation-ecological-memory-null.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40575621-tmr-rem-negative-images-lpp.md]
- tTIS human evidence now includes a larger systematic review, but cognition still depends on envelope frequency and target geometry rather than on a generic "deep stimulation" label.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41167554-human-applications-temporal-interference-stimulation.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40925398-temporal-interference-experimental-studies-human-review.md]

## Canonical Fields

| Field | Why it belongs in the ontology | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Native dose unit | Different families do not share a common exposure currency | mA, current density, Hz, pulses, nm, irradiance, fluence, pressure, MI, cue latency |
| Target / site | The intervention is usually target-specific before it is dose-specific | DLPFC, precuneus, auricular site, scalp site, deep target, sleep spindle, slow oscillation |
| Session structure | Total effect often depends on how dose is chunked over time | Single session, repeated sessions, trains, bursts, cue windows, nightly cycles |
| State coupling | Many effects only appear when the stimulation is paired with a task or sleep state | Online task coupling, offline after-training use, sleep-stage gating, REM replay, sleep-loss rescue |
| Monitoring burden | Safety and reproducibility often depend on calibration or screening | MRI/neuronavigation, skull modeling, hydrophone checks, AE questionnaires, vitals, sleep EEG |
| Control class | A sham can be sensory-matched, biologically active, or both | Sham, active sham, withdrawal-reversal comparator, no-cue control |

## Normalization Rules

1. Do not compare intensity values across families as if they were commensurate. `mA`, `Hz`, `nm`, `kPa`, and `dB` are not interchangeable units.
2. Treat frequency carefully. It can mean stimulation frequency, carrier frequency, envelope frequency, or cue cadence depending on the family.
3. Keep session structure separate from total exposure. One long session and many short sessions can produce different state effects even when the nominal dose looks similar.
4. Treat online/offline coupling as a real dose dimension. In several families, whether the stimulus is paired with a task, sleep, or recovery state changes the interpretation more than the nominal value does.
5. Record monitoring burden on the same page as the dose. If a method needs calibration, imaging, contraindication screening, or sleep EEG, that burden is part of the protocol.
6. Keep control class explicit. A sham arm, an active control, and a biologically inert control are not the same thing.^[raw/articles/pubmed-34043386-taVNS-healthy-cognition-meta-analysis.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-30214966-tdcs-tacs-adverse-events-review.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41072763-itrusst-biophysical-safety-transcranial-ultrasound-stimulation.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40626105-targeting-sleep-spindles-closed-loop-auditory-stimulation.md]

## What To Record

| Minimum line item | Preferred wording |
|---|---|
| Family | `tDCS`, `tACS`, `rTMS`, `tPBM`, `taVNS`, `tTIS`, `tFUS`, `CLAS`, or `TMR` |
| Native dose | The family-specific quantity, not a generic "intensity" |
| Target | Site, network, or sleep event |
| Schedule | Single session, repeated sessions, nightly, or event-linked |
| Coupling | Task, sleep stage, rest, recovery, or state challenge |
| Control | Sham, active sham, or no-cue comparator |
| Monitoring | Calibration, questionnaires, imaging, or physiologic checks |

## Open Contradictions

- A method can be well specified in one family and under-specified in another, so "dose" is not a common currency.
- Some methods are delivered as obvious states rather than stimuli, so their real dose is partly behavioral and partly temporal.
- A sham can match sensation and still be biologically active, especially in taVNS, tDCS/tACS, and closed-loop sleep cueing.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39815765-taVNS-memory-pupil-dilation.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40958978-taVNS-stress-regulation-eeg-questionnaire.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-30214966-tdcs-tacs-adverse-events-review.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41497605-closed-loop-acoustic-stimulation-cathodal-tdcs-retention.md]
- tFUS has the strongest need for geometry and calibration language, while CLAS/TMR has the strongest need for phase- and event-timing language.
