# Molecule Intake Triage Table

This page is the intake gate that sits before the ranked recommendation pages. Its job is not to decide whether a molecule is "good"; its job is to decide where the candidate belongs in the wiki before it gets compared against stronger or weaker branches. For the molecule-by-molecule coverage ledger that separates medicinal signal, anecdotal signal, route, and missing entity pages, see [[cognitive-improvement-molecule-signal-atlas]].

The default rule is conservative routing. If a molecule fits more than one bucket, keep the bucket that best matches the evidence context and add the caution label elsewhere rather than flattening everything into a single enhancement row.

## Routing Order

1. Route obvious human benchmark drugs to the comparator layer first.
2. Route state-dependent rescue or withdrawal-reversal effects to the state-restoration bucket.
3. Route disease-first human programs to the clinical/recovery bucket.
4. Route standardized extracts and food-derived compounds to the nutraceutical proxy bucket.
5. Route molecules with no convincing human cognition program to the preclinical-only bucket.
6. Route repeated nulls, failed programs, and cautionary analogues to the negative-class bucket.

## What Changed Since 2024

- The comparator stack is now explicit enough that caffeine, modafinil-class drugs, methylphenidate, solriamfetol, pitolisant, and nicotine can be routed before anyone starts ranking a new candidate as "better."
- Several branches that once looked like broad enhancers are now better treated as disease-first or state-restoration candidates, especially semaglutide, exenatide, and intranasal insulin.
- The negative-class lane is stronger than it used to be because 5-HT6 antagonists now have enough failed human history to function as a cautionary comparator, not just a one-off story.
- Product identity and sourcing have become decisive for nutraceuticals and peptides, so the intake table has to separate API-like drugs from extract- and supply-chain-sensitive candidates.
- Preclinical-only routing is now a protection mechanism, not a placeholder; it keeps mechanistically attractive but untranslated candidates out of ranked recommendation pages until human evidence exists.

## Practical Rules

- Keep the most conservative bucket that still fits the evidence.
- Split by phenotype when a candidate has both rescue and enhancement data.
- Preserve a negative-class caution even when the molecule also has a disease-context signal.
- If the molecule is really a device, a formulation, or a supply-chain problem, route it to the relevant device or sourcing page instead of forcing it into this table.
