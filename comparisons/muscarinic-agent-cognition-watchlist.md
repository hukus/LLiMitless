# Muscarinic Agent Cognition Watchlist

This page keeps the muscarinic branch molecule-addressable without collapsing symptom-first schizophrenia programs, older AD cognition signals, and healthy-volunteer target engagement into one nootropic claim.

The strongest direct cognition signal in this watchlist is still older [[xanomeline]] in AD, not the modern [[xanomeline-trospium]] approval. Cobenfy proves the muscarinic branch is clinically real, but its approval and pivotal schizophrenia trials are not cognition-first evidence. [[vu0467319-vu319]] and [[emraclidine]] show the modern M1/M4 development path, but one is target-engagement-only and the other is symptom-first.^[raw/articles/fda-cobenfy-approval-2024.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-38691387-xanomeline-trospium-schizophrenia-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-9109749-xanomeline-alzheimer-cognitive-behavior.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-9876958-xanomeline-ad-cntb.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40598606-vu0467319-m1-pam-healthy-participants.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-36528376-emraclidine-m4-phase1b.md]
