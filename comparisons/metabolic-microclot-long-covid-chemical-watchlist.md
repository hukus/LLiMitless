# Metabolic Microclot Long-COVID Chemical Watchlist

This page tracks metabolic, mitochondrial, endothelial, and microclot-adjacent chemical leads for long-COVID fatigue and brain fog. It complements [[post-viral-brain-fog-chemical-watchlist]], which focuses more on neuroimmune, mast-cell, prefrontal, psychotropic, and stimulant-state candidates.

## Category Verdict

The key point is directionality. [[metformin]] has the cleanest formal signal, but it is acute-COVID prevention of long COVID, not established-long-COVID brain-fog treatment. [[axa1125]] has a blinded fatigue signal with a negative primary bioenergetics endpoint. [[coenzyme-q10]] has a direct post-COVID symptom RCT that was negative. [[nattokinase]] has the strongest microclot-style anecdote traffic and an in-vitro spike-protein mechanism, but no captured controlled long-COVID brain-fog trial and real bleeding/interactions caveats.^[raw/articles/sciencedirect-2023-covidout-metformin-long-covid-prevention-rct.md]^[raw/articles/sciencedirect-2023-axa1125-long-covid-fatigue-rct.md]^[raw/articles/sciencedirect-2023-coq10-post-covid-crossover-rct.md]^[raw/articles/molecules-2022-nattokinase-spike-protein-in-vitro.md]^[raw/articles/mskcc-2026-nattokinase-safety.md]

## Decision Rules

1. If the question is prevention after acute SARS-CoV-2 infection, [[metformin]] is the cleanest chemical lead in this page, but only within prescription-risk and eligibility boundaries.
2. If the question is established long-COVID fatigue with cognitive-fatigue overlay, [[axa1125]] is trial-watch but not proven objective cognitive repair.
3. If the question is mitochondrial supplement treatment for post-COVID symptoms, [[coenzyme-q10]] has a direct negative trial and should not be promoted above that.
4. If the question is microclot-style self-experimentation, [[nattokinase]] should be labeled anecdote-plus-mechanism only, with bleeding and drug-interaction screening first.
5. If the claimed benefit is simply more energy that enables overexertion, route back to pacing/PEM interpretation rather than upgrading the molecule.

## Research Watch

- Trials of metformin for established post-COVID condition, especially fatigue/brain-fog endpoints, not just prevention during acute infection.
- Larger AXA1125 or related endogenous-metabolic-modulator trials with objective cognitive endpoints and PEM-aware activity outcomes.
- Any controlled nattokinase / serrapeptase / lumbrokinase long-COVID trial that measures symptoms, coagulation markers, bleeding, and cognitive outcomes.
- CoQ10 replication only if phenotype-enriched or formulation-enriched enough to overcome the negative crossover trial.
