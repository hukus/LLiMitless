# Energy-Substrate Comparator Map

This page compares the main "brain fuel" or fuel-adjacent comparators the wiki keeps circling back to: creatine, ketone esters, lactate-support strategies, glucose stability, and caffeine. The point is not that these are interchangeable. The point is that they are all used in practice as state-control tools under strain, so the same endpoint battery should be applied before any one of them gets called a meaningful enhancer.

I am treating glucose stability as a state target rather than a product. That matters because the relevant evidence sits in CGM, postprandial handling, hypoglycemia avoidance, and daily-life fluctuation studies, not in a supplement-like efficacy literature.

For the intervention pages behind this map, see [[creatine]], [[creatine-cognitive-support]], [[ketone-esters-under-sleep-loss-and-high-load]], [[lactate-shuttle-and-cognition]], and [[pharmacology-comparator-map]]. For the measurement layer, see [[practical-cognitive-measurement-stack]] and [[endpoint-dictionary-for-cognition-interventions]].

## What Changed Since 2024

- Creatine is now the cleanest low-cost strain-state amplifier in the set: the 2024 meta-analysis narrowed the signal to memory, attention time, and processing-speed time, the 2025 older-adult systematic review suggested an older-phenotype signal is still there, the 2024 high-dose and 2026 lower-dose sleep-deprivation RCTs plus 2025 hemisphere asymmetry analysis made the state-dependent rescue story more credible, and the 2026 menopause RCT showed that formulation/phenotype can matter enough to move reaction time and frontal brain creatine in a small female cohort.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39070254-creatine-cognition-adults-meta-analysis.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40971619-creatine-cognition-older-adults-systematic-review.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-38418482-creatine-sleep-deprivation-cognition.md]^[raw/articles/mdpi-2026-single-dose-creatine-sleep-deprivation-cognition.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40520504-creatine-sleep-deprivation-hemispheric-asymmetry.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40854087-creatine-menopause-cognition-rct.md]
- Ketone esters remain phenotype-, context-, and product-sensitive. The obesity crossover trial is still the strongest positive cognition signal, a 2024 trained-female postexercise study adds a smaller exercise-state positive, the acute hypoxia-plus-exercise trial was cognition-null, STAK-Sleep is still recruiting/no-results rather than direct sleep-loss evidence, kCBF is unknown-status/no-results, product anecdotes are mixed and formulation-confounded, and the 2026 Frontiers meta-analysis only modestly improves the branch's pooled estimate over the 2025 preprint rather than turning it into a universal enhancer story.^[raw/articles/pubmed-34605026-ketone-monoester-obesity-cognition-cbf.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-38051034-ketone-monoester-carbohydrate-postexercise-trained-females.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40315254-ketone-ester-hypoxia-null.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct05519644-stak-sleep-ketone-ester-record-2026-05-04.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct06032156-kcbf-ketone-dose-record-2026-05-04.md]^[raw/articles/reddit-2023-11-02-ketone-iq-ultramarathon-thread.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40349325-acute-ketone-monoester-ingestion-lowers-resting-cerebral-blood-flow.md]^[raw/articles/medrxiv-2025-09-17-25335999-exogenous-ketone-bodies-cognition-systematic-review-meta-analysis.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41001501-exogenous-ketone-bodies-cognition-systematic-review-meta-analysis.md]
- Lactate moved from a pure mechanism story into a live human clamp study. LEAN reported acute cognition improvement and AD biomarker reductions, 2025-2026 human flux studies showed preferential brain lactate oxidation during both infusion and exercise plus task-linked regional lactate production, and the 2026 review layer now frames lactate as a sleep/exercise bridge and a context-sensitive dual modulator. The evidence is still procedural and not yet a durable outpatient supplementation story.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41376120-lean-lactate-infusion-cognition-biomarkers.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41037311-preferential-lactate-metabolism-human-brain-hyperlactataemia.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39862181-task-activation-regional-13c-lactate-signal-human-brain.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41595786-lactate-nexus-physical-activity-sleep-cognitive-enhancement.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41836313-lactate-cognition-dual-modulator.md]
- Caffeine is still the benchmark acute comparator for vigilance under sleep loss. The newer repeated-dose and chronic-restriction papers reinforce that it works, but it also changes the state being measured and can distort next-day sleep and tolerance interpretation.^[raw/articles/pubmed-35791877-repeated-low-dose-caffeine-sleep-deprivation-cognition.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-34014758-caffeine-selectively-mitigates-cognitive-deficits-sleep-deprivation.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-38830861-repeated-caffeine-intake-sleep-restriction-gm.md]
- Glucose stability is no longer just a vague hypothesis. The wiki now has both a healthy-adult postprandial/glucose-regulation crossover study and the GluCog CGM+EMA methodology paper showing how glycemic excursions and cognition are being paired in daily life.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39579950-breaking-up-sitting-postprandial-glucose-regulation.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-36602848-glucog-glycemic-variability-cognitive-status-type1-diabetes.md]
- The 2025 T1D review now makes the risk side explicit: hypoglycemia and hyperglycemia both impair cognition through different pathways, and CGM is better than A1C alone for capturing the relevant variability.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41522270-glucose-extremes-cognitive-function-type1-diabetes.md]

## Comparator Stack

| Comparator | Best evidence context | Human cognition signal | Main downside | Wiki role |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Creatine | Sleep loss, heat stress, aging, menopause phenotype, and other high-demand states | Modest memory / attention-time / processing-speed signal; strongest when demand is high or reserve is low; now has two acute sleep-deprivation challenge trials at 0.35 and 0.2 g/kg plus a small menopause-phenotype reaction-time signal in the 2026 HCl trial | Not a universal enhancer; dose, formulation, and phenotype matter | Low-cost context amplifier and the best "state rescue" baseline in this set |
| Ketone esters | Obesity / metabolic vulnerability, mental fatigue, exercise strain; sleep-loss question still protocol-only | Mixed: positive obesity crossover, small mental-fatigue benefit, mixed hypoxia results, acute healthy-young-adult CBF lowering, and STAK-Sleep no-results status | GI burden, formulation/product-identity sensitivity, expensive, context dependent, and mechanistically not a simple vasodilator | Experimental rescue tool when glucose or energy supply is the hypothesized bottleneck |
| Lactate-support strategies | Infusion / clamp settings, AD-like metabolic vulnerability, exercise physiology | Promising but narrow: LEAN was positive acutely, older AD infusion was null | Procedural burden, infusion context, and no durable outpatient enhancer evidence | Mechanistic comparator for substrate delivery, not a consumer-style nootropic |
| Glucose stability | Postprandial control, hypoglycemia avoidance, T1D daily-life fluctuations, sedentary-break paradigms | Indirect and sparse: neuroelectric attention changes, CGM+EMA fluctuation-mapping, and T1D review-level evidence that glucose extremes impair memory, attention, and executive function | Mostly a state marker; causality is hard to separate from behavior and disease state | Monitoring / covariate layer that belongs in the battery, not in the supplement tier |
| Caffeine | Total or partial sleep deprivation, long duty cycles, sustained wakefulness | Most reliable acute vigilance, reaction-time, and sleep-loss rescue signal in the stack | Sleep disruption, tolerance, withdrawal confounding, anxiety, and next-day-state distortion | The first comparator any new fatigue-rescue claim should beat |

## Shared Battery

Use the same endpoint battery across all five comparators:

| Domain | Preferred readout | Why it matters here |
|---|---|---|
| Arousal | Brief PVT | Separates wakefulness rescue from broader cognition |
| Throughput | Timed performance composite | Captures speed-accuracy work under load |
| Resilience | Same-day or next-morning challenge performance | Measures whether the comparator preserves function under sleep loss / strain |
| Switching | Task-switching or Stroop-style inhibition | Helps distinguish arousal from executive control |
| Consolidation | Delayed recall | Keeps memory from being collapsed into alertness |
| State context | EMA fatigue, actigraphy, HRV, CGM | Needed to interpret whether the participant was actually in a strain state |
| Safety | Adverse-event log, sleep disruption, GI burden, BP/HR if relevant | These comparators often change the state that the next day measures |

## Effect Size Snapshot

- Creatine: the current adult meta-analysis shows small-to-moderate memory and processing-speed effects, with the clearest support under strain; the sleep-deprivation line now has both a high-dose 0.35 g/kg RCT and a lower-dose 0.2 g/kg crossover pointing in the same protective direction; the older-adult systematic review adds a small but mostly positive age-specific signal, the menopause RCT adds a small reaction-time and frontal creatine signal, and the safety review does not show an overall excess side-effect burden.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39070254-creatine-cognition-adults-meta-analysis.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-38418482-creatine-sleep-deprivation-cognition.md]^[raw/articles/mdpi-2026-single-dose-creatine-sleep-deprivation-cognition.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40971619-creatine-cognition-older-adults-systematic-review.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40854087-creatine-menopause-cognition-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40198156-creatine-safety-side-effects-adverse-event-reports.md]
- Ketone esters: the obesity crossover is the best positive human signal; the trained-female postexercise trial and the mental-fatigue soccer trial are modest, direct sleep-loss evidence is still protocol-only, the hypoxia branch is state-split, and the acute CBF trial points the wrong way for a simple blanket vasodilation story. The preprint-to-final meta-analysis upgrade is useful for confidence, but it does not erase the phenotype split or product-identity problem.^[raw/articles/pubmed-34605026-ketone-monoester-obesity-cognition-cbf.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-38051034-ketone-monoester-carbohydrate-postexercise-trained-females.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-36297060-ketone-ester-simulated-soccer-mental-fatigue.md]^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct05519644-stak-sleep-ketone-ester-record-2026-05-04.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40315254-ketone-ester-hypoxia-null.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40349325-acute-ketone-monoester-ingestion-lowers-resting-cerebral-blood-flow.md]^[raw/articles/reddit-2023-11-02-ketone-iq-ultramarathon-thread.md]^[raw/articles/medrxiv-2025-09-17-25335999-exogenous-ketone-bodies-cognition-systematic-review-meta-analysis.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41001501-exogenous-ketone-bodies-cognition-systematic-review-meta-analysis.md]
- Lactate: LEAN is positive acutely, but it is still an infusion study rather than a practical supplement protocol. The 2025 hyperlactatemia study adds a useful mechanistic number by showing that lactate accounted for about 24% of cerebral oxidative metabolism at 8 mmol/L, and the 2026 reviews improve the mechanism bridge, not the trial count.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41376120-lean-lactate-infusion-cognition-biomarkers.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41037311-preferential-lactate-metabolism-human-brain-hyperlactataemia.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41595786-lactate-nexus-physical-activity-sleep-cognitive-enhancement.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41836313-lactate-cognition-dual-modulator.md]
- Glucose stability: the best available evidence is still mostly a correlation / method layer, but the 2025 T1D review adds a clearer disease-context claim that glucose extremes impair cognition.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39579950-breaking-up-sitting-postprandial-glucose-regulation.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-36602848-glucog-glycemic-variability-cognitive-status-type1-diabetes.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-41522270-glucose-extremes-cognitive-function-type1-diabetes.md]
- Caffeine: the strongest acute rescue signal for vigilance and reaction time, but not a durable broad-cognition enhancer.^[raw/articles/pubmed-35791877-repeated-low-dose-caffeine-sleep-deprivation-cognition.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-34014758-caffeine-selectively-mitigates-cognitive-deficits-sleep-deprivation.md]

## Safety / Practical Burden

- Creatine is the lightest lift: low cost, low safety burden, and relatively easy to standardize, though the menopause RCT suggests the exact salt/formulation can matter.
- Ketone esters are tolerable enough for trials, but GI burden, taste, cost, and product-identity complexity are real.
- Lactate infusion is clinically interesting but not a home protocol; the safety and logistics burden are high by design.
- Lactate now has a live phase 2 severe-TBI safety trial, which keeps the branch active on the registry side even though it remains biomarker-first.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-nct06701760-sodium-lactate-severe-tbi.md]
- Glucose stability is usually a monitoring issue, not a direct intervention risk, unless the person is prone to hypoglycemia, hyperglycemia, or aggressive dietary manipulation.
- Caffeine is the easiest comparator to deploy, but it has the cleanest ability to contaminate the next night of sleep and the next morning of testing.

## Open Contradictions

- Creatine looks strongest under strain, now with two acute sleep-deprivation trials, but the healthy-young-adult baseline enhancement story remains weak.
- Ketones look promising in some metabolic phenotypes, but the nulls show they are not a universal rescue strategy.
- Ketones also do not appear to work through a uniform vasodilatory CBF increase; acute KME lowered resting CBF in healthy young adults.
- Lactate may be a real fuel and signal, yet the direction of causality still depends on whether the source is supply, utilization, or disease state. The 2025 hyperlactatemia study also showed infusion and exercise converge on lactate oxidation but still differ in CBF and systemic physiology, so route cannot be collapsed into one generic effect.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41037311-preferential-lactate-metabolism-human-brain-hyperlactataemia.md]
- Glucose stability is still mostly a context variable, so it should not be overpromoted as an enhancer, even though disease-context cognition harms are now clearer.
- Caffeine is benchmark-good, but repeated use changes the comparison state enough that poor scheduling can create false conclusions.
