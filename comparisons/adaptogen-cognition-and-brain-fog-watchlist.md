# Adaptogen Cognition And Brain-Fog Watchlist

This page catches adaptogen-style botanicals and fungi that are common in brain-fog, fatigue, and nootropic communities but whose formal evidence is usually state-dependent, formula-limited, or product-specific. Use [[standardized-herbal-extracts-for-cognition]] for ranked botanical evidence and [[biohacker-anecdote-methodology]] for anecdote scoring.

## Freshness Notes

The 2025 Bacumen trial is the main negative update in this pass. It tested adults with self-reported memory and attention problems and did not improve the primary cognition outcomes, while stress reactivity and post-task fatigue/stress moved favorably. That pushes [[bacopa]] toward a stress/fatigue-state read rather than a broad healthy-adult enhancer.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41091332-bacumen-bacopa-healthy-adults-rct.md]

[[cordyceps-cordycepin]] has the most interesting new branch because the direct SCD trial, if independently verified, would connect cognition, sleep, inflammation, and BDNF. For now it remains a watch item because the better-established 2024 human trial measured immune outcomes rather than cognition, and the anecdotes split purified cordycepin from ordinary Cordyceps supplements.^[raw/articles/garuda-2025-cordyceps-militaris-scd-cognition-sleep-rct.md]^[raw/articles/nature-2024-cordyceps-militaris-immune-healthy-adults-rct.md]^[raw/articles/reddit-2022-cordycepin-cognition-brainfog-report.md]^[raw/articles/reddit-2021-cordyceps-fatigue-sleep-thread.md]

The Schisandra/Eleuthero/Rhodiola cluster should be read as formula evidence. ADAPT-232 improved acute attention/speed/accuracy in stressed healthy women, and Eleuthero-plus-Drynaria improved a narrow figure-recall outcome in healthy adults. Neither result tells us what happens with a generic Schisandra tincture or Siberian-ginseng capsule.^[raw/articles/sciencedirect-2010-adapt232-stressed-healthy-adults-cognition-rct.md]^[raw/articles/mdpi-2020-eleuthero-drynaria-healthy-adults-cognition-rct.md]

## Anecdote And Safety

The anecdote layer is useful for prioritization but not ranking. Cordycepin has the clearest captured brain-fog report; Cordyceps extract has mixed clarity/endurance and fatigue/sleep tradeoffs; Schisandra has symptom-state brain-fog salience but heavy endometriosis/gut/hormone confounding.^[raw/articles/reddit-2022-cordycepin-cognition-brainfog-report.md]^[raw/articles/reddit-2021-cordyceps-fatigue-sleep-thread.md]^[raw/articles/reddit-2024-schisandra-endo-brainfog-thread.md]

Safety gates are not interchangeable: Cordyceps has glucose and bleeding cautions, Schisandra has CYP/P-gp/tacrolimus/lab-interaction concerns, and Eleuthero has digoxin and identity warnings. These gates matter more than the generic adaptogen label.^[raw/articles/mskcc-2026-cordyceps-safety.md]^[raw/articles/mskcc-2026-schisandra-safety.md]^[raw/articles/mskcc-2026-siberian-ginseng-safety.md]

## Verdict

The adaptogen branch is useful for state-restoration hypotheses: tired, stressed, sleep-disturbed, inflammatory, or recovery-state users may report clearer cognition even when rested-baseline cognitive enhancement is unproven. The wiki should keep these candidates visible, but route them through product identity, state challenge, and safety-stop rules before any ranking.
