# Monoamine Reuptake Anorectic Stimulants Cognition Watchlist

This page covers tesofensine and nearby monoamine-reuptake anorectic / stimulant chemicals where public interest in focus, energy, appetite suppression, or brain fog can outrun direct cognition evidence.

The class-level lesson is simple: appetite, wakefulness, and catecholamine target engagement can feel cognitive, but they are not the same as durable cognitive improvement.

For classic sympathomimetic diet-pill stimulants such as [[phentermine]], see [[sympathomimetic-anorectic-stimulant-cognition-watchlist]]. For nonstimulant ADHD pipeline chemicals that overlap mechanistically but are not anorectic-first, see [[nonstimulant-adhd-pipeline-attention-chemicals-watchlist]]. For SNDRI / triple-reuptake antidepressant and analgesic comparators such as [[ansofaxine-toludesvenlafaxine]], [[liafensine]], [[amitifadine]], [[gsk372475]], [[csti-500]], and [[nefopam]], see [[triple-reuptake-inhibitor-cognition-watchlist]].

## Why Tesofensine Gets Its Own Page

Tesofensine is currently the loudest compound in this branch, including the common misspelling "tesonfensine." It has formal phase 2 obesity efficacy, PET DAT occupancy, old AD/PD CNS exposure, current sponsor obesity-status pages, and a visible 2025-2026 Reddit signal around executive function, energy, appetite, and long-half-life side effects. Tesomet remains useful as cardiovascular-mitigation context, but Saniona's 2026 page says its phase 2b rare-obesity trials are voluntarily paused due funding limitations.^[raw/articles/pubmed-18950853-tesofensine-obesity-phase2.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-24239329-tesofensine-dat-occupancy-pet.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-18474731-tesofensine-advanced-parkinson-advans.md]^[raw/articles/pmc-2000606-tesofensine-alzheimers-pk-phase2.md]^[raw/articles/saniona-2026-tesofensine-pipeline-status.md]^[raw/articles/saniona-2026-tesomet-pipeline-status.md]^[raw/articles/reddit-2025-2026-tesofensine-focus-energy-threads.md]

The obesity data are real but not cognitive: 203 adults with obesity had dose-dependent weight loss over 24 weeks, and a 32-man study showed appetite/satiety effects over 2 weeks. The practical failure mode is mistaking appetite suppression, less food noise, caffeine reduction, or stimulation for improved cognition.^[raw/articles/pubmed-18950853-tesofensine-obesity-phase2.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-20479765-tesofensine-energy-metabolism-appetite.md]

## Similar-Chemical Read

[[sibutramine]] is the class warning: SCOUT found more composite cardiovascular outcome events with sibutramine than placebo in a high-risk obesity population, and FDA's hidden-ingredient notice states sibutramine was removed from the U.S. market in 2010 for safety reasons and can substantially increase BP/HR.^[raw/articles/pubmed-20818901-sibutramine-scout-cv-outcomes.md]^[raw/articles/fda-2015-sibutramine-hidden-ingredient-withdrawn.md]

[[brasofensine]] and [[mazindol]] show why "dopamine reuptake" is not automatically a cognition claim. Brasofensine was tolerable in eight PD patients but did not change UPDRS motor performance, while mazindol's old and modern ER evidence is wakefulness / narcolepsy oriented, including a 67-person phase 2 SLEEP abstract with ESS and cataplexy signals.^[raw/articles/pubmed-11847938-brasofensine-parkinsons-levodopa.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-525256-mazindol-narcolepsy.md]^[raw/articles/sleep-2023-mazindol-er-narcolepsy-phase2.md]

[[nomifensine]] is useful only as a historical caution: PubChem records worldwide withdrawal in 1986 due acute hemolytic anemia with intravascular hemolysis.^[raw/articles/pubchem-2026-nomifensine-withdrawn-hemolytic-anemia.md]

[[solriamfetol]] is the cleanest modern comparator because it is approved for sleepiness disorders and has a direct OSA-with-EDS cognition trial. That does not validate tesofensine, but it gives the branch a more mature benchmark for wakefulness-state restoration claims.^[raw/articles/dailymed-sunosi-solriamfetol-tablet-label.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39528111-solriamfetol-sharp-osa-cognition.md]

## Interpretation Rules

1. Do not treat appetite suppression or less food noise as cognition proof.
2. For focus claims, compare against [[methylphenidate]], [[modafinil]], and [[solriamfetol]] before calling the effect unusually clean.
3. For brain-fog claims, separate stimulant masking, caffeine reduction, caloric deficit, sleep loss, dehydration, anxiety reduction, and ADHD/neurodivergent baseline.
4. For safety, treat long half-life, HR/BP, insomnia, mood flattening, serotonergic/dopaminergic stacking, cardiovascular history, renal function, and product identity as first-order variables.
5. For sports or tested-athlete contexts, route tesofensine through [[regulatory-and-sports-visibility-map]] because the 2026 WADA list names it in S6.B specified stimulants.^[raw/articles/wada-2026-stimulant-list-afinils-pemoline-tesofensine.md]
6. Route all monoamine-reuptake anorectic anecdotes through [[safety-interaction-matrix-for-multi-modal-stacks]] before treating appetite, wakefulness, executive-function, or brain-fog changes as molecule-specific cognition evidence.
