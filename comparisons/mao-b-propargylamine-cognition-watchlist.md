# MAO-B Propargylamine Cognition Watchlist

This page routes MAO-B / propargylamine cognition claims across marketed Parkinson drugs, failed or mixed AD/MCI programs, reversible-MAO-B frontier candidates, and nootropic-community anecdotes. It complements [[pharmacology-comparator-map]], [[anecdote-led-chemical-heat-map]], and [[safety-interaction-matrix-for-multi-modal-stacks]].

## Current Read

The class is `low/state-bound`, not healthy-adult cognition-positive. A 2026 network meta-analysis of 13 RCTs in Parkinson disease found rasagiline improved global cognition versus placebo, while selegiline and safinamide did not; no MAO-B inhibitor significantly improved specific domains including attention, executive function, memory, language, or visuospatial ability.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41524120-maob-inhibitors-pd-cognition-network-meta.md]

The practical split is:

| Candidate | Status / mechanism | Cognition signal | Main caveat | Route |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [[selegiline]] / deprenyl | Prescription irreversible MAO-B inhibitor; metabolites and dose-selectivity matter | Short-lived AD meta-analysis signal plus PD history and moderate nootropic lore | Interactions, tyramine/MAO selectivity, serotonergic/opioid/stimulant stack risk | Disease-context and anecdote anchor, not casual nootropic |
| [[rasagiline]] | Prescription selective MAO-B inhibitor for PD | Best PD signal in the class: small attention/executive RCT and 2026 global-cognition meta signal; AD FDG-PET proof-of-concept without ADAS-Cog/ADL benefit | Prescription interactions, serotonin syndrome, BP, sleep attacks, impulse/hallucination risks | Most relevant marketed comparator |
| [[safinamide]] | Reversible MAO-B inhibitor plus glutamatergic/non-dopaminergic claims; adjunct to levodopa/carbidopa | Small exploratory PD executive-control signal; separate 6-month PD study was cognition-neutral while improving fatigue/apathy/off-time | Broad contraindications with MAOIs, opioids, serotonergic drugs, stimulants, dextromethorphan; retinal-pathology warning | PD wearing-off / executive-state hypothesis |
| [[ladostigil]] | Investigational propargylamine / cholinesterase-MAO logic | 3-year MCI phase 2 did not delay AD conversion or improve cognitive/function scales, despite atrophy hint | Development miss; biomarker/atrophy hint cannot substitute for cognition endpoint | Negative/mixed MCI comparator |
| [[sembragiline]] | Potent long-acting reversible MAO-B inhibitor | Strong PET target engagement but phase 2 AD primary and secondary cognition/function endpoints missed | Target engagement did not translate | Negative target-engagement comparator |
| [[kds2010-tisolagiline]] | Reversible selective MAO-B inhibitor; astrocyte-GABA hypothesis | APP/PS1 mouse learning/memory rescue; active AD/MCI phase 2a protocol, no human efficacy result yet | Preclinical/protocol-only as of 2026-06-12; exclusions show interaction risk | Live watch item |
| [[lazabemide]] | Legacy reversible MAO-B inhibitor | Early untreated-PD ADL/motor-context trial; no cognition endpoint | Never became cognition evidence | Legacy class comparator |

## What Changed Since 2024

- The 2026 network meta-analysis makes rasagiline the only MAO-B inhibitor with a significant Parkinson global-cognition signal, while explicitly limiting domain-specific claims across the class.^[raw/articles/pubmed-41524120-maob-inhibitors-pd-cognition-network-meta.md]
- KDS2010/tisolagiline now has an active registered phase 2a AD/MCI trial with cognitive, functional, and biomarker endpoints, but no posted human result yet.^[raw/articles/clinicaltrials-2026-kds2010-ad-phase2a.md]
- Safinamide has small PD executive/non-motor studies, but one cognition-neutral prospective study and one uncontrolled/exploratory executive study keep the branch below rasagiline for formal cognition interpretation.^[raw/articles/pubmed-34031800-safinamide-nonmotor-cognition-pd.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-33068177-safinamide-executive-functions-pd.md]
- Sembragiline remains a useful caution: target engagement was strong, but the AD phase 2 cognition/function endpoints missed.^[raw/articles/pubmed-27633250-sembragiline-maob-pet-target-engagement.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-28550255-sembragiline-mayflower-road-ad-phase2.md]

## Safety Rules

1. Treat all marketed drugs in this branch as prescription-interaction compounds, not supplements.
2. Do not combine MAO-B inhibitors by analogy with stimulants, serotonergic drugs, opioids, dextromethorphan, St. John's wort, linezolid, or other MAOIs without label-level medical oversight.
3. Separate disease-state attention/executive rescue from healthy-adult enhancement.
4. Separate target engagement, FDG-PET, atrophy, or astrocyte-GABA biomarkers from objective cognitive endpoint success.
5. Keep anecdote at `low` confidence unless controlled evidence in the same phenotype supports the same claim.
6. Route all MAO-B self-experiment or stack anecdotes through [[safety-interaction-matrix-for-multi-modal-stacks]] before ranking. The key confounds are stimulant history, phenethylamine or catecholamine precursor use, serotonergic drugs, dextromethorphan/AUVELITY, [[methylene-blue]], tyramine/BP, sleep attacks, insomnia, withdrawal/washout, and disease-state dopamine restoration.
