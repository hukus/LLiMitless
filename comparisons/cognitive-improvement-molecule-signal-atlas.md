# Cognitive Improvement Molecule Signal Atlas

This is the molecule-first map for the wiki. It excludes devices, cognitive training, sleep protocols, light, ultrasound, and other non-molecular interventions except when a molecule, extract, nutrient, peptide, or drug is the active unit. For routing logic, use [[molecule-intake-triage-table]] first; for cross-family ranking, use [[cognition-intervention-master-table]] and [[most-promising-methods-for-cognition]].

## Signal Rules

`Medicinal signal` means controlled human, clinical, regulatory, or preclinical evidence, not community enthusiasm. Labels: `high`, `moderate`, `low`, `preclinical-only`, `negative`, or `risk-first`.

`Anecdote signal` uses the wiki's standard labels: `weak`, `moderate`, `strong`, or `very-high`. Anecdote can make a molecule worth tracking, but it cannot raise a medicinal signal by itself.

## Practical Readout

The wiki is now broad enough that the next limiting factor is not source discovery; it is molecule-level normalization. Every candidate should enter through three gates: formal medicinal signal, anecdotal signal, and safety/sourcing burden. The strongest practical molecules still look state- or phenotype-bound rather than universally cognition-enhancing: caffeine/modafinil-class comparators for wakefulness, creatine for energy-buffered stress states, methylene blue as a narrow consolidation/redox lead, PDE4 as a phenotype-bound plasticity lane, Cerebrolysin as the peptide exception, and standardized botanicals as product-specific low-to-moderate candidates.
