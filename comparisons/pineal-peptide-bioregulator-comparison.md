# Pineal Peptide Bioregulator Comparison

This page splits the older peptide-bioregulator cluster into four practical buckets: `pineal/longevity-first` peptides, `legacy recovery/anxiolytic peptides`, `cortex/ischemia recovery peptides`, and `the clinically grounded exception` represented by Cerebrolysin. The point is not to rank a supplement stack. It is to keep identity, evidence tier, and sourcing risk separate so the wiki does not collapse a very mixed literature into one nootropic bucket.^[raw/articles/pubmed-24738258-neuroprotective-peptides-age.md]^[raw/articles/fda-bulk-drug-substances-peptide-risk-2026.md]

## What Changed Since 2024

- Epitalon gained a newer human cell-line telomere paper in 2025, plus a 2022 mouse-oocyte aging paper that pushes the antioxidant / mitochondrial-aging lane further without creating a cognition endpoint.^[raw/articles/pubmed-40908429-epitalon-telomere-length-human-cell-lines.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-35413689-epitalon-mouse-oocyte-aging.md]
- Two older intranasal rat papers make the epitalon mechanism layer more concrete without changing the human-evidence grade: one reports stress-state pineal activation and one reports rapid neocortical firing after dosing.^[raw/articles/pubmed-12500171-epitalon-pineal-secretion-stress-rats.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-17955380-epitalon-neocortex-neuron-activity.md]
- FDA's 2026 bulk-drug-substance page explicitly flags Semax, Selank, and Epitalon-adjacent research peptides as low-data or risk-listed compounds, and the 2026 PCAC agenda separately puts Epitalon- and Semax-related bulk substances on the advisory-committee track. A Jan 2026 warning letter also uses Epithalon in compounded-product label examples, which is a sourcing-quality signal rather than efficacy evidence.^[raw/articles/fda-bulk-drug-substances-peptide-risk-2026.md]^[raw/articles/fda-2026-advisory-epitalon-semax.md]^[raw/articles/fda-warning-letter-genogenix-epithalon-2026.md]
- Cortexin is no longer just a review-level name in this branch: older human studies now include an open brain-ischemia trial, a multicenter randomized chronic-ischemia study, and an alcohol-encephalopathy study, while a 2026 mechanistic paper adds fresh preclinical support.^[raw/articles/pubmed-25403301-cortexin-brain-ischemia-open-clinical-trial.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-30335070-cortexin-chronic-cerebral-ischemia-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-20517210-cortexin-alcohol-encephalopathy-polyneuropathy.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-40783844-cortexin-ischemia-reperfusion-2026.md]
- Cerebrolysin picked up a 2024 anaphylaxis case report and a 2024 aMCI comparative study, so its human branch is now both stronger and more safety-bounded than the rest of the cluster.^[raw/articles/pubmed-39055722-cerebrolysin-anaphylaxis-case-report.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-39435777-cerebrolysin-mci-dementia-prevention.md]
- Pinealon and Kortagen still do not have a modern human cognition layer in the current sweep, so they remain mostly search-structure nodes rather than efficacy winners.^[raw/articles/pubmed-21978084-pinealon-cell-viability.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-22567179-pinealon-hyperhomocysteinemia-rat-offspring.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-24738258-neuroprotective-peptides-age.md]

## Identity Normalization

The cleanest sequence-anchored nodes in the current wiki sweep are Epithalon/Epitalon (`Ala-Glu-Asp-Gly` / AEDG) and Pinealon (`Glu-Asp-Arg`). The rest of the cluster is easier to compare as legacy drug or bioregulator classes than as single public sequences: Semax is a regulatory peptide used in older Russian recovery-oriented studies, Selank is its anxiolytic companion peptide, Cortexin is a cerebral-cortex bioregulator / polypeptide preparation with older human ischemia studies, Kortagen is a legacy short-peptide label that often appears as `cortagen` in PubMed, and Cerebrolysin is a porcine brain-derived peptide preparation with the clearest human recovery evidence.^[raw/articles/pubmed-24738258-neuroprotective-peptides-age.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-25403301-cortexin-brain-ischemia-open-clinical-trial.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-30335070-cortexin-chronic-cerebral-ischemia-rct.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-11276314-cortagen-sciatic-nerve-regeneration.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-11552768-cerebrolysin-alz-28-week-rct.md]

The naming layer matters because the literature often trades on family resemblance rather than strict identity. That is especially true for Epithalamin versus Epithalon, and for the cortex/bioregulator literature around Cortexin and Kortagen.^[raw/articles/pubmed-14523363-epithalamin-human-life.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-15452611-epithalamin-melatonin-elderly.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-24738258-neuroprotective-peptides-age.md]

## Practical Read

If the question is "which of these look like genuine human cognition candidates?", Cerebrolysin is the only one that already has a durable human branch. If the question is "which of these are biologically interesting but still mostly translational?", Semax and Selank sit in the middle, with Semax more recovery-oriented and Selank more stress/anxiety-oriented. If the question is "which are mostly longevity or preclinical search nodes?", Epithalon, Pinealon, and Kortagen still dominate that bucket, while Cortexin now sits in the narrower ischemia/recovery lane with some real human history but no modern standardized cognition program.^[raw/articles/pubmed-14523363-epithalamin-human-life.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-15452611-epithalamin-melatonin-elderly.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-22451889-epithalamin-15-year-followup.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-21978084-pinealon-cell-viability.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-22567179-pinealon-hyperhomocysteinemia-rat-offspring.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-25403301-cortexin-brain-ischemia-open-clinical-trial.md]^[raw/articles/pubmed-30335070-cortexin-chronic-cerebral-ischemia-rct.md]

The other useful cut is regulatory. Semax, Selank, and Epitalon are now clearly in the FDA's low-data / research-peptide warning surface, and Epitalon plus Semax are also on the 2026 advisory-committee agenda. Cortexin and Kortagen do not have the same explicit FDA bulk-substance posture in the current sweep, but that does not rescue them from sourcing uncertainty. The evidence grade and the sourcing grade have to stay separate.^[raw/articles/fda-bulk-drug-substances-peptide-risk-2026.md]^[raw/articles/fda-2026-advisory-epitalon-semax.md]

## Open Contradictions

- The family resemblance is strong, but the identity layer is not equally clean across the whole cluster.
- Public nootropic marketing often treats "peptide bioregulator" as a single class, while the wiki needs to keep human exposure, mechanism, and cognition evidence separate.
- Cortexin has real regional human recovery data, but the endpoint mix is still ischemia and rehabilitation, not healthy-adult enhancement.
- Kortagen is especially muddy in the current sweep: searchable as a legacy bioregulator label, but not yet normalized into a modern human-evidence node.
- Cerebrolysin is the exception that proves the rule: the human branch exists, but it is disease- and recovery-oriented rather than a broad enhancer claim.
